Key Takeaways
Tilly’s delivered a sharply narrower loss as comparable sales and margins improved.
Revenue rose year over year.
Management expects slower but continued sales growth and a potential return to profitability in the next quarter.

Tilly’s (NYSE:TLYS) narrowed its quarterly loss as store and online sales jumped and profit margins improved. The specialty retailer of casual apparel, footwear, and accessories serves teens and young adults with a mix of third-party and proprietary brands. Reporting its first quarter fiscal 2026 results on June 3, 2026 (for the thirteen weeks ended May 2), the company outperformed its own objectives but signaled that the exceptional pace of growth may slow in coming quarters. Overall, the quarter marked another step forward as Tilly’s moves closer to full-year profitability.

Notable developments
Comparable net sales, which include both physical stores and e-commerce, increased by 22.9%, benefiting from strong new product and inventory management.
Gross profit margin improved 9.1 percentage points to 28.9%, reflecting an ongoing focus on selling products at full price and reducing discounts.
Inventory fell 6.4% year over year, supporting better margin performance while store count declined as part of a real estate optimization strategy.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects revenue for the next quarter to be between $154 million and $160 million, with comp sales growth moderating to 6%–10%. Net income is forecasted to swing positive, signaling a return to profitability if momentum holds. Investors will want to watch whether Tilly’s can maintain sales and margin gains as comp growth is expected to slow.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure was at the high end of guidance, driven by robust gains in both brick-and-mortar and e-commerce channels. Physical store comparable sales increased 20.8%, while the online business grew more than 30%. E-commerce accounted for nearly 23% of revenue, up from 20% a year ago.

Gross margin expanded thanks to improved full-price selling and fresher inventory. Product margins rose 4.0 percentage points, outpacing management’s target for the period. The company attributed these gains to inventory that is newer and more aligned to current customer demand.

Store count declined by 7.6% year over year as Tilly’s continued to close underperforming locations. Despite operating fewer stores, both total and comp store sales rose, reflecting greater productivity from the remaining footprint. Inventory reductions also supported the company’s profitability strategy and helped minimize markdowns.

What Tilly’s does

Tilly’s is a specialty retailer selling casual apparel, footwear, and accessories in physical stores and online, targeting teens and young adults.
