Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management described the current business growth as sustainable and highlighted continued momentum in subsea project awards and client diversification. However, it did not issue formal forward guidance for margins or profit. Investors should monitor whether expense levels decrease as expected in coming quarters and if gross margin rebounds toward previous targets of the high 30s percent range.

Quarterly Highlights

The company secured several notable contracts, including a manufacturing deal with an international offshore firm, a subsea distribution system assignment for a Gulf of America project, and a monitoring contract in West Africa. Together, these awards reflected Koil’s push to become more integrated and gain exposure to clients beyond its traditional base.

Contribution from new clients jumped to about a quarter of total revenue, compared to ten percent for all of fiscal 2025. This marks significant progress in Koil’s effort to lower customer concentration and diversify its revenue streams.

On the cost side, expenses for the quarter were higher due to increased staffing and one-time charges for marketing and audit work. Management noted these costs will not recur in upcoming periods, though the overall expense base and pressure on margins remain important points for investors to watch going forward.
