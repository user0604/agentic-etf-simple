Key Takeaways
Revenue and profit grew strongly, led by enterprise segment gains.
Earnings per share increased 29%, with improved margins.
Management gave no outlook, and external risks remain.

Enterprise networking equipment specialist Ubiquiti (NYSE:UI) posted strong year-over-year growth fueled by demand in its enterprise division, while service provider sales slipped. The quarter ending March 2026, released on May 8, 2026, featured better profitability and greater cost control, though revenues fell slightly from the previous period because of earlier holiday-driven sales. Overall, the quarter underlined business strength but left the near-term picture uncertain as management did not provide guidance.

Notable Developments
Enterprise Technology revenue grew 22.6% year over year, reaching $717.9 million, but declined sequentially from the previous quarter.
Gross margin improved by 2.5 percentage points from last year due to a shift in product mix and lower inventory and shipping charges, partially offset by higher tariffs.
The company declared a quarterly dividend of $0.80 per share and reported repaying all outstanding debt, resulting in low interest expense.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide an updated financial forecast for future quarters, citing ongoing uncertainties such as tariffs and supply chain factors. The focus remains on regular dividend payments and controlling costs. Investors should monitor the sustainability of enterprise-driven revenue growth and management's handling of external risks in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Enterprise segment results led the period, with sales rising over 22% from the previous year, reflecting demand for networking platforms and success in North America. However, the Service Provider segment saw sales fall over 10% year over year, showing ongoing challenges as demand softened compared to both the previous quarter and the same quarter last fiscal year.

Profitability improved, with gross margin up by 2.5 percentage points compared to twelve months ago. Cost controls and a better mix of products sold helped, though tariffs continued to weigh on costs, and operating expenses climbed since last year because of growth in research and development and sales support.

The company strengthened its balance sheet by eliminating debt during the quarter and maintaining a steady share count. The dividend was declared at $0.80 per share, with interest expense dropping sharply alongside the debt repayment.

What Ubiquiti Does

Ubiquiti provides networking and wireless communication equipment and software for enterprises, service providers, and consumers worldwide.
