Notable Developments
The company achieved four consecutive quarters of service revenue growth, with quarterly service revenue up 5% from the prior year.
Gross margin declined due to a mix shift toward usage-based business, which now makes up a growing share of total revenue.
Operating cash flow rose significantly to $14.4 million as the company continued to pay down its debt.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects total revenue between $727 million and $747 million for fiscal 2027 and expects non-GAAP gross margin to decrease further as usage-based business expands. Guidance points to a cautious approach, with flat to modest revenue growth and some ongoing margin pressure. Investors should closely monitor gross margin trends and the performance of usage-driven product lines in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue topped the company's prior guidance range, led by strong growth in usage-based segments that leverage artificial intelligence and communications platform-as-a-service capabilities. The company’s newest offerings -- including the 8x8 Engage customer experience suite and AI Studio for workflow automation -- extended its platform innovation focus. Management stressed ongoing margin compression as usage-based revenue expands, but highlighted commitment to growing gross profit and cash flow through efficiency measures and product enhancements.

The growth in usage-based business was pronounced, with quarterly usage revenue up over 70% year over year. While this segment drove top-line gains, it also contributed to a decline in gross margin from 68% to 63% as these newer offerings carry structurally lower initial profitability. The company is investing in optimizing operations and platform functionality to address these margin challenges as part of its long-term strategy.

On the cash flow and balance sheet front, free cash flow accelerated sharply, supporting continued debt reduction. The company ended the quarter with $95.0 million in cash and cash equivalents following ongoing deleveraging, reflecting disciplined capital management amidst a period of rapid product transformation and industry competition.
