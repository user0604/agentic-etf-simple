Notable Developments
Net revenue decreased 21%, as management prioritized product refreshment over immediate customer bookings for the quarter.
Gross margin improved to 70.8% due to lower costs and a focus on higher-value offerings, suggesting operational discipline.
Operational cash burn narrowed by $0.6 million to $2.7 million, leaving nearly $98 million in cash and no debt at period end.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management provided no quantitative guidance but stated it is investing in sales leadership and modernizing demand generation to rebuild commercial momentum. The company emphasized that its efforts to refine and “productize” its solutions, especially AI-powered offerings, set it up for future growth. Investors should watch for any tangible sales acceleration or expanded customer wins as new hospitality products go to market through 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue for the period was significantly lower compared to the prior year, reflecting the company’s deliberate trade-off of slower near-term bookings growth to focus on platform and product improvements. Management explained this as part of “tightening the product foundation that our commercial growth depends on.”

On the cost side, gross margin jumped by nearly nineteen percentage points, largely due to reductions in cost of revenue and improvements in product delivery. The company’s research and development spending also increased, underlining a continued emphasis on technology and artificial intelligence innovation.

Cash management showed progress, as net cash used in operations improved to $2.7 million for the quarter, aided by reduced operating expenses. The company ended the period with a sizeable cash balance and no debt, supporting claims of financial capacity to maintain its strategic pivot.
