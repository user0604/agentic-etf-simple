Key Takeaways
Inovio Pharmaceuticals reported another quarter without product revenue, while advancing its lead drug candidate toward regulatory review.
Earnings per share improved to $(0.28), reflecting lower expenses and share count changes compared to prior year.
Management projects its available cash will fund operations beyond the expected FDA decision date for INO-3107.

Inovio Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ:INO) reported a holding pattern quarter, continuing its cost controls while working to secure future approval for its lead drug. The clinical-stage biotechnology company focuses primarily on DNA-based medicines, with INO-3107 for Recurrent Respiratory Papillomatosis (RRP) as its core asset. The first-quarter update, released May 13, 2026, covered results for fiscal 2026’s first period. Overall, the quarter brought continued progress on expense discipline but left key regulatory and commercial questions unresolved.

Notable developments
The Biologics License Application (BLA) for INO-3107 remains under FDA review with a decision target of October 30, 2026, though regulatory eligibility for accelerated approval remains uncertain.
Total operating expenses dropped 12.7% year over year, reflecting declines in both research and development and general administrative costs.
Following a $16.0 million equity raise in April, management now expects current cash and investments to fund operations into the first quarter of 2027.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management continues to highlight prudent spending and expects its available resources will last past the key FDA decision for INO-3107. However, it underscores that securing accelerated approval is critical for its commercial future. Investors should monitor regulatory feedback and resolution of the FDA’s eligibility question, as this will determine whether Inovio can launch its main product and begin generating revenue.

Quarterly highlights

Regulatory progress remained the top priority, with the company's lead candidate, INO-3107 (a DNA medicine for RRP), still awaiting FDA feedback on whether it qualifies for accelerated approval. The agency completed a mid-cycle review, but had not resolved key eligibility questions by quarter end. In the prior quarter, management had hoped for clarity by now, so this ongoing delay continues a pattern of regulatory limbo.

Financial discipline was evident, as total operating expenses declined year over year. Reduced spending came from lower compensation costs, reduced consultant fees, and lower inventory expenses, which collectively helped lower the quarterly net loss per share.

On the cash front, Inovio’s balance of $37.7 million at the end of March—excluding its recent $16 million equity raise—was lower than at the end of last year. Nevertheless, the combined cash position now gives management confidence that the company can operate through the first quarter of 2027, including past the scheduled FDA decision on INO-3107.

What Inovio Pharmaceuticals does

Inovio Pharmaceuticals develops DNA-based medicines and vaccines, targeting treatment and prevention of infectious diseases and cancers.
