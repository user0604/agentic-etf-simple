Conduent (NASDAQ:CNDT) posted continued profit improvements, though revenue again declined compared to a year ago. The global business process services provider benefited from higher performance in its Government and Transportation segments, helping offset an ongoing lag in Commercial, according to its release for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026. Overall, the quarter marked another step forward in margin expansion and cost savings, even as top-line growth remained elusive.

Notable developments

Adjusted EBITDA climbed to $49 million, up 32.4% year over year, lifting the margin to 6.8% from 4.9% as company-wide cost cuts continued.
Adjusted free cash flow improved by $59 million year over year, but stayed negative at $(15) million for the quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its priorities: driving efficiency, simplifying its business mix, and improving cash flow and profitability. Guidance for fiscal 2026 targets $2.8 to $2.9 billion in revenue and $160 to $190 million in adjusted EBITDA, while positive adjusted free cash flow remains a target for 2027. The critical factor for investors will be whether progress in Commercial segment results can support a return to revenue growth.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue continued to decline as the Commercial segment dragged on company-wide totals, but Government and Transportation segments both saw year-over-year growth. These two areas accounted for the gain, with the Government segment’s $10 million increase and Transportation’s $3 million rise reflecting consistent demand in public sector and mobility services.

Profitability measures showed clear improvement, with operating costs falling and margin gains outpacing recent quarters. Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 6.8%, and adjusted operating income turned positive to $2 million from $(11) million a year ago. Cost of services and selling, general, and administrative expenses shrank by a combined $60 million, supporting the margin expansion.

Management reported a higher annual contract value for new business at $114 million and grew the qualified sales pipeline to $3.5 billion. This signals improving future sales momentum, even though immediate free cash flow and net income remained negative for the period.
