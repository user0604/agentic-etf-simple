Management outlook and what to watch

Management now expects full-year fiscal 2026 revenue between $1.466 billion and $1.474 billion and has raised its non-GAAP operating income target. Investors should watch for the ongoing impact of currency headwinds and whether momentum with large customers can offset flatter results in smaller segments.

Quarterly highlights

The number of paid customers contributing more than $500,000 in annual recurring revenue increased to 99, a 74% rise from the prior year period. This group now represents 6% of total annual recurring revenue, up from 5% last year.

The rollout of the AI Work Platform marks a significant product milestone, combining new native AI agents with a seats-plus-credits pricing approach. Management believes these innovations will help automate more complex workflows and improve efficiency for clients.

Despite flat operating margins and higher revenue, adjusted free cash flow declined 6% year over year to $102.8 million. Management attributed this to increased AI investment, currency impacts from a strong Israeli shekel, and share buybacks reducing cash balances.
