Hotel operator H World Group (NASDAQ:HTHT) expanded its network and grew revenue, but earnings dipped as costs increased. The company operates a large portfolio of hotel brands from budget to luxury, mainly in China and Europe. Its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, released May 15, 2026, showed progress on growth targets, offset by margin and profit pressures in both domestic and international businesses. Overall, the quarter highlighted both momentum in new openings and emerging cost and earnings challenges.

Notable Developments
The hotel network grew to 13,215 properties, with 537 new hotels opened in China and 360 net adds this quarter.
Manachised and franchised hotel revenue jumped 20.3%, supporting the company’s ongoing shift away from directly owned and leased hotels.
Despite higher revenue, net income fell 8.6% as costs and selling, general and administrative expenses increased.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its goal to open 2,200 to 2,300 hotels in fiscal 2026 and restated its asset-light expansion focus. No updates were made to revenue or margin guidance. Investors should watch how the company manages rising costs and the performance of mature hotels in China, as well as further progress in its international segment turnaround.

Quarterly Highlights

The company extended its network to over 13,200 hotels, nearly all under its high-margin manachised and franchised model. China accounted for the vast majority of openings and rooms, while international expansion delivered slower growth and faced recent profitability challenges.

Revenue growth was driven by a 20.3% rise in manachised and franchised hotel income, offsetting a small decline in leased and owned hotels. However, overall profitability was impacted, as net income dropped due to cost increases and higher investments in marketing and management.

Key operational metrics such as revenue per available room (RevPAR) grew modestly in both China (up 3.0%) and international markets (up 5.0%), but “same-hotel” RevPAR across mature Chinese properties slipped and occupancy rates declined slightly. This signals that while network growth remains robust, established hotels are experiencing softer demand or pricing power.

H World Group is a major hotel operator managing a portfolio of brands spanning budget to luxury segments, with a primary footprint in China and a growing international presence.
