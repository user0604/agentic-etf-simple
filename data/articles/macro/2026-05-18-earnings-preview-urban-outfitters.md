Earnings Preview: Urban Outfitters

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Urban Outfitters (NASDAQ:URBN), a specialty retailer focused on apparel, accessories, and home goods for young adults, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 after the market closes on Wednesday, May 20, 2026.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

Urban Outfitters posted record results for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, with total revenue rising 10% to $1.8 billion and adjusted earnings per share up 38%. These results beat analyst estimates for both revenue and EPS. All retail brands delivered positive comparable sales, and the company saw especially strong growth from its Nuuly apparel rental subscription and the FP Movement activewear line. Gross profit margin improved to 33.3%, driven by lower markdowns and better occupancy leverage, despite ongoing tariff pressures. Operating income increased 27% year over year, and net income reached $131 million. The Urban Outfitters brand returned to profitability for the full year, a notable turnaround from prior losses.

Positives
Broad-based sales growth across all brands. All retail segment brands posted positive comparable sales, showing strong consumer demand and effective merchandising strategies.
Nuuly subscription business delivered rapid expansion. Nuuly’s revenue grew 43% year over year, with over 120,000 new average active subscribers, highlighting the growing demand for apparel rental.
Improved gross profit margin despite tariff headwinds. The company managed to increase its gross margin by over 100 basis points, thanks to lower markdowns and better control over occupancy and delivery costs.
Urban Outfitters brand returned to profitability. After several years of losses, the core Urban Outfitters brand achieved modest profitability, supported by strong sales in both North America and Europe.
Negatives
Tariffs continued to pressure product margins. Increased tariffs reduced gross and operating profit rates by approximately 75 basis points in the quarter, and the future impact remains uncertain due to changing trade policies.
SG&A expenses grew faster than sales. Selling, general, and administrative costs increased 9%, mainly due to higher store payroll and marketing investments, which could limit future margin expansion if not offset by higher sales.
Weather-related store sales softness late in the quarter. Extreme weather events, particularly on the East Coast, negatively impacted store traffic and sales in February, though digital sales remained stable.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Comparable sales trends by brand and channel: Watch for whether Urban Outfitters can maintain high single-digit comparable sales in its namesake brand, and if Anthropologie and Free People continue their positive momentum, especially given recent weather disruptions.
Gross margin performance amid tariff changes: Management expects gross margin to decline by 25 to 50 basis points year over year in Q1, mainly due to higher tariffs. Any updates on tariff refunds or mitigation could affect profitability.
Nuuly subscriber and revenue growth: Nuuly is expected to continue mid-double-digit revenue growth. Progress here will indicate ongoing consumer appetite for rental and the success of marketing investments.
Impact and timing of technology investments: The company is increasing spending on technology, including agentic artificial intelligence tools, to speed up product cycles and improve execution. Investors should look for commentary on the benefits and costs of these initiatives.
Store expansion and inventory management: Urban Outfitters plans to open 57 new stores in FY2027, with a focus on FP Movement, Free People, and Anthropologie. Updates on store openings, inventory levels, and capital spending will be important for assessing growth and risk.
