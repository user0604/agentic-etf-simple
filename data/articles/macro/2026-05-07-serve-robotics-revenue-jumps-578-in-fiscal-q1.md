Serve Robotics (NASDAQ:SERV) delivered rapid top-line growth in its latest quarter but reported operating losses that outpaced its sales gains. The company, a provider of AI-driven autonomous delivery robots for restaurants, retailers, and healthcare facilities, released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period showed accelerating expansion and product uptake, but profitability remains a significant challenge.

Notable developments
Revenue climbed 578% year over year to $3.0 million, driven by expansion into 44 cities and the integration of Diligent Robotics.
Daily active robots increased more than tenfold, reaching 812 units, as the company focused on boosting productivity from its existing fleet rather than expanding the number of robots deployed.
Cash and cash equivalents fell to $47.1 million from $197.8 million a year ago, reflecting ongoing high investment and cash burn as the company scales operations.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management maintained its full-year revenue guidance of about $26 million and continued to invest in operational efficiency and recurring revenue streams. The main watchpoint is whether the company can improve gross margin and reduce operating losses before additional funding is needed, as cash burn remains high compared to revenue.

Quarterly highlights

The acquisition of Diligent Robotics marked Serve's entry into healthcare, with management noting a growing hospital pipeline and integration proceeding as planned. This step diversifies revenue and builds on the company’s technology platform.

Just under half of Serve's total sales were recurring revenue in the quarter, while software services contributed approximately one-third of revenue.

While the number of robots deployed is holding at around 2,000, management is prioritizing higher utilization and productivity per robot. Daily supply hours and active robot metrics showed sharp gains, indicating improved operational use of the fleet.

What Serve Robotics does

Serve Robotics develops and operates autonomous robots that use artificial intelligence for contactless deliveries across restaurant, retail, and healthcare sectors.
