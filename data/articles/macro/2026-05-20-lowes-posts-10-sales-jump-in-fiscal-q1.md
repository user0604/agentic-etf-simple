Lowe's Companies (NYSE:LOW) started fiscal 2026 with strong revenue gains but saw tighter margins due to recent acquisitions. The company, one of North America's largest home improvement retailers, released its earnings for the quarter ended May 1, 2026, on May 20, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed healthy sales and progress in key growth areas, while profit growth lagged behind revenue expansion.

Notable developments
Total sales rose 10.3% as the company benefited from integrating new Pro-focused acquisitions, FBM and ADG.
Gross margin declined 0.7 percentage points, as mix from lower-margin wholesale businesses weighed on profitability.
Online sales grew 15.5% during the period, outpacing overall sales growth and supporting the Total Home strategy.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, forecasting total sales between $92 billion and $94 billion, and adjusted operating margin of 11.6% to 11.8%. It continues to expect flat to 2% comparable sales growth. The most important factor to monitor next will be how quickly the company’s Pro and online segment gains translate into improved profitability as the year progresses.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure grew at a double-digit rate year over year. Acquisitions of FBM and ADG drove much of this increase, particularly in Pro customer and commercial categories, showing management’s focus on expanding the business beyond traditional do-it-yourself retail.

Despite the sales boost, gross and operating margins shrank compared to the prior year due to the lower-margin nature of recently acquired businesses and associated costs. Management had highlighted this margin dilution would be a short-term result of acquisition integration.

Digital initiatives delivered a standout performance, with online sales growth far above the overall company average. Investments in omnichannel retail and delivery services are providing tailwinds, even as some other areas remain flat. The dividend was raised 4.3% to $1.20 per share.

What Lowe's Companies does

Lowe’s Companies operates a large chain of home improvement stores and online retail platforms, serving both individual consumers and professional (Pro) customers with products, installation services, and building materials.
