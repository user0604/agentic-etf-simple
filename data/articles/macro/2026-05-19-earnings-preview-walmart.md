Earnings Preview: Walmart

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Walmart (NASDAQ:WMT), the largest U.S. retailer and global discount store operator, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Thursday, May 21, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

The fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 saw Walmart deliver strong revenue growth and improved profitability, with consolidated revenue rising 4.9% in constant currency. eCommerce was a standout, growing 24% globally, and adjusted operating income increased by 10.5%, outpacing sales growth. All three business segments reported profits growing faster than sales, supported by disciplined inventory management and gains in higher-margin areas such as advertising and membership. The company’s results for the quarter beat analyst estimates for both sales and earnings per share (EPS).

Comparable sales in the U.S. rose 4.3% for the year, with eCommerce contributing significantly to this growth. The company’s omnichannel strategy—integrating physical stores with digital platforms—continued to drive market share gains, especially among higher-income households. Inventory increased at a slower pace than sales, reflecting improved efficiency. Membership income and advertising revenue also grew at double-digit rates, further strengthening the company’s diversified profit streams.

Positives
eCommerce momentum and profitability: eCommerce sales grew 24% globally and 27% in the U.S., with the segment now consistently profitable and generating double-digit incremental margins. This reflects the success of Walmart’s digital investments and omnichannel approach.
Strong growth in advertising and membership income: Advertising revenue rose 37% globally, while membership income increased more than 15%, driven by Walmart+ and Sam’s Club. These higher-margin businesses now represent nearly one-third of operating income.
Disciplined inventory management: Inventory grew just 2.6%, about half the rate of sales growth, reducing markdown risk and improving working capital efficiency.
Operating income outpacing sales growth: Adjusted operating income grew 10.5%, more than double the rate of sales growth, indicating improved business mix and expense discipline.
Negatives
Rising operating expenses: Operating expenses as a percentage of net sales increased, driven by higher self-insured general liability claims and increased depreciation from capital investments. This partially offset gains from improved margins.
International segment margin pressure: Walmart International’s gross profit rate declined due to ongoing mix shifts and strategic investments in pricing and delivery, which weighed on segment operating income.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Sales and operating income growth compared to guidance: Management expects constant currency sales growth of 3.5% to 4.5% and operating income growth of 4% to 6% for Q1. Analyst consensus for sales is $174.8 billion, with EPS at $0.66, closely matching the company’s guidance range.
Progress on automation and technology investments: The company is at the peak of its annual spending on supply chain automation and store remodels, aiming for further gains in productivity and delivery speed. Watch for updates on the rollout and impact of these initiatives.
Growth in higher-margin businesses: Advertising and membership income are expected to remain key profit drivers. Investors should look for continued double-digit growth in these areas and any updates on the expansion of Walmart+ and new digital services.
Consumer spending trends and inflation impacts: Management noted resilient spending among higher-income households but ongoing pressure for lower-income consumers. Watch for commentary on how changing inflation and tax refund dynamics are affecting customer behavior and sales mix.
Status of Sparky and AI-driven commerce initiatives: Walmart’s AI-powered shopping assistant, Sparky, is driving higher average order values and engagement. Updates on customer adoption, rollout to new markets, and the impact on digital sales will be important for assessing future growth potential.
