Earnings Preview: Monro

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Monro (NASDAQ:MNRO), a company that operates retail stores providing automotive repair and tire services, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of its fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Monro reported sales of $293.4 million, which represented a 4.0 percent decrease from the prior year period, primarily due to the closure of 145 underperforming stores. However, comparable store sales from continuing locations increased by 1.2 percent. Net income was $11.1 million, with diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.35. On an adjusted basis, diluted EPS was $0.16, which beat analyst expectations, while revenue slightly missed consensus estimates. Gross margin improved by 60 basis points year over year to 34.9 percent, driven by lower material and occupancy costs, partially offset by higher technician labor costs. The company continued to execute its operational improvement plan, including the ongoing disposition of real estate from closed stores and reinvestment in marketing and customer acquisition initiatives.

Positives
Improved gross margin rate: Gross margin expanded to 34.9 percent, reflecting effective cost management and a favorable product mix, which is important for profitability in a challenging retail environment.
Positive comparable store sales growth: Comparable store sales rose 1.2 percent, showing that Monro’s efforts to drive customer traffic and ticket size are gaining traction despite broader consumer demand softness.
Continued inventory reduction: Inventory levels were reduced by over $7 million in the quarter, improving working capital efficiency and reducing carrying costs.
Progress on store closure and real estate plan: The company exited 32 leases and sold 20 owned locations in Q3, generating $17.3 million in proceeds and moving closer to completing the planned real estate dispositions.
Negatives
Total sales declined: Overall sales were down 4.0 percent year over year, mainly due to the impact of store closures, which continues to weigh on top-line growth.
Adjusted EPS down year over year: Adjusted diluted EPS fell to $0.16 from $0.19 in the prior year quarter, reflecting ongoing operational improvement costs and increased marketing spend.
Traffic remains challenged: Store traffic declined by mid-single digits, with average ticket size increases offsetting the drop in customer visits. Sustained traffic declines could limit future growth if not addressed.
Mixed regional performance: Strength was seen in the Northeast, but the West region underperformed, highlighting uneven results across Monro’s geographic footprint.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Comparable store sales trend and tax refund impact: Look for continued positive comparable store sales, especially as management expects higher consumer tax refunds to boost demand in the fourth quarter.
Gross margin performance versus guidance: Management expects full-year gross margin to be consistent with the prior year. Q4 results will need to show margin improvement to meet this target.
Status of remaining store and real estate dispositions: Investors should monitor the completion of the sale or exit of the last remaining closed store locations, as this affects cash flow and ongoing cost structure.
Effectiveness of marketing and customer acquisition initiatives: Watch for updates on the rollout and impact of digital marketing, CRM (customer relationship management) programs, and call center support, which are key to driving traffic and sales growth.
Labor cost pressures and operating expense management: Monitor how Monro manages wage inflation and other operating expenses, especially as it continues to invest in store improvements and marketing.
