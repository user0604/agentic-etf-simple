Key Takeaways
CorMedix delivered a business win with much higher revenue and profit, led by strong product adoption and portfolio expansion.
Revenue rose year over year, jumping 226%.
Management raised full-year guidance but cautioned about reimbursement and cost headwinds in later quarters.

CorMedix (NASDAQ:CRMD) surged well past its prior revenue outlook thanks to strong adoption of DefenCath and a full quarter of revenue from its newly integrated Melinta portfolio. The specialty pharmaceutical company focuses on infection prevention products for hospital and dialysis use. Reporting first quarter results on May 14, 2026, for the period ending March 31, management lifted its full-year targets but highlighted looming reimbursement and cost pressures. Overall, the quarter marked standout growth with notable non-recurring benefits boosting results.

Notable Developments
DefenCath sales made up $97.5 million of revenue, including a one-time $9.0 million favorable adjustment related to prior sales allowances.
The Melinta portfolio added $29.9 million in revenue for the quarter, making this the first full reporting period since the acquisition.
Total operating expenses increased 139%, largely due to the costs from integrating Melinta's business for the full quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

CorMedix raised its full-year revenue guidance to $325–$345 million and expects adjusted EBITDA of $115–$135 million for fiscal 2026. Management anticipates that first-half results will outpace the rest of the year because of pending changes in reimbursement policy for DefenCath. The main issue to follow is how DefenCath revenue fares once the TDAPA (Transitional Drug Add-On Payment Adjustment) expires and new pricing negotiations take effect.

Quarterly Highlights

DefenCath, an antimicrobial catheter lock for dialysis patients, continued to dominate sales but benefited from a non-recurring $9.0 million positive adjustment in the quarter. Management noted that future revenue may moderate as price and volume reflect changes after TDAPA reimbursement expires.

The Melinta portfolio, which includes anti-infectives such as Minocin IV and Vabomere, delivered steady revenue. Its integration diversified CorMedix's revenue stream, and results tracked with expected seasonal purchasing patterns, but management cautioned that future quarters may be more variable after inventory buildup in the first quarter.

Rising operating expenses tracked with the enlarged portfolio, with noteworthy increases in selling, marketing, and research costs. Management stated these costs reflected the combined business structure after the Melinta acquisition and noted that margin performance could fluctuate as revenue mix and reimbursement rates evolve.

What CorMedix Does

CorMedix develops and markets infection prevention drugs and devices, with its main product DefenCath serving hemodialysis patients in the United States.
