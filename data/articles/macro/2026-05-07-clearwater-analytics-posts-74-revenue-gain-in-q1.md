Key Takeaways
Clearwater Analytics reported robust revenue growth and new client wins in the first quarter.
Revenue rose 74% year over year.
The company did not provide guidance due to its pending acquisition.

Clearwater Analytics (NYSE:CWAN) delivered a strong quarter, marked by rapid expansion and new global clients. The leading software-as-a-service provider for investment accounting and analytics released its first quarter results on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period showed standout revenue momentum but also highlighted pressures on profitability and cash flow.

Notable Developments
Annualized recurring revenue reached $872 million, up 77% year over year, reflecting strong client growth and upselling.
Gross margin under GAAP declined from 73.3% to 65.8%.
Free cash flow dropped by more than half compared to last year, ending at $11.2 million, while total debt remained above $800 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide a formal outlook, citing its agreed acquisition by private equity. The main factor for investors to monitor is the completion of the transaction, which still depends on regulatory approval in Australia. The company has paused earnings conference calls and forward guidance for now.

Quarterly Highlights

New client wins in Asia-Pacific and structured credit markets, including Orange Investment Advisors and Dunamis Asset Management, helped push annualized recurring revenue to $872 million. These client additions show the platform’s adoption across both traditional institutions and hedge funds, supporting topline performance.

Despite the strong revenue figure, GAAP gross margins and profitability came under pressure as research, sales, and financing costs increased. The company reported a GAAP net loss for the period, influenced by higher expenses and interest related to recent acquisitions.

Cash flow became a key concern, with free cash flow declining sharply even as non-GAAP earnings metrics improved. High debt levels and shrinking cash balances highlight the importance of preserving financial flexibility as the business transitions to private ownership.

What Clearwater Analytics Does

Clearwater Analytics provides cloud-based software that automates investment accounting, regulatory compliance, and performance analytics for institutional investors, insurers, and corporations.
