Boyd Group Services Posts 28% Revenue Jump in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 9:11 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Boyd Group Services expanded its business through a major U.S. acquisition but faced weak organic sales growth.
Adjusted earnings per share rose sharply from the prior year quarter.
Management confirmed its long-term growth and margin targets but flagged integration and leverage as near-term priorities.

Boyd Group Services (NYSE:BGSI) delivered a business win by completing its acquisition of the Joe Hudson’s chain, which added significant scale but only modest organic growth in existing locations. The collision repair network operator, a leading consolidator across North America, reported results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (released May 13, 2026), showing strong headline revenue growth and improved adjusted profitability, fueled largely by this acquisition. Overall, the period was marked by extensive integration work and robust cost savings, offset by continued weakness in organic sales and higher debt levels.

Notable developments
The Joe Hudson’s acquisition, finalized in January, added 258 locations and contributed $168.0 million in sales during the quarter.
Adjusted profitability improved, with adjusted EBITDA margin climbing to 12.3%, driven by cost efficiencies from Project 360 and synergy benefits.
Net debt rose sharply to $2.0 billion, reflecting acquisition funding and higher finance costs, while the company raised its quarterly dividend by 2% to C$0.156 per share.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its long-term targets for annual same-store sales growth of 3–5% and a return to a 14% adjusted EBITDA margin. It is working to capture more integration synergies and savings from Project 360 throughout 2026. Investors should watch the pace of organic same-store sales improvements and how quickly the company can close the gap toward its profitability goals while managing the elevated debt load.

Quarterly highlights

The headline sales growth for the period was powered by the Joe Hudson’s acquisition, with new locations accounting for over $200 million in incremental revenue. Same-store sales growth was just 1.7%, held back by adverse winter weather and flat average repair costs; without the weather impact, growth would have approached 2.6%.

Profitability improved as adjusted EBITDA margin expanded by 2 percentage points, buoyed by $20 million in synergy and efficiency gains in the quarter. Ongoing transformation initiatives, including Project 360, have delivered $60 million in cost savings to date toward a $140 million multi-year goal.

The company’s balance sheet now reflects significant leverage, with net debt jumping to $2.0 billion after funding the Joe Hudson’s deal. Operating cash flow was robust, but integration and transformation costs, alongside higher interest expense, weighed on reported net earnings.

What Boyd Group Services does

Boyd Group Services operates one of North America’s largest networks of non-franchised collision repair centers, serving customers and insurance partners through repair, auto glass, and calibration services.
