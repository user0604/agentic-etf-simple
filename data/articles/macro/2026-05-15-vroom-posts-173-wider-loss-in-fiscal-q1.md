Notable developments
Interest income at UACC, the lending arm, dropped 4.4% to $42.5 million, while credit losses rose 22.1% to $24.8 million.
CarStory, the AI analytics and digital services platform, saw revenue fall sharply by 52.8% year over year to $1.33 million.
Management updated its 2026 adjusted net loss guidance to a range of $(25) million to $(30) million, signaling continued losses through the full year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains focused on technology investments such as dealer portals and credit engines. It noted early signs of improved loan performance but warned that losses would remain elevated in fiscal 2026. Investors should watch for updates on UACC’s credit losses and any improvement in adjusted net loss as key indicators of business health.

Quarterly highlights

UACC’s performance, now the main contributor to revenue, suffered from a combination of falling interest income and rising write-offs on loans. The adjusted net loss at UACC more than doubled compared to the prior year, reflecting ongoing credit challenges.

CarStory, the technology and analytics division, reported a steep revenue decline and swung to a loss, even as management cited new product features and technology investments. Operating expenses in CarStory dropped but did not offset the rapid revenue contraction.

On the capital front, Vroom ended the period with $14.5 million in cash and will rely on newly issued preferred stock and an upcoming convertible note exchange to help fund ongoing losses and planned platform improvements. Liquidity remains a top focus for management as it works to stabilize operations after restructuring.

What Vroom does

Vroom is an automotive technology company that focuses on vehicle financing for consumers through dealers and delivers analytics solutions for auto retailers.
