Livestock health company ImmuCell (NASDAQ:ICCC) posted a strong first quarter, benefiting from peak seasonal demand for its core animal health product. The company specializes in preventive solutions for calf diseases and released its quarterly results on May 14, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The period saw record sales and improved manufacturing, but management cautioned that future growth could slow as comparisons become more challenging.

Notable Developments
Product sales increased 28.4% to $10.4 million, led by growth in the First Defense product line and a reported 3 percentage point gain in U.S. market share.
Gross margin improved to 45.0%, attributed to higher manufacturing efficiency and better pricing, while free cash flow was $3.4 million.
The company resolved all disputes with its former contract manufacturer, securing a net payment of $2 million to fund capacity expansion efforts.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management indicated that growth rates are likely to slow for the rest of the year, as the company faces tougher comparisons after clearing last year’s backlog. It remains focused on investing in commercial infrastructure and expanding production capacity. The key metric to watch is revenue performance as seasonal demand softens and backlog effects fade.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales of First Defense, ImmuCell’s preventive treatment against calf scours (intestinal disease), accounted for most of the growth, with overall product sales rising more than a quarter year over year and U.S. market share up by 3 percentage points of animals treated. International sales dropped by 30%, highlighting the company’s reliance on domestic markets during the quarter.

Manufacturing output increased, with average monthly units produced rising from about 380,000 last year to over 450,000 in the period, reflecting successful scaling efforts. Cash flow improved as well, and the company ended the quarter with a cash position of $6.8 million.

Resolving a contract manufacturing dispute enabled ImmuCell to receive $2 million in net payments, which will support planned expansion for First Defense production. Expenses grew faster than revenue in some areas -- especially in sales, marketing, and administration -- suggesting that expense discipline will be important as growth begins to normalize.

What ImmuCell Does

ImmuCell develops, manufactures, and markets animal health products aimed at preventing and treating diseases in dairy and beef calves.
