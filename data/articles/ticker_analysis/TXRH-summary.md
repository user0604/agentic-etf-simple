TXRH Company Overview
Texas Roadhouse operates and franchises a network of casual dining restaurants that specialize in hand-cut steaks and made-from-scratch side dishes. The company generates revenue through food and beverage sales at its proprietary locations and by collecting royalty fees from franchise partners. A focus on dinner-only service and a unique partnership model for restaurant managers helps maintain operational consistency across its three distinct brands while building strong ties to local communities.

Texas Roadhouse Grows Sales as Tech Enhances Guest Experience

Texas Roadhouse runs casual dining restaurants known for hand-cut steaks and made-from-scratch sides in the U.S. and some global locations. The company often attracts high guest traffic, and even acquired some franchise units this quarter to keep growing its store count. Over the last five years, the stock returned 55.6%, underperforming the S&P 500 by 19.4 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $1,633.2 million (up 12.8% YoY)
EPS (diluted): $1.87 (up 9.6% YoY)
Comparable Sales Growth (All Company Concepts): 7.1% (up from 3.5% YoY)

Latest Developments

Texas Roadhouse raised its quarterly dividend by 10%, which rewards shareholders and shows ongoing confidence in its cash flow even as costs rise.
The company is rolling out technology, like handheld order tablets, aiming to improve service speed and guest experience, which could help battle inflation’s pressure on profits.
