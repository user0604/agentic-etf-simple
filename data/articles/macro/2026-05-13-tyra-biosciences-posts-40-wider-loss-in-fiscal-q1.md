Tyra Biosciences (NASDAQ:TYRA) moved forward with each of its core clinical studies and extended its financial runway, reflecting strong operational progress. Notable developments
Tyra dosed the first patient in its SURF303 trial for LG-UTUC, starting a potentially registrational Phase 2a/b study.
The company raised $147.9 million through an at-the-market equity program, extending its expected cash runway into the second half of 2028.
Research and development spending rose 34%, mostly from advancing multiple Phase 2 dabogratinib trials and related start-up activities.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects continued high investment as clinical programs progress, with no product revenue anticipated before initial regulatory submissions. Investors should closely watch for key upcoming clinical readouts, including three-month data from the SURF302 trial, expected in August 2026.

Quarterly highlights

Clinical activity continued to ramp up: Tyra began its first potentially registrational trial of oral dabogratinib in LG-UTUC and enrolled more than 20 patients in the IR NMIBC study, reinforcing momentum across all focus indications. Management reported that all clinical-stage trials remain on or ahead of previously forecasted timelines, with no significant safety events to date in the achondroplasia (ACH) cohort.

Cash and marketable securities increased sharply to $383.5 million, aided by proceeds from the at-the-market offering. This funding is expected to support operations and trial progression well into fiscal 2028, well beyond what management previously indicated.

General and administrative expenses grew by 23% as headcount increased to support expanded research and corporate functions, in line with ongoing pipeline scale-up and later-stage clinical development.
