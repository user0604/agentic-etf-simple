Hain Celestial Posts 13% Sales Drop in Fiscal Q3

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 7:20 AM

Key Takeaways
Hain Celestial Group’s turnaround progress is offset by ongoing declines in sales and margins.
Revenue fell 13.3% year over year for the quarter.
Management remains focused on cash flow, debt reduction, and stabilizing organic sales.

Hain Celestial Group (NASDAQ:HAIN)’s efforts to improve its finances are hampered by continuing sales declines. The natural and organic foods and personal care company is working through a multi-year turnaround and recently divested its North American snacks division. For the fiscal third quarter reported on May 11, 2026, portfolio changes and cost-cutting produced stronger cash flow, but the core business saw lower sales, slimmer profit margins, and volumes under pressure. Overall, the quarter delivered clear progress on debt and cash but left the business searching for growth.

Notable Developments
Core sales fell by 6% as volume dropped 11%, only partially offset by 5% higher prices.
North America segment profit margin rose 2.2 percentage points after the snacks business sale, but segment sales fell 23%.
Free cash flow improved sharply to $35 million, helping reduce net debt to $505 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not give numeric guidance for the year and remains committed to reducing debt, controlling costs, and stabilizing sales. The company continues to prioritize cash generation and debt repayment while working to stem volume declines in key categories. Investors should watch for organic sales growth and margin recovery as indicators that the turnaround may be gaining traction.

Quarterly Highlights

The North America business, after the snacks divestiture, recorded a large drop in sales but realized a modest improvement in profit margins. Segment EBITDA margin improved to 10%, supported by productivity gains and SG&A reduction.

International results included stable reported sales due to currency movements, but organic sales fell 8%. Pressure continued in categories like meal prep and baby and kids foods, with international gross margin declining almost 3 percentage points.

Company-wide free cash flow moved sharply higher, supported by working capital improvements and the sale of the snacks business. This cash enabled a reduction in net debt from $650 million at the start of the year.

What Hain Celestial Group Does

Hain Celestial Group makes and markets natural and organic foods and personal care products, with brands in categories such as tea, yogurt, baby and kids, and meal preparation, serving customers in over 70 countries.
