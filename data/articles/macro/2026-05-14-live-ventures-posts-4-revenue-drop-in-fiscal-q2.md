Key Takeaways
Live Ventures posted mixed results as operating improvements in some business segments were offset by weakness in others.
Revenue declined 3.8% from the prior year.
Management remains focused on cost control, but caution remains due to ongoing headwinds and profit volatility.

Live Ventures (NASDAQ:LIVE) delivered a mixed quarter as gains in its Retail-Entertainment and Flooring Manufacturing segments were offset by sharp declines in Retail-Flooring and a significant impairment charge. The diversified holding company, which owns businesses in retail, flooring, manufacturing, and steel, reported its second fiscal quarter 2026 results on May 14, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter highlighted both resilience in some areas and notable short-term challenges for the company.

The period underscored the company’s ongoing volatility, with operational improvements in select units unable to fully counteract broader headwinds.

Notable developments
Retail-Entertainment revenue rose 14.8% to $21.2 million, with operating income up 32.8% to $3.3 million.
Retail-Flooring posted a 26.2% decrease in revenue, widening its segment loss to $4.6 million.
A $4.0 million goodwill impairment in Steel Manufacturing led to a swing from segment profit to loss.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide explicit guidance for upcoming periods but continues to emphasize operational efficiency and cost controls, particularly in the struggling Retail-Flooring segment. Ongoing macroeconomic pressures, especially in housing-related businesses, are likely to impact near-term results. Investors should closely monitor further segment volatility, the potential for new acquisitions, and any change in demand across end markets.

Quarterly highlights

The Retail-Entertainment segment sustained double-digit revenue growth and increased profit, offsetting some of the decline elsewhere. This unit benefited from robust consumer demand, but its gross margin narrowed slightly due to a shift toward lower-margin products.

The Retail-Flooring segment experienced the steepest year-over-year fall in both sales and profit. While management prioritized cost reductions, these measures were not enough to counter reduced demand and lower sales to both retail and contractor customers.

Steel Manufacturing reported revenue growth but incurred a segment loss after recording a significant non-cash goodwill impairment. Excluding this one-time charge, the segment would have shown an operating profit, largely due to gains in tool and die businesses.

What Live Ventures does

Live Ventures is a diversified holding company that acquires and operates businesses in sectors including retail, flooring, entertainment, manufacturing, and steel.
