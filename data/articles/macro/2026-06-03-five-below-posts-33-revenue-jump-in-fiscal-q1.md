Key Takeaways
Five Below delivered an exceptional quarter with strong sales growth and improved profitability.

Discount retailer Five Below (NASDAQ:FIVE) reported a major business win as broad-based demand drove robust sales growth and higher profits. The company, which specializes in selling trendy merchandise at low prices, saw customers respond positively to new products and improved in-store experiences. For the first fiscal quarter of 2026 (reported June 3, 2026), Five Below exceeded its own guidance across key measures, setting a new high for comparable sales growth. Overall, the quarter reflected standout momentum across merchandising, store expansion, and operational controls.

Notable Developments
Revenue jumped 32.5%, driven by comp sales growth of 22.7%, which was well above management’s outlook for the period.
Operating income more than tripled year over year, reaching $154.2 million, reflecting strong sales leverage and lower expenses as a share of revenue.
The company opened 49 net new stores, bringing its total to 1,970 at quarter end, on track for 150 openings this year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its full-year fiscal 2026 guidance, calling for total revenue between $5.40 and $5.48 billion and comparable sales growth of 6% to 8%. For the next quarter, it expects comps of 7% to 9%, and plans around 50 new store openings. Investors should watch whether the strong double-digit sales comp can hold as the company cycles tougher results from prior quarters and contends with any changes to tariffs or cost inflation.

Quarterly Highlights

Five Below outperformed its March 2026 guidance, with both net sales and comps coming in well ahead of targets. Comparable sales, which measure sales at stores open at least a year, rose by a double-digit margin--a sign that new products and marketing campaigns resonated broadly.

Profitability also improved sharply, as operating income rose over 200% and expenses like selling, general and administrative costs rose at a much slower pace than revenue. This expense control allowed the company to expand operating margin and deliver much higher net income and EPS.

Store growth remained a key theme, with 49 net new openings completed during the quarter. Five Below closed the period with 1,970 stores in 46 states, reaffirming plans for roughly 150 new locations in fiscal 2026.

What Five Below Does

Five Below operates specialty discount stores that sell a wide range of trend-forward products, mostly priced at $5 or below, with some items offered at slightly higher 'beyond' price points.
