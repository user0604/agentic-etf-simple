Revenue at Moving iMage Technologies (NYSEMKT:MITQ) declined as the company faced lower project demand in its seasonally weakest period. The digital cinema solutions provider reported progress in margins and early sales uptake of its new DCS loudspeakers line. The fiscal third quarter results, released on May 19, 2026 and covering the quarter ending March 31, point to operational improvement but continued top-line softness. Overall, the period saw better profitability but highlighted the importance of upcoming large project deliveries in sustaining improvement.

Notable Developments
The DCS cinema loudspeaker line contributed $460,000 in quarterly sales, expanding the firm's higher-margin product mix.

Net cash fell to $2.36 million, reflecting investments in inventory and a rise in accounts receivable linked to recent project timing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management forecasts a substantial revenue increase for the fourth quarter, aiming for about $5.3 million in sales and gross margin of 25% to 30%. This outlook depends on the timely delivery of several large upgrade projects for cinema customers. Investors should watch for the conversion of the sales backlog and successful billing and collection, especially as higher receivables and lower cash levels remain key financial watchpoints.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter saw DCS loudspeakers, a new proprietary product line, deliver initial sales of $460,000 and help drive the higher gross margin. This segment is targeted at both domestic and growing international markets, where new distributor relationships are taking hold.

Gross margin rose to 34.8% compared to 29.8% the previous year, showing that improvements in product mix and cost management translated to better profitability. Operating loss narrowed by half, though the company remained unprofitable overall, highlighting the need for further business scale.

The end-of-quarter net cash position dropped sharply, standing at $2.36 million, due to increased working capital needs, higher inventory balances (especially DCS stock), and slower collection of project payments. Although management indicates that some receivables have already been collected after quarter end, liquidity remains a critical area as the business ramps up new initiatives.
