SIGA Technologies Posts 11% Revenue Drop in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:25 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
The quarter reflected a business setback as major contract deliveries were deferred to later periods.
Operating loss widened as revenue declined from the prior year.
Management expects significant product deliveries and improved results in the next two quarters.

Revenue fell as SIGA Technologies (NASDAQ:SIGA) faced quieter first-quarter results while awaiting large upcoming shipments. The biotechnology company specializes in antiviral treatments for orthopoxvirus infections and is best known for TPOXX, its oral and intravenous smallpox medicine. On May 7, 2026, it reported first quarter results for the period ended March 31, describing the quarter as a transition phase with financial performance below last year due to the timing of key government and international deliveries.

Overall, the period showed reduced revenues and deeper losses, but management reiterated that major product shipments in the next two quarters will be essential to meet annual targets.

Notable Developments
Total revenue for the quarter fell year over year, as shipment timing delayed major product deliveries into Q2 and Q3.
The operating loss more than doubled, with cost of sales up sharply to support upcoming large shipments.
A special dividend of $0.60 per share was declared and paid in April, returning about $43 million to shareholders.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled that the bulk of 2026 revenue and profit will depend on the successful fulfillment of over $35 million in oral and intravenous TPOXX deliveries expected in the next two quarters. Investors should watch the timing and completion of these large contract shipments, as well as progress in international licensing agreements.

Quarterly Highlights

The decrease in total revenue reflected a planned lull ahead of scheduled product deliveries to both the U.S. Strategic National Stockpile and an Asia Pacific customer. Management noted this back-end loaded pattern is consistent with its strategy and government procurement schedules.

The widening operating loss was coupled with a sharp increase in cost of sales and a buildup in inventory, now $56.5 million at quarter-end. This inventory build supports announced shipments slated for the remainder of the year, particularly under government contracts.

On the international front, SIGA entered an exclusive license with Hikma MENA FZE to commercialize oral TPOXX across the Middle East and North Africa. The company also planned a $13 million shipment to an Asia Pacific client in the next period, expanding its geographic reach.

What SIGA Technologies Does

SIGA Technologies develops and supplies antiviral products for serious infectious diseases, specializing in treatments for orthopoxvirus infections such as smallpox.
