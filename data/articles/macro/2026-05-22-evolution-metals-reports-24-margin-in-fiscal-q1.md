Notable Developments
The company's first consolidated revenue reached $1.9 million, reflecting initial commercial production and sales of rare earth permanent magnets.
Net loss was heavily affected by a one-time non-cash charge of $425 million from financial instrument revaluations, which have now been derecognized and are not expected to recur.
After the quarter ended, the company secured $100 million in financing and placed binding orders for 13 rare earth magnet production machines, poised to grow capacity to 10,000 metric tons per year by November 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management characterized the quarter as a foundation for rapid scale-up and expects significant advances in capacity, equipment installation, and commercial activity throughout fiscal 2026. Key items to monitor include the commissioning of new production equipment, progress toward annualized magnet output of 10,000 metric tons, and ongoing capital needs as the company enters a capital-intensive expansion phase.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue appeared on the income statement for the first time following the Nasdaq listing and subsidiary consolidation, driven by commercial sales of both bonded and sintered rare earth permanent magnets. Gross margin was 24%, reflecting initial pricing and the limited volume of reported operations during the transition quarter.

The company's reported net loss was unusually large due to a $425 million non-cash adjustment related to pre-merger financial instruments. When excluding this charge, the adjusted net loss was $15.1 million, indicating a higher ongoing operating cost base as EMAT prepared for commercial ramp-up.

Post-quarter events brought both a $100 million capital injection and major equipment orders, essential to increasing magnet production ahead of the U.S. Department of Defense's domestic sourcing deadline in January 2027. These developments set the stage for substantial scaling and future revenue growth, but progress will hinge on successful execution and further funding.

What Evolution Metals & Technologies Does

The company operates a vertically integrated supply chain for extracting, refining, recycling, and manufacturing rare earth permanent magnets and battery materials, serving automotive, defense, and industrial markets.
