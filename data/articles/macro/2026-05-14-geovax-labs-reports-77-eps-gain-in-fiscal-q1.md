Quarterly highlights

Revenue was absent during the quarter because the company’s BARDA government contract, which supported its COVID-19 vaccine, ended in 2025 and was not replaced. This left GeoVax Labs reliant on its remaining cash and raised the risk of significant near-term liquidity challenges.

Research and development expenses fell to $3.9 million. The reduction in operating costs resulted from the end of BARDA-supported activities and a funding pause for the company’s COVID-19 vaccine program, allowing resources to be shifted toward its main pipeline initiatives.

The pivotal upcoming Phase 3 trial for GEO-MVA moved a step closer as GeoVax Labs completed clinical-grade manufacturing and made vital progress on regulatory alignment. These operational achievements set the stage for a late-stage trial but do not yet provide any new revenue stream.
