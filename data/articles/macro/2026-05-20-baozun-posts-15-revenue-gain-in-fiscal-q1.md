Baozun (NASDAQ:BZUN) posted a business win with a return to non-GAAP operating profitability and robust revenue increases across both its e-commerce and brand management divisions. The leading provider of e-commerce solutions and brand management services in China announced its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 20, 2026, covering the three-month period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter showed clear improvements in sales, profit margins, and capital efficiency offset by continued GAAP net losses.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 15.3% year over year to RMB2,381.1 million, with e-commerce up 10.4% and brand management up 38.8%.
Non-GAAP net income attributable to shareholders improved to RMB1.4 million, compared to a non-GAAP loss of RMB57.2 million a year ago.
Working capital turnover days fell to 109 from 193, highlighting more efficient operations and resource management.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its confidence in sustaining operating profitability but did not issue specific targets for the next quarter or fiscal year. The company continues to aim for long-term non-GAAP profit growth through improved brand partnerships and innovation. Investors should monitor the pace of profitability improvements, especially in the brand management segment, and any updates on quantitative guidance.

Quarterly Highlights

Both e-commerce (BEC) and brand management (BBM) segments posted double-digit revenue growth, with BEC revenue rising 10.4% and BBM surging 38.8%. BBM benefited particularly from the Gap brand and from growing its managed store count to 176 by quarter-end.

Profitability metrics improved notably: the e-commerce segment returned to an adjusted operating profit of RMB13.0 million after a loss of RMB45.8 million in the prior year, while BBM sharply narrowed its loss to RMB4.9 million. This came alongside tighter cost controls, as fulfillment and general administrative expenses decreased or remained stable despite higher sales volumes.

The company’s capital efficiency stepped up, with working capital turnover days dropping 84 days year over year. Cash and cash equivalents also rose to RMB1,149.7 million at quarter-end, and accounts receivable declined slightly, reflecting improved collections.

What Baozun Does

Baozun provides end-to-end e-commerce solutions and brand management services for domestic and international brands operating in China.
