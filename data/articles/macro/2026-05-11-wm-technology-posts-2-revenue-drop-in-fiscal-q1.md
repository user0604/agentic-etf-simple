Business overview

WM Technology operates a technology platform and marketplace that connects cannabis consumers with licensed retailers. It provides software, data, and compliance solutions for cannabis businesses, helping clients manage operations and reach customers in a highly regulated industry.

Quarterly performance

Revenue for the quarter was $43.6 million, down 2.2% from the prior year, as management cited continued softness across core markets. Adjusted EBITDA decreased to $5.9 million from $10.1 million in the prior year period, as greater client churn and spending pressures led to margin contraction. Average monthly paying clients dropped to 4,983, but average revenue per paying client rose slightly to $2,914, reflecting retention of higher-spending clients.

Profitability weakened, with both net income and adjusted EBITDA falling sharply, and operating cash flow turning negative. Company cash and short-term investments increased to $57.0 million, providing some financial buffer despite softer core operations. Federal rescheduling of medical cannabis marked a positive regulatory step, but management stressed it will take time for the full effects to unfold. No major product launches or technology upgrades were announced, and management reiterated its focus on long-term market leadership and cost discipline.

Looking ahead

Management expects revenue to decline by a low-single digit percentage sequentially in the second quarter of fiscal 2026 from $43.6 million. It remains cautious, noting that persistent operating pressures could drive additional churn and softer advertising spend. Investors should watch client retention rates and operating cash flow as key signals of the company’s resilience in challenging market conditions.
