Aktis Oncology (NASDAQ:AKTS) made headway this quarter by progressing pipeline programs and securing a stronger cash position from its IPO. The clinical-stage biotechnology company focuses on novel radiopharmaceuticals for cancer, with two proprietary programs advancing through early clinical trials. Reporting on May 11, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Aktis Oncology’s results reflected continued operational scaling and spending to support clinical, regulatory, and manufacturing milestones. Overall, the quarter showed steady progress toward internal timelines, but operating losses increased as the company ramped up readiness for pivotal clinical data in 2027.

Notable Developments
Collaboration revenue rose to $3.2 million, up from $1.4 million, reflecting increased activity with Eli Lilly.
Research and development expenses rose to $20.0 million from $15.9 million, primarily due to expanded clinical trials and new hiring for ongoing studies.
End-of-quarter cash jumped to $538.5 million, mainly from the initial public offering in January, extending the cash runway into 2029.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its timelines for clinical programs, highlighting expectations of preliminary data in early 2027 for its leading drug candidates. It plans major investments in manufacturing with a new facility set to begin operations in the second half of 2026. Investors should closely watch the delivery of clinical milestones and any updates on manufacturing execution, as these are critical for future commercial potential.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue more than doubled due to higher collaboration payments from Eli Lilly, as Aktis continued to advance the development agreement tied to its miniprotein radioconjugate technology. These partnerships offer short-term revenue but the company still has no product sales and remains dependent on further clinical progress.

The sharp rise in research and development expenses was driven by a growing number of clinical sites and a ramp up in trial activity for both its Nectin-4–targeted radioconjugate and B7-H3–targeted radioconjugate programs. Higher staffing and costs linked to operating as a public company also contributed to a 59.5% year-over-year increase in general and administrative expenses.

The balance sheet is substantially stronger following the IPO, with cash and investments at $538.5 million, which management expects can fund operations into 2029. The company’s in-house manufacturing facility, planned to open in the second half of 2026, aims to support larger clinical trials and future commercialization efforts but will require continued disciplined spending and timely execution.

What Aktis Oncology Does

Aktis Oncology develops targeted radiopharmaceutical therapies for cancer, leveraging its proprietary technology to deliver radioactive drugs directly to tumors while minimizing effects on healthy tissue.
