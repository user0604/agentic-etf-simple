Leatt (OTC:LEAT) extended its strong run of double-digit growth, supported by expanding product sales and potent cash generation. Notable Developments
Helmet revenue increased 59% and body armor sales rose 25%, highlighting demand for new protective gear.
Cash from operations climbed to $4.55 million, up nearly six-fold, while inventory levels dropped 22% from the prior quarter.
Consumer direct and dealer direct sales grew 49% and 30%, helping strengthen the company's global market presence.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled ongoing confidence in future growth and remains committed to investing in product development and brand expansion. However, it pointed to global geopolitical uncertainty as a risk and called out inventory levels as a potential constraint to monitor. Investors should watch product category and channel strength, as well as whether inventory stays aligned with continued sales growth.

Quarterly Highlights

The company saw broad-based revenue gains, with helmets and body armor leading product growth—helmet revenue increased 59% and body armor by 25%. This strong performance was echoed across sales channels, with consumer direct sales up 49% and dealer direct up 30% as both domestic and international networks expanded.

Profitability improved as net income grew 58%, significantly outpacing the top-line increase and reflecting stable gross margins and operating leverage—meaning fixed costs did not rise as quickly as sales. Cash generation was especially robust, with cash from operations up by over $3.7 million year over year and balance sheet liquidity hitting $17.2 million.

Inventory was reduced by $4.5 million from the previous quarter, a notable development that signals efficient working capital management but also warrants monitoring to ensure sales momentum can continue unimpeded. Management is continuing to boost spending on research and development and marketing, in line with its stated strategy to drive long-term growth and further globalize the brand.

What Leatt Does

Leatt develops, markets, and sells protective gear and apparel for motocross, mountain biking, and other sports, offering products such as helmets, neck braces, and body armor.
