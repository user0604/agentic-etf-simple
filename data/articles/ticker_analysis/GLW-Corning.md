GLW Company Overview
Corning manufactures specialized glass, ceramics, and optical physics products for the telecommunications, electronics, and automotive industries. The company generates revenue by selling optical fiber, display glass for mobile devices and televisions, and ceramic filters that control vehicle emissions. Its business model relies on deep technical partnerships and a massive portfolio of proprietary patents, which creates high switching costs for manufacturers that depend on its unique material formulations and precise engineering capabilities.
