Key Takeaways
Precigen’s launch of PAPZIMEOS delivered significant commercial progress in its first full quarter on the market.
The company’s revenue surged over sixteen-fold year over year, supported by broad product uptake.
Management reaffirms its expectation to reach cash flow break-even by the end of 2026.

PAPZIMEOS, the new therapy from Precigen (NASDAQ:PGEN), registered a strong debut as the company pushed well past its initial commercial revenue targets for the quarter. The pioneering gene and cell therapy developer focuses on rare diseases and advanced cancers through its proprietary technology platforms. In its earnings released on May 13, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Precigen showed marked growth from the initial launch of PAPZIMEOS and ongoing moves in its clinical pipeline. Overall, the quarter highlighted accelerating commercial uptake and progress on operational milestones while the company continued to post a net loss.

Notable Developments
PAPZIMEOS net product revenue reached $21.6 million, far surpassing the company’s prior $18 million projection for this quarter and marking its first full quarter of commercial sales.
SG&A costs rose year over year as commercialization scaled, increasing to $21.0 million and reflecting expanded marketing and distribution efforts behind PAPZIMEOS.
Cash, cash equivalents, and investments totaled $56.7 million as of March 31, 2026, excluding about $25.7 million in uncollected PAPZIMEOS-related receivables since product launch.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management repeated its expectation to achieve cash flow break-even by late 2026, depending on both continued PAPZIMEOS revenue and efficient collections from accounts receivable. The company also noted that progress on international approvals, especially the pending European Medicines Agency review for PAPZIMEOS, represents the most important forward milestone for future expansion.

Quarterly Highlights

The launch of PAPZIMEOS, a gene therapy for adults with recurrent respiratory papillomatosis, delivered $21.6 million in revenue and patient enrollment in the support hub reached around 400 by quarter end. The drug’s commercial reach quickly expanded across both major institutions and community clinics, and insurance coverage now extends to about 297 million covered lives in the US.

Selling, general, and administrative expenses were $21.0 million for the first quarter of 2026, up from $12.4 million in the prior year period, mainly as a result of sales and marketing efforts tied to PAPZIMEOS. Year over year, operating loss narrowed substantially, and the combination of lower research and development spending and rising revenue led to improved earnings per share.

Pipeline advancement continued with PRGN-2009, the company’s investigational immunotherapy for HPV-associated cancers, remaining in Phase 2 trials. The European marketing application for PAPZIMEOS achieved validation, and upcoming real-world evidence presentations are planned at major scientific conferences.

What Precigen Does

Precigen is a biotechnology company focused on developing and commercializing gene and cell therapies for rare diseases and cancer using its proprietary platforms.
