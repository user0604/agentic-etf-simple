Key Takeaways
Euroholdings delivered record adjusted profit in the period, supported by strong product tanker market conditions.
Revenue increased sharply year over year.

Shipping company Euroholdings (NASDAQ:EHLD) posted a record quarter for adjusted earnings, driven by robust product tanker rates and increased fleet capacity. The company focuses on container and tanker shipping services, with its strategy shifting further toward product tanker exposure during the quarter reported on May 21, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the company achieved substantial growth in revenue and operating profit, though rising costs and higher leverage will be points to monitor going forward.

Notable Developments
Adjusted earnings per share more than doubled year over year, reaching a company high of $0.84 (on an adjusted basis) on strong spot-market tanker rates.
Revenue climbed to $7.64 million, reflecting fleet expansion and sharply higher time charter equivalent rates, especially in the tanker segment.
The company declared its fifth consecutive quarterly dividend at $0.14 per share and agreed to acquire an additional MR product tanker for $39.25 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects continued strong profitability in the next quarter as tanker market conditions remain favorable and a second MR product tanker joins the fleet mid-year. The key factor to monitor will be how cost inflation and higher financial leverage affect overall margins as the balance sheet grows with the new vessel acquisition.

Quarterly Highlights

Underlying profitability surged as adjusted earnings per share and adjusted EBITDA both reached all-time highs for the company. Higher spot market rates for product tankers and 100% fleet utilization contributed to these gains, offsetting increased operating costs per vessel.

The average time charter equivalent (TCE) rate, a measure of net revenue generated per day by ships, rose nearly 80% year over year to $28,388 per day, and revenue more than doubled, supported by recently acquired tanker capacity and full fleet employment for the period.

Operating costs per vessel climbed to $9,175 per day, up about 8% from the prior year, with management pointing to inflation in vessel supply and voyage expenses influenced by geopolitical risks, including the ongoing conflict in Iran. The company ends the period with $5.83 million in cash, and net cash from operations increased to $3.27 million.

What Euroholdings Does

Euroholdings provides international shipping services through its owned fleet of container carriers and product tankers, earning revenue from both short-term spot voyages and time-chartered contracts.
