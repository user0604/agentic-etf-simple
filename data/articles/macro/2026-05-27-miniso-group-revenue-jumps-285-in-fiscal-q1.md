MINISO Group Revenue Jumps 28.5% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Group profit surge was driven by a one-off gain rather than core retail performance.
Revenue rose 28.5% year over year for the March quarter.
Management remains focused on global expansion and product innovation, with investors watching margins and cost control going forward.

The discount retail chain MINISO Group (NYSE:MNSO) reported a headline profit jump for its March quarter, but most of the increase came from a one-off investment gain rather than its main retail business. The company, a global retailer known for affordable lifestyle and toy products, showed strong sales growth both in China and internationally. Released on May 27, 2026 for its first quarter of fiscal 2026, the report revealed that revenue beat company expectations, yet profit margins narrowed and core expenses increased. Overall, the quarter had rapid sales expansion and aggressive store openings, but underlying profitability came under pressure.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 28.5%, with the MINISO brand expanding in both China and overseas regions, and the fast-growing TOP TOY brand posting 51.4% growth.
Profit margins declined as adjusted operating margin dropped to 13.3%, and adjusted net profit fell to RMB550.6 million from RMB587.2 million despite strong top-line gains.
A one-time unrealized investment gain of RMB 874.6 million in the AI sector was the primary source of the sharp rise in reported net profit.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains committed to expansion, highlighting plans for deeper globalization and an increased focus on intellectual property and product variety. It expects to balance growth with “stable and foreseeable returns” and may consider share repurchases if conditions allow. The key issue for investors to watch is whether cost discipline and profitability can keep up as the company continues to grow globally.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales momentum continued, with revenue up 28.5% and a net increase of 797 stores year over year, particularly in the Chinese mainland and overseas markets. MINISO brand revenue in China jumped by nearly 30%, while overseas growth lagged but still posted a double-digit increase. The TOP TOY product line, which sells pop culture and collectible toys, was a standout, with over 50% revenue growth and 75 new stores added over the past year.

Despite these gains, underlying profitability measures softened. The adjusted operating margin contracted by 3.3 percentage points compared to last year, as store costs, marketing, and licensing fees increased faster than revenue. The large surge in reported profit was not from core retail sales but from a non-recurring gain linked to early investment in an artificial intelligence company, which added significant volatility to earnings for the quarter.

Global expansion remains a core strategy, with 56% of all new MINISO brand stores opened outside of China over the last twelve months. However, overseas markets’ share of MINISO brand revenue actually declined, indicating that domestic sales growth outpaced international progress for the period. Management noted a continued rise in advertising and licensing investments to boost brand strength and consumer engagement.

What MINISO Group Does

MINISO Group operates a global network of retail stores offering affordable lifestyle products and toys, focusing on design, everyday essentials, and popular culture items.
