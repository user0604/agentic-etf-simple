Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 39% year over year, driven by increased demand and a 14% rise in shipping days to 5,947 days.
Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) rates—a measure of daily earnings from chartering vessels—were 20% above major dry bulk market averages.
The company expanded its integrated logistics platform by launching operations at two new port locations during the quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management emphasized ongoing port and terminal expansion as a key driver for future growth, with a focus on premium market segments and discipline in cost management. The company noted continued demand strength, citing strong bookings and premium TCE rates for the second quarter to date.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s TCE rate outperformed market benchmarks, reflecting its specialized fleet and focus on niche shipping routes. This approach allowed it to secure higher daily earnings, supporting improved profitability compared to the prior year.

Operating scale increased as Pangaea expanded its chartered-in fleet by 54% and grew its total shipping days, but this also brought a 122% jump in charter hire expenses. Efficient fleet management remains critical as increased cost exposure could affect margins if market rates soften.

The period saw notable expansion in shore-side logistics, with new terminal operations in Lake Charles and Aransas. The upcoming launch at Port Tampa Bay is expected to further enhance the company’s integrated logistics offering.

What Pangaea Logistics Solutions Does

Pangaea Logistics Solutions provides dry bulk shipping, logistics, and port terminal services worldwide through a fleet of specialized vessels and integrated logistics operations.
