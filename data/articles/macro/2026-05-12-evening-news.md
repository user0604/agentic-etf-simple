1. Google's Android Goes Full Agentic

Alphabet's (NASDAQ: GOOG) Google just declared its phone software an "intelligence system" -- not an operating system. Gemini can now hop across apps, read your screen, and handle multi-step errands. Picture asking it to check your barbecue guest list, build a menu, and load up an Instacart (NASDAQ: CART) cart for your approval. The rollout hits Samsung (OTC: SSNLF) and Pixel devices this summer, then spreads to watches, cars, and laptops. Both Apple and Alphabet are recommended by both Team Hidden Gems and Team Rule Breakers.

Before Apple steals the spotlight: This lands just weeks before Apple (NASDAQ: AAPL) is expected to unveil a shinier Apple Intelligence at WWDC. Awkwardly, Gemini is already powering part of Apple's artificial intelligence (AI) push, meaning Google wins either way.
The scoreboard: Alphabet's stock is up 140%-plus in the past year; Apple has managed roughly 40%. Wall Street is voting with its dollars on who's winning the AI phone wars.
2. "Square's the Beef?" Ask Nelson Peltz.

Shares of Wendy's (NASDAQ: WEN) were up 17% on news rumors that activist investor Nelson Peltz -- who actually ran Wendy's as chairman from 2008 to 2024 -- wants his old burger joint back. He's reportedly raising capital for a takeover bid, and with Trian Fund Management already owning about 40% of shares, he's not exactly starting from scratch. JPMorgan (NYSE: JPM) is more bearish. The bank downgraded the stock on Monday, citing leadership uncertainty and debt.

Why now? Wendy's stock is down 35% over the past year, trading at just 12 times forward earnings, while McDonald's (NYSE: MCD) commands more than 20 times. Peltz clearly thinks the market is leaving money on the table.
The bad news hasn't gone away: U.S. same-store sales cratered 7.8% last quarter, and beef and labor costs keep biting -- so this is a turnaround bet, not a sure thing. Peltz is intentionally buying into a mess.
3. Tesla Is Spending $250 Million in Berlin

Tesla (NASDAQ: TSLA) is throwing $250 million at its Berlin-Brandenburg gigafactory to ramp battery cell production, with its sights set on 1 million cars built on-site -- it just hit 750,000. After a brutal stretch of declining European sales, March brought a near-doubling of sales. Time to declare the comeback tour officially on? TSLA is recommended by both Team Hidden Gems and Team Rule Breakers. Since its April 2024 recommendation in Hidden Gems, shares have nearly tripled, while the S&P 500 is up 49% during the same period.

The competition isn't waiting around: BYD (OTC: BYDDY) has been eating Tesla's European lunch, while Porsche (OTC: POAHF) is cutting jobs and Volkswagen (OTC: VWAGY) is in full crisis mode.
Batteries are the real story: The money targets cell production specifically, meaning Tesla isn't just building cars in Europe; it's gunning to own more of the supply chain.
