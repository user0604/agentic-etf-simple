Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains focused on broadening YUTREPIA’s reach, supporting new patient starts, and advancing both pivotal and label-enhancing clinical studies for its pipeline. The main risk continues to be the unresolved patent litigation, which could threaten YUTREPIA sales if decided unfavorably. Investors should monitor both regulatory/legal developments and trends in new prescriptions and patient starts for signals about future growth resilience.

Quarterly Highlights

YUTREPIA, an inhaled therapy for pulmonary hypertension, drove nearly all of the company’s revenue with over 4,500 unique prescriptions and a high prescription-to-start conversion rate of at least 85 percent since its launch in June 2025. The prescriber base surpassed 980 physicians since launch, while the number of prescribers with at least 5 patients increased by approximately 25% since the end of February to approximately 270, affirming deepening adoption and more repeated prescribing within the provider community. Service revenue from generic treprostinil injection, partnered with Sandoz, remained stable at $3.0 million, while investments in future programs such as L606 caused research and development spending to nearly double year over year.

Liquidia achieved its third consecutive quarter of both net income and positive adjusted EBITDA, with cash and cash equivalents rising by $32.1 million since year-end. This was fueled by operational profitability, enabling the company to fund a rising cost structure as it scales commercialization and invests in pipeline studies. Selling, general and administrative expenses climbed to $46.9 million, up from $30.1 million in the prior-year period, driven by personnel, commercialization, and infrastructure spending, even as litigation-related legal expenses declined.

Despite robust operational results, management emphasized that YUTREPIA’s continued approval and commercialization are subject to ongoing litigation with United Therapeutics. A potential adverse outcome could block sales for one or both of YUTREPIA’s main indications, making the litigation result the most significant near-term risk for Liquidia.
