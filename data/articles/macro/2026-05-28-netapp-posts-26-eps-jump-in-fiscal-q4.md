NetApp Posts 26% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 28, 2026, 4:19 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
NetApp delivered record quarterly results with revenue and cash flow both rising year over year.
Earnings per share grew 26% compared to the same quarter last year.
Management forecast continued, but moderating, growth and expects slightly lower profit margins in the coming year.

NetApp (NASDAQ:NTAP) posted its strongest quarterly results ever, driven by demand across its enterprise storage and cloud data services. The data storage and management company reported these achievements for its fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 (released May 28, 2026), as it navigates continued industry changes in cloud adoption and technology spending. Overall, the quarter showed clear financial gains, with growth in both sales and profitability.

Notable developments
NetApp achieved double-digit revenue growth for the quarter, with sales reaching $1.95 billion, and all-flash array revenue up 18% year over year.
Profitability improved as the company expanded its operating margin by over 7 percentage points, helped by higher gross margin and consistent spending on research and development.
The quarterly dividend remained steady at $0.52 per share, with $1.36 billion returned to shareholders in fiscal 2026 through buybacks and dividends.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects continued revenue growth for fiscal 2027, guiding for a 6%–9% increase and ongoing investment in innovation and partnerships. However, it expects profit margins to tighten slightly, mainly due to inflation in memory and components, as well as increased costs in developing new products. Investors should keep an eye on gross margin shifts and the pace of Public Cloud segment growth over the next year.

Quarterly highlights

Sales of all-flash storage systems reached a record $1.22 billion in the quarter, supporting the company's focus on high-performance solutions for artificial intelligence and data management. The Hybrid Cloud segment (data infrastructure software and hardware integrated with major public clouds) saw a 13% revenue increase, while the Public Cloud segment grew by 11%, but was up only 3% for the full year.

Profitability set new highs as operating margin rose to 27.3%, gross margin approached 70.1%, and free cash flow jumped to $900 million. These gains were achieved despite persistent inflation in the price of memory chips and storage hardware that continues to pressure product gross margins.

Management maintained its commitment to research and development, spending nearly $1 billion over the year to support new launches in cloud, AI, and data protection. Major partnerships were expanded with Google Cloud, Nutanix, and Commvault, and the indirect sales channel represented three-quarters of quarterly revenue.

What NetApp does

NetApp provides enterprise data storage, management, and cloud integration solutions for businesses worldwide, enabling customers to manage data securely and efficiently across both traditional and public cloud environments.
