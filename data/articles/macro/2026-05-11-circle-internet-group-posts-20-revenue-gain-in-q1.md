Circle Internet Group (NYSE:CRCL) edged ahead in its core business, posting robust growth in USDC adoption and platform revenue despite rising costs weighing on profit. The leading regulated stablecoin and digital asset infrastructure provider reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026. Notable Developments
USDC in circulation climbed 28% to $77.0 billion, while total onchain transaction volume grew 263% year over year to $21.5 trillion.
Operating expenses surged 76% to $242 million, driven mainly by higher compensation costs from post-IPO stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes.
Circle completed a $222 million presale for its new ARC Token at a $3 billion network valuation, but this new business line is not included in current-year financial guidance.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, including an RLDC margin of 38–40% and adjusted operating expenses of $570–$585 million. The company continues to target stablecoin network expansion while investing in infrastructure and compliance. Investors should closely monitor expense growth and changes in net income margin as the company balances growth with profitability.

Quarterly highlights

The platform's core driver, USDC stablecoin, saw robust momentum with average in-circulation amounts up 39% year over year. Growth in meaningful wallets and business use on the Circle Payments Network also highlighted greater end-user adoption and use cases for stablecoins across payments and settlements.

Cost discipline emerged as an area to watch: operating expenses rose sharply owing to increased stock-based compensation and investment in technology and regulatory readiness. While top-line performance was strong and RLDC margin expanded, this expenditure surge led to a 15% drop in net income from continuing operations versus the prior-year quarter.

Technological innovation was a theme, as the company launched its Agent Stack product suite and advanced Arc Blockchain development. The ARC Token network presale provided substantial new capital, with major institutional investors participating, setting the stage for further product launches later in the year.

What Circle Internet Group does

Circle Internet Group operates a digital financial platform centered on USD Coin (USDC), providing regulated stablecoin infrastructure, blockchain payments, and related technology for businesses and developers worldwide.
