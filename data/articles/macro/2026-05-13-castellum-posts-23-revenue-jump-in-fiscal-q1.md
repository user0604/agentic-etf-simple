Notable Developments
Revenue grew 23%, driven by large, long-term government contracts secured in the previous year, resulting in a record contract backlog of $273.3 million.
Gross profit rose in Q1 2026, but gross margin decreased to 35.4%, reflecting changes in the contract mix.
The company ended the quarter debt-free, increasing cash reserves to $15.8 million and reducing interest expenses.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects to recognize about 16% of the current backlog, or nearly $44 million, as revenue within the next year. Executives are focused on executing recently-won government contracts, investing in new cyber certifications, and taking a selective approach to potential acquisitions.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue for the period was driven by the production ramp on prior-year wins with federal agencies such as the U.S. Navy. The total contract backlog reached a new company record, and the qualified opportunity pipeline grew to $938 million, indicating a strong flow of potential business in the coming years.

Gross profit improved, but a lower gross margin signals that new contracts, while boosting the top line, may carry tighter margins due to contract mix. Castellum also achieved a substantial improvement in operating efficiency, with stock-based compensation expense reduced year over year.

On the balance sheet, Castellum finished the quarter with no long-term debt and higher cash. This stronger financial position gives it greater flexibility to invest in growth initiatives and explore selective acquisitions, supporting disciplined capital management.

What Castellum Does

Castellum provides cybersecurity, electronic warfare, and specialized IT services primarily to U.S. defense and intelligence customers through long-term government contracts.
