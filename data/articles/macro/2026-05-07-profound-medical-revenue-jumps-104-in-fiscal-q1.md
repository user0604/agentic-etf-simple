Profound Medical Revenue Jumps 104% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Strong start to the year as core business growth accelerates with key payer adoption.
Revenue more than doubled year over year.
Management issued cautious full-year guidance despite a robust first quarter.

Profound Medical (NASDAQ:PROF) delivered a clear business win as clinical use and payer coverage milestones fueled substantial growth across its core operations. The medical technology company specializes in incision-free, MRI-guided therapies for prostate disease and other conditions. On May 7, 2026, it reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, highlighting rapid adoption of its TULSA-PRO system and a breakthrough U.S. reimbursement contract.

Overall, the quarter showcased robust improvements in both financial results and commercial momentum, balanced by a conservative outlook for the rest of the year.

Notable developments
Humana (NYSE:HUM), the first national commercial insurer, began covering the TULSA Procedure for prostate disease, adding about 8.5 million covered lives in the U.S.
Recurring revenue continued to grow, with same-site procedures up 59% at the company’s key TULSA INDEX20 clinical locations.
Operating expenses fell by 9% year over year as CAPTAIN trial enrollment concluded and clinical spending shifted to device and workflow improvements.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management guided for full-year fiscal 2026 revenue of approximately $25 million, which would represent 56% growth over the prior year, with expected gross margins of at least 70%. While the strong first quarter exceeds the pace implied by this guidance, management cited continued quarterly revenue unpredictability related to capital equipment installations. Investors should closely watch the ramp-up in system installations and utilization, as realizing these will be key to driving the recurring revenue model forward.

Quarterly highlights

The TULSA-PRO system’s installed base rose from 78 to 80 operational sites, with six additional systems shipped but not yet installed; turning these shipments into activated sites will be critical for sustaining revenue growth.

Procedure volumes at key sites saw significant year-over-year increases, with the company reporting an average of 11.3 procedures per site during the quarter and a shift toward higher utilization as payer coverage improves nationwide.

The quarter marked a key U.S. reimbursement milestone as Humana became the first national commercial insurer to cover the TULSA Procedure, a development expected to accelerate adoption in the months ahead, especially following the U.S. Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services’ new coverage path effective January 2025.

What Profound Medical does

Profound Medical develops image-guided, incision-free therapeutic systems for the treatment of prostate disease and other conditions, focusing on improving patient outcomes through minimally invasive technology.
