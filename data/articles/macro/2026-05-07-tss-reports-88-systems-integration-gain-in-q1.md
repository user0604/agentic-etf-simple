The quarter highlighted robust growth in the core systems integration business but saw a steep drop in procurement revenues, leading to a much lower overall sales result. Notable developments
Systems integration revenues nearly doubled, up 88%, driven by increased demand for AI infrastructure services.
Procurement revenue fell 56%, underscoring the volatility and lower margin nature of this business segment for the company.
Gross profit stayed resilient, down just 4% year over year despite a 44% drop in total revenue, supported by an improving sales mix and higher margin contributions.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance of $20 million to $22 million, indicating confidence in achieving results near the higher end. The company continues to prioritize expansion of its systems integration business, particularly in AI rack integration. Investors should closely monitor the sustainability of growth in high-margin services and continued progress in customer diversification.

Quarterly highlights

The sharp rise in systems integration revenue reflects strong performance in AI rack integration, which the company sees as its main engine for profitable growth. This segment delivered 88% growth as the Georgetown, Texas facility continued to scale output for enterprise customers deploying advanced AI infrastructure.

Procurement, historically a large driver of total revenue but with more volatility and lower profitability, saw a significant drop as the company lapped uniquely high volumes from the prior year. Management notes this swing is typical for the segment and is not indicative of operational issues in the core business.

Gross profit declined slightly as improved sales mix and margin discipline offset most of the impact from lower overall sales. Depreciation is now being allocated to the cost of goods sold, further influencing the gross profit line and highlighting an ongoing shift toward higher-value, higher-margin service offerings.

What Tss does

Tss designs, builds, and integrates mission-critical data center infrastructure, with a growing focus on advanced AI rack solutions for large customers in the technology sector.
