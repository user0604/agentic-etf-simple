Nordson (NASDAQ:NDSN), a maker of precision equipment for applying adhesives and coatings in manufacturing, is expected to report earnings for its fiscal second quarter on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Nordson delivered record results, with sales reaching $669 million, representing an 8.8% increase year over year. Organic growth was 6.5%, led by strong demand in semiconductor-related applications within the Advanced Technology Solutions (ATS) segment. Net income rose to $133 million, or $2.38 per diluted share, up 44% from the prior year. Adjusted earnings per share were $2.37, which beat analyst estimates for the quarter. EBITDA margin held steady at 30%, despite some headwinds from geographic and product mix. Cash flow remained robust, supporting continued share repurchases and dividends.

Positives
Record sales and profit growth: Nordson set new first-quarter records for both sales and adjusted earnings per share, showing broad-based demand and strong execution.
Semiconductor-driven momentum in ATS: The Advanced Technology Solutions segment grew sales by 23%, fueled by semiconductor and electronics applications, which are currently a key growth driver for the company.
Strong cash generation and capital deployment: Free cash flow conversion exceeded 100% of net income, enabling $86 million in share repurchases and $46 million in dividends during the quarter.
Improved profitability in Medical and Fluid Solutions (MFS): EBITDA margin in MFS increased by 310 basis points, benefiting from a recent divestiture and higher-margin product mix.
Negatives
Margin pressure in Industrial Precision Solutions (IPS): EBITDA margin in the IPS segment fell to 33.7% from 37.5% a year ago, due to less favorable product and geographic mix, especially as growth was concentrated in lower-margin Asia Pacific markets.
Slower start in Medical and Fluid Solutions: Organic growth in MFS was only 2.7%, below expectations, partly because of winter weather disruptions that temporarily impacted production and supply chains.
Limited growth in Americas and Europe: Sales in the Americas and Europe were flat or down slightly, with most growth coming from Asia Pacific. This highlights ongoing regional demand imbalances.

Margin recovery in IPS and geographic mix: Watch for signs of margin stabilization or improvement in the IPS segment, especially if growth in higher-margin regions like North America and Europe picks up.
Medical and Fluid Solutions growth trajectory: Look for evidence that MFS is returning to its expected mid-single-digit growth, as management has guided, now that weather-related disruptions have passed.
