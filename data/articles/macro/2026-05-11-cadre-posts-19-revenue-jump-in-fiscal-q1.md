Key Takeaways
Cadre delivered strong revenue growth, but faced sharply lower profitability as costs rose. Adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 13.6% from 15.8% a year ago. Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook, citing robust backlog and continued acquisition activity.

Cadre (NYSE:CDRE) delivered robust sales gains in the first quarter, but profitability was pressured by acquisition-related costs and rising leverage. The global safety equipment provider completed the purchase of TYR Tactical in January and saw record orders backlog, though higher expenses weighed on net income. The company released its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 11, 2026. Overall, the period demonstrated strong demand and revenue growth but margin compression due to the impact of recent acquisitions and increased spending.

Notable Developments
Orders backlog reached a record $355 million at quarter end, rising $166 million since December 2025, supported by contract wins and acquisitions.
The Product segment delivered sales of $140.6 million, up 24.8% year over year, while the Distribution business fell 27.2% to $20.3 million.
Net debt increased to $324.6 million and cash reserves fell to $41.3 million, reflecting the $153.6 million outlay for TYR Tactical’s acquisition.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance, with net sales expected between $736 million and $758 million and adjusted EBITDA of $136 million to $141 million. The key element to monitor is margin recovery in coming quarters, as the company seeks to improve profitability and fully realize acquisition benefits while managing higher leverage.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth was strong, with net sales up more than 19% from the prior-year quarter, largely due to newly acquired businesses and robust demand for body armor and tactical gear. However, gross profit margin dropped by 4.4 percentage points to 38.7% as inventory step-up amortization and less favorable product mix outweighed improved pricing.

The Product segment was the main growth driver, supported by the TYR Tactical acquisition and a large contract for Med-Eng blast attenuation seats. By contrast, Distribution segment sales and margins declined due to weaker agency demand for hard goods.

Significant spending on acquisitions contributed to a sharp rise in net debt and a drop in cash on hand. Nevertheless, Cadre maintained its $0.10 quarterly dividend, highlighting ongoing capital returns even as it absorbs higher interest expense and integration costs.

What Cadre Does

Cadre designs and manufactures mission-critical safety equipment such as body armor, bomb disposal gear, and duty gear for law enforcement, military, and government agencies.
