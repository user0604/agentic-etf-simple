Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to stress careful cost controls and targeted product launches, but refrained from issuing specific new guidance, citing market and regulatory uncertainty. With fewer new products and lower managed assets, the coming quarters depend on stabilization in digital asset prices. Investors will want to monitor any recovery in asset inflows and progress on product innovation as a path to future revenue growth.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue from management fees and staking in the Valour (exchange-traded product and asset management) segment saw sharp declines, reflecting lower asset values and outflows. Fees from staking and lending dropped to $1.89 million, down nearly half from the prior year, while managed product assets dropped by almost a third since December.

The Stillman Digital trading desk saw trading commissions improve year over year, but this was not enough to offset steep mark-to-market losses and drops in other segment profits. Despite discipline in reducing marketing and share-based compensation costs, overall operating expenses stayed high due to elevated legal and professional fees tied to an ongoing class action lawsuit and regulatory compliance needs.

Liquidity remained a strong point, with cash and cash equivalents reaching $87.6 million. This buffer is significant as the company faces ongoing volatility in digital asset prices and a reduced base of assets under management, leaving future revenue generation closely linked to a turnaround in the broader crypto market.
