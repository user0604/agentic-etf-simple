Earnings Preview: PVH

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

PVH (NYSE:PVH), a global apparel company best known for its Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger brands, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

PVH delivered a strong finish to fiscal 2025, exceeding its guidance on revenue, operating profit, and earnings per share (EPS). For the fourth quarter, the company reported revenue of $2.51 billion and EPS of $3.82, both of which beat analyst consensus estimates. The company’s gross margin showed sequential improvement across all regions, and operating margin reached 10% for the quarter, despite ongoing tariff pressures. PVH’s two core brands, Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger, continued to drive the majority of results, with focused investments in product innovation and marketing. The company also returned over $560 million to shareholders through share repurchases in the year.

Positives
Revenue and EPS beat expectations: Both top and bottom line results for Q4 surpassed analyst consensus, signaling stronger-than-expected demand and operational execution.
Sequential improvement in gross margin: The company achieved better gross margin performance quarter over quarter, reflecting progress in cost management and tariff mitigation.
Strong performance in key product categories: Calvin Klein’s underwear and denim, as well as Tommy Hilfiger’s cable knit sweaters, saw notable growth, demonstrating effective product innovation and marketing strategies.
Shareholder returns through buybacks: PVH repurchased nearly 8 million shares, returning over $560 million to shareholders, which represented 15% of shares outstanding.
Negatives
Tariffs continued to pressure margins: The impact of U.S. tariffs reduced gross margin by approximately 170 basis points in the quarter, and the company expects further headwinds in 2026.
Mixed regional performance: While the Americas grew, Europe and Asia Pacific faced softer consumer demand, with EMEA revenue down in constant currency and APAC only returning to growth after adjusting for calendar effects.
Direct-to-consumer (DTC) traffic challenges: DTC revenue in the Americas declined mid-single digits due to lower store traffic, though partially offset by e-commerce growth.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Tariff impact and mitigation progress: PVH expects a significant gross margin headwind from tariffs in Q1, with about half of the 230 basis point impact planned to be offset by mitigation actions. Investors should watch for updates on how effectively the company is managing these costs.
Direct-to-consumer growth and brand momentum: Management has guided to DTC growth across both Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger in all regions. Early spring season trends have been positive, so the pace of DTC recovery and e-commerce performance will be key indicators.
Wholesale channel trends and timing shifts: The company expects lower wholesale revenue in Q1 due to shipping timing and cautious partner ordering, especially in Europe and the Americas. The extent of this decline and any signals of improvement will be important to monitor.
Marketing investments and consumer engagement: PVH is front-loading marketing spend in Q1 to amplify new campaigns and partnerships, including high-profile collaborations and sports tie-ins. The effectiveness of these investments in driving sales and brand relevance will be a focus.
Updates on operational initiatives: The company’s Growth Driver 5 Actions for cost savings are largely completed, but some initiatives continue into 2026. Progress on these actions, as well as any commentary on supply chain, inventory, or digital investments, will be relevant for assessing future profitability.
