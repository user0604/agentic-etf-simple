Management outlook and what to watch

Management emphasized a strong project pipeline and increasing regulatory support for future quarters but cautioned that government policy and access to external financing remain substantial risks. Investors should monitor progress on contract awards, incentive renewals, and the company’s liquidity position as key indicators for fiscal 2027.

Quarterly highlights

PowerBank continued to diversify its project mix, breaking ground on multiple solar and battery energy storage sites in New York and Ontario. These new ventures add to the company’s development pipeline, further supporting contracted revenue through incentive eligibility and government programs.

The company’s loss improvement came mostly from the absence of last year’s large impairment and investment write-downs, as well as a more stable margin profile. Its adjusted EBITDA loss held flat but did not swing into profit, showing that the core business remains focused on reinvestment and growth rather than full operating profitability.

Operating cash burn accelerated as the company invested in construction and project mobilization. While working capital turned positive, ongoing reliance on third-party financing remains a crucial area to watch as project outlays grow.
