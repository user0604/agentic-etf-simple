Design software provider Autodesk (NASDAQ:ADSK) posted a strong start to fiscal 2027, reporting growth in revenue, profit, and cash flow ahead of its forecast. The company, a leader in design and engineering software, raised its revenue guidance on May 28, 2026, after first-quarter results exceeded the growth rate previously projected for the year. Overall, the period showed healthy business momentum, especially in core segments, while ongoing restructuring introduces some caution for the quarters ahead.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 18% year over year, supported by strong growth in the Design and Make product segments.
Operating margin improved by 14 percentage points, reaching 28.0%, showing a large increase in profitability.
Management increased its full-year revenue and billings outlook after seeing continued traction in core markets.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year guidance for both revenue and billings, pointing to robust customer demand and successful execution in the period. However, it cautioned that some disruption is possible as it continues with a restructuring of its sales organization. Investors should closely watch how this restructuring affects billings and customer acquisition over the next several quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Growth was strongest in the Design segment, where revenue expanded 18% year over year, and the Make segment, which was up 25%. The Americas, EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa), and APAC (Asia-Pacific) regions all delivered double-digit growth in revenue compared to the prior year quarter, reflecting solid global demand.

Operating margin reached 28.0%, up substantially from the previous year due to higher net income and lower restructuring-related charges. Free cash flow increased by 57.8%, aided by increased profits and careful management of working capital.

The company announced the pending acquisition of MaintainX, which, once completed, is expected to strengthen Autodesk’s platform for asset operations. Management noted the outlook and guidance excluded any impact from this deal until the acquisition closes.

What Autodesk Does

Autodesk produces software used by professionals in architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, and media to design, simulate, and manage buildings, products, and digital content.
