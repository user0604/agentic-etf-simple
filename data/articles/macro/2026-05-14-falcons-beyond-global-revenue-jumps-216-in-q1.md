Notable Developments
Revenue was $5.4 million for the quarter, supported by attractions services, new product sales, and shared services from its creative subsidiary.
Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $4.6 million, an improvement of $3.5 million year over year; however, the company remains unprofitable on this normalized basis.
Falcon’s signed two $9 million contracts with VAI Amusement Park, supporting a future pipeline now valued at $29.2 million for its creative division.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide new guidance for fiscal 2026, but highlighted confidence in long-term prospects, citing a growing project pipeline and major contract wins. Despite the momentum, it acknowledged ongoing risks tied to liquidity and a concentrated customer base. Investors should closely monitor cash flow, customer diversification, and the progression of large project milestones.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth far outpaced the prior year, led by $1.7 million from new product sales in the Attractions division and increased services activity. The creative division, Falcon’s Creative Group, contributed $13.0 million in unconsolidated revenue, validating strategic investments in proprietary technologies and creative assets.

Profitability improved on both a net and adjusted basis, but the shift to a $6.1 million net profit was largely attributable to a one-time $11.1 million credit from the reversal of earlier transaction expenses. Adjusted EBITDA showed a sequential narrowing of losses, though the company has not yet reached breakeven on underlying operations.

Liquidity remained tight, with cash and cash equivalents ending the quarter at $1.2 million after operating cash outflows of $2.8 million. Falcon’s continues to rely on related party credit facilities and faces elevated customer concentration, as large projects still dominate the revenue mix.
