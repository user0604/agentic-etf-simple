Key Takeaways
Large one-time licensing agreement boosted earnings and reversed losses for the quarter.
Earnings per share rose to $0.37 from a $(1.97) loss the prior year.

Biotech firm Hcw Biologics (NASDAQ:HCWB) swung to profit thanks to a major licensing deal, but ongoing liquidity issues and uncertain funding remain concerns. The immunotherapy company, which specializes in developing multi-functional protein therapeutics targeting chronic inflammation and cancer, released first quarter results on May 14, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the period showed a sharp financial turnaround driven by a single major transaction, but left questions about longer-term sustainability.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose from $5,065 to $6.5 million, mainly due to a licensing deal with Beijing Trimmune Biotech that included $3.5 million in cash and $3.5 million in equity.
Operating income turned positive, reaching $3.2 million, as both research and general administrative expenses dropped compared to last year.
The company’s cash position declined to $1.2 million, even with the licensing income, and it warned of continued risks around staying solvent in the coming year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership did not provide financial guidance for the rest of fiscal 2026 and continues to flag the need for new funding or partnerships to support ongoing operations. Investors should watch for a preliminary clinical readout from its lead candidate HCW9302 in the first half of this year, as well as updates on potential Nasdaq delisting risk.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s revenue surge was almost entirely from the closing of a licensing agreement for the HCW11-006 program. Under the deal, the company received both cash and a minority equity stake in Beijing Trimmune Biotech, but only the cash portion immediately added to liquidity.

R&D expense dropped by 15% to $1.3 million, reflecting reduced manufacturing and clinical costs. This allowed reported operating income to swing to a positive result, despite higher taxes and new indirect tax expenses not present the prior year.

Despite headline gains, the company’s balance sheet remained under pressure, with cash declining to $1.2 million and management highlighting “substantial doubt” about its ability to remain a going concern without further funding. Compliance with Nasdaq’s minimum bid price remains unresolved, and further dilution from new warrants is possible.

What Hcw Biologics Does

Hcw Biologics develops and licenses immune-modulating protein therapeutics for cancer and autoimmune diseases based on its proprietary platforms.
