Lifeloc Technologies (OTC:LCTC) posted a narrower net loss this quarter, but it continues to face balance sheet pressures as operating cash flow stayed negative. The company is a developer and manufacturer of professional-grade breath alcohol testers and is making progress on its new SpinDetect™ drug detection technology. The results, reported on May 19, 2026, cover the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter showed slight top-line growth and technical progress, while liquidity and future revenue inflection remain key concerns.

Notable Developments
The net loss narrowed to $(153,000) with operating losses falling, as spending was held in check and gross margin improved by three percentage points year over year.
The company’s cash balance decreased to $569,000, while it arranged a $500,000 insider loan at 10.5% interest after reviewing external financing options.
SpinDetect™, Lifeloc’s oral fluid drug analyzer platform, reached a production tooling milestone and is now preparing for beta testing, but commercialization remains upcoming.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide quantitative guidance but emphasized that resource discipline on research and product development will continue. Leaders highlighted new financing as a bridge while the company prepares for SpinDetect™ market entry. Investors should watch for upcoming customer validation of SpinDetect™ and further liquidity developments.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure showed minimal growth compared to the same period last year, reflecting only incremental gains in core breath alcohol device sales. While traditional product lines—like the FC-Series and Phoenix® 6.0 BT workplace testers—remained stable, larger scale revenue expansion will likely require successful launch of new offerings.

Gross margin rose from 40% to 43%, attributed by management to both pricing and a better mix of higher-margin products. Meanwhile, total research and development expense was trimmed, but still exceeded 18% of revenue as engineering investments into the SpinDetect™ platform continued.

Liquidity pressure increased as cash and equivalents declined from $746,000 to $569,000, and accounts payable rose from $302,000 to $615,000 compared to the previous quarter.

Lifeloc secured a $500,000 related-party loan in May 2026, with management stating that these terms were more favorable than alternatives available from commercial sources and that the financing aims to support cash flow until new products generate material sales.
