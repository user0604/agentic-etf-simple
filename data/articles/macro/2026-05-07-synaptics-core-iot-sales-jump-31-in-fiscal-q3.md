Notable Developments
Core IoT product sales rose 31% over the prior year, outpacing overall company revenue growth and continuing its role as the primary growth driver.
Non-GAAP operating income increased to $53.1 million, reflecting better profitability after adjusting for acquisition-related costs and share-based compensation.
Management affirmed guidance for continued double-digit Core IoT growth, with a target of over $385 million in segment sales for full-year fiscal 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

The company forecasts sequential revenue growth in the fourth quarter and expects Core IoT product sales to grow more than 40% year over year for fiscal 2026. Management also points to an expanding pipeline of AI-related design wins in new areas such as robotics and physical AI, though meaningful revenue from these design wins is targeted for 2027 and beyond. Investors should watch for further progress turning these innovations into revenue and narrowing the gap between GAAP and non-GAAP profits.

Quarterly Highlights

Core IoT was the standout segment, with sales up 31% year over year for the quarter. This part of the business includes chips for smart home, industrial, and connected devices, and remains the main engine of company growth. Management projects Core IoT will surpass $385 million in annual revenue in fiscal 2026, representing growth of over 40% compared to last year.

Non-GAAP performance was solid across most metrics. Earnings per share for the period reached $1.09 on a non-GAAP basis. Non-GAAP gross margin was 53.6%, essentially flat year over year.

However, GAAP results still showed a net loss of $8.0 million, narrower than the $21.8 million loss in the year-ago quarter. The company’s high operating expenses, including share-based pay and acquisition charges, contributed to the gap between adjusted and standard accounting results. Inventory and accounts receivable also rose, driven by investments to support IoT demand, but could represent a future risk if sales growth slows.
