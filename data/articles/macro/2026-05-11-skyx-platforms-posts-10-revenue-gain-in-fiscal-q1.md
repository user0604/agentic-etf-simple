Business overview

The company focuses on developing plug-and-play, smart home electrical technologies that make installing lighting, ceiling fans, and related products safer and easier.

Quarterly performance

The period marked nine consecutive quarters of year-over-year revenue growth, but most sales still came from legacy lighting products. Gross margin improved, supported by a higher-margin product mix and cost control, even as selling and administrative expenses increased faster than revenue. The company raised $29 million in new equity, which significantly increased its cash position and reduced near-term liquidity risks.

New product introductions, such as the Turbo Heater Fan and all-season ceiling fan categories, contributed to operational progress. The company also upgraded its e-commerce platform with artificial intelligence tools, aiming to improve sales conversion and create more recurring revenue opportunities. Despite these advances, net losses remain substantial and operating expenses, especially general and administrative costs, rose faster than the revenue figure, making operating profitability an ongoing challenge.

Looking ahead

Management believes its cash reserves are sufficient to pursue growth and aims for positive cash flow in 2026. However, operating cash burn remains significant, and a large-scale shift to advanced, smart home technology solutions is still in early stages. Investors should watch for evidence that higher-margin smart product sales can accelerate and reduce dependence on equity funding.
