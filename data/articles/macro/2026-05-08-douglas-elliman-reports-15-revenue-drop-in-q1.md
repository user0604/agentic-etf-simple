Douglas Elliman Reports 15% Revenue Drop in Q1

Key Takeaways
Douglas Elliman reported higher losses as key transaction metrics declined.
Revenue fell by 15.4% compared to the prior year quarter.
Management emphasized long-term positioning but did not provide specific near-term targets.

Douglas Elliman (NYSE:DOUG)’s luxury residential brokerage business experienced another difficult quarter, as fewer high-value home sales drove wider losses amid ongoing industry weakness. The company, a top U.S. real estate brokerage focused on luxury markets like New York, Florida, and California, released its first quarter 2026 results on May 8, 2026, covering the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter showed that cost efforts could not offset sharply lower transactions and revenue.

Notable developments
Quarterly revenue fell 15.4%, while transactions dropped to 4,393 from 4,908 a year earlier.
Operating losses widened to $17.5 million, even with lower agent commissions and general expenses year over year.
The company grew its development marketing pipeline to $27.2 billion, with new international and U.S. market expansions.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management provided no explicit guidance for revenue or profit in coming quarters, pointing instead to platform strength and luxury pipeline as foundations for future recovery. The most important area to watch is whether transaction volumes will rebound enough to narrow losses and begin utilizing the $27.2 billion development pipeline.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue declined from the prior year, partly due to fewer high-value transactions and the absence of property management fees after the October 2025 business sale. Even after cost cuts, the operating loss grew sharply as lower commissions (from fewer closings) did not offset the revenue shortfall.

Gross transaction value dropped by 13.1%, with both the number of closings and average sale price drifting lower, reflecting broad luxury market weakness. The company reported 4,393 transactions at a $1.96 million average price, slightly under the prior year's $2.02 million.

Despite headwinds, Douglas Elliman expanded into markets including Canada, France, Monaco, and U.S. regions in California and Virginia. It continued investing in technology such as its Elli AI agent tool, aiming to support agent productivity and brand stature within luxury real estate.

What Douglas Elliman does

Douglas Elliman operates as a major luxury residential real estate brokerage focused on high-value U.S. and global markets, with offerings spanning home sales, marketing, and related services.
