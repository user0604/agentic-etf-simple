Key Takeaways
Picard Medical delivered significant revenue growth and returned to positive gross profit in the first quarter.

Medical device company Picard Medical (NYSEMKT:PMI) posted expanded commercial progress, highlighted by improved product uptake and better use of its artificial heart rental program. The company specializes in total artificial heart systems, with the SynCardia device used for patients with advanced heart failure. On May 21, 2026, it reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, covering the period that ended March 31. Overall, the period saw operational advances offset by renewed concern over losses and stockholder equity.

Notable Developments
Core revenue climbed to $1.2 million as SynCardia Total Artificial Heart sales and Freedom Driver rental revenue both rose strongly.
Gross margin improved to 24%, reversing a loss position, as manufacturing efficiencies and higher rental program use supported operating leverage.
Stockholders' equity dropped to a $1.4 million deficit, resulting in NYSE American compliance notices and a risk of future delisting.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management is prioritizing further SynCardia adoption and progress toward its new Emperor Total Artificial Heart clinical study goal, set for 2028. The team is also working to return equity to compliance levels and strengthen commercial and manufacturing performance. Investors should pay close attention to the company’s NYSE compliance process and progress in stabilizing its finances over the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

SynCardia Total Artificial Heart, a device that replaces both heart ventricles in severe heart failure cases, drove revenue and adoption gains. In particular, the Freedom Driver rental program, which powers the artificial heart outside the hospital, saw revenue expand to $0.2 million -- up sharply from the prior year’s $7,000.

Gross profit turned positive, as the company moved from a negative margin to a 24% margin in the quarter. This came alongside lower manufacturing costs and an uptick in recurring rental income.

On the capital front, Picard Medical paid down substantial debt, raised new equity, and reduced warrant liabilities. However, its equity deficit worsened, triggering NYSE listing compliance failures. The company now faces a June 7 deadline to submit a remediation plan to the exchange.

What Picard Medical Does

Picard Medical designs, makes, and sells total artificial heart systems and related devices for advanced heart failure.
