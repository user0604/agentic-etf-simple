Earnings Preview: The TJX Companies

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

The TJX Companies (NYSE:TJX), a leading off-price retailer of apparel and home goods, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

The fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was a strong one for TJX. Net sales reached $17.7 billion, up 9% from the prior year, with comparable sales (comp sales) rising 5% across all divisions. Adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $1.43, a 16% increase year over year. Both sales and EPS were well above analyst expectations. The company also achieved an adjusted pretax profit margin of 12.2%, up from 11.6% the previous year. Growth was broad-based, with both apparel and home categories performing well. Inventory levels increased, positioning the company to take advantage of strong merchandise availability in the market. TJX ended the year with $6.2 billion in cash and returned $4.3 billion to shareholders through buybacks and dividends.

Positives
Broad-based comp sales growth across divisions: Every division posted comp sales growth of at least 4%, which shows TJX’s appeal to a wide range of shoppers and geographies.
Profitability exceeded expectations: Adjusted pretax profit margin and EPS were both well above plan, driven by lower inventory shrinkage and strong expense management.
Strong cash flow and shareholder returns: TJX generated $6.9 billion in operating cash flow and returned $4.3 billion to shareholders, reflecting robust financial health.
Improvement in inventory shrinkage: Shrink levels are now back to pre-pandemic norms, helping margins and reflecting effective operational controls.
Negatives
Higher incentive compensation expenses: Some of the SG&A leverage was offset by increased incentive compensation accruals, which limited further margin improvement.
Inventory build-up: Inventory per store rose 10% year over year, which could pose a risk if demand unexpectedly weakens, even though management views current levels as healthy.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Comparable sales performance versus guidance: Management has guided for 2% to 3% comp sales growth in Q1. Investors should watch whether actual results meet or exceed this range, especially given the strong start noted by management early in the quarter.
Gross margin improvement from favorable inventory hedges: The company expects Q1 gross margin to rise 40 to 50 basis points year over year, helped by better inventory hedge comparisons and merchandise margin. Watch for confirmation of this benefit.
Impact of higher store wage and payroll costs: SG&A expenses are expected to be higher as a percentage of sales due to increased store payroll. Investors should see whether these costs are offset by sales leverage as planned.
Progress on store openings and remodels: TJX plans to open 146 net new stores and remodel about 540 locations in fiscal 2027. Updates on the pace and early performance of these initiatives will be important for assessing longer-term growth.
Developments related to tariffs and potential refunds: Recent changes in U.S. tariff policy could affect merchandise costs or result in refunds. Investors should monitor any updates on how these factors are impacting sourcing costs or profitability.
