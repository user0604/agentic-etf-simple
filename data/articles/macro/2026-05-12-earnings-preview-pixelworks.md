Pixelworks (NASDAQ:PXLW), which develops and licenses video processing technology for cinematic and display applications, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Thursday, May 14, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025, Pixelworks completed a major transformation by finalizing the sale of its Shanghai-based semiconductor subsidiary. This transaction, which closed in early January 2026, marked the company's exit from the semiconductor hardware business and its shift to a pure-play technology licensing model focused on cinematic visualization solutions, such as the TrueCut Motion platform. For the full year 2025, revenue from continuing operations was $0.7 million, essentially flat compared to the prior year. Operating expenses decreased due to cost control measures and restructuring actions. The company ended 2025 with $11.2 million in cash, and after the sale of the subsidiary, began 2026 with approximately $62 million in cash and cash equivalents. Gross margins remained high, reflecting the asset-light nature of the new business model. These results met analyst expectations for the quarter.

Positives
Successful business transformation completed: The sale of the Shanghai subsidiary was completed as announced, giving Pixelworks a clean exit from hardware and a clear focus on licensing.
Strengthened balance sheet: The company received $51 million in net cash from the sale, resulting in a starting 2026 cash balance of about $62 million and providing significant financial flexibility.
High gross margins from continuing operations: The company reported gross profit of $0.6 million on $0.7 million in revenue, highlighting the asset-light and high-margin nature of its licensing activities.
New partnerships in premium cinema: Pixelworks announced completed partnerships with Marcus Theatres and Odeon Cinemas Group to expand the use of TrueCut Motion in premium large format screens, which could drive future licensing demand.
Negatives
Flat revenue growth: Revenue from continuing operations remained unchanged year over year at $0.7 million, indicating that the new licensing model has yet to generate meaningful top-line growth.
Ongoing net losses: The company continued to report net losses from continuing operations, reflecting the early stage of the new business model and ongoing R&D and administrative costs.
Non-recurring patent sales: The $3 million gain from selling non-core patents in 2025 will not recur, so future results will depend entirely on licensing income.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on TrueCut Motion adoption: Investors should watch for updates on new or expanded partnerships with premium cinema chains and studios, as management indicated additional deals are in discussion.
Revenue from licensing and services: With the business now focused on technology licensing, any increase in revenue from TrueCut Motion or other platforms would signal early traction for the new model.
Cash usage and expense control: Management expects operating cash usage to drop to about $2 million per quarter starting in Q2. Q1 results will show the initial impact of restructuring and severance costs, so investors should look for the ending cash balance and any guidance on future cash burn.
Status of home entertainment ecosystem initiatives: Pixelworks aims to expand TrueCut Motion into home entertainment devices. Any updates or announcements about device certification or licensing agreements would be important milestones.
Resolution of remaining escrow funds: The company expects to receive about $1.2 million from escrow related to the subsidiary sale. Confirmation of this cash inflow would further strengthen the balance sheet.
