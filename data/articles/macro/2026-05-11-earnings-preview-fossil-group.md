Earnings Preview: Fossil Group

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 11:04 AM

Comments

Fossil Group (NASDAQ:FOSL), a global designer and marketer of watches and accessories, is expected to report its first quarter fiscal 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, May 13, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Fossil Group delivered fourth-quarter results that exceeded analyst expectations, marking a significant step forward in its ongoing turnaround plan. Net sales for the quarter were $274 million, reflecting a 20% decline year over year, which included a four-point impact from store closures. Despite the revenue drop, the company achieved a gross margin of 57.4%, up 350 basis points from the prior year, and reported adjusted operating income of $11 million for both the quarter and the full year. These results were driven by a shift to full-price selling, strict cost controls, and improved supply chain management. The company ended the year with $96 million in cash and $67 million in available credit, and inventory levels down 15% from the prior year. The net loss for the year was $78.3 million, or $1.45 per share, which was an improvement compared to the previous year’s loss.

Positives
Gross margin expansion signals healthier business mix. The move to a full-price selling model and better supply chain management led to a gross margin of 57.4%, indicating the company’s ability to sell more at higher prices and reduce discounting.
Turnaround plan execution led to improved profitability. Adjusted operating income turned positive at $11 million for the year, reversing two years of losses and reflecting successful cost-cutting and operational changes.
Inventory and store footprint were right-sized. Inventory was reduced by 15% and 49 underperforming stores were closed, resulting in a more efficient operation and improved cash flow prospects.
Wholesale traditional watch business returned to growth. Core licensed watch brands saw 2% global growth in wholesale, showing resilience in key markets like the U.S. and India.
Negatives
Net sales continued to decline sharply. Total revenue fell 20% in the quarter and 12.3% for the full year, with particular weakness in direct-to-consumer and e-commerce channels due to reduced promotional activity and store closures.
Smartwatch and leather categories saw steep declines. Smartwatch sales dropped 52.6% and leathers declined 37.1% year over year, reflecting strategic exits and weaker demand in those segments.
Net loss persisted despite operational improvements. The company reported a net loss of $78.3 million for the year, although this was narrower than the previous year’s loss.
Tariffs and macroeconomic uncertainty remain headwinds. Tariffs reduced gross margin by about 140 basis points, and ongoing trade policy volatility could continue to pressure margins and costs.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on top-line stabilization: Investors should look for signs that sales declines are moderating, especially as management guides to a return to growth by the end of 2026. Q1 consensus sales estimate is $204.7 million, and actual performance versus this benchmark will be closely watched.
Sustainability of gross margin improvements: Management expects gross margins to remain in the mid to upper 50% range. Watch for any impact from new or changing tariffs and how the company continues to manage pricing and promotions.
Impact of new product launches and collaborations: The recent introduction of the BigTick collection and upcoming Signature premium line are key initiatives. Early consumer response and sell-through rates for these products will be important indicators of future growth potential.
Further cost structure optimization: The company plans to close about 15 more stores in 2026 and continue streamlining operations. Updates on SG&A expense trends and any incremental savings will be relevant to profitability outlook.
Performance in key growth markets: India remains a strategic focus, with plans to expand distribution and open new retail locations. Progress in this market, as well as continued strength in wholesale channels, will be important for the company’s path back to growth.
