Key Takeaways
Revenue climbed but the net loss widened as structural costs remained high.
Gross profit rose year over year, with operational EBITDA up strongly.
No updated financial guidance was issued; management signaled ongoing focus on growth and balance sheet strength.

Commercial imaging specialist Eastman Kodak (NYSE:KODK) turned in another quarter of rising sales and gross profit, but its bottom line losses increased as lingering structural costs outpaced operating improvements. The company manufactures print systems, specialty materials, and licenses its brand for a variety of consumer and industrial products worldwide. For the first quarter of fiscal 2026 (results released May 7, 2026), Kodak posted its third straight period of revenue and operational EBITDA growth, though cost headwinds kept net losses elevated.

Overall, the quarter showed progress on profitability at the product level and further steps in debt reduction, but cash use and net loss trends remain challenges to monitor.

Notable Developments
Revenue grew 7% year over year, driven by improvements in the Print segment and stable contributions from Advanced Materials & Chemicals (AM&C).
Operational EBITDA jumped to $15 million from $2 million, indicating stronger profitability at the product and segment levels even as GAAP net loss increased to $(16) million.
Period-end cash and equivalents of $299 million, down from $337 million at year-end, reflected both a major $50 million debt repayment and higher inventory tied to increased raw material costs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated a commitment to driving “future growth” by investing in core businesses, serving customers, and fortifying the balance sheet, but did not provide updated financial guidance for the rest of 2026. The absence of numeric targets leaves investors watching for continued improvement in operational profitability and closer management of non-operating costs.

Quarterly Highlights

The Print segment was a standout, with revenue advancing and a return to positive operational EBITDA, aided by pricing actions and product mix. This segment includes digital plates, print systems, and consumables, making up the majority of company sales.

The Advanced Materials & Chemicals segment grew revenue modestly and was roughly flat on operational EBITDA. Higher manufacturing costs offset topline gains, and there was little detail on innovation progress in pharmaceuticals or functional printing lines this quarter.

Kodak’s Brand licensing arm added incremental revenue but remains a small contributor overall. Gross margin improvements company-wide helped boost total gross profit, even with pressures from raw materials like silver and aluminum.

What Eastman Kodak Does

Eastman Kodak produces commercial print systems, specialized materials, and licenses its brand for cameras, films, and consumer goods across the globe.
