Cardiff Oncology (NASDAQ:CRDF) advanced its lead drug candidate and met critical development milestones in the first quarter. The clinical-stage oncology company is focused on developing therapies for cancer patients with hard-to-treat tumors. In its earnings announcement released on May 14, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Cardiff carried out its previously outlined plans and maintained a disciplined approach to costs while continuing its clinical progress. Overall, the quarter showed significant momentum in clinical programs, though financial losses remain a factor.

Notable developments
Cardiff held a successful End-of-Phase 2 meeting with the FDA, confirming plans for a Phase 3 trial for onvansertib in RAS-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC).
Total operating expenses decreased by 11.0% year over year, reflecting reduced research and development spending offset by higher administrative costs.
Cash and investments ended the quarter at $46.1 million, with runway projected into the first quarter of 2027.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects to provide added detail on the upcoming Phase 3 clinical trial and regulatory plans by mid-2026 and will present new trial data at major industry conferences. The company did not provide guidance for revenue or profit, but it affirmed that current financial resources are expected to support planned activity into early 2027. Investors should primarily watch for updates on the pivotal Phase 3 onvansertib trial and any changes to the company’s cash position.

Quarterly highlights

Cardiff’s lead investigational drug, onvansertib, showed confirmed objective response rates and improvements in progression-free survival for patients with RAS-mutated mCRC, with no significant added toxicity according to the company. This supports continued regulatory discussions and the move toward a larger registration trial.

The drop in operating costs came mainly from reduced spending on clinical and preclinical operations, while increases in general and administrative expenses related to recent management changes and severance payments. These leadership updates included new CEO, CFO, and COO appointments announced in April.

Modest royalty revenue, reflecting the company’s pre-commercial stage, declined substantially year over year. Cardiff’s ability to stretch its remaining cash through lower spending is critical as it continues to fund research and regulatory activities without product sales.

What Cardiff Oncology does

Cardiff Oncology is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing precision cancer medicines targeting genetic and molecular vulnerabilities in tumors.
