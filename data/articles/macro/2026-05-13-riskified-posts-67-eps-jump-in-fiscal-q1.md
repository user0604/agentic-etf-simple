Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership now forecasts slightly higher revenue and profitability for fiscal 2026. The updated view reflects continued wins with new merchants, an expanded product range, and solid operational discipline. The sustainability of revenue acceleration and adoption of new products remain key factors to follow in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue rose as more merchants adopted multiple Riskified products, including new solutions aimed at alternative payments like ACH (Automated Clearing House) transfers and verticals beyond e-commerce retail. The company highlighted that five of its top ten new contracts in the period were outside the United States, spanning three different industries.

Profitability improved, with adjusted EBITDA up substantially thanks to higher margins, modest revenue growth, and a decline in share-based compensation expenses. Operating expenses held nearly flat year over year on a non-GAAP basis, even as investments continued in platform innovation and global expansion.

Riskified used its strong free cash flow and cash reserves to repurchase 6.2 million shares during the quarter, aiming to increase long-term shareholder value. The firm ended the quarter with no debt and $276 million in cash, deposits, and investments.

What Riskified Does

Riskified provides software and artificial intelligence-driven services that help online merchants detect and prevent payment fraud, improve payment approval rates, and streamline compliance with regulations.
