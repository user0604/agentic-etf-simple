# Research Structure — Equity Themes

This file defines the universe of research themes available to the Portfolio
Manager (Agent B). When planning a run, B selects 8–12 themes from this list
that best fit the current macro brief (Fed/BOJ policy, USD/JPY outlook, sector
tailwinds/headwinds). Selected themes become research tasks dispatched to X
researchers.

Themes are grouped by geography. Each includes the rationale, suggested sub-sectors
or angles, and screening criteria for the researcher.

---

## United States

### US-01 — AI & Data Center Infrastructure
**Sectors:** Semiconductors, Cloud, Data Center REITs, Networking
**Why:** Multi-year enterprise and hyperscaler capex cycle driven by AI/ML
  training and inference workloads. GPU demand, custom silicon (ASICs), and
  data center build-out are secular trends not tied to a single business cycle.
**Screening:** >20% YoY revenue growth in data-center segments, gross
  margin >60%, forward P/E <35, positive free cash flow.
**Examples:** NVDA, AMD, AVGO, MRVL, EQIX, DLR

### US-02 — Enterprise Software & Cloud
**Sectors:** SaaS, Cloud Platforms, Cybersecurity, Enterprise AI
**Why:** Enterprise digitisation and AI copilot adoption create durable
  recurring revenue. Security spend is non-discretionary; cloud migration
  has multi-year runway in mid-market and government.
**Screening:** Net dollar retention >115%, gross margin >70%, operating
  margin >20%, revenue growth >15%.
**Examples:** MSFT, CRM, NOW, PANW, CRWD, ADBE, ORCL

### US-03 — Healthcare & Life Sciences
**Sectors:** Large-cap Pharma, Biotech, MedTech, Managed Care
**Why:** Aging demographics, GLP-1/GIP class expansion, and precision
  medicine create structural demand independent of macro cycles. Patent
  cliffs are known and priced; pipeline optionality is underappreciated.
**Screening:** Pipeline NPV >50% of market cap, dividend yield >2.5%,
  R&D spend >15% of revenue, P/E <20 for large-cap pharma.
**Examples:** LLY, UNH, MRK, ABBV, JNJ, ISRG, TMO, VRTX

### US-04 — US Financials
**Sectors:** Money-Center Banks, Regional Banks, Asset Managers,
  Insurance, Payment Processors
**Why:** Net interest margin expansion from higher-for-longer rates,
  investment banking fee recovery, insurance float income. Payment
  processors benefit from secular cash-to-digital shift.
**Screening:** CET1 ratio >12%, ROE >13%, NIM >3% (banks), operating
  margin >25% (processors), tangible book value growth >5% YoY.
**Examples:** JPM, GS, BLK, V, MA, AXP, BRK.B, MET

### US-05 — US Defense & Aerospace
**Sectors:** Prime Defense, Sub-systems, Space, Commercial Aerospace
**Why:** Elevated global tensions (NATO spending targets, Indo-Pacific)
  drive multi-year budget cycles. Backlog-to-sales ratios at decade highs.
  Commercial aerospace benefits from travel demand and fleet replacement.
**Screening:** Backlog >3x annual revenue, government backlog
  visibility >5 years, FCF yield >4%, net debt/EBITDA <2x.
**Examples:** LMT, RTX, NOC, GD, HWM, GE, BA

### US-06 — US Energy & Industrial Transition
**Sectors:** Integrated Energy, Midstream, Electrical Equipment,
  Grid Infrastructure
**Why:** Electrification, grid modernisation, and reshoring drive
  industrial electricity demand growth unseen since the 2000s. Midstream
  energy has stable cash flows with inflation-pass-through contracts.
  AI data centre power demand adds incremental catalyst.
**Screening:** FCF yield >5%, dividend growth >5% p.a., revenue exposure
  to electrification >30%, debt/EBITDA <2.5x.
**Examples:** EPD, ET, ETN, NEE, CE, CAT, PWR

### US-07 — US Consumer & Retail
**Sectors:** Consumer Staples, Discount Retail, Premium Brands,
  E-commerce, Restaurants
**Why:** Consumer bifurcation — premium and discount ends outperform
  the squeezed middle. E-commerce penetration continues to grow. Staples
  provide portfolio ballast with real pricing power.
**Screening:** Gross margin >35%, ROIC >15%, same-store/comp sales
  growth >2%, debt/EBITDA <2x.
**Examples:** COST, WMT, AMZN, MCD, SBUX, PEP, KO, TJX

## Japan

### JP-01 — Japan Financials (Rate Normalisation)
**Sectors:** Mega-banks, Regional Banks, Life Insurance, Securities
**Why:** BOJ rate normalisation from negative to positive territory
  structurally improves net interest margins for the first time in
  a decade. Japan's mega-banks have accumulated large foreign bond
  portfolios that become more profitable as rates normalise.
**Screening:** NIM improvement >20bps YoY, ROE >8%, domestic loan
  growth >2%, foreign bond portfolio duration <5yrs.
**Examples:** 8316.T (MUFG), 8316.T (SMFG), 8306.T (Mitsubishi UFJ),
  8766.T (Tokio Marine), 8604.T (Nomura)

### JP-02 — Japan Industrials & Factory Automation
**Sectors:** Robotics, Factory Automation, Precision Machinery,
  Industrial Components
**Why:** Japan's dominance in precision manufacturing and robotics
  is structural — competitors cannot easily replicate the ecosystem
  of component suppliers and integration expertise. Capex cycle recovery
  in China and reshoring in the US drive demand.
**Screening:** Overseas revenue >50%, operating margin >12%, R&D
  spend >4% of revenue, order backlog growth >5%.
**Examples:** 6954.T (Fanuc), 6861.T (Keyence), 6273.T (SMC),
  6367.T (Daikin), 6326.T (Kubota)

### JP-03 — Japan Semiconductor Equipment
**Sectors:** Wafer Fabrication Equipment, Test & Assembly,
  Materials, Chemical Supply
**Why:** Japan holds critical positions in semiconductor supply chain
  (photoresists, deposition tools, test equipment) that benefit from
  global fab construction regardless of chip demand cycles. China
  fab buildout adds near-term demand.
**Screening:** Revenue exposure to semiconductor >50%, gross margin
  >40%, order book >1x annual revenue, net cash position.
**Examples:** 8035.T (Tokyo Electron), 6146.T (Disco), 7735.T (SCREEN),
  6981.T (Murata), 4063.T (Shin-Etsu Chemical)

### JP-04 — Japan Trading Companies (Sogo Shosha)
**Sectors:** General Trading, Resources Trading, Commodity Supply
  Chain, Infrastructure Investment
**Why:** Unique Japanese business model with diversified earnings
  across resources, infrastructure, food, and healthcare. Recent
  corporate governance reforms have driven buybacks, dividend hikes,
  and P/B re-rating. Beneficiaries of commodity price cycles and
  global infrastructure spending.
**Screening:** P/B <1.5, dividend growth >5% p.a., ROE >10%, net
  income from non-resources >40% for portfolio resilience.
**Examples:** 8058.T (Mitsubishi Corp), 8031.T (Mitsui & Co),
  8001.T (Itochu), 8038.T (TonenGeneral), 2768.T (Sojitz)

### JP-05 — Japan Tech & Entertainment
**Sectors:** Consumer Electronics, Gaming, Entertainment IP,
  Imaging, Music & Film
**Why:** Japan's globally competitive entertainment IP (gaming, anime,
  music) has expanding global distribution via streaming platforms.
  Weak JPY benefits overseas revenue translation. Structural shift
  to digital distribution improves margins for gaming studios.
**Screening:** Overseas revenue >60%, operating margin >15%, net
  cash position, IP catalogue monetisation rate (revenue per title).
**Examples:** 6758.T (Sony), 7974.T (Nintendo), 9684.T (Square Enix),
  4324.T (Dentsu), 9432.T (NTT)

### JP-06 — Japan Consumer & Defensive
**Sectors:** Food & Beverage, Cosmetics & Toiletries,
  Pharmaceuticals (domestic), Drug Distribution
**Why:** Defensive earnings with pricing power from brand equity
  and distribution network depth. Cosmetics benefit from inbound
  tourism (Japan's travel boom) and Asian export demand. Drug
  wholesalers have stable regulated margins.
**Screening:** Operating margin >8%, ROE >8%, domestic market
  share >15%, dividend growth >3% p.a.
**Examples:** 4452.T (Kao), 9983.T (Fast Retailing/Uniqlo),
  2502.T (Asahi), 2802.T (Ajinomoto), 4502.T (Takeda),
  3387.T (Create SD Holdings)

### JP-07 — Japan Auto & Mobility
**Sectors:** Auto Manufacturing, Auto Parts, Two-wheelers,
  Mobility Services
**Why:** Weak JPY structurally boosts overseas earnings translation.
  Hybrid/EV transition plays to Japan's strength in powertrain
  efficiency and battery technology. Parts suppliers benefit from
  platform consolidation and aftermarket demand.
**Screening:** Overseas revenue >70%, operating margin >5%,
  hybrid vehicle mix >30%, R&D spend on EV/battery >3% of revenue.
**Examples:** 7203.T (Toyota), 7267.T (Honda), 6902.T (Denso),
  5334.T (NGK Insulators), 7246.T (Press Kogyo)

### JP-08 — Japan Real Estate & REITs
**Sectors:** REITs (Office, Retail, Logistics, Residential),
  Real Estate Developers
**Why:** BOJ rate normalisation creates near-term headwinds but
  also signals a healthier economy. Logistics REITs benefit from
  e-commerce penetration growth. Residential REITs have stable
  occupancy and rent growth in urban areas. Developers benefit
  from inbound tourism & hotel development.
**Screening:** LTV <45%, occupancy >95%, dividend yield >4%,
  NAV discount >15%.
**Examples:** 8951.T (Nippon Building Fund), 8960.T (United Urban),
  3288.T (Open House), 8830.T (Mitsui Fudosan),
  3466.T (LaSalle Logiport REIT)
