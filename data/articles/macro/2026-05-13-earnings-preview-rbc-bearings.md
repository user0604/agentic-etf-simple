Earnings Preview: RBC Bearings

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

RBC Bearings (NYSE:RBC), a maker of precision bearings and components for aerospace, defense, and industrial equipment, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, RBC Bearings delivered net sales of $461.6 million, up 17% year over year. The company reported adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $3.04, which beat analyst estimates. Gross margin remained steady at 44.3%. The aerospace and defense (A&D) segment was the key driver, with sales up 41.5% and particularly strong growth in defense, which increased 86.2%. Industrial segment sales grew 3.1%, supported by higher demand in aggregate and cement, food and beverage, and warehousing markets. Free cash flow reached $99.1 million, and the company paid down $81 million in debt during the quarter. The backlog reached $2.1 billion, reflecting robust order activity, especially in A&D. The recently acquired VACCO business contributed $29.2 million in sales and $5.9 million in gross margin, with integration ongoing.

Positives
Aerospace and defense momentum continues: The A&D segment delivered impressive growth, driven by higher commercial aircraft build rates and strong defense demand, supporting both top-line and margin expansion.
Backlog at record levels: The company’s backlog reached $2.1 billion, providing strong visibility for future revenue, especially with over 90% of the backlog tied to A&D programs.
Debt reduction accelerates: RBC paid down $81 million in debt during the quarter and another $67 million after quarter-end, strengthening its balance sheet and reducing interest expense.
Free cash flow conversion remains high: Free cash flow reached $99.1 million, with a conversion rate of 147%, reflecting effective working capital management.
Negatives
Industrial growth remains modest: The industrial segment grew just 3.1% year over year, trailing the robust performance of A&D and reflecting ongoing softness in certain end markets like mining and grain.
Gross margin impact from acquisition accounting: Gross margin in A&D was negatively affected by $4.2 million in purchase accounting adjustments from the VACCO acquisition, though this is expected to phase out in FY2027.
SG&A costs increased: Selling, general, and administrative expenses rose 11.1% year over year, partly due to higher personnel costs and VACCO integration.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Revenue and margin guidance versus consensus: Management guided Q4 sales to $495 million to $550 million and adjusted gross margin to 45% to 45.25%. Analyst consensus now sits at $505.9 million in sales and EPS of $3.33. Investors should watch for actual results relative to these targets.
Progress on VACCO integration and margin lift: The integration of VACCO, completed in July 2025, remains ongoing. Watch for updates on cost synergies, contribution to A&D margins, and any commentary on new applications in space and satellite markets.
Industrial segment recovery signals: Management has described Q4 industrial guidance as conservative. Investors should look for order trends and commentary on whether recent strength in short-cycle industrial markets and semiconductors is translating into improved sales growth.
Backlog sustainability and defense pipeline: The record $2.1 billion backlog is heavily weighted to A&D. Updates on new contract wins, duration of defense orders, and any changes in defense spending priorities will be important for assessing future growth.
Capital allocation and debt paydown progress: The company expects to pay off its term loan by November 2026. Watch for updates on cash flow, capital expenditures (expected to remain under 4% of sales), and further debt reduction.
