Credo Technology Group (NASDAQ:CRDO) delivered standout results as demand from AI and data infrastructure markets fueled substantial gains. The leading provider of high-speed connectivity solutions for data centers and AI hardware reported its fiscal fourth quarter results for the period ended May 2, 2026, on June 1, 2026. The quarter brought record revenue and profitability, but signs of margin normalization and persistent customer concentration give investors areas to watch.

Notable Developments
Revenue in the quarter was $437.0 million, up 157.0% year over year, exceeding management's own forecast range of $425-435 million.
Non-GAAP earnings per share rocketed to $1.16 from $0.35 a year ago, as gross margin held above long-term targets at 68.2%.
Cash and short-term investments ended the period at $1.4 billion, but inventory also rose sharply to $250.8 million from $90.0 million last year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Looking ahead, management expects revenue to rise again in the current quarter, targeting between $465 million and $475 million. Gross margins are forecast in the 67-69% range for Non-GAAP basis, suggesting some moderation from recent peaks. Investors should monitor customer concentration and inventory levels, as well as signs of how new connectivity products impact overall sales mix.

Quarterly Highlights

Growth was led by new AI and data center demand, with revenue up both year over year and from the previous quarter. Sales of new product lines—including ZeroFlap Active Electrical Cables, ZF optical transceivers, and OmniConnect memory solutions—helped drive the record revenue result.

Profitability surged on both a GAAP and Non-GAAP basis, as earnings per share and net income expanded far faster than operating costs. Gross margin remained strong at 68.2%, well above the company's stated long-term target of 63-65%.

On the balance sheet, cash and short-term investments finished at $1.4 billion, giving the company flexibility for further investment. However, the sharp jump in inventory—up nearly threefold—means the company is building up stock to meet anticipated future demand, introducing risk if forecasts do not materialize as planned.

What Credo Technology Group Does

Credo Technology Group designs and sells high-speed connectivity solutions, including chips and integrated modules, that enable faster and more energy-efficient data movement in AI systems and data centers.
