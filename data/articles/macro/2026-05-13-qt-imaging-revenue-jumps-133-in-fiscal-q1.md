Strong top-line growth marked the most recent period for Qt Imaging (NASDAQ:QTI), thanks to higher scanner shipments in its core and new international markets. The medical imaging company, which specializes in low-energy, radiation-free breast imaging technology, released its first quarter 2026 earnings on May 13, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. The quarter highlighted expansion in commercialization and regulatory progress, but also revealed pressures from increased expenses and continued cash burn.

Overall, the period displayed significant momentum in growing market presence, while underscoring the importance of scaling up recurring revenue streams and controlling costs.

Notable Developments
Revenue surged on higher shipment volumes, with 13 Breast Acoustic CT scanners shipped during the quarter versus 6 in the prior-year quarter.
Gross margin normalized to 41%, as last year’s figure was elevated by one-time sales of heavily depreciated scanners.
Cash and equivalents declined to $7.0 million, with operating cash outflows of $3.7 million and an extension of key debt maturity to March 2029.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue target of about $39 million, banking on increased U.S. and Gulf region shipments as well as initial revenue from its cloud-based platform. The team expects recurring SaaS (software-as-a-service) revenue to begin late in 2026, with broader U.S. reimbursement for procedures unlocking in 2027. Investors should keep a close eye on shipment execution and the pace of SaaS adoption as key factors for future financial flexibility.

Quarterly Highlights

Unit shipments met key distribution obligations, supporting a steep rise in recognized revenue compared to the same period last year. Growth came from both domestic sales via established U.S. partners and early progress under multi-year Gulf region agreements.

Gross margin narrowed significantly to 41%, reflecting a shift to regular product mix after a prior period of unusually high-margin legacy scanner sales. The margin level will be important as the business scales its installed base and adds cloud and SaaS revenue later this year and into 2027.

Despite revenue growth, operating expenses rose faster—driven by higher research and development spending and general administrative costs. That led to higher underlying operating losses, and net cash used in operations increased to $3.7 million, with management extending a major loan but at a higher interest rate.

What Qt Imaging Does

Qt Imaging develops and commercializes advanced medical imaging systems, focused on providing 3D, radiation-free breast scans using ultrasound tomography for safer and more comfortable cancer screening.
