Notable Developments
The company’s revenue grew by 12.8% year over year, reaching RMB132.6 million, with packaged tours as the main contributor.
Gross margin decreased to 55.5%, down from 59.0% last year, with gross profit growth lagging revenue growth.
Operating expenses fell by 3.5%, but sales and marketing spending rose 16.9% year over year, reaching RMB50.5 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects net revenue in the second quarter of 2026 to range from RMB134.9 million to RMB141.6 million, slowing dramatically to 0–5% year-over-year growth. The company maintains a focus on product supply chain and collaboration with partners but offers no guidance on profitability or margins. Investors should watch whether slower revenue growth is temporary or a longer-term shift, and how the company manages costs to support sustained profitability.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue from packaged tours, which include organized and self-guided trips, climbed 10.8% year over year, reflecting expanded offerings and partnerships with tourism boards. Revenue from other segments, like advertising services, grew by 23.5% as the company deepened relationships with travel authorities and sought new business opportunities.

Cost of revenues increased by 22.6%, which outpaced revenue growth and led gross margin to decrease by 3.5 percentage points. Despite this margin pressure, the company achieved its fifth consecutive quarter of non-GAAP profitability, with adjusted net income at RMB2.2 million.

Sales and marketing expenses jumped 16.9% compared to last year, rising more rapidly than overall revenues. General and administrative costs dropped by 40.7%, owing mainly to the absence of one-time impairment charges that affected the previous year’s results.
