Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its outlook for above-target earnings growth for fiscal 2026, citing continued strength in flagship fund launches across infrastructure, private equity, and credit. The company expects the Just Group insurance mandate to add $100 million in annual base fee revenue starting in the second quarter. Investors should watch for updates on capital deployment and the integration of new partnerships, including the impact of cost trends on profitability.

Quarterly highlights

Fee-bearing capital, the money managed for clients that generates fee income, climbed to $614 billion on March 31, 2026. This rise reflects continued demand from both institutional and private wealth investors, and broad fundraising successes across the platform.

The quarter saw $34 billion in new capital deployed or committed, with sizeable investments in infrastructure, credit, private equity, and energy/transition segments. Fundraising was particularly strong in credit, where $13 billion was raised and deployment kept pace at $12 billion.

On the cost side, compensation and operating expenses increased in line with business growth and integration of acquisitions such as Oaktree and Primary Wave. While higher costs affected reported margins, management noted these were expected outcomes as new business lines are included in consolidated results.
