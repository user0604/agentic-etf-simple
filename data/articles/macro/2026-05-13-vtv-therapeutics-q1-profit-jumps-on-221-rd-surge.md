vTv Therapeutics (NASDAQ:VTVT)’ Q1 profit jumped, powered by a large licensing deal, as its lead clinical program stayed on schedule. The clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company is advancing cadisegliatin, an oral therapy for type 1 diabetes (T1D), with enrollment in its critical Phase 3 trial progressing as planned. The company reported results for the first quarter on May 13, 2026. Overall, it stabilized its finances for the next stage of development, though its future hinges on new trial data.

Notable Developments
Revenue came in at $36.8 million, reflecting a one-time licensing payment and not ongoing product sales.
Research and development expenses more than tripled to $9.0 million, driven by increased activity in the Phase 3 CATT1 trial of cadisegliatin.
Cash holdings climbed to $98.1 million, supported by the Newsoara Biopharma agreement and enabling the company to fund core programs through near-term milestones.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated that existing funds are adequate to complete enrollment and reach topline results for the pivotal cadisegliatin trial, expected in the third quarter of 2026. No new guidance was provided on profitability or commercial product launches. The most important milestone to track is the readout from the CATT1 trial, as it will shape regulatory and commercial prospects.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s $36.8 million revenue was almost entirely from an amended license deal with Newsoara Biopharma for a non-core asset, bringing a $20 million upfront payment and possible future milestone and royalty income. This one-time inflow was the main driver of the move to a net profit this quarter.

Investment in research and development rose sharply to $9.0 million, reflecting heavy spending to support cadisegliatin’s pivotal clinical trial. The company highlighted successful site activation and continued patient enrollment but reported no new clinical data or safety findings in the period.

Thanks to the licensing payment, cash and cash equivalents increased to $98.1 million, putting the company in a stable position to fund ongoing operations and the completion of its key study. No additional partnerships for cadisegliatin or regulatory changes were announced, and the business remains focused on advancing its lead candidate.
