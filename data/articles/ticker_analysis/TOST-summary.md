TOST Company Overview
Toast provides a digital technology platform specifically designed for restaurants to manage point-of-sale transactions, online ordering, and back-office operations. The company generates revenue through software subscriptions, financial technology services like payment processing, and the sale of durable hardware. By integrating front-of-house service with payroll, inventory, and kitchen management into a single system, the company creates a unified operating environment that encourages long-term customer loyalty and high switching costs.
