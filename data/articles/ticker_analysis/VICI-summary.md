VICI Company Overview
VICI Properties operates as a real estate investment trust that owns a diverse portfolio of gaming, hospitality, and entertainment destinations across North America. The company generates revenue by leasing these facilities to established operators under long-term agreements that require tenants to cover all property expenses. By owning essential physical assets like Caesars Palace and investing in specialized wellness and leisure projects, the company creates high barriers to entry within the experiential real estate market.
