Tvardi Therapeutics (NASDAQ:TVRD) moved closer to key clinical readouts while managing costs and reducing quarterly losses. The clinical-stage biopharma company, which develops oral STAT3 inhibitors for cancer and fibrotic diseases, released its first quarter results on May 8, 2026, reporting for the period ended March 31. Notable Developments
The first-in-class oral STAT3 inhibitor TTI-109's healthy volunteer study continues, with topline data due in June 2026.
Research and development spending grew 58%, driven by TTI-109 progression, while TTI-101 clinical expenses tapered as studies advanced.
Management reaffirmed its cash runway should last through major clinical trial milestones and into the fourth quarter of 2026.
Management outlook and what to watch

Leadership remains focused on clinical trial milestones, stating that cash reserves should fund planned operations until after pivotal readouts for TTI-109 and TTI-101 later in 2026. Upcoming clinical data will be crucial, as positive or negative trial outcomes could shape future funding and development strategy. Investors should monitor the timing and results of these studies, as well as future capital needs if programs expand or experience delays.

Quarterly highlights

The company continued developing its next-generation STAT3 inhibitor pipeline, notably expecting first topline data from a Phase 1 TTI-109 trial in healthy volunteers by June 2026. Meanwhile, its TTI-101 franchise reported new data from a Phase 2 fibrosis study showing a 9.4% reduction in fibrosis score for treated patients, compared to 2.4% for placebo, and a supportive decline in IL-6 levels, a key inflammatory marker.

Research and development expenses rose to $4.9 million, mostly due to increased investment in TTI-109, signaling the shift in focus toward advancing newer clinical programs. General and administrative costs climbed to $2.1 million, largely reflecting higher personnel and compliance costs following the company's public listing.

Despite these spending increases, the net loss narrowed year over year to $6.8 million. Cash holdings at quarter-end stood at $25.0 million, which management believes should cover operations through key data milestones.

What Tvardi Therapeutics does

Tvardi Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on discovering and developing oral medicines that directly inhibit STAT3, a protein involved in cancer and fibrotic diseases.
