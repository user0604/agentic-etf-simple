Heron Therapeutics Posts 26% Oncology Drop in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 8:13 AM

Comments

Key Takeaways
Business loss as acute care growth did not offset oncology declines and margin compression.
Earnings per share shifted from a small profit last year to a loss this quarter.
Management reaffirmed full-year guidance but significant improvement is needed to meet targets.

Acute care product momentum was not enough to counter a steep oncology contraction and reduced profit margins for Heron Therapeutics (NASDAQ:HRTX) in the latest period. The specialty pharmaceutical company focuses on acute care and oncology support treatments. The first-quarter report, released May 11, 2026, covers the three months ended March 31, and marks a challenging start to fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter saw the company's acute care segment grow, but this was more than offset by declining oncology sales and lower profitability.

Notable Developments
Acute care segment revenue rose 32.3% year over year, driven by higher sales of ZYNRELEF (an extended-release anesthetic) and APONVIE (anti-nausea injection).
Oncology product revenue fell over 26%, with ongoing declines in CINVANTI (chemotherapy-induced nausea injection) and a planned wind-down of SUSTOL (anti-nausea injection).
Gross profit margin decreased nearly 9 percentage points from last year’s quarter, reflecting a less profitable sales mix and higher inventory costs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its 2026 full-year guidance for both net revenue ($173 to $183 million) and adjusted EBITDA ($10 to $20 million), despite the slow start to the year. Reaching these goals will require a sharp boost in both acute care sales and profitability in the next three quarters. Investors should watch for progress in the scale-up of acute care products and signs of stabilization in gross margin from now to mid-year.

Quarterly Highlights

The acute care business—anchored by ZYNRELEF and APONVIE—increased sales, with ZYNRELEF up 27% and APONVIE up 50% from the prior year quarter. This growth included higher demand units, broader formulary coverage, and early progress with improved reimbursement policies. Management credited demand programs and inclusion in clinical guidelines as key contributors.

In contrast, the oncology supportive care segment shrank as legacy product SUSTOL was wound down and CINVANTI saw both price and volume pressures. The company noted that just maintaining oncology market share is now a challenge, even as it worked through patent litigation and reshaped its commercial strategy for that portfolio.

Profit margins came under pressure largely due to high-cost inventory and a greater sales mix from lower-margin oncology products. The company expects this margin squeeze to be temporary, projecting normalization over the next two quarters as older inventory is replaced and the product mix continues to shift toward acute care.

What Heron Therapeutics Does

Heron Therapeutics develops, manufactures, and commercializes specialty pharmaceutical products for pain management and chemotherapy-induced side effects in hospital and cancer care settings.
