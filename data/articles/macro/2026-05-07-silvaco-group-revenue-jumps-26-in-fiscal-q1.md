Silvaco Group (NASDAQ:SVCO) reported marked progress in the first quarter, as its technology investments continued to pay off with higher sales and improved margins. Notable Developments
Revenue grew 26%, driven by expansion in both its AI-driven Fabrication Technology Co-Optimization (FTCO) solutions and semiconductor intellectual property segments. Gross margin improved by 8 percentage points.
SIP revenue surged 270%, now making up almost a quarter of total revenue as over 160 new IP parts launched and the Mixel acquisition contributed meaningfully.
While operating losses narrowed, EDA software revenue declined 20%, reflecting continued weakness in that area and underscoring the company’s mix shift toward higher-growth products.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management forecasts continued sequential growth, with second-quarter revenue targeted at $18.0 million +/- 10% and bookings at $19.0 million +/- 10%, alongside steady gross margins. The company reiterated its target of achieving profitability in the upcoming quarter, underpinned by higher demand for AI-based tools and record SIP contribution. Investors should closely track cash flow, operational profitability, and progress in EDA stabilization as the company seeks to lock in its turnaround.

Quarterly Highlights

The biggest driver of growth this quarter was the robust adoption of AI-embedded process optimization tools within the TCAD (Technology Computer-Aided Design) product line. This family helps semiconductor engineers simulate and optimize chipmaking processes with reduced time and cost, and now accounts for more than half of total company sales.

Meanwhile, the SIP (Semiconductor Intellectual Property) business nearly quadrupled its revenue share year over year, benefitting from new product launches and synergy with the recently acquired Mixel portfolio. Over 160 new IP parts, including foundational building blocks and advanced interfaces used in chip design, reached customers during the period.

The older EDA (Electronic Design Automation) software segment, which helps design integrated circuits, remains the primary area of weakness with sales down 20%. While the company invested in faster, AI-driven modeling tools within EDA, that business now makes up a much smaller share of overall revenue compared to a year ago.

What Silvaco Group Does

Silvaco Group develops and sells software tools and intellectual property blocks that enable semiconductor companies to design, simulate, and manufacture new chips more efficiently.
