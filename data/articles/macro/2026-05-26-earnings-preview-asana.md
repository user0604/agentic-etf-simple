Earnings Preview: Asana

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Asana (NYSE:ASAN), a provider of work management software that helps teams organize and track projects, is expected to report its Q1 FY2027 earnings on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In Q4 FY2026, Asana delivered revenue of $205.6 million, representing 9% growth year over year. The company reported non-GAAP operating income of $18.2 million, with a non-GAAP operating margin of 9%. Net income was $19.9 million, or $0.08 per diluted share. These results beat analyst estimates for both earnings per share and revenue. The company highlighted ongoing momentum in its artificial intelligence (AI) offerings, particularly with the AI Studio and AI Teammates products, and noted improving customer retention metrics. The quarter also saw continued cost discipline and margin expansion, as well as strong free cash flow generation.

Positives
Revenue and earnings beat expectations: Asana exceeded analyst consensus for both revenue and earnings per share, indicating stronger-than-expected business performance.
AI product traction is accelerating: AI Studio annual recurring revenue surpassed $6 million, growing over 50% quarter over quarter, and the company onboarded over 200 customers into the AI Teammates beta program. This suggests growing customer interest in Asana's AI capabilities.
Improved customer retention and expansion: Net revenue retention (NRR) stabilized at 96%, with core customer NRR at 97%. The top 10 renewals in the quarter were above 100% NRR, reflecting strong relationships with large enterprise customers.
Margin expansion and strong cash flow: Operating margin improved by 10 percentage points year over year. Adjusted free cash flow reached $25.7 million, or 13% of revenue, showing improved operational efficiency.
Negatives
Product-led growth (PLG) remains a headwind: The self-serve and small business segment continued to face challenges due to changes in how customers discover and evaluate software, especially with the rise of AI-driven search. This dynamic is expected to remain a drag on growth through FY2027.
General and administrative expenses increased: These costs rose by 27% year over year, driven partly by impairment charges related to subleased office space and higher personnel costs, which could pressure future profitability if not contained.
Tech sector exposure still a risk: While the technology vertical stabilized, management remains cautious about calling a full recovery, and the company’s guidance assumes only modest improvement in this area.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
AI Teammates general availability and initial adoption: Investors should look for updates on the rollout status of AI Teammates, which is expected to become generally available for sales-led customers by the end of Q1. Early customer adoption and use cases will be important indicators of future growth potential.
Revenue growth in enterprise versus self-serve segments: The enterprise segment has shown strength, while product-led (self-serve) growth remains under pressure. Watch for any signs of improvement in self-serve or further acceleration in enterprise wins.
Net revenue retention and customer expansion: Management expects only modest improvement in NRR for FY2027. Any upside surprise in retention or expansion rates, especially among large customers, could support a more positive outlook.
Progress on margin expansion and cost discipline: Asana has guided for operating margin expansion in FY2027. Monitor whether the company continues to deliver on this, especially as it invests further in AI product development and go-to-market initiatives.
Impact of share repurchase program: The board recently increased share repurchase authorization, with nearly $200 million available. Updates on buyback activity and its effect on share count and earnings per share will be relevant for shareholders.
