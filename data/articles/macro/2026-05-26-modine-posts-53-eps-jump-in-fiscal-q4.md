Modine Posts 53% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 4:54 PM

Key Takeaways
Business win: Modine delivered record-breaking quarterly results fueled by explosive growth in its data center cooling business.
Performance: Adjusted earnings per share climbed 53% over the same quarter last year.
Outlook: Management projects another year of double-digit sales and profit growth, led by data center contracts.

Thermal management solutions provider Modine Manufacturing (NYSE:MOD) capped its fiscal fourth quarter with record top-line and profit results, as strong demand for data center cooling drove unprecedented growth. The company reported these results for the three months ended March 31, 2026, in a release dated May 26, 2026. Overall, the quarter saw robust expansion but was marked by higher costs and margin pressures linked to rapid scale-up efforts.

Notable Developments
Sales in the Climate Solutions segment rose 87% year over year to $665.9 million, led by data center cooling products.
Free cash flow surged by 464%, reaching $152.8 million for the quarter, mainly from higher operating earnings and effective working capital management.
Margins declined, with gross profit margin dropping from 25.7% to 22.5%, due to temporary expansion costs and increased tariffs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Modine’s management expects double-digit growth to continue in fiscal 2027, targeting between 20% and 35% net sales growth and a jump in adjusted EBITDA to $650 million–$680 million. The company plans further expansion in the data center segment, supported by its $4 billion long-term chiller contract. Investors should watch for progress on margin recovery and the successful execution of the planned Performance Technologies spin-off.

Quarterly Highlights

The Climate Solutions segment achieved an 87% sales increase, driven by record growth in cooling systems for data centers, including revenues from three recent acquisitions. This exceptional demand led to the company’s largest-ever capacity expansion, setting the stage for sustained momentum but resulting in temporarily higher expenses.

While operating earnings and cash flow improved, margins dropped as expansion and integration costs rose. The quarter’s gross margin fell 3.2 percentage points year over year, reflecting not only manufacturing scale-up but higher tariffs and input costs.

The Performance Technologies business saw sales remain flat and margins contract, affected by weaker stationary power demand and higher material costs. The segment’s upcoming spin-off is a key step in Modine’s plan to focus on higher-growth, higher-margin businesses.

What Modine Manufacturing Does

Modine designs and manufactures thermal management products — such as heat exchangers, cooling systems, and HVAC equipment — for data centers, transportation, commercial, and industrial markets worldwide.
