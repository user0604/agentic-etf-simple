EAT Company Overview
Brinker International owns and franchises casual dining locations through its primary brands, Chili's Grill and Bar and Maggiano's Little Italy. The company earns revenue by selling food and beverages at corporate-owned sites and collecting royalties from franchise partners globally. Customers are encouraged to return through digital convenience tools and a rewards program that offers complimentary items and personalized marketing, fostering brand loyalty within a competitive restaurant market.

Brinker Raises Outlook as Chili’s Drives Steady Growth

Brinker International runs Chili’s Grill & Bar and Maggiano’s Little Italy restaurants in the U.S. and abroad. Chili’s has posted growth for 20 straight quarters, thanks in part to new menu items and promotions. Over the last five years, the stock returned 92.4%, beating the S&P 500 by 21.8 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Earnings per share (Non-GAAP): $2.90 (up 9.0% YoY)
Revenue: $1,470.2 million (up 3.2% YoY)
Chili’s comparable sales: up 4.0% YoY

Latest Developments

Management raised full-year revenue and earnings guidance, which matters because it shows confidence in ongoing sales momentum even with rising costs.
Maggiano’s sales fell again, so investors should watch if turnaround efforts at this smaller segment can help overall profit in future quarters.
