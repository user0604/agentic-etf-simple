Squeezed Shoppers Flock to Costco in Q3

Gas brought them in, but the warehouse kept them spending.

Alicia Alfiere, MBA

Jun 5, 2026, 9:16 AM

Comments
9.5

Squeezed consumers flocked to Costco (NASDAQ:COST) in its fiscal third quarter. The company reported record-breaking volumes in the third quarter and said - “All three four-week fiscal periods of the quarter set successive all-time company volume sales records, with the final five weeks of the quarter becoming our top five volume weeks ever.”



Gas was a big draw and Costco saw “unprecedented demand,” with many warehouse locations requiring several daily deliveries to meet demand. Customers definitely showed up for gas, and Costco benefited with net sales growing 11.6% and same store sales increasing 9.8%.

But customers didn't just fill up their tanks and leave. They shopped in warehouses too. Excluding gas sales and adjusting for foreign exchange, comparable sales grew 6.6% in the third quarter. Shopping frequency, average ticket, memberships, and membership upgrades all increased in the quarter as well. All of which hints at the strong valuation proposition that Costco has - even when consumers are feeling strapped.

The company's bottom line was pretty strong too - net income per diluted share was $4.93 in the quarter, up from $4.28 last year. And the company continued to generate cash, with free cash flow numbers coming in at $6.9 billion for the first 36 weeks of the fiscal year.
