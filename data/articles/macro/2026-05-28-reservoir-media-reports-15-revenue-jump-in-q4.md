Reservoir Media Reports 15% Revenue Jump in Q4

Key Takeaways
Reservoir Media delivered strong revenue growth due to record digital and international expansion.
Earnings per share increased 75% year over year in the quarter.
Management expects growth to continue, but at a slower pace for fiscal 2027.

Reservoir Media (NASDAQ:RSVR) expanded its music catalog and digital reach, driving financial results to new highs. The music rights and publishing company, active across global markets and genres, reported its fiscal fourth quarter results on May 28, 2026, covering the period ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected healthy growth in key business lines, but rising costs and debt tempered net earnings gains.

Notable Developments
Digital revenue in both core segments saw double-digit growth, with digital in Recorded Music up 17% and Music Publishing digital up 24% for the quarter.
High-profile acquisitions included the complete Miles Davis publishing and master rights, and the purchase of India-based Musicraft Entertainment, strengthening international presence.
Net debt increased 17% year over year, now at $429.8 million, as the company continued to fund acquisitions mainly through borrowing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects revenue to grow by approximately 7% for fiscal 2027, with a slightly slower pace expected for profits. The company aims to continue expanding its catalog and digital platforms while closely monitoring acquisition costs and leverage. Investors should watch for how well new acquisitions and emerging market investments convert into higher profitability and cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

Digital subscriptions and streaming drove much of the growth in both the Music Publishing and Recorded Music divisions, with digital now accounting for the largest share of revenue in each unit.

Catalog expansion continued at a brisk pace, featuring the purchase of the Miles Davis estate and strategic deals in rapidly growing markets like India and the Middle East/North Africa region. The PopIndia and PopArabia subsidiaries contributed to diversification and provided a foothold for future industry growth.

While operating income and OIBDA moved up by double digits, net income rose minimally, as higher interest expenses and amortization associated with new acquisitions absorbed much of the improvement. The rising debt load from acquisitions bears monitoring in coming quarters.

What Reservoir Media Does

Reservoir Media is a global independent music company that owns, develops, and manages music copyrights, recorded music, and related rights across multiple genres and regions.
