TDW Company Overview
Tidewater operates a global fleet of offshore service vessels that provide transportation and marine support for energy projects, including subsea construction and windfarm development. The company generates revenue through short-term and multi-year charter contracts with integrated energy firms and offshore drilling contractors. By maintaining specialized equipment like platform supply vessels and anchor handling tugs, Tidewater creates high switching costs for clients who rely on its technical capabilities and established safety standards across major maritime regions.

Tidewater Expands Offshore Reach With Brazil Acquisition

Tidewater helps offshore energy companies by moving supplies, equipment, and crews to platforms at sea. The company is growing its reach by buying a Brazilian rival to enter a bigger market. Over the last five years, the stock returned 547.9%, beating the S&P 500 by 475.2 percentage points.

Latest Developments
