Notable Developments
PEMGARDA product revenue for the quarter reached $13.7 million, all from its COVID-19 antibody therapy.
Research and development expenses nearly tripled year over year, mainly due to expanded DECLARATION clinical trial costs for VYD2311.
Enrollment for the pivotal DECLARATION Phase 3 trial concluded, but top-line data delivery shifted to Q3 2026 following a sample size increase.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management believes current cash resources can support operations through the upcoming DECLARATION readout and potential VYD2311 product launch. The most critical issue for investors is whether VYD2311, its next-generation monoclonal antibody, will prove successful in the pivotal trial scheduled to report in Q3 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

All revenue during the period came from PEMGARDA, a COVID-19 antibody authorized under emergency use allowance for immunocompromised patients. The revenue figure grew 22% compared to the previous year, signaling stable, if modest, demand in a specialized market.

The company’s main development focus was the DECLARATION Phase 3 study for VYD2311, a new intramuscular antibody. After achieving enrollment of over 2,300 participants, the company increased the trial size based on interim safety reviews to better ensure statistical power, moving the expected data release back by about two months.

Spending to advance its pipeline, including VYD2311 and other candidates for RSV and measles, widened total net loss to $41.4 million. While the cash balance remains strong for now, the firm will need to manage costs as it approaches multiple clinical inflection points.

What Invivyd Does

Invivyd researches and develops monoclonal antibody therapies to prevent and treat viral infectious diseases, with an initial focus on COVID-19.
