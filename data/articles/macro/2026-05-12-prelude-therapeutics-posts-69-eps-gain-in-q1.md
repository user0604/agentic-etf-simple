Notable Developments
First clinical trial enrollment began for PRT12396, a mutant-selective JAK2 inhibitor for blood cancers, marking progress toward human testing.
New revenue was recognized for the first time, totaling $4.6 million.
Research and development costs declined over 50%, mainly from a pause in the company’s SMARCA2 clinical program.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management anticipates reaching critical regulatory and clinical milestones in mid-2026, including filing for a new clinical study in breast cancer. With the recent financing completed after the quarter, leadership expects to fund operations into the second quarter of 2028. Investors should watch for data from early-stage clinical trials, especially for PRT12396 and advancement of the KAT6A degrader program.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s drug pipeline advanced as it began first-in-human studies for PRT12396, targeting certain genetic mutations in polycythemia vera and myelofibrosis (types of blood cancer). Early regulatory steps for the KAT6A selective degrader program in breast cancer remain on track, with important trial filings expected by midyear.

First quarter revenue emerged through partnerships with other biotechnology companies, reflected in increased deferred revenue and up-front collaboration payments. The company reported $4.6 million in revenue, absent in the prior-year quarter, demonstrating partners’ ongoing interest in its platform.

A sharp reduction in research and development spending was achieved, primarily by pausing a more expensive clinical program. The narrower net loss and stated extension of the cash runway reduce immediate funding concerns, especially with the successful $90 million capital raise after quarter-end.
