Sana Biotechnology (NASDAQ:SANA)’s flagship cell therapy programs advanced as operational priorities aligned with public milestones and fresh support arrived from new partners. The Seattle-based biotechnology company, developing next-generation cell and gene therapies, released its first quarter 2026 results on May 11, 2026. Notable developments
The company secured a strategic partnership with Mayo Clinic, featuring a $25.0 million equity investment and added external backing for its lead diabetes program.
Non-GAAP operating cash burn fell 20.6% compared to the prior year, evidence of continued cost discipline while preparing for clinical trial launches.
First-quarter research and development expenses declined 22.8% from last year, marking lower headcount and scaled-back spending on non-core programs.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its goal to start first-in-human clinical trials for two leading programs—one for type 1 diabetes (SC451) and one for lymphoma (SG293)—later this year, with cash on hand projected to extend into 2027. The upcoming clinical trial initiations, along with early human data readouts for SG293, are set to be crucial milestones to monitor as the company seeks to demonstrate the value of its technology in patients.

Quarterly highlights

The Mayo Clinic partnership validates commercial interest in Sana’s hypoimmune technology, marked by new clinical data showing extended insulin production in a type 1 diabetes patient after HIP-modified cell transplantation. This capital infusion also provides a financial boost for continued program development.

The company’s operating cash burn improved compared to the prior year, supported by lower research and development expenses as Sana prioritized its two most advanced programs. Costs fell in part due to winding down other pipeline efforts and emphasizing streamlined manufacturing partnerships.

Although cash usage and net loss narrowed year over year, Sana ended the quarter with $101.1 million in cash and equivalents. Including post-period investments and stock sales, pro forma cash stood at $128.9 million, which management believes is enough to fund operations into 2027.

What Sana Biotechnology does

Sana Biotechnology develops cell and gene therapies using engineered cell technologies and aims to deliver new treatments for serious diseases like diabetes, cancer, and autoimmune disorders.
