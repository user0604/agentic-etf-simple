Marti Technologies (NYSEMKT:MRT) saw strong platform growth, as demand for its mobility services remained robust and gross margins more than doubled. Notable Developments
User metrics accelerated sharply, with trips up 93.3% and unique platform consumers up 88.9%, due to expansion in ride-hailing and the launch of subscription packages.
Gross profit margin climbed to 72.0%, from 36.8% the prior year, as subscription revenue improved overall unit economics and cost leverage.
Net loss narrowed by 26%, but Marti continued to use significant cash for operations, with $4.8 million in cash at quarter end and new convertible notes issued for liquidity.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to focus on achieving its fiscal 2026 targets, including $70.0 million in annual revenue and positive Adjusted EBITDA, with cost efficiency as a core theme. The most important area for investors to watch is Marti’s ability to improve cash flow while balancing growth and managing regulatory and macroeconomic risks within Turkey.

Quarterly Highlights

Trips on Marti’s platform rose to 16.22 million from 8.39 million a year ago, with the introduction of subscription packages playing a key role. Expansion in ride-hailing outpaced other mobility types, and both user and driver milestones exceeded management’s quarterly operational targets. These achievements point to growing adoption and engagement across services, even while the number of vehicles deployed fell, indicating higher utilization per fleet unit.

The company delivered a major improvement in gross margin, surging to 72.0%, as a greater share of revenue came from the higher-margin subscription products and as cost of revenues grew much more slowly than total revenue. This supported a higher gross profit figure despite rising expenses related to technology, marketing, and regulatory requirements. Loss from operations and net loss both narrowed year over year, but Marti remains unprofitable and reliant on capital raises to finance growth.

Cash and equivalents totaled $4.8 million as of March 31, 2026, while net cash used in operations was $5.7 million during the quarter, roughly flat from last year. To support ongoing growth and meet liquidity needs, Marti issued $2.7 million of convertible notes in the quarter and announced a $2.5 million share repurchase program set to run until October 2026.

What Marti Technologies Does

Marti Technologies operates a digital platform for ride-hailing and micro-mobility services, including electric scooters, bikes, and mopeds, primarily across urban areas in Turkey.
