Notable Developments
The Data Center segment set new records, posting $75.2 billion in revenue, up 92% from the prior-year quarter.
Gross margin advanced by 14.4 percentage points, reflecting both efficiency improvements and demand for high-margin AI products.
The board authorized an additional $80 billion share repurchase program and increased the quarterly dividend to $0.25 per share, up from $0.01 per share previously.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management forecasts revenue of $91.0 billion (plus or minus 2%) for the upcoming quarter, with gross margins expected to hold steady at current high levels. The updated outlook assumes no Data Center compute revenue from China and signals steady investment in research and partnerships. Investors should closely watch ongoing growth in the Data Center segment, as this area remains the primary driver behind results and future forecasts.

Quarterly Highlights

Demand for AI hardware continued to surge, with Data Center compute and networking leading the way. Data Center networking nearly tripled from the prior year, and ongoing investments supported rollouts of new products like the Vera Rubin platform, which is designed to accelerate agentic AI workloads.

Operating expenses increased but were outpaced by revenue and operating income growth. Research and development spending rose to $6.32 billion in the period, underlining management’s focus on rapid innovation while still delivering record profitability and free cash flow.

Diversification efforts produced steady results in Edge Computing, with revenue climbing 29% year over year. The board’s decision to boost capital returns, including a new $0.25 dividend and expanded buybacks, reflects confidence in the business’s financial strength.
