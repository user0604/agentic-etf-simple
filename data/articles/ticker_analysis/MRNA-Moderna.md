MRNA Company Overview
Moderna develops and commercializes medicines using messenger RNA, a genetic molecule that instructs human cells to produce proteins for treating or preventing diseases. The company generates revenue primarily through the sale of vaccines against respiratory viruses and maintains a competitive position by leveraging a shared technology platform and global manufacturing network. This integrated approach allows it to rapidly design and test new therapeutic candidates across oncology, rare diseases, and infectious health.

Moderna Expands Vaccine Portfolio With New European Launches

Moderna makes vaccines and medicines using messenger RNA technology to fight infectious diseases, cancer, and rare illnesses. The company recently launched the world’s first combination flu and COVID vaccine in Europe, showing its ability to create new products. Over the past five years, the stock has fallen 76.1% while the S&P 500 gained 73.1%, underperforming by 149.3 percentage points.
