American Public Education (NASDAQ:APEI) delivered a business win in the first quarter, showing improvement across core metrics and surpassing its own targets. Overall, the period featured solid execution and progress on strategic initiatives, though margin gains in some areas were modest.

Notable Developments
Revenue reached $174.7 million, up 6.2% from the prior year, with both Military+ and Health+ segments reporting higher enrollments and sales.
Net income available to common stockholders more than doubled to $17.7 million, while operating cash flow increased 71.1% year over year.
The company completed a legal merger of its institutions in March and expects operational integration to finish by the third quarter, following regulatory approval.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 guidance for revenue, net income, adjusted EBITDA, and earnings per share, citing strong demand in both core segments and progress on integration activities. The main focus for investors will be whether the company can improve profit margins in the Health+ segment and fully realize planned synergies from the recent institutional combination.

Quarterly Highlights

The Military+ segment, which includes American Public University System and related programs, posted revenue gains of 6.5%. Student registrations grew 4.0%, despite modest government funding delays, reinforcing the stability of the military and veteran-focused student base.

The Health+ segment, which includes Rasmussen University and Hondros College of Nursing, saw revenue advance 11.0%, supported by a 7.8% rise in student enrollment and the opening of a new campus in Orlando, Florida. While enrollment and top-line growth were strong, Health+ segment margins improved by two percentage points from the prior year due to increased marketing and operational investments.

The company strengthened its cash position, ending the quarter with $221 million in cash and equivalents after refinancing that is expected to save about $3.7 million in annual interest. In March, it also authorized a $50 million share repurchase program, primarily to offset stock compensation.

What American Public Education Does

American Public Education provides accredited online and campus-based higher education, focusing on military, veteran, and healthcare students through its institutions including APUS, Rasmussen, and Hondros College of Nursing.
