Management outlook and what to watch

Management confirmed that key clinical data for MICVO, its lead antibody-drug conjugate, will be shared in mid- and late 2026, and it expects its current funds will support operations into the fourth quarter of the year. The most important thing for investors to track is the upcoming release of efficacy and safety data that will determine whether recent changes to MICVO’s dosing strategies are successful.

Quarterly highlights

The company completed enrollment for its MICVO Phase 1 monotherapy trial in patients with recurrent or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, maintaining its clinical timelines and moving to new dosing strategies designed to address prior safety events in higher body weight patients.

Operational spending rose, with research and development costs up year over year due to increased activity in both monotherapy and combination therapy trials, while lower employee-related costs brought general and administrative expenses down by over a quarter.

Cash and short-term investments decreased from year-end levels, leaving the company with $42.5 million at March 31, 2026, and management acknowledging it may need further funding after the fourth quarter unless new partnerships or capital sources are secured.
