Sky Harbour Group Revenue Jumps 56% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Revenue climbed sharply as new campuses opened and stabilized facilities outperformed leasing forecasts.
Revenue rose 56% year over year.

Sky Harbour Group (NYSE:SKYH) delivered a strong business win as new campus openings and higher occupancy drove a significant increase in revenue. The developer and operator of Home Base Operator (HBO) hangar campuses for business aviation released its first-quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026. Overall, the period featured an expanding footprint, ongoing cash flow improvement, and solid capital resources, even as profitability remains a work in progress.

Notable developments
Revenue per square foot on stabilized campuses surpassed forecasts, with economic occupancy reaching 103% at mature sites.
OPF Phase 2 in Miami opened at 68% occupancy and is achieving higher initial rents than Phase 1, indicating strong lease-up momentum.
Liquidity remains solid, supported by $187.6 million in cash and Treasuries, and an undrawn $180.6 million facility from JP Morgan as of March 31, 2026.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management is guiding for an annualized revenue run rate of $42–46 million and a return to positive adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization) by year-end. The top priority will be scaling new campus lease-up and increasing operating efficiency to reach that profitability milestone.

Quarterly highlights

The period saw continued expansion as constructed assets and projects in progress reached $350 million at quarter end, up 27% from a year earlier. OPF Phase 2 in Miami, which opened in May, reached 68% occupancy shortly after launch, with lease rates surpassing those achieved in earlier phases.

Revenue per square foot continued to trend higher as legacy leases were renewed at premium rates. With stabilized campuses exceeding 100% economic occupancy, the company reinforced its view that strong demand and supply constraints support ongoing rent escalation.

Liquidity remained robust, with nearly $188 million in cash and Treasuries, plus substantial undrawn financing capacity to fund additional campus builds. This capital strength should allow for the scheduled rollout of new projects in Dallas, Hartford, Salt Lake City, and other expansion markets in 2026 and beyond.

What Sky Harbour Group does

Sky Harbour Group develops, owns, and operates specialized aircraft hangar campuses at major U.S. airports, providing dedicated facilities and premium services to business aviation operators.
