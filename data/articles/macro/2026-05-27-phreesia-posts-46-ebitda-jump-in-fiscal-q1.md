Key Takeaways
Phreesia reported stronger margins and a return to profitability as its payment and financing platform grew.
Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) rose 46.4%, with revenue up 13% over the prior year period.
Management reaffirmed full-year revenue and earnings guidance, but flagged continued spending uncertainty in its Network Solutions business.

Phreesia (NYSE:PHR) returned to profitability this quarter, buoyed by expansion in its payment solutions business and steady cost control. The leading healthcare technology company that streamlines patient intake, payment, and provider workflows released results for its fiscal first quarter (reported May 27, 2026, for the period ended April 30, 2026). Overall, the period brought notable margin gains and solid financial improvements, though some revenue headwinds persisted in specific business segments.

Notable Developments
Payment solutions revenue rose 40% year over year, driven by the full integration of AccessOne and greater managed payment volumes.
Profit margins improved: Adjusted EBITDA jumped to $30.5 million, while net income swung to positive $3.0 million from a loss in the prior year.
Network Solutions revenue grew 15%, but management maintained a cautious outlook for the segment due to reduced life sciences spending in the second half of the year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its full-year outlook unchanged, expecting revenue between $510 million and $520 million, and adjusted EBITDA of $125 million to $135 million. The main focus going forward is on maintaining cost discipline and watching for volatility in Network Solutions revenue. Investors should pay close attention to trends in life sciences client spending as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

The payment solutions segment, boosted by the integration of AccessOne (a provider financing and patient payment plan platform), led all business lines with substantial revenue growth. A $100 million expansion of the PNC Bank securitization facility in the quarter enabled Phreesia to scale this product for more providers.

Disciplined cost controls contributed materially to margin expansion. Sales and marketing expense fell by 7%, and research and development costs declined 11% year over year, with artificial intelligence adoption cited as a driver of operating efficiency.

Network Solutions, which supports digital patient outreach and marketing for life sciences companies, grew in the quarter but remains the company’s primary area for revenue uncertainty. Lower spending commitments in this vertical may lead to erratic growth for the rest of the fiscal year, according to management.

What Phreesia Does

Phreesia provides a software platform that helps healthcare organizations manage patient intake, payments, clinical engagement, and communications.
