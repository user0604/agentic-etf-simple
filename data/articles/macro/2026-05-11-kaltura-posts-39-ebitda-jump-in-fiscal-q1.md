Key Takeaways
Kaltura delivered solid operational progress despite continued revenue decline.
Adjusted EBITDA rose 39% while revenue fell year over year.
Management expects a return to revenue growth beginning next quarter.

Kaltura (NASDAQ:KLTR) delivered operational improvements even as its top-line revenue continued to decline. The enterprise video and digital experience software provider reported first quarter results for the period ended March 31, 2026, released on May 11, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed better profitability and cash flow, even as weakness in key customer segments weighed on revenue.

Notable Developments
Adjusted EBITDA improved by 39%, reaching $5.7 million in the quarter, and operating cash flow was positive in the first quarter for the first time.
Revenue declined 5% year over year, driven by a 17% fall in the Media & Telecom segment; Enterprise, Education and Technology segment revenue fell only 1%.
Kaltura launched new AI-based avatar products and completed the PathFactory acquisition, enhancing content intelligence and journey orchestration capabilities.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue growth to resume with a 2–3% increase projected for the second quarter, and a similar step-up for full-year 2026. The outlook highlights stabilization in core enterprise and education customers, and early market adoption of new artificial intelligence products. Investors should watch whether AI-driven offerings and improved retention translate into sustained growth in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability improved, as adjusted EBITDA jumped to $5.7 million. Operating cash flow was positive for the first time in a first quarter, reflecting lower operating expenses and a higher gross margin of 72%.

Revenue contracted by 5%, but the loss was almost entirely due to continued weakness in the Media & Telecom segment. This segment's revenue fell 17% to $10.5 million, while the core Enterprise, Education and Technology segment was nearly flat year over year at $34.2 million.

Kaltura rolled out new conversational AI avatar products and closed its acquisition of PathFactory, which adds content intelligence features and broadens the product suite. The company also reported several new six and seven-figure customer deals in the quarter, and achieved ISO/IEC 42001 certification for its artificial intelligence management systems.

What Kaltura Does

Kaltura provides cloud-based software for enterprise video, digital events, and AI-powered digital experience management, serving businesses, educational institutions, and media organizations.
