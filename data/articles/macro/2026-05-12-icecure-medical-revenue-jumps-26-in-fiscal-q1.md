IceCure Medical Revenue Jumps 26% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 9:41 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Strong commercial momentum continued, driven by increased U.S. account activity after FDA clearance.
Revenue climbed 26%, with gross profit up and the net loss widening.
Management expects robust customer growth and further U.S. expansion in the coming quarters.

Commercial expansion accelerated for IceCure Medical (NASDAQ:ICCM) as hospitals and clinics quickly adopted its tumor-freezing technology after regulatory clearance. A medical device developer specializing in minimally invasive cancer treatments, it marked early progress in broadening U.S. access to its ProSense system. The company reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 12, 2026 (covering the three months ended March 31, 2026), confirming significant gains in uptake and revenue, but alongside increasing operating losses. Overall, the quarter delivered impressive adoption in North America but highlighted ongoing financial pressures as expenses grew faster than revenue.

Notable Developments
Active U.S. customer accounts using ProSense rose 46% from 13 to 19 since FDA clearance, supporting strong commercial growth.
Operating expenses grew 17% year over year, mainly on higher research and development spending, driving a wider net loss.
Gross margin increased two percentage points to 32%, reflecting higher-margin U.S. sales, though profitability remains a challenge.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management plans to further expand its sales reach across eight U.S. regions and expects more hospitals and clinics to begin offering ProSense. New clinical studies, including a 30-site post-marketing trial, are set to support regulatory and reimbursement progress. Investors should closely follow the pace of new system installations and customer adoption in the months ahead.

Quarterly Highlights

Adoption of the ProSense cryoablation system sharply increased, fueled by pent-up demand following FDA marketing clearance. Hospitals like Thomas Hospital in Alabama and imaging centers such as Shero Imaging in Missouri became new customers, illustrating growing appeal across a range of facilities.

Revenue growth benefited from stronger sales in North America, even as Asian markets saw some decline. The number of active U.S. accounts rose to 19, giving the company a larger base of recurring procedure revenue but still leaving room for much more market penetration.

Operating expenses rose, particularly in research related to the post-marketing CHoICE study. While the U.S. sales mix helped gross margin improve, the overall net loss widened due to rising costs and increased investment in commercial and clinical initiatives.

What IceCure Medical Does

IceCure Medical develops and markets minimally invasive cryoablation systems for treating cancer, using extreme cold to destroy tumors without surgery.
