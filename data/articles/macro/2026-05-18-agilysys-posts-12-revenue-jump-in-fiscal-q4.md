Key Takeaways
Agilysys delivered record sales and continued subscription revenue momentum, capping its fiscal year with strong business expansion.
Revenue rose year over year.

Agilysys marked another record quarter as cloud and subscription revenues maintained strong growth, further advancing its transition to a recurring revenue business model. The leading provider of hospitality software solutions, Agilysys released results for its fourth fiscal quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 18, 2026. Overall, the period featured robust revenue and profit growth across core segments, improved cash generation, and renewed confidence in future performance.

Notable Developments
Recurring revenue set a quarterly record, rising to $54.4 million and making up 65.5% of total revenue for the period.
Subscription revenue increased 24.1% year over year and represented 68% of recurring revenue, reflecting advancement of the cloud model.
The gross margin improved by 3.7 percentage points, while free cash flow reached $35.4 million, contributing to a year-end cash balance of $116.9 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its guidance for fiscal 2027, now targeting total revenue of $365–$370 million and adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) at 24% of revenue, with at least 30% growth in subscription revenue. The company faces the challenge of maintaining this strong subscription growth at scale, so investor attention will be on the ongoing shift to cloud and recurring revenue, as well as operational discipline supporting profit margins.

Quarterly Highlights

Subscription and recurring revenue continued to drive results, with quarterly recurring sales climbing to over $54 million and now comprising nearly two-thirds of total revenue. The mix of subscription revenue within recurring sales increased—highlighted by a 24% year-over-year subscription jump—underlining Agilysys’ progress in its cloud and software-as-a-service (SaaS) transformation.

The company’s margin profile showed meaningful improvement, as gross margin climbed to 64.4% and adjusted EBITDA rose 45%, signaling effective cost control while operating expenses grew slower than revenue. Free cash flow was robust, growing to $35.4 million, and period-end cash rose to $116.9 million, leaving the company debt-free and further strengthening its balance sheet.

Steady innovation continued, with management highlighting the launch of new AI-enabled software modules and strong customer retention, as well as notable client wins across hotel, resort, and casino sectors. Product and professional services revenue also saw gains, but the most significant contribution to growth remained Agilysys’ expanding subscription base and recurring revenue model.

What Agilysys Does

Agilysys provides cloud-based enterprise software solutions for the hospitality industry, including property management, point-of-sale systems, and guest experience enhancements for hotels, resorts, casinos, and related businesses.
