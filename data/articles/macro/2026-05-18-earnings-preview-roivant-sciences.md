Earnings Preview: Roivant Sciences

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Roivant Sciences (NASDAQ:ROIV), a biotechnology company focused on developing new medicines for autoimmune and rare diseases, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025 on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2025 Recap

For the third quarter ended Dec. 31, 2025, Roivant reported revenue of $2 million and a net loss attributable to shareholders of $265.9 million. The company’s reported loss per share of $0.24 beat analyst estimates, while revenue fell short of expectations. The quarter was marked by significant progress in Roivant’s clinical pipeline, especially with positive Phase II results for brepocitinib in cutaneous sarcoidosis, and continued advancement of other late-stage assets. Roivant ended the quarter with a strong cash position of $4.5 billion, supporting ongoing research and development activities.

Positives
EPS beat analyst expectations: The company reported a loss per share of $0.24, which was better than the consensus estimate of a $0.27 loss. This suggests Roivant is managing expenses effectively despite ongoing high R&D investment.
Positive Phase II results for brepocitinib in cutaneous sarcoidosis: Brepocitinib showed a significant improvement in disease activity, with a 21.6-point placebo-adjusted mean improvement, and 100% of high-dose patients achieving meaningful benefit. This strengthens the case for advancing to Phase III trials.
Strong cash position: Roivant reported $4.5 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities at quarter end, providing ample resources to fund its pipeline and potential commercial launches.
Multiple clinical milestones achieved: The company completed enrollment in several key studies, including Phase IIb for IMVT-1402 in difficult-to-treat rheumatoid arthritis and Phase II for mosliciguat in pulmonary hypertension associated with interstitial lung disease, setting up a catalyst-rich year ahead.
Negatives
Revenue missed consensus estimates: Reported revenue of $2 million was below analyst expectations, reflecting the company’s current lack of commercialized products and reliance on milestone or licensing revenue.
Operating expenses increased year over year: Research and development expenses rose by $23.8 million compared to the same quarter last year, mainly due to expanded clinical activity and higher share-based compensation. This contributed to a wider net loss.
General and administrative costs rose sharply: G&A expenses increased by $33.5 million, driven by higher share-based compensation and an impairment loss from relocating the U.S. headquarters.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2025's Report
Progress on brepocitinib Phase III programs: Investors should look for updates on the initiation and enrollment pace of Phase III trials in cutaneous sarcoidosis and ongoing studies in non-infectious uveitis. Any new guidance on regulatory timelines or data readouts will be important.
Updates on IMVT-1402 development: Watch for news on the fully enrolled difficult-to-treat rheumatoid arthritis study and any early signals from ongoing trials in Graves’ disease, myasthenia gravis, and other autoimmune conditions.
Cash runway and capital allocation: With a large cash balance, Roivant’s spending plans, potential share buybacks, or new in-licensing deals could impact future results and investor sentiment.
Legal proceedings and milestones in the Moderna case: The upcoming March 2026 jury trial and any related legal developments could affect Roivant’s financial outlook and potential for milestone payments.
Revenue trends and guidance: Since revenue remains minimal, any new licensing deals, milestone payments, or early commercialization signals will be closely watched, especially in light of the Q3 revenue miss and consensus Q4 sales estimate of $3.4 million.
