Key Takeaways
Nordson posted record sales and profit, topping its own forecasts.
Adjusted earnings per share rose 18% from the prior year’s quarter.

Nordson (NASDAQ:NDSN) exceeded its own expectations with strong organic growth and profit records for the quarter. The precision technology manufacturer, known for its solutions in industrial, medical, and electronics production, released its results for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 20, 2026. Overall, the quarter marked broad-based growth across segments and geographies, while cash flow conversion remained a core strength.

Notable developments
All business segments achieved record second-quarter sales, led by a 10% increase in Industrial Precision Solutions, including positive impacts from currency and recent acquisitions.
Free cash flow rose sharply, with the company converting 113% of net income to cash so far this year; this supports ongoing dividends and reinvestment.
Management raised full-year sales and adjusted earnings targets on the back of a growing order backlog, which increased 18% from the prior year’s quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management increased its full-year outlook based on growing demand and order momentum, now projecting fiscal 2026 sales to reach between $2.93 billion and $3.01 billion and adjusted earnings per share to range from $11.30 to $11.80. The key issue for investors in the coming quarters will be whether segment margins remain stable and the sustainability of international sales growth, especially as currency gains and one-off items shaped this quarter’s comparison.

Quarterly highlights

The Industrial Precision Solutions group posted a 10% sales jump and a higher EBITDA in the quarter, with sales reaching $350 million (up 10% from the prior year) and EBITDA rising to $124 million (up 9%). Medical and Fluid Solutions, supplying medical device and fluid management products, experienced 5% sales growth as recent organic gains outpaced the drag from a prior divestiture, with operating profit steady despite margin pressure. Advanced Technology Solutions delivered a 10% sales increase and higher profitability in the quarter, with sales reaching $178 million (up 10% from the prior year) and EBITDA rising 22% to $48 million.

Order momentum strengthened across all business units, as backlog grew 18%, with demand broadening in Asia Pacific, Europe, and the Americas. About 67% of total revenue came from non-US markets in fiscal year 2025, highlighting the value of Nordson’s global reach and geographic diversification. Currency changes contributed roughly 3% to total revenue growth this period, which helped offset incremental cost pressures reported in some divisions.

The quarter saw the completion of a bolt-on acquisition in North American precision agriculture, alongside ongoing investment in new product development. Cash generation kept pace, supporting dividends and capital return programs.

What Nordson does

Nordson engineers, manufactures, and markets precision dispensing and material processing technologies used in industrial, medical, electronics, and consumer product manufacturing worldwide.
