Notable Developments
Cash and equivalents dropped sharply compared to year-end 2025, as the company used $4.5 million for operations and $8.5 million for investments in the quarter.
Operating expenses climbed 59.1%, with the increase mainly in general and administrative spending, while research and development shrank as the business pivoted to new products.
AIxCrypto converted all Series B preferred stock into common shares, simplifying its capital structure and boosting common shares outstanding to 20.2 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management restated its goal to drive initial product launches and generate first revenue by mid to late 2026. The focus over the next two quarters will remain on operational build-out, regulatory compliance, and reaching proof-of-concept milestones in EAI, RWA tokenization, and AI agent platforms. Investors should monitor cash usage and the ability to move from testing to revenue generation, as liquidity risk will remain high until commercial success is proven.

Quarterly highlights

The EAI Data Platform initiative entered the contract drafting stage, focusing on scenario design, architecture, and operational agreements for both business and consumer-facing elements. This project targets a mid-2026 proof-of-concept milestone, although no revenue or commercial contracts have been announced yet.

In the Agentir AI Agent Arena, technical buildout reached a critical internal review, and management finalized a commercial model based on access pass and arena fees. The phased public launch—expected soon—marks a step forward, but the business will need to demonstrate real user adoption to deliver meaningful revenue.

The RWA tokenization product advanced from strategy to operational planning, including a $10 million investment in Faraday Future stock as the proof-of-concept asset. Despite increased exposure, the actual platform and first tokenized equity launch are still in development, with execution and regulatory hurdles remaining key challenges.
