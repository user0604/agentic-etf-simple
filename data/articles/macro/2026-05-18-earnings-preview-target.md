Earnings Preview: Target

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Target (NYSE:TGT), a leading U.S. retailer selling everyday essentials and discretionary goods, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Target reported its fourth-quarter fiscal 2025 results on Mar. 3, 2026. Net sales for the quarter were $104.8 billion for the full year, reflecting a 1.7 percent decrease from the prior year. Comparable sales declined 2.6 percent, driven by a 2.2 percent drop in traffic and a 0.4 percent decrease in average transaction amount. Despite these declines, adjusted earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter came in at $2.44, which beat analyst estimates. Management highlighted improved sales trends in December and January, with further acceleration into February, signaling early momentum as the new fiscal year began. Key categories such as Food & Beverage, Beauty, and Toys posted growth, while digital and membership revenues also showed strong gains.

Positives
Adjusted EPS beat expectations: Target delivered adjusted EPS above consensus, which reassured investors about profitability despite lower sales.
Acceleration in sales and traffic trends late in the quarter: Sales and customer visits improved in December and January, with management noting continued healthy growth into February. This suggests potential stabilization after several quarters of declines.
Growth in key categories and digital initiatives: Food & Beverage, Beauty, and Toys grew, and same-day delivery and marketplace revenue increased over 30 percent year over year. Membership revenue more than doubled, reflecting traction in digital and loyalty programs.
Inventory shrink improvement: Inventory losses returned to pre-pandemic levels, helping gross margin and indicating progress on operational challenges.
Negatives
Overall sales and comparable sales declined: Net sales fell 1.7 percent and comparable sales dropped 2.6 percent for the year, showing continued pressure from cautious consumer spending and competition.
Traffic remained negative for the year: Customer visits were down 2.2 percent, indicating ongoing challenges in attracting shoppers, though trends improved late in the quarter.
Gross margin rate decreased: The gross margin rate slipped to 27.9 percent from 28.2 percent, reflecting higher markdowns and changes in sales mix.
Operating income and adjusted EPS declined year over year: Operating income fell 8.1 percent and adjusted EPS dropped 14.5 percent, signaling that profitability was still under pressure for the full year.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales and traffic trends: Investors should look for signs that the late-quarter momentum in sales and customer visits has continued into the first quarter, as management cited “very healthy top-line growth” in February.
Progress on merchandising and store initiatives: Target’s multi-year transformation—including the Fun101 hardlines revamp, home category reset, and new shop-in-shop concepts—was announced and is underway. Watch for early evidence of impact from these changes, especially in Beauty, Home, and Food & Beverage.
Margin performance amid ongoing investments: The company is making over $2 billion in incremental investments in stores, technology, and guest experience for 2026. Monitor how these investments are affecting gross margin and operating income, especially with continued pricing and promotional activity.
Digital and membership growth: Same-day delivery, marketplace (Target Plus), and membership revenue all posted strong growth in Q4. The pace of growth in these areas will be important as digital channels become a larger part of the business.
Tariff and regulatory impacts: The recent U.S. Supreme Court ruling on tariffs and subsequent regulatory changes have introduced uncertainty. Management is still evaluating the financial impact, so any updates or commentary on cost recovery or future pricing actions will be closely watched.
