Notable Developments
Revenue grew 46% year over year to $333.4 million, with broad-based customer and enterprise seat expansion.
Net Dollar Retention Rate improved to 139%, the highest in over two years, showing strong customer upsell and retention.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year guidance for both revenue and operating income, now forecasting mid-30% growth for the year and pointing to robust early results from new AI monetization efforts. The most important area to watch is whether Figma can translate strong customer and revenue growth into sustained margin expansion as heavy investment continues.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue saw a significant jump, up by 46% from the same period last year. This growth was driven by uptake of AI-powered features like Figma Make and strong expansion among both smaller pro teams and large enterprise customers.

Customer engagement reached new highs: the company now counts about 690,000 paying customers, with paid customers spending over $100,000 per year growing 48%. Net Dollar Retention Rate, which measures the ability to expand spending among existing clients, hit 139%.

Profitability showed mixed results as expenses rose quickly, including a large increase in stock-based compensation following the company’s IPO. Although non-GAAP earnings improved, free cash flow fell compared to the prior year mainly due to one-off bonus payments and continued high levels of reinvestment.
