Management projects that its cash balance will fund operations into the back half of 2028. The focus remains on advancing Varseta-M toward a pivotal late-stage colorectal cancer trial, with new clinical data expected later in 2026. Quarterly Highlights

Revenue declined significantly compared to the prior year as major collaboration revenues with Bristol Myers Squibb and Amgen were recognized last year and have now largely ended. This puts added emphasis on the company's own clinical milestones to drive future income.

Total operating expense increased by about 6%, reflecting continued investment in the pipeline. The company is prioritizing research and development spending to support multiple clinical programs, including Varseta-M for colorectal cancer and CX-801 for melanoma.

The company finished the quarter with $346.7 million in cash, cash equivalents, and investments after an equity financing. This strong capital position provides a lengthy operational runway to support ongoing studies, the anticipated pivotal trial for Varseta-M, and expansion into combination therapies and earlier lines of treatment.

What CytomX Therapeutics Does

CytomX Therapeutics develops conditionally activated oncology therapies, including antibody-drug conjugates and immunotherapies, designed to target tumors while limiting effects on healthy tissue.
