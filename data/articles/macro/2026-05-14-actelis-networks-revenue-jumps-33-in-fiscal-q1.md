Actelis Networks Revenue Jumps 33% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Actelis Networks secured increased revenue from Intelligent Transportation and U.S. federal opportunities.
Revenue rose 33% compared to the prior year quarter.
Management expects improved profitability with focus on margin improvement and Nasdaq relisting efforts.

Actelis Networks (OTC:ASNS) achieved an uptick in sales as municipal and U.S. federal clients chose the company for intelligent transportation and modernization projects. The communications solutions provider specializes in hybrid networking and cybersecurity systems for infrastructure operators. The first quarter 2026 report, released on May 14, 2026, showed expanding top-line results but highlighted ongoing losses and margin contraction. Overall, the quarter offered evidence of commercial progress, but higher costs undercut earnings and margin improvement.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 33% year over year, helped by major U.S. federal and city infrastructure wins and growth in Intelligent Transportation Systems projects.
Gross margin dropped by almost 10 percentage points to 25%, mainly because of a one-time, low-margin deal and higher foreign exchange and warranty costs.
Cash balance improved markedly after raising $6.9 million through share sales and reducing some liabilities, but overall losses and Nasdaq delisting highlight ongoing financial risks.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management says it will focus on margin recovery, continuing cost control, and potential product pricing increases. Efforts to relist shares on Nasdaq and complete the planned Exaware acquisition are also priorities. The most important thing for investors to monitor is sustained margin improvement and progress toward profitable growth, especially given the company’s recent Nasdaq delisting and reliance on major contracts.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue rise was driven by new and follow-on orders from Intelligent Transportation and U.S. federal agencies, including a large Federal Aviation Administration project and multiple smart city contracts. Actelis also delivered solutions for legacy network modernization in North America and cyber-focused services for European and Asian clients, underscoring its position in hybrid networking and cybersecurity.

Despite the top-line advance, gross margin fell significantly because of an atypical low-profit deal and cost pressures, including impacts from currency changes and warranty expenses. Management expects this margin pressure to ease with a better mix of software-related revenue and higher overall volumes.

Liquidity improved as the company raised $6.9 million from at-the-market share issuances, lifting its cash position to $7.5 million at quarter-end. However, total operating and net losses widened, and the loss per share figure signals continued structural unprofitability, even as expenses stayed roughly flat year over year.

What Actelis Networks Does

Actelis Networks provides networking and cybersecurity solutions that enable high-speed, secure data connections over existing copper and fiber infrastructure for transportation, government, and service provider networks.
