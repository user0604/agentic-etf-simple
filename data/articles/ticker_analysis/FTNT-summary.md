FTNT Company Overview
Fortinet provides hardware and software solutions that combine networking functions with digital protection for businesses and government organizations. The company generates revenue through equipment sales and recurring subscriptions for automated threat intelligence and technical support services. By integrating various tools into a unified platform managed by a single operating system, the company creates high switching costs and simplifies complex security environments for its global customers.

Fortinet Raises Outlook as Product Sales Accelerate

Fortinet makes cybersecurity products and services to help organizations keep their data and networks safe from hackers. The company stood out this quarter by selling much more equipment than usual, which is rare for a business that relies mainly on ongoing service fees. Over the last 5 years, the stock returned 112.4%, beating the S&P 500’s 73.9% gain by 38.5 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $1.85 billion (up 20% YoY)
Non-GAAP EPS: $0.82 (up 41% YoY)
Product revenue: $645 million (up 41% YoY)

Latest Developments

Management raised their full-year guidance for revenue and earnings, giving investors more confidence in the company's outlook.
Product sales grew much faster than service revenue, making it important to watch if strong hardware sales will boost future recurring service income.
