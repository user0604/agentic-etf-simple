Key Takeaways
Gen Restaurant Group experienced a tough quarter as restaurant sales and profitability declined.
Revenue fell 6.0% and restaurant-level profitability was cut by more than half year over year.
Management maintained its full-year guidance for 2026, targeting revenues of $215 million to $225 million and restaurant-level adjusted EBITDA margins in the 15% to 15.5% range.

Gen Restaurant Group (NASDAQ:GENK)’s restaurant business delivered disappointing results as customer demand and profitability continued to fall. The operator of Korean barbecue restaurants and a fast-growing packaged goods division announced first quarter 2026 earnings on May 14, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter highlighted worsening restaurant-level margins but notable early momentum in the new grocery segment.

Notable Developments
Total revenue for the quarter dropped 6% as same store sales fell 8.8%, though sequential sales declines improved from the previous quarter.
Restaurant-level adjusted EBITDA, a measure of core store profitability, was more than cut in half from the prior year, falling to $4.0 million on a 7.4% margin.
The new Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) division achieved over 1,100 grocery placements and is targeting 2,000 by year-end, but is not yet large enough to offset ongoing losses in restaurants.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not revise its full-year 2026 outlook, which calls for $215–$225 million in revenue and restaurant-level adjusted EBITDA margins of 15–15.5%. With quarterly results running at the low end of this annual range and profitability far below target, results for the rest of the year must improve. Investors should closely watch same store sales trends and the scale-up of the CPG segment as key drivers of future results.

Quarterly highlights

The restaurant business continued to struggle, with revenue falling and same store sales dropping 8.8% compared to a year ago, though this was slightly better than in the prior quarter. Restaurant-level adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 7.4%, less than half the margin management aims to achieve for the full year, mainly due to high costs for ingredients and labor. The company added two new restaurants, bringing the total to 59, and slowed down new store development, pausing construction on six planned locations to focus on cash management.

The Consumer Packaged Goods segment gained speed, with products now in over 1,100 grocery stores and more than 50 items available, from frozen meats to beverages. Management projects further expansion, aiming for over 2,000 grocery placements by year-end, and anticipates the CPG business could deliver high profit margins once it achieves scale. However, at current size, CPG is not yet large enough to change the overall financial picture for the company.

On costs, the company’s percentage of revenue going to food and labor both increased. Ingredient costs rose due to commodity inflation, and general and administrative expenses went up both in dollar terms and as a share of sales. While payroll spending dropped slightly in absolute dollars, it grew as a percent of revenue, reflecting the pressure of falling sales on a somewhat fixed cost structure.

What Gen Restaurant Group does

Gen Restaurant Group operates full-service Korean barbecue restaurants in the United States and South Korea, and sells branded consumer packaged goods like ready-to-cook meals and beverages in grocery stores.
