Key Takeaways
Ani Pharmaceuticals reported robust growth in its Rare Disease segment, led by Cortrophin Gel.
Revenue rose 20.5% year over year in the quarter.
Management raised its full-year revenue and earnings guidance for 2026.

Ani Pharmaceuticals posted another strong period for its Rare Disease business, capturing the spotlight with expansion in key products. The specialty pharmaceutical company is focused on rare diseases and high-value generics, with growing contributions from its recent product acquisitions. The results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 were announced on May 8, 2026, covering January through March. Overall, Ani Pharmaceuticals delivered solid improvement in revenue and profit, but also faced ongoing margin pressure and segment shifts.

Notable Developments
The Rare Disease segment saw 36.9% growth in net revenue, with Cortrophin Gel sales up 42.1% as the new commercial team ramped up efforts in acute gout.
Gross margin narrowed by 2.3 percentage points, as increased royalties on top-selling products like Cortrophin Gel weighed on profitability.
The company raised its 2026 financial guidance, targeting $1.08–$1.14 billion in revenue and adjusted earnings per share of $9.19–$9.69.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains confident in continued revenue growth, especially as Cortrophin Gel expands into new patient groups such as those with acute gouty arthritis. Investors should closely follow progress on the Phase 4 ILUVIEN trial and the cadence of Cortrophin Gel sales as the company expects momentum to build in the second half of the year.

Quarterly Highlights

Rare Disease therapies continued to drive results. Net revenue from Cortrophin Gel, Ani's lead adrenocorticotropic hormone formulation for inflammatory and autoimmune conditions, climbed over 40% from the prior year, accounting for a major share of overall revenue growth. ILUVIEN, a long-acting eye implant for diabetic macular edema, added $19.3 million in sales in the first quarter.

The Generics segment posted steady improvement, supported by new product launches, but its year-over-year growth rate slowed versus 2025. Six new generic drugs came to market in the quarter, maintaining Ani's target launch pace.

Gross margin contracted to 60.6%, as the sales mix shifted more toward products with high third-party royalty costs. Though the company projects overall revenue and profits to increase for the year, the margin guidance midpoint still sits slightly below last year's actual results.

What Ani Pharmaceuticals Does

Ani Pharmaceuticals develops, manufactures, and markets medicines for rare diseases and complex generics, focusing on delivering new treatment options through both internal research and strategic acquisitions.
