Quarterly Highlights

Recurring subscription revenue led growth, making up almost three-quarters of total sales and up 21% compared to the prior year. This aligns with management’s business model, which is driven by ongoing subscription fees for telematics services like vehicle tracking and stolen vehicle recovery.

Product sales rose alongside subscriptions, but the gross margin for products declined from 23.6% to 18.7%, signaling a less profitable mix or rising competition in hardware. This changing margin profile is important to track if product sales continue to expand.

Liquidity remained robust, with $108.0 million in net cash despite spending on shareholder returns, including a $10 million quarterly dividend and $0.5 million of share buybacks. The dividend was maintained at $0.50 per share, reflecting management's steady approach to capital returns.
