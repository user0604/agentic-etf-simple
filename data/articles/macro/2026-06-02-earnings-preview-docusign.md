Earnings Preview: DocuSign

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

DocuSign (NASDAQ:DOCU), a leading provider of electronic signature and agreement management software, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, DocuSign delivered revenue of $837 million, representing 8% year-over-year growth. The company’s annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $3.3 billion, also up 8% from the previous year. Billings exceeded $1 billion for the first time in company history, growing 10% year over year. Non-GAAP operating margin for the quarter was 29.5%, and free cash flow for the year surpassed $1 billion. DocuSign’s Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) platform continued to drive growth, accounting for over $350 million in ARR, or roughly 11% of the total. The company’s Q4 results beat analyst consensus, with non-GAAP EPS of $1.01 (versus $0.95 expected) and revenue of $836.9 million (versus $828.2 million expected). DocuSign also announced a $2.0 billion increase to its share-repurchase program, bringing total authorization to $2.6 billion.

Positives
Revenue and EPS beat estimates: DocuSign outperformed analyst expectations on both revenue and non-GAAP earnings per share, indicating strong execution and demand for its products.
Billings milestone achieved: Billings surpassed $1 billion in a single quarter for the first time, signaling robust sales momentum and healthy customer renewals and expansions.
IAM platform adoption accelerating: The IAM platform reached over $350 million in ARR after just 18 months, with early renewal cohorts showing better retention than the company average. This suggests the new platform is resonating with customers and driving higher engagement.
Strong free cash flow and buybacks: Free cash flow exceeded $1 billion for the year, and the company ramped up share repurchases, returning significant capital to shareholders.
Negatives
Gross margin slightly declined: Non-GAAP gross margin for Q4 was 81.8%, down 50 basis points year over year, mainly due to ongoing costs from cloud infrastructure migration. This reflects higher operating costs as DocuSign transitions its technology backbone.
Professional services revenue decreased: Revenue from professional services and other non-subscription sources fell 9% year over year, suggesting slower growth in onboarding or integration services.
Stock-based compensation remains high: Total stock-based compensation expense for the year was $622 million, which continues to be a significant cost, though management noted efforts to reduce it as a percentage of revenue.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
IAM platform growth and customer adoption: Investors should look for updates on IAM’s share of ARR, progress with new functional modules (such as IAM for HR and procurement), and customer expansion, especially among larger enterprises.
Guidance for ARR and revenue: Management has guided for Q1 revenue of $822 million to $826 million and expects ARR growth to accelerate to 8.25% to 8.75% for fiscal 2027. Watch for any changes to these forecasts or commentary on demand trends.
Impact of new consumption-based pricing: DocuSign is launching IAM consumption-based subscription pricing in Q1. The effect this has on sales cycles, customer expansion, and overall ARR mix will be important to monitor.
Operating margin and investment plans: The company plans to maintain operating margins while increasing R&D investment, particularly in AI and legal tech. Investors should look for details on expense trends and how these investments are expected to drive future growth.
Share repurchase activity and capital allocation: With $2.6 billion authorized for buybacks, updates on actual repurchase activity and its impact on share count and EPS will be a key metric for shareholders.
