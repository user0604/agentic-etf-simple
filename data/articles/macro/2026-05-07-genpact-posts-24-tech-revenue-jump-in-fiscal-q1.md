Key Takeaways
Genpact achieved a solid win as its Advanced Technology Solutions segment surged and total revenue surpassed its own outlook.
Adjusted diluted EPS rose 16.7% from the prior-year period.
Management expects ongoing strong growth in advanced technology and is focused on sustained margin improvement.

Genpact (NYSE:G) delivered growth in its Advanced Technology Solutions division, driving results above its own revenue forecast for the quarter. The leading business process transformation and digital consulting company continued its transition toward more modern, AI-powered offerings in the period ending March 31, 2026 (reported May 7, 2026). The quarter featured robust performance in technology segments but was offset somewhat by slower growth in core services and a reversal in operating cash flow.

Overall, the period reflected successful execution on Genpact’s technology ambitions, balanced against new questions on cash generation and the pace of further gains in core areas.

Notable Developments
Advanced Technology Solutions revenue jumped 24.3% to $345 million, now making up 27% of the company’s top line.
Gross margin improved for the twelfth consecutive quarter, reaching 36.4%—a gain of 1.1 percentage points year over year.
Operating cash flow turned negative at $24 million used, contrasting with $40 million generated in the same period a year ago.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects continued strong growth in the Advanced Technology Solutions segment, now expecting at least 20% growth both next quarter and for full year 2026, and reaffirmed its overall revenue and margin guidance. Investors should watch for the sustainability of high-technology segment momentum, as well as whether positive operating cash flow resumes after this quarter’s outflow.

Quarterly Highlights

The Advanced Technology Solutions business, which includes Genpact's AI, digital, and advisory services, led company growth with revenue rising to $345 million—substantially above the company's own forecast and now representing over a quarter of total sales. This reflects a deliberate shift toward higher-value technology solutions.

The company’s profitability expanded as gross margin improved again this quarter, with total adjusted diluted EPS moving up at nearly three times the rate of overall revenue. This suggests the company kept expenses under control even as it ramped up technology investment.

However, Genpact’s cash flow became a source of concern, switching from an inflow to an outflow within operating activities, a change the company did not explain in its results. Investors will also note that Core Business Services, which makes up most revenue, grew only modestly at 1.4%.

What Genpact Does

Genpact is a global provider of digital transformation and business process management services, helping businesses modernize operations with technology solutions.
