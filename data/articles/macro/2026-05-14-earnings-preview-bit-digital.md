Earnings Preview: Bit Digital

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 2:58 PM

Comments

Bit Digital (NASDAQ:BTBT), a company that manages Ethereum staking and operates high-performance computing data centers for artificial intelligence (AI) workloads, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Thursday, May 14, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Bit Digital reported revenue of $32.3 million, up from $25.8 million in the prior year period. For the full year, revenue reached $113.6 million, a 5% increase over 2024. The company continued its strategic transition away from Bitcoin mining and toward Ethereum staking and AI infrastructure, primarily through its majority stake in WhiteFiber. Staking revenue nearly quadrupled year over year, while cloud and colocation services also grew strongly. However, the company posted a net loss of $84.9 million for 2025, down from net income of $28.3 million in 2024, mainly due to non-cash losses from digital asset revaluation. Adjusted EBITDA for the year was negative $24.9 million, compared to positive $73 million in 2024. These results missed analyst estimates for both earnings per share and revenue.

Positives
Strong growth in cloud and colocation revenue: Cloud services revenue increased 50% year over year to $68.8 million, and colocation revenue rose from $1.4 million to $8.9 million, showing successful expansion in AI infrastructure services.
Rapid scaling of Ethereum staking: Staking revenue grew nearly 300% for the year, with almost half generated in the fourth quarter, reflecting the company’s deliberate shift toward recurring, yield-generating assets.
Robust balance sheet and liquidity: Cash and cash equivalents rose to $118.4 million, with total digital assets at $415.7 million, providing flexibility for future investments and operations.
Reduced exposure to Bitcoin mining volatility: The company continued winding down its Bitcoin mining business, lowering related revenue and costs, and focusing on less volatile, recurring income streams.
Negatives
Significant net loss driven by asset revaluation: The $84.9 million net loss was mainly due to unfavorable changes in the value of digital assets, reversing the prior year’s gains and impacting overall profitability.
High operating expenses and negative adjusted EBITDA: General and administrative costs doubled year over year, and adjusted EBITDA turned negative, highlighting the cost of transition and expansion.
Legacy mining business still impacts results: Despite the ongoing wind-down, digital asset mining revenue dropped sharply, and the segment continues to weigh on overall margins and results.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on M&A and new business initiatives: Management indicated active pursuit of acquisitions in cash-generating, Ethereum- or AI-adjacent businesses, with a goal to close a deal in 2026 if the right opportunity arises. Any updates or announcements on this front could be significant.
Recurring revenue mix and margin trends: Watch for further growth in staking, cloud, and colocation services, and whether these segments are offsetting the decline in Bitcoin mining. Gross margin trends will show if the business model shift is improving profitability.
Status of WhiteFiber and AI infrastructure: WhiteFiber remains a core asset, with no plans to monetize the stake in 2026. Updates on contracted revenue, data center capacity expansion, or new customer agreements will be important for assessing long-term growth.
Ethereum staking strategy and yield enhancements: The company continues to stake most ETH natively with Figment and is exploring yield enhancement strategies. Any changes in staking approach, counterparty risk management, or restaking activities could affect future income.
Updates on hosting transitions and mining asset redeployment: With the conclusion of several hosting partnerships in early 2026, investors should look for details on alternative arrangements for miners previously hosted at GreenBlocks and Soluna, and the financial impact of these changes.
