Evi Industries Posts 8% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q3

Key Takeaways
Evi Industries reported record revenue and gross profit but saw net income decline.
Revenue rose 8% over the prior-year quarter, and gross margin improved.
Management is focused on driving operating efficiencies and converting inventory into cash.

Evi Industries (NYSEMKT:EVI) achieved record sales and gross profit, but increased expenses held back profit growth. The distributor and service provider for commercial laundry equipment reported results for its fiscal third quarter ended March 31, 2026, with the release issued on May 11, 2026. The quarter brought higher gross margins and ongoing growth through acquisitions, though earnings and cash flow lagged behind revenue gains.

Source: Company filing. Note: All metrics are GAAP unless noted. Adjusted EBITDA is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, excluding certain non-cash and one-time items.

Notable Developments
Gross margin improved by 2.5 percentage points as the company focused on higher-quality, recurring revenue streams.
Selling, general, and administrative expenses rose 19% year over year to $30.6 million, outpacing sales growth.
Inventory climbed to $80.2 million, up 21% since June 2025, with about 65% committed to confirmed customer orders.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted efforts to streamline operations and leverage recent technology investments to boost future profitability. Converting higher inventory levels into completed sales and strengthening cash generation remain top priorities for the next quarter. Investors should monitor whether operating expenses are brought in line with revenue gains and whether net income returns to growth.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter brought another record for revenue, as recent acquisitions and organic sales combined to lift the top line. Despite higher gross profit, operating income was flat compared to last year, held back by a sharp increase in operating expenses and higher interest costs.

Investment in field service and enterprise technology continues, with completed system rollouts supporting more than 27,500 service appointments and year-over-year productivity improvements for technicians. Customer loyalty remains strong, with repeat purchases accounting for a large share of parts sales.

Premier Chemical Solutions, the division focused on detergents and cleaning chemicals, accelerated growth by adding new accounts and expanding recurring revenue. However, overall net income and cash flow fell as higher expenses and investment offset gains elsewhere.

What Evi Industries Does

Evi Industries provides commercial laundry equipment sales, leasing, installation, and ongoing service to clients in multiple industries.
