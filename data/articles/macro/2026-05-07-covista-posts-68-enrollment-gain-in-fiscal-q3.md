Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased expectations for both revenue and adjusted earnings, citing strong enrollment at Chamberlain and Walden. New campus launches and technology-driven programs are planned to support further growth. Investors should watch for continued margin pressure and regulatory compliance as Covista invests in expansion and student programs.

Quarterly Highlights

Enrollment reached a record 100,585 students across all Covista institutions. Chamberlain University returned to positive total enrollment growth, and Walden University achieved its eleventh straight quarter of gains. The Medical and Veterinary segment posted revenue of $103.5 million, up 8.9% year over year, along with strong graduate placement outcomes.

Covista repurchased $66 million in shares during the quarter and refinanced its outstanding debt, reducing net leverage to 0.7 times adjusted EBITDA. Deferred revenue, which represents payments for educational programs not yet delivered, rose significantly and points to strong forward enrollment activity. Cash flow from operations remained robust, supporting continued investment in campus expansion and new program launches.

Program innovation included new healthcare-focused artificial intelligence certificates developed with Google Cloud, which drew high enrollment. Regulatory and accreditation processes allowed Chamberlain to advance new campus openings, while Walden added seven new degree programs. The medical segment maintained high residency and placement rates, supporting both student outcomes and Covista’s reputation in healthcare education.

What Covista Does

Covista provides career-focused education in healthcare, nursing, and professional fields through institutions such as Chamberlain University, Walden University, and its medical and veterinary schools.
