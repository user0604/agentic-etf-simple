Quarterly Highlights

Revenue grew substantially due to the addition of Luminar Semiconductor and NuCrypt, with new streams emerging from acquired customers and early sales in photonic and quantum products. Management noted that small-batch production at its Fab 1 site has started to deliver early revenue, a step forward from its research and development phase. A $16 million contract backlog at quarter end suggests early signs of customer adoption, but the pace of backlog conversion to realized revenue remains a key question.

Expenses rose sharply, with research and development, sales and marketing, and general administration cost categories all increasing—driven by the expanded workforce and integration of acquired companies. The increase in costs, along with higher overall business activity, contributed to a negative gross margin and a net loss of $4.1 million versus net income in the prior-year period.

The company closed the quarter with a strong liquidity position, holding $1.4 billion in cash and investments. This financial stability gives it runway to continue funding manufacturing expansion—such as advancing plans for a higher-capacity facility, “Fab 2”—while enabling ongoing investment in research, commercialization, and integration of its technology roadmap.
