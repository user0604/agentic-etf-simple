Keysight Posts 31% Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 4:20 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Keysight Technologies delivered record results across both revenue and profit in its latest quarter.
Non-GAAP earnings per share grew 68.8% year over year.
Management raised its full-year outlook but signaled that growth may normalize in the second half.

Keysight Technologies (NYSE:KEYS) delivered record results as demand stayed strong across its major product lines. The leading provider of test and measurement solutions for electronic systems recorded its best-ever quarter, reflecting robust order growth and expanding profit margins. These results, released on May 19, 2026, for its fiscal second quarter, support management’s decision to increase its financial outlook for the full year. Operational gains were broad-based, though one-time tariff benefits added to reported profits.

Overall, the quarter demonstrated exceptional execution with strong revenue gains, margin expansion in both major segments, and robust cash generation.

Notable Developments
Total quarterly revenue rose 31% to $1.72 billion, fueled by 56% growth in orders and broad demand across end markets.
The Communications Solutions Group posted a 33.0% operating margin, up 7.0 percentage points, on 35% revenue growth driven by commercial communications.
A one-time $100 million benefit from tariff refunds increased net income, while a $40 million revenue reduction partially offset this gain.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year guidance, citing strong first-half momentum and a robust pipeline. For the next quarter, it expects revenue of roughly $1.74 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share of $2.46. Investors should watch for the sustainability of order growth and updates on integration of recent acquisitions, as the company noted growth could moderate later in the year.

Quarterly Highlights

Orders reached $2.05 billion, growing 56% from the year-ago quarter—a sign that near-term demand remains healthy. Management pointed to strong bookings in both major operating segments, with particular strength in commercial communications and electronic industrial segments.

Profit margins expanded sharply, with the Communications Solutions Group reaching a 33.0% operating margin and the Electronic Industrial Solutions Group at the same level—marking double-digit percentage point gains year over year.

One-time items played a notable role this period. Keysight benefited from $100 million in tariff-related refunds, increasing net income, but recognized a $40 million reduction to revenue reflecting tariff surcharge pass-backs to customers. These factors were not part of ongoing business trends and may not repeat in future quarters.

What Keysight Technologies Does

Keysight Technologies supplies hardware, software, and services that help engineers design, test, and validate electronic and communications systems used in industries such as wireless communications, automotive, aerospace, and semiconductors.
