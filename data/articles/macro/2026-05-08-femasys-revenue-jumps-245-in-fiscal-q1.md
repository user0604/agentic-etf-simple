Key Takeaways
Business win driven by initial commercial traction for fertility and birth control products.
Revenue rose 24.5% year over year in the quarter.

Women’s health company Femasys (NASDAQ:FEMY) advanced commercialization for its lead fertility and birth control products this quarter, but the business is still operating with high expenses and a narrowing cash runway. The developer of in-office, non-surgical solutions for infertility and permanent contraception reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 8, 2026. Overall, the period saw growing revenue from new product launches but ongoing operating losses and looming capital needs.

Notable Developments
Sales increased 24.5% year over year due to momentum from FemaSeed Complete and FemBloc product launches, plus new partner agreements in Israel, Switzerland, and U.S. clinics.
Research and development spending dropped 55.9% as products transitioned out of development and into commercial inventory, contributing to lower overall operating expenses.
The quarter produced net income due to non-cash gains tied to the fair value of financial instruments, masking a core operating loss of $4.2 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reported that the company’s cash is sufficient to fund operations into the third quarter of 2026 and is prioritizing global commercialization and completion of pivotal clinical trials. The company did not provide financial outlook targets for upcoming quarters. Investors should closely monitor cash flow, the pace of revenue growth from new products, and updates on progress toward U.S. approval for FemBloc.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s year-over-year revenue growth was fueled by expanding sales volumes of the company’s core products, supported by launches at major medical conferences and new international distribution partnerships. While the overall revenue base remains modest, partnerships in Israel and Switzerland extended market reach, and new CPT code approval in the U.S. for FemaSeed may aid future reimbursements.

Operating expenses fell sharply as clinical development programs for products like FemaSeed Complete and FemBloc moved into the sales phase. Research and development expense dropped to $1.31 million, down from $2.97 million in the prior year quarter, reflecting this shift. However, the overall expense base remains well above current revenues.

The transition to net income resulted almost entirely from accounting gains related to changes in the estimated fair value of financial instruments, not core operations. Excluding these non-cash items, the quarter showed a substantial operating loss. Cash and cash equivalents fell to $5.39 million, a drop of over $3.8 million since December 2025, highlighting a pressing need for additional funding if revenue growth does not accelerate rapidly.

What Femasys Does

Femasys develops and commercializes women’s reproductive health products addressing infertility and permanent birth control with in-office, non-surgical solutions.
