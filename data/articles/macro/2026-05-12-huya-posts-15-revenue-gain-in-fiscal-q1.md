HUYA Posts 15% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 6:25 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
HUYA made gains growing high-margin game-related revenues, but core live streaming contracted. Revenue increased 14.6% from the prior year. Management remains focused on service diversification but did not give quantitative guidance for the current quarter.

Online entertainment platform HUYA (NYSE:HUYA) recorded another quarter of revenue growth as it pushed further into higher-margin game-related services, but profitability improvements remain a work in progress. The company is a major provider of game live streaming and social entertainment in China, with a growing footprint in publishing, content-driven advertising, and in-game sales. These results, published May 12, 2026, cover the first quarter ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter saw progress diversifying away from legacy live streaming, though sustainable net profitability still has not been achieved.

Notable Developments
Game-related, advertising and other revenue soared 69.4% from the prior year to RMB 627.4 million, making up 36.3% of total revenue in the quarter.
Gross profit increased 34.3% year over year as gross margin improved to 14.6%, reflecting a higher share of higher-margin business lines.
The non-GAAP operating loss narrowed to near break-even at RMB 2.7 million, yet GAAP net profitability was not achieved.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its focus on expanding game-related services, new game publishing, and in-game sales to drive both growth and higher margins. No explicit financial forecasts were provided for the next quarter or full year. Investors should watch for new product launches and user engagement trends, as the company did not release updated monthly active user figures this period.

Quarterly Highlights

The biggest highlight was the rise in game-related and advertising revenues, which jumped to over a third of the total business. This segment benefited from successful titles such as “Goose Goose Duck” mobile, which management said reached a top 5 spot on the China Apple App Store chart in April. This progress marks a significant shift from the company’s earlier, heavier reliance on live streaming.

Gross profit grew sharply, boosted by a better mix of game publishing, advertising, and higher-margin in-game purchases. Cost control remained important, though sales and marketing expenses rose 45.1% from last year, primarily to support the new mobile game’s rollout. General and administrative expenses also ticked up due to higher share-based compensation.

Core live streaming revenue continued to decline, falling 3.3% from the prior year as the industry in China remains fiercely competitive. Profitability on a GAAP basis remained elusive, reflecting higher spending and a drop in interest income after special dividend payments. The company ended the quarter with RMB 3.5 billion in cash and deposits, down from RMB 3.8 billion at the start of the year.

What HUYA Does

HUYA operates interactive live streaming platforms and distributes online games in China and overseas, generating revenue from in-stream purchases, advertising, and value-added services.
