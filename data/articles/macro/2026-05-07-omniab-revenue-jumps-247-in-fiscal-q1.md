Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised 2026 revenue guidance to a range of $28 million to $33 million and expects year-end cash of $33 million to $38 million. The company also increased its expected total costs and operating expenses, pointing to higher non-cash amortization. Investors should watch the timing and size of partner-driven milestones, as these continue to drive business volatility.

Quarterly Highlights

Milestone and license payments provided an outsized boost to the revenue figure, with service and technology fees, including xPloration platform revenue, making up only a small share. The company, however, continues to diversify its sources, with xPloration revenue growing nearly 30% year over year.

Tight control of cash-based costs helped offset an increase in intangible asset amortization, including a $2.9 million impairment charge tied to discontinued legacy programs. Non-cash charges now make up a larger portion of overall expenses, reflecting changes in the company’s portfolio.

At quarter’s end, OmniAb’s partner and program base remained stable: 107 partners and 409 active programs, with 32 in later-stage clinical or commercial development. Management highlighted upcoming partner trial readouts and technology launches, signifying future milestone and royalty opportunities.
