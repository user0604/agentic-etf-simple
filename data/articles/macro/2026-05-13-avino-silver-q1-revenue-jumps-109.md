Notable Developments
La Preciosa contributed a full quarter of revenue for the first time, accounting for more than 11% of total mining sales, which supported the jump in total revenue.
Silver sales represented 61% of revenue, up sharply from 37% a year ago, reflecting the company’s transition to a primary silver producer.
The period saw significant share issuance, with the total share count rising 20% from the previous year as capital was raised for growth initiatives.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted further scaling of La Preciosa and investment in plant capacity as key upcoming initiatives. They noted that continued robust performance is tied to commodity price strength and careful management of costs and dilution. Investors should pay close attention to whether the new production levels and higher profitability continue as the year progresses.

Quarterly highlights

The first full-quarter ramp-up of the La Preciosa project delivered $4.5 million in revenue and $2.8 million in mine operating income, reducing dependence on the Avino property alone. This diversification aligns with previous management strategy aimed at lowering single-asset risk.

Avino's revenue composition shifted, with silver’s share rising to 61%, a strategic goal for the company as it aims to be recognized primarily as a silver mining business. Gold and copper combined made up 41% of the quarter’s sales, giving flexibility against metals price swings.

The company’s liquidity position strengthened, ending the quarter with $138.6 million in cash. The company’s share count increased by about 19% year over year due to at-the-market stock issuances and option exercises—meaning that while liquidity improved, existing shareholders experienced dilution.
