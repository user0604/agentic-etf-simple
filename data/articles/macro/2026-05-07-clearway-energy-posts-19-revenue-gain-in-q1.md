Notable Developments

Cash from operating activities was up over fourfold, mainly due to $282 million in one-time tax credit transfers, a benefit that will not recur in future quarters.
The Honeycomb battery storage project reached completion in May 2026, while the Mesquite Sky wind facility secured a new 15-year contract with stronger pricing and reduced market risk.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance, projecting $470 million to $510 million in cash available for distribution and adjusted EBITDA between $1,441 million and $1,481 million. Executives stated these targets remain in reach, though results required a ramp-up in later quarters. Investors should monitor recurring cash generation closely, as one-off benefits from items like tax credit transfers lifted reported operating cash this period.

Quarterly Highlights

Growth investments pushed total renewables and storage output up 8% compared to the prior year, with solar energy output especially strong, reflecting new projects entering service. This helped lift overall operating revenues by nearly a fifth, even as key profitability metrics such as adjusted EBITDA and CAFD showed minimal or negative year-on-year change.

Although net loss narrowed from $104 million to $68 million, Clearway Energy’s core distributable cash flow fell 9.1% year over year, attributed in part to timing of certain cash receipts. The bounce in overall cash from operations was largely due to tax credit transfer proceeds, which are not expected to repeat.

Progress on high-profile projects continued. The Mesquite Sky wind facility restructured its supply contract, securing a long-term power purchase agreement with a major technology company, while the Honeycomb battery energy storage system reached substantial completion and is now supported by a 20-year utility agreement. The late-stage development pipeline expanded 20% to 12.7 gigawatts, reinforcing long-term growth visibility.

What Clearway Energy Does

Clearway Energy operates a portfolio of utility-scale wind, solar, and battery storage facilities, selling electricity primarily under long-term contracts to utilities and large customers.
