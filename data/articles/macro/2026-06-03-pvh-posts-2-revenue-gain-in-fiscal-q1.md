PVH Posts 2% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

4:34 PM

Key Takeaways
PVH reported a business win, as strong direct-to-consumer growth helped offset regional and channel challenges.
EPS (earnings per share) fell 13% while revenue rose 2% year over year.
Management lowered its full-year revenue outlook, citing continued headwinds in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa.

Fashion house PVH (NYSE:PVH) posted a mixed start to fiscal 2026 as healthy direct-to-consumer and product category gains balanced out wholesale and regional challenges. The owner of Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger, both major global apparel brands, saw strong performance in online and store channels but signaled caution for the rest of the year. The results, released on June 3, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showed PVH holding margins near its internal targets but cutting its outlook for revenue growth for the year. Overall, the quarter reflected steady execution with notable resilience, but ongoing pressures in EMEA and the wholesale channel remain key concerns.

Notable Developments
Direct-to-consumer sales grew 6%, driven by both owned stores and digital channels, pushing total DTC revenue to $869 million in the quarter.
Operating margin narrowed to 6.5% (non-GAAP), reflecting increased investment in marketing and higher tariffs, but remained within the company’s guidance range.
Inventory decreased by 5% year over year to $1.51 billion, showing ongoing focus on supply chain productivity and cost discipline.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now forecasts flat full-year revenue, lowering guidance from a prior expected slight increase, mainly due to ongoing consumer softness in key EMEA markets linked to wider geopolitical issues. The company reaffirmed its operating margin target for the year, supported by expected tariff refunds and cost controls. Investors should monitor how continued regional volatility and wholesale trends impact overall sales and margin in the coming months.

Quarterly Highlights

Direct-to-consumer sales in both stores and online saw robust gains, led by hero categories such as Calvin Klein underwear and Tommy Hilfiger outerwear. Digital commerce revenue climbed 11% year over year as PVH boosted investment in e-commerce and in-store experiences. Wholesale business remained challenging, with revenues flat as reported and declining 6% in constant currency, reflecting cautious ordering by retail partners and the impact of license transitions.

Gross margin held steady at 58.6%, as tariff headwinds and promotional activity were mitigated by cost actions and favorable product mix. The company continued to shift previously licensed women’s sportswear for its two largest brands to in-house management, impacting revenue mix but supporting future margin initiatives. Cash reserves rose to $592.5 million, while inventory reductions surpassed $85 million year over year.

Regionally, PVH experienced solid reported growth in Asia-Pacific (up 10%) and EMEA (up 2%), although the latter declined on a constant currency basis due to softer demand and geopolitical disruption. Americas revenue dipped 1%, as gains in direct-to-consumer were offset by persistent wholesale weakness. Leadership reiterated its focus on inventory discipline and plans at least $300 million in share buybacks for the full year.

What PVH Does

PVH designs, markets, and sells branded apparel and accessories worldwide, with flagship labels Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger accounting for the vast majority of its revenue.
