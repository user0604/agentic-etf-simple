On Holding (NYSE:ONON) achieved record profitability in the first quarter, but sales growth in the Americas slowed. The Swiss company designs and sells premium athletic footwear, apparel, and accessories for sport and lifestyle consumers worldwide. Results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, released May 12, 2026, showed strong gains in Asia-Pacific and margin expansion across its business. Overall, the period brought higher profits and mixed regional momentum.

Notable developments
Revenue reached CHF 831.9 million, up 14.5%, with Asia-Pacific sales rising 44.4% and now making up more than 20% of total company sales.
Gross margin improved by 4.3 percentage points to 64.2%, supported by a higher share of direct-to-consumer sales and a premium product mix.
Management raised its full-year gross margin and adjusted profitability targets, citing ongoing margin expansion despite currency and tariff pressures.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its target for at least 23% constant-currency sales growth in fiscal 2026 and now expects gross margin at or above 64.5%, with adjusted EBITDA margin guidance up to 20%. The key item to monitor is whether growth in the Americas stabilizes or accelerates, as this region’s reported growth lagged the company’s historical rates this quarter.

Quarterly highlights

Asia-Pacific’s rapid expansion, with sales up over 44% in the period and now accounting for more than 20% of total revenue, highlighted the benefits of the company’s geographic diversification and investments in markets such as China and South Korea.

The gross profit margin reached a record level, reflecting more full-price sales, a favorable product mix, and efficiencies in direct-to-consumer distribution, even as higher U.S. tariffs added cost pressure during the quarter.

Product innovation supported growth in new categories, with apparel and accessories both outpacing the company average, helping to broaden the brand’s reach beyond core footwear.
