Key Takeaways
Alpha Metallurgical Resources posted decreased coal shipments and a narrower net loss in Q1 2026.
Adjusted EBITDA rose sharply year over year.

Alpha Metallurgical Resources (NYSE:AMR) delivered a mixed start to the new fiscal year as shipment volumes slipped and costs stayed elevated, but its loss narrowed compared to the previous year. The leading U.S. producer of metallurgical coal, crucial for steelmaking, announced results for the first quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 8, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected progress in price realization and improved operating margins, despite external cost headwinds.

Notable Developments
Net loss narrowed to $0.86 per share, a significant improvement from a $2.60 per share loss in the prior-year period.
Adjusted EBITDA jumped to $30.0 million from $5.7 million year over year, despite almost flat revenue and increased per-ton input costs driven by higher diesel and supplies.
The company committed and priced 48% of its 2026 metallurgical coal volume at an average of $132.37 per ton.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year cost guidance, expecting operating performance to improve if input costs like diesel ease and shipment volumes normalize after planned facility outages. The main area to monitor is whether ongoing geopolitical uncertainty will drive further increases in costs, potentially forcing a revision of coal sales cost guidance later in fiscal 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

Alpha’s non-GAAP realized sales price per ton in its metallurgical segment increased to $124.39, while non-GAAP cost per ton edged lower compared to the previous year, helping lift non-GAAP coal margin per ton to its highest level in several quarters. The company attributed persistently higher input costs to “war-related increases to diesel and other supply prices,” emphasizing the effect of external market shocks on operating expenses.

Coal shipment volumes slipped to 3.6 million tons versus 3.8 million a year earlier, reflecting planned maintenance outages at Dominion Terminal Associates and softer global steel demand. Nevertheless, export sales remained robust, with the best prices achieved on Australian-indexed export contracts in the first quarter.

Alpha ended the period with solid liquidity, reporting $476.2 million in available resources and continuing its share repurchase program. Management continues to target annualized cost efficiency and pricing discipline, even as market and regulatory risks remain visible.

What Alpha Metallurgical Resources Does

Alpha Metallurgical Resources produces metallurgical coal, a key raw material used in global steel manufacturing, and supplies both domestic and international customers.
