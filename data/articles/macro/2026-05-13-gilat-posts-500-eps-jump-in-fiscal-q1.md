Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its 2026 outlook for revenue of $500 million–$520 million and adjusted EBITDA of $61 million–$66 million, anticipating growth but expecting some quarter-to-quarter variability. The guidance was not upgraded, reflecting management’s view that some gains may not repeat in later quarters. Investors should monitor how the company manages rising costs and turns growth into positive cash flow through the rest of the year.

Quarterly highlights

All core business areas reported revenue gains. The commercial segment brought in $72.8 million, up 13%, while defense reached $25.4 million, also up by double digits. The Peru segment saw revenue more than double to $12.3 million.

Profitability improved sharply, with $12.5 million in non-GAAP operating income (a 140% increase year over year). Adjusted EBITDA nearly doubled, reflecting a broader shift from losses to gains across the business. However, operating expenses rose due to higher administrative and support costs, eating into some of the margin expansion.

Cash flow from operations was negative, partly due to increases in trade receivables and other assets as business activity scaled up. This trend is a key watchpoint, as growth in reported profits did not translate to positive operating cash flow in the quarter.
