Ovintiv (NYSE:OVV)’s sharpened focus on its Permian and Montney oil and gas assets produced strong operational results, but a large non-cash impairment led to an overall net loss this quarter. The exploration and production company relies on its diversified North American resource base, cost discipline, and active risk management. Notable developments

Management outlook and what to watch

Ovintiv management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, projecting production of 620–645 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day and capital investment between $2.25 billion and $2.35 billion. The company plans to maintain a disciplined capital program and focus on operational efficiency in its Permian and Montney assets. The most important area for investors to watch is how commodity prices and the recent portfolio changes influence cash flow and future production costs.

Quarterly highlights

Post-acquisition, production averaged 678.9 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day, at the high end of guidance, reflecting successful integration of NuVista assets and ongoing well productivity in both the Permian and Montney basins. Capital expenditures landed at the low end of management’s range, allowing for a 1.9% year-over-year reduction even as production volumes expanded.

The reported net loss came from a $1.2 billion non-cash impairment tied to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission’s required 12-month trailing oil price calculation, which reduced book asset values but did not impact operational cash flow. On an adjusted, Non-GAAP basis, underlying profitability improved, with adjusted earnings increasing and cash from operating activities exceeding $1.0 billion.

Following the Anadarko sale, net debt dropped below $3.3 billion as of April 30, 2026, reducing leverage and freeing up flexibility for increased shareholder returns. The company maintained its quarterly dividend at $0.30 per share and returned additional capital through share repurchases during the period.

What Ovintiv does

Ovintiv is an oil and natural gas producer focused on developing large-scale resource basins in North America, with operations centered in the Permian Basin of Texas and the Montney in Western Canada.
