Private credit lender Kayne Anderson BDC (NYSE:KBDC) posted a quarter marked by steady dividend coverage but also rising signs of portfolio stress. The specialty finance company focuses on senior secured loans to middle-market firms, and its latest results (released May 11, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026) showed higher net investment income, yet a continued slide in its net asset value per share.

Overall, the quarter demonstrated stable income distribution even as non-accruals ticked up. The ongoing balance of cautious lending and mild credit pressures was a key theme for the period.

Notable developments
Net investment income per share rose to $0.43, up from $0.40 a year ago, again covering the $0.40 dividend.
Net asset value per share declined to $16.23, dipping for a second straight quarter due to realized and unrealized losses.
Non-accrual investments increased sharply, rising to 2.5% of debt investments at fair value compared to 1.4% the prior year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its focus on preserving dividend coverage and actively rotating the loan portfolio toward higher-quality assets. It continues to highlight careful credit selection, ongoing share buybacks, and liquidity as supports for stability. The most important factor to monitor will be the trajectory of non-accruals, as rising credit stress impacts returns and balance-sheet value.

Quarterly highlights

Net investment income per share reached $0.43, topping the dividend again. This marks continued success in generating core income above distributions, aided by disciplined expense management and a focus on senior secured loans, which comprised over 92% of the portfolio.

The net asset value fell for the second quarter in a row to $16.23 per share. Company disclosures attributed this to $11.3 million in realized and unrealized losses, partially offset by income above the dividend and accretive share repurchases.

Credit concerns increased as non-accrual investments -- loans that have stopped paying interest -- rose to 2.5% of debt investments at fair value. This was a significant jump from recent quarters and signals emerging pockets of stress that warrant close tracking in the coming periods.

What Kayne Anderson BDC does

Kayne Anderson BDC is a business development company that provides senior secured loans and other credit solutions to private middle-market companies, primarily in the United States.
