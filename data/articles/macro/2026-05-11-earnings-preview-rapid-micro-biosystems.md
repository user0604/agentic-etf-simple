Earnings Preview: Rapid Micro Biosystems

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 5:03 PM

Comments

Rapid Micro Biosystems (NASDAQ:RPID), a company that sells automated microbial quality control systems for pharmaceutical manufacturers, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Rapid Micro Biosystems delivered record revenue of $11.3 million, representing 37% growth year over year. The company placed 16 Growth Direct systems, ending the year with 190 systems installed globally and 155 validated. Product revenue grew 78% for the quarter, driven by strong system placements, while consumable revenue increased 11%. Recurring revenue, which includes consumables and service contracts, accounted for 53% of total revenue for the full year. However, gross margin was negatively affected by a $1.1 million inventory write-off, resulting in a negative overall gross margin for the quarter. The company’s net loss for the quarter was $12.5 million, with a net loss per share of $0.28. These results exceeded the company’s updated guidance and met or beat analyst expectations for the quarter.

Positives
Record system placements and revenue growth: The company achieved its highest-ever quarterly revenue and system placements, indicating strong demand for its Growth Direct platform.
Strong recurring revenue base: Recurring revenue made up more than half of total revenue for the year, highlighting the stability and predictability of the business model as more systems are validated and used regularly.
Successful expansion with large customers: Notable multi-system orders from Amgen and Samsung Biologics demonstrate the company’s ability to secure repeat business and expand within major pharmaceutical and contract manufacturing organizations.
Progress in cost reduction initiatives: The company locked in material cost reductions for consumables that are expected to benefit margins in 2026, showing ongoing operational improvements.
Negatives
Gross margin impacted by inventory write-off: A one-time charge for unusable consumable inventory led to a negative gross margin for the quarter, which offset otherwise positive trends in manufacturing efficiency.
Service revenue and margin declined: Service revenue and related margins were lower compared to the prior year, mainly due to fewer validation completions and the timing of customer projects, which can create quarter-to-quarter variability.
Continued net losses: The company remains unprofitable, with net losses widening compared to the prior year, reflecting ongoing investments and some higher costs.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
System placements and validation progress: Management guided to at least five system placements and at least three validations in Q1. Investors should watch for updates on the pace of installations and how quickly new systems are validated, as this drives future recurring revenue.
Consumable revenue growth: The company expects consumable revenue in Q1 and Q2 to be slightly higher than in Q4 2025. This metric will help indicate whether customers are ramping up usage of installed systems.
Gross margin improvement: Q1 gross margin is expected to be in the mid-single digits, with product margin still negative but improving. Watch for commentary on the impact of cost reduction efforts and whether the company remains on track to achieve its full-year gross margin target of approximately 20%.
MilliporeSigma partnership contributions: The distribution and collaboration agreement with MilliporeSigma is entering its second year. Investors should look for any early signs of system placements or revenue contributions from this partnership, as well as updates on ongoing negotiations for supply and service agreements.
Development of next-generation software platform: The company plans to release a new cloud-native software platform in the second half of 2026. Any updates on its development, pilot customer feedback, or plans for broader rollout could signal future opportunities for recurring high-margin software and analytics revenue.
