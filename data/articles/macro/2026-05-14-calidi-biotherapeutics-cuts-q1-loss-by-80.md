Quarterly Highlights

The company’s greatest operational win came from a marked decrease in general and administrative costs, which dropped nearly 39% from the prior year period due to lower staffing, consultant, and rental expenses. This helped reduce overall net loss for the quarter compared to last year.

Development of the RedTail platform took key steps forward. CLD-401 remained on track for late 2026 IND filing, and Calidi entered a partnership with TransferAI to speed up IND submission and regulatory documentation. Presentations at major scientific conferences showcased the potential of the platform to activate immune responses in tumors—an important differentiator in cancer immunotherapy.

The cash position improved slightly after a recent equity raise, reaching $6.6 million at the end of the period. However, with ongoing net losses, this funding provides limited runway absent new capital raises. The company continues to report no product revenue as it remains in the pre-commercial phase.
