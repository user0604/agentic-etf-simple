Key Takeaways
Amphastar Pharmaceuticals reported a mixed quarter as profits and margins declined while revenue stayed flat.
Non-GAAP EPS fell sharply compared to the prior year.
Management did not issue updated guidance, leaving investors watching for stabilization in earnings and margins.

Amphastar Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ:AMPH) posted a challenging start to fiscal 2026 as profits tumbled despite nearly unchanged revenue levels. The specialty pharmaceuticals company, known for injectable and inhalation therapeutics, experienced margin pressure and a notable earnings decline due to pricing headwinds and increased R&D spending. The company's financial results for the first quarter, released May 7, 2026, reflect difficult conditions that contrast with its prior growth ambitions and strategic investments. Overall, the period revealed clear margin and profit setbacks even as product launches and pipeline activity continued.

Notable Developments
Profitability declined significantly as net income dropped 75% from a year ago, driven by lower prices on key products and higher manufacturing expenses.
Gross margin contracted by nearly 9 percentage points, due to increased cost of goods and persistent price competition.
Research and development spending surged 33% year over year, including a $2.0 million upfront payment for a new product license, reflecting continued pipeline investment despite near-term cost pressure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide updated financial guidance for the remainder of 2026. Previously, it aimed for mid-to-high single-digit revenue growth and recognized margin challenges, but the current results fell short of these expectations. Investors should focus on whether recent product launches and ongoing pipeline progress can help stabilize both revenue and profitability in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue was essentially flat from the prior year as new product launches and strong growth in "other products" offset major price and volume declines in high-margin legacy drugs. Segment leaders included BAQSIMI (a nasal glucagon rescue therapy) and Primatene MIST (an over-the-counter asthma inhaler), though BAQSIMI sales fell 15% and glucagon sales dropped 56% year over year due to intensifying competition and pricing challenges. Newer launches such as albuterol, iron sucrose, and teriparatide contributed to a 34% increase in the "other products" category, but these gains were not sufficient to drive overall growth or improve margins.

Cost of goods sold climbed 18% over last year, outpacing the modest gains in revenue. This resulted in a gross margin decline from 50.0% to 41.1%, significantly sharper than the single-digit compression anticipated in the prior management outlook. Higher input costs and major investments in facility expansion, especially at Rancho Cucamonga, played a role in margin pressures.

Operating expenses rose meaningfully. General and administrative costs grew by 13%, while research and development jumped 33% after factoring in both organic pipeline investments and an upfront licensing expense. Despite these outlays, the company maintained a healthy cash balance of $291.9 million and operating cash flow of $47.8 million, supporting future investment and share repurchases.

Amphastar Pharmaceuticals is a specialty drug manufacturer focused on developing, making, and selling injectable and inhalation medicines for hospital and outpatient needs.
