Quarterly highlights

Weakness in the Technology & Shopping segment remained a central challenge. This business, which includes online shopping guides and product recommendations, faced structural online-search headwinds and declining affiliate marketing revenue, resulting in a 12.9% sales drop and a negative segment operating result.

The positive news came from the Gaming & Entertainment and Cybersecurity & Martech segments. Both posted growth due to stable consumer engagement and rising demand for digital protection tools, with Gaming & Entertainment jumping 7.2% and Cybersecurity & Martech by 3.6%.

Despite higher cash generation from operations, free cash flow was negative as the company increased technology and capital spending. At the same time, Ziff Davis spent $51.6 million on share repurchases, reducing its share count and supporting per-share earnings.
