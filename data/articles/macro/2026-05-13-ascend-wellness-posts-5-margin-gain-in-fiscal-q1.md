Ascend Wellness Posts 5% Margin Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:31 PM

Key Takeaways
Revenue and retail network growth faced near-term headwinds as store expansion continued.
Adjusted gross margin improved by 5.3 percentage points year over year despite falling revenue.
Management expects modest revenue growth and stable margins for the next quarter as new store openings ramp up.

Ascend Wellness (OTC:AAWH)’s business progress was mixed as retail network expansion and better margins partially offset falling sales. The multistate cannabis retailer and cultivator continued to grow its store presence but saw notable revenue and earnings declines in the first quarter. The latest numbers, released on May 13, 2026 for its fiscal first quarter, reveal ongoing margin improvement but persistent pressure on revenue and net income.

Overall, the quarter showed that efficiency gains helped, but revenue and liquidity declined given industry challenges and capital outflows.

Notable Developments
Total revenue was $116.9 million for Q1 2026, with retail now generating 71% of company sales and five new dispensaries added year-to-date.
Adjusted gross margin climbed to 46.1%, up 5.3 percentage points year over year, reflecting greater efficiency and a stronger retail product mix.
Cash and equivalents fell to $60.9 million from $100 million, mainly due to large interest and legal settlement outflows.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management is forecasting a 2–3% increase in revenue and expects adjusted EBITDA margin to hold steady in the low-20% range for the next quarter. Its leadership points to recently opened stores and improving seasonality as drivers for the anticipated improvement. The key area to monitor is how revenue trends evolve as the store base approaches the company’s 60-location target in heavily regulated markets.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue for the quarter declined year over year as price competition and temporary store disruptions offset gains from new locations, even as retail generated a larger share of sales and the company’s store count grew to 51. Despite the revenue drop, adjusted gross margin improved helped by greater vertical integration—meaning more products sold in-house from cultivation to customer—and efficiency in retail operations. Cash outflows were higher than in prior periods, largely due to a scheduled $19.1 million interest payment and a one-time $17 million legal payout, rather than core operating losses.

Brand innovation continued, with the Ozone lifestyle brand relaunch and High Wired’s leading market share in the infused flower product category. Ascend reported 34% more customers enrolled in its loyalty program, with loyalty purchases now comprising nearly all retail transactions. These product and customer engagement initiatives are positioned as core areas of future growth.

Regulatory compliance and market expansion remain central, as ten new dispensaries are planned by year-end. Future growth depends on timely licensing approvals and favorable developments at both the state and federal level, such as anticipated movement on cannabis rescheduling which could ease tax burdens and boost profitability.

What Ascend Wellness Does

Ascend Wellness operates cannabis cultivation facilities and a network of retail dispensaries, offering in-house and partner cannabis brands across several regulated states.
