Key Takeaways
Guardant Health delivered strong top-line growth but continues to operate at a loss.

Guardant Health (NASDAQ:GH) delivered a business win with rapid expansion in both cancer screening and oncology test adoption. The precision oncology firm, known for its advanced blood-based and tissue tests to detect and manage cancer, reported results for the first quarter of 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed robust growth in test volumes and revenue, though significant investments in expansion kept it in a loss position.

Notable developments
Screening revenue grew over 600%, reflecting a jump in Shield cancer screening test adoption and higher reimbursement rates in the segment.
Oncology segment revenue grew 36%, with around 86,000 oncology tests conducted, up 47% year over year.
Full-year revenue guidance increased, now projected at $1.30–$1.32 billion, marking 32% to 34% growth versus prior expectations of 27% to 30%.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its guidance for 2026, reflecting confidence in continued test adoption and expanding reimbursed markets. Leaders expect sustained oncology volume growth and further acceleration in Shield screening. The most important factor to monitor is whether the momentum in test adoption and reimbursements can offset growing expenses, as the company works toward its target of company-wide free cash flow breakeven by the end of 2027.

Quarterly highlights

The screening segment saw especially rapid gains, with Shield screening test volume reaching about 44,000 tests in the quarter, compared to 9,000 a year earlier. Direct-to-consumer marketing, health system partnerships, and payer coverage supported this dramatic adoption curve. This segment’s growth contributed to a sharp increase in segment revenue, but ongoing investments in commercial expansion resulted in widening free cash flow losses.

Oncology test volume hit approximately 86,000 tests, a 47% increase year over year, attributed to growing clinical integration and new FDA approvals supporting the Guardant360 test portfolio. There was notable progress in the use of these tests for both therapy selection and therapy response monitoring.

The company continued to spend aggressively on sales, marketing, and research, with non-GAAP operating expenses rising to $268.1 million. It ended the quarter with a strong cash position of $1.2 billion, yet the quarter’s free cash flow burn reached $71.2 million, emphasizing the importance of scaling revenue to keep up with expansion spending.
