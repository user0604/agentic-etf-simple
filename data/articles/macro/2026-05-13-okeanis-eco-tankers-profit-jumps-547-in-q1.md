Okeanis Eco Tankers Profit Jumps 547% in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:33 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Okeanis Eco Tankers delivered a standout quarter as profits surged on record tanker rates.
Earnings per share jumped 547% year over year on an adjusted (non-GAAP) basis.
Management signals ongoing market strength, but notes costs and leverage require monitoring.

Okeanis Eco Tankers (NYSE:ECO) profits soared as high charter rates and fleet expansion fueled strong growth throughout its operations. The prominent Athens-based owner and operator of crude oil tankers reported its first quarter results for the period ending March 31, 2026, on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter featured sharply higher revenue and profitability, offset by increased expenses and continued spending on new vessels.

*Adj. EBITDA (Non-GAAP) is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization. Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) is a widely used industry metric that allows comparison of period-to-period operating performance by measuring daily revenue on a per-vessel basis, excluding voyage expenses.

Notable Developments
Revenue more than doubled year over year, reaching $170.2 million on the back of high spot market rates and fleet growth.
Fleetwide daily TCE rate rose to $93,100, a 142% increase that drove a significant rise in both net profit and adjusted EBITDA.
The company declared a $2.00 per share dividend, up from $0.35 in the prior-year quarter, underlining its commitment to returning cash to shareholders.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted ongoing market strength with a large proportion of future tanker days already fixed at even higher rates for the second quarter. However, it cautioned that rising costs and balance sheet expansion from buying new vessels must be monitored. The most important issue for investors to watch is whether these strong charter rates can be sustained as new fleet additions and macroeconomic trends evolve.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenues rose 112% year over year in Q1 2026, enabled by both strong charter rates and an expanded fleet of 8 Suezmax and 8 Very Large Crude Carrier (VLCC) tankers equipped with emission-reducing scrubber technology.

Profitability metrics showed similar momentum. Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) increased 239% year over year and adjusted earnings per share (non-GAAP) rose to $2.33 from $0.36. The fleetwide daily Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) rate (non-GAAP) climbed to $93,100. This surge reflects strong spot and term contracts, including several high-profile VLCC charter deals.

The company returned much of its profit to shareholders, declaring a dividend of $2.00 per share, a 471% increase from a year ago. While operating expenses and general and administrative costs also rose as the company grew, management maintained a focus on balance sheet discipline. Cash balances increased to $176.5 million, and leverage was managed through a combination of new equity and structured financing facilities.

What Okeanis Eco Tankers Does

Okeanis Eco Tankers operates a fleet of modern crude oil tankers that transport oil worldwide, with a particular focus on efficiency and environmental compliance.
