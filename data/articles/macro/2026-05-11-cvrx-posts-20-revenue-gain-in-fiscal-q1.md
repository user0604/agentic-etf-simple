CVRx Posts 20% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 4:26 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
CVRx posted higher U.S. sales and gross margin progress but continued to report net losses. Gross margin rose to 87% from 84% in the prior-year period. Management reaffirmed its outlook for fiscal 2026 revenue and raised its gross margin forecast.

CVRx (NASDAQ:CVRX) expanded commercial adoption of its Barostim heart failure device in the U.S., driving a solid period of revenue growth and improved margin. The neuromodulation device maker specializes in implantable technology for heart failure, mainly in the U.S. market. Reporting its first-quarter results on May 11, 2026, for the period ended March 31, management pointed to operational improvement but acknowledged another period of net losses and persistent cash burn. Overall, the quarter showed good execution in the U.S. but flat international progress and ongoing financial challenges.

Notable developments
Revenue climbed 20%, reaching $14.8 million, as U.S. sales advanced while international revenue stayed subdued.
Gross margin improved to 87%, and management increased its full-year margin outlook to a range of 85%-87%.
The first patient was enrolled in the BENEFIT-HF clinical trial, a study that may triple the potential market for Barostim.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its fiscal 2026 revenue target of $63.0 million-$67.0 million and lifted the gross margin outlook, citing strong operational execution. The company highlighted continued U.S. commercial strength and focus on disciplined cost management. Investors should pay close attention to the pacing and results of the BENEFIT-HF clinical trial, as this could meaningfully expand Barostim’s addressable population.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue growth remained concentrated in the U.S. as commercial activity increased. Sales of Barostim, an implantable neuromodulation device for heart failure, rose with new sales territories and more active implanting centers, but revenue from Europe fell slightly and units declined, underlining the company's reliance on one major market for growth.

Profitability metrics showed modest improvement with a smaller net loss and gross margin rising by 3.0 percentage points from the previous year. Operating expenses continued to increase, fueled by higher research and development spending, especially for advancing clinical programs.

The company marked important progress in its BENEFIT-HF clinical trial by activating its first site and enrolling a patient. If successful, this trial could increase the qualifying patient pool for Barostim from about 339,000 currently to over 983,000 in the U.S., highlighting the importance of execution in this study for future growth.

What CVRx does

CVRx develops and markets implantable neuromodulation devices, primarily Barostim, for the treatment of heart failure with reduced ejection fraction.
