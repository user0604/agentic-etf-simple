Earnings Preview: Take-Two Interactive Software

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Take-Two Interactive Software (NASDAQ:TTWO), a leading publisher of video games and mobile entertainment, is expected to report its fourth-quarter and full-year fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 21, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

Take-Two delivered a strong third quarter for fiscal 2026, with net bookings of $1.76 billion, which came in above the high end of management’s guidance and beat analyst expectations. The company’s core franchises, including NBA 2K, Grand Theft Auto, and several mobile titles, all contributed to the outperformance. Net revenue grew 25% year over year to $1.7 billion, and recurring consumer spending accounted for more than three-quarters of net bookings. Despite the significantly higher revenue, the company reported a net loss of $92.9 million, which was an improvement from the prior year’s loss and better than the consensus estimate for the quarter. Management raised full-year guidance for both net bookings and operating cash flow, citing continued strength across its portfolio.

Positives
Net bookings exceeded expectations: Third-quarter net bookings of $1.76 billion were above both company guidance and analyst consensus, reflecting strong demand for key franchises.
Recurring consumer spending saw robust growth: Revenue from in-game purchases, add-ons, and advertising rose 23% year over year, indicating high player engagement and effective monetization.
Mobile portfolio delivered standout performance: Titles like TuneBlast, Match Factory, and Color Block Jam posted double-digit growth, with TuneBlast surpassing $3 billion in lifetime net bookings.
Operating cash flow outlook improved: Management raised its full-year operating cash flow forecast to $450 million, up from $250 million, reflecting better profitability and working capital management.
Negatives
Company remained unprofitable on a GAAP basis: Take-Two reported a net loss of $92.9 million for the quarter, though this was an improvement from the prior year and better than analyst expectations.
Operating expenses increased: Total operating expenses rose 10% year over year, driven by higher performance-based compensation and investments in development and marketing.
Gross margin slightly declined: Gross profit margin dipped to 55.6% from 55.9% in the prior year, due to higher amortization of software costs and internal royalties.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Performance of new and recently expanded titles: Investors should monitor the launch of WWE 2K26, the release of Sid Meier’s Civilization VII on Apple Arcade, and the continued rollout of PGA Tour 2K25 on Nintendo Switch 2 for their impact on bookings and player engagement.
Trends in recurring consumer spending: Management expects a 7% year-over-year increase in this metric for Q4, with NBA 2K projected to see a high 20% growth rate and mobile to deliver mid-single-digit gains.
Grand Theft Auto franchise momentum: With anticipation building for Grand Theft Auto VI’s November 2026 release, investors should watch for any signals on engagement with GTA Online and GTA V, as well as updates on the GTA+ membership program.
Direct-to-consumer (DTC) growth and margin impact: The company is expanding DTC efforts in mobile, which could improve margins by reducing third-party fees. Progress here may be discussed or reflected in Q4 results.
Initial outlook for fiscal 2027 and pipeline visibility: Management has indicated it will provide its first three-year pipeline and initial fiscal 2027 guidance with Q4 results, which will be key for understanding the growth trajectory as Grand Theft Auto VI launches.
