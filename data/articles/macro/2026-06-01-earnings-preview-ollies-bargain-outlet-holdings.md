Earnings Preview: Ollie's Bargain Outlet Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Ollie's Bargain Outlet Holdings (NASDAQ:OLLI), a discount retailer specializing in closeout and excess inventory household goods, is expected to report first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Ollie’s reported a strong finish to fiscal 2025, with both comparable store sales and earnings exceeding analyst expectations. Net sales for the fourth quarter rose 17% to $779 million, driven by robust new store openings and a 3.6% increase in comparable store sales. The company opened a record 86 new stores during the fiscal year, and Ollie’s Army loyalty program membership grew by more than 12% to 17 million members. Gross margin for the quarter was 39.9%, slightly below the prior year due to planned price investments but still above internal forecasts. Adjusted net income climbed 16% to $85 million, and adjusted earnings per share rose 17% to $1.39, both ahead of consensus estimates. The company ended the year with $563 million in cash and investments and no meaningful long-term debt.

Positives
Comparable store sales growth beat expectations: Same-store sales increased 3.6%, outpacing the company’s plan, even with disruptions from severe winter weather. This reflects strong consumer demand for value and successful merchandising strategies.
Record store expansion completed: Ollie’s opened 86 new stores in fiscal 2025, well above its previous record, supporting long-term growth and market penetration.
Loyalty program momentum: Ollie’s Army membership rose by over 12%, indicating effective customer engagement and marketing initiatives that are expanding the customer base.
Strong balance sheet and cash generation: The company finished the year with $563 million in cash and investments, providing flexibility for future growth and share repurchases.
Negatives
Gross margin declined year over year: Fourth-quarter gross margin was 39.9%, down about 80 basis points from the prior year, mainly due to planned price investments to remain competitive.
New store sales slightly below plan in Q4: While new store openings were robust, sales from these locations were a bit lower than expected, partly due to the soft opening strategy and timing during the holiday season.
Higher pre-opening and capital expenses: Capital expenditures and pre-opening costs were elevated, reflecting the accelerated pace of store openings and investments in distribution capacity.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable store sales momentum: Investors should watch for updates on same-store sales trends, especially after a strong Q4 and management’s guidance for 2% growth for the full year.
Progress on new store openings: The company plans to open 75 new stores in fiscal 2026, with most openings front-loaded in the first half. Monitoring the pace and productivity of these new stores will be important.
Gross margin stability: Management has set a target of 40.5% gross margin for fiscal 2026. Watch for any commentary on pricing, supply chain costs, or tariff impacts that could affect margins.
Loyalty program and customer acquisition: Continued growth in Ollie’s Army membership and customer engagement will be key indicators of marketing effectiveness and long-term sales growth.
Share repurchase activity and capital allocation: The company has committed to returning about 50% of free cash flow to shareholders through buybacks, with $100 million targeted for fiscal 2026. Updates on repurchase progress and capital spending will be relevant for investors tracking shareholder returns.
