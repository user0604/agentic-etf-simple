Notable developments
Revenue reached $159.9 million, an increase of 28%, outpacing Figs’s management outlook for “low 20%” growth.
Active customers surpassed three million for the first time, up 12.2% year over year.
Free cash flow moved into negative territory at $(5.6) million, largely due to an $11.4 million rise in inventory within the quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 revenue growth guidance to a range of 14% to 16% and sees adjusted EBITDA margin improving to between 13% and 13.2%. The quarter’s strong start supports confidence in hitting these new targets, but elevated inventory and cash outflows mean investors should closely monitor Figs’s cash conversion and inventory discipline in coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Figs saw sales expand across both core scrubwear products and its growing non-scrubwear lines, such as underscrubs, outerwear, and accessories. Scrubwear revenue climbed 27.2%, while non-scrubwear categories grew even faster at 31.3%, together confirming healthy demand beyond legacy products.

The company’s active customer base topped three million for the first time, reflecting both customer acquisition and strong repeat order rates. Net revenues per active customer and average order value also grew, with average order value rising 4.2% year over year.

Despite these wins, free cash flow turned negative as inventory rose 9% sequentially. This inventory build appears tied to new product launches and expanded international presence, but it adds pressure on working capital and future cash generation.

What Figs does

Figs designs and sells premium medical apparel and accessories directly to healthcare professionals, relying on a digital-first direct-to-consumer model.
