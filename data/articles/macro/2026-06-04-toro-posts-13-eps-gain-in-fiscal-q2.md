Notable Developments
Professional segment revenue reached $1.11 billion, up 9.1%, supported by the Tornado acquisition and strong demand for underground construction equipment and zero-turn mowers.
Operating margin improved by 0.4 percentage points, reflecting higher prices and productivity gains, even as material and freight costs increased.
Free cash flow for the first half of the year was $265.5 million, more than triple the same period last year, due to better inventory and working capital management.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its fiscal 2026 outlook, now expecting net sales growth of 4.0% to 6.5% and adjusted earnings per share between $4.50 and $4.62. Leadership cited ongoing demand in core construction and landscaping markets, but noted that inflation and international market uncertainties remain important factors to monitor. Investors should watch for persistent effects from rising costs and any lag in overseas market recovery.

Quarterly Highlights

The Professional segment saw robust growth, with sales rising to $1.11 billion. This increase was driven by contributions from the Tornado acquisition and higher shipments of underground construction equipment and zero-turn mowers, which are ride-on lawn mowers designed for efficient turning.

The Residential segment posted sales of $310.4 million, representing a 4.4% rise, helped by improved pricing and higher shipments of zero-turn mowers. Segment earnings surged 88% compared to last year, with margin gains supported by cost controls and a better product mix.

Inventory dropped by $196.4 million compared to last year, freeing up cash and contributing to a more than 200% jump in free cash flow for the first six months. The company returned $228 million to shareholders through buybacks and dividends, and long-term debt declined slightly.
