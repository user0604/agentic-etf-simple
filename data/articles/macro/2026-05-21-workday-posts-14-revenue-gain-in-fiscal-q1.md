Notable developments
AI-driven product adoption accelerated, with more than 4,000 customers now using at least one AI agent, a figure that more than doubled from the prior quarter.
Subscription revenue, Workday’s largest segment, increased 14.3% year over year, and total free cash flow improved by 46.3% to $616 million.
Management updated its full year non-GAAP operating margin guidance to 30.5%, maintaining its subscription revenue forecast for fiscal 2027 at $9.925 billion-$9.950 billion.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its outlook for ongoing double-digit growth in subscription revenues, supported by heightened demand for AI-powered platform features. The company raised its non-GAAP operating margin target for fiscal 2027 to 30.5%, indicating a focus on operational efficiency as investments in artificial intelligence continue. Investors should monitor the scaling of AI agent adoption and the pace of monetization through new pricing models such as Flex Credits.

Quarterly highlights

AI innovation delivered tangible results, as the company more than doubled its customer count for AI agents in just one quarter, and its Recruiting Agent played a role in 14 million hiring processes, up 44% year over year. Launched new solutions such as Sana for IT Service Management, a Travel Agent, and fresh offerings for the government sector, reinforcing its push to lead in enterprise software automation.

Workday’s partner ecosystem expanded, with strengthened collaborations including Microsoft (NASDAQ:MSFT) and new offerings from partners like Achievers and Insperity. Entry into Vietnam and enhanced data residency options in Europe bolstered its global reach and industry-specific solutions for customers in new markets.

Robust free cash flow supported $1.6 billion in share repurchases during the quarter. While cash holdings decreased to $4.4 billion, strong operating cash flow allows ongoing investment in AI, product development, and customer support as the company scales its cloud platform.

What Workday does

Workday provides cloud-based human resources and financial management software to businesses, government agencies, and educational institutions globally.
