Biotech firm 4d Molecular Therapeutics (NASDAQ:FDMT) reported a business win as collaboration revenue climbed, but heavy research spending led to a deeper net loss. The company, which develops gene therapies for serious diseases, continued to advance its late-stage clinical programs and completed key patient enrollment ahead of schedule. It released first-quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026, highlighting important operational updates and financial changes for the period. Overall, the quarter brought meaningful pipeline milestones while underscoring the company’s continued need for funding to support its research ambitions.

Notable Developments
Collaboration and license revenue climbed to $3.0 million, primarily from increased cost-sharing with Otsuka, compared to $14,000 a year earlier.
Research and development expenses surged nearly 60% to $65.0 million, driven by progress in the pivotal Phase 3 trial for the company’s lead gene therapy candidate.
The cash position at the quarter’s end stood at $458 million, and management reaffirmed that this is expected to fund operations into the second half of 2028.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed that existing cash and anticipated partnership payments will fund operations into the back half of 2028. The company expects major clinical milestones in late 2026 and 2027 for its lead gene therapies. Investors should watch for data readouts from the 4D-150 and 4D-710 trials as key events over the next year.

Quarterly Highlights

In the quarter, the company completed patient enrollment and randomization ahead of schedule for its Phase 3 4FRONT-1 study testing 4D-150, a gene therapy for wet age-related macular degeneration.

Topline data from the pivotal 4FRONT-1 trial is expected in the first half of 2027.

Meanwhile, expenses related to late-stage research drove a sharp rise in net loss. R&D spending now comprises the majority of overall company costs, reflecting heavy investment in multiple late-stage clinical trials.

The ongoing partnership with Otsuka generated stronger collaboration revenue. This collaboration is now a meaningful contributor to the cash runway, helping to support future development and operations even as product-based revenue remains out of reach.

4d Molecular Therapeutics is a biotechnology company focused on developing gene therapies for serious diseases using its proprietary vector engineering platform.
