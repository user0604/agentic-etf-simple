Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed that its main programs, IM1240 and IM1305, remain at the preclinical stage, with plans for first-in-human studies in 2027. The company’s ability to progress these therapies and form industry partnerships will be key to extending its cash runway past 2027. Investors should watch for progress in clinical development and strategic collaborations over the next several quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Research and development expenses increased to $1.2 million, primarily driven by more spending on manufacturing and scientific activities for the CAPTN-3 platform. This reflects the company's decision to concentrate resources on developing novel tri-specific antibodies.

However, the adjusted net loss, which excludes this non-cash item, widened to $2.1 million, giving a clearer view of the core operating deficit.

Purple Biotech ended the quarter with $6.4 million in cash, which management estimates will be sufficient to fund operations into 2027. With paused development on legacy programs and no new commercial-stage products, the company’s near-term prospects hinge on attracting new funding or partnerships.
