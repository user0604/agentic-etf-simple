Icf International Posts 10% Revenue Drop in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:20 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue disappointed due to delayed project timing in commercial energy and international government business.
Non-GAAP EPS fell 22.7% year over year as margins remained steady but profits declined.
Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 growth guidance, counting on deferred project revenue recovery.

Professional services firm Icf International (NASDAQ:ICFI) faced a challenging first quarter, primarily because of project timing delays in both its commercial energy and international government segments. The company is a major provider of advisory, technology, and program implementation services spanning energy and environment, technology modernization, and public sector consulting. Reporting results on May 7, 2026 for the quarter ended March 31, it saw profits and revenue fall, but margins held up and its long-term outlook remains intact. Overall, the quarter showed operational resilience but left unanswered questions about federal contract recovery.

Notable Developments
Federal government revenue fell 23.7% year over year to $182.3 million, as project cancellations from the prior year weighed on results.
Non-government sectors provided some offset, with commercial energy revenue rising 1.9% year over year.
The operating margin edged up by 0.1 percentage points year over year to 8.0%, driven by a favorable business mix and cost controls.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 outlook, relying on recovery of delayed revenues and double-digit growth in commercial and international business. The company expects company-wide revenue growth of 3% at the midpoint for fiscal 2026. Investors should watch the pace of federal contract wins and the ability to realize deferred energy and international projects as key factors for future performance.

Quarterly Highlights

The proportion of revenue from energy, environment, and infrastructure services increased to 53%, reflecting strategic focus on these segments amid strong demand for sustainability and modernization expertise.

Despite the revenue decrease, margins remained steady because of a favorable mix of higher-margin work and continued cost discipline, with selling expenses and indirect costs both falling from the prior year.

The company maintained strong contract visibility, ending the period with a $3.4 billion backlog—51% of which is already funded—and a book-to-bill ratio above 1.0, indicating more new business booked than recognized as revenue.

What Icf International Does

Icf International delivers advisory and technology solutions that help public and private sector clients manage programs and meet climate, infrastructure, digital, and regulatory needs.
