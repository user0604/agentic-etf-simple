Regis Posts 33% EPS Jump Despite Q3 Revenue Drop

Key Takeaways
Regis reported slightly improved profitability and cash generation, even as revenue and franchise locations continued to contract.
Earnings per share rose to $0.26 from $0.08, while revenue declined 8.1% for the quarter.
Management remains focused on operational efficiency and reducing debt, but did not provide new specific forward guidance.

Regis (NASDAQ:RGS) saw another quarter of franchise network contraction but managed to deliver stronger profits and continued positive operating cash flow. The national hair salon operator, whose portfolio includes Supercuts, SmartStyle, and company-owned locations, published its results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed better margins and comp sales improvement in select banners, even as the franchise revenue base continued to shrink.

Notable developments
Profitability improved, with non-GAAP diluted EPS up 32.6% and adjusted EBITDA up 8.5% despite total revenue falling to $52.4 million.
The franchise network lost 279 locations year over year, and franchise revenue fell 12.4% mainly due to fewer franchise salons and lower rental income.
Company-owned salons showed strong operational gains, achieving 9.6% same-store sales growth compared to a 6.8% decline the previous year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its focus on improving operational efficiency, growing cash flow, and reducing debt but did not offer new forward guidance in the earnings release. The company highlighted continued investments in technology, cost control, and targeted marketing to drive results. Investors should closely watch the pace of further franchise salon closures and updates regarding debt refinancing as key areas in coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability metrics improved even as revenue contracted. Adjusted EBITDA reached $7.7 million, driven by lower general and administrative expenses and better company-owned salon margins, while non-GAAP diluted EPS rose to $0.57.

The franchise segment remained under pressure, with revenue falling to $33.3 million due to a drop in salon locations and lower rental income. The franchise segment's adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 41.3%, reflecting efficiency gains despite a lower revenue base.

Company-owned salons benefited from the Alline acquisition, plus operational investments and labor management. Same-store sales in this segment climbed 9.6%, reflecting both price and operational improvements. Meanwhile, SmartStyle and other value-focused brands trailed system leaders like Supercuts, highlighting performance gaps across the portfolio.

What Regis does

Regis operates, franchises, and owns hair salons across North America under several brands, with a focus on value-oriented hair care services.
