Business overview

During the quarter, revenue increased compared to the prior year, driven by growth in recurring software and services. Subscription revenue rose 7% to $40.8 million, while hardware sales growth remained modest. The company continued to focus on expanding its product offerings and building strategic partnerships to support network expansion. However, cash and cash equivalents declined by $45.8 million to $95.8 million at the end of the period, reflecting ongoing negative cash flow and debt repayment.

Quarterly performance

ChargePoint exceeded its own revenue guidance, with the period’s revenue coming in $1.8 million above the high end of management’s target. The company launched Express Solo, a next-generation fast charger capable of delivering up to 600 kilowatts to a single port, reinforcing its focus on innovation and addressing the need for faster charging options in the EV market.

Strategic partnerships remained central to expansion efforts. ChargePoint announced a deal with OBE Power to provide approximately 2,500 charging ports at multifamily residences and secured one of its largest transit fleet deals for Santa Monica’s Big Blue Bus, signaling further network growth in key markets.

Despite these developments, the company’s cash position continued to decline, and losses persisted. Cost discipline was evident, with non-GAAP operating expenses falling 4% year over year, but recurring software revenue will need to offset slow hardware growth for ChargePoint to reach breakeven in future quarters.

Looking ahead

Management projects second-quarter revenue between $100 million and $110 million, indicating flat to slightly stronger performance in the near term. There was no specific forecast for margins or profitability, but recurring subscription revenue and the impact of new product launches remain focal points. Investors should monitor ongoing cash burn and whether recurring software revenue can help the company move closer to breakeven.

What ChargePoint does

ChargePoint builds, sells, and operates EV charging solutions for commercial, fleet, and residential customers, focusing on both hardware and recurring software services.
