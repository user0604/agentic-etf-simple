Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains focused on preserving core credit quality and continues to diversify its investment portfolio. However, it acknowledges that pressure on net investment income and net asset value could continue if market volatility persists. The most important factor for investors to monitor next is whether future earnings will be strong enough to keep funding current dividend levels, especially supplemental distributions.

Quarterly highlights

Net investment income per share, a key measure that indicates cash earnings available to shareholders, slipped to $0.35, down from both the prior quarter and a year earlier. This decline put pressure on dividend coverage, even as the company continued its base payout policy.

Portfolio yield rose to 11.73%, which may be viewed as compensation for taking on greater risk, but the yield improvement did not offset a $48.3 million hit from realized and unrealized losses, as portfolio values fell sharply in sectors like software and IT.

Debt-to-equity leverage reached 1.70x, up both sequentially and year over year, meaning the company is relying more heavily on borrowed funds to support investments as net asset value declines. This higher leverage adds risk if portfolio marks remain under pressure in future periods.

What Palmer Square Capital BDC does

The company provides private credit and debt investments to middle-market businesses, focusing on floating-rate, senior secured loans across a range of industries.
