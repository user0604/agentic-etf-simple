Key Takeaways
Hesai Group registered a business win with rapid LiDAR shipment growth and a return to profitability.

Hesai Group (NASDAQ:HSAI) achieved a significant milestone as LiDAR unit shipments more than doubled and the company returned to profitability. The leading provider of advanced LiDAR sensors for automotive and robotics needs delivered strong operational execution and expanded technical partnerships. The company released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 19, 2026, highlighting robust demand for both driver-assistance and robotics applications. Overall, the period reflected steady expansion, but margin pressure and increased technology investment remain important to monitor.

Notable Developments
LiDAR unit shipments climbed 140.9%, reaching 471,723 units, supported by both automotive and robotics applications.
Net revenue grew to RMB 680.6 million, within management's guidance range for the quarter.
Gross margin declined by 2.6 percentage points to 39.1%, reflecting a shift toward high-volume, lower-margin LiDAR models for driver assistance systems.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects revenue for the next quarter to rise to RMB 850–900 million, representing 20–27% year-over-year growth, with initial revenue from its Strategic Growth Initiatives segment beginning in that period. The most important factor for investors to monitor is how rapidly the company can scale new higher-margin business lines to offset ongoing margin pressure from core LiDAR sales.

Quarterly highlights

Shipments for advanced driver assistance system (ADAS) LiDAR more than doubled, with 353,441 units delivered, and robotics LiDAR also saw significant expansion, reinforcing the company’s technology leadership claims. Management highlighted a key exclusive supply agreement for Mercedes-Benz autonomous vehicle programs and the unveiling of Kosmo, an AI-enabled 3D sensing platform, which marks Hesai’s first step into recurring revenue service models.

The period’s gross margin fell to 39.1%, as the mix shifted increasingly to high-volume, lower-priced LiDAR sensors used in standard driver-assist features, while service revenue sharply decreased. Research and development spending increased nearly 12% as the company invested in next-generation “Spatial Intelligence” solutions, notably the Picasso 6D LiDAR and supporting technology for real-world data mapping.

Cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, short-term investments, and long-term time deposits totaled RMB 7.2 billion at quarter-end, supporting ongoing global expansion, product launches, and continuing investment in new business segments. While overall operating profitability strengthened, management flagged that future returns depend on new platform technology adoption and maintaining cost discipline amid heightened international competition.

What Hesai Group does

Hesai Group develops, manufactures, and sells high-performance LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) sensors and related solutions used in autonomous driving, robotics, and smart infrastructure applications.
