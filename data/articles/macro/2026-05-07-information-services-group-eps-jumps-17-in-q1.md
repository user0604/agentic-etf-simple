Notable Developments
Signed a record multiyear contract valued up to $17 million, providing governance services for a large global manufacturer’s AI transformation.
European revenue rose 25% year over year, while the Americas and Asia Pacific segments posted declining revenues.
Recurring revenue increased 9%, underscoring the company’s focus on subscription-based and multi-year contracts.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects second-quarter 2026 revenue of $62.5 to $63.5 million, and adjusted EBITDA between $8.0 and $9.0 million, anticipating continued year-over-year growth. The most important factor for investors to monitor is the performance of the Americas and Asia Pacific regions, as revenue in both areas declined this period despite strong gains in Europe and overall profitability improvement.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability improved meaningfully, with operating income up more than 47% compared to the same quarter a year ago and adjusted EBITDA margin expanding. The company links this growth to higher recurring revenues and improved efficiency in its delivery of technology-focused advisory and governance services.

The firm’s largest-ever client contract aims to support an AI-enabled transformation and is expected to contribute to revenue predictability and future growth. This deal underscores Information Services Group’s effort to expand platform-enabled solutions—which include tools for supplier management, benchmarking, and digital transformation analytics—at scale.

Although the European business delivered strong results, revenue in the Americas fell 3% and Asia Pacific posted a 15% drop from the prior year quarter. Management attributes the Asia Pacific softness to ongoing delays in public sector project spending and flags regional volatility as a key watchpoint, especially with macroeconomic uncertainty continuing in 2026.
