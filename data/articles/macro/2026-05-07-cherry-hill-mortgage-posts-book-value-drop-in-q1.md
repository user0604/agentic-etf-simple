Quarterly Highlights

The book value decrease was mainly due to large unrealized losses in residential mortgage-backed securities (RMBS). These assets are sensitive to interest rate changes, and recent market instability led to their values falling in the quarter.

Operating income from the company’s mortgage servicing rights (MSRs) and hedging activities helped offset some market declines. The MSR portfolio generated stable fee income, while active use of swaps and futures led to a $6.1 million gain on derivatives, cushioning some investment losses.

Unrestricted cash at quarter-end stood at $46.7 million, down from $54.9 million at December 31, 2025. Leverage edged higher as Cherry Hill sought to support earnings generation, but liquidity remains adequate for near-term operations and ongoing dividend support.
