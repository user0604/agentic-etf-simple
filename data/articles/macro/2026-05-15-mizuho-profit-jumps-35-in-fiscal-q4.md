Mizuho Profit Jumps 35% in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 15, 2026, 7:05 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Profit for the year was driven by one-time gains and strong fee income.
Ordinary profits rose 34.6% compared to the prior year.
No forward guidance or financial outlook was provided for the next fiscal year.

Mizuho Financial Group (NYSE:MFG) delivered a strong profit increase for the year, fueled by one-time gains and growth in fee-based businesses. The leading diversified financial group provides banking, trust banking, securities, and asset management services both in Japan and overseas. The earnings release, issued May 15, 2026 for the full fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, showed profits well ahead of its own forecast, though top-line growth was much more limited. Overall, the period saw a marked rise in profitability driven by non-recurring items.

Notable developments
Profit Attributable to Owners increased 10.4% year over year, reaching ¥1,248.6 billion, driven by one-time asset sales and strong fee business.
Ordinary Profits surged 34.6%, reflecting gains from the sale of cross-holding stocks and improved non-interest income.
Ordinary Income (total revenue) was nearly flat, up 0.6% to ¥9,085.4 billion, indicating limited underlying growth outside special items.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide an outlook or formal guidance for the next fiscal year in this release. Investors should monitor developments in recurring fee income and progress in reducing reliance on non-recurring profits from asset disposals. The sustainability of recent profit gains is the most important factor to watch in upcoming results.

Quarterly highlights

Profit growth for the year was disproportionately driven by hefty gains from selling cross-holding stocks.

Fee income, which includes commissions from advisory, asset management, and transaction services, advanced strongly both in Japan and abroad. However, the company did not provide detail on which geographies or specific product areas led this growth.

Ordinary Income, which captures all main revenue streams, was almost unchanged year over year. This suggests the jump in bottom-line profit came mainly from improved efficiency and non-repeat items, not from increases in lending or other core activities.

What Mizuho Financial Group does

Mizuho Financial Group is a Japan-based financial holding company offering commercial banking, trust banking, securities, and asset management services worldwide.
