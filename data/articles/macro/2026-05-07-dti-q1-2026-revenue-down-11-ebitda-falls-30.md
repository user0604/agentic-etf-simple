Notable Developments
Revenue fell 11.4%, with tool rental activity dropping 16.2% year over year, due in part to seasonal weakness in Canada.
Adjusted EBITDA, a profitability measure, slid to $7.5 million from $10.8 million, highlighting margin pressure.
Cash spending on capital projects rose 52%, contributing to negative free cash flow and increasing net debt to $48.9 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, targeting modest revenue and cash flow growth compared to fiscal 2025. Leaders expect relatively flat activity through the first half, with a rebound as Canadian drilling conditions improve and international momentum continues.

Quarterly Highlights

Rental revenue, which makes up the majority of the business, declined sharply due to slower demand in North America and a weaker seasonal period in Canada. Product sales, on the other hand, grew 8%, supported by increased uptake of the company’s technology-enabled lines, such as the ClearPath and Drill-N-Ream tool families designed for complex drilling operations.

Profitability was pressured by the revenue decline, resulting in a 30% year-over-year reduction in adjusted EBITDA and turning free cash flow negative for the quarter. At the same time, spending on new equipment and international service center expansion increased, which management says is needed to capture incremental demand abroad.

During the period, the final shares held by the company’s former private equity sponsor were distributed to investors, increasing public trading liquidity. Management also refreshed the company’s board and highlighted a strategy focused on geographic expansion and high-value technology adoption for future growth.

What Drilling Tools International Does

Drilling Tools International provides renting and manufacturing of specialized downhole tools for the oil and gas industry, supporting efficient and complex drilling operations worldwide.
