Earnings Preview: NetApp

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

NetApp (NASDAQ:NTAP), a provider of enterprise data storage hardware and software, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, NetApp reported revenue of $1.71 billion, representing a 4% increase year over year. Excluding the divested Spot business, revenue rose by 6%. Non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) reached $2.12, up 11% from the prior year. Both revenue and EPS exceeded analyst estimates for the quarter. The company highlighted record results in operating income and EPS, driven by growth in all-flash storage, public cloud services, and storage-as-a-service offerings. NetApp continued to return capital to shareholders, with $303 million distributed through share repurchases and dividends in the quarter. The company ended the period with $3.0 billion in cash and short-term investments and a net cash position of $522 million.

Positives
Record all-flash array revenue: All-flash storage sales reached $1.0 billion, up 11% year over year, showing strong demand for high-performance storage solutions as enterprises modernize data centers.
Public cloud momentum: Public cloud revenue, excluding the divested Spot business, grew 17% year over year, with first-party and marketplace services up 27%, reflecting NetApp’s ability to expand its cloud customer base and deliver value-added services.
Growth in storage-as-a-service (Keystone): Keystone revenue increased approximately 65% year over year, indicating rising customer preference for flexible, consumption-based storage models.
Operational discipline and profitability: The company achieved record operating income and EPS, with operating margin rising to 31.1%, reflecting effective cost management and a focus on profitable growth.
Negatives
Product gross margin pressure: Hybrid cloud product gross margin declined due to higher component costs, especially for memory, and an unfavorable revenue mix. This margin pressure is a direct result of industry-wide inflation in memory prices.
Flat public cloud revenue headline: Total public cloud revenue was flat year over year when including the impact of the Spot business divestiture, which may obscure the underlying growth in ongoing cloud services.
Ongoing restructuring charges: The company recorded $22 million in restructuring charges in the first nine months of fiscal 2026 as it redirected resources to higher-return activities, reflecting continued adjustments to its cost structure.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Revenue and margin guidance versus consensus: NetApp has guided for Q4 revenue of $1.87 billion plus or minus $75 million, with non-GAAP EPS expected between $2.21 and $2.31. Investors should compare actual results to the current analyst consensus and management’s guidance band.
Impact of memory price inflation on margins: Watch for further commentary on how rising memory and component costs are affecting product gross margin and whether recent price increases are sufficient to offset these pressures.
Adoption and early revenue from new AI offerings: The AFX storage system for artificial intelligence workloads shipped its first quarter in Q3, and the AI Data Engine (AIDE) is expected to become generally available in Q4. Investors should look for initial customer adoption, revenue contribution, and management’s outlook for these products.
Trends in cloud and Keystone business growth: Continued growth in first-party public cloud services and Keystone storage-as-a-service will be key indicators of NetApp’s ability to capture demand from customers shifting to cloud and flexible consumption models.
Progress on restructuring and cost control initiatives: Updates on the completion of restructuring activities and any changes in operating expense trends will signal how effectively NetApp is managing costs in a dynamic demand and supply environment.
