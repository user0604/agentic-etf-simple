Earnings Preview: Guidewire Software

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Guidewire Software (NYSE:GWRE), a provider of core software systems for property and casualty (P&C) insurers, is expected to report earnings for its third fiscal quarter of 2026 on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q2 FY2026 Recap

Guidewire delivered another strong quarter in Q2 FY2026, with total revenue reaching $359 million, representing a 24% increase year over year. Subscription and support revenue grew to $237 million, up 33% from the prior year, while services revenue climbed to $62 million, a 30% increase. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) ended the quarter at $1.121 billion, reflecting 22% growth. The company’s non-GAAP operating income was $87 million for the quarter, and gross margin improved to 68%. These results surpassed analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings, and management raised its full-year outlook across all major financial metrics.

Positives
Subscription and support revenue growth was robust. The 33% year-over-year increase highlights continued momentum in cloud adoption and strong demand for Guidewire’s core platforms.
Annual recurring revenue (ARR) growth remained strong. ARR grew 22% year over year, underscoring the durability of Guidewire’s subscription model and its alignment with customer growth.
Customer retention rates were exceptionally high. InsuranceSuite ARR retention, including downsell activity, exceeded 99%, indicating deep customer reliance on Guidewire’s systems.
Large and long-term deals increased. The average contract term for new InsuranceSuite deals exceeded six years, reflecting insurer confidence and willingness to make long-term commitments.
Negatives
License revenue declined as expected. License revenue fell 7% year over year due to ongoing migrations from on-premise licenses to cloud subscriptions, which is consistent with Guidewire’s business transition but does create short-term headwinds.
Operating expenses increased. Research and development, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expenses all rose, driven by higher headcount and investments in product development and go-to-market capabilities.
What to Watch in Q3 FY2026's Report
ARR and subscription revenue momentum: Investors should track whether Guidewire maintains its double-digit ARR growth and strong subscription revenue expansion, especially as more insurers migrate to the cloud.
Adoption and pipeline for new products like PricingCenter and ProNavigator: Management highlighted early traction for PricingCenter and rapid adoption of ProNavigator, its AI-powered assistant. Watch for updates on deal flow and customer feedback for these offerings.
Progress on cloud migrations and implementation efficiency: Guidewire is investing in tools and services to accelerate customer migrations to its cloud platform. Any commentary on reduced implementation timelines or increased services margins would be notable.
Impact of true-up activity and direct written premium (DWP) trends: The company noted healthy true-up activity in Q2. Shifts in customer premium volumes, which drive Guidewire’s pricing, could affect revenue in Q3 and beyond.
Status of share repurchase program and capital allocation: Guidewire began executing a new $500 million share repurchase authorization in Q2. Updates on buyback progress and cash deployment will be important for shareholders.
