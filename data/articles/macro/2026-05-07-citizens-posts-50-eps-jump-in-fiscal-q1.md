Key Takeaways
Citizens delivered improved profitability with steady premium growth in its Domestic Insurance segment.
Earnings per share (Non-GAAP) rose 50.0% year over year.

Citizens (NYSE:CIA) returned to profitability as steady gains in Domestic Insurance outpaced weaker results in international markets. The U.S.-based life insurance provider, known for serving both domestic and international policyholders, released first quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter featured progress in book value and new sales, but only modest growth in core revenues as some headwinds emerged in international operations.

Notable developments
The Domestic Insurance segment direct insurance premiums rose 12.8% to $18.4 million, while international premiums dipped slightly.
Net income rebounded to $2.3 million from a loss in the prior year, reflecting improved investment-related results and lower claims.
Book value per share increased 5.9% (Non-GAAP), marking a thirteenth straight quarter of growth for this capital metric.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated guidance for both revenue and profit growth for fiscal 2026, highlighting continued expansion in recurring revenues and new business wins. The company sees operating leverage and technology initiatives supporting long-term earnings per share gains. Investors should closely monitor trends in international premiums and operating cash flow, as these areas showed mixed performance in the quarter.

Quarterly highlights

The Domestic Insurance segment acted as the key growth engine, with direct insurance premiums climbing to $18.4 million, up 12.8% from the prior-year quarter. Management cited new product offerings and a larger agent force as reasons for this momentum. The segment also posted higher pre-tax income compared to the previous year.

The International Insurance segment showed contrasting results. While total revenue increased 10.2%—primarily from investment gains—direct insurance premiums edged down nearly 2%, suggesting some headwinds in policy sales or renewals internationally. This may signal early signs of increased competition or regulatory shifts abroad.

Investment income remained stable, with a portfolio yield of 4.54% and an investment portfolio valued at $1.4 billion. Although operating expenses rose on investments in technology and business support, Citizens maintained a debt-free balance sheet and posted another quarter of book value growth. However, a temporary net cash outflow from operations—attributed to matured endowment payments and higher expenses—was noted, and management expects a return to positive cash flow as these payments subside later in the year.

What Citizens does

Citizens is a life insurance company that provides U.S. dollar-denominated insurance and related financial products to customers in the United States and select international markets.
