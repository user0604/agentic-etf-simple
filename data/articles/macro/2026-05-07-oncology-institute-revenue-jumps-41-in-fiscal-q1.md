Notable Developments
Specialty pharmacy revenue jumped 77.6%, reaching a record $87.5 million, fueled by higher Medicare Part D prescription fills and better member engagement.
Florida operations reached profitability in the quarter, as new capitated contracts matured and delegated risk models scaled up.
Free cash flow loss narrowed from $(4.0) million a year earlier to $(2.4) million, and management raised the full-year outlook to $5–$15 million in positive cash flow.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its 2026 guidance for revenue, gross profit, and adjusted EBITDA, and it boosted the free cash flow target for the year. Leadership cited ongoing momentum in its core business lines and operational improvements while noting a continued focus on sustainable profitability. Investors should closely watch the company’s ability to generate sustained positive cash flow and further scale its capitated, risk-bearing care model in Florida and other markets.

Quarterly Highlights

Specialty pharmacy set another record, with revenue up sharply as more patients filled prescriptions under Medicare Part D, and attachment rates improved across the network. Management highlighted that pharmacy revenue now serves as one of the biggest drivers of overall growth, especially as more clinic and plan members utilize in-network pharmacy services.

Florida became profitable on an operating basis for the first time, marking a milestone for the capitated and delegated contract approach. This success supports the company's strategy to take on more risk contracts, shifting from fee-for-service to value-based agreements where it receives a set amount per patient and manages the total cost of care.

While net losses persist, cost controls and margin improvements led to a sharp narrowing of adjusted EBITDA losses and smaller free cash flow usage. The improved financials allowed management to raise its full-year free cash flow forecast from potentially negative to a projected $5–$15 million positive outcome.

What Oncology Institute Does

The company operates a national network of oncology clinics and specialty pharmacies focused on value-based care, managing cancer treatment for large patient populations through risk-sharing agreements with health insurers.
