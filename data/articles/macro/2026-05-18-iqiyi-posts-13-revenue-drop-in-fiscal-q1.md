Streaming service iQIYI (NASDAQ:IQ) slipped this quarter as lower output of new shows and continued softness in online advertising led to weaker financial results. Notable Developments
Revenue dropped 13%, reflecting weaker membership and sharp declines in content distribution and other non-core revenues.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management emphasized cost discipline, ongoing investments in artificial intelligence (AI), and growth in overseas membership. No formal outlook or quantified guidance was provided for the coming quarters. The most important factor to watch is a recovery in content volume and quality, as membership and ad revenues appear very sensitive to the content pipeline.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure fell to RMB 6.23 billion, with core membership service revenue down 5% and online advertising revenue down 7% from the same period last year. Content distribution and “other” business categories saw even larger declines, both down over 40% year over year.

Spending on content edged just 1% lower, but with revenue falling 13%, cost reductions were not enough to shore up profitability. As a result, the company posted both operating and net losses, and free cash flow shrank to just over RMB 100 million.

International business saw relative strength, with management pointing to a record quarter for overseas membership revenue, though it was not enough to offset softer results at home. Capital return was a priority, with the buyback program and note redemption signaling management’s intent to support shareholder value during a rough period.

What iQIYI Does

iQIYI is a major Chinese online video platform offering streaming of original and licensed movies, TV shows, and variety programs through memberships, ads, and distribution partnerships.
