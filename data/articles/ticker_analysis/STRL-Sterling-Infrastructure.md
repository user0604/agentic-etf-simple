Sterling Infrastructure Rises on AI Data Center Demand

Analyst Brent Thielman at Oppenheimer initiated coverage of Sterling Infrastructure on June 4 with an Outperform rating, identifying the company as a primary beneficiary of the nationwide surge in artificial intelligence data center construction. The firm set a $950 price target, noting that Sterling provides the critical mechanical and electrical systems essential for high-power computing facilities. This bullish outlook follows a first quarter where the company reported earnings per share (EPS) — a measure of profitability for each share held — of $3.59, which significantly outperformed the $2.19 consensus estimate.

STRL Company Overview
Sterling Infrastructure manages civil construction projects for data centers, power generation facilities, and large-scale transportation networks across the United States. The company generates revenue by providing site development, specialized electrical services, and concrete foundation work for public agencies and private homebuilders. Its integrated service model and ability to handle complex, time-sensitive infrastructure needs foster long-term partnerships with government authorities and large corporations that depend on reliable project delivery for essential operations.

Sterling Infrastructure Lifts Outlook on Strong Tech Project Demand

Sterling Infrastructure builds and improves digital, transportation, and building projects across the United States. The company's biggest growth comes from its work in making infrastructure for important tech like data centers and semiconductor factories. Over the last 5 years, the stock has soared 2235.6%, beating the S&P 500’s 72.7% return by 2162.9 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $825.7 million (up 92% YoY)
EPS (Diluted, Non-GAAP): $3.59 (up 120% YoY)
Backlog (as of quarter end): $3.80 billion (up 78% YoY)

Latest Developments
