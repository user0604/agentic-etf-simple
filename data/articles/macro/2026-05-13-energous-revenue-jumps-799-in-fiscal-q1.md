Key Takeaways

Revenue grew sharply to $3.1 million in the quarter.

Energous extended its streak of revenue growth but saw expansion to new sites and customers proceed at a more measured pace. The provider of wireless power technology for enterprise Internet-of-Things (IoT) applications reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. The quarter showed sharply higher revenue and margin expansion, even as operating losses continued and sequential growth moderated.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 799% year over year to $3.1 million, with growth supported by ongoing Fortune 10 customer deployments and international expansion.
Gross margin was 36% for the three months ended March 31, 2026, reflecting the first quarter of augmented capacity with a new U.S.-based contract manufacturer, and the company maintained its quality performance record, with zero product returns since commercial production of PowerBridge PRO began in 2024.
The company ended the quarter with $36.6 million in cash, up from $10.4 million at year end, following a $31.9 million equity raise through an at-the-market program.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide formal financial guidance but emphasized the company’s ability to cover liquidity needs for at least the next 12 months due to its increased cash balance. Execution on converting customer trials into commercial deployments, especially with large enterprise clients, remains the biggest point to watch through the rest of fiscal 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

Energous (NASDAQ:WATT) continued to expand programs with Fortune 10 enterprises, including large retail and e-commerce customers. The company's technology was in use at over 1,500 U.S. retail sites, with more than 4,700 targeted, and its second major deployment extended internationally to 14 facilities, targeting 35 for 2026. This demonstrates ongoing but gradual scaling among its largest customers.

Gross profit climbed considerably, and gross margin expanded to 36%. This improvement reflected higher production volumes and efficiencies gained from bringing a second contract manufacturer operational in the United States. Notably, the company reported zero returns across deployed PowerBridge PRO products, underlining quality and reliability.

On the balance sheet, cash and cash equivalents rose to $36.6 million by March 31, up sharply from the prior quarter, thanks to an equity raise that management does not expect to repeat in the near term. Operating expenses, as measured on a non-GAAP basis, increased compared to the same prior year period, driven primarily by significant non-recurring and non-cash related adjustments recorded in the first quarter of 2025.

What Energous Does

Energous develops wireless power transfer technology that delivers radio-frequency energy to charge and power devices over the air, targeting industrial and commercial Internet-of-Things applications.
