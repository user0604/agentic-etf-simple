Rubrik (NYSE:RBRK), a software company specializing in data security and cyber resilience for enterprises, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Rubrik delivered results that exceeded analyst estimates across key performance metrics. Subscription annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $1.46 billion, representing 34% year-over-year growth. Subscription revenue for the quarter was $365 million, up 50% from the prior year. The company’s net new subscription ARR hit a record $115 million for the quarter. Rubrik’s subscription net retention rate remained above 120%, indicating strong customer expansion and retention. Free cash flow for the quarter was $70 million, and for the full year, it reached $238 million, a significant increase from the prior year. The number of customers with $100,000 or more in subscription ARR grew 25% to 2,805. Rubrik also reported a notable improvement in subscription ARR contribution margin, which rose to 12% for the year, up from 2% a year ago. These results were supported by continued adoption of the Rubrik Security Cloud suite and early momentum in identity protection solutions.

Positives
Subscription ARR growth was robust. The company grew subscription ARR by 34% year over year, reflecting strong demand for its data security platform as enterprises prioritize cyber resilience.
Customer expansion accelerated. The number of customers with $100,000 or more in subscription ARR increased by 25%, signaling Rubrik’s growing traction among large enterprises.
Free cash flow improved significantly. Full-year free cash flow rose to $238 million from $22 million the prior year, giving Rubrik greater financial flexibility for investments and strategic initiatives.
Profitability metrics improved. Subscription ARR contribution margin increased to 12%, indicating better operating leverage as the business scaled.
Negatives
GAAP net loss remained substantial. Despite improved cash flow and margins, Rubrik reported a net loss of $349 million for the year, reflecting ongoing investments and stock-based compensation.
Non-recurring revenue tailwinds are expected to fade. Revenue benefited from material rights related to the cloud transition, but these are projected to decline in fiscal 2027, which could impact reported growth rates.
Macroeconomic and supply chain risks persist. The company noted that lengthening sales cycles and hardware supply constraints could affect customer purchasing and deployment timelines in future periods.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Subscription ARR and net new ARR growth: Investors should track whether Rubrik can maintain strong ARR momentum, as this is the primary measure of business health and future revenue potential.
Adoption of new products, especially in identity and AI agent operations: The general availability of Rubrik Agent Cloud and continued growth in identity protection solutions are key areas for expansion. Early customer proof-of-concepts and initial sales traction will be important signals.
Normalization of revenue growth as material rights decline: With the cloud transition largely complete, revenue growth rates may moderate. Investors should focus on underlying ARR trends rather than headline revenue growth.
Profitability and cash flow trajectory: Watch for updates on subscription ARR contribution margin and free cash flow, as these indicate Rubrik’s ability to balance growth investments with improved efficiency.
