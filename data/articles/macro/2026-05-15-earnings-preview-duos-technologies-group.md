Earnings Preview: Duos Technologies Group

May 15, 2026, 1:03 PM

Comments

Duos Technologies Group (NASDAQ:DUOT), a provider of modular data centers and edge computing solutions, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Monday, May 18, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Duos Technologies Group reported total revenue of $27 million for the fourth quarter and full year 2025, representing a significant year-over-year increase from $7.3 million in 2024. This growth was driven mainly by services and consulting revenue tied to the asset management agreement (AMA) with New APR Energy, as well as initial contributions from its new Technology Solutions division. The company achieved positive adjusted EBITDA for the second consecutive quarter, signaling improved operating performance. However, reported revenue came in slightly below the company’s prior projection of $28 million, and Duos posted a net loss of $9.8 million for the year, a modest improvement from the prior year.

Positives
Substantial revenue growth year over year: Revenue increased by more than 270% compared to 2024, reflecting strong execution of new contracts and the ramp-up of new business lines.
Positive adjusted EBITDA for consecutive quarters: Achieving this milestone in both Q3 and Q4 2025 indicates the company’s cost structure is improving as it scales.
Strong gross margin improvement: Gross profit reached $7.9 million with a margin of 29%, up significantly from the previous year, due to higher-margin service contracts and operational efficiencies.
Successful capital raises and strengthened balance sheet: The company ended 2025 with $63 million in total assets and increased cash reserves, supporting future growth initiatives.
Negatives
Missed internal revenue target: Reported revenue was slightly below the $28 million projection, mainly due to timing of revenue recognition and delays in certain business lines.
Continued net loss: The company posted a net loss of $9.8 million for 2025, though this was a smaller loss than the prior year.
Legacy business underperformance and divestiture: The railcar inspection portal segment remained flat, leading to the decision to divest this line within 60 days, reflecting ongoing challenges in that market.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Execution and revenue recognition from new Technology Solutions division: Investors should look for updates on the $10 million backlog and how quickly this business line is converting bookings into recognized revenue, as well as any growth in its sales funnel.
Progress on Edge Data Center (EDC) deployments and utilization: The company completed 15 EDCs in 2025 and has 5 more in production. Watch for updates on occupancy, customer wins, and recurring revenue from these sites, especially in underserved markets.
Impact of GPU-as-a-Service and high-power colocation contracts: Duos recently signed its first GPU-as-a-Service contract and a high-power colocation deal, with deployment expected to begin generating revenue around August 2026. Investors should monitor any early revenue contributions, customer expansion, and pipeline growth in these high-margin segments.
Status of rail business divestiture and cost savings: The company announced plans to divest its rail division within 60 days. Updates on the completion of this process and resulting reductions in operating expenses will be important for future profitability.
Guidance and margin trends for the remainder of 2026: Management has provided full-year revenue guidance of $50 million to $55 million, with expectations for gross margin to improve to around 76% in the second half. Investors should watch for any changes to this outlook and commentary on timing of revenue recognition, especially as new contracts ramp up.
