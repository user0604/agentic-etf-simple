Key Takeaways
Aveanna Healthcare delivered strong growth across all segments but faced margin pressure and negative free cash flow.

Aveanna Healthcare (NASDAQ:AVAH) extended its growth streak, but rising costs and weak cash generation weighed on the quarter. The national provider of pediatric and adult home care posted double-digit gains in revenue and profit for the three months ended April 4, 2026 (reported May 14, 2026), as it updated guidance to reflect ongoing momentum. The company also addressed risks tied to spending and a major pending acquisition. Overall, the quarter showed continued top-line strength with some financial caution signals for the rest of fiscal 2026.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 15.9%, with all three segments contributing--Private Duty Services grew 16.4%, Home Health & Hospice rose 17.4%, and Medical Solutions increased 7.4%.
The company generated negative free cash flow of $(3.8) million, despite higher operating profits and improved net operating cash compared to the prior year.
Full-year revenue and adjusted EBITDA guidance ticked up to $2.56–$2.58 billion and $328–$332 million, though guidance excludes the upcoming Family First acquisition.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised full-year guidance for both revenue and adjusted earnings, citing continued segment growth and improved partnerships with payers. The company stressed the need to watch cash generation and liquidity, as the pending Family First acquisition will reduce available cash and borrowing capacity. Investors should monitor post-acquisition integration and efforts to restore positive free cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

Growth in Private Duty Services led the quarter, with higher care volumes and revenue per hour, but gross margin declined to 27.9% due to increased wage and branch costs.

Home Health & Hospice segment saw a jump in both total admissions and episodic volume, and while revenue per episode increased slightly, margins edged lower from the prior year.

Negative free cash flow was a weakness in the quarter, contrasting with previous full-year performance and highlighting typical seasonality as well as the effect of ongoing investment and acquisition preparation.

What Aveanna Healthcare Does

Aveanna Healthcare provides home health, hospice, and medical solutions for medically complex pediatric and adult patients across the United States.
