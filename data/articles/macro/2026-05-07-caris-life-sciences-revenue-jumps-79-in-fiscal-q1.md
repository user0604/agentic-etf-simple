Key Takeaways
Caris Life Sciences delivered strong revenue growth and improved profitability in the first quarter.
Revenue rose 79% year over year, supported by advances in molecular profiling and higher clinical case volume.
Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook, aiming for sustained revenue and volume growth while investing in new technology launches.

Caris Life Sciences (NASDAQ:CAI) posted a solid start to the year as top-line growth in its core business offset some softness in smaller areas. The leading precision oncology and molecular diagnostics company benefited from increased demand for its molecular profiling services and expanding clinical adoption. The earnings release, published on May 7, 2026, covered its first quarter results for fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter featured substantial gains in revenue and margins, even as growth slowed compared to late 2025 and spending stepped up to support new initiatives.

Notable Developments
Total revenue grew 79% led by an 85% increase in molecular profiling services, while pharma R&D revenue fell 21% to $5.4 million.
Gross margin improved by 18 percentage points to 65%, though it declined sequentially from the prior quarter's 75% as the company faced seasonal and mix shifts.
Operating expenses rose 18% to $136.1 million, mainly due to hiring and higher stock-based compensation; management cited ongoing investments in research and commercial infrastructure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue target of $1.0–$1.02 billion, expecting therapy selection volume to climb around 20%. It plans to continue investing in its pipeline and new product launches. Investors should track the pace of clinical case volume growth and trends in pharma R&D revenue, as these areas will be important for meeting annual goals and sustaining margin gains.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s molecular profiling business, which analyses genetic information from cancer samples to help guide therapy, delivered the bulk of revenue growth. Clinical therapy selection case volume rose 15% to roughly 52,800 cases, and management highlighted higher average sales prices contributed to gains. Revenue from the pharma research and development segment contracted, in line with a pattern of lower first-quarter sales and expectations for a stronger second half in this area.

Gross margin expanded to 65%, reflecting a more favorable product mix and improved reimbursement for profiling services compared to the prior year. The margin figure fell from the prior quarter, a change attributed by management to normal seasonal effects and fewer accounting adjustments that had boosted late 2025 results. Cost discipline helped produce positive adjusted EBITDA and operating cash flow, though planned spending increases may weigh on margins in coming quarters as the company invests in people, marketing, and research capabilities.

Caris continued to advance its product pipeline and technology portfolio. The quarter saw the launch and approval of ChromoSeq, a new whole genome profiling assay for myeloid cancers, along with Caris MI Clarity, an artificial intelligence tool for breast cancer prognosis. The company also announced positive results from a clinical study supporting its Caris Detect multi-cancer early detection test, and its clinico-genomic dataset surpassed 1,070,000 profiles—a scale that reinforces its competitive position.

What Caris Life Sciences Does

Caris Life Sciences applies advanced genomic and molecular analysis to cancer samples, providing data and technology that help doctors and drug developers personalize cancer diagnosis and treatment.
