Key Takeaways
The company advanced new clinical milestones and extended its financial runway, but operating losses widened.
Revenue nearly doubled year over year, driven by higher collaboration income.

BioAge Labs (NASDAQ:BIOA) advanced its clinical development programs and bolstered its funding, but the period saw sharply increased expenses and a wider loss. The clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focuses on therapies targeting aging and metabolism-related diseases. On May 8, 2026, it reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, with operations supported by public offering proceeds and collaboration revenues. Overall, the quarter showed solid scientific and financing progress, offset by a notable increase in research spending and net loss.

Notable developments
BGE-102, the company’s lead drug candidate, achieved an 86% median reduction in inflammation marker hsCRP in Phase 1 data, with 87–93% of trial participants reaching target levels.
Collaboration revenue rose to $2.8 million from $1.5 million, reflecting higher activity from the Novartis partnership.
A follow-on public offering added $132.3 million in gross proceeds, raising cash and marketable securities to $384.9 million as of March 31, 2026.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management maintained guidance for its main clinical programs, keeping Phase 2 trials for BGE-102 in cardiovascular and ophthalmology indications scheduled to begin mid-2026 with topline results expected by late 2026 or mid-2027. The company projects its cash resources are sufficient to fund operations through 2029. Investors should focus on progress toward those pivotal trial milestones, given the rising costs and the pre-commercial status of the pipeline.

Quarterly highlights

BGE-102, an oral NLRP3 inhibitor (a drug targeting inflammation in metabolic and eye diseases), met its primary Phase 1 endpoints with strong reductions in hsCRP and favorable safety. All treatment-related side effects were mild or moderate, with no serious events observed, and the therapy advanced to late-stage planning on original timelines.

The company’s operating loss deepened as research and development spending climbed 83.8% from the prior year, reflecting costs for new trials and scale-up of the BGE-102 and APJ agonist (obesity/metabolic disease therapy) programs. General and administrative expenses also increased, mainly due to personnel and legal fees.

BioAge Labs completed an upsized equity offering, boosting its end-of-quarter liquidity position. The increase in revenue this quarter came entirely from higher collaboration income, while commercial revenue remains several years away. Strategic partnerships with Novartis and Eli Lilly contributed to collaborative revenue and research progress.

What BioAge Labs does

BioAge Labs develops oral medicines targeting the biology of aging to address age-related diseases such as cardiovascular disorders, metabolic diseases, and eye conditions.
