Key Takeaways
Nerdy delivered stronger operating margins and higher revenue, but active member counts continued to decline year over year.
Gross margin jumped to 66.2%, and adjusted EBITDA swung to a $1.0 million profit.

Online education platform Nerdy (NYSE:NRDY) made progress on cost structure and profitability as its new AI-powered platform helped drive better margins, even as overall member numbers remained down from last year. The company, a provider of live and on-demand learning services connecting students and experts in real time, reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed tangible operating improvements and successful product innovation offset by persistent challenges in growing its user base.

Notable Developments
Adjusted EBITDA improved to a $1.0 million profit from a $6.4 million loss, reflecting strong cost control and the impact of the new AI-driven platform.
Active members continued to decline, down 8.9% year over year, although the pace of decline narrowed for the third quarter in a row.
Gross margin expanded by 8.2 percentage points year over year, as operational efficiency and higher average revenue per member offset lower total membership.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance for $180–190 million in revenue and approximately breakeven adjusted EBITDA, despite expecting near-term moderation in average revenue per member and continued weakness in institutional revenue through the second quarter. Looking ahead, the most important metric for investors to watch is a return to growth in active members, which management now forecasts for the end of 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

The launch of Nerdy’s AI-native "V3" learning platform powered both cost reductions and a faster pace of new product introductions, such as the "Maya" AI concierge and upgraded mobile apps. These innovations contributed to higher gross margin and lower operating expenses across sales, marketing, and product development. While these improvements improved profitability, active member numbers are still below the prior year, limiting top-line growth.

Average revenue per member rose 12% compared to the prior year, benefiting from a price increase and more engaging features on the new platform. However, management cautions that this growth rate will slow in the second quarter as the prior price hike is fully absorbed. The consumer segment continues to account for 80% of revenue, while the institutional (business-to-business) segment was down 1% from last year and is expected to remain soft in the near term due to slower bookings and funding uncertainties.

Expense discipline remains a focus, with sales and marketing expenses down 9% and general and administrative costs down 12% year over year (on a non-GAAP basis). Free cash flow improved but remains negative, with $44.7 million in cash on hand at the end of the period. Management points to further AI-enabled improvements coming in the next quarter, including expanded self-paced content and new educational features.

What Nerdy Does

Nerdy delivers an online platform that matches students with experts for live and self-guided learning experiences, integrating artificial intelligence to personalize and automate educational services.
