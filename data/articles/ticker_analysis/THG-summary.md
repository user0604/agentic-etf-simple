THG Company Overview
The Hanover provides a broad range of property and casualty insurance products for individuals and businesses through a network of independent agents. The company generates revenue by collecting premiums from policyholders for coverage against risks like property damage, personal injury, and liability. Customer loyalty is maintained through a localized service model and specialized industry expertise that integrates the company deeply into the business operations of its commercial clients.

Hanover Insurance Group Sets New Profit Records

Hanover Insurance Group sells property and casualty insurance to businesses and individuals through independent agents. The company stands out for its focus on quality underwriting and sticking to careful pricing across commercial, specialty, and personal lines. Over the last five years, the stock gained 28.3%, trailing the S&P 500’s 70.5% return by 42.2 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

EPS (Non-GAAP): $5.25 (up 35.7% YoY)
Revenue: $1.70 billion (up 8.3% YoY)
Combined Ratio: 91.7 (improved from 94.1 a year ago)

Latest Developments
