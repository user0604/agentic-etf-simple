FinVolution Reports 42% Profit Drop in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
FinVolution Group faced a challenging quarter as profitability in its core China market declined sharply.
Earnings per share fell 42.1% year over year while revenue dropped 7.8%.
Management reaffirmed full-year revenue guidance but highlighted continued regulatory and credit headwinds in China.

Online lending platform FinVolution Group (NYSE:FINV) reported a difficult start to the year as its Chinese business came under pressure from regulatory and economic challenges. The company, which operates an automated consumer loan platform and is expanding internationally, released its first quarter results on May 26, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. The period saw a sharp drop in domestic profitability, though growth overseas helped offset some of the impact.

Notable Developments
Group revenue fell 7.8% from a year earlier, pulled down by a 20% drop in revenue from China’s mainland operations.
The overseas segment saw revenue jump 34.5% year over year, with unique overseas borrowers more than doubling to 4.5 million.
Dividend was raised 10.5% to US$0.306 per American depositary share, and management continued share buybacks totaling US$39.4 million in the quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

FinVolution’s management reaffirmed its revenue guidance for fiscal 2026, targeting RMB 11.5 to 12.9 billion, while noting the ongoing impact of China’s regulatory environment. The company plans to continue expanding internationally, focusing on technology and risk management. Investors should watch credit quality in China and the profitability of international growth.

Quarterly Highlights

China’s business saw active borrower numbers fall 22.7% compared to the same quarter last year, and transaction volume contracted by 21.6%. This led to a significant year-over-year decline in group profit and operating margins.

International operations provided a boost, with revenue from overseas markets making up nearly 30% of group sales. Unique overseas borrowers increased 155% year over year, showing strong momentum in Indonesia, the Philippines, and Australia.

On the cost side, provision for loans receivable more than doubled from a year earlier, signaling increased risks in on-balance sheet lending. Cash and equivalents reached RMB 4.7 billion as of March 31, 2026, supporting continued dividends and buybacks.

What FinVolution Group Does

FinVolution Group operates an online platform that connects individual borrowers with financial institutions and investors, providing automated short-term and installment consumer loans in China and select overseas markets.
