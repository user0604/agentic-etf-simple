Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Marchex completed the migration of roughly 1,000 customers to its new technology platform, Engage. This milestone marked the end of a major infrastructure project and allowed the company to focus on accelerating revenue growth and improving margins for 2026. Revenue for the quarter was $10.8 million, which declined from $11.5 million in the prior quarter, mainly due to expected revenue dilution from the platform migration. Cash on hand at year-end was $9.9 million, down from $10.3 million at the end of Q3 2025. The company’s adjusted EBITDA was positive, and management highlighted efficiencies from organizational realignment and technology investments. Marchex’s Q4 revenue met analyst consensus, according to available estimates.
