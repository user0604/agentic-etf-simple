Key Takeaways
Revenue fell as government contract awards were delayed into the second half.
Gross margin improved 3.9 percentage points year over year to 35.2%.
Management expects record full-year revenue with a strong rebound in the final two quarters.

Defense optics supplier Optex Systems (NASDAQ:OPXS) reported lower quarterly sales as U.S. government funding delays pushed key revenue into later months. The company provides sighting systems, periscopes, and optical assemblies for military and commercial applications, supplying both the U.S. Department of Defense and selected international markets. The latest results, released on May 12, 2026, cover its fiscal second quarter, a period marked by supply timing challenges but also steady demand and stronger margins. Overall, the quarter showed resilient order flow and higher profitability on each sale, but saw higher operating costs and lower income.

Notable Developments
Revenue decreased 10.3% from a year ago, largely due to contract delays tied to the federal appropriations process.
Gross margin improved by 3.9 percentage points, driven by higher pricing and finishing older, unprofitable contracts.
Operating expenses rose sharply, including costs for leadership change, compliance investments, and R&D, moderating overall profitability.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects full-year revenue between $43 million and $45 million, expecting a strong second half as delayed contracts move forward. Adjusted EBITDA for fiscal 2026 is anticipated in the $7.5 million to $8.5 million range, about flat to slightly above last year’s $8.0 million. Investors should focus on the pace and size of new government contract awards in the coming quarters, as these will be the main driver for meeting full-year targets.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales in the second quarter reflected a shortfall caused by delays in U.S. federal appropriations, with management noting that several awards were postponed into the second half. Despite this, new orders for the first six months reached $16.3 million, up 3.8% year over year, indicating continued demand.

Gross margin improved significantly as the company completed loss-making contracts and benefited from better pricing and efficiencies across its two main business segments. This lift in profitability occurred even with lower revenue than the prior year period.

Operating costs rose sharply due to transition and investment expenses. Spending increased for leadership changes, higher stock compensation, compliance with new cybersecurity requirements, and upgrades to internal systems, pointing toward future readiness but weighing on current earnings growth.

What Optex Systems Does

Optex Systems designs and manufactures precision optical products, including periscopes and vision systems, primarily for military vehicles and defense contractors.
