Notable Developments
Revenue rose 8.3%, supported by improved product availability and higher demand.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expressed optimism about continued growth but did not provide financial guidance for future quarters. The most important item to monitor is progress on the delayed logistics center, as it is expected to improve cost efficiency and distribution capabilities once operational.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenue figure increased compared to the prior year, reflecting expanded product availability and steady demand. Gross profit margin improved, which management attributes to favorable currency movements and a focus on more profitable products.

Selling expenses rose sharply, mainly due to increased advertising, resulting in operating profit remaining nearly unchanged from the prior year. Cash and liquid asset balances stayed strong, with net cash from operating activities exceeding net profit, supporting ongoing liquidity.

Inventory levels declined by about a quarter compared to the end of December, indicating effective demand fulfillment and inventory management. The logistics center project, intended to modernize supply capabilities and lower long-term costs, was further delayed due to the ongoing war situation in Israel. Management continues to view this investment as critical for future growth and operational efficiency.
