HWM Company Overview
Howmet manufactures high-performance components for jet engines and airframes, alongside forged aluminum wheels for heavy-duty commercial vehicles. The company generates revenue by selling these precision-engineered parts and specialized fasteners to aircraft manufacturers and transportation fleets across global markets. Customer loyalty is maintained through long-term contracts and the high costs of switching suppliers, as its mission-critical products must meet rigorous safety certifications and technical specifications that are difficult to replicate.
