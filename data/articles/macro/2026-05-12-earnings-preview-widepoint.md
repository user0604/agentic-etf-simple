Earnings Preview: WidePoint

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

WidePoint (NYSEMKT:WYY), a provider of technology management and security services for government and enterprise clients, is expected to report its Q1 FY2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

WidePoint reported fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of $42.3 million, which beat analyst expectations. However, the company posted a net loss of $0.09 per share, missing the consensus estimate of a $0.01 loss per share. The quarter saw continued revenue growth, particularly from government contracts, and marked the 34th consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA and the ninth consecutive quarter of positive free cash flow. Management highlighted steady progress on new high-margin contracts and ongoing cost control efforts. The company continued to navigate delays in federal contract awards, especially the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) CWMS 3.0 contract, but maintained a stable operational and financial position throughout the period.

Positives
Revenue growth driven by government contracts: Revenue rose 12% year over year, mainly due to new and expanded federal contracts, including a significant task order with Customs and Border Protection (CBP) for 30,000 new service lines.
Margin improvement in managed services: Gross margin for managed services increased to 38% in Q4, up from 36% the previous year, reflecting a shift toward higher-margin offerings.
Strong cash and liquidity position: The company ended the year with $9.8 million in unrestricted cash and maintains a $4.0 million credit facility, providing ample flexibility for operations and potential investments.
Progress on SaaS and Device as a Service (DaaS) initiatives: Implementation of a $40 million to $45 million SaaS contract with a major telecom carrier is underway, and the company has started converting existing clients to its DaaS model, which is expected to improve revenue predictability and margins.
Negatives
EPS miss and ongoing net losses: The company reported a net loss of $0.09 per share, missing the consensus estimate, and annual net loss widened to $2.8 million for 2025.
Delayed contract awards impacting growth: Several large opportunities, including the DHS CWMS 3.0 contract, were pushed into 2026 due to federal government shutdowns and funding delays, slowing the pace of new business wins.
Lower adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow year over year: Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow both declined compared to 2024, reflecting timing delays in the sales pipeline.
Increased operating expenses: Sales, marketing, and general administrative expenses rose in dollar terms, driven by investments in personnel and higher health insurance costs, although these are expected to stabilize as a percentage of revenue.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Update on DHS CWMS 3.0 contract award or extension: Investors should look for any announcement or update regarding the long-delayed DHS CWMS 3.0 contract. Management expects news by mid-second quarter, which could significantly impact revenue visibility and growth outlook.
Progress on SaaS and DaaS revenue ramp: The start of revenue recognition for the new SaaS contract and success in migrating clients to the DaaS model will be important for margin expansion and recurring revenue growth.
Commercial revenue momentum: Watch for growth in commercial contracts, particularly managed services outside the federal sector, as WidePoint seeks to diversify its customer base and reduce reliance on government spending cycles.
Cost control and margin trends: Monitor whether the company maintains improvements in gross margin and keeps operating expenses stable as a percentage of revenue, especially as new contracts scale.
Cash usage and capital allocation plans: With a strong cash position and no immediate plans for share buybacks or acquisitions, any commentary on future uses of cash, including potential acquisitions or investments, will be notable for shareholders.
