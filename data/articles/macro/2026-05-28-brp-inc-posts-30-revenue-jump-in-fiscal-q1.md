Recreational products maker BRP Inc. (NASDAQ:DOO) delivered robust sales gains this quarter, even as bottom-line profits softened. The leading manufacturer of powersports vehicles and marine products posted higher sales in nearly every segment and region. The results, announced on May 28, 2026 for its fiscal first quarter, show broad-based top-line growth but also point to higher costs and a lack of new guidance. Overall, the quarter showed impressive market demand offset by rising expenses and no updated company outlook.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed to nearly $2.4 billion, up 29.5% from the prior year, with double-digit gains in both Year-Round and Seasonal Products.
Net income from continuing operations fell 21%, reflecting higher operating expenses and a much higher effective tax rate this quarter.
Total cash rose to $696.8 million, more than double the year-ago figure, strengthening the company’s financial position.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue updated financial guidance this quarter, leaving investors with no direct commentary on the rest of fiscal 2027. The sharp increase in costs and higher provisions could shape future results. The most important area to monitor is whether profit margins can recover as revenue growth remains strong.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s Year-Round Products—which include all-terrain vehicles (ATVs), personal watercraft, and three-wheel motorcycles—delivered $1.45 billion in revenue, up 31% year over year. Seasonal Products, such as snowmobiles, jumped 36%. Growth was broad, with Canada showing a 75% revenue gain and the United States delivering the largest absolute market sales.

Operating income more than doubled compared to last year. However, total net income declined due to a much higher income tax bill and increased spending on research and marketing. Research and development expenses grew 13%, reflecting an ongoing focus on new product innovation.

Inventory levels and provisions rose in the period. Management booked a $15.3 million inventory write-down, which could signal slower product movement or caution about changing demand. At the same time, product-related obligations—such as warranties—are well provisioned and did not show any regulatory setbacks this quarter.

What BRP Inc. Does

BRP Inc. designs, manufactures, and sells recreational vehicles and marine products—such as all-terrain vehicles, snowmobiles, watercraft, and engines—under brands like CAN-AM and SKI-DOO.
