Key Takeaways
ALX Oncology advanced its clinical programs while reducing expenses.

ALX Oncology (NASDAQ:ALXO) made progress on its cost-cutting efforts and continued to advance its drug pipeline in the latest quarter. The clinical-stage immuno-oncology company is focusing on its lead CD47-blocking drug, evorpacept, and its second program, ALX2004. For fiscal first quarter, reported on May 8, 2026 (period ended March 31, 2026), it maintained financial discipline while moving forward with its main studies. Overall, the period showed progress in both clinical execution and cash preservation.

Notable developments
The ongoing Phase 1b/2 trial of evorpacept in combination with zanidatamab showed a 60% confirmed response rate in the HER2-positive group, with responses most pronounced in patients with high CD47 expression.
Total research and development expenses declined to $13.6 million from $23.9 million, reflecting a focus on core programs and a reduced workforce.
A public equity raise increased available cash and investments to $169.1 million, extending funding runway into the first half of 2028.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its expectation of completing key clinical trials on time, targeting safety results for ALX2004 in the second half of 2026 and top-line data on evorpacept in mid-2027. The main focus for investors is whether upcoming trials will confirm strong results in CD47-high patients, as this would support potential regulatory approval and commercialization.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s lead asset, evorpacept, produced new early-phase trial data showing a higher response rate in breast cancer patients whose tumors expressed both HER2 and high levels of CD47 protein. All five patients in this group responded to the therapy, with a median duration of response exceeding 20 months and no new safety issues reported.

Cost discipline was evident, with research and development as well as general and administrative costs declining by more than 30% compared to the same period last year. This improvement followed strategic reductions in both workforce and the number of active clinical trials, as outlined in previous quarters.

Cash and investments reached $169.1 million at the end of March 2026 after a $150 million equity raise in February. Management states this is enough to fund planned operations into 2028, providing a buffer to reach milestone clinical data releases over the next two years.

What ALX Oncology does

ALX Oncology develops immunotherapies for cancer, focusing on CD47-blocking drugs for biomarker-driven treatment strategies and a pipeline that includes antibody-drug conjugates.
