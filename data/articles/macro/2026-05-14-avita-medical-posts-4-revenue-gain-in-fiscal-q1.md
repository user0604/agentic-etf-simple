Key Takeaways
Avita Medical delivered modest revenue growth but continues to operate at a loss.
Gross margin narrowed as portfolio expansion diluted profitability, with revenue up 4% year over year.

Avita Medical (NASDAQ:RCEL)’s latest quarter brought measured business progress as it grew revenue but remained unprofitable. The leading developer of advanced wound care solutions and cell therapy products reported results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (released May 14, 2026). Overall, the period showed steady execution against plans, with positive growth offset by continued margin pressure and cash outflows.

Notable developments
Gross profit margin narrowed by three percentage points, primarily due to greater sales of lower-margin products like Cohealyx and PermeaDerm.
Operating expenses declined 11% versus prior year as Avita Medical established a more disciplined cost base, especially in sales, marketing, and research spending.
Net cash used in the period was $9.9 million, with the company pointing to seasonal factors and one-time payouts, and expecting lower cash drain in the next quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its forecast for full-year 2026 revenue of $80–$85 million, signaling confidence in ongoing growth. It highlighted expectations for reduced cash outflows as one-off costs abate and collections improve in the coming quarter. Investors should watch the pace of RECELL adoption and margin stabilization as portfolio mix evolves, both of which are crucial for moving toward profitability.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue grew 4% over the year-ago quarter, supported by improved ordering patterns of flagship RECELL cell therapy kits and contributions from Cohealyx, a newly commercialized wound dressing. The normalization of reimbursement for RECELL, now with all seven U.S. Medicare Administrative Contractors posting payment rates, underpinned product utilization and sales momentum.

Expanding the product range with Cohealyx and PermeaDerm, both next-generation wound care dressings, diluted gross margin as these products carry lower profitability than RECELL. Gross margin stood at 81.7%, compared to a RECELL-only gross margin of 85%, reflecting the impact of product mix and some inventory adjustments.

Operating expenses dropped to $24.5 million, down 11% from the prior year as Avita Medical maintained tight discipline across all spending categories. Despite this, negative cash flow persisted, with $9.9 million used during the period and a decreased quarter-end cash and securities balance of $14.3 million. The company remains unprofitable, but its net loss narrowed from last year’s result.

What Avita Medical does

Avita Medical develops and commercializes regenerative medical devices that enable the healing of wounds and skin defects using the patient’s own skin cells.
