Alliance Entertainment Reports 21% Q3 Revenue Jump

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 5:42 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Alliance Entertainment delivered strong revenue and profit growth, led by gains in physical media and collectibles.
Earnings per share rose 25% year over year to $0.05 on a 21% revenue increase.
Management did not provide formal guidance, but pointed to continued investment in exclusive content and collectibles platforms.

Alliance Entertainment (NASDAQ:AENT) posted another period of robust growth with healthy sales of music, movies, and collectibles supporting improved profitability. The distributor and platform provider for physical entertainment and specialty collectibles saw higher revenue and net income in its fiscal third-quarter results issued May 14, 2026 (covering the quarter ended March 31). Overall, the quarter showed revenue acceleration and solid profit gains, although margin compression and heavier use of working capital present ongoing operational areas to watch.

Notable Developments
Collectibles revenue jumped 48%, reaching $8 million as the company expanded premium offerings and its Handmade by Robots brand.
Physical media business remained strong, with vinyl sales up 15% and CD sales nearly doubling from a year earlier.
Gross profit improved year over year, but the gross margin percentage narrowed to 12.8%, mainly due to product and channel mix.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management continues to focus on premium content partnerships, technology-driven platforms, and expanding in collectibles, but offered no formal outlook for upcoming quarters. The most important area for investors to monitor is the impact of these newer technology platforms on both growth and profitability, as Alliance Entertainment looks to scale its position in authenticated collectibles.

Quarterly highlights

Growth in physical media remained a key engine: revenue from vinyl records reached $99 million and CDs delivered a sharp 90% increase as demand for new and special releases drove activity. The company credited exclusive content partnerships, such as with Paramount and Amazon MGM Studios, for supporting its strength in home entertainment sales.

Collectibles continued to expand rapidly, benefiting from both expanded product offerings and higher selling prices. Alliance Authentic, a proprietary platform for authenticated collectibles, and the integration of owned brands such as Handmade by Robots helped boost segment profits and differentiation.

While overall profit and operating expense control improved, the gross margin slipped versus last year, suggesting some cost pressure or a heavier mix of products with lower inherent profitability. The company noted progress in automating warehouses and refining distribution, but cash levels remain tight and inventory has increased, underscoring a need for disciplined working capital management.

What Alliance Entertainment does

Alliance Entertainment distributes physical music, movies, games, electronics, and collectibles to retailers and consumers, while also building proprietary platforms and brands focused on authenticated and premium collectible products.
