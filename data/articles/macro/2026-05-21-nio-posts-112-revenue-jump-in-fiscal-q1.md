Key Takeaways
Nio delivered year-over-year profit improvement but faced a sharp sequential slowdown.
Revenue more than doubled compared to the prior-year quarter.

Nio (NYSE:NIO) delivered major year-over-year profit gains but experienced a notable slowdown coming off a record fourth quarter. The leading China-based smart electric vehicle maker, known for its innovation in electric SUVs and battery swap technology, reported its first quarter results on May 21, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter reflected clear cost discipline and margin improvement, though sequential declines in sales and deliveries remain a concern.

Notable Developments
Vehicle deliveries rose nearly 100% year over year, supported by robust demand across Nio, ONVO, and FIREFLY brands.
Vehicle margin expanded to 18.8%, a gain of 8.6 percentage points, driven by a shift to higher-value models.
Operating expenses in research and development fell 40.7% compared to the prior year, as the company aimed for efficiency and leaner spending.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects vehicle deliveries to rebound sharply in the next quarter, forecasting 110,000–115,000 units and revenue of RMB32.8–34.4 billion. The focus remains on scaling new models, launching flagship vehicles, and maintaining cost discipline. Investors should watch if seasonal volume recovery materializes and whether the company sustains improved margins with its expanded vehicle lineup.

Quarterly Highlights

Vehicle deliveries for the period reached 83,465 units, almost doubling from the previous year’s quarter. However, this was a significant decrease from the all-time high volume reported in the fourth quarter, reflecting expected seasonal trends and the timing of new model launches.

Profitability metrics showed substantial improvement: the company’s adjusted operating profit turned positive, while overall vehicle margin rose to 18.8% from just 10.2% in the prior-year period. The margin improvement was mainly due to a favorable product mix and reduced operating expenses such as research and development.

On the product side, Nio’s three-brand approach gained traction, with the Nio, ONVO, and FIREFLY lines each contributing meaningfully to total deliveries. Major launches—including the ES9 and ONVO L80 flagship SUVs—are set to drive future volumes. Cash and liquidity remained robust at RMB48.2 billion, supporting continued investment in innovation and infrastructure.

What Nio Does

Nio designs, manufactures, and sells smart electric vehicles while also offering power solutions like battery swapping, home charging, and related services.

Review Nio’s latest quarter to see how evolving product launches and volume recovery may shape the next phase of its growth.
