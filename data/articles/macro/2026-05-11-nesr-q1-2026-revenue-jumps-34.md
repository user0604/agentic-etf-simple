NESR Q1 2026 Revenue Jumps 34%

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 6:17 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Record revenue and profit gains driven by strong demand in core MENA markets.
Adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP) rose 85.7% year over year.
Management expects continued growth, but is focusing on improving margins and cash flow.

Oilfield services provider National Energy Services Reunited (NASDAQ:NESR) posted record revenue and a sharp profit rise as contract wins powered business in the Middle East and North Africa. The company, a major regional supplier of production and drilling services, highlighted strong results from its hydraulic fracturing services and growing activity in strategic new projects. These Q1 2026 results were announced on May 11, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter delivered meaningful financial progress but also presented challenges including margin compression and persistent free cash flow pressures.

Notable Developments
Revenue reached an all-time high, supported by expanded contracts in Saudi Arabia’s Jafurah gas development and new multi-year wins in Kuwait and North Africa.
Net income more than doubled year over year, as profitability gains stemmed from both increased service activity and improved operational scale.
The company announced a new quarterly dividend of $0.10 per share starting in Q4 2026, alongside a $50 million share buyback authorization.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management sees further growth potential as its project pipeline expands and elevated activity continues across its core markets. The company is emphasizing steady margins and improved free cash flow generation as priorities for the rest of fiscal 2026. Investors should watch for progress in working capital management and whether net debt remains in check as large contracts ramp up.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth continued to be fueled by hydraulic fracturing and related production services, with special mention of work in Saudi Arabia’s Jafurah unconventional field. These services, crucial for extracting oil and gas from tight rock formations, saw increased volumes and contract expansion this period.

Management cited ongoing regional security issues but stressed its “uninterrupted activity at scale” thanks to robust supply chains and localized service delivery.

While cash generated from operations rose year over year, free cash flow stayed negative at $5.3 million, mainly because of higher capital spending to support new business. The modest increase in net debt during the quarter points to ongoing demands on liquidity as NESR grows its contract base.

What National Energy Services Reunited Does

National Energy Services Reunited delivers oilfield services such as production, drilling, completion, and testing to customers mainly across the Middle East and North Africa.
