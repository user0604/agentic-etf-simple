ServiceTitan (NASDAQ:TTAN), a provider of cloud-based software for contractors and field service businesses, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, ServiceTitan reported total revenue of $254 million, representing 21% year-over-year growth, with subscription revenue up 23% and usage revenue up 22%. The company’s platform gross margin improved to 80%, and operating income reached $27.1 million, translating to an operating margin of 10.7%. ServiceTitan ended the quarter with approximately 10,800 active customers, up 14% from the prior year. The company’s results beat analyst consensus for both revenue and EPS, with reported EPS of $0.27 versus an estimate of $0.18. Management highlighted strong adoption of its new Max AI-driven platform and continued expansion into commercial and roofing trades.

Positives
Revenue and EPS beat expectations: ServiceTitan exceeded analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings per share, signaling continued demand for its platform despite macroeconomic uncertainty.
Strong subscription and usage growth: Subscription revenue grew 23% and usage revenue 22% year over year, showing robust customer engagement and increased adoption of add-on products.
Improved profitability and cash flow: Operating margin rose to 10.7%, and free cash flow for the quarter was $35 million, up from $11 million a year earlier. This demonstrates improved cost discipline and operating leverage.
Early success with Max and Virtual Agents: The Max platform, combining AI and automation, showed promising results in pilot deployments, including significant increases in customer revenue and efficiency. Virtual Agents, recently launched, started to contribute to usage revenue.
Negatives
Growth impact from weather and business days: Q4 growth was dampened by one fewer business day and unusually warm weather, as well as a late-quarter ice storm that temporarily slowed customer activity.
Ongoing net loss on a GAAP basis: Despite non-GAAP profitability, ServiceTitan reported a GAAP net loss of $159.9 million for fiscal 2026, reflecting continued high investment in R&D, sales, and stock-based compensation.
Capacity constraints for Max rollout: Demand for the new Max platform exceeded initial rollout capacity, leading to a phased expansion approach and potential short-term delays in broader customer adoption.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Max platform expansion and customer adoption: Investors should monitor how quickly ServiceTitan scales the Max program, including updates on onboarding efficiency, customer outcomes, and revenue contribution as capacity doubles in Q1.
Growth in usage-based and AI-driven products: Watch for further traction in Virtual Agents and other AI-powered features, as management expects usage revenue to potentially outpace gross transaction volume growth in fiscal 2027.
Commercial and roofing segment momentum: The company is targeting commercial and roofing as key growth drivers. Updates on new customer wins, product enhancements, and market penetration will be important.
Operating margin and cash flow trends: ServiceTitan has guided to a 25% incremental operating margin framework for fiscal 2027. Track whether the company maintains or improves its profitability and free cash flow generation as it invests in AI and product development.
Management’s commentary on seasonality and macro trends: Given the impact of weather and business days on recent results, any updates on Q1 demand patterns, customer retention, and macroeconomic headwinds or tailwinds will be closely watched.
