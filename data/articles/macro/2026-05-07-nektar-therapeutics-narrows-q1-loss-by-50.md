Nektar Therapeutics Narrows Q1 Loss by 50%

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 5:00 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Nektar Therapeutics reported steady progress, backed by a significantly improved cash position.
Earnings per share loss narrowed to $1.82, while revenue showed a modest year-over-year increase.
Management affirmed timelines for key Phase 3 trials, with funding secured to support its clinical ambitions.

Nektar Therapeutics (NASDAQ:NKTR) built momentum by shoring up its balance sheet and making progress in developing its lead treatment for autoimmune diseases. The biopharmaceutical company specializes in immunotherapies, with a major focus on rezpegaldesleukin for atopic dermatitis and alopecia areata. The latest earnings release came out on May 7, 2026, and covered the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter showed steady operational progress with financial discipline and clear scientific milestones, even as near-term revenue remained limited.

Notable Developments
Total cash and investments reached $731.6 million at the end of the period; this figure excludes net proceeds of approximately $351 million from a secondary offering completed after the period on April 23, 2026.
Operating costs fell by 9.3 percent as general and administrative spending dropped, while research and development increased to fund late-stage trials.
The company confirmed Phase 3 initiation for rezpegaldesleukin in atopic dermatitis by July 2026, marking a major pipeline milestone.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its prior full-year guidance, projecting royalty-based revenue between $40 million and $45 million and R&D spending from $200 million to $250 million for fiscal 2026. The most important area for investors to monitor is the launch and progress of the Phase 3 studies for rezpegaldesleukin. These late-stage clinical milestones will be decisive for the company’s future revenue and regulatory trajectory.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure was in line with earlier expectations and came solely from non-cash royalty streams, with no new product or collaboration revenue recognized. Management highlighted this stability as tracking with the forecast for the year, indicating no unexpected gains or shortfalls during the quarter.

Research and development (R&D) expense rose to support preparations for late-stage trials, especially for the lead immune therapy candidate. Management cited this increase as “primarily due to increased expenses for the development of rezpegaldesleukin as we commenced activities to support a Phase 3 program in atopic dermatitis.” General and administrative (G&A) costs, meanwhile, dropped sharply compared to the year-ago quarter, helped by a reduction in legal expenses.

Cash reserves grew substantially due to successful equity financings completed during and after the quarter. At the close of the period, cash and marketable securities stood at $731.6 million, excluding $351 million in additional funds raised in April. Adjusting for the April raise, pro forma cash exceeded $1 billion, placing the company in a strong financial position as it ramps investment into its pipeline.
