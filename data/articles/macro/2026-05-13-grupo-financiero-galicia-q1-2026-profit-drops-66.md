Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not update its full-year targets but acknowledged that return on equity of 3.2% and a 12.2% cost of risk are below prior goals. The most important area for investors to watch next is asset quality within the consumer finance unit, where delinquency remains exceptionally high and continues to drag on profits.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability declined sharply compared to last year, as net income fell by 66%, while net operating income fell by 14% year over year. Net fee income and net results from financial instruments were lower, reflecting soft demand and reduced trading activity, while loan-loss provisions stayed high.

The bank's core operations remained relatively stable. Banco Galicia posted net income of 47.65 billion pesos, a 24% year-over-year increase, but risk indicators such as the non-performing loan ratio rose to 7.7%. In contrast, the Naranja X consumer finance segment had a net loss of 18.64 billion pesos and suffered from non-performing loans of 16.9% in its credit card portfolio.

On the cost front, efforts to improve efficiency paid off as administrative expenses fell 21% year over year. Digital banking reached over 90% customer usage in Banco Galicia, with management highlighting technology investment as a key contributor to reduced spending and better margins.
