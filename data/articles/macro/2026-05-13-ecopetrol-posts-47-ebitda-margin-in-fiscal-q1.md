Key Takeaways
Ecopetrol reported resilient profitability despite declines in revenue and production.
EBITDA margin reached 47.0%, expanding 4.7 percentage points year over year.
Management maintains focus on cost control and capital discipline but highlights ongoing risks tied to production declines and the global oil environment.

Ecopetrol (NYSE:EC) saw profitability strengthen even as revenue and oil and gas output declined year over year. The leading Colombian integrated energy group, operating across oil and gas production, transport, and refining, faced lower sales and a drop in natural gas production during the period. It released first quarter 2026 results on May 12, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter featured mixed results, with strong performance in refining and downstream operations offset by weaker production upstream.

Notable Developments
EBITDA margin reached a record 47.0%, beating the company's long-term 40% target despite lower revenues.
Hydrocarbon production dropped 2.7% from the prior year, falling below the guided range due to lower natural gas and field declines.
The dividend payout was COP 121 per share, representing 55.1% of 2025 net income and payments totaling COP 4.4 trillion.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management continues to target capital discipline, operational flexibility, and strong cost controls for the remainder of fiscal 2026. The company reaffirms its focus on keeping investment plans within COP 22–27 trillion and maintaining a robust cash position. Investors should closely watch trends in hydrocarbon production, especially natural gas output and new project developments, as these will help determine if topline results stabilize or face further downside.

Quarterly highlights

The company's profitability improved as its EBITDA margin increased by 4.7 percentage points year over year, the strongest result in several years, driven by better refining margin and cost efficiency gains. Management cited "significant improvements" from maximizing refinery throughput and capturing higher-value sales in its downstream business.

Despite operational gains in refining and transport, overall hydrocarbon production slipped below the company's guidance. Total output for the period was 725.2 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day, mainly due to lower natural gas production, declines in the Guajira field, and weaker sales volume, while U.S. Permian output exceeded management's plan.

Investment remained balanced between domestic and international projects, with COP 5.3 trillion deployed in the quarter and 67% directed to Colombia. The group continued to diversify by adding grid projects in Brazil and making progress on renewables and gas regasification infrastructure as part of its energy transition push.

What Ecopetrol does

Ecopetrol is an integrated Colombian energy company engaged in the exploration, production, transport, refining, and marketing of oil, natural gas, refined products, and electricity, with a growing presence in power transmission and energy transition assets.
