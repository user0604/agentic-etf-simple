Key Takeaways
Organogenesis reported a sharp business setback as core Advanced Wound Care sales fell dramatically.
Revenue was down 58% and gross profit margin declined significantly for the quarter.
Management lowered its full-year outlook and now expects only gradual recovery through 2026.

Organogenesis (NASDAQ:ORGO) suffered a major setback as its core Advanced Wound Care sales dropped sharply and losses deepened. The regenerative medicine company, which develops products for wound care and surgical applications, released its first quarter results on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period highlighted severe financial strain and ongoing challenges tied to reimbursement policy changes.

Notable developments
Advanced Wound Care sales fell 63% to $29.5 million, reflecting the impact of recent Medicare reimbursement changes.
Operating expenses declined 6% year over year to $106.1 million, but gross profit margin dropped from 73% to 29% and could not offset the revenue decline.
The company submitted a Biologics License Application for ReNu, a key research milestone, while cash on hand decreased by $2.2 million during the quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management now expects full-year revenue between $270 million and $310 million, a significant reduction from its previous forecast. The company anticipates only gradual improvement in the next quarter, with no rapid rebound expected. Investors should watch for updates on reimbursement policy and signs that Advanced Wound Care volumes begin to stabilize.

Quarterly highlights

Sales in Advanced Wound Care, which includes products for treating chronic wounds, dropped to $29.5 million, a 63% decline from the prior-year period. This was directly linked to new Medicare reimbursement rules and confusion among healthcare providers about coverage criteria.

Profitability worsened, as gross margin fell from 73% to 29%. Operating expenses decreased slightly, but this was not enough to offset the loss of high-margin sales. Research and development spending increased, showing continued investment in future product approvals despite immediate cost pressures.

The submission of the Biologics License Application for ReNu, an injectable treatment for knee osteoarthritis, marked a major pipeline event. However, any revenue from this product remains several quarters away and did not offset the current operational disruption.

What Organogenesis does

Organogenesis develops and manufactures regenerative medicine products, mainly for advanced wound care and surgical use.
