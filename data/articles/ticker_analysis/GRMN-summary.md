GRMN Company Overview
Garmin develops and sells global positioning system technology and sensor-based devices for the aviation, marine, outdoor, and fitness markets. The company generates revenue by manufacturing specialized hardware like flight decks, sonar equipment, and adventure watches, which it pairs with proprietary software platforms and subscription-based satellite communication services. This integrated approach encourages customer retention through a hardware-software ecosystem that provides essential navigation and data analysis tools for pilots, sailors, and athletes.

Garmin Sees Fitness Growth Drive Strong Quarter

Garmin makes GPS devices and smartwatches for people who love fitness, outdoor adventures, flying, or boating. Over the last 5 years, the stock returned 80.6%, outperforming the S&P 500 by 10 percentage points.
