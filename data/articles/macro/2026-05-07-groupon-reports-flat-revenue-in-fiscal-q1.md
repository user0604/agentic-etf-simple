Groupon Reports Flat Revenue in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Groupon reported flat business activity as transformation efforts have yet to yield improvement.
Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP financial measure) fell to $12.8 million for the quarter.
Management maintained a full-year outlook but signaled the need for acceleration to meet goals.

Online local marketplace Groupon (NASDAQ:GRPN) struggled to gain momentum in its first quarter as ongoing investments in technology and platform evolution have not yet produced financial gains. The company, known for connecting consumers with local experiences and deals, released first quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter highlighted ongoing challenges in profitability and cash flow despite some growth in active customers and international revenue.

Notable Developments
Revenue was unchanged from the prior year, while global billings declined 1%, showing that new technology investments have not yet driven sales growth.
Profitability weakened as non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) dropped by 16% year over year and non-GAAP free cash flow deteriorated to negative $13.5 million.
Active customers grew 5% to 16.2 million, but unit purchases fell by 5%, indicating a dip in average buying frequency.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year targets, projecting 3% to 5% growth in billings and revenue, adjusted EBITDA between $70 million and $75 million, and at least $60 million in free cash flow. However, the first quarter’s results were below the pace needed to meet these projections. The key factor to watch is whether investments in artificial intelligence and technology can drive a measurable pickup in revenue and earnings, especially in North America.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability fell as net income swung from $8 million in profit last year to a $12.6 million loss this period. This was due in part to declining billings and increased expenses tied to ongoing platform transformation efforts.

Adjusted EBITDA dropped from $15.3 million to $12.8 million, and free cash flow worsened, indicating continued cash burn as the business invests for future growth.

International local revenue was a relative bright spot, gaining 10% year over year, while North America local revenue fell 1%. New features in the international platform, such as improved “Things to Do” experiences, drove higher customer activity; by contrast, North America continued to experience weak merchant engagement and customer purchasing activity.

What Groupon Does

Groupon is an online marketplace that connects consumers with local merchants by offering discounted deals on experiences, services, and activities in cities around the world.
