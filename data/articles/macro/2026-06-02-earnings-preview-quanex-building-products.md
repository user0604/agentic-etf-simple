Earnings Preview: Quanex Building Products

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Quanex Building Products (NYSE:NX), a supplier of window, door, and cabinet components for building product manufacturers, is expected to report its second-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

Quanex reported Q1 FY2026 results that were generally in line with management’s expectations, reflecting the typical seasonal slowdown experienced during winter. Net sales for the quarter were $409.1 million, up 2% year over year, with the increase mainly driven by favorable foreign exchange and tariff pass-throughs. The company posted an adjusted net loss of $0.01 per diluted share, which beat analyst consensus estimates of a $0.05 loss per share. Revenue also came in above the consensus estimate of $405.75 million. Adjusted EBITDA was $27.4 million, down from $38.5 million a year earlier, due to lower volumes and temporary operational costs, particularly in the Hardware Solutions segment. The company’s liquidity remained solid at $331.6 million as of Jan. 31, 2026, and management reiterated its full-year guidance, expecting revenue growth and margin expansion in the second half of the year.

Positives
Adjusted EPS and revenue beat estimates: Quanex’s adjusted net loss per share and revenue both surpassed analyst expectations, indicating better-than-anticipated performance despite a challenging environment.
Hardware Solutions plant stabilized: The operational issue at the Monterrey, Mexico facility was resolved, removing a major source of earnings drag for future quarters.
Custom Solutions segment gained share: The cabinet components business benefited from new customer wins as companies shifted production back to North America, helping offset broader market softness.
Liquidity remains strong: The company ended the quarter with over $62 million in cash and ample borrowing capacity, giving it flexibility to manage through near-term challenges.
Negatives
Adjusted EBITDA declined year over year: Lower operating leverage from reduced volumes and higher temporary costs led to a significant drop in adjusted EBITDA compared to the prior year.
Free cash flow was negative: The company used $31.5 million in free cash flow during the quarter, reflecting both seasonal working capital needs and the longer cash conversion cycle from the Tyman acquisition.
Extruded Solutions margin pressure: Despite stable revenues, margins in this segment declined due to inflation and lower volumes, highlighting ongoing cost challenges.
Market demand remains soft: Management cited weak consumer confidence and macroeconomic uncertainty as continuing headwinds for the core residential markets.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Progress on margin recovery in Hardware Solutions: Investors should look for signs that the now-stabilized Monterrey plant is contributing to improved segment margins, as management expects the prior cost headwinds to abate.
Volume trends in key segments: Watch for any improvement in volumes across Hardware, Extruded, and Custom Solutions, as management has indicated that a stronger second half depends on demand picking up from the current soft levels.
Update on new product launches and adjacent market expansion: Management has highlighted ongoing initiatives in Extruded and Custom Solutions, including new product development and entry into adjacent markets. Progress here could support future growth and margin gains.
Cash flow and leverage trajectory: The company expects to remain a net borrower through the first half, but aims to reduce net leverage to near 2.0 times by year-end. Investors should monitor cash generation and debt repayment progress.
Management’s outlook and guidance for the second half: With Q2 seen as another challenging quarter, any updates to full-year guidance or commentary on market conditions will be important for assessing the timing and strength of the expected recovery.
