Gravity Posts 33% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q1

May 8, 2026, 6:21 AM

Key Takeaways
Gravity posted a significant rebound driven by strong game launches and improved profitability.
Earnings per share rose 33.4% while revenue increased 17.8% year over year.
Management is focused on new game releases but highlights risks tied to high concentration in key game titles.

Gravity (NASDAQ:GRVY) delivered a major rebound as new game launches powered growth and higher profits across its portfolio. The online gaming company, best known for the Ragnarok series, expanded in key Asian markets and benefited from improved cost control during the period ended March 31, 2026 (reported May 8, 2026). Overall, the quarter featured strong momentum, with robust cash flows and a sharp rise in both revenue and net profit.

The quarter was marked by successful launches and disciplined spending, though the company continues to face risks from high concentration in key game titles.

Notable developments
Mobile game revenue rose 15.0% compared to the prior year, driven by launches in Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Southeast Asia.
Operating expenses fell 17.6%, mainly due to reduced advertising and research spending.
Cash and short-term financial assets reached $401.5 million, supporting a strong liquidity position.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue specific forecasts for the next quarter or the full year. The upcoming pipeline features several launches for the Ragnarok series in new regions and across multiple platforms. Investors should monitor the performance and engagement level of these new titles, as the company’s growth remains tied to its core game brands.

Quarterly highlights

The period saw major gains in the mobile gaming category, driven by the introduction of Ragnarok: The New World in Taiwan and increases in existing titles across Asia. These launches led to an immediate revenue boost and show the importance of regional launches to company success.

Cost discipline supported profitability, as lower advertising and research and development expenses helped offset a large increase in the cost of revenue. This balance between spending and revenue growth contributed to the improved operating and net profit figures for the quarter.

The company maintained its focus on brand and technology expansion, releasing the official Ragnarok soundtrack on streaming platforms and preparing for launches of new games including Ragnarok Online 3. While these efforts broaden revenue potential, the impact of non-game sources remains limited for now.

What Gravity does

Gravity develops and publishes online and mobile games, most notably the Ragnarok franchise, focusing on Asian markets and expanding into global regions.
