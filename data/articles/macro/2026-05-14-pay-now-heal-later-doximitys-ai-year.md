Pay Now, Heal Later: Doximity's AI Year

This is a stock that demands conviction and time.

Sanmeet Deo, CFA

May 14, 2026, 9:21 AM

Comments
9.3

Doximity (NYSE: DOCS) just posted a genuine beat -- Q4 revenue of $145 million grew 5% year over year and topped guidance, while free cash flow hit a record $107 million, the company's first-ever nine-digit quarter. So why is the stock cratering 20% in pre-market?

The answer is entirely in the guidance. Management projected full-year fiscal 2027 revenue of $664 million to $676 million, implying just 4% growth at the midpoint, roughly $50 million below what Wall Street expected. EBITDA margins are guided to compress from 55% to 49%, stock-based compensation is heading toward the low-20s as a percent of revenue, and management was explicit: "minimal AI revenue contribution this fiscal year."

That last point is the crux. The bull case for Doximity rests on its transformation into the largest physician AI platform. The engagement proof points are genuinely staggering -- 800,000 quarterly active workflow prescribers up 30% year over year, nearly half now using AI tools, and 140 health systems signed up for the Clinical AI Suite in just two quarters. Top-20 pharma companies are reportedly mandating 10%–20% AI budget allocations. A multibillion-dollar AI search total addressable market may be real. The physician network moat is actually widening.

The bear case is that "pay now, collect later" is a thesis that requires patience the market doesn't have. Revenue growth has decelerated from 25% to 4% in twelve months. The pharma ad market is soft with shorter client commitments reducing visibility. And meaningful AI monetization won't arrive until the fiscal back half at the earliest. This is a stock that demands conviction and time.
