Notable developments
Digital revenue made up 20.9% of total revenue in the quarter, up from 7.4% last year, reflecting rapid scaling of new platforms.
EBITDA margin moderated to 53.5%, down 1.4 percentage points, as faster digital growth added lower-margin revenue to the mix.
The mobile subscriber base declined 3.0% to 22 million, but multiplay users increased 31.6% to 8.1 million, now accounting for nearly 40% of active customers.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its commitment to investing in network resilience, digital platform scaling, and targeted acquisitions. While the company pointed to opportunities in multiplay growth and new digital services, it also warned about ongoing risks from the war in Ukraine, regulatory changes, and potential profitability pressure as digital services expand. Investors should closely monitor shifts in segment margins, digital uptake, and the impact of any macro or regulatory developments.

Quarterly highlights

Digital services have become central to the company’s growth, with digital revenue up more than 250% year over year. Acquisitions such as the Uklon ride-hailing platform and Tabletki online pharmacy contributed strongly, with Uklon generating $32.9 million in revenue and Tabletki accounting for $5.3 million this quarter alone.

Despite a decline in total mobile subscribers as some customers dropped secondary SIM cards, multiplay offers saw sharp growth. Multiplay revenue rose 39.3%, and these bundled services now account for a much larger share of monthly active users.

Capital expenditure reached $67 million, up 32%, reflecting ongoing investment in network infrastructure and backup systems. Kyivstar rolled out thousands of generators and new batteries to meet regulatory standards for energy resilience.

What Kyivstar Group does

Kyivstar Group is a telecommunications and digital services company offering mobile, broadband, digital entertainment, cloud, and enterprise solutions, mainly in Ukraine.
