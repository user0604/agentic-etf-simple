Notable Developments
Licensing and related revenues reached a three-year high, standing at $17.8 million, boosted by 14 new agreements and strong edge AI demand.
Royalty revenues were flat compared to last year, with Wi-Fi and cellular IoT strength offsetting continued softness in smartphone royalties.
Expenses rose 13%, notably in research and development, contributing to a wider GAAP net loss even as cash and marketable securities remained solid.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted ongoing pipeline strength in edge AI, Wi-Fi, and new connectivity markets, stating the period aligned with its revenue and expense targets. No updated full-year revenue or profit guidance was provided. Investors should focus on the conversion of recent licensing wins into royalties, especially as smartphone markets remain weak and as R&D investment rises.

Quarterly Highlights

Licensing activity set a recent record, with 14 agreements signed and over 20% of licensing revenue now tied to artificial intelligence, fueling robust segment growth. These deals included major customer wins for Bluetooth High Data Throughput and expanded engagements for 5G Non-Terrestrial Networks and Ultra-Wideband technology.

Royalty revenue held steady at $9.2 million, as gains in Wi-Fi, cellular Internet-of-Things (IoT), and automotive applications compensated for headwinds in the smartphone sector. This shift demonstrates increased exposure to new end markets, balancing cyclicality in legacy categories.

Operating expenses climbed as research and development spending reached $19.8 million, up from $17.6 million a year ago, reflecting continued investment in technology innovation. Despite solid liquidity -- with $21.4 million in cash and $194.3 million in marketable securities by quarter-end -- cash use increased, and GAAP net loss widened to $4.5 million from $3.3 million.

What Ceva Does

Ceva supplies wireless connectivity, sensing, and artificial intelligence IP used by chipmakers and device makers in products ranging from smartphones to connected cars and smart home devices.
