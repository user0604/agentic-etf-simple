Key Takeaways
Global Partners delivered a strong quarter with profit nearly quadrupling over last year. Earnings per share rose sharply to $1.85, and revenue was up year over year. Management kept the distribution steady and did not issue new financial guidance.

Fuel supplier Global Partners (NYSE:GLP) posted a notably strong performance, driven by sharply higher profitability and expanded product margins across its main business lines. The integrated energy infrastructure operator manages a large network of terminals and fuel stations focused in the Northeast and Gulf Coast. In its most recent release on May 8, 2026, reporting for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, the company showed clear progress in margin improvement, offset by higher expenses and a larger working capital requirement. Overall, the period marked robust growth, especially in Wholesale, but challenges remain in cost control and station volumes.

Notable Developments
Net income surged to $70.1 million, with fuel margin gains most pronounced in the Wholesale segment, which saw its product margin rise to $154.1 million.
Selling, general and administrative expenses rose by $25.7 million, an increase of nearly 35%, eating into some profitability improvement.
The quarterly distribution was maintained at $0.7650 per unit, aligning with the company's stated distribution policies from prior periods.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its focus on leveraging Global’s integrated logistics network and continuing infrastructure investment, but did not provide quantitative guidance for future quarters. The sharp increase in working capital needs and cost inflation underscore the importance of monitoring expense trends and cash flow in upcoming periods.

Quarterly Highlights

The Wholesale segment was the main growth driver, as its product margin jumped over 60% year-over-year thanks to favorable market conditions for gasoline and blendstocks. Compared to last year, Wholesale’s total volume climbed to 1.6 billion gallons, reflecting strong demand through the company’s terminal and supply platform.

Gasoline Distribution and Station Operations, which includes both distribution and retail stations, saw its product margin rise moderately, but segment volume slipped by 7%, raising questions about underlying retail trends even as per-gallon margins improved.

Operating costs remained stable but other expenses, such as SG&A, increased significantly, partially due to expanded investment in data and network management mentioned in prior reports. The company also reported higher levels of accounts receivable and inventory, both of which contributed to a significant use of operating cash during the quarter.

What Global Partners Does

Global Partners owns and operates a large U.S. energy infrastructure network, distributing gasoline, distillates, and renewable fuels to retailers, wholesalers, and commercial clients while running a significant portfolio of fuel stations and convenience stores.
