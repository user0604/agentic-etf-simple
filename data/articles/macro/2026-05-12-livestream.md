Dream Finder’s Founder and CEO Patrick Zalupski called out Beazer’s suboptimal capital allocation strategy and lack of scale as solid reasons for the deal. DFH has proven to be a good home for the businesses they acquire. Dream Finders’ asset light business model is built for the real estate cycles. The pressure is now on Beazer’s board to respond to this very solid offer.
