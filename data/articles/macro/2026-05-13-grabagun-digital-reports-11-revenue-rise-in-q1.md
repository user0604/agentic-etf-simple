Notable developments
Firearms sales increased 10.5% year over year to $21.7 million, outpacing the 1.6% increase in industry background checks.
Gross margin improved to 10.7%, but adjusted EBITDA fell to a loss of $2.0 million due to higher stock-based compensation and public company expenses.
PEW Logistics launched in January and contributed $0.1 million in new service revenue, with two firearms manufacturers already onboarded as partners.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains positive about prospects for growth and regulatory readiness but did not provide detailed financial guidance for upcoming periods. The company will focus on expanding PEW Logistics, improving operational efficiency, and monitoring regulatory changes that could enable digital firearm transfer and delivery. Investors should watch for further progress on profitability and the pace of new B2B partnerships for PEW Logistics.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter’s revenue growth was driven by both firearms and non-firearms products, with firearms sales growing significantly faster than industry-wide unit checks, supporting evidence of ongoing market share gains. The launch of the PEW Logistics service introduced a new business-to-business channel, with $0.1 million in service revenue and two early manufacturing partners brought on board.

Operating expenses more than doubled year over year, largely due to higher headcount, new costs associated with being a public company, and increased stock-based compensation, which contributed to negative operating income and adjusted EBITDA performance despite improved gross profit margins.

Mobile commerce continued to be essential, making up 64% of revenue and 70% of transactions, reflecting the company’s mobile-first digital strategy. Customer lifetime value increased by 4.2% to $906, and website traffic was up 12.6%, suggesting effectiveness of digital marketing and engagement tools. Share repurchases totaled $2.4 million during the quarter, with $8.7 million remaining authorized, partially offsetting cash outflows amid otherwise modest cash burn.

While the company’s regulatory management system and digital platform position it well for possible changes in firearm transfer laws, profitability will depend on managing expenses, scaling new service offerings within PEW Logistics, and turning top-line growth into consistent earnings. Cash and liquidity remain strong at period end, helping support planned growth and technology investment.

What GrabAGun Digital does

GrabAGun Digital operates an online platform for firearms, ammunition, and sporting goods, complemented by technology-driven logistics and compliance solutions for manufacturers and retailers.
