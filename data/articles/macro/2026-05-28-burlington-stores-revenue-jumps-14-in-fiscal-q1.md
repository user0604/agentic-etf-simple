Key Takeaways
Sales and earnings exceeded company guidance, bolstered by strong comparable sales and ongoing store expansion efforts.
Revenue rose 14% in the quarter compared to the prior year.

Burlington Stores (NYSE:BURL) delivered a clear business win in its latest quarter, with results surpassing its own guidance as store traffic and expansion efforts paid off. The leading off-price retailer, known for discounted branded apparel and home goods, reported higher sales and profits as it continued to open new locations and attract value-focused shoppers. Reporting fiscal first quarter results on May 28, 2026, the company posted strong growth across both revenue and earnings compared to the same period last year. Overall, the quarter reflected solid operational progress and strategic momentum.

Notable developments
Comparable store sales increased 6% year over year, exceeding management's prior guidance and supporting stronger total sales.
The company operated 1,242 stores as of the end of the first quarter of fiscal 2026, up from 1,212 at the end of fiscal 2025.
Gross margin improved by 0.3 percentage points to 44.1%, driven by merchandise margin gains and supply chain productivity.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year outlook, now expecting sales growth of 9% to 11%, adjusted earnings per share between $11.45 and $11.80, and 115 net new store openings. In the coming quarters, investors should watch the pace of inventory growth and the company’s ability to manage higher sourcing costs as it continues rapid expansion.

Quarterly highlights

Comparable store sales, which track performance at locations open at least one year, rose 6%, showing steady consumer demand for discounted branded products. This result was higher than management’s initial guidance for the quarter.

The company continued its expansion strategy, adding 30 net new stores and moving closer to its long-term goal of 2,000 total locations. Investments in distribution centers and supply chain upgrades supported this store growth.

Profitability improved as the gross margin rate increased to 44.1%. The company achieved this while managing higher product and sourcing costs, balancing inventory investments with disciplined cost controls.

What Burlington Stores does

Burlington Stores operates a national chain of off-price retail stores, offering discounted branded apparel and home merchandise to consumers seeking value.
