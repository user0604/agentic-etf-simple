TTEC Holdings Posts 7% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

May 7, 2026, 8:23 PM

Key Takeaways
First quarter results disappointed, as revenues and earnings declined across both main business segments.
Non-GAAP earnings per share dropped nearly 50% from the prior year’s period.
Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, expecting improvement in the following quarters.

TTEC Holdings (NASDAQ:TTEC) reported a challenging start to the year, as both sales and profits moved lower compared to the same period last year. The global provider of technology-enabled customer experience solutions faced declines in its primary business segments for the first quarter, with the update released on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter marked a setback, although management kept its annual outlook unchanged and stressed that several challenges were temporary.

Notable Developments
Consolidated revenue dropped 7.1% compared to the prior year, reflecting declines in both the Engage and Digital segments.
Total non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, a measure of profitability before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization, fell nearly 19% year over year.
Free cash flow, which measures cash generated after expenses and investments, rose over 30% from last year’s figure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its full-year 2026 revenue and profit guidance, citing timing factors behind the weak start and expecting a stronger performance later in the year. It projects revenue between $2,005 million and $2,055 million and aims for a higher adjusted EBITDA margin, supported by new client contracts and further adoption of artificial intelligence-powered solutions. Investors should monitor segment-level sales and profitability in the coming quarters, as well as evidence of progress in digital services and AI-driven offerings.

Quarterly Highlights

Both main operating segments saw lower revenue this period, with the Engage division (focused on technology-enabled customer care and fraud prevention) declining 7.5% and the Digital segment (specializing in AI-enabled solutions) falling 5.7% year over year. Profit margins also declined in both business areas, with non-GAAP operating margin slipping to 6.4%, down 1.4 percentage points from the prior year.

Despite these declines, TTEC improved its free cash flow, generating $21.1 million compared to $16.2 million last year, and made progress reducing net debt from $881.4 million to $802.7 million. The company attributed the brighter cash performance to tighter cost controls and operational changes, even as revenue fell.

Management pointed to several new customer wins in artificial intelligence customer experience solutions and emphasized a growing pipeline of future business. However, actual revenue contributions from these new deals did not offset declines elsewhere in the business during the quarter, and the company continues to face execution challenges as it shifts to more AI-powered services.

What TTEC Holdings Does

TTEC Holdings provides customer experience solutions combining advanced technology, analytics, and outsourced services to help large organizations interact more effectively with their customers.
