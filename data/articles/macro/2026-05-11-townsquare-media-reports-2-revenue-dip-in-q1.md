Townsquare Media Reports 2% Revenue Dip in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 6:16 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Townsquare Media reported mixed results as digital gains were offset by continued broadcast declines.
Adjusted EBITDA fell 9.7% from the prior year as total revenue slipped 1.9%.
Management reaffirmed its guidance for fiscal 2026 and maintains a dividend, signaling ongoing confidence.

Digital advertising growth provided a bright spot for Townsquare Media (NYSE:TSQ) in a challenging first quarter, but legacy broadcast and subscription segments continued to decline. The multi-platform local media company, focused on digital transformation outside the top 50 U.S. markets, issued its results for the quarter ending March 31, 2026, on May 11, 2026. Overall, digital strategy progress was evident, yet profit margins came under pressure and debt remained high.

Notable Developments
Digital advertising made up 59% of total revenue, its highest share yet, but overall digital segment profit slipped 4.8%.
Subscription digital marketing revenues dropped 7.9%, and the segment achieved a 34% profit margin.
The declared quarterly dividend remained at $0.20 per share despite a net loss per share on an adjusted basis and high leverage.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed both its second-quarter and full fiscal 2026 outlook, projecting continued digital growth and emphasizing the importance of controlling costs and reducing leverage. It expects net revenue of $420–$440 million and adjusted EBITDA of $87–$93 million for the full year. Investors should closely monitor digital profit margins and the sustainability of the dividend, given elevated debt and tightening cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

Digital advertising, driven mainly by the Townsquare Ignite platform, saw revenue climb nearly 7% from the prior year, helping digital reach a record 59% of company-wide revenue. However, digital segment profit fell as expenses outpaced the gains, pointing to cost pressures in the fast-growing business line.

Broadcast advertising continued to decline, with revenue falling 6.6% and segment profit dropping even faster. This ongoing weakness increases the urgency for Townsquare’s digital transition and calls attention to ongoing declines in traditional media channels.

Cash and liquidity remained tight as operating cash flow was positive, but total debt stood at $457.5 million. Despite this, the company maintained and slightly increased its quarterly dividend, representing a yield near 12% as of the end of the quarter.

What Townsquare Media Does

Townsquare Media operates a portfolio of local radio stations, digital marketing solutions, and online content platforms aimed at small businesses and local audiences primarily outside the 50 largest U.S. markets.
