Key Takeaways
Assembly Biosciences delivered steady operational progress but saw lower collaboration revenue as major studies wound down.
Earnings per share loss narrowed from prior year, reflecting a higher share count despite continued net losses.

Assembly Biosciences (NASDAQ:ASMB) posted solid clinical execution but experienced slower revenue as its Gilead research partnership advanced toward completion. The clinical-stage biotechnology company focuses on developing antiviral medicines for diseases with significant unmet needs. The company released its first quarter financial results on May 7, 2026 for the three months ended March 31. Overall, the period showed diligent cost controls and ongoing pipeline work, but lacked near-term revenue growth or new late-stage data.

Notable Developments
Collaboration revenue decreased as services for the Gilead partnership approached completion, dropping over $1 million year over year for the quarter.
Research and development expenses stayed stable, as higher internal costs were offset by lower external spending after ending certain studies.
Strong liquidity remained a highlight, with $226.6 million in cash and investments projected to fund all planned operations into 2028.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects the current cash position to sustain the business for at least two more years while it awaits key developments, including Gilead’s decision on the U.S. profit-sharing option for the herpesvirus program and Phase 2 initiation for the HDV therapy. Investors should closely watch for partnership updates and any new clinical data in mid-to-late 2026 as potential catalysts.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s revenue decline was anticipated as the Gilead partnership entered its next stage, providing less collaborative income but maintaining a flow of milestone funding for research on helicase-primase inhibitor therapies targeting herpesvirus.

Costs for research and development held steady, as internal investments in new discovery programs balanced out reduced external expenses from completed trials. General and administrative expenses increased slightly due to greater stock-based compensation tied to performance milestones.

The company’s cash and securities position ended the quarter healthy at $226.6 million, giving management flexibility to pursue clinical programs and to make strategic decisions as collaboration milestones approach.

What Assembly Biosciences Does

Assembly Biosciences is a biotechnology company that develops investigational antiviral therapies for viral diseases including herpesvirus, hepatitis delta virus, and hepatitis B virus.
