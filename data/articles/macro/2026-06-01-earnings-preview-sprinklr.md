Earnings Preview: Sprinklr

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Sprinklr (NYSE:CXM), a provider of software that helps large companies manage customer interactions across digital channels, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Sprinklr reported total revenue of $220.6 million, representing 9% growth year over year. Subscription revenue reached $193.4 million, up 6% from the prior year, and professional services revenue came in at $27.1 million. The company delivered non-GAAP operating income of $37.7 million, with a 17% non-GAAP operating margin. Free cash flow for the quarter was $15.9 million. These results came ahead of analyst expectations, and the quarter was generally viewed as a positive surprise by the market.

Sprinklr’s net dollar expansion rate for subscription revenue was 103%, showing a slight sequential improvement. The company ended the quarter with 141 customers contributing at least $1 million in annual subscription revenue, which was down from the previous quarter, though the average revenue per customer in this group increased to over $3 million. Renewal rates improved, reaching their highest level in the past year. The company also announced a new $200 million share repurchase program, including a $125 million accelerated buyback to be completed in the coming year.

Positives
Revenue and profit beat expectations: Both total revenue and non-GAAP operating income exceeded analyst estimates, signaling stronger demand and operational discipline.
Improved renewal rates: Renewal rates reached their best level in over a year, indicating better customer retention and satisfaction.
Strong free cash flow: Free cash flow of $15.9 million in Q4 and $142 million for the full year reflect improved collections and cost management.
AI product adoption accelerating: Annual recurring revenue from Sprinklr’s generative AI-native Service products grew 50% year over year, showing robust enterprise interest in AI-driven solutions.
Negatives
Decline in large customer count: The number of customers contributing at least $1 million in subscription revenue fell by four from the previous quarter, which may signal challenges in upselling or retaining the largest clients.
Elevated churn earlier in the year: Higher customer churn, especially in the first half of FY2026, weighed on overall growth and required focused recovery efforts.
Professional services margin remains thin: Professional services gross margin was just 1%, reflecting ongoing investments and the low-margin nature of this business line.
Macroeconomic and geopolitical headwinds: The company faced disruptions in the Middle East due to regional conflict, which impacted business activities and posed risks to customer data and demand in that geography.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Subscription revenue growth and customer retention: Watch for continued improvement in renewal rates and whether subscription revenue meets the guided $193 million to $194 million range, as this is key for long-term growth.
Progress on AI product adoption: Monitor updates on the adoption and expansion of Sprinklr’s AI-driven solutions, especially as generative AI becomes a larger part of customer contracts and revenue.
Impact of share repurchase program: Look for commentary on the execution of the new $200 million share buyback, including the $125 million accelerated repurchase, and its effect on share count and earnings per share.
Professional services trends: Pay attention to the expected step down in professional services revenue and margin, as the company transitions away from large implementation projects and increases reliance on partners for service delivery.
Execution of transformation initiatives: Assess management’s progress in the second phase of its transformation plan, including cost structure optimization, go-to-market changes, and the "Bear Hug" customer engagement program, as these are intended to set up Sprinklr for future acceleration and scale.
