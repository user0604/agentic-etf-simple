Key Takeaways

Management forecasted cautious revenue growth for the next fiscal year due to ongoing uncertainty in clean energy markets.

Sono-Tek (NASDAQ:SOTK) delivered higher profits and improved margins in its latest quarter, supported by a focus on premium system sales and rising U.S. orders. The precision ultrasonic coating technology provider reported results for the quarter ended February 28, 2026, in a release dated May 28, 2026. The period reflected continued profitability momentum even as some business segments posted softer sales.

Overall, the quarter showed a favorable mix of improved profitability and robust backlog, but future revenue growth remains sensitive to shifts in clean energy demand and geographic sales trends.

Notable Developments
Gross profit climbed 15% year over year, reaching $2.79 million, helped by a higher mix of premium, high-average selling price systems and strong U.S. margin contribution.
Net income grew sharply, up 70% to $557,000, driven by improved profitability and continued cost discipline.
Customer deposits and year-end backlog both increased, supporting a near-record $9.12 million backlog, which points to solid future demand.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management anticipates modest sales growth for fiscal 2027, guided by strength in medical systems and ongoing demand for high-value equipment. However, it cautioned that revenue gains may be limited given uncertainty in the clean energy sector and the timing of complex, high-value orders. Investors should watch sales trends in the clean energy segment and overall geographic diversification as potential sources of variability in future results.

Quarterly Highlights

The company achieved its eighth consecutive quarter of sales exceeding $5 million and marked a sixteenth straight year of profitability, highlighting operational stability. Growth in medical device applications— particularly balloon catheter and stent coating systems—helped medical sales rise 54% for the year, making it a strong contributor this quarter as well.

Profitability benefited from a product sales mix skewed toward higher-value, production-scale systems and from proportionally more revenue in the U.S, where margins are higher due to lower international business costs. Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $14.8 million at year-end, with no outstanding debt, providing the company with notable financial flexibility for ongoing investment.

Despite strong North American sales (up 12% for the year), international sales growth remained a challenge. Sales in Europe, Asia Pacific, and Latin America declined, and alternative energy sector sales dropped because of changes in government policy, offset only partially by gains in other end markets.

What Sono-Tek Does

Sono-Tek designs and manufactures ultrasonic coating equipment used to deposit fine films and coatings in medical devices, electronics, alternative energy, and other high-precision industrial applications.
