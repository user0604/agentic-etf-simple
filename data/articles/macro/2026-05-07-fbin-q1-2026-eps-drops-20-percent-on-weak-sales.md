Key Takeaways
Business win/loss: The company faced a tough period, lowering its annual outlook as sales and profitability declined.
Performance: Earnings per share before charges dropped 19.7% compared to the same quarter last year.
Outlook: Management cut full-year sales, earnings, and free cash flow guidance due to higher costs and weaker demand.

Home and security products producer Fortune Brands Innovations (NYSE:FBIN) delivered a difficult quarter as it reduced its full-year guidance and reported lower sales and earnings. The firm is a leading maker of branded water, outdoors, and security products, with a portfolio that includes names like Moen, House of Rohl, and Master Lock. In results released May 7, 2026, for its first quarter of fiscal 2026, it cited rising costs and a more cautious consumer environment as key factors hurting profitability. Overall, the quarter saw broad-based margin declines and a step down in performance versus both the previous year and the company’s own forecasts.

Notable Developments
Revenue for the quarter slipped to just over $1.01 billion as demand softened in core water, outdoors, and security businesses.
Operating margin, a measure of profitability as a percent of sales, dropped by 3.4 percentage points compared to last year, reflecting higher production costs and a less favorable sales mix.
Full-year profit and sales forecasts were lowered; management now expects "was down low single digits" rather than flat to slightly higher.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership revised its outlook, forecasting lower sales and reduced earnings for the rest of fiscal 2026. The company cited higher commodity costs and weaker remodeling and construction markets as reasons for the more cautious stance.

Quarterly Highlights

Top-line performance showed weakness across core business segments. Water Innovations, the company’s largest division featuring faucets and connected home water products, saw sales dip less than 1%, with a larger drop in operating margin due to inflation in manufacturing and limited ability to raise prices. Outdoors and Security divisions both posted declines in sales, with the Outdoors segment impacted by a facility fire and softer demand that drove a 3.4% decrease in net sales.

The company’s focus on key brands such as Moen and House of Rohl helped limit the decline in the Water Innovations segment outside of China, but this resilience was not enough to offset overall weakness. Management also highlighted continued investment in product innovation, especially in digital and sustainability features, though near-term financial benefit has not been fully realized due to slow market conditions.

Free cash flow, which measures cash generated after operations and investments, was negative in the quarter. This is partly typical for the season but reflects deeper pressure on profits and inventory levels compared to early last year. Net debt rose to $2.5 billion, with leverage increasing, though liquidity remains solid with $223 million in cash and additional borrowing capacity.

What Fortune Brands Innovations Does

Fortune Brands Innovations manufactures and sells branded products for residential water management, outdoor living, and security, with a growing emphasis on digital and sustainable solutions.
