CI&T Posts 23% Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
CI&T delivered a strong quarter with accelerating revenue growth but continued margin pressures.
Revenue grew 23.2% year over year.
Management raised full-year revenue guidance but kept margin targets unchanged, with cash flow and profit margins remaining key watch points.

Technology services provider CI&T (NYSE:CINT) posted strong sales gains as its artificial intelligence-led transformation picked up speed, though the benefits did not fully reach profit margins. The global digital transformation specialist reported results for the first quarter (released May 11, 2026) that highlighted growth across major markets and industry verticals, balanced by higher costs and pressured margins. Overall, the quarter showcased robust commercial momentum, but margin compression and limited cash generation indicate challenges on the path to scalable profitability.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 23.2% year over year, outpacing management’s minimum guidance of $134.7 million for the quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA margin fell by 2.4 percentage points, with cost growth outpacing sales gains due to increased payroll taxes and investment in the CI&T Flow AI platform.
Twenty percent of new sales in the period used value-based pricing tied to outcomes, a pivotal change in the company's commercial approach.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management lifted its full-year revenue guidance to a new range of $555.8 million to $575.3 million, now targeting organic growth of 13.5% to 17.5%. It maintained its outlook for adjusted EBITDA margin at 17% to 19%, but actual margins will require improved cost discipline or efficiencies from recent digital investments. Investors should focus on whether the rollout of new pricing models and AI-driven services can translate into clear cash flow and margin improvements in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales gains this period were both broad and deep, with double-digit revenue growth in nearly all regions and industries except consumer goods, which remained steady. Growth in Latin America was particularly strong, registering a 33% increase compared to the prior year, while North America and new markets contributed 16% and 11% growth, respectively.

The introduction and adoption of outcome-based pricing are reshaping how CI&T captures value, with 20% of new business already structured on these models. Management noted that the monetization of artificial intelligence tools and its proprietary CI&T Flow platform is expected to support margin recovery in future periods, though current results do not show a significant impact yet.

Total headcount reached 8,015, up 8.3%, with a 13.3% rise in employees dedicated to building AI-enabled solutions ("AI-builders"). While these investments help sustain double-digit organic revenue growth, expenses on payroll, taxes, and platform development drove profits and operating cash flow lower relative to the pace of topline expansion.

What CI&T Does

CI&T delivers technology consulting and custom software development services, with a special focus on artificial intelligence and digital transformation for enterprise clients worldwide.
