Key Takeaways
Identiv’s Q1 2026 results benefited from the early fulfillment of a major customer order.
Revenue climbed nearly 40% year over year, and net losses narrowed.
Management guided for lower revenue in Q2 due to order timing effects.

Identiv (NASDAQ:INVE) delivered an early win in the first quarter, landing larger sales thanks to a key customer pulling forward its 2026 order. The leading provider of RFID (radio-frequency identification) and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) solutions for Internet of Things applications saw effects from this timing shift in results released on May 13, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter saw operational progress but also raised questions about sales consistency for the rest of the year.

Notable developments
Revenue reached $7.4 million, substantially boosted by a large customer’s full-year order being placed in the first quarter.
Non-GAAP gross margin jumped to 23.8%, up 13.0 percentage points, as production moved fully to Thailand and old Singapore costs dropped out.
Cash on hand ended at $124.5 million, with inventory rising to $8.9 million as the company ramped up for major projects.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects second quarter revenue to fall to $5.4 million to $6.0 million, citing the lack of repeat early orders after the large deal in the first quarter. Investors should pay close attention to whether new customer wins and ongoing projects can support steady growth as one-time benefits subside.

Quarterly highlights

The period’s revenue was markedly higher than a year ago, but management explained this bump was primarily due to a customer advancing its annual order into the first quarter. This means the strong sales figure might not be sustained in following quarters.

Profitability improved, with non-GAAP gross margin nearly doubling compared to the prior year. This uptick was mainly due to lower production costs after moving all manufacturing to Thailand and eliminating expense overhang from the former Singapore facility.

Cash reserves remained solid, but inventory grew as Identiv prepared for more large-scale projects. Management noted inventory growth reflected positioning for key customers.

What Identiv does

Identiv creates RFID and Bluetooth-enabled technology that brings digital identity and tracking capabilities to everyday objects and packaging in sectors such as healthcare, supply chain, and consumer goods.
