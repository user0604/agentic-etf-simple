Battalion Oil (NYSEMKT:BATL) improved operational reliability and increased production, but reported a wider net loss for the quarter. Notable developments
Average daily production reached 12,578 barrels of oil equivalent per day, up 5.7% year over year.
Unit costs improved, with lease operating expense per barrel down 7.8% to $8.92, and total operating cost per unit also falling considerably.
Net debt fell to $108.3 million from $180.2 million, supported by proceeds from an asset sale and a private equity placement.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects to begin “cube”-style drilling and launch a new oil pipeline for the Monument Draw area in the coming quarters, aiming for further cost savings and production gains. The key factor for investors will be the company’s ability to execute on infrastructure and capital-raising plans to improve profitability and manage debt. Progress on these initiatives will be important for future financial stability.

Quarterly highlights

Production growth was driven by development in Monument Draw, with recent acquisitions adding 7,090 net acres to the company’s portfolio. While total volumes increased, the percentage of oil in the production mix slipped to 47%, which slightly lowered average realized prices and revenue per unit produced.

Cost management showed clear results. Lease operating expense and other unit costs dropped compared to the prior year, helped by a new long-term midstream partnership and pipeline investments that are expected to save up to $6 million per year once operational. General and administrative expense, which measures overhead, remained essentially flat after adjusting for non-recurring items.

Liquidity improved significantly. The company reduced net debt by about $72 million during the quarter, ending with $54.3 million in available cash and liquidity. Battalion Oil used proceeds from its West Quito asset sale and an equity raise to strengthen its balance sheet, supporting new development and infrastructure projects.

What Battalion Oil does

Battalion Oil is an independent oil and gas producer with core operations and development focused on liquids-rich acreage in the Delaware Basin in West Texas.
