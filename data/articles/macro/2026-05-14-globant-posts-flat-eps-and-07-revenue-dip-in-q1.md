Key Takeaways
Revenue surpassed the top end of company guidance despite stalling overall client expansion. EPS remained flat and revenue slipped 0.7% year over year. Management forecast continues to point to only slight growth ahead, with margins compressed.

AI services specialist Globant (NYSE:GLOB) delivered revenue above its forecast range as demand strengthened in core AI-driven offerings, but broader business metrics show mixed signals. The company focuses on digital transformation and artificial intelligence consulting and platform solutions for enterprises worldwide. In its earnings release dated May 14, 2026, covering the first quarter, it reported progress in scaling AI subscriptions while traditional service demand and margins remain under pressure. Overall, the quarter highlighted stable execution with continued margin caution and slowing customer expansion.

Notable Developments
Q1 revenue reached $607.1 million, slightly down 0.7% from the prior year, but above the $604 million high end of the company’s outlook.
Annual recurring revenue from the AI Pods subscription service surged to $32.8 million, up from $20.6 million three months earlier.
Globant ended the quarter with 28,510 employees, a decrease of 8.3% from the previous year, signaling ongoing organizational streamlining and efficiency efforts.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects second-quarter revenue between $610 million and $616 million, which would represent revenue staying nearly flat year over year. Full-year guidance is unchanged from last quarter, calling for up to 2.2% growth and stable operating margins. Investors will want to watch whether momentum in AI-based products can eventually counter the drag from shrinking legacy business and declining large-client count.

Quarterly Highlights

The AI Pods business continued to scale, with annual recurring revenue for these subscription-based, artificial intelligence-powered solutions rising to $32.8 million. This advance is important as management targets $60 million to $100 million in AI Pods annual revenue by year-end.

Operating margins compressed for another quarter, with the adjusted operating margin falling 0.7 percentage points compared to a year ago, partly due to currency pressures and increased payroll costs in key regions. Despite that, free cash flow remained positive and the company launched a new $125 million share repurchase plan.

Globant’s total headcount decreased for the fourth consecutive quarter and the number of customers generating over $1 million of annual revenue also shrank.

What Globant Does

Globant provides technology consulting and digital transformation services, with a particular focus on artificial intelligence, data analytics, and cloud-based solutions for global enterprises.
