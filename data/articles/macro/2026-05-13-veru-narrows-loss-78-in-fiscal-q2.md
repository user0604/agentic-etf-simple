Veru Narrows Loss 78% in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 7:13 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Veru delivered a narrower loss driven by continued progress in its clinical pipeline and tighter cost controls.
Net loss per share improved to $(0.12), while R&D and administrative expenses both dropped.
Management sees current cash funding operations through the next major trial milestone, but additional capital will be needed after that.

Veru (NASDAQ:VERU) continued to make progress advancing its clinical programs while sharply reducing operating expenses. The clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on treatments for obesity and cardiovascular disease reported fiscal second-quarter results on May 13, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter was defined by disciplined cost management and steady pipeline execution as cash burn slowed and the PLATEAU clinical trial remained on track.

Notable Developments
The PLATEAU Phase 2b clinical trial for enobosarm in combination with semaglutide is actively enrolling, with interim analysis expected in the first quarter of 2027.
Both research and development and general and administrative costs saw substantial year-over-year decreases, with R&D down 20% and G&A down 22%.
Veru ended the quarter with $27.6 million in cash, providing enough resources to support operations through the next interim clinical trial readout.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated that it will prioritize cash for the ongoing PLATEAU clinical trial targeting obesity, with funding sufficient to reach interim results in early calendar 2027. However, the company will need additional financing to accelerate its pipeline beyond this point. Investors should watch for enrollment updates in PLATEAU and any announcements on new capital raising or clinical data progress.

Quarterly Highlights

Veru’s focus remained squarely on clinical advancement, with its main enobosarm and semaglutide trial recruiting on time and no delay to planned interim data. The study targets older adults with obesity and aims to demonstrate preserved lean muscle and fat loss, building on promising findings from the earlier QUALITY study.

Cost control was a clear priority this period, as both research and development and administrative expenses decreased significantly compared to the prior year. These ongoing cuts helped narrow the net operating and net loss, even as clinical priorities continued.

With no commercial revenues, the company relied on its cash position, which strengthened to $27.6 million at quarter-end. This balance supports current clinical operations, but Veru has made clear that bringing additional candidates like sabizabulin forward will depend on future fundraising.

What Veru Does

Veru is a biopharmaceutical company developing oral medicines for obesity and cardiovascular disease by advancing late-stage clinical trials with a focus on muscle preservation and inflammation control.
