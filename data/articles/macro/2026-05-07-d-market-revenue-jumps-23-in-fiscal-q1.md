Key Takeaways
Order and gross merchandise value continued to climb, but net losses widened further.
Revenue rose 22.9% year over year.
Management gave no numerical forward guidance and points to further growth investments.

Turkey’s major e-commerce marketplace posted bigger sales and marketplace growth, but also saw its net losses climb even with adjusted profits improving. The leading online platform, D-Market Elektronik Hizmetler Ve Ticaret A.s. (NASDAQ:HEPS), reported its earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter balanced robust customer activity with ongoing expense and cash flow pressures.

Notable Developments
Orders grew 22.1%, reaching 20.3 million, and gross merchandise value increased 28.4% to TRY 57.8 billion, signaling expanded platform use.
EBITDA nearly tripled to TRY 420.3 million, improving as a percentage of GMV, but net losses widened due mostly to higher financial charges and operating costs.
Marketplace share remained flat at 68.9% of GMV, while delivery service revenue increased 22.6% as HepsiJet expanded off the marketplace platform.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to prioritize growing order volumes and customer engagement without giving specific financial targets or numerical guidance. They note ongoing investments to drive future growth in both e-commerce activity and delivery services, yet acknowledge that higher expense levels are impacting current profitability. Investors should watch for future updates on Hepsipay, the company’s financial services business, and whether net losses can narrow as scale increases.

Quarterly Highlights

The platform experienced strong growth in core commerce activity, with total orders and gross merchandise value both increasing by over 20% year over year. Most of the revenue mix came from first-party product sales and a stable marketplace share, indicating a steady base of both direct and merchant sales. This period also saw 2.5% growth in active customers and a 13.6% climb in order frequency per user, both of which reinforced the company's customer engagement focus.

Despite improvements in EBITDA (which is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization), net loss for the period more than doubled compared to the previous year’s quarter. Higher advertising and shipping expenses contributed, but the steepest impact came from a nearly doubled financial expense figure, reflecting increased fees and charges related to credit card receivables and financing growth investments. While free cash flow remained negative, the outflow improved modestly, largely due to lower net operating cash usage and capital expenditures during the quarter.

Revenue from delivery services grew rapidly with HepsiJet’s expansion beyond the company’s own marketplace, while “other” revenue categories—including premium memberships and consumer finance—declined. The mix and performance of these segments matter because high-margin areas like consumer finance and fulfillment, which can boost overall profitability, saw declining contributions this period. Management highlighted investments in merchant shipping subsidies and continued logistics improvements, as well as expanded social programs supporting women entrepreneurs.

What D-Market Elektronik Hizmetler Ve Ticaret A.s. Does

It operates Hepsiburada, a leading Turkish e-commerce marketplace connecting millions of buyers and sellers for a wide range of consumer goods, supported by integrated logistics and payment solutions.
