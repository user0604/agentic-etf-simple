Notable developments
Revenue for the logistics and warehousing business dropped to $92,700, down 80.7% from the same quarter in 2025, reflecting lost client volume and reduced demand.
Operating loss narrowed by 20.4% to $764,319, due to a 23.0% drop in general and administrative expenses, mainly from lower professional fees and payroll costs.
The company completed capital raises, signing a share transfer agreement for an acquisition in Hong Kong and conducting private placement and At-The-Market financings during and after the period.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide specific revenue or profit guidance for upcoming quarters, instead emphasizing ongoing cost control and additional efforts to reposition the company. Investors should closely monitor Cheetah Net’s liquidity, revenue stabilization in the logistics segment, and integration of new acquisitions in the coming months.

Quarterly highlights

Logistics and warehousing revenue fell by over 80%, with both core segments -- Edward Transit Express Group and TW & EW Services -- showing large declines. Edward contributed 42.8% of total revenue with $39,700, and TWEW made up 57.2% at $53,000, both down meaningfully year over year.

Operating expenses were sharply reduced, led by lower legal and accounting fees and payroll, which helped narrow the operating loss compared to the prior year. Despite this, gross profit and gross margin have stayed weak, and the company continues to report net losses every quarter.

Capital management remained a priority, with capital raises through private placement and an At-The-Market offering. Closing of a planned acquisition in Hong Kong is scheduled for May 2026, marking an attempt to broaden the revenue base, but current cash reserves and high cash burn raise questions about financial sustainability.
