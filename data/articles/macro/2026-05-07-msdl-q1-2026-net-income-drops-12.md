Net asset value and investment gains fell as dividend coverage held steady for Morgan Stanley Direct Lending Fund (NYSE:MSDL). The business provides private credit by directly lending to mid-sized U.S. companies, with a focus on senior secured loans backed by private equity sponsors. The fund reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026, showing modest deterioration in investment income, assets, and book value per share.

Overall, the quarter reflected persistent pressure on earnings from lower interest rates and valuation declines, offset somewhat by continued risk controls and capital management.

Notable Developments
Dividend per share decreased to $0.45 from the prior quarter's $0.50, in line with the dip in net investment income.
Unrealized and realized losses for the period totaled $45.0 million, driving the decline in net asset value per share.
The company launched a joint venture, Capstone Lending LLC, with around 47% of its $200 million commitment called by quarter end.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management noted that stable credit quality and strong portfolio diversification supported resilience, while short-term headwinds from lower interest rates continue. The company expects gradual positive contributions from its new joint venture going forward. Investors should closely monitor the pace of realized and unrealized losses as well as any change in dividend coverage in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Net investment income covered the dividend, but both fell compared to the prior year period, with reduced interest income reflecting the impact of lower base rates. The company maintained coverage, noting the dividend adjustment aims to match underlying earnings power and protect its distribution track record.

Net asset value per share dropped by 2.2% compared to the prior quarter, due to heavier investment valuation losses and some realized credit portfolio losses. The share buyback program continued, with more than 940,000 shares repurchased at prices below net asset value, supporting shareholders through accretive capital management.

The start of the Capstone Lending LLC joint venture adds a potential source of incremental future income, though it had only a modest initial financial impact this period. The composition of the loan portfolio remained conservative, with nearly 94% in first lien debt and minimal loans on non-accrual status.

What Morgan Stanley Direct Lending Fund Does

Morgan Stanley Direct Lending Fund is a business development company specializing in private lending to middle-market U.S. companies, primarily through directly originated senior secured loans.
