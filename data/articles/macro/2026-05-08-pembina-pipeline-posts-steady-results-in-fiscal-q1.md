Notable Developments
The dividend was raised 3.5% to $0.735 per share this quarter, continuing Pembina's record of steady dividend growth.
Management increased its full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance by $175 million, citing stronger-than-expected marketing segment earnings.
Pembina completed major projects on time and budget, including the Wapiti gas expansion and K3 cogeneration facility, with further facilities set to come online soon.
Management outlook and what to watch

Pembina management now expects adjusted EBITDA of $4.35–$4.55 billion for fiscal 2026, up from its previous outlook. The company attributes this to higher commodity prices benefiting its marketing business, alongside solid performance from its fee-based infrastructure. Investors should watch commodity price swings and the contribution from the marketing segment, as these will strongly influence results for the rest of the year.

Quarterly highlights

The pipeline segment maintained high utilization, renewing and adding contracts covering 110,000 barrels per day on its Peace Pipeline, though lower Alliance Pipeline revenue contributed to a year-over-year earnings decline within this segment. Contract renewals help underpin predictable, future cash flows for the core infrastructure business.

Facilities and storage provided a bright spot, with adjusted EBITDA up 5% year over year. The Wapiti Expansion and K3 Cogeneration Facility were completed according to budget and timeline, while the RFS IV fractionator is trending under budget and expected online by May, pointing to strong operational discipline.

Marketing and New Ventures experienced a 10% decrease in adjusted EBITDA from the prior year, primarily due to narrower gas liquids price differentials, with partial offset from exposure to premium propane prices in Asian markets. Exposure to fluctuating commodity markets continues to drive volatility in this segment, though management’s revised hedging strategy should help reduce sharp swings later in the year.
