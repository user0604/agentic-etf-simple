Notable Developments
The company missed prior guidance by announcing only one new commercial agreement—the Riot Platforms memorandum of understanding for up to 4 GW of nuclear power for AI data centers—just after quarter end.
Regulatory progress included U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission approval of a key safety methodology, but fewer topical reports were submitted than foreseen in previous announcements.
Operating expenses increased sharply, with research and development and general and administrative costs more than doubling year over year, driving a wider net loss.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue updated financial or operational targets for the remainder of 2026 in this release, instead highlighting the company’s strong liquidity and anticipated continued investment in core technology and regulatory strategies. The single most important area for investors to monitor is the conversion of preliminary agreements like memoranda of understanding into revenue-generating contracts, alongside expense control.

Quarterly Highlights

The period's key regulatory achievement came when the Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved Terrestrial’s Postulated Initiating Events Topical Report, an important foundation for future reactor license applications in the United States. This regulatory step allows later projects to reference the safety work already accepted, which may speed up future approvals.

On the commercial front, the only new partnership announced was a memorandum of understanding with Riot Platforms to explore nuclear-powered solutions for data centers, representing the fast-growing AI energy sector. However, this partnership remains at the exploratory stage with no firm contracts or revenue commitments yet secured.

The company's costs increased quickly, with research and development spending growing nearly threefold and administrative expenses more than doubling compared to the prior year. Cash burn also rose, but liquidity remains robust, with nearly $290 million in cash and investments at the end of the period.
