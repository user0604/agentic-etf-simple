Notable Developments
Consumer Loan Marketplace volume more than doubled to $2.9 billion from $1.37 billion in the prior year, with blockchain-native Figure Connect driving strong gains.
Profitability improved rapidly, with adjusted EBITDA margin rising 17 percentage points year over year to 49.6%.
Figure added 80 new partners in the quarter, growing its ecosystem to 387 active partners and diversifying its lending products.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management forecasts Consumer Loan Marketplace volume between $3.8 billion and $4.1 billion in the next quarter, indicating further acceleration after the already-strong start to the year. The key area for investors and partners to monitor is whether Figure maintains both rapid growth and margin expansion in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The cornerstone Consumer Loan Marketplace delivered $2.9 billion in funded loans, up 113% year over year, as Figure Connect and partner banks fueled more transactions through its platform. Blockchain-based products such as YLDS (a registered, yield-bearing stablecoin) and Democratized Prime lending also expanded rapidly, with YLDS in circulation ballooning to $598 million compared to just $3 million a year ago.

Figure’s cost management delivered operating leverage alongside topline growth, with adjusted EBITDA nearly tripling and margin rising to nearly 50%. At the same time, operating expenses, especially stock-based compensation, rose more than topline results, suggesting that expense discipline will be a future area to watch.

Record new partnerships strengthened Figure’s network, bringing the active partner count to 387 by quarter end. Notable additions included the country’s sixth-largest mortgage lender and a new relationship with Flagstar Bank, with partner launches supporting diversification into segments like small and midsize business lending.

What Figure Technology Solutions Does

Figure Technology Solutions provides a blockchain-powered platform to modernize loan origination, trading, and digital asset management for banks, lenders, and financial institutions.
