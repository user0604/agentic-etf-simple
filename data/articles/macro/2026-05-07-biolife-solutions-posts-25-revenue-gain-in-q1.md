Key Takeaways
BioLife Solutions delivered strong revenue growth but faced margin pressures in the first quarter. Net income per share doubled year over year and revenue increased 25%. Management reiterated full-year guidance and signaled confidence in margin recovery.

BioLife Solutions (NASDAQ:BLFS) made commercial progress in cell and gene therapy tools but saw profitability pressures in its latest quarter. The leading supplier of biopreservation media and cell processing tools reported its first quarter results on May 7, 2026 (for the period ended March 31, 2026), reflecting both upbeat revenue gains and margin challenges. Overall, the quarter showed solid top-line growth alongside early signs of persistent operating headwinds.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 25% year over year, outpacing recent quarters and aligning with management’s full-year guidance of $112.5–$115 million.
Gross margin declined to 64% from 68% (Non-GAAP), reflecting continued pressure from production mix and yields.
Cash and securities stood at $111.5 million, supporting R&D and future strategic actions despite $0.5 million cash used in operations for the quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year targets, projecting revenue growth of 17–20% and maintaining "mid-60s" gross margin guidance. They expect margin recovery later in 2026 as production improvements take hold, noting the need for execution on bag yield fixes. Margin trends and the pace of innovation beyond core biopreservation media remain key factors to monitor.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue grew strongly, driven by demand for biopreservation media used in 250 U.S. clinical trials and 17 commercial therapies. This market position builds recurring sales, a focus area for the company.

Margins came under pressure again, driven by product mix shifts toward lower-margin bags and lingering manufacturing yield challenges. Management continues to target remediation by the fourth quarter of 2026, but short-term improvement remains limited.

Despite higher R&D spending (up nearly 84% to $2.7 million), adjusted net income and EBITDA both advanced. However, the increase in operating expenses offset some operating leverage from higher sales.

What BioLife Solutions Does

BioLife Solutions develops and supplies specialized preservation media and storage solutions enabling the safe transport and viability of cells used in advanced therapies.
