Key Takeaways

nLIGHT (NASDAQ:LASR) reported a strong quarter as growth in its aerospace and defense business continued to drive broad financial gains. The high-power laser manufacturer specializes in advanced solutions for defense systems, industrial manufacturing, and microfabrication. Results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (reported on May 7, 2026) showed sharply improved profitability and growth compared to the same period last year. Overall, the quarter marked sustained operational progress and strengthened its position in targeted end markets.

Notable developments
Aerospace and defense revenue soared 68.7%, reaching nearly 70% of companywide sales as nLIGHT benefited from strong military and government project demand.
Gross margin expanded by 6.4 percentage points year over year, reflecting a higher mix of defense-related business and scaling of operations.
The company finished the quarter with over $298 million in cash, thanks to a recent equity offering, improving its financial flexibility for future growth investments.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects second-quarter revenue between $75 million and $81 million, pointing to a possible seasonal or program-driven pause after the recent surge. Gross margin is expected to hold between 29% and 33%.

Quarterly highlights

The aerospace and defense business was the star of the quarter, with defense product sales nearly doubling year over year. These lasers and systems are used in applications such as directed energy weapons and advanced sensing for military platforms.

Industrial and microfabrication product lines, which support industries like precision manufacturing and electronics, both showed steady growth as nLIGHT completed its exit from the less profitable cutting and welding segment. The mix of products shifted further toward repeatable sales, away from more unpredictable project-based revenue.

nLIGHT’s balance sheet strengthened after it raised over $190 million from a recent stock offering. Coupled with stable operating expenses as a percent of revenue, this positions the company to invest in new research, development, and capacity expansion as demand grows—especially in defense markets.

What nLIGHT does

nLIGHT designs and manufactures high-power semiconductor and fiber laser technologies for defense, industrial, and microfabrication applications worldwide.
