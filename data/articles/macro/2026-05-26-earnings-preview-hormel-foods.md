Earnings Preview: Hormel Foods

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Hormel Foods (NYSE:HRL), a leading producer of branded protein foods and snacks, is expected to report its second-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 28, 2026, before the market opens.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

Hormel Foods delivered first-quarter results that showed continued progress in its protein-focused strategy. Organic net sales grew 2% to just over $3 billion, marking the fifth consecutive quarter of organic net sales growth. Adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $0.34. This result met analyst expectations for EPS and sales. The Foodservice and International segments posted high single-digit organic net sales growth, while the Retail segment saw a decline. The company reaffirmed its full-year guidance for both organic net sales and adjusted EPS.

Positives
Consistent organic net sales growth signals resilience: The company achieved its fifth straight quarter of organic net sales growth, demonstrating underlying demand for its branded protein offerings despite a mixed consumer environment.
Foodservice and International segments showed strong momentum: Both segments delivered high single-digit organic net sales growth and improved profits, reflecting successful pricing actions and strong demand for prepared protein products.
Cash flow from operations improved: Operating cash flow increased 13% year over year to $349 million, supporting continued investment and dividend payments.
Strategic focus sharpened with whole-bird turkey business sale announced: Hormel reached a definitive agreement to sell its whole-bird turkey business, aiming to reduce exposure to commodity volatility and focus on higher-margin, value-added products. The transaction is expected to close by the end of Q2 FY2026.
Negatives
Retail segment underperformed: Net sales in the Retail segment declined 2% and segment profit fell 19%, mainly due to higher raw material and logistics costs and the strategic exit from certain private label nut items.
Gross margin compressed despite sales growth: Gross profit margin decreased to 15.5% from 15.9% a year ago, as higher input and transportation costs outpaced the benefit of pricing actions.
Adjusted EPS declined year over year: Adjusted diluted EPS of $0.34 was down 3% from the prior year, reflecting ongoing cost pressures even as sales increased.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Impact of second wave of retail pricing: Investors should look for signs that the recently implemented retail price increases are helping to offset higher input and logistics costs, and whether these actions are supporting margin recovery in the Retail segment.
Benefits from restructuring and Transform and Modernize initiative: The company expects its restructuring program and ongoing supply chain improvements to deliver more meaningful financial benefits starting in Q2. Watch for updates on realized cost savings and operational efficiencies.
Progress on whole-bird turkey business divestiture: The sale is expected to close by the end of Q2. Investors should monitor any updates on timing, transition arrangements, and the early impact on reported results, especially as most 2026 holiday whole-bird sales will still be included this fiscal year.
Commodity and logistics cost trends: Management noted that beef and nut costs remain elevated and that logistics pressures intensified late in Q1. Any commentary on the direction of these costs and their effect on profitability will be important for the outlook.
Retail segment volume and mix: With volume declines driven by the exit from private label nuts and some softness in certain brands, look for indications that core branded products are regaining momentum and that the mix is shifting toward higher-margin items.
