Earnings Preview: DICK'S Sporting Goods

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

DICK'S Sporting Goods (NYSE:DKS), a major U.S. retailer selling sporting goods, apparel, and footwear, is expected to report its first quarter fiscal 2026 earnings before the market opens on Wednesday, May 27, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The fourth quarter of fiscal 2025 marked a strong finish for DICK'S Sporting Goods. The company reported consolidated net sales of $6.23 billion, driven by a 3.1% comparable sales increase for the core DICK'S business and a significant contribution from the newly acquired Foot Locker business. Non-GAAP earnings per diluted share for DICK'S business reached $4.05, up nearly 12% year over year, while consolidated non-GAAP EPS was $3.45. These results beat analyst estimates for both revenue and adjusted earnings per share. Gross margin for the DICK'S business expanded by 67 basis points, reflecting improved merchandise margins. The company also completed the major inventory cleanup at Foot Locker, positioning that business for improved profitability going forward.

Positives
Core DICK'S business delivered strong comp sales growth: Comparable sales rose 3.1% in Q4, building on a 6.6% increase the previous year. This demonstrates continued market share gains and healthy consumer demand.
Gross margin expansion in core business: Merchandise margin improvements led to a 67 basis point increase in gross margin, despite a promotional retail environment.
Foot Locker inventory cleanup completed: The company finished its planned inventory reduction at Foot Locker, improving inventory quality and setting the stage for higher margins in future quarters.
House of Sport and Fieldhouse expansion: DICK'S opened 16 new House of Sport and 15 new Fieldhouse locations in 2025, supporting long-term growth and enhancing the in-store experience.
Negatives
Foot Locker segment posted a Q4 operating loss: Despite progress, Foot Locker contributed a $5.9 million operating loss in the quarter, in line with management's expectations during the turnaround phase.
Consolidated gross margin declined due to acquisition mix: The addition of the lower-margin Foot Locker business reduced overall gross margin by 303 basis points compared to last year.
Transaction-driven increase in share count diluted EPS: The acquisition of Foot Locker increased the average share count, which had a negative impact on consolidated EPS.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales growth versus guidance: Management expects slightly higher comp sales in the first half of 2026, mainly due to the FIFA World Cup. Investors should watch if Q1 sales momentum matches the anticipated boost.
Foot Locker business progress post-inventory cleanup: Look for updates on early sales and margin trends at Foot Locker, especially in stores converted to the Fast Break format, as these are pivotal for the turnaround plan.
House of Sport and Fieldhouse expansion pace: The company plans to open 14 House of Sport and 22 Fieldhouse locations in 2026. Progress on these openings and their initial performance will be important for future growth.
Gross margin trends in a promotional environment: DICK'S achieved margin gains in Q4 despite heavy promotions. Investors should monitor if this continues in Q1, especially with World Cup-related product launches and category mix shifts.
Updates on digital initiatives and GameChanger platform: Management has highlighted GameChanger and the DICK'S Media Network as long-term growth drivers. Any new features, user growth, or monetization updates could influence the company's digital strategy outlook.
