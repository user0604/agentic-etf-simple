Notable Developments
Zola coconut water unit volumes grew 18%, but reported revenue fell 8% year over year due to an absence of a one-time benefit seen in the prior period.
Total operating expenses jumped to $1.9 million, mainly because the prior year included $1.75 million in nonrecurring gains, not repeated this period.
Management executed cost cuts with selling, general, and administrative expenses at their lowest level since becoming a public company, but flagged urgent funding needs as a major challenge.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management continues to focus on cost reduction and is preparing to launch a new Zola product in the fall. It cautions that additional funding is urgently needed and access to capital is uncertain. Investors should watch both the pace of Zola sales growth and any developments in the review of strategic alternatives.

Quarterly highlights

Zola coconut water remains the core product, with unit volumes up 18% despite an 8% revenue decline stemming from a reserve release that lifted the prior-year result. Management raised prices, helping offset rising freight and product costs, and signaled more innovation ahead with a planned product launch later this year.

Total operating expenses increased sharply, but this mostly reflects last year's one-time gains from patent portfolio sales and fair value adjustments. Actual cash costs, particularly in administrative areas, were reduced to historic lows for the company.

No new partnerships or strategic deals were signed, and the previously discussed merger with Roosevelt Resources has been abandoned. Management repeatedly signaled that more funding will be needed soon, and that various strategic options remain under review.
