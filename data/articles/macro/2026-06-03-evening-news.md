1. CrowdStrike Swings to Profit, Raises Bar

Cybersecurity heavyweight CrowdStrike (NASDAQ: CRWD) swung to a first-quarter profit and nudged its full-year outlook higher. CRWD is recommended by both Team Hidden Gems and Team Rule Breakers, and since its most recent rec in Stock Advisor (Team HG) in the fall of 2023, shares have gained 365%; compare that to a 31% increase in the S&P 500.

Comeback quarter: Net income landed at $27.8 million ($0.11 per share), a tidy flip from last year's $104.3 million loss, and revenue jumped 26% to $1.39 billion. Earlier this year, CRWD fell amid concerns about the threat of agentic artificial intelligence (AI); contributing Motley Fool analyst Jason Hall said that the "serious threat" is "also a massive opportunity."
The number CEO George Kurtz actually cares about: Annual recurring revenue (ARR) climbed 24% to $5.51 billion, and management cranked its full-year ARR target up to as much as $6.56 billion.
2. Broadcom's $16 Billion Flex Leaves Street Dizzy

Rule Breakers rec Broadcom (NASDAQ: AVGO) strolled into earnings and casually flexed. Fiscal Q2 revenue rocketed 48% to $22.19 billion, beating the Street, while adjusted earnings of $2.44 a share also cleared the bar. Net income nearly doubled to $9.31 billion. The showstopper, though, was AI chip revenue, which more than doubled to $10.8 billion on hungry demand for custom accelerators and networking gear.

Mic-drop moment: CEO Hock Tan says AI chip sales will more than triple to $16 billion this quarter -- and tossed out $29.4 billion in total guidance, leaving the $28.25 billion analysts penciled in looking a touch shy. Still, shares were down more than 10% after hours.
Deep-pocketed company it keeps: Broadcom is cozying up to Alphabet (NASDAQ: GOOG) on AI chips and feeding computing muscle to Anthropic -- basically AI's biggest spenders on speed dial.
