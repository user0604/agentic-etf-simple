Earnings Preview: Hovnanian Enterprises

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Hovnanian Enterprises (NYSE:HOV), a leading U.S. homebuilder focused on single-family and townhome construction, is expected to report earnings for its second fiscal quarter on Thursday, May 21, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Hovnanian reported total revenues of $632 million, which was near the high end of management’s guidance. The company’s adjusted gross margin was 13.4%, and selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses were 13.3% of revenue. Adjusted EBITDA reached $63 million, and adjusted pre-tax income was $31 million. These profit figures were above the company’s own forecasts. However, compared to the same quarter last year, revenues fell 6% and profit declined 24%, mainly due to lower home deliveries and higher incentives offered to buyers. The company’s results met or exceeded analyst estimates for Q1.

Positives
Profitability exceeded guidance despite market headwinds. Both adjusted EBITDA and pre-tax income were above management’s forecast, showing strong execution in a challenging environment.
Sales pace held steady due to incentives. The use of mortgage rate buydowns and other buyer incentives helped maintain contract activity, with net contracts up 3.1% year over year.
Quick move-in home inventory declined. The number of unsold quick move-in homes (QMIs) per community fell for the fourth consecutive quarter, reflecting better alignment of inventory with demand.
Liquidity position improved. The company ended the quarter with $471 million in liquidity, one of its highest levels in recent years, after funding land purchases and share repurchases.
Negatives
Year-over-year profit and revenue declined. Revenues dropped 6% and profit fell 24% from the prior year, mainly because of lower deliveries and increased incentives to drive sales.
Gross margin compressed due to higher incentives. Adjusted gross margin fell to 13.4% from 18.3% a year ago, as the company prioritized sales pace over price, increasing the use of incentives to support affordability.
SG&A ratio increased. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue rose to 13.3% from 12.9% last year, as revenue declined faster than the company could reduce costs.
Backlog and backlog value decreased. The number of homes in contract backlog fell 16.5% and the dollar value dropped 16%, reflecting higher conversion of backlog to deliveries and fewer homes remaining to be delivered.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Revenue and margin performance versus guidance: Management has guided for Q2 revenues between $625 million and $725 million, with adjusted gross margin expected in the 13% to 14% range. Analyst consensus is near the low end of this range, so any deviation could move the stock.
Impact of incentives on profitability: The company continues to use mortgage rate buydowns and other incentives to support sales. Watch for any change in the size or frequency of these incentives and how they affect gross margin.
Sales mix shift between quick move-in and to-be-built homes: The share of to-be-built homes (customized for buyers) has started to rise, and these typically carry higher margins. A continued shift could improve profitability in the second half of the year.
Progress on land strategy and community openings: Hovnanian has been shifting toward acquiring land for higher-margin move-up homes and active adult communities. Updates on new community openings and land acquisitions will be important for assessing future growth and margin potential.
SG&A trends and investment in technology: The company is investing in process and technology improvements, which has kept SG&A elevated. Investors should look for commentary on when these investments might begin to reduce costs or improve efficiency.
