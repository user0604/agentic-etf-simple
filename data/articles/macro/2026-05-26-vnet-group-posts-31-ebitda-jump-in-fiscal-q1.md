Vnet Group Posts 31% EBITDA Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 6:33 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business win as wholesale data center revenues surpassed retail for the first time.
Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) rose 30.6% year over year.
Management reaffirmed full-year revenue and profit guidance while noting ongoing investment requirements.

Wholesale data center services fueled a business milestone for Vnet Group (NASDAQ:VNET), as the segment overtook retail revenues for the first time. The operator of data infrastructure and internet data centers in China delivered strong revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (results published May 26, 2026). Still, large-scale investments and a sharp net loss due to tax expenses remain challenges. Overall, the quarter captured significant growth in capacity and bookings, with persistent pressure on profitability and cash flows.

Notable Developments
Wholesale internet data center (IDC) revenues grew 58.1% year over year and surpassed retail for the first time, reaching RMB1.06 billion in the period.
Net loss widened to RMB532 million, driven mostly by a RMB486 million tax expense related to capital transactions.
Operating cash flows declined to RMB174 million despite revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth, highlighting the high capital demands of ongoing expansion.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, targeting RMB11.5 to RMB11.8 billion in total net revenue and adjusted EBITDA between RMB3.55 and RMB3.75 billion. The outlook depends on sustaining large-scale capacity additions and improving utilization rates. Investors should closely monitor the pace of new data center buildouts and the ability to ramp up these assets to profitable capacity as commitments increase.

Quarterly Highlights

The wholesale IDC segment became the largest revenue contributor, with RMB1.06 billion in sales and robust new order wins. This segment now leads the business, reflecting major contracts such as a 510 megawatt commitment from a top internet client in the Beijing area.

Profitability measures improved at the operating level: adjusted EBITDA rose over 30%, while cash gross margin increased to 45.0%, supported by higher wholesale volumes. However, the net margin remained negative due to steep tax and capital-related expenses linked to financing and asset transactions.

The company made further progress on its asset monetization strategy, listing two real-estate investment trust (REIT) projects to recycle capital and support continued expansion. At the same time, high research and development spending signaled a focus on AI data center innovation and value-added services to serve growth in artificial intelligence and cloud computing demand.

What Vnet Group Does

Vnet Group operates data centers and provides related infrastructure, hosting, and cloud services to enterprises and technology companies in China, supporting the digital needs of hyperscale and retail clients.
