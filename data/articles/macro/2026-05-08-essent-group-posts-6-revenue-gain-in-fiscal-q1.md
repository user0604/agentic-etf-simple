Notable Developments
New mortgage insurance written (NIW) increased 11.4% year over year to $11.1 billion, signaling healthy new business despite a modest housing market.
Loans in default rose to 20,332, up 14.5% from the prior year, with cure rates dropping to 30% from 89%, resulting in higher credit loss costs.
The board raised the quarterly dividend by 13%, bringing it to $0.35 per share, reflecting continued shareholder returns.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide updated guidance for fiscal 2026. Prior communications indicated a focus on capital strength and strong cash flow, but no new forecasts were given for new insurance volumes or market growth. Investors should closely monitor credit quality trends and any future statements on expected mortgage insurance growth.

Quarterly Highlights

New mortgage insurance written, which represents the total of new home loans insured by the company for the quarter, reached $11.1 billion, up from $9.9 billion year over year. This increase was achieved despite a slow U.S. mortgage market. Overall insurance in force was stable at $247.9 billion.

Portfolio credit quality saw pressure, as the number of loans in default increased and the cure rate, which measures how many defaulted loans return to being current, fell sharply compared to last year. Provision for credit losses jumped in response, and claim severity -- the cost per default -- also moved higher year over year.

The quarterly dividend was raised 13% to $0.35 per share. The company also continued to return capital to shareholders by repurchasing 3.5 million shares year-to-date through April, totaling more than $214 million.

What Essent Group Does

Essent Group provides private mortgage insurance, which helps lenders manage risk and enables homebuyers to qualify for loans with lower down payments.
