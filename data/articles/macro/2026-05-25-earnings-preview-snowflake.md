Comments

Snowflake (NYSE:SNOW), which provides cloud-based data analytics and artificial intelligence (AI) tools for businesses, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, May 27, 2026.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Snowflake reported product revenue of $1.23 billion, representing 30% year-over-year growth. Total revenue for the quarter was $1.28 billion, which beat analyst estimates. Earnings per share (EPS) came in at $0.32, also ahead of consensus expectations. The company’s net revenue retention rate held steady at 125%, indicating continued expansion within its existing customer base. Remaining performance obligations reached $9.77 billion, up 42% year over year, showing strong future demand. The quarter included the closing of the Observe acquisition, which is expected to contribute to growth in the coming year. Management highlighted the rapid adoption of new AI offerings, including Snowflake Intelligence and Cortex Code, and pointed to continued operational efficiency gains, with non-GAAP operating margin expanding to 10.5% for the full year.

Positives
Product revenue growth exceeded expectations: The 30% year-over-year increase in product revenue outpaced analyst estimates, reflecting strong demand for Snowflake’s platform.
EPS beat consensus: Earnings per share of $0.32 came in above analyst expectations, showing improved profitability.
Record customer and contract wins: The company added 740 net new customers in the quarter and signed its largest-ever contract, over $400 million in total value, along with seven additional nine-figure deals. This demonstrates Snowflake’s growing importance to large enterprises.
Rapid adoption of AI products: Over 2,500 accounts adopted Snowflake Intelligence in just three months, and more than 4,400 customers are using Cortex Code, indicating strong early traction for these new offerings.
Negatives
Ongoing net loss: The company reported a net loss of $1.33 billion for the full year, reflecting continued high investment in growth and stock-based compensation.
Stock-based compensation remains high: While it declined as a percentage of revenue, stock-based compensation was still 34% of revenue for the year, which continues to be a concern for some investors.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Product revenue growth and guidance: Management has guided for Q1 product revenue between $1.262 billion and $1.267 billion, representing 27% year-over-year growth. Investors should watch whether Snowflake meets or exceeds this range and how guidance for the rest of the year shapes up.
Integration and impact of the Observe acquisition: The acquisition of Observe, completed in early February, is expected to add about one percentage point to product revenue growth in fiscal 2027. Look for updates on customer adoption and cross-selling opportunities.
Adoption trends for AI products: Continued momentum for Snowflake Intelligence and Cortex Code will be important. Watch for updated customer counts and evidence that these products are driving increased platform usage.
Operating margin and efficiency improvements: Snowflake is targeting a non-GAAP operating margin of 9% for Q1 and 12.5% for the full year. Progress on cost control and margin expansion will be key, especially as AI workloads grow.
