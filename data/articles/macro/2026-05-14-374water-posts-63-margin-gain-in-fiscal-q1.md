374Water Posts 63% Margin Gain in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Commercial deployment advanced with strong margin gains, but net losses widened.
Gross margin expanded to 63%, up 38 percentage points from a year ago.
Management expects recurring revenue growth from new municipal contracts, but stresses the need to secure ongoing funding.

PFAS-waste destruction provider 374Water (NASDAQ:SCWO) made significant headway on operational milestones and expanded its margin profile, but the story was clouded by deepening net losses and ongoing capital needs. The pioneering environmental technology company focuses on supercritical water oxidation systems to address persistent contaminants, mostly for municipalities and industrial clients. Its latest earnings release, posted May 14, 2026, covers the first quarter of fiscal 2026, and illustrates both growing commercial traction and the challenges ahead. Overall, the period delivered mixed results as gross margin soared but bottom-line losses and funding requirements persisted.

Notable Developments
Gross margin increased sharply by 38 percentage points from the prior year, as the company shifted further toward recurring service revenue.
Net loss widened by 23.6%, mainly due to commercialization costs and strategic investments, plus one-time charges for financing and franchise taxes totaling over $340,000.
Operational cash burn improved by 28%, but management signaled continued need for fresh capital, with $1.9 million raised through new financing around quarter end.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects the Orlando Waste Destruction Services (WDS) facility to drive recurring revenue and projects monthly revenue of $100,000 to $200,000 as operations ramp in late 2026, potentially exceeding $400,000 monthly by late 2027. The company is actively building partnerships and expanding deployments but highlights execution and capital sufficiency as ongoing priorities. The most important metric for investors to watch next is the progression from lumpy project-based revenue to a predictable recurring revenue stream as new facilities come online.

Quarterly Highlights

Gross margin improvement was the standout in the period, climbing to 63% from the year-ago 25%, driven by a growing mix of high-margin Waste Destruction Services and operational discipline. The company booked about $482,000 in service revenue from its full-scale demonstration at the City of Orlando, now being developed into a dedicated WDS hub. Critical contracts signed during the quarter included a 5-year recurring service agreement with Orlando and a $2.2 million order from Garney Construction for Olathe, Kansas, signaling early recurring revenue traction.

The company continued expanding deployments of its modular AirSCWO (supercritical water oxidation) systems. These systems destroy toxic "forever chemicals" like PFAS, with third-party validation showing over 99.9% destruction efficiency. The technology's flexibility, particularly its ability to handle difficult and variable waste streams, is a key differentiator in the waste management market.

On the financial side, the company’s cash flow from operations improved more than 28% from the prior year as it cut overhead and focused resources on contracts with near-term revenue upside. However, operating losses remain steep and cash coverage is highly limited, underscoring management’s continued efforts to secure capital and reduce dilution risk by shifting to convertible debt financing.

What 374Water Does

374Water designs, builds, and operates modular platforms that use supercritical water oxidation to safely destroy hazardous environmental contaminants in municipal and industrial waste.
