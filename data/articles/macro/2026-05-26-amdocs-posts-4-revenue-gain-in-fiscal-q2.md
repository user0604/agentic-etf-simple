Notable Developments
Revenue reached $1.172 billion, up 3.9% from the prior year, with growth across all regions led by Europe and Rest of World.
Net income declined 15.4% to $138.8 million, impacted by increased operating expenses and leadership transition costs.
Share buybacks totaled $138 million, and the quarterly dividend was increased to $0.569 per share.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year fiscal 2026 revenue and profit guidance, projecting 2%–4% constant-currency revenue growth and 5%–7% EPS growth. Investments in new cloud-native and agentic (AI-driven) solutions are highlighted as critical for future acceleration, but meaningful impact is expected in later periods. Investors should pay close attention to early revenue contributions from innovation initiatives like the aOS agentic platform and how quickly these drive growth beyond core managed services.

Quarterly Highlights

Managed services continued to anchor company performance, with segment revenue increasing and strong renewal rates with key clients such as AT&T/Cricket and Vodafone Spain. This category accounted for about 65% of company-wide revenue in the quarter.

Innovation and product launches, such as the CES21 cloud-native suite and aOS agentic platform, saw new customer adoption but have not yet generated significant revenue. At PLDT, more than 90% of customer requests are now resolved through the aOS platform, supporting improved customer operations.

Liquidity and cash flow remained healthy, though there was a drop in cash and increased short-term financing, following $138 million in share buybacks and the acquisition of Connect44 in Europe for $21 million. Management noted ample borrowing capacity and reiterated a free cash flow target of $710–$730 million for the full year, excluding restructuring payments.
