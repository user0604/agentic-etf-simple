Citi Trends Posts 14% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Citi Trends posted a strong start to the year, driven by double-digit same-store sales growth.
Revenue rose 14.4% year over year in the quarter.
Management maintained its fiscal year outlook but adjusted margin expectations due to higher supply chain costs.

Citi Trends (NASDAQ:CTRN) delivered a strong business win as customer traffic and average basket size both increased, fueling growth across its stores. The value-focused retailer, which specializes in apparel and home goods for Black families through community-centric locations, reported results for the quarter ended May 2, 2026, released on June 2, 2026. Overall, the period featured broad-based gains but also included a revised outlook for gross margin due to higher freight costs.

Notable Developments
Comparable store sales increased 13.9%, far outpacing the full-year guidance of 8–10%.
Profitability improved, with adjusted EBITDA more than doubled year over year to $13.9 million and net income reaching $7.8 million in Q1 2026.
Cash balances nearly doubled year over year, and inventory growth remained controlled at 4.8%.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

The company reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 outlook for comparable store sales growth of 8%–10% and adjusted EBITDA of $35 million–$40 million. However, it now expects gross margin improvement to be slightly less than previously projected due to higher fuel-related freight costs. Investors should monitor gross margin and expense discipline closely in the coming quarters as supply chain costs and sustained sales momentum remain key factors for profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

The company continued its focus on serving Black families with apparel and accessories, and cited customer engagement as a major driver of sales outperformance. Customer traffic and average basket size both rose, with results described as “accelerated across all merchandise categories and geographies.”

On the cost side, the company achieved meaningful leverage on selling, general and administrative expenses (SG&A). SG&A fell as a percentage of sales by 2.5 percentage points, supporting the more than doubling in profitability even as inventory was expanded to meet demand.

Citi Trends maintained its debt-free position with no borrowings on its $75 million revolver and closed the quarter with $81.1 million in cash. Store expansion continued with two new stores opened, one closed, and 25 remodels completed, supporting its ongoing real estate strategy.

What Citi Trends Does

Citi Trends operates a chain of discount apparel and home goods stores with a focus on serving Black communities through curated merchandise and accessible locations.
