Key Takeaways
Journey Medical delivered prescription growth and improved financial results in the latest quarter.
Revenue rose 21% year over year, and non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA per share turned positive to $0.02.
Management expects continued revenue growth and further operating leverage as new contracts and product access expand.

Journey Medical posted steady growth in its core prescription business as demand for its leading rosacea treatment continued to rise. The specialty dermatology company delivered another quarter of revenue gains and narrowed its net loss, with improved cash generation and prescription coverage for key product Emrosi. Released May 13, 2026, the first quarter 2026 results highlight ongoing progress with scale, but show some margin challenges due to a one-time inventory charge. Overall, the quarter marked solid progress with strong top-line growth and steps toward breakeven profitability.

Notable Developments
Emrosi prescriptions rose to nearly 30,000, reflecting prescription growth of over 10% quarter over quarter and threefold year-over-year sales expansion.
The company recorded a one-time $1.3 million inventory write-down related to its Qbrexza acquisition, which reduced gross margin to 61.0%.
Cash and cash equivalents grew to $27.2 million, up from $24.1 million at year-end, supported by positive operational cash flow.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects continued revenue growth and further cash generation as Emrosi gains broader patient access through new group purchasing contracts. The company plans to launch one or two new dermatology products in the coming year. Investors should watch the net revenue per Emrosi prescription for improvement, as well as further moves toward net profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

Emrosi, Journey Medical’s oral capsule for rosacea, accounted for much of the quarter’s revenue growth, with prescription volume reaching 29,968 and coverage expanding to 85% of U.S. commercial patients after a new group purchasing contract win. Prescription growth outpaced seasonal headwinds, helping push overall company revenue up 21% from the prior year quarter.

Cost discipline was evident, as selling, general, and administrative expenses fell $0.5 million year over year, demonstrating initial operating leverage after last year’s heavy product launch investment. Despite higher overall operating expenses, positive adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization) was achieved, and the net loss narrowed to $2.2 million.

Gross margin dipped versus prior year, but this was due almost entirely to a single non-recurring inventory write-down related to the Qbrexza acquisition; management indicated that, absent this charge, margin would have stayed consistent or slightly improved. Cash reserves increased to $27.2 million, providing flexibility for future product launches and potential acquisitions.

What Journey Medical Does

Journey Medical develops and markets prescription pharmaceutical products for dermatological conditions, including rosacea, acne, and hyperhidrosis.
