Earnings Preview: Algorhythm Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

Algorhythm Holdings (NASDAQ:RIME), which operates an artificial intelligence (AI) logistics platform focused on reducing waste in freight transportation, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Friday, May 15, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

For the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025, Algorhythm Holdings reported results that reflected a year of major transformation. The company completed the sale of its legacy consumer electronics business and is now fully focused on its SemiCab AI-powered logistics platform. Revenue for the year ended Dec. 31, 2025, reached $4.4 million, up from $300,000 in the prior year, driven by the May 2025 acquisition of SemiCab India. The company’s annualized revenue run rate approached $10 million by year-end, and management stated that this figure was already higher in early Q1 FY2026. Gross margin remained negative, with a gross loss of $1.3 million for 2025, but management described this as a necessary investment period as the network scales. The net loss from continuing operations for 2025 was $15.2 million, an improvement from the $18.9 million loss in 2024. These results met analyst expectations for sales and earnings per share.

Positives
Substantial revenue growth from logistics platform: The company’s pivot to AI-driven logistics produced a 1,370% increase in annual sales, indicating strong early customer adoption in India.
Successful customer expansion and contract wins: Four new Fortune 500 clients were secured in India in 2025, and all pilot customers have expanded their involvement, suggesting high satisfaction and recurring demand.
Improved cash position and reduced liabilities: Cash on hand increased to $10.9 million as of March 25, 2026, and liabilities were reduced by nearly 50% year over year, strengthening the balance sheet.
Clear transition to higher-margin SaaS business: The launch of the SemiCab Apex platform, a software-as-a-service (SaaS) offering, is expected to improve margins as it scales, with management highlighting a robust sales pipeline.
Negatives
Continued negative gross margin: The managed services model in India is still in an early ramp-up phase, leading to ongoing gross losses as truck utilization is not yet optimized.
Ongoing operating losses: Despite improvement, the company remains unprofitable, with a net loss from continuing operations of $15.2 million for 2025.
Heavy reliance on Indian market for current revenue: Almost all revenue is currently generated from the SemiCab managed services business in India, with U.S. and European expansion still in the early stages.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress in SemiCab Apex SaaS adoption: Investors should look for updates on new SaaS customer signings and early revenue contribution from the Apex platform, as this is key to improving margins and scaling globally.
Improvement in gross margin: Watch for signs that truck utilization rates are increasing in India, which should help reduce gross losses as the network matures.
Expansion into new geographies: Any progress or disclosed contracts in the United States or Europe would signal early success in diversifying revenue beyond India.
Cash burn and capital needs: Given the company’s ongoing operating losses, investors should monitor updates on cash usage and any commentary about the need for additional financing.
Status of customer pilots and contract expansions: Updates on the conversion of pilot programs into commercial contracts, especially with large enterprise shippers, will be important for assessing the pace of growth and recurring revenue potential.
