Key Takeaways

Cytek Biosciences (NASDAQ:CTKB) posted incremental top-line growth while profitability came under renewed pressure from rising legal and administrative expenses. The life sciences instrumentation provider, known for its full spectrum cell analysis technology, shared results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed healthy recurring revenue growth but highlighted ongoing bottom-line challenges, despite management standing by its annual sales outlook.

Notable Developments
Service revenue increased 15% from the prior year, reaching $15.4 million as the installed instrument base grew by 125 units to 3,789.
General and administrative expense jumped 43% year over year to $18.5 million, mostly due to litigation and consulting costs, driving the net loss higher.
Recurring revenue—from service and reagents—comprised 35% of total revenue on a trailing twelve-month basis, up from 31% a year earlier.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $205 to $212 million, representing growth of 2% to 5% compared to fiscal 2025. The company’s focus remains on expanding its installed base and recurring revenue, while also aiming to stabilize margins. Investors should closely monitor the trend in general and administrative expenses and any updates on litigation or administrative cost containment as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Installed instrument placements grew by 125 units during the quarter, supporting elevated service and reagent sales. This led to service revenue rising 15% and recurring revenue now making up over one-third of total sales. Product sales, in contrast, registered only modest growth and reflected typical first-quarter seasonality following a stronger fourth quarter historically.

Profit margins declined: adjusted gross margin slipped by one percentage point to 51%, partly reflecting higher production costs and overhead. Profitability further deteriorated, with the adjusted EBITDA loss deepening to $9.1 million. The main factor here was a surge in general and administrative costs, which management attributed to higher spending on litigation and consulting.

The company’s cash and marketable securities balance remained steady at $262.2 million, giving it a strong liquidity cushion. Despite this, rising operating losses make cost control a growing priority for the remainder of the year. The company did not announce any new product launches or material technological milestones for the quarter.

What Cytek Biosciences Does

Cytek Biosciences develops and sells advanced cell analysis systems and reagents for biomedical research, offering comprehensive solutions built on its proprietary Full Spectrum Profiling technology.
