Berkshire Hathaway (NYSE:BRK.B) delivered steady operating growth this quarter, with key insurance and railroad units both strengthening. The diversified holding company controls major businesses in insurance, rail transport, energy, manufacturing, and retail. The first-quarter earnings release, dated May 7, 2026, provided an update for the quarter ended March 31 and revealed a strong operational quarter despite continuing investment volatility. Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide any new forward earnings targets or project updates, sticking with its ongoing emphasis on long-term value and cautioning not to focus on quarterly swings. The company highlighted that net earnings can be misleading because of required mark-to-market accounting for investments. Investors should continue monitoring changes in insurance float and segment profitability, as well as the ongoing volatility in unrealized investment gains or losses, which can affect reported results from quarter to quarter.

Quarterly Highlights

Insurance operations posted a substantial rise in underwriting profit. Insurance underwriting is the profit earned on premiums minus claims and expenses, and a 28.6% jump points to strong pricing and claims discipline this quarter. However, investment income from insurance activities dropped by 7.4%, a reversal likely influenced by shifts in the asset mix or yield, as management did not provide further detail.

BNSF Railway, the holding company’s large U.S. freight rail business, saw its earnings climb by 13.4%. This gain marks a solid recovery over the prior year’s difficult comparison, helping reinforce overall group performance for the quarter.

Berkshire Hathaway Energy, its utilities and energy arm, recorded modest after-tax profits up 1.5%, reflecting a stable but slow-growing segment. There was no mention in the filing of any large new renewable energy investments or major regulatory changes this quarter.
