Key Takeaways
Aquestive Therapeutics achieved strong revenue growth and improved profitability in the latest quarter.
Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed significantly from the prior year.

Specialty drugmaker Aquestive Therapeutics (NASDAQ:AQST) delivered a business win with a sharp increase in revenue and narrowed quarterly losses. The company, which develops and manufactures oral film-based medicines primarily for allergy and central nervous system conditions, released its first-quarter results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter showed meaningful progress in both top-line growth and expense control, as the firm continued navigating regulatory and commercial milestones.

Notable Developments
Total revenue rose 66%, reflecting higher license, royalty, and manufacturing revenue as well as new contributions from third-party products.
Operating expenses dropped, as lower legal, regulatory, and commercial costs led to a significant narrowing in net loss and adjusted EBITDA loss.
Management reaffirmed full-year revenue guidance and targets for regulatory milestones on flagship Anaphylm and international filings.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership maintained its guidance for fiscal 2026, targeting $46 to $50 million in revenue with an anticipated adjusted EBITDA loss of $30 to $35 million. The company is focused on resubmitting its Anaphylm new drug application (NDA) to U.S. regulators in the third quarter and expanding its commercial team in readiness for approval. Investors should closely watch progress with FDA resubmission and any updates on anticipated launch timing for Anaphylm, as future revenue growth depends heavily on this milestone.

Quarterly Highlights

The reported revenue figure reflected both increased manufacturing output and a surge in license and royalty income, particularly from partnerships. Aquestive manufactured about 33 million doses during the period, up more than 20% from the same quarter last year, highlighting growth in third-party business alongside its proprietary portfolio.

Cost controls continued, with selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses falling from the prior year as legal fees and one-time regulatory costs subsided. Research and development investment also moderated, reflecting lower trial activity for pipeline drugs, especially Anaphylm, after completing important studies the FDA had requested.

From a pipeline perspective, the Anaphylm (oral epinephrine film) regulatory path became clearer following a successful FDA meeting that set the stage for resubmitting its application later this year. The company has already increased its planned field sales team by 50% to prepare for launch, while international filings in Canada, Europe, and the UK remain on track based on existing clinical data.

What Aquestive Therapeutics Does

Aquestive Therapeutics develops and manufactures oral thin film medicines using proprietary drug delivery platforms, with a focus on allergy and central nervous system disorders.
