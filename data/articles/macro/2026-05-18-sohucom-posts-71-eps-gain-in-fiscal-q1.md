Sohu.com Posts 71% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q1

May 18, 2026, 6:22 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Core profitability improved, but marketing services revenue declined and advertising remains under pressure.
Non-GAAP EPS loss narrowed sharply from the prior year.
Management expects a weaker second quarter, especially for online games.

Online gaming growth helped partially offset ongoing ad weakness for Sohu.com (NASDAQ:SOHU) in the latest quarter. The digital media and online gaming provider delivered first quarter 2026 results on May 18, 2026, showing operational improvements and user momentum for its core games division, but persistent challenges for its ad-driven business. Overall, the quarter saw efficient cost control and better online game metrics, though limited marketing revenue and a soft outlook point to continued uncertainty.

Notable Developments
Online game revenue rose 6% year over year to $124.6 million, with PC games reporting a 17% increase in average monthly active user accounts compared to the prior year.
Marketing services revenue fell 8% year over year, reflecting continued challenges in digital advertising.
Non-GAAP operating loss improved to $6.5 million, down from a $19.0 million loss in the prior-year quarter, thanks to tighter cost controls.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects a weaker second quarter, guiding for online game revenue between $104 million and $114 million, a decline from the first quarter. The outlook also calls for deeper overall net losses. Investors should keep a close eye on whether the company’s new gaming content and marketing initiatives are enough to offset industry headwinds in mobile gaming and advertising.

Quarterly Highlights

The online games segment, particularly PC-based role-playing games like TLBB: Return, delivered another quarter of user growth and higher bookings. PC games monthly active users rose to 2.7 million, a 17% increase over the same quarter last year, though payer activity eased slightly from the previous quarter.

Marketing services revenue continued to decline, dropping to $12.6 million. The company cited a tough advertising climate in China and has increased its focus on unique event-driven content and monetization opportunities to counteract traditional advertising weakness.

Cost control and disciplined operations contributed to significantly narrower operating and net losses this quarter in both GAAP and non-GAAP terms. The company held $1.2 billion in cash and investments as of March 31, 2026, and continued its share repurchase program with $116 million in buybacks completed this year.

What Sohu.com Does

Sohu.com provides digital content, online news, video, and operates a portfolio of online games through multiple platforms in China.
