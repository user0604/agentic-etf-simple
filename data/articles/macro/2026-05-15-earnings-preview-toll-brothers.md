Earnings Preview: Toll Brothers

The Motley Fool Team

May 15, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

Toll Brothers (NYSE:TOL), a leading builder of luxury homes in the United States, is expected to report its second-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Toll Brothers delivered 1,899 homes and generated $1.85 billion of homebuilding revenue, which exceeded the midpoint of company guidance. Net income for the quarter reached $210.9 million, translating to $2.19 per diluted share—a 25% year-over-year increase and a result that beat analyst estimates. The company’s adjusted gross margin was 26.5%, also beating guidance. Net contracts signed were flat in units but up 3% in value compared to the prior year, reflecting higher average sales prices. The company ended the quarter with a backlog valued at $6.02 billion and maintained a strong liquidity position with $1.2 billion in cash and $2.2 billion in available credit.

Positives
Revenue and earnings exceeded guidance and analyst estimates: This shows Toll Brothers was able to outperform expectations despite a challenging housing market.
Gross margin and SG&A margin both beat guidance: Operational efficiency and cost control contributed to better-than-expected profitability.
Luxury segment resilience supported higher average prices: The company’s focus on affluent buyers helped offset broader affordability pressures in the housing market.
Strong liquidity and low net debt: A healthy balance sheet provides flexibility for future investments and shareholder returns.
Negatives
Flat unit growth in net contracts signed: The number of homes contracted was unchanged year over year, indicating limited volume growth.
Backlog declined 13% in value and 20% in units: This reflects fewer homes in the pipeline, which could impact future revenue if not reversed.
South and Mountain regions experienced weaker results: Both regions saw declines in deliveries and income before taxes, mainly due to softer demand and increased incentives.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Home deliveries and revenue versus consensus: Analyst estimates call for about 2,400 to 2,500 homes delivered and sales near $2.42 billion. Watch if actual results meet or exceed these targets.
Gross margin trends and regional mix: Management guided to a sequential decline in adjusted gross margin to 25.5% due to less contribution from higher-margin regions. Investors should look for commentary on whether margin pressure is temporary and how the mix may shift in the second half of the year.
Contract activity and community count growth: The company expects to be selling from 455 communities by quarter-end, up from 445. Monitor contract signings per community and any early signs of a spring selling season boost.
Progress on exiting the Apartment Living business: Toll Brothers completed the sale of about half of this portfolio in Q1 and plans to fully exit over several years. Updates on additional asset sales and related gains or charges will be important for modeling future earnings and capital allocation.
Incentive levels and pricing power: Incentives remained stable at 8% of sales price for three straight quarters. Any changes in these levels or in the company’s ability to raise prices, especially in the North and Pacific regions, could signal shifts in demand or competitive dynamics.
