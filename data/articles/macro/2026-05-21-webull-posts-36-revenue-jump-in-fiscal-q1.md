Notable Developments
Customer assets surged to $24.0 billion, up 90% from the prior year, reflecting strong net deposits and ongoing user growth.
Adjusted operating expenses rose 63.7%, reaching $145.1 million, outpacing the 36.3% growth in revenue for the period.
Daily active trading metrics hit new highs: daily average trades grew 42% while options contract volume climbed 31% year over year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its priorities around platform investment, technology innovation, and international expansion but did not give detailed financial guidance for future quarters. The most important area for investors to monitor is whether Webull can better align expense growth with revenue growth, since sharp increases in costs weighed heavily on profits this quarter.

Quarterly Highlights

Webull’s top-line performance reflected ongoing momentum in its core brokerage business. Trading-related revenue increased 36% year over year, and equity notional trading volumes more than doubled compared to the prior year. The platform also launched new AI-driven research and trading tools, beta testing its “Vega Analyst” product and rolling out advanced trading features in anticipation of regulatory changes.

Profitability was challenged by a large jump in costs tied to user acquisition, global rollout, expanded product offerings, and higher share-based compensation. This resulted in a swing from net income to net loss, with adjusted net income tumbling more than 50% year over year despite the revenue increase.

Webull now serves 27.6 million registered users, up 15% year over year, and 5.1 million funded accounts, up 8% year over year. Customer engagement also reached new highs, as daily trading activity and options volumes reflected strong user activity and continued platform adoption.

What Webull Does

Webull operates a digital trading platform offering commission-free equity, options, and other investment services to individual and institutional clients worldwide.
