Notable Developments
Total operating revenues climbed 14% to $247.0 million, supported by higher activity levels and more rigs in service.
Operating expenses surged 28% year over year, driven by a 32% jump in rig operating and maintenance costs and a notable increase in general and administrative expenses.
Management expanded the fleet to 29 rigs following the integration of recently acquired rigs, while announcing joint venture agreements for further fleet growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue specific financial guidance for 2026, noting that recent fleet expansion puts added focus on cost discipline and converting new capacity into profitable contracts. The most important issue for investors is whether Borr Drilling can bring expenses under control and improve profitability as new rigs enter service. Ongoing fleet integration and the ability to absorb higher operating costs remain key trends to monitor in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Fleet activity remained robust, with total operating revenues growing 14% as more rigs secured dayrate contracts and deployments in regions such as the Middle East and West Africa. Bareboat charter revenue rose sharply to $26.6 million from $7.6 million, reflecting six rigs under charter compared to just two a year earlier. Management contract revenue increased 60% as more rigs operated under customer management agreements than in the prior period.

Costs grew significantly this quarter. Operating expenses jumped by $44.2 million to $201.0 million, pushed higher by a $8.4 million provision for credit losses, increased maintenance for new rigs, and rising personnel costs tied to fleet scale-up. General and administrative expenses rose 24%, outpacing topline expansion on a percentage basis.

The company completed a $300 million convertible debt offering due 2033 and repaid $195.2 million in earlier notes to extend debt maturities. The company also advanced plans for a joint venture acquisition of five additional jack-up rigs, aiming to strengthen its position across multiple geographies.
