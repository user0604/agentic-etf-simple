Klarna Group (NYSE:KLAR) achieved a major milestone by posting its first profitable quarter, reversing a string of prior losses. Notable Developments
Revenue grew by 44%, reaching the $1.0 billion mark for the first time and signaling strong global transaction growth.
Profitability turned positive, with operating income at $17 million after a loss of $90 million a year earlier.
The number of active consumers increased 21% to 119 million, while the merchant network grew by 49% year over year to over 1 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated full-year 2026 guidance and provided direction for the next quarter. It expects continued growth in transaction volume and revenue, but cautions that margins may moderate in the near term due to ongoing investment in lending products like Fair Financing. Investors should monitor loan performance and the pace of revenue growth versus operating expenses as the year progresses.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure for the period rose sharply, driven by a 33% increase in gross merchandise volume and expansion in high-growth products. Product adoption was especially strong in the Fair Financing segment, where installment loan volume surged 138%, supporting growth but slightly increasing provisions for credit losses.

Operating metrics improved across the board, with active consumers reaching 119 million (up from 98 million last year), and the merchant network surpassing 1 million global partners. The company also launched peer-to-peer payments in 13 European countries and grew the Klarna Card user base to 5 million across 16 markets.

While transaction margins and profitability improved, management noted a slight uptick in provision expense as a percentage of gross volume (now 0.55%). This reflects the shifting business mix and upfront provisioning required for the fast-scaling Fair Financing product, a trend to watch as Klarna balances growth with sustainable risk management.

What Klarna Group does

Klarna Group provides digital payments, installment financing (buy now, pay later), and financial services to consumers and merchants globally.
