Fashion rental platform Rent The Runway (NASDAQ:RENT) grew its subscriber base and posted sharply higher sales, but ongoing cash burn and margin declines highlight persistent hurdles in its latest results. The company, a leading online subscription service for rental apparel and accessories, reported results on June 3, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, management called out topline outperformance but signaled mixed progress toward lasting profitability.

Notable Developments
Revenue grew 29.2% year over year, reaching $89.9 million, and beat management’s prior guidance.
Active subscribers rose to 155,692, a 5.8% increase from the prior year period, reflecting continued user growth.
Gross margin fell to 25.9% from 31.5% a year ago, while free cash flow worsened to negative $13.6 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook for double-digit revenue growth and Adjusted EBITDA margin of 4% to 7%. It expects second-quarter revenue between $91 million and $95 million, with margin improvement forecasted. Investors should closely monitor the company’s gross margin trend and cash usage, as ongoing losses and cash outflows remain top risks for Rent The Runway.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure rose 29.2% to $89.9 million, supported by robust gains in the company’s add-on offerings and engagement initiatives. Add-on revenue, including items like extra rentals or accessories, climbed over 70% year over year, driven by new product features and a large inventory refresh.

The subscriber base continued to rise, with active subscribers totaling 155,692, though the growth rate slowed compared to the strong prior-year gain of 20.1%. Average active subscribers increased more than 12%, a sign that existing customers remained engaged, and “hearting” behavior—favoriting products—was up 11% through the quarter.

Despite operational progress, key challenges persisted. Gross profit inched up only 6.4% as gross margin fell sharply. Net loss narrowed from the prior year, but cash on hand fell from $70.4 million to $37.1 million, while free cash flow became more negative due to rising operational outlays.

What Rent The Runway Does

Rent The Runway is a digital subscription service that enables customers to rent designer clothing and accessories on a recurring or one-time basis.
