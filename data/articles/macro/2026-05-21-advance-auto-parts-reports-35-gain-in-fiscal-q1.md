Advance Auto Parts Reports 3.5% Gain in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Advance Auto Parts delivered its best comparable sales growth in five years, but total revenue remained nearly flat after accounting for closed stores.
Adjusted operating income margin improved to 3.8%, and gross profit margin reached 45.1%.
Management reaffirmed full-year guidance, including targets for revenue, earnings, and margin expansion.

Advance Auto Parts (NYSE:AAP) returned to top-line growth with stronger sales to professional customers, yet total sales momentum was limited by recent store closures. The leading automotive aftermarket parts provider serves professional repair shops and do-it-yourself (DIY) customers across North America. On May 21, 2026, it announced results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showing clear progress in profitability and operational execution. While the gross profit margin hit a milestone, the period highlighted the continued importance of supply chain investments and a focus on sustainable growth. Overall, the quarter delivered margin gains and reaffirmed management’s full-year outlook.

Notable Developments
Comparable sales grew by 3.5% with professional customers (“Pro”) leading with mid-single-digit growth and DIY posting low-single-digit gains. Total sales reflect a net increase after normalizing for stores closed last year.
Profit margins expanded, with adjusted operating income margin rising 4.1 percentage points year over year, partly from cycling previous restructuring costs and improved product strategies.
Though still negative, free cash flow improved to a $75 million outflow from a $198 million outflow, as inventory investments supported supply chain optimization initiatives.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year guidance across revenue, margin, earnings, and free cash flow, targeting $8.49–$8.58 billion in net sales and an adjusted operating income margin of 3.8%–4.5% for fiscal 2026. Leaders expect margin rates to ramp further in the second half due to supply chain and labor productivity initiatives. Investors should watch for sustained cash flow improvement and execution on both supply chain transformation and store-level productivity as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Comparable sales grew 3.5% in the quarter, marking the fastest growth in five years. Pro customer sales set the pace, as Advance’s focus on professional clients continued to outperform the DIY segment.

The company achieved a gross profit margin of 45.1%, reaching its stated target for 2026 ahead of schedule. Management attributes this to improved product mix and pricing, as well as the elimination of one-time margin headwinds from last year’s store closures.

Cash flow remained a focus, with the $75 million outflow representing a substantial improvement year over year. The inventory build, now totaling $3.8 billion, supports efforts to offer faster same-day or next-day service as supply chain initiatives ramp up.

What Advance Auto Parts Does

Advance Auto Parts supplies automotive parts, maintenance items, and accessories to professional garages and DIY customers across North America through a network of retail stores and distribution centers.
