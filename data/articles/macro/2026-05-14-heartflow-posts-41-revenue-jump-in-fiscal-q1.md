Key Takeaways
HeartFlow delivered a business win as commercial adoption accelerated and financial targets were raised.
Revenue rose 41% year over year, with non-GAAP gross margin improving by over five percentage points.
Management raised its full-year outlook, but investors should monitor whether cost discipline improves as the company scales.

HeartFlow (NASDAQ:HTFL) posted a clear business win this quarter as strong commercial uptake pushed both revenue and gross margins higher, prompting it to raise full-year financial targets. The leading provider of advanced, AI-driven cardiac diagnostics serves hospitals and clinicians with non-invasive solutions for coronary artery disease. In results published May 14, 2026 (for the first quarter of fiscal 2026), management highlighted ongoing growth but noted that operating losses remain substantial. Overall, the period showed rapid adoption offset by continued heavy spending.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed to $52.6 million, a 41% increase from the prior-year period, with U.S. product sales representing the bulk of growth.
Non-GAAP gross margin improved 5.2 percentage points to 80.5% due to higher case volumes and AI-enabled efficiency in clinical analysis.
Operating expenses continued to grow quickly, totaling 110% of revenue on a non-GAAP basis, and included a $7.5 million facility impairment charge.
Management outlook and what to watch

Leadership boosted its full-year revenue target to $228–$232 million, projecting around 30% growth for fiscal 2026, and tightened gross margin forecasts to approximately 81%. Management emphasized robust adoption and AI-driven productivity as reasons for raising guidance. Investors should closely watch whether improved efficiency translates into narrowing losses as operational scale increases and spending normalizes.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue gains remained strong, with U.S. revenue up 42% and international revenue growing 34%, showing adoption in both core and new markets. The company’s main FFRCT analysis service (a non-invasive test for heart disease) continued to drive results, while adoption of its newer Plaque Analysis product also accelerated.

Profitability improved on a margin basis as gross margin expanded to 80.5% on a non-GAAP basis, reflecting AI-driven productivity. However, this was offset by operating expenses that rose sharply, matching revenue growth and leading to another quarter with a sizeable net operating loss.

Operational efficiency gains came alongside a $7.5 million non-cash impairment related to headquarters relocation, signaling investments and one-time costs remain elevated. Overall, the business appears to be building scale, but a clear path toward consistent profitability has not yet emerged.

What HeartFlow does

HeartFlow develops and sells AI-powered software solutions that help clinicians diagnose and manage coronary artery disease non-invasively using CT imaging data.
