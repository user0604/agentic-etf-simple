Notable Developments
Profitability moved even higher, with Non-GAAP operating income at $14.1 million—up 12.8% from a year ago as margins improved.
The number of customers spending $30,000 or above in annual recurring revenue climbed to 3,875. This marks a 12% increase and underscores the company’s pivot toward larger clients.
Free cash flow was strong at $24.7 million, up almost 27%, and the company introduced a $50 million share repurchase program to return capital to shareholders.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue for the second quarter to range from $121.7 million to $122.5 million, with continued focus on operating margin improvement and larger clients. For the full year, management expects revenue between $492.5 million and $495.5 million. Investors should watch whether the shift to high-value enterprise customers can sustain growth as total revenue expansion slows.

Quarterly Highlights

The company continued to increase its focus on enterprise customers, as shown by the 12% year-over-year rise in customers spending at least $30,000 annually. This group now accounts for over 60% of subscription revenue, signaling stronger reliance on high-value accounts.

Sprout Social further developed its artificial intelligence (AI) offerings, such as “Trellis,” a proprietary AI agent, and earned recognition as the top social listening product in a leading third-party industry report. While the platform remains a leader in integrating data from multiple social networks, no new strategic partnerships were announced in the period.

Profitability and free cash flow saw double-digit gains, with non-GAAP measures showing that cost control and disciplined growth are at the center of the company’s approach. The first-ever $50 million share buyback signals management’s view of Sprout Social’s financial strength and maturity.

What Sprout Social Does

Sprout Social provides a cloud-based platform that helps businesses manage social media, analyze trends, and engage customers using automation and data analysis.
