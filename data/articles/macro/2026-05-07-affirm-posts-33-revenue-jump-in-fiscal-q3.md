Key Takeaways
Affirm delivered strong growth, expanding its consumer and merchant networks and product offerings.
Revenue rose 33% year over year, and net income increased sharply.

Affirm (NASDAQ:AFRM) extended its run of rapid growth, expanding its consumer and merchant base while seeing solid gains from its core buy now, pay later offerings. The financial technology platform connects consumers to merchants, offering flexible payment options with no late fees or hidden charges. Affirm reported its financial results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026.

Overall, the quarter showed strong revenue and profit gains, steady network expansion, and continued investment in new products.

Notable Developments
The platform expanded active consumers by 22% to 26.8 million and grew its merchant network 44% to 515,200 partners.
Gross merchandise volume (GMV) rose 35%, while net income moved sharply higher as profitability improved across the business.
Affirm Card, its flexible payment card, saw GMV more than double, highlighting strong consumer adoption and product expansion.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects continued growth in the fourth quarter, guiding for GMV between $13.15 billion and $13.45 billion and revenue up to $1.11 billion. Margins are projected to remain robust, with operating margin of up to 11.5%. Investors should monitor credit quality metrics, as allowance for credit losses edged upward, and the product mix continues shifting toward lower-yield, high-velocity options.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s network reach broadened rapidly as both active consumers and merchants posted double-digit increases, fueled by wallet and software vendor partnerships. Transactions per active consumer rose 20%, showing that users are engaging with Affirm more frequently for everyday purchases.

Profitability improved from both higher revenue and cost discipline. Adjusted operating income represented 27% of revenue, up nearly 5 percentage points, and more than 70% of incremental revenue after transaction costs flowed to operating profits, highlighting growing efficiency.

The Affirm Card continued to scale at a fast pace, with spending volume rising 146% year over year to $2.1 billion and cardholders more than doubling. Product innovation remained a focus, supported by artificial intelligence investments aimed at improving platform capabilities.

What Affirm Does

Affirm operates a technology-driven platform that allows consumers to pay for purchases over time while merchants benefit from higher sales conversion, with no late fees or hidden fees for consumers.
