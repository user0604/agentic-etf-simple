What Callaway Golf Does

Callaway Golf is a global designer, manufacturer, and distributor of golf equipment, golf balls, and premium golf apparel and accessories.

Quarterly Performance
Net sales reached $687.5 million, up 9.2%, and exceeded the high end of management’s earlier forecast by $22.5 million.
The company’s gross margin improved to 47.7%, a 2.6 percentage point rise, helped by successful price changes and new product strength.
Callaway Golf completed share repurchases of $79 million through April and ended the quarter with a net cash balance after major debt repayments.

Golf club sales led segment gains, increasing 11.9% year over year, as new products contributed to both higher sales and profit margins. Operating income in the Apparel, Gear, and Other segment jumped nearly 47%, with TravisMathew apparel noted as a particular standout.

Cash and liquidity strengthened, as the company finished with $499.5 million in cash and no net leverage following the full payoff of a $258 million convertible note. Share buybacks totaled $79 million, signaling management’s confidence in future value creation.

Despite positive results, Callaway Golf faced $18 million in new tariff expenses and reported a $27.7 million loss on its 40% Topgolf investment. Operating cash flow was negative for the period, a pattern shaped by seasonal inventory swings and major debt repayments.

Looking Ahead

Management raised its full-year 2026 forecasts, now expecting net sales of $2.015 billion to $2.070 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $211 million to $233 million. It cited strong demand and progress on operating initiatives but noted some seasonal sales timing that benefited the first quarter. Investors should monitor Asia sales trends and the ongoing financial impact from its minority stake in Topgolf.
