Notable Developments
Phase 2a clinical trial of GRI-0621 for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis met primary safety and tolerability endpoints, with no serious adverse events in the active arm.
Research and development expenses fell to $0.4 million, driven by the completion of the Phase 2a study; net loss decreased by one-third year over year.
Cash at quarter end was $11.0 million, buoyed by $6.8 million in share issuances, supporting operations into the second quarter of 2027 but not the next large clinical trial stage.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management plans to advance the GRI-0621 program, but signals that moving to a Phase 2b clinical trial will require substantial new funding or strategic partnerships. The company believes its current funds support near-term activities and operating expenses into the second quarter of 2027. Investors should watch for any announcements about new financing or partnerships as the key next milestone.

Quarterly highlights

GRI-0621, Gri Bio’s investigational oral drug for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, cleared its safety bar in the completed Phase 2a trial, with zero drug-related serious events and strong tolerability signals compared to placebo. The drug also produced measurable reductions in symptoms like cough, dyspnea, and diarrhea, and resulted in an approximate 100 mL improvement in forced vital capacity, a key measure of lung function.

The company’s spending dropped sharply after concluding the main clinical study, as research and development expenses decreased by 75% compared to the same period last year. This cost discipline, along with proceeds from an At The Market share offering, helped extend its cash runway into the following year.

The early-stage autoimmune disease drug GRI-0803 advanced toward preclinical development. The company also filed new patent applications based on biomarker data linked to its pipeline programs, seeking to reinforce its competitive position in rare lung and immune disorders.

What Gri Bio does

Gri Bio is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing small molecule drugs targeting immune cell pathways for serious fibrotic and autoimmune diseases.
