Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue new earnings or business guidance in this release. In the prior quarter, management noted an intention to increase leverage for funding new development projects; the current filings reflect higher debt and lower cash availability. Investors should closely monitor IRSA’s liquidity and progress on its expansion projects as these trends continue.

Quarterly highlights

The shareholders’ equity number increased year over year, supported by rising asset values and completion of development projects. However, lower net equity compared to the prior quarter suggests some pressure from non-operating losses, dividend payments, or changes in asset valuations.

Cash and cash equivalents dropped to ARS 54.5 billion, sharply lower than prior periods, while borrowings increased to ARS 896.9 billion. This shift highlights a transition to a more leveraged financial position as development activity ramps up.

IRSA’s portfolio investments continued to grow, with investment properties and trading properties under development posting gains. These numbers reinforce the company's strategy of expanding and renewing its portfolio, especially in commercial and residential property segments across Argentina.
