Key Takeaways
Profitability rebounded as margin gains outweighed lower sales.
Adjusted EBITDA increased 40% year over year.
Management is focused on marketing and menu initiatives to improve traffic and sustain margin recovery.

Good Times Restaurants (NASDAQ:GTIM) delivered improved profitability despite lower sales in the most recent quarter. The operator of Bad Daddy’s Burger Bar and Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard achieved stronger margins on effective cost control and operational efficiency. The company reported its results for the fiscal second quarter, which ended March 31, 2026, on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected stabilization in earnings, but growth in customer visits and revenues remains a challenge.

Notable Developments
Revenue dropped 3.1% as comparable restaurant sales, or same-store sales, declined 0.8% for both primary brands.
Adjusted EBITDA climbed to $1.4 million from $1.0 million, as food and packaging costs as a percentage of sales fell at both chains.
The company improved its cash position and lowered long-term debt to $1.0 million, supporting financial flexibility.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management aims to boost guest traffic and restore sales growth through targeted marketing and menu innovation in the coming quarters. The company plans to reintroduce popular items and launch new promotional campaigns, with major efforts ramping up late in the third quarter. Investors will want to track whether these traffic-driving efforts lead to sustained positive same-store sales.

Quarterly highlights

While revenue fell for the quarter, margin gains reflected lower food and packaging costs. Both Bad Daddy’s and Good Times brands reduced food-related expenses as a proportion of sales, contributing directly to higher restaurant-level profit margin.

The company maintained strong control of payroll and administrative costs. Payroll expenses, which include wages and benefits, held steady as a percentage of sales. General and administrative outlays dropped year over year.

Good Times paused expansion, ending the period with 37 Bad Daddy’s and 26 Good Times units after a year ago total of 39 and 27, respectively. No new restaurants opened during the quarter, reflecting a disciplined and cautious approach in a tough market.

What Good Times Restaurants does

Good Times Restaurants operates and franchises full-service Bad Daddy's Burger Bar locations and quick-service Good Times Burgers & Frozen Custard restaurants, with a focus on made-to-order burgers and specialty menu offerings.
