Key Takeaways
Net sales continued to fall as prescription declines persisted.
Revenue was down 15.6% from the prior year period.
Management set its focus on operational discipline and new B2B partnerships to try to stem losses.

Pet pharmacy specialist PetMed Express (NASDAQ:PETS) saw sales decline further as prescription medication revenue continued to slide. The company, a national online pet pharmacy and retailer, reported fourth quarter and full year 2026 results on June 2, 2026, for the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter featured persistent revenue decline but showed progress in cutting costs and narrowing net loss.

Notable Developments
Quarterly revenue dropped 15.6%, mostly due to ongoing prescription sales declines, though slight sequential improvement was noted.
The net loss for the quarter shrank to $4.1 million from $11.6 million, driven by lower costs and one-time benefits, offset by lower gross profit.
PetMed Express rejected two buyout offers of $4.00–$4.25 per share, as the board chose to continue operating independently despite financial pressures.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not give explicit revenue or profit guidance for the next quarter or fiscal year. It indicated a focus on stabilizing the core business, expanding new business-to-business (B2B) pharmacy partnerships, and boosting customer retention with fresh operational strategies. Investors should watch for the early impact of B2B agreements and signals of improved customer loyalty in the coming period.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales, especially in prescription medications, kept falling, pulling overall revenue down by 15.6% year over year. Management pointed to a modest increase in quarterly sales compared to the prior quarter, but yearly totals remain much lower than last year.

The company continued efforts to contain costs, which helped shrink its net loss from $11.6 million to $4.1 million, even though adjusted EBITDA stayed negative. Gross profit also declined, and cash reserves dropped by more than 60%, raising liquidity concerns if losses persist.

PetMed Express expanded its range of pet health solutions through the PetCareRx acquisition and launched new B2B pharmacy services, including a master agreement with Rural King.

These new initiatives are at an early stage.

What PetMed Express Does

PetMed Express is a national online pet pharmacy and retailer that supplies prescription medications, health products, and pet supplies for dogs and cats.
