Notable Developments
The company’s Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reached $493.0 million, up 21%—matching the high end of prior guidance.
Adjusted EBITDA of $30.6 million exceeded management’s forecast for the period, though free cash flow declined to $16.8 million.
SCG Canada was acquired, adding drone forensics capabilities, and development of AI-powered solutions such as Guardian Investigate and Genesis advanced significantly.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects ARR of $510 million to $513 million and revenue of $130 million to $133 million for the second quarter, pointing to continued subscription and product adoption. Full-year expectations remain intact with an emphasis on recurring revenue strength and adjusted EBITDA margin recovery. Investors should watch the integration of new AI and drone forensics solutions and any improvement in cash flow margins.

Quarterly Highlights

Subscription revenue was $117.9 million, representing approximately 91.9% of total revenue for the quarter.

Total Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reached $493.0 million.

The dollar-based net retention rate—a measure of recurring revenue growth from existing customers—was 115% for the period, signaling steady upsells but not marked acceleration.

Cellebrite’s investment in research and development grew 32% compared to the prior year. This spending drove the launch of major new digital investigation solutions, including Guardian Investigate for AI-enhanced case management, Genesis for rapid digital data analysis, and expanded access to new device types such as drones through the SCG Canada acquisition.

Margin contraction was evident. Although adjusted EBITDA rose by 29%, the GAAP operating margin fell to 7.1% from 11.4% a year ago, mainly due to higher R&D, sales, and acquisition integration costs. Free cash flow for the quarter slipped 9% year over year, landing well below levels seen in the trailing twelve months.
