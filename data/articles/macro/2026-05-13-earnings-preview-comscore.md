Earnings Preview: comScore

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

comScore (NASDAQ:SCOR), a company that provides audience measurement and analytics for media and advertising, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

comScore’s fourth-quarter 2025 results reflected a mixed performance. Total revenue for the quarter was $93.5 million, which represented a 1.5% decrease year over year. The company’s Content & Ad Measurement segment saw a 2.7% decline, mainly due to lower national TV and syndicated digital product sales. However, growth in cross-platform offerings and the movies business helped offset some of this decline. Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter reached $14.7 million, up 3.3% from the prior year, with an improved margin of 15.7%. These results were in line with management’s guidance and met analyst expectations for the quarter.

Positives
Cross-platform solutions continued to grow: Revenue from cross-platform products increased nearly 10% year over year, showing ongoing demand for unified audience measurement across TV, digital, and connected devices. This area remains a key driver for the business.
Movies business posted solid growth: Revenue from the movies segment rose 5.5% compared to the prior year’s quarter, reflecting resilience in this part of the business despite broader advertising market challenges.
Adjusted EBITDA margin improved: The margin increased to 15.7%, supported by cost controls and lower employee compensation and data costs in the quarter.
Recapitalization simplified capital structure: The transaction completed at year-end eliminated $18 million in annual preferred dividends and converted $80 million in preferred shares to common stock, improving financial flexibility.
Negatives
Overall revenue declined: Total revenue decreased 1.5% year over year, reflecting continued pressure in national TV and syndicated digital products.
Cross-platform growth slowed: Growth in cross-platform revenue was lower than prior quarters, mainly due to a strategy shift by a large retail media client, which management expects to be temporary.
Research & Insights Solutions remained soft: Revenue in this segment was down 3.1% for the full year, though it saw some improvement in Q4 due to new business from consumer brand health products.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Cross-platform revenue reacceleration: Management has guided for a return to double-digit growth in cross-platform offerings in 2026. Investors should look for signs that growth is picking up after the Q4 slowdown.
Impact of recent partnerships and product launches: The announced partnership with Yahoo DSP to advance connected TV (CTV) political advertising is expected to benefit revenue in 2026. Progress updates or early results from this and other partnerships will be important.
Progress on strategic actions post-recapitalization: comScore is evaluating additional strategic actions following its recapitalization. Any announcements or updates on cost structure, balance sheet improvements, or new initiatives could affect the outlook.
Local TV and movies segment trends: Both segments showed double-digit or solid growth in 2025. Investors should watch for continued momentum, especially as local TV provides a foundation for cross-platform measurement.
Cost discipline and cash flow: Management has committed to disciplined spending and improving cash flow. Watch for updates on operating expenses and any changes to cash flow guidance for the year.
