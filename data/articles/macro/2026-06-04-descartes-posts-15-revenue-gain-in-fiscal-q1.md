Notable Developments
Recurring services revenue made up 93% of total revenue in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, rising 15% year over year to $180.5 million.
Gross margin improved by 2 percentage points to 78% in the first quarter, with professional services and other revenues gross margin declining to 39% from 46% a year earlier.
The company completed two AI-focused acquisitions (OrderMine and Idelic) for a combined $27.6 million to strengthen its technology leadership in logistics.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled a steady approach heading into the next quarter, maintaining a baseline revenue calibration but withholding full-year guidance. Leaders highlighted continued investment in cloud, AI, and innovation but flagged operating cost increases and risks from shifts in global trade policies. The key area for investors to track next is expense discipline -- especially sales, marketing, and the impact of new technology acquisitions on future earnings.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw revenue climb nearly 15% year over year, with notable strength from sales of business-critical services like global trade intelligence and routing solutions. This underlines the company’s continuing migration to cloud-based, subscription offerings, as services accounted for the overwhelming majority of total revenue in the quarter.

Margin expansion continued, boosted by the growing share of high-margin recurring contracts. However, margin slipped in professional services, due in part to a changing revenue mix and slightly less profitable consulting and implementation work. Gross profit gains also reflected effective integration of acquisitions and successful product cross-selling.

On the acquisition front, Descartes completed purchases of OrderMine (for AI-powered forecasting in e-commerce) and Idelic (for AI-based driver safety management). These moves align with its push to automate logistics operations and provide customers with more advanced technology, feeding into its broader cloud and AI strategy for future growth.
