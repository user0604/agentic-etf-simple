Earnings Preview: 8x8

The Motley Fool Team

May 15, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

8x8 (NASDAQ:EGHT), a provider of cloud-based communications software for businesses, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, 8x8 delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations across key metrics. Total revenue reached $185 million and service revenue was $179.7 million, both above the high end of company guidance and consensus estimates. The company reported net income of $5.1 million and operating income of $9.7 million on a GAAP basis, with operating margin at 5.2 percent. Non-GAAP earnings per share came in at $0.12, above consensus. Cash flow from operations was strong at $20.7 million, and the company ended the quarter with $88.2 million in cash and equivalents. Growth was driven by usage-based offerings, including communications platform APIs and AI-powered solutions, which grew nearly 60 percent year over year and now account for over 20 percent of service revenue. The company completed the upgrade of all acquired Fuze customers to the 8x8 platform, consolidating its customer base onto a single system. However, this transition resulted in higher churn among former Fuze customers, creating a near-term revenue headwind that will continue into Q4 and early fiscal 2027. Despite this, management raised full-year guidance for both revenue and operating margin, reflecting confidence in ongoing momentum.

Positives
Return to top-line growth for the third consecutive quarter: This demonstrates the company’s ability to drive revenue expansion after a period of stagnation, which is important for investor confidence.
Strong performance in usage-based and AI offerings: Nearly 60 percent year-over-year growth in these areas shows that 8x8 is successfully shifting its business model to align with industry trends and customer demand for flexible, outcome-based pricing.
Completion of Fuze customer migration to 8x8 platform: This milestone streamlines operations and enables greater efficiency, setting the stage for improved customer satisfaction and future expansion opportunities.
Disciplined cost management and improved profitability: Operating expenses declined year over year, and the company delivered positive net income and cash flow, which strengthens the balance sheet and reduces risk.
Negatives
Churn from former Fuze customers created a revenue headwind: Not all legacy customers transitioned to the 8x8 platform, leading to higher churn that will continue to impact year-over-year comparisons in the near term.
Gross margin declined sequentially: The shift toward lower-margin, usage-based revenue reduced gross margin to 63.9 percent, down from 64.8 percent in the prior quarter, though this was expected as part of the business model transition.
General and administrative expenses increased: Legal and regulatory costs rose by $4.2 million year over year, which weighed on overall expense control.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Impact of Fuze-related churn on service revenue growth: Investors should monitor how much the loss of former Fuze customers continues to weigh on revenue, as management expects this headwind to persist through most of fiscal 2027 before rolling off.
Adoption and scaling of usage-based and AI-driven solutions: Watch for updates on customer uptake of communications APIs and AI features, which are key to 8x8’s long-term growth and competitive positioning.
Progress in expanding channel partnerships and indirect sales: The company has highlighted early momentum from new partner programs. Investors should look for evidence that these efforts are translating into sustained pipeline growth and new customer wins.
Profitability and cash flow trends: With operating margin guidance for Q4 in the 8.5 to 9.5 percent range and cash flow expected to be lower due to seasonal factors, it is important to see if 8x8 can maintain positive earnings and operational cash generation.
Execution on cost control and expense management: Investors should assess whether 8x8 continues to deliver on its commitment to disciplined spending, especially in research and development and sales and marketing, while still investing in innovation and growth.
