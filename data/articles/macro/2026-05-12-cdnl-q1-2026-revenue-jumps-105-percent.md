Notable Developments
Revenue doubled compared to the prior year, with organic growth accounting for 64% of the increase.
Backlog reached a record $854 million, up 60% due to new wins in markets such as data centers and manufacturing.
Adjusted EBITDA margin declined to 16.0% as acquisition-related costs and public company expenses increased.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its 2026 full-year revenue guidance to $675–685 million, citing continued backlog growth and strong demand across all served regions. It reaffirmed its target of an adjusted EBITDA margin above 20% for the year, but did not increase this target due to anticipated integration and acquisition-related costs. Investors should closely monitor margin performance and the impact of ongoing acquisitions on profitability in the upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The company expanded its in-house service offerings, moving into new project types such as data center infrastructure and manufacturing. This helped drive strong organic revenue growth and deepen relationships with large home builders and commercial clients.

Backlog growth reflected the benefit of recent strategic acquisitions, including the mid-quarter addition of A.L. Grading Contractors, which management said made “strong contributions from day one.” These acquisitions also led to increases in intangible assets and leverage on the balance sheet.

Despite record revenue and backlog, adjusted EBITDA margin declined by 1.8 percentage points compared to the prior year as the company faced higher costs tied to integrations and public company requirements, including audit and reporting expenses. Management noted that future margin improvement remains a focus but flagged that scaling pains may continue in the near term.

What Cardinal Infrastructure Group Does

Cardinal Infrastructure Group provides integrated site development and infrastructure construction services, such as grading, utility installation, and paving, typically delivered in-house for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.
