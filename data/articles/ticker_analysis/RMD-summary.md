RMD Company Overview
ResMed develops and sells medical devices and cloud-based software to treat sleep apnea and other chronic respiratory conditions. The company generates revenue through equipment sales and subscription-based software services that help healthcare providers monitor patients remotely and manage business operations. By integrating its hardware with digital therapy management tools, the company creates a connected health ecosystem that encourages long-term user compliance and deepens institutional customer loyalty across the global healthcare market.

ResMed Grows Revenue as Device Sales Outpace Software

ResMed makes medical devices and software that help people with sleep apnea and breathing issues outside of hospitals. Its popular AirTouch F30i Comfort face mask and focus on cloud-connected care have helped it stand out in digital health. Over the past 5 years, the stock returned 9.8% and underperformed the S&P 500 by 62 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $1.43 billion (up 11% YoY)
Non-GAAP EPS: $2.86 (up 21% YoY)
Sleep and breathing health revenue: up 11% YoY, led by devices, masks, and accessories growth

Latest Developments

The integration of the VirtuOx acquisition and increased marketing costs made total expenses grow faster than revenue, raising future profitability questions for investors.
Residential Care Software segment growth slowed to 6%, so management is now focused on speeding up this area to support long-term earnings.
