Agilysys (NASDAQ:AGYS), a provider of software for hotels, resorts, casinos, and other hospitality businesses, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Monday, May 18, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Agilysys reported record revenue of $80.4 million, representing a 15.6% increase year over year. Subscription and maintenance revenue grew 17.2%, and professional services revenue rose 22%. The company’s recurring revenue reached $52.0 million, with subscription revenue up 23.1%. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter came in below analyst expectations, while revenue was roughly in line with or slightly above consensus estimates. The company raised its annual revenue guidance to $318 million and reiterated its target of 29% subscription revenue growth for the full year. Profitability, as measured by adjusted EBITDA, remained on track at 20% of revenue.

Positives
Record revenue and subscription growth: The company delivered its sixteenth consecutive record revenue quarter, highlighting strong demand for its hospitality software solutions.
Raised full-year revenue guidance: Management increased its fiscal 2026 revenue target to $318 million, reflecting confidence in the sales pipeline and backlog.
Strong recurring and subscription revenue: Recurring revenue made up nearly two-thirds of total revenue, and subscription revenue growth remained robust, supporting predictable future cash flow.
Improved implementation efficiency: The company reported better project implementation speed, which helps convert sales into revenue more quickly and reduces service costs.
Negatives
Adjusted EPS missed expectations: Earnings per share fell short of analyst estimates, mainly due to higher product development and implementation costs during the holiday period.
Gross margin slightly declined: Gross profit margin decreased to 62.5% from 63.0% a year ago, reflecting a higher mix of lower-margin services and product revenue.
International sales volatility: International sales were described as lackluster in the quarter, with management noting continued quarter-to-quarter variability.
Product development costs increased: Spending on product development rose 26.4% year over year, which weighed on short-term profitability.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Progress and financial impact of the Marriott PMS rollout: The mass implementation phase for the property management system (PMS) at Marriott hotels is underway. Investors should look for updates on rollout pace and any early revenue or margin effects, as this project is excluded from current sales and backlog figures.
Subscription revenue growth rate: Management reiterated 29% growth guidance for the year, but Q4 is expected to show a lower growth rate due to year-over-year comparisons with the Book4Time acquisition. Watch for commentary on underlying organic growth and the sustainability of 20% to 25% growth rates into fiscal 2027.
Profitability trends and operating leverage: Adjusted EBITDA is guided to 20% of revenue for the year. Investors should monitor whether higher product development and implementation costs persist or if margin improvement is visible as AI tools and efficiencies scale up.
Sales momentum in key verticals: The gaming and casino segment saw a temporary slowdown in Q3, but management expects catch-up demand in coming months. Updates on sales trends in gaming, foodservice management, and international markets will be important for assessing future growth.
