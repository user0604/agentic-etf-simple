Earnings Preview: Lifeway Foods

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Lifeway Foods (NASDAQ:LWAY), a producer of probiotic dairy products such as drinkable kefir, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The fourth quarter of 2025 capped off a record year for Lifeway Foods. The company posted net sales of $55.4 million for the quarter, representing an 18% increase year over year. This marked the 25th consecutive quarter of year-over-year net sales growth. Gross profit margin for the quarter reached 27.8%, up 2.5 percentage points from the prior year, reflecting improved operational efficiency and favorable milk pricing. Net income swung to $2.5 million, or $0.17 per basic share, compared to a net loss in the same quarter last year. These results beat analyst estimates for both sales and earnings per share, highlighting ongoing momentum in the company’s core business.

Positives
Record sales and profitability achieved in Q4: Lifeway Foods delivered its highest-ever quarterly net sales and returned to meaningful profitability, signaling strong demand for its kefir and cheese products.
Gross margin expansion driven by volume and efficiency: The company’s gross profit margin improved significantly, reflecting benefits from higher production volumes and investments in manufacturing upgrades.
Distribution gains for Farmer Cheese product: Lifeway expanded Farmer Cheese to 2,000 Walmart stores, which should increase brand exposure and future sales opportunities.
New product innovation and partnerships launched: The quarter saw the rollout of new products like Kefir Butter and Muscle Mates, along with a licensing agreement for pet nutrition, diversifying revenue streams.
Negatives
Increased selling and administrative expenses: Operating costs rose as a percentage of sales, mainly due to higher marketing and legal expenses, which could pressure margins if not offset by growth.
Ongoing legal and governance costs: The company incurred significant legal fees related to board changes and shareholder actions, which may continue to impact expenses in the near term.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Sales growth relative to analyst consensus: Investors should watch whether Lifeway can meet or exceed the analyst sales estimate of $60.8 million for the quarter, as continued double-digit growth would reinforce the company’s momentum.
Gross margin trends as input costs fluctuate: The sustainability of recent margin improvements will be important, especially if milk or other input prices change in early 2026.
Progress on Waukesha facility expansion: The company is on track to double manufacturing capacity by the end of 2026, and any updates on construction milestones or capital spending will be key for future growth.
Impact of new product launches and expanded distribution: Early sales data or commentary on new products like Muscle Mates, Kefir Butter, and the Farmer Cheese rollout at Walmart will indicate how well these initiatives are resonating with consumers.
Board and governance developments following the Danone agreement: Investors should monitor any updates on board composition, shareholder meetings, or related legal costs, as these could affect management focus and future expenses.
