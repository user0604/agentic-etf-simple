Key Takeaways
National Vision delivered strong margin growth but faced persistent softness in self-pay customer traffic.
Net income more than doubled year over year.
Management upheld its full-year outlook while cautioning that some quarterly benefits may not recur.

National Vision (NASDAQ:EYE) delivered a solid quarter, with profit rising sharply even as traffic from self-pay customers continued to lag. The leading U.S. optical retailer and vision care provider saw strong contribution from its managed care business and disciplined cost controls. The results covered the period ending April 4, 2026, and were announced on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed marked improvement in earnings and profitability, helped by both ongoing initiatives and some favorable one-time factors.

Notable Developments
Net income margin doubled, with net income up 119.7%, driven by higher average ticket size and careful expense management.
Adjusted operating margin reached 10.2%, a rise of 2.1 percentage points, reflecting strong cost discipline, especially in payroll and advertising.
Store count increased 3.0% to 1,274 locations, fueled in part by the addition of 20 new military channel stores through a key partnership.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance across revenue, profit, and earnings. It pointed out that the quarter benefited from a timing-related boost in unearned revenue, which is not expected to repeat at the same level. The most important factor to watch is whether strong managed care and product mix can offset ongoing weakness in self-pay traffic and cost pressures from premium products.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability improved sharply thanks to careful control of selling, general, and administrative costs, which rose only 0.2% as revenue grew 6.6%. Earnings also received a one-time lift from the way unearned revenue was recognized in the period, adding about $7.1 million to adjusted operating income.

Growth was strongest in segments serving managed care customers, and new store openings included 20 military base locations. The company expanded its retail footprint by a net of 5 stores in the core America’s Best chain, despite a few closures that point to further portfolio optimization.

Comparable store sales growth held up at 4.5% on an adjusted basis, though this was below the prior year’s 5.5% gain. Company leaders noted that customers continued to opt for more premium lens and frame options, supporting higher ticket sizes but adding some pressure to product costs.

What National Vision Does

National Vision operates a network of retail optical stores across the U.S, selling eyewear and providing eye exams under several brands focused on value and accessibility.
