Key Takeaways
Negative free cash flow marked a transitional quarter following portfolio realignment.
Adjusted net income was $5.3 million, reflecting core asset performance.
Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance, expecting results at the high end of the range.

Amplify Energy (NYSE:AMPY) entered the first quarter following major asset sales and saw a challenging stretch for free cash flow as high capital spending weighed on results. The independent oil producer now focuses on its Beta and Bairoil fields after exiting East Texas, Oklahoma, and Eagle Ford. The earnings release dated May 11, 2026, covered the first quarter, reflecting the company’s refocused profile and early impacts from new asset concentration. Overall, the quarter revealed stabilization after restructuring, with steady progress on its core projects.

Notable Developments
Portfolio restructuring is complete, with operations now centered on the Beta and Bairoil oil fields after asset divestitures totaling more than $220 million.
The Beta field secured royalty relief, cutting its royalty rate from about 25% to 12.5%, which incrementally boosts net production by 600 barrels per day.
Negative free cash flow of $18.1 million reflected high up-front capital spending, while the company ended the quarter with $41.5 million in cash and no debt under its credit facility.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for fiscal 2026, projecting net daily production between 6,700 and 7,900 barrels, all oil, and adjusted EBITDA between $20 million and $45 million. The team expects to reach the higher end of its adjusted EBITDA target as commodity prices remain favorable. Investors should closely watch the pace of well completions and the realized benefits from Beta’s royalty relief.

Quarterly highlights

Beta field projects advanced with the C04 well showing strong initial output, but delays in other planned wells have shifted the expected completion timeline to early third quarter. Royalty relief at Beta became effective in May and is anticipated to add $1 million per month in incremental revenue at current oil prices, provided certain volumes and pricing thresholds are met. Operational challenges at Bairoil, notably compressor downtime, temporarily reduced production, but maintenance steps have improved reliability going forward.

Lease operating expenses and general and administrative (G&A) costs moved in line with management’s internal budgets, as the company focused on cost control amid its narrowed asset base. After divesting several properties, the company’s production now comes entirely from oil, reducing exposure to natural gas pricing swings. High capital expenditures directed at Beta this period weighed on free cash flow but are expected to support higher output and efficiency in future quarters.

Amplify maintained a strong liquidity position with $41.5 million in cash and access to a $25 million credit line, ending the quarter debt-free under its revolver. The balance sheet reflects both the proceeds from asset sales and a conservative approach to new borrowings, while hedging activities protected against price volatility but produced a notable non-cash loss for the period.

What Amplify Energy does

Amplify Energy develops and produces oil, with current operations focused on the offshore Beta field in California and the Bairoil field in Wyoming.
