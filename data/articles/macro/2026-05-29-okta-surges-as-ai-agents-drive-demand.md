Okta Surges as AI Agents Drive Demand

The rise of AI agents could be the ultimate near-term tailwind for this cybersecurity giant.

Jason Moser

May 29, 2026, 10:08 AM

Comments
10.0

It's been a bit of a slow start to the year for Okta (NASDAQ: OKTA), but the company's first quarter earnings report has investors optimistic about what's to come, and shares are having a banner day. Revenue grew 11% and the company exceeded expectations on all fronts with non-GAAP EPS of $0.91 up modestly from a year ago. Dollar-based net retention ticked up slightly to 107% and customers representing greater than $100K in annual contract value (ACV) grew 6% to 5,180. As often is the case, it all comes down to guidance, and that's what has the market seeing the glass half-full today.

Leadership is guiding for current quarter revenue growth of 9% and full-year growth of 9%-10%. No, that's not necessarily lighting the world on fire, and it basically matches what they said last quarter, but clearly the Street's expectations were lower. One thing that's going well for Okta is adoption of new products. In fact, new products drove 25% of total bookings for the quarter and the leadership noted that they see a 40% ACV uplift when new products are included in a deal.

The biggest near-term tailwind for Okta going forward is in AI agents. As workforces around the world continue to incorporate agents into their daily workflow (hey, we're doing it here at the Fool, too), security becomes one of the (if not the) highest priorities -- and that, of course, is where Okta shines. If the company can capitalize on this opportunity, that should help it reaccelerate top-line growth, leading to more investor enthusiasm in the future.
