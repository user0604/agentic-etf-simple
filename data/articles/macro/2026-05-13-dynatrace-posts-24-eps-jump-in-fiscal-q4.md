Dynatrace Posts 24% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 6:45 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Dynatrace delivered solid results, crossing the $2 billion annual recurring revenue milestone but projected slower growth ahead.
Earnings per share (Non-GAAP, diluted) rose 24% year over year in the fiscal fourth quarter.
The company forecasts mid-teens percentage growth for ARR and revenue in fiscal 2027, representing a modest deceleration.

Dynatrace (NYSE:DT) marked a business win by finishing its fiscal year with another quarter of double-digit growth and surpassing the $2 billion annual recurring revenue mark. The leading provider of AI-powered observability and automation software reported its fiscal fourth quarter results on May 13, 2026, for the period ending March 31. However, looking forward, the company's outlook indicates a moderation in growth rates for both revenue and recurring revenue compared to prior years. Overall, the quarter was characterized by strong execution and profitability, but signals emerged that expansion may be slowing as the market matures.

Notable Developments
Annual recurring revenue reached $2.05 billion, up 18 % compared to the prior year quarter, with log management consumption more than doubling.
Profitability strengthened: free cash flow rose 46.0% year over year, and non-GAAP operating margin edged up to 27% despite a lower GAAP margin due to a one-time impairment charge.
Management forecasts slower growth for fiscal 2027, with ARR and revenue guidance in the mid-teens percentage range, noting a slight step down from recent periods.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Looking ahead to fiscal 2027, management expects annual recurring revenue to increase approximately 15.5% to 16.5% in constant currency terms, and revenue growth to land between 14% and 15%. Operating margins are expected to remain stable at around 29.5% on a non-GAAP basis. Key items for investors to monitor are the pace of large-deal signings and how the company balances platform innovation with these more moderate growth rates.

Quarterly Highlights

Dynatrace’s platform expansion delivered strong customer adoption. Log management was again the stand-out, with consumption growth over 100% year over year as customers increased use of analytics and observability features.

Profitability improved on several measures. Free cash flow increased significantly to $212.4 million, aided by higher subscription revenue and improved operational discipline, though GAAP operating margin fell due to a non-recurring $18.5 million charge.

Strategic initiatives remained a focus. The company exceeded $1 billion in Amazon Web Services Marketplace sales and deepened integrations with AI and cloud providers. It also accelerated its share buyback program, repurchasing $224 million of its own stock in the quarter.

What Dynatrace Does

Dynatrace provides cloud-based software that uses artificial intelligence to monitor, analyze, and optimize the performance and security of enterprise IT systems.
