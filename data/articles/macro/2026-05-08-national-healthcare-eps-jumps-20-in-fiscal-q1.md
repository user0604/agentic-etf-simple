Quarterly Highlights

Profitability outstripped revenue growth in the period as adjusted net income surged 21.1% on only 2.2% higher revenue, largely because expenses increased at a slower rate than sales.

Healthcare pricing remained strong. The average skilled nursing per diem (payment per patient per day) rose 3.2%, with increases across all payer types, including Medicare, Medicaid, managed care, and private insurers. This supported revenue growth even as skilled nursing patient days grew less than 1%.

Balance sheet strength stood out, with all outstanding debt repaid by March 31, lowering interest expense sharply. Stockholders’ equity increased 2.1%, and the quarterly dividend was raised to $0.64 per share, continuing National HealthCare’s steady return of capital to shareholders.
