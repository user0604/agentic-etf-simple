Alarm.com Posts 11% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Alarm.com raised its outlook after recurring revenue and total revenue grew faster than previously guided.
EPS (earnings per share) on a Non-GAAP basis increased 14.0% from the prior year quarter.
Management now expects higher full-year SaaS and license revenue, raising its 2026 forecast by $6 million at the midpoint.

Alarm.com (NASDAQ:ALRM) lifted its full-year forecast as recurring software revenue and hardware sales both exceeded management’s earlier targets. The leading provider of cloud-based security and smart property technology reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period featured strong revenue growth, though higher costs weighed on earnings.

Notable developments
SaaS and license revenue rose 10.8% to $181.5 million, surpassing management’s guidance for the quarter.
Non-GAAP free cash flow increased to $49.7 million, reflecting stronger operating cash generation.
GAAP net income fell 15.7%, as higher research and development and marketing costs offset revenue gains.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 guidance for SaaS and license revenue, total revenue, and adjusted EBITDA, citing continued demand. The new outlook calls for $749.5 million–$750.5 million in SaaS and license revenue and $1.0595 billion–$1.0705 billion in total revenue, both above the prior forecast. Investors should monitor cost discipline and profit margins, as rising expenses affected net income even as the business expanded.

Quarterly highlights

The recurring revenue segment, which includes SaaS and license sales, continued to expand, supported by the company’s network of service providers and growing adoption of its cloud-based platforms. This performance exceeded the high end of management’s guidance and led to a raised annual revenue forecast.

Technology investments remained a focus, with research and development spending up 5.4% from the prior year quarter. New features, such as AI-driven video analytics for enterprise clients and automation in user apps, were introduced to support future growth across both residential and commercial customers.

Hardware and other revenue grew at a similar rate to software revenue, up 11.5%, reflecting strong demand for connected security devices. However, hardware costs also increased, so margins in this area will be important to watch if cost trends persist.

What Alarm.com does

Alarm.com provides cloud-based security, video, and automation solutions for homes and businesses through its network of service providers.
