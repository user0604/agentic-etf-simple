Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue financial targets or margin forecasts for the rest of fiscal 2026. It emphasized continued technical progress and system delivery as main priorities. Investors will want to follow how quickly Rigetti can broaden its customer base beyond large government and academic contracts and to what extent new system deployments drive recurring revenue and margin improvement.

Quarterly Highlights

The launch of Rigetti's first 108-qubit modular quantum system resulted in its strongest revenue growth to date, confirming delivery aligned with management’s previous guidance. The company recognized about half the value of prior Novera system sales in this period, with the remainder expected next quarter.

Rising investments in engineering and commercialization increased total operating costs by over $4 million year over year. The company attributed the higher loss mainly to scaled-up research as it expanded its product lineup, including ongoing work on next-generation chiplet-based quantum architectures.

While Rigetti continued to win research institution contracts and share technology with partners globally, the majority of its sales this quarter were still concentrated in one-off hardware deployments. The company’s planned investments in the United Kingdom and partnerships with cloud providers such as Amazon Braket and Microsoft Azure strengthened its global footprint, but commercial customer adoption beyond government and academia remains limited for now.

What Rigetti Computing Does

Rigetti Computing designs and manufactures superconducting quantum computers, offering access to its systems through cloud-based and on-premises solutions for research, government, and enterprise clients.
