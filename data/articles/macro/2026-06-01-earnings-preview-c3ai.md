Q3 FY2026 Recap

The third quarter of fiscal 2026 was challenging for C3.ai. The company reported total revenue of $53.3 million, with 90% coming from subscription sales and the remainder from professional services. The company posted a non-GAAP operating loss of $63.4 million and a non-GAAP net loss of $56.4 million, or $0.40 per share. While the reported loss per share was slightly better than analyst expectations, revenue and bookings fell short of consensus. Despite the weak quarter, C3.ai highlighted strong growth in federal, defense, and aerospace bookings, which now account for a majority of total bookings.

Positives
Federal and defense momentum: Bookings in federal, defense, and aerospace grew by 134% year over year, now representing 55% of total bookings. This shift suggests C3.ai is gaining traction in government and allied sectors, which could provide more stable, long-term contracts.
Restructuring and cost reductions: The company announced a $135 million reduction in annualized operating expenses, with headcount-related changes already substantially complete. This move is intended to improve profitability and operational efficiency in future quarters.
Strong cash position: C3.ai ended the quarter with $621.9 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities. This provides a solid financial cushion to support ongoing investments and restructuring efforts.
Productivity enhancements using AI: The company is leveraging its own Agentic AI tools to automate internal processes, aiming to increase productivity across sales, marketing, and engineering functions. This could lead to faster execution and improved margins over time.
Negatives
Revenue and bookings miss: Both revenue and bookings fell short of analyst estimates, reflecting weak sales execution, especially in North America and Europe. This underperformance raises concerns about the company's ability to grow its commercial business.
Declining gross margin: Total gross margin dropped to 17% from 59% a year earlier, mainly due to lower subscription revenue and higher payroll costs. This significant decline puts pressure on overall profitability.
Reduced customer acquisition activity: The number of initial production deployment agreements signed in the quarter fell to 14 from 50 a year ago. This reflects a more selective approach but also signals slower new customer growth.
Ongoing operating losses: The company continues to report sizable operating and net losses, with negative free cash flow of $56.2 million for the quarter. Sustained losses could limit flexibility if revenue does not recover.
