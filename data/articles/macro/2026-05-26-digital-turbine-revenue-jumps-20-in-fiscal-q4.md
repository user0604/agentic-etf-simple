Key Takeaways
Digital Turbine returned to double-digit revenue growth and improved profitability.
Earnings per share increased 60% on a non-GAAP adjusted basis compared to the prior year quarter.
Management expects continued growth in fiscal 2027, targeting $630–$650 million of revenue.

Digital Turbine (NASDAQ:APPS) delivered a business rebound, returning to double-digit revenue growth and stronger operating results. The company provides digital advertising software that helps mobile carriers, device makers, and app publishers distribute and monetize apps. In its earnings release on May 26, 2026, covering the fiscal fourth quarter ended March 31, management pointed to rising demand for its App Growth Platform and expanding margins. Overall, the period showed significant improvement and renewed momentum.

Notable developments

Revenue grew 20% compared to the prior year, with the App Growth Platform segment up 57% to $52.1 million in the quarter. Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) increased 53% from the prior year to $31.4 million. Non-GAAP free cash flow was negative $3.0 million for the quarter, down from positive $5.5 million last year, as accounts receivable rose sharply.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management projected fiscal 2027 revenue of $630 to $650 million and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA between $135 and $145 million. The company expects that growth driven by proprietary data and artificial intelligence will continue as first-party solutions attract more advertisers. Investors should monitor whether ongoing investments in technology lead to additional profitability and improved cash flow, as well as trends in receivables and debt servicing.

Quarterly highlights

The App Growth Platform, which helps mobile app publishers acquire users and monetize engagement, was a standout in the quarter. Net revenue in this segment increased 57% from the prior year, as more advertisers sought data-driven targeting tools powered by first-party data and artificial intelligence features like SingleTap and Ignite Graph.

On Device Solutions, the larger business segment that delivers app distribution and marketing partnerships with phone makers and wireless carriers, grew 5% in the period. This area continues to benefit from stable demand but at a slower rate than the software-focused App Growth Platform.

While profitability and margins improved, the company ended the quarter with negative free cash flow and saw accounts receivable climb to $251.2 million. Management noted higher interest costs and expects ongoing investment in product development and data infrastructure to remain a priority.

What Digital Turbine does

Digital Turbine provides software and data solutions that enable mobile phone carriers, manufacturers, and app publishers to distribute and monetize apps on smartphones globally.
