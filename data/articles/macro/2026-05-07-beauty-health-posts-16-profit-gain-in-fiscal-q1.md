Key Takeaways
Profitability improved despite revenue decline.
Adjusted EBITDA rose to $8.5 million while net sales fell compared to last year.
Management lowered the full-year revenue outlook but kept profitability guidance unchanged.

Beauty Health (NASDAQ:SKIN) focused on margin improvement, but saw another quarter of top-line challenges. The leading provider of Hydrafacial systems and consumables reported lower revenue but delivered stronger profitability in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (results released May 7, 2026). Overall, cost control and margin resilience partly offset slow device placements and continued sales declines.

Notable Developments
Net sales fell 6.7% year over year, with declines in both device and consumables segments; Americas net sales reached $44.6 million.
Adjusted EBITDA climbed to $8.5 million, a 16.4% improvement aided by reductions in adjusted operating expenses, which fell by over 10% to $38.4 million.
Management revised 2026 revenue guidance downward to $280–295 million but kept full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance at $35–45 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains focused on cost discipline and delivering adjusted profitability within guidance, but now expects lower revenue for the full year. The company says pressure on device placements will continue to impact top-line results. Investors should monitor trends in device placements and consumables sales for any signs of improving demand in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Device sales and placements continued to slow, with total delivery systems sold down to 746 from 862 one year ago. Weakness in both Americas and EMEA weighed most on unit sales while APAC placements rose, though sales in that region contracted due to channel transitions.

Consumables revenue, which forms the recurring part of the business, also fell in all regions except for slight resilience in the Americas. This segment remains foundational to the company's financial model, but the year-over-year decline suggests missed utilization opportunities from the installed base.

Cost control drove margin improvement: adjusted operating expenses dropped by 10.3%, and adjusted EBITDA rose over 16%. However, the higher adjusted profit did not fully offset the lower revenue and persistent net losses, and the company's cash position decreased by $28.3 million over the quarter.

What Beauty Health Does

Beauty Health develops, manufactures, and markets professional skin health devices and consumables centered around its Hydrafacial technology, which provides non-invasive skin treatments for clinics, spas, and medical offices worldwide.
