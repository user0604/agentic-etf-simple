Earnings Preview: Agilent Technologies

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Agilent Technologies (NYSE:A), a provider of laboratory instruments and related services for life sciences and applied markets, is expected to report earnings for its second fiscal quarter on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In its first fiscal quarter ended Jan. 31, 2026, Agilent reported revenue of $1.80 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) of $1.36. Both figures came in slightly below analyst consensus, which had expected $1.81 billion in revenue and $1.37 in EPS. The quarter was affected by a winter storm in the U.S. that delayed about $10 million in shipments, most of which were recovered in early February. Despite these challenges, the company saw growth across its major business segments and key end markets, especially in pharmaceuticals and advanced materials. Operating margin was 24.6% on a non-GAAP basis, in line with management expectations. Agilent maintained its full-year core growth outlook and increased its full-year EPS guidance slightly due to favorable currency movements.

Positives
Solid growth in key end markets: Pharmaceuticals grew 7% and chemicals and advanced materials rose 9%, demonstrating resilience and healthy demand in Agilent’s largest markets.
Innovation pipeline delivering results: New product launches, such as the Altura column for biopharma and the Pro iQ LC/MS instrument, saw strong adoption and contributed to growth, especially in biopharma workflows and analytical labs.
Services and recurring revenue momentum: The Agilent CrossLab segment grew 9%, with robust demand for consumables and service offerings, supporting more stable, recurring revenue streams.
Operational execution on cost controls: The Ignite Operating System and ongoing restructuring initiatives helped offset tariff headwinds and support operating margins, despite higher costs and wage pressures.
Negatives
Missed analyst expectations for revenue and EPS: Both top and bottom line results were slightly below consensus, with the weather-related shipping delay cited as a factor but not the sole reason.
Softness in smaller end markets: The academia and government segment declined 8%, and the food market was down 4%, reflecting ongoing funding constraints and less robust demand in these areas.
Margin pressure from tariffs and business mix: Gross margin declined year over year due to higher tariffs and a greater contribution from lower-margin specialty contract manufacturing, partially offsetting gains from pricing and cost savings.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Recovery of weather-delayed revenue: Investors should look for confirmation that the majority of the $10 million in Q1 shipment delays have been recognized in Q2, as management indicated most was already recouped.
Progress on margin improvement: Agilent guided to a sequential improvement in operating margin for Q2, driven by higher volumes and further benefits from the Ignite Operating System. Watch for updates on tariff mitigation and cost savings.
Performance in pharmaceuticals and advanced materials: Continued strength in these markets is key for Agilent’s growth. Pay attention to trends in GLP-1 drug-related demand, specialty contract manufacturing (CDMO), and semiconductor-driven advanced materials sales.
Updates on restructuring and cost initiatives: The FY25 restructuring plan is expected to be substantially completed by the end of FY2026. Investors should monitor progress on cost reductions and any impacts on segment profitability.
Impact of new product launches and service wins: Watch for early results from recent launches like the Altura column for PFAS testing and the S-540-MD Slide Scanner System, as well as the ramp-up of new enterprise service contracts with major pharma customers.
