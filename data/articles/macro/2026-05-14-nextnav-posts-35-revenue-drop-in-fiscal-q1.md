NextNav (NASDAQ:NN) posted continued losses as commercial revenue opportunities remained limited and costs rose. The location technology provider, which develops positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT) solutions, released its first quarter 2026 results on May 14, 2026.

Notable Developments
Revenue decreased to under $1 million, a drop from the prior year, as commercialization of PNT services remains in early stages.
Net loss improved primarily due to non-cash items unrelated to operations, with core operating losses increasing on higher research spending.
Cash reserves ended the quarter at $30.6 million, with management emphasizing runway to support future regulatory and commercial steps.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to stress the importance of securing key regulatory approvals, particularly ongoing processes with the Federal Communications Commission for spectrum use. No quantitative guidance was given, and leadership signaled that progress on commercialization hinges on achieving these regulatory milestones. Investors should watch for any updates on the FCC process or new strategic partnerships that could drive future revenue growth.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue fell sharply year over year, reflecting the absence of significant new contracts or deployment activity for NextNav’s PNT services. The ongoing regulatory approval process is critical for unlocking broader market opportunities, as commercial traction remains closely tied to FCC action and resulting permissions for spectrum usage.

Research and development expenses rose by almost 50%, showing NextNav’s commitment to advancing its 5G-integrated technology and building intellectual property. The company reported joining new industry groups focused on next-generation network integration, and announced a demonstration of 5G and RFID coexistence as part of its technical milestones.

While net loss narrowed, the improvement was primarily due to changes in the valuation of derivative liabilities and other accounting adjustments, rather than improvements in the underlying business. Core operating losses grew, and cash burn persisted despite efforts to keep selling and administrative expenses stable.

What NextNav Does

NextNav develops technology and operates networks that provide precise location, navigation, and timing services, often used as a backup to GPS and integrated with public safety and 5G applications.
