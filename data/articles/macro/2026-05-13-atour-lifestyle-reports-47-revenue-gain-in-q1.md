Atour Lifestyle Reports 47% Revenue Gain in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 6:19 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Atour Lifestyle delivered strong top-line and bottom-line growth as both its hotel and retail businesses expanded.
Adjusted earnings per share rose 42.7% year over year in the quarter.
Management expects revenue growth to normalize but remain solid for the rest of 2026.

Atour Lifestyle (NASDAQ:ATAT) reported powerful growth across its hotel and retail businesses this quarter, continuing to build on prior network expansion. The leading Chinese hospitality brand and lifestyle retailer released its first quarter 2026 results on May 13, 2026, showing robust gains from both hotel openings and retail momentum. Overall, the period marked notable progress, though management signaled that the rate of expansion is set to moderate in the months ahead.

Notable Developments
The company expanded its hotel network to 2,088 locations with 232,298 rooms, representing growth of 20.9% and 19.4% year over year, respectively.
Retail revenue surged 54.4% year over year, now making up nearly 38% of total company revenue for the quarter.
Operating costs and expenses rose 43.1%, as hotel cost ratios increased but retail costs as a percentage of revenue improved slightly.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management updated its guidance for fiscal 2026, projecting revenue growth between 24% and 28% compared to 2025, higher than its prior range but lower than this quarter's rapid pace. The company stated that strong first quarter results may not be matched in coming quarters as growth normalizes. Investors should monitor cost ratios in the hotel business and trends in occupancy rates for early signs of operational pressure.

Quarterly Highlights

Atour Lifestyle continued its rapid network expansion, opening 110 new hotels in the quarter and bringing the total pipeline under development to 751 manachised hotels. The manachised franchise model remains the core driver, with revenue from this segment up 51.9% from the prior year period.

The company’s retail business, centered around branded sleep and lifestyle products, delivered especially strong results. Retail revenue reached nearly RMB 1.1 billion, up more than 54% year over year, and higher-margin products improved overall profitability in this category.

Profitability also strengthened, with adjusted net income rising 42.0% and adjusted EBITDA up 51.1%. However, operating cost ratios in hotels rose to 67.4% of segment revenue from 63.4% a year ago, indicating rising variable costs that could weigh on margins if not managed carefully.

What Atour Lifestyle Does

Atour Lifestyle operates a portfolio of hotels and develops branded lifestyle products, combining hospitality with a retail business focused on sleep and wellness.
