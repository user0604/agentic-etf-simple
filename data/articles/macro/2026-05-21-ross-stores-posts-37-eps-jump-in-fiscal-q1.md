Key Takeaways
Ross Stores delivered broad-based gains led by customer traffic and merchandising execution.
Earnings per share jumped 37% year over year in the quarter.
Management raised full-year guidance, but signals slower comparable sales gains ahead.

Ross Stores (NASDAQ:ROST) delivered a clear business win, with strong execution on merchandising and customer acquisition driving robust results. The leading off-price retailer, specializing in offering branded apparel and home goods at discounts, saw customer traffic and efficient operations fuel gains across both store banners. The company released its earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 21, 2026, reporting results well ahead of its own guidance. Overall, the quarter set a new high bar, though future growth rates are expected to moderate as performance comparisons toughen.

Notable Developments
Comparable store sales climbed 17%, doubling the growth predicted in the prior company outlook.
Operating margin improved to 13.4%, attributed to higher sales leverage and merchandising execution.
Ross repurchased 1.5 million shares for $319 million and paid $143.6 million in dividends, reflecting an ongoing focus on shareholder returns.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now expects full-year comparable store sales to grow 6%–7%, up from the 3%–4% guidance offered after the prior quarter. Earnings per share is forecast at $7.50–$7.74 for fiscal 2026. Investors should watch whether robust sales and margin levels can persist as the company faces tougher year-over-year comparisons going forward.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales gains were driven mostly by increased customer visits and strong response to spring merchandise assortments, with comp sales growth up 17 percentage points from the prior year. Management cited both Ross Dress for Less and dd’s DISCOUNTS stores as contributors, with positive momentum described as “broad-based strength across the business.”

Profitability came in well ahead of expectations, as the operating margin reached 13.4% due to sales leverage offsetting higher expenses. The company noted "superb execution throughout the business" as an important support for these margin results.

Ross deployed $319 million for share repurchases this quarter and paid over $143 million in dividends, continuing a shareholder return strategy under a newly authorized $2.55 billion repurchase plan. The share count declined by roughly 6 million compared to the prior year, supporting earnings growth per share.

What Ross Stores Does

Ross Stores operates a nationwide chain of off-price retail stores that offer branded clothing, footwear, and home products at significant discounts to department and specialty store prices.
