Notable Developments
Revenue declined by 5.7% in the first quarter of 2026, with continued weak demand blamed on slow home sales and industry uncertainty.
Adjusted for the tariff refund, gross profit margin would have been 28.6% of net sales in the first quarter of 2026, supported by cost cuts and a $3.3 million nonrecurring tariff refund.
Selling and administrative expenses fell 5.3% year over year, reflecting execution of the Profit Improvement Plan.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains focused on cost reductions and expects further year-over-year profit benefits from efficiency moves and price increases to offset rising raw material costs. However, it did not issue specific revenue or earnings guidance due to uncertain market conditions. The most important factor for investors is whether higher costs and weak flooring demand will undermine these profit improvements in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Gross margin saw its strongest increase in years, thanks in part to the $3.3 million tariff refund and underlying improvements tied to the Profit Improvement Plan. Excluding this one-time benefit, management pointed to a 1.7 percentage point margin gain, signaling better operational efficiency even with lower sales volume.

Revenue dropped as housing market softness persisted, with management pointing to slow existing home sales, high prices, and Middle East conflict as key factors suppressing demand. Order entry at the start of the second quarter is tracking similar to last year, suggesting stabilization but no near-term rebound in sales levels.

Selling and administrative expenses fell $0.9 million year over year, as cost controls took effect. Interest expense rose to $1.9 million, with a $2.1 million increase in short-term debt reflecting working capital needs to support seasonal inventory builds ahead of the next quarter.

What Dixie Group Does

Dixie Group is a manufacturer and marketer of premium residential floorcoverings, including carpet, luxury vinyl, and hardwood products sold mainly through high-end independent dealers and designers.
