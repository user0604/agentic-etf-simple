Earnings Preview: Caleres

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Caleres (NYSE:CAL), a footwear retailer and wholesaler focused on brands like Famous Footwear and Sam Edelman, is expected to report earnings on Thursday, Jun. 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Caleres reported sales of $695.1 million, representing an 8.7% increase year over year. This growth was mainly driven by the acquisition of Stuart Weitzman, completed in August 2025. On an organic basis, excluding Stuart Weitzman, sales were essentially flat. The company posted a GAAP loss per share of $0.36, which was better than analyst consensus estimates for the quarter. Adjusted earnings per share also exceeded guidance, while sales were modestly above expectations. Gross margin for the quarter was 42.9%, down slightly from the prior year, with margin pressure mainly in the Brand Portfolio segment due to tariffs and markdowns. Famous Footwear sales declined 1.2%, but comparable sales were up 0.1%, and e-commerce continued to outperform store sales.

Positives
Adjusted earnings per share beat expectations: The company’s adjusted EPS exceeded both internal guidance and analyst consensus, suggesting better-than-expected cost management and execution.
Brand Portfolio growth driven by acquisitions and e-commerce: The Brand Portfolio segment saw sales growth, particularly from the integration of Stuart Weitzman and strong owned e-commerce performance, highlighting successful execution of strategic priorities.
Market share gains in key categories: Caleres gained market share in women’s fashion footwear and total footwear, indicating competitive strength in its lead brands.
Famous Footwear’s e-commerce and store remodels showed traction: E-commerce sales at Famous Footwear grew double digits, and stores remodeled under the Flair concept outperformed the rest of the fleet, validating ongoing investments in the in-store experience.
Negatives
GAAP net loss and margin pressure: The company reported a GAAP loss for the quarter, with margin compression in the Brand Portfolio segment due to tariffs and higher markdowns, which offset sales gains.
Famous Footwear sales declined: While comparable sales were flat, overall sales in the Famous Footwear segment fell 1.2%, reflecting continued softness in store traffic.
SG&A expenses increased significantly: Selling, general, and administrative expenses rose 18.3% year over year, primarily due to costs associated with the Stuart Weitzman acquisition and integration.
Inventory levels elevated due to acquisition: Total inventory increased $45.0 million, mainly from Stuart Weitzman, though organic inventory was down slightly, indicating some ongoing working capital pressure.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Sales growth from Stuart Weitzman integration: Investors should look for evidence that the completed Stuart Weitzman integration is driving the expected mid- to high-single-digit consolidated sales growth, as guided by management.
Gross margin recovery and tariff impact: Management has guided to a 120 to 140 basis point improvement in gross margin year over year, mainly from tariff mitigation and improved mix. Watch for updates on how new tariffs and sourcing strategies are affecting margins.
Performance of lead brands and e-commerce channels: Continued outperformance from key brands like Sam Edelman and Allen Edmonds, along with double-digit e-commerce growth, will be important indicators of the company’s ability to gain market share and drive profitable growth.
Famous Footwear comparable sales and Flair remodel expansion: The company expects Famous Footwear sales to be down low single digits to flat, with comps between down 2% and up 1%. Progress on additional Flair remodels (targeting 65 to 75 by year end) and their impact on sales will be key metrics.
Progress toward Stuart Weitzman breakeven target: Management expects Stuart Weitzman to reach breakeven in 2026, following significant cost reductions and inventory actions. Watch for quarterly updates on profitability and operational improvements in this brand as a sign of integration success.
