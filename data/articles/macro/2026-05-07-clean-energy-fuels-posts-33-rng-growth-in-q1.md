Quarterly Highlights

The 33% jump in RNG sales volume was enabled by new renewable gas projects moving into operation, particularly the East Valley dairy project in Idaho. This expansion underpinned broader volume growth, with total fuel gallons delivered up 27% compared to the same period a year ago.

Environmental credit revenue, which includes proceeds from RINs and LCFS credits, contributed a $5.4 million year over year gain. Regulatory policy and credit pricing remain volatile, making this revenue source critical but unpredictable in shaping quarterly earnings.

Capital investments in RNG infrastructure continue, with cash and investments falling by nearly $30 million during the period. This reflects Clean Energy’s strategy to secure a larger RNG supply for customers, but also highlights the need to monitor cash usage and future funding requirements.
