Comments

Argan (NYSE:AGX), a company that builds power plants and industrial facilities, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

Argan delivered record results in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, with revenue reaching $262.1 million and diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $3.47. The company also reported a gross margin of 25% for the quarter. Net income was $49.2 million, and EBITDA reached $56 million. These results beat analyst consensus estimates for both revenue and EPS. The company ended the quarter with a project backlog of $2.9 billion and held $895 million in cash and investments, with no debt. Argan increased its quarterly dividend to $0.50 per share during the year, marking the third consecutive annual increase.

Positives
Record revenue and earnings beat estimates: Both quarterly revenue and diluted EPS exceeded analyst expectations, reflecting robust demand and strong project execution.
Significant backlog growth: The project backlog more than doubled year over year to $2.9 billion, providing strong visibility into future revenues.
Strong balance sheet with no debt: The company ended the year with $895 million in cash and investments and no debt, supporting flexibility for growth and shareholder returns.
Dividend increase and ongoing buybacks: Argan raised its quarterly dividend and continued share repurchases, signaling confidence in ongoing cash flow generation.
Negatives
Rising selling, general and administrative expenses: SG&A expenses increased to $17.9 million for the quarter, up from $14.9 million the prior year, mainly due to higher incentive and stock-based compensation.
Project mix and margin variability: Management noted that gross margins can fluctuate significantly depending on project timing and mix, which may lead to less predictable quarterly profitability.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report

Gross margin trends and project execution: Management has indicated that margins can vary with project mix and execution. The first quarter will provide insight into whether recent high margins are sustainable as new projects ramp up.
Industrial and Teledata segment momentum: The Industrial segment ended the year with a record backlog, and Teledata continues to grow. Progress in these segments could diversify revenue sources and support overall growth.
Capital allocation and shareholder returns: Look for any updates on dividend policy, share repurchases, or potential acquisitions, given the company’s strong cash position and recent increases in shareholder returns.
