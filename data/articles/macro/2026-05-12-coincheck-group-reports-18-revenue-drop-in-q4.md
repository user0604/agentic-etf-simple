Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue a formal financial outlook but emphasized the importance of executing recently announced partnerships, such as the agreement with KDDI. The company highlighted that future results will depend on successful integration of acquired businesses and improvement in trading activity. Investors should watch for tangible progress from these strategic alliances and whether core user engagement leads to higher transaction revenue.

Quarterly Highlights

Core adjusted revenue, which strips out low-margin transactional flow, dropped sharply from the prior year, with management citing a decline in Marketplace trading activity as the main driver. This trading slowdown also pulled down profitability, as one-time severance, transaction, and software impairment costs compounded the negative impact, turning both net income and adjusted EBITDA negative for the quarter.

Coincheck continued to grow its user base, posting a 10% increase in verified accounts year over year, and reached 2.53 million by March 31, 2026. Growth in staking and asset management revenues began to offset some softness in trading, as new offerings and the 3iQ acquisition contributed recurring revenue streams.

The company secured a $65 million investment and new partnership with KDDI, a major Japanese telecom firm, as well as an ETF collaboration with Scotiabank through its asset management subsidiary. However, management flagged that successful conversion of these alliances into stronger volumes and profits remains essential, especially with continuing pressure on cash flow and core earnings from lower trading activity.

Coincheck Group operates digital asset and cryptocurrency trading, asset management, and web3-related services for retail and institutional clients, primarily in Japan.
