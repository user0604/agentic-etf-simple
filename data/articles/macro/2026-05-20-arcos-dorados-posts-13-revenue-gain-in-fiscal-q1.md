Notable Developments
Total revenue set a new first-quarter record at $1.2 billion, lifted by 16% systemwide comparable sales growth and strong digital expansion.
Adjusted EBITDA margin rose by 1.2 percentage points to 9.7%, reflecting improved food, paper, and administrative cost efficiency.
Cash and cash equivalents fell to $266 million from $422 million at year-end, mainly due to debt payments and capital investments.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to emphasize expansion, digital innovation, and disciplined costs, aiming to build on its market position. However, it did not provide new full-year guidance. Looking ahead, investors should monitor cash usage and sales momentum in Brazil, as these will shape financial flexibility and regional performance.

Quarterly Highlights

The company reached historic revenue as digital channel sales expanded 21% year over year and now comprise about 64% of total system sales. Loyalty program membership and modern restaurant formats also helped drive strong guest engagement and steady growth across most markets.

Margin improvement was supported by tighter controls on food, packaging, and general expenses, partly aided by gains from sub-franchisee transactions in Central and South Latin America. Systemwide comparable sales growth of 16% reflected both company-operated and franchised restaurants, a key indicator of franchise model health.

Cash generation turned positive on a last twelve months basis, with adjusted free cash flow swinging from negative to $109 million. However, a sharp net decrease in cash and a sizable debt repayment this quarter reduced cash on hand, underscoring the importance of effective capital and liquidity management as expansion continues.

What Arcos Dorados Does

Arcos Dorados operates and franchises McDonald’s restaurants in over 20 Latin American and Caribbean markets, making it the region’s largest quick-service restaurant operator.
