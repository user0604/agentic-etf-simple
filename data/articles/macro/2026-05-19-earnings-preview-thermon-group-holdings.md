Earnings Preview: Thermon Group Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Thermon Group Holdings (NYSE:THR), which provides industrial heating and temperature control solutions for process industries, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of its 2026 fiscal year on Thursday, May 21, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

Thermon reported record third-quarter results, with revenue increasing 10% year over year to $147.3 million and adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.66. The company’s adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 24.2%. These results beat analyst consensus for both revenue and EPS. Growth was driven by strong project activity, especially in large capital projects, and continued demand for products supporting electrification and decarbonization trends. The company also raised full-year guidance for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA, reflecting momentum in orders and backlog.

Positives
Record revenue and profitability achieved: Thermon delivered its highest-ever quarterly revenue and profitability, signaling effective execution of its growth strategy and operational improvements.
Strong order growth and backlog increase: Orders rose 14% year over year, with a book-to-bill ratio of 1.1 and backlog up 10%, providing visibility into future revenue.
Margin expansion despite higher project mix: Gross margin improved to 46.6%, even with a greater share of large project revenue, indicating success in pricing and operational efficiency initiatives.
Momentum in new product platforms: The company shipped its first liquid load bank units for data centers and grew its medium voltage heater backlog, highlighting progress in new growth markets.
Negatives
Operating expense growth outpaced revenue: Selling, general, and administrative expenses rose 12%, faster than revenue, mainly due to investments in growth initiatives and higher performance-based compensation.
Net income flat year over year: Despite higher revenue and gross profit, net income was $18.3 million, down slightly from $18.5 million last year, reflecting higher expenses and no restructuring gains this year.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Progress on the proposed CECO Environmental combination: Investors should monitor any updates on the recently announced strategic combination with CECO Environmental, as this could significantly affect Thermon's future business mix and financials. The transaction remains in the proposed stage.
Order trends and backlog conversion: Watch for continued strength in orders and how much of the increased backlog is converted to revenue, especially from large capital projects and new product lines.
Performance of data center and medium voltage heater initiatives: Look for further updates on sales, bookings, and production ramp for liquid load banks and medium voltage heaters, as these are key to Thermon's long-term growth strategy.
Margin sustainability and cost discipline: Assess whether Thermon can maintain its improved gross and EBITDA margins, particularly as project mix and investment in growth initiatives evolve.
Capital allocation and liquidity management: Pay attention to capital expenditure levels, share repurchase activity, and debt reduction efforts, especially as the company balances growth investments with maintaining a strong balance sheet.
