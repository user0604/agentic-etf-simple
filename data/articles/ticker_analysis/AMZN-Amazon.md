Amazon Grows Cloud and Ads to Diversify Beyond Shopping

Amazon runs one of the biggest online shopping sites and offers cloud computing, ad, and AI services to people and businesses worldwide. The company stands out because its cloud business has major deals with tech leaders like OpenAI and Meta, plus a growing custom chip segment. Over the last five years, the stock returned 51.7%, underperforming the S&P 500 by 18.8 percentage points.
