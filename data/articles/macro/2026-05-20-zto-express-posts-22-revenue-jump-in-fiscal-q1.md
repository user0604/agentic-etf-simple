Parcel volume reached 9.67 billion, an increase of 13.2%, outpacing typical industry growth rates.

The company introduced a $1.5 billion share buyback plan and maintained annual parcel guidance of 10–13% growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its guidance for full-year parcel volume growth of 10–13%, which suggests expectations for high demand to continue. The company cited cost management, particularly around pickup and dispatching fees, as a strategic focus for upcoming quarters. Investors should closely watch how the balance shifts between rapid growth and cost control as the business pursues more key account and e-commerce return business.

Quarterly Highlights

Parcel shipments surged, with volume up over 13%, driven by strength in direct sales to large e-commerce customers. Parcel shipments grew 13.2% year over year in Q1 2026, outpacing the industry average by 7.4 percentage points and resulting in market share gains.

Top-line growth came alongside a sharp increase in "other costs," especially expenses associated with serving key account customers. These costs grew much faster than parcel volume, holding back improvements in operating margin despite higher average selling prices per delivery.

The company’s large share repurchase authorization, up to $1.5 billion, reflected its strong cash balance and strategic focus on capital returns. Management also highlighted the importance of ongoing regulatory compliance and network optimization, particularly given shifts in China’s logistics sector policy and partnership models.
