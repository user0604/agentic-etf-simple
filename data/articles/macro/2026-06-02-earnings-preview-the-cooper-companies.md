Earnings Preview: The Cooper Companies

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

The Cooper Companies (NASDAQ:COO), a global supplier of contact lenses and fertility and women’s health products, is expected to report its next quarterly earnings after the market closes on Thursday, June 4, 2026.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, The Cooper Companies reported consolidated revenues of $1.02 billion, representing a 6.2% year-over-year increase. Organic growth was 2.9%. The company’s adjusted earnings per share (EPS) reached $1.10, which beat analyst estimates, while revenue came in slightly below consensus. CooperVision, the contact lens division, generated $695 million in revenue, up 7.6% (3.3% organic), and CooperSurgical, the fertility and women’s health division, delivered $329 million, up 3.3% (2.2% organic). Operating margins improved, and free cash flow was strong at $159 million. Management raised full-year adjusted EPS guidance following the quarter.

Positives
Adjusted EPS beat estimates and guidance was raised: The company’s $1.10 EPS exceeded analyst expectations, and management increased full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $4.58 to $4.66, reflecting confidence in ongoing operational improvements.
Strong cash flow and capital deployment: Free cash flow was $159 million for the quarter, enabling $92 million in share repurchases and further debt reduction, supporting shareholder value and financial flexibility.
Ongoing product launches and market share gains: CooperVision continued to roll out its MyDay daily silicone hydrogel contact lenses and MiSight myopia control lens, helping the company gain market share for the 18th consecutive year.
Improved operating margins from cost controls and restructuring: Operating expenses declined as a percentage of sales, aided by last year’s reorganization and technology investments, especially in AI-driven efficiencies.
Negatives
Revenue missed analyst consensus: While EPS beat, total revenue fell short of expectations, mainly due to weaker performance in the Asia Pacific region.
Asia Pacific headwinds weighed on organic growth: Sales in Asia Pacific declined 4% year over year, largely because of softness in Japan’s older hydrogel products, offsetting gains in the Americas and EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa).
Tariff pressure remains a drag on margins: Tariffs are expected to cost the company around $24 million for the year, limiting further margin expansion.
Fertility market uncertainty in the Middle East: Although fertility trends are improving, ongoing conflict in the Middle East poses a risk to this segment, which accounts for about 2% of total sales.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Organic revenue growth trajectory in Asia Pacific: Investors should monitor whether new product launches and expanded private label contracts can offset continued softness in Japan and return the region to growth, as management expects improvement by the third fiscal quarter.
Execution of new product launches and contract wins: Watch for updates on the rollout of MyDay MiSight and other new lens products in Europe, Asia Pacific, and North America, as well as the impact of recently signed private label and branded contracts.
Margin performance and tariff impact: Pay attention to how the company manages operating margins amid ongoing tariff costs and whether further efficiency gains from restructuring and technology can offset these pressures.
Fertility and women’s health segment recovery: Look for signs of continued stabilization or improvement in fertility product demand, especially in the U.S. and Europe, and any updates on the Middle East’s influence on this business.
Status of the strategic review process: The company’s board-initiated strategic review, announced in December and still in progress, could have implications for capital allocation, business structure, or future growth priorities. Any updates or outcomes may be material for investors.
