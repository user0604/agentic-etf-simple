Key Takeaways
Operational delivery was strong, but non-cash charges led to a headline loss for the quarter.
Revenue rose year over year, supported by record realized gold prices.
Management maintained full-year production and cost guidance and highlighted progress on major growth projects.

Allied Gold (NYSE:AAUC) delivered solid operational results, but non-cash charges resulted in a net loss for the quarter. The gold miner increased production and benefited from higher gold prices, which lifted revenue. The company reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026, and management reaffirmed its full-year outlook while updating on major expansion projects. Overall, the period showed operational momentum, offset by cost and accounting pressures.

Notable developments
Gold production increased 14% to 96,016 ounces, with the Bonikro mine outperforming internal plans and Agbaou also raising output.
All-in sustaining cost (AISC) rose to $2,264 per ounce, mainly due to higher gold prices driving increased royalty expenses.
Net cash from operations declined year over year, as working capital swings offset higher operating cash flow before those changes.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed both production and cost guidance for fiscal 2026, highlighting ongoing expansion at Sadiola and progress toward first gold at Kurmuk. The company expects cost relief at key mines as new ore zones come online and operational improvements take effect. Investors should monitor delivery on expansion milestones and improvement in unit costs, especially at Sadiola.

Quarterly highlights

Higher realized gold prices drove revenue to a new high for the period, even as the quantity of gold sold declined compared to the prior year. The average realized gold price per ounce rose to $3,936, up substantially year over year.

Costs increased, with the company’s all-in sustaining cost per gold ounce up 25% from a year ago. Management attributes roughly $80 per ounce of the increase to price-related royalties, indicating that much of the cost pressure is tied to positive gold price effects.

Operating cash flow before working capital improved sharply, but changes in short-term liabilities and deferred revenue led to a net cash outflow in working capital. As a result, net cash from operations dropped more than 50% year over year, ending the period at $57.3 million.

What Allied Gold does

Allied Gold is a gold mining company focused on exploration, development, and operation of mines in Africa, with key assets in Mali, Côte d’Ivoire, and Ethiopia.
