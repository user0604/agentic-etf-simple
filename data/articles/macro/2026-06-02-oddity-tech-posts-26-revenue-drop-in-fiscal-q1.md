Oddity Tech Posts 26% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:13 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Oddity Tech delivered a business loss as advertising disruptions drove a sharp contraction in new customer acquisition and profitability.
Adjusted diluted EPS moved from $0.69 last year to a loss of $(0.17) in the quarter.
Management expects another major revenue decline in Q2 but aims for improved profitability in the second half of the year.

Oddity Tech (NASDAQ:ODD) faced a difficult start to fiscal 2026 as severe disruptions in its digital advertising channels led to a drop in new customer growth and a steep fall in profits. The digital-first beauty and wellness company saw net revenue decline but did perform better than its own outlook for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (earnings released June 2, 2026). Overall, the quarter revealed serious financial and operational pressures, with only limited early signs of stabilization.

Notable Developments
The cost-per-acquisition (CPA) index for the main IL MAKIAGE brand soared 83% year over year, causing a sharp drop in first-time customer orders and new sales.
Despite these pressures, Oddity continued investing in its ODDITY Labs technology and launched a $200 million share buyback, repurchasing 6.1 million shares in the quarter.
Year over year, gross margin contracted over 5 percentage points and free cash flow swung from positive $87.3 million to negative $21.1 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects another major revenue decline of 25%–30% year over year for the second quarter, with some sequential improvement expected in the year’s second half. Adjusted EBITDA (a measure of profitability before certain charges) is guided to return to positive territory in Q2 but remain well below historical levels. The most important factor to watch is whether the advertising channel disruptions are resolved and if first-time customer growth recovers.

Quarterly Highlights

The largest driver of this quarter’s results was a sharp spike in acquisition costs through Oddity’s digital advertising platforms, with the cost-per-acquisition index for IL MAKIAGE (company’s core makeup brand) nearly doubling compared to last year. That led to a severe drop in new customer orders, which management says will weigh on follow-on sales for the rest of the year.

Oddity continued to focus on technological initiatives such as expanding its ODDITY Labs molecule discovery platform, but the period saw no new product launches or major technical breakthroughs reported. The digital-first model remained intact, with 98% of revenue coming from direct online sales, illustrating continued commitment to its core commerce approach.

On the financial side, the company closed the quarter with $667.4 million in cash and investments, even after deploying $82.3 million toward share repurchases. However, free cash flow turned negative, and gross margin fell notably, mainly because of elevated acquisition costs, changes in product mix, and lower average order values.

What Oddity Tech Does

Oddity Tech builds and scales digital-first beauty and wellness brands, using proprietary technology to personalize product recommendations and experiences for consumers online.
