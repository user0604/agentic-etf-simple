Tenaris (NYSE:TS) posted higher sales and profits as demand for its steel pipe products continued, but comprehensive income was impacted by negative currency swings and hedging results. The leading global supplier of steel pipe and related services to the energy industry shared its financial update for the first quarter (released May 7, 2026), covering results through March 31. Overall, the period showed incremental progress in revenues and cash flow, with some cost pressures and lower translated income from exchange-rate movements.

Notable developments
Revenue climbed by 6.1% year over year, reflecting steady demand for oil and gas industry products.
Gross profit increased 4.9%, and operating income was up 6.2%, but selling and administrative expenses also rose modestly.
Total comprehensive income declined 4% due to adverse currency and hedging effects, despite higher net profit for the period.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide formal guidance for the rest of fiscal 2026. Investors are watching for further signals about demand in core oil and gas markets, and monitoring whether currency swings or inflation could impact future quarters. Attention will also remain on cost control and liquidity.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure for the period climbed 6.1% over the prior year quarter, pointing to continued orders from energy sector customers for specialized steel tubing used in drilling and well operations.

Gross profit and operating results moved up slightly, though inflation and higher expenses in categories like selling, general, and administrative costs offset some of these gains. The company's gross margin (gross profit as a share of sales) held mostly steady, implying that cost discipline continued even as revenues rose.

Total comprehensive income retreated to $549 million, about 4% lower than last year, largely due to a swing in currency translation and hedging losses. Currency translation adjustment, an accounting line that tracks gains or losses on assets held in different currencies, was especially negative in this quarter compared to a gain last year.

What Tenaris does

Tenaris manufactures and sells steel pipe products and related services for the global oil and gas industry, with its core business supplying products used in exploration and production activities worldwide.
