Fidelis Insurance (NYSE:FIHL) delivered one of its strongest quarters in recent memory, with a marked improvement in underwriting results and robust book value growth. Notable Developments
Underwriting profitability improved sharply, with the combined ratio dropping by 29 percentage points to 86.6%, due largely to normalized catastrophe losses.
Book value per diluted share grew to $26.22, helped by $232.7 million in capital returned to shareholders, including share buybacks and an increased quarterly dividend of $0.15 per share.
Net premiums earned fell 5.7%, a result of slower revenue recognition on longer-earning specialty products and a strategic exit from less profitable business lines.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its commitment to "balancing profitable underwriting with meaningful capital returns" and continues to forecast steady mid-single-digit premium growth for the full year. Investors should watch for trends in the attritional loss ratio (reflecting ongoing, smaller claims), as its rise could weigh on future margins if not controlled.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability rebounded, with non-GAAP operating return on average equity reaching 15.2% following normalized catastrophe and large loss levels. The combined ratio, a measure of insurance profitability where lower is better, improved significantly, driven by fewer catastrophic events compared to last year's severe wildfires.

The company returned $232.7 million to shareholders, mostly through share buybacks at an average price of $19.00 per share, and raised the dividend to $0.15 per share. Book value growth was the strongest since the IPO, with a 7.2% increase in the period including dividends.

Segment trends varied: the insurance segment grew gross premiums written 13% to $1.44 billion, supported by new distribution partners, while the reinsurance segment saw lower premiums due to fewer one-time events. Management continues to focus on expanding specialty offerings and adjusting its portfolio for profitability.

What Fidelis Insurance Does

Fidelis Insurance provides specialty insurance and reinsurance solutions, focusing on complex risks such as property, energy, marine, aviation, and specialty treaty products across major international markets.
