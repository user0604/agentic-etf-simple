Online brokerage platform eXp World (NASDAQ:EXPI) posted a solid quarter as revenue grew and operational efficiency improved, while ongoing integration of a major acquisition marked a strategic shift. Notable Developments
Revenue rose 5% year over year to $1.0 billion, surpassing management’s guided range due to increased agent productivity.
Operating expenses decreased 3% year over year, aided by efficiency gains from the cloud-based platform and technology investment.
The NextHome acquisition was completed without taking on debt, expanding the firm’s geographic reach and market presence.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its full-year 2026 guidance, projecting revenue of $4.85 billion to $5.15 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $50 million to $75 million. For the next quarter, it expects revenue between $1.36 billion and $1.45 billion and operating expenses of $93 million to $97 million. Integration of NextHome and improvement in agent retention and satisfaction emerged as top priorities for the months ahead.

Quarterly Highlights

Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP financial measure) almost doubled year over year, reflecting improved operational discipline and further cost savings. Despite rising technology and development spending, expense control elsewhere supported bottom-line improvement and narrowed the net loss.

The agent Net Promoter Score, a key measure tracking satisfaction, dropped to 67 from 78 a year ago, underscoring retention risk and highlighting some integration friction following the NextHome deal. Agent count grew 1% relative to a shrinking industry base, but this pace was modest compared to prior years.

The NextHome acquisition marked a transformative expansion, boosting eXp’s market presence and adding a new franchise vehicle within its technology-driven ecosystem. Management funded the purchase with existing cash, retaining a healthy liquidity cushion and preserving its debt-free status.

What eXp World Does

eXp World provides a cloud-based real estate brokerage platform that connects agents and brokers digitally, offering them tools, technology, and a virtual infrastructure to manage transactions and grow their businesses.
