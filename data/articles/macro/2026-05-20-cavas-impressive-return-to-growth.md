CAVA's Impressive Return to Growth

Same-store sales dwarf all other news in the report.

Tim Beyers

May 20, 2026, 11:05 AM

Comments
10.0

Shares of CAVA Group (NYSE: CAVA) are up just about 5% in morning trading after the company reported impressive first-quarter results and tasty guidance.

Same-store sales dwarf all other news in the report. In Q1, CAVA grew comps by 9.7% year-over-year. That's an enormous improvement over Q4's disappointing 0.5% and much closer to the 10.8% same-store sales growth the company reported in last year's Q1.

Increased guest traffic drove 6.8% of this quarter's comps gain, which in turn led to a mouth-watering result on the top line. Overall revenue jumped 32.2%. versus a comparatively bland 21.2% last quarter and 28.2% in last year's Q1.

Unsurprisingly, attracting more hungry patrons to CAVA's growing store network led management to raise guidance.

For the full year, CAVA now expects to open at least 75 new stores this year (up from at least 74) and generate full-year comps of at least 4.5% (up massively from at least 3% previously).

So growth is back. Interestingly, it's back at a time when macro indicators suggest consumers with less will prioritize how they spend. A delicious Mediterranean bowl is apparently still near top of the list.
