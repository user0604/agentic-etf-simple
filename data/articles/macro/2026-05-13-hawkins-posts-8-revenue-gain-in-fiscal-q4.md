Hawkins Posts 8% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:26 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Hawkins delivered record revenue, but profit slipped on higher acquisition and finance costs.
Revenue rose 8% year over year.
Management expects growth in revenue, operating income, and earnings per share in the next fiscal year.

Hawkins (NASDAQ:HWKN) posted another quarter of revenue growth, as its Water Treatment business offset higher expenses and weakness in Food and Health Sciences. The diversified chemicals maker, which supplies water treatment, food, and industrial markets, reported results for the quarter ended March 29, 2026, with its earnings release issued on May 13, 2026. Overall, the period showed strong sales but thinner margins due to acquisition-driven costs.

Notable developments
Water Treatment segment sales grew 16% in the quarter, continuing as the main revenue engine and benefiting from recent acquisitions.
Net earnings per share declined 5.1%, affected by $4.4 million of increased costs tied to amortization and interest from the company's acquisition strategy.
Paid cash dividends of $0.75 per share for the year, a 7% increase over the prior year, marking 41 consecutive years of dividend payments.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects all three business segments to grow revenue and operating income in fiscal 2027, with an increase in earnings per share also anticipated. The company plans to use operating cash flow to balance growth investments and debt reduction. Investors should watch the ongoing integration of recent acquisitions, especially in Water Treatment, and monitor whether Food and Health Sciences can return to revenue and margin growth.

Quarterly highlights

Water Treatment, which includes chemicals, filtration, and systems for municipal and industrial clients, contributed half of all company revenue and drove most of the growth, up 16% for the quarter. Acquisitions, especially the purchase of WaterSurplus, expanded the segment's product offerings and geographic reach.

Profitability declined, as net earnings per share slipped from the prior year due mainly to higher costs. These included $4.4 million in amortization and interest related to financing acquisitions and increased fair value adjustments on earnout liabilities, factors management highlighted in the earnings release.

Cash flow from operations strengthened, supporting continued capital investments. Despite higher leverage, with debt up 64% over last year, management expects to reduce the leverage ratio to about 1x adjusted EBITDA by the end of fiscal 2027 through strong cash generation and disciplined capital spending.

What Hawkins does

Hawkins supplies specialty chemicals, ingredients, and related solutions for water treatment, food processing, health sciences, and industrial customers across North America.
