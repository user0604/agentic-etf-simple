Key Takeaways

Electrovaya (NASDAQ:ELVA) continued its profitable streak as it expanded US manufacturing and ramped up technology investments. The leading developer and producer of advanced lithium-ion batteries reported results for its fiscal second quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 15, 2026. The period showed steady growth in revenue and profits, but the company remains highly dependent on a small group of customers.

Overall, the quarter delivered further progress in operational expansion and technology development, but risks related to customer concentration and high capital spending remain significant.

Notable developments
Revenue from large-format batteries remains the primary driver, accounting for nearly all sales, while other categories contributed just $34,000 this quarter.
Two customers supplied 99% of total revenue and accounts receivable, raising elevated customer concentration risk.
Capital investment accelerated, with $21.7 million deposited for new equipment at the Jamestown, NY manufacturing facility to support ongoing US expansion.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue new quantitative guidance for upcoming quarters. However, it emphasized that strategic investments in manufacturing, energy storage, and robotic solutions are expected to underpin future growth. Investors should monitor progress in expanding the customer base, and the timeline for new product commercialization, especially in energy storage and robotics.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure for the quarter climbed 20%, supported by growing demand for the company’s large-format lithium-ion batteries, the main product powering material handling and industrial electric vehicles. United States customers accounted for 98% of sales, with minimal contributions from Canada and other markets.

Profitability improved as gross margin rose to $6.04 million ($6,037,000), reflecting the benefit from scale and incremental operational efficiencies. Operating income and net income grew at an even faster rate, while earnings per share held flat year over year due to a rising share count caused by new equity issuance.

Capital outlays intensified, driven mainly by construction and equipment deposits for the new Jamestown, NY facility. The company also increased research and development spending to support future technology offerings, including next-generation high-voltage battery systems and pilot activities in robotics and defense applications.

What Electrovaya does

Electrovaya designs, manufactures, and sells advanced lithium-ion batteries for use in materials handling vehicles, energy storage systems, and other industrial applications.
