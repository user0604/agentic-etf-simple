Earnings Preview: Clipper Realty

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Clipper Realty (NYSE:CLPR), which owns and manages apartment and commercial buildings in New York City, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Thursday, May 14, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Clipper Realty saw continued strong performance from its residential portfolio, with occupancy and rents at or near record highs. The company benefited from robust demand for rental housing in New York City, with nearly all stabilized residential properties leased and new leases signed at significantly higher rates than expiring ones. However, the quarter was affected by the loss of a major office tenant and the ongoing lease-up of a new development, which weighed on overall financial results. Compared to analyst expectations, the company’s Q4 results missed consensus estimates for both revenue and adjusted funds from operations (AFFO).

Positives
Residential leasing remained very strong: The company reported 99% occupancy across stabilized residential properties, with new leases signed at rates 13% above previous rents. This demonstrates continued demand and pricing power in its core apartment business.
Record rent levels achieved: Average rents reached all-time highs across the portfolio, including $89 to $95 per square foot at key Manhattan and Brooklyn properties.
Successful initial lease-up at new Prospect House development: The recently completed 953 Dean Street property reached 78% leased by quarter end, supporting future revenue growth as it approaches stabilization.
Strong rent collections: The company collected approximately 98% of billed residential rents, indicating a healthy tenant base and effective management of arrears.
Negatives
Loss of major office tenant impacted results: The termination of the New York City lease at 250 Livingston Street in August 2025 led to a $4.0 million revenue decline and a $3.8 million drop in net operating income (NOI) from that property, putting pressure on overall financials.
Lower overall revenue and AFFO: Total Q4 revenue fell to $37.1 million from $38.0 million a year earlier, and AFFO dropped to $1.7 million from $8.1 million, both missing analyst expectations due to the office vacancy and costs associated with lease-up at the new development.
Ongoing expenses from new development lease-up: The Prospect House project, while a future growth driver, contributed higher expenses than revenues in its early lease-up phase, reducing near-term profitability.
Unresolved office property challenges: The company continues to negotiate with lenders and potential tenants for its vacant 250 Livingston Street office property, with no resolution yet finalized as of quarter end.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Residential rent growth and occupancy trends: Investors should look for updates on whether high occupancy and double-digit rent increases have continued into early 2026, especially as the New York City rental market remains tight.
Progress on Prospect House lease-up and revenue contribution: The pace at which the new Dean Street property reaches full occupancy will be key for revenue growth and margin improvement in upcoming quarters.
Status of office property resolutions: Watch for updates on the 250 Livingston Street office property, including any progress on debt restructuring, sale negotiations, or new leasing activity, as this remains a significant overhang on results.
Dividend policy and cash position: With a high dividend yield and $30.8 million in unrestricted cash reported at year end, investors should monitor whether the company maintains its quarterly dividend and preserves liquidity amid ongoing property challenges.
Guidance versus consensus expectations: Analyst consensus for Q1 2026 calls for revenue of $39.6 million. Any company guidance or commentary on expected revenue, cost trends, or AFFO will be important for assessing whether Clipper Realty is on track to meet or exceed market expectations.
