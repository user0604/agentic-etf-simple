Notable Developments
Vehicle deliveries rose 2.5% year over year to 95,142 units, exceeding management’s guidance range of 85,000 to 90,000 vehicles.
The company’s gross margin fell sharply from 20.5% to 7.9%, driven mainly by an unfavorable product mix.
Free cash flow moved further into negative territory, reaching negative RMB 7.4 billion.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guided to a second quarter with lower deliveries and revenue compared to the prior year, forecasting 95,000 to 100,000 vehicles and RMB 24.1 billion to RMB 25.4 billion in total revenue. Leadership called out a continued focus on product innovation and operational improvements but warned that market competition and ongoing product transitions are likely to pressure financial results. Investors should closely monitor the ramp and market acceptance of the all-new L9 flagship and progress on stabilizing margins and cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

Vehicle deliveries in the period surpassed expectations, yet the growth rate slowed significantly versus last year, highlighting both successful execution and market pressures. Exceeding prior company guidance for unit deliveries suggests demand remains resilient, especially for its premium model lineup, but raises questions about profitability at current pricing levels.

Gross and vehicle margins both deteriorated sharply, with gross profit dropping by 66.0% compared to the prior year. Management attributed this to a shift in the product mix and raw material cost fluctuations.

Free cash flow turned deeply negative, reflecting both lower profitability and higher investment activity. Despite maintaining a robust cash position of RMB 94.3 billion at quarter’s end, negative operating and free cash flow highlight the importance of restoring margin and operational discipline as competition intensifies.
