QuinStreet (NASDAQ:QNST) delivered a strong business win in its latest quarter, fueled by robust demand in its Auto Insurance and Home Services segments. Notable developments

Home Services segment revenue increased 63% year over year, reaching $114.3 million. This growth followed the HomeBuddy acquisition and higher market demand.

Gross margin improved to 11.9%, up from 10.3% a year ago, as profit growth outpaced the increase in operating expenses.

Accounts receivable rose to $184.8 million from $135.8 million, reflecting more revenue billed but not yet collected and highlighting a key area to watch.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects revenue for the next quarter in the $350–$370 million range, with adjusted EBITDA of $37–$43 million—both would be new quarterly highs. The main area for investors to monitor is how well QuinStreet integrates recent acquisitions while managing client concentration and regulatory risk.

Quarterly highlights

Home Services was the main growth engine, with revenue soaring 63% year over year as the HomeBuddy acquisition added scale and digital reach. The company’s proprietary technology, especially its artificial intelligence optimization tools, expanded into new areas, boosting both revenue and operational efficiency across business lines.

Auto Insurance in the Financial Services segment also set new records, driven by stronger demand from insurance carriers and increased consumer shopping activity. While operating expenses rose by 36%, strong top-line growth and cost management led to an even faster increase in operating income and cash flow for the period.

QuinStreet maintained a positive net cash position despite rising working capital needs from the higher accounts receivable. Debt increased to support the acquisition, but the company kept capital spending and cash flow strong, providing financial flexibility for future initiatives.

What QuinStreet does

QuinStreet helps large brands in regulated industries acquire customers online by delivering targeted digital marketing and lead generation services.
