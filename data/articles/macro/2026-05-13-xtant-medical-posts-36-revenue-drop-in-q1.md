Xtant Medical Posts 36% Revenue Drop in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 7:15 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue and profit dropped sharply following non-core business sales and vanishing license income.
Revenue fell 36.5% year over year as margins and adjusted EBITDA turned negative.
Management raised 2026 revenue guidance, driven by new product and distribution contributions.

Xtant Medical (NYSEMKT:XTNT) posted a marked downturn as it moves away from old business lines toward a more streamlined platform. The spinal biologics and surgical solutions firm, focused on bone graft substitutes, wound care, and surgical instruments, delivered first-quarter results on May 13, 2026, reflecting revenue and margin contraction largely explained by its recent asset sales and lost licensing streams. Overall, the period featured significant downsizing, but left it positioned for growth from a higher-quality, innovation-driven business base.

Notable Developments
The company completed the sale of its Coflex/CoFix and international hardware assets, receiving $21.4 million to reduce debt and focus on biologics.
Revenue dropped to $20.9 million as license income vanished and product revenue declined after asset disposals.
Gross margin fell to 57.3% due to the absence of high-margin licensing, while operating expenses shrank alongside the company’s scaled-down operations.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $101–$105 million, citing new contributions from acquired hemostatic products and an expanded sales force. It stated that no additional outside capital is anticipated to be needed. Investors should watch execution around integration of new distribution deals and whether margin pressures persist as the higher-margin product mix is built out.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenue decline mainly stemmed from two factors: the strategic sale of non-core portfolios and the end of lucrative licensing agreements tied to amniotic membrane products. These changes repositioned the business on its biologics core, with “product revenue” now the primary source, but lower than before asset sales.

After finalizing its $21.4 million asset sale, the company funneled proceeds into paying down $13.3 million of debt and reduced both cash and debt balances. This left $12.2 million in debt and $12.1 million in cash at quarter-end, improving its liquidity position relative to liabilities.

In product innovation, it launched Trivium Shaped, a new bone graft matrix, and secured exclusive rights to distribute Dilon Technologies’ HEMOBLAST Bellows (a surgical bleeding control device) in the U.S. It also hired a dedicated, expanded sales team to support growth in this broader market.

What Xtant Medical Does

Xtant Medical develops, manufactures, and markets regenerative medicine products for orthopedic, neurosurgical, and wound care applications focusing on biologic implants and surgical instruments.
