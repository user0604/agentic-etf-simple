1. Could Meta's Next Act Be a Cloud Empire?

At Meta Platforms' (NASDAQ: META) most recent shareholder meeting, CEO Mark Zuckerberg confirmed a cloud computing business is "on the table" and that Meta has already fielded requests from outside companies seeking capacity. The company is recommended by both Team Hidden Gems and Team Rule Breakers. "Meta is aggressively pursuing AI opportunities," Team Hidden Gems contributor Dan Caplinger wrote this week, adding that "We expect CEO Mark Zuckerberg to accentuate the tangible results of Meta's AI efforts in the months and years to come."

Already getting calls: Meta's infrastructure is so large that outside companies have proactively approached it for cloud access, suggesting real demand exists before any product launches.
The ad-only problem: Meta still derives nearly all revenue from advertising. Peers Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN), Microsoft (NASDAQ: MSFT), and Alphabet (NASDAQ: GOOG) all run high-margin, fast-growing cloud businesses -- and Meta has the data center scale to compete.
2. Chip Giants Descend on Taiwan

Next week is a big one for chip investors: Computex Taipei (June 2 through June 5) brings together the CEOs of Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA), AMD (NASDAQ: AMD), and Intel (NASDAQ: INTC) -- all Stock Advisor recs -- as well as Qualcomm (NASDAQ: QCOM) for what could be a market-moving week.

Jensen's big number: Nvidia now spends upwards of $150 billion annually in Taiwan. That's up from $10 billion to $15 billion four years ago. Plus, Huang teased a mystery product at a dinner this week.
The rally is waiting: Analysts say Huang left investors cold on the last earnings call, with questions swirling around the Vera Rubin rollout and custom-chip competition from cloud giants. A strong Computex showing could set the stage for Nvidia's next leg higher.
3. SpaceX's Next Billion-Dollar Mission

In the latest sign that Elon Musk's rocket company has become one of the most important players in U.S. national security, pre-initial public offering (IPO) SpaceX just pocketed a $4.16 billion Space Force contract to build a space-based sensing layer that tracks and targets airborne threats.

Golden Dome or bust: The Pentagon's Golden Dome system combines space-based sensors, secure communication links, and ground processing to complement traditional airborne sensing.
A Fool's perspective: As for investing in SpaceX, whose IPO is coming up, Team Rule Breakers contributor Jim Mueller reminded Fools that IPOs are designed to drum up excitement. "But the harsh reality is that the vast majority of them end up losing money for investors over the following three to five years," Mueller said.
