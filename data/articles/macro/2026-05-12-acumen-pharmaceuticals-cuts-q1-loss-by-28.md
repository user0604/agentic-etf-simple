Notable Developments

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed it expects its current cash and investments to fund operations into early 2027, including all planned clinical and research activities.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s main research focus remains sabirnetug, a monoclonal antibody therapy designed to target toxic proteins involved in Alzheimer’s disease. Clinical trial enrollment for ALTITUDE-AD, its pivotal Phase 2 study, was completed earlier, and topline results are still expected in late 2026. There was no new efficacy or safety data disclosed, but management confirmed the study is on track and operating smoothly within previously communicated timelines.

On the financial side, improved cost discipline showed up in both research and administrative spending. R&D expenses, which form the bulk of operating costs, fell sharply to $16.5 million from $25.3 million as the company moved into less resource-intensive stages of its trial. General and administrative expenses also saw a moderate reduction due to lower legal and consulting fees.

Strategic partnerships took a step forward as the company confirmed plans to exercise its licensing option on up to two new clinical candidates through its collaboration with JCR Pharmaceuticals in the coming quarter. This marks progress toward broader research efforts, including the development of new brain delivery technologies scheduled for regulatory submission in mid-2027.
