Key Takeaways
CalciMedica recorded a business win with a return to net profitability, driven by one-time fair value adjustments. EPS improved from a loss to $0.30, reflecting a substantial swing year over year. The company’s outlook now depends on imminent FDA guidance for Auxora and its clinical pipeline.

Biopharma company CalciMedica (NASDAQ:CALC) posted a dramatic return to profitability this quarter, benefiting from significant non-operating gains. The developer of novel therapies for serious inflammatory conditions, such as acute pancreatitis and acute kidney injury, released its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 12, 2026. Overall, the period saw lower operating expenses and a swing to positive net income, though sustained profitability will depend on pipeline and regulatory progress.

Notable Developments
The company reported net income of $5.0 million this period, compared to a net loss of $5.0 million in the prior-year quarter, primarily due to $10.8 million in fair value gains related to financial instruments.
Research and development expenses fell 16.5%, reflecting reduced activity in clinical trials and manufacturing controls.
Management expects its $8.2 million cash position to fund operations into the fourth quarter of 2026. No new partnerships or collaborations were announced.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted upcoming decisions from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration as critical for Auxora’s pivotal program in acute pancreatitis and future plans in acute kidney injury. Investors should watch closely for regulatory feedback in the second quarter, which may determine timing and future funding needs for core programs.

Quarterly Highlights

Net profitability resulted almost entirely from a one-time, non-operating boost: the fair value adjustments on financial instruments added $10.8 million to other income, offsetting an ongoing operating loss from business activities. This headline profit does not indicate sustained profitability from operations.

The company continued to advance its pipeline, most notably with Auxora for acute pancreatitis. Recent Phase 2b study results published in eClinicalMedicine highlighted meaningful clinical benefits, including reductions in time to food tolerance and new-onset severe respiratory failure. Pending FDA feedback will guide the pivotal trial design, a crucial near-term milestone.

The phase 2 trial for acute kidney injury (AKI) was paused for safety review, then cleared of drug-related toxicity concerns. The program’s future now awaits further regulatory guidance; progress here could expand Auxora’s clinical value but remains uncertain.

What CalciMedica Does

CalciMedica develops therapies aimed at treating severe inflammatory and immune-mediated diseases by targeting CRAC channels, with a lead candidate in clinical development for acute pancreatitis and acute kidney injury.
