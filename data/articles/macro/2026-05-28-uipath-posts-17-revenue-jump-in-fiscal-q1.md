Automation software provider UiPath (NYSE:PATH) achieved a business win in the first quarter as expanding platform adoption and careful cost management produced its first-ever Q1 operating profit and higher sales across core segments. The company, a leader in enterprise automation software, released results for the quarter ended April 30, 2026, on May 28, 2026. Notable Developments
Revenue grew 17% over the prior year as licenses, subscriptions, and services all posted gains; licenses reached $149.3 million, and subscriptions were $252.9 million.
The company recorded its first-ever Q1 operating profit of $28 million on a GAAP basis, a turnaround from a loss in the prior year period.
Annualized Renewal Run-Rate, a key measure of recurring revenue, grew to $1.90 billion, while net new ARR additions slowed to $49 million, and the net retention rate was 109%.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its full-year revenue guidance to a range of $1.776 billion to $1.781 billion, signaling confidence in continued growth and operational discipline. However, near-term guidance points to a sequential dip in second quarter revenue and a modest outlook for new ARR. Investors should watch the pace of ARR growth and changes in customer retention, which have both shown early signs of slowing from past highs.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter saw major gains in both revenue and profit, with total sales reaching $418 million and Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share climbing to $0.15. Improved cost management contributed to the first positive operating income in a Q1 for the company, while operating cash flow rose to $132 million.

New product launches included the "UiPath for Coding Agents" platform for deploying software automation, deeper integrations with cloud and AI partners such as Databricks and Google Cloud, and security automation features with Microsoft. These innovations targeted industry-specific needs and expanded the company's offerings, helping to drive existing customer upselling rather than large gains in new customer additions.

Annualized Renewal Run-Rate, a recurring revenue metric that sums up all expected annualized billings from ongoing contracts, increased by 12% compared to the prior year. Net additions to ARR came in lighter than previous quarters at $49 million, while dollar-based net retention rate rose to 109 percent. The company also continued substantial share buybacks, spending heavily but maintaining no debt on its balance sheet.

What UiPath Does

UiPath provides enterprise software and cloud-based solutions that automate repetitive business tasks using artificial intelligence and robotic process automation.
