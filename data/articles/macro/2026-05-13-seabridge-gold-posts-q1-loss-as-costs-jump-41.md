Overall, the quarter reflected solid operational focus, though profit declined and funding options narrowed, setting the stage for continued watch on liquidity and capital structure.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management continues to focus on advancing project construction, particularly KSM, and progressing the Courageous Lake spin-out. However, with the ATM share issuance program now exhausted and future funding needs significant, investors should watch closely for updates on strategic partnerships or new financing. Upcoming decisions on the renewal or replacement of large secured notes could be the next key event for liquidity planning.

Quarterly highlights

Exploration and construction-related investment increased, with $26.9 million directed towards KSM mineral interests and equipment.

Administrative and corporate costs rose sharply, up 41% year over year, mainly driven by higher stock-based compensation and swelling professional fees. This contributed to the reversal from prior-year profit to a net loss in the quarter.

On capital management, the company closed out its ATM equity financing after issuing over 759,000 shares and raising $35.8 million, but will need to identify new funding avenues for continued development. Extensions granted for secured notes give it more time for project financing, but potential future dilution and sensitivity to metal prices remain significant risks.
