Key Takeaways
Disciplined cash management and innovation offset weak core whiskey demand.
Revenue rose year over year in the fourth quarter.

Premium spirits maker Brown-Forman (NYSE:BFA) posted stable results as new product launches and efficient cash flow helped balance continued weakness in its core Jack Daniel’s whiskey business. The company, which produces well-known whiskey, tequila, and ready-to-drink products for global consumers, shared its earnings for the quarter ended April 30, 2026, in a release dated June 4, 2026. The period showed healthy cash generation but highlighted ongoing pressure in its largest brand family and a muted outlook for the coming year.

Notable Developments
Revenue for the quarter increased 2%, supported by innovation such as Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Blackberry and strong ready-to-drink cocktail sales.
Operating income dropped 53%, largely due to higher impairment charges and increased selling, general, and administrative expenses.
Gross margin improved by 5.3 percentage points, aided by product mix, lower costs, and positive foreign exchange impacts.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects flat organic sales and operating income to decline by 3% to 5% in fiscal 2027. It will focus on extracting benefits from recent cost controls and the launch of new products, but highlights ongoing "macroeconomic pressures" in developed markets. Investors should watch for performance in the Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Whiskey line and the ready-to-drink portfolio to gauge recovery in core brand strength.

Quarterly Highlights

Despite new product momentum, the core Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Whiskey product saw volume decline by 3% for the full fiscal year ended April 30, 2026. Ready-to-drink cocktails, especially the New Mix brand in Mexico and the U.S, delivered standout 41% sales growth for the full fiscal year, providing some offset to the whiskey category’s declines.

Impairment charges rose sharply as the company wrote down values of recently acquired gin and rum brands, highlighting risks in its acquisition strategy. Gross margin improvement was notable, but operating income and diluted earnings per share both fell year over year, reflecting higher costs and valuation adjustments.

The company continued to generate strong cash flow, more than doubling its free cash flow from the prior year, and returned substantial capital to shareholders through both cash dividends and share repurchases. The quarterly dividend was raised 2% to $0.2310 per share.

What Brown-Forman Does

Brown-Forman is a global producer and marketer of alcoholic spirits and ready-to-drink cocktails, best known for Jack Daniel’s Tennessee Whiskey, and also offers tequila, gin, and other premium brands in over 170 countries.
