Notable developments
Revenue climbed 22.4%, driven in part by positive pricing adjustments on zinc concentrate sold to its single U.S. customer.
General and administrative costs surged 138%, with additional spending on graphite demonstration and feasibility projects, weighing on profitability.
Titan Mining recorded a $13.2 million fair value loss on derivative financial instruments, which was the core reason for the net loss in the quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management will prioritize expanding operations at the Empire State Mine and moving forward on graphite feasibility work. Upcoming quarters will hinge on managing project costs, reducing cash outflows, and navigating risks from financing-related derivatives. Investors should closely watch for changes in cash position and progress on the graphite project as potential indicators of future stability.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue gains were supported by favorable zinc sales pricing finalizations and a recovery from negative price adjustments that affected the same period last year. All reported sales came from zinc concentrate delivered to a single U.S. customer, reinforcing Titan Mining's continued reliance on its core asset and market.

Profit from mine operations climbed 135.8% year over year, but escalating administrative and graphite project costs reduced these operating gains. The company newly spent $905,000 on graphite demonstration activities and $1.37 million on graphite feasibility studies in the quarter, highlighting strategic moves into battery materials but adding short-term cost pressure.

The largest financial swing stemmed from a $13.2 million loss tied to revaluing derivative financial instruments. This loss, primarily associated with special warrant conversions, outweighed mine profits, causing Titan Mining’s net results to move sharply negative even as underlying assets and mine operations expanded.

What Titan Mining does

The company owns and operates the Empire State Mine, producing zinc, and is expanding into graphite mining and processing through new demonstration and feasibility projects.
