Earnings Preview: Prestige Consumer Healthcare

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 5:03 PM

Comments

Prestige Consumer Healthcare (NYSE:PBH), a manufacturer and marketer of over-the-counter health and personal care products, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

For the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Prestige Consumer Healthcare reported revenue of $283.4 million, reflecting a 2.4% decrease compared to the prior year. The company’s gross margin remained steady at 55.5%. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $1.14, which was in line with management’s prior guidance and met analyst expectations. The company generated $209 million in free cash flow year to date, up 13% from the previous year, and continued its disciplined capital allocation by repurchasing shares and completing the acquisition of Pillar Five. The quarter was marked by ongoing supply challenges in the eye care category, particularly with the Clear Eyes brand, but sequential supply improvements were noted for the second consecutive quarter. Management reiterated confidence in its diversified business model and maintained its full-year outlook for revenue, EPS, and free cash flow.

Positives
Stable gross margins despite revenue decline: Gross margin held at 55.5%, reflecting effective cost management and a favorable product mix in North America, even as sales softened in some categories.
Strong free cash flow generation: Year-to-date free cash flow reached $209 million, up 13% from the prior year, supporting share repurchases and the Pillar Five acquisition without increasing leverage.
Progress in eye care supply chain: The company reported sequential improvements in Clear Eyes supply for the second quarter in a row, aided by the completed Pillar Five acquisition and new third-party suppliers.
Disciplined capital deployment: Over $150 million in shares were repurchased year to date, demonstrating management’s commitment to shareholder returns and flexibility in capital allocation.
Negatives
Revenue and EPS declined year over year: Both revenue and adjusted EPS were down compared to the prior year, mainly due to ongoing supply constraints in eye care and softness in categories like analgesics and cough and cold.
Supplier loan write-off: The company wrote off a $10.3 million supplier loan after a third-party manufacturer shut down, highlighting risks in supply chain relationships.
Increased general and administrative expenses: G&A costs rose due to higher bad debt allowances for one customer and timing of certain expenses, pressuring profitability.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Clear Eyes supply recovery and production ramp: Investors should monitor updates on supply chain improvements and the impact of the Pillar Five acquisition, as management expects continued sequential supply gains and a gradual restocking of retail shelves.
Sales trends across key categories: Watch for signs of stabilization or recovery in categories that were weak in Q3, such as analgesics and cough and cold, as well as ongoing strength in gastrointestinal and oral care products.
Free cash flow and capital allocation: The company has guided to at least $245 million in free cash flow for the full year. Any updates on share repurchases, debt reduction, or new acquisition activity will be important for understanding management’s priorities.
Retailer and consumer order patterns: The company has cited ongoing volatility in retailer ordering and consumer channel shifts. Investors should look for management commentary on whether these trends are easing or expected to persist into the next fiscal year.
Guidance for fiscal 2027 and product pipeline: Any preliminary outlook for fiscal 2027, especially regarding Clear Eyes supply normalization and new product launches (such as the recent Compound W SkinTag extension), will be key for assessing growth prospects.
