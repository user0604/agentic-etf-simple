Earnings Preview: Petco Health and Wellness

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Petco Health and Wellness (NASDAQ:WOOF), a national pet supply and services retailer, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Petco Health and Wellness reported net sales of $1.52 billion, a decrease of 2.4% compared to the prior year. Comparable sales declined 1.6% for the quarter. These results were in line with the company's outlook and met analyst expectations for sales. Despite the sales decline, the company delivered improved profitability, with adjusted EBITDA rising 10.6% to $106 million and adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 7.0%. Operating profit increased 83% year over year, and free cash flow for the year improved significantly. The company ended the quarter with 1,382 stores in the U.S. and continued to focus on closing underperforming locations to support profitability.

Positives
Improved profitability and cash flow: Adjusted EBITDA increased by over 10% in Q4 and by 21% for the full year, reflecting successful cost management and margin expansion.
Lower leverage and stronger balance sheet: The company reduced its net debt to EBITDA ratio from 4.2 times to 3.0 times by year-end, increasing financial flexibility.
Disciplined inventory management: Inventory was down 9.7% year over year, supporting the company’s improved free cash flow and reducing risk of excess stock.
Operating margin expansion: Operating margin improved by 98 basis points in Q4, showing the impact of expense controls and a focus on higher-margin sales.
Negatives
Declining sales and comparable sales: Net sales fell 2.4% and comparable sales declined 1.6% in Q4, reflecting the company’s strategy to exit unprofitable sales but also indicating ongoing top-line pressure.
Net store closures: The company closed 16 net stores in FY2025, which contributed to the sales decline and may limit near-term growth opportunities.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Sales trajectory and comp sales inflection: Management guided for Q1 net sales to be down 1% to flat, with comparable sales roughly flat at the midpoint. Watch for signs of stabilization or early growth as new initiatives roll out.
Progress on growth initiatives: The company is launching new national brands, expanding fresh food offerings with more in-store freezers, and increasing the frequency of product launches. Early traction in these areas could drive improved customer engagement and sales.
Services business momentum: Petco is focused on increasing productivity at existing veterinary hospitals and growing its grooming and training services. Investors should look for updates on service revenue growth and utilization rates.
Omnichannel and loyalty program updates: The company is piloting a revamped loyalty program and enhancing digital capabilities. Any commentary on customer adoption or digital sales growth will be important for assessing long-term strategy execution.
Expense discipline and margin sustainability: With adjusted EBITDA guidance of $92 million to $94 million for Q1, monitor gross margin and SG&A trends to see if profitability improvements are sustainable as the company invests in growth.
