Quarterly Highlights

The company posted record revenue growth, with sales from its RenovoCath device surging as the number of active commercial cancer centers doubled from 8 at year-end to 16 by early May. This expanded customer base reflected increasing early-stage adoption but still represents a modest fraction of the company’s estimated $400 million peak U.S. market opportunity.

Despite improved gross profit margin, administrative expenses rose significantly, offsetting savings in research and development. As a result, the net loss increased, and the company remains unprofitable as it scales commercial operations and seeks continued customer ramp-up for sustainable growth.

Enrollment in the company’s pivotal Phase III TIGeR-PaC clinical trial advanced as planned, reaching over 90% completion by mid-May. Final trial results, expected by mid to late 2027, are a critical milestone for possible regulatory expansion and broader device usage in cancer treatment.
