RingCentral (NYSE:RNG) improved on its operational discipline this quarter, posting record cash flow and stronger margins as it capitalized on demand for AI-powered communication tools. The leading provider of cloud-based communication and collaboration platforms saw its core subscription revenues remain robust while it made progress on strategic AI and partnership initiatives. The results announced on May 7, 2026, cover the first quarter of fiscal 2026 and reflect a period of solid execution but only moderate overall revenue growth.

Overall, the quarter displayed notable improvement in profitability and expense control, but highlighted the need for faster expansion in non-subscription revenue lines.

Notable developments
Free cash flow rose to $141 million, driven by higher operating profits and lower costs, marking a new quarterly record for the company.
Subscription revenue was $623 million, making up 97% of total revenue; this segment grew 6% year over year, while non-subscription business saw flat to declining results.
Paid AI product revenues more than doubled year over year and now represent over 10% of RingCentral’s annual recurring revenue.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised full-year 2026 guidance for revenue, margins, and free cash flow, signaling increased confidence in execution. The company projects total revenue between $2.62 billion and $2.64 billion for the year. The most important area for investors to monitor is the scale and monetization of AI-powered product lines and their effect on overall subscription growth.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s subscription revenue climbed to $623 million, confirming the strength of its cloud communications platform even as total revenue growth slowed to just above 5%. Management noted that nearly all revenue now comes from subscription services, with "other" revenue remaining flat or down year over year.

Non-GAAP operating margin improved by 1.1 percentage points, reflecting ongoing reductions in stock-based compensation, tighter operating expenses, and an increase in higher-margin AI product sales. Free cash flow rose to $141 million, fueled by increased collections and more efficient operations.

Several new AI-driven product launches, including AIR Pro and expanded international messaging capabilities, contributed to customer adoption. Share repurchases totaled $81 million, and the company declared its second quarterly dividend of $0.075 per share.

What RingCentral does

RingCentral provides cloud-based software for business communications, including messaging, video meetings, phone, and AI-powered contact center tools.
