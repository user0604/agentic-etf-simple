Key Takeaways
Ampco-Pittsburgh reported higher sales but slipped to a quarterly loss as costs and restructuring charges weighed on earnings.
Revenue increased 3.9% year over year.
Management expects improving demand and profitability as the year progresses.

Ampco-Pittsburgh (NYSE:AP) faced mixed results this quarter, achieving higher sales but posting a net loss as profitability lagged behind revenue gains. The industrial manufacturer, which supplies engineered steel products and thermal equipment for industrial and power generation markets, released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 12, 2026. Overall, the period showed progress in top-line growth and cash flow, but underlying profit margins and earnings remain under pressure from ongoing restructuring and operational headwinds.

Notable Developments
Air and Liquid Processing segment sales jumped 17.3%, thanks to strong orders in defense and power markets.
Forged and Cast Engineered Products segment profitability declined as unfavorable shipment mix and lingering shutdown costs continued to affect results.
Free cash flow improved significantly, with operating cash flow turning positive after last year's outflow.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects better demand and margin recovery in the coming quarters, pointing to a rising backlog and stronger order flow. The company stands by its earlier guidance that margins, especially in the Forged and Cast Engineered Products segment, will improve by the second half of 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

The Air and Liquid Processing segment delivered record adjusted operating income, driven by higher volumes and improved product mix, particularly for nuclear heat exchangers and naval aftermarket items. This performance helped offset broader cost and margin challenges across the company.

The Forged and Cast Engineered Products segment continued to feel the effects of last year’s steel industry downturn and costs from facility closures, leading to lower sales and operating margins this quarter. While European operations improved, U.S. volumes were flat, and overall segment profitability remains below normalized levels.

Free cash flow improved substantially, supported by better working capital management and positive operating cash flow. However, net debt increased modestly year over year, meaning continued focus on cash generation and debt reduction will be important for future results.

What Ampco-Pittsburgh Does

Ampco-Pittsburgh manufactures engineered steel rolls, custom air handling systems, and heat exchange equipment for the metals, industrial, defense, and power generation sectors.
