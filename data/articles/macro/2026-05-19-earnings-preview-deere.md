Earnings Preview: Deere

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Deere (NYSE:DE), a leading maker of agricultural and construction equipment, is expected to report its fiscal second-quarter earnings on Thursday, May 21, 2026, before the market opens.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Deere delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations for both earnings per share and revenue. Net sales and revenues rose 13% year over year to $9.611 billion, with equipment operations net sales up 18% to $8.001 billion. Net income attributable to Deere & Company was $656 million, or $2.42 per diluted share. The company’s diversified business segments—Production & Precision Agriculture, Small Agriculture & Turf, and Construction & Forestry—all posted higher net sales compared to the prior year, with particularly strong growth in Small Ag & Turf and Construction & Forestry. However, operating margins were pressured by higher tariffs and an unfavorable sales mix, especially in the Production & Precision Agriculture segment.

Positives
All major segments posted higher sales year over year: Small Agriculture & Turf and Construction & Forestry saw net sales increase by 24% and 34% respectively, reflecting strong demand in these markets.
Results beat analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings: Third-party coverage confirms that the quarter’s performance was ahead of Wall Street expectations, signaling operational outperformance.
Order books and retail activity strengthened: The company reported growing order banks, especially for construction equipment and small ag tractors, providing visibility into future quarters.
Progress in inventory management: Used equipment inventories, particularly in North America, continued to decline, supporting a healthier replacement cycle.
Negatives
Operating margins declined in Production & Precision Agriculture: Margins dropped to 4.4% from 11.0% a year ago, due to higher tariffs, unfavorable regional and product mix, and increased warranty costs.
Tariff expenses weighed on profitability: Incremental tariffs totaled $361 million pretax in the quarter, impacting cost of sales and overall margins.
Net income decreased year over year: Despite higher sales, net income fell compared to the prior year, affected by tariffs and the absence of prior period tax benefits.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Margin recovery in Production & Precision Agriculture: Management expects double-digit margins to return in this segment for the remainder of the year as North American production ramps up and sales mix improves. Investors should watch for evidence of this margin rebound in Q2.
Tariff relief and cost mitigation: The U.S. Supreme Court recently invalidated certain tariffs, which could provide future cost relief. Deere is still evaluating the impact, so any updates or financial effects in Q2 will be important to monitor.
Order trends and backlog visibility: Deere’s order books for large tractors and combines now extend into the fourth quarter. Watch for any changes in order velocity or backlog, especially as it relates to North American and South American ag markets.
Construction & Forestry segment momentum and new product launches: The segment is benefiting from strong infrastructure spending and recently completed the acquisition of Tenna (completed in Q1), which enhances digital fleet management capabilities. The launch of new Deere-designed excavators is also underway. Progress on these initiatives and their impact on sales and margins will be key areas to watch.
Status of legal and regulatory matters: Deere remains in preliminary discussions with the Federal Trade Commission regarding the right-to-repair lawsuit. Any updates on this matter or related regulatory developments could affect future costs and business practices.
