Earnings Preview: LivePerson

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

LivePerson (NASDAQ:LPSN), a provider of customer messaging and artificial intelligence (AI) solutions for enterprises, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, LivePerson delivered results that outperformed its guidance on both revenue and adjusted EBITDA. Revenue for the quarter was $59.3 million, which came in above the high end of the company’s forecast. Adjusted EBITDA reached $10.8 million, also exceeding expectations. These results reflected improved cost controls, renewed commercial traction with enterprise customers, and early success with new product launches. The company noted strong adoption of its core Conversational Cloud platform and the successful launch of Syntrix, its new AI assurance platform. Management highlighted progress in customer renewals, particularly in regulated sectors like financial services and telecommunications, and pointed to deepening partnerships, especially with Google Cloud.

Positives
Revenue and adjusted EBITDA beat guidance: Both top and bottom line results exceeded the company’s own forecast, signaling improved operational execution and cost discipline.
Successful launch and early traction for Syntrix: The new AI assurance platform moved from early access to general availability, with initial paying customers in banking, telecommunications, and technology sectors. This positions LivePerson for growth in the emerging enterprise AI governance market.
Major customer renewals and expansions: The company renewed contracts with several large enterprises, including seven financial services institutions, two airlines, three telecom providers, and a major healthcare provider. Over 40% of these renewals included expanded commitments, supporting revenue stability.
Strengthening partnerships, notably with Google Cloud: LivePerson secured a multimillion dollar renewal and upsell through the Google Cloud Marketplace. This channel is expected to become a significant revenue contributor by the end of 2026.
Negatives
Year-over-year revenue decline persists: Total revenue from hosted services fell 15% compared to the prior year, reflecting ongoing customer churn and downsells.
Net revenue retention remains under pressure: Net revenue retention dropped to 78% from 80% in the previous quarter, indicating continued challenges in retaining and expanding existing customer spend.
Professional services revenue contracted sharply: This segment declined 36% year over year, which may limit future cross-sell and upsell opportunities.
Guidance implies further revenue declines in 2026: Management’s outlook points to a continued decrease in revenue through the year, with growth not expected to resume until the second half.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Revenue trajectory and customer retention: Investors should monitor whether revenue stabilizes near the company’s guidance range of $53 million to $55 million, and if net revenue retention shows improvement after recent declines.
Syntrix adoption and commercial impact: The pace of new customer signings and upsells for the Syntrix platform will be a key indicator of LivePerson’s ability to capture growth in AI assurance and governance.
Progress on platform modernization: The company remains on track to complete its multiyear shift to a unified architecture in the first half of 2026. Completion of this project is expected to support higher AI traffic and improve platform resiliency.
Partnership channel momentum, especially with Google Cloud: The contribution of marketplace-driven renewals and expansions, as well as any signs of new business from this channel, will be important for future growth.
Merger agreement with SoundHound AI: On April 21, 2026, LivePerson announced a pending merger with SoundHound AI. Investors should watch for updates on the regulatory process, expected timeline, and any impact on LivePerson’s operations or financials.
