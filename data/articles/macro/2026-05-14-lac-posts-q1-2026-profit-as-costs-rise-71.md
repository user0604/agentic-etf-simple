Notable Developments
Net income swung to a profit due to derivative and interest gains, masking underlying operating losses and higher project expenses.
Operating expenses increased 70.8%, driven by expanded hiring, community investment, and higher regulatory and professional fees over the period.
The company raised $189.7 million from new share issuance and drew $432 million from a federal loan to strengthen liquidity for the Thacker Pass build.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed guidance for mechanical completion of Phase 1 at Thacker Pass late in 2027, with capital expense guidance held steady at $1.3 to $1.6 billion for fiscal 2026. Executives also cautioned about exposure to $80 million to $120 million in potential tariffs, which could increase overall costs if not offset. The most important issue for investors to follow next is the continued execution of project milestones and cost containment amid ongoing construction and global supply chain uncertainty.

Quarterly Highlights

Construction activities at the Thacker Pass project reached a new phase, with over 1,300 workers on site and procurement exceeding 70% of planned equipment and materials. Over 75% of structural steel orders were sourced and delivered or in transit, despite disruptions in global shipping routes.

The company achieved a total of 2.43 million workhours at Thacker Pass without any serious or lost-time accidents, highlighting project safety according to company data. These statistics are critical for project continuity and regulatory compliance.

Lithium Americas closed the quarter with $1.2 billion in cash and restricted cash, supported by both government loan draws and new share offerings. This liquidity provides a resource base to continue construction without interruption, but the company reported a substantial increase in operating expenses as it moved further into active development and compliance activities.

What Lithium Americas Does

Lithium Americas is a mineral development company working to bring its Thacker Pass lithium mining project in Nevada into production, aiming to supply lithium for batteries used in electric vehicles and energy storage.
