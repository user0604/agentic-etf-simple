Slide Insurance Secures Record $5.5 Billion Reinsurance Limit

Slide Insurance completed its 2026-2027 catastrophe reinsurance program, securing a record $5.463 billion in aggregate coverage to buffer against major hurricane losses. The program represents a significant expansion of the company’s safety net as it grows its footprint in storm-prone markets.The insurer increased its first-event catastrophe reinsurance limit to $3.981 billion, a $1.424 billion jump over the previous treaty year. This move allows the Florida-based company to maintain financial stability while underwriting more policies in catastrophe-exposed regions like Florida and South Carolina.

SLDE Company Overview
Slide provides property and casualty insurance for coastal homes and condominiums using a proprietary technology platform to analyze risk and issue policies. The company generates revenue by underwriting specialty insurance products and collecting policy fees through independent agents and direct consumer channels. Integrating extensive datasets and automated systems allows Slide to offer a streamlined experience for distribution partners and policyholders in underserved regions.
