Semiconductor designer and manufacturer Semtech (NASDAQ:SMTC) delivered a business win this quarter, with expanding sales in its target markets. The company makes chips and connectivity solutions that enable advanced data centers, Internet of Things (IoT), communications, and industrial infrastructure. In its release dated May 26, 2026, for its first quarter of fiscal 2027, Semtech posted higher revenue and adjusted earnings, reflecting focused execution across its business lines. Overall, the quarter marked progress in sales and profitability, though operating costs remain an area for continued monitoring.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects higher sales and margin improvements for the next quarter, targeting record net sales and increased profitability. The focus remains on accelerating growth in data center and LoRa wireless platforms, as well as careful management of operating expenses amid elevated investment. Investors should monitor the company’s ability to translate new product launches into further gains while controlling costs.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth was broad-based but especially pronounced in the Signal Integrity business, which benefited from strong demand in the data center market and new product introductions aimed at high-capacity networking.

Research and development spending increased, mirroring the company’s ongoing push to innovate in advanced chip technologies. This led to higher operating expenses, offsetting much of the gross profit improvement achieved from scale and improved product mix.

The company devoted capital to building inventories and integrating recent technology acquisitions, such as indium phosphide laser solutions, setting the stage for expanded capabilities and market reach in the coming quarters.

What Semtech Does

Semtech designs and sells analog and mixed-signal semiconductor chips used in data centers, wireless communications, IoT devices, and industrial equipment.
