Key Takeaways
Net income rose on strong net interest income, but loan growth remained modest.
Earnings per share increased year over year.

Community-focused bank Union Bankshares (NASDAQ:UNB) posted higher profits as growth in interest income more than offset increased operating costs. The Vermont- and New Hampshire-based lender reported its results on May 7, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The period showed improved earnings, asset growth, and stability in credit quality, while expense growth and slower loan expansion became notable trends.

Notable Developments
Net income and book value per share both rose, driven by growth in interest income and steady credit costs.
Total loans increased by 1.3% to $1.18 billion, signaling slow loan demand despite a generally stable economy.
Noninterest expense grew 9.8% to $10.8 million, reflecting investments in staff and technology.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management issued no forward financial targets or detailed guidance for upcoming quarters. Instead, it described local market conditions in Vermont and New Hampshire as stable, with moderate inflation pressures and steady employment. Investors should watch expense trends, the pace of core loan growth, and the impact of future interest rate shifts on interest income and funding costs.

Quarterly Highlights

Profits increased as net interest income (the difference between interest earned on loans, securities, and similar assets, and what the bank pays on deposits and borrowings) climbed year over year. The bank pointed to a larger asset base and higher yields as the main reasons for this growth, with total assets now at $1.63 billion.

While asset quality stayed steady, the loan book grew modestly from $1.16 billion to $1.18 billion. This slow growth came even as the bank maintained active lending, including selling $24.1 million in mortgage loans during the period.

Operating expenses, including salaries, benefits, and technology costs, went up nearly 10%. Management described these outlays as investments to support operations and future growth. However, rising expenses may place pressure on profits if income growth slows.

What Union Bankshares Does

Union Bankshares is a bank holding company that provides retail and commercial banking services, primarily serving customers in Vermont and New Hampshire through local branches.
