Key Takeaways
Monster Beverage posted strong sales growth and set a new quarterly revenue record. Earnings per share (non-GAAP) rose 23.7% over the prior year. The company did not provide new financial guidance for upcoming periods.

Monster Beverage posted a strong business win as it achieved record quarterly sales, driven by robust demand for its energy drink products and accelerating international growth. The leading global energy beverage company released its first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026, reporting broad-based growth but also some pressure on profit margins due to higher input costs. Overall, the quarter delivered excellent expansion in core products and international sales, though profitability faced some challenges.

Notable Developments
International net sales increased 44.9%, now accounting for 45% of total revenue for the period.
Gross profit margin, excluding Alcohol Brands, fell by 1.8 percentage points due to higher aluminum and freight-in costs.
The Alcohol Brands segment continued to struggle, reporting a 5.9% drop in net sales year over year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not give specific financial guidance for upcoming quarters, but emphasized ongoing growth efforts in core products and product innovation. The focus remains on expanding globally and adapting to input cost pressures. The most important area to watch is whether the company can maintain its profit margins as costs and the global mix change.

Quarterly Highlights

The Monster Energy Drinks segment spearheaded growth, with net sales rising 27.6% to $2.19 billion, supported by higher volumes and new product launches. Strategic Brands, which include affordable energy drink lines, saw sales climb 28.9%, while international markets became a larger piece of total revenue.

Profit margins faced some decline as adjusted gross margin (non-GAAP, excluding Alcohol Brands) slid from 57.1% to 55.3%, with the company stating that higher aluminum and freight-in expenses contributed most to this drop. Nonetheless, operating income and net income both saw double-digit percentage increases compared to the prior year.

While core energy drinks and affordable brands performed well, challenges persisted in the Alcohol Brands and Other segments, which continued to report falling sales. Share repurchases totaled $100 million in the quarter, signaling ongoing shareholder returns even as no dividend is paid.

What Monster Beverage Does

Monster Beverage produces and markets energy drinks and alternative beverages, with a portfolio including Monster Energy, Reign, Bang, and other brands sold globally.
