Notable Developments
Net income reached $9.9 million, a meaningful improvement from a $13.7 million net loss in the prior-year quarter, supported by improved vessel utilization and profitable fleet transactions.
Total voyage revenue stood at $111.5 million for the period, including $104 million from third parties and $7.5 million from related parties, reflecting a focus on both external and platform-driven chartering.
Operating cash generation improved sharply; net cash provided by operating activities rose to $18.9 million, reversing cash outflows seen last year and bolstering liquidity.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management withheld formal guidance for the rest of the fiscal year, citing ongoing industry volatility and customer concentration risk. The company pointed to continued uncertainty around freight demand, market rates, and the pace of wind-down in trading activities. Investors should watch for further developments in charter contract renewals, customer mix, and additional transactions impacting the owned and chartered-in fleet.
