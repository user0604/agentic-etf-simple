Earnings Preview: Netskope

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Netskope (NASDAQ:NTSK), a provider of cloud-based security and networking software for businesses, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Netskope delivered results that exceeded analyst estimates on both revenue and profitability. The company reported revenue of $196 million, representing 32% growth year over year. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $811 million, up 31% from the prior year. Net new ARR was a record $57 million for the quarter. The company achieved a net retention rate of 116%, indicating strong customer expansion and low churn. Gross margin improved to 76%, and operating margin improved by five percentage points to negative 10%. Notably, Netskope generated $4 million in free cash flow for the quarter and $12 million for the full year, marking its first year of positive free cash flow. The average number of products per customer increased to 4.4, and 56% of customers now use four or more products from the Netskope One platform.

Positives
Revenue and ARR growth outpaced expectations: Both revenue and ARR grew over 30% year over year, beating analyst consensus and reflecting strong demand for Netskope’s platform.
First year of positive free cash flow: The company generated $12 million in free cash flow for fiscal 2026, demonstrating improved financial discipline and operating leverage.
Gross margin expansion driven by efficiency: Gross margin rose to 76%, helped by the scaling of the NewEdge private cloud network and operational improvements.
Strong customer expansion and retention: Net retention rate of 116% and growth in large customers show that existing clients are increasing their use of Netskope products.
Negatives
Operating margin remains negative: Despite improvement, the company reported a negative operating margin of 10%, reflecting continued investment in growth and innovation.
Guidance reflects near-term headwinds: Management cited ongoing investments and a transition to annual billings, which are expected to pressure free cash flow and margins in the first half of FY2027.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Revenue growth in the context of guidance: Management guided for Q1 revenue between $197 million and $199 million, which implies a sequential slowdown in growth. Investors should watch whether Netskope meets or exceeds this range, especially as new sales reps ramp up productivity.
Free cash flow impact from billing transition: The company expects negative free cash flow of $50 million to $60 million in Q1 due to the shift to annual billing on multiyear contracts. This is a one-time adjustment, but the scale of the impact and pace of recovery will be important to monitor.
Adoption and performance of new AI-native products: Netskope announced several new AI security modules and its AI Index in Q4. Early traction, customer feedback, and contribution to ARR from these products will be key signals of the company’s ability to capitalize on the AI security trend.
Progress on sales rep ramp and go-to-market initiatives: Many new sales reps are expected to reach full productivity in the second half of the year. Updates on hiring, ramping, and pipeline development will help gauge the sustainability of growth into the rest of FY2027.
Customer expansion and multiproduct adoption: The average number of products per customer and the percentage of customers using multiple products are both rising. Watch for continued momentum in cross-selling and upselling, as these drive higher retention and revenue per customer.
