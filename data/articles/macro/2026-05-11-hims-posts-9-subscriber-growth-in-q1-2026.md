Hims & Hers Health (NYSE:HIMS) posted another quarter of subscriber gains, but costs surged and profitability declined sharply. The consumer health and telemedicine provider reported first-quarter results on May 11, 2026, for the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter showcased robust international expansion and subscriber growth, but U.S. revenue contracted and margins deteriorated.

Notable Developments
International revenue reached $78.2 million, up 969% from the prior year off a low base, while U.S. revenue contracted 8% to $529.9 million.
Gross margin fell to 65% from 73%, as the company booked $33.5 million in restructuring charges and a $15 million legal settlement related to weight loss product changes.
Monthly revenue per average subscriber dropped 6% year over year to $80, even as total subscribers grew by 9%.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to $2.8–3.0 billion, signaling confidence in accelerating growth for the remainder of the year. Second-quarter guidance calls for $680–700 million in revenue—about 12–15% sequential growth—yet adjusted EBITDA guidance of $35–55 million signals ongoing margin pressure. Investors should watch whether U.S. revenue reaccelerates and if the company can restore margins amid continued expansion and operational complexity.

Quarterly Highlights

Subscriber count reached 2.58 million, up nearly 9% from the previous year, continuing the company's expansion and market penetration focus. However, average monthly revenue per subscriber fell to $80, reflecting a shift in product mix—especially a move into branded weight loss medications and a broader customer base.

International operations delivered a near tenfold increase in revenue, bringing Rest of World to 13% of total sales. This growth was driven by acquisitions and new market rollouts in Europe and Canada, though these markets have not yet achieved profitability.

Profitability metrics weakened as costs rose: gross margin dropped 8 percentage points, and adjusted EBITDA fell by half. The period included $33.5 million in restructuring charges related to a pivot from compounded to branded GLP-1 weight loss drugs, plus a $15 million legal settlement and elevated spending on technology, operations, and legal needs.

What Hims & Hers Health Does

The company provides telehealth services, subscription products, and pharmacy fulfillment focused on personal health and wellness needs.
