HURN Company Overview
Huron provides professional consulting and digital services to large organizations within the healthcare, higher education, and various commercial sectors globally. The company generates revenue by advising these clients on financial improvements and implementing specialized technology systems to manage their complex data. Integrating proprietary software and recurring managed services into daily operations creates long-term relationships and significant barriers to changing providers.

Huron Grows Consulting Revenue as Digital Demand Softens

Huron Consulting Group helps healthcare, education, and commercial organizations improve performance through consulting and digital services. The company stands out for its strong healthcare consulting work, which now makes up over half its revenue. Over the last 5 years, the stock has returned 134.7%, beating the S&P 500 by 62.0 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue before reimbursable expenses (non-GAAP): $443.7 million (up 12.1% YoY)
Adjusted EPS (non-GAAP): $1.73 (up 3.0% YoY)
Number of revenue-generating professionals: 5,346 (up 12.6% YoY)

Latest Developments

Management kept its full-year growth outlook, but rising costs and softer digital demand could pressure future earnings and margins.
