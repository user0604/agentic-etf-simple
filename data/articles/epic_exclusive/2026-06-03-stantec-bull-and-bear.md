Stantec Bull and Bear

The Motley Fool Team

3:06 PM

Comments

Stantec Inc. (NYSE:STN) is a global provider of sustainable engineering, design, and consulting services spanning infrastructure, water, environmental, and buildings markets. The company’s growth strategy balances organic expansion, acquisition-driven scale, and geographic diversification, aiming to capitalize on worldwide infrastructure renewal, public sector investment, and secular demand for resiliency solutions. With a focus on operational excellence and prudent capital allocation, Stantec pursues long-term outperformance through market leadership and disciplined execution.

Potential Bull Cases
1. Multi-Year Revenue and Margin Growth Validates a Compounding Earnings Base

Sustained double-digit revenue and profit expansion positions Stantec for attractive long-term shareholder returns. In fiscal 2025, Stantec delivered net revenue growth of 10.7% year over year (to $6.5 billion Canadian dollars), while adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) rose nearly 17% and margins reached 17.9%, exceeding strategic targets a year ahead of schedule. This accelerating trend continued into Q1 2026 with a 9.1% net revenue increase and 14.7% adjusted earnings per share (EPS) growth. The company’s ability to expand not only its top line but also drive margin leverage—achieving 70 basis points of EBITDA margin expansion in the latest quarter—demonstrates operational discipline and the scalability of its business model. As management continues to outperform their published financial targets, Stantec appears well positioned to compound long-term earnings, supporting shareholder value with consistent double-digit annual growth.

2. Diversified Backlog Anchors Future Revenue and Shields Against Sector Volatility

A record $9.0 billion backlog provides high revenue visibility and resilience against cyclical downturns. At the end of Q1 2026, Stantec reported its largest-ever backlog—equivalent to 13 months of net revenue—which anchors near-term performance and confirms strong underlying demand. Backlog growth has been broad-based, with wins across water, infrastructure, and buildings, and is driven by both public sector projects and regulated industries globally. This forward book not only cushions the company from unexpected market contractions but also provides compelling evidence of strong client relationships, repeat business, and continued success in securing major contracts like the Greater Western Water program in Australia. Investors benefit from this revenue visibility, which supports management’s confidence in meeting or exceeding guidance and reduces earnings risk during periods of macroeconomic uncertainty.

3. Market Leadership Enables Pricing Power and Expands Global Opportunities

Stantec’s track record of winning flagship contracts in key markets reinforces its premium status and supports above-average growth. The company’s role as a preferred vendor for major water, infrastructure, and mission-critical facility projects—such as the multi-billion pound Scottish Water enterprise in the United Kingdom and significant data center design mandates in the United States—demonstrates competitive strength and brand recognition. Stantec’s global reach enables it to pursue high-value contracts in growth regions, compete successfully in joint ventures (such as with Jacobs in Australia), and capture synergies from cross-border expertise. This market positioning allows the company to secure attractive project terms, maintain high utilization rates, and consistently deliver superior margins compared to less diversified or regionally focused peers.

4. Prudent Capital Allocation and Robust Cash Flow Drive Total Shareholder Returns

Disciplined capital strategy translates strong earnings into rising dividends and potential share buybacks. In 2025, Stantec’s free cash flow conversion of 1.3x net income and a 43% increase in operating cash flow illustrate operational quality and sound financial management. The board continued its long-standing dividend growth track record—raising the dividend by 8.9% to $0.98 per share (annualized)—reflecting confidence in the business’s underlying health. With modest leverage (1.3x net debt/adjusted EBITDA), Stantec retains flexibility to pursue further acquisitions or opportunistic share repurchases, enhancing total shareholder return. This balanced capital deployment model provides downside protection while rewarding investors through both income and capital appreciation.

5. Secular Infrastructure and Sustainability Tailwinds Support Multi-Year Demand

Global megatrends in public infrastructure spending, water security, and sustainability fuel long-term growth potential. Stantec’s core markets are buoyed by rising investment in aging infrastructure, environmental resilience, and advanced manufacturing across North America, the United Kingdom, and Australia. Regulatory support and government stimulus, coupled with a growing focus on the energy transition and climate adaptation, provide durable demand for engineering, design, and consulting services. Multi-year programs—like United States federal infrastructure acts, the U.K.’s water sector modernization, and defense-related development in Canada—create long-lasting revenue streams and insulate Stantec from short-cycle market swings. By aligning its business with these tailwinds, the company is positioned to benefit from above-average sector growth for years to come.

Potential Bear Cases
1. Artificial Intelligence May Accelerate Margin Pressure Across the Consulting Industry

Advances in artificial intelligence risk eroding traditional engineering profit pools faster than expected. Although Stantec management has described AI as a tool for efficiency and quality control, there is growing market concern—reflected in recent negative share price reactions—that automation could commoditize parts of engineering and design, leading to fee compression and competitive pricing. While Q1 2026 results showed earnings resilience, the longer-term risk is that disruptive technologies could reduce billable hours and alter customers’ willingness to pay for premium consulting services, especially as AI capabilities advance and clients gain access to alternative solutions. If margin headwinds intensify industry-wide, Stantec’s profitability could face structural challenges, limiting future earnings growth and shareholder returns.

2. Overreliance on Public Sector Funding Introduces Political and Budgetary Risk

Heavy exposure to government and regulated clients exposes revenue pipelines to policy shifts and funding delays. Stantec’s backlog and ongoing growth are tied closely to public infrastructure and regulated-sector spending, which can be susceptible to changes in government priorities, election cycles, and fiscal constraints. For instance, U.S. federal infrastructure funds are subject to annual allocation decisions and shifting legislative agendas, while economic downturns may force governments to defer or scale back project awards. Although current backlog levels are record-high, an adverse change in public sector commitment could slow new contract inflows or trigger heightened project delays, putting earnings targets at risk.

3. Acquisition Integration and Global Expansion Create Execution Risks That Could Disrupt Growth

Expansion into new markets and integration of multiple acquisitions heighten operational and cultural complexity. Stantec’s disciplined approach to mergers and acquisitions has supported historical growth and diversification, but as the company continues to acquire new businesses and expand geographically, it must navigate risks such as cultural misalignment, overextension of management resources, and failure to realize anticipated synergies. If future acquisitions are less accretive or integration challenges emerge, expected benefits such as margin expansion and cross-selling may not fully materialize. In a global operating environment marked by inflation and supply chain disruptions, any stumbles in project delivery or control could compromise margins and erode investor confidence in the growth model.
