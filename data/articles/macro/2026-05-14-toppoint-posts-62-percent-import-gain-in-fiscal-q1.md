Toppoint (NYSEMKT:TOPP) delivered strong gains in its newer business lines, while its main waste paper unit saw a decline. The logistics and commodity transport provider reported notable expansion in import and metal transportation segments in results released on May 14, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period highlighted both the benefits and costs of rapid diversification, as operating losses widened despite incremental efficiency gains.

Notable Developments
Import revenue rose 61.8%, mainly from acquiring new customers and boosting shipment volumes before new tariffs.
Waste paper revenue, the largest segment, fell 20.2%, reflecting lower domestic recycling output.
Net cash used in operations improved by roughly $93,650 year over year, helped by fewer tax and working capital outflows.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects continued strength in import and metal transportation for the balance of fiscal 2026 and is raising service prices to respond to cost pressures. They highlighted ongoing investments in logistics technology, which they believe will provide a competitive advantage. Investors should focus on whether higher prices and technology initiatives will translate into restored margins and improved earnings in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Import activities continued to expand, lifting import revenue 61.8% as new markets such as Houston and the Gulf Coast contributed meaningful growth. This result was tied to new customer wins and equipment that allowed for flexible container handling, helping offset ongoing sector volatility.

Scrap metal transportation—which covers the movement of non-ferrous metals used by domestic and foreign mills—posted revenue growth of 164.8%, confirming robust demand as international buyers increased orders.

Despite these wins, waste paper transportation—a key legacy business—declined 20.2%. The company attributed this to a reduction in domestic recycling volume and industrywide softness in recycled commodities.

What Toppoint Does

Toppoint delivers logistics and freight services for recycled materials—including waste paper and metals—alongside import, brokerage, and specialty trucking services across North America.
