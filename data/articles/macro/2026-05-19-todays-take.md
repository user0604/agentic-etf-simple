1. Amazon: Buying and Selling for the Wrong Reasons

By Tim Beyers
TEAM RULE BREAKERS

In late 1999, I decided to parlay my interest in tech into a fresh position in shares of Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN). Co-founder Jeff Bezos had been named Time’s Person of the Year!

Dumb idea. Amazon crashed, and I sold very near the bottom. I bought for the wrong reason (look at the hype!) and sold for the wrong reason (the stock is terrible, the business must be too!).

The lesson I’ve never forgotten: Patience matters. Had I just held on and studied what was happening at Amazon, I’d have emerged with six figures of excess returns within a few years. Thankfully, I’ve since been rewarded by holding my nerve with great businesses whose stocks crashed.

2. Don’t Be the Gambler

By Jason Moser
TEAM RULE BREAKERS

Remember that little thing called the Great Recession? I sure do. Many financial institutions found themselves in very dangerous territory, and there was even talk of nationalizing the banking system. Washington Mutual was on the ropes, and I decided to take a (small) gamble. Fast-forward, and we all know what happened. It was the largest bank failure in the history of the FDIC. I knew going in that what I was doing was dumb. I had no concept of how deep the bank’s problems ran. I was gambling, plain and simple. And while my loss was very minimal, it serves as a great reminder even to this day that when it comes to investing, it pays to avoid a gambling mentality.
