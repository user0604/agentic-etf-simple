Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 8.6% compared to the prior-year quarter, reaching $12.47 billion driven by higher pipeline volumes.
Adjusted EBITDA dropped 3.2% year over year to $730 million, reflecting continued weakness in the natural gas liquids segment.
The quarterly cash distribution per unit was raised 10% to $0.42, continuing a multi-year pattern of dividend growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA target by $130 million to reflect a constructive oil macro environment and extended ownership of its Canadian NGL business into May 2026. The sale of its Canadian natural gas liquids business is expected to close in May 2026, with leverage forecasted to improve after proceeds are used for debt reduction. Investors should pay close attention to how quickly debt levels return to the company’s stated target range following the NGL divestiture.

Quarterly Highlights

Crude oil operations showed strength, with segment EBITDA rising 4% and total crude pipeline volumes increasing 10% over the prior year. The company noted that volume growth in the Permian Basin, spurred by acquisitions like the Cactus III pipeline, offset some contract resets that reduced rates on legacy assets.

The natural gas liquids segment remained a drag on results as adjusted EBITDA slumped 23%, largely due to weaker market prices and lower volumes caused by unseasonably warm weather. This segment’s pending sale is expected to make the company a focused crude oil midstream provider later in 2026.

Plains kept capital spending controlled, investing $102 million in growth and maintenance projects for the quarter. At the same time, it maintained a capital allocation plan that prioritizes returning cash to unitholders and lowering debt, even as leverage finished the quarter just above the long-term target range.

What Plains All American Pipeline Does

The company operates a network of pipelines, storage terminals, and processing facilities that transport, store, and market crude oil and natural gas liquids across North America.
