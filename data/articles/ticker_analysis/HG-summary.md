HG Company Overview
Hamilton provides specialty insurance and reinsurance, which involves taking on complex financial risks from other insurers and commercial businesses. The company operates through global platforms offering coverage for specialized areas such as cyber security and marine activities. It generates revenue from policy premiums and investments, using in-house analytics software and a strategic investment relationship to price risks accurately and maintain long-term financial stability.

Hamilton Insurance Returns More Cash to Shareholders

Hamilton Insurance Group offers insurance and reinsurance for commercial property, casualty, and specialty risks around the world. The company stands out by returning large amounts of cash to shareholders, including a special dividend this quarter. Over the last two years, the stock returned 141.3%, beating the S&P 500 by 98.1 percentage points.
