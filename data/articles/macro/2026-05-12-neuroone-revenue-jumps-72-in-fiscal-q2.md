NeuroOne Medical Technologies (NASDAQ:NMTC) delivered strong product sales growth, but ongoing losses continued to weigh on its cash position. The medical device company develops thin film electrode technology and radio frequency ablation systems for neurological disorders. For the second quarter of fiscal 2026 (results released May 12, 2026), it reported robust revenue growth and steady costs, though cash burn and the absence of license revenue remain concerns. Overall, the quarter showed notable top-line momentum but highlighted the need for continued execution and funding through the rest of the year.

Notable developments
Product revenue climbed 72% year over year, driven by higher sales of OneRF products.
Total operating expenses were nearly flat at $3.4 million, reflecting careful cost control as the company grows.
Cash decreased to $2.8 million, highlighting continued cash burn and the need to accelerate collections or explore new funding if revenue increases do not materialize.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year sales growth goal. The company remains focused on commercial expansion of the OneRF ablation systems, advancing its drug delivery device, and progressing toward ISO 13485 certification to enable international market access. The key metric to watch is whether product revenue accelerates in the second half to meet these goals while preserving cash.

Quarterly highlights

Product sales growth was strong in the period, with much of the increase coming from higher adoption of the OneRF Trigeminal Nerve Ablation System for facial pain and continued use of its brain ablation platform. Clinical results for these systems included reports of all patients achieving pain relief in recent procedures.

Gross margin, which measures profitability after direct product costs, slipped to 53.8%, slightly below the prior-year period. This modest decline suggests some pricing pressures or higher production costs as volumes have increased.

Cash and equivalents fell from $3.6 million at December 2025 to $2.8 million at the end of March 2026. The company’s balance sheet requires close attention as net losses continue, and future fundraising may be needed unless accounts receivable are quickly converted to cash.

What NeuroOne Medical Technologies does

NeuroOne Medical Technologies is a medical device company that develops and sells thin film electrodes and ablation systems for diagnosing and treating neurological disorders such as epilepsy, chronic pain, and brain tumors.
