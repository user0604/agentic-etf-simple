Earnings Preview: Burlington Stores

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Burlington Stores (NYSE:BURL), a leading U.S. off-price retailer focused on branded apparel and home goods, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings before the market opens on Thursday, May 28, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Burlington Stores delivered a strong finish to fiscal 2025. Total sales for the fourth quarter rose 11% year over year, building on a 10% increase in the prior year. Comparable store sales increased 4%, which exceeded both internal guidance (0% to 2%) and analyst expectations. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $4.99, up 21% from the previous year and also above consensus estimates. Operating margin expanded by 100 basis points, driven by higher merchandise margins and supply chain efficiencies. The company ended the quarter with $2.2 billion in liquidity and a total store count of 1,212.

Positives
Sales and EPS beat expectations: Both revenue and adjusted EPS for Q4 exceeded analyst estimates, which drove a positive market reaction and a notable increase in the stock price after the release.
Operating margin expansion: Operating margin improved by 100 basis points, reflecting successful cost control and gross margin gains, even in a challenging tariff environment.
Elevation strategy success: The strategy to offer more recognizable brands at higher price points led to a mid-single-digit increase in average unit retail and higher customer spending per transaction.
Strong liquidity and inventory position: Burlington ended the quarter with $2.2 billion in liquidity and deliberately increased store inventory by 12% to prepare for anticipated higher demand in early 2026.
Negatives
Tariff-driven assortment gaps limited some sales: The company intentionally reduced exposure to home and gifting categories most affected by tariffs, which held back potential sales in key holiday segments.
Margin-driven sales tradeoff: Actions to protect earnings growth from tariff impacts led to lower sales in certain categories, highlighting a necessary but limiting tradeoff between margin and top-line growth.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales performance versus guidance: Management guided for 2% to 4% comp store sales growth in Q1, with optimism for upside driven by resilient customer demand and higher tax refunds. Actual results will show if this momentum is sustained.
Gross margin and supply chain costs: The company expects some margin pressure in Q1 due to markdown timing shifts and start-up costs for the new Savannah distribution center, which is scheduled to open in Q2. Watch how these factors impact profitability early in the year.
Progress on Burlington 2.0 and localization initiatives: Store Experience 2.0 remodels and expanded regional merchandising capabilities are ongoing. Updates on the rollout and early impact of these programs will be important for long-term growth.
New store openings and relocations: Burlington plans to open 110 net new stores in fiscal 2026, with 60% of these openings in the first half. Investors should look for updates on the pace and performance of these new locations and ongoing store downsizing efforts.
Inventory levels and off-price merchandise availability: The company increased inventory at the end of Q4 to chase higher expected demand in Q1. Watch for commentary on inventory turnover, quality, and the overall off-price buying environment, as these will influence sales and margin trends for the rest of the year.
