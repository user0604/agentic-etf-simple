SeaStar Medical Revenue Jumps 69% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
SeaStar Medical posted strong revenue growth but continued to record net losses.
Revenue rose 69% over the prior year quarter.
Management aims for $2 million in net product revenue in 2026 and continued pediatric market expansion.

SeaStar Medical (NASDAQ:ICU) showed significant expansion in its pediatric market for the quarter, but continued to operate at a loss despite solid revenue growth. The developer of medical devices for severe kidney and inflammatory diseases added several top children's hospitals as customers, increasing its market footprint. The company released its first quarter 2026 results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter demonstrated progress in commercial adoption and clinical trial advancement, though financial sustainability remains a concern.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 69%, driven by the addition of 7 new pediatric hospital customers, up from 10 to 17 sites.
Cash dropped to $9.35 million at quarter end after a $2.63 million quarterly cash burn, highlighting the importance of future financing or accelerated revenue growth.
Enrollment for the NEUTRALIZE-AKI adult trial reached 198 of 339 patients, moving the company closer to potential adult market approval.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects approximately $2 million in net product revenue for fiscal 2026. Management plans to broaden QUELIMMUNE adoption to include 15 additional top-ranked children’s medical centers by year-end. Plans include completing adult clinical trial enrollment and seeking broader regulatory approvals.

Quarterly Highlights

The company experienced robust pediatric market expansion, with 7 top-ranked children’s hospitals added as customers, a jump exceeding 60% quarter over quarter. The total customer count now stands at 17, consistent with management’s goal to more than double pediatric sites during the year.

SeaStar Medical completed required enrollment in its SAVE registry for pediatric therapy and reported new published data. No device-related safety issues were found, and patient survival rates supported previous findings of a near 50% reduction in loss of life compared to historical benchmarks.

For the adult market, enrollment in the NEUTRALIZE-AKI pivotal trial advanced to over 58% completion, positioning the company for a future regulatory submission. While this marks progress, full commercial expansion into the much-larger adult market is unlikely before 2027 when results and regulatory reviews are expected.

What SeaStar Medical Does

SeaStar Medical develops and commercializes medical device therapies for acute kidney injury and other severe hyperinflammatory conditions, focusing on unique filtration devices for hospital critical care settings.
