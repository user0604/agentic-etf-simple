Earnings Preview: Genesco

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

Genesco (NYSE:GCO), a specialty retailer focused on footwear and accessories for teens and young adults, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Friday, May 29, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

Genesco delivered a strong finish to fiscal 2026, with fourth-quarter results that beat analyst expectations for both earnings per share (EPS) and revenue. The company reported adjusted EPS of $3.74 and revenue of $800 million, both above consensus estimates. Comparable sales increased 9% in the quarter, led by double-digit growth at Journeys, Genesco’s largest business. Store traffic and conversion improved, especially during the peak holiday weeks. Digital sales also accelerated, particularly at Schuh in the U.K., where e-commerce penetration exceeded 50% of sales. However, Schuh’s profitability was pressured by heightened promotions. The company ended the year with a clean inventory position and positive momentum heading into fiscal 2027.

Positives
Journeys delivered double-digit comp growth and profit improvement: Journeys’ comparable sales rose 12% in Q4, building on a strong prior year. This outpaced the broader footwear market and reflected the success of its strategy targeting style-focused teens, especially girls.
Store optimization and cost discipline supported margins: The company closed 42 net stores over the year, which was accretive to operating income. Additional cost actions, including rent reductions and efficiency improvements, helped leverage selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses.
Strong cash flow and balance sheet position: Genesco generated $164 million in free cash flow in Q4 and ended the year with a positive net cash position, providing flexibility to invest in growth initiatives.
Digital and omnichannel capabilities continued to expand: E-commerce accounted for over 25% of direct-to-consumer sales, and the company added new omnichannel features and loyalty members, supporting multichannel growth.
Negatives
Schuh gross margin declined due to heavy promotions: Schuh’s focus on clearing inventory in a highly competitive U.K. market led to increased promotional activity, which weighed on profitability.
Genesco Brands Group faced sales and margin pressure: The wind-down of Levi’s and other licenses, along with tariff impacts, contributed to a 4% sales decline and a significant drop in operating margin for this segment.
Operating margin improvement was modest at the company level: Despite higher sales and cost actions, overall operating margin increased only 10 basis points year over year, reflecting ongoing challenges in certain segments.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Journeys comp sales momentum and impact of new store formats: Investors should look for updates on whether Journeys can sustain mid-single-digit comp growth early in the year and how the rollout of 80 additional 4.0-format stores is affecting traffic, conversion, and sales mix.
Schuh’s gross margin recovery and promotional reset: Management is prioritizing margin improvement at Schuh by reducing discounting. Watch for signs that these efforts are starting to show up in the quarterly results, even if sales are pressured in the short term.
Progress on cost savings and SG&A leverage: With continued store closures and technology investments, it is important to monitor whether Genesco can maintain cost discipline and offset any deleverage from flat sales.
Inventory management and channel mix: The company exited Q4 with clean inventories. Investors should check if this continues, especially as Genesco invests in key items for Journeys and manages lower inventory at Schuh and Brands Group.
Updates on new brand launches and licensing transitions: The Wrangler footwear launch is planned for later in the year. Any early commentary on initial orders, distribution strategy, or timing for mass-market expansion could be significant for future growth in the Brands Group segment.
