Notable Developments
Infrastructure revenue climbed 35.1% on strong commercial steel fabrication activity, but gross margin compressed by about 1.4 percentage points.
The Life Sciences segment achieved a key European regulatory milestone for MediBeacon but saw revenue drop by nearly half year over year.
Spectrum segment revenue continued to decline as network losses and muted advertising dampened results; over 60 new license applications were filed in the period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated confidence in Infrastructure’s project pipeline but maintained a strong focus on progressing with planned divestitures. Looking ahead, the company flagged execution on asset sales and commercial traction in the Life Sciences segment—especially mediation of losses and translating regulatory wins into sales—as the areas investors should monitor most closely.

Quarterly Highlights

Infrastructure performance led group results, with revenue surging to $357.9 million and net income doubling compared to last year’s quarter. Although profit improved, margin pressure emerged due to project mix and cost factors, making future trends in profitability a critical watch point.

The Life Sciences segment, which develops novel healthcare and medical technologies, saw MediBeacon secure a CE mark (an important regulatory approval required to sell in Europe) for its kidney monitoring system. Still, the segment’s revenue shrank year over year, and reductions in operating loss reflected mainly lower costs and fewer equity losses rather than commercial sales momentum.

Spectrum—the group’s broadcast operations—posted weaker results as network terminations and challenging advertising conditions weighed on revenue and profit. Over 60 new license applications aimed to expand the broadcast footprint, and further strategic partnerships and new technology launches are slated for the second half of 2026.
