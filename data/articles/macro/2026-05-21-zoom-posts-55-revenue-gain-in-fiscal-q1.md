Key Takeaways
Zoom Communications posted a solid quarter with continued strength in its enterprise business. Revenue rose 5.5% compared to the same period last year.

Zoom Communications (NASDAQ:ZM) notched another business win as ongoing enterprise growth and monetization of AI-powered features propelled its latest results. The global video-first unified communications company serves organizations of all sizes with video, phone, and collaboration tools. The company’s quarterly update, released May 21, 2026, covers the first quarter of fiscal 2027 and showed steady progress as enterprise sales and profitability improved while online growth remained slow.

Overall, the quarter brought both advancement in core areas and fresh signals about evolving industry challenges, with new AI uptake balanced against a maturing online business.

Notable Developments
Enterprise revenue, which is sales from large business customers, grew 7.2% year over year to $755.7 million, outpacing the company's total revenue growth.
Profitability improved with a 4.5 percentage-point jump in operating margin, helped by growing adoption of AI-powered products and new features.
Online segment growth slowed to 2.8%, and average monthly churn—the rate at which users cancel services—increased slightly to 3.0%.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year revenue forecast modestly to a range of $5.08–$5.09 billion and kept its free cash flow target unchanged. Leadership stated it will focus on further expanding enterprise sales, continuing AI innovation, and maintaining robust cash generation. Investors should monitor how well the company monetizes its AI-centric offerings and manages churn in the online segment.

Quarterly Highlights

Growth continued in large business accounts, with the number of customers spending over $100,000 annually rising 8.2% to 4,534. This group is key since it accounts for much of the overall revenue growth and is a major focus for the company.

Zoom’s AI product momentum was evident, marked by a 184% increase in paid users of its AI Companion feature, and over 1.5 million licensed users of its new My Notes tool just months after launch. Management credited these products with boosting customer retention and engagement, though the specific dollar impact of AI sales remains undisclosed.

Profitability and cash flow also rose, with free cash flow up 8.0% and strong margin gains. The company’s buyback program expanded, with $1.0 billion in new authorization, indicating continued use of cash for shareholder returns.
