Key Takeaways
Zhihu improved non-GAAP profitability and controlled costs, but revenue declined.
Gross margin increased from the prior quarter, while revenue fell 10.7% from last year.

Online platform Zhihu (NYSE:ZH) made progress on profitability and cost control, even as its revenue continued to decline. The company operates China’s largest question-and-answer-based content community, where users share knowledge and experiences across a wide range of topics. Reporting first quarter results on June 3, 2026, for fiscal 2026, Zhihu posted stronger non-GAAP net income and improved margins compared to the previous quarter, but both revenue and user numbers fell compared to the same period last year. Overall, the quarter showed operational improvement but ongoing challenges in monetizing users and growing revenue.

Notable Developments

- Total revenue decreased by 10.7% compared to the prior year, mainly due to lower paid content and other service revenues.

- Gross margin improved from the previous quarter, reaching 59.6%, but remained below last year’s level.

- Non-GAAP net income rose to RMB17.2 million as the company reduced operating expenses, especially in research and marketing.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management emphasized continued discipline in cost control and further integration of artificial intelligence into operations. For the coming quarters, Zhihu aims to strengthen profit margins and develop new AI-driven products. Investors should watch for signs of renewed revenue growth or a sustained increase in paying subscribers, as these will be important for future recovery.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue declined across all major segments, with paid content and intellectual property operations down and “other” services falling by nearly half. This reflects ongoing pressure as Zhihu shifts away from vocational training and legacy business lines.

The company cut total operating expenses by 10.4% year over year, with significant reductions in research and marketing spending, which helped boost non-GAAP net income.

Average monthly subscribing members increased to 13.1 million from 12.2 million in the previous quarter, but this figure remains below last year’s level, showing continued challenges in converting engagement into paid growth.

What Zhihu Does

Zhihu operates a user-driven online platform in China where people share and discover in-depth knowledge and experiences through questions and answers on a wide range of topics.
