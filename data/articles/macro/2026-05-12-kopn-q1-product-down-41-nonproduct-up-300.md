KOPN Q1 Product Down 41% Nonproduct Up 300%

The Motley Fool Team

Key Takeaways
Kopin posted mixed results as major contract wins and collaborations offset continued weakness in its core product business.
Product revenues fell dramatically while non-product (collaboration and funded R&D) revenues nearly quadrupled year over year.
Management reiterated its full-year revenue outlook, emphasizing the need for product sales recovery and sustained collaboration momentum.

Kopin (NASDAQ:KOPN) notched several high-profile contract wins and collaborations even as its traditional product business showed continued weakness. The leading U.S.-based developer of microdisplays and wearable display systems reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 12, 2026, with the period reflecting a major sales mix shift and increased cost pressures. Overall, the quarter underscored robust momentum in strategic partnerships and government awards, but also exposed margin and revenue challenges within its legacy product operations.

Notable Developments
Product revenues dropped by 41%, reaching the lowest level since at least the prior quarter, mainly due to reduced shipments for thermal weapon sights and liquid crystal displays.
Non-product revenues, which include government-funded research and partnership-related income, surged 292%, now equaling product sales and driven by new contracts like U.S. Army MicroLED and Fabric.AI collaborations.
Operating expenses rose significantly, with research spending up 133% due to externally funded programs, while the company ended the period with $34.1 million in cash and $59.5 million in total liquidity, ensuring funding well into fiscal 2027.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its prior full-year 2026 revenue range of $52–60 million but gave no new official guidance with its Q1 update. It highlighted the importance of ramping product sales and sustaining collaboration wins to meet this target. Investors should closely monitor product revenue recovery, margin improvement, and continued strength in government and AI collaboration awards.

Quarterly Highlights

Product sales struggled during the period, as shipments for legacy applications like thermal weapon sights and displays fell, pushing product margins negative and highlighting an unsustainable cost structure if volumes remain low. Growing reliance on government awards and collaboration milestones, such as a major $15 million Fabric.AI order and multiple military contracts, provided vital revenue support during this quarter.

Funded research and partnerships were bright spots, nearly quadrupling and matching the size of product revenues, with Kopin being recognized for its MicroLED and NeuralDisplay technologies in U.S. Army and Fabric.AI projects. The company expanded its manufacturing capacity, acquiring advanced U.S. OLED equipment at its Massachusetts site to strengthen domestic supply for defense and commercial customers.

However, Kopin's increased R&D and selling, general, and administrative expenses led to rising costs. While much of the R&D growth was externally funded, the company's higher cost base increases execution risk if collaboration wins slow or legacy product improvements do not materialize. No new profit, cash burn, or adjusted operating income figures were disclosed for the quarter.

What Kopin Does

Kopin develops and manufactures advanced microdisplays and related wearable technology for defense, industrial, and emerging technology markets.
