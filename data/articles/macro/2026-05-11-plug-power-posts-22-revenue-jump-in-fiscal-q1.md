Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed expectations for revenue growth in fiscal 2026, expressing high confidence in the existing project backlog. It continues to target breakeven EBITDAS (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization, and share-based expenses) by the fourth quarter, supported by ongoing cost reduction and asset monetization. Investors should watch operating cash flow and progress on large, international hydrogen partnerships as key markers of Plug Power’s path to profitability.

Quarterly highlights

The material handling segment, which includes GenDrive fuel cells and GenFuel hydrogen systems, contributed to strong sales, with expansion at customer sites such as Amazon and Walmart. Global electrolyzer projects also supported results, with over 320 megawatts of capacity now deployed and ongoing work in Portugal, Spain, and Canada.

Margins improved sharply, driven by lower per-unit service costs (down more than 30%), improved execution, and a 54 percentage-point boost in hydrogen fuel margins. However, the quarter remained seasonally soft, with losses narrowing but still significant.

Operating cash outflow was $150 million, and unrestricted cash balances fell from $368 million at year-end to $223 million. Plug Power expects further liquidity from the sale of data center assets and investment tax credits later in the year.

What Plug Power does

Plug Power develops and sells hydrogen fuel cell systems, electrolyzers for clean hydrogen production, and associated hydrogen infrastructure for industrial, commercial, and logistics markets.
