Notable developments
Net income per share climbed to $1.48, up 448% from the prior year’s first quarter, reflecting higher property earnings and successful refinancing.
Portfolio occupancy edged up to 90.5%, while total revenues reached $147.1 million, an increase of 1.3% from the prior year’s first quarter.
The company acquired Gateway Mall for $43.5 million, raised its regular quarterly dividend by 39%, and closed major refinancing transactions to free up cash flow.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised full-year 2026 guidance for both adjusted funds from operations and net income, expecting further improvement as recent acquisitions and refinancing benefits take hold. The company projects same-center net operating income, which measures rental and operating profitability for properties managed at least a year, to be flat to slightly up for the full year. The most important item for investors to watch is the impact of future retail tenant bankruptcies and closures on occupancy and rental income.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability improved sharply, as net income to common shareholders grew from $0.27 per share last year to $1.48 this quarter. This was driven by strong rental revenues, cost controls, and refinancing that reduced ongoing interest expenses.

Leasing activity remained strong, with over 580,000 square feet of leases signed and notable rent increases, including a 5.7% average rent uptick on new and renewed comparable leases. Larger rent spreads on new leases (55.5%) reinforced pricing power, while renewal increases were modest at 0.5%.

The quarterly dividend was raised 39% to $0.625 per share, reflecting both stronger cash flows and management’s confidence in future earnings stability after refinancing a $634 million term loan and acquiring new properties.
