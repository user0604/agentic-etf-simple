Notable developments
The FDA granted Fast Track designation for QRX003 and indicated that a single Phase 3 study may be enough for approval in Netherton Syndrome in the U.S.
Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities ended the period at $14.0 million, down from $18.75 million at year-end, but management expects this to fund operations into 2027.
The basic weighted average ADSs outstanding increased nearly five-fold to 2.83 million, reflecting dilution from recent equity raises and lowering loss per ADS even as total loss grew.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects recruitment for the pivotal Phase 3 trial of QRX003 to finish by the end of 2026, with a possible regulatory filing in 2027. Cash on hand is projected to support operations into 2027, though additional funding may be needed if timelines change. Investors should watch for clinical milestones for QRX003 and any further share offerings that could affect dilution and financial flexibility.

Quarterly highlights

The company made regulatory progress as QRX003, its investigational topical therapy, received Fast Track status from the FDA. The agency also showed openness to an alternative study design, which could simplify the approval path for Netherton Syndrome.

Research and development expense increased, supporting expansion of both adult and pediatric clinical programs and driving pipeline progress, but also contributing to a larger net loss for the quarter. The company expanded its commercial footprint by setting up a Japanese subsidiary for future self-commercialization and strengthening partnerships in the Middle East and North Africa.

Cash reserves ended at $14.0 million, down from $18.75 million at the previous year-end, reflecting ongoing investments in clinical and regulatory milestones. Management remains confident these resources are sufficient for key near-term goals, though future equity raises could increase dilution risk for existing shareholders.
