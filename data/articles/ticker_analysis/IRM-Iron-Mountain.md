IRM Company Overview
Iron Mountain manages and protects physical and digital information for businesses through storage services, data center operations, and asset disposal. It generates revenue primarily through recurring rental fees for storing physical records and long-term leases for data center space. The company builds customer loyalty by integrating secure physical archives with digital transformation tools, creating high switching costs for organizations that rely on its global logistics and security standards.

Iron Mountain Lifts Outlook as Digital Services Gain Momentum

Iron Mountain helps businesses safely store records, run data centers, and manage old IT equipment around the world. The company’s core business in physical storage is still growing, even as digital services take off. Over the last 5 years, the stock delivered a 185.6% return, beating the S&P 500 by 115.4 percentage points.
