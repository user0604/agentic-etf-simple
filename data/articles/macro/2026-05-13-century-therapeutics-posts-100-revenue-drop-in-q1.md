Century Therapeutics (NASDAQ:IPSC) returned to operating losses as it reported no collaboration revenue, reversing last year’s one-time profit from a major partnership payment. The biotechnology company focuses on developing cell therapies for autoimmune diseases and cancer. It issued its first quarter 2026 results on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed continued clinical progress but highlighted the company’s early-stage risks and reliance on external funding.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed timelines for advancing its clinical pipeline, including an Investigational New Drug (IND) application for CNTY-813 by the end of 2026 and first human data in the second half of 2027. The most important factor for investors to watch is the successful progress and clinical validation of CNTY-813 and CNTY-308, as the company is highly reliant on these programs for future growth.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s revenue for the quarter was zero, in contrast to the prior-year period, when collaboration payments led to a brief profit. This absence reflects the lack of new partnership activity and the company’s pre-commercial stage status.

Operating expenses decreased, driven by a 35.7% drop in research and development costs and a 21.4% reduction in general administrative spending. Management attributes these cuts to reduced staffing and fewer ongoing clinical studies, which helped slow cash outflows.

The cash position more than doubled to $217.0 million after completing a $135 million private financing in January 2026. This strong cash balance gives the company operational funding projected into early 2029 while advancing its lead pipeline assets.
