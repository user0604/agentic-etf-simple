Printed circuit board maker Eltek (NASDAQ:ELTK) faced a tough start to the year as operational disruptions and negative currency effects led to a notable loss. The company specializes in high-reliability, high-density circuit boards for sectors like defense, medical, and aerospace. In results released on May 19, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Eltek reported sharply lower sales and a steep swing into the red as cost inflation and external challenges undermined performance. Overall, the period marked a setback, clouded by reduced revenue, negative margins, and persistent external pressures.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management cites a significantly larger order backlog as evidence of sustained demand for its high-technology printed circuit boards, but gives no forward revenue or profit guidance. The company expects gradual improvement as it addresses internal operational bottlenecks and external disruptions. Investors should closely watch Eltek’s ability to convert its backlog into revenue and regain profitability in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the quarter dropped 18.4% year over year, reversing gains seen in prior periods. The drop was mainly driven by delays in fulfilling existing orders, currency headwinds that increased costs, and ongoing security-related disruptions in Israel.

Profitability collapsed for the period, with gross margin turning sharply negative. The company attributed about $1.3 million in cost increases to the sharp depreciation of the U.S. dollar against the Israeli shekel, which raised local operating expenses when translated into dollars. Operating and net income both swung from profit to significant loss.

Despite these setbacks, Eltek finished the period with $11.1 million in cash and short-term deposits and no debt after pulling funds from deposit accounts. However, cash used in operations was negative, and both inventory and receivables dropped, reflecting the slowdown in sales and production activity.

Eltek designs and manufactures complex, high-quality printed circuit boards for markets such as defense, aerospace, medical, and advanced industrial equipment.
