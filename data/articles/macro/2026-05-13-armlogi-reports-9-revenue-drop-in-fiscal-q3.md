Armlogi Reports 9% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q3

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:32 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Armlogi reported widening losses as transportation services revenue fell and temporary labor costs hit margins.
Revenue for the period declined 9.1% year over year.
Management maintains focus on warehousing growth and customer diversification but gave no firm financial guidance.

Armlogi (NASDAQ:BTOC) faced a tough quarter as logistics network expansion and shifting customer mix failed to offset pressures in its transportation business. The e-commerce logistics provider, focused on U.S. warehousing and transportation for cross-border merchants, saw revenue decline and losses deepen in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (results released May 13, 2026). Overall, results showed that gains in warehousing could not yet balance out margin pressures and slowing transportation demand.

Notable Developments
Warehousing services revenue grew 7.3%, supported by ramp-up in Georgia, Illinois, and Ontario, California warehouses.
Gross margin fell 5.1 percentage points to negative 4.5%, mostly due to $1.3 million of extra labor for inventory reorganization.
Cash and restricted cash fell to $7.1 million, less than half of the position nine months earlier, as net cash used in operations matched the previous year's usage.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its focus on expanding warehousing operations, customer diversification, and investing in technology to boost long-term efficiency. However, the company provided no forward-looking revenue or earnings guidance for the upcoming period. Investors should closely monitor whether warehousing gains and cost controls can start improving overall margins and cash flow before liquidity constraints become acute.

Quarterly Highlights

Armlogi’s warehousing business saw continued revenue growth, with twelve active warehouses and strong contributions from facilities in Georgia, Illinois, and Ontario, California. This expansion drove warehousing revenue higher, but group-wide results were weighed down by declines in transportation services, which fell 19.1% year over year as customer business increasingly favored bundled e-commerce delivery platforms.

Profitability deteriorated as a temporary labor surge, primarily linked to inventory reorganization projects in California warehouses, pushed the company to a $1.9 million gross loss and a negative gross margin for the period. Despite a 25.7% cut in general and administrative expenses, the company’s net loss rose to $5.1 million, reflecting both higher costs and softer top-line results.

The company’s cash and restricted cash position dropped sharply over nine months to $7.1 million, with operating cash outflows remaining steady compared to last year. Persistent net losses, coupled with falling liquidity, mean that capital management and securing fresh funding could become critical if operating leverage does not improve soon.

What Armlogi Does

Armlogi provides warehousing and transportation logistics services focused on the cross-border e-commerce sector, operating a network of large-scale warehouses across key U.S. locations.
