Medtronic (NYSE:MDT), a global leader in medical devices for cardiac, neurological, spinal, and diabetes care, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal year 2026 earnings on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Medtronic reported revenue of $9.017 billion, up 9% year over year, and adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $1.36. Both revenue and EPS came in slightly above analyst consensus estimates. The company saw its highest quarterly organic revenue growth in ten quarters, driven by strong performances in Cardiac Ablation Solutions, Cardiac Rhythm Management, and Diabetes. Management reiterated full-year guidance for both organic revenue growth and EPS, reflecting confidence in the business outlook. The quarter also featured the completion of two strategic acquisitions—CathWorks and Anteris—supporting growth in coronary, renal denervation, and structural heart markets.

Positives
Cardiac Ablation Solutions delivered outstanding growth: Revenue in this business increased 80% year over year, with pulsed field ablation (PFA) products driving significant market share gains. This strength highlights Medtronic’s ability to innovate and compete in a fast-growing area of heart rhythm treatment.
Cardiovascular segment posted double-digit growth: Cardiovascular sales rose 14%, with Cardiac Rhythm & Heart Failure up 20%. This marks the strongest cardiovascular growth in a decade, excluding pandemic-related comparisons, and reflects robust demand for new devices.
Diabetes business accelerated, especially internationally: Diabetes revenue rose 15%, led by double-digit growth outside the U.S. New product launches, such as the MiniMed 780G system and Simplera Sync sensor, contributed to this momentum.
Operating margin and cash flow remained healthy: Adjusted operating margin was 24.1%, ahead of expectations. Free cash flow for the first nine months of FY26 reached $3.34 billion, supporting ongoing investments and shareholder returns.
Negatives
Neuroscience segment growth was below expectations: Neuroscience sales grew 4%, with some softness in specialty therapies and slower-than-expected contributions from new launches. Management expects improvement in the coming quarters as new products ramp up.
Gross margin faced headwinds from product mix and tariffs: The mix of lower-margin capital equipment sales in Cardiac Ablation Solutions and early-stage Diabetes manufacturing, along with $93 million in tariff costs, weighed on reported gross margin. These impacts are expected to moderate over time.
Structural Heart growth was modest in the U.S.: The segment grew at a low single-digit rate, with competitive pressures and the annualization of prior product launches affecting results. International performance was stronger, but U.S. growth lagged.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Progress of new product launches in Cardiac Ablation Solutions and Robotics: Investors should monitor updates on the Sphere-360 catheter (commercialized in Europe and in U.S. trials) and the Hugo surgical robot (recently cleared for urologic procedures in the U.S.). Early adoption rates and physician feedback may indicate future growth potential.
Ramp-up of Symplicity Spyral and Altaviva launches: Both the renal denervation device for hypertension and the tibial neurostimulation device for urge urinary incontinence are in early launch phases. Watch for metrics such as new account openings, physician training numbers, and patient access milestones, as these are leading indicators for future revenue.
Impact of tariffs and product mix on gross margin: The company expects a $75 million tariff impact in Q4 and ongoing mix effects from new product launches. Any signs of improvement or further headwinds will affect profitability and guidance for FY27.
Status and timeline of the Diabetes business separation: The planned spin-off of the Diabetes unit (MiniMed Group, Inc.) is on track for completion by the end of calendar 2026. Updates on timing, expected financial impact, and any regulatory steps will be important for investors.
Integration and financial contribution from recent acquisitions: Medtronic completed the CathWorks and Anteris transactions during Q3. Investors should look for updates on integration progress, any early revenue contributions, and commentary on future M&A strategy.
