POWL Company Overview
Powell designs and manufactures custom-engineered systems that distribute and control electrical energy for heavy industrial markets like oil and gas refining and electric utilities. The company generates revenue by building integrated power control rooms and specialized switchgear used to protect large-scale electrical equipment from damage. Customers rely on these highly technical, site-specific solutions and ongoing maintenance services, creating high switching costs due to the complexity of integrating these systems into critical infrastructure.
