Walmart, Dollar Tree, and the Consumer

Walmart and Dollar Tree are both winning over budget-conscious shoppers —but they’re using two completely different playbooks to do it.

Alicia Alfiere, MBA

May 29, 2026, 9:24 AM

Comments

Walmart's (NASDAQ: WMT) value proposition for consumers is simple -- come here for affordable goods. In its latest earnings call, it was clear that the company is aiming to meet strapped consumers where they are -- and helping those consumers find value, by having over 7,000 rollbacks (temporary sales) in place and extending some of the rollbacks it started in the back half of 2025.

And it's paying off for the retailer, as net sales grew 5.7% (constant currency basis), it beat its own guidance, and all of its business segments showed strength. The company's ad business also clocked a 37% increase, one of its best quarters yet. Walmart also reported strong growth in grocery and general merchandise categories and believes that it made gains in market share across categories and income groups -- including in upper-income households.

Dollar Tree (NASDAQ: DLTR) is a little bit different. Its consumers generally shop based on need and will visit the store more around the holidays. It's been trying to attract customers on a more regular basis, saying: "come for the holiday, stay for the everyday." The company increased its prices last year, moving to a multiprice model that includes a $1.25 entry price point as well as items for $1.50, $2, and $5. That increase in prices helped drive a 4.5% increase in average ticket. But a shorter Easter selling season (Easter was a bit early this year) impacted traffic, which was down 1%.

Despite this, the company seems a beneficiary of the uncertain consumer environment, with a 7.2% increase in sales and a 3.5% increase in comparable sales. Dollar Tree's CEO said during the earnings call: "... The consumer environment remains dynamic, especially for lower-income households navigating higher fuel costs and broader macro uncertainty. Customers are shopping thoughtfully and closer to need with a continued focus on affordability, convenience and trip efficiency. Customers value the ability to shop nearby and quickly to stretch their budgets through smaller and more affordable pack sizes...our model is built for environments like this."
