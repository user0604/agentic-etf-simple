SentinelOne (NYSE:S), a company that provides AI-powered cybersecurity software for businesses, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, SentinelOne reported revenue of $271.2 million, which represented a 20% increase year over year. The company’s annualized recurring revenue (ARR) reached $1.1191 billion as of Jan. 31, 2026, also up 22% from the prior year. For the full year, revenue crossed the $1 billion mark for the first time, growing 22% year over year. Importantly, the company achieved full-year operating profitability, with a non-GAAP operating margin of 3.5%. Q4 earnings per share (EPS) came in at $0.07, beating analyst expectations, while revenue was essentially in line with consensus estimates. The company’s customer base continued to expand, with 1,667 customers generating at least $100,000 in ARR, an 18% increase from the previous year. Retention rates remained strong, especially among large customers.

Positives
EPS beat expectations: Q4 EPS of $0.07 exceeded analyst consensus, indicating better-than-expected profitability for the quarter.
Record ARR growth: ARR reached $1.1191 billion, up 22% year over year, reflecting healthy demand for the company’s platform and strong customer expansion.
Operating profitability milestone: Achieving full-year operating profitability for the first time demonstrates improved cost discipline and business scale.
Broader product adoption: A growing percentage of customers are using multiple modules, with 65% of enterprise customers now using three or more solutions, showing success in cross-selling and platform adoption.
Negatives
Net loss under GAAP: Despite non-GAAP profitability, the company reported a GAAP net loss of $450.7 million for the year, mainly due to stock-based compensation and a significant tax settlement.
Large tax expense: The company incurred a $180.9 million tax expense in fiscal 2026 due to a settlement with Israeli tax authorities, which will impact cash flows over several years.
Restructuring charges: SentinelOne recorded $12.3 million in restructuring costs related to severance, contract terminations, and asset impairments as part of its cost optimization efforts.

Investors should watch whether reported results align with these targets and if the company maintains or updates its full-year outlook.
Net new ARR and customer growth: The company expects improved seasonality and a strong start to the year, with a focus on both new customer acquisition and expansion within its base. Progress here will be key to sustaining growth rates.
Adoption of emerging solutions: SentinelOne is seeing rapid growth in newer product areas such as AI security, data, cloud, and managed security services (Wayfinder). Watch for updates on ARR or adoption rates in these segments, as they are important for long-term growth.
