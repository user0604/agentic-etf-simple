Notable Developments

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted confidence in ongoing growth, underpinned by a 92% pre-leased development pipeline and high occupancy rates. The company continues to focus on disciplined capital allocation and tenant diversification, citing robust regional logistics demand. Investors should watch for trends in rental growth and potential impacts from further regional tax or political events.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue and net operating income showed significant gains, with the $14.398 million revenue result driven by a 39.9% increase in rental revenues in Peru, 24.8% in Colombia, and new additions in Mexico. The same-property cash net operating income metric, which isolates results from properties held at least one year to reveal organic growth, rose nearly 11%, showing strength beyond portfolio expansion.

The average rent per square foot increased 9.8% year over year to $8.74, and the stabilized occupancy rate reached a perfect 100% as demand stayed strong. The company maintained its 57-tenant roster, including anchor tenants like PepsiCo and DHL, with tenant industries spanning third-party logistics, e-commerce, and consumer goods.

Although overall operating costs fell 4.1%, general and administrative expenses rose due to a special tax in Colombia, which management characterized as non-recurring. The weighted average lease term edged down to 4.7 years from 5.0 years, a modest change that may warrant monitoring in future periods.
