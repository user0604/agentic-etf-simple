Key Takeaways

Revenue rose 34.5% year over year in the period.

Construction Partners (NASDAQ:ROAD), the major infrastructure construction contractor in the U.S. Sunbelt, delivered results that surpassed its own financial outlook as new project wins and acquisitions fueled growth. The company updated investors with its fiscal second-quarter report on May 8, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, operational execution and a record project backlog stood out in a quarter marked by expansion and higher guidance for the year.

Notable Developments
Revenue grew 34.5% in the quarter, driven by contributions from acquisitions and 11% organic growth.
The project backlog reached a record $3.14 billion at quarter end, up 10.6% from a year earlier.
General and administrative expenses rose to $63.6 million, slightly increasing as a percentage of revenue compared to last year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management has raised the full-year fiscal 2026 outlook for revenue, adjusted net income, and adjusted EBITDA following the strong second quarter and recent acquisitions. The firm expects continued strength, supported by government infrastructure projects and a robust backlog. Investors should monitor how effectively Construction Partners manages costs and integrates new businesses in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Construction Partners continued its acquisition strategy, closing a deal in Tennessee after the quarter that expands its presence in that region. Acquired operations, combined with organic growth, contributed the majority of the quarter’s revenue increase, supported by ongoing demand for infrastructure projects across the Sunbelt states.

The backlog, representing signed contracts for future work, rose to $3.14 billion. This level provides significant visibility into upcoming revenue, as 78% of the backlog typically converts to revenue within a year.

General and administrative costs increased both in dollar terms and slightly as a percentage of revenue, reflecting the larger scale from acquisitions and potential integration expenses. Interest expense and debt also increased, highlighting the company’s reliance on capital for recent expansion.

What Construction Partners Does

Construction Partners builds and maintains roads, highways, and related infrastructure projects, serving public and commercial customers mainly in the southern United States.
