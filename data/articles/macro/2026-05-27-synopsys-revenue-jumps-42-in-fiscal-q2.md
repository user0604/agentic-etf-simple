Synopsys Revenue Jumps 42% in Fiscal Q2

Key Takeaways
Synopsys delivered a business win as quarterly revenue exceeded its prior guidance. Non-GAAP earnings per share decreased year over year. Management raised its full-year revenue and earnings guidance.

Synopsys (NASDAQ:SNPS) delivered higher revenue than it had targeted, as the rapid integration of AI-driven tools and a substantial boost from its Ansys acquisition helped offset challenges in legacy segments. The leading electronic design automation and semiconductor intellectual property provider reported results for its second fiscal quarter, released May 27, 2026, which confirmed both strong sales growth and a clear divergence between reported profits depending on accounting method. Overall, the quarter highlighted robust top-line progress amid transition-related margin pressures.

Notable developments
Revenue rose 42%, reaching $2.28 billion, pushed above management’s stated outlook by Ansys contributions and AI-driven design tools.
GAAP net income dropped sharply as a result of increased amortization charges and restructuring expenses, while non-GAAP profit was much stronger.
Operating margin in the Design Automation segment improved to 43.3%, but the Design IP segment saw its margin fall to 24.4% amid continued portfolio shifts.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management now anticipates full-year revenue of $9.665 billion at the midpoint, marking an upward revision from earlier projections. They also raised the non-GAAP earnings target to $14.76 per share, reflecting higher expected operating margin and synergy realization from the Ansys acquisition. Investors should watch for continued improvement in Design IP performance and the pace of integration for Ansys, as these remain critical for future earnings quality.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue in its Design Automation segment, which offers electronic design software and now includes Ansys engineering simulation products, surged 62% year over year. The segment benefited from expanded demand for AI-driven design tools and successful integration efforts following the large Ansys acquisition.

The Design IP segment, focused on providing pre-designed chip building blocks, reported a 6% decline in revenue compared to the prior year. Management continued to flag 2026 as a “transitional year” for this business, partly due to the planned divestiture of its Processor IP Solutions portfolio and ongoing product shifts.

GAAP net profit was notably reduced by non-cash costs associated with integrating major acquisitions, especially higher amortization expenses and restructuring charges. These items drove a 96% year-over-year decline in reported GAAP earnings per share, contrasting with the more stable, but still slightly lower, non-GAAP profit which strips out one-time and share-based compensation costs.

What Synopsys does

Synopsys is a global supplier of electronic design automation software and semiconductor intellectual property used to create advanced integrated circuits and systems.
