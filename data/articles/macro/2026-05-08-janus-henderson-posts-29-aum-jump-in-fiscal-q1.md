Janus Henderson Group Plc (NYSE:JHG) delivered a solid business win as client assets and profit increased from a year ago, even as market declines and merger-related costs weighed on results. The global investment manager provides mutual funds and other pooled investment vehicles to clients worldwide. On May 8, 2026, it reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showing growth in several key areas but also some margin compression and lower one-time fee income. Overall, the quarter balanced steady progress in core operations with the impact of market volatility and strategic changes.

Notable developments
Assets under management grew to $480.0 billion at quarter-end, up 29% from the prior year.
Adjusted earnings per share increased by 13.9% to $0.90 compared to the prior-year quarter.
Operating margin edged down to 31.5% from 32.2% compared to the prior-year quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide a formal outlook in this release, citing the pending take-private transaction with Trian and General Catalyst expected to close in mid-2026. Investors should closely follow updates on the merger process and any cost-saving or integration developments emerging from the process.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s assets under management reached $480.0 billion as of March 31, 2026, compared to $373.2 billion a year earlier. This increase was mainly due to net inflows of $2.9 billion for the quarter and strong fixed income and alternatives product momentum, partially offset by negative impacts from market movements and currency exchange.

Adjusted revenue climbed to $541.6 million, an 11.3% increase over the prior-year period. Much of this came from higher recurring management fees, while performance fees saw a negative contribution as the previous year’s quarter included outsized one-time performance fee income.

Operating expenses rose because of merger- and litigation-related costs and increases in general administrative spending. As a result, the adjusted operating margin fell slightly to 31.5%, even as adjusted net income attributable to shareholders increased to $138.8 million for the period.
