Key Takeaways
Upexi booked a sizable net loss as digital asset markdowns outweighed gains from its Solana-focused strategy.
Revenue rose 43%, driven by digital asset revenue contributing $3.5 million in the quarter.
Management expects cash expenses will soon be covered by staking revenue if current Solana prices hold.

Upexi’s pivot toward digital assets brought higher revenues, but large unrealized cryptocurrency losses led to a deep net loss this quarter. The digital asset-focused business, anchored by large Solana holdings, now accounts for the vast majority of reported revenue. The company released results for its fiscal third quarter on May 13, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31.

Overall, the period was a mixed one for Upexi—growing its digital asset footprint but seeing major swings in reported numbers due to the volatile valuation of Solana on its balance sheet.

Notable Developments
Digital asset revenue rose to $3.5 million, making up the majority of total revenue.
The company recorded a net loss of $109.3 million, largely due to $92.3 million of unrealized losses on digital assets it holds, which reflect the effect of fair value accounting for its large Solana position.
Short-term debt was reduced by $7.6 million, and Upexi continued to buy back its own shares, retiring nearly 2.9 million shares so far this fiscal year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management states that by July 1, 2026, ongoing cash costs for running the business and servicing debt should be less than the staking income it earns on its Solana holdings—if current cryptocurrency prices and staking rates remain steady. The main factor to watch now is whether the value and returns from Solana can consistently support operations in quarters to come, given the company’s concentrated exposure.

Quarterly Highlights

Digital asset activities became the clear financial driver for Upexi this quarter, with staking and token gains pushing revenue up 43% from the prior year’s period. The company expanded its Solana treasury by about 189,000 tokens over the quarter, and this position continues to be unhedged, exposing results fully to price swings in a single digital asset.

Major losses were reported—$109.3 million in total—almost entirely stemming from non-cash markdowns in the fair value of Solana, with cumulative unrealized losses on digital assets exceeding $178 million for the fiscal year to date. These large swings in reported net results are a direct result of the requirement to mark digital assets to their end-of-quarter market value.

Cost controls and capital moves continued, as management reduced the workforce (now just 10 employees), cut warehouse and general expenses, and eliminated $7.6 million of short-term debt. The company also completed further share buybacks and secured new financing with $181 million in convertible notes, which brings attention to future dilution risks as well as increased financial leverage.

What Upexi Does

Upexi develops and distributes consumer products and manages a digital asset treasury centered on Solana, earning revenue through staking and token activities.
