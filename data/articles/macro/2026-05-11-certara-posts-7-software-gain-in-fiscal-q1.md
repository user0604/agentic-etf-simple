Business Overview

Certara (NASDAQ:CERT) provides software and technology-enabled services that help biopharmaceutical companies and regulatory agencies develop and approve new medicines more efficiently. Its offerings leverage biosimulation, data analytics, and regulatory science solutions to streamline drug development and regulatory submissions.

Notable Developments
Software revenue climbed 7%, reaching nearly even footing with services revenue after recent portfolio changes.
Profitability fell as adjusted diluted earnings per share dropped to $0.09, reflecting both higher expenses and service revenue decline.
The sale of the Regulatory and Medical Writing Business to Veristat for up to $135 million will significantly shift Certara’s revenue base for the rest of the year.
Quarterly Performance

The software segment delivered a 7% increase in revenue, with bookings up 20% compared to the prior year’s first quarter. This improvement in software followed a softer finish last year and reflected successful sales of biosimulation and regulatory platforms.

Service revenue declined 4%, and bookings for services dropped by 14%, as execution and go-to-market issues continued. These weaker results in services remain a key challenge, affecting near-term revenue growth and overall earnings performance.

The completed divestiture of the Regulatory and Medical Writing Business brought in up to $135 million and is expected to shift Certara toward an even split between software and services revenues. Management expects this change to focus the company on higher-growth areas, but flat to modest revenue growth guidance signals ongoing caution.

Looking Ahead

Management guides for flat to low-single-digit core revenue growth after completing its divestiture, with full-year revenue forecasted at $395 million to $405 million. Certara expects profitability and revenue mix to evolve as execution improves in its services business and as it accelerates development in AI-driven software. Investors should watch for progress in resolving service execution challenges and tangible results from the company’s AI initiatives.
