Amc Networks Posts 34% Profit Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 8, 2026, 7:15 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Amc Networks reported lower revenue and profitability as streaming growth was not enough to offset declines elsewhere.
Adjusted operating income fell 34% from the prior year.
Management expects stronger performance in the second half and reaffirmed its full-year goals.

Streaming gains could not balance falling profits from legacy businesses at Amc Networks (NASDAQ:AMCX) this quarter. The global entertainment company, known for its original TV series and films, released earnings on May 8, 2026, covering its first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period highlighted ongoing challenges in subscriber counts and margin, despite positive momentum in digital platforms.

Notable Developments
Revenue fell 2.4%, mainly because affiliate (traditional pay TV) revenue in the U.S. dropped 16% as subscribers continued to leave cable platforms.
Streaming-related revenue grew 11% due to higher prices, but paid streaming subscribers dipped to 10.1 million from 10.2 million last year.
Adjusted operating income decreased 34%, outpacing the revenue decline, reflecting pressure on profitability and no improvement yet in overall cost structure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Company leaders reaffirmed their full-year targets for revenue, operating income, and free cash flow. They say earnings will improve later in the year, citing seasonal effects and project timing.

Quarterly Highlights

Streaming services delivered strong top-line growth, with domestic streaming revenue now more than one-third of total U.S. revenue. Yet, the paid streaming subscriber base fell slightly, suggesting that higher prices did not fully offset customer drop-off. Ad-supported bundles grew sharply but are not counted in the reported streaming subscriber total, so future monetization and reporting of these users deserve close attention.

Traditional pay TV (affiliate) revenue continued its rapid decline, down 16% in domestic operations. This trend showed no signs of stabilizing and weighed on overall revenue, far outpacing the rate of digital growth. Domestic advertising revenue declined 5%, less than the full-year decline management expected, but there was no clear turnaround in legacy ad channels.

Profitability measures were under strain, with adjusted operating income and free cash flow falling by more than 30% year over year. While free cash flow remains positive and management took steps to manage debt and preserve cash, leverage increased and the full-year guidance implies much stronger results are needed in the second half.

What Amc Networks Does

Amc Networks creates, owns, and distributes original television programming and films through a mix of cable networks and subscription streaming services for audiences around the world.
