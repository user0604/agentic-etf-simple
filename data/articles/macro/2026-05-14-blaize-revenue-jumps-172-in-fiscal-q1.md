Blaize Revenue Jumps 172% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Blaize delivered rapid revenue growth as new platform partnerships drove improved financial results.
Revenue rose 172% year over year in the quarter.
Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook and expects a steep ramp in revenue from large contracts.

Blaize (NASDAQ:BZAI) posted strong sales momentum as its software and hardware partnerships reached key milestones during the quarter. The developer of edge artificial intelligence (AI) chips and services reported first-quarter results on May 14, 2026, covering the three months ended in March. Overall, the period featured pronounced gross margin gains and confirmed progress toward higher-margin, recurring business, offset by ongoing sizable losses.

Notable Developments
Blaize closed new contracts, including a $50 million NeoTensr deal, with $11 million expected to be fulfilled in the next quarter.
Gross margin surged to 58%, up from 11% in the prior sequential quarter, reflecting a shift to proprietary and platform-based revenues.
The company raised $35 million in fresh capital through a registered equity offering, increasing shares outstanding to approximately 142 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $130 million, anticipating substantial revenue ramp from major deals later this year. Executives stressed the importance of disciplined execution to deliver large existing orders and scale the software platform. Investors will want to watch upcoming contract deliveries and the sustainability of margin improvements as new products launch.

Quarterly Highlights

The company expanded its business reach with the launch of a new AI Services platform at GITEX AI, beginning with face recognition as its first application-level service. This signals a move towards recurring, per-query revenue and less dependence on longer-cycle, lumpy hardware deals.

Sales to edge data center customers advanced, highlighted by the award of a $50 million NeoTensr contract in Asia Pacific. Management expects to fulfill an $11 million purchase order for the NeoTensr contract in the second quarter.

Blaize broadened its market presence through strategic partnerships, notably with Winmate, where chip shipments for ruggedized and critical infrastructure platforms are projected to generate about $15 million in business during the first year. These deals underscore ambitions to diversify into defense, healthcare, and public safety sectors.

What Blaize Does

Blaize designs and sells energy-efficient AI processors and software platforms for edge computing in industries like automotive, enterprise AI, and critical infrastructure.
