Cronos Group Posts 40% Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Cronos Group delivered record revenue and significantly higher profits in the latest quarter.
Revenue rose 40% year over year.
Management remains focused on brand growth, international expansion, and maintaining financial discipline, while regulatory risks persist.

Cronos Group (NASDAQ:CRON) edged out a business win as it continued to build momentum behind its global cannabis brands and posted strong financial gains. The diversified cannabis company reported its first-quarter 2026 results on May 11, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed solid advances across most areas, balanced by minor pressure on margins and regulatory uncertainties.

Notable developments
Revenue grew 40% from the prior year, driven by the Spinach and PEACE NATURALS brands and broader international reach.
Profitability improved, as net income more than doubled and adjusted EBITDA rose sharply compared to last year.
Management highlighted record brand achievements, while warning of risks tied to regulatory investigations in Israel and pending deals in Europe.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its growth strategy, emphasizing investments in brands and maintaining cost discipline. It reiterated its focus on sustainable revenue and profitability expansion but flagged regulatory risk in Israel and Europe as the main hurdles. Investors should keep an eye on the CanAdelaar acquisition in the Netherlands and watch for further international market updates.

Quarterly highlights

The period saw another revenue record, with Canadian sales up 26%, Israeli sales up 53%, and other international markets up 97%. Cannabis flower was the largest product category by dollars, but strong momentum in vapes and edibles further fueled results.

Brand strength stood out: Spinach held the number one national share in vapes in Canada for the very first time and maintained leading positions in edibles. PEACE NATURALS retained its top slot in Israel, delivering nine consecutive quarters of record revenue there.

Despite the strong results, gross margin fell to 42% from 43%, mainly due to a $0.7 million inventory write-down—signaling some operational inefficiency. However, tight management of spending and capital investment offset these minor setbacks, and the company said it remains well capitalized with over $820 million in cash.

What Cronos Group does

Cronos Group develops, manufactures, and sells cannabis products — both for medical and adult-use markets — across Canada, Israel, and select international locations.
