Donaldson Posts 7% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q3

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 6:17 AM

Key Takeaways
Donaldson delivered record sales and adjusted margins, offsetting weakness in Industrial Solutions with growth elsewhere.
Adjusted earnings per share rose 7.1% year over year.
Management raised full-year sales and Life Sciences outlooks while cautioning about gross margin and segment challenges.

Donaldson (NYSE:DCI) reported a strong performance as it set new records for quarterly sales, operating margin, and earnings per share on an adjusted basis. The leading filtration solutions provider, serving industries from transportation to healthcare, saw gains in its Mobile Solutions and Life Sciences segments help counter weakness elsewhere. The company released its fiscal third quarter 2026 results on June 1, 2026, covering the three months ended April 30.

Overall, the quarter reflected solid execution on key initiatives, though ongoing challenges in the Industrial Solutions segment and gross margin compression were noted.

Notable Developments
Mobile Solutions and Life Sciences segments both posted strong revenue growth, with Life Sciences up 12.7% year over year and Mobile up 8.1%.
Free cash flow increased 52.8% to $111.6 million, reflecting improved earnings and disciplined expense management.
The company closed its $829 million acquisition of Facet Filtration in early May, aiming to expand Industrial Solutions’ product offerings and recurring revenue.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its outlook for organic sales growth and Life Sciences revenue, while slightly narrowing and lowering guidance for adjusted operating margin as the year progresses. It expects further gains from recent acquisitions but sees ongoing operating challenges in Industrial Solutions and modest gross margin pressure. Investors should watch upcoming results from Facet integration and whether gross margin recovers as production inefficiencies are addressed.

Quarterly highlights

Mobile Solutions segment continued to drive company performance, with revenue up due to gains in Off-Road markets, particularly construction, and a robust aftermarket, including double-digit growth in the independent channel. Segment earnings before taxes increased over 20%, and margins expanded from 18.1% to 20.2%.

Life Sciences saw sales climb 12.7%, largely on new equipment demand from Food and Beverage and Disk Drive product lines. Earnings before taxes advanced 17.2%, and the segment’s margin edged higher, supported by technology and market expansion.

Industrial Solutions segment revenue decreased 0.6%, held back by a 13.5% drop in Aerospace and Defense production due to supply chain constraints and delayed projects. Project execution remains a challenge for this segment, and management’s outlook remains cautious for the rest of the year.

What Donaldson does

Donaldson manufactures filtration products for mobile equipment, industrial machinery, and specialized life sciences applications worldwide.
