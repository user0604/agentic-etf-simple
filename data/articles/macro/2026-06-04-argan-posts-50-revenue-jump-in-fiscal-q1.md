Key Takeaways
Argan delivered record revenue growth and margin expansion this quarter.
Earnings per share more than doubled compared to the year-ago period.

Argan (NYSE:AGX) posted standout results with record revenue and robust profitability gains. The engineering and construction specialist, focused on large-scale energy and infrastructure projects, reported its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 (released June 4, 2026, for the period ended April 30, 2026). The period reflects continued strong project execution, though a small drop in project backlog and higher costs require monitoring.

Overall, Argan demonstrated significant progress in scaling up operations and maintaining healthy margins while staying well-capitalized.

Notable developments
The Power segment drove company-wide growth, with ongoing construction on major contracts and improved project mix boosting results.
Project backlog was approximately $2.8 billion, down from approximately $2.9 billion at the end of the prior quarter, reflecting project completions outpacing new wins this period.
Argan raised its quarterly dividend to $0.50 per share and continued share buybacks, supported by a cash position of $973.6 million and no debt.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management described its project pipeline as robust and stated that demand for its core energy and infrastructure services remains high. Although no formal guidance was issued for the rest of fiscal 2027, the most important factor to monitor is the pace of new contract awards replacing the declining backlog.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s Power segment, which specializes in engineering and constructing energy generation facilities, remained the primary revenue generator and delivered significant gross margin expansion, reflecting effective project execution and a favorable contract mix.

Project backlog ended the quarter at $2.77 billion, a small decrease from the start of the year. Backlog matters because it indicates reported revenue in future quarters; ongoing replenishment will be key as large projects are finished.

Financial health remains a strength: Argan reported $973.6 million in cash and investments, continued to pay higher dividends, and repurchased $20.6 million in shares without taking on debt.

What Argan does

Argan provides engineering, procurement, and construction services, primarily for large power generation, renewable energy, industrial, and communications infrastructure projects.
