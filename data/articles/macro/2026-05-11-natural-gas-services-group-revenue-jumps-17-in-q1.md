Key Takeaways
Core rental compression business drove higher profits and a dividend increase.

Natural Gas Services Group (NYSE:NGS) saw a strong start to 2026 as its rental compression business delivered robust growth and supported a substantial dividend increase. The leading provider of compression equipment rentals for natural gas producers continued to expand its fleet and improved profitability. In its earnings release on May 11, 2026, the company reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected broad-based gains across revenue, margin, and cash flow measures.

Notable Developments
The rental compression fleet expanded by 17,000 horsepower, with fleet utilization reaching 86.9% at quarter end.
Adjusted gross margin from rentals rose to 63.7% of rental revenue, the highest in five quarters, recovering from a one-time adjustment last quarter.
The company increased its quarterly dividend 36% to $0.15 per share, effective the next quarter, reflecting management’s confidence in cash generation.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA outlook to between $92.5 million and $97.5 million, citing ongoing strength in fleet demand and utilization. Capital spending plans remain unchanged, with a focus on deploying large horsepower compression units. Investors should closely watch the company's customer concentration risks, as two customers historically account for the majority of revenue.

Quarterly Highlights

The rental business continues to dominate, making up 97% of total revenue for the quarter while aftermarket services and product sales contributed marginally. Management added only large-horsepower units, most with electric drives, resulting in a new record for both rental revenue and utilization--with rented horsepower up 16.7% from a year ago.

Adjusted gross margin in the rental segment recovered to its highest level since mid-2025 at 63.7%. This sharp rebound follows a fourth-quarter margin dip caused by an inventory adjustment and demonstrates improved pricing and fleet mix toward larger, higher-value units.

Thanks to stronger profitability and higher operating cash flows, management raised the dividend for the second time in less than a year. Net debt declined modestly, leaving the company with one of the lowest leverage ratios among publicly traded compression peers at quarter end.

What Natural Gas Services Group Does

Natural Gas Services Group provides compressor equipment rentals—mainly to natural gas and oil producers—enabling them to transport natural gas from wells through pipelines and processing facilities.
