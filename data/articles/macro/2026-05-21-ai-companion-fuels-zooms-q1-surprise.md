AI Companion Fuels Zoom's Q1 Surprise

Who knows, maybe there's a comeback in the making here!

Yasser El-Shimy, PhD, MBA

May 21, 2026, 5:13 PM

Comments
9.5

Zoom's (NASDAQ: ZM) Q1 FY27 is a clean beat-and-raise that meaningfully advances the AI-monetization narrative. Revenue of $1.24B grew 5.5% YoY -- the fastest pace since FY22 (Zoom had seen better growth days during COVID) -- beating consensus and the high end of guidance. Non-GAAP EPS of $1.55 beat the $1.42 Street view (+9.5%), non-GAAP operating margin hit 41.1%, and free cash flow margin reached a record-Q1 40.4%.

AI Companion paid users surged 184% YoY, "My Notes" reached 1.5M licensed users in four months, and Zoom Customer Experience continued accelerating high-double-digit growth. Enterprise net dollar retention (NDR) ticked back to 99%. Management raised FY27 revenue to $5.08–$5.09B and EPS to $5.96–$6.00, and added $1.0B to buyback. Shares jumped ~7% after-hours.

Who knows, maybe there's a comeback in the making here!
