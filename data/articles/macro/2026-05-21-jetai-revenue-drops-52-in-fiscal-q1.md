Key Takeaways
Business win marked by continued progress in data center development milestones and increased cash reserves.

Jet.AI (NASDAQ:JTAI) pressed ahead with its transition toward AI infrastructure, advancing site development and boosting cash reserves, but continued to record operating losses as revenue from its aviation business declined. The specialist in developing powered real estate for artificial intelligence workloads reported results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, with the earnings release out on May 21, 2026. Overall, the period highlighted strategic project milestones and a stronger balance sheet, but financial results are not yet reflecting the benefits of the pivot.

Notable developments
Revenue dropped by 51.6% year over year as aviation operations wind down and no substantial new revenue from AI infrastructure has started.
The company ended the quarter with $13.5 million in cash after a $19.8 million equity raise, and authorized a $5 million share buyback.
Jet.AI advanced important milestones in its joint venture data center projects, securing permits and progressing power supply agreements in Manitoba and Maritime Canada.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management underscored its focus on closing the planned merger with flyExclusive and completing the exit from aviation, with the next major catalyst being the shareholder vote in June 2026. No financial outlook was provided for the current year, but commentary emphasized new project milestones and efforts to become a pure-play AI infrastructure developer. Investors should watch for evidence that these strategic moves begin generating recurring revenue from data center operations.

Quarterly highlights

Jet.AI’s revenue more than halved compared to the same period last year, reflecting the completion of aviation business sales and the lack of new income streams from the new AI-focused ventures. This drop in revenue contributed to a continued but narrowing net loss, and the company’s gross margin stayed negative due to transitional operating costs in the legacy segment.

The company ended the quarter with a stronger balance sheet: $13.5 million in cash, reflecting proceeds from a large equity fundraising, and no debt. Jet.AI also increased its investment stake in its joint venture data center project to $2.77 million, demonstrating further commitment to the transition strategy.

Operationally, the company achieved key development milestones at several North American data center sites, including securing environmental permits and natural gas supply for the Manitoba project. However, these achievements have not yet translated into new top-line revenue, heightening the importance of executing the next phase of the strategy and closing the aviation exit.

What Jet.AI does
