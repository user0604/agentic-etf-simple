Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management has chosen not to issue formal forecasts, citing ongoing transition and volatility across its app portfolio. It is prioritizing further cost control while investing in direct-to-consumer growth and scaling new games. The most important trend for investors to monitor is whether user metrics such as daily active users and payer conversion rates can stabilize or recover in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Direct-to-consumer efforts continued to reshape the revenue mix, nearly tripling from the prior year and significantly easing reliance on third-party platforms. Management credits this shift for improved platform economics, though absolute revenue continued to decline.

User monetization improved, with average daily revenue per user up 19%, even as total daily active users fell by over 20%. New product launches like Tetris Block Party contributed to this, yet their scale remains small compared to the broader drop in the player base.

Cost management was aggressive, with $29 million in annualized savings achieved and four studios closed. Selling and marketing spending rose as resources shifted toward acquiring new users and developing new products, reflecting a deliberate reinvestment in future growth rather than near-term profitability.
