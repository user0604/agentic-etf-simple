e.l.f. Beauty (NYSE:ELF), a leading U.S.-based cosmetics and skincare company, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, May 20, 2026. Investors will be watching closely to see how the company builds on its recent performance and responds to ongoing industry trends.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, e.l.f. Beauty delivered net sales of $489.5 million, representing a 38% increase compared to the same period last year. The quarter marked the company’s twenty-eighth consecutive quarter of net sales growth. This result was driven mainly by the contribution from the recently acquired Rhode brand, which added $128 million in sales. Organic net sales, which exclude Rhode, grew approximately 2% year over year. Adjusted EBITDA rose 79% to $123 million. Adjusted net income was $74 million, or $1.24 per diluted share. Gross margin was 71%, down slightly year over year due to tariffs but up sequentially from the previous quarter. The company ended the quarter with $197 million in cash and a strong liquidity position. Both earnings per share and revenue for the quarter beat analyst consensus estimates.

Positives
Rhode acquisition delivered significant sales growth. Rhode contributed $128 million in Q3 sales, far exceeding initial expectations and driving the majority of overall growth.
Continued market share gains in core cosmetics. The e.l.f. Cosmetics brand grew U.S. sales by 8% and increased its market share by 130 basis points, outperforming the broader beauty category.
Strong innovation pipeline supported by successful launches. New products like the Glow Reviver Slipstick and Soft Glam Satin Concealer received positive early feedback and quickly became top sellers on digital platforms.
Solid cash position and share repurchases. The company ended Q3 with nearly $200 million in cash and repurchased $50 million of its own stock, reflecting confidence in its long-term prospects.
Negatives
Organic sales growth slowed outside of acquisitions. Excluding Rhode, net sales grew just 2% year over year, below prior expectations due to softer trends in key international markets.
International headwinds, especially in the UK and Germany. The company faced weaker demand in the UK, its largest international market, and was cycling a major launch in Germany, which affected growth rates.
Gross margin pressured by tariffs. Gross margin declined by 30 basis points year over year, mainly because of higher tariff costs, though this was partially offset by price increases and product mix.
Higher SG&A spending anticipated in Q4. Some selling, general, and administrative expenses shifted from Q3 into Q4, and marketing spend is expected to increase, which may impact fourth-quarter margins.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Performance of spring product launches and innovation pipeline: Investors should monitor the early sales and consumer response to new products such as the Glow Reviver Slipstick and Soft Glam Satin Concealer, as these are rolling out across retail partners during the quarter.
Organic sales trends and international recovery: Watch for signs of improvement in organic growth, particularly in the UK and Germany, where management has implemented new marketing and leadership initiatives to address recent softness.
Impact of increased marketing investments: The company has ramped up marketing spend, including a major campaign around the "big game" and new collaborations. The effectiveness of these investments in driving brand awareness and sales will be a key focus.
Rhode brand expansion and global rollout status: Rhode’s launches in Australia and New Zealand with Mecca are underway. Updates on international sales mix and future rollout plans will be important for understanding the brand’s global potential.
Tariff and margin developments: The current 45% tariff rate remains a headwind, but management expects some relief as the company cycles higher rates from earlier in the year. Any updates on cost mitigation, price adjustments, or supply chain shifts will be closely watched for their impact on profitability.
