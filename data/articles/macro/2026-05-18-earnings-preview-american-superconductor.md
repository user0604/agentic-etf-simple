Earnings Preview: American Superconductor

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

American Superconductor (NASDAQ:AMSC), a company that provides grid and wind power equipment and solutions, is expected to report earnings for its fiscal fourth quarter on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2025 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2025, AMSC delivered revenue of $74.5 million, which beat analyst estimates and exceeded the company’s own guidance. This represented a 21% increase year over year. The quarter included the first partial contribution from the recently completed acquisition of Comtrafo, a Brazilian transformer manufacturer. Gross margin improved to 31% from 27% in the prior year, reflecting both higher sales and a favorable product mix. The company reported net income of $117.8 million, but this figure was largely driven by a one-time $113.1 million non-cash tax benefit from the release of a valuation allowance. Excluding this benefit, non-GAAP net income was $10.5 million. AMSC achieved its sixth consecutive quarter of profitability on a GAAP basis and its tenth on a non-GAAP basis. The balance sheet remained strong, with $147.1 million in cash at quarter end, even after the Comtrafo acquisition.

Positives
Revenue growth exceeded expectations: The company’s 21% year-over-year revenue increase, driven by both organic growth and the Comtrafo acquisition, beat analyst estimates and guidance, showing strong demand across core markets.
Gross margin expansion: Gross margin rose to 31%, up from 27% the prior year, reflecting improved pricing and a better mix of higher-margin products.
Diversified revenue streams: Revenue was spread across traditional energy, renewables, military, utility, and materials sectors, reducing reliance on any single segment and supporting business stability.
Strong backlog and bookings: The 12-month backlog grew to over $250 million, providing visibility into future revenue and supporting continued growth.
Negatives
One-time tax benefit inflated net income: The large reported net income was mostly due to a non-cash tax benefit, which does not reflect ongoing operating performance and may confuse less experienced investors.
Operating expenses increased: Research and development and selling, general, and administrative expenses rose significantly year over year, partly due to acquisition-related costs and higher compensation, which could pressure margins if not managed carefully.
Integration risk from recent acquisitions: The company is still in the early stages of integrating Comtrafo, and while management is optimistic, there is always risk in realizing expected synergies and managing cross-border operations.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2025's Report
Revenue growth and guidance for the new fiscal year: Management has guided to revenue exceeding $80 million for Q4, above analyst consensus. Investors should watch for actual results and any updates to growth expectations for fiscal 2026.
Profitability excluding one-time items: With the Q3 tax benefit behind, focus will return to operating and non-GAAP net income. Analyst consensus expects non-GAAP EPS around $0.19, so results here will be closely watched.
Progress on Comtrafo integration and Brazil expansion: The Comtrafo acquisition was completed in early Dec. 2025. Investors should look for updates on integration, contribution to revenue, and plans for increasing output in Brazil to meet local demand.
Order backlog and demand trends: The company’s backlog reached over $250 million in Q3. Any change in backlog or commentary on new bookings, especially in utility, data center, and traditional energy sectors, will be important for assessing future growth.
Capital spending and production capacity: Management has indicated that capital expenditures may rise as the company scales up, particularly in transformer manufacturing. Updates on actual spending and any plans for further expansion will be key to understanding AMSC’s ability to meet demand and support growth.
