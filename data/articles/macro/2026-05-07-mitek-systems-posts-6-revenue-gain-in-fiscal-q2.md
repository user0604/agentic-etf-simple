Mitek Systems (NASDAQ:MITK) delivered another period of revenue growth as its core fraud and identity business posted strong gains. The identity verification and digital fraud prevention company reported results for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected good momentum in newer segments while legacy products continued to experience stability.

Notable Developments
The fraud and identity solutions segment delivered 28% revenue growth, far outpacing the rest of the business.
Profitability improved, with adjusted EBITDA margin rising by 1.7 percentage points to 40.7%.
The company used cash reserves to retire all of its convertible debt, reducing liabilities but sharply lowering available cash.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year fiscal 2026 revenue guidance to $189 million–$198 million and increased its adjusted EBITDA margin outlook to 30%–33%. For the next quarter, it forecasts revenue in the $49 million–$53 million range. Investors should monitor gross margins and cash generation as the business mix continues shifting toward fast-growing but lower-margin SaaS and service offerings.

Quarterly Highlights

The fraud and identity solutions product line, which includes advanced biometric authentication and AI-powered fraud detection, accounted for $25.7 million in revenue. This segment grew 28% year over year and now represents a much larger share of total sales.

Profitability also advanced, with adjusted EBITDA (a measure of operating performance before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization) up nearly 10% from the prior year’s period. However, gross margins narrowed as more revenue came from SaaS subscriptions and services, which tend to have lower profitability than software licenses.

On the balance sheet, the company paid down $155 million in convertible notes, which reduced total liabilities and simplified its debt structure. As a result, cash and investments dropped from $196.5 million at the end of last September to $77.6 million at March 31, 2026.

What Mitek Systems Does

Mitek Systems provides software and cloud-based solutions that help businesses verify identities and prevent fraud using technologies such as biometrics and image analysis.
