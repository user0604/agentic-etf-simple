Profit came in well ahead of management’s targets, while sales remained stable without picking up speed. The leading provider of artificial intelligence (AI)-driven customer service software, eGain (NASDAQ:EGAN), released its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 14, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed solid operating leverage and improving profitability, though growth in total revenue reflects a steady rather than rapidly rising business.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased by 7%, in line with the company’s guidance range for the quarter, showing steady but non-accelerating top-line performance.
Profitability far outpaced last year; the company’s adjusted EBITDA rose to $3.2 million and non-GAAP EPS jumped to $0.11 from $0.03 year over year.
Annual recurring revenue from the AI Knowledge Hub, its main product for centralizing AI-driven customer knowledge, grew 26% to $48.0 million and stayed at 64% of all SaaS recurring revenue.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue next quarter to remain flat or dip slightly, guiding to $21.5 million to $22.0 million for the June period. Profit margins are forecast to drop due to higher costs and seasonality, while full-year guidance has tightened to reflect little growth acceleration.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s AI Knowledge Hub, a software platform for enterprise customer knowledge management, continued to drive growth. This offering’s annual recurring revenue grew 26% year over year, reflecting ongoing interest from large enterprises and validating its role at the core of eGain’s business.

Disciplined expense management resulted in a small 2% increase in total operating expenses, much lower than the rate of profit expansion, enabling a sharp rise in both net income and EBITDA. The gross margin improvement was attributed to efficiency gains and improved mix of revenue from the company’s SaaS subscriptions.

Cash and cash equivalents rose significantly to $80.5 million, leaving eGain with no debt at quarter-end and maintaining a healthy financial cushion. However, deferred revenue dropped, which could signal slower future bookings and should be watched as a possible early indicator of softer sales cycles.

What eGain Does

eGain provides AI-powered software solutions that help businesses centralize knowledge and automate customer service across digital channels.
