Largo Doubles Q1 Output but Revenue Slips 2.5%

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 11:48 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Largo delivered strong operational gains but continued to face financial losses and revenue pressure.
Vanadium pentoxide production more than doubled, while revenue declined year over year.
Management expects tariff relief and inventory sales to support improved commercial results in the next quarter.

Largo (NASDAQ:LGO) achieved a significant operational recovery at its Maracás Menchen Mine, but commercial results remained under pressure due to U.S. tariff impacts early in the year. The vertically integrated vanadium producer, with core operations in Brazil, reported first-quarter results on May 14, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter featured much stronger production but only modest improvement in the bottom line as revenue stayed soft and profitability remained negative.

Notable developments
Vanadium pentoxide production reached 2,616 tonnes, up 102%, hitting the upper end of company guidance.
Revenue declined by 2.5% as high tariffs limited U.S. sales despite the production surge.
Net loss narrowed as operating costs fell 19%, but adjusted EBITDA losses deepened to $4.3 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed full-year production and cost targets, citing expected sales improvement from tariff relief and inventory releases in the next quarter. The key area to monitor is whether higher production volumes will translate into stronger revenue and profit margins as trade conditions evolve.

Quarterly highlights

Vanadium pentoxide, a metal used in steel, specialty alloys, and grid-level energy storage, saw production more than double compared to the same period last year. This put Largo’s operations at the upper end of its own production guidance, supported by improved ore grades and increased throughput at the Maracás Menchen Mine.

Commercial sales lagged production volumes, with management attributing this to high U.S. tariffs on Brazilian vanadium imports at the start of the quarter. These tariffs eased partway through the period, and management expects most of the benefit to appear in the next quarter’s results.

The company reduced operating costs to $34.5 million, a 19% improvement year over year, but profitability remained elusive. Revenue slipped despite much higher production, and adjusted EBITDA losses widened. Liquidity remains a concern, with new capital raised through share issuance rather than cash flow, and exposure to vanadium price swings continues to be a central risk.

What Largo does

Largo is a resource company focused on producing high-purity vanadium products from its Maracás Menchen Mine in Brazil, serving steel, aerospace, and energy storage markets.
