Key Takeaways
Caesarstone delivered margin improvement but continued to face revenue declines and operating losses.
Adjusted gross margin (non-GAAP) rose 2.7 percentage points year over year as revenue fell.
Management expects to return to positive adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter of 2026 but sees ongoing headwinds.

Caesarstone (NASDAQ:CSTE) showed progress controlling costs, but revenue continued to fall and operating losses deepened. The surfaces manufacturer, a leading supplier of quartz, porcelain, and natural stone countertops and architectural surfaces, released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter highlighted improved cost structure but ongoing challenges from weak demand and legal risks.

Notable Developments
Revenue declined 10.9%, with U.S. market sales down 18.6% while Australia rose 23.4%.
Adjusted gross margin improved to 23.9%, supported by transition to third-party manufacturing and facility closures.
Legal contingencies climbed, with 711 silicosis claims pending and provisions now at $48.8 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated it remains on track to deliver positive adjusted EBITDA (a profitability measure excluding certain expenses) by the third quarter of 2026, barring further shocks in the market or legal environment. The company has not issued detailed revenue or margin forecasts for upcoming quarters. Investors should closely watch developments around U.S. tariffs and resolution of legal claims, as both factors have major implications for profits and cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenue fell sharply compared to the same period last year, with the United States -- its largest market -- seeing the most pronounced drop. In contrast, the Australia segment posted robust growth, helping to offset part of the North American weakness.

Gross margin, which reflects profitability after covering production costs, improved despite lower sales. This was achieved through shifting more production to third-party partners and completing the closure of higher-cost manufacturing facilities.

Legal exposures grew as more cases related to silicosis (a lung disease linked to some engineered stone products) moved forward, pushing provisions for legal settlement higher and highlighting ongoing financial risks related to litigation.

What Caesarstone Does

Caesarstone designs, manufactures, and sells premium surfaces used for kitchen countertops, bathroom vanities, and interior cladding, offering quartz, porcelain, and natural stone products through a global distribution network.
