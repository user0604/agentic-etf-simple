Dycom Industries Q1 Revenue Jumps 56%

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 7:16 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Delivered a business win as record revenues and backlog surpassed internal targets.
Non-GAAP Adjusted EPS (earnings per share) jumped 84.9% year over year.
Management raised full-year outlook, citing continued strong demand.

Dycom Industries (NYSE:DY) outpaced its own expectations, with revenues and profitability rising sharply on booming demand for digital infrastructure. The leading provider of specialty contracting services for telecommunications and electrical utilities, it benefited from increased fiber and data center development. The company reported first-quarter results for fiscal 2027 on May 27, 2026, reflecting broad-based gains across business lines. Overall, the quarter featured standout growth and improved margins, with management boosting its full-year outlook to match the strong industry backdrop.

Notable Developments
Total contract revenue set a record at $1.97 billion, beating the company's top-end outlook by about $250 million.
Adjusted EBITDA, a profitability measure, increased 74.6% from the prior year, while the communications segment margin improved by 0.4 percentage points.
Backlog climbed 46.5% to a record $11.91 billion, with management expecting $6.4 billion of that to convert into revenue within 12 months.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year targets, now projecting up to $7.65 billion in revenue due to unprecedented demand for fiber infrastructure and data centers. It also expects further margin expansion, especially in Building Systems, as the company integrates new acquisitions. Investors should watch how Dycom manages its large project backlog and absorbs new businesses in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue sharply exceeded management’s anticipated range as both newly acquired and existing operations benefited from surging demand in telecom and data center construction. More than two-fifths of the growth was organic, not due to acquisitions, highlighting strong underlying end-market appetite for Dycom’s core services.

Profitability improved as adjusted EBITDA margin moved up to 13.4% company-wide, aided by disciplined project selection and efficiency gains. Communications segment margin increased, while Building Systems, Dycom’s newest line, performed especially well in its first full quarter.

Backlog reached a new high, providing significant future revenue visibility. The company also moved to acquire National Technology Integrators, adding "inside-plant" data center capabilities and broadening its infrastructure services.

What Dycom Industries Does

Dycom Industries specializes in construction and maintenance services for telecommunications and electrical networks, serving clients such as telecom carriers, data center operators, and utilities.
