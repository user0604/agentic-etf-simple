Key Takeaways
Newsmax narrowed its net loss year over year as affiliate and licensing revenues surged.
Revenue rose 14.0% compared to the prior-year quarter.

Newsmax (NYSE:NMAX) delivered a business win this quarter by sharply reducing its net loss and growing its core broadcasting segment. The multi-platform news provider expanded its audience reach and shifted more revenue toward affiliate fees and licensing. The results, released on May 14, 2026 for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, show progress on growth plans but ongoing challenges in digital operations and continued investment in new platforms. Overall, the quarter featured strong revenue gains and improved loss metrics, though digital headwinds remain.

Notable Developments
The broadcasting segment grew 20.8%, driven by a 75.2% increase in affiliate fees and a 697.1% jump in licensing revenue, offsetting a 3.7% decline in advertising.
Net loss improved sharply, falling to $(2.2) million from $(17.2) million a year ago, mainly due to lower legal expenses, but adjusted EBITDA turned negative as content and production costs rose.
Management maintained its full-year revenue guidance at $212–216 million, citing ongoing investment in programming and new digital offerings.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 revenue guidance and emphasized continued investment in content, broader distribution, and deeper audience engagement. With digital revenues declining, the key area for investors to monitor is whether expanded content and distribution strategies can drive sustainable digital growth and a return to company-wide profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

The broadcasting segment led the period, with affiliate fees up 75.2% and licensing revenue rising sharply as Newsmax expanded international partnerships. This growth offset declines in advertising and subscription revenue within broadcasting, reflecting a shift toward more stable, fee-based income streams.

The digital segment remained under pressure, with revenue down 12.7% due to lower advertising and subscription numbers, despite a significant increase in content offerings on the Newsmax+ streaming platform. Management is investing in premium digital content to stabilize this business line.

Net loss narrowed significantly, mainly due to reduced legal costs after prior-year settlements. However, adjusted EBITDA slipped below zero as operating costs increased with new content and technology investments, leaving the path to sustained profitability uncertain.
