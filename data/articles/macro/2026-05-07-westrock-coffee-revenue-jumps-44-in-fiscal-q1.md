Key Takeaways
Westrock Coffee delivered a strong quarter with robust revenue and profit growth but remains unprofitable. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA more than tripled compared to last year. Management reaffirmed annual EBITDA guidance and aims for free cash flow positivity in the second half of 2026.

Westrock Coffee (NASDAQ:WEST) posted robust improvement in sales and operating profit, continuing to scale its beverage platform but reporting another net loss. The integrated coffee, tea, and beverage solutions provider saw gains from both new and existing customers, with all production lines now fully commercialized at its Arkansas facility. The company released first quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter marked significant operational progress but underscored the continued importance of achieving sustainable profitability and cash flow improvement.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 44.4% to $308.8 million, supported by demand across beverage solutions and sustainable sourcing.
Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA more than tripled year over year, reflecting strong operating leverage as new production lines reached commercial scale.
Capital expenditures dropped from $41.3 million to $7.1 million, highlighting the company’s shift from heavy investment to a focus on generating cash flow.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed guidance for full fiscal 2026 adjusted EBITDA between $90.0 million and $100.0 million. Westrock Coffee plans to prioritize margin consistency, keep capital expenditures low, and target free cash flow positivity in the second half of the year. Investors should closely watch progress toward positive free cash flow, as the company’s net losses remain a key pressure point.

Quarterly Highlights

The company delivered strong revenue gains in both operating segments, with Beverage Solutions net sales climbing by nearly half versus the prior year. This growth reflected both core product momentum and volume from new customers as its Arkansas facility achieved full commercial output.

Profitability improved sharply, as adjusted EBITDA increased to $26.0 million, driven by scale-up benefits and cost controls. The Beverage Solutions segment’s adjusted EBITDA more than doubled, pointing to the benefits of higher volumes flowing through its vertically integrated platform.

Capital spending dropped over 80% as the company decreased investments in new facilities, leading to improved free cash flow compared to a year ago. However, it still used more cash than it generated, so continued attention to operating performance and liquidity will be critical in the coming quarters.

What Westrock Coffee Does

Westrock Coffee provides integrated beverage solutions across coffee, tea, flavors, extracts, and ready-to-drink products for retail, restaurant, and hospitality customers worldwide.
