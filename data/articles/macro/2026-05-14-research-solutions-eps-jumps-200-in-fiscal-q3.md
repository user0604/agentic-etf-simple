Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to focus on building recurring business-to-business (B2B) revenue streams and deepening AI integration in its product lineup. The main factor to monitor next is whether platform customer deployment momentum rebounds, as slower growth here could impact future recurring revenue gains.

Quarterly Highlights

Platform revenue climbed 6.6% to $5.2 million, increasing its share of the business as transactional revenue dropped by 11%. This mix shift helped improve overall gross margin, which reached 51.7% compared to 49.5% a year ago.

Recurring annual revenue (ARR) rose 8.5% year over year, led by 16.6% growth in B2B ARR, though B2C ARR fell by 7.5%. The company attributed the weaker B2C result to heightened competition and lower subscriber conversion rates.

On the profitability side, both net income (up to $0.03 per share) and adjusted EBITDA improved, boosted by lower operating expenses and continued margin gains. Operating cash flow fell to $1.0 million for the quarter but remains strong over the past twelve months.
