Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year fiscal 2026 guidance, anticipating revenue to remain flat or rise by as much as 1.5% versus last year, and set a target for adjusted EBITDA between $415 and $430 million. Executives plan to focus on cost discipline, margin gains, and leverage improvement. The most important area for investors to monitor is whether increased sales and margin progress will translate into sustainable cash generation and net profitability as the year continues.

Quarterly Highlights

The company reversed a multi-quarter decline in comparable sales, posting 0.7% growth. Sales in the core products category declined year over year, while services and other revenue increased by approximately 6.8%.

Profitability metrics improved, with gross margin up 0.2 percentage points and operating income increasing by over 50% versus the prior year. Adjusted EBITDA also topped management’s original outlook for the period.

Despite progress, net losses widened to $15.1 million and free cash outflow deepened. Inventory levels fell 1.9% year over year, suggesting continued supply chain discipline, but further improvement in overall cash flow remains an area for attention.
