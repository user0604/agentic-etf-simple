Post Posts 38% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:57 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Profitability improved, underpinned by segment strength and ongoing acquisitions.
Adjusted diluted EPS (non-GAAP) rose 37.6% year over year.
Full-year guidance for Adjusted EBITDA was affirmed.

Packaged foods company Post (NYSE:POST) posted a solid quarter, with higher profit and stronger cash flow largely backed by solid gains in Foodservice and Refrigerated Retail. The diversified consumer packaged goods company reported results for its fiscal second quarter (released May 7, 2026), affirming its full-year guidance even as core volumes in cereal and pet food remained soft. Overall, the quarter showed continued margin expansion, with acquisition-led revenue growth masking some underlying challenges in legacy product categories.

Notable Developments
Foodservice segment Adjusted EBITDA jumped 47.9%, reflecting both higher protein shake volumes and improved operating leverage.
Refrigerated Retail saw segment profits grow 36.4%, helped by strong side dish volumes as well as new private label product launches.
Post continued its acquisition strategy, with $145 million in incremental sales from the recently added 8th Avenue brand in Consumer Brands, while organic core volumes in cereal and pet food declined.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management affirmed its full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) target of $1,550–$1,580 million, highlighting continued confidence in both integration of recent deals and organic segment growth. The key area to monitor is organic volume improvement in core categories like ready-to-eat cereal and pet food, which saw ongoing declines this quarter. Investors should also watch leverage levels, given high buyback activity and ongoing acquisition spending.

Quarterly Highlights

Foodservice, which includes eggs and potato products for restaurants and institutional clients, delivered the quarter’s largest profit gain. Adjusted EBITDA increased sharply as higher volume and better production in protein-based shakes added to performance. Management continues to invest in growth for cage-free egg capacity and new customer service initiatives in this segment.

In the Refrigerated Retail business, products such as side dishes, eggs, cheese, and sausage all registered positive volume growth. Side dishes in particular rose 12.4%, with both branded and private label innovation adding new customers and seasonal holiday sales driving further momentum.

Within Post Consumer Brands, the core ready-to-eat cereal and pet food businesses experienced further volume contraction, even as overall revenues rose due to the acquisition of 8th Avenue. While diversification through new products and acquisition helped offset the impact, management will need to turn around these categories to drive organic profitability over the longer term.

What Post Does

Post is a consumer packaged foods company that manufactures and sells a broad range of branded and private label products, including cereals, refrigerated foods, eggs, potato products, protein shakes, and pet food, distributed through retail and foodservice channels.
