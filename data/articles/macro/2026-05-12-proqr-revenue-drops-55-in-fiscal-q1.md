ProQR Revenue Drops 55% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 7:10 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue fell sharply due to the lack of new milestones or clinical updates.
Earnings per share were a loss of €0.13 versus a loss of €0.10 in the prior year period.
Management expects existing cash to support operations into mid-2027, but new revenue triggers remain uncertain.

RNA therapeutics developer ProQR Therapeutics (NASDAQ:PRQR) reported a challenging start to fiscal 2026, with lower revenue and a wider operating loss amid a continued absence of new clinical or milestone achievements. The company, focused on building its Axiomer RNA editing platform and collaborations, released results for the quarter ending March 31, 2026, on May 12, 2026. Overall, the quarter highlighted ongoing cash burn and limited progress on product development milestones, despite management’s assurance of more than a year of funding runway.

Notable Developments
Revenue in the period declined 55%, with no new milestones achieved under the Eli Lilly partnership.
Operating loss widened to €13.6 million as reduced revenue outpaced modest declines in research spending.
Cash reserves dropped to €81.1 million, providing funding capacity into mid-2027 assuming no acceleration in spending.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated that current funding levels are sufficient for at least twelve months, and cited ongoing collaborations and pipeline expansion. The most important factors to watch are updates on clinical progress for Axiomer platform programs and any new milestone achievements that could boost revenue or extend cash runway.

Quarterly Highlights

The company continued to recognize revenue only from the upfront and equity elements of its partnership with Eli Lilly, as no new performance milestones or partnership expansions occurred. No progress updates or major clinical trial data on key candidates—such as sepofarsen or ultevursen—were disclosed in the quarter, leaving future revenue timing uncertain.

Cash and cash equivalents ended at €81.1 million, down from €132.4 million a year earlier. This drop reflects ongoing operating losses, but the company did not carry out any fund-raising or new borrowing during the period, preserving existing share count for now.

ProQR announced a new strategic partnership with Ginkgo Bioworks, aiming to leverage artificial intelligence (AI) for drug discovery and to accelerate experimental data generation. The company also formed an AI Advisory Board to guide the integration of AI in its pipeline development activities.

What ProQR Therapeutics Does

ProQR Therapeutics develops RNA-based therapies to treat rare genetic diseases, often through collaborations and proprietary technology like its Axiomer RNA editing platform.
