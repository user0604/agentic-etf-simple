The company, a developer of a fully integrated mine-to-magnet rare-earth platform, is moving to secure all links in the rare-earth supply chain for the U.S. and allied markets. Overall, the quarter underscored a phase of build-out and capital inflows, with full commercial viability yet to be tested.

Notable Developments
The company generated $5.7 million in revenue, reflecting its first material product sales after several years of development and infrastructure investment.
A $1.5 billion private placement raised in January 2026 pushed quarter-end cash to $1.75 billion, significantly improving short-term liquidity and supporting capital projects.
Operating expenses accelerated, causing adjusted net loss to widen to $24.1 million, as the company invests in facility build-out, research and development, and acquisition integration.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains focused on closing a planned $1.6 billion government funding agreement by May 2026 and expects key plant commissioning milestones later in the year. The most important factor to monitor is the transition from infrastructure ramp-up to stable, recurring commercial sales, which is critical for future profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue expansion was driven by metal-making operations in the United Kingdom, marking a shift from zero sales a year ago to initial product shipments. However, the scale remains small compared to planned capacity, and no order backlog data was disclosed.

The Stillwater magnet facility in Oklahoma hit a key milestone with the commissioning of Phase 1a, launching the production of neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) magnets, while customer deliveries are set for the coming quarter. The facility aims to reach a capacity of 600 metric tons per year by the final quarter of 2026.

Substantial capital was deployed toward acquiring more mineral reserves and expanding into Europe and Brazil, highlighted by a $2.8 billion agreement to acquire Serra Verde’s rare earth mine and plans to add a 3,750 metric ton-per-year alloy facility in France.

What USA Rare Earth Does

USA Rare Earth develops and operates assets that mine, process, and manufacture rare earth metals and magnets, aiming to secure the full critical materials supply chain outside of Asia.
