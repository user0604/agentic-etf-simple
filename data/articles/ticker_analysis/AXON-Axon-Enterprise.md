Axon Grows Software Revenue and Raises Long Term Outlook

Axon Enterprise makes TASER energy devices, police body cameras, and public safety software for law enforcement. The company stands out because its recurring software and services revenue is now its largest segment, boosting predictable income. Over the past five years, the stock returned 186.4%, outperforming the S&P 500 by 112.5 percentage points.
