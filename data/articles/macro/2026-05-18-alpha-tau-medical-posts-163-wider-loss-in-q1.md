Notable Developments
Alpha Tau DaRT technology achieved Japanese marketing approval for head and neck cancer, while U.S. pivotal trials for skin and brain cancers advanced in patient enrollment and data readout.
Research and development spending increased 52.8% year over year as clinical trial activity and related personnel costs expanded.
Net loss per share widened, in part due to a significant non-cash charge tied to revaluing outstanding warrants, contributing to a $9.6 million financial expense for the quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed that several key clinical trial recruitment and data milestones are expected later in 2026 and early 2027, especially for U.S. pivotal studies in skin, brain, and pancreatic cancers. The company did not provide financial guidance, which is typical for its early development stage. The critical item to monitor next is the readout of top-line results from ongoing pivotal clinical trials that could determine regulatory and commercial prospects for Alpha DaRT therapy.

Quarterly highlights

The company made tangible progress with Alpha DaRT, its targeted alpha particle radiotherapy product, including regulatory milestones in Japan and pivotal trial completions in the United States. In the quarter, it received Japanese market approval for Alpha DaRT in certain head and neck cancers, began post-approval surveillance, and completed patient enrollment for its U.S. ReSTART trial in cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma.

Expenses rose sharply, mainly driven by higher research and development costs due to more active patient recruitment and new clinical sites. Administrative costs went up 24% year over year, while net financial expenses swung deeply negative owing to warrant revaluation—a non-cash accounting effect from changes in the value of previously issued warrants, which are financial instruments giving holders the right to buy shares at preset prices.

The cash position was $80.2 million as of March 31, 2026, and the company’s net loss more than doubled year over year. No product revenue has yet been recorded as Alpha Tau remains in the clinical development and regulatory phase for its therapies.

What Alpha Tau Medical does

Alpha Tau Medical develops and advances the Alpha DaRT (Diffusing Alpha-emitters Radiation Therapy) technology for targeted cancer treatment via clinical trials and regulatory pathways.
