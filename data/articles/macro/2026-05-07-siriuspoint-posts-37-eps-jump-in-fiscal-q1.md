Notable Developments
Underwriting results improved, with the core combined ratio—a key insurance profitability metric—falling to 88.9% from 95.4%, reflecting a drop in catastrophe losses and better pricing discipline.
Total revenue rose 6.5% compared to the prior year’s quarter, reaching $774.6 million, supported by Insurance & Services premium growth of 8% and disciplined underwriting in reinsurance.
The company returned $242 million to shareholders, including $42 million in buybacks, and raised its share repurchase commitment for fiscal 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained a cautious but positive outlook, highlighting growth opportunities in Insurance & Services and ongoing discipline in Reinsurance. The company re-emphasized its commitment to returning capital to shareholders and prudent risk selection. Investors should watch how SiriusPoint manages rising expense ratios and works to stabilize services income in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Underwriting profit was a standout this quarter, with a sharply improved core combined ratio, as catastrophe losses were much lower than last year. Core underwriting income more than doubled, supported by strong risk selection across both major segments.

Insurance & Services premium volumes increased 8%, even as the reinsurance book was trimmed by 10% to manage risk, shifting more of the company’s portfolio toward lower volatility business. This portfolio mix change aligns with the strategy to reduce earnings swings and increase capital efficiency.

On the downside, fee-based services income from managing general agent partnerships fell sharply year over year, due mainly to divestitures. While acquisitions like Assist America and World Nomads should help over time, this drop signals a challenge in diversifying earnings immediately.

SiriusPoint is a global specialty insurer and reinsurer, providing property and casualty coverage, risk services, and insurance capacity to clients worldwide.
