Key Takeaways
Earnings surged as lithium demand lifted results far above the prior year.

Lithium producer Sociedad Química Y Minera De Chile (NYSE:SQM) delivered a standout quarter thanks to sharp gains in core lithium and specialty fertilizer businesses. The company is a leading global supplier of lithium, iodine, and specialty plant nutrients, serving key battery and agricultural markets worldwide. On May 26, 2026, it released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showing substantial growth across major business lines. Overall, the quarter marked a period of strong revenue and profit gains, along with upgraded guidance for the full year.

Notable Developments
Lithium and derivatives revenue more than doubled, hitting $1,185.5 million, as both sales volumes and average prices rose sharply.
Gross profit margin expanded, with gross profit up 156% year over year, helped by slower growth in costs vs. revenue.
Guidance was raised: management now expects 15% growth in lithium sales volumes and 10% in fertilizer volumes for full fiscal 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now projects even stronger performance in its lithium and specialty fertilizer businesses for the year, guided by robust demand and firmer pricing. The upgraded volume targets in these key areas position the company for further revenue growth. Investors should watch both realized selling prices and margin trends, which remain sensitive to market and cost fluctuations.

Quarterly Highlights

Lithium sales drove most of the quarterly growth, with both volumes and prices significantly higher than the prior year. The company sold 69,000 metric tons of lithium carbonate equivalent, while the main Nova Andino Litio operation averaged $17.80 per kilogram--up nearly 95% from last year.

Specialty plant nutrients, including potassium nitrate and other fertilizers, also saw higher volumes and prices, resulting in a 13% revenue increase as Asian market supply tightened. Management raised its full-year outlook for this division, now targeting 10% sales growth compared to earlier, more modest projections.

Meanwhile, the iodine business held steady with improved volumes and slightly higher prices, and the potassium segment continued to shrink as the company shifted resources to its higher-value lithium business. Potassium volumes dropped 31%, in line with updated capacity priorities.

What Sociedad Química Y Minera De Chile Does

Sociedad Química Y Minera De Chile is a global supplier of lithium chemicals for batteries, iodine for technology and healthcare, and specialty fertilizers that support crop production worldwide.
