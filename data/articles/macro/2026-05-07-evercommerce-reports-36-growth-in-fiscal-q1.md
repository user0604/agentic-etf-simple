Key Takeaways
EverCommerce delivered slower revenue growth as operating costs climbed and adjusted EBITDA declined.
Earnings per share from continuing operations rose to $0.04, while adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization fell year over year.
Management maintained its full-year outlook, expecting growth to strengthen in the second half of the year.

Software platform EverCommerce (NASDAQ:EVCM) posted mixed results as growth slowed and operational spending rose. The company specializes in vertical software platforms for service-based small and medium businesses (SMBs) in home, health, and wellness sectors. Its first-quarter report for the period ended March 31, 2026, was published on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed limited progress in reaccelerating growth, with pressures on profitability and no evidence yet of a strong upturn.

Notable Developments
The company’s revenue grew 3.6%, just above the midpoint of its previous guidance, but this growth rate slowed from prior quarters.
Adjusted EBITDA, a profitability measure, declined nearly 10%, pressured by higher costs for sales and marketing that outpaced revenue gains.
EverCommerce repurchased 1.3 million shares for $13.9 million, continuing its focus on returning capital to shareholders.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its full-year and second-quarter guidance unchanged, indicating confidence in a pickup during the second half of 2026. While it expects growth to accelerate, evidence of a turnaround in revenue or margin trends has not yet appeared in results. Investors should closely watch for new customer growth, successful monetization of AI solutions, and updates on cross-sell or product adoption rates.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure increased but growth slowed compared to both the sequential and previous year’s quarters. The company's core subscription and transaction fees business, which forms the majority of revenue, continued to register modest gains but did not display notable momentum relative to the broader SaaS (software as a service) sector.

Adjusted EBITDA fell in the period, with margins tightening to the lowest level in recent quarters. Management cited increased spending in sales and marketing, which rose 15% year over year, as it aims to drive new business and product adoption.

EverCommerce continued share repurchases at a steady clip, using $13.9 million to buy back common stock and reduce average shares outstanding. No new acquisitions or significant divestitures were announced during the period, and no new updates were provided on major product launches or AI monetization.

What EverCommerce Does

EverCommerce provides vertical software platforms and digital solutions designed for service-based small and medium businesses in the home, health, and wellness industries.
