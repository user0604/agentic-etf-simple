Dollar General Posts 12% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 7:06 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Dollar General delivered a business win with higher earnings and margin expansion.
Earnings per share increased 12.4%, and same-store sales rose 2.0%.
Management raised full-year profit guidance while keeping sales targets steady.

Discount retailer Dollar General (NYSE:DG) delivered a solid first quarter, with margin gains and growth across all major product categories. The company, which operates more than 21,000 small-format stores across the United States and Mexico, reported results for its fiscal first quarter on June 2, 2026. The period showed disciplined cost management and improved profitability, even as growth in customer traffic and spending moderated compared to previous quarters.

Overall, the quarter featured robust earnings and operating profit growth, offset by slower topline momentum and higher operating expenses.

Notable Developments
Earnings per share reached $2.00, a 12.4% increase, due to improved operating margin and profit growth.
Same-store sales rose 2.0%, with growth across each major category and rising customer visits.
Management raised full-year profit targets, but did not change revenue or same-store sales guidance.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership increased its projected 2026 earnings per share to $7.20–$7.45, citing ongoing margin improvements and effective cost controls. The outlook for sales and store traffic remains unchanged, reflecting caution around customer demand trends.

Quarterly Highlights

The company expanded store count by 162 net new locations, including further rollout in Mexico. Remodeling projects and upgraded store formats continued to drive growth in all major product categories, with apparel and seasonal goods recording the highest sales increases year over year.

Gross margin improved as the company managed shrink (inventory loss or damage), markups, and per-store inventory growth. General expenses increased as a share of sales, mainly because of higher depreciation, utilities, and property taxes.

Although cash flow from operations was strong, it decreased compared to last year due to the timing of working capital needs and higher capital investment in store upgrades. The company maintained its quarterly cash dividend at $0.59 per share.

What Dollar General Does

Dollar General operates a chain of discount retail stores, offering low-priced household goods, consumables, apparel, and seasonal products in convenient small-box locations across the U.S. and Mexico.
