MU Company Overview
Micron designs and manufactures essential semiconductor technologies, specializing in memory devices that store data and storage products that retain information when power is removed. The company generates revenue by selling these components to manufacturers of smartphones, personal computers, and servers used in data centers. By integrating its hardware into the foundational architecture of artificial intelligence and mobile infrastructure, the company maintains long-term supply relationships with global technology enterprises and automotive manufacturers.

Micron Rides AI Demand With Broad Business Growth

Micron Technology makes memory and storage chips used in servers, personal devices, cars, and more. The company is riding the surge in artificial intelligence, with its advanced memory products in high demand worldwide. Over the last 5 years, the stock gained 410.1% versus the S&P 500's 69.9%, outperforming by 340.3 percentage points.
