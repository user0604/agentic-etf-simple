Nature’s Sunshine Products (NASDAQ:NATR) extended its track record of growth with another quarter of higher sales and improved profits. The leading natural health supplements and wellness company delivered strong top-line and margin gains across all regions in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (results released May 7, 2026). Notable Developments
Revenue grew 9% year over year to $122.9 million, with all regions contributing to the increase.
Operating income increased to $9.5 million from $6.2 million year over year, reflecting higher gross margin and better cost control.
Net cash from operating activities turned negative to $(1.8) million, largely on higher working capital needs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for fiscal 2026, targeting full-year net sales of $500 to $515 million and adjusted EBITDA in the $50 to $54 million range. Executives highlighted ongoing investments in digital marketing, consultant support, and auto-ship subscription programs as future growth drivers. Investors should closely monitor cash flow generation and the impact of foreign currency swings on profitability as the year unfolds.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth was broad, with the Asia segment up 7.3%, North America up 9.4%, Europe up 9.5%, and Latin America and Other up 9.7%. This came as Nature’s Sunshine continued to expand its range of supplements and wellness products through both digital and direct consultant channels.

The company’s gross profit margin improved to 73.2%, benefiting from cost savings initiatives, favorable market mix, and favorable foreign exchange. Operating income increased even faster as selling, general, and administrative expenses (SG&A) rose more slowly than sales, indicating increasing efficiency.

Despite stronger earnings, net cash used by operating activities was $(1.8) million, a reversal from positive cash flow last year. This was mainly due to higher accounts receivable and changes in working capital, signaling the need for continued attention to cash discipline.

What Nature’s Sunshine Products Does

Nature’s Sunshine Products develops, manufactures, and sells nutritional supplements and wellness products through a global network of independent consultants and digital channels.
