Movado Group Posts 8% Sales Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 7:41 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Movado Group delivered solid growth and improved profitability to start the year.
Adjusted earnings per share jumped to $0.32, while revenue grew 8.1% year over year.
Management withheld full-year guidance due to ongoing global uncertainty.

Watchmaker Movado Group (NYSE:MOV) marked a strong start to its fiscal year with higher sales and better profit margins. The company, known for designing, manufacturing, and distributing a portfolio of owned and licensed watch brands, saw progress in its United States and European businesses during the first quarter (reported May 27, 2026, for the quarter ended April 30). The period was also marked by a robust balance sheet and a higher dividend, although management remains cautious due to economic and geopolitical risks. Overall, the quarter delivered operational gains but provided limited visibility on the rest of the year.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased in both the United States and Europe, helping offset declines in the Middle East region where conflict impacted sales.
Gross margin improved by 3.2 percentage points, benefiting from changes in product mix and the scale provided by higher sales volume.
The company raised its quarterly dividend 14% to $0.40 per share, citing a strong cash position with no debt at quarter’s end.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide a full-year outlook, continuing a stance carried over from the previous quarter. Its caution reflects ongoing uncertainty from global conflicts and changing currency impacts. Investors should watch how sustainable recent sales growth and gross margin gains prove to be in upcoming quarters, especially as the company expects growth to moderate in the second quarter.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales grew in the company’s two largest markets, the United States and Europe, with U.S. net sales up 8.7% and international sales up 7.6%. However, a large portion of international sales growth was due to favorable foreign currency exchange, as constant-currency growth was just 1.6%.

Profitability climbed as gross margin rose to 57.3%, driven by an improved mix of sales channels and product types, alongside higher total sales. Operating expenses increased overall but declined as a share of total sales, allowing for greater income leverage.

The company took a small non-recurring charge tied to an investigation of its Dubai subsidiary. Cash provided by operations was positive and liquidity increased, supporting a larger quarterly dividend approved by the board and modest share repurchases.

What Movado Group Does

Movado Group designs, sources, and distributes watches and jewelry under a portfolio of owned and licensed brands, serving both wholesale and direct-to-consumer markets worldwide.
