Intercorp Financial Services (NYSE:IFS) recorded a strong start to the year, delivering significant profit growth and improved returns. The diversified Peruvian financial group offers banking, insurance, and wealth management services. In its earnings release dated May 11, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, it highlighted improved performance and market execution balanced by rising costs and continued competition. Notable Developments
Profitability improved, as net profit rose 34.9% year over year and annualized return on equity increased to 19.4%.
Risk costs fell sharply, which helped drive overall margins higher—impairment losses declined by 46.3% year over year to S/184.3 million.
Digital client engagement remained high, with 84% of retail customers using digital channels and digital sales representing 67% of total sales.
Management outlook and what to watch

While management did not provide new forward guidance for the year, past statements called for fiscal 2026 return on equity around 17% and single-digit loan growth. Actual results this quarter surpassed those benchmarks. Investors should continue to monitor expense increases and loan market share, as well as the sustainability of favorable risk trends in a competitive market environment.

Quarterly highlights

Profit growth outpaced both last year and internal company targets, with net profit reaching S/601.9 million and ROE exceeding prior targets. This improvement was supported by strong performances in both banking and wealth management businesses, as well as reduced credit losses.

The risk profile continued to improve, evidenced by sharply lower impairment losses on loans. Better payment behavior among clients and improved asset quality brought the cost of risk to its lowest level in several years.

Digital transformation advanced, with high levels of digital engagement and automated channels supporting growth in new products and services. Efforts in app-based mutual fund distribution and digital insurance sales contributed to new fee streams and customer acquisition, though overall market share in some retail lending segments decreased due to increased competition.

What Intercorp Financial Services does

Intercorp Financial Services is a Peruvian financial services group providing banking, insurance, and wealth management products to individuals and businesses throughout Peru.
