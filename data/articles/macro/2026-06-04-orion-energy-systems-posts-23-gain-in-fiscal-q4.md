Orion Energy Systems delivered strong results as LED lighting projects drove business gains.

Orion Energy Systems (NASDAQ:OESX) posted improved results as large-scale lighting installations bolstered quarterly business performance. The designer and manufacturer of energy-efficient lighting systems, controls, and electric vehicle charging solutions saw gains led by its LED lighting segment. Its earnings release, published June 4, 2026, covers the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 (three months ended March 31, 2026). Overall, the period reflected positive operational momentum marked by strong margin improvement and a narrowed net loss.

Notable developments
LED lighting projects drove LED segment revenue to $20.3 million in the quarter, up 86% year over year, supported by more and larger customer deployments.
The company improved gross margin by 9.5 percentage points to 37.0%, reflecting increased pricing discipline and cost structure changes.
Orion ended the quarter with a $30 million backlog for fiscal 2027, up $13 million compared to the previous year’s start.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects fiscal 2027 revenue to rise to between $95 million and $97 million, while also anticipating continued positive adjusted EBITDA. The company plans to grow in both lighting and EV charging business lines. The key area to watch is execution on its growing project backlog and further diversification of its customer base to reduce revenue concentration risks.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter’s strongest area was LED lighting, as higher project volumes and expanded customer relationships drove the segment’s revenue to increase by approximately 86% year over year. This came as Orion rolled out new energy-efficient LED systems and expanded deployments, highlighting its continued innovation and market demand for industrial and commercial lighting upgrades.

Profitability improved across the business, highlighted by record gross margin. The company credits a “structural reset” of its pricing and supply chain practices for this gain, and it has now delivered positive adjusted EBITDA for six straight quarters, indicating steady operational progress.

Orion also strengthened its financial position, ending the period with improved liquidity and a growing project backlog. It completed the earnout for its Voltrek acquisition, integrating EV charging capabilities, and maintained working capital and cash by reducing debt and raising funds through a recent stock offering.

What Orion Energy Systems does

Orion Energy Systems designs, manufactures, and installs energy-efficient LED lighting systems and related controls, and provides electric vehicle charging solutions and facility maintenance services for commercial, industrial, and public-sector clients.
