The company focuses on electric commercial vehicles and is ramping production following its recent merger with Motiv Electric Trucks. The earnings release, published May 14, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, highlighted improved sales momentum but also growing financial strain. Overall, the quarter was a mix of commercial traction and persistent losses, with the path to profitability and liquidity still uncertain.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership confirmed its goal of exiting fiscal 2026 at a $20 million annualized cost synergy run rate, driven by consolidation and streamlined operations. The most important factor for investors to monitor is whether the company can secure additional funding or sharply improve cash flow before reserves are exhausted.

Quarterly Highlights

Orders for electric commercial trucks accelerated, with new 100-unit deals from Gateway Fleets and Purolator contributing to a contracted order backlog of more than 200 vehicles since the Motiv merger. Gateway deliveries are set for July, while Purolator will expand its existing electric fleet by year-end.

Although sales expansion demonstrated market traction, operating efficiency did not keep pace. The company’s costs rose even faster than revenue, resulting in persistent negative gross margin and a wider operating loss for the period.

Facility consolidation in Union City, Indiana was completed and management maintains its synergy targets. However, falling to under $1 million in cash at quarter-end puts intense focus on liquidity and ongoing access to financing as critical next steps.
