Notable Developments
Revenue fell 29% from the prior-year period, mainly due to a sharp drop in healthcare prescription sales as management anticipated.
Gross margin reached a quarterly record at 35%, up 14 percentage points, driven by higher-margin contract services and improved drug costs.
Total operating expenses dropped 9% year over year, reflecting workforce reductions and tighter cost controls as part of restructuring.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated expectations for continued high gross margins and predicts the business will achieve positive operating income in the second half of fiscal 2026, as new contract revenues scale up. The most important thing for investors to monitor is whether contract wins and nationwide healthcare expansion will offset ongoing prescription revenue declines.

Quarterly Highlights

The Healthcare segment, which contributes the majority of revenue, saw a steep drop in prescription sales but nearly doubled its gross margin, supported by new contract activity and regulatory pricing changes. E-commerce operations recorded modest revenue growth and secured a $0.8 million Internet of Things (IoT) hardware contract for a NATO military customer, boosting the government and military sales pipeline above $1.2 million.

The company reported $11 million in cash and stable current liabilities, but a rise in receivables and inventory relative to shrinking revenue introduces short-term working capital risk if collection delays persist.

What NextPlat Does

NextPlat specializes in technology-enabled healthcare and e-commerce services, including pharmacy fulfillment and satellite communications products for both consumers and government customers.
