Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its adjusted EBITDA guidance for fiscal 2026 to a range of $605 million to $625 million, a notable increase from its prior forecast. The updated revenue guidance no longer includes results from future acquisitions, signaling greater emphasis on organic growth. Investors should watch whether high margins and occupancy are sustainable without recurring incentive program windfalls and as acquisition-driven growth slows.

Quarterly highlights

Profit surged due to both operational improvements and a $16.3 million non-recurring benefit from California’s Workforce & Quality Incentive Program, which boosted adjusted EBITDA. Even without this one-time item, profit growth remained substantial at 58% year over year.

The company continued selective acquisitions, investing $86.5 million in real estate, and ended the quarter with 324 healthcare operations and 290 skilled nursing facilities. Same-store revenue from skilled nursing facilities increased 8%, while occupancy improved to 90.9%.

Clinical quality remained a point of strength, with 78.4% of skilled nursing facilities rated 4 or 5 stars by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. Mature facilities averaged 4.4 stars, well above industry norms, and overall skilled mix improved compared to last year.

What Pacs Group does

Pacs Group operates and manages skilled nursing and post-acute care facilities, providing both short-term rehabilitation and long-term care services across the United States.
