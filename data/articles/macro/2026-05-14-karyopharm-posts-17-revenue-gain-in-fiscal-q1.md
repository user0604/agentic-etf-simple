Karyopharm Therapeutics (NASDAQ:KPTI) posted higher product revenue but saw softer demand for its core medicine due to competition. The oncology biotech, focused on commercializing and developing therapies like XPOVIO for multiple cancers, released its latest earnings results on May 14, 2026, for the quarter ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter featured mixed operational progress, with headline revenue growth supported by accounting effects rather than underlying prescription strength.

Notable developments
U.S. XPOVIO net product revenue increased 38% year over year to $29.2 million, but the company stated this was due mainly to lower discounts and returns, not volume growth. Prescription demand was lower than the previous year as new competing cancer drugs entered the market.
Total company revenue rose 17%, helped by modest growth in international royalties and a smaller contribution from license and other revenue compared to the prior period.
R&D and other operating expenses were nearly flat, while cash and equivalents rose to $91.2 million -- supported by a recent equity financing that extended the cash runway into late third quarter 2026.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its 2026 guidance for both total revenue ($130–$150 million) and U.S. XPOVIO net product revenue ($115–$130 million), though results for this quarter are at the low end of these ranges if annualized. Investors should watch upcoming Phase 3 clinical trial results, especially in endometrial cancer and myelofibrosis, which are expected in mid-to-late 2026 and could impact the company’s future revenue and strategic options.

Quarterly highlights

XPOVIO, a tablet approved to treat several blood cancers, generated higher reported net product revenue compared to the prior year, but management made clear that underlying patient demand fell, mainly because of new competitor launches. The improvement in net revenue largely came from lower gross-to-net (discount and return) adjustments rather than new prescription growth.

The company’s pipeline advanced with mixed clinical results. Its SENTRY Phase 3 trial in myelofibrosis met a key efficacy benchmark (reduction in spleen volume) but did not show a statistical benefit in symptom improvement compared to standard therapy, making future regulatory or commercial gains less certain. Also, enrollment completed for the XPORT-EC-042 trial in endometrial cancer, with a crucial data readout expected mid-2026.

Karyopharm’s cash position improved to $91.2 million, thanks to new funding, allowing the company to operate into late in the third quarter of 2026. The company continues to carry significant debt, and remains reliant on future trial success or additional fundraising to extend operations further.

What Karyopharm Therapeutics does

Karyopharm Therapeutics develops and commercializes cancer therapies, with its main product XPOVIO used to treat multiple myeloma and other blood cancers.
