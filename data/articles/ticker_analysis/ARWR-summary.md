ARWR Company Overview
Arrowhead creates medicines that treat difficult diseases by silencing the genes responsible for them through a natural process called RNA interference. The company earns revenue by partnering with major pharmaceutical firms to develop these gene-silencing therapies and by selling its own approved treatments for rare conditions. By maintaining proprietary delivery technology and specialized manufacturing facilities, the company establishes a technical moat that supports its clinical pipeline and long-term collaboration agreements.

Arrowhead Builds Pipeline as REDEMPLO Prescriptions Rise

Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals creates medicines that silence problem genes to treat genetic and heart-related diseases. The company stands out for bringing its first RNA interference drug, REDEMPLO, to market. Over the past five years, the stock returned 17.9%, trailing the S&P 500’s 75.0% gain by 57.1 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $73.7 million (down 86.4% YoY)
EPS (Diluted): ($0.93), compared to $2.75 last year
REDEMPLO prescriptions: Over 400 written in the U.S, shipments made to about 180 patients

Latest Developments

REDEMPLO saw more than 40% growth in new prescriptions recently, which could help drive future sales as the company builds its commercial business.
Arrowhead increased cash resources to $1.78 billion through new financings, giving it more flexibility to invest in research and expand internationally.
