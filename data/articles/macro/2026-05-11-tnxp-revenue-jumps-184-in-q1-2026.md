Notable Developments
TONMYA, approved in late 2025 for fibromyalgia, completed its first full quarter on the market with over 2,100 prescribing healthcare providers and about 5,400 prescriptions filled.
Total revenue reached $6.9 million, reflecting the uptake of TONMYA and legacy migraine products, but operating expenses climbed to $48.4 million.
Cash decreased to $185.5 million as the company’s increased investments in commercialization and research led to a net loss of $40.2 million for the quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management states cash on hand, plus recent fundraising, will support operations into early Q2 2027. It has not issued explicit revenue or profit guidance for the year but highlights expanding payer coverage and accelerating TONMYA prescriptions as priorities. Investors should watch prescription growth, payer access, and upcoming pipeline trial milestones as key indicators of future performance.

Quarterly Highlights

The period marked the first full commercial quarter for TONMYA, the company’s newly launched fibromyalgia treatment. Over 2,100 physicians wrote prescriptions and nearly 3,600 patients started therapy, with Medicaid coverage expanding to 38 states. However, most prescription fills so far came from initial or bridge scripts, and broad commercial payer access is still rolling out.

Revenue and costs both ballooned, as the company grew its commercial infrastructure and continued advancing its drug pipeline. Selling, general, and administrative expenses nearly tripled from the prior year, largely due to TONMYA’s launch and added headcount. Research and development spending also more than doubled, supporting a broad portfolio of clinical and preclinical programs ranging from depression to rare diseases and vaccines.

With operating losses of $40.2 million and a sharply higher cash burn, liquidity remains a concern. While management expects sufficient cash into early Q2 2027, spending on commercialization and R&D continues to far exceed incoming revenue, so further product uptake or additional capital may be required if that balance does not shift.

What Tonix Pharmaceuticals Does

The company develops and commercializes prescription medicines with a focus on central nervous system conditions, including fibromyalgia and rare diseases.
