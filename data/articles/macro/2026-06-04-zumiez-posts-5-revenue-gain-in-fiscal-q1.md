Zumiez Posts 5% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 4, 2026, 4:26 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Zumiez delivered sales growth and improved margins but remains unprofitable for the quarter.
Revenue increased 4.9% compared to the prior year period.
Management expects continued operating losses in Q2 and plans further store closures for fiscal 2026.

Zumiez (NASDAQ:ZUMZ) grew its top line and narrowed losses, but the bottom line stayed negative as operating improvements were not enough to turn a profit. The specialty action sports retailer, known for its youth-oriented apparel and gear, detailed these results in its fiscal first quarter update released June 4, 2026 (covering the period ended May 2, 2026). Overall, the quarter showed moderate progress, with better sales and gross margin, yet the business signaled caution given softening early Q2 trends and a continued focus on optimizing its store footprint.

Notable developments
Comparable sales rose 4%, at the high end of company guidance, with growth in both North America and Europe.
Gross margin improved by 1.7 percentage points year over year, reaching 31.7% as product margins and cost controls took hold.
Zumiez ended the period with $124.2 million in cash and marketable securities, and repurchased 0.3 million shares for $6.2 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects second quarter revenue between $210 million and $215 million, but expects another quarterly loss per share between $0.23 and $0.08. The company plans to close about 26 stores and open 5 in fiscal 2026. Investors should watch May and early Q2 sales performance closely, as early quarter data showed a notable slowdown from Q1 levels.

Quarterly highlights

Net revenue climbed 4.9% year over year, reaching the top end of management’s expectations. Comparable store sales (sales from locations open at least twelve months, a retail benchmark for underlying growth) improved 4%, with strength in both the United States and international markets, notably Europe.

While the company shrank its operating loss to $15.2 million, and gross margin widened to 31.7% of sales, it still posted a net loss for the quarter. Interest income fell from the prior year, slightly offsetting the gains from better operating performance. Importantly, loss per share was marginally worse than last year, due to a lower share count from ongoing share buybacks which mathematically increase loss per share when the company is unprofitable.

Zumiez continued efforts to optimize its store base, planning for a net reduction of 21 stores in fiscal 2026, while focusing on higher-productivity locations. The integration of digital and store channels remains a key focus, even as overall store count is trimmed to match traffic and retail trends. Liquidity remains strong with $124.2 million in cash and marketable securities at quarter-end and no debt on the balance sheet.

What Zumiez does

Zumiez is a specialty retailer offering apparel, footwear, accessories, and hardgoods for action sports and streetwear customers through mall-based stores and online channels.
