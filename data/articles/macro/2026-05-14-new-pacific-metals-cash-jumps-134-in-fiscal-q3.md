Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintains that its cash reserves are sufficient to meet all planned expenditures for at least the next year as it advances its main assets, particularly Silver Sand. The company plans to keep investing in project development and maintain operational stability. Investors should watch for proof of new mineral resources, key exploration results, and developments in Bolivia’s legal and political environment, as these could directly affect long-term prospects.

Quarterly Highlights

The company finished the quarter with $39.86 million in cash after a large equity raise, more than doubling its liquidity and easing near-term funding pressure. This positions it to continue exploration and development without immediate need for new financing.

Operating loss remained steady as core projects are still in the exploration phase, and most costs relate to staffing, developing projects, and corporate activities needed to advance these assets. There was a meaningful uptick in operating expenses tied to project work and corporate growth.

Investments at the Silver Sand property rose sharply, consistent with its status as the flagship asset. Meanwhile, spending at other sites was flat or down, reflecting a targeted approach to allocating resources and advancing the most promising projects.
