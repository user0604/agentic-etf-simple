Sigma Lithium (NASDAQ:SGML) delivered improved profitability after recovering from supply disruptions, though cash balances remain thin. Notable developments
Sales revenue fell to $42.3 million as shipment volume of lithium concentrate dropped year over year, but new product streams like lithium fines cushioned the decline.
Gross profit nearly doubled due to a marked decline in mining and processing costs, as the company transitioned operations in-house and improved plant efficiency.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects a continued ramp-up in production with increased sales of both high-grade and lower-grade lithium products, supported by multi-year customer agreements. The company acknowledges the need to resolve negative working capital and fix internal control weaknesses. Investors should pay close attention to liquidity levels and the impact of supplier disputes and arbitration claims on future cash flow.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue in the period declined from the prior year as total lithium concentrate shipments, including lower-grade material, dropped to 23.6 thousand tonnes from 61.6 thousand tonnes. However, the introduction of product streams like high-purity lithium fines brought in nearly $35.5 million in net revenue and supported overall sales.

Profitability strengthened, as cost of goods sold fell by more than 50% to $16.8 million. Much of this came from the in-sourcing of mining activity, which cut direct mine and processing costs to $1.5 million from $19.8 million in the preceding year, while overall plant operating expenses remained in check.

Despite the improved earnings and reduced debt, cash balances shrank to a low level and the firm ended the quarter with negative working capital of $144.5 million. While prepayments from new customer offtake deals provided some relief, management flagged this as a real risk for ongoing operations.

What Sigma Lithium does

Sigma Lithium produces battery-grade lithium concentrate from its mining complex in Brazil for use by electric vehicle and battery manufacturers worldwide.
