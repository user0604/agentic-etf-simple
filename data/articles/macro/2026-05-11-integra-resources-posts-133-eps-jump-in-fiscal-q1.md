Notable Developments
Revenue increased 8%, driven by a record realized gold price of $4,854 per ounce, despite gold sold dropping by 36% year over year.
Costs surged as cash costs per gold ounce rose 20% to $2,422, and mine-site all-in sustaining cost jumped 41% to $3,310, both exceeding management’s annual guidance.
Free cash flow dropped 69%, primarily due to inventory builds and higher capital spending, even as the company improved its balance sheet by raising $57.5 million in new capital.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept full-year gold production guidance unchanged at 70,000–75,000 ounces, expecting deferred production to be recovered later in the year. However, cost pressures remain a challenge, as cash cost and all-in sustaining cost metrics already exceed annual targets. Investors should closely monitor progress at Florida Canyon to see if the company can recover deferred ounces and manage costs in future quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Gold sales volumes declined sharply, but higher prices allowed revenue and adjusted earnings to climb year over year. The average realized gold price nearly doubled, providing a significant boost to profitability despite operational setbacks related to leach pad constraints.

Mining costs per ounce were well above management’s forecasts, due to a combination of deferred production, higher diesel prices, and increased royalties tied to stronger gold prices. The company stated both cash costs and all-in sustaining cost rose above guidance ranges for the period.

Despite cost overruns, Integra Resources strengthened its balance sheet by completing a $57.5 million capital raise. This supports the continued permitting and early development work at its DeLamar project, which has the potential to drive future growth once in production.

What Integra Resources Does

Integra Resources is a precious metals mining company focused on the exploration and development of gold and silver projects in the United States, with its current production centered at the Florida Canyon Mine and future growth aimed at the DeLamar project in Idaho.
