Motorola Solutions Reports 7% Revenue Rise in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:44 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Motorola Solutions posted revenue and earnings growth, setting a record for first-quarter backlog.
Non-GAAP EPS rose 6.0% year over year.
Management raised full-year guidance, citing strong demand and a robust backlog.

Motorola Solutions (NYSE:MSI) saw strong demand for its software and services, fueling growth and prompting an increase to its full-year guidance. The leading provider of mission-critical communications and security technology reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. The period featured non-GAAP earnings growth, a record backlog, and continued cash flow pressures.

Notable developments
The Software and Services segment led growth, with sales up 18% and non-GAAP operating margin expanding to 34.2%.
Total company free cash flow declined to $389 million, mainly due to higher inventories and larger interest and tax payments.
Backlog reached a first-quarter record of $15.7 billion, increasing 11% year over year, supported by new contract wins and strong orders.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 outlook, now targeting $12.8 billion in revenue and non-GAAP EPS of $16.87–$16.99, citing record orders and backlog as key factors. The outlook for the second quarter calls for revenue growth of about 8.5% and non-GAAP EPS between $3.82 and $3.88. Investors should watch whether the backlog converts to revenue and if higher-margin trends in software continue.

Quarterly highlights

The Software and Services segment provided the key lift, as sales grew 18% to $1,155 million and non-GAAP operating earnings rose 40%. Growth was driven by mission-critical networks, cloud-based command center offerings, and video security products, according to company statements. These solutions are designed to help agencies and enterprises manage safety operations more effectively.

Products and Systems Integration saw little sales growth, and margins dropped sharply. Operating margin for this segment fell 9.1 percentage points year over year to 13.7%, with costs pressured by higher earnouts related to acquisitions and increased intangible asset amortization.

Cash generation weakened, with free cash flow dropping 17.8% year over year to $389 million. The company attributed this to higher inventory levels, increased tax and interest paid, and continued investment in strategic acquisitions including Exacom (cloud-based recording) and Hyper (AI solutions for public safety).

What Motorola Solutions does

The company provides mission-critical communications, command center software, video security, and access control solutions to public safety, government, and enterprise customers worldwide.
