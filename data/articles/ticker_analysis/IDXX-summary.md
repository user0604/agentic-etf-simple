IDEXX Expands Diagnostic Testing for Companion Animal Parasites

IDEXX Laboratories has expanded its veterinary diagnostic menu by broadening its Fecal Dx antigen testing to detect taeniid tapeworms in companion animals. The new panel includes the medically significant Echinococcus species, a zoonotic parasite that can spread between animals and humans, addressing a growing concern for public health officials and veterinarians. This expansion builds on the company's focus on preventive care and comprehensive diagnostic workflows inside clinics.

IDXX Company Overview
IDEXX provides diagnostic and software solutions for the animal health and water testing industries. The company generates revenue by selling medical instruments and recurring testing supplies to veterinary clinics, while also operating a global network of reference laboratories. By integrating these diagnostic tools with its proprietary practice management software and cloud-based data platforms, the company creates a unified ecosystem that embeds its technology into the daily workflows of veterinary professionals worldwide.

Idexx Raises Outlook as Recurring Sales Grow

Idexx Laboratories makes tests and software to help vets, water labs, and farms check animal and water health. The business stands out because most of its sales come from recurring products that pet clinics and labs order again and again. Over the last five years, the stock returned 5.6% while the S&P 500 gained 71.3%, underperforming by 65.7 percentage points.
