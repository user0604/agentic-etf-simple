Earnings Preview: Lionsgate Studios

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Lionsgate Studios (NYSE:LION), a producer and distributor of movies and television series, is expected to report its next quarterly earnings on Thursday, May 21, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

The third quarter of fiscal 2026 saw Lionsgate Studios deliver revenue of $724.3 million, which was above analyst expectations. However, the company reported a net loss attributable to shareholders of $46.2 million, or $0.16 per share, with adjusted earnings per share coming in at $0.01. This adjusted figure was in line with some non-GAAP expectations, but the reported loss per share missed the consensus EPS estimate of -$0.02. The quarter was marked by strong box office performances from new releases such as The Housemaid and Now You See Me: Now You Don’t, while television production revenue was impacted by the timing of episodic deliveries. The company’s film and television library also reached record trailing 12-month revenue of $1.05 billion, reflecting ongoing demand for its content.

Positives
Revenue beat expectations: Quarterly revenue of $724.3 million was above the consensus estimate, driven by successful theatrical releases and international performance.
Box office momentum from new franchises: The launch of The Housemaid and strong international results from Now You See Me: Now You Don’t contributed to growth in the Motion Picture segment.
Record library revenue: The company’s content library achieved its fifth consecutive record quarter, with trailing 12-month revenue reaching $1.05 billion, showing the value and demand for its film and TV assets.
Television series renewals: Renewals for 12 out of 13 scripted series provide visibility for future episodic deliveries and support management’s targets for doubling scripted output in fiscal 2027.
Negatives
EPS missed consensus: The reported net loss per share of $0.16 was worse than the expected -$0.02, reflecting higher expenses and some non-operating charges.
Profitability under pressure: Segment profit for Motion Pictures declined year over year, mainly due to higher marketing and promotional expenses for multiple wide releases.
Television revenue declined: TV production revenue fell 25% compared to the prior year quarter, largely because of fewer episodic deliveries and tough comparisons to last year’s releases.
Leverage remains elevated: Net debt stood at $1.75 billion with leverage at 7.4 times trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA, though management expects this to improve as earnings and cash flow recover.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Carryover impact from The Housemaid: Investors should look for the full financial contribution of The Housemaid in Q4, as most of its box office revenue will be recognized in this period and is expected to drive results.
Motion Picture segment profit rebound: Management has guided for a material improvement in adjusted operating income before depreciation and amortization (OIBDA) in Q4, as the film slate benefits from both theatrical and pay-one window releases.
Progress on debt reduction: Watch for updates on leverage, as the company anticipates a meaningful decline from current levels due to improved cash flow and earnings, with a target of mid-4 times leverage by mid-fiscal 2027.
Television scripted deliveries and backlog: The pace of episodic deliveries and the size of the backlog (which reached $1.5 billion, up 26% year over year) will be key indicators for future revenue and cash flow visibility.
Status of new initiatives and franchise development: Investors should monitor updates on pending or announced projects such as sequels to The Housemaid, new productions like John Rambo and Dirty Dancing, and the rollout of live events and video game adaptations, as these are expected to support growth in fiscal 2027 and beyond.
