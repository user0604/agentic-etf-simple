Key Takeaways
Outset Medical delivered stronger profitability but saw a drop in total revenue from product sales.
EPS loss narrowed and gross margin improved significantly year over year.
Management maintained its full-year guidance, anticipating improved sales in later quarters.

Outset Medical (NASDAQ:OM) delivered a solid quarter for profitability but faced lower revenue in its latest report. The medical technology company, known for its Tablo Hemodialysis System used in hospitals and homes, reported first-quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected strong cost discipline and margin expansion, but new product sales declined, raising the importance of future sales acceleration.

Notable developments
Gross margin expanded by 6.2 percentage points from the prior year, reaching 43.8% on a non-GAAP basis despite a revenue decrease.
Recurring revenues, mainly from cartridges and services, held steady even as product revenue declined 13% compared to the prior year period.
Net cash usage in operating activities was reduced by half year over year, ending the period with $161 million in liquidity.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management confirmed full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $125–$130 million and gross margin in the low to mid-40% range. Achieving this will require a step up in sales in later quarters, especially after the soft start for product revenues. Investors should focus on signs of accelerated system placements and adoption of the next-generation Tablo system as the most important metric for upcoming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability metrics improved across the board, with a sharper focus on gross margin and lower cash burn. The company’s loss per share narrowed and net cash usage in daily operations was cut significantly, reflecting tighter expense controls.

Product revenue, which represents new system sales and upgrades, fell notably compared to both the prior year and previous quarter. In contrast, service and recurring revenues were stable or improved, showing enduring utilization by existing customers even as new installations softened.

The company maintained its guidance, indicating management’s confidence in achieving a rebound in sales and continued margin strength. There were no disclosures of regulatory or operational disruptions, and Outset Medical entered the next quarter with a strong cash position and clear targets.

What Outset Medical does

Outset Medical designs and manufactures the Tablo Hemodialysis System, a user-friendly device simplifying dialysis delivery for hospitals, clinics, and home use.
