Business overview
Specialty and oncology distribution drove significant profit growth, with Oncology & Multispecialty segment adjusted operating profit up 53% year over year.
Free cash flow (Non-GAAP), an important measure of cash available for shareholders and reinvestment, increased 7% to $3.2 billion for the quarter.
The company raised its quarterly dividend by 15% to $0.82 per share and expanded its share buyback authorization by $5 billion during the period.
Quarterly performance

Oncology and specialty distribution businesses led growth, with segment profit in Oncology & Multispecialty rising by over half, fueled by organic gains and the integration of recent acquisitions such as Prism Vision and Core Ventures.

The North American Pharmaceutical segment also registered meaningful progress, as strong prescription and specialty medication demand offset slower growth in branded drug volumes, resulting in profit up 11% compared with the prior-year quarter.

In contrast, the Medical-Surgical Solutions unit experienced weak profitability, with profit declining 5% and margin softening, highlighting ongoing end-market challenges. The planned divestiture of this slower-growth segment is a key watch item for investors.

Looking ahead

Management projects adjusted earnings per share to climb 12–14% in fiscal 2027, supported by ongoing investments in oncology, technology, and specialty distribution. The most important area to monitor next is the planned separation of the Medical-Surgical Solutions segment—this shift could change both profit mix and risk exposure as McKesson sharpens its focus on faster-growing, higher-margin businesses.

What McKesson does

McKesson distributes pharmaceuticals and medical supplies and provides technology, logistics, and value-added healthcare services to pharmacies, hospitals, and providers across North America.
