The company, a publicly traded Bitcoin miner with additional specialty finance operations, reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 15, 2026 (covering the period ended March 31, 2026). Overall, the quarter brought mining production records, but profitability and cash reserves came under renewed pressure.

Notable Developments
Revenue fell 11.1% year over year to $2.1 million as the average Bitcoin price declined, even as the company mined a record 26.1 Bitcoins in the period.
Mining margin narrowed sharply to 24.1%, with management citing a lower Bitcoin market price as the main cause of compressed profitability.
Cash at quarter end dropped to $0.8 million, raising liquidity concerns given $22.7 million in total liabilities and continued net losses.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its commitment to execution in mining operations and treasury strategy but did not provide specific financial guidance for the next quarter or full year. Its stated priorities include maintaining operational efficiency, expanding mining capabilities, and preserving financial flexibility. Investors should closely monitor the company’s cash position and any impacts from further Bitcoin price movements, as these factors directly affect both operating results and liquidity.

Quarterly Highlights

The company achieved its highest Bitcoin production, with 26.1 BTC mined, thanks to a record energized hashrate of 790 petahash per second (PH/s). This was supported by new hardware deployments, notably 300 Bitmain S19 XP mining machines, and infrastructure expansions at its Oklahoma and Mississippi sites. Higher output did not translate into higher revenue, as the average realized Bitcoin price dropped by 19% compared to the prior-year quarter.

Profitability came under pressure, with mining margin falling significantly from the prior year and the company posting a much larger Core EBITDA loss. This was mainly the result of $7.0 million in non-cash charges tied to fair value adjustments on Bitcoin holdings and collateral, along with higher running costs from a larger mining fleet. These accounting charges reflect the lower period-end value of Bitcoin, which decreased $14,200 per coin from a year earlier.

The balance sheet took a hit as cash reserves dropped and total liabilities edged up. While the company holds substantial Bitcoin on its balance sheet -- 338.2 BTC valued at $23.1 million at quarter-end -- quickly converting these assets to cash during weak markets could be challenging and potentially unprofitable. The extension of a key loan facility provided some short-term relief, but liquidity remains a concern as operational cash outflows persist.

What Lm Funding America Does

Lm Funding America is a cryptocurrency mining company that operates Bitcoin mining facilities in Oklahoma and Mississippi, with a small specialty finance segment focused on lending to nonprofit community associations.
