Vaxart (OTC:VXRT) delivered a business win this quarter, returning to profitability as contract revenue surged. The vaccine developer specializes in oral tablet vaccines, a model it believes can simplify immunization. Reporting on May 7, 2026, for its first quarter of fiscal 2026, the company highlighted stronger funding but noted delays in major clinical milestones. Notable developments
Revenue nearly doubled year over year, driven mainly by government funding, especially from the BARDA contract. Net income shifted to a positive $5.2 million.
Clinical data releases for the COVID-19 oral vaccine faced delays, with the sentinel cohort readout now scheduled for the second quarter and the main data pushed to early 2027.
The company’s cash runway extends into the second quarter of 2027, and Vaxart has added a $25 million equity purchase agreement for additional flexibility.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects sufficient cash to fund operations through at least the second quarter of 2027 and is focusing on controlling costs and expanding partnerships. The most important item for investors is the upcoming COVID-19 trial data, which will determine future regulatory and commercial plans.

Quarterly highlights

Contract revenue, mainly from BARDA and the Dynavax partnership, led to sharply higher revenue and operating income compared to the prior year. Profitability in the quarter was primarily due to recognized government contract revenue, not product sales.

The COVID-19 Phase 2b trial enrolled about 5,400 participants but key trial results were delayed. The 400-participant sentinel cohort data is now expected in the coming quarter, with the larger KP.2 cohort results moved to early 2027.

Research and development costs decreased, mainly due to lower personnel and norovirus program expenses, while cash and investments ended at $61 million. Vaxart also relocated its headquarters to contain costs and signed a facility allowing $25 million in equity financing if needed.

What Vaxart does

Vaxart develops oral tablet vaccines designed to prevent infectious diseases, using a proprietary delivery technology.
