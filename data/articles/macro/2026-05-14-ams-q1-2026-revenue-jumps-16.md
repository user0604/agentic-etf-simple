Notable Developments
Direct patient services revenue jumped 30.2%, with higher treatment volumes from Rhode Island and Puebla centers.
Gross margin rose by 2.8 percentage points as higher case loads outpaced the growth in fixed costs.
Cash increased over year-end, but debt discussions with the main lender continue and net losses remain.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated that patient volumes are trending higher into the second quarter, expecting further margin improvement as utilization rises. However, the company remains in talks with its lender regarding the terms of its debt, as current liabilities outpace current assets. Investors should monitor whether patient growth will be enough to tip the company to sustainable profitability and how quickly the debt and liquidity situation stabilizes.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter brought another strong step in direct patient care, as those services generated about three in every five dollars of revenue, reflecting the company's strategic pivot from equipment leasing to clinical operations. Gamma Knife and proton therapy procedures grew more than 10% and 20%, respectively, highlighting rising utilization of advanced radiation treatment offerings.

Gross margin improvement followed higher patient volumes at upgraded and new centers, particularly in Rhode Island and Mexico. Although this lifted operational cash generation, the gains were partly offset by higher spending on staff, maintenance, and expiring equipment warranties.

The balance sheet showed modest improvements, including increased cash on hand and slightly lower short-term debt. Still, ongoing debt covenant discussions and a persistent net loss mean the company’s financial footing will need continued strengthening in upcoming quarters.

What American Shared Hospital Services Does

American Shared Hospital Services provides advanced radiation therapy equipment leasing and operates direct patient care centers, focusing on cancer treatment with technologies like Gamma Knife and proton therapy.
