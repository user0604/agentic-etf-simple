1. Figma's Beat-and-Raise Q1

Figma (NYSE: FIG), recommended in Rule Breakers, had a Thursday night to remember. The digital design software company posted first-quarter revenue of $333.4 million -- up 46% year over year and well past estimates -- while earnings per share (EPS) of $0.10 nearly doubled the $0.06 consensus. Shares jumped 8% after hours. Oh, and Figma raised its full-year revenue guidance to between $1.42 billion and $1.43 billion, above the prior $1.37 billion consensus.

AI isn't just a buzzword here: CFO Praveer Melwani pointed to early artificial intelligence (AI) monetization gains -- via products like Figma Make and Figma Weave -- as a real driver of the beat, alongside stronger-than-expected seat expansion across organizations.
Customers are multiplying: Paying customers grew 54% year over year, $100,000+ annual recurring revenue (ARR) accounts rose 48%, and net dollar retention (NDR) hit 139% -- the company's best reading in more than two years.
2. Crypto Bill Clears Senate Committee

Crypto just got a big win in Washington. The Senate Banking Committee passed the CLARITY Act 15-9, advancing a bill that would finally give digital assets a clear regulatory home -- divvying up oversight between the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). Coinbase (NASDAQ: COIN) surged as much as 9%; Gemini Space Station (NASDAQ: GEMI), Robinhood (NASDAQ: HOOD), and SoFi (NASDAQ: SOFI) all caught the tailwind, too. Bitcoin (CRYPTO: BTC), a Hidden Gems recommendation, was up 2.1%, flirting with $82,000.

Clock ticking on Democratic holdouts: The bill needs seven Democratic floor votes to pass, but the two Democrats who crossed the aisle in committee stopped short of a floor commitment. That leaves the outcome genuinely uncertain.
Stablecoin fight isn't over: Banks and crypto platforms are still feuding over whether platforms can pay interest on stablecoin holdings -- a dispute that matters enormously to Coinbase, whose USDC partnership with Circle (NYSE: CRCL) is built around exactly that feature.
3. Your Broker Is Now a Prediction Machine

Want to bet on whether the U.S. will enter a recession? Now you can -- through your broker. Stock Advisor (Team HG) rec Interactive Brokers (NASDAQ: IBKR) is rolling out a prediction market platform, letting users trade yes-or-no contracts via Kalshi, CME Group (NASDAQ: CME), and its own ForecastEx.

"Is this gambling?" (They say no.) IBKR is keeping things buttoned-up, and contracts are limited to elections, climate, and economic topics. No Timothée Chalamet Knicks attendance contracts here...for now.
Big money smells opportunity: Kalshi says institutional demand has surged eightfold in six months, and IBKR founder Thomas Peterffy sees commodities hedging and disaster risk as the killer apps -- once there's enough liquidity to support institutional-sized trades. Skeptics worry that algorithm-armed institutions will eat retail traders' lunch.
4. Your Take

Figma's 139% NDR means its existing customers are collectively spending 39% more than they were a year ago -- a sign that the product is expanding within organizations, not just adding new ones.
