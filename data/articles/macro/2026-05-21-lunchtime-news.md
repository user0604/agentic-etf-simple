1. Spotify Pops on 2030 Guidance

★ SPOT is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team RB)

Spotify (NYSE: SPOT) shares jumped 15% after the music streaming platform issued long-term guidance at its first investor day since 2022. The company called 1 billion subscribers and $100 billion in revenue its "north star" by 2030.

Growth roadmap: Spotify forecast revenue growth at a compounded annual rate in the mid-teens and gross margins between 35% and 40% by 2030. Since 2022, the platform has added more than 340 million users and 110 million subscribers.
Reshuffle in motion: The investor day marks the debut of co-CEOs Gustav Söderström and Alex Norström, who took over after founder Daniel Ek stepped down at the start of 2026. Shares have lost a quarter of their value year to date as the company pushes deeper into audiobooks and podcasts.
2. Nvidia Cedes China to Huawei
★ NVDA is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team HG)

Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA) CEO Jensen Huang said the company has "largely conceded" China's AI chip market to Huawei as U.S. export restrictions reshape the global semiconductor landscape. The comments came alongside another blockbuster quarter, with revenue surging 85% to $81.6 billion and an $80 billion buyback announcement.

China shut out: China once made up at least a fifth of Nvidia's data center revenue, but the company has been effectively locked out since the Trump administration required licenses for advanced chip exports in April. Huang told investors to "expect nothing" on near-term approvals.
Bigger picture bet: Huang said Nvidia is investing across what he calls the AI industry's "five-layer cake" of energy, chips, infrastructure, models, and applications. He floated the idea of Nvidia becoming a "many times larger company" as it pours cash into supporting its supply chain.
3. Eli Lilly's Next Weight Loss Drug Delivers

Eli Lilly (NYSE: LLY) said its next-generation obesity drug retatrutide cleared a crucial late-stage trial, delivering an average 28.3% weight loss -- or 70.3 pounds -- at the highest dose over 80 weeks. That tops the roughly 20% to 22% seen with Lilly's blockbuster Zepbound and brings the weekly injection closer to approval.

Surgery-level results: Chief scientific officer Dan Skovronsky called the near-30% weight loss figure "incredible," noting it had previously only been associated with bariatric surgery. Roughly 45% of the 2,500 trial patients hit that 30% threshold, and TD Cowen analysts have estimated retatrutide could generate $3.8 billion in 2030 sales.
Lilly extends its lead: Lilly held 60.1% of the U.S. obesity and diabetes drug market in Q1 versus Novo Nordisk's (NYSE: NVO) 39.4%. Novo is racing to catch up after agreeing to pay up to $2 billion last year for the rights to a similar triple-hormone drug from China's United Laboratories International, but that treatment is years behind retatrutide.
