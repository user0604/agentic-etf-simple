Key Takeaways
Loan and asset sales moved quickly, but resulted in heavy near-term losses.
Earnings per share dropped sharply year over year, with revenue also declining.
Management expects book value pressure to ease after additional planned asset sales in the next quarter.

Loan liquidations and asset sales generated fresh cash at Ready Capital (NYSE:RC), but came with severe losses this quarter. The diversified real estate finance firm sold a significant portion of its loan portfolio, focusing on debt reduction and repositioning ahead of major 2026 obligations. The results, released May 7, 2026, cover the first quarter and show rapid progress on management's stated plan, along with the costs of executing such a strategy. Overall, the quarter marked a difficult period, with a steep decline in profitability and book value, but important steps toward reducing leverage and future risk.

Notable developments
Management sold $1.0 billion in loans, generating $1.4 billion in gross cash and using this to pay off over $1.1 billion in asset-level financing and $184 million in corporate debt. Net proceeds were $177 million after debt paydowns.
Portfolio credit risk remains elevated, with 14.8% of the core commercial real estate loans delinquent by 60 days or more. This is up sharply following the recent asset sales.
The quarterly common share dividend was cut to $0.01 from $0.125 a year ago to conserve capital during the repositioning phase.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects to complete additional large-scale asset sales by the end of next quarter, which could help book value stabilize. The company points to a lower-leverage platform and renewed growth in small business and commercial real estate loans as medium-term goals. Investors should monitor the completion of remaining asset sales and any signs of credit deterioration in the core loan portfolio.

Quarterly highlights

Loan originations totaled $464 million in the first quarter, with most activity in lower middle market commercial real estate loans, U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) 7(a) loans, and U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) loans. Interest income nearly halved compared to the prior year, as ongoing asset liquidation shrank the company’s lending base.

The company’s core commercial real estate loan portfolio saw delinquencies climb to 14.8% following aggressive asset disposition. Portfolio risk remains high despite recent improvements in some real estate assets, such as sales momentum and higher occupancy at key hotel holdings.

Management reduced remaining 2026 corporate debt maturities to $450 million, following the retirement of senior unsecured notes earlier this year. Liquidity resources, including $200 million in cash and $730 million in unencumbered assets, will support ongoing repositioning efforts.

What Ready Capital does

Ready Capital is a real estate finance company that focuses on originating, acquiring, and managing commercial mortgage loans and small business loans across the United States.
