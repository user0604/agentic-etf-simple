Key Takeaways
Earnings momentum driven by fertilizer and ethanol strength was offset by weakness in food and agriculture and increased leverage.
Revenue climbed 22.5% year over year, while adjusted EBITDA more than doubled compared to the prior year.
Management expects fertilizer and ethanol strength to continue but aims to address profitability and reduce debt in future quarters.

Adecoagro (NYSE:AGRO) delivered a strong operating quarter, driven by newly consolidated fertilizer operations and record ethanol production, but struggled with weak food and agriculture results and sharply higher debt. The diversified agro-industrial company reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 11, 2026, as integration of its major fertilizer acquisition, Profertil, helped boost group-level earnings, while legacy segments faced margin compression. Overall, the quarter showed strong operational momentum amid rising financial risks and segmental volatility.

Notable developments
Fertilizers segment adjusted EBITDA surged 333.9%, benefiting from a nearly 10% rise in urea production and higher prices after integrating the Profertil business.
Sugar, Ethanol & Energy segment achieved a record sugarcane crushing of 2.2 million tons and shifted to a 96% ethanol production mix as ethanol volume and price rose.
Food & Agriculture adjusted EBITDA fell 91.9% year over year, as product prices for peanuts, rice, and cheese dropped sharply and overall segment sales declined 18%.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management anticipates continued strength in fertilizer earnings as global urea prices remain high and plant utilization normalizes. The company looks to recover profitability in food and agriculture once new crop sales commence. Investors should monitor margins in core segments and progress toward reducing net debt, which has increased significantly due to recent acquisitions.

Quarterly highlights

The formal addition of fertilizer operations through the Profertil acquisition rapidly changed the company’s earnings mix. Urea prices jumped 16.2% year over year, and higher operating rates pushed fertilizer segment sales up nearly 68% over pro forma figures from the prior year.

Sugar, Ethanol & Energy delivered operating records as crushing volumes increased nearly 50% and the ethanol product mix maximized profits. Ethanol sales volumes soared 125% with a price increase of 17.8%, even as sugar sales volumes fell sharply, reflecting a strategic response to shifting commodity prices.

Food & Agriculture was the clear weak spot, with adjusted EBITDA collapsing as prices for key products declined steeply. Segment sales fell to $162 million and margins eroded further, with recovery hinging on the sale of the new harvest later in the year.

What Adecoagro does

The company operates a diversified portfolio of agricultural and agro-industrial businesses across South America, including production of crops, sugar, ethanol, electricity, fertilizers, and dairy products.
