Notable Developments
Production averaged 36.2 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day, up 19% year over year, reaching the top end of company guidance.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects a temporary reduction in production and higher operating costs in the upcoming quarter due to a planned maintenance turnaround at a major facility. Full-year guidance for production and costs remains unchanged, while management highlights ongoing efforts in acquisition and operational efficiency. Investors should watch for execution on cost controls and production recovery in the second half of 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

Production for the period was steady at 36.2 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day, with oil making up about 40% of total output. This level matched the fourth quarter and came in at the high end of prior guidance, even after adverse weather impacts.

The revenue figure rose 16% compared to the prior year, supported primarily by higher production volumes. Notably, average realized prices for oil climbed to $69.52 per barrel, while a leaner cost structure improved margins and cash generation.

The company’s cost efficiency continued to improve, with lease operating expenses dropping to $66.1 million and per-unit costs falling 22% from the prior year period. Despite this, general and administrative expenses increased due to higher non-cash share-based compensation, a trend the company attributed to its share price performance.
