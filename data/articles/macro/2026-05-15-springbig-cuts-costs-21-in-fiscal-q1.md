Key Takeaways
SpringBig saw modest top-line decline but achieved clear improvement through major cost reductions.
Gross profit fell due to one-time messaging costs, while messages distributed on the platform rose 11% year over year.
Management stressed a focus on operational discipline.

SpringBig Holdings (OTC:SBIG) reduced operating expenses through decisive cost controls even as revenue slipped slightly. The marketing automation provider for cannabis retailers and brands saw operating expenses drop steeply and key engagement metrics rise for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (earnings released May 14, 2026). Overall, the period reflected operational discipline but also highlighted the importance of reigniting revenue growth.

Notable Developments
Total operating expenses fell by $1.0 million, or 21%, with general and administrative costs down 24% and sales and marketing down 37%.
Gross profit decreased $0.7 million due to a one-time vendor cost, but management stated this “established a more sustainable cost structure on a go-forward basis.”
Messages distributed through the platform grew 11% year over year, underscoring continued user engagement despite flat revenue.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management said it is prioritizing ongoing operational optimization and noted that lower vendor costs should help future profitability. Although no explicit financial guidance for fiscal 2026 was issued, leadership remains focused on expense control and expanding into non-cannabis industries. Investors should monitor progress in revenue growth and any further diversification into new regulated markets.

Quarterly highlights

SpringBig’s revenue was nearly flat compared to the prior year, driven by stable subscriptions that made up 88% of total revenue. The company highlighted over 200% quarter-over-quarter growth in non-cannabis revenues, signaling early progress in diversification efforts.

The sharp drop in operating expenses was the key profitability driver for the period. General and administrative expenses lowered by $0.6 million year over year while selling, servicing, and marketing costs cut by more than a third.

Total messages distributed—a major platform usage metric—climbed 11% to 147 million. This engagement growth indicates customers remain active on the platform, even as net revenue retention stayed flat at 86%.

What SpringBig does

SpringBig provides cloud-based marketing, loyalty, and customer relationship management software focused on regulated industries such as cannabis and gaming.
