Notable Developments

The store count at period end was 463, which is 26.8% fewer than a year earlier.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guided for continuing revenue declines in the near term, with sales in the upcoming quarter expected between $232 million and $240 million. For the full fiscal year, it projects sales of $940 million to $960 million and aims to grow adjusted EBITDA despite the headwinds. Investors should watch for tangible improvements in comparable sales and margin progress as Torrid’s marketing and loyalty initiatives ramp up.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the quarter fell 7.6% year over year, the result of completed store closures and a still-negative comparable sales figure. Despite achieving sales and adjusted EBITDA at the upper end of its internal guidance, the gains were not enough to reverse core declines.

Profitability was under pressure, as gross margin slipped to 35.3%. Margin erosion reflected both lower sales volume and active promotions; adjusted EBITDA, a measure of core operating profit, dropped sharply to $17.6 million from the year-ago period.

The company closed 20 stores during the period, finalizing its optimization project. While this reduction is expected to improve future productivity and profitability, it has also contributed to the year-over-year revenue contraction and has yet to deliver a clear rebound in per-store performance.
