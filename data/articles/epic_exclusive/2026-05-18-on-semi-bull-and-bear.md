ON Semi Bull and Bear

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 3:10 PM

Comments

ON Semiconductor Corporation (NASDAQ:ON) is a leading provider of intelligent power and sensing solutions, playing a critical role in global megatrends such as AI data centers, electric vehicles, and energy infrastructure. Its growth strategy centers on portfolio optimization—exiting lower-margin businesses while doubling down on advanced power semiconductors, analog mixed-signal, and silicon carbide (SiC) and gallium nitride (GaN) technologies. By focusing on high-growth verticals and expanding content per device, the company aims to deliver sustainable margin expansion and long-term shareholder returns.

Potential Bull Cases
1. Accelerating AI Data Center Demand Drives Outsized Growth and Margin Expansion

AI data center momentum is becoming a transformative revenue and margin engine for the company. In Q1 2026, AI data center revenue more than doubled year over year and grew over 30% sequentially, handily outperforming management’s expectations. This segment is expected to double again for the full year compared to 2025, reflecting strategic wins across multiple hyperscaler and XPU platforms. Advanced power conversion (including vertical GaN) and a robust $1.5 billion design funnel provide durable visibility. The shift toward higher-value AI infrastructure expands gross margin, underpinned by system solutions and deeper platform integration, positioning ON Semiconductor as a crucial partner in the ongoing global buildout of artificial intelligence compute infrastructure.

2. Automotive Electrification and Content Gains Drive Durable Revenue Streams

The company is translating EV platform wins and technology leadership into rising automotive power content and long-term growth. For Q1 2026, automotive revenue reached $797 million, up almost 5% year over year—the first positive comparison after nearly two years of declines. ON Semiconductor captured 55% silicon carbide (SiC) content share in new electric vehicle models showcased at the 2026 Beijing Auto Show, thanks to expanded partnerships with Chinese automakers like Geely and NIO on next-generation 900-volt platforms. With global adoption of higher-voltage and software-defined architectures, the company secures a growing share of value per vehicle, limiting exposure to auto sales cycles and creating a strong foundation for recurring growth as electrification accelerates worldwide.

3. Portfolio Rationalization and Cost Actions Catalyze Structural Margin Improvement

Sharp focus on portfolio quality and manufacturing discipline positions the company for sustainable profitability gains. By executing $300 million in non-core business exits across 2026, ON Semiconductor improves both revenue and margin quality, with proceeds and resources funneled into high-growth, high-margin areas such as AI, SiC/GaN, Treo, and advanced analog. Q1 2026 gross margin expanded to 38.5%—the third consecutive quarter of improvement—despite ongoing underutilization headwinds. Structural cost initiatives (FabRight), coupled with a richer product mix, are expected to power sequential margin expansion throughout 2026, with management targeting long-term gross margin potential above 50%. These strategic moves increase operating leverage and reduce volatility as the business scales.

4. Robust Free Cash Flow and Disciplined Capital Allocation Underpin Shareholder Returns

Strong cash generation and prudent deployment enable meaningful capital return and future growth investment. In Q1 2026, ON Semiconductor generated $217 million in free cash flow (non-GAAP) and returned $346 million—about 160% of that free cash flow—to shareholders through opportunistic share repurchases. With $3.9 billion in liquidity, minimal immediate capital intensity needs, and a new $1.3 billion zero-coupon convertible note due 2031, the company is well-positioned to fund R&D, make targeted acquisitions, or flex up buybacks during periods of share price dislocation. Management’s commitment to returning 100% of free cash flow over the cycle aligns incentives with long-term investors and supports upside potential as growth vectors accelerate.

5. Industrial and Energy Infrastructure Outperformance Expands End Market Optionality

The company’s differentiated SiC and hybrid modules are driving notable share gains in energy storage systems and industrial markets. Despite seasonal weakness, industrial segment revenue in Q1 2026 exceeded expectations, with energy storage/microgrid deployments projected to grow over 40% year over year and market share approaching 60%. Collaborations, such as with Sungrow Electric, and the AI-driven demand halo, are boosting relevance in global power grids and renewable adoption. This broad-based industrial strength, supported by cross-pollinated innovation from the AI segment, diversifies the company’s revenue base and mitigates reliance on any single cyclical driver, resulting in enhanced resilience and growth prospects.

Potential Bear Cases
1. Cyclical and Macro Risks Could Disrupt Demand Recovery Across Key Segments

ON Semiconductor remains exposed to cyclical slowdowns, regional volatility, and broader macroeconomic risks that could derail growth momentum. Lead times stretched to 26 weeks and inventory days increased. Regional uncertainties—such as China policy risk, shifting trade dynamics, or uneven industrial recovery—could negatively impact order stability or result in temporary revenue gaps, especially with more concentrated exposure to specific growth markets.

2. Execution Risks Remain as Ramping New Technologies and Automotive Programs Introduce Complexity

The company’s future hinges on flawless execution as major AI, automotive, and power programs transition from design to high-volume production. ON Semiconductor’s growth strategy depends on the successful ramp of new platforms such as Treo, silicon carbide/IGBT inverters, and vertical GaN—all requiring precise manufacturing scale-up and customer adoption. Any missteps—such as yield issues, qualification delays, or changes in customer sourcing preferences—could create temporary cracks in both revenue growth and margin leverage. The company has acknowledged direct sourcing shifts by auto OEMs, intensifying competitive pressure, and the need for sustained internal and customer alignment to avoid disruptive setbacks as markets evolve.

3. Intensifying Competition in Core Power and Sensing Markets May Erode Share and Pricing Power

Ongoing margin and growth expansion could be threatened by rising competition in both silicon carbide power and image sensor markets. Management and analysts flagged growing competitive pressures, notably from Sony in complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor (CMOS) image sensors and from multiple rivals in the global SiC power segment. As more players target the lucrative AI data center and electric vehicle verticals, ON Semiconductor risks margin compression if price competition escalates, or if new competitors obtain key design wins with major hyperscalers or auto OEMs. Maintaining its pricing power and differentiated technology edge remains essential—any sustained loss of share or weakened barriers could reduce long-term profitability and slow upside from core strategic bets.
