AvePoint (NASDAQ:AVPT) delivered a business win this quarter as demand for its cloud-based data protection and governance solutions continued to climb. The leading provider of software for managing enterprise data across cloud platforms reported results on May 7, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period showcased broad-based growth with progress in recurring revenue and product innovation, though gross margins eased as service mix shifted.

Notable Developments
SaaS revenue reached $93.4 million, up 35% from the prior year.
Cash flow from operations improved sharply, rising to $24.3 million from $0.5 million last year.
The company renewed its share repurchase plan for up to $150 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management is increasing annual recurring revenue targets to a range of $523.4–$529.4 million, reflecting continued cloud adoption despite foreign currency headwinds. Full-year fiscal 2026 revenue guidance is now set at $509.4–$515.4 million, unchanged at the midpoint from last quarter’s outlook. Investors should watch gross margin performance in the coming quarters as service revenue expands and cost pressures persist.

Quarterly highlights

SaaS, or software-as-a-service, results stood out with $93.4 million in sales, advancing 35% versus the year-ago period and representing 80% of AvePoint’s mix. This sustained migration from older, less predictable term licenses further tilted the business towards recurring revenue.

Operating income on a Non-GAAP basis rose by over 50% to $20.5 million, helped by steady customer retention and expanding partner distribution. Cash from operations also jumped, reflecting both improved profitability and disciplined working capital management.

Gross margin narrowed by 1.6 percentage points due to a higher share of services revenue, which generally carries lower profitability, and ongoing impacts from foreign currency movements. Management flagged these as areas needing close attention as investments in marketing and new product rollout continue through what is described as an “investment year.”

What AvePoint does

AvePoint provides software that helps organizations manage, protect, and govern their cloud-based data across platforms like Microsoft 365 and Google Cloud.
