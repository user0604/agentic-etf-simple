Cvd Equipment (NASDAQ:CVV) faced another tough period as low order activity in its core chemical vapor deposition business drove deeper operating losses. The company, which designs and manufactures advanced deposition equipment for aerospace, industrial, and semiconductor customers, now operates as a single segment after selling its SDC division. The first quarter report, released May 14, 2026, covers results for the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed significant revenue pressure, some progress on transformation, and improved liquidity.

Quarterly highlights

The company completed the SDC division sale on April 1, 2026, adding $14.8 million in net cash and raising its cash position to $23 million. This transaction removed a non-core asset and gives the company more flexibility to pursue options for its main business.

Despite the cash proceeds, the quarter’s results were weak as revenue from continuing operations dropped by about 71% and gross margin fell sharply, reflecting low utilization and poor absorption of fixed costs. Orders increased, but only for spare parts, not for core chemical vapor deposition equipment systems.

Cost-saving actions included outsourcing fabrication and reducing the workforce, which are expected to save $1.8 million annually in fiscal 2026. However, operating losses widened to $1.8 million for the period as low revenue outweighed expense reductions.
