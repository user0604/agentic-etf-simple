Quarterly highlights

PALSONIFY, a once-daily oral treatment for acromegaly, drove the quarter’s revenue as prescriber adoption expanded, with 263 unique healthcare providers initiating therapy since launch. Roughly 70% of patients now receive insurance reimbursement for the drug, marking an improvement from 50% in the launch quarter and reflecting broadening payer coverage.

Pipeline investment again climbed, with research and development costs up to $100.1 million and non-GAAP operating expenses at $120.1 million. The company maintained progress in its clinical studies, including moving its atumelnant candidate toward a Phase 2/3 trial for Cushing’s syndrome and continuing international regulatory submissions.

The balance sheet remains strong after a $380 million equity offering in January 2026, bringing liquidity to $1.3 billion at quarter end. This stockpile is expected to support efforts well into the next decade despite rising losses as commercialization and research activities accelerate.
