Notable Developments
Net income attributable to shareholders increased 10.7% due to higher fee income and stronger loan book performance.
Net interest income fell 8.0%, as lower results from securities and foreign operations weighed more than growth in lending volumes.
The quarterly dividend and interest on capital payment increased 43.0% to R$0.33 per share (after tax), reflecting ongoing capital returns to shareholders.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management is emphasizing disciplined, risk-adjusted growth and continued investment in digital capabilities. They stress the need for further cost control and vigilance over squeezing margins and capital ratios. Investors should watch trends in net interest margin and regulatory capital metrics in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw recurring return on equity reach 22.9%, showing that Itaú Unibanco extracted more value from each unit of shareholder capital. This improvement came even as headline operating revenue declined from the prior year as net interest income decreased.

Loan portfolio expansion continued across individuals and companies, with overall loan book growth of about 7%. Insurance contracts and private pension income grew 16.5% year over year as the company continued to diversify revenue sources beyond core lending.

Expense growth was contained at 3.0%, mainly due to wage adjustments agreed in a 2025 collective bargaining agreement. The efficiency ratio, which shows costs as a percentage of operating revenues, improved slightly, helping support overall profitability even in the face of revenue headwinds.
