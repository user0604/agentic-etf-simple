Quarterly Highlights

LOQTORZI, an immune checkpoint inhibitor for nasopharyngeal carcinoma, extended its commercial reach by adding new patient starts and deepening participation with existing prescribing accounts. Despite strong year-over-year sales growth, the product saw a sequential decline from the previous quarter, which management explained as linked to normal seasonality and one-time weather events.

Research and development costs continued to fall, reflecting a focus on the clinical advancement of casdozokitug and tagmokitug, two pipeline antibodies now in mid-stage studies for a range of solid-organ cancers. Enrollment was completed for a key Phase 2 casdozokitug trial, and multiple additional studies are slated for data readouts later in the year.

The capital position improved as the company completed an equity raise, and operating losses narrowed from a year ago. However, the company continues to spend more than it earns, highlighting the ongoing need for both commercial expansion and pipeline success to reach self-sufficiency.
