STAAR Surgical (NASDAQ:STAA) rebounded sharply from last year’s challenges as surging China sales fueled a return to profitability. The medical device maker, which focuses on implantable lenses used in vision correction, reported its results for the first quarter (three months ended April 3, 2026) on May 13, 2026. Overall, the period showed decisive progress in sales, margins, and operating discipline after a tough prior year.

Notable developments
Net sales set a new quarterly record at $93.5 million, with revenue growth of 119.6% year over year, powered by China’s recovery after the prior inventory reset.
Gross margin improved to 73.6%, up 7.8 percentage points, reflecting cost reductions, a better product mix, and lower supply-chain costs.
Despite a positive net income of $5.2 million, operating cash flow was negative at $(21.7) million, as accounts receivable grew sharply following the China rebound.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management voiced optimism about maintaining disciplined spending and capturing further growth, especially from premium ICL lens products and China demand. However, it did not provide detailed financial guidance for upcoming quarters. Investors should watch China sales sustainability and working capital trends, as these will have a major impact on results during the coming year.

Quarterly highlights

The surge in China contributed to more than half of company-wide revenue, with net sales from China rebounding from negative levels a year ago to $47.4 million this quarter. Markets outside China delivered just 6% net sales growth, led by double-digit gains in the United States (22% growth to $6.7 million), but flat performance in EMEA (Europe, Middle East, Africa), and slowing growth in some Asia-Pacific markets.

This was the first full quarter of commercialization for the EVO+ ICL lens in China, which carries a premium price and is expected to help profit margins as adoption increases. Lens unit shipment volume jumped 134% year over year, demonstrating both market demand and inventory normalization in distribution channels.

Operating expenses decreased 28.7% from the prior year, reflecting strict cost controls and reduced restructuring costs. While the company returned to profitability, net cash used in operating activities was $(21.7) million, driven mostly by rising accounts receivable as customers in China restocked product after last year’s pullback.

What STAAR Surgical does

STAAR Surgical designs, manufactures, and sells implantable lenses used in vision correction surgery for the treatment of myopia and other refractive errors.
