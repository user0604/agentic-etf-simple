Notable developments
R&D expense climbed 58.1% to $4.9 million as trial preparations accelerated, marking a significant increase from the prior-year period.
A $5.7 million non-cash gain on warrant derivatives significantly reduced the reported net loss versus last year.
The company completed a milestone regulatory meeting, aligning with the FDA on a key late-stage trial, and began activating study sites.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management stated the pivotal RESTORE trial for chronic tetraplegia remains on track to begin mid-2026, with key trial sites already in the activation process. The most important upcoming event is the anticipated CONNECT SCI gait analysis results, expected in the second quarter, which will provide new data on the company’s lead therapy. Investors should watch the financial burn rate alongside trial milestones, as additional funding may be required to sustain operations through late-stage development.

Quarterly highlights

R&D investment rose sharply as the company advanced its NVG-291 program, the lead candidate designed to repair nervous system damage. This boost in spending reflects the transition from early-phase research to preparing for pivotal phase 3 studies.

General and administrative costs held steady year over year, reflecting ongoing discipline in non-research expenses. The net loss fell compared to last year, though mostly because of a non-cash accounting gain related to warrant derivatives, and not from lower real-world spending.

Operationally, the company completed a “successful End-of-Phase 2 meeting” with the FDA, which confirmed the registrational design for the RESTORE study. With trial site activation now underway, the focus shifts to upcoming clinical data that will shape the next phase of value creation.

What NervGen Pharma does

NervGen Pharma develops innovative therapies aimed at repairing damage to the nervous system, including treatments for spinal cord injury and related conditions.
