Earnings Preview: MongoDB

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

MongoDB (NASDAQ:MDB), a company that provides cloud and on-premises database software for developers and businesses, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, MongoDB delivered total revenue of $695 million, representing a 27% increase year over year. The company reported non-GAAP earnings per share of $1.65 and a non-GAAP operating margin of 23%. Atlas, MongoDB’s cloud-based database service, grew revenue by 29% year over year and surpassed the $2 billion annual run rate for the first time. Non-Atlas revenue, which includes on-premises and hybrid offerings, grew 20% year over year. These results beat analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings. The company added 2,700 customers in the quarter, ending with over 65,200 customers globally. Management highlighted strong demand from both large enterprises and AI-focused companies, as well as notable multi-year deals with major technology and financial services firms. The company also continued to generate strong free cash flow and ended the quarter with $2.4 billion in cash and investments.

Positives
Revenue and earnings beat expectations: Both top-line and bottom-line results exceeded consensus estimates, reflecting strong execution and demand across products.
Atlas cloud platform momentum: Atlas revenue grew 29% year over year and now represents 72% of total revenue, showing continued customer adoption of MongoDB’s cloud offering.
Record large enterprise deals: The company closed its largest total contract value deal ever, illustrating growing traction among large, regulated customers who value hybrid and on-premises solutions.
Healthy customer growth and expansion: Customer count grew to over 65,200, with a 60% increase in annual customer additions, and net annualized recurring revenue (ARR) expansion rate rose to 121%.
Negatives
Gross margin pressure from cloud mix: Overall gross margin declined to 72% (from 73% a year ago), mainly because Atlas, which carries higher infrastructure costs, is a larger part of the revenue mix.
Leadership transitions underway: The company announced changes in its go-to-market leadership, including the departure of its President of Field Operations and Chief Revenue Officer, which could introduce some near-term uncertainty.
Macroeconomic headwinds remain: Management noted ongoing macroeconomic pressures, particularly in the growth rate of existing Atlas applications, which could weigh on near-term expansion.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Atlas revenue growth sustainability: Watch for Atlas revenue growth near the guided 26% year-over-year pace and whether customer adoption remains steady as Atlas becomes a larger part of the business.
Non-Atlas business trends and large deal timing: Monitor growth in on-premises and hybrid solutions, especially after a strong Q4, and any updates on multi-year deal closures or pipeline visibility.
Progress on AI and vector search adoption: Look for updates on customer uptake of AI features, including vector search and Voyage AI embedding models, and whether AI-native companies are contributing more meaningfully to revenue.
Operating margin and cash flow execution: Evaluate if MongoDB maintains its targeted operating margin expansion and healthy cash conversion, especially as it invests in growth and absorbs leadership changes.
Status of go-to-market leadership transition: Pay attention to commentary on the Chief Revenue Officer search and integration of the new Chief Customer Officer, as these changes could impact sales execution and customer relationships in the near term.
