Notable developments

Revenue for the period was $1.31 billion, which was 4.0% above management’s own forecast. Non-GAAP operating margin more than doubled to 30.6%, reflecting cost controls and higher factory utilization. Inventory dropped by $22.3 million, helping distributor inventory reach 26 days, at the low end of the company’s typical range.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management guides for continued growth in the upcoming quarter, with revenue expected between $1.44 billion and $1.47 billion and profitability set to improve. The most important area for investors to watch is the pace of manufacturing utilization and progress toward long-term margin goals, as these factors impact future earnings potential and capital allocation flexibility.

Quarterly highlights

Product demand strengthened, especially for high-speed connectivity products used in data center and artificial intelligence applications. The company launched new Gen6 PCIe retimer solutions, and management noted “expanding design activity” in both AI and automotive markets, which it sees as key growth drivers.

Operational execution showed marked improvement, with free cash flow rising over 25% and $320.9 million in inventory reduction since late 2024. Factory utilization increased, though management says full normalization has yet to be achieved, which is necessary to reach ambitious long-term profit targets.

The board declared another common dividend of $0.455 per share for June, continuing a track record of returning cash to shareholders. Most improvements in cash flow have been allocated to paying down debt and supporting ongoing dividend payments, as the company aims to strengthen its balance sheet after a prior cycle of high leverage.

What Microchip Technology does

Microchip Technology designs, makes, and sells microcontrollers, analog chips, and related semiconductors used in a broad range of electronics products worldwide.
