Tomi Environmental Solutions (NASDAQ:TOMZ)’s business transformation delivered standout growth in equipment and consumable sales, even as profitability remained under pressure. Notable Developments
Product revenue rose 31% while service revenue dropped 41%, showing a shift toward equipment and consumables over higher-margin services.
Gross margin narrowed by 10 percentage points to 50%, mainly due to price discounts and more lower-margin equipment sales.
The quarter ended with a $1.7 million order backlog and a $4.3 million integration project pipeline across 13 customers, highlighting commercial progress.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management plans to execute the proposed merger with Carbonium Core, target recurring revenue through increased BIT Solution sales, and expand internationally. It did not specify when profitability might be reached. The main point for investors to monitor will be the success of the merger process and the company’s ability to turn its sales backlog into recognized revenue, especially as liquidity remains tight.

Quarterly Highlights

Equipment and Custom Engineered System (CES) sales led revenue growth, helping increase product revenue by $311,000 but weighing on margins. Applicator (consumable) sales grew more than 139% year over year, already surpassing last year's annual total, and BIT Solution sales are tracking higher than in fiscal 2025, both supporting a push for more recurring income.

Operating expenses fell 15% due to reductions in general, selling, and professional costs. While gross profit declined, these cost controls helped reduce the operating loss to $626,000. Adjusting for non-recurring income in the prior period, the net loss improved modestly year over year.

Positive operating cash flow of $296,000 was a notable improvement from last year’s cash outflow. However, cash remained low at period end, and total equity stood at just $550, underscoring ongoing financial pressures as the company works to secure additional funding or benefits from the planned merger.

What Tomi Environmental Solutions Does
