Your Take: Strategies for Healthcare Investing

The Premium Team

May 11, 2026, 3:49 PM

Comments

In Friday's Breakfast News, we asked members, "Do you invest in healthcare and biotech stocks? If so, what gives you conviction – the science, the management team, the commercial opportunity, or something else?" Here's what you had to say.

S

skunkfart

Yes, I invest in large-cap biotech value stocks like Amgen (NASDAQ: AMGN), and it's an income stock paying $2.52 dividend per share. Big biotechs are good having multi-blockbuster drugs, and they do M&A's with smaller companies to acquire successful new drugs in their pipeline and ones actively in the FDA process to stay competitive vs companies making biosimilars to ones they have. Also, I've invested in the iShares Biotechnology ETF (NASDAQ: IBB), and will do so again, because it's wise thing to do and have during inflationary times.

In addition, I stayed invested in UnitedHealth Group (NYSE: UNH), despite TMF saying to sell along with having Welltower (NYSE: WELL). Again, it's more keeping a defensive stance by having managed healthcare and REIT healthcare sector stocks to balance my portfolio against whipsawing market volatility. This yr, 2026, has shaken many people out of stocks I'm sure they regret not keeping, because 5-10% increases and decreases daily are normal and no longer uncommon with geopolitics, increasing oil prices, rising US dollar and fluctuating 10 yr bond yields. Biotechs and Healthcare defensive stocks help achieve portfolio balance, IMHO.

S

StanleyS

I have (and have had) investments in biotech. Unlike @Lainka I have no specialized knowledge or specialized interest that would enable me to chery pick winners from what seems like a large and competitive pool of small biotech companies. If I could buy a dozen or more I might do that, but I don't have the space in my portfolio for it. Instead, I look for larger pharma companies with multiple products and for companies that are leaders or innovators in significant or promising technologies.

Like @zhangwhart I made money with Intuitive Surgical (NASDAQ: ISRG), which years ago I sold to make other investments. (I should probably have held and may buy again.) I've also had success with Veeva Systems (NYSE: VEEV), if you want to count health information services in this group.

Otherwise, I made a little money on Pfizer (NYSE: PFE) one time and lost a little another time. I'm up a bit and holding on GSK (NYSE: GSK). In medical devices I'm up with Koninklijke Philips (NYSE: PHG) and flat on Medtronic (NYSE: MDT) if you throw in the dividend. With vaccines I'm down with BioNTech (NASDAQ: BNTX) but up nicely with Moderna (NASDAQ: MRNA).

I view the sector as too important to ignore. I like to be diversified within it but tend to avoid the more speculative "opportunities" (even when you can make a good case for some of them). I will trade some possibility of spectacular gains for the greater likelihood of solid gains, and sometimes I get those.
