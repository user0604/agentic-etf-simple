2. The Starbucks Layoff Train Chugs On
★ SBUX is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team HG and Team RB)

Starbucks (NASDAQ: SBUX) is at it again -- another 300 U.S. corporate jobs are gone, and regional offices in Chicago, Atlanta, Dallas, and Burbank are getting shuttered. CEO Brian Niccol is hunting $2 billion in cost cuts by fiscal 2028, and apparently no org chart is safe. Tech, marketing, finance, and R&D are all taking hits; baristas, however, can breathe easy. Nashville or bust: While one hand layeth off, the other is building a shiny new $100 million Nashville campus for 2,000 workers, so this looks less like a shrink than a reshuffle.
Golden handcuffs for the cutters: Executives who hit the savings targets pocket stock bonuses worth $6 million. That's one way to make sure the knife stays sharp.
Analyst chatter: "Maybe Starbucks has found its bottom when it comes to performance in the United States," Team Rule Breakers analyst Nick Sciple recently said on The Morning Show. "But I think there is still more room to execute across the store base and continue to improve operations. If they can maintain this momentum and get back to multi-year growth, then I think it could be an interesting story here the next several years."
