Key Takeaways
Eagle Materials posted record revenue, but profit margins declined due to weaker core product pricing.
Revenue rose 1.9%, while diluted earnings per share slipped 4.5% year over year.
Management gave no financial guidance for the coming quarter, citing ongoing uncertainty in end markets.

Eagle Materials (NYSE:EXP) posted record revenue for the latest quarter as strong performance in Cement and Aggregates helped offset continued weakness in its key Wallboard business. The construction materials producer operates cement, concrete, aggregates, gypsum wallboard, and paperboard plants across high-growth U.S. markets. The company released results for its fourth fiscal quarter on May 19, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter demonstrated solid execution in Heavy Materials, but ongoing margin pressure and lower Light Materials performance limited earnings gains.

Notable developments
Revenue reached an all-time high for a fourth quarter, with growth in Heavy Materials led by Cement and Aggregates segment volume increases.
Operating earnings in Light Materials fell 14%, reflecting lower prices and sales volume in gypsum wallboard due to ongoing residential construction softness.
General and administrative expenses rose 21% for the fiscal year, impacted by technology upgrades and higher compensation costs.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not give financial projections for the next quarter, but reiterated its focus on investing in plant modernizations and returning capital to shareholders. The company's public comments indicated near-term uncertainty for demand, especially in residential markets, while emphasizing ongoing efforts toward cost control and operational efficiency. Investors should watch the progress of plant upgrades and any changes in cement and wallboard pricing as key indicators for future results.

Quarterly highlights

The Heavy Materials segment, which includes cement, concrete, and aggregates, posted sharply higher operating earnings for the quarter, jumping 83% from the same period last year. This was driven by a 15% gain in cement revenue and improving cost management as maintenance expenses fell.

Light Materials, consisting of gypsum wallboard and recycled paperboard, saw continued headwinds. Segment operating earnings dropped nearly 14% due to lower sales volumes and pricing in wallboard. Residential construction demand remained sluggish, resulting in a sequential wallboard price decline and volume contraction.

The company stepped up capital investments during the period, advancing major modernization projects at its Wyoming cement facility and Oklahoma wallboard plant. At quarter end, Eagle Materials held $298 million in cash and repurchased 338,000 shares for $71.5 million.

What Eagle Materials does

Eagle Materials manufactures and sells basic construction materials, including cement, concrete, aggregates, gypsum wallboard, and paperboard, with operations focused in key U.S. growth regions.
