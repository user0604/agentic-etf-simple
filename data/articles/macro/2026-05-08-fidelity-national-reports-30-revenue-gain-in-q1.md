Notable Developments
Revenue rose 30% year over year, driven by the integration of Total Issuing Solutions into Banking Solutions.
Profitability improved as adjusted EBITDA margin expanded by 1.8 percentage points, reflecting cost controls and a higher share of recurring revenue.
Free cash flow more than doubled, up 111% to $474 million, due to operational efficiency and better working capital management.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 outlook, targeting 30–31% adjusted revenue growth and an 8–10% increase in adjusted EPS. These goals are supported by ongoing integration of acquisitions and a focus on recurring revenue. Investors should watch whether organic growth can keep pace after a year of acquisition-driven expansion.

Quarterly Highlights

The Banking Solutions segment led performance, with revenue up 45% and pro forma revenue (which adjusts for acquisitions) rising 7.7%. The segment’s recurring revenue, which comes from long-term contracts or subscriptions for banking technology, reached $2.0 billion and supported margin gains.

Capital Market Solutions posted 5% GAAP revenue growth, with recurring revenue up 3.6% year over year. Adjusted EBITDA margin in this segment increased to 51.6%, reflecting cost management. This business provides solutions for trading, risk management, and financial market infrastructure.

Integration of Total Issuing Solutions played a key role in improving both scale and the quality of the company’s revenue mix. Management also noted a $2.2 billion gain from the recent Worldpay divestiture, which sharpened the focus on recurring, high-value businesses. Dividend payments reached $232 million, up from $220 million in the prior year.

What Fidelity National Information Services Does

Fidelity National Information Services provides financial technology software and services to banks and capital market firms, including transaction processing, payment solutions, and risk management platforms.
