Universal (NYSE:UVV) saw a tough quarter as inventory write-downs and an impairment charge weighed on results. The leading global supplier of leaf tobacco and specialty ingredients reported its fiscal fourth quarter for the period ended March 31, 2026, on May 28, 2026. The period featured higher revenue but much lower profitability due to one-time and ongoing operating challenges.

Notable developments
The company recorded a $41.1 million impairment charge related to its Shank’s ingredients operation and tobacco inventory write-downs of $52.0 million for the year.
Tobacco operations revenue rose 3% in the quarter, but operating income in the segment dropped 42% due to inventory write-downs and lower demand for some products.
Ingredients operations revenue fell 7% during the period, and segment operating income declined 58% amid higher fixed costs and weaker market demand.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects uncommitted tobacco inventory levels to return to target ranges during fiscal 2027 and has started measures to improve profitability at Shank’s ingredients. The company is focusing on execution, cost control, and segment performance recovery. Investors should watch for updates on inventory normalization and any improvement in margins, especially in the Ingredients segment.

Quarterly highlights

Tobacco operations delivered higher revenue thanks to shipment timing and steady demand for core products, but profit margins shrank because of significant inventory write-downs, especially for dark air-cured tobacco.

The Ingredients segment, which includes botanical extracts and flavorings from Shank’s, saw a reduction in revenue and a sharp drop in profitability after a large impairment charge and continued fixed-cost pressures.

Operating income and non-GAAP EPS both fell, driven by one-off charges and compressed gross profit margins. The company paid out $81.3 million in dividends during the year, maintaining its payout discipline.

What Universal does

Universal is a global supplier of leaf tobacco to consumer product companies and produces specialty ingredients for food and beverage manufacturers.
