Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted the critical need to sustain cash flow-positive operations and comply with debt terms over coming quarters. A going concern warning remains in effect because of ongoing exposure to metal prices and the need for continued covenant waivers. Investors should focus on upcoming tests of the company’s ability to generate positive operating cash flow and manage short-term liabilities.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue more than doubled from the previous year, with both the Cosalá Operations and the Galena Complex driving the performance as silver, copper, and antimony sales increased following the EC120 project ramp-up and completion of the Crescent Mine acquisition.

The company's gross margin improved sharply as higher sales volumes and greater diversification into copper and antimony offset rising costs in general and administrative spending and exploration outlays.

Balance sheet liquidity stayed robust, with $122.4 million in cash and equivalents, but short-term debt and contract liabilities increased, and management warned of risks should commodity prices turn lower or debt covenant waivers expire.
