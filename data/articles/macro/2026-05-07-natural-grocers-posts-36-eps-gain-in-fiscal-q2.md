Natural Grocers Posts 3.6% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:23 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Modest quarterly win as earnings improved despite a slow sales backdrop.
Earnings per share increased 3.6% year over year.
Management tightened comparable store sales guidance while lifting the low end of EPS outlook.

Natural Grocers By Vitamin Cottage (NYSE:NGVC) delivered steady earnings growth in its latest quarter, even as sales growth remained subdued. The nationwide natural and organic grocery chain continued to focus on margin management and cost control to navigate cautious consumer spending. The results, released May 7, 2026, for the fiscal second quarter, reflect a careful balance of restrained revenue and resilient profitability. Overall, the quarter demonstrated better control of expenses, but also softer traffic and a more conservative outlook for growth through the rest of fiscal 2026.

Notable developments
Sales growth was modest, with revenue up 0.5% and comparable store sales increasing 0.5% for the quarter.
Profitability improved, as diluted EPS rose 3.6% and operating margin moved from 5.2% to 5.4%.
Management narrowed full-year comparable store sales guidance by lowering the top end, but raised the minimum for annual EPS from $2.00 to $2.07 per share.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management now expects daily average comparable store sales growth of 1.5% to 2.5% for fiscal 2026, guiding lower than prior hopes of up to 4.0%. Annual earnings per share guidance was raised at the low end, to a range of $2.07 to $2.15 per share. The most important metric for investors to monitor next is transaction volume, as the number of daily customer visits fell year over year for the quarter.

Quarterly highlights

The period saw only slight sales growth, with revenue advancing 0.5% and comparable store sales posting a similar increase. Traffic was sluggish, as daily average customer transactions declined by 1.1%, though this was helped by a 1.6% rise in average purchase size per visit.

Profit margins benefited from tight expense controls. Store expenses fell 1.6% to $71.6 million, and operating margin improved over the prior year, with adjusted EBITDA advancing 4%. Administrative costs increased, in large part from technology spending, but cost control at the store level offset those pressures.

Store expansion remains on track with one net new location opened in the quarter, bringing the total to 169 stores. Management confirmed its plan to open 6 to 8 new stores and relocate or remodel 2 to 3 others by fiscal year end. The dividend was maintained at $0.15 per share, reflecting confidence in cash generation.

What Natural Grocers By Vitamin Cottage does

Natural Grocers By Vitamin Cottage operates a chain of grocery stores that sell natural and organic foods, dietary supplements, household products, and nutrition education services across the United States.
