Key Takeaways
Opendoor Technologies posted lower sales volume and continued losses as it transitions its business model.
Revenue fell 37.6% year over year.
Management forecasts a return to quarterly revenue growth and expects adjusted EBITDA profitability in coming quarters.

Opendoor Technologies (NASDAQ:OPEN) reported fewer home sales and ongoing losses as it focused on operational improvements and business transformation. The leading digital real estate platform provided its first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed tighter cost control and better unit margins but highlighted the company’s ongoing challenge to return to growth and profitability.

Notable Developments
Revenue dropped to $720 million, down from $1,153 million a year earlier, mainly from fewer homes sold as the company cleared out older inventory.
Inventory health improved, with aged homes (those on the market over 120 days) falling to 10% from 33% in the prior quarter, reflecting faster turnover.
The company increased home acquisitions by over 45% from the prior quarter, ending with more than 5,000 acquisition contracts—the highest since 2022.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue to grow around 25% in the next quarter and aims for an adjusted EBITDA result close to breakeven. It also reiterated its target to achieve adjusted net income profitability by the end of 2026 on a forward 12-month basis. Investors should closely track whether faster home acquisitions lead to growth in sold homes and improved margins as new inventory cycles through the business.

Quarterly Highlights

Top-line revenue fell sharply year over year as Opendoor sold fewer homes, driven by a deliberate focus on clearing out older inventory and pivoting to newer, higher-quality property cohorts. While the drop in homes sold reduced immediate revenue, it was paired with management’s focus on improving the mix and velocity of sales.

Margin performance stabilized, with the contribution margin holding at 4.4%, close to the prior year, and well above recent quarters. The percentage of homes on the market for more than 120 days—the industry’s measure of “aged” inventory—improved substantially, down to 10% from 33% in the previous quarter and from 27% a year earlier, highlighting success in inventory turnover driven by AI-powered risk management and pricing.

The company continued to grow its home acquisition activity, signing more contracts than any period since 2022. Fixed operating expenses fell by $6 million compared to the prior year, showing progress in controlling costs as volumes begin to scale up again.

What Opendoor Technologies Does

Opendoor Technologies operates a digital platform for buying and selling residential real estate, aiming to simplify transactions through technology and efficient processes.
