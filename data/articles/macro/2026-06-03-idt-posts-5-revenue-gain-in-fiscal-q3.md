Key Takeaways
IDT delivered another strong quarter as its high-margin digital and communications businesses drove growth.

IDT (NYSE:IDT) posted another solid quarter as its digital and communications platforms continued to drive gains in key growth areas. The leading provider of fintech, point-of-sale, and communications solutions for retailers and global consumers reported results for its third fiscal quarter, covering the period ended April 30, 2026 (released June 3, 2026). Overall, the quarter maintained positive momentum in high-margin businesses, but showed pressure on cash flow and some rising costs that warrant attention.

What IDT does

IDT operates a portfolio of fintech, communications, and retail technology businesses, including digital money transfer, point-of-sale, and cloud software for enterprises and small businesses.

Quarterly highlights

National Retail Solutions, the company’s point-of-sale technology for small retailers, delivered a 22% revenue increase and achieved a Rule of 40 score of 50. This metric, which combines recurring revenue growth and operating margin, is well above industry benchmarks for software businesses. However, gross margin in this segment decreased by 1.1 percentage points, and advertising and data revenue fell 3%, even as other services like Merchant Services grew.

BOSS Money, the digital money transfer platform, and the broader Fintech segment reported a 17% revenue increase, led by a 27% gain in digital channel revenue and a surge in digital transactions. Operating profit in Fintech grew 29%, and Adjusted EBITDA climbed 30%, but retail channel revenue continued to decline and average revenue per transaction remained flat.

net2phone, which provides cloud communications services, showed 11% revenue growth and a 30% increase in Adjusted EBITDA, outpacing its seat count growth as customers adopted higher-value services. The company attributed much of this result to new artificial intelligence product features and continued strong performance in Latin America, while preparing for future monetization of new AI offerings in the next fiscal year.

Notable developments
High-margin digital and software businesses led growth, with National Retail Solutions revenue up 22% and Fintech revenue up 17%.
Adjusted EBITDA rose 13%, and income from operations climbed 12%, but cash flow from operations dropped sharply due to working capital timing.
The company raised full-year Adjusted EBITDA guidance, declared a $0.07 per share dividend, and completed a technology-focused acquisition after the quarter ended.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management increased its Adjusted EBITDA guidance for fiscal 2026 to $150–$152 million, reflecting confidence in digital adoption and business performance. Leaders expect continued momentum in high-margin fintech and communications segments, but caution that investors should monitor elevated costs and the stability of cash flow. Margin improvement in National Retail Solutions and sustainable operating cash flow will be important areas to watch next quarter.
