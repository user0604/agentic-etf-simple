Notable developments
Product segment sales jumped 25.0% to $466.1 million, driven mainly by networking, cloud, and collaboration solutions, even as gross margin in that unit fell.
Gross margin dropped to 24.6%, down 1.9 percentage points from a year ago, as a larger share of lower-margin products weighed on overall profitability.
Cash increased by $21.4 million year over year, and the quarterly dividend rose 8% to $0.27 per share.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management pointed to a mid-single-digit growth target for revenue, gross profit, and adjusted EBITDA in fiscal 2027, after two years of rapid expansion. The company described industry fundamentals as solid but noted the pace of gains will likely moderate. Investors should watch closely for stabilization in profit margins and working capital, especially inventory and receivables growth versus sales.

Quarterly highlights

Product segment growth outpaced other business lines, with networking and cloud gross billings climbing. However, sales of security products fell slightly in the quarter, breaking a recent streak of strong gains.

Service lines like professional and managed services together generated $110.0 million in sales, but professional services growth slowed to just 1.6%. Managed services gross profit rose at a faster pace and margins in that unit reached 30.5% for the quarter.

Cash and liquidity remained strong even as inventory and accounts receivable climbed much faster than overall sales. The declared dividend increase and ongoing share buybacks signal balance sheet confidence, but the rise in working capital use is a key risk for upcoming periods.
