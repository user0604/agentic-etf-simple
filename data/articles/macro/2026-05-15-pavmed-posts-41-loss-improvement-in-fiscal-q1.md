Lucid Diagnostics processed 3,177 EsoGuard tests with $1.3 million in revenue, down from 3,664 tests and $1.5 million revenue the prior quarter.
Veris Health advanced toward FDA 510(k) submission for its implantable physiological monitor, with pre-submission activities set to begin soon.
Cash and cash equivalents rose to $6.5 million, aided by Lucid’s $17 million public equity raise, while operating expenses increased year over year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted that near-term performance continues to hinge on gaining Medicare reimbursement and regulatory approvals, particularly for Lucid’s EsoGuard test. It did not provide explicit financial or volume targets for upcoming periods. Investors will want to watch for updates on coverage decisions, especially the anticipated Medicare determination for EsoGuard, as well as regulatory milestones at Veris Health.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s performance at Lucid Diagnostics, PAVmed’s main commercial subsidiary, saw both test volume and revenue decline compared to the prior quarter. This followed a period of optimism when test counts had previously exceeded management’s targeted range. The drop puts emphasis on the pending Medicare coverage decision, which management sees as the most important catalyst for future growth.

Veris Health, which is developing an implantable physiological monitor for patient vital sign tracking, made progress in preparing for FDA premarket review by starting presubmission and testing activities. It continued its engagement with The Ohio State University’s James Cancer Hospital, but the release did not provide metrics on patient use or department coverage.

PAVmed had $6.5 million in cash and cash equivalents as of March 31, 2026, supported by proceeds from an equity raise at Lucid, while operating expenses and cash burn continued at a higher pace than last year. The company stressed its simplified capital structure and extended liquidity but noted that operational improvements now rely on external triggers like coverage and regulatory clearances.
