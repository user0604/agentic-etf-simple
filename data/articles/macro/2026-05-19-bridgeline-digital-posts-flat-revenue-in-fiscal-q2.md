Key Takeaways
Bridgeline Digital achieved record new contract wins but saw flat recognized revenue growth in the period.
Earnings per share (EPS) improved to $(0.04), from $(0.10) in the prior year quarter.
Management is focused on shifting revenue toward its AI-powered core products, targeting 70% core mix by fiscal 2026 end.

Bridgeline Digital (NASDAQ:BLIN) reported strong momentum in new business but its headline financial growth remained subdued. The enterprise search and eCommerce software provider continues to advance its AI product lineup and win higher-value contracts. In its earnings release on May 18, 2026, covering the second quarter of fiscal 2026, the company made noticeable gains in contract value and customer mix, yet revenue and gross profit were little changed. Overall, the quarter reflected meaningful execution progress inside a competitive market, but top-line acceleration and margin recovery are still needed.

Notable Developments
New contract activity reached a quarterly record, with 19 subscription contracts signed totaling $2.8 million in value and $875,000 in annual recurring revenue.
The average annual contract value for new core product customers more than doubled year over year to $44,000.
Gross margin declined to 64%, dropping below management’s previous guidance range of 65–67% amid stable revenue levels.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains focused on increasing the proportion of revenue from its AI-driven core products, aiming for a 70% mix by year-end fiscal 2026. The number to watch next is whether recognized revenue growth starts to match the strong new contract bookings. Investors should also monitor any further gross margin compression, as sustained pressures here could impact profitability goals.

Quarterly Highlights

The company posted its best-ever quarter for new customer wins, signing 19 new subscription contracts and adding $875,000 in new recurring revenue. This outpaces the prior quarter’s 13 new deals and shows strength in sales execution and customer acquisition.

Despite record sales activity, recognized revenue for the period was flat year over year and gross profit edged down. This could reflect the timing of revenue booked under long-term contracts or delays in the activation of newly sold services.

Bridgeline launched several enhancements to its HawkSearch platform, including new AI-powered assistants for shopping and analytics. These innovations received strong initial customer response and are intended to further differentiate its core product suite in a crowded marketplace.

What Bridgeline Digital Does

Bridgeline Digital develops software solutions that use artificial intelligence to power search, product discovery, and customer engagement for eCommerce and digital marketing platforms.
