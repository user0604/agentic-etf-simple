Ctw Cayman (NASDAQ:CTW) experienced a setback as it posted a net loss and flat revenue while seeing its user base shrink sharply. The digital entertainment platform operator, known for its instant-play, ad-free games on the G123.jp service, faced both rising costs and performance challenges across core markets. The company released its results for the six months ended January 31, 2026, on May 13, 2026. Overall, the period highlighted operational growing pains, with monetization among paying users improving even as total engagement fell off.

Notable Developments
Revenue slipped 0.7% to $40.9 million, with Japan declining as a share of overall sales but international regions growing, particularly Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau, up 39%.
Monthly active users fell 39% to just under 2.0 million, but average revenue per monthly active user rose more than 60% to $4.08.
Sales and marketing expenses dropped 15%, yet overall costs rose due to higher payroll and server expenses, contributing to the operating loss.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its focus on research and development and global expansion, but acknowledged that new game launches during the period did not meet performance expectations. The company plans to refine its approach and invest in new titles, but warns that slipping retention and user acquisition pose risks. Investors should closely monitor whether the company can stabilize or grow its user base and generate returns from recent R&D and platform investments.

Quarterly Highlights

The steep decline in monthly active users, down by 39% year over year, was the period’s most urgent issue. While revenue was nearly unchanged, the company relied heavily on higher monetization from a smaller group of core, paying users.

The international business offered some growth, with Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau revenue climbing by nearly 40%, but these regions remain far smaller than Japan, which itself shrank in both relative and absolute terms.

Cost discipline in marketing improved advertising efficiency and return on ad spend, but fixed costs like payroll and R&D rose sharply, pushing the company into a net loss despite flat revenue and lower marketing outlays.

What Ctw Cayman Does

Ctw Cayman operates an online platform featuring free-to-play, browser-based games and provides game development, publishing, and marketing services to developers worldwide.
