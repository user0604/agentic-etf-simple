Data Storage (NASDAQ:DTST) continued its business transformation this quarter, advancing its new artificial intelligence (AI) initiative while reporting a wider net loss compared to the prior year. The company provides managed voice, telecom, and emerging AI infrastructure services for regulated industries. It released first-quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 15, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed moderate business growth but highlighted the need for strict cost control as strategic initiatives ramped up.

Notable Developments
The company launched Sovereign AI Solutions, a subsidiary focused on compliance-driven AI infrastructure, but the platform is still in early development stage.
Recurring revenue in Nexxis, the telecom and managed services unit, grew 10.9%, but operating costs rose even more, widening net loss to $631,272 from $24,078 a year earlier.
Cash levels fell to $114,622, after Data Storage completed a major share repurchase in the quarter, returning $29.5 million to shareholders and sharply reducing shares outstanding.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management plans to keep building its new AI continuity initiative through Sovereign AI Solutions and is seeking potential acquisitions to strengthen its technology portfolio. The company did not issue explicit revenue or profit guidance for 2026. The most important factor to watch will be Data Storage’s ability to manage costs while trying to scale up new business areas and reduce operating losses.

Quarterly Highlights

The launch of Sovereign AI Solutions marks a key shift in strategy toward regulated AI infrastructure and compliance, with management highlighting this business as a potential growth engine over time. However, the platform is not yet generating revenue, and updates on commercial progress were limited to statements of ongoing development work.

Nexxis, Data Storage’s continuing source of recurring revenue, showed a 10.9% increase in sales and a notable improvement in gross margin to 53.7%, with management citing continued demand for managed telecom and internet services and increased spending from existing customers.

Despite this growth, the business remains small relative to company-wide expenses, making it difficult to cover corporate overhead.

Expenses, particularly selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) costs, increased by more than 70% to $1.47 million, driven largely by higher stock-based compensation and startup costs for new initiatives. This rise in expenses resulted in continued operating losses, outpacing improvement in gross profit.

What Data Storage Does

Data Storage provides managed voice, internet, and IT services through its Nexxis unit and is developing AI-driven infrastructure solutions for highly regulated industries.
