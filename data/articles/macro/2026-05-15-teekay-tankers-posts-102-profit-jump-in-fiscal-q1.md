Notable developments
Total revenue rose 23.5% year over year, reaching $286.1 million, driven by strong voyage and time-charter revenues.
Net operating cash flow more than doubled to $119.6 million, reflecting both higher earnings and continued tight cost control.
The quarterly dividend was maintained at $0.25 per share, and a special dividend of $1.00 per share was also declared for the period.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide updated financial or operational guidance in this release, deviating from its usual practice in prior quarters. The absence of new commentary increases uncertainty around expectations for the rest of fiscal 2026. Investors should closely watch for future updates on fleet deployment and market exposure, given current market volatility and geopolitical risks.

Quarterly highlights

Total revenue was lifted mostly by higher voyage charter activity and a strong market for tanker services. Time-charter revenues, which are generated by leasing ships for set periods, climbed over 100% from the prior year, reflecting improved rates and vessel mix.

Operating expenses such as voyage costs and charter hire fell sharply from the year-ago quarter, helping deliver a net margin of 53.7%. This improvement signals effective cost management despite rising general and administrative expenses.

The company recorded a gain of $22.7 million from vessel sales and asset write-downs as it continued its strategy of renewing its fleet. The balance sheet remained strong, with $722 million in cash and no reported debt, supporting capital flexibility.

What Teekay Tankers does

Teekay Tankers transports crude oil and refined petroleum products worldwide with its fleet of owned and chartered-in oil tankers.
