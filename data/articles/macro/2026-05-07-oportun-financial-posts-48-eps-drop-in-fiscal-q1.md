Oportun Financial Posts 48% EPS Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:54 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business win as all guidance metrics were achieved, but key profit and growth figures weakened versus last year.
Adjusted earnings per share fell 47.5%, and revenue declined 3.0% year over year.
Management maintained full-year guidance and highlighted upcoming risk-based product initiatives for later in the year.

Profit at Oportun Financial (NASDAQ:OPRT) slipped as several earnings and growth metrics fell over the past year, even though management delivered on its stated guidance. The company, a nonprime lender offering personal and secured loans to underserved U.S. consumers, reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter featured cost control and met internal targets, but core earnings and originations decreased compared to the same period the prior year.

Notable Developments
Revenue fell 3.0% from the prior year, mainly due to lower origination fees as loan volume decreased under a conservative lending approach.
Profitability remained under pressure, with adjusted EPS dropping nearly half and risk-adjusted margin shrinking by 2.8 percentage points to 14.4%.
Secured personal loans, which have lower loss rates and deliver higher revenue per loan, climbed to 9% of the loan portfolio and expanded to eight states.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 outlook, expecting originations growth in the mid-single digits and adjusted EPS between $1.50 and $1.65. The company plans to launch risk-based pricing later in the year to access more customers and improve margins, but executives flagged that credit improvement and earnings growth require further progress. Investors should watch for execution on new product rollouts and cost controls as key drivers over the next few quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Core revenue and originations both declined, as the company balanced risk in a challenging credit environment by keeping lending strict and limiting marketing spend. The cost of debt dropped from 8.2% to 7.0%, supporting net interest margins, but overall risk-adjusted returns weakened due to higher net charge-off rates.

The secured personal loan product, which uses vehicles as collateral, continued to grow as a share of the portfolio and is delivering twice the revenue per loan versus unsecured loans, according to management. This strategy is seen as a step toward better credit outcomes and diversification.

Operating efficiency improved, with the adjusted operating expense ratio falling from 13.3% to 12.7% year over year. The company achieved all first-quarter guidance metrics and maintains healthy liquidity with debt-to-equity improving to 6.8 times and $210 million in total cash.

What Oportun Financial Does

Oportun Financial is a consumer finance company that provides personal loans, secured personal loans, and related financial products to individuals underserved by traditional banks, primarily through online, phone, and retail channels.
