Key Takeaways
Revenue fell and losses deepened as pricing and product mix pressures weighed on the quarter.
Gross margins held up at 79.3% even though sales declined 10% year over year.
Management expects improvement later in 2026, with full-year revenue guidance reaffirmed.

Treace Medical Concepts (NASDAQ:TMCI) faced a challenging quarter as product mix and lower prices led to a decline in sales and deepened net losses. The minimally invasive foot and ankle device company reported financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 8, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the period showed that gross margin resilience helped cushion the impact of weaker sales, but the company remains in a recovery phase.

Notable Developments
Revenue decreased by 10% year over year in the first quarter of 2026, reflecting lower demand for higher-priced products.
Gross margin only slipped 0.4 percentage points year over year, holding steady despite product and pricing pressures.
Operating expenses dropped by $2.9 million, thanks mainly to lower selling, general, and R&D costs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue outlook of $202 million to $212 million, a decline of up to 5% compared to fiscal 2025. Executives project that sales growth and profit improvement should return in the second half of the year as new products gain traction and headwinds from price and mix fade. The top item to monitor next is adoption of recently launched products like the SuperBite Compression Screw System and their effect on overall sales.

Quarterly Highlights

The company delivered a limited release of the SuperBite Compression Screw System for midfoot and hindfoot procedures, with initial cases already underway. This addition rounds out a product portfolio that includes the Lapiplasty 3D Bunion Correction System, Adductoplasty, Nanoplasty, and Percuplasty devices—tools designed to address a wide range of foot deformities and surgical preferences.

Cost discipline was evident, as sales and marketing expenses dropped from the prior year while research and development investment also decreased slightly. These efforts helped partially offset revenue losses, though not enough to prevent wider net and EBITDA losses compared to the previous year.

Treace continues to invest in growth, expanding its patent portfolio beyond 130 granted patents and maintaining robust support for surgeon training and product education. Management said that more than 200 patent applications remain pending worldwide, underscoring efforts to protect and grow its innovations.

What Treace Medical Concepts Does

Treace Medical Concepts develops and sells surgical systems and tools for bunion and related foot deformity correction, focusing on procedures that enable minimally invasive treatment and improved patient outcomes.
