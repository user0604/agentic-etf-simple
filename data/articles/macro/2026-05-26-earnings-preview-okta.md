Earnings Preview: Okta

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Okta (NASDAQ:OKTA), a provider of cloud-based identity and access management software for businesses, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Okta delivered results that were ahead of analyst expectations. The company reported revenue of $761 million and non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) of $0.90. Both figures beat consensus estimates for the quarter. Okta continued to see momentum with large enterprise customers and strong adoption of its newer products, especially those targeting the growing use of artificial intelligence (AI) agents in business workflows. New products, including offerings for AI agent security, contributed significantly to bookings. Okta also maintained a healthy balance sheet and initiated a share repurchase program during the quarter.

Positives
Revenue and EPS exceeded expectations: Okta beat both revenue and non-GAAP EPS consensus for the quarter, reflecting strong customer demand and effective execution.
Strong adoption of new products: Approximately 30% of Q4 bookings came from new products, including those focused on AI agent security, driving a 40% average uplift in contract value when included in deals.
Growth in large enterprise customers: The number of customers with annual contract value above $100,000 rose to 5,100, up from 4,800 a year ago, signaling continued traction with larger organizations.
Healthy cash flow and capital allocation: Okta ended the quarter with $2.5 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, and began a $1 billion share repurchase program, signaling confidence in its financial position.
Negatives
Slower net retention rate: Dollar-based net retention rate declined to 106% from 107% the prior year, indicating slower expansion within existing customers.
Professional services revenue to decline: Okta announced it will shift more professional services work to partners, which will reduce professional services revenue in future quarters. This may impact total revenue growth, though it is intended to support long-term growth with larger customers.
Competitive and macroeconomic pressures: Okta noted ongoing competition in the identity and security market, as well as potential headwinds from broader economic uncertainty and past cybersecurity incidents.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Progress of new AI agent security products: Watch for updates on customer adoption and revenue contribution from Okta for AI Agents and Auth0 for AI Agents, which are still in early stages but seen as key growth drivers.
Subscription revenue growth versus total revenue: Okta guided to 9% total revenue growth and 10% subscription revenue growth for Q1 FY2027, with a shift in professional services expected to lower total revenue growth relative to subscription growth.
Operating margin and cash flow trends: Management forecast a non-GAAP operating margin of 23% to 24% and free cash flow margin of 33% to 35% for Q1. Investors should look for evidence of continued spending discipline and margin expansion.
Impact of partnerships and go-to-market changes: Okta is relying more on global system integrators and channel partners. Updates on how this transition is affecting deal sizes, sales cycles, and customer wins will be important.
Updates on large customer wins and net retention: Investors should monitor whether Okta can sustain growth in large enterprise customers and improve its net retention rate, which has been flat to slightly down in recent quarters.
