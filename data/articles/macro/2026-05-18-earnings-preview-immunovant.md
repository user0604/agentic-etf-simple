Immunovant (NASDAQ:IMVT), a biotechnology company developing antibody-based therapies for autoimmune diseases, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

For the third quarter ended Dec. 31, 2025, Immunovant reported a net loss of $110.6 million, which was an improvement versus the prior year and represented an earnings per share (EPS) result that beat analyst expectations. The company did not report any product revenue, consistent with its status as a clinical-stage company. Research and development (R&D) expenses increased compared to the prior year, mainly due to higher costs for ongoing and new clinical trials, particularly for the IMVT-1402 program. General and administrative expenses decreased year over year, reflecting lower personnel and market research costs.

Operationally, the company announced full enrollment of its difficult-to-treat rheumatoid arthritis (D2T RA) trial for IMVT-1402, with topline data expected in the second half of 2026. Immunovant also completed a significant equity financing, ending the quarter with $994.5 million in cash and cash equivalents. No product launches or regulatory approvals occurred during the quarter, and the company remains focused on advancing its clinical pipeline.
