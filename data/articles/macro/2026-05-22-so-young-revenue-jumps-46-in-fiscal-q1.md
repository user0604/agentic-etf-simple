Key Takeaways

Cosmetic services operator So-Young International (NASDAQ:SY) continued its rapid branded clinic expansion, but growing losses and digital platform declines tempered the gains. The company, which runs an online marketplace and direct clinics for medical aesthetics across China, reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 22, 2026. Overall, the quarter delivered strong top-line growth but underlined profitability and business mix risks.

Notable Developments
The branded clinic network reached 54 operating centers, with 41 centers now profitable and 48 generating positive cash flow during the period.
Aesthetic treatment services revenue grew 185.8% year over year, reflecting strong user demand and rapid network expansion.
Platform (information and reservation services) revenue dropped 34% from the prior-year quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects branded clinic segment revenues to increase again in the second quarter, with guidance for aesthetic treatment services revenue between RMB307 million and RMB317 million (up over 112% year over year for the segment). The company did not comment on expected profitability for the full group. Investors should watch whether new center revenue growth leads to a reduction in overall company losses and for stabilization in the digital platform segment.

Quarterly Highlights

The company's branded clinics drove the large revenue increase, with network expansion to 54 centers and more mature clinics generating higher per-location revenue. As clinics aged, their average revenue per center climbed, supporting management’s emphasis on scale and maturity.

User engagement metrics improved, with verified treatment visits rising to 148,000 (from 54,400) and active users exceeding 213,000. Over 80% of segment revenue came from repeat “core members,” a sign of strong network and service loyalty.

The online platform, once the company's primary business line, posted a sharp revenue decline as fewer medical providers subscribed to its services. This contraction has reduced total group gross margin and contributed to growing company-wide losses, as operating costs grew alongside clinic expansion.

What So-Young International Does

So-Young International operates a digital marketplace and branded clinics in China that connect users with medical aesthetic services, including cosmetic treatments and related procedures.

To stay up to date on shifts in company strategy, members should review the next quarterly update and watch for margin improvements as the clinic network matures.
