PTGX Company Overview
Protagonist Therapeutics develops oral and injectable peptide-based drugs to treat blood disorders and chronic inflammatory diseases. The business generates revenue by licensing its therapeutic candidates to global pharmaceutical partners in exchange for milestone payments and royalties on future sales. This collaboration model allows the company to leverage the commercial infrastructure of large partners while maintaining a proprietary technology platform that uses molecular design to address biological targets.

Protagonist Gains FDA Approval for Psoriasis Drug

Protagonist Therapeutics makes new oral medicines for autoimmune and metabolic diseases. Its big focus is developing pills instead of shots for conditions like psoriasis. The stock soared 254.3% over the last five years, beating the S&P 500 by 181.6 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $56.4 million (up 99.3% YoY)
EPS (Diluted): $0.05 (up from -$0.19 YoY)
Research and Development Expense: $46.7 million (up 30.1% YoY)

Latest Developments

The FDA approved Protagonist's first major drug, ICOTYDE, for psoriasis, triggering a large milestone payment and pushing the company to a profit this quarter.
Protagonist left its partnership for rusfertide and now stands to receive a $200 million payment if the FDA approves the drug, which could also lead to more future royalties.
