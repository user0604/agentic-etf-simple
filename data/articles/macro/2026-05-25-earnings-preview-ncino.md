Earnings Preview: nCino

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

nCino (NASDAQ:NCNO), which provides cloud-based banking software to financial institutions, is expected to report its Q1 FY2027 earnings on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, nCino reported total revenues of $149.7 million, representing a 6% increase year over year. Subscription revenues reached $133.4 million, up 7% from the prior year, while non-GAAP operating income was $34.7 million, or 23% of total revenues. The company posted non-GAAP net income of $42.8 million, or $0.37 per diluted share. These results exceeded analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings per share. Annualized contract value (ACV) climbed 17% year over year to $602.4 million, and the ACV net retention rate improved to 112%, reflecting strong customer expansion and adoption of new AI-driven capabilities. The quarter also included record international bookings and a notable surge in usage of nCino's AI features across its customer base.

Positives
Beat on all key financial metrics: nCino exceeded both revenue and earnings expectations, which demonstrates strong operational execution and demand for its platform.
Significant growth in AI adoption: Over 170 customers purchased AI intelligence units by year-end, and usage of AI-powered features increased sharply. This suggests nCino’s AI investments are gaining traction and could drive future growth.
Improved customer retention and expansion: The ACV net retention rate rose to 112%, indicating existing customers are expanding their use of the platform, particularly for new AI capabilities.
Record international performance: International bookings hit an all-time high, and the company secured a major new customer in Austria as well as a large Japanese bank, highlighting global momentum.
Negatives
Flat professional services revenue and margin pressure: Professional services revenue was essentially unchanged year over year, and gross margin for this segment declined, reflecting ongoing investments and lower utilization rates.
Continued headwinds in U.S. mortgage business: The company maintained a cautious outlook for U.S. mortgage-related revenue due to ongoing market challenges, which could limit growth in this segment.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Progress on AI monetization and customer adoption: Watch for updates on the number of customers using AI features, growth in intelligence unit consumption, and any commentary on how AI is contributing to revenue and customer outcomes.
Subscription revenue growth versus guidance: The company guided to Q1 subscription revenue of $137 million to $139 million. Compare actual results to this range and to the consensus estimate of $155.8 million in total sales.
Impact of new pricing model transitions: Management reported 38% of ACV is now on platform-based pricing. Look for further progress and any effects on renewal rates, contract duration, and overall revenue mix.
International expansion and large deal ramp-up: nCino highlighted strong international momentum and large customer wins in EMEA and Japan. Monitor updates on implementation progress, revenue recognition timing, and new international bookings.
Updates on recent acquisitions and product integration: The integration of Sandbox Banking and DocFox was completed or is nearing completion. Watch for any early signs of cross-selling, new product launches, or customer wins tied to these acquisitions.
