Earnings Preview: Ciena

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Ciena (NYSE:CIEN), a provider of high-speed networking equipment and software for global telecom and cloud companies, is expected to report earnings for its fiscal second quarter on Thursday, June 4, 2026, before the market opens.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

Ciena delivered record financial results in its first quarter of fiscal 2026. Revenue reached $1.43 billion, representing a 33% increase from the prior year period. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $1.35. The company’s gross margin was 43.8% on a GAAP basis, with adjusted gross margin reported at 44.7%. These results beat analyst estimates for both revenue and EPS. The company also reported a significant increase in order intake, driving its backlog to a record $7 billion, with approximately 80% of this backlog tied to products and software. Demand was broad-based, with strong contributions from both cloud and service provider customers, and notable momentum in managed optical fiber networks (MOFN) and data center interconnect solutions.

Positives
Record revenue and EPS performance beat expectations: The company’s revenue and adjusted EPS both exceeded analyst consensus, reflecting strong demand and execution.
Significant backlog growth provides future visibility: Backlog rose by $2 billion to $7 billion, with most new orders scheduled for delivery in fiscal 2027, supporting confidence in sustained growth.
Strong order activity across customer segments: Orders were robust from service providers, hyperscalers, and emerging neoscalers, indicating broad market strength.
Improved product gross margins from cost reductions: Product gross margin increased by 90 basis points year over year, supported by cost efficiencies and favorable product mix.
Negatives
Services gross margin declined: Services gross margin fell by 420 basis points compared to the prior year, mainly due to a less favorable mix of services.
Ongoing supply chain constraints limited revenue: Management noted that revenue could have been higher if not for component and manufacturing supply limitations, which are expected to persist for several quarters.
Operating expenses increased: Total operating expenses rose 11.5% year over year, driven by higher research and development spending and employee-related costs, partly due to the Nubis Communications acquisition.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Q2 revenue and margin guidance versus consensus: Management guided Q2 revenue to about $1.5 billion plus or minus $50 million and gross margin between 43.5% and 44.5%. Analyst consensus expects revenue of about $1.5 billion and EPS near $1.46. Watch for how actual results compare to these targets.
Progress on supply chain and capacity expansion: The company is investing heavily to expand manufacturing and secure component supply. Updates on easing constraints or further impacts could affect future growth and margins.
Impact of pricing actions and product mix on margins: Price increases are expected to benefit results mainly in the second half of the year. The ramp of 800ZR pluggable optics and new HyperRail solutions may also influence gross margin trends.
Status of new product initiatives and customer wins: Watch for updates on the rollout of the RLS HyperRail platform, Vesta optical engine, Nitro Linear Redriver, and DCOM solution. Note any new customer deployments or design wins, especially with hyperscalers and neoscalers.
Backlog growth and demand sustainability: Management expects backlog to continue rising through the year, with most new orders for 2027 delivery. Any changes in order rates or commentary on early ordering trends could signal shifts in demand patterns or customer behavior.
