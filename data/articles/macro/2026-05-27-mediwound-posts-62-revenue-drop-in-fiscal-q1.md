MediWound Posts 62% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 7:16 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
MediWound posted a significant quarterly loss as revenue dropped sharply on delayed government contracts.
Revenue fell by 62.5% year over year, with a wider net loss and higher R&D costs.
Management reaffirmed full-year guidance, anticipating a revenue rebound in the second half due to government contract timing.

MediWound (NASDAQ:MDWD), a specialty biopharmaceutical company focused on burn and wound care, reported a disappointing start to fiscal 2026 as first-quarter revenue plunged due to delayed government payments and shipment disruptions. The company released results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 27, 2026, citing lower funding activity and operating losses tied to increased investment in its clinical pipeline. Overall, the quarter was marked by operational progress but a steep financial shortfall, with management betting on a second-half recovery.

Notable developments
The quarter's revenue fell sharply as government-related development contracts were pushed to the second half of the year, while gross margin increased from 18.7% to 21.9% due to a more favorable sales mix.
Research and development spending rose to $5.2 million, largely driven by the ongoing Phase III VALUE trial for EscharEx, MediWound’s pipeline chronic wound treatment.
U.S. adoption of NexoBrid, a topical burn debridement gel, expanded as new government contracts were secured, but commercial supply from the new manufacturing site is still pending regulatory approval.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue target of $24 million to $26 million, basing this on expected recognition of delayed government revenue and new contract deliveries in the second half of the year. The most critical factor for investors is whether MediWound can close its second-half revenue gap by completing key BARDA (Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority) projects and resolving shipment delays.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue was hit hard by the delayed timing of BARDA-related government income and shipment postponements due to regional conflict, driving the lowest quarterly revenue in recent years. Despite the lower sales, gross margin increased, suggesting a shift in sales mix or improved cost discipline.

The company invested heavily in research and development, with expenses jumping to $5.2 million from $2.9 million a year earlier as it advanced the EscharEx Phase III trial, although slower-than-expected enrollment has now pushed completion into early 2027.

NexoBrid saw U.S. market traction and benefited from new long-term government contracts but remains dependent on regulatory signoff for expanded manufacturing output. Several major wound care firms increased collaboration with MediWound during the quarter, broadening its ecosystem.

What MediWound does

MediWound develops and commercializes bio-therapeutic products for treating severe burns and chronic wounds.
