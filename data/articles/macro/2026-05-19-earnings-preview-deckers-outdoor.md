Earnings Preview: Deckers Outdoor

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Deckers Outdoor (NYSE:DECK), a global footwear and apparel company best known for its HOKA and UGG brands, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 after the market closes on Thursday, May 21, 2026.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

Deckers delivered record third-quarter results, with net sales rising 7.1% year over year to $1.96 billion. Growth was driven by strong demand for both HOKA, a performance and lifestyle footwear brand, and UGG, a premium lifestyle footwear brand. HOKA revenue grew 18.5% while UGG increased 4.9%, each showing balanced gains across both direct-to-consumer (DTC) and wholesale channels. International sales were especially robust. Gross margin came in at 59.8%, slightly below last year due to higher tariffs and promotions, but above internal expectations. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) reached $3.33, up 11% from the prior year. These results exceeded analyst estimates for both revenue and EPS.

Positives
Strong double-digit HOKA growth: HOKA’s 18.5% revenue increase highlights the brand’s growing global appeal and successful product launches, especially in both performance and lifestyle categories.
Balanced channel and geographic performance: Both DTC and wholesale channels grew, and international markets posted a 15% revenue gain, showing Deckers’ ability to execute across regions and sales models.
Record EPS and robust cash position: EPS of $3.33 set a new high, and the company ended the quarter with $2.1 billion in cash and no debt, supporting ongoing share repurchases and future investments.
Effective inventory and marketplace management: Improved in-stock positions and disciplined channel allocation helped maintain high levels of full-price selling, limiting the need for heavy promotions.
Negatives
Gross margin pressure from tariffs and promotions: Gross margin fell 50 basis points year over year, reflecting increased U.S. tariffs and a slight uptick in promotional activity, though the impact was less severe than initially forecast.
Rising SG&A expenses: Selling, general, and administrative costs increased 4% as Deckers invested in marketing, retail expansion, and technology, which could weigh on margins if not matched by future sales growth.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
HOKA’s largest-ever quarterly revenue and growth trajectory: Management expects HOKA to deliver 13% to 14% year-over-year growth in Q4, marking its biggest quarterly revenue to date. Investors should watch for confirmation of this target and any commentary on the sustainability of HOKA’s momentum, especially in international markets.
UGG revenue trends and timing effects: UGG’s Q4 revenue is expected to be roughly flat year over year due to some orders shipping early in Q3. Pay attention to how this timing shift impacts reported results and whether underlying demand remains healthy heading into fiscal 2027.
Tariff impact on gross margin: Deckers anticipates a 200 basis point headwind to gross margin in Q4 from tariffs, representing the largest quarterly impact this year. The company’s ability to offset this through pricing or cost controls will be a key focus.
Direct-to-consumer channel performance: Both HOKA and UGG showed improved DTC results in Q3, helped by new membership programs and product launches. Investors should look for updates on DTC growth rates, customer engagement, and the contribution of new products or loyalty initiatives.
Status of key product launches and initiatives: Deckers highlighted several new products for both brands, including HOKA’s Cielo X1 3.0 racing shoe and UGG’s Minimel sneaker and Otzo clog. Watch for management updates on the market reception of these launches and any progress on announced, pending, or completed expansion initiatives, such as new store openings or distribution partnerships.
