Key Takeaways
Freightos faced a mixed quarter as revenue grew only modestly and losses widened.

Freightos (NASDAQ:CRGO) encountered a challenging start to 2026 as robust transaction growth failed to translate into meaningful revenue acceleration and losses increased compared to a year ago. The global digital freight platform connects carriers, forwarders, and shippers through its online marketplace and data solutions. Reporting results for the first quarter on May 26, 2026, Freightos demonstrated ongoing network expansion but revised its outlook to reflect slower core growth. Overall, the period showed progress in volume, but a notable deceleration in revenue and mounting profitability hurdles.

Notable developments
Transactions on the platform rose 15% to 425,000, reflecting network expansion despite weaker performance in some customer segments.
Gross booking value (the value of all booked shipments) increased 24% to $343 million even as revenue grew just 3%.
Net loss widened to $6.5 million, driven by $1.5 million in reorganization costs and persistent investment in platform development.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management has lowered its full-year revenue guidance to growth of 3–6%, citing softer demand in some segments and challenging global freight conditions. It still expects to reach breakeven at the adjusted EBITDA level by the end of the year. Investors should monitor whether Freightos can improve revenue conversion from growing transaction and booking volumes while controlling cash burn.

Quarterly highlights

Freightos processed 425,000 transactions during the quarter, a 15% rise compared to last year, as new carriers like Ethiopian Cargo joined its platform. However, revenue only increased 3%, showing a growing gap between activity metrics and top-line growth, partly due to underperformance in software subscription and customs services.

The platform’s gross booking value hit $343 million, up 24% year over year, supported by higher freight rates from global supply disruptions, especially in the Middle East. Despite this, the company’s adjusted earnings loss improved slightly, but a significant $1.5 million restructuring charge resulted in a higher net loss.

Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term bank deposits totaled $23.5 million at quarter’s end, down from $27.9 million three months prior, underscoring the importance of achieving break-even in the near term. Management maintains that, if plans succeed, cash can stabilize near $20 million by year-end.

What Freightos does

Freightos offers a digital marketplace and software tools for booking and managing global freight shipments, connecting buyers, carriers, and logistics providers online.
