Key Takeaways

Agilent Technologies (NYSE:A) achieved broad-based growth across all its business segments in the latest quarter. The company is a global supplier of laboratory instruments, software, and services for life sciences, diagnostics, and applied chemical markets. Reporting results for its fiscal second quarter on May 27, 2026, it showed strong demand and improved profitability. Overall, the period reflected solid operational momentum and a more optimistic outlook for the rest of the year.

Notable Developments
Total revenue increased 10%, led by 12% growth in Life Sciences and Diagnostics and 14% in Applied Markets.
Non-GAAP operating margin expanded by 1.3 percentage points, with notable gains in both Applied Markets and Life Sciences and Diagnostics segments.
Management raised its full-year outlook, now targeting revenue of $7.39–$7.49 billion and non-GAAP EPS of $6.00–$6.10.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its guidance for both revenue and earnings for fiscal 2026, citing healthy order trends and operational improvements. The company expects continued demand across its core markets. Investors should watch for sustained margin performance as Agilent continues to invest in research and development.

Quarterly Highlights

The Life Sciences and Diagnostics segment delivered 12% revenue growth and improved its operating margin to 22.0%. This segment benefited from strong demand in pharmaceutical and diagnostics end markets.

The Applied Markets segment posted a 14% revenue increase, with operating margin rising by 3.8 percentage points. Growth was driven by advanced materials and instrumentation used in semiconductor and chemical analysis labs.

Agilent CrossLab, which provides laboratory services and consumables, maintained high margins but saw slower core growth compared to other segments. Service contracts and consumables supported steady results, while segment margin remained flat year over year.

What Agilent Technologies Does

Agilent Technologies supplies laboratory instruments, software, consumables, and services for research, diagnostics, and applied testing markets worldwide.
