Key Takeaways
Block posted a strong quarter with standout performance in key business lines.

Block (NYSE:SQ) delivered a standout first quarter, achieving notable business gains in its core financial and commerce ecosystems. The financial technology company, which operates the Square payments network and consumer-facing Cash App, filed results on May 7, 2026, covering its fiscal Q1 2026. Overall, the quarter featured significant progress in profitability, margin, and product innovation, tempered by ongoing bottom-line losses due to restructuring and legal charges.

Notable developments
Adjusted operating income rose to $728 million, up 56% year over year, as cost savings from restructuring took effect.
Cash App gross profit increased 38%, outpacing growth in active users and highlighting improved monetization per customer.
Management raised full-year guidance for gross profit to $12.33 billion, up from the prior estimate of $12.2 billion.
Management outlook and what to watch

Block management has increased its full-year profit outlook, now expecting gross profit growth of 19% and an even sharper rise in adjusted operating income. The outlook points to continued margin expansion as restructuring, AI adoption, and efficiency initiatives reduce costs. Investors should closely watch the Cash App active user growth and lending performance, as these are central to sustaining profit gains.

Quarterly highlights

Cash App, Block’s consumer financial services product, was the main engine of growth, delivering $1.91 billion in gross profit. Higher profit per user outweighed slower growth in active users, with lending originations expanding 82% compared to the prior year.

Square, Block’s business-facing payment service, showed continued expansion, with gross profit rising 9% as gross payment volume climbed 13%. International markets provided much of this momentum, while new seller acquisition efforts scaled up meaningfully.

AI-driven initiatives and a major restructuring earlier in the year helped lower non-GAAP product development expenses by 9%, despite elevated legal and restructuring charges that impacted GAAP results. Block ended the period with $9.1 billion in liquidity and continued significant share repurchases.

What Block does

Block is a global technology company providing payment, banking, and commerce solutions for businesses and consumers through connected platforms such as Square and Cash App.
