Earnings Preview: Marvell Technology

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Marvell Technology (NASDAQ:MRVL), a provider of chips for data centers and networking equipment, is expected to report its earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

Marvell reported record revenue of $2.219 billion for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, representing 7% sequential growth and 22% growth year over year. Non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) reached $0.80, which exceeded the midpoint of company guidance and met analyst consensus. The company’s data center business was the main driver, accounting for 74% of total revenue and growing 21% year over year. Communications and other end markets also showed improvement, with revenue up 26% year over year. Marvell’s operating margins and cash flows improved, and the company continued to return capital to shareholders through buybacks and dividends.

Positives
Record quarterly revenue driven by data center demand: The data center segment saw strong growth, reflecting robust demand for Marvell’s interconnect, switching, and storage products, as well as custom silicon solutions.
Non-GAAP EPS exceeded guidance: Earnings per share of $0.80 were above the midpoint of guidance and aligned with analyst expectations, indicating solid execution and cost management.
Improved operating leverage and margins: Non-GAAP operating margin reached 35.7%, expanding year over year, which shows the company is benefiting from scale and higher-value product mix.
Strategic acquisitions completed: The acquisitions of Celestial AI and XConn were finalized, positioning Marvell to address emerging opportunities in AI networking and advanced switching technologies.
Negatives
Inventory build to support growth: Inventory increased to $1.39 billion, which could pose a risk if demand expectations are not met, though management linked this to anticipated revenue growth.
Exposure to China-related risks: The company continues to face uncertainty from U.S. export restrictions and potential customer shifts in China, which could impact future revenue streams.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Data center revenue growth and product mix: Investors should watch for updates on the sequential growth in the data center segment, especially the ramp of 1.6-terabit optical interconnects and the balance between 800-gigabit and 1.6-terabit products.
Progress integrating Celestial AI and XConn: The status of these recently completed acquisitions and any early contributions or integration milestones will be key, as these are expected to drive future growth in AI scale-up networking and advanced PCIe/CXL switching.
Custom silicon and XPU attach programs: Look for commentary on the ramp of new custom chip programs, including next-generation XPU (accelerator) projects and the doubling of XPU attach revenue, which are critical for Marvell’s long-term growth targets.
Operating expense trends and margin outlook: The company guided for higher non-GAAP operating expenses in Q1 due to seasonality and acquisition-related costs. Investors should monitor whether operating leverage is maintained as revenue grows.
China and supply chain risks: Any new disclosures about the impact of export controls, customer inventory behavior in China, or supply chain constraints could affect the outlook for the rest of fiscal 2027.
