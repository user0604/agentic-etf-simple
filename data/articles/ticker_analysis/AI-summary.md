AI Company Overview
C3.ai develops enterprise artificial intelligence software products that enable organizations to build and operate large-scale applications across many different sectors. The company primarily generates revenue through multi-period subscriptions for its application development platform and specific pre-built software tools. By integrating with major cloud providers and industrial partners, the firm creates high switching costs that keep its clients within a dedicated software ecosystem.
