Key Takeaways
Business win as Brand Portfolio growth offset softness in Famous Footwear.
Revenue rose 8.5% compared to the same quarter last year.
Management raised the low end of full-year earnings guidance, forecasting modest sales and meaningful profit growth.

Caleres (NYSE:CAL) posted a strong start to fiscal 2026, as gains in its fashion and comfort brand segment outweighed pressure in its retail shoe stores. The leading footwear company, owner of brands like Sam Edelman and Famous Footwear, released its first quarter results on June 4, 2026, for the period ended May 2. Overall, the quarter marked important progress on margin expansion and profit recovery, even as certain areas require further attention.

Notable Developments
Brand Portfolio segment sales jumped 20.6% to $356.3 million, benefiting from the addition of Stuart Weitzman and ongoing lead brand strength.
Gross margin improved by 2.0 percentage points to 47.3%, reflecting a better product mix and successful efforts to mitigate tariffs.
Inventory rose to $609.1 million, but excluding the Stuart Weitzman addition, the company's inventory was down $22.7 million—a sign of improved working capital management.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised the low end of its full-year adjusted earnings guidance to $1.40 per share and now expects modest sales growth with meaningful profit expansion for 2026. Direct-to-consumer channels and Brand Portfolio momentum remain the company’s focus, while a turnaround in Famous Footwear will be critical to future results. Investors should watch whether continued gross margin gains and cost control are enough to offset ongoing retail store softness.

Quarterly Highlights

Brand Portfolio, which includes owned and licensed brands across fashion and comfort categories, showed broad-based sales growth, especially with its recently acquired Stuart Weitzman luxury brand. Excluding Stuart Weitzman, organic sales rose nearly 6%, with operating margin more than doubling from the previous year. Segment profitability benefited from product mix and improved supply chain results.

The Famous Footwear segment saw top-line decline and margin pressure, offset only partially by e-commerce and remodeled FLAIR stores, which outperformed the broader chain. Comparable store sales fell 2.3%, and the segment recorded a slight operating loss. Despite this, management cited market share gains in kids’ shoes and further online sales growth as positives.

Total direct-to-consumer sales, representing 67% of company-wide revenue, underlined ongoing omni-channel retail strategy progress. Inventory build reflected the addition of Stuart Weitzman, while a decrease in core inventory signaled tighter execution. Cash flow from operations was negative, highlighting the need for sequential improvement as the year progresses.

What Caleres Does

Caleres operates and licenses a portfolio of footwear brands and retail chains, selling products through wholesale partners, company-owned stores, and e-commerce channels.
