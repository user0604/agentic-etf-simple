Business Overview
Eton Pharmaceuticals develops and markets specialty medicines for rare diseases, focusing on commercializing and expanding a diversified rare disease portfolio.
Product revenue increased sharply, supported by the launch of DESMODA, an oral desmopressin solution, and the relaunch of HEMANGEOL for rare diseases.
Gross profit improved as product sales diversified, but gross margin slipped from 69% to 67% due to lower-margin ex-U.S. sales and the absence of licensing revenue.
Management raised its full-year revenue forecast to above $120 million, citing the contributions of new products and portfolio expansion.
Quarterly Performance

Product sales and royalties for the quarter rose compared to the prior year, driven by strong performances from core rare disease products and the introduction of new offerings like DESMODA and KHINDIVI. These launches expanded Eton's market reach and supported portfolio diversity, with DESMODA gaining early adoption and HEMANGEOL transitioning smoothly after its U.S. relaunch in May.

Gross profit increased year over year, but the gross margin percentage dropped slightly due to changes in product mix. Higher general and administrative costs, including new FDA program fees and expanded staffing, also pushed operating expenses up. However, adjusted EBITDA margin rose, reflecting a greater share of revenue converting to operating profit.

Research and development spending increased as Eton invested in clinical programs for label expansions and began studies for pipeline assets such as ET-700, an extended-release zinc acetate for Wilson disease. Early results from KHINDIVI's label expansion trials are due in the coming quarter, with additional regulatory submissions planned.

Looking Ahead

Management now expects total revenue for fiscal 2026 to exceed $120 million, an increase from previous guidance. The team forecasts an adjusted EBITDA margin of at least 30% for the year. Investors should monitor the uptake of new launches, especially DESMODA and HEMANGEOL, as well as research progress on label expansion for existing therapies.
