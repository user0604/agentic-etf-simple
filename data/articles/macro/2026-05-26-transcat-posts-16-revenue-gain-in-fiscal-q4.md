Transcat (NASDAQ:TRNS) reported robust revenue growth but struggled with rising costs that caused a drop in operating and net income. The leading provider of calibration services and instrument distribution for regulated industries expanded its top line, though bottom-line results did not keep pace. The results, released May 26, 2026, cover the fiscal fourth quarter ended March 28. Overall, the quarter highlighted solid sales momentum but flagged concerns about profit margins and expenses.

Notable developments
Revenue in the Service segment was up 18.4% in the quarter, with organic service growth of 7.0% and recent acquisitions adding $5.8 million.
Operating expenses rose 37.8% year over year, driven by higher costs for acquisitions, integration, stock compensation, and leadership transition.
Total debt at quarter-end grew to $99.9 million from $32.7 million the prior year, reflecting funding for acquisitions and increased investment.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects continued high single-digit organic service revenue growth in fiscal 2027, provided economic conditions remain steady. There is no specific outlook provided for margin recovery or earnings per share. Investors should monitor expense trends and cash flow as the company continues to invest in growth and acquisitions.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue growth was strong, led by gains in both service and distribution businesses. Service segment revenue made up nearly 69% of total sales, reflecting demand from regulated industry clients and contributions from recent acquisitions, including a new base in Latin America.

Profitability came under pressure as operating expenses jumped, mainly due to costs tied to acquisitions, higher amortization, stock compensation, and CEO transition charges. While adjusted EBITDA—earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization with certain expenses excluded—rose to $14.8 million, the operating margin fell to 4.8%.

The company’s balance sheet showed the impact of its growth strategy, with debt more than tripling to support acquisitions and expansion. Cash flow from operations was down year over year, largely because of increased investment in service capacity and rental assets.

What Transcat does

Transcat provides accredited calibration services and distributes test, measurement, and control instruments to industries where precision and compliance are required.
