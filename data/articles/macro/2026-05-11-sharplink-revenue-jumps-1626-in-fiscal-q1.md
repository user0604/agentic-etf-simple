Key Takeaways
Sharplink delivered a large revenue increase but faced a substantial non-cash accounting loss owing to ETH market decline.

Sharplink (NASDAQ:SBET) posted a notable business win through rapid expansion of its ETH treasury and strong revenue growth, yet the quarter was marked by a deep accounting loss following a sharp drop in the market value of Ethereum. The digital asset treasury specialist has positioned itself as a key institutional player in Ethereum management, overseeing a large ETH and staking operation. The earnings release, published on May 11, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, reflected the scale and volatility of Sharplink’s current strategy. Overall, the quarter demonstrated increased ETH accumulation and operational scale, but highlighted the challenges of operating under strict accounting rules tied to digital asset prices.

Notable Developments
Revenue jumped to $12.1 million from $0.7 million, with staking making up $11.5 million of the total.
The company booked a $685.6 million net loss, driven almost entirely by non-cash losses and ETH market declines.
ETH holdings rose to 870,821 by quarter end, and Sharplink announced a strategic MOU with Galaxy Digital to pursue onchain yield funds.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its focus on expanding its ETH holdings and executing institutional treasury strategies without giving precise guidance for future quarters. It highlighted continued development in staking, yield opportunities, and institutional partnerships. For investors, the main item to watch is ongoing ETH market volatility, as realized and unrealized changes in ETH value drive reported results regardless of operational progress.

Quarterly Highlights

Sharplink’s main business line, ETH staking and treasury management, powered the large jump in revenue over the prior year. The increase reflects more ETH under management and higher productivity from yield-generating activities. Affiliate marketing, the smaller segment, saw revenue slip modestly and continues to shrink in importance as the company concentrates on digital assets.

The net loss this quarter was significant and chiefly non-cash, mostly due to a sharp decline in the ETH market price and related impairment charges on tokens held and deployed in staking. Management has repeatedly emphasized these charges do not lower the actual units of ETH held, but changes in ETH price can greatly affect reported profit under current accounting requirements. Expenses also climbed, with SG&A rising to $9.9 million, a result of increased spending on systems, talent, and scaling its internal asset management team.

Sharplink moved forward with key partnerships, including a non-binding memorandum of understanding with Galaxy Digital to develop the Galaxy Sharplink Onchain Yield Fund. This potential $125 million partnership could strengthen its position in institutional-grade ETH yield and expand its product offerings to outside investors.

What Sharplink Does

Sharplink is a public company specializing in institutional Ethereum treasury management and staking, while also operating an affiliate marketing business in the online gaming sector.
