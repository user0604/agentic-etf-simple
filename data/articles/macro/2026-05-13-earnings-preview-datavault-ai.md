Earnings Preview: Datavault AI

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Datavault AI (NASDAQ:DVLT), a company that builds and licenses secure platforms for managing and monetizing data, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Datavault AI reported its first ever profitable quarter on a generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) basis. The company achieved adjusted EBITDA of over $8 million and ended the year with more than $115 million in working capital. Full-year revenue reached $39.1 million, marking a 1,362% increase from the prior year, driven mainly by new patent license revenue and the acquisition of CompuSystems Inc. (CSI). Gross margin improved to 78% for the year, up from 14% in 2024, due to higher-margin licensing deals. These results beat analyst estimates for both earnings and revenue in the quarter.

Positives
First profitable quarter on a GAAP basis: This milestone demonstrates that the company’s licensing and platform strategy is starting to deliver sustainable financial results.
Significant revenue growth: Revenue for the year ended Dec. 31, 2025, grew over 1,300% year over year, reflecting strong demand for Datavault’s patented technology and the impact of acquisitions.
Improved gross margin: Gross margin increased to 78% for 2025, showing that higher-margin licensing revenue now makes up a larger share of the business.
Strengthened balance sheet: The company ended the year with over $115 million in working capital and substantially eliminated its debt, reducing financial risk.
Negatives
Rising operating expenses: Research and development, sales and marketing, and general and administrative costs all increased significantly due to higher headcount, licensing fees, and legal expenses. This could pressure future profitability if not managed carefully.
High interest expense and debt-related charges: Interest expense and costs tied to convertible notes and warrant liabilities were substantial in 2025, although the company has since reduced its debt load.
Cash flow remains negative: Despite profitability in the quarter, Datavault used $23.6 million in net cash for operating activities during the year, which may concern investors looking for sustained positive cash flow.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on integrating recent acquisitions: Investors should look for updates on the completed acquisitions of CompuSystems Inc. (CSI), API Media, and NYIAX, including how these are contributing to revenue and platform development.
Execution of Event Citadel and new exchange launches: Watch for details on the status of Event Citadel, the company’s internal project to unify its event and data platforms, and the rollout of new exchanges like the Sports Illustrated Exchange and International Elements Exchange.
Guidance and momentum toward 2026 revenue targets: Management reiterated a full-year 2026 revenue goal of $200 million, with growth expected to accelerate in the second half. Any changes to this outlook or early signs of progress will be important.
Operating expense trends and cash flow: Monitor whether the company is controlling its rising expenses and moving closer to positive cash flow, as this will affect long-term sustainability.
International expansion efforts: The company has announced new offices and partnerships in Asia and Europe. Updates on international customer wins or regulatory progress could indicate how quickly Datavault can scale outside the U.S.
