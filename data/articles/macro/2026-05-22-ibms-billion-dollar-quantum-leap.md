IBM's Billion-Dollar Quantum Leap

Was the big jump justified? Is IBM a buy today?

Lou Whiteman

May 22, 2026, 10:01 AM

Comments
9.0

IBM (NYSE: IBM) shares gained 11% yesterday on news Big Blue is getting $1B from the U.S. Commerce Department to fund its quantum computing effort.

Let's set aside discussions about the government picking winners and taking stakes in business. That's not a debate we can resolve here. My questions are the most basic ones for investors: Was the big jump justified? Is IBM a buy today?

Based solely on quantum, my answer is no. We are still very early, and this funding is more about experimenting with what is possible and economically feasible, rather than bringing products to market. IBM views quantum as a multibillion-dollar opportunity... by 2040.

But the investment is a reminder that IBM, which has seemingly been left for dead numerous times since the mainframe era, keeps chugging along and will likely be around in 2040. The jury is still out on whether that is true of some of the younger tech companies in the spotlight today.

And IBM's mix of consulting and tech is doing a good job winning AI business these days.

IBM stock is basically flat over the past year, even after Thursday's rally. Priced at 22 times earnings, it isn't outrageous for a tech company. I don't think the chart will resemble that of Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA), but for Fools looking for Big Tech ballast in the portfolio, IBM looks like a worthy candidate.
