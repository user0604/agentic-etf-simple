Grove Collaborative delivered a mixed quarter, reporting increased profitability despite continued declines in sales and customer numbers. Notable Developments
Adjusted EBITDA swung to a $0.3 million profit, as operating expenses fell nearly 22% from last year.
Revenue dropped by 16.8%, with active direct-to-consumer customer counts down 18.5% year over year.
Management raised full-year guidance for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA, anticipating sequential revenue growth in coming quarters.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now forecasts fiscal 2026 revenue between $142.5 million and $152.5 million, up from previous guidance, and expects breakeven to modestly positive adjusted EBITDA for the year. It highlighted improvements in gross margin and repeat order rates as reasons for this more optimistic stance. Investors should closely watch for growth in active customer numbers and improvement in order volumes following recent platform and loyalty program investments.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter featured a significant focus on expense discipline. Operating expenses declined to $20.8 million, benefiting from a reduction in staff and lower advertising costs. These cuts helped to lift gross margin and deliver a second consecutive quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA.

On the demand side, the company saw continued drops in both active customers and total orders through its direct-to-consumer platform. However, net revenue per order increased to $67.79, aided by higher-priced product mix and targeted promotions through the Grove Green Rewards loyalty program.

Product and technology initiatives advanced with the launch of a redesigned mobile app, which now accounts for about half of non-subscription orders. Efforts to expand ingredient restrictions and reduce plastic waste strengthened the sustainability credentials that differentiate Grove Collaborative in the market.

What Grove Collaborative Does

Grove Collaborative is an online retailer specializing in sustainable household, personal care, and wellness products, selling to consumers via its direct-to-consumer digital platform.
