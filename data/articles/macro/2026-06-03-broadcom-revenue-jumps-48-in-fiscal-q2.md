Key Takeaways
Broadcom notched a major win in AI semiconductor and networking sales, fueling record financial results.
Revenue increased 48% year over year.
Management projects continued sharp revenue growth, with AI chip sales expected to expand further next quarter.

Broadcom scored a major business win as AI chip and networking sales drove record financial results. The leading provider of custom semiconductor solutions and enterprise infrastructure software capitalized on surging demand for advanced AI accelerators and networking gear. Reporting on June 3, 2026, for its second fiscal quarter, Broadcom continued to widen its lead in next-generation AI hardware and software integration. Overall, the period marked strong progress in core growth areas while raising questions about customer concentration risks.

Notable Developments
AI semiconductor revenue reached $10.8 billion, up 143%, representing the majority of Semiconductor Solutions sales in the quarter.
Infrastructure Software revenue, supported by recent acquisitions such as VMware, delivered $7.2 billion, climbing 9% year over year.
Research and development spending rose to $3.0 billion, or about 13.5% of quarterly revenue, supporting new custom chip and AI technologies.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects even stronger growth next quarter, forecasting total revenue to rise 84% year over year to about $29.4 billion. The company projects AI semiconductor revenue alone will reach $16.0 billion in Q3 fiscal 2026, with non-GAAP operating income and adjusted EBITDA margins expected to remain high. Investors should pay close attention to AI growth concentration and how much revenue depends on just a few major technology customers.

Quarterly Highlights

The Semiconductor Solutions segment delivered $15.0 billion in sales, a 79% increase supported almost entirely by surging orders for custom AI accelerators and networking. This reflects Broadcom's deep role in supplying hardware for machine learning and large language models to hyperscale cloud clients.

Infrastructure Software, which includes software-focused acquisitions like VMware, contributed $7.2 billion in revenue and maintained single-digit growth. While this segment provided important recurring revenue, its pace trails the fast expansion seen in AI-driven semiconductors.

Broadcom maintained strong cash flow, generating $10.3 billion in free cash flow. It ended the quarter with $19.6 billion in cash and kept its quarterly dividend steady at $0.65 per share, paying out $3.1 billion to shareholders.

What Broadcom Does

Broadcom designs and manufactures high-performance semiconductor chips and enterprise software used by global technology, data center, and industrial customers.
