Research and development spending increased as late-stage clinical testing expanded, especially for pivotal Phase 3 trials in intermediate- and high-risk bladder cancer. General and administrative costs grew as the firm increased its headcount and prepared operationally for a possible future commercial launch.

Meanwhile, a large cash raise in the quarter brings financial stability as Cg Oncology advances clinical programs, but net losses also widened as a result of higher investments and minimal revenue.
