Earnings Preview: Fennec Pharmaceuticals

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Fennec Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ:FENC), which develops treatments to prevent hearing loss from chemotherapy in children and young adults, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings before the U.S. markets open on Thursday, May 14, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Fennec Pharmaceuticals delivered net product sales of $13.8 million, an increase of about 75% from $7.9 million in the same period last year. This marked the fifth consecutive quarter of growth in product sales, driven by expanding adoption of PEDMARK, the company’s flagship therapy for preventing cisplatin-induced hearing loss. The quarter also saw the company enhance its commercial team and invest in outreach to the adolescent and young adult (AYA) cancer patient segment. Cash and cash equivalents rose to $36.8 million by year-end, supported by a successful equity offering and full repayment of outstanding debt. The company reported a net loss for the year, reflecting increased investments in commercial expansion and medical affairs. Analyst estimates for Q4 were not specified, but the company’s sales growth and operational milestones were notable.

Positives
Strong product sales growth: Net product sales rose 75% year over year, showing robust demand for PEDMARK and successful expansion into new patient segments.
Expansion of commercial and medical teams: The company increased its customer-facing staff and medical affairs capabilities, which positions it for further growth in both academic and community oncology settings.
Record patient support program performance: The Fennec HEARS program achieved all-time highs in patient enrollments and conversion rates, helping more patients access therapy and supporting higher product utilization.
Resolution of patent litigation: Settlement with Cipla ensures U.S. market exclusivity for PEDMARK until at least September 2033, reducing legal expenses and supporting long-term revenue potential.
Negatives
Operating loss for the year: Despite higher sales, the company reported a net loss of $9.7 million for 2025, mainly due to increased spending on commercial expansion and higher general and administrative costs.
Rising operating expenses: Selling, marketing, and G&A expenses rose, reflecting the costs of scaling up operations and expanding the team, which weighed on profitability.
Lack of new licensing revenue: Total revenue decreased year over year due to the absence of one-time licensing revenue recognized in 2024 from the Norgine partnership.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Net product sales momentum and AYA segment growth: Investors should track whether the strong sales growth seen in Q4 continues, especially in the adolescent and young adult population, which is a key focus for expansion.
Impact of expanded commercial team: The company recently increased its field force. Watch for early signs that this investment is translating into broader market reach and higher sales, particularly in new and existing accounts.
Cash flow and profitability trends: Management indicated Q1 2026 could see positive cash flow due to timing of receivables. Investors should look for confirmation of improved operating cash flow and any updates on the path to profitability.
Progress on international partnerships and milestones: Monitor updates on the Norgine partnership in Europe, including launches in new countries and potential milestone payments, as well as developments in Japan and other regions.
Status of ongoing clinical and real-world studies: Several investigator-initiated studies in new tumor types and adult populations are underway or pending. Updates on these initiatives could signal future label expansion opportunities and support for broader clinical adoption.
