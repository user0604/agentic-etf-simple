PLMR Company Overview
Palomar provides specialty property and casualty insurance for residential and commercial customers, covering risks such as earthquakes, floods, and specialized liability. The company generates revenue by collecting premiums on these policies and earning fees through partnerships where it issues insurance for other firms. Its proprietary technology platform and granular data analytics allow for precise risk pricing, while deep integrations with retail agents and wholesale brokers create a stable distribution network.

Palomar Expands Specialty Insurance Amid Rising Costs

Palomar sells specialty insurance for things like earthquakes, property, and crops across the U.S. This company stands out because it rapidly grows by targeting insurance markets that big carriers often ignore. Over the last five years, the stock returned 63.8%, underperforming the S&P 500 by 10.1 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue (Non-GAAP): $262.8 million (up 59.4% YoY)
EPS (Non-GAAP): $2.31 (up 23.5% YoY)
Gross Written Premiums: $629.8 million (up 42.5% YoY)

Latest Developments

The company saw major premium growth across all product lines, with Surety & Credit jumping due to the recent Gray Surety acquisition, meaning Palomar is expanding its customer base.
Management kept its full-year profit outlook the same, showing confidence, but rising combined and loss ratios mean investors should watch company costs closely going forward.
