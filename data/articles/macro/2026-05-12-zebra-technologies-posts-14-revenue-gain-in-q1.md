Key Takeaways

Zebra Technologies (NASDAQ:ZBRA) delivered a stronger first quarter as its expanded product lineup and recent acquisitions drove solid sales growth. The global leader in mobile computing, barcode scanners, and automation technologies reported quarterly results on May 12, 2026, covering the three months ended April 4, 2026. Despite increasing costs, the company reported improved profitability and raised its forecasts for the rest of the year.

Overall, the period featured broad-based growth and higher margins, with management signaling confidence that the positive momentum will extend into coming quarters.

Notable Developments
Revenue grew 14.3% year over year, helped by the integration of Elo Touch and organic growth of 4.3% across main business segments.
Operating expenses rose significantly, up 17.1%, driven by higher R&D, selling and integration costs from acquisitions.
The company repurchased $300 million in shares as part of ongoing capital return efforts, with total cash flow from operations steady at $176 million for the period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management lifted its full-year 2026 sales and profit guidance, projecting consolidated revenue growth of 10%–14% and higher adjusted earnings targets. The outlook for the next quarter is also strong, with sales expected to grow 14%–17% from the prior year period. Investors should watch for how margin performance holds up against anticipated inflation in memory component costs.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw strong sales performance, as total revenue climbed and both of Zebra’s main segments posted gains. The Connected Frontline segment, which includes mobile computers and barcode scanners, grew sales by 20.6% to $825 million, while the Asset Visibility & Automation segment rose 7.4% to $670 million.

Profit margins improved at both the gross and adjusted EBITDA level, even as operating expenses jumped due to increased research and development and integration efforts. Non-GAAP net income rose 13%, and adjusted EBITDA margin climbed by nearly one percentage point year over year.

Zebra continued to execute on its capital return program, repurchasing $300 million in shares during the quarter. Free cash flow showed modest improvement and the company ended the quarter with $114 million in cash on hand.

What Zebra Technologies Does

Zebra Technologies provides mobile computing devices, barcode scanners, RFID readers, and software that help companies automate workflows and track assets in industries like retail, healthcare, and logistics.
