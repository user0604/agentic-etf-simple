Calumet Posts 95% Wider Loss in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 8, 2026, 7:27 AM

Key Takeaways
Calumet posted a significant loss due to operational outages and non-cash charges.
Revenue ticked higher from the prior year quarter, but profitability metrics weakened.
Management expects margin conditions to improve in future quarters following facility turnarounds.

Operational incidents and heavy non-cash expenses weighed on Calumet (NASDAQ:CLMT)'s business during the first quarter, leading to a pronounced loss. The company, a producer of specialty hydrocarbon products and renewable fuels, faced downtime at key facilities along with accounting charges that strained its reported results. Announced on May 8, 2026, this release summarizes performance for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The quarter marked a tough patch, but management is signaling better prospects with expansions now coming online.

Notable Developments
Unplanned outages, especially at the Shreveport specialty facility, reduced overall profitability and resulted in a loss of about 750,000 barrels of production for the period.
Operating cash outflow rose to $86.2 million, reflecting higher inventories and receivables as well as weakened operational profitability.
The Montana Renewables unit completed its major turnaround in April and began its expanded MaxSAF 150 operations in early May, which management says will improve future results.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains positive about margin opportunities in both traditional and renewable fuels in the upcoming quarters, citing new regulations and expanded facility capacity. The company signals that second quarter results should benefit from the ramp-up of MaxSAF 150 and normalized specialty operations. Investors should watch upcoming results for evidence of recovery in both earnings and cash flow, as well as any progress on reducing high debt levels.

Quarterly Highlights

The Specialty Products and Solutions segment, which includes lubricating oils and solvents, saw profit margins compressed by both unexpected outages and rising feedstock costs, despite higher overall sales. This segment’s adjusted EBITDA fell to $44.3 million, down from $56.3 million the prior year, and margin shrank from 8.7% to 6.3%.

In the Montana Renewables business, which produces renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), operating earnings benefited from the value of tax credits, lifting segment results even though core operations were down due to the planned plant turnaround. Adjusted EBITDA in this segment swung positive with tax attributes, reaching $10.2 million, compared to $3.3 million a year earlier.

Performance Brands, led by the consumer-facing TruFuel product line, set a sales record; however, its margins declined following the divestment of a high-margin business last year. Segment adjusted EBITDA dropped 20% year over year in the first quarter of 2026.

What Calumet Does

Calumet is a North American producer of specialty hydrocarbon products like lubricants, solvents, and waxes, alongside renewable fuels such as renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel.
