Notable Developments
Revenue declined by $2.7 million compared to the prior year, mainly due to the end of an agreement with a core carrier partner in September 2025.
The company’s gross margin improved to 54%, an increase of 21 percentage points, as it shifted its service mix and improved operational efficiency.
General and administrative expenses rose because of legal costs related to the pending merger with Open World Ltd.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management is prioritizing the integration with its new shipping partner and preparing to launch Premium service offerings in the next quarter. No financial guidance was provided, but the upcoming rollout of new services will be important for stabilizing revenue. Investors should watch for customer adoption of Premium offerings and any impact on future sales.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue contraction followed the September 2025 exit of a major carrier, which led to a transition of most ProActive logistics customers to a new partner. The company’s ability to recover lost sales will depend on the success of its Premium product launch in the next quarter.

Operating expenses fell to $1.7 million as spending was reduced in most areas except legal costs, which increased due to merger activities. Cash at the end of the quarter was $3.5 million, and the company received an additional $2.1 million after the quarter from a loan repayment.

The planned merger with Open World Ltd. would result in Open World shareholders owning 90% of the combined business, pending regulatory and shareholder approval. No details on merger synergies or integration plans were provided this quarter.

What VerifyMe Does

VerifyMe provides predictive analytics and logistics solutions for temperature-sensitive goods, helping companies manage the delivery of perishable products with real-time data and controls.
