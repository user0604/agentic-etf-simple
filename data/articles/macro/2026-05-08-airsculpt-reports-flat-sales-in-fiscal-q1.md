Notable developments
Revenue was unchanged year over year, while same-center sales grew by 1% compared to the prior-year period.
The adjusted EBITDA margin declined to 8.4%, reflecting higher selling, general and administrative expenses, even as net loss narrowed.
The company raised $14.6 million in new equity and paid down $11.4 million in debt, more than doubling its cash balance to $16.7 million at quarter-end.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year fiscal 2026 outlook for revenue between $151 million and $157 million and adjusted EBITDA of $15 million to $17 million. It expects momentum to build as the year progresses, especially with new procedures and enhanced marketing. The most important area for investors to watch is whether increased marketing and innovation translate into more rapid case growth and margin recovery in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s top-line performance was stable, with total revenue unchanged compared to the same period last year, though same-center sales—which measure growth among centers open for at least one year—rose by 1%. Case volumes also saw minimal movement, with total cases up just 0.2% and average revenue per case remaining flat.

Profitability showed a mixed picture: adjusted EBITDA, which is a profit measure that excludes non-cash and non-recurring costs, decreased from $3.8 million to $3.3 million year over year, resulting in a lower adjusted EBITDA margin. Net loss narrowed to $2.4 million, helped by improved operating cash flow of $5.3 million this quarter versus $0.9 million a year earlier.

The balance sheet strengthened as the company raised $14.6 million through an at-the-market equity offering and paid down $11.4 million in debt. The total number of centers declined by one to 31, indicating a focus on productivity over expansion. No new locations opened during the period.

What AirSculpt Technologies does

The company specializes in minimally invasive body contouring procedures using its patented AirSculpt method, which removes fat without needles, scalpels, or stitches, and targets patients seeking alternatives to traditional liposuction.
