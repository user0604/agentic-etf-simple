Earnings Preview: Tilly's

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Tilly's (NYSE:TLYS), which operates mall-based stores and an online shop selling casual apparel and accessories for teens and young adults, is expected to report its fiscal first-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, June 3, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The fourth quarter of fiscal 2025 marked a significant turnaround for Tilly's. The company reported total net sales of $155.1 million, up 5.3% year over year despite having 17 fewer stores. Comparable net sales grew 10.1%, with both physical stores and e-commerce contributing to the gains. Gross margin improved to 33.2% of net sales, a notable increase from 26% in the prior year. Selling, general and administrative (SG&A) expenses fell to 31.5% of net sales, down from 35.6%. Tilly’s delivered operating income of $2.6 million and net income of $2.9 million ($0.10 per diluted share), swinging from a loss the previous year. These results beat analyst estimates, as confirmed by positive post-earnings coverage and the company’s own guidance.

Positives
Return to profitability in Q4: Tilly’s posted its first profitable fourth quarter since fiscal 2021, reflecting improved execution and cost control.
Strong comparable sales growth: Comparable net sales rose 10.1%, driven by gains in both physical stores and e-commerce, showing broad-based demand recovery.
Significant margin improvement: Gross margin increased by 720 basis points, supported by better inventory management and reduced markdowns.
Effective cost reductions: SG&A expenses fell both in dollar terms and as a percentage of sales, helping drive the return to profitability.
Negatives
Continued annual net loss: Despite the strong quarter, Tilly’s reported a net loss of $17.5 million for the full year, highlighting that the turnaround is still in progress.
Reduced store footprint: The company ended the year with 223 stores, down from 240, which could limit sales growth if not offset by higher productivity.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales momentum and guidance versus consensus: Management guided for a 16% to 22% increase in comparable net sales, well above the analyst consensus. Investors should watch if reported results meet or exceed the high end of this range.
Gross margin improvements: The company expects product margin gains of 310 to 330 basis points in the first quarter. Progress here will signal whether inventory and pricing strategies remain effective.
Store openings and closures: Tilly’s plans to open four to six new stores in fiscal 2026 and close a handful in Q1. The pace and economics of these changes will be important for long-term growth.
Progress on operational initiatives: The rollout of an AI-driven merchandise allocation tool and the planned launch of RFID inventory tracking later in the year are pending. Early updates on these projects could indicate future efficiency gains.
Path to profitability for the full year: Management has stated that an 8% to 9% annual comparable sales increase could return the company to annual profitability. Investors should look for commentary on whether Q1 results keep Tilly’s on track for this goal.
