Key Takeaways
Endava lost ground as revenue and profitability declined more than expected. Adjusted diluted EPS fell sharply compared to the prior year. Management sees revenue declines moderating next quarter, but profitability remains thin.

Technology services provider Endava (NYSE:DAVA) faced a difficult quarter with revenue and profit falling notably short of analyst expectations. The company specializes in digital transformation and modern software development, helping clients across payments, banking, and other sectors address complex technology needs. In results posted on May 21, 2026 for its fiscal third quarter, it outlined weaker operational results due to ongoing caution from clients and a steep one-time goodwill impairment. Overall, the quarter highlighted continued operational and demand pressures that have yet to be offset by expansion in new services and innovation.

Notable developments
Revenue fell 8.4% year over year to £178.5 million as clients delayed project launches and cut technology spending.
The company recognized a non-cash goodwill impairment of £364.6 million, and an additional £23.2 million deferred tax asset write-down, which drove a large net loss.
AI-driven business lines increased to 15% of total revenue, up from 5% the prior year, showing rapid growth in this area despite overall declines.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects revenue for the next quarter to continue declining year over year, but at a slower rate, with profitability remaining subdued. The key factor for investors will be whether Endava’s investments in artificial intelligence and payments technology can start to drive a broader turnaround in overall financial performance.

Quarterly highlights

Endava’s topline decline was driven by both macro headwinds and slower client technology spending. The number of large clients generating over £1 million in annual revenue slipped to 129 from 136, and revenue concentration among the largest ten clients increased to 40%.

The company recorded a large one-time goodwill write-down reflecting lower expected future profitability from past acquisitions, and a challenging outlook for legacy operations. Operating margin contracted deeply into negative territory due to this and other charges, while cash flow from operations also swung negative for the quarter.

Offsetting some of these pressures, Endava continues to grow its AI-focused revenue rapidly, with payments sector revenue rising as a share of the total. High-profile wins with partners such as Mastercard suggest traction, but these have not yet been enough to counter broader softness in demand.

What Endava Plc does

Endava Plc provides technology consulting and software development services, helping businesses launch digital solutions and modernize their IT, with a growing focus on artificial intelligence and payments.
