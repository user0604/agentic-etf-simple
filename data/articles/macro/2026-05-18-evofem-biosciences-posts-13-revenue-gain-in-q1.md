Notable Developments
Revenue ticked up by about 13% from the year-ago period, mainly due to higher pricing and SOLOSEC ex-factory sales.
Operating expenses came down by nearly 10%, but the company still posted a sizable operating loss of $5.5 million for the quarter.
Evofem established a new African distribution deal for SOLOSEC and made progress with regulatory filings in the UAE, as it accelerated global expansion efforts.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated that international market expansion and new partnerships are keys to unlocking non-dilutive funding this year. However, no quantitative guidance was provided for 2026. The main issue to watch is whether new ex-U.S. licensing agreements and regulatory milestones can translate into meaningful revenue and help offset still-severe cash constraints.

Quarterly Highlights

The top-line increase came amid a notable shift in product mix, with higher SOLOSEC sales helping to compensate for weaker PHEXX demand after prior wholesale restocking.

While total operating expenses decreased compared to the prior year, Evofem continued to spend more than it earned, resulting in another quarter of negative net income. Cost management initiatives remain vital, especially as the company aims for a 55-60% reduction in manufacturing costs by 2027.

On the international front, new distribution with Clovis Davis Pharmaceuticals in Sub-Saharan Africa and regulatory advances in the UAE for both PHEXX and SOLOSEC signal more diversified revenue streams could be on the horizon. Management emphasized the goal of generating non-dilutive capital from these partnerships, though the timeline and magnitude remain uncertain.
