The company, a provider of television, digital, and cross-platform analytics to media companies and advertisers, reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026. Notable Developments
Cross-platform solutions revenue jumped 30%, offsetting a 4.7% decline in legacy syndicated audience products, but the latter still make up over 70% of total revenue.
Profitability slipped, with adjusted EBITDA margin dropping to 5.9% from 8.6% a year ago, reflecting costs outpacing flat revenue.
The company paid down $5 million of its senior term loan and ended the quarter with $25.1 million in cash and no revolver borrowings, signaling a conservative capital approach.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its focus on expanding cross-platform measurement and local TV offerings, while indicating further details on full-year financial guidance will follow before the end of May. The most important metric to watch is whether the company’s growth in new digital segments can build enough scale to offset the rapid decline in traditional syndicated audience lines, which remain the bulk of revenue.

Quarterly Highlights

Cross-platform products, which measure audiences across television, mobile, and digital platforms, continued to outpace the rest of the business—posting 30% revenue growth year over year. This strength came from both new client wins and expanded relationships in data-driven digital advertising and content analytics.

The legacy syndicated audience offerings, which track viewership and digital traffic for traditional media clients, fell 4.7% in the period. This segment now accounts for 71% of total revenue, spotlighting the challenge for comScore in pivoting its revenue base without losing scale.

While operating cash flow improved to $12.5 million, overall expenses rose 2.4%, mainly due to higher systems and professional fees. The company's net loss widened, and profitability, measured by adjusted EBITDA, slipped as cost increases outpaced gains in newer business lines.

What comScore Does

comScore provides media measurement and analytics to help advertisers and media companies understand consumer audiences across television, digital, and cross-platform content.
