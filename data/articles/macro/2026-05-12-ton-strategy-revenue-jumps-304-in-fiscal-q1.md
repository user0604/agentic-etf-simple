Ton Strategy Revenue Jumps 304% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

Key Takeaways
Ton Strategy achieved a business win with strong expansion of staking activities and growth in social commerce. EPS loss narrowed and revenue climbed over 300% year over year. Management points to higher staking yields and TON value rebound as key factors to watch in the coming quarter.

Ton Strategy (NASDAQ:TONX) expanded its digital asset operations and grew its legacy social commerce segment, despite volatile digital asset prices. The leading public company dedicated to Toncoin accumulation and staking reported first quarter 2026 results on May 12, 2026, covering the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter saw significant growth in recurring revenue but continued to face large mark-to-market losses on digital assets.

Notable developments
Staking activities produced $3.0 million in revenue, with the company holding 221.9 million Toncoin and staking almost all of it by March 31, 2026.
The MARKET.live segment posted revenue of $1.7 million, up 204% over the prior year, driven by growth in TikTok Shop partnerships and higher livestream shopping demand.
Ton Strategy reported an operating loss of $3.9 million, while its net loss was dominated by an $87.9 million mark-to-market loss on Toncoin holdings.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlights a recent fourfold rise in staking yield after TON network upgrades in April, which it expects will drive higher recurring revenue in the next quarter. The most important variable for investors remains the price of Toncoin, as mark-to-market fluctuations have a large impact on net income. Ongoing attention to expense management, and further diversification of revenue sources, are priorities under new CEO Kevin Wilson.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s core digital asset strategy saw staking revenue continue at scale, with 221.9 million Toncoin held by period-end and 221.2 million units staked. Staking rewards totaled 2.2 million TON for the quarter, nearly matching the full-year reward level seen in 2025 after staking was established.

Social commerce through the MARKET.live video commerce and livestream platform continued to diversify revenue. Segment revenue more than tripled from the prior year to $1.7 million, as new partnerships, especially with TikTok Shop, became a primary performance driver.

Despite strong revenue growth, operating costs and mark-to-market fair value accounting for digital asset holdings meant the company posted an overall net loss. However, as of early May, TON asset values had rebounded, indicating that the Q1 mark-to-market loss was largely temporary.

What Ton Strategy does

Ton Strategy operates as a digital asset treasury company, focusing on acquiring and staking Toncoin and developing digital-commerce businesses such as MARKET.live and Go Fund Yourself.
