The video and online entertainment platform Bilibili (NASDAQ:BILI) returned to profitability as advertising revenue surged in the most recent period. The company operates an ecosystem of video content, mobile games, and value-added services geared toward young digital audiences in China. Its latest quarterly update, released on May 19, 2026, covers the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed substantial improvement in advertising and profitability but revealed slower growth in value-added services and ongoing pressure in the mobile games segment.

Notable Developments
Advertising revenue accelerated, up 30% compared to the prior year, outpacing growth in other segments.
Profitability improved, with adjusted net profit growing 62% year over year alongside a margin increase to 7.8%.
The mobile games business saw a 12% revenue decline, reflecting ongoing challenges with legacy titles.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue numerical guidance for the next quarter or the rest of fiscal 2026. Executives emphasized the growing role of artificial intelligence in driving engagement and advertising efficiency, sharing confidence in continued progress. Investors should watch for developments in the mobile games portfolio and the ability to sustain advertising growth as user and engagement numbers expand.

Quarterly Highlights

Advertising stood out this quarter, with revenue rising to RMB2.59 billion, up nearly 30% year over year, attributed to improved product offerings and stronger ad efficiency. This segment has increasingly become a key driver of top-line growth as the company leverages AI tools for better targeting and engagement.

Profitability sharply improved. Adjusted net profit reached RMB585.4 million, a 62% increase from the same period last year. This gain came as total operating expenses rose just 3%—well below the pace of revenue growth—highlighting ongoing cost discipline even as research and development spending increased 9% with a focus on AI initiatives.

The mobile games segment continued to contract, with revenue falling 12%, reflecting the expected maturity of flagship titles and the lack of major new launches. Value-added services, mostly driven by paid memberships and premium features, showed weaker growth than past periods, suggesting potential market saturation or subscription fatigue.

Bilibili operates a digital entertainment and video community platform focused on young users in China, offering streaming video, mobile games, and premium memberships.
