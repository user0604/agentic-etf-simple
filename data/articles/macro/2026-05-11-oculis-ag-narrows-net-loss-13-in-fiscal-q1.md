Oculis AG Narrows Net Loss 13% in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Oculis AG reported steady progress in its late-stage pipeline but continued to operate at a net loss.
Net loss narrowed 13.1% year over year, while revenue fell nearly 27%.
Management highlighted strong cash resources and expects pivotal trial data in the coming quarter.

Oculis AG (NASDAQ:OCS) continued to advance its late-stage drug candidates while managing losses as it remains focused on clinical development. The biopharmaceutical company specializes in topical therapies for ophthalmic diseases, with several product candidates in late-stage trials. In its earnings release for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (reported on May 11, 2026), management pointed to a stable cash position and the approaching topline clinical trial results as major milestones. Overall, the quarter reflected a disciplined approach to R&D investment, steady financial management, and key pipeline progress.

Notable developments
Net loss narrowed to CHF 28.9 million from CHF 33.2 million a year earlier, reflecting reduced research spending as Phase 3 trials near completion.
Cash and cash equivalents ended the quarter at CHF 64.6 million, up 7.9% year over year, as share sales boosted liquidity.
Operating expenses increased by 7.9% to CHF 21.9 million, largely driven by higher general and administrative costs, notably share-based compensation.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed that the company’s available cash will cover financial obligations for at least the next 12 months. The most important short-term milestone is the topline data readout from the pivotal Phase 3 OCS-01 trial for diabetic macular edema, expected in Q2 2026. Investors should closely watch for updates on this clinical milestone, as it could determine future regulatory and commercial pathways.

Quarterly highlights

Pivotal clinical trials progressed on schedule, with OCS-01 (an eye drop for diabetic macular edema) nearing its critical data readout and Licaminlimab (for dry eye disease) remaining in late-stage development. Management noted a decrease in external research costs as these trials advance toward conclusion, with research and development spending down compared to the prior year.

The company’s cash position strengthened thanks to equity sales under its at-the-market program, adding CHF 22.4 million in gross proceeds during the quarter. This enabled Oculis AG to maintain a stable liquidity base, covering both ongoing operations and further development work.

While research and development expenses edged down, general and administrative expenses rose significantly year over year, with personnel and share-based compensation now accounting for a larger portion of overall spending.

What Oculis AG does

Oculis AG is a biopharmaceutical company developing topical treatments for major eye diseases, with a focus on late-stage clinical candidates targeting diabetic macular edema and dry eye disease.
