Tandy Leather Factory (NASDAQ:TLF) ended the quarter with higher sales and a stronger gross margin, though rising costs limited much of that operational upside. The specialty retailer of leather, tools, and artisan supplies reported results for its fiscal first quarter on May 11, 2026, covering the three months that ended March 31. Overall, the period reflected stable progress but little on future strategy or digital growth.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed to $19.7 million, marking a 3.2% increase over the same period a year ago. Gross profit and margins both improved as well.
Gross margin expanded by 4.7 percentage points to 61.0%, with management citing pricing changes as the main driver of this gain.
Cash reserves dropped to $10.1 million, with the company distributing a special $0.75 per share dividend and covering relocation and tax expenses connected to last year's asset sale.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue financial guidance or an outlook for the coming quarters. The company highlighted its ability to pay a substantial dividend but offered no detail on growth initiatives, digital commerce, or international plans. Investors should monitor whether operational improvements in margin can be sustained as costs and cash outflows continue to rise.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue increased modestly from the year-ago quarter, with improvements in both gross profit and gross margin reflecting successful pricing strategies. However, higher operating costs, including investments in operations, technology, and new rental expenses for the main headquarters and flagship store, partially offset these gains.

Operating income rose slightly as stronger sales and improved margins managed to outrun the 11.2% climb in operating expenses. Bottom-line net income, however, declined sharply versus last year, mainly due to an outsized gain in 2025 from a non-recurring asset sale.

Cash and cash equivalents fell by over one-third, driven by a special dividend of $0.75 per share and ongoing tax and relocation payments linked to last year's sale. The company provided no update on its 100-store footprint, digital initiatives, or progress in e-commerce, which remains a key area to watch given retail industry changes.

What Tandy Leather Factory Does

Tandy Leather Factory makes and sells leather, tools, craft supplies, and DIY kits through a network of company-operated retail stores and its online channels.
