Key Takeaways
Baidu marked a business milestone as AI-powered operations became its main revenue driver.
Earnings per share decreased approximately 59%, while total revenue was roughly flat compared to the previous year.
Management gave no explicit outlook for upcoming quarters, noting a pivotal phase in its business transformation.

Baidu (NASDAQ:BIDU) reached a turning point as its AI-powered operations became the main source of revenue, overtaking older business segments. The leading Chinese internet and technology company now generates most of its general business revenue from artificial intelligence, signaling a significant shift in its business model. Reporting results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (released May 18, 2026), Baidu’s overall financial growth was sluggish, with progress in AI offset by continued weakness in legacy online marketing and muted revenue growth.

Notable Developments
AI-powered operations generated 52% of Baidu General Business revenue, climbing 49% year over year and overtaking legacy segments for the first time.
Revenue from legacy online marketing declined by 22%, now representing less than half of the company's general business revenue.
The Apollo Go robotaxi unit delivered 3.2 million fully driverless rides in the quarter, up 120% from the prior year, and expanded to 27 global cities.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

The company did not provide formal financial guidance for the next quarter. Management highlighted ongoing investment in cloud and advanced AI technology, but cautioned that the shift away from legacy online marketing continues to weigh on growth and margins. Investors will be watching for improved top-line expansion and updates on the profitability of new AI-led segments, especially Apollo Go.

Quarterly Highlights

AI-driven business lines took the lead this period, with AI Cloud Infrastructure growing at 79% year over year. This marks a pivotal structural change, as AI Cloud activity and subscription-based GPU Cloud services led growth despite flat performance in some AI application areas.

Legacy online marketing, long the company’s historical engine, continued to contract, now accounting for only 48% of Baidu General Business revenue. This ongoing decline highlights continued pressure from competition, regulation, and shifting advertiser demand.

Autonomous driving made notable progress operationally, as Apollo Go hit new ride milestones and expanded internationally. However, the company gave limited detail on financial outcomes for these emerging business lines, leaving the sustainability of this rapid expansion an open question.

What Baidu Does

Baidu specializes in internet search, online marketing, cloud services, artificial intelligence, and autonomous driving, serving consumers and businesses in China and globally.
