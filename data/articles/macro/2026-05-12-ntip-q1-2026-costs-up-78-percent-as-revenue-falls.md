Quarterly Highlights

Legal expenses outpaced all other costs in the period, with litigation and professional fees hitting $775,000 compared to $121,000 in the prior year. These costs now make up over half of total operating spending, squeezing profit margins during periods with no new settlements or licensing deals.

The company booked a one-time investment gain of $1.05 million after the revaluation of its ILiAD Biotechnologies stake, as ILiAD closed a large financing. This boost partially offset losses from marketable securities, but such gains are not repeatable core income sources.

Major legal milestones included a federal appeals court overturning a non-infringement finding in a Google/YouTube patent case, potentially opening the door for future wins. Additionally, the U.S. patent board rejected Samsung’s attempts to invalidate key Internet of Things-related patents, supporting the company's litigation path. No new licenses or settlements closed during the quarter.
