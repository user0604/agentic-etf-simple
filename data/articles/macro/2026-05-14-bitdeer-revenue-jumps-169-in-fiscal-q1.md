Key Takeaways
Bitdeer Technologies Group posted major revenue growth but still reported a substantial net loss.
Revenue almost tripled year over year as mining operations expanded.
Management is focused on growing AI infrastructure, with profitability still dependent on new contracts and continued cost controls.

Bitcoin miner and infrastructure operator Bitdeer Technologies Group (NASDAQ:BTDR) reported soaring revenue in the latest period, but continued to post a large net loss driven by higher costs and cryptocurrency market volatility. The company, a global provider of cryptocurrency mining, data center management, and AI infrastructure, shared results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected rapid operational growth that has yet to translate into consistent profitability.

Notable Developments
Total revenue jumped 169.4% as self-mining grew and new revenue streams like AI Cloud gained traction.
Net loss widened to $159.5 million, mainly as costs outpaced revenue and with higher interest expense due to heavier borrowing.
Cash and restricted cash climbed to $297.7 million, while total company borrowing nearly doubled to $1.9 billion over the year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expressed optimism about signing new tenants for large AI datacenter projects and highlighted ongoing expansion in AI Cloud services. However, there was no specific guidance on upcoming revenue or profitability. The most important area for investors to watch is execution on new colocation agreements and the pace at which AI business lines scale up to offset the volatile crypto core.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue soared almost threefold due to a 551% increase in average self-mining hashrate, reflecting both scale and improved efficiency in proprietary mining rigs. The self-mining operation produced 2,033 bitcoins, up from 350 a year ago, while AI Cloud revenue more than doubled, showing early signs of diversification beyond crypto mining.

Despite top-line growth, profitability was under pressure. The gross margin deteriorated further into negative territory as cost of revenue outpaced sales, with losses on digital asset values and a sharp rise in interest expense from new debt. Management noted efficiency improvements, including a leap in fleet mining efficiency from 29.0 joules per terahash to 16.4, but this was not enough to counter higher depreciation, staffing, and power costs.

The company pressed forward in expanding its global data center footprint, particularly in Norway and the US, targeting both bitcoin mining and new AI infrastructure markets. The Tydal facility in Norway, for example, is billed to become that country’s largest AI data center, but success is tied to the company landing significant tenant contracts in the coming quarters.

What Bitdeer Technologies Group Does

Bitdeer Technologies Group operates cryptocurrency mining, manages data centers, and develops AI cloud infrastructure across multiple regions worldwide.
