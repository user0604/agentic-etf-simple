Lionsgate Studios Posts 164% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 21, 2026, 4:18 PM

Key Takeaways
Lionsgate Studios posted a strong quarter, driven by hit films and expanding library revenue.
Adjusted earnings per share rose by more than 2.5 times year over year.
Management anticipates larger TV deliveries and ongoing momentum in branded franchises for the next year.

Movie studio Lionsgate Studios (NYSE:LION) delivered notable gains this quarter, powered by blockbuster film performance and continuing strength from its content library. The diversified independent entertainment company produces and distributes films and television, with a vast asset base of over 20,000 titles. In its earnings release dated May 21, 2026, for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Lionsgate posted higher profits and margin expansion, though it faced pressure in the television business due to the timing of episodic deliveries. Overall, the quarter showed clear progress on key priorities and a rebound from challenges earlier in the year.

Notable Developments
The Motion Picture segment grew revenue 23% and segment profit 39% with strong results from films like The Housemaid.
Trailing 12-month library revenue stayed above $1 billion for a third consecutive quarter, up 5% year over year.
Television Production revenue declined from the prior year quarter due to the timing of episodic deliveries, and management expects to double scripted deliveries in fiscal 2027 relative to fiscal 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled optimism, pointing to a larger pipeline of branded franchises and a plan to double scripted TV deliveries for fiscal 2027. No formal numerical outlook was issued, but the ability to deliver more episodic TV and sustain strong film releases will be critical. Investors should watch for the pace of new franchise launches and whether costs are kept in line as the slate expands.

Quarterly Highlights

Lionsgate's Motion Picture segment reached its best quarterly profit in years, led by The Housemaid, which grossed nearly $400 million globally and achieved record revenue across digital and streaming distribution channels. Revenue for the segment climbed to $651.9 million, while profit increased to $187.1 million, reflecting both hit-driven theatrical performance and strong sales of older titles.

The company's film and television library generated $1 billion in revenue over the past year, marking the third straight quarter at or above this level. This library, which spans more than 20,000 titles, provides stable, recurring income as Lionsgate licenses content across new platforms like free ad-supported streaming.

In Television Production, revenue dropped to $254.6 million,the  and profit declined to $30.5 million, mainly due to timing of series deliveries. Management said it expects to double the scripted TV episodes released in the next fiscal year, which would help restore growth in this segment.
