Notable developments
Profit returned as net income rose to $2.7 million from a net loss of $382,000 in the prior year quarter.
Average assets under management jumped to $1.6 billion, boosted by strong performance in gold, defense, and travel strategies.
The monthly dividend was maintained at $0.0075 per share, and share repurchases slowed modestly, down 1% from a year ago.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide specific financial guidance for upcoming quarters but remains confident in its positioning within gold, defense, and shipping investment themes. The sustainability of earnings driven mostly by investment gains, rather than core operating revenue, will be a key issue to monitor alongside efforts to grow ETF assets to industry-relevant scale. Investors should watch for any shifts in “other income” and whether recent asset growth can be maintained in changing markets.

Quarterly highlights

Core business operations improved, with revenue up 31.4% from the prior year as the firm benefited from rising demand for specialized investment strategies in gold, natural resources, and defense. The company’s WAR ETF focused on defense and artificial intelligence nearly doubled assets to $20 million, while gold funds like GOAU, USERX, and UNWPX also attracted inflows.

Most of the net income for the quarter came from “other income,” which includes investment and currency gains that can fluctuate significantly between periods. Operating margin on recurring management fees remains slim, with only $88,000 in operating income for the quarter against $2.8 million in revenue.

Shareholder returns continue to be a focus, as reflected by ongoing monthly dividends and a 9.96% trailing shareholder yield, though buyback activity declined compared to last year. The company also maintained a strong liquidity position with $24.6 million in cash and no debt.

What U.S. Global Investors does

U.S. Global Investors is an investment management firm specializing in mutual funds and ETFs focused on thematic sectors such as gold, natural resources, defense, airlines, and shipping.
