Cabaletta Bio Posts 45% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 7:16 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Cabaletta Bio posted a wider net loss as research spending ramped, but advanced clinical and manufacturing progress.
Earnings per share loss narrowed to $0.39 from $0.71 year over year.
Management expects cash reserves, boosted by recent fundraising, to support operations into mid-2027 as pivotal trials progress.

Cabaletta Bio (NASDAQ:CABA) continued its push to advance its lead therapy, resulting in a larger net loss as investment in research increased. The clinical-stage biotech, specializing in engineered cell therapies for autoimmune diseases, focused on driving its principal candidate, rese-cel, through key trials and ramping up manufacturing partnerships. The company released first quarter results on May 14, 2026, detailing the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter saw foundational clinical achievements offset by higher costs and the absence of revenue.

Notable developments
Clinical trial progress included six-to-nine month data in pemphigus vulgaris showing half of patients achieved drug-free clinical responses at the lowest tested dose.
Research and development spending jumped nearly 29%, reflecting expanded clinical activity and new manufacturing partnerships to support potential launch.
The company raised $150 million in new funding after the quarter, bringing cash runway guidance to mid-2027 as pivotal myositis trial enrollment proceeds.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects that combined cash on hand and recent fundraising will fund operations into mid-2027, supporting an expanded clinical program and scaling up manufacturing. Investors should watch for major clinical trial readouts later in 2026 and progress toward the first regulatory submission projected for 2027.
