4. Berkshire Hathaway’s Portfolio Shakeup

Berkshire Hathaway (NYSE: BRKB) revealed significant changes to its stock portfolio on Friday, marking the first full quarter under new CEO Greg Abel. According to a regulatory filing, the conglomerate acquired a $2.6 billion stake in Delta Air Lines (NYSE: DAL), returning to a stock it previously exited in 2020. Berkshire also added a $55 million stake in Macy’s (NYSE: M) and bought nearly 40 million additional shares of Alphabet (NASDAQ: GOOG).

Chevron (NYSE:CVX) stake cut by $2.4 billion: Abel also reduced Berkshire’s Bank of America (NYSE: BAC) holding by $3.4 billion. And he exited UnitedHealth (NYSE: UNH) – which subsequently fell around 4% in pre-market trading – along with Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN), Domino’s Pizza (NASDAQ: DPZ), Mastercard (NYSE: MA), and Visa (NYSE: V), which were all relatively small holdings.
Maintaining a concentrated approach to investing: Abel, who took over in January, noted at Berkshire's recent annual meeting that he and Warren Buffett still collaborate on stock purchases – and he left Berkshire’s big stake in Apple (NASDAQ: AAPL) unchanged.
