Key Takeaways
Shimmick reported strong gross margin progress despite a sharp revenue decline and persistent net losses.
Gross margin rose to 12% while revenue fell 28% year over year.
Management reaffirmed full-year guidance, citing a record backlog and strong new project awards.

Shimmick (NASDAQ:SHIM) made meaningful gains in project margin and backlog but continued to face lower revenue and losses in its most recent quarter. The national infrastructure contractor, specializing in water, electrical, and transportation projects, released its first-quarter 2026 results on May 14, 2026, covering the three months ended April 3. Overall, the quarter highlighted early progress on operating goals, with improving profitability offset by top-line softness and continued, though narrowing, net losses.

Notable developments
Gross margin expanded to $10.9 million, up from $4.7 million, driven by a shift to higher-margin core projects and improved mix.
Backlog rose to $944 million at quarter-end, a record since 2024, reflecting robust demand and a 2.6x book-to-burn ratio.
Revenue declined sharply year over year to $88.0 million, mainly due to near-complete wind-down of legacy non-core projects.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook for revenue of $550 million–$600 million and adjusted EBITDA of $15 million–$30 million, pointing to a seasonally stronger second half and high probability new awards. The key indicator for investors to watch is the pace at which pending project awards convert into booked backlog and recognized revenue, as this underpins the guidance for 2026.

Quarterly highlights

Gross margin as a percentage of revenue rose substantially to 12%, up 7 percentage points from the same quarter last year, reflecting a nearly complete transition to core water, electrical, and infrastructure projects. The mix improvement also drove a positive adjusted EBITDA of $2.6 million, marking the third straight quarter of positive operating earnings, though the company still reported a net loss.

Shimmick’s backlog reached $944 million, up about 20% from $793 million at the start of the year, indicating a strong future pipeline. The book-to-burn ratio, which compares new project awards to work completed, hit a record 2.6x, greatly improving revenue visibility for subsequent quarters.

The company’s core Shimmick Projects segment accounted for almost all revenue, while legacy Non-Core Projects—now less than 3% of backlog—dropped to minimal levels. Management emphasized that nearly all new work targets water infrastructure, climate resilience, energy transition, and electrical contracting, especially in California and Texas.

What Shimmick does

Shimmick is a U.S.-focused infrastructure contractor specializing in water, transportation, and electrical projects for public and private clients.
