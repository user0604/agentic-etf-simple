Airship Ai (NASDAQ:AISP) secured important new contracts but continued to report operating losses in the first quarter. The provider of edge artificial intelligence (AI) and real-time data processing solutions saw recurring services expand and gross margin improve, while also making progress in developing its sales pipeline. The company released its fiscal first-quarter results on May 11, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter highlighted further market expansion but underscored the challenge of sustained profitability despite stronger operating cash flow.

Notable developments
Gross margin was 50.0% in the quarter, up 42% year over year, due in part to increased solution sales with more in-house software and hardware offerings.
Recurring post contract support revenue accounted for 38% of total sales in the quarter, rising sharply while product revenue declined year over year.
Validated sales pipeline grew to $165.3 million, with recent federal awards including contracts for security events and border projects.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management signaled confidence about the rest of fiscal 2026, referencing continued margin improvement and expected increases in federal procurement activity as new budgets are finalized. Cash flow positivity before the end of the year remains a stated goal. Investors should watch the pace at which Airship Ai converts its sizable pipeline, particularly as it relies on short-term contracts and government funding approvals.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter marked a major shift in revenue mix as post contract support nearly doubled, offsetting a 12% reduction in product revenue. This signaled traction for Airship Ai’s efforts to expand recurring software and service sales as a stabilizing factor in future results.

Gross margin rose substantially, driven by a growing share of proprietary hardware and bundled software within its product suite. This margin improvement helped narrow operating losses, but total operating expenses increased 20% year over year, reflecting higher research, development, and selling costs.

Recent contract wins included a $1.9 million award for a national security event with the Department of Homeland Security and a $3.0 million commercial contract. Management expanded its team and introduced new features and packaged solutions for its Outpost AI and Ask Airship platforms, aiming to boost adoption in both government and commercial markets.

What Airship Ai does

Airship Ai delivers edge AI-based solutions and software platforms that help organizations process, manage, and analyze real-time data, serving enterprises and government agencies.
