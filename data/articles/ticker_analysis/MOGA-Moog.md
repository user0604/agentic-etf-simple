MOGA Company Overview
Moog designs and manufactures precision motion and fluid control systems for the aerospace, defense, and industrial markets. The company generates revenue by providing flight controls for aircraft, propulsion components for spacecraft, and specialized machinery for medical and energy applications. Customers rely on these highly engineered systems for critical operations where failure is not an option, creating long-term relationships through significant technical integration and specialized aftermarket maintenance services across global fleets.
