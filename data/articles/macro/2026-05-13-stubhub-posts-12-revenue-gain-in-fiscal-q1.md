Key Takeaways
StubHub delivered strong growth in revenue and profitability in the first quarter, returning to profitability after a year-ago loss.
Adjusted EBITDA rose 50%, with revenue up 12% year over year.
Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 outlook and highlighted continued investment in technology and advertising initiatives.

StubHub (NYSE:STUB) delivered a return to profitability as its online ticketing marketplace saw both revenue and profits improve from the prior year. The company, which connects buyers and sellers of tickets for live events around the world, announced its first-quarter results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed accelerating cash generation and good progress on long-term growth plans.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased to $446.0 million, up 12% from a year prior, outpacing the 7% rise in gross merchandise sales.
Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 16% from 12%, reflecting improved operating efficiency and lower relative marketing spend.
The company reduced net leverage to 4.0 times adjusted EBITDA and made a further $100 million debt payment after quarter end.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its guidance for fiscal 2026, aiming for gross merchandise sales between $9.9 billion and $10.1 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $400 million to $420 million. Leaders highlighted ongoing investment in technology, open distribution, and advertising as key to long-term growth. The most important area for investors to watch is StubHub’s ability to scale new revenue streams, especially in advertising and open distribution, over the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s marketplace business saw revenue outpace volume growth, with the take rate (the percentage of each ticket value kept as revenue) reaching 20%, up from 18.9% in the prior year.

Operating efficiency improved as adjusted sales and marketing expenses grew only 2%, while as a share of revenue these costs fell from 55% to 50%. General and administrative expenses showed a bigger rise due to increased stock-based compensation and legal costs, but adjusted figures strip out most one-time items.

StubHub’s ongoing investments in technology, such as artificial intelligence tools and platform modernization, were highlighted as supporting both current progress and new product rollouts. Management also underlined global reach, with services in more than 200 countries and territories and acceptance of over 45 currencies.

What StubHub Does

StubHub operates an online marketplace that enables individuals and organizations to buy and sell tickets for live events around the world.
