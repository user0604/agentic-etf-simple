Earnings Preview: ePlus

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

ePlus (NASDAQ:PLUS), a technology solutions provider specializing in security, cloud, networking, and artificial intelligence (AI) services for businesses, is expected to report its earnings for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, ePlus delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations. Net sales rose 24.6% year over year to $614.8 million, while adjusted earnings per share (EPS) from continuing operations reached $1.45. This performance beat consensus estimates for both EPS and revenue. The company saw particular strength in its product segment, with sales driven by demand for AI-enabled infrastructure, cloud, networking, and security solutions. Managed services revenue increased, but professional services revenue declined due to delayed projects in the retail sector. Gross profit and adjusted EBITDA also grew at a double-digit pace, reflecting operating leverage and disciplined cost management. The company’s board approved a quarterly dividend of $0.25 per share, and over 200,000 shares were repurchased during the quarter.

Positives
Strong sales growth across key technology areas: Net sales and product revenue both grew by double digits, led by demand for AI, cloud, networking, and security solutions. This indicates that the company’s focus on high-growth technology segments is paying off.
Operating leverage improved margins: Operating expenses increased only modestly compared to revenue growth, resulting in higher operating and EBITDA margins. This shows effective cost control and scalability in the business model.
Security business continued to grow: Security-related product and service billings increased 16.4% year over year, reflecting ongoing customer investment in cybersecurity as AI adoption raises new risks.
Capital return to shareholders: The company paid a quarterly dividend and repurchased shares, demonstrating financial flexibility and a commitment to returning capital.
Negatives
Professional services revenue declined: Revenue from professional services fell 7.8% year over year due to project delays, particularly from retail sector customers. This segment’s gross margin also declined, partly due to the integration of the lower-margin Bailiwick acquisition.
Inventory levels increased: Inventory at quarter-end more than doubled from the prior year, which led to a longer cash conversion cycle. This was attributed to higher demand and longer customer delivery schedules, but could present a risk if demand slows.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Impact of memory chip shortages and pricing: Management has flagged a global shortage of memory chips, which is causing price increases and could affect product delivery timing and costs. Watch for any updates on how this supply constraint is impacting sales or margins.
Follow-through on large enterprise and mid-market projects: The previous quarter benefited from outsized deals in the enterprise segment. Investors should look for signs of continued momentum or normalization in project size and frequency.
Professional services recovery and project timing: Management indicated that delayed retail projects may not resume until fiscal 2027. Monitor whether there is any improvement in professional services revenue or updates on the timing of these projects.
Progress in managed services and recurring revenue: Managed services remain a strategic priority. Growth here supports more predictable, recurring revenue streams. Watch for updates on new service offerings or customer wins in this area.
Execution on updated full-year guidance and capital allocation: The company raised its full-year guidance for net sales, gross profit, and adjusted EBITDA after Q3. Investors should assess whether Q4 results align with these targets and whether additional share repurchases or dividends are announced.
