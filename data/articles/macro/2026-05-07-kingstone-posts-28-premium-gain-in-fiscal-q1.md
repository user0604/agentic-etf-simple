Business overview

Kingstone Companies is a regional insurance company that underwrites personal and commercial lines, primarily homeowners policies, focused on the Northeast United States. It works with over 700 producers and is expanding into new markets to reduce geographic concentration risk. The company’s strategy includes leveraging technology for efficiency and maintaining strong relationships with producers to drive policy sales.

Quarterly performance

Direct premiums written grew 19.6%, driven by strong momentum in the core New York homeowners business and higher average premiums. Policies in force at quarter-end rose 7.2% over the prior year, reflecting solid retention and new business growth.

Catastrophe losses from historic winter weather drove the net combined ratio to 112.0%, with 26.0% of premiums impacted by catastrophe claims. This was a sharp rise from just 1.7% the year prior, as eleven separate winter events hit the Northeast.

Despite weather-related setbacks, the underlying combined ratio improved by 5.1 percentage points, showing progress in core underwriting profitability. Operating expenses as a percent of premiums decreased, indicating that pricing, risk selection, and discipline contributed to better structural margins.

Looking ahead

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, citing confidence in premium growth and underlying profitability despite the first quarter loss. It highlighted ongoing strategic expansion into new markets as a driver for future growth and risk diversification. Investors should closely monitor evidence of premium and policy growth outside New York as a sign of progress in geographic diversification.
