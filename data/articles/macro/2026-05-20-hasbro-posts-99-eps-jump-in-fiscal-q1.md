Notable Developments
The Wizards and Digital Gaming segment surged 26% year over year, reaching $582.0 million in revenue, driven by MAGIC: THE GATHERING and digital launches like Monopoly Go.
Operating margin improved by 7.8 percentage points company-wide, reflecting a more profitable sales mix and lower costs, even as Consumer Products posted a deeper operating loss.
Hasbro paid a quarterly dividend of $0.70 per share, amounting to $99 million returned to shareholders, with no change from the previous year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its full-year guidance unchanged, aiming for total revenue growth of 3 to 5% in constant currency and an adjusted operating margin of 24 to 25%. The outlook factors in ongoing investment in top brands, improved operational discipline, and advances in digital gaming. Investors should track progress in Consumer Products and monitor how costs related to the recent cybersecurity breach affect results in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The Wizards and Digital Gaming unit stood out, fueled by MAGIC: THE GATHERING’s strong new product launches and continued digital expansion. Digital and licensed gaming together grew 3%, reflecting modest growth from both core brands and digital offerings.

Consumer Products revenue remained flat year over year at $397.9 million, with stronger sales in Europe offsetting declining North America demand and operating losses widening due to normal seasonality, incremental tariff expenses, and challenging licensing comparisons versus the prior year.

The Entertainment segment posted a 24% decline in revenue, but improved its operating profit by lowering royalty expenses.
