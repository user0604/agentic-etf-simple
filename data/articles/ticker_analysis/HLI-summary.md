Houlihan Lokey Taps New Managing Director for Real Estate Expansion

HLI Company Overview
Houlihan Lokey provides advisory services for corporate finance transactions, financial restructurings, and asset valuations to a global client base. The firm generates revenue through fees earned by managing mergers, acquisitions, and debt offerings while assisting organizations through complex bankruptcy and reorganization processes. By integrating specialized industry expertise across multiple segments, the company builds long-term relationships with institutional clients that rely on its technical opinions for critical business decisions.
