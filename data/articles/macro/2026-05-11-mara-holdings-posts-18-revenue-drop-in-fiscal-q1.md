Business overview

Mara Holdings operates large-scale Bitcoin mining facilities and is expanding into digital infrastructure for artificial intelligence (AI) and high-performance computing. The company is working to diversify its business beyond cryptocurrency mining by investing in new energy assets and preparing its infrastructure to serve technology clients.

Quarterly performance

Revenue fell 18% due to a sharp decline in the average price of Bitcoin, which affected both mining and asset values. The company increased its energized hashrate by 33% to 72.2 EH/s, reflecting ongoing investment in mining infrastructure even as Bitcoin production was nearly flat. Mara advanced its AI and high-performance computing initiatives, preparing infrastructure for technology clients, but has not yet recorded any AI or data center lease revenue.

The company’s results were again driven by changes in Bitcoin’s market price. Revenue and earnings both declined compared to the prior year, mainly because the average value of Bitcoin was lower. Mara sold significantly more Bitcoin than it mined during the period, using proceeds to pay down debt and support liquidity.

Operationally, the business expanded its mining fleet and achieved a record energized hashrate, showing strong output even as mining profitability was pressured by higher network difficulty and energy costs. Energy costs per Bitcoin mined rose 12%, while cost-cutting and restructuring led to a 15% workforce reduction and new annual savings.

Diversification into AI and high-performance computing moved forward through new partnerships and acquisitions, such as the Long Ridge data center acquisition, but these segments have not yet produced meaningful revenue. The company remains highly exposed to Bitcoin price fluctuations until these new businesses mature and generate contracted cash flow.

Looking ahead

Management reiterated its focus on diversification, with expectations to sign at least one AI or data center tenant lease by the end of 2026. The shift from cryptocurrency reliance to contracted digital infrastructure revenue is underway, but not yet visible in results. The key factor to monitor is progress in securing tenants for new data center and AI assets, as these will shape future revenue stability.
