Key Takeaways

Interlink Electronics (NASDAQ:LINK) showed operational progress in the first quarter but stayed unprofitable as it worked to expand in core and adjacent markets. The global force-sensing and printed electronics specialist reported its results on May 14, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. While losses narrowed and margins expanded, the period continued to show both strengths and areas for caution.

Overall, the quarter brought higher revenue, a much smaller net loss, and improved cost control as the company balanced growth initiatives with financial discipline.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 15% year over year as strong shipments of force-sensing and printed electronics products offset weaker gas-sensor sales.
Gross margin improved 7.9 percentage points, largely due to favorable product mix.
R&D expense dropped 30%, possibly reflecting project timing but raising questions about future innovation investment.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide quantitative guidance for the next quarter or full year. However, it highlighted upcoming launches in medical and wearable devices, expansion with a new R&D facility in the UK, and plans for further acquisitions. Investors should watch both the pace of new product introductions and the impact of integration if the pending acquisition closes.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s top line grew with higher sales of its key force-sensing and printed electronics products, offsetting a decline in gas-sensor product sales. This higher-margin business mix drove a significant improvement in gross margin, helping reduce the company’s net loss compared to the same period last year.

Operating expenses stayed flat, with a sharp reduction in engineering and research and development outlays and a modest increase in selling, general, and administrative expense. The drop in R&D spending may reflect the completion of specific programs or cost measures, but it is important to monitor whether it affects new product flow.

Strategically, the company signed a non-binding letter of intent to acquire a manufacturing solutions provider focused on semiconductor, defense, and related high-tech sectors, reinforcing its history of growth through targeted acquisitions.

What Interlink Electronics Does

Interlink Electronics designs and manufactures advanced force-sensing, printed electronics, and sensor solutions used in medical, industrial, automotive, and consumer technology applications worldwide.
