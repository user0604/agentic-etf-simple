Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Ralph Lauren delivered a strong set of results, surpassing analyst expectations for both revenue and earnings per share. The company reported net revenues of $2.406 billion and net income of $361.6 million, with diluted earnings per share of $5.82. This performance reflected double-digit revenue growth and significant margin expansion, driven by robust demand across all regions and channels. The company’s gross margin rose to 69.9%, and operating margin improved to 19.6%. Management highlighted broad-based growth, with Asia leading at over 20% revenue growth, and strong digital and full-price sales globally. Ralph Lauren also raised its full-year outlook, citing continued momentum and healthy consumer demand.

Positives
Revenue and earnings exceeded expectations: Ralph Lauren beat analyst consensus for both sales and earnings per share, signaling strong operational execution and resilient demand across its markets.
Gross margin expansion despite tariffs: The company expanded gross margin by 150 basis points year over year, driven by higher average selling prices and lower cotton costs, more than offsetting increased U.S. tariffs.
Balanced growth across geographies and channels: Asia posted over 20% revenue growth, while North America and Europe also saw gains. Both direct-to-consumer and wholesale channels contributed to the strong results.
Strong new customer acquisition and digital engagement: Ralph Lauren added 2.1 million new direct-to-consumer customers and increased its social media following, reflecting the effectiveness of its marketing and digital initiatives.
Negatives
Europe operating margin declined: Despite sales growth, Europe’s operating margin fell by 150 basis points due to increased marketing investments and a deliberate pullback in outlet promotions, which management described as a strategic move for long-term brand health.
Higher restructuring and transformation charges: The company incurred $31.8 million in restructuring and transformation-related charges in the quarter, which reduced net income per share by $0.40.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Revenue growth versus consensus: Analysts expect sales of about $1.85 billion for the quarter. Investors should watch whether Ralph Lauren can deliver mid-single-digit revenue growth as guided, especially given planned reductions in off-price wholesale and timing shifts in shipments.
Gross and operating margin trends: Management has guided for a year-over-year contraction in margins in Q4 due to higher tariffs, increased marketing spending around the Olympics and fashion events, and seasonal timing. The extent of margin pressure versus expectations will be key.
Progress on tariff mitigation and cost management: The company anticipates tariffs to be a headwind through the first half of next fiscal year. Look for updates on actions to offset these costs, such as supply chain adjustments and pricing strategies.
Direct-to-consumer and digital momentum: Continued growth in full-price sales, digital channels, and new customer acquisition will be important, especially as the company shifts away from the off-price channel and invests in digital engagement tools like its AI-powered shopping assistant.
Status of strategic initiatives and transformation projects: Ralph Lauren’s multi-year Next Generation Transformation project remains underway, with ongoing investments in technology, logistics, and a unified global platform. Updates on milestones and spending will help investors gauge the pace and impact of these efforts.
