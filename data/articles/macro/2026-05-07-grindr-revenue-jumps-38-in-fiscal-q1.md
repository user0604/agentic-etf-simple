Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its revenue and adjusted EBITDA forecasts for fiscal 2026, citing ongoing product innovation and subscription momentum. Executives highlighted the upcoming global launch of Edge, an AI-powered premium tier, and continued efficiency gains. Investors should watch for updates on user base and engagement, as the company did not disclose quarterly active or paying user metrics.

Quarterly Highlights

Platform monetization stood out in the quarter, with revenue growth of nearly 40% driven by expanded premium offerings and AI-backed features under the Edge label. This acceleration followed last year's 28% growth, and management noted that new initiatives are not yet fully reflected in guidance.

Profitability remained strong at the adjusted EBITDA level, but net income margin slipped to 20.6% from 28.8% as non-cash expenses, higher litigation costs, and increased investment in new services weighed on the bottom line.

Regulatory and legal spending increased, with litigation-related costs rising to $0.6 million. The company also flagged ongoing compliance risks related to new health and wellness products under the Woodwork initiative.

What Grindr Does

Grindr operates a social networking platform and dating app focused on the LGBTQ community, generating revenue through premium subscriptions and digital advertising.
