American Superconductor Revenue Jumps 30% in Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 4:20 PM

Key Takeaways
American Superconductor delivered a business win with record quarterly and annual results, fueled by strong Grid segment performance and successful acquisition integration.
Non-GAAP earnings per share rose 150% year over year.
Management expects continued demand but notes headline profits included a one-time tax benefit; future operating margins and acquisition integration are key areas to watch.

American Superconductor (NASDAQ:AMSC) posted a business win as it achieved record highs for both its quarterly and annual results, reflecting strong execution and demand for its grid solutions. The company, a specialist in grid enhancement, power electronics, and wind energy integration, reported its fiscal fourth quarter and full-year 2025 results on May 27, 2026, for the period ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter featured robust revenue growth and a much-improved bottom line, though headline profits were boosted by a one-time tax event.

Notable developments
Revenue increased nearly 30%, reaching a record $86.4 million, with the Grid segment contributing about 85% of the total.
Profitability improved sharply, with Non-GAAP earnings per share rising 150% year over year. Net income for the fiscal year was driven by a one-time $113 million tax benefit recorded in the third quarter.
The company’s backlog expanded to $280 million, up about 40% from the prior year, reflecting new orders from traditional energy and data center markets.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects fiscal first quarter 2026 revenue to exceed $85 million and non-GAAP net income over $8 million, pointing to ongoing demand in utilities and renewables. However, it cautioned that the headline 2025 net income was boosted by a non-recurring tax item, so investors should monitor operating margins and the pace of integration from the Comtrafo acquisition for a clearer view of underlying performance.

Quarterly highlights

The Grid segment remained the main driver, generating over $73 million in fourth-quarter revenue, up 33% from a year ago. This result highlights strong demand for power control and grid enhancement solutions within both renewable integration and traditional utility infrastructure.

The period included the first full quarter of sales from Comtrafo, a Brazilian transformer manufacturer acquired in December 2025. This addition helped expand market reach into Latin America and contributed to both higher revenue and increased operating expenses.

Backlog growth was sharp, with orders nearing $100 million in the quarter—especially from utilities, energy projects, and data centers. The total $280 million backlog now extends visibility for future growth but has also led to rising inventory and accounts receivable balances as project cycles lengthen.

What American Superconductor does

American Superconductor designs and manufactures advanced grid solutions, including power control systems and high-efficiency electrical products, primarily serving the utility, renewable energy, and industrial markets.
