Hovnanian Posts 3% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 21, 2026, 10:23 AM

Key Takeaways
Second quarter profitability pressured by shrinking margins and a shift to quick-move-in home sales.
Revenue declined 2.8% year over year, but management said results fell within its guidance range.
Management provided cautious guidance for the next quarter, citing ongoing market uncertainties.

Homebuilder Hovnanian Enterprises (NYSE:HOV) saw profitability slip as it worked through lower-margin inventory and continued heavy use of buyer incentives. The company, one of the largest homebuilders in the United States, operates across key regions with a focus on quick-move-in (QMI) homes and capital flexibility. For the second quarter, covering February through April and released on May 21, 2026, results landed within management’s guidance, but profit levels fell notably from the previous year. Overall, the quarter displayed a balanced delivery on targets, but ongoing pressure on margins and backlog weighed on performance.

Notable Developments
Revenue was $667.6 million, within management’s $625–$725 million guidance range.
Homebuilding gross margin declined 3.6 percentage points to 10.2%, while adjusted EBITDA fell 33% year over year.
Quick-move-in home inventory dropped 31.9% to 731 units.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management set next-quarter guidance between $650 million and $750 million in revenue and targets slightly higher gross margins. However, company leaders cautioned that visibility beyond the coming quarter remains limited, given ongoing uncertainty in buyer demand and cancellation rates. Investors should closely track trends in home sales backlog and cancellation rates, as these will signal when profit margins may begin to recover.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability per home continued to face headwinds, with gross margin slipping to 10.2%, notably below the prior year. Management reported that heavy sales incentives and a larger share of quick-move-in homes contributed to this margin decline.

The company significantly reduced its quick-move-in inventory, reflecting a deliberate effort to align construction starts with actual market demand. This reduced inventory turnover time but also led to a year-over-year decrease in contract backlog and homes awaiting delivery.

Geographic performance remained mixed: Northeast segment contracts rose 8.5%, while the West and Southeast segments both saw contract declines. Overall contract backlog value fell 4.5% year over year, and gross domestic contract cancellations increased from 15% to 17%.

What Hovnanian Enterprises Does

Hovnanian Enterprises builds and sells homes across multiple U.S. regions, with a focus on offering a range of home options and providing related mortgage and title services.
