The cost base saw a major improvement, with general and administrative expenses down 43%, reinforcing management’s commitment to streamlining the business. These efficiency gains allowed the company to shrink its net loss and post much stronger adjusted EBITDA.

Consumer insights delivered $5.0 million in revenue, but the filing did not detail whether this area grew or contracted year over year.

The balance sheet remains under pressure, with a stockholders’ deficit now at $77.9 million. Cash declined sharply during the quarter, and high interest costs continue to weigh on net results, keeping liquidity as a key risk to monitor.
