Key Takeaways

Business win: Modular solutions provider WillScot (NASDAQ:WSC) delivered a solid quarter, driven by increased demand for large projects and enterprise customers. The company leases modular workspace and portable storage solutions across North America, serving industries such as construction, energy, and events. Released on May 7, 2026, the first-quarter results showed operational progress in project-driven areas, but recurring leasing revenue and profit margins continued to face pressure. Overall, the period reflected promising growth in project work but ongoing margin contraction compared to the prior year.

Notable Developments
Delivery and installation services revenue increased 12.3% to $99.5 million, helping offset lower recurring leasing revenue.
Gross profit margin declined to 52.1%, reflecting a higher share of project-driven revenue and increased variable costs.
Management raised full-year 2026 guidance for revenue and adjusted profit, citing strong project demand but ongoing caution for local market recovery.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its 2026 targets to $2.25 billion in revenue and $915 million in adjusted EBITDA, pointing to strong demand for enterprise and project work. However, it noted that local markets remain flat to weak and expects recurring leasing revenue to improve in the second half of the year. Investors should watch for signs of margin recovery and growth in recurring leasing activity as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter featured mixed results across revenue streams. Recurring leasing revenue, which includes modular space and portable storage, declined by 2.0%, while project-driven delivery and installation services grew significantly. Value-added products, such as security features and fencing, provided steady revenue, and management introduced new offerings to compete for larger projects.

Profit margins continued to contract compared to the same period last year. The shift toward large project activations, which carry higher setup and transport costs, contributed to lower gross profit margin and adjusted EBITDA margin. Management stated that these costs should help drive future lease revenue as new projects ramp up.

The company increased capital expenditures to support higher-value fleet and new technology. It continued rolling out scheduling and route optimization tools to improve efficiency across its network of approximately 260 branch locations.

What WillScot Does

WillScot provides modular workspace and portable storage solutions, focusing on flexible leasing with value-added services for businesses across North America.
