Action camera maker GoPro (NASDAQ:GPRO) reported steep financial losses as camera sales and subscription numbers declined, even as its new professional camera line debuted. The company, a global leader in digital imaging devices and action cameras, released its first quarter 2026 results on May 11, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter was marked by acute margin pressure and falling cash balances, despite targeted cost-cutting and product innovation.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management is focusing on launching new premium products, cutting operating costs, and exploring strategic alternatives with the help of external financial advisors.

Quarterly Highlights

The launch of the MISSION 1 Series -- a line of compact, professional cinema cameras -- represents a key strategic shift into higher-end video equipment. While management described the launch as a critical milestone, there are no reported sales figures or early uptake data yet, so the impact is unknown.

Services and subscription business faced headwinds. Subscription and service revenue remained flat at $27 million compared to the prior year, as the subscriber base fell by 8%. The company previously guided for growth in this segment, but actual results did not meet those projections, with recurring revenues now a critical watchpoint.

Financial metrics deteriorated substantially. Gross margin dropped by 27.8 percentage points due in part to a $24.5 million inventory-related charge. Cash at quarter end fell to $40.7 million, with short-term debt rising and equity turning negative, marking a challenging financial position.
