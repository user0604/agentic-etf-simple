Key Takeaways
Camtek’s first quarter marked a modest business win, with strong orders but muted bottom-line growth.
Revenue rose year over year.
Management expects a notable revenue surge in the second half of 2026.

Camtek (NASDAQ:CAMT) posted a stable start to the year, capturing robust customer demand for next-generation inspection technology while facing profitability pressures. The leading provider of semiconductor inspection and metrology systems reported its first quarter results on May 12, 2026 (covering the three months ended March 31, 2026), showing small revenue gains amid lower profits. Overall, the period revealed steady market positioning but highlighted the need for future margin recovery.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 2.5% year over year, reflecting consistent customer demand and incremental growth in advanced packaging, memory, and AI-related systems.
Operating expenses rose 21% versus the prior year, with research and development investment up 38% to $14.3 million, supporting Camtek’s push into AI-powered inspection technology.
Gross margin declined by 1.1 percentage points to 51.0% (Non-GAAP) as heavy investments in innovation and higher expenses outpaced revenue gains.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed higher expectations for the remainder of the year, guiding to $129–131 million in revenue for the second quarter and projecting over 25% revenue growth in the second half of 2026 versus the first half. The most important area for investors to monitor will be whether Camtek’s elevated order levels in advanced semiconductor technology effectively translate into stronger sales and improved margins in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue rose from the previous year’s first quarter, largely matching management’s guidance, but growth was modest compared to prior periods and typical of early-year industry seasonality. Gross and operating margins softened due to substantial increases in research and development expenses, especially in the development of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms for wafer inspection tools.

Camtek completed its acquisition of the AI company Visual Layer in the quarter, aiming to accelerate the integration of proprietary AI into its inspection and metrology platforms. This acquisition directly targets the need for smarter, more precise detection and classification features in its product lineup.

Customer orders reached unprecedented levels, driven by demand for advanced packaging, high-bandwidth memory (HBM), and chiplet architectures that require sophisticated inspection solutions. However, the revenue impact of this order momentum will only become clear in the second half of 2026, as management’s guidance points to a much stronger finish to the year.

What Camtek Does

Camtek supplies automated inspection and metrology systems used by semiconductor manufacturers to ensure quality and precision across advanced chip technologies.
