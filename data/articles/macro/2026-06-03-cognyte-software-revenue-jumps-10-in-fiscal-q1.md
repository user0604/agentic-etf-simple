Notable developments
Recurring revenue made up nearly half of all sales, at $51.9 million, growing 10% from a year ago thanks to higher subscription adoption.
Operating margin (non-GAAP) climbed to 10.1%, up 2.2 percentage points from the prior-year quarter, showing efficiency gains and strong product demand.
Net cash from operations was a negative $4.7 million, reversing from a positive $1.7 million last year, driven by inventory build-up and the shift to subscriptions.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for fiscal 2027, targeting $448 million in revenue and $68 million in adjusted EBITDA for the full year. Executives expect recurring revenue to grow faster than total sales as the software subscription model expands. Investors should closely track margin sustainability and operating cash flow in future quarters, given the negative cash swing this period.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure grew from the previous year, with strong performance in software and software services, which now represent more than 90% of the business. Billings — a measure of sales activity — rose 31.2%, outpacing top-line growth and indicating solid demand ahead.

Recurring revenue, which comes from ongoing software subscriptions or service contracts, made up almost half of total sales. This line advanced year over year, reflecting successful adoption of the subscription approach and providing increased revenue predictability.

Profitability improved at the operating level, with adjusted EBITDA (a measure of profit before non-cash and financing charges) up more than 30%. However, foreign exchange losses and higher tax charges reduced the company’s net earnings compared to the prior year, and cash flow from operations turned negative as inventory and customer receivables increased.
