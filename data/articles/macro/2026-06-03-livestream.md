SpaceX's Space Data Center Dream Is Years Away

12:05 pm

By Lou Whiteman
TEAM HIDDEN GEMS

Look to the sky, and the benefit of placing massive infrastructure in orbit is evident: a massive 24/7 energy source that would instantly solve one of the biggest hurdles to the terrestrial data center build-out.

Unseen are the physics of space, which will make Elon Musk and SpaceX's vision for orbital data centers hard to achieve.

Space is a vacuum, which means the rules governing things like conduction and convection, taught as gospel in every high school physics classroom, do not apply. That means servers cannot be cooled using traditional fans or liquid-cooled heat exchangers.

Instead, data centers would rely entirely on thermal radiation, requiring massive, heavy radiator panels larger than anything currently in orbit just to cool a single rack of AI chips.
