Sila Realty Trust (NYSE:SILA) delivered another quarter of solid financial performance, showing steady income growth and continued portfolio quality. Notable developments
Net income grew to $0.22 per share, up 57.1% from the prior year, reflecting improved profitability and portfolio performance.
The company acquired a fully leased, 53,000-square-foot inpatient rehabilitation facility in Oklahoma City for $43.3 million, and sold four properties for $25.1 million in total proceeds.
The quarterly dividend remained unchanged at $0.40 per share for the period, consistent with past quarters, and maintaining a payout ratio of 66.7% of AFFO.
Management outlook and what to watch

With the pending all-cash acquisition by Blue Owl Real Estate Capital affiliates expected to close in the second or third quarter of 2026, management's outlook centers on transaction completion. The company did not provide updated forward guidance and suspended earnings calls for this period. Investors should primarily monitor progress on the merger process and any updates regarding its approval or closing timeline.

Quarterly highlights

The company posted stronger financials compared to the same period last year, with the adjusted funds from operations per share and net income per share each showing double-digit percentage increases. Revenue for the quarter was $52.7 million, up 9.1%.

Portfolio activity featured the acquisition of an Oklahoma City healthcare facility on a long-term lease, supporting the core focus on the healthcare sector. Sila also sold four properties, reinforcing its approach of active asset management.

Same store cash net operating income, which measures the performance of properties owned continuously for both periods, grew by 0.7%. This limited organic growth highlights continued portfolio stability, but signals that new acquisitions and capital recycling are the main sources of income growth.

Sila Realty Trust owns and manages a diversified portfolio of healthcare-focused, net-leased real estate properties throughout the United States.
