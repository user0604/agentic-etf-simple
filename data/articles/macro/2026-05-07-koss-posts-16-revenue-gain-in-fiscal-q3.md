Quarterly Highlights

Sales increased slightly, driven by stronger performance in the direct-to-consumer segment, which management said is now the company’s largest revenue source. However, this limited growth was not enough to counterbalance weak sales to European distributors, which remained negatively affected by “extended stock replacement cycles” and broader reductions in consumer spending abroad.

Profitability declined sharply, with gross margin for the nine-month period dropping by 2.9 percentage points due to lingering impacts from inventory purchased at higher tariffs and shipping costs. While better product mix toward domestic and higher-margin channels provided some support, these gains did not offset legacy supply chain issues.

Operating expenses continued to climb, as SG&A costs rose faster than sales. This increase, paired with higher cost of goods, widened the quarterly net loss to $547 thousand from $317 thousand in the prior year period. Meanwhile, the company reported a $250 thousand nonrecurring gain in other income over the nine-month period, but this was insufficient to meaningfully offset the increased operating loss.
