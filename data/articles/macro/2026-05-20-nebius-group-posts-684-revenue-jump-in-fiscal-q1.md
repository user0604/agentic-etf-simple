What Nebius Group Does

Nebius Group is a developer and operator of AI-focused cloud infrastructure platforms, delivering computing power and software tools for artificial intelligence applications worldwide.

Notable Developments
AI cloud segment revenue soared 841%, reaching $389.7 million, and swung to positive adjusted EBITDA of $174.0 million for the quarter.
Customer prepayments supplied $3.2 billion in cash, helping fuel heavy capital expenditure of $2.47 billion on data centers and equipment.
Net income leapt to $621.2 million because of a $780.6 million one-time gain on the revaluation of the ClickHouse equity investment.
Quarterly Highlights

The AI cloud platform dominated results, with revenue surging by over eightfold year over year and profitability rapidly improving. This success was attributed to infrastructure scaling, strong pricing, and high utilization, with customer demand consistently outpacing supply and substantial prepayments supporting fast expansion.

Capital spending increased sharply, as Nebius invested $2.47 billion in new property, equipment, and technology, raising its full-year infrastructure investment target to $20 billion-$25 billion on the back of secured multi-year contracts with major customers. Operating cash flow more than offset these capital outlays, thanks to unprecedented levels of upfront payments from new and existing clients.

Non-core business segments like Avride, which develops autonomous driving technology, and TripleTen, focused on workforce reskilling, continued to post losses and weighed on group profitability, though their revenues saw minor growth. Meanwhile, Nebius booked a one-time gain from revaluing its equity in ClickHouse, dramatically boosting net income for the period.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its forecast for annualized run-rate revenue of $7 billion to $9 billion and group adjusted EBITDA margins nearing 40% for fiscal 2026, supported by continued customer demand and a robust pipeline. Leaders caution that margins will dip in the next quarter as major capacity investments occur before returning to current levels by year-end. The most critical metric to track is the utilization of newly added AI cloud capacity, which underpins both revenue and margin targets.
