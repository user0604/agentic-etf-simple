Luxury resale platform RealReal (NASDAQ:REAL) delivered a strong business performance as its marketplace grew across all core metrics. Notable Developments
Gross merchandise value (GMV) rose 24% to $606 million, outpacing management’s upper guidance range.
Profitability improved sharply: Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 6.9% for the quarter, compared to 2.6% in the prior year period.
Twelve-month active buyers climbed 10% to 1,083,000, with Gen Z and Millennial users making up much of this growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its full-year revenue and adjusted EBITDA targets, now aiming for up to $784 million in sales and $67 million in adjusted EBITDA for fiscal 2026. Leaders highlighted buyer growth and sustained operational investments as key drivers. Investors should monitor gross margin trends and ongoing technology investments in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Marketplace momentum pushed results beyond company expectations, with both GMV and revenue growth exceeding prior guidance. Higher marketplace activity was due in part to a 15% rise in average order value, reflecting successful pricing and demand for high-value items.

Gross margin slipped by 0.5 percentage points to 74.5%, as increasing sales of high-value goods with lower commission rates put pressure on overall margins. However, the company increased total gross profit dollars and expanded adjusted EBITDA margin by more than four percentage points.

While operational and technology costs rose 9% from the prior year, these investments supported stronger platform automation and authentication. The company closed the quarter with $124 million in cash and expects improving cash generation in the second half of the year.

What RealReal Does

RealReal operates an online marketplace and retail locations for authenticated pre-owned luxury goods, using proprietary technology and in-house experts to verify every item.
