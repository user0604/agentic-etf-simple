Elastic Posts 16% Revenue Growth in Fiscal Q4

Key Takeaways
Elastic capped the year with subscription and cloud growth, supported by larger customer commitments.
Non-GAAP EPS reached $0.61 for the quarter, up 29.8% from the prior year.
Management guides for continued double-digit revenue growth and stronger operating margins in fiscal 2027.

Search software company Elastic (NYSE:ESTC) closed its fiscal year with significant growth in enterprise subscription deals and notable momentum in cloud offerings. The company, known for its unified search, observability, and security platform, reported strong performance for the fourth quarter (released May 28, 2026), as it increased both revenue and cash flow while making further inroads with large customers. Overall, the quarter reinforced Elastic’s position in the AI-driven search software market, though profitability on a GAAP basis remains an area for improvement.

Notable Developments
Subscription revenue comprised 94% of total revenue, up 17%; cloud revenue accounted for 48% of all revenue with 20% growth.
Customers with contracts above $100,000 in annual value rose to over 1,720, marking an increase of 14% from the prior year period.
Deferred revenue and performance obligations grew solidly, with remaining performance obligations up 28% reaching $1.98 billion.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects double-digit revenue gains for fiscal 2027, with full-year guidance calling for up to $2.0 billion in revenue and an improved non-GAAP operating margin near 19%.

Quarterly Highlights

Cloud-based solutions continued to drive revenue, with cloud sales now making up nearly half of the company’s business and annual Elastic Cloud revenue expanding briskly. Enterprise expansion remained a strong theme, demonstrated by the rise in customers with contracts above $100,000 and a net expansion rate of 112%, capturing greater wallet share from existing clients.

Elastic rolled out new AI and automation features, including support for hybrid cloud deployments, embedded AI models, and workflow automations within its search, security, and observability tools. These enhancements target broader adoption across large regulated enterprises and public sector organizations, evident in new government wins and recognitions such as Google Cloud Partner of the Year.

Deferred revenue and performance obligations rose meaningfully, signaling larger contracts and greater revenue visibility. However, the company continued to post GAAP operating losses as increased investments in research and development and sales limited margin improvements.

What Elastic Does

Elastic builds software that enables organizations to search, analyze, and secure large volumes of data using both on-premises and cloud-based platforms, leveraging artificial intelligence for enhanced search and analytics.
