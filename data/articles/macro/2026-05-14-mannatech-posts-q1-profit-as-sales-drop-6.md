Mannatech (NASDAQ:MTEX) posted a quarterly profit thanks to improved margins and lower expenses, but persistent revenue declines and falling distributor numbers remain worrying. The global nutrition and wellness company depends on independent associates for product sales and growth. On May 14, 2026, it reported first-quarter 2026 results for the period ending March 31, showing both operational gains and structural challenges. Overall, the quarter highlighted significant declines in its Americas business and continued contraction in its customer network, counterbalanced by margin gains and a net profit driven by non-operating items.

Notable Developments
Americas sales dropped sharply by 25.5%, falling from $9.0 million to $6.7 million, while Asia/Pacific modestly grew.
Active associate and preferred customer positions fell 14% year over year, down to 111,000 from 129,000 a year earlier.
Net income swung to a $1.0 million profit, mostly due to $1.4 million in other income, not core sales improvement.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue guidance or new financial targets for the year. Leadership emphasized attention to cost control and highlighted ongoing risks from trade policies and regional uncertainties. Investors should closely watch trends in associate recruitment and retention, as further declines could make sustainable revenue recovery increasingly difficult.

Quarterly Highlights

The decline in total sales was mainly driven by a substantial drop in the Americas, which lost $2.3 million in regional revenue year over year, while Asia/Pacific managed a small gain and grew its share of total sales. Despite lower sales, gross profit margin improved by 1.4 percentage points, reflecting disciplined expense management and fewer cost of goods sold as a percentage of sales.

Network contraction accelerated, evidenced by a 15.6% drop in new associate and customer recruitment and a 14% overall reduction in active positions, signaling continued distributor disengagement. This shrinkage in the associate base threatens future topline performance, as the network marketing model relies heavily on distributor participation for both sales and further recruiting.

The bottom-line improvement to net income was largely the result of a $1.4 million boost in non-operating "other income," offsetting core sales weakness. Operating loss shrank from $0.8 million to $0.2 million, but absent this one-time income, ongoing revenue declines and negative equity would likely remain key risks for the company.

What Mannatech Does

Mannatech develops and sells nutritional supplements, weight management products, and personal care items through a network marketing (multi-level marketing) model using independent distributors.
