Earnings Preview: lululemon athletica

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

lululemon athletica (NASDAQ:LULU), a leading maker of athletic apparel and accessories, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes. Investors will be watching closely for updates on the company’s ongoing turnaround efforts and outlook for the rest of the year.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, lululemon reported net revenue of $3.6 billion, representing a 1% increase year over year. Excluding the extra week in the prior year, revenue rose 6% (4% on a constant currency basis) and comparable sales increased 2%. The company’s gross profit was $2.0 billion, or 54.9% of net revenue, down from 60.4% a year earlier. Diluted earnings per share came in at $5.01, compared to $6.14 in the prior year’s quarter. These results missed analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings per share. The quarter was marked by continued strength in international markets, especially China, but ongoing challenges in North America, where revenue was flat and comparable sales declined 2%. Higher tariffs and increased markdowns weighed heavily on margins and profits. The company ended the quarter with $1.8 billion in cash and $1.7 billion in inventory, up 18% year over year.

Positives
International growth remains robust: Revenue in China Mainland increased 28%, with comparable sales up 26%. This demonstrates strong brand momentum and effective local marketing, helping offset weaker performance in North America.
New product innovation gaining traction: Recent launches such as Unrestricted Power, ShowZero, and ThermoZen have received positive customer feedback. These innovations are key to refreshing the brand’s appeal and are already showing early signs of success.
Improved inventory management: Inventory unit growth was 6%, below guidance, reflecting better alignment with demand and a more disciplined approach to stock levels.
Strong digital channel performance: Digital sales rose 9%, contributing $1.9 billion to revenue. This highlights the effectiveness of ongoing investments in e-commerce and digital marketing.
Negatives
North America struggles continue: Revenue in the region was flat, with U.S. sales down 1% and comparable sales down 2%. This reflects ongoing challenges with consumer demand, markdowns, and competitive pressures.
Gross margin under significant pressure: Gross margin fell 550 basis points year over year, mainly due to higher tariffs and increased markdowns. This led to a sharp drop in operating margin and net income.
Elevated markdown activity: Markdowns increased by 130 basis points in Q4, signaling continued difficulty in selling products at full price and potential brand dilution risk.
Operating expenses rising: Selling, general, and administrative expenses grew to 32.5% of revenue, up from 31.5% a year ago, driven by higher marketing, labor, and technology costs.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on full-price sales recovery in North America: Investors should monitor whether the company’s efforts to reduce markdowns and introduce more new products are translating into improved full-price sales, especially as management expects a sequential improvement but not a full turnaround until the second half of the year.
Impact of new product launches and activations: The rollout of innovations like Unrestricted Power and ShowZero, along with marketing activations such as Studio Yet and tennis sponsorships, will be important for driving customer engagement and sales momentum.
Tariff and cost pressures on margins: The company expects continued headwinds from higher tariffs and related costs. Watch for updates on how much of these impacts are being offset by efficiency initiatives and selective price increases.
International segment performance: China and other international markets have been key growth drivers. Investors should look for continued strength in these regions and any signs of changing trends.
Status of leadership transition and board changes: The recent appointment of Chip Bergh to the Board and the ongoing CEO search are important for long-term strategy and stability. Any updates on these fronts could influence investor sentiment.
