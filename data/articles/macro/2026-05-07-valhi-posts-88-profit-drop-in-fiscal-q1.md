Valhi Posts 88% Profit Drop in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Profitability fell sharply despite higher revenue, primarily due to margin pressure in the chemicals business.
Revenue rose 4% year over year.
Management did not issue formal financial guidance for the next quarter or fiscal year.

Valhi (NYSE:VHI)’s business momentum faltered as steep profit declines outweighed modest revenue growth across its main segments. The diversified holding company is primarily known for its chemicals, component products, and real estate operations. The company released its first quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed higher sales but exposed persistent margin pressure and earnings volatility.

Notable developments
Total revenue increased 4%, driven by sales growth in both the chemicals and real estate segments.
Operating income in the chemicals segment dropped by nearly two-thirds as lower average titanium dioxide prices and production volumes hit margins.
Real estate operating income jumped after recognizing a one-time commercial parcel sale and an infrastructure reimbursement.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue formal financial guidance for the next quarter or fiscal year, citing continued caution around titanium dioxide pricing and market demand. Its remarks focused on ongoing operational, regulatory, and competitive risks. Investors should closely watch for further updates on pricing recovery in the chemicals business and the pace of real estate transactions, as these are likely to impact profit levels in coming periods.

Quarterly highlights

The chemicals segment, centered on titanium dioxide pigment used in paints, plastics, and paper, saw net sales edge higher. Gains were mostly from higher sales volumes in North America and Latin America, offset by a 6% drop in average selling prices and softness in Europe. Operating income plunged as lower product pricing and unfavorable currency exchange more than canceled out modest cost savings and volume improvement.

The component products business saw its operating income grow 20%, outpacing a less than 1% rise in revenue. This jump came from improved gross margins in security products and higher sales of marine components, underscoring effective product mix and cost management strategies in that segment.

Real estate management and development posted a sharp jump in operating income to $11.3 million, up nearly threefold from a year earlier. This reflected the sale of a final commercial property parcel and a $5.4 million infrastructure reimbursement, both considered non-recurring items. Management signaled that development activities are nearing completion, which could limit future upside in this area.

What Valhi does

Valhi is a diversified holding company with core businesses in chemicals (titanium dioxide pigment), component products (security and marine components), and real estate management and development.
