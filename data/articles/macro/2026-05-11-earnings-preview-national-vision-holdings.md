Earnings Preview: National Vision Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 1:03 PM

Comments

National Vision Holdings (NASDAQ:EYE), a leading U.S. optical retailer focused on affordable eye exams and eyewear, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, National Vision Holdings delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations. Net revenue grew 15.1% year over year to $503 million, with adjusted comparable store sales up 4.8%. Adjusted operating income reached $17.6 million, and adjusted operating margin improved to 3.5% from 0.7% in the prior year. The company reported adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.15, a turnaround from a loss of $0.04 per share in the same quarter last year. These results were driven by strong execution of strategic initiatives, particularly in attracting higher-value customers and expanding premium product offerings.

Positives
Strong revenue and margin expansion: Net revenue and adjusted operating margin both improved meaningfully, showing the company’s transformation strategy is delivering financial results.
Growth in managed care and premium customer segments: The company saw traffic growth among customers with vision insurance, those purchasing progressive lenses, and those bringing outside prescriptions, improving overall profitability.
Effective product and pricing initiatives: The shift toward more premium frames and branded assortments increased average ticket size and attracted new, higher-income customers.
Cost discipline and SG&A leverage: Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue declined, reflecting both higher sales and ongoing cost optimization efforts.
Negatives
Decline in overall customer traffic: Total traffic fell 2.5% year over year, as gains in managed care were offset by declines in self-pay customers, reflecting the ongoing shift in customer mix.
Continued softness among cash pay customers: The company noted that self-pay customers remain sensitive to macroeconomic pressures and have not fully returned to previous purchasing patterns.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable store sales trends and customer mix: Monitor how the balance between managed care and self-pay customers is evolving, especially given management’s guidance for mid-single-digit comp growth and the ongoing shift toward higher-value segments.
Impact of premium product and smart eyewear expansion: Track the rollout of Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses to all stores by the end of Q2 and the introduction of additional premium frame brands, as these are expected to drive higher average ticket values.
Progress on lens simplification and premium lens penetration: Watch for updates on the new lens pricing structure and the introduction of advanced lenses like Stellest and tier-four progressives, which aim to increase premium lens sales as a percentage of the mix.
Store growth and fleet optimization: Note the pace of new store openings (targeting 30 to 35 for the year) and planned store closures, as well as any commentary on the impact of these changes on overall revenue and profitability.
SG&A expense control and margin trajectory: Analyze the company’s ability to continue leveraging selling, general, and administrative expenses as sales grow, supporting further operating margin expansion in line with full-year guidance.
