Notable Developments
The company secured a multi-year renewal with a Tier-1 operator, expanding its ACE platform's reach into more AI-driven network operations. This renewal deepened relationships with key customers rather than adding new large clients.
Operating margin rose by 2.9 percentage points year over year in Q1 2026, helped by revenue growth and controlled costs. Non-GAAP net income grew 14.6% year over year in Q1 2026, reflecting improved profitability.
Five directors, including the Chairman and Executive Chairman, stepped down ahead of the annual meeting scheduled for May 20, 2026. Management emphasized that this board turnover was intended to avoid a proxy contest and minimize potentially harmful effects of a prolonged process.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue growth guidance of 8–12%, translating to approximately $78.6 million at the midpoint. The team remains focused on expanding product capabilities and deepening installed-base relationships. Investors should closely monitor progress on new customer acquisitions and the impact of board changes on long-term strategy.

Quarterly Highlights

The company pushed into AI-led service assurance with the launch of Neura, an agent suite aimed at enabling autonomous, intent-driven telecom networks. This aligns with the broader industry move toward automation and actionable insights within telecom infrastructure.

Revenue reached $18.6 million for the quarter, up 12% compared to the same period last year. However, revenue growth over the prior quarter was modest, showing consistent but not accelerating momentum.

The balance sheet remained healthy with $108.4 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term deposits, and no debt. This offers flexibility for future research and development or potential acquisitions to broaden the product and customer base.

What Radcom Does

Radcom provides cloud-native analytics and service assurance software that helps telecom operators monitor and optimize the quality and reliability of their networks.
