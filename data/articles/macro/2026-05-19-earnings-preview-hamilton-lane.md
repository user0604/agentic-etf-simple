Earnings Preview: Hamilton Lane

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Hamilton Lane (NASDAQ:HLNE), a global private markets investment solutions provider, is expected to report earnings for its fiscal fourth quarter on Thursday, May 21, 2026. The company typically reports results before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

The third quarter of fiscal 2026 was a strong one for Hamilton Lane. The company reported total revenues of $198.6 million, up from $167.5 million in the prior year period, and delivered diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $1.37 on a GAAP basis. Non-GAAP EPS came in at $1.55, which beat analyst consensus estimates. Fee-related earnings for the year to date reached $254.6 million, reflecting 37% growth year over year. Assets under management (AUM) grew to $146 billion, up 8% from the previous year, while assets under advisement (AUA) reached $871.5 billion, a 6% increase. The company also declared a quarterly dividend of $0.54 per share, keeping it on track for a 10% increase in the annual payout.

Positives
Revenue and EPS beat estimates: Both revenue and non-GAAP EPS exceeded analyst consensus, showing continued operational outperformance.
Strong growth in fee-related earnings: Fee-related earnings rose 37% year over year, driven by higher management fees and performance revenues, indicating healthy underlying business momentum.
Evergreen platform expansion: The Evergreen suite, which offers more liquid private market access, saw AUM surpass $16 billion, up over 70% year over year. This demonstrates successful product scaling and broadening client adoption.
Strategic partnership with Guardian completed: The partnership with Guardian Life Insurance closed at the end of December, adding nearly $5 billion in assets and setting up recurring annual commitments for the next decade. This will start contributing to results in the next quarter.
Negatives
Rising expenses: Total expenses increased 21% year over year, with compensation and benefits up 26%. This reflects higher headcount and bonus accruals, which could pressure margins if not matched by revenue growth.
Decreased incentive fee compensation: Incentive fee compensation fell compared to the prior year, largely due to lower carried interest revenue. This can introduce some earnings variability.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Initial financial impact of the Guardian partnership: The Guardian deal, which closed on Dec. 31, 2025, will begin to contribute to management fees and AUM in Q4. Investors should look for details on the size and profitability of these new mandates.
Evergreen platform growth and flows: The Evergreen suite has been a major growth engine. Watch for updated AUM, net inflows, and any commentary on institutional versus retail adoption, as well as the launch timing for the US registered credit fund.
Fee-earning AUM trajectory: The pace of net new assets, especially in specialized funds and customized separate accounts, will be key for future fee growth. Management’s outlook on fundraising and deployment will be important.
Expense management and FRE margin: With expenses rising, investors should monitor how the company balances investment in growth with maintaining or improving fee-related earnings margins.
Dividend policy and shareholder returns: The company is targeting a 10% dividend increase for fiscal 2026. Confirmation of this target and any commentary on future capital return plans will be important for income-focused investors.
