Notable Developments

• Adjusted EBITDA more than quadrupled from the prior-year period, reflecting stronger operating efficiency and higher sales volumes.

• Full-year guidance for revenue and gross margin was raised, with management now expecting $230 million to $233 million in revenue and 79% gross margin.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management now expects 2026 revenue in the range of $230 million to $233 million and gross margin near 79%, both slightly higher than prior forecasts. Leadership reiterated its aim for positive adjusted EBITDA for the year. Investors should watch the pace of new product adoption, reimbursement developments, and progress toward break-even free cash flow.

Quarterly highlights

Commercial momentum continued, with over 1,650 active U.S. physicians now working with SI-Bone products, an increase of 17% year over year. Average revenue per sales territory reached $2.2 million over the trailing 12 months. International revenue growth outpaced the U.S. on a percentage basis, benefiting from launches in Europe and Australia.

SI-Bone rolled out new devices, including the INTRA Ti system for ambulatory surgery centers and the iFuse TORQ TNT in Europe. The early-stage partnership with Smith+Nephew for trauma market access is underway and expected to contribute more meaningfully later in 2026.

Net loss narrowed to $4.3 million, a 33.8% year over year improvement. Free cash flow also improved, though it remains negative as the company continues to invest in sales infrastructure and research and development.

What SI-Bone does
