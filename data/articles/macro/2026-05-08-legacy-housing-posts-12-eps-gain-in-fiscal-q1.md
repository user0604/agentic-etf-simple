Legacy Housing Posts 12% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q1

May 8, 2026, 6:17 AM

Key Takeaways
Legacy Housing posted improved profits despite a drop in revenue.
Earnings per diluted share rose 12.2% year over year.
Management did not provide updated guidance but expects significant new order deliveries in the next quarter.

Legacy Housing (NASDAQ:LEGH) saw profits climb as it managed expenses and margins well, even though its sales dipped. The builder and financier of affordable manufactured homes reported first-quarter 2026 results on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. While revenue declined, operational efficiency and a focus on key growth channels supported the bottom line. Overall, the quarter showed resilient profitability, though softer revenue and lack of guidance suggest continued caution is warranted.

Notable developments
Profits increased even as revenue fell, with net income climbing 6.3% while revenue slipped by 3.7% from a year ago.
A single customer made a non-refundable $7.1 million deposit for a large workforce housing order, with deliveries set for the next quarter.
The company repurchased 30,740 shares for $573,000, while cash and credit access remained strong at quarter end.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue new financial guidance in this release but highlighted the $7.1 million workforce housing order as an important growth driver for the coming quarter. Continued focus on affordable housing demand and delivering on committed orders remains a strategic priority. Investors should watch execution on the major workforce housing delivery and any future commentary on sales channel performance or margin trends.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue declined 3.7% year over year, reflecting a shortfall in volume growth despite prior optimism from strong order pipelines mentioned in previous quarters. However, operational efficiencies or a favorable shift in sales mix led to gains in operating and net income, as seen in the improved earnings per share. Notably, the company secured a large new customer order and maintained a healthy balance sheet, with ample cash and virtually no borrowings drawn.

The $7.1 million deposit for workforce housing highlights strong demand in the affordable segment—a key focus for the company as nearly half of U.S. households fall into its target market for manufactured homes. This workforce housing order will begin deliveries in the second quarter of 2026, and management has highlighted it as an important opportunity for the company.

Legacy Housing kept up its share repurchases, buying back 30,740 shares, and ended the period with $14.1 million in cash and an undrawn credit line. While the company did not provide detail on loan portfolio metrics this quarter, management stated that loan performance continues to be strong, which is notable given ongoing challenges in consumer lending for this sector.

What Legacy Housing does

Legacy Housing manufactures and finances affordable manufactured homes, selling through company-owned stores, independent retailers, and various financing programs across 15 U.S. states.
