MEDP Company Overview
Medpace is a contract research organization that manages clinical trials to help biotechnology and pharmaceutical firms develop new medical drugs and devices. The company generates revenue by providing specialized laboratory testing, regulatory guidance, and patient recruitment services through a unified global operating model. This integrated approach creates high switching costs for clients who rely on its proprietary software and medical experts to navigate complex regulatory requirements during the multi-year development process.

Medpace Delivers Growth as Leadership Transition Begins

Medpace helps drug and device makers run clinical trials from the early planning phase through to government approval. The company stands out with its focus on full-service clinical development, attracting biopharma customers looking for streamlined support. Over the past 5 years, the stock returned 165.8%, outperforming the S&P 500 by 95.1 percentage points.
