MSCI Grows Revenue as Index Business Attracts More Clients

MSCI helps investors by creating stock market indexes, portfolio analysis tools, and data about companies’ sustainability. The company is known for its strong focus on innovation, especially in using artificial intelligence to improve its products. Over the past five years, MSCI’s stock returned 20.2%, trailing the S&P 500’s 71.9% gain by 51.7 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $850.8 million (up 14.1% YoY)
Adjusted EPS (Non-GAAP): $4.55 (up 13.8% YoY)
Overall recurring sales retention rate: 95.4% (high and stable YoY)

Latest Developments

Recurring sales to banks and hedge funds hit a record, showing that MSCI’s core index business keeps attracting major clients globally.
The Sustainability and Climate segment lost momentum, raising concerns about future growth as customer retention dropped and sales slowed in a competitive market.
