Business overview

Buckle operates retail stores across the United States, offering casual apparel, footwear, and accessories with a focus on denim and private label brands.

Quarterly performance

Sales increased 6.1% year over year, with comparable store sales up 5.1% and online sales up 2.8%. Net profit rose 33.2%, but this gain included a $19.1 million legal settlement that is not expected to repeat. Inventory climbed 13.4% even as revenue grew by only 6.1%, leading to potential risk if demand dips.

The period’s strong profit growth was boosted by the one-time $19.1 million benefit from settling interchange fee litigation, recognized as a reduction in selling expenses. While this improved net income figures, underlying business growth was less dramatic once adjusted for this event.

Sales growth stayed steady, led by comparable store gains and the opening of three new stores in strategic markets such as Wisconsin, Nebraska, and Arizona. Buckle ended the quarter with 442 stores after a net increase compared to the same period the prior year. Inventory levels increased sharply to $150.2 million, outpacing sales gains and raising questions about demand and merchandise planning for future quarters. The gross margin narrowed slightly compared to the previous year, suggesting some cost or pricing pressure in the product mix.

Looking ahead

Management did not provide forward earnings or sales guidance in this quarter’s release, consistent with recent company practice. The store opening and relocation plan remains on schedule, with three new locations opening and one closing after the quarter ended. Investors should closely monitor how Buckle manages inventory ahead of peak sales seasons and whether online sales growth can recover from its recent slowdown.
