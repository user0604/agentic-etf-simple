Notable Developments
Broadband revenue jumped 43% year over year, supported by a 78% surge in contributions from customers outside the top two accounts.
Operating profit in the broadband segment reached $20.4 million, nearly triple last year’s level, while segment gross margins slightly declined amid higher memory costs.
Cash and cash equivalents fell to $109.0 million, reflecting $43.0 million in share repurchases and ongoing investment in inventory ahead of expected demand.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management has raised its full-year 2026 broadband revenue target to a range of $475 million to $495 million, citing record backlog and resilient bookings. It expects stable to slightly lower gross margins, as higher component costs are only partly offset by greater software adoption. The most important areas to watch are customer diversification, the ramp in software-based sales, and the expected closing of the pending Video business sale.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the broadband division rose, exceeding management’s earlier guidance by $16.7 million at the high end, driven in part by growth with customers outside the top two accounts. The top two customers represented 58% of segment revenue, a reduction from 67% a year prior, showing real progress toward a more balanced customer base.

While operating profit nearly tripled compared to the prior-year period, the broadband segment’s gross margin dropped by 2.5 percentage points, largely due to rising memory and component costs. Management expects these headwinds to continue but points to a gradual benefit from higher-margin software and services as they increase in the mix.

The sale of Harmonic’s Video business is on track to close in the second quarter and is expected to provide $145 million in cash, reinforcing the company’s focus as a pure broadband specialist. Backlog and deferred revenue also stood at $582.1 million at quarter-end, offering strong support for future sales visibility.

What Harmonic Does

Harmonic provides software-based broadband access platforms and hardware solutions that enable cable and fiber network operators to deliver high-speed internet and video services to consumers and businesses.
