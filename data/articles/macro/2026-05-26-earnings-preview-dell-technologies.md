Earnings Preview: Dell Technologies

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Dell Technologies (NYSE:DELL), a major supplier of personal computers, servers, and storage systems, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

The fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was a record period for Dell Technologies, with revenue reaching $33.4 billion, up 39% year over year. Non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) came in at $3.89, up 45%. Both revenue and EPS beat analyst consensus, which had anticipated revenue near $31.9 billion and EPS around $3.52 to $3.54. The company’s performance was driven by surging demand for AI-optimized servers, continued growth in its infrastructure business, and solid execution in its personal computer (PC) division. Cash flow from operations also hit a record, and capital returns to shareholders accelerated.

Positives
AI-optimized server momentum was exceptional: AI server revenue grew more than fourfold year over year, with $34.1 billion in orders and $9.5 billion in shipments during the quarter. This reflects broad-based demand from cloud providers, governments, and enterprises, and contributed to a record $43 billion AI backlog exiting the quarter.
Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) delivered robust growth: ISG revenue rose 73% to $19.6 billion, marking eight consecutive quarters of double-digit growth. Traditional servers and networking revenue also grew 27% year over year, showing that AI demand is lifting broader IT infrastructure spending.
PC business gained market share: Client Solutions Group (CSG) revenue increased 14%, with commercial PC revenue up 16%. Dell expanded its buyer base and outperformed the overall PC market, which grew 10% in the same period.
Strong cash generation and capital returns: Operating cash flow for the quarter was $4.7 billion, supporting $2.2 billion in shareholder returns through buybacks and dividends. For the year, Dell returned $7.5 billion to shareholders and doubled its share repurchases compared to the prior year.
Negatives
Gross margin percentage declined: The overall gross margin rate fell to 20.5%, down from the prior year, largely due to a higher mix of lower-margin AI servers. This shift in product mix is expected to continue as AI hardware becomes a larger part of total sales.
Consumer PC revenue was flat: While commercial PCs performed well, consumer revenue showed little growth, reflecting weaker demand and pricing pressure in this segment.
Component cost inflation and supply constraints: The company faced higher input costs, especially for memory and storage components, and industry-wide supply tightness. These factors pressured margins and required rapid pricing adjustments, particularly in the PC business.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
AI server revenue and backlog conversion: Investors should monitor progress on converting the $43 billion AI server backlog into revenue. Management has guided to $13 billion in AI server revenue for the quarter, reflecting over 100% year-over-year growth in ISG.
Margin trends and pricing discipline: With ongoing component cost inflation, pay attention to gross margin rates and the effectiveness of recent pricing actions in both ISG and CSG. Dell has implemented shorter quote validity and dynamic pricing to offset rising costs.
PC market share and refresh cycle: Dell expects modest 2% growth in CSG revenue for the quarter, despite broader market headwinds. Watch for updates on commercial PC demand, share gains, and the impact of the ongoing refresh cycle.
Storage business performance: Storage revenue is expected to grow in the mid-single digits for the year, with a focus on Dell's proprietary platforms like PowerStore and PowerMax. Look for signs that storage growth is accelerating, particularly in all-flash arrays and AI-related workloads.
Status of new product cycles and technology transitions: Dell is preparing for the next wave of AI hardware, including the transition to Vera Rubin-based systems, which are expected to ship in the second half of the year. Updates on customer adoption and supply chain readiness will be important for assessing future growth potential.
