Key Takeaways
Ollie's Bargain Outlet delivered a business win with strong sales and earnings growth for the quarter. EPS climbed 21.3% year over year on an adjusted basis, highlighting robust profit performance. Management raised its full-year earnings outlook as margins and store expansion exceeded earlier plans.

Ollie's Bargain Outlet (NASDAQ:OLLI) started its new fiscal year with momentum, posting solid gains in both revenue and earnings. The discount retailer, known for selling closeout and excess inventory at deep discounts, continues to expand rapidly, adding more stores and growing customer loyalty through its Ollie’s Army program. Results for the quarter ended May 2, 2026, were released on June 3, 2026 and showed broad-based operational strength. Overall, the period saw higher margins and disciplined cost control, with store growth boosting revenue but some moderation in comparable sales growth.

Notable Developments
Gross margin rose to 41.9%, an improvement helped by lower supply chain costs and better merchandise sourcing.
The company opened 27 new stores, ending the quarter with 672 locations, signaling aggressive expansion.
Loyalty program membership climbed 12.6% year over year, reaching 17.5 million members and supporting recurring sales.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its adjusted earnings per share guidance for fiscal 2026, pointing to continued strength in gross margin and disciplined expenses. The outlook also reaffirms plans to open 75 new stores for the full year, with comparable sales expected to grow around 2%. For investors, the key issue is whether the company can sustain sales growth, especially as comparable sales growth slows and more revenue comes from new stores.

Quarterly Highlights

The company outperformed its own expectations on gross margin, which increased by 0.8 percentage points compared to the same quarter last year. This was attributed to improved sourcing of discounted products and efficiencies in the supply chain. Management’s strategy focuses on opportunistic buying, meaning it purchases inventory at reduced prices from closeouts or excess stock, helping maintain profitability.

Store expansion was a major driver, with 27 new locations added during the period. Store count grew 15.1% compared to a year ago, making new store openings the primary source of total sales growth, while comparable sales increased at a slower rate of 1.7%. There were no store closings in the quarter, and the company reiterated its full-year plan to open 75 new sites.

The Ollie's Army loyalty program expanded rapidly, up 12.6% year over year to 17.5 million members. This program is significant because it represents over 80% of company sales, showing that repeat shoppers remain highly engaged as store count grows.

What Ollie's Bargain Outlet Does

The company operates discount retail stores that sell brand name merchandise at reduced prices, sourcing products through closeouts and excess inventory deals from manufacturers and other retailers.
