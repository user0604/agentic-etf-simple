Earnings Preview: BK Technologies

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

BK Technologies (NYSEMKT:BKTI), a maker of two-way radios for public safety agencies, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 14, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The fourth quarter of 2025 capped a year of strong financial and operational performance for BK Technologies. The company reported revenue of $21.5 million for the quarter, representing a 20% increase year over year. This growth was driven by higher order volumes from state and local agencies, especially for the BKR Series radios. Gross margin expanded to 50.4%, up from 41.2% in the same quarter last year, reflecting a favorable product mix and increased sales of the higher-margin BKR 9,000 multiband radio. Adjusted EBITDA rose to $4.7 million, with a margin of 22%. Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share (EPS) reached $1.17, and the company ended the year with a record cash balance of $22.8 million and no debt. These results beat analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings, highlighting the company's momentum and operational improvements.

Positives
Significant revenue growth year over year: The 20% increase in fourth-quarter sales demonstrates strong demand for BK's products, especially from new and existing public safety customers.
Gross margin expansion to over 50%: The improvement in gross margin reflects a shift toward higher-margin products and successful cost management, which supports future profitability.
Record cash position and debt-free balance sheet: Ending 2025 with $22.8 million in cash and no debt gives BK flexibility for investment and shields it from interest rate risk.
Consistent operating leverage and profitability gains: Adjusted EBITDA margin exceeded 20% for the third straight quarter, showing that BK is scaling efficiently as it grows.
Negatives
Sequential revenue decline from Q3: Sales in Q4 were lower than in the previous quarter, reflecting typical order patterns in the public safety sector, but this may concern investors looking for steady quarter-to-quarter growth.
Higher operating expenses, especially SG&A: Selling, general, and administrative costs rose to $6.6 million, mainly due to increased marketing and stock-based compensation, which could pressure margins if not offset by further sales growth.
Tax rate normalization impacting future EPS: The company expects a higher effective tax rate in 2026, which will reduce reported earnings per share by about $0.55 compared to 2025.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress toward 2026 revenue and margin guidance: Investors should monitor whether Q1 sales and gross margin are on pace to meet the full-year targets of at least $90 million in revenue and 50% or higher gross margin.
BKR 9,000 sales momentum and customer adoption: Continued growth in BKR 9,000 shipments is key to BK's market share gains and margin expansion. Watch for updates on new agency wins and repeat orders.
Updates on BKR 9,500 development and launch timeline: The new BKR 9,500 mobile radio is still in development, with shipping expected in 2027. Any changes to this timeline or initial customer feedback could impact future growth expectations.
Operating expense trends and investment in R&D: Track how spending on marketing and engineering is evolving, especially as BK shifts to expensing development costs for the BKR 9,500, which will affect reported earnings in 2026.
Capital allocation and share repurchase activity: With a strong cash position and no debt, investors should look for updates on potential acquisitions, further share buybacks, or other uses of capital that could support shareholder value.
