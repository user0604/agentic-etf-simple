Anthropic IPO Filing Steals SpaceX's Thunder

Lou Whiteman

Jun 2, 2026, 8:47 AM

Comments
10.0


TEAM HIDDEN GEMS

On Monday, Anthropic submitted confidential IPO paperwork with the Securities and Exchange Commission. Elon Musk is likely hoping the filing remains confidential for at least the next two weeks.

Initial public offerings are like beauty pageants and social media profiles: You put the best version of yourself out there, hoping to distract from any flaws or shortcomings and instead focus on the positive.

In SpaceX’s case, that positive was the potential for its nascent AI business. SpaceX sees a $23 trillion potential market in enterprise AI. But it is almost entirely potential: SpaceX reported just $818 million in AI revenue in the first quarter of 2026, which was not enough for the unit to be profitable, even if capex is excluded.

We don’t yet know how Anthropic did during the quarter, but analysts believe the company is currently on pace to generate about $50 billion annually. That’s a comparison that SpaceX doesn’t want investors focused on as it gears up to sell $80 billion in stock.

The Anthropic filing would also provide the company’s perspective on its deal to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion per month for compute power. That deal was one of the highlights from SpaceX’s filing and should be a major driver of revenue for the SpaceX AI business this year. If there is any fine print yet to be revealed, it probably wouldn’t help SpaceX’s pitch.

Finally, we still don’t know what valuation Anthropic is targeting. It is tough to make an apples-to-apples comparison because SpaceX is a combination of AI and space, but if Anthropic values itself at less than SpaceX’s $1.8 trillion target, with an AI business that seems well out in front of SpaceX, it could make it harder for SpaceX to justify its target.

When two companies go public at the same time, there is inevitably some competition for dollars between the two of them. SpaceX has plenty to brag about. But given the relative maturity of the two AI businesses, SpaceX would likely rather Anthropic remain on the sidelines until its offering is out the door.
