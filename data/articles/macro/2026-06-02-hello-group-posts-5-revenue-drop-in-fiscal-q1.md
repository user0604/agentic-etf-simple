Hello Group (NASDAQ:MOMO) continued to face pressure as losses in its core domestic market offset strong overseas momentum. The social entertainment and dating platform operator, whose main brands include the Momo and Tantan apps, reported results for the first quarter of 2026 on June 2, 2026 (the quarter ended March 31). For the period, domestic contraction continued, overseas revenue growth helped somewhat, and overall profit and user numbers declined.

What Hello Group does

Hello Group operates mobile social networking and entertainment platforms focused on live streaming, audio- and video-based interactions, and dating services, primarily in China and select overseas markets.

Notable developments
Domestic net revenues from Chinese mainland fell 15.1% year over year in the first quarter of 2026, with paying users shrinking to 3.7 million for Momo and 0.6 million for Tantan.
Overseas revenue jumped 44.1%, now accounting for 24% of total sales, driven by strong growth in new audio and video apps.
Profitability weakened: net income attributable to shareholders dropped 18.7% and operating cash flow declined by a third.
Quarterly highlights

Domestic business continued to decline, with paying users on Momo falling year over year and sequentially, while paying users on Tantan fell year over year and remained flat sequentially. The company noted that most of the regulatory pressures on its home market have stabilized, but monetization challenges remain.

International expansion is now a major revenue driver, as audio and video-based products in regions such as the Middle East and North Africa posted substantial sales gains. International revenues accounted for approximately 25% of total group revenue in the first quarter of 2026, helping offset some of the domestic headwinds.

Despite stable operating income, net profits contracted more sharply, hurt by lower interest income and reduced returns from equity investments. Management paid a special dividend and continued share buybacks, which, along with weaker operating cash flow, shrank its cash cushion.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects second-quarter revenue between RMB2.45 billion and RMB2.55 billion, which would mean a year-over-year decrease of 6.5% to 2.7%. This signals hope that the contraction could slow, but ongoing user losses remain a concern. Watch user numbers and the pace of overseas revenue growth closely next quarter.
