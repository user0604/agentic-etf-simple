Earnings Preview: Beam Global

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 3:03 PM

Comments

Beam Global (NASDAQ:BEEM), which makes solar-powered charging and energy storage equipment for vehicles and city infrastructure, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Beam Global reported revenue of $9 million, which was a 7% increase year over year and a 56% increase compared to the third quarter. This sequential rebound followed a difficult period caused by a sharp drop in U.S. federal government orders for electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure. The company’s gross margin for the quarter was 18%. For the full year, revenue was $28.2 million, down 43% from 2024, mainly because of the loss of federal sales. However, Beam Global made significant progress in diversifying its customer base, with commercial customers making up 72% of 2025 revenue, up from 38% the previous year. International sales also grew, accounting for 42% of annual revenue. The company ended the year with $6 million in backlog, no debt, and access to a $100 million undrawn credit facility.

Positives
Strong sequential revenue growth: Revenue increased 56% from the prior quarter, showing the company’s ability to rebound after a challenging period for its main customer segment.
Shift to commercial and international markets: Commercial customers accounted for 72% of revenue and international sales made up 42%, reducing reliance on U.S. federal contracts and broadening the company’s market exposure.
Improved gross margins on an adjusted basis: Excluding non-cash items, adjusted gross margin improved to 23% for 2025 from 21% in 2024, reflecting better unit economics even with lower total sales.
Expanded product portfolio: 70% of Q4 revenue came from new or recently expanded products, including smart city infrastructure and energy storage solutions, indicating successful product diversification.
Negatives
Significant year-over-year revenue decline: Total revenue for 2025 fell 43% compared to 2024, mainly due to the loss of federal government sales, which is a risk for companies dependent on large customers.
Net loss increased: The company reported a net operating loss of $27.4 million for the year, higher than the previous year, driven by lower revenue and a non-cash goodwill impairment charge.
Gross margin pressure from lower volumes: Reported gross margin fell to 12.5% from 14.8% in 2024, as lower sales volumes led to less efficient use of fixed costs.
Cash balance decreased: Cash at year-end was $1 million, down from $4.6 million a year earlier, though working capital remained positive and management highlighted access to additional liquidity.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Revenue mix and growth from new products: Investors should look for continued momentum in sales from smart city infrastructure, energy storage, and international markets, as these areas drove most of Q4’s growth.
Gross margin trends and cost control: Watch for updates on gross margin improvement and whether cost reductions and process improvements are offsetting lower volumes, as management has targeted higher unit economics.
Backlog conversion and order pipeline: The company ended Q4 with $6 million in backlog and reported $9 million in backlog by March 31, 2026. Timely conversion of this backlog into revenue will be important for near-term results.
Status of Beam Middle East joint venture: The joint venture with Platinum Group in the United Arab Emirates (announced and operational since late 2025) is a key initiative. Any early contract wins or updates on regional expansion could affect growth expectations.
Product deployment and recurring revenue opportunities: Progress on integrating products like BeamSpot, BeamBike, and wireless AV charging into bundled solutions for customers may signal future recurring revenue streams and broader adoption.
