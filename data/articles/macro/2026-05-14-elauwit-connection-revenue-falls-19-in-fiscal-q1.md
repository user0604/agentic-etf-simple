Key Takeaways
Elauwit Connection posted a business setback as quarterly revenue declined and losses widened.
Revenue fell 19.0% year over year.
Management expects strong future growth driven by increased billed units and backlog.

Quarterly results from Elauwit Connection (NASDAQ:ELWT) revealed a business setback as revenue declined sharply while operating losses grew. The provider of high-speed internet and managed network services to multifamily housing reported financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026. Overall, the quarter saw record growth in future recurring revenue indicators, but short-term financial pressures increased.

Notable Developments
Revenue dropped by 19.0% year over year, as a result of “the timing of certain client construction and installation project revenues” that tend to be inconsistent.
Operating expenses surged 87%, outpacing gross profit, mainly due to investments in a new sales team and software upgrades.
Billed units leapt 114.8% to 20,059, reflecting a rapid shift toward recurring revenue and a larger customer base.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management sees significant future growth, citing sharp increases in contracted and billed units as well as a robust backlog now at $38.1 million. The most important metric to track next is whether the surge in billed units and pipeline activity translates into sustained revenue and improved margins in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue decreased from the prior-year period because construction and installation sales are uneven, though management emphasized that this does not reflect lost customers. While gross profit shrank, the company continued to ramp up operating expenses in building its sales force and infrastructure for future growth.

The number of billed units more than doubled, a sign that Elauwit’s recurring managed service and network subscriptions are now a larger part of its revenue base. Backlog—a measure of contracted future revenue—more than doubled year over year, providing improved visibility into future sales streams.

The new sales team secured verbal awards for around 40 properties in Q1, translating to more than 11,000 potential new units and support for the company’s national expansion plans. However, both net loss and cash burn increased sharply, making financial discipline and operating leverage critical in the near term.

What Elauwit Connection Does

Elauwit Connection provides high-speed internet and managed network services for multifamily apartment buildings and similar communities nationwide.
