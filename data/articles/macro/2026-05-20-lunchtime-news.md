1. Intuit Crunches Numbers, Cuts 17%

★ INTU is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team RB)

Intuit (NASDAQ: INTU) is laying off about 3,000 workers, or roughly 17% of staff, according to a Reuters report citing an internal memo from CEO Sasan Goodarzi. Shares fell as much as 5.9% in early trading. The company reports earnings today after market close.

Efficiency push: The cuts are aimed at reducing complexity and simplifying the company's structure. Intuit cut 10% of its workforce in July 2024 but said at the time it expected to rehire the same number in other areas.
Tech layoff wave: Intuit joins a growing list of tech companies slashing headcount this year. Meta Platforms (NASDAQ: META) began alerting thousands of employees Tuesday as part of a previously announced restructuring tied to heavy AI investment.

3. A $650 Million Bet Against Opioids

Medtronic (NYSE: MDT) plans to acquire privately held SPR Therapeutics for about $650 million in cash to expand its chronic pain treatment portfolio. The deal is expected to close in the first half of Medtronic's fiscal 2027, pending regulatory approval.

The product: SPR's SPRINT system delivers 60 days of pain relief without a permanent implant. The company focuses on non-opioid, non-surgical, minimally invasive pain management.
Strategic fit: The Dividend Investor stock said the deal will help it reach more patients with less invasive options, broadening its existing pain management franchise.
