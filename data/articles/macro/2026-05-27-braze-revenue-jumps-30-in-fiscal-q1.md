Notable Developments
Revenue growth accelerated for the fourth consecutive quarter, reaching $211.0 million and surpassing management’s outlook by over $5 million.
Non-GAAP operating income increased nearly fourfold year over year to $10.5 million, while gross margin declined to 67.4% from 69.3%.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now expects full-year revenue of $895–$899 million and non-GAAP operating income of $70–$74 million. Leaders pointed to strong demand for AI-powered solutions and higher customer engagement as key growth factors. Investors should watch gross margin, as increased messaging and onboarding costs continue to affect profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

Braze (NASDAQ:BRZE) expanded its total customer count to 2,713, a 16% increase from the prior year, and grew its large customer base (those with $500,000 or more in annual recurring revenue) by 33% to 349. This growth was driven by adoption of AI modules such as BrazeAI Operator and Agent Console, which help brands automate and personalize communications across digital channels.

Non-GAAP gross margin narrowed to 67.4% from 69.3% a year earlier, as higher messaging volume, onboarding, and hosting costs weighed on profitability. While operating income and cash flow improved, margin pressures remain an important area to monitor as new premium products are introduced.

Remaining performance obligations, which represent future contracted revenue not yet recognized, reached $1.08 billion, up from the previous quarter and supporting management’s outlook for future growth. During the period, Braze completed a $50 million share repurchase, demonstrating disciplined capital management.

What Braze Does

Braze provides a cloud-based platform that enables companies to deliver personalized, real-time customer engagement across digital channels using first-party data and artificial intelligence.
