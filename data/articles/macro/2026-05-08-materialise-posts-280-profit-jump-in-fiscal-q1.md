Materialise Posts 280% Profit Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 8, 2026, 7:19 AM

Key Takeaways
Materialise delivered a business win, with improved profitability despite essentially flat revenue.
Non-GAAP Adjusted EBIT surged 280.0% year over year, as gross margin widened and Medical segment sales grew.
Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance and will maintain strategic focus on Medical and Software while streamlining its portfolio.

Materialise (NASDAQ:MTLS) overcame stagnant sales to post a marked improvement in profitability during the first quarter. The global 3D printing software and services provider focuses on healthcare, industrial, and software applications across multiple regions. Reporting for the period ended March 31, 2026, and released on May 8, 2026, the results show a solid financial foundation and effective expense management, with Medical segment strength offsetting weaker trends elsewhere. Overall, the quarter balanced progress in margin improvement with flat top-line performance.

Notable Developments
Profitability increased as Adjusted EBIT reached €2.5 million, up from €0.6 million the prior year, mainly due to improved gross margin and stable operating expenses.
The Medical segment saw revenue rise 6.7%, to €33.2 million, accounting for half of overall sales, though its margin dipped to 27.8%, from 29.1%.
Portfolio streamlining accelerated: the Rapidfit divestiture closed, and the Eyewear business was spun out; management expects future impairment charges linked to these actions.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 outlook, targeting revenue of €273–283 million and Adjusted EBIT between €10–12 million for the full year, despite macroeconomic uncertainty. Its focus remains on core Medical and Software businesses, and high-value niches in Manufacturing. Investors should watch for signs of renewed growth in the Software and Manufacturing segments, as well as future Medical margin trends.

Quarterly Highlights

Materialise’s revenue remained flat, but operating profit and Adjusted EBIT increased sharply because of higher gross margin and disciplined spending. Gross margin expanded by 1.9 percentage points to 57.2%, as product mix and cost control improved, even as total sales held steady.

Medical segment performance anchored the quarter, with sales climbing nearly 7%, underlining the company’s reliance on healthcare applications for top-line growth. However, a slight contraction in Medical margin points to intensified competition or pricing pressures that could limit future profit expansion.

Strategic actions to sharpen the portfolio are reshaping the company. The disposal of Rapidfit and transfer of the Eyewear business signaled a stronger focus on the core areas, but will introduce one-off impairment impacts in coming quarters as per management guidance.
