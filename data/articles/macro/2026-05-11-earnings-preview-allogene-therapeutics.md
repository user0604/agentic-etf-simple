Earnings Preview: Allogene Therapeutics

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 3:03 PM

Comments

Allogene Therapeutics (NASDAQ:ALLO), a biotechnology company developing off-the-shelf cell therapies for cancer and autoimmune diseases, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Allogene Therapeutics reported its fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 results on Mar. 12, 2026. The company posted a net loss of $38.8 million for the quarter, or $0.17 per share, which met analyst consensus estimates for earnings per share. Revenue remained negligible, in line with expectations, as the company is still in the clinical stage and does not yet have approved products on the market. Operating expenses declined compared to the prior year, reflecting a strategic shift to focus resources on its most promising programs. Allogene ended the year with $258.3 million in cash, cash equivalents, and investments, and management expects this cash runway to last into 2028.

Positives
Cash position extended into 2028: The company’s cash and investments, bolstered by additional equity raised and proceeds from arbitration, provide financial stability to advance key trials over the next several years.
Focused clinical priorities: Management has concentrated resources on the ALPHA-3 and RESOLUTION trials, which target large B-cell lymphoma and autoimmune diseases, respectively. This prioritization could improve execution and data quality.
Progress in pivotal ALPHA-3 trial: The ALPHA-3 study, testing cema-cel (formerly ALLO-501A) as a first-line consolidation therapy for large B-cell lymphoma, has over 60 active sites and is on track for an interim analysis in April 2026.
Operational discipline after workforce reduction: The company completed a 28% workforce reduction in mid-2025, reducing costs and aligning staffing with its narrowed focus. Inventory of key clinical products is sufficient for current trials.
Negatives
Continued operating losses: Allogene remains unprofitable, with a net loss of $38.8 million for Q4 and $190.9 million for the full year 2025. The company expects to continue incurring losses until products reach the market.
No product revenue yet: Sales remain near zero because all programs are still in clinical development. This is typical for early-stage biotech but means the company is reliant on external funding.
Adverse event led to protocol change: The ALPHA-3 trial closed one treatment arm after a serious adverse event linked to the use of ALLO-647, a monoclonal antibody. The study now proceeds with a different, standard lymphodepletion regimen.
Ongoing competitive and regulatory risks: The company faces uncertainty around evolving treatment standards and regulatory pathways, especially as new therapies and diagnostics enter the lymphoma and autoimmune disease spaces.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
ALPHA-3 interim results and MRD clearance data: Investors should look for the April 2026 interim analysis from the ALPHA-3 trial, which will report on minimal residual disease (MRD) clearance rates and early safety data. This is a key milestone that could validate the company’s approach in first-line large B-cell lymphoma.
Clinical progress for ALLO-329 in autoimmune diseases: Updates on the RESOLUTION trial, including patient enrollment and any early signals, will be important ahead of the expected proof-of-concept data in June 2026. The trial is testing a dual-target CAR T cell therapy with the potential to work with minimal or no chemotherapy.
Cash burn and expense trends: Watch for updates on operating cash expense guidance (expected to be about $150 million for 2026) and whether the company is maintaining its projected cash runway into 2028.
Status of partnerships and geographic expansion: Any new or expanded collaborations, especially in Europe, Asia, or with diagnostic partners, could affect the company’s clinical and commercial prospects. The company has ongoing regulatory and operational activities in Australia, South Korea, and the EU.
Updates on TRAVERSE and other pipeline programs: While not the main focus, any news on the TRAVERSE trial in renal cell carcinoma or preclinical pipeline advances could provide additional context for Allogene’s long-term strategy.
