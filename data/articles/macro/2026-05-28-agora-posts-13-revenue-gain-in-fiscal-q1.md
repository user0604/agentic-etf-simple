Key Takeaways
Agora delivered strong revenue growth in the quarter but faced margin pressure from higher costs and subscale AI products.
Revenue climbed 13.5% year over year to $37.7 million.
Management guides for further revenue acceleration next quarter but does not update margin guidance.

Cloud communications provider Agora (NASDAQ:API) outpaced its own revenue outlook in the first quarter, while margin compression remained a headwind. The company, a global platform for embedding real-time video, voice, and AI into apps, achieved its sixth straight quarter of net profitability, according to results released May 28, 2026, for the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter reflected continued expansion in top-line growth and customer metrics, even as conversational AI investments dampened margins.

Notable Developments
Revenue reached $37.7 million, topping the company’s prior guidance range of $36.0–$37.0 million due to strong usage growth in live shopping, entertainment, and financial services.
Gross margin declined to 63.4%, a drop of 4.6 percentage points from the prior year, as costs for bandwidth and conversational AI infrastructure rose faster than revenues.
The company launched Agent Studio, a no-code platform for building AI voice agents, supporting its push into conversational AI and driving customer growth to nearly 4,000 active users.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects further revenue growth for Q2 2026, forecasting $39.0–$40.0 million and indicating expectations for continued organic acceleration. Gross margin or profitability outlook remains unchanged, with no new details provided on when conversational AI products might reach scale to ease margin pressure. Investors should watch the pace at which AI product economics improve, as this will be crucial to future margin recovery and sustained profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

Top-line expansion outpaced company guidance, with revenue up 13.5% year over year, reflecting strong underlying demand for real-time engagement tools in shopping, entertainment, and finance apps. Customer retention showed improvement, as the dollar-based net retention rate, which tracks growth from existing clients, reached 99% versus 95% a year ago.

Margin pressure was notable, driven by a 29.9% year-over-year increase in cost of revenues, due especially to higher infrastructure expenses and the early-phase economic profile of conversational AI products. Gross profit grew more slowly than revenue, and the company’s gross margin reached its lowest level in at least five quarters.

Agora continues to invest in its AI platform, launching Agent Studio, its no-code builder for voice AI agents, as part of its strategy to expand platform capabilities. While conversational AI remains an area of heavy R&D spending and limited scale, management’s continued focus on innovation, customer expansion, and share buybacks underscores commitment to long-term growth.

What Agora Does

Agora provides a cloud platform that enables developers to embed real-time voice, video, and AI-powered engagement features into applications for a variety of industries worldwide.
