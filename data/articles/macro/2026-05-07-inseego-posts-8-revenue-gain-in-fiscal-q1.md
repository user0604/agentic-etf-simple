Wireless broadband provider Inseego (NASDAQ:INSG) advanced its product pipeline and secured new carrier partnerships, but higher investment spending weighed on short-term profitability. The company specializes in 5G fixed wireless access and intelligent edge connectivity for mobile operators, enterprises, and service providers. On May 7, 2026, it reported results for the fiscal first quarter ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the period reflected management’s plan for front-loaded investment and set the stage for anticipated growth later in the year.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 8% year over year, with stronger sales in fixed wireless access offsetting a drop in mobile solutions revenue.
Operating expenses rose 31%, driven by a jump in research and development as well as sales and marketing spending.
Inseego announced plans to acquire Nokia’s fixed wireless access business, a deal expected to double its annual revenue after closing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its target of about $190 million in full-year 2026 revenue, with a significant ramp expected in the second half. Investments in product development and go-to-market activities are concentrated early in the year, so the launch of new products and integration of the Nokia acquisition will be important. Investors should watch how quickly these efforts translate into higher revenue and whether margin expansion occurs as planned.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue for the quarter reached $34.3 million, supported by growth in enterprise fixed wireless access and software and services, which helped offset lower mobile hotspot sales. Adjusted EBITDA declined as operating costs increased, with research and development up 28% and sales and marketing up 43% compared to the prior year, reflecting investment in new product innovation and customer acquisition.

Gross margin improved to 48.3%, marking the fifth consecutive quarter above 40%, due to a greater mix of software and services and disciplined pricing. Despite this margin progress, the company reported a wider net loss as it accelerated spending to support future growth.

The company announced a planned acquisition of Nokia’s fixed wireless access portfolio, which, if completed, could double annual revenue and expand relationships with major carrier customers. The deal also includes a new cash investment and would make Nokia a significant shareholder.

What Inseego Does

Inseego provides 5G and wireless connectivity devices and software, enabling mobile operators and enterprises to deliver secure, high-speed internet over cellular networks.
