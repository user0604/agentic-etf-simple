AST SpaceMobile (NASDAQ:ASTS) advanced its strategic goals this quarter as it achieved a major U.S. regulatory clearance and expanded its global telecom partnerships. The mobile satellite communications company is building a direct-to-smartphone broadband network using satellites, targeting coverage for areas underserved by traditional cellular networks. For the first quarter of fiscal 2026, reported on May 11, 2026, AST SpaceMobile saw a marked increase in revenue but a widening net loss as it accelerated manufacturing and deployment. Overall, the quarter reflected important progress toward a commercial launch, but the business remains highly capital-intensive and loss-making as it continues to scale.

Notable developments
The company received authorization from the FCC to offer its commercial SpaceMobile Service in the United States, supporting market entry plans for 2026.
Revenue soared from $0.7 million to $14.7 million year over year, mainly from gateway equipment sales and U.S. government contract milestones rather than recurring services.
Cash on hand ended the quarter at $3.5 billion, after significant financing activity, with long-term debt rising to $3.0 billion to fund its satellite deployment and manufacturing scale-up.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue outlook of $150 to $200 million, noting that nearly half of this figure is backed by existing contracts. The company emphasized the importance of ramping up commercial service launches later in the year. Investors should monitor the pace of satellite deployment and the transition from milestone-based to recurring service revenue.

Quarterly highlights

This quarter, AST SpaceMobile achieved a key regulatory milestone as the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) granted permission to begin direct-to-device satellite broadband in the U.S. This unlocks access to the American mobile market and allows commercial operations for up to 248 satellites.

Revenue increased nearly twentyfold year over year, but most of this growth came from equipment deliveries and government projects, not recurring service subscriptions. Commercial service activation is targeted for later in 2026, which will be critical for meeting annual revenue guidance.

The company’s global partner network grew to nearly 60 mobile network operators, now covering more than 3 billion subscribers, while new technology milestones were reached, including peak data rates near 100 Mbps from its Block 1 satellites and expectations to nearly double these speeds with Block 2 satellites already in orbit.

What AST SpaceMobile does

AST SpaceMobile builds and operates satellites designed to deliver cellular broadband internet directly to standard mobile devices without extra hardware.
