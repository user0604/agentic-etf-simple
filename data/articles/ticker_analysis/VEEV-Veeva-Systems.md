VEEV Company Overview
Veeva provides cloud-based software and data services designed specifically for the life sciences industry, supporting functions from drug research to commercial sales. The company generates revenue primarily through subscription fees for its specialized applications and professional consulting services. By integrating regulatory compliance tools and clinical data management into a unified platform, the company creates high switching costs for pharmaceutical and biotechnology firms that rely on its deeply embedded digital infrastructure.

Veeva Gains Customers With New Life Sciences Software

Veeva Systems makes cloud-based software that helps drug companies manage research, regulations, and sales work. The company stands out by focusing only on life sciences, including new tools powered by artificial intelligence and a growing Vault CRM platform. Over the last five years, the stock lost 38.1%, underperforming the S&P 500 by 116.6 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $882.9 million (up 16.4% YoY)
EPS (Non-GAAP): $2.24 (up 13.7% YoY)
Subscription Revenue: $730.2 million (up 15.0% YoY)

Latest Developments

Veeva added 27 new Vault CRM customers this quarter, showing momentum as more drug companies start using its newest digital tools.
Management expects double-digit growth for the full year but says growth may slow, highlighting the need to watch how new products like AI tools are received.
