Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled confidence about launching a pivotal Phase 3 trial for its lead asset, supported by a recent $405 million equity financing. The company did not provide revenue or profitability guidance but pointed to its ability to operate for several years without additional capital needs. The key thing to monitor next will be updates on Phase 3 trial design and progress toward regulatory engagement.

Quarterly Highlights

This quarter’s clinical milestone came from the LOTUS Phase 2 trial of abdakibart, Avalo’s investigational monoclonal antibody for moderate to severe hidradenitis suppurativa, where the drug showed a 42.5% response versus 25.6% for placebo on the main endpoint (HiSCR75). These results exceeded the company’s previous targets and outperformed benchmarks set by comparable studies, according to management.

Research and development expenses increased sharply to $14.0 million due to the scale and requirements of the LOTUS clinical program, reflecting the company’s focus on advancing abdakibart toward late-stage development. General and administrative spending also climbed as the company added staff and prepared for larger operations.

At quarter’s end, Avalo had $82.0 million in cash and investments, but a significant equity financing completed in May added approximately $405 million in new funds. This financial boost means management expects to fund operations through 2029, supporting planned Phase 3 trials and future commercialization steps.

The company is a biotechnology firm developing innovative antibody-based therapies for immune and inflammatory diseases, with a primary focus on hidradenitis suppurativa.
