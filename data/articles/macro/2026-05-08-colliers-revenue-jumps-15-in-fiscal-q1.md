Key Takeaways
Colliers International Group delivered robust growth as commercial real estate and engineering revenue expanded but margins remained pressured by higher costs.
Revenue rose year over year by 15%.

Colliers International Group (NASDAQ:CIGI) advanced its growth strategy with solid gains in core commercial and engineering segments, but earnings margins declined due to increased costs and ongoing integration of new businesses. The global diversified real estate services provider reported its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 8, 2026. Overall, the period saw strong revenue expansion balanced by rising expenses and temporary margin pressure.

Notable developments
Revenue rose 15% from the prior year, aided by both internal growth and acquisitions, with commercial real estate up 14% and engineering up 23%.
Adjusted EBITDA margin dropped to 9.5%, reflecting investment in investment management and higher integration costs across newly acquired operations.
The company completed or announced several acquisitions, including the $700 million Ayesa Engineering deal in Spain, expanding its European footprint and capabilities.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for mid-teens percentage growth in revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted EPS for fiscal 2026. The main things to watch are how well Colliers integrates its recent and pending acquisitions and whether margin pressure from higher costs and subdued cash flow begins to ease in coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Commercial real estate services drove much of the revenue growth, with particularly strong gains in capital markets and leasing. While the segment benefited from better transactional activity, project management revenue declined due to changes in how services are reported, not from demand issues.

Engineering delivered its strongest revenue growth rate, jumping 23% year over year with important support from acquisitions. This included the planned $700 million Ayesa Engineering acquisition, which will further boost Colliers’ scale in the sector but temporarily increase its debt and integration costs.

Investment management continued to scale assets under management, which reached $109.3 billion, but profitability dipped as Colliers invested in integration and modernization. Margins in this segment are expected to recover after these initiatives complete.

What Colliers International Group does

Colliers International Group is a global provider of diversified real estate services, including brokerage, property management, engineering, and investment management for corporate and institutional clients across multiple regions.
