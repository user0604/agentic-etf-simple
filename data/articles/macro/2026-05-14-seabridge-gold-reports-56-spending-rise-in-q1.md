Seabridge Gold (NYSE:SA) reported a business setback as it moved from a profit to a loss in the first quarter. The mineral exploration and development company focuses on large-scale gold and copper projects in North America. Quarterly performance

Seabridge Gold increased its spending on mineral exploration and development, with most of the $22.3 million invested at KSM. This spending was up nearly 56% from the prior year and included updating KSM’s mineral resources using industry-standard metal prices to improve comparability with other major mining companies.

The company reported its first mineral resource at the Snip North deposit, adding to its overall mineral inventory and supporting its exploration goals. It also announced a shareholder meeting to approve the planned spin-out of the Courageous Lake project, which would simplify its project portfolio.

Seabridge Gold raised $35.8 million through its at-the-market program, resulting in a working capital balance of $131.3 million at the end of the quarter. However, higher administrative expenses and a non-cash loss on secured notes contributed to the net loss compared to the previous year.

Looking ahead

Management is focused on advancing the KSM project toward a partnership and moving forward with the Courageous Lake spin-out. The next key event for investors is the decision on the Courageous Lake separation, along with updates on KSM partnerships or regulatory approvals. The company’s financial stability will depend on successful project development and continued access to capital as spending increases.
