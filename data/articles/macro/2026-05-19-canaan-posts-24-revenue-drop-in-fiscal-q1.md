Management outlook and what to watch

Management guided for further revenue contraction in the second quarter, projecting $35 million to $45 million as market and Bitcoin price pressures persist. The company will focus on scaling its sustainable mining infrastructure and controlling costs. Investors should watch mining operations growth and progress on green mining initiatives as key signals of strategic resilience.

Quarterly highlights

Product revenue, mainly from Bitcoin mining machines, suffered a steep drop as weaker Bitcoin prices dampened demand and triggered significant inventory write-downs. The product line had benefited in previous quarters from large North American orders, but lumpy sales could not offset an overall decline.

The company’s mining segment generated $19.1 million in revenue, showing some stability even as Bitcoin price volatility and weather limited mining output. Installed mining computing power rose over 10% sequentially, boosting overall self-mining capacity in a period of sector softness.

Operating expenses were curtailed, dropping to $31.4 million from $38.3 million a year earlier, reflecting tight cost control. However, large non-cash charges linked to the falling value of company cryptocurrency holdings and derivative contracts contributed to widening overall losses in the period.
