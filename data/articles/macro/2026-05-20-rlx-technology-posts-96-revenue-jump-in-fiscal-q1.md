RLX Technology (NYSE:RLX) strengthened its market position with a surge in sales but faced mixed signals from its cash flow. Notable Developments
Revenue nearly doubled to RMB 1,585.8 million, driven by international sales now representing 72.3% of total revenue.
Gross margin expanded by 3.2 percentage points due to supply chain improvements and product mix changes.
Operating cash flow turned negative at RMB 68.8 million, down from a positive RMB 207.2 million in the previous year’s first quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects continued growth by focusing on global expansion and new product innovation, especially in Europe. However, it urges caution as some revenue drivers, like recent export policy changes, may not recur.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales growth far outpaced last year, with nearly all of the increase coming from outside China due to a successful European acquisition. International operations now anchor the revenue mix, showing RLX’s shift from a China-centric profile to a global one.

Profitability metrics rose across the board, with gross and operating margins improving. These gains resulted from supply chain optimization efforts at its “Super Factory” and positive shifts in product and regional mix.

Despite strong profits, the company reported negative operating cash flow as working capital needs and expense growth accelerated with expansion. Management gave no numeric guidance and did not explain the sudden outflow, signaling this as a critical watch area for future reports.

What Rlx Technology Does

RLX Technology designs, manufactures, and sells branded electronic vapor devices and related products primarily for adult smokers.
