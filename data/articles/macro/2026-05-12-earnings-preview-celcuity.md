Earnings Preview: Celcuity

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Celcuity (NASDAQ:CELC), a biotechnology company developing targeted therapies for solid tumors, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Celcuity reported a net loss of $51 million, or $0.97 per share, which was wider than the prior year’s net loss of $36.7 million, or $0.85 per share. The company’s non-GAAP adjusted net loss was $38.4 million, or $0.73 per share. Research and development expenses increased to $37.6 million, reflecting ramp-up for commercial launch activities and ongoing clinical trials. General and administrative expenses also rose to $11.6 million, mainly due to higher employee and consulting costs. Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments at year-end totaled $441.5 million, which management expects will fund operations through 2027.

The quarter was marked by significant clinical and regulatory progress. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) accepted Celcuity’s New Drug Application (NDA) for gedatolisib, its lead therapy for advanced breast cancer, and granted it priority review with a Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target date of July 17, 2026. The company completed enrollment for both wild-type and mutant cohorts in its pivotal Phase III VIKTORIA-1 trial and published positive data for the wild-type cohort in a leading medical journal. Operationally, Celcuity largely completed its commercial build-out in preparation for a potential drug launch. The Q4 results met analyst expectations for earnings per share.

Positives
FDA acceptance and priority review for gedatolisib NDA: The FDA’s acceptance of the NDA and assignment of a July 17, 2026 PDUFA date is a major regulatory milestone, increasing the likelihood of a timely approval and commercial launch.
Strong clinical data for VIKTORIA-1 wild-type cohort: Gedatolisib demonstrated a statistically significant and clinically meaningful progression-free survival benefit, setting new benchmarks for second-line advanced breast cancer treatment after CDK4/6 inhibitor therapy.
Completion of commercial infrastructure build: The company finished building its sales force and internal systems, positioning itself for a smooth transition to a commercial-stage company if approval is granted.
Solid cash position: With $441.5 million in cash and investments, Celcuity is well-capitalized to support ongoing trials, launch preparations, and potential commercialization.
Negatives
Widening net loss year over year: Increased spending on research, development, and commercial readiness led to a larger net loss, which may concern investors focused on near-term profitability.
No product revenue yet generated: Celcuity remains pre-commercial with no sales to date, so its financials are entirely dependent on investment and financing activities until potential drug approval.
Rising operating expenses: Both research and development and general and administrative costs grew significantly, reflecting the company’s transition but also increasing cash burn.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress toward VIKTORIA-1 mutant cohort data release: Topline results for the PIK3CA mutant cohort are expected in the second quarter of 2026. Investors should look for any updates on timing or regulatory plans related to this critical milestone.
Updates on VIKTORIA-2 trial and study design: The safety run-in for the Phase III VIKTORIA-2 trial (first-line endocrine-resistant breast cancer) has been completed. Watch for details on the final study design and enrollment progress, as these will shape future growth opportunities.
Commercial launch readiness for gedatolisib: With the NDA under priority review, management’s commentary on payer outreach, physician education, and market access efforts will be key indicators of launch preparedness.
