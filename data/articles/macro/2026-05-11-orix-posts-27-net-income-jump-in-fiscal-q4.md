Orix Posts 27% Net Income Jump in Fiscal Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 1:53 PM

Key Takeaways
Orix reported a business win with broad-based profit growth, particularly strong in renewables and private equity.
Revenue rose 15.9% year over year.
Management forecasts continued net income growth but warns that recent gains are partly nonrecurring.

Orix (NYSE:IX) outpaced its goals, with earnings boosted by gains in its renewable energy and private equity businesses. The diversified financial services and investment group operates globally, offering everything from leasing to asset management and banking. In its earnings release dated May 11, 2026, covering the quarter ended March 31, Orix posted double-digit growth across key income metrics. Overall, the quarter delivered a strong rebound, though a notable share of profit came from one-off asset sales.

Notable Developments
Net income attributable to shareholders climbed 27.2%, beating the company’s full-year forecast by more than ¥7 billion.
Segment profits increased in 7 out of 10 major divisions, with standout turnarounds in Environment and Energy and gains in private equity investments.
Orix increased its annual dividend by 30%, setting payout at ¥156.10 per share and expanded its share repurchase program.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects net income to rise another 18.5% to ¥530 billion in fiscal 2027, with a maintained dividend payout ratio of 39%. However, leaders caution that a large portion of this year’s profits came from capital gains and investment sales, which may not repeat. Investors should watch the mix of recurring profits versus one-off earnings as the key risk for the coming year.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw revenue and net income expand significantly, driven by nonrecurring investment gains, including the transfer of Greenko Energy Holdings shares and strong private-equity exits. Management confirmed that profits rose in most segments, particularly Environment and Energy, which swung from a prior-year loss to a remarkable profit, mostly due to asset sales and reduced asset write-downs.

Orix's Real Estate and Insurance segments saw steady advances, with higher income from management fees and insurance premiums. Conversely, Banking & Credit and Orix USA reported declines in profit, mainly due to reduced investment gains in Banking & Credit and increased impairment and loss provisions in Orix USA, marking them as areas requiring closer attention.

Shareholder returns remained a focus. The annual dividend was raised by 30%, and the company accelerated share repurchases, with most of its ¥150 billion buyback program already completed by January. This increases the return of capital to investors even as management signals some caution about how repeatable this year's gains will be.

What Orix Does

Orix is a diversified global financial group offering services such as leasing, asset management, banking, real estate, insurance, private equity, and renewable energy solutions.
