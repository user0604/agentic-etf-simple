Earnings Preview: STAAR Surgical

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 1:03 PM

Comments

STAAR Surgical (NASDAQ:STAA), a company that makes implantable lenses for vision correction procedures, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, May 13, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, STAAR Surgical reported total net sales of $57.8 million, an increase from $49 million in the same quarter last year. The company saw a partial recovery in China, its largest market, with net sales there rising to $17.5 million from $7.8 million a year ago. However, outside of China, net sales declined by 2% year over year. The Americas region grew 18%, while Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) sales fell 20% due to distributor transitions and merger-related uncertainty. Gross profit margin improved to 75.7% from 64.7% last year, reflecting cost reductions and the impact of earlier inventory actions in China. Adjusted EBITDA showed a significant improvement, with a loss of $200,000 compared to a $20.8 million loss in the prior year period. The company ended the quarter with $187.5 million in cash and no debt. Compared to analyst expectations, STAAR's Q4 results missed consensus estimates for both sales and earnings.

Positives
China inventory normalization positions for growth: Distributor inventory in China was reduced to contractual levels by year-end, which should allow sales to better reflect actual demand in 2026.
Gross margin expansion reflects operational improvements: Margin gains were driven by cost reductions and the timing of prior inventory shipments, showing that cost control measures are taking effect.
Cash balance remains strong despite headwinds: The company maintained a robust cash position with no debt, providing flexibility to invest in growth and innovation.
Launch of EVO+ lens in China completed: The first new lens in over a decade for China has been launched, with early demand described as encouraging and production scaling up in Switzerland.
Negatives
Q4 results missed analyst estimates: Both revenue and adjusted earnings fell short of consensus, reflecting ongoing operational and market challenges.
Sales outside China declined year over year: EMEA was especially weak, impacted by distributor transitions and uncertainty from the terminated Alcon merger.
Merger and restructuring costs weighed on profitability: The company incurred $11.9 million in one-time expenses related to the terminated Alcon merger and restructuring efforts.
Operating expenses remain elevated as a percent of sales: Even after cost cuts, total operating expenses were higher than sales in the quarter, highlighting the need for further scaling and efficiency.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
China sales rebound and inventory dynamics: Investors should monitor whether normalized distributor inventory and improved end-market demand in China translate into higher reported sales, especially given the typical seasonal strength in the second quarter.
Progress on cost discipline and operating leverage: Watch for evidence that the company is maintaining its reduced expense run rate and beginning to achieve profitability, as targeted for full-year 2026.
Adoption and pricing of EVO+ in China: Early demand for the new EVO+ lens and its impact on average selling prices and margins will be key, as management expects this product to contribute to both growth and profitability.
U.S. and ex-China market momentum: Look for signs of continued double-digit growth in the U.S. and stabilization or recovery in other international markets, following the disruption from the failed Alcon merger.
Status of innovation and operational initiatives: Updates on the rollout of the Lioli injector in EMEA, the Oracle ERP system implementation (expected to complete early in the second half of 2026), and the Stella online ordering platform will indicate progress on long-term efficiency and innovation goals.
