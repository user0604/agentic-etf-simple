EnerSys Posts 7% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q4

Key Takeaways
EnerSys delivered solid adjusted earnings growth but faced margin pressures and muted volume in key segments.
Adjusted diluted EPS rose 7% to $3.19 for the quarter.
Management expects moderate sales and earnings for the next quarter, with innovation and cost actions ongoing.

EnerSys (NYSE:ENS) posted a resilient quarter, delivering higher adjusted earnings even as sales volumes lagged in several segments. The leading provider of stored energy solutions for industrial applications reported its results for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 20, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed disciplined execution but highlighted margin and volume challenges under the surface.

Notable Developments
Adjusted diluted EPS increased 7.4%, while gross margin declined by 1.8 percentage points, reflecting higher costs and ongoing restructuring.
Organic sales volume dropped 6% year over year as gains from price and foreign exchange offset underlying weakness in Motive Power and some industrial end markets.
Free cash flow improved 25% to $131.2 million, enabling $78.9 million to be returned to shareholders through buybacks and an 8% higher dividend.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects first-quarter fiscal 2027 sales of $915 million–$955 million and adjusted diluted EPS of $2.70–$2.90, including credits from production tax incentives (IRC 45X). The company continues to prioritize growth in lithium and energy storage solutions while driving cost improvements. Investors should watch for progress in Motive Power order recovery and margin stabilization as the year unfolds.

Quarterly Highlights

Adjusted earnings rose from the prior-year period, mainly due to a 4% increase in pricing and a 3% lift from foreign currency translation. These benefits helped offset a notable 6% drop in organic sales volume as demand in some industrial and transportation markets remained muted. Management highlighted progress in new technology — such as lithium solutions for data centers and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) — now entering customer trials and commissioning.

The gross margin fell to 29.4%, down 1.8 percentage points from last year, reflecting cost inflation and $19.7 million in restructuring charges. These one-time costs are part of an ongoing strategy to optimize the manufacturing footprint, including the accelerated plant transition from Monterrey to Kentucky. Segment performance was mixed: Energy Systems and Specialty both showed margin improvements and higher sales, while Motive Power continued to face volume pressure and profits were roughly flat year over year.

EnerSys demonstrated strong free cash flow, up 25% year over year, and made sizeable capital returns to shareholders. Share repurchases jumped to $69.3 million for the quarter, while the quarterly dividend rose 8% to $0.26 per share. The company exited the quarter with a net leverage ratio of 1.1x and $438.7 million in cash, giving it further flexibility to invest in innovation and capital projects.

What EnerSys Does

EnerSys manufactures and supplies industrial batteries and energy storage systems for applications across data centers, telecommunications, motive power, aerospace, defense, and other sectors worldwide.
