Nutanix (NASDAQ:NTNX), a provider of software that helps businesses manage data and applications across private data centers and public clouds, is expected to report its third-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q2 FY2026 Recap

In the second quarter of fiscal 2026, Nutanix reported revenue of $723 million, which was above its guidance range and beat analyst estimates. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) grew 16% year over year to $2.36 billion. The company added more than 1,000 new customers, marking its strongest quarterly new customer additions in eight years. Non-GAAP operating margin reached 26.2%, also above guidance, and free cash flow was $191 million. However, Nutanix began to see a more challenging supply environment for server components, which affected the timing of some revenue and cash flow. The company updated its full-year outlook to reflect these supply chain impacts, but emphasized that the underlying business demand and bookings remain strong.

Positives
Revenue and earnings beat expectations: Nutanix’s Q2 revenue and non-GAAP EPS both came in above consensus estimates, showing resilience in customer demand despite a tougher hardware supply backdrop.
Strong customer growth: The company added over 1,000 new customers, its best quarterly result in eight years, which suggests continued success in winning new business, especially as customers look for alternatives to legacy infrastructure providers.
Solid free cash flow and margin performance: Free cash flow margin was 26% and non-GAAP operating margin was 26.2%, both reflecting efficient operations and cost management even as the company continues to invest in growth initiatives.
Progress on strategic partnerships and product enhancements: Nutanix announced a multi-year strategic partnership with AMD (pending completion), aimed at developing an enterprise AI platform, and delivered general availability of support for EverPure (formerly Pure Storage) in its platform.
Negatives
Supply chain constraints delayed revenue recognition: Shortages of CPUs and memory led to longer lead times for servers, delaying some customers’ ability to deploy Nutanix software and shifting a portion of expected revenue and free cash flow out of the current fiscal year.
Net dollar-based retention rate (NRR) declined: NRR fell to 107% from 109% a year earlier, partly due to timing delays in renewals, especially in the U.S. federal business, and the impact of shifting revenue recognition.
Guidance for Q3 below consensus: The company’s Q3 revenue guidance of $680 million to $690 million was below analyst consensus, reflecting ongoing supply chain headwinds and a higher mix of bookings with future start dates.
What to Watch in Q3 FY2026's Report
Resolution of supply chain delays: Investors should look for updates on server component availability and whether Nutanix’s mitigation efforts—such as offering more hardware choices and public cloud options—are helping to reduce deployment delays and accelerate revenue recognition.
Customer growth and renewal trends: Pay attention to the pace of new customer additions and the net dollar-based retention rate, especially as delayed renewals from the federal sector are expected to catch up in Q3.
Progress on the AMD partnership and AI initiatives: The AMD strategic partnership, announced but not yet completed, is expected to deliver its first joint platform by late 2026. Any updates on the timeline, customer interest, or early revenue contributions from AI solutions will be important.
Free cash flow and margin performance: With supply chain issues impacting cash collections, the timing and magnitude of free cash flow in Q3 versus Q4 will be a key focus, along with Nutanix’s ability to maintain its full-year margin guidance.
Product adoption and competitive migrations: Watch for commentary on customer migrations from legacy providers (such as VMware), adoption of new offerings like external storage support, and traction for Nutanix’s cloud and AI platforms as these are central to the company’s long-term growth plans.
