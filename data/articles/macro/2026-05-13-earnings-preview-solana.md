May 13, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

Solana (NASDAQ:HSDT), a digital asset treasury focused on acquiring and managing Solana tokens and also producing a neuromodulation device for neurological wellness, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Solana Company delivered its first full quarter of results under its new digital asset treasury (DAT) strategy. The company reported revenue of $5.2 million, almost entirely from staking rewards on its SOL holdings. Net income for the quarter was $325.6 million, or $4.25 per share, driven by a significant non-operating gain related to changes in the fair value of derivative liabilities from its September 2025 PIPE transaction. However, the company also reported a large unrealized loss on digital assets due to the decline in SOL prices by year-end.

Compared to analyst expectations, Solana Company’s Q4 results beat estimates for both revenue and earnings per share, primarily due to the derivative-related gain. The company increased its SOL per share by 14% over the six months since launching its DAT strategy, reflecting active management and capital markets activity. Operating expenses rose sharply, mainly from non-cash charges tied to the SOL price decline and higher compensation and professional fees.

Positives
Staking yield outperformance: The company achieved a 6.8% average net staking yield in Q4, above the system-wide average, supporting higher recurring revenue from its SOL holdings.
Accretive capital management: Solana Company grew SOL per share by 14% in six months through a mix of share issuance and buybacks, which demonstrates disciplined use of capital markets tools.
Strong non-operating income: A large gain from changes in derivative liability valuation led to positive net income, which was a notable swing from prior periods.
Expanded institutional partnerships: The company announced a collaboration with Anchorage Digital and Kamino to enable borrowing against staked SOL, setting up new yield opportunities and risk management tools.
Negatives
High operating expenses: Total operating expenses reached $206.1 million in Q4, driven by unrealized losses on SOL and increased compensation and professional fees, which could pressure future profitability if not managed.
Exposure to SOL price volatility: The company’s results are highly sensitive to changes in the price of SOL, which declined by 33% from year-end to late March 2026 and may impact future results.
Ongoing regulatory uncertainty: Recent regulatory releases clarified some aspects of digital asset classification but did not resolve all uncertainties, which could affect Solana Company’s operations and growth plans.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Staking yield consistency and new yield initiatives: Investors should look for updates on the average staking yield achieved in Q1 and any early results from the Anchorage Digital and Kamino partnership, which aims to unlock additional yield from staked SOL.
Impact of SOL price movements: The sharp decline in SOL price since December 2025 will likely affect the reported value of digital assets and may drive unrealized losses or gains in Q1 results.
Progress on the Pacific backbone initiative: The company announced plans to build low-latency infrastructure in Asia-Pacific, with node activation expected to begin immediately and product launches in the next 12 to 18 months. Watch for any updates on implementation or early customer interest.
Cost management and expense trends: With Q4 operating expenses elevated by non-cash charges and higher professional fees, investors should monitor whether these costs are stabilizing or declining as the DAT strategy matures.
Status of share repurchase program and capital formation: Solana Company began repurchasing shares in Q1 2026 and continues to evaluate new capital markets tools. Updates on buyback activity, share count changes, or new financing structures could influence per-share value and investor returns.
