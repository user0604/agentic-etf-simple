EWBC Company Overview
East West operates as a bridge between the United States and Asia, providing personal and commercial banking services to individuals and businesses involved in international trade. The company generates revenue by collecting interest on diverse loan products, including real estate and trade finance, while charging fees for wealth management and foreign exchange services. Its deep cross-border expertise and physical presence in both major markets create high switching costs for clients needing specialized financial coordination.

East West Bancorp Grows Wealth Management Business

East West Bancorp is a bank that provides loans and banking services in the U.S. and Asia, focusing on Asian-American and cross-border markets. The bank stands out for its rapid growth in wealth management and its ability to serve businesses involved in trade between the U.S. and Asia. Over the past five years, the stock returned 64.7%, lagging the S&P 500 by 6.1 percentage points.
