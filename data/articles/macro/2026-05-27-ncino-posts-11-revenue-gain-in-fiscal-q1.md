Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects second-quarter total revenue between $157.75 million and $159.75 million, which would be flat to slightly down compared to the recent quarter. Guidance points to continued robust profitability and cash generation for the full fiscal year, but highlights muted growth in professional services and cautious U.S. mortgage activity. Investors should monitor nCino’s ability to drive revenue growth from AI and international expansion, especially given the company’s increasing debt from share repurchases.

Quarterly highlights

Subscription revenue saw double-digit growth and hit $140.9 million, fueled by broad adoption of nCino’s banking platform and strong renewal activity from large institutions, especially those adopting new AI features. The flat professional services line, at $18.5 million, confirmed nCino’s strategy to focus on scalable software and shift away from low-margin services work.

Profitability was a standout, with operating margin jumping to 13% and non-GAAP margin hitting 28%. These results reflected efficiency gains even as research and development spending on AI features and expanded platform capabilities continued, with R&D expense actually declining from last year.

The company closed several major customer deals, including a five-year renewal and AI expansion with a top-5 Canadian bank and its largest-ever credit union contract. Its annual user conference set a record for attendance, emphasizing engagement with both existing and potential clients across a global base.

What nCino does

nCino provides a cloud-based platform to help banks and credit unions digitize loan origination, account opening, and risk management, with growing integration of AI-powered tools.
