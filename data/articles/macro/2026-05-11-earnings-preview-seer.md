Earnings Preview: Seer

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 11:04 AM

Comments

Seer (NASDAQ:SEER), a company that develops proteomics research tools for large-scale biological studies, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Seer reported revenue of $4.2 million, which represented a 5% increase from the same quarter in 2024. This result missed analyst estimates, mainly because of delayed customer purchases linked to uncertainty around U.S. National Institutes of Health (NIH) funding. The company’s gross margin improved to 52% from 51% a year earlier, helped by higher sales of consumable kits. Operating expenses declined 23% year over year, reflecting reduced spending on research, development, and administrative costs. The net loss for the quarter was $16 million, a narrower loss compared to $21.7 million in Q4 2024. At year-end, Seer had $241 million in cash and investments, supporting its ongoing product innovation and market expansion efforts.

Positives
Growth in installed base and kit usage: The number of Proteograph instruments in use rose 67% to 82, and consumable kit volume grew 69% year over year. This suggests increasing adoption of Seer’s platform by research institutions and commercial customers.
Strong underlying revenue growth (excluding related party): When excluding revenue from related parties, total revenue grew 33% year over year, showing solid demand for Seer’s core products and services.
Improved cost control: Operating expenses dropped 23% from the prior year’s quarter, reflecting disciplined management of research and administrative spending.
Continued product innovation: The company launched its third-generation assay and second-generation automation instrument in June 2025, boosting throughput and scalability for customers.
Negatives
Revenue missed expectations: Quarterly revenue came in below analyst consensus, mainly because of delayed customer purchases tied to NIH funding uncertainty.
Ongoing net losses: Seer continues to operate at a loss, with a net loss of $16 million for the quarter, though this was an improvement year over year.
Lower per-instrument pull-through: The average consumable revenue per instrument declined, as many new customers are still ramping up usage after recent installations.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Revenue trajectory amid funding pressures: Investors should watch whether Seer’s Q1 sales approach the analyst consensus of $3.6 million, given ongoing caution among academic customers and continued uncertainty around NIH funding.
Adoption and utilization trends for Proteograph platform: Key metrics will include growth in the installed base, consumable kit volumes, and the pace at which new customers ramp up usage. These figures will indicate whether the company’s recent placements are translating into recurring revenue.
Progress on product innovation and roadmap: Updates on the planned release of the fourth-generation Proteograph Analysis Suite (PAS) later in 2026, including new artificial intelligence (AI) features, will be important. Investors should also look for any early data or milestones from the next-generation detector project, which is expected to be shared in the second half of the year.
Status of large-scale population studies and partnerships: Any new agreements or expansions of existing collaborations, especially those that could drive higher instrument placements or consumable sales, could provide upside to current guidance.
Updates on commercial leadership and sales initiatives: The search for a Chief Commercial Officer, announced and still pending, may be discussed. Investors should watch for commentary on how new leadership could impact go-to-market strategy and customer engagement in 2026.
