Key Takeaways
Blue Dolphin Energy posted a sharp business win thanks to much higher margins and profit.
Earnings per share soared to $0.99 from $0.15, but revenue declined slightly, year over year.
Management gave no outlook or guidance for future quarters.

Refining specialist Blue Dolphin Energy delivered a standout profit jump as margins expanded sharply, even though revenue declined from a year ago. The company, which operates a single refinery and terminal complex in Nixon, Texas, announced its first-quarter results on May 18, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter featured dramatically higher earnings but left major questions about the sustainability of this performance.

Notable Developments
Profitability surged as net income rose to $14.7 million, mainly due to higher gross margins and a $1.0 million regulatory settlement gain.
Total revenue fell $2.2 million year over year, but gross profit margin improved from 7.3% to 25.2%, pointing to stronger pricing and lower input costs.
Cash fell from $2.0 million to $1.4 million during the quarter, and no new progress was reported on addressing the company's $44.4 million debt burden as of December 31, 2025.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide formal guidance for future quarters or offer updated targets. The biggest factor for investors to monitor is whether these unusually high refining margins are repeatable, as the company remains highly exposed to swings in market conditions and significant debt risk persists.

Quarterly Highlights

The standout result was the surge in gross profit margin, which jumped to 25.2% following a sharp reduction in cost of goods sold and favorable product price differentials. This came despite lower overall revenue, signaling that margin changes—rather than higher sales—drove the gains.

Segment results show the Refinery Operations segment accounted for nearly all of the improvement, with EBITDA climbing to $18.6 million in Q1 2026 from $4.9 million in Q1 2025, while results in the Tolling & Terminaling segment remained stable.

Liquidity remains a key challenge: although net income and EBITDA improved, cash on hand shrank by $0.6 million between December 31, 2025 and March 31, 2026. The company’s high debt load—last reported at $44.4 million—remains unresolved, and a portion is in default, underscoring continued financial risk.

What Blue Dolphin Energy Does

Blue Dolphin Energy operates a single-site oil refinery and terminaling facility in Nixon, Texas, producing and storing refined petroleum products.
