Key Takeaways
BingEx posted a business loss as revenue and margins declined versus last year.
Gross profit fell 16.5%, as revenue decreased 2.7% year over year.
Management gave no detailed financial guidance for upcoming quarters.

BingEx (NASDAQ:FLX) lost ground this quarter as competitive pressure led to shrinking revenue and margins. The leading on-demand courier platform in China, BingEx, faced falling gross profit and a deeper net loss for the three-month period ending March 31, 2026 (results released May 21, 2026). The period highlighted deeper cost cuts and continued product innovation, but it also showed mounting short-term uncertainty for the business.

Overall, BingEx could not reverse the revenue and profit decline seen in previous quarters.

Notable Developments
Revenue declined 2.7% to $135.6 million, marking the second straight year-over-year decrease as competition intensified.
Gross margin narrowed from 13.2% to 11.3%.
Non-GAAP net income shifted from a profit of $7.2 million last year to a loss of $1.6 million this quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted ongoing investment in artificial intelligence (AI) and a focus on operating discipline, but did not offer forward-looking revenue or margin guidance for the upcoming quarters. The key variable for investors to watch is whether BingEx can drive revenue and gross profit improvement without further sacrificing investment in product innovation as it controls costs.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure fell below the level seen in the prior-year quarter, signaling continued market share pressure. Management attributed the drop to "intensifying market competition," and noted stability in order volumes, with 57.9 million fulfilled.

Gross profit and margin both declined, as cost reductions in areas like research and development and marketing could not fully mitigate the effects of lower sales and higher promotional expenses. Operating expenses fell 18.7% year over year, aided by the adoption of AI for efficiency across customer service and logistics.

Profitability weakened sharply at the bottom line, as net investment returns turned negative. Unlike last year, when BingEx posted non-GAAP net profit, this quarter it reported a net loss, with one key reason being investment losses totaling $5.2 million after positive results in Q1 2025.

What BingEx Does

BingEx operates an on-demand courier and delivery platform that matches consumers and businesses with freelance couriers for fast, same-day service, primarily in China.
