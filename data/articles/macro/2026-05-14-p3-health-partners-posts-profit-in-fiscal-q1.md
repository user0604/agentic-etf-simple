P3 Health Partners Posts Profit in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 4:24 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
P3 Health Partners achieved a return to profitability after several loss-making quarters.
Revenue rose year over year, while profitability and margins improved sharply due to operational changes and some non-recurring items.
Management raised its full-year guidance, while cautioning about the sustainability of one-time tailwinds and signaling ongoing focus on execution risks.

P3 Health Partners (NASDAQ:PIII) delivered a sharp turnaround as it posted its first quarterly profit in over a year, supported by strong operational efficiency and the benefit of certain one-time income items. The national provider of physician-led, value-based healthcare for Medicare Advantage patients continued to tighten its business focus, intentionally reducing its at-risk membership base to boost per-member economics. The latest earnings, released on May 14, 2026 for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, marked a period of both margin improvement and evolving strategic priorities. Overall, the quarter reflected operational progress, though part of the positive results came from non-recurring settlements and development adjustments.

Notable Developments
Adjusted EBITDA improved dramatically to $25.8 million from a loss last year, reflecting sharp progress in operational efficiency and cost discipline.
Medical margin expanded over threefold to $73.7 million, though management noted that one-time payer settlements contributed a portion of the gain.
At-risk membership declined by 10 percent to 106,000, representing a strategy of focusing on higher-value contracts after network consolidation.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 guidance for adjusted EBITDA to a range of $20 million to $60 million, citing confidence in ongoing operational improvements. The company expects revenue of $1.5 billion to $1.65 billion for full-year 2026, and has guided at-risk membership to a range of 103,000 to 110,000. Management noted that future results may not include benefits from the non-recurring items seen in the first quarter. Investors should watch whether core profitability can be sustained without the help of settlements and if membership numbers begin to stabilize or grow in coming periods.

Quarterly Highlights

The sharp gain in profitability was due in part to non-recurring benefits, such as prior-year payer settlements, which boosted the company’s medical margin. Management clarified that, excluding these items, underlying profitability also improved strongly but to a lesser extent than the headline figures indicate.

Total revenue increased 4 percent compared to the same period last year despite a 10 percent decline in at-risk membership. This rise was driven by higher rates and better performance in risk-adjusted contracts for Medicare Advantage patients, meaning the average revenue per member increased.

The company deliberately reduced its at-risk membership base, exiting less profitable contracts and focusing resources on higher-value care arrangements. This effort drove operational improvements and set the stage for margin expansion, but it also signals that scale growth may be on pause unless new contracts or network expansions are announced in future quarters.

What P3 Health Partners Does

P3 Health Partners is a healthcare company that manages physician-led networks delivering coordinated, value-based care to Medicare Advantage members across select U.S. markets.
