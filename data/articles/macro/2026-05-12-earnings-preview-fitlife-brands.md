Earnings Preview: FitLife Brands

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

FitLife Brands (NASDAQ:FTLF), a company that sells nutritional supplements and wellness products through retail and online channels, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The fourth quarter of 2025 marked the first full quarter with Irwin Naturals included in the results following its acquisition in August 2025. Total revenue for the quarter was $25.9 million, up 73% year over year, mainly because of the Irwin acquisition. Wholesale revenue rose sharply to $15.5 million, while online revenue grew modestly to $10.5 million. However, the gross margin declined to 34.5% from 41.4% in the prior year, reflecting Irwin’s historically lower margins. Net income was $1.6 million, down from $2.1 million a year earlier, and adjusted EBITDA increased 14% to $3.5 million. These results met analyst consensus for both sales and earnings per share.

Positives
Irwin Naturals contributed significant revenue growth. The acquisition added $12.6 million in revenue, with organic growth of 6% for Irwin compared to the prior year’s quarter. This helped offset declines in legacy brands.
Online sales for Irwin accelerated. Irwin’s Amazon revenue grew rapidly, reaching a $9 million to $10 million annual run rate by the end of the quarter, and subscriber counts rose from 500 to over 3,600 in early 2026.
Debt reduction efforts are ahead of schedule. The company paid down $1.9 million in term loan debt in Q4 and further reduced its revolving credit balance in early 2026, improving its financial flexibility.
Negatives
Legacy FitLife brands experienced revenue declines. Excluding Irwin, total revenue for legacy brands fell 12% year over year, with both wholesale and online channels down double digits, reflecting weak consumer demand for discretionary products.
Gross margin compressed due to Irwin’s lower margins. Consolidated gross margin dropped to 34.5%, and even after adjusting for acquisition-related inventory costs, margins remained below prior-year levels.
Net income declined despite higher overall revenue. Profitability was impacted by transaction expenses and amortization related to the Irwin deal, as well as weaker performance from legacy brands.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Irwin Naturals’ online momentum and out-of-stock resolution: Investors should look for updates on whether Irwin’s Amazon growth continues and if supply chain improvements have reduced product shortages that limited sales in Q1.
Gross margin recovery efforts: Management has begun transitioning Irwin products to a longer three-year shelf life to cut inventory write-offs. Watch for early signs of margin improvement as new products reach the market.
Performance of legacy brands amid weak consumer demand: The company noted that Q1 was tracking similarly to Q4, with continued softness. Pay attention to any signs of stabilization or further decline in these brands, especially MRC and MusclePharm.
Progress on cross-selling and new product launches: FitLife is leveraging Irwin’s sales team to expand distribution of other brands and has three new Irwin products set for launch in the third quarter. Any updates on new retail placements or pipeline progress are important.
Impact of marketing initiatives and Amazon algorithm changes: The company has increased off-Amazon marketing, including a new partnership for Dr. Tobias and expanded social media efforts. Investors should watch for commentary on whether these strategies are driving more traffic and improving online sales trends.
