Notable Developments
Revenue grew 14.5%, underpinned by subscription and strong professional services sales, with Rest of World sales up nearly 39%.
Operating expenses climbed 19.4%, with $6.16 million of severance and costs from the 365Talents acquisition driving a swing to net loss.
Over $62.9 million was spent on share repurchases, reducing shares outstanding by more than 10% as cash outflows accelerated.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Investors should watch the pace of cost discipline and signs of successful integration and profitability from recently acquired businesses. The most important area to monitor is whether expenses from acquisitions and restructuring can be contained as the benefits of top-line growth materialize.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue posted a double-digit increase, reaching $65.6 million as professional services soared almost 60% and international sales fueled growth outside North America. Subscription sales from the core learning management platform remained the largest source, supported by expansion in Europe following the 365Talents acquisition.

Rising operating costs, led by almost $6.2 million in severance and higher amortization from recent acquisitions, contributed to a $1.5 million operating loss and negative diluted earnings per share. Management detailed that these costs stemmed from integration activities and workforce reductions related to recent deals.

Cash flow from operations improved materially, yet large outflows for acquisitions and share repurchases reduced the cash balance and increased debt to $79.1 million as of March 31, 2026. After quarter-end, Docebo completed another acquisition in Germany, further advancing its presence in the European market for AI-driven workforce analytics.

What Docebo Does

Docebo provides cloud-based learning management systems and artificial intelligence-driven tools to help enterprises manage and measure internal and external workforce training.
