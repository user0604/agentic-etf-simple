FICO Company Overview
Fair Isaac develops decision management software and predictive analytics, most notably the FICO Score used to measure consumer credit risk. The company earns revenue by licensing its scoring models to lenders and credit bureaus while providing cloud-based software that automates business processes like fraud detection and loan origination. This integration into the global financial infrastructure creates high switching costs, as banks and insurers rely on these standardized tools for daily transaction and risk management decisions.

The company stands out because its FICO Score is a standard tool across the financial industry, used for most U.S. consumer lending decisions. Over the past five years, the stock returned 89.6%, beating the S&P 500’s 69.4% gain by 20.2 percentage points.
