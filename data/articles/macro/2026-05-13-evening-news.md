1. Cisco Surges as AI Orders Roll In

Cisco (NASDAQ: CSCO) just had its best earnings moment in years -- stock up 18.5% after hours -- as artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure orders piled up faster than the company could count them. Revenue hit $15.84 billion, topping estimates, and networking revenue jumped 25%. CSCO is recommended in Hidden Gems, and since joining the scorecard in 2024, shares have more than doubled, outpacing the market by 74 percentage points.

From laggard to leader: Cisco raised its full-year AI order target from $5 billion to $9 billion and bumped its AI revenue forecast from $3 billion to $4 billion. Hyperscalers, it turns out, need a lot of cables and switches.
Cutting to grow: Nearly 4,000 jobs are going -- under 5% of the workforce -- as Cisco bets more heavily on AI. CEO Chuck Robbins put it bluntly: The companies that win the AI era will be the ones willing to make hard calls.
2. Netflix Deepens NFL Ties With New Game

Netflix (NASDAQ: NFLX), recommended by both Team Hidden Gems and Team Rule Breakers, just went back to the NFL buffet for seconds. A new four-year deal lands the streamer an opening-week international game, a brand-new Thanksgiving Eve game, and a late-season closer -- plus an extension of its Christmas Day rights. A month ago, Motley Fool analyst Sanmeet Deo wrote that NFLX "looks like more of a bargain given it can still generate EPS growth of 20-25% going forward. I'd be a buyer of the stock on weakness."

Turkey, tinsel, and touchdowns: Netflix is becoming a football destination. The NFL likes what it sees in Netflix's younger, globally distributed audience.
Old media got a bone, too: Fox (NASDAQ: FOX) picked up two extra games, NBC snagged one, and CBS can flip a Sunday afternoon game to Saturday prime time.
3. Doximity: The AI Is In

Rule Breakers rec Doximity (NYSE: DOCS) just dropped its fiscal 2026 results. Full-year revenue hit $644.9 million (up 13%), while free cash flow jumped 19% to $317.5 million. Why are shares down 19.4% in after-hours trading? Q4 was the softer spot -- revenue up just 5% -- but the real story is what's happening under the hood with AI.

The waiting room is packed: "Doximity has to prove its ability to keep adopting and integrating new AI-powered tools into the platform," Motley Fool contributor Dan Caplinger said last month on a Scoreboard video. More than 800,000 active prescribers used Doximity's workflow tools in Q4, with nearly half using its clinical AI. Prompts per user nearly doubled from January to April alone.
New partners, new blood: Tie-ups with Aledade and Photon bring Doximity's Clinical AI Suite to thousands of independent practices. Fiscal 2027 guidance calls for $664 million to $676 million in revenue -- growth, but a step down in pace.
