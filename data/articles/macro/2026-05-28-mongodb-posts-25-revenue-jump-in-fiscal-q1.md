Notable Developments
Atlas, the cloud-based database-as-a-service, delivered more than 29% revenue growth and accounted for 73% of total revenue in FY 2026.
Management increased its fiscal 2027 revenue and profit guidance following strength in the quarter, especially from Atlas-led growth.
The company reported free cash flow of $197.5 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year revenue forecast to $2.92–$2.96 billion and lifted earnings outlooks for both operating income and non-GAAP earnings per share. Atlas was described as the “main driver” behind these higher targets. Investors should monitor the ongoing transition of sales and product leadership and watch for evidence of Atlas sustaining its current growth rate.

Quarterly Highlights

Cloud-based Atlas continued to outpace expectations, with revenue up more than 29% to $512.5 million. Atlas now represents about three-quarters of business mix, as legacy and on-premise offerings grew at a slower 13% rate. The company attributed this strength to both surging market demand and product enhancements, especially those supporting artificial intelligence (AI) workflows.

Profitability improved at multiple levels: MongoDB generated $4.4 million in net income (GAAP) versus a loss last year. Free cash flow nearly doubled, underpinned by disciplined spending as both research and development, plus sales and marketing, rose at a lower pace than revenue growth. Gross margin edged up by one percentage point, marking incremental operational gains.

Customer metrics remained healthy, with total customers exceeding 67,700, an 18.6% increase from the prior year. The federal and government verticals gained more focus after the acquisition of Clarity Business Solutions. International sales also increased, and Atlas’ share continued to rise, supported by new partnerships and features tailored to AI application developers.

What MongoDB Does

MongoDB provides a document-based, cloud-enabled database platform that enables developers and businesses to build, store, and manage data-driven applications efficiently across environments.
