Notable Developments
Revenue sharply increased to $1.4 million, up from $0.2 million in the same quarter last year, reflecting booking conversion across operating companies.
General and administrative expenses fell 35%, pointing to progress in cost control as management continued its expense-reduction measures.
Net cash used in operating activities rose to $34.0 million, indicating higher cash consumption compared to the prior year quarter as the company invests in scaling.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management described 2026 as an “inflection year,” highlighting growing commercial bookings and continued cost discipline. No specific forward guidance was issued in the release, but management restated its focus on reaching operating cash flow at the subsidiary level and eventually achieving positive company-wide cash flow by 2028. Investors should closely monitor the conversion of contracted bookings into recognized revenue and the pace of operating cash burn in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the period rose over fivefold, driven primarily by increased commercial bookings at both Accelsius (data center cooling systems) and AeroFlexx (packaging technology), with both units seeing positive order momentum. However, recognized revenue is still small compared to the total backlog and booked opportunities, underscoring the early stage of scale-up across the operating portfolio.

Expense management continued to be a key focus, as general and administrative costs were cut by more than a third year over year. This reflects ongoing efforts to shift overhead to the operating company level as these businesses mature and raise their own capital.

Despite these improvements, Innventure reported elevated cash burn and ongoing losses, with net cash used in operations more than doubling from the same period last year. The share count also continued to climb as the company raised equity capital to support expansion and offset losses.

What Innventure Does

Innventure builds, acquires, and operates technology-driven businesses that originate from collaborations with major corporations, targeting disruptive innovation in high-growth markets.
