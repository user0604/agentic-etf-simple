Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management’s outlook centers on completing further analyses from the NAVIGATE trial, presenting new findings at the European Association for the Study of the Liver (EASL), and meeting with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to discuss belapectin’s next regulatory steps. The single most critical focus for investors in the near term is the outcome and feedback from the upcoming FDA Type-C meeting, which will shape potential drug approval pathways and influence the company’s future resource requirements.

Quarterly Highlights

This quarter, the company highlighted new analyses from its main clinical study, NAVIGATE, which evaluated belapectin, a galectin-3 inhibitor, for treating advanced MASH (metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis) cirrhosis. The updated data included statistically significant reductions in liver stiffness, an indicator of liver scarring, and a lower rate of disease worsening compared to placebo. Management noted that these biomarker and efficacy trends reinforced belapectin’s potential, but also clarified that further regulatory and clinical evaluation is needed before advancing to drug approval.

The financial picture showed improved loss figures, driven by a two-thirds reduction in research and development expenses as trial costs fell. At quarter-end, cash and cash equivalents stood at $14.1 million, while management stated that available funding, including an undrawn credit line, will support operations and ongoing research plans through at least May 2027.

Despite scientific and financial progress, there was no announcement of new partnerships or collaborations. Advancement of any additional drug programs outside of belapectin remains on hold until the company secures the right external partners or receives favorable regulatory input for future trials.
