Key Takeaways
Instrument sales disappointed while consumables set a new record for the company.
Non-GAAP operating expenses fell 19.2% year over year.
Management expects accelerated revenue growth is needed to meet its annual guidance.

Instrument sales disappointed even as consumables set a new record for Pacific Biosciences of California (NASDAQ:PACB) in its latest quarterly report. The gene-sequencing toolmaker focuses on high-fidelity long-read sequencing technology, supporting research and clinical customers in genomics. It released results for the three months ended March 31, 2026, on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter marked stable total revenue but highlighted pressure in key business areas and the need for renewed sales acceleration.

Notable developments
Consumable revenue climbed 8% to a record, reaching $21.8 million as long-read sequencing saw broader adoption in clinical and EMEA markets.
Instrument revenue fell to $9.7 million from $11.0 million, and system placements were flat or slightly down compared to the previous year.
Gross margin decreased by 3 percentage points year over year, with management highlighting higher computing component costs and promotional discounts as drivers.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue target of $165 million to $175 million, but noted that this will require a significant ramp-up in the coming quarters. The company is counting on the broader launch of its updated SPRQ-Nx sequencing chemistry and increased clinical adoption to boost both consumables and instrument revenue. Investors should watch whether system sales recover and if margin improvements develop as the year progresses.

Quarterly highlights

The company reported record consumable sales, driven largely by the expanding clinical adoption of its HiFi long-read sequencing kits, especially in EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa), where regional revenue rose 17% from the prior year.

Instrument sales were a weak spot, with revenue from system purchases declining 12% and Vega placements falling slightly to 27 units. This shortfall was not matched by an equivalent rise in consumables pull-through per system, which actually dipped compared to last year’s level.

Operational discipline continued as non-GAAP operating expenses fell nearly one-fifth year over year, supporting improved losses per share. However, the company’s cash and investments decreased to $276.0 million, despite proceeds from the sale of its short-read sequencing assets.

What Pacific Biosciences of California does

Pacific Biosciences of California develops and sells advanced gene-sequencing instruments and consumables, specializing in high-accuracy, long-read DNA sequencing technology for research and clinical applications.
