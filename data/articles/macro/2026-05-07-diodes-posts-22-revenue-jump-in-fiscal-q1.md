Notable developments
Revenue jumped 22% from the prior year, lifted by strong demand in automotive, industrial, and AI-server applications.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management guided for second quarter revenue to rise again, plus a one percentage point improvement in gross margin. Investors should watch progress in margin expansion and ongoing sales momentum in high-growth markets such as automotive and AI.

Quarterly highlights

Demand in key markets drove most of the revenue gain, with automotive and industrial products now accounting for 44% of product sales, up from 42% the previous quarter. Increased European orders, especially from auto buyers, added to Diodes' top-line momentum.

Cost controls and greater factory utilization contributed to the improvement in gross profit margin. Management attributes this to better operational execution and a focus on higher-value product areas.

Operating cash flow exceeded $64 million for the period, with free cash flow totaling $32.4 million. This allowed Diodes to end the quarter with $409.4 million in cash and equivalents and modest total debt of $55 million.
