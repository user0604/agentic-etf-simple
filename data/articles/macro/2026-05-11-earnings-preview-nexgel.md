Earnings Preview: NEXGEL

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 5:03 PM

Comments

NEXGEL (NASDAQ:NXGL), a maker of specialty hydrogels for wound care and consumer health products, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, May 13, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

NEXGEL reported fourth-quarter 2025 results that fell below management’s earlier guidance and missed analyst expectations. Net revenue for the full year 2025 reached $11.4 million, up 31.5% from the prior year, driven mainly by growth in branded consumer products. However, the fourth quarter itself was described by management as “surprisingly light,” mainly due to underperformance in new product launches and a setback in the Silly George beauty brand. Gross profit for the year improved to 39.5%, reflecting better product mix, but the company continued to post operating losses.

Positives
Revenue growth in consumer products: The company saw a significant increase in full-year revenue, mainly from its consumer brands, which suggests traction in this segment despite a weak fourth quarter.
Improved gross margin: Gross margin rose to 39.5% for the year, up from 31.6% in 2024, indicating better cost management and product mix.
Completion of transformative acquisition: The acquisition of Celularity’s degenerative wound segment closed in April 2026, positioning NEXGEL for higher revenues and immediate profitability, according to management.
New strategic partnership secured: Sequence LifeScience invested $5.5 million, providing capital and manufacturing support, which strengthens NEXGEL’s operational capacity and product development pipeline.
Negatives
Fourth-quarter sales missed expectations: Q4 revenue was below both internal guidance and analyst consensus, mainly due to weaker-than-expected performance in new product launches and the Silly George brand.
Continued operating losses: Despite margin improvement, NEXGEL posted an operating loss for the year, with management noting that profitability is not expected until the benefits of the Celularity acquisition are fully realized.
Decreased working capital: Working capital fell to $1.4 million at year-end, down from $2.6 million the previous year, reflecting ongoing cash burn and the need for external financing.
Limited R&D spending: Research and development expenses dropped significantly, raising questions about the pace of internal innovation outside of acquired products.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Initial impact of Celularity acquisition: Investors should look for early signs of revenue and margin improvement from the integration of Celularity’s wound care products, which began generating sales under NEXGEL as of April 2026.
Recovery in consumer brands: Management indicated a return to normal sales trends for Silly George and other consumer products in Q1, following a weak Q4. Watch for evidence of this rebound in reported results.
Progress on sales force integration: The company is hiring a new national sales manager and re-engaging hospital sales reps to reinvigorate product distribution. Updates on these efforts will be important for assessing future growth potential.
Contribution margin and profitability outlook: Management expects a blended contribution margin near 52% with the new portfolio. Investors should watch for commentary or data on how close the company is to achieving positive EBITDA, especially as the year progresses.
Status of new product pipeline: There are three 510(k) medical devices in development, with launches scheduled for 2026, 2027, and 2028. Any updates on regulatory progress, launch timing, or early demand for these products will be key for long-term growth expectations.
