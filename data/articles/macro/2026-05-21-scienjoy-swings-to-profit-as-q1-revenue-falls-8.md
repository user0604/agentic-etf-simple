Key Takeaways

Scienjoy (NASDAQ:SJ) returned to profit this quarter, but its revenue and paying user counts continued to fall. Notable developments

Revenue dropped 8%, and total paying users declined 18.9% to 123,266, reflecting intensified competition.
AI-powered platform features remained in early development, with no immediate revenue impact reported this quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted ongoing investment in artificial intelligence and new platform features, aiming to position the business for future growth and enterprise opportunities. There was no specific financial guidance or near-term user growth target given for the upcoming periods. Investors should watch for user base stabilization, progress in live-streaming monetization, and any material impact from the company’s AI-driven initiatives.

Quarterly highlights

This quarter, the main story was a sharp cost reduction: operating expenses fell 15.2%, driven by cuts in sales and marketing and lower administrative costs. While this strategy helped Scienjoy return to a net profit, it did not offset a significant drop in paying users and virtual item sales.

Gross margin contracted by 1.7 percentage points, reflecting weaker revenue and limited improvement in underlying cost structure. Core business activities—mainly live-streaming revenue from virtual items—remained under pressure from a shrinking user base and tougher industry competition.

Cash and cash equivalents increased by 6% from the prior period, strengthening the balance sheet. This provides financial flexibility for continued investment in research and development, especially for artificial intelligence and product innovation, which management sees as vital for long-term growth.

What Scienjoy does

Scienjoy operates interactive mobile live-streaming platforms in China, earning revenue from virtual item sales and technical services.
