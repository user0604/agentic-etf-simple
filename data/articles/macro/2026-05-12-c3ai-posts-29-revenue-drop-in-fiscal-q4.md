C3.ai (NYSE:AI) delivered on its cost-cutting goals but struggled to reverse its revenue decline. The enterprise artificial intelligence software provider is undergoing significant restructuring, including workforce reductions and leadership changes. Notable developments
Revenue declined 28.7% from the prior year, landing within management’s guidance range of $48.0 million to $52.0 million.
The company’s cost reduction plan, including a 26% workforce reduction, led to annualized non-GAAP cost savings of about $135 million.
C3.ai signed 28 agreements in the quarter, but bookings were lower than expected, indicating muted customer demand.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide a specific revenue or margin outlook for fiscal 2027, instead highlighting the completion of cost restructuring and an expected improvement in operating leverage in the second half of the year. The main focus for investors will be whether top-line growth returns as cost reductions take effect. Bookings and new customer wins will be key indicators to monitor in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure for the quarter matched the company’s internal range, reflecting ongoing sales challenges and slower deal activity. Management attributed the result to lower bookings and fewer conversions of initial deployments to full-scale commercial contracts.

Non-GAAP operating losses improved relative to recent guidance but remained significant, at $54.4 million, an increase over the prior year. This improvement is attributed to a sweeping cost-reduction program, including a 26% reduction in headcount and targeted expense cuts across business operations. Management expects cost savings of $135 million to be substantially realized by the second half of fiscal 2027, which should help reduce cash burn.

Cash and investments on the balance sheet ended the quarter at $575.4 million, down from $621.9 million the prior quarter, reflecting ongoing losses and restructuring costs. The company did not announce new products or major technological updates, and no new “lighthouse” customer wins or large-scale commercial launches were highlighted in the release.
