XPeng (NYSE:XPEV)’s first quarter results signaled renewed challenges as sales volumes and revenue declined substantially from last year. The company, a major designer and manufacturer of smart electric vehicles (EVs), published its quarterly earnings for the period ending March 31, 2026, on May 28, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected persistent headwinds for demand, but with some strength in profitability improvements.

Notable Developments
Vehicle deliveries fell 33.3% from the prior year, totaling 62,682 for the quarter amid weaker demand.
Gross margin reached 20.6%, up 5.0 percentage points year over year, driven by improved product mix and efficiency.
Cash position declined to RMB 42.09 billion, reflecting elevated spending on research and development and operating losses.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects a marked rebound in the second quarter, targeting deliveries between 100,000 and 106,000 units and revenue between RMB 19.60 and 20.80 billion. These targets would represent a strong sequential increase, though year-over-year growth may be modest. Investors should closely monitor the company's ability to increase deliveries and capitalize on upcoming model launches.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw a drop in vehicle deliveries and revenue compared to last year, as XPeng's unit shipments fell by one-third. The company still reached the midpoint of its own delivery guidance, but current volumes reflected both seasonal weakness and competitive pressure.

Gross margin, which reflects sales profitability before other expenses, improved to 20.6%, up from 15.6% a year earlier. This margin gain stemmed from XPeng’s focus on higher-value models and reducing manufacturing costs.

Spending on research and development rose 46.8%, supporting efforts in new vehicle technologies and artificial intelligence projects, such as robotaxi and humanoid robotics initiatives, even as cash burn increased in the quarter.

What XPeng Does

XPeng designs and manufactures smart electric vehicles and associated mobility technologies, including advanced driver-assist features and connected services for the Chinese and international markets.
