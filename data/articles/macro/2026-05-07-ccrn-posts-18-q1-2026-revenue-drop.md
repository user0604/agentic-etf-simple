Notable developments
Revenue reached $241.1 million, coming in above the company's guided range and driven by sequential growth in the travel nurse and allied division.
Profitability declined, with adjusted EBITDA nearly halved to $3.9 million and gross margin falling by 0.3 percentage points versus last year.
The company licensed its Intellify workforce platform to another top-ten staffing provider and signed four new managed services contracts, expanding its tech and customer footprint.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue new financial projections, citing the pending acquisition by Knox Lane as a reason. Earlier long-term goals targeted improvements in recurring revenue and margin recovery, though this quarter's results highlight challenges in achieving those. Investors should watch for progress on the merger and any updates on monetizing technology initiatives.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue topped company guidance as travel nurse and allied assignments saw a 4% sequential bump, though overall staffing volume remains below prior-year levels. The nurse and allied staffing business accounted for 84% of total revenue, with average field staff numbers down 14% year over year but holding steady compared to the prior quarter.

Profitability continued to contract. Adjusted EBITDA dropped from $8.6 million in the year-ago period to $3.9 million, and the quarter closed with a net loss of $4.3 million. Gross margin stood just below management's intended 20% mark.

The firm's technology focus showed early external traction: it licensed its Intellify software platform to another major industry provider, expanding a new line of recurring service revenue. At the same time, four new managed service provider and vendor system contracts were inked. Community care services, a newer and more diversified segment, posted double-digit annual growth, helping offset weakness in physician staffing.

What Cross Country Healthcare does

Cross Country Healthcare provides workforce solutions and staffing services for the healthcare industry, supplying temporary and permanent clinical professionals to hospitals and other care facilities nationwide.
