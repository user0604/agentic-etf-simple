Notable Developments
Rumble’s global monthly active users climbed to 56 million, up 8% from the prior quarter, helped by strong engagement in Rumble Shorts and international growth.
Revenue reached $25.5 million, a record for the first quarter, as audience monetization efforts—subscriptions and advertising—added $2.6 million year over year.
Net loss widened significantly to $30.3 million, mainly due to one-time costs related to the Northern Data acquisition and the absence of prior-year financial gains.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to prioritize growing user engagement, rolling out monetization features for Rumble Shorts later in 2026, and closing the Northern Data acquisition by June. The company highlights coming advertising ramps, cloud and AI initiatives, and expects these efforts to drive much of the next phase’s revenue gains. Investors should closely monitor the integration of Northern Data and the success of converting growing user engagement into higher revenue per user.

Quarterly Highlights

User growth rebounded with global monthly active users reaching 56 million, an 8% increase from the previous quarter. Rumble attributed this to record engagement in its short-form video product, Rumble Shorts, and expanding its international footprint.

Revenue topped $25.5 million due to gains from subscriptions and advertising. Monetization efforts showed traction, with much user growth coming from regions with less spend per user.

Operating losses expanded, reaching a net loss of $30.3 million, with higher acquisition-related and research investments. Cash outflows also increased, but cost controls were evident in areas like general and administrative expense, which fell 37% year over year.

What Rumble Does

Rumble operates an online video-sharing platform focused on supporting creators through monetization tools and open content policies, and is expanding into cloud and AI infrastructure services.
