Deckers Outdoor (NYSE:DECK) achieved record sales as strong international growth offset flat U.S. performance. Notable developments
Deckers achieved record quarterly revenue of $1.12 billion, fueled by double-digit international sales growth, while domestic sales were nearly flat at just 0.3% higher.
Operating income dropped nearly 10% year over year due to higher selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses that rose faster than sales.
The HOKA brand delivered 14.5% growth in the quarter, but the “Other Brands” segment slipped by 35.6%, continuing its multi-quarter decline.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects fiscal 2027 sales to grow between 7% and 8%, anticipating further strength from HOKA and UGG but lower gross and operating margins. Investors should watch for Deckers’ ability to control costs as rising SG&A expenses and expected margin pressure could impact profit growth.

Quarterly highlights

International sales were a standout, growing 25.5% while U.S. sales remained basically unchanged, demonstrating Deckers’ shifting growth engine. The HOKA brand, which specializes in performance and lifestyle footwear, expanded strongly and now accounts for a large share of total sales.

Despite the bright spots, operating income fell as total SG&A expense increased to $487.9 million, consuming a greater share of revenue. Meanwhile, gross margin improved year over year to 57.6%, partly offsetting some cost pressure.

“Other Brands” (including Teva and Sanuk) continued to shrink, lowering diversification and putting more emphasis on the ongoing success of HOKA and UGG. The company emphasized its expanded share repurchase plan but did not declare a dividend.

What Deckers Outdoor does

Deckers Outdoor designs, markets, and sells performance and lifestyle footwear and apparel, with brands such as HOKA for runners and UGG for premium casual footwear.
