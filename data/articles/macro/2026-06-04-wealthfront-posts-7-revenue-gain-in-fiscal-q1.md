Key Takeaways
Wealthfront delivered revenue growth but faced profit pressure from higher stock-based compensation and margin compression.
Revenue rose 7% year over year, while diluted EPS fell to $0.07.

Wealthfront (NASDAQ:WLTH) achieved higher revenue but saw profits decline as costs rose. The digital-first financial platform, which provides automated cash management, investment advisory, and home lending, reported fiscal first quarter 2027 results on June 4, 2026, for the period ended April 30, 2026. Overall, the quarter balanced strong client engagement and asset growth with notable expense and margin pressure.

Notable developments
Total platform assets rose 19% year over year to a record $96.6 billion, while funded clients reached 1.46 million, up 15%.
Expenses increased sharply, with stock-based compensation rising to $17.1 million, reflecting equity awards recognized after the IPO.
Investment advisory revenue grew 32%, but cash management revenue declined by 1% as deposit outflows continued and fee rates fell.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management emphasized continued investment in automation, engineering talent, and expanding home lending and cash management offerings. While no new full-year guidance was provided, management stressed the need to balance cost discipline with innovation, especially given recent profit margin challenges. Investors should monitor future expense trends and net deposit momentum, particularly in cash management.

Quarterly highlights

Client growth and platform adoption remained strong, with funded accounts increasing by 15% and cross-product adoption reaching 62.5% of assets. This was supported by incentives encouraging clients to use both cash management and investment advisory products.

Profitability came under pressure as stock-based compensation surged more than eightfold year over year, totaling $17.1 million due to post-IPO equity vesting. Even after adjusting for these costs, profit margins narrowed as expenses for product development and cloud services rose faster than revenue.

The product suite expanded, with home lending now generally available in Colorado and Texas, and new features added to cash and stock accounts. Net deposits totaled $554 million for the quarter, but cash management saw $477 million in outflows, offset by strong $1.03 billion inflows to investment advisory products.

What Wealthfront does

Wealthfront provides automated financial solutions, including investment management, cash accounts, and lending services, through a digital-first platform for retail clients.
