Corpay (NYSE:CPAY) delivered a business win in its latest quarter, driven by momentum in its core Corporate Payments segment and a significant share buyback. Notable developments
Adjusted earnings per share rose 29%, surpassing management’s outlook and reflecting strong core profitability.
Corporate Payments segment revenue climbed 46%, supported by a 71% increase in spend volume, even as revenue per transaction declined.
The company repurchased 2.4 million shares for $786 million, marking a substantial return of capital to shareholders.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 guidance for both revenue and adjusted earnings, citing continued strength in Corporate Payments and operational scalability. The company plans to focus on integrating recent acquisitions and maintaining margin discipline as it invests in growth. Investors should watch for improvements in Lodging segment growth and the sustainability of yield levels in Corporate Payments.

Quarterly highlights

The Corporate Payments segment delivered 16% organic revenue growth, even as revenue per dollar of spend declined due to a higher share of large enterprise and cross-border clients. The segment processed $81.85 billion in payments, up sharply from the prior year.

Vehicle Payments revenue increased 19%, mainly from improved pricing rather than higher transaction volume. Lodging Payments revenue was flat, as a drop in room nights processed was offset by higher revenue per room night. Management expects Lodging growth to recover in the second half as new customer implementations ramp up.

Expenses rose faster than revenue, with processing, selling, and general administrative costs all increasing. The company attributed these increases to technology upgrades and recent acquisitions, but cost control and margin stability remain important themes going forward.

What Corpay does

Corpay provides business payments and expense management solutions to organizations worldwide, with specialized offerings in corporate, vehicle, and lodging payment services.
