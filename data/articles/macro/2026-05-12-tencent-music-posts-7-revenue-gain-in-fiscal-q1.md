Key Takeaways
Music platform Tencent Music Entertainment Group posted steady gains despite moderating growth in core subscription revenue. Non-GAAP earnings per share rose 6.6% year over year. Management gave no updated guidance but cited strong progress outside of membership services and continued investment in content and technology.

Tencent Music Entertainment Group (NYSE:TME) delivered solid results, with growth in its core music services partly offset by weaker social entertainment revenue. The leading online music and audio entertainment platform in China continued to pivot towards diversifying its revenue streams and investing in premium music content. The company announced first quarter results on May 12, 2026, covering the quarter ended March 31. Overall, the quarter featured resilient growth in music-related services but highlighted rising costs and softer subscription momentum.

Notable Developments
Music related services revenue climbed 12.2%, driven by strong growth in membership services and offline performances.
Selling and marketing expenses jumped 36.2%, signifying higher investment in channel promotions and user acquisition.
Offline performances generated over RMB100 million in sales for a single album-release event.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue new full-year guidance but reiterated its commitment to broadening the music ecosystem and diversifying revenue sources. Executives highlighted continued investment in technology, premium content, and strategic partnerships. Investors should closely monitor further shifts in membership growth, marketing spending, and the evolving contribution from non-subscription services in the quarters ahead.

Quarterly Highlights

The period showed ongoing decline in social entertainment services, with revenue down 11.0%, while music-related segments remained the main growth driver. Non-subscription offerings in music (such as live concerts, artist merchandise, and advertising) grew much faster than core subscriptions, offsetting slower subscription gains. Marketing and promotional expenditures rose sharply, reflecting intensified efforts to attract and retain users in a highly competitive landscape.

Gross margin strengthened to 44.9%, up from 44.1% a year earlier, as growth in higher-margin music revenues and cost controls helped offset the impact from weaker social entertainment. Adjusted EBITDA (a measure of profitability that excludes items like interest and taxes) climbed by 10.5% compared to the prior-year quarter, signaling resilience in the underlying business.

Management cited successful partnerships with leading music labels and highlighted the company’s use of artificial intelligence to enhance content supply and user experience. Investment in these areas, as well as the expansion of offline performance and fan engagement, continue to support Tencent Music's strategic transition toward a more diversified and stable revenue model.

What Tencent Music Entertainment Group Does

The company operates leading music streaming and audio entertainment platforms in China, providing services including online music streaming, live concerts, ticketing, merchandise, and social entertainment features.
