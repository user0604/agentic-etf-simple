The clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focuses on innovative antibody-based therapies, with a pipeline led by its partnership asset zanidatamab and newer drug candidates built on its proprietary platforms. Overall, the quarter reflected weak top-line results but steady advancement in research and development and preparation for major upcoming regulatory milestones.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its prior guidance, expecting adjusted expenses for 2026 to be roughly 20% lower than in fiscal 2025 and citing strong cash reserves. The most important item to follow is progress toward planned regulatory milestones for zanidatamab, which would unlock significant milestone payments and larger future royalty streams.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the quarter dropped to $2.4 million as the company recorded far fewer one-time milestones than in the prior year, with ongoing revenue now reflecting both collaboration activity and increased royalties on licensed products. Alongside this, Zymeworks maintained tight control of its operating expenses, reducing adjusted gross operating expense by 8% year over year as it shifted R&D efforts from late-stage projects toward early development.

The pipeline advanced with notable clinical results: ZW191, an antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) targeting ovarian and endometrial cancer, demonstrated confirmed objective response rates of 61% in ovarian and 57% in endometrial cancers for certain dose levels. ZW191 also received FDA Fast Track designation, supporting continued portfolio development.

Key regulatory milestones are on the horizon. The FDA accepted a Priority Review submission for zanidatamab, branded as Ziihera, in first-line HER2-positive gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma (GEA). Zymeworks could receive up to $250 million in milestone payments tied to a successful United States approval, with further payments possible from approvals in China and elsewhere.

What Zymeworks Does

Zymeworks develops and licenses antibody-based therapeutics, with a business focused on platform-driven innovation, strategic partnerships, and commercializing new cancer treatments through royalty and milestone agreements.
