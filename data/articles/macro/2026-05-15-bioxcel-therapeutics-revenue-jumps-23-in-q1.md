Notable developments
The FDA accepted the company’s supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) for at-home use of IGALMI, targeting a decision by Nov. 14, 2026.
Revenue for IGALMI rose marginally to $0.21 million, with operating loss largely unchanged; research and development expenses fell 35% compared to the prior year period.
Cash and equivalents dropped sharply to $17.2 million as management initiated a strategic review to seek options like a sale or partnership due to ongoing “going concern” risks.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management emphasized the importance of securing approval for IGALMI’s at-home use and described efforts to maximize value, including exploring potential corporate transactions. The company is seeking financing or strategic partners to extend its cash runway. Investors should watch for any announcements regarding new partnerships, fundraising, or regulatory milestones in the coming months.

Quarterly highlights

The FDA accepted the sNDA submission for at-home use of IGALMI, which represents a key regulatory milestone for expanding the lead product’s reach. IGALMI is an orally dissolving film for treating acute agitation associated with bipolar disorder or schizophrenia; the new application targets patients outside of hospital settings.

Commercial traction for IGALMI remained limited, with revenue inching higher to $0.21 million while cost of goods sold surged well beyond product sales, resulting in continued negative gross margin. Operating expenses reflected further cost control in research and development, while selling, general, and administrative costs jumped, attributed mostly to professional fees tied to the company’s strategic review and regulatory preparation.

The company reported a 40% sequential decline in cash and equivalents, raising urgent concerns over financial sustainability. With an explicit “going concern” disclosure and a growing deficit, the company’s future depends on strategic alternatives or new capital long before the potential November 2026 FDA decision can materialize.
