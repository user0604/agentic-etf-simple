Quantum-Si Revenue Drops 69% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:44 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
The company executed key development steps but reported sharply lower near-term revenue as it focused on preparing for a major new product launch.
Revenue fell compared to the prior year, while adjusted operating expenses decreased slightly.
Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance, expecting increased business momentum as development progresses.

Quantum-Si (NASDAQ:QSI) reported a challenging start to the year as it invested heavily in the development and early validation of its next-generation Proteus proteomics platform, leading to a substantial revenue drop and ongoing losses. The life science tools company specializes in single-molecule protein sequencing technology for research applications. This quarterly update, released on May 7, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, reflected progress on technical goals but continued near-term commercial weakness.

Overall, the quarter validated the transition-year approach, showing operational discipline and clear product roadmap progress but exposing short-term financial strain as customer buying pauses ahead of the Proteus launch.

Notable developments
The company achieved key technical milestones for the Proteus platform, such as sequencing customer samples on a prototype and automating integrated runs.
Revenue decreased sharply, primarily due to planned customer purchase delays ahead of the new platform launch, causing a significant year-over-year drop.
Operating expenses declined modestly as cost controls offset higher investment in research and development, and cash reserves remain large with $190.4 million at quarter end.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, anticipating around $1 million in revenue and keeping adjusted operating expenses at, or below, $98 million. The company expects business activity to pick up later in the year as development continues and early access programs expand. The most important factor for investors is whether the Proteus platform progresses toward its targeted product launch milestones and starts to gain traction with early customers.

Quarterly highlights

The company executed on its top priority of advancing next-generation technology, including achieving integrated, automated sequencing and detecting 17 amino acids with its developmental kit. Although this result is just shy of the 18-target goal for commercial launch, customer sample sequencing on the Proteus prototype marks a crucial validation step.

Revenue continued to contract—falling 69% from the prior year quarter—as customers delayed purchases of existing products in anticipation of Proteus and as the company embedded upgrade credits for current buyers. The reported service revenue rose 24% year over year, but overall sales remain muted.

Operating discipline helped offset some pressure, with adjusted operating expenses down about 6% compared to last year and management reiterating that available cash will fund operations through the second quarter of 2028. Investment continues, especially in research and development, to support Proteus platform readiness for the targeted commercial launch at the end of 2026.

What Quantum-Si does

The company develops and markets next-generation protein sequencing platforms that enable researchers to analyze proteins at single-molecule resolution.
