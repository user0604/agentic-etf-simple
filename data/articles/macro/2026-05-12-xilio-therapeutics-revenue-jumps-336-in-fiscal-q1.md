Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects current cash reserves to support operations into early 2028, excluding potential additional milestone and option payments. The main milestone for investors is the planned Investigational New Drug (IND) submission for XTX501 in mid-2026, with clinical trial initiation by the end of the year.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter saw significant growth in collaboration and license revenue, primarily driven by an increase in revenue recognized under the collaboration and license agreements with AbbVie and Gilead. Additional collaboration payments and a February follow-on offering further strengthened the company’s cash position.

Research and development expenses rose as Xilio moved masked immuno-oncology programs like XTX501 and XTX601 toward clinical phases. This increase supported IND-enabling studies and preclinical activities essential for upcoming trials in solid tumor indications.

General and administrative spending declined nearly 19% year over year. Management attributed this decrease to lower professional and consulting costs and some reductions in personnel expenses.

What Xilio Therapeutics does

Xilio Therapeutics develops tumor-activated immuno-oncology therapies using proprietary masking technology designed to enhance treatment selectivity and safety in cancer care.
