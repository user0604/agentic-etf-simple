Key Takeaways
Urban One reported a broad-based revenue decline as business fell short of its internal forecasts.
EPS loss reduced to $(0.69) per share compared to the prior year’s loss.
Management cut its 2026 Adjusted EBITDA outlook to $60 million, adjusting for continued weak advertising demand.

Urban One (NASDAQ:UONE) posted weaker results than planned, with revenue declines across every business segment and a lower profit outlook. The leading multimedia company serving African-American and urban audiences continued to face soft advertising demand in radio, digital, television, and its Reach Media unit. The company released these results on May 14, 2026, reporting for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period brought lower revenue and profits, with financial management offsetting some operational challenges.

Notable developments
All business segments, including radio, digital, television, and Reach Media, posted revenue declines. Digital revenue fell 33.5% and television dropped 18.5% year over year.
Management reduced the company’s 2026 profit outlook, now expecting $60 million in adjusted EBITDA, reflecting ongoing soft advertising demand.
The company continued its debt reduction plan, cutting long-term debt by $60.2 million year to date, which will lower annual interest costs by $4.6 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management updated its 2026 outlook to a lower profit target, responding to weaker than expected operating results and advertising uncertainty. The company is optimistic for improvement in digital revenues later in the year, but execution in digital and Reach Media will be essential. Investors should watch for further updates on the pace of advertising recovery and tangible improvement in digital segment performance.

Quarterly highlights

Radio, Urban One’s core business, saw revenues fall in the quarter, but outperformed overall local radio industry declines. National radio revenue decreased more sharply, signaling continued challenges for the traditional advertising base.

The digital segment posted the steepest decline, with revenue falling by a third as demand for advertising related to diversity and inclusion campaigns nearly evaporated. Management expressed some optimism for a recovery in the second half of the year, citing improvements in the sales pipeline.

Debt management remained a focus, with the company repurchasing and retiring a significant amount of its outstanding notes at a discount. While this has not yet translated into a profit gain due to accounting rules, the moves will reduce future interest expense and improve liquidity flexibility.

What Urban One does

Urban One is a multimedia company focused on radio broadcasting, cable television, and digital media for African-American and urban audiences.
