Notable developments
Total revenue climbed to $8.4 million CAD, up approximately 640% from the prior-year quarter, led by Drone as a Service sales after 21 surveying firm acquisitions.
Operating expenses soared to $30.0 million, with significant jumps in wages, marketing, and stock-based compensation following integration of new businesses.
Cash at period end was $8.5 million, with improvements in working capital driven by fundraising and debt reduction, but ongoing operating cash outflows persisted.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management cited strong liquidity and available credit lines to support operating needs over the next year, though it signaled that the pace of expansion and ongoing mergers and acquisitions will keep expenses high. The company did not issue specific financial guidance. Investors will want to monitor whether ZenaTech can begin to control costs and move toward profitability as it continues integrating dozens of acquired businesses.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue growth was driven almost entirely by the Drone as a Service business, which generated $7.8 million as recently acquired survey companies were added to consolidated results. Meanwhile, Software as a Service sales declined, mainly due to lower demand for legacy products and a focus on integrating product lines.

Operating expenses rose sharply, with stock-based compensation more than doubling and payroll, support fees, and marketing scaling up alongside the expanded workforce and rebranding efforts. ZenaTech’s loss per share widened to ($0.50), reflecting heavy dilution as shares outstanding increased to nearly 62 million from about 25.5 million a year earlier.

The cash position ended higher at $8.5 million, supported by new financing and a decrease in total liabilities. However, ZenaTech’s first-quarter operating activities used nearly $18.9 million in cash, emphasizing the company’s dependence on ongoing fundraising during this rapid growth phase.

What ZenaTech does

ZenaTech develops and integrates cloud-based enterprise software and proprietary drone technology, offering both Software as a Service and Drone as a Service solutions for commercial surveying and analysis.
