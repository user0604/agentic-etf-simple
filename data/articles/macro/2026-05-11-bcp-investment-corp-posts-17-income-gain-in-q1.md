Key Takeaways
Quarter delivered improved income but saw a steep net asset value decline.
Net investment income per share rose 17.0% year over year.

BCP Investment Corp. (NASDAQ:BCIC) delivered higher investment income but faced continued erosion in net asset value as market-driven markdowns in its software investments weighed on results. The business development company focuses on generating income and capital appreciation from a diversified portfolio of debt and equity investments in middle-market companies. For the first quarter of fiscal 2026, reported on May 8, 2026, management acknowledged progress on income generation but highlighted ongoing portfolio valuation challenges. Overall, the period reflected a mixed picture, with improved credit quality but persistent pressure on asset values.

Notable Developments
Total investment income increased 45.4% from the prior year, reflecting higher yields and loan deployment.
Non-accruals — loans not currently paying interest — fell to 2.6% of the portfolio at fair value, the lowest in several quarters.
Net asset value per share dropped by 6.5% quarter over quarter amid significant unrealized losses, with about 40% of markdowns connected to software sector investments.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its plan to prioritize disciplined investment, ongoing share repurchases, and steady distributions. It noted “continued focus on repositioning and balancing near-term volatility,” especially within software investments. Investors should watch for further changes in the value of the portfolio, particularly unrealized losses and the pace of repayments versus new investments, as these affect future income power and net asset value.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s investment income climbed, as both core and total investment income rose sharply from the previous year. That growth was mainly due to increased interest from a broadly diversified debt portfolio concentrated in first lien loans.

Credit quality improved, with non-accrual investments shrinking both as a percentage of the portfolio and in the number of affected borrowers. This suggests management’s focus on credit discipline and risk management is delivering some results despite the backdrop of market volatility.

Despite income gains, the quarter saw a steep decline in net asset value per share — driven largely by unrealized markdowns in software and technology-related investments. Management described these as “market-driven valuation pressure rather than fundamental credit deterioration,” but they remain a key concern for shareholders.

BCP Investment Corp. is a business development company that invests in secured loans and equity of middle-market companies to produce income and capital growth for its shareholders.
