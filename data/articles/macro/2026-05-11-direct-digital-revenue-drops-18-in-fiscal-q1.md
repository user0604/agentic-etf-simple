Key Takeaways
Direct Digital reported another challenging quarter with lower demand from key advertising customers.
Revenue declined 18% year over year.
Management did not provide specific guidance but highlighted ongoing cost control and strategic focus.

Direct Digital (NASDAQ:DRCT)’s first quarter 2026 results showed a difficult period as it faced shrinking demand from core demand-side advertising customers. The digital ad platform provider, which connects brands and agencies to targeted audiences using programmatic technology, struggled to offset a sharp drop in demand-side platform (DSP) spending. The company released its quarterly results on May 11, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter brought improvements in margins and cost controls, but saw revenue fall and liquidity concerns remain high.

Notable developments
Revenue dropped $1.5 million compared to the prior-year period, mainly due to a $2.0 million reduction in demand from DSP customers.
Gross margin improved to 34%, up five percentage points from a year earlier, helped by lower costs, and product mix.
Net loss narrowed by $0.3 million year over year, but less than $1 million in cash remains on the balance sheet as of quarter end.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management focused on ongoing cost management, capital discipline, and product innovation, but did not offer specific financial guidance for the coming quarters. Leaders repeated their aim for improved performance in the second half of fiscal 2026, but the significant revenue shortfall in the first quarter signals this could be difficult. Investors should watch for signs that product launches like Ignition+, and new client outreach efforts can bring back top-line momentum and address ongoing liquidity risks.

Quarterly highlights

The steep revenue decline was driven by lower spending from programmatic advertising customers, with DSP-related revenue dropping sharply. This eroded top-line results despite attempts to broaden offerings to enterprise and new verticals. One-time costs for debt restructuring and settlements added further strain to reported earnings.

While overall revenue fell, gross profit held steady and gross margin improved by five percentage points. Management attributed this to mix and cost control strategies, including reductions in operating expenses, which dropped 13% compared to last year’s first quarter.

Liquidity remains a concern, as cash and cash equivalents totaled just $0.8 million at quarter end, compared to $28.3 million in current liabilities. The company acknowledged “substantial doubt” about its ability to continue as a going concern if funding options are not secured.

What Direct Digital does

Direct Digital operates a cloud-based ad technology platform that enables brands, agencies, and publishers to buy and sell digital advertising placements via programmatic tools, with a focus on serving small and mid-sized businesses.
