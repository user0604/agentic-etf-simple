Ooma Posts 75% Profit Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 4:28 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business subscription growth and recent acquisitions fueled a standout quarter for Ooma.
Non-GAAP EPS rose 75.0% year over year, reflecting substantial profit improvement.
Management raised full-year revenue and profit outlook, citing ongoing business momentum.

Ooma (NYSE:OOMA) delivered a notable win in its latest quarter thanks to strong growth in business services and a boost from two recent acquisitions. The cloud-based communications provider integrated new assets from FluentStream and Phone.com, expanding its customer base and recurring revenues. Reporting on May 26, 2026, for its fiscal first quarter ended April 30, the company saw broad-based gains that led it to increase guidance for the full year. Overall, the quarter featured broad strength in both subscriptions and profitability, supported by operational execution and continued product development.

Notable developments
Business subscription revenue surged after the additions of FluentStream and Phone.com, which contributed $11.5 million in first-quarter revenue, including $11.2 million of business subscription revenue.
Profitability improved sharply, as non-GAAP net income climbed to $9.7 million, and Adjusted EBITDA grew 78% year over year to $11.8 million.
Operating cash flows increased to $6.4 million, while Ooma directed cash toward paying down $5 million in debt.
Management outlook and what to watch

Ooma raised its full-year fiscal 2027 revenue outlook to a range of $326 million to $328.5 million and expects non-GAAP net income between $37.5 million and $39 million. Management anticipates continued growth from AirDial and acquisition synergies but flagged integration and potential average revenue per user (ARPU) dilution as challenges to monitor. Investors should closely watch the company's progress in realizing further cost synergies and delivering on new product contributions, especially from AirDial and Ooma AI.

Quarterly highlights

Business services remained central to quarterly gains, with subscription and services revenue representing 92% of the total and accelerating after the added impact of FluentStream and Phone.com, which together delivered $11.5 million in revenue. AirDial, Ooma’s device for replacing aging copper phone lines, posted rising sales as the company captured new customers in a growing niche.

Operating profitability saw sharp improvement, with Adjusted EBITDA growing 77.5% versus the prior year, driven by the larger customer base and steady gross margins despite a higher mix of hardware sales in product revenue. Non-GAAP net income rose to $9.7 million, reflecting ongoing cost discipline as operating expenses increased more slowly than revenue.

Ooma continued to invest in technology, launching new AirDial features, introducing Ooma AI for communication management, and rolling out “MyPhone”, a residential service targeted at families seeking alternatives to cell phones for kids. These product innovations are designed to fuel future growth and support customer retention in both business and residential segments.

What Ooma does

Ooma provides business and residential cloud-based communications services and devices, focusing on voice, messaging, and collaboration solutions.
