Earnings Preview: PagerDuty

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

PagerDuty (NYSE:PD), which provides software that helps businesses manage and automate digital operations, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

PagerDuty delivered fourth-quarter revenue of $125 million, which was up 3% from the prior year and above analyst expectations. The company also reported a non-GAAP operating margin of 24%, which exceeded its guidance range. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) exited the quarter at approximately $499 million, reflecting a shift toward larger enterprise customers. The company achieved its first full year of GAAP profitability, supported by disciplined cost management and operational efficiency initiatives, particularly around artificial intelligence (AI) adoption. Dollar-based net retention was 98%, and total paid customers reached 15,351, a 2% increase year over year.

Positives
Revenue and margin outperformance: Both revenue and non-GAAP operating margin came in above guidance and consensus, indicating effective execution and cost control.
First full year of GAAP profitability: PagerDuty achieved sustained GAAP profitability for the first time, which demonstrates improved operational leverage and financial discipline.
Growth in large enterprise customers: The number of customers spending over $1 million in ARR increased 10% year over year, showing traction in the high-value enterprise segment.
Successful transition to flexible pricing: The new usage-based pricing model was well received by large customers, leading to several sizable multiyear deals and supporting future expansion opportunities.
Negatives
Modest ARR and customer growth: ARR grew only 1% year over year and total paid customer growth was limited, reflecting ongoing headwinds from seat-based pricing compression and macroeconomic pressures.
Churn in mid-sized customer segment: The number of customers with more than $100,000 in ARR grew just 1%, as some mid-sized customers reduced spending or churned, likely due to cost pressures and lower requirements for advanced features.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Progress on flexible and usage-based pricing adoption: Investors should watch for updates on how quickly existing customers are moving to the new pricing model and whether this is driving higher expansion rates or improved retention, especially among large enterprises.
ARR and customer cohort trends: The pace of ARR growth and changes in the number of large customers (over $100,000 and $1 million in ARR) will be important indicators of whether the company is successfully offsetting seat-based compression with expansion in strategic segments.
Guidance for revenue and margins: Management’s outlook for Q2 and the full year will provide insight into how PagerDuty expects to balance flat revenue growth with further operating margin expansion, as indicated in their initial FY2027 guidance.
Retention and renewal rates: The company has implemented new renewal initiatives and expects gross retention to improve in Q1. Watch for any reported progress in stabilizing or increasing dollar-based net retention and gross retention rates.
Updates on product innovation and AI partnerships: PagerDuty has announced new AI agent capabilities and expanded its ecosystem with partners like Anthropic and LangChain. Any early customer adoption metrics or new partnerships could signal future growth drivers.
