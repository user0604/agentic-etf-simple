Functional beverage progress was not enough to offset steep declines in overall sales and profitability for Synergy Chc (NASDAQ:SNYR). The consumer health and wellness company saw rapid expansion in its beverage division, but overall trading in the three months ended March 31, 2026 (reported May 14, 2026) was deeply negative compared to the same period a year ago. Overall, the quarter featured sharp revenue contraction, increased losses, and liquidity pressure despite notable gains in new product segments.

Notable Developments
Functional beverage revenue surpassed $650,000 in the quarter, already outpacing the total beverage sales for all of fiscal 2025.
The company ended the period with only $0.29 million in cash and had to raise $2.7 million via an equity program just after quarter close.
General and administrative expenses rose $0.74 million year over year, as beverage business expansion increased overhead.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects significant growth opportunities in beverages and expects the momentum in retail partnerships to continue through 2026. However, it did not provide concrete financial guidance for the rest of the year. The most important development to watch next is whether beverage revenue growth can scale enough to offset continued losses and address working capital constraints.

Quarterly Highlights

The standout result was the rapid growth in functional beverages, where revenue in this division for the quarter already exceeded the full prior year result. This marks meaningful progress for new products aimed at the “ready-to-drink” functional beverage market, supported by growing retail and distributor relationships.

At the same time, total company revenue dropped sharply year on year, as last year's one-time license revenue did not recur and out-of-stock situations severely limited online sales for the Flat Tummy supplement brand. These operational challenges weighed heavily on the broader business, with product sales down 18% and total sales falling by a third.

Cost control came under particular pressure as general and administrative expenses rose with investments in new product launches, while net losses deepened to $2.57 million and available cash plunged. Working capital also moved from a surplus to a deficit during the quarter, requiring an equity raise post-period to shore up liquidity.

What Synergy Chc Does

Synergy Chc is a consumer health and wellness company specializing in supplements and functional beverages, operating with an asset-light model through retail and online channels.
