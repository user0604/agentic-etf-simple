Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management confirmed its plan to maintain profitable growth, focusing on recurring and contracted revenue expansion, investment in its maintenance operations, and maximizing fleet efficiency. The company expects further margin improvement over time as more flights are fulfilled with its core fleet. Investors should closely monitor ongoing membership growth and retail sales trends, as early signs of weakness have begun to appear in some product segments.

Quarterly Highlights

Operational control improved, as flyExclusive eliminated three additional non-performing aircraft, trimmed operating losses tied to idle planes below $300,000 per month, and sustained 100% operational control of its 85-aircraft fleet. Fleet utilization increased, with a 15% jump in use per aircraft and a 7% increase in flight hours despite a reduced fleet size.

The company expanded its in-house Maintenance, Repair & Overhaul services, growing segment revenue by 14.0% year over year. It added a new maintenance facility and increased its mobile service units to thirteen, enhancing both cost efficiency and external revenue opportunities.

Although contractually committed and recurring revenue accounted for nearly half of total company revenues, JetClub retail sales declined by 11% year over year and retail member growth was modest. This may hint at either a shift in focus or competitive pressures in the private flying membership market.

What flyExclusive Does

flyExclusive provides private jet charter, fractional ownership, and jet club membership services using its own fleet, with extensive in-house maintenance and refurbishment capabilities.
