Key Takeaways
PDD Holdings delivered strong revenue growth but saw a notable decline in net income.
Revenue rose 11% compared to the prior year quarter.
Management signaled ongoing investment focus, offering no forward guidance.

PDD Holdings (NASDAQ:PDD) edged up its top line but faced a drop in net income as the company deepened investments in platform development and supply chain initiatives. The multinational e-commerce operator, best known for the Pinduoduo and Temu online platforms, reported its first-quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 28, 2026. Overall, the quarter displayed robust revenue growth yet highlighted the cost of PDD’s strategic pivot toward long-term transformation.

Notable Developments
Transaction services revenue rose 20%, while online marketing revenue increased only 2%, reflecting a shift in segment momentum.
Operating profits saw double-digit growth, outpacing overall revenue gains and suggesting improved operational efficiency for the period.
Net income dropped 15%, impacted by large non-operating losses, including negative investment and other income items.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management provided no quantitative or qualitative forward guidance, instead repeating its focus on long-term platform investment and transformation. With spending on research and development up 22% and significant resources devoted to supply chain upgrades, the company remains in an aggressive reinvestment phase. Investors should monitor how increased costs and a revenue mix shift between transaction and online marketing services affect future margins and earnings quality.

Quarterly Highlights

Transaction services, which include direct facilitation of e-commerce sales, grew sharply. Revenue in this segment increased from RMB 47.0 billion to RMB 56.3 billion, highlighting expanding platform use and potential user engagement improvement.

Online marketing services, PDD’s higher-margin advertising business, delivered only modest growth this quarter. Revenue from this area rose approximately 2.5% year over year.

Operating profit rose faster than revenue, reaching RMB 19.6 billion, while net income declined due to losses in non-operating items like investment income and other income. This difference implies that while PDD’s core platform operations strengthened, bottom-line results remain sensitive to factors beyond normal business activities.

What PDD Holdings Does

PDD Holdings operates global e-commerce platforms, including Pinduoduo and Temu, connecting buyers and sellers of a wide range of consumer products.
