Kalaris Therapeutics (NASDAQ:KLRS) reported steady business progress as it advanced its lead eye-disease drug into further clinical testing and kept its spending plan on track. The clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focuses on developing new therapies for retinal disease, led by its investigational drug TH103 for neovascular Age-related Macular Degeneration. The company released its first quarter results on May 12, 2026, covering the three months ending March 31, 2026. Overall, the period met management’s guidance, with important operational updates and continued financial discipline but no new clinical efficacy or safety data disclosed.

What Kalaris Therapeutics Does

Kalaris Therapeutics develops novel injectable therapies for eye diseases, focusing on retinal disorders such as neovascular Age-related Macular Degeneration.

Quarterly Highlights

The manufacture of new batches of TH103 incorporated incremental process improvements, aiming to further lower impurities as the company seeks optimized quality for larger studies. While no new safety or efficacy data was released for the quarter, Kalaris highlighted that patient screening is underway in the critical mid-stage clinical trial for TH103, with plans to inform future Phase 3 development.

Total research and development spending rose 26.7% year over year, in line with the opening of new trial sites and higher costs tied to expanding clinical activity; general and administrative expenses were unchanged. The net loss per share sharply decreased as a result of a higher average share count, which reflects prior equity capital raised to support trial activities through late 2027.

The company closed the quarter with $104.9 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities, stating that this balance will fund ongoing development plans and anticipated upcoming trial milestones. No revenue was reported, as is typical for companies still in clinical trial stages without an approved product.

Looking Ahead
TH103’s clinical program advanced as planned, with new manufactured batches and the start of patient screening in the Phase 1b/2 study.
Research and development expenses grew by 26.7% from the same quarter last year, reflecting stepped-up clinical activity.
The company’s cash holdings totaled $104.9 million, providing funding into the fourth quarter of 2027 and through planned key milestones.

Management reiterated that current cash resources are projected to support operations through late 2027 and major trial milestones. The next key event for the company is the first announcement of data from the ongoing Phase 1b/2 trial, expected in the first half of 2027. Investors should closely monitor R&D spending and any updates on safety or efficacy as the clinical program progresses.
