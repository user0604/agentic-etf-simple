Key Takeaways
The company posted steady revenue growth but saw further declines in net profit compared to the previous year.

LexinFintech (NASDAQ:LX) posted a mixed set of results as it continued to recover from major regulatory changes, with solid lending volume and revenue growth but ongoing pressure on profits. The leading online consumer finance platform in China reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 26, 2026, covering a period in which business volumes held up even as profitability weakened further. Overall, the quarter highlighted operational stability but did not resolve deeper concerns over credit risk and cost pressures.

Notable Developments
Loan origination volumes climbed 12.2%, driven in part by growth in installment e-commerce and a rising active user base of 5.2 million, up 8.6% year over year.
Despite the revenue increase, net income fell by 53.3% compared to the previous year as loan loss provisions and operating costs remained high.
Delinquency rates for loans more than 90 days overdue increased to 3.5%, reversing the improvement seen in the prior quarter, prompting continued risk provisioning.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management described lingering uncertainty in both the macroeconomic and regulatory environment and expects overall loan origination volumes for the second quarter to be relatively flat. No guidance was given for profit or margin levels, with the focus remaining on maintaining compliance and risk controls.

Quarterly Highlights

Loan originations reached RMB 57.9 billion, marking a return to growth after past declines, and nearly half of this came from non-traditional business lines such as installment e-commerce instead of consumer lending. This diversification has helped offset slower growth in the core consumer finance services, but the shift has not yet translated to stronger overall profits. The volume growth also lifted active users, with both registered user and repeat borrowing rates remaining high.

Profitability remained under pressure as net income fell sharply year over year and stayed flat from the previous quarter, even with revenue gains. Higher provisioning for potential loan losses, rising delinquency rates, and only slight reductions in funding costs weighed on margins and led to a net income margin of just over 6%. Operating expenses for items such as sales, marketing, and research remained roughly stable year over year, limiting progress on efficiency.

The company continues to emphasize its investment in technology and risk controls, but there was no new quantitative update on technology-driven gains this quarter. Management highlighted that business mix changes and ongoing adaptation to new regulations remain top priorities. The company also repurchased $39 million worth of stock since July 2025, reflecting a focus on shareholder returns despite tighter profits.

What LexinFintech Does

LexinFintech operates a technology-driven platform that connects Chinese consumers with financial products such as loans, installment e-commerce, and related services.
