Earnings Preview: HealthEquity

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

HealthEquity (NASDAQ:HQY), a provider of health savings accounts (HSAs) and other consumer-directed benefit solutions, is expected to report earnings after the market closes on Thursday, May 28, 2026.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, HealthEquity delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations. The company reported revenue of $334.6 million, representing 7% growth year over year, and adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.95, which beat consensus estimates of $0.90. Net income rose 89% to $49.7 million, while adjusted EBITDA increased 23% to $132.9 million, with margin expanding by over 500 basis points to 40%. The company added a record 550,000 new HSAs in the quarter, bringing total accounts to 17.8 million and HSA assets to over $36 billion. Management highlighted strong operating leverage, significant margin expansion, and deepening member engagement through digital and mobile channels. The company also returned over $300 million to shareholders via share repurchases in fiscal 2026.

Positives
Margin expansion and cost efficiency: Gross margin rose to 68% from 61% a year ago, driven by reduced fraud costs and improved service efficiency, reflecting the impact of technology and AI investments.
Record HSA account and asset growth: The company added over 1 million new HSAs for the year, and HSA assets grew 14% year over year, indicating continued demand for tax-advantaged health savings solutions.
Strong digital engagement: Mobile app downloads reached 3.6 million, showing increased adoption of digital-first healthcare management among members.
Shareholder returns: Over $300 million was returned to shareholders through share repurchases, reducing diluted shares outstanding by approximately 3%.
Negatives
Service revenue growth lagged other segments: Service revenue increased just 2% year over year, held back by lower average service fees per account, even as total accounts and HSA investments grew.
Early-stage marketplace revenue: The newly launched health marketplace (offering programs like weight loss and wearables) showed encouraging early engagement but has not yet contributed materially to revenue or guidance.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Progress on ACA exchange HSA adoption: Investors should monitor the pace of HSA enrollments from individuals selecting bronze plans on Affordable Care Act (ACA) exchanges, as this regulatory change expands the addressable market and could drive new account growth.
Custodial yield and interest rate impact: Watch for updates on the average yield on HSA cash, as management expects a rise to about 3.8% for fiscal 2027, which could influence custodial revenue given ongoing transitions from basic to enhanced rates contracts.
Marketplace traction and engagement metrics: Look for any updates on member participation and retention in the new health marketplace, as well as expansion into additional product categories, since early results have not yet materially impacted financials.
AI-driven operational efficiency: Pay attention to commentary on further cost reductions and service improvements from AI initiatives, especially in automating member and client service interactions, as these could support additional margin expansion.
Capital allocation and acquisition pipeline: Investors should note any updates on share repurchase activity under the remaining $178 million authorization, as well as management’s outlook for potential acquisitions or further debt reduction, given the company’s strong cash flow and balance sheet flexibility.
