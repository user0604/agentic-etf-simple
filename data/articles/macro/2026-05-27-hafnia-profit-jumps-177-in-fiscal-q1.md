Hafnia (NYSE:HAFN) delivered a strong quarter as higher freight rates and strategic fleet actions drove profit and dividends sharply higher. Notable developments
Net profit reached $179.7 million, while Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) income rose to $282.5 million.
Fleet renewal advanced as the company sold six older vessels and ordered eight new builds, increasing net asset value to $4.0 billion.
The quarterly dividend was $0.2877 per share, with a payout ratio set at 80%, returning $143.8 million to shareholders.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects an even stronger second quarter, with most vessel days already booked at higher rates. However, it cautions that geopolitical events and oil market shifts could impact freight demand and rates. Investors should monitor changes in shipping routes and vessel off-hire days that may affect utilization in the coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability improved as shipping rates climbed, with net profit per share more than doubling compared to the prior year. The revenue figure also increased by over 20%, driven by higher Time Charter Equivalent rates, especially for larger vessels such as LR2 and LR1 tankers that transport refined oil and chemical products.

Strategic fleet actions included selling six older vessels and securing contracts for eight new medium-range product tankers, which helped lower the average fleet age and strengthen the balance sheet. These steps contributed to a higher net asset value and a reduced net loan-to-value ratio, providing more financial flexibility.

On capital returns, the dividend payout rose more than fourfold over the prior year. Management maintained its policy of distributing 80% of net profit and also benefited from its investment in TORM, which contributed both dividend income and a valuation gain in the period. Hafnia’s new in-house artificial intelligence platform, Complexio, began to show early results by enabling quicker operational decisions across commercial and finance activities.

What Hafnia does

Hafnia operates one of the world's largest fleets of product and chemical tankers, transporting refined oil products and chemicals across global shipping routes.
