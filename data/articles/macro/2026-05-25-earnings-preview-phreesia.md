Earnings Preview: Phreesia

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Phreesia (NYSE:PHR), a provider of digital intake and payment solutions for healthcare organizations, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Phreesia reported revenue of $127.1 million, representing a 16% increase year over year. Growth was led by Payment Solutions, bolstered by the recently completed acquisition of AccessOne. Excluding AccessOne, revenue rose 7% year over year. Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was $29.4 million, with a margin of 23%. The company ended the quarter with 4,658 average healthcare services clients (AHSCs), up from the previous quarter, including 80 clients added through AccessOne. For the full fiscal year, Phreesia achieved positive GAAP net income for the first time as a public company, posting $2.3 million in net income. Cash flow from operations and free cash flow also reached record highs.

Positives
Achieved first full-year GAAP net income: The company posted $2.3 million in net income for fiscal 2026, a significant turnaround from a $58.5 million net loss the prior year. This milestone reflects improved operational efficiency and a shift to profitability.
Strong cash generation and margin expansion: Operating cash flow rose to $78.8 million for the year, with free cash flow at $54.4 million. Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $101.5 million, showing that margin expansion efforts are delivering results.
AccessOne acquisition integration progressing well: The acquisition, completed in November 2025, is performing in line with expectations. AccessOne contributed to revenue growth and expanded the company’s presence in healthcare payment solutions.
Operational leverage maintained despite revenue headwinds: The company kept its Adjusted EBITDA outlook unchanged for fiscal 2027, even while lowering its revenue forecast, indicating that cost controls and efficiency initiatives are working.
Negatives
Q4 EPS missed analyst consensus: The company reported Q4 EPS of $0.02, which missed the analyst consensus estimate of $0.07. This shortfall contributed to a negative market reaction.
Lowered revenue guidance for fiscal 2027: Management reduced its revenue outlook for the coming year to $510 million to $520 million, down from the prior range of $545 million to $559 million. This was mainly due to reduced visibility and lower spending commitments from certain pharmaceutical clients in the Network Solutions segment.
Network Solutions facing external headwinds: The company cited macroeconomic and regulatory factors, including reduced vaccine and public health marketing budgets, as reasons for lower Network Solutions revenue expectations. This segment is expected to have more variability and less growth in the near term.
Cash balance declined year over year: Cash and cash equivalents fell to $73.8 million at year-end from $84.2 million the previous year, primarily due to the AccessOne acquisition and related financing activities.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Network Solutions revenue stability: Investors should monitor whether spending commitments from pharmaceutical and life sciences clients stabilize or improve, as this segment’s outlook was the main reason for the lowered annual revenue guidance.
Progress on AccessOne integration and scaling: The company is working to expand AccessOne’s capital base and cross-sell its solutions to existing clients. Updates on new client wins, integration milestones, or expanded financing capacity could be significant.
Adoption and early results from ProviderConnect: ProviderConnect, a new marketing solution for healthcare provider marketers, launched in March 2026. While still small, any early traction or revenue contribution could help offset Network Solutions headwinds.
Impact of artificial intelligence initiatives on margins: Management highlighted ongoing use of AI to automate manual processes and reduce reliance on outsourced resources. Investors should look for evidence of continued margin improvement or cost savings attributed to these efforts.
Updated guidance or commentary on full-year outlook: Given the company’s lowered revenue forecast and external uncertainties, any new commentary on spending trends, client commitments, or revised guidance for fiscal 2027 will be important for assessing the trajectory for the rest of the year.
