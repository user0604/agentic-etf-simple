Willdan Group Posts 44% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:47 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Willdan Group raised its full-year targets after a strong start to 2026.
Adjusted diluted EPS increased 44.4% year over year.
Management now forecasts higher revenue and profits, but cash flow and cost discipline remain critical to watch.

Willdan Group (NASDAQ:WLDN) posted a business win this quarter as raised targets followed robust growth across its key segments. The energy solutions provider, known for delivering energy efficiency projects and consulting services nationwide, released results for its first fiscal quarter on May 7, 2026. This period showed improving profitability, above-expected earnings, and progress on strategic initiatives, though working capital metrics and rising costs signal areas requiring close attention.

Overall, the quarter reflected notable momentum in commercial markets and marked continued execution against its multi-year growth plans.

Notable Developments
Net revenue grew to $92.4 million, up 8.3% as commercial and energy solutions demand accelerated. The Energy segment saw net revenue increase by 11.3% to $67.0 million.
Profitability improved sharply, with net income up 82% year over year and adjusted EBITDA rising 25.4%.
Operating cash flow worsened to a use of $24.4 million, mainly due to increased receivables and falling accrued liabilities; general and administrative expenses rose 10.3% to $55.9 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 outlook, projecting adjusted net revenue of $410–$425 million, adjusted EBITDA of $100–$105 million, and adjusted diluted EPS of $4.90–$5.05. It attributes the increased targets to sustained demand, new commercial client wins, and margin expansion. The most important area to watch is whether the company can improve operating cash flow and manage growth in general expenses through the remainder of the year.

Quarterly highlights

Commercial markets continued to drive growth, supported by the integration of the Burton Energy Group acquisition and a shift in customer mix. Net revenue in the energy segment rose to $67.0 million, as the company increased services to Fortune 500 clients and broadened its geographic footprint. These gains offset only modest increases in the engineering and consulting segment.

Profitability saw considerable year-over-year improvement. Gross profit margin expanded by nearly three percentage points, and adjusted EBITDA increased 25.4%, aided by a decrease in subcontractor costs (now 40.4% of revenue, down from 44.0%). This was attributed to greater productivity and growth in higher-margin commercial business.

Working capital usage and higher costs were red flags. Operating cash outflows of $24.4 million reversed last year’s inflow, largely due to timing of receivables and settlements of accrued liabilities as large contracts ramped up. General and administrative expenses, especially in salaries and stock-based compensation, climbed ahead of revenue growth, highlighting the need for ongoing cost controls.

What Willdan Group does

Willdan Group delivers energy efficiency, engineering, and consulting services to utilities, public agencies, and private-sector clients across North America.
