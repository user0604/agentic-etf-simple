eToro Group (NASDAQ:ETOR) posted a robust business win as commodities trading surged and user growth accelerated. The global fintech platform operates a social trading network that lets users invest across stocks, cryptocurrencies, and commodities. Notable Developments
Commodities trading generated approximately 60% of trading commissions, with volumes up nearly fourfold from the prior year, offsetting softness in crypto trading.
Funded user accounts climbed 12% to 4.02 million as marketing investment rose, supporting continued platform expansion.
eToro launched several AI-powered features and finalized its acquisition of Zengo, advancing its strategy in both artificial intelligence and crypto wallet technology.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to invest in marketing, technology, and new product launches, emphasizing long-term user and asset growth over short-term guidance. For investors, the most important metrics to monitor are ongoing funded account growth and how well new product rollouts—particularly in AI and crypto—translate into consistent contribution and engagement in future quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Commodities trading volumes reached record levels, taking over as the primary revenue driver after weaker results from crypto trading. Management noted that commodity products represented the majority of trading commissions, showing the benefit of eToro's multi-asset approach.

The platform's user base saw a notable boost, with funded accounts up 12% versus the prior-year period. This growth came directly from higher marketing spend aimed at acquiring and retaining new users worldwide.

In technology, eToro rolled out the eToro App Store and several AI tools, including new agent-powered portfolios and sentiment integration. The completed acquisition of Zengo further expanded its crypto and decentralized finance capabilities, supporting its bid to serve both traditional and on-chain investors.

What eToro Group Does

eToro Group operates a global online platform enabling users to trade and invest across multiple asset classes, including stocks, cryptocurrencies, commodities, and more, with built-in social networking features.
