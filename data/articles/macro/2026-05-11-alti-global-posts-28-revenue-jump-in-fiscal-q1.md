Key Takeaways
AlTi Global reported improved revenue and profit on stable asset management operations.
Adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) rose 21% year over year in the quarter.
Management cited continuing cost-cutting but offered no formal full-year guidance.

AlTi Global (NASDAQ:ALTI)’s steady asset management business delivered strong revenue and profit growth compared to the previous year, even as expenses stayed high. The global wealth management and advisory firm focuses on ultra-high-net-worth clients and alternative investments, operating in 19 cities worldwide. The firm released its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026, showing continued evidence of progress on some cost and integration efforts balanced against ongoing efficiency challenges.

Overall, the quarter was marked by clear gains in recurring revenue streams and profitability, but operating expenses remain in focus as management continues its multi-year restructuring and integration plan.

Notable Developments
Recurring management and advisory fees made up nearly three-quarters of revenue, supported by high client retention and a 9% increase in assets under management to $48.7 billion.
Operating expenses increased in line with revenue, reflecting higher compensation due to acquisitions and integration costs, but normalized expenses fell sharply from the previous quarter as cost-cutting initiatives took effect.
Distributions from alternative investments were up 75% year over year, counterbalancing a sharp decline in performance-linked incentive fees compared to last quarter’s unusually strong showing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management pointed to further expense reductions over the coming quarters as a key priority, citing early progress from renewed cost discipline and benefits of zero-based budgeting. The most important area for investors to monitor will be whether the company sustains its efforts to bring down non-core expenses and drive positive operating income. No specific full-year financial guidance was provided in this release, reflecting a cautious but focused outlook.

Quarterly Highlights

Recurring revenue remained stable at 74% of the total, demonstrating the resilience of management and advisory fee streams. The company’s client retention remained very high at 96%, with the average client tenure near ten years, helping underpin the increase in assets under management and management fees.

Total operating costs increased year over year due to higher compensation linked to integration of recent acquisitions such as Kontora and increased professional fees. However, excluding these non-recurring items, normalized operating expenses dropped sharply from the prior quarter, suggesting new cost initiatives are beginning to show results.

The alternative investments segment contributed significantly to gross revenue through distributions, particularly after robust fund performance in 2025. Incentive fees, which can fluctuate sharply quarter to quarter, had little impact this period after being a major driver in the previous quarter’s results.
