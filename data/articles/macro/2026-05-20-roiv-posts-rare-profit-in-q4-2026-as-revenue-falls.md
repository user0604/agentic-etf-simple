Key Takeaways
Litigation gains drove Roivant to a rare profit, but core operating losses remain.
EPS swung from negative to positive year over year.
Management maintains guidance on major drug launches and clinical milestones for late 2026.

Roivant Sciences (NASDAQ:ROIV) posted a rare profit, thanks to a large litigation settlement, while operating losses excluding special items remained high. The biopharmaceutical group specializes in creating and operating “Vants”—subsidiaries focused on innovative therapies for autoimmune, inflammatory, and rare diseases. Reporting for its quarter ended March 31, 2026 (earnings released May 20, 2026), the company highlighted pipeline progress but again recorded minimal product revenue. Overall, the period showed disciplined clinical advancement amid continued reliance on its sizeable cash reserves.

Notable Developments
The company reported a $770.2 million one-time gain from settling patent litigation with Moderna, helping swing to a quarterly profit.
Research and development expenses increased 37 percent from a year ago, as key clinical programs advanced and the company wound down the discontinued batoclimab program.
Quarterly revenue fell 67 percent from last year, reflecting minimal commercial activity as major products remain in late-stage development.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to project that all major clinical development timelines remain on track, including the expected commercial launch of brepocitinib for dermatomyositis by September 2026. The main focus for investors will be late-2026 data readouts and the ability of new drugs to generate meaningful commercial revenue amid continuing operating losses.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s profit was almost entirely from a large legal settlement: Moderna agreed to pay Roivant’s Genevant subsidiary $950 million in July 2026 and another $1.3 billion if a related appeal is successful. This one-time event added $770.2 million in new gains for the period but does not reflect ongoing business operations.

Most product revenue remains out of reach, as the company continues to invest in its pipeline. Research and development spending rose sharply to support key studies for potential drugs like IMVT-1402 (an anti-FcRn antibody for difficult-to-treat rheumatoid arthritis), brepocitinib (for several autoimmune disorders), and mosliciguat (for lung disease).

The company discontinued its batoclimab program after it failed phase 3 trials, leading to about $39 million in related costs, but other late-stage drugs stayed on schedule and new clinical trials started as planned.

What Roivant Sciences Does

Roivant Sciences is a biopharmaceutical company that develops and commercializes innovative medicines by launching and managing focused subsidiary companies called “Vants.”
