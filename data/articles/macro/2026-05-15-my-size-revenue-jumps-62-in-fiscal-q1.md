Key Takeaways
Revenue rose sharply, but net losses also widened as costs outpaced growth.
Gross margin improved by nearly 11 percentage points over the prior year period.

Fashion technology firm My Size (NASDAQ:MYSZ) delivered another quarter of strong sales growth as it expanded its platform and product offerings. The company specializes in AI-driven size recommendations, managed inventory, and fashion resale solutions for brands and retailers worldwide. Its earnings release on May 15, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showed rapid top-line progress but also highlighted rising costs and widening losses. Overall, the period marked material revenue gains and technology integration, but at the expense of profitability.

Notable Developments
Revenue jumped 62% year over year, driven by growth in fashion e-commerce and the consolidation of the Percentil resale platform.
Gross margin expanded by nearly 11 percentage points compared to the prior-year quarter.
Net loss increased to $1.48 million as operating expenses, especially research and development, accelerated faster than revenue.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its commitment to growth through technology innovation and operational efficiency, but did not provide updated financial targets or break-even timelines. The company highlighted ongoing investment in AI sizing and continued integration of acquired businesses. Investors will be closely monitoring expense discipline and cash levels in coming quarters as operating losses and cash burn remain elevated.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenue figure climbed sharply, due to expanded e-commerce sales through the Orgad platform and the first full quarter of results from Percentil, the managed secondhand marketplace. Percentil’s integration helped diversify the company's reach into circular fashion and sustainability solutions for brands across Europe.

Gross profit and margin improvement were significant, underpinned by the scale-up of Naiz Fit, My Size’s AI-driven sizing solution for apparel and footwear, enhanced by new technology assets such as ShoeSize.Me. This underscores the importance of proprietary technology and data-driven services as a driver of higher-value sales.

Rising operating expenses, notably a near-tripling of research and development outlays, signaled investment in product innovation and infrastructure but also pushed net losses higher. The company’s cash balance ended the quarter at $910 thousand, so liquidity and funding are key areas to watch in future periods.

What My Size Does

My Size develops AI-powered sizing technology and managed platforms for fashion brands, including digital fit tools, surplus inventory solutions, and secondhand fashion marketplaces.
