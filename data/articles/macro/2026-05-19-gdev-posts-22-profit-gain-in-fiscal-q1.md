Mobile game publisher Gdev (NASDAQ:GDEV) improved its profitability as it squeezed more value from each user, but its base of paying players continued to decline. The company is a global developer and publisher of free-to-play games for mobile, desktop, and social platforms. On May 19, 2026, it released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showing signs of strong cost control but raising questions about the sustainability of its growth. Overall, the quarter provided a mix of improved profit margins and shifting revenue sources, but ongoing user base declines warrant close attention.

Notable developments
Profit increased 22% year over year, driven by disciplined spending and higher average bookings per user.
Monthly paying users dropped 5% to 269,000, while average bookings per paying user climbed 8% to $97.
Booking share shifted away from the US and Asia, with increased contributions from Europe and other regions.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue new financial targets or formal guidance for fiscal 2026. It pointed instead to ongoing marketing discipline and monetization efforts. The most important metric to watch next is the stabilization or recovery of the monthly paying user base, as declines here could weigh on future growth.

Quarterly highlights

Profit growth and margins improved due to a 13% reduction in selling and marketing expenses, reflecting a tighter approach to attracting high-value players versus broad acquisition. This was achieved even as total revenue edged up just 2%, underscoring the importance of efficiency gains over pure expansion.

The revenue figure was mainly the result of stronger in-app purchasing by a shrinking group of users. While average spending per paying user rose 8%, the number of monthly paying users fell 5%, showing that the company's growth is coming from more engaged existing customers rather than from adding new ones.

Geographically, the mix of bookings shifted as the US and Asia contributed a combined 49% (down from 54%), with Europe and “Other” markets making up a rising share. In platform terms, mobile gaming accounted for 64% of bookings against 59% a year ago, pointing toward increasing reliance on mobile and more competition in that segment.

What Gdev does

Gdev develops and publishes free-to-play games for mobile, PC, and social platforms and generates revenue mainly through in-app purchases and advertising.
