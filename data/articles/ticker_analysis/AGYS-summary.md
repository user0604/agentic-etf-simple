AGYS Company Overview
Agilysys provides specialized software for the global hospitality industry, offering property management systems for hotel bookings and point-of-sale tools for processing restaurant transactions. The company generates revenue through ongoing subscription fees for its cloud-based services and professional installation support for hotels, casinos, and stadiums. By integrating essential operations like reservations and payments into one platform, the company creates a unified environment that encourages customer loyalty and significant switching costs.

Agilysys Grows Recurring Revenue With New AI Software

Agilysys makes cloud software that helps hotels, casinos, and resorts manage their properties and improve guest experiences. The company stands out because most of its revenue now comes from subscriptions, showing a shift to steady, recurring business. Over the last five years, the stock returned 49.2%, underperforming the S&P 500 by 30.6 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $82.9 million (up 11.7% YoY)
Adjusted EBITDA (Non-GAAP): $21.5 million (up 45.3% YoY)
Recurring revenue: $54.4 million (up, now 65.5% of total revenue)

Latest Developments

The company increased its guidance for next year, showing management’s confidence in future growth and progress toward more recurring and subscription revenue.
Agilysys rolled out new AI-powered software and won more clients in hotels, resorts, and casinos, which could drive long-term customer loyalty and growth.
