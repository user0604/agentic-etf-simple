Key Takeaways
American Eagle Outfitters delivered record revenue growth, driven by Aerie but challenged by core brand softness.
Revenue rose 10% compared to the prior year.
Management reiterated its full-year outlook despite headwinds from elevated inventory and anticipated margin pressure.

American Eagle Outfitters (NYSE:AEO) posted a record quarter, with strong gains at its Aerie brand offsetting a decline in its core American Eagle business. The retailer, which targets teens and young adults with apparel and accessories, released results for the 13 weeks ended May 2, 2026, on May 28, 2026. Overall, the period showed robust sales growth and improved profitability, but inventory and gross margin remain areas to watch.

Notable developments
Aerie, the active and intimates brand, led growth, with revenue up approximately 34% and comparable sales up 25% year over year.
The American Eagle brand saw revenue decline 2% and comparable sales drop 2% in the period.
Inventory increased 27% compared to last year, rising to $817 million, which may restrict margin flexibility in coming quarters.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management kept its full-year guidance unchanged, projecting mid single-digit comparable sales gains and operating income of $390 million to $410 million for fiscal 2026. However, it expects gross margin pressure to persist into the next quarter, partly due to inventory growth and increased tariffs. Inventory levels and the ability of the core American Eagle brand to recapture sales momentum are the key factors to monitor going forward.

Quarterly highlights

Aerie continued to power the overall business, achieving record revenue and strong customer acquisition, as management spotlighted the impact of its “100% Aerie REAL” inclusivity campaign. Results show that multi-channel investments, which enable customers to shop in-store and online seamlessly, remain central to Aerie’s double-digit growth.

American Eagle, the company’s flagship apparel brand, experienced a sales dip, with management noting challenges in its women’s business and ongoing efforts to improve product selection and marketing effectiveness. Despite these challenges, investments continued in store updates and digital strategies aiming to restore growth.

Inventory outpaced sales growth, with a 27% increase year over year, raising concerns about future margin impact if demand slows or product sell-through lags. This aligns with management’s guidance that gross margin could face near-term pressure from both elevated inventory and higher anticipated tariffs during the second quarter.

What American Eagle Outfitters does

American Eagle Outfitters is an apparel and accessories retailer with brands including American Eagle and Aerie, selling primarily to teens and young adults through stores and digital channels in North America and select international markets.
