HomesToLife (NASDAQ:HTLM) posted a strong quarter, led by growth in export markets and margin improvement. Notable developments
Export revenue grew 17%, led by Europe (up 26%) and North America (up 14%), while Asia-Pacific dipped 2%.
Gross margin improved to 29.0%, credited to a more favorable sales mix as retail expansion and higher-margin products gained share.
Operating cash flow swung positive, reaching $2.0 million compared to an outflow in the prior year, highlighting better working capital management.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management offered full-year fiscal 2026 revenue guidance of $400 million to $420 million, pointing to continued strength in the export market. There was no update on margin or profit forecasts, but management noted ongoing supply chain and cost challenges. The most important item for future quarters is whether the retail division can make progress toward profitability while export momentum remains intact.

Quarterly highlights

The export business delivered strong gains, generating $87.1 million in revenue as European sales led the way. Retail sales in Korea, while still a small part of the total, jumped 72% and signaled early success from store expansions, though the segment posted another operating loss.

Gross profit increased 34%, resulting in higher gross margin thanks to more sales of higher-value and differentiated products. Rising logistics and selling expenses—up 32%—helped drive operating costs higher, but the company still more than doubled operating income year over year.

On the balance sheet, cash ended above $26.6 million and short-term borrowings fell 65% from the prior year, improving liquidity. Net cash provided by operating activities improved to $2.0 million, attributed to better working capital discipline.

What HomesToLife does

HomesToLife designs, manufactures, and sells customized leather and fabric-upholstered furniture to consumers through export and retail channels with a focus on differentiated product offerings.
