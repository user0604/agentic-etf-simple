Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects sustained profit growth, citing positive trends in Canadian and U.S. banking along with ongoing cost management. The main area to monitor is the timeline and final cost of regulatory compliance efforts, particularly anti-money laundering remediation in the United States, which may pose future expense risks.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability improved as non-GAAP earnings per share climbed to $2.38, and the return on equity reached 14.4%. These gains reflect stronger underlying performance across Canadian personal and commercial banking, U.S. operations, and wholesale banking activities.

Credit quality remained healthy, with provision for credit losses dropping by over $300 million from the prior year. This decrease signals stable loan performance and only modest increases in impaired loans, as management had forecasted.

While expenses grew, the bank emphasized ongoing cost reduction efforts and digital transformation to help offset regulatory compliance spending. Management reaffirmed its plan to keep adjusted expense growth within a 3–4% range for the full year, despite pressures from required investments in risk and governance processes.
