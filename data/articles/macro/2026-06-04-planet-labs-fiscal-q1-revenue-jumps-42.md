Planet Labs Fiscal Q1 Revenue Jumps 42%

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 4, 2026, 4:21 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Record revenue growth driven by new government and commercial contracts.
Revenue rose 42% year over year.
Management raised full-year guidance and projects a return to adjusted EBITDA profitability next quarter.

Earth imaging company Planet Labs PBC (NYSE:PL) delivered a quarter of record revenue, reporting strong demand from new and existing customers. The company provides daily satellite imagery and analytics to government and commercial clients worldwide. In its results for the first quarter ended April 30, 2026 (announced June 4, 2026), Planet Labs grew its backlog substantially, though margin pressures and a temporary adjusted EBITDA loss tempered profitability. Overall, the quarter showed robust business momentum matched with heavy investment and continued growth in future contract commitments.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed to $94.2 million from $66.3 million a year ago, led by large defense contracts and strong commercial demand.
Backlog soared 72% to $906 million, with about 40% expected to convert to revenue within 12 months.
Free cash flow dipped to negative $2.5 million as Planet Labs increased capital spending on new satellite deployments and software development.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised fiscal 2027 full-year revenue guidance to $425–441 million, reflecting continued strong contract signings and execution. It expects to return to adjusted EBITDA profitability in the second quarter and forecasts gross margins slightly narrowing to 52–54% for the year. Investors should monitor margin trends and the pace at which significant backlog turns into realized revenue as new contracts ramp up.

Quarterly Highlights

Planet Labs expanded its contract backlog sharply during the period, with remaining performance obligations reaching $816 million, up 81% from last year. New government deals—such as an eight-figure satellite capacity contract with Sweden and a $21.9 million extension with the U.S. National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency—added to its growth pipeline. The company also signed new commercial agreements, including awards from John Deere and other agricultural and environmental partners.

Profitability softened due to higher operating costs and $6 million in litigation expenses recognized this quarter. Gross margin on a Non-GAAP basis came in at 56%, above the guided range but down by 3 percentage points from last year. Adjusted EBITDA slipped into negative territory, but management noted this was still better than its forecasted loss for the quarter.

Technology advances continued with three new Pelican satellites launched—including a rapid delivery for Sweden's defense program—and a beta release of an AI-powered imagery search tool. These innovations are designed to keep Planet Labs at the front of the Earth observation data sector and appeal to both government and commercial clients alike.

What Planet Labs PBC Does

Planet Labs operates a fleet of Earth observation satellites, providing daily global imagery and data analysis services to customers in government, agriculture, environmental monitoring, and other sectors.
