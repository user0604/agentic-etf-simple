Key Takeaways
Sol Strategies completed a major acquisition but saw a decline in key staking participation.
Total SOL staked in STKESOL is lower in the May 2026 update (624,274 SOL) compared to the approximately 768,000 SOL reported at the end of March 2026.
Management highlights diversification efforts, with an optimistic but non-quantitative outlook for the rest of 2026.

Blockchain infrastructure company Sol Strategies (NASDAQ:STKE) closed its acquisition of Houdini Swap, expanding its revenue sources and platform reach. The business, which focuses on staking, validator infrastructure, and liquid staking services in the Solana blockchain ecosystem, released its second-quarter update on June 3, 2026, for the quarter ended March 31, 2026. The period featured progress in diversification but a notable drop in key staking metrics, raising questions about growth momentum.

Notable Developments
The Houdini Swap acquisition closed on June 1, 2026, adding new crypto swap services and revenue streams; Houdini generated $13 million in revenue during 2025.
Total SOL staked in the STKESOL protocol dropped from approximately 768,000 SOL at the end of March 2026 to 624,274 SOL in May, showing a decline in this key metric.
The company maintained 100% validator uptime and delivered a peak annual percentage yield (APY) of 5.92%, outperforming the network average of 5.59%.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management points to the Houdini Swap acquisition as a step toward greater revenue diversity and plans to integrate services across more blockchain networks. No quantitative financial guidance was provided for upcoming quarters. Investors should watch for more detailed financial reporting and updates on STKESOL participation to assess growth beyond acquisitions.

Quarterly Highlights

The closing of the Houdini Swap deal marks a move to reduce reliance on Solana-only services and broaden the company’s product mix. Houdini processed $2.5 billion in total swap volume before joining Sol Strategies and now routes crypto swaps across more than 100 blockchain networks.

Operationally, Sol Strategies reported 3,649,585 SOL in assets under delegation and maintained perfect validator uptime, supporting reliability in its infrastructure offerings. Despite these technical strengths, STKESOL protocol usage declined by about 19% from 768,000 SOL to 624,274 SOL from the end of the previous quarter.

The company’s treasury balance at the period’s end was 521,174 SOL, valued near CAD $57.6 million, down slightly from earlier levels due to cryptocurrency price changes. Management noted that the Houdini acquisition was funded without reducing the treasury’s SOL holdings.

Sol Strategies provides digital-asset infrastructure services, including validator operations and liquid staking products within the Solana blockchain ecosystem, and now operates a multi-chain crypto swap platform through Houdini Swap.
