Key Takeaways
Production reached a new high, but profits dropped due to rising costs and hedging losses.
EPS fell sharply year over year as expenses climbed, while revenue edged higher.
Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance but flagged cost pressures and disciplined spending.

Production growth for Granite Ridge Resources (NYSE:GRNT) hit record levels, but rising costs and losses from hedging contracts weighed down profits this quarter. The independent oil and gas producer with assets across key U.S. basins reported its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period saw strong output but margin pressures from both higher expenses and non-cash losses.

Notable Developments
Average daily production jumped 17.8%, reaching 34,467 barrels of oil equivalent per day, led by new wells in the Permian Basin.
Lease operating expenses increased 55% to $29.7 million, or $9.57 per barrel of oil equivalent, far above the company’s annual cost guidance.
The regular quarterly dividend remained steady at $0.11 per share, underlining management’s continued focus on balanced returns.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance for production, spending, and costs, but noted that lease operating expenses have run above forecast. Investors should pay close attention to whether cost pressures, particularly around water disposal and equipment in key basins, ease as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Production hit an all-time high, with output up nearly 18% from the prior-year period. Most of this was driven by higher oil and gas volumes in the Permian and other major U.S. basins, though the number of new wells brought online dropped sharply as capital spending became more selective.

Costs rose much faster than production, with lease operating expenses per barrel up 55% year over year, mainly due to higher water management costs and nonrecurring charges. Management cited these as a key reason for margin compression and called certain expense increases nonrecurring, but monitoring future expense containment is critical.

Profitability was also hit by non-cash derivative losses from the company’s hedging program, which locks in commodity prices but can lead to large accounting losses if market prices move unfavorably. Adjusted earnings per share, stripping out these non-cash charges, fell sharply from a year ago.

What Granite Ridge Resources Does

Granite Ridge Resources is an independent energy company focused on acquiring and developing oil and natural gas assets across multiple U.S. basins through operated partnerships and non-operated investments.
