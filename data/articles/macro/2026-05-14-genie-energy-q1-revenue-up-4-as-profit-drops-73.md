Key Takeaways
Genie Energy recorded record revenue, but saw a sharp decline in profit and narrowed its full-year guidance.
Earnings per share fell to $0.11 from $0.40 year over year.
Management expects profitability to recover in the coming quarters, but lowered its adjusted EBITDA target range for fiscal 2026.

Genie Energy (NYSE:GNE) posted record sales for the quarter, but profit dropped sharply as higher costs squeezed margins. The global energy supplier and renewables developer provides electricity, natural gas, and green energy to customers in 19 US states and Washington, D.C. It released its first quarter fiscal 2026 results on May 14, 2026, showing the impact of increased expenses and lower segment margins on its bottom line. Overall, the quarter reflected strong sales expansion but declining profitability across its major business lines.

Notable developments
Record consolidated revenue reached $142.3 million, up 4.0% from the prior year, while gross profit fell by 20.2% as cost pressures persisted.
The core retail energy business reported declining profitability; income from operations dropped over 60% year over year, with gross margin for this segment sinking to 21.6%.
Renewables segment revenue surged 74.2% to $7.5 million, but the unit posted a greater operating loss and saw gross margin contract sharply due to an inventory write-down and higher upfront spending.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management lowered its guidance for full-year adjusted EBITDA to $32.5–$40 million, down from the previous $40–$50 million range. It expects retail energy profit margins to rebound later in the year as commodity market conditions stabilize and customer acquisition efforts support growth in its base. Investors should watch for signals of margin recovery in both core retail and renewables businesses during the next two quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The retail segment—focused on supplying electricity and natural gas—generated 1.7% more revenue than the prior year, but higher energy prices and acquisition expenses squeezed profitability. The number of Residential Customer Equivalents (RCEs), which indicates the active retail customer base, fell to 354,000, an 11.7% year-over-year decline, despite aggressive efforts to attract new customers.

In renewables, Genie Solar contributed to a 74.2% jump in segment revenue, mainly from panel sales and new project expansions. However, the renewables business remained unprofitable, facing a larger loss than last year, as well as a drop in gross margin due to a solar panel inventory write-down and increased early-stage investment.

Although the company posted a quarterly record for revenue, both gross profit and cash from operations declined compared to last year. Operating margin fell from 9.9% to 1.3%, reflecting rising costs in both retail energy and renewable initiatives. The balance sheet remained strong, with just under $200 million in cash and marketable securities at quarter-end.

What Genie Energy does

Genie Energy provides retail electricity, natural gas, and renewable energy solutions—including solar power and energy advisory services—to residential and business customers across the United States.
