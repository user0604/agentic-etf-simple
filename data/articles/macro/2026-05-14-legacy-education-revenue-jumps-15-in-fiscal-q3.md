Career education provider Legacy Education (NYSEMKT:LGCY) reported a strong quarter as its student population rose and new healthcare programs launched. The company, which operates healthcare training campuses in California, released its fiscal third quarter numbers for the period ending March 31, 2026, on May 14, 2026. Overall, growth in both enrollment and operating scale was balanced by rising expenses, especially in general and administrative costs.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 15%, reflecting growth in new student starts and a larger enrolled student base at quarter-end.
Operating expenses increased, with general and administrative costs up 33.5% due to higher marketing and professional fees.
New healthcare programs were launched at High Desert Medical College and Integrity College of Health, helping drive student enrollment to 3,550.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide a formal financial forecast, but continued to emphasize ongoing program expansion and disciplined execution. The company’s focus remains on growing its student base and launching new programs, while investors should closely monitor margin trends given rising general and administrative expense growth outpacing revenue. Regulatory compliance and successful geographic diversification are likely to remain important watch points for upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the period climbed to $21.4 million, a 15% increase fueled by new program launches and growing student demand in healthcare fields. Enrollment at quarter-end reached 3,550, up 9.4%, reinforcing continued robust demand for healthcare education and the impact of new programs such as Surgical Technology and Sterile Processing Technician.

Net income improved to $3.0 million, up 7.5%, while adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization) rose 12.6% to $4.4 million. However, the increase in operational profitability was partially offset as general and administrative expenses rose faster than revenues, driven by higher marketing outlays, professional fees, and increased non-cash compensation.

On the campus side, facility expansions at both the Lancaster and Temecula locations supported student growth, and a new branch campus letter of intent signaled future expansion. The strong balance sheet, with $21.7 million in cash and ample working capital, provides stability for ongoing investments in program offerings and campus upgrades.

What Legacy Education Does

Legacy Education provides career-focused education programs in healthcare fields, operating a portfolio of campuses and online offerings aimed at preparing students for allied health careers.
