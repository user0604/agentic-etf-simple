Notable Developments
Revenue dropped 11% from the prior year, with both technology and professional services segments declining.
Adjusted EBITDA rose 46% year over year, supported by lower costs and expenses.
The quarter included a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $95.5 million, which contributed to the $111 million GAAP net loss.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects continued revenue contraction for the remainder of fiscal 2026, with annual revenue guidance of $260 million to $265 million and adjusted EBITDA of $30 million to $33 million. The key issue to monitor is the execution of the company’s technology platform migration and its effect on client retention, as up to $65 million in recurring revenue is currently at risk during this transition.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue fell as both the technology segment (down 4%) and professional services (down 24%) posted declines. Technology now accounts for 70% of total revenue, reflecting a shift toward higher-margin offerings even as overall sales decreased.

Cost-cutting and restructuring efforts reduced adjusted operating expenses by 17% from the prior year, and stock-based compensation was more than halved. These actions helped offset weaker revenue, resulting in improved adjusted gross margin and higher adjusted EBITDA.

The company recorded a $95.5 million non-cash goodwill impairment tied to its technology segment, which drove most of the reported net loss. Management expects ongoing client churn and further professional services weakness as it completes the platform migration through 2027, making client retention and new wins important areas to watch.
