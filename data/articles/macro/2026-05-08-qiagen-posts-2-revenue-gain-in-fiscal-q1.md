Qiagen Posts 2% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 8, 2026, 6:20 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Qiagen posted mixed results as growth in core product lines offset declines in some diagnostic segments.
Earnings per share (adjusted, non-GAAP) held steady at $0.54 while revenue rose 1.9%.
Management lowered its full-year 2026 outlook, citing continued demand challenges and increased uncertainty.

Qiagen (NYSE:QGEN) struggled to sustain its recent momentum as strong gains in core product categories were offset by weaker demand for key diagnostics. The molecular diagnostics and life sciences supplier reported first quarter results for the period ending March 31, 2026 (released May 7, 2026) that highlighted both resilience and ongoing challenges. Overall, the quarter showed stable profitability but revealed pressure on growth targets and a more cautious outlook for the year.

Notable Developments
Sales in Sample Technologies rose to $170 million, up 13% year over year, partly due to demand for automation and consumables.
QuantiFERON, the company’s latent tuberculosis testing solution, saw sales fall to $113 million as immigration screening demand dropped in key markets.
Management reduced its full-year 2026 sales and adjusted earnings targets, now expecting only 1–2% currency-adjusted growth for revenue and at least $2.43 in adjusted earnings per share.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management now forecasts slower growth for fiscal 2026, with sales and earnings guidance cut due to lower QuantiFERON demand, ongoing caution among United States customers, and global uncertainty. Leaders anticipate stronger performance later in 2026 as new products and acquisitions begin contributing more meaningfully. Investors should closely monitor progress in high-growth segments and signs of demand stabilization in key diagnostic categories.

Quarterly highlights

Sample Technologies delivered 9% constant exchange rate revenue growth for the quarter. That performance was driven by increased adoption of automation and ongoing integration of the Parse acquisition, a recent purchase aimed at strengthening Qiagen’s position in single-cell analysis.

QuantiFERON, which provides tests for latent tuberculosis infection, saw demand shrink in both the United States and Middle East as immigration-related testing volumes dropped sharply. Management described this as more of a reset than a long-term decline for that testing group.

Revenue from PCR and nucleic acid amplification products--essential tools for analyzing genetic material--declined year over year, reflecting both softer market demand and a pause after rapid expansion in previous years. Digital and genomics categories showed modest gains, reinforcing the importance of technology innovation and recurring revenue streams.

What Qiagen does

Qiagen produces sample and assay technologies used in molecular diagnostics and life sciences research, supporting applications like disease detection and genetic analysis worldwide.
