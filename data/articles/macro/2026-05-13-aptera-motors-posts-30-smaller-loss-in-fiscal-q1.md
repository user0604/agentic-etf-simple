Management did not provide new financial guidance for the quarter and continues to highlight the need for additional capital to support production scaling. Quarterly Highlights

Aptera rolled its first five validation vehicles off the assembly line in Carlsbad, a step toward limited production after years of development. The company increased its headcount by 54 percent year over year, with most new hires in engineering roles, showing a focus on building production capabilities.

Operating expenses fell by 8% as Aptera tightened spending, but recurring net losses and a higher adjusted loss show it is still in investment mode without offsetting revenue. A $17.1 million capital raise boosted cash reserves to $17.7 million, but the company’s financial health remains dependent on continued access to outside funding, with an equity line of credit available but not fully drawn.

On the legal front, Aptera’s settlement of the Zaptera litigation removed a legal overhang, but other regulatory challenges, such as the ongoing SEC investigation, persist. The company’s reservation count was flat at 49,000 by period end, and Aptera did not provide further detail this quarter, leaving future demand and conversion to sales as open questions.
