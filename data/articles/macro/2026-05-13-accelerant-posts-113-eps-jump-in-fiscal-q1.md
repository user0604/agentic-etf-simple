Accelerant Posts 113% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:27 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Accelerant reported strong growth in fee-based revenues and continued its shift to a capital-light model.
Adjusted earnings per share more than doubled while adjusted EBITDA margin expanded year over year.
Management nudged full-year guidance upward, with increased targets for key premium and profit measures.

Accelerant (NYSE:ARX) extended its transition to a more profitable, capital-light business model with strong growth in fee-based revenues and expanding third-party participation. The specialty insurance platform facilitates connections between managing general agents (MGAs) and risk capital providers using a proprietary technology-driven exchange. The company released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026, showing broad progress but with some emerging areas for monitoring. Overall, the period demonstrated expanding margins and segment profitability, even as net revenue retention declined sharply.

Notable Developments
Third-party premium participation climbed to 41% of exchange volume, supporting the fee-based model shift.
Net revenue retention fell to 116% from 157% the prior year, reflecting portfolio changes following a planned member exit.
The company repurchased 828,333 Class A shares for $11 million as part of its authorized buyback program.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 targets slightly, forecasting at least $5.2 billion in exchange written premium and at least $285 million in adjusted EBITDA. Guidance for the second quarter points to sequential growth in written premium, while profit is expected to be flat to slightly lower compared to the first quarter. The evolution of net revenue retention and operating cash flow are important items to monitor next, given recent declines in retention and negative cash from operations.

Quarterly Highlights

The shift toward a capital-light, fee-driven model accelerated as third-party risk capital partners increased their share of the premium pool. That third-party share doubled year over year, which helped expand adjusted profitability and reduced the portion of risk held directly by the company.

Membership in the exchange grew to 296, up 28% versus last year, providing a broader base of specialty insurance offerings across 22 countries and roughly 700 products. This robust growth supports diversification but came with a notable reduction in net revenue retention from the prior year’s high level, as the impact of a terminated, low-margin member worked through results.

Profitability improved across most major metrics on an adjusted basis, with adjusted EBITDA margin rising to 24% from 22% the prior year and adjusted earnings per share more than doubling. The company did post a GAAP net loss and reported negative operating cash flow, which management attributed partly to working capital shifts and investments during the quarter.

What Accelerant Does

Accelerant operates a technology-powered insurance exchange platform that connects managing general agents and risk capital providers to facilitate specialty insurance underwriting in a scalable, capital-light manner.
