International travel demand pushed passenger volumes to new highs for Corporación América Airports (NYSE:CAAP). The leading global private airport operator, headquartered in Luxembourg, reported first-quarter results on May 11, 2026, for the period ending March 31. The quarter delivered strong growth in both traffic and profitability, with cost controls and record international flows offsetting some regulatory and inflationary pressures.

Notable Developments
Passenger traffic reached a record 12.5 million travelers in the quarter, up 5.4% from the prior year, with international passengers up 18.3%.
Operating profit rose 31.1%, supported by improved margins and effective cost controls, while net income more than doubled.
Regulatory negotiations with Argentine authorities remain unresolved, creating tariff and concession risk despite compliance with current commitments.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains cautiously optimistic, noting international growth should continue but at a more moderate rate, with full recovery in the domestic segment expected by the second half of 2026. Ongoing regulatory negotiations and inflation remain risks, especially regarding tariff adjustments and concession terms. Investors should monitor developments in Argentina’s regulatory environment, as outcomes could significantly affect revenue and profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

International passenger volumes set new highs, with records reached in each month of the quarter. The domestic segment saw a small decline, down 2.2%, mainly due to fleet availability issues affecting local airlines.

Non-aeronautical revenue, which includes retail and commercial activities in airports, made up about 38% of total revenue, slightly lower as a share but up 8.2% by value year over year.

Operating profitability improved as gross margin rose to 39.7%, and the company continued to reduce debt, with financial debt declining to $709,928 million and a lower debt-to-capitalization ratio of 28.3%.

What Corporación América Airports Does

Corporación América Airports operates dozens of airports across Latin America, Europe, and Eurasia, generating revenue from both passenger flights and a range of commercial services located in the airports it manages.
