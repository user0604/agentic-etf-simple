Web development platform Wix.com (NASDAQ:WIX) expanded its user base and showed strong adoption of its AI-powered products but faced lower profits this quarter as expenses increased. The company, which offers tools for users and businesses to create and manage websites, delivered results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter balanced significant top-line growth with margin pressure and higher spending as new investments took center stage.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 14% year over year as both creative subscriptions and business solutions segments expanded, with business solutions up 17%.
Non-GAAP gross margin declined by 2 percentage points to 66%, reflecting investments in growth and a larger share of lower-margin transaction revenue.
The company completed a $1.6 billion share repurchase, reducing its outstanding shares by about 30% and impacting free cash flow margins due to increased debt and interest expense.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its full-year outlook for revenue and bookings growth in the mid-teens percent range and expects free cash flow margin (excluding acquisitions) to be in the high teens. Executives highlighted rapid growth of the AI-driven Base44 platform and continuing strong new user cohorts. Investors should closely watch how higher spending on innovation and marketing impacts future profitability and cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter saw strong user engagement and new product adoption, especially with nearly 50% year-over-year growth for new user cohort bookings. The Base44 platform, which focuses on AI-powered site and app creation, reached $150 million in annual recurring revenue by May, up sharply from $100 million just months prior.

Expenses grew rapidly, with research and development (R&D) costs up 40% and selling and marketing spending up 79% compared to the prior year. These investments helped drive adoption but contributed to shrinking profit metrics and lower free cash flow, even as the revenue figure increased.

Wix’s remaining shares outstanding dropped to 41.8 million after a large share buyback. The company reported a GAAP net loss for the period, mainly due to higher costs and acquisition-related expenses.

What Wix.com Does

Wix.com is a web development platform that enables individuals and businesses to easily create, manage, and grow their online presence using intuitive design tools and a range of services.
