TEAM RULE BREAKERS

How well do you know personal finance? Do you know it at least as well as a high schooler?

Apparently, financial literacy among some high schoolers is now a cool thing to have. In fact, there’s even a national competition for it. This year, some 20,000 competed in the national competition that saw 37 teams from 33 states make it to the finals.

The tournament, hosted by the Council for Economic Education, is in its 15th year and the finals played out earlier this week. It tested their knowledge on all kinds of financial terms, many of which they wouldn’t normally be able to use until they’re much older.

Which type of retirement account does not require an annual distribution to begin at a certain age?

What type of U.S. government bonds adjust the principle value as inflation goes up or down?

What level should your credit utilization ratio be under in order not to negatively affect your credit score? (Do you even know what a credit utilization ratio is?)

Knowing the answers to questions like that should be de rigueur for anyone dealing with money. And I think it’s fantastic that some of our kids, at least, are finding it a way to show off. Make a game of it, I guess, and they’ll play (and even learn something).
