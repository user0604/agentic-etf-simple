Power Solutions Q1 Profit Falls 57% as Sales Dip

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 4:27 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business win/loss: The company faced a challenging start to the year, with profit and sales both declining from a strong prior-year period.
Performance: Adjusted earnings per share fell 57% year over year.
Outlook: Management expects sales growth in the second half of 2026 as major Power Systems orders move into production, but refrained from issuing formal full-year guidance.

Industrial engine maker Power Solutions International (NASDAQ:PSIX) struggled in the first quarter of 2026, with profitability and revenue both slipping compared to the exceptionally strong results it posted last year. The company supplies alternative-fueled and conventional engines for applications in energy, industrial, and transportation markets. In the earnings release dated May 11, 2026, it reported a transitional period marked by cost pressures and lagging demand in key segments. Overall, results highlighted margin compression and operational challenges, but with potential improvement expected in the second half of the year.

Notable Developments
Sales in the Power Systems segment dropped by $10.2 million, reflecting softer oil and gas demand and timing of data center shipments.
Gross margin fell by 6.8 percentage points due to elevated production costs and less favorable product mix.
Operating cash flow improved to $19.1 million, aided by efficient collections and working capital management despite profit pressure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue formal full-year guidance, instead pointing to a likely sales rebound in the second half of 2026 as large Power Systems orders tied to data centers are fulfilled. The main watch item is whether these projects convert to recognized sales as expected, especially given the continued volatility in oil and gas orders and ongoing production ramp-up costs in Wisconsin.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s revenue decline was driven mainly by weakness in the Power Systems business, with $10.2 million less in sales from that segment. Industrial and transportation end markets offered limited relief, showing modest sales increases of $3.0 million and $0.3 million, respectively. This underscores the company’s reliance on Power Systems for overall performance, while revealing some benefit from diversified market exposure.

Margins came under heavy pressure, with gross margin down nearly 7 percentage points year over year. This was due to a product mix shift away from oil and gas and additional costs tied to ramping up data-center-related production facilities in Wisconsin. The company did note margin improvement compared to the prior quarter, as operational changes began to take effect.

On the expense side, selling, general, and administrative costs rose 17%, with spending on research and development up 13%. R&D as a share of sales increased as the company continued investing in new engine programs, supporting its strategy in alternative fuel engines and data center power solutions.

What Power Solutions International Does

Power Solutions International designs, engineers, and manufactures alternative-fueled and conventional engines used in power systems, industrial equipment, and transportation vehicles for customers worldwide.
