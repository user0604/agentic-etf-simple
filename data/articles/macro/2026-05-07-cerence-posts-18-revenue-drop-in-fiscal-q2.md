Cerence (NASDAQ:CRNC) outperformed its earlier financial targets this quarter but saw notable declines compared to last year’s results. The specialist in automotive artificial intelligence (AI) voice and connectivity solutions reported earnings for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected progress on guidance, though ongoing variability in license revenue continues to impact comparisons year over year.

Notable Developments
Revenue for the quarter was $64.2 million, down from $78.0 million the previous year, mainly due to fewer large one-time software license deals.
Adjusted EBITDA dropped to $7.2 million as rising operating expenses, including increased research and development costs, weighed on profitability.
Free cash flow reached $13.6 million and the company increased its full-year earnings, revenue, and cash flow guidance midpoints, reflecting improved business visibility.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year targets for revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and free cash flow after exceeding its own guidance ranges this quarter. Leaders highlighted ongoing progress in recurring connected services, new AI-powered products entering production, and a focus on stabilizing revenue streams. Investors should monitor further adoption of Cerence’s next-generation AI solutions and how well recurring revenue replaces lumpy fixed license deals.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s revenue figure declined year over year, primarily due to much lower fixed license revenue—$5.8 million, compared with $21.5 million in the same period last year. These large, one-off contracts continue to cause swings in topline results, making comparisons unpredictable from quarter to quarter.

Connected services, which provide ongoing software and data services to carmakers, grew to $15.3 million—up 21% from the prior year—helping offset some of the volatility in other revenue streams. At the same time, professional services revenue decreased, in line with the company’s move to focus resources on higher-margin business.

The company’s operating expenses increased notably, especially in research and development, as Cerence invested further in its xUI interface and Audio AI platform. Although higher expenses reduced adjusted EBITDA, management pointed out that free cash flow rose and the cash balance strengthened, ending the period at $108.3 million.

What Cerence Does

Cerence provides artificial intelligence-powered voice, audio, and connectivity technologies for automotive manufacturers and suppliers worldwide.
