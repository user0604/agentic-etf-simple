Earnings Preview: Macy's

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Macy's (NYSE:M), a leading U.S. department store operator with national and luxury banners, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Macy's delivered results that exceeded both internal guidance and analyst expectations. Net sales reached $7.6 billion, with comparable sales rising 1.8% on an owned, licensed, and marketplace basis. Adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $1.67, well above the company’s guidance range. Bloomingdale’s, Macy’s luxury division, led growth with a 9.9% comparable sales increase, while the Macy’s nameplate posted 0.6% growth in go-forward locations. Bluemercury, the company’s beauty-focused banner, also saw positive comparable sales. The company ended the year with $1.2 billion in cash, a strong free cash flow position, and progress on its Bold New Chapter strategy, which emphasizes store reinvention, digital investments, and operational efficiency.

Positives
Results exceeded expectations across key metrics: Net sales, comparable sales, and adjusted EPS all beat analyst consensus and company guidance, indicating strong execution and positive customer response.
Broad-based growth across banners: Bloomingdale’s posted its best holiday season on record, and Macy’s Reimagine 125 stores outperformed the rest of the fleet, demonstrating the impact of targeted investments.
Healthy balance sheet and cash flow: Macy’s generated $1.4 billion in operating cash flow and ended the year with $1.2 billion in cash, supporting ongoing investments and shareholder returns.
Progress on strategic initiatives: The Bold New Chapter strategy delivered measurable improvements, including higher Net Promoter Scores and continued digital sales growth (35% of net sales).
Negatives
Gross margin rate declined year over year: Gross margin fell by 40 basis points to 38.0% of net sales, mainly due to tariff impacts, offsetting some operational gains.
Store closures weighed on total sales: The closure of 64 non-go-forward Macy’s stores contributed to a 2.4% decline in total net sales compared to the prior year, despite positive comparable sales in the remaining fleet.
Asset sale gains were lower than expected: Gains from real estate sales totaled $3 million in Q4, below both the company’s expectations and the prior year’s results, reflecting timing shifts in transactions.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales performance versus guidance: Management expects Q1 comparable sales to rise 0.5% to 1.5%. Investors should watch if Macy’s can maintain positive momentum, especially as the company laps strong prior-year results and faces a cautious consumer environment.
Gross margin pressure from tariffs: Tariffs are expected to reduce gross margin by 40 to 60 basis points in Q1. The impact of these headwinds on profitability and any updates on mitigation efforts will be important to monitor.
Progress of the Reimagine 200 initiative: The company expanded its store reinvention program to 200 locations (nearly 60% of the Macy’s fleet). Investors should look for early performance data from these stores and commentary on customer response and sales lift.
Bloomingdale’s and Bluemercury growth trends: Both banners delivered strong results in Q4. Updates on category performance, new brand launches, and market share gains will be key to assessing the sustainability of luxury and beauty momentum.
SG&A expense trends and investment pace: Macy’s plans 1% to 2% SG&A growth in fiscal 2026, with the highest spend in Q1 and Q3. Any deviation from this plan, or new details on cost control and investment returns, could affect earnings trajectory for the rest of the year.
