Praxis Precision Medicines (NASDAQ:PRAX) advanced its late-stage clinical programs and secured important regulatory milestones in the first quarter, positioning itself for potential product launches within a year. The neuroscience-focused company develops precision therapies for neurological disorders, targeting conditions such as essential tremor and rare epilepsies. Notable Developments
The U.S. Food and Drug Administration accepted two New Drug Applications: ulixacaltamide for essential tremor and relutrigine for rare developmental and epileptic encephalopathies, with relutrigine receiving priority review.
Research and development expenses increased to $78.0 million, up 28.3% from the prior year, reflecting expanded late-stage clinical trials and investment in the company’s platforms.
Cash and marketable securities at quarter end totaled $1.4 billion, a significant increase after a recent public offering, supporting operations into 2028.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects two potential product launches within the next twelve months, pending regulatory review. It is also preparing for key clinical milestones, including a Phase 3 readout for vormatrigine in epilepsy. The most important factor for investors to monitor is the outcome of regulatory reviews and launch preparations for ulixacaltamide and relutrigine.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s lead programs advanced, with the NDA for ulixacaltamide accepted for standard review and a possible U.S. launch in early 2027. Relutrigine’s NDA for SCN2A and SCN8A developmental epilepsies received priority review, moving its decision date earlier than a standard review would allow.

General and administrative expenses doubled compared to the prior year, driven by increased hiring, commercial infrastructure build-out, and launch readiness activities for late-stage products.

The cash balance benefited from a major equity raise, providing runway to support ongoing research and development and planned commercial launches. Management reiterated that this cash position is expected to fund operations into 2028, consistent with previous guidance.

What Praxis Precision Medicines does

The company develops precision therapies for neurological disorders, focusing on genetically defined and mechanistically targeted treatments for conditions such as essential tremor and rare epilepsies.
