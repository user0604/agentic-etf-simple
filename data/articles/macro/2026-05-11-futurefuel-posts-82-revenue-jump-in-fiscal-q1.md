May 11, 2026, 4:26 PM

Key Takeaways
FutureFuel delivered robust revenue growth but remained unprofitable due to one-time costs and operational disruptions.
Adjusted EBITDA improved year over year but remained negative.
Management maintains a positive outlook for full-year underlying profitability, highlighting regulatory and customer-driven opportunities.

Specialty chemical and biofuel producer FutureFuel (NYSE:FF) posted strong sales gains in both its Chemicals and Biofuels businesses, but continued to report losses due to weather events and derivative-related charges. The company, which operates a large integrated manufacturing site in Batesville, Arkansas, released first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026. Overall, the quarter demonstrated notable growth in business activity but also highlighted persistent cost and earnings pressures.

Notable Developments
Total revenue rose 82%, mainly from strong chemicals segment growth and higher sales volumes in both business lines.
Adjusted EBITDA loss improved by $2.3 million compared to the prior year but remained negative at $(13.8) million, largely impacted by a $9.1 million derivative loss and weather disruptions.
The company slashed its quarterly dividend from $0.06 to $0.01 per share to prioritize growth investments, with $25 million in authorized share repurchases remaining unused.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its expectation to deliver positive Adjusted EBITDA for the full year (excluding the impact of changes in commodity derivatives). The outlook is anchored by recent regulatory support for biofuels and a pipeline of customer-funded capacity expansion in chemicals. Investors should watch for improved profitability and stabilization of cash flows as new programs and regulatory changes take effect.

Quarterly Highlights

The Chemicals segment more than doubled revenue, rising 109.6% from the previous year, supported by increased sales to energy customers and the launch of new customer programs. Plant utilization at the company’s main site improved from 33% to 54%, demonstrating better use of capacity after customer wins and operational enhancements.

Biofuels revenue increased 50.7%, but segment profitability remained pressured by derivative losses tied to market volatility and regulatory credits. Weather-related operational disruptions also weighed on results, affecting both output and repair costs.

Cash flow from operations was negative $20 million as operating losses, working capital investments, and higher capital expenditures reduced liquidity. The company’s cash balance dropped sharply to $22.4 million, while no amounts were drawn on its credit facility as of the end of the quarter.

What FutureFuel Does

FutureFuel manufactures specialty chemicals and biofuels, serving industrial, agricultural, and energy end-markets from its integrated U.S. facility.
