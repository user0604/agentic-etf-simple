Biotech developer Phio Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ:PHIO) reported steady clinical progress but saw costs and losses rise steeply in the first quarter. The company focuses on gene-silencing therapies for cancer, with its lead candidate, PH-762, completing an important trial phase. The results, announced on May 7, 2026 for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, reflect the ongoing risks and costs typical of drug development stages. Overall, spending surged as clinical programs advanced, but leadership maintains operations are funded well into 2027.

Notable Developments
Phio completed the clinical phase of its PH-762 Phase 1b trial, enrolling 22 patients; final data analysis is underway.
Research and development expenses jumped to $2.8 million, more than tripling from the prior year, due to increased activity in clinical and manufacturing operations.
The company ended the quarter with $17.0 million in cash, down from $21.0 million at year-end, and established a new $6.36 million equity facility in April 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership reaffirmed plans to submit PH-762 trial results to the FDA in the second quarter, seeking guidance for future studies. Management expects research costs to continue climbing as programs advance, but says the recent capital raise supports operations into the first half of 2027. The most important near-term milestone is the FDA submission and feedback regarding PH-762’s next steps.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s main achievement in the quarter was completing the clinical stage of the PH-762 trial, a gene-silencing therapy aimed at cancers such as cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma. Final results of this trial, which enrolled 22 patients, are now under review and will guide upcoming regulatory steps.

Operating expenses more than doubled from the year-ago period, with research and development costs driven higher by trial operations, manufacturing, and toxicology work needed to advance PH-762. General and administrative expenses also rose, mainly due to increased personnel and professional service costs.

Phio ended the quarter with $17.0 million in cash and cash equivalents after using $4.0 million over the three-month period. It bolstered liquidity with a new at-the-market equity facility and continues to expect its cash runway to extend through the first half of 2027, despite rising expenditures.
