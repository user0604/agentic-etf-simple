Notable Developments

Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed guidance that Legend Biotech is on track for company-wide adjusted profitability by the end of 2026. Investors should monitor gross margin performance as well as the pace of commercialization and pipeline progress, as the company’s ability to control operating and manufacturing costs will shape long-term results.

Quarterly highlights

CARVYKTI, a chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy for advanced multiple myeloma, drove outstanding growth. Net sales of this therapy climbed 62% year over year, propelled by a broader launch footprint and improved manufacturing metrics, with a reported 99% manufacturing success rate.

Operating performance improved, with net loss cut nearly in half compared to the same period last year. This was supported by reduced research and development (R&D) expense, as major late-stage trial costs fell, and by healthy top-line expansion in core products and partnerships.

While revenue strengthened and costs of collaboration revenue increased 152%—primarily due to capacity and expansion investment—cash flow was not positive for the quarter. Selling and administrative costs also rose as the company scaled globally and invested in additional supporting infrastructure.
