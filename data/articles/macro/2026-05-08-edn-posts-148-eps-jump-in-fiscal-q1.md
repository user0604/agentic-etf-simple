Profitability rebounded sharply as tariff changes and cost cuts drove earnings into much higher territory for Empresa Distribuidora Y Comercializadora Norte Sociedad Anónima (NYSE:EDN). The company is the largest electric power distributor in Argentina, serving millions of residential and business customers in Buenos Aires and nearby areas. Reporting results for the first quarter released on May 8, 2026, it recorded transformative gains in several critical financial measures. Overall, the quarter reflected operational momentum but preserved caution on future macroeconomic and regulatory headwinds.

Notable Developments
Tariff normalization, including automatic monthly adjustments linked to inflation, underpinned stable revenues at ARS 846.71 billion for the quarter.
Operating expenses fell 9%, particularly from declines in staff and pension costs, boosting EBITDA by 127% year over year.
Operational quality reached record levels, with service outage metrics (SAIDI and SAIFI) showing the best performance since 2017.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management pointed to a positive long-term outlook anchored by predictable tariff adjustments and continued investment in infrastructure. However, future results will depend on managing cash flow, controlling energy loss rates, and adapting to Argentina’s economic changes. Investors should pay close attention to how regulatory updates and the proposal for recovering past-due revenues (“regulatory asset”) develop in the coming months.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue was nearly unchanged year over year, as the new regulatory formula allowed the tariff to track inflation, offsetting lower electricity demand driven by economic and seasonal factors. Industrial demand softened, but the company still served 3.4 million customers, a 1.4% increase from the prior year.

The company pushed margins higher by aggressively cutting expenses, notably reducing salaries, pension outlays, and service costs. These actions helped drive net income and EBITDA to more than double year over year, delivering the strongest result in several years.

Operational improvements were significant. Outages, as measured by SAIDI (average duration) and SAIFI (average frequency), reached the company’s best levels in at least eight years. The firm executed over 56,000 energy loss inspections in the quarter, raising their detection efficiency and focusing investments on network expansion and modernization.

What Empresa Distribuidora Y Comercializadora Norte Sociedad Anónima Does

It distributes electricity to homes and businesses throughout the northern region of Buenos Aires, supplying more than 3 million customers through a regulated network.
