RCI Hospitality (NASDAQ:RICK) posted improved results in key operating areas, but reported a net loss for the quarter due to non-cash impairment charges. The company, a leading operator of adult nightclubs and Bombshells-themed restaurants, released its fiscal second quarter results on May 28, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the period showed resilient core earnings alongside ongoing cost and segment challenges.

Notable developments
Non-GAAP earnings and adjusted EBITDA improved, with non-GAAP EPS up 20.0% and adjusted EBITDA up 9.9% year over year.
Nightclubs, the largest segment, grew revenues by 4.8%, but segment operating margin narrowed due to higher impairment charges.
Bombshells segment revenue rose slightly, but operating losses widened and only the Houston location posted stronger sales, showing limited progress with the new beverage strategy.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its focus on a five-year capital allocation plan, aiming to grow free cash flow for buybacks, debt reduction, and acquisitions. No specific financial guidance was issued for upcoming quarters, but the company continues to emphasize operational improvements and cost discipline. Investors should monitor the impact of impairment charges and whether Bombshells can improve profitability across more locations.

Quarterly highlights

Non-GAAP profitability and free cash flow increased, supporting the company’s plans for share buybacks and debt repayment. RCI Hospitality reduced its average share count by 12.6% year over year through buybacks, paid $617,000 in dividends, and lowered total debt slightly from the end of 2025.

The Nightclubs segment delivered higher sales, with new or reformatted clubs contributing $4.8 million. Growth came mainly from higher service revenue, but was offset by a small decline in alcoholic beverage sales and increased operating expenses, resulting in a lower GAAP segment margin compared to the prior year. Non-GAAP operating income improved, reflecting underlying margin gains before one-time charges.

Bombshells revenue increased from the previous year, mostly from new locations. Same-store sales fell for the wider segment, but the Houston location saw a 3.6% same-store improvement. Despite this, the Bombshells segment remained unprofitable as segment-wide losses widened compared to the prior year.

What RCI Hospitality does

RCI Hospitality operates adult entertainment nightclubs and Bombshells-themed restaurant and bar venues, generating revenue from beverages, food, and service activities across the U.S.
