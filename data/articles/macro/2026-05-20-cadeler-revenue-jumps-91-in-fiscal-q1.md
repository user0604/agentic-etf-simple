Key Takeaways
Cadeler A/s saw robust revenue growth but posted a net loss as profitability fell.
Diluted earnings per share turned negative, while revenue nearly doubled year over year.
Management kept its full-year revenue and EBITDA guidance unchanged, citing a strong order backlog.

Cadeler A/s (NYSE:CDLR) delivered strong growth in revenue as its expanded fleet contributed more contracted days, but higher financing expenses and softer vessel utilization led to a net quarterly loss. The offshore wind installation specialist, based in Denmark, nearly doubled revenue as its fleet of wind turbine installation vessels grew, but operating efficiency came under pressure. The company reported earnings for the first quarter of 2026 on May 20, 2026, covering the results through March 31. Overall, the quarter reflected robust contract activity but mixed operational execution as fleet expansion outpaced improvements in profitability.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 revenue and EBITDA targets, expecting revenue between EUR 854 million and EUR 944 million, and EBITDA between EUR 420 million and EUR 510 million. They pointed to a nearly full order book for 2026 as a strength. The top item for investors to monitor is the recovery in fleet utilization, as improved capacity use will be needed to lift profitability in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The company nearly doubled revenue, reflecting the impact of adding three more offshore wind installation vessels to its operating fleet. The number of contracted days increased from 284 to 428 as fleet size grew from seven to ten vessels, but utilization fell to 47.6% as not all vessels operated at capacity.

Profitability was affected by rising financial expenses, as Cadeler expensed a larger portion of interest costs with vessel deliveries finalized. This helped lift EBITDA but turned the bottom line negative, with a net loss of EUR 7.0 million compared to a small profit in the prior year quarter.

The firm raised EUR 175 million in new capital, boosting cash and strengthening the balance sheet. Already, 2026 order backlog has reached EUR 2.7 billion, building confidence in sustained demand but also underscoring the need to convert backlog into efficient, profitable fleet activity.

What Cadeler A/s Does

Cadeler A/s specializes in providing marine and engineering services for the installation, maintenance, and decommissioning of offshore wind turbines, mainly using its fleet of specialized vessels.
