KRYS Company Overview
Krystal develops and sells genetic medicines for rare diseases using a modified virus to deliver therapeutic genes directly to a patient's cells. The company generates revenue by commercializing topical therapies that can be administered in a home or clinical setting, addressing the underlying causes of genetic skin conditions. By controlling its own manufacturing and utilizing a proprietary delivery platform, the firm maintains a specialized position within the biotechnology industry.

Krystal Biotech Expands Reach With Gene Therapy Growth

Krystal Biotech makes gene therapies to help people with rare and serious diseases. The company is interesting because its main product, VYJUVEK, is the first approved gene therapy that can be used more than once for a severe skin condition. Over the last five years, the stock beat the S&P 500 by 226.6 percentage points, returning 299.3% versus the index's 72.7%.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $116.4 million (up 32.0% YoY)
EPS (Diluted): $1.83 (up 52.5% YoY)
U.S. VYJUVEK prescribers: 570+ since launch, with 60+ new prescribers added this quarter

Latest Developments

VYJUVEK reached more patients in the U.S. and abroad, showing the product’s growing demand and helping the company expand its business globally.
The company got new FDA designations for its gene therapy platform, making future programs less risky and speeding up the development of other treatments in the pipeline.
