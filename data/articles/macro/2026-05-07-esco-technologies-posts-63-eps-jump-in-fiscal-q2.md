ESCO Technologies Posts 63% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q2

Key Takeaways
ESCO Technologies posted another strong quarter, fueled by broad sales and margin gains.
Earnings per share from continuing operations on an adjusted basis rose 63.2% year over year.
Management raised its full-year profit outlook, but kept revenue guidance unchanged.

ESCO Technologies (NYSE:ESE) delivered another quarter of healthy growth across all major business segments. The diversified provider of engineered products and solutions for aerospace, utilities, and industrial sectors reported its fiscal second quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, the quarter illustrated robust execution in core areas but flagged pockets of weakness in renewables.

Notable developments
Order volume rose 42% to $378 million, sending total backlog to a record $1.5 billion.
Sales in the Aerospace & Defense segment grew 68%, boosted by the recent Maritime acquisition and robust government orders.
Utility Solutions Group profit increased as strong Doble sales offset continued weakness in NRG’s renewable energy business.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management increased its full-year profit forecast, with adjusted earnings per share guidance up to $8.00–$8.25, but kept revenue guidance unchanged at $1.29–$1.33 billion. This signals confidence in profit margin improvements driven by efficiency and scale. Investors should watch the pending Megger acquisition and the recovery progress in NRG’s renewable segment as key factors going forward.

Quarterly highlights

The Aerospace & Defense segment delivered strong results with sales rising over two-thirds year over year. The acquisition of Maritime contributed both to sales and sizable new orders, including a $53 million inflow from its naval products line.

The Utility Solutions Group increased profit even as total segment sales growth was modest, with Doble’s diagnostic solutions for electric utilities outperforming but NRG’s wind and solar product sales declining 36%. Despite that area of weakness, overall orders at Doble increased 20%.

RF Test & Measurement posted double-digit sales and profit growth, helped by demand for electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) test systems and industrial shielding for government data center projects. The segment’s order backlog rose as well, aided by growth in both the U.S. and Europe, Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) regions.

What ESCO Technologies does

ESCO Technologies designs, manufactures, and sells engineered products for aerospace, defense, utility, and industrial customers worldwide.
