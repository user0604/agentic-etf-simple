Entera Bio Posts 105% R&D Surge in Fiscal Q1

May 8, 2026, 4:17 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business win: Entera Bio advanced its lead osteoporosis drug candidate but remains pre-commercial with zero revenue.
Performance: Net loss widened as R&D expenses more than doubled year over year.
Outlook: The company awaits critical FDA feedback and expects its cash position to fund operations through early 2027.

Entera Bio (NASDAQ:ENTX) advanced its core drug pipeline in the first quarter, but the absence of revenue underlined its ongoing pre-commercial status. The clinical-stage company focuses on developing oral peptide therapies for osteoporosis and rare endocrine disorders. Reporting results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 (released May 8, 2026), Entera highlighted progress in regulatory submissions but posted wider losses due to higher research spending. Overall, the period showed operational progress, but clinical and regulatory milestones still drive the company’s outlook.

Notable developments
The company submitted its streamlined Phase 3 trial protocol for EB613, its lead osteoporosis drug candidate, to the Food and Drug Administration in March 2026 and awaits feedback.
Research and development spending more than doubled, driven by higher activity on EB613 and expanded collaboration programs with OPKO.
Entera completed a fundraising round in April, bringing its post-quarter cash balance to $20.4 million, with $7.8 million restricted for OPKO programs.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects its current cash to support operations into the first quarter of 2027 and remains focused on advancing EB613 to pivotal trial initiation. Investors should watch for the Food and Drug Administration’s feedback on the Phase 3 trial, as this will determine key development timelines and funding needs for the lead asset.

Quarterly highlights

During the period, Entera achieved a critical milestone by submitting all necessary documents for the Phase 3 osteoporosis trial of EB613, including a statistical plan and trial extension, and completed a Phase 1 study to support the single-tablet formulation. However, without Food and Drug Administration response yet, the timeline to start the trial remains uncertain but is expected to clarify soon. The company also expanded its partnership with OPKO for its hypoparathyroidism therapy, EB612, and confirmed preparations are on track for an investigational new drug (IND) application planned for late 2026.

R&D expenses surged to $2.3 million, more than double the prior year, reflecting the advancement of both EB613 and EB612 as well as preclinical work on a metabolic disease candidate. Despite this spending increase, general and administrative expenses stayed steady at $1.3 million as the company focused resources on clinical and regulatory work for near-term milestones.

The balance sheet showed a significant boost in liquidity following an April capital raise. At May 8, cash and equivalents totaled $20.4 million of which $7.8 million is committed to OPKO-partnered programs. Management stated that this cash runway extends beyond the expected start of EB613’s pivotal trial but further progress depends on regulatory clearance and trial results.

What Entera Bio does

Entera Bio develops oral tablet therapies for conditions that currently require injections, including osteoporosis and rare hormone disorders.
