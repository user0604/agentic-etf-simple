KNSL Company Overview
Kinsale provides property and casualty insurance specifically for high-risk or unusual accounts that traditional insurers typically decline. The company generates revenue by underwriting specialized coverage for small to medium-sized businesses through a network of independent brokers. By utilizing a proprietary technology platform to rapidly process quotes and manage claims in-house, it maintains a cost-effective operational model that attracts brokers seeking quick decisions on complex, hard-to-place risks.

Kinsale Grows Profit With Strong Risk Selection

Kinsale Capital Group provides insurance for hard-to-insure risks that regular insurers avoid. The company stands out by using strict risk selection and technology to handle unusual or tough insurance cases. Over the past five years, the stock returned 97.9% versus the S&P 500’s 69.6%, outperforming by 28.3 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

EPS (Non-GAAP): $5.11 (up 37.7% YoY)
Net Earned Premiums: $406.9 million (up 11.2% YoY)
Combined Ratio: 77.4% (improved by 4.7 percentage points YoY, showing better underwriting profitability)

Latest Developments

Underwriting profits hit a new high because of careful risk selection and fewer losses from natural disasters, showing the company’s focus on quality over quick growth.
The commercial property insurance division saw a big drop in sales due to more competition, which could affect future premium growth.
