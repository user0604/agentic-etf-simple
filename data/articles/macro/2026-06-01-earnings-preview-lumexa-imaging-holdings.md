Earnings Preview: Lumexa Imaging Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Lumexa Imaging Holdings (NASDAQ:LMRI), a provider of outpatient diagnostic imaging centers in the United States, is expected to report earnings for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of 2026, Lumexa Imaging Holdings reported consolidated revenues of $253 million, representing a 3.1% increase year over year. System-wide revenue growth, which includes all centers operated by the company, reached 4.0%. Adjusted EBITDA was $51.2 million, flat compared to the prior year, and adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.3%. The company posted net income of $1.7 million, reversing a net loss from the prior year, and reported adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $0.18. These results were in line with analyst estimates. The quarter was shaped by typical seasonality and significant weather-related disruptions, which management estimated had a $4 million impact on EBITDA. Despite these headwinds, advanced imaging modalities, especially positron emission tomography (PET) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), showed strong growth, helping to offset slower recovery in routine scans.

Positives
Strong growth in advanced imaging modalities: PET scan volume grew 23.1% year over year and MRI increased 8.2%, supporting higher revenue per scan and improved mix.
Expansion of center footprint and partnerships: The company completed two acquisitions and opened two new de novo centers, including its first joint venture (JV) site with UPMC in Pennsylvania and a new site with Advocate Health in North Carolina. These moves enhance Lumexa’s presence in key markets.
Operational efficiency initiatives on track: The rollout of FastScan MRI software continued, with the company on pace to reach two-thirds of its MRI fleet by the end of 2026. Early data shows centers with FastScan are achieving higher advanced imaging volumes.
Improved cash flow and reduced interest expense: Operating cash flow improved to $2.9 million from a negative $14 million in the prior year, largely due to lower interest payments after refinancing debt in late 2025.
Negatives
Flat adjusted EBITDA despite revenue growth: Adjusted EBITDA was unchanged year over year, as higher costs and weather-related disruptions offset revenue gains.
Routine scan volumes slow to rebound: Routine imaging, such as mammography, was slower to recover after winter storms, which limited the full benefit from increased patient volumes.
Increased general and administrative expenses: G&A costs rose 16.3% year over year, driven by higher public company expenses and stock-based compensation following the company’s IPO.
Minimal immediate contribution from recent acquisitions: Newly acquired centers require integration and network credentialing, so their financial impact in 2026 is expected to be limited.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Advanced modality growth and mix: Monitor continued expansion in PET and MRI volumes, as these drive higher revenue per scan and margin improvement. Sustained growth here is key to meeting full-year targets.
Ramp and integration of new centers and JVs: Track progress on the ramp-up of recently opened de novo centers and the integration of new JV sites, especially with UPMC and Advocate Health. Updates on the number of de novo openings toward the annual goal of 8 to 10 will be important.
Execution on technology initiatives: Watch for updates on the adoption rate of FastScan MRI software and other AI-driven efficiency tools, including their impact on throughput and cost structure.
Margin trends and cost management: Assess whether adjusted EBITDA margin improves sequentially as volumes recover and fixed costs are leveraged. Look for commentary on controlling G&A expense growth as public company costs normalize.
Status of cybersecurity remediation and impact: The company disclosed a vendor-related data breach in April 2026. Investors should look for updates on remediation actions, regulatory compliance, and confirmation that the incident does not materially affect financial results, as previously stated.
