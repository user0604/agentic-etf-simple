Earnings Preview: Copart

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

Copart (NASDAQ:CPRT), a leading provider of online vehicle auctions for insurance companies and other sellers, is expected to report earnings for the third quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 21, 2026, after the market closes.

Q2 FY2026 Recap

In the second quarter of fiscal year 2026, Copart reported consolidated revenue of $1.12 billion, a decrease of 3.6% year over year. Excluding last year’s impact from major hurricanes, revenue increased slightly by 1.3%. Global gross profit fell 6.2% to $492.8 million. Net income was $350.7 million, down 9.5% from the prior year, and diluted earnings per share (EPS) declined 9.2% to $0.36. These results missed analyst estimates for both revenue and EPS. The company continued to see strong average selling prices (ASPs) for vehicles, especially in its U.S. insurance segment, but overall unit volumes declined. Copart ended the quarter with a robust liquidity position, holding $5.1 billion in cash and no debt.

Positives
Record average selling prices for U.S. insurance vehicles: ASPs increased 6% year over year, or 9% excluding the impact of last year’s catastrophic events, outpacing industry trends and supporting revenue per vehicle.
Strong international growth in noninsurance units: International noninsurance units increased 9.1%, with notable growth in the U.K. and Canada, helping to offset some domestic volume weakness.
Continued investment in technology and artificial intelligence: Copart has expanded its use of AI across business analytics, document processing, and operational workflows, which has improved productivity and cycle times.
Robust liquidity and share repurchases: The company ended the quarter with $6.4 billion in liquidity and repurchased over 13 million shares fiscal year-to-date, signaling confidence in its long-term outlook.
Negatives
Declining unit volumes in core insurance business: Global insurance units fell 9% year over year (4% excluding catastrophic events), reflecting lower claims activity and consumer pullback in auto insurance coverage.
Lower overall revenue and profit: Both revenue and net income declined year over year, missing analyst expectations, due to fewer vehicles processed and lower service revenues in the U.S.
Increased international operating costs: Facility operations expenses rose 16.6% internationally, driven by higher costs to process vehicles and new facility investments.
One-time international tax expense: The quarter included a $6.8 million one-time value-added tax accrual, which weighed on gross profit.
What to Watch in Q3 FY2026's Report
Trends in insurance unit volumes and assignment growth: Investors should monitor whether insurance claims activity and policyholder behavior begin to recover, which could drive a rebound in vehicle volumes processed by Copart.
Average selling price (ASP) momentum: The sustainability of higher ASPs, particularly in the U.S. insurance segment, will be key for revenue growth, especially if unit volumes remain under pressure.
Progress on technology and AI-driven initiatives: Updates on how Copart’s expanded use of artificial intelligence is impacting operational efficiency, cycle times, and customer outcomes will be important for long-term competitiveness.
International segment performance and cost control: Watch for continued growth in international noninsurance units and any signs that facility and processing costs are stabilizing after recent increases.
Capital allocation and share repurchase activity: Look for disclosures on additional share buybacks or other uses of Copart’s large cash balance, as well as any updates on potential acquisitions or facility expansions.
