Priority Technology (NASDAQ:PRTH) delivered strong gains in its key businesses this quarter, with each segment contributing to higher revenue and profits. The payments and financial technology provider serves merchants, enterprises, and partners with solutions for payment processing, payables automation, and treasury management. The results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, released on May 11, 2026, highlighted improvements in profitability and operating margins alongside revenue growth. Overall, the quarter showed positive momentum while management kept its outlook unchanged.

Notable developments

Management outlook and what to watch

Management affirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, expecting revenue between $1.01 billion and $1.04 billion. The company pointed to momentum across its business lines and continued focus on integration and cost control. Investors should watch card processing volumes and the impact of embedded finance adoption in relation to future growth rates.

Quarterly highlights

Growth was broad-based across the company's core Merchant Solutions, Payables, and Treasury Solutions areas. Merchant Solutions revenue grew 6.6%, supported by $18.13 billion in processed card volume and rising transaction counts. Payables, which provides automated payment and workflow tools, delivered 35.6% revenue growth and a 55.1% jump in segment EBITDA, fueled by expansion in buyer-funded card programs and automated clearing house (ACH) transaction activity.

Treasury Solutions generated $58.8 million in revenue, up 17.5%, aided by a sharp increase in average account balances managed on behalf of clients, which climbed 36.3% to $1.42 billion. However, average monthly enrollments for CFTPay, its automated payment enrollment product, declined 9.9%, a trend noted for monitoring as it could affect future revenue streams.

The company’s continued operating leverage and profit growth supported margin expansion. This efficiency, combined with increased cash generation, allowed closing cash and equivalents to reach $92.2 million, giving Priority flexibility for investments and deleveraging.
