Barnwell Industries Narrows Losses in Fiscal Q2

Key Takeaways
Barnwell Industries narrowed its net loss as it cut expenses but continued to see revenue fall.
Earnings per share improved to a loss of $0.09 from a year-ago loss of $0.12.
Management expects more cost savings ahead but strategic uncertainty remains around pending asset sales.

Barnwell Industries (NYSEMKT:BRN) trimmed its losses this quarter by controlling costs, though overall sales decreased as the company continued to reduce its asset base. The diversified energy and land investment firm is working through a period of transition, focusing on cost-cutting initiatives and evaluating key divestitures. On May 26, 2026, Barnwell released results for its fiscal second quarter, ending March 31, showing it made progress on operational efficiency but still faces ongoing revenue pressure as it adjusts its business profile. Overall, the quarter displayed steady improvement in expenses and liquidity, even as major strategic questions loom.

Notable Developments
Cash general and administrative expenses (a non-GAAP measure) fell to $1.39 million, down 8% from the prior quarter as Barnwell completed its headquarters move to Houston.
Revenue dropped nearly 29% year over year, mainly due to asset sales and smaller oil and gas operations.
Cash increased to $4.02 million, supported by new equity raised through its “at-the-market” share issuance program.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted its focus on a leaner cost structure and ongoing efforts to maximize value from remaining assets. No specific financial guidance was provided, but the company expects future cost savings from recent operational changes. Investors should watch for updates on the potential sale of the Canadian oil and gas business and Hawaiian real estate, which could reshape Barnwell’s operations.

Quarterly Highlights

Administrative and general costs dropped as Barnwell consolidated operations in Texas, marking progress on plans to streamline the business. Reductions in salaries, wages, and bonuses led to tighter spending even as the company’s asset base shrank.

Sales fell as Barnwell reported significantly lower revenue, following the sale of several oil and gas interests over the past year.

The balance sheet strengthened with an increase in cash, helped by over $1.1 million from share sales under the at-the-market program. However, higher outstanding share count also means increased dilution for current shareholders and is an important factor for those tracking future earnings per share.

What Barnwell Industries Does

Barnwell Industries operates in oil and natural gas production as well as land investment, with its main assets in Canada and real estate holdings in Hawaii.
