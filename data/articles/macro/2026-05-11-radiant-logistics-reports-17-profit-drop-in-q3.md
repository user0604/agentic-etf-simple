Radiant Logistics Reports 17% Profit Drop in Q3

Key Takeaways
Adjusted earnings declined as core freight profitability remained under pressure.
Revenue held steady at $214.1 million compared to the prior-year period.
Management expects margin improvement as domestic freight markets recover, though international headwinds persist.

Adjusted profit fell as Radiant Logistics (NYSEMKT:RLGT) continued to face pressure on core freight margins. The non-asset-based logistics provider navigated a difficult global trade environment, with ongoing tariff volatility and shipping disruptions. In its results for the fiscal third quarter ended March 31, 2026 (released May 11, 2026), the company reported flat revenue and lower adjusted profitability. Overall, the period showed resilience in revenue but highlighted growing pressure on operational earnings.

Notable developments
Revenue was essentially flat at $214.1 million for the period, nearly unchanged from the prior year despite ongoing volatility in both domestic and international freight markets.
Adjusted EBITDA, a measure of core operating earnings, fell 17% year over year to $7.8 million as adjusted gross profit and adjusted net income also declined from 2025 levels.
Cash and equivalents grew to $39.7 million, while the company maintained a strong balance sheet and repurchased 585,050 shares during the first nine months of the fiscal year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management is cautiously optimistic that tightening in the domestic freight market will start to improve earnings and margins in future periods, though international disruptions remain a risk. The outlook focuses on technology deployment, improving domestic operations, and selective acquisitions. Investors should monitor whether expected margin improvements and technology benefits begin to appear in the company’s adjusted financial results over the coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Adjusted (non-GAAP) profitability measures fell, with adjusted EBITDA dropping to $7.8 million and the adjusted EBITDA margin on adjusted gross profit decreasing by 2.4 percentage points. Management pointed to international shipping volatility and tariff headwinds as key factors driving softer freight volumes and higher operating costs in key markets during the quarter.

The company reiterated its commitment to technology, highlighting progress in its Navegate global trade management platform and its new AI-powered agent, Ray. However, the benefits of these initiatives have not yet materially improved reported financial results, as margin pressure has continued and adjusted metrics declined year over year.

Radiant Logistics’ balance sheet remains a source of flexibility, with $39.7 million in cash and minimal debt. The company continued its share buyback program but did not announce any new acquisitions. Management emphasized readiness to pursue expansion opportunities once market conditions become more favorable.

What Radiant Logistics does

Radiant Logistics is a third-party logistics company that arranges transportation and logistics services through a network of independent agents and technology platforms, rather than owning transportation assets.
