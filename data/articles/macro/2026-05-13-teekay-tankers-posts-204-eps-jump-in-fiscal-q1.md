Notable Developments
Spot tanker rates increased sharply, with Suezmax vessels averaging $62,124 per day and Aframax/LR2 vessels at $59,934 per day during the period.
Fleet renewal moved forward as the company added three newer Aframax tankers for $141.5 million and sold two older Suezmax ships, generating $73.0 million in proceeds and $22.7 million in gains.
The company declared a combined $1.25 per share in dividends for the quarter, including a $1.00 per share special dividend, reflecting strong cash generation and capital return policy.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not issue formal guidance for the rest of fiscal 2026 due to highly unpredictable market conditions tied to ongoing geopolitical disruptions. Leadership emphasized continued focus on modernizing the fleet, maintaining flexibility, and returning cash to shareholders. Investors should closely monitor spot tanker rates and the potential resolution of Middle East conflicts, which are driving current market volatility.

Quarterly Highlights

Spot rates for major vessel categories reached near-record highs, due chiefly to shipping disruptions following the closure of the Strait of Hormuz and global oil trade realignment. These elevated rates propelled first quarter revenue and profit sharply higher, with revenue rising by double digits over the prior year period and operating gains nearly doubling compared to Q1 2025.

The company executed its fleet strategy by purchasing three mid-aged Aframax tankers and selling two older Suezmax vessels, further reducing the fleet’s average age and generating sizable gains on sales. As of May, its total fleet stood at 38 tankers, with 24 of those unencumbered by financing arrangements.

Liquidity remained strong, as cash, short-term investments, and available credit rose to $1.2 billion at quarter end, increasing financial flexibility. The quarterly dividend was lifted sharply, including a $1.00 per share special dividend, as the board sought to return surplus capital to shareholders.

What Teekay Tankers Does

Teekay Tankers is a global provider of marine transportation services, specializing in the shipping of crude oil and refined petroleum products using its fleet of mid-sized tankers.
