Notable developments
Revenue climbed to $792.4 million, driven by regulated electricity rate hikes in California, with additional natural gas rate hikes in Massachusetts effective after the quarter.
Net earnings attributable to shareholders fell approximately 11.3% as higher operating expenses and $19.2 million in restructuring costs offset revenue gains.
The company kept its quarterly dividend at $0.065 per share, maintaining the annualized rate at $0.26.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its previous guidance for adjusted earnings per share for fiscal 2026 and 2027, citing expected benefits from recently approved rate cases. It emphasized the need for ongoing cost and efficiency improvements to turn regulatory wins into higher profits. Investors should watch whether expense controls can narrow the gap between authorized and earned returns in coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue growth came mainly from regulated utilities, with regulated electricity distribution up 12.7% and regulated natural gas up 22.4% year over year. These gains reflect the impact of newly approved utility rates, especially a $48.6 million base revenue increase from California operations that was applied retroactively.

Profitability lagged revenue expansion, as operating expenses before depreciation rose about 20%, notably due to higher purchased natural gas and one-time restructuring charges. Operating income saw only a modest increase despite double-digit revenue growth, underlining continued pressure from rising costs.

Capital expenditures dropped sharply to $129 million in the three months ended March 31, 2026, aligning with management's focus on financial discipline and a more selective investment approach.

What Algonquin Power & Utilities does

Algonquin Power & Utilities is a regulated utility providing electricity, natural gas, and water services across the United States, Canada, Chile, and Bermuda, with a focus on regulated operations after divesting most of its renewable energy portfolio.
