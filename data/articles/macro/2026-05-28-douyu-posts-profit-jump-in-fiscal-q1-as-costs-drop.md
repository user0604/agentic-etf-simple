DouYu International (NASDAQ:DOYU) posted a return to profitability as cost reductions outpaced falling revenues. The leading interactive entertainment and game live streaming platform in China continued to face declines in both users and revenue during the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (results released May 28, 2026). Overall, the period showed improved profitability, but ongoing declines in user engagement raise questions about future growth.

Notable developments
Net income turned positive at RMB27.4 million, reversing a loss in the prior-year quarter as overall costs declined faster than revenue.
Revenue dropped 13.2% year over year, mainly due to fewer paying users on the live streaming platform and reduced in-app promotions.
Gross margin improved by 3.7 percentage points to 15.7%, driven by lower revenue-sharing fees and a sharp drop in marketing and content costs.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management is focusing on investing in platform content and user experience, aiming for long-term value rather than immediate growth. No explicit forecasts were provided, with attention on new content events and eSports tournaments. The key issue for investors is whether the company can stabilize or revive engagement levels, as continued declines could threaten future profitability.

Quarterly highlights

The return to net profit was driven by sharp reductions in costs, especially in sales and marketing and revenue-sharing with content creators. Cost of revenues fell 16.9% from the prior year, outpacing the revenue decline.

Revenue continued to fall in the core live streaming segment, with fewer monthly active users and paying users. Live streaming revenue dropped by 18.5% year over year, and overall platform user activity also weakened.

The company ended the period with a strong cash position. Management plans to invest this capital in content expansion and offline events to reinvigorate engagement.

What DouYu International does

DouYu International operates an interactive entertainment and game live streaming platform in China, connecting users, streamers, and eSports through video and live event content.
