Notable Developments
Rocket Lab signed 31 new Electron and HASTE launch contracts plus five Neutron launches during the period, accelerating its future manifest past prior annual totals.
Gross margin expanded about eight percentage points year over year, supported by higher launch rates and cost efficiencies from vertical integration.
Liquidity reached over $1.2 billion in cash and equivalents, bolstered by a major capital raise that supports further growth and acquisitions.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue of $225 million to $240 million in the next quarter, but forecasts lower gross margin percentages and a wider adjusted EBITDA loss due to a greater mix of lower-margin space systems business and higher investment in Neutron development. For investors, the most important area to monitor is the pace and cost of Neutron progress, which remains key to future profitability and margin improvement.

Quarterly Highlights

Rocket Lab’s launch division set new records for signed launches, with 31 Electron and HASTE contracts and five Neutron bookings secured in just one quarter. This lifted the company’s backlog to $2.2 billion, giving Rocket Lab greater revenue visibility than ever before and highlighting robust demand from defense and commercial customers alike.

Profitability metrics improved across the board, as gross margins rose sharply and adjusted EBITDA losses narrowed from prior-year levels. Rocket Lab closed the Mynaric acquisition and signed a definitive agreement to acquire Motiv, with both deals intended to enhance vertical integration and address supply chain constraints.

Cash and balance sheet strength improved further, driven by over $1.2 billion in cash and capital market actions that cut convertible debt by more than 75%. While ongoing losses and high research spending continue, Rocket Lab’s financial flexibility supports ongoing R&D and potential future acquisitions.

What Rocket Lab Does

Rocket Lab designs and manufactures rockets, spacecraft, and satellite systems, providing launch services and space technology for both government and commercial organizations.
