Protara Posts 50% Wider Loss in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 8:18 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Protara Therapeutics reported increased research and development spending as it advanced its clinical pipeline.
Earnings per share decreased to $(0.31), while net loss rose nearly 50% year over year.
Key clinical and regulatory milestones remain several quarters away, with updated trial data expected later in 2026.

Protara Therapeutics (NASDAQ:TARA) posted another quarter of deepening losses as it continued investing in multiple late-stage clinical programs. The clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company is focusing on developing therapies for rare and specialty diseases. In its earnings release on May 13, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, the company highlighted ongoing clinical trial progress but reported rising costs and no new revenue sources. Overall, the period showed sustained operational momentum but no major breakthroughs or de-risking events.

Notable Developments
Research and development expense rose to $13.6 million, driven by direct clinical trial costs and new program start-up expenses.
Net loss increased to $17.8 million, up almost 50% from the prior year, due to higher investment in clinical operations.
Cash and investments totaled $177.4 million at quarter’s end, sufficient to fund planned operations into 2028 according to management.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed that its current cash position supports operations through 2028, even as it advances multiple pivotal clinical trials over the next two years. The most important development to watch is the release of new interim and landmark safety and efficacy data for its lead programs, expected at major medical conferences in the second half of 2026.

Quarterly highlights

The company maintained steady progress across its three core trials: STARBORN-1 for lymphatic malformations, ADVANCED-2 and ADVANCED-3 for non-muscle invasive bladder cancer, and THRIVE-3 for IV Choline Chloride in patients needing parenteral nutritional support. While all programs stayed on timeline, no updated efficacy or safety results were reported this quarter. Management announced that future trial data presentations are scheduled for May and later in the year, potentially impacting the outlook for regulatory submissions and approvals.

Operating expenses grew sharply due to direct and indirect research costs, particularly for ongoing and upcoming pivotal studies. General and administrative expenses also increased, largely from higher personnel and stock-based compensation outlays. With no products on the market, all activities are funded through the company’s cash reserves, which decreased roughly $20 million from the prior quarter.

No product revenue was generated as all drug candidates remain investigational and under regulatory review. Protara continues to pursue key FDA designations, including Breakthrough Therapy and Orphan Drug status, for its pipeline assets while managing the sizable risks and capital needs associated with developing first-in-class treatments in rare disease and oncology settings.

What Protara Therapeutics does

The company develops investigational therapies for rare and specialty diseases, focusing on advancing late-stage clinical programs in oncology and pediatric conditions.
