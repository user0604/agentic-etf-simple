Nexxen Posts 14% Programmatic Jump in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Nexxen International delivered record programmatic revenue growth but saw margin decline and a net loss.
Revenue rose year over year.
Management raised key revenue guidance but kept full-year profit expectations unchanged, signaling focus on margin recovery in later quarters.

Nexxen International (NASDAQ:NEXN) posted strong top-line gains in its most recent period, marked by robust growth in its core digital advertising solutions but a notable contraction in profitability. The global advertising technology provider, known for its integrated demand and supply side platforms, reported first-quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter delivered operational momentum but highlighted concerns around rising expenses and profitability pressures.

Notable Developments
Revenue in programmatic advertising, which lets software automate digital ad buying, rose to $81.9 million, up 14% from the previous year.
Profitability measured by adjusted EBITDA dropped 30% year over year in Q1 2026, with adjusted EBITDA margins on a Contribution ex-TAC basis falling from 31% in Q1 2025 to 19%.
Cash and cash equivalents fell to $94.6 million, partly due to working capital shifts and planned investments in technology and infrastructure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised guidance for full-year programmatic revenue and contribution ex-TAC following a strong start in enterprise customer onboarding and data-driven media solutions. However, it kept the profit guidance (adjusted EBITDA) stable, emphasizing the need to rebuild margins in the coming quarters. Investors should watch whether expense growth slows and whether margin recovery takes hold as the company continues its technology investments.

Quarterly Highlights

The share of programmatic revenue compared to total sales increased to 94%, while video formats made up a lower proportion of that programmatic mix than a year ago.

The company highlighted expansion of its AI-powered demand side platform (DSP), with new data partnerships and product enhancements like exclusive content recognition data deals in Europe. Adoption of its Nexxen TV Home Screen solution also broadened, unlocking native ad inventory on major smart TV brands.

Rising costs were evident, with selling, marketing, and research and development expenses each climbing at a double-digit pace. While management cited these as strategic investments to scale the platform and enterprise business, the expense growth currently outweighs revenue gains and has led to a net loss for the quarter.

What Nexxen International Does

Nexxen International operates as a digital advertising technology company providing end-to-end software and data platforms for advertisers and media owners across connected TV, mobile, and web-based digital channels.
