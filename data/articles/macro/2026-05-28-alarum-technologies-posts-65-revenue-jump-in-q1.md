Notable Developments
Revenue reached $11.7 million, up 64.2% from the prior year period, as the company expanded its data platform and onboarded new customers in sectors like AI and eCommerce.
Gross margin slipped to 61.7%, declining by 5.8 percentage points year over year, due to higher infrastructure and third-party costs linked to new product launches.
Net retention rate was 0.93 at quarter end, indicating some churn in lower-value customer segments as the company shifted focus toward larger AI accounts.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 to be about $12.2 million, up about 39% from the prior year but slower than the 64.8% gain reported this period. Adjusted EBITDA is forecast to dip to approximately $1.8 million, reflecting continued investment in product capabilities. Investors should closely watch customer net retention and gross margin for signals on whether the company can stabilize profitability as it expands its AI data offerings.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw large growth in demand for AI-ready datasets, Website Unblocker, and Search Engine Results Page (SERP) solutions, which collectively now make up a greater share of the revenue mix. These are products aimed at helping enterprise clients collect and analyze web data in support of AI model development and digital intelligence.

Operating expenses increased to $6.4 million, driven by higher research and development costs as the company added employees to support its platform expansion and future launches, such as planned agentic workflow features due later in the year. Cash reserves rose by $1.7 million from year-end, supporting ongoing investments.

The company's net retention rate, which measures revenue from existing clients factoring in upsells and churn, was below the key 1.0 threshold at 0.93. This was largely due to purposeful shifting away from legacy and lower-value customers to larger accounts in the AI and large language model markets, a transition that management acknowledges may pressure short-term retention.
