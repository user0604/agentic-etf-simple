I3 Verticals Posts 10% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 5:06 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Modest business win as the company grew recurring revenue and delivered higher adjusted earnings.
Adjusted diluted EPS rose 10.3% year over year.
Management narrowed and lowered full-year guidance, signaling near-term caution.

Recurring revenue growth helped public sector software provider I3 Verticals (NASDAQ:IIIV) post a modest business win in its latest quarter. The company, which delivers enterprise software to government clients in justice, transportation, and utilities, reported results for the three months ended March 31, 2026, on May 7, 2026. The period saw higher adjusted earnings but also tighter margins and a slightly softer annual outlook.

Overall, the quarter showed progress in recurring revenue, though expense growth weighed on profitability.

Notable Developments
Recurring revenue sources continued to drive growth, with ARR up 11.6% year over year to $183.5 million, and about 76% of revenues now recurring.
Operating expenses increased 10% to $53.8 million, outpacing revenue growth and leading to a 0.5 percentage point drop in adjusted EBITDA margin.
Fiscal 2026 revenue guidance was narrowed and lowered to $221–229 million, reflecting some short-term softness in nonrecurring areas.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Executives cited continued investment in new product areas and ongoing efficiency initiatives. Investors should closely track the company’s ability to stabilize margins and integrate recent acquisitions while maintaining robust recurring revenue growth.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter underscored the strength of the recurring business model, as the company’s annualized recurring revenue climbed 11.6% even against a tempered environment for nonrecurring professional services. This steady base ensures a high degree of financial visibility.

Despite achieving revenue and adjusted EPS growth, total operating expenses rose faster than sales, led by higher selling, general, and administrative costs and increased equity-based compensation. This pressured margins, with adjusted EBITDA margin slipping to 28.8% from 29.3% a year ago, and operating income dropping by 26% year over year.

Management proceeded with its acquisition strategy, closing a significant insurance verification software deal in the transportation vertical. The acquired business brings strong profitability and sector fit, though the associated investing outflows and recent share buybacks drove a sharp decline in cash holdings and a new long-term debt balance of $81 million.

What I3 Verticals Does

I3 Verticals provides enterprise software solutions for public sector clients such as courts, public safety agencies, utilities, transportation, and educational institutions across the United States and Canada.
