Earnings Preview: Olaplex Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Olaplex Holdings (NASDAQ:OLPX), a company specializing in professional-grade hair care products for salons and consumers, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Olaplex Holdings reported net sales of $105.1 million, representing a 4.3% increase year over year. Adjusted EBITDA for the quarter was $12.9 million, with a margin of 12.2%. The company ended the year with $318.7 million in cash and cash equivalents and $352.3 million in debt. These results met or exceeded analyst expectations for both sales and profitability, according to management commentary and consensus data. The professional channel was a key driver, growing 18.9% year over year, while direct-to-consumer (DTC) sales also saw growth. Specialty retail declined, reflecting a strategic shift away from certain retail partners. Olaplex also completed the acquisition of Pervala Bioscience during the quarter, expanding its innovation capabilities. The company stabilized sales after prior years of decline and exited the year with improving sell-through in key accounts.

Positives
Professional channel growth was robust: Sales in the professional channel increased nearly 19% year over year, showing renewed engagement with salon partners and validating the company's focus on its core market.
Direct-to-consumer channel expanded: DTC sales grew by 6.6% in the quarter, reflecting successful digital marketing and improved online engagement, which is important for reaching new customers directly.
Cash flow and liquidity remained strong: The company generated positive operating cash flow and ended the year with over $318 million in cash, supporting continued investment and stability through its transformation phase.
Brand relaunch and innovation initiatives were completed: The company finished its 360-degree marketing relaunch and launched several new products, including top-performing industry launches and a refreshed visual identity, positioning it for future growth.
Negatives
Specialty retail channel declined: Sales in this channel fell 14.5% year over year in Q4 and 8.3% for the full year, reflecting a deliberate shift but also highlighting ongoing challenges in retail distribution.
Adjusted EBITDA margin decreased year over year: The margin dropped to 12.2% in Q4 from 17.4% in the prior year, mainly due to higher marketing and personnel investments, which pressured short-term profitability.
Overall sell-through remained down for the year: While trends improved by year-end, total sell-through was still lower compared to the previous year, indicating that demand recovery is still in progress.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Impact of No. 3+ product launch: The new No. 3+ treatment, which builds on Olaplex’s signature bond-building technology, was launched in early 2026. Investors should watch for early sales trends and feedback from both professionals and consumers, as this is a key initiative for the year.
Professional channel momentum and education efforts: The company relaunched its core pro-focused products and rolled out a global retraining program for stylists. The effectiveness of these programs in driving professional sales and loyalty will be important to monitor.
Sales channel mix and specialty retail performance: With continued strategic shifts away from some retail partners, investors should look for updates on how this affects overall sales growth and whether DTC and professional channels are offsetting retail declines.
Cost structure and margin trends: Management has indicated that Q1 EBITDA will be pressured by front-loaded marketing spend for No. 3+. Investors should assess how this impacts margins and whether the company is on track to achieve its full-year margin guidance of 21% to 22%.
Status of Henkel acquisition: The proposed acquisition by Henkel US Operations Corporation, announced in late March 2026, is pending and expected to close in the second half of 2026. Any updates on regulatory approvals or closing timeline could affect investor outlook.
