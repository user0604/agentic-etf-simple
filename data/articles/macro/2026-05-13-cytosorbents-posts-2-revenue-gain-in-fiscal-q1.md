Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to prioritize operational discipline and targets operating cash flow breakeven in the second half of the year, despite regulatory submission delays and external market headwinds. Investors should watch for progress on U.S. regulatory filings for the DrugSorb-ATR product, as well as whether commercial gains outside Germany can compensate for ongoing weakness in its core European market.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue increased only marginally year over year, supported by direct sales in markets outside Germany, which rose 13% as the company expanded into new territories. However, sales in Germany, its largest direct market, fell slightly compared to last year. Distributor sales were unchanged, with a $0.5 million order delay in the Middle East attributed to regional instability.

Gross margin narrowed from the prior-year period, dropping to 69%. Lower production, an intentional move to reduce inventory and strengthen working capital, accounted for much of the pressure -- a shift that helped improve operating margins but limited potential for immediate leverage as sales growth remained muted.

Cash and equivalents dropped by $6.7 million year over year, finishing the quarter at $6.4 million. This shrinking balance places heightened importance on cost controls and the pursuit of operational breakeven, particularly as continued investment is required for product development and regulatory compliance in the U.S.
