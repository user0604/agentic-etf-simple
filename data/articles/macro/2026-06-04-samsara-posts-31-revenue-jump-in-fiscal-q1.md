Key Takeaways
Samsara delivered a clear business win with growth well above its own outlook and a third straight quarter of GAAP profitability.
Non-GAAP earnings per share rose 54.5% year over year.
Management expects full-year growth to slow but aims to maintain profitability and margin improvement.

Samsara (NYSE:IOT) outperformed its own targets, achieving robust growth and sustained profitability in the recent quarter. The leading provider of connected operations software for physical asset monitoring and optimization continued to expand its business with strong customer demand and disciplined expense management. The company reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on June 4, 2026, providing a detailed look at these gains and its outlook for the rest of the year. Overall, the quarter stood out for top-line acceleration, but management expects growth rates to moderate as the year progresses.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 31.0% from the prior year, far exceeding management’s guidance for the quarter.
The company’s non-GAAP operating margin improved by 5 percentage points, reaching 19% as operating expenses grew slower than revenue.
Adjusted free cash flow grew 60.2%, reflecting healthy profit conversion and increased operating efficiency.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now projects slower full-year revenue growth of around 24%, with continued focus on profitability and operating margin gains. It expects to maintain GAAP profitability even without a sizable one-time legal award recorded in this quarter. Investors should monitor gross margin trends and whether product innovation, especially in artificial intelligence (AI), sustains the company’s competitive edge as rivals increase their efforts in this area.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter saw revenue growth of 31.0% and ended with annualized recurring revenue just below $2.0 billion, showing that large enterprise adoption remains strong. Non-GAAP operating margin improved to 19%, indicating that expense discipline supported expanding profitability across core business operations.

Net income also reflected a $30.3 million legal settlement gain, but management notes ongoing efforts to deliver profitability even in periods without such one-time items. Gross margin slipped by 2 percentage points year over year, which the company attributes to some increased hardware and supply chain costs—this is an area to watch in coming quarters.

Samsara continues investing in AI-driven product features, such as workflow automation and safety analytics, though the current report was light on new product adoption data. Platform integration remains extensive, with 350+ third-party integrations now offered, and expanding multi-product use among major customers.

What Samsara Does

Samsara provides a cloud-based platform that connects, monitors, and analyzes data from vehicles, equipment, and other physical assets to help businesses improve efficiency, safety, and sustainability.
