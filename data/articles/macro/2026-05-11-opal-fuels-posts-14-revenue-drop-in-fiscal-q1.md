OPAL Fuels Posts 14% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 7:34 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue and profit declined as environmental credit prices remained soft.
Adjusted EBITDA fell 17.0% to $16.7 million for the period.
Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 targets despite ongoing market headwinds.

Renewable natural gas producer OPAL Fuels (NASDAQ:OPAL) faced a difficult start to fiscal 2026 as lower environmental credit prices pressured results. The vertically integrated company, which captures biogas and converts it into transportation fuel, reported its first-quarter results on May 11, 2026. Despite production growth, the period revealed clear strains on revenue and operating profit as the company navigated persistent market and regulatory headwinds.

Overall, the quarter showed operational improvements in RNG output, but falling prices and a return to net losses signal mixed progress compared to the prior year.

Notable Developments
Total revenue dropped 14% year over year, largely due to lower Renewable Identification Number (RIN) prices and prior year regulatory catch-ups that did not repeat.
RNG (renewable natural gas) production increased 9%, reaching 1.2 million MMBtus despite difficult weather conditions and seasonal softness.
Capital expenditures more than doubled to $24.4 million as the company invested heavily in new RNG projects and infrastructure.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its full-year 2026 guidance, targeting adjusted EBITDA between $95 million and $110 million and RNG production of 5.4 to 5.8 million MMBtus. The company cited improved production efficiency and a more supportive regulatory backdrop as key reasons for its confidence. Investors should monitor environmental credit pricing and the pace of new project ramp-ups as crucial factors for meeting those targets.

Quarterly Highlights

The main driver of the revenue decline was a weaker market for environmental credits, especially RINs, which fell to an average realized price of $2.42. Fuel Station Services segment revenue and volumes also dipped, following multi-quarter highs last fiscal year and amid ongoing market fragmentation.

RNG production efficiency improved, with design capacity utilization rising to 72%, up from 69% in the prior-year quarter, supporting a 9% lift in RNG produced even as extreme winter weather challenged operations.

Capital investments increased substantially: consolidated capital expenditures grew 110% year over year. Management used proceeds from monetized tax credits and new financing arrangements to fund new project development and shore up liquidity, which stood at $232.5 million by period end.

What OPAL Fuels Does

OPAL Fuels develops, constructs, owns, and operates renewable natural gas production facilities and associated fueling stations, converting landfill and dairy biogas into transportation fuel for commercial fleets.
