Key Takeaways
Overall performance was in line with management’s expectations, as ICU Medical navigates its strategic transition.
Earnings per share increased 14.5% year over year, while revenue declined due to last year’s divestiture.
Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook, emphasizing continued margin improvement and cash flow gains later in 2026.

ICU Medical (NASDAQ:ICUI) delivered a stable first quarter, keeping its focus on profitability as sales declined. The specialty medical device maker supplies infusion therapy systems and related hospital products to healthcare providers worldwide. The latest report, released May 7, 2026, covers the first quarter and marks the company’s first full year after divesting the IV Solutions business. Overall, the period showed solid cost discipline and improving profit margins, but weaker revenue growth.

Notable developments
Gross margin rose to 39%, up 4 percentage points from the prior year, driven by the exit from the lower-margin IV Solutions business.
Infusion Systems products, including infusion pumps, delivered 6% organic revenue growth, though overall company organic revenue growth slowed to 1%.
Free cash flow fell 25% year over year, impacted by restructuring, and continued spending on systems integration and quality remediation.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed previous full-year goals: organic revenue growth in the low- to mid-single digits, and steady improvement in adjusted profit margins. Leadership expects most benefits from ongoing integration and cost-saving efforts will become clear in the second half of 2026. Investors should watch for updates on new product clearances and further margin improvement in coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s profit margins improved as it continued to shift away from lower-margin product lines following last year’s IV Solutions divestiture. Most of the margin gain resulted from exiting that business and executing manufacturing efficiency projects.

Infusion Systems, including hospital and ambulatory infusion pumps, were the top performers among core segments, helping partially offset weaker growth from Vital Care and Consumables. However, overall revenue growth outside of acquisition and divestiture effects was modest, indicating that stronger product innovation or market expansion may be needed to accelerate growth.

Cash flow from operations and free cash flow came under pressure this quarter, reflecting higher spending on compliance, restructuring, and legacy integration projects. The company’s cash position declined modestly, while its stated goal remains to reduce debt to improve future flexibility.

What ICU Medical does

ICU Medical designs, manufactures, and sells infusion therapy systems, IV consumables, and critical care medical devices for hospitals worldwide.
