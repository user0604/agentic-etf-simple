Key Takeaways
BitFuFu posted a wider net loss as revenue and margins fell sharply.
Revenue declined 6.8% year over year amid segment weakness and higher costs.
Management is focused on operational upgrades and liquidity but offers no forward guidance.

BitFuFu (NASDAQ:FUFU) saw a wider loss this quarter as unfavorable cryptocurrency market conditions pushed revenue and margins downward. The leading cloud mining and mining technology platform faced ongoing pressures, with management reporting fiscal first-quarter results on May 29, 2026 (for the period ended March 31). Overall, the quarter highlighted operational progress in cloud mining, but profitability deteriorated significantly.

Notable Developments
Cloud Mining Solutions revenue rose 7.1% to $57.5 million, making up 79% of total revenue this quarter.
Self-mining revenue dropped 35%, and equipment sales fell to zero amid lower Bitcoin prices and market demand.
Gross margin collapsed to 0.5% as cost of revenue increased despite lower overall revenue, resulting in a net loss of $35 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide specific financial guidance for upcoming quarters, instead emphasizing continued upgrades to mining equipment and disciplined liquidity management. Ongoing investment in fleet efficiency and technology remains a stated focus. The most important thing to monitor next is how quickly BitFuFu can improve margins and absorb market volatility, especially if Bitcoin prices remain weak.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue in the company’s Cloud Mining Solutions business increased to $57.5 million and now accounts for the majority of the company's total, illustrating both growing customer adoption and dependence on this segment. However, that expansion did not fully offset a sharp fall in self-mining and the disappearance of mining equipment sales, highlighting the business’s vulnerability to cyclical weakness in cryptocurrency demand.

Gross profit nearly vanished as the cost of providing mining power, including third-party hashrate costs signed before the Bitcoin price drop, rose year over year, while average selling prices and produced coins both declined. Operating expenses were stable, but non-cash losses on digital asset values drove bottom-line losses much higher.

Operationally, BitFuFu’s managed hashrate grew to 25.9 exahashes per second, up over 25% year over year, suggesting continued capacity investment. Bitcoin holdings remained significant, with 1,794 coins at period end, providing a liquidity buffer despite price-related markdowns this quarter.

What BitFuFu does

BitFuFu provides digital asset mining technology and cloud mining services, connecting individuals and institutions to mining capacity and infrastructure.
