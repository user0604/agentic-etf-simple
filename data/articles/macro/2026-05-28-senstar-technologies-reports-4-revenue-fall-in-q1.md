Key Takeaways
Senstar Technologies reported a sharp setback due to project delays and higher costs.
Earnings per share swung to a loss of $(0.04) from $0.04 a year ago.

Project delays and higher costs led to a significant setback for security solutions provider Senstar Technologies (NASDAQ:SNT) in the most recent quarter. The company, which develops advanced perimeter intrusion detection systems and intelligent sensing technology, recorded a marked reversal in profitability as expenses outpaced revenue growth and gross margins narrowed. The results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, released on May 28, 2026, highlighted challenges related to delayed project revenue and the early impact of the Blickfeld acquisition. Overall, the quarter reflected pronounced financial pressure, with continued LiDAR sales growth standing out as a rare bright spot.

Notable Developments
Revenue dropped 4% due to continued project delays, especially in U.S. government sectors affected by a federal shutdown.
Operating expenses rose 18% mostly because of the Blickfeld acquisition and a major trade show, pushing the firm to a $(0.6) million operating loss.
LiDAR product sales grew about fourfold from the prior year, now playing a much larger role in company revenue.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled continued confidence in customer demand across energy, critical infrastructure, data centers, and intelligent sensing applications. However, it did not issue explicit revenue or earnings targets for the next quarter. Investors should monitor progress in converting delayed projects to revenue and the pace at which Blickfeld integration improves both revenue and margins.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue for the period fell as anticipated projects remained delayed, with lower activity in U.S. government sectors affected by temporary shutdowns. The revenue figure marked both a year-over-year and sequential decline, underscoring ongoing project-conversion headwinds.

Profit margins narrowed sharply: gross margin fell 7.2 percentage points compared to the same quarter last year, reflecting changes in product mix and lower seasonal volume. Blickfeld’s early contribution brought higher operating expenses ahead of expected revenue synergies, contributing to a notable operating loss.

The company’s LiDAR product segment emerged as a key point of strength, with consolidated sales increasing about fourfold from the same period a year ago. Management emphasized its belief that LiDAR solutions will support medium-term growth as new markets and applications are unlocked.
