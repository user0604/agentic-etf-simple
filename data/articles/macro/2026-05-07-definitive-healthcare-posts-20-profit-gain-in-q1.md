Key Takeaways
The company posted a business win in profitability and retention, but revenue continues to decline.
Adjusted net income per share rose 20% compared to the prior year quarter.

Definitive Healthcare (NASDAQ:DH) delivered improved profitability and customer retention, but its overall sales continued to fall. The leading provider of commercial intelligence and analytics for the healthcare ecosystem reported better non-GAAP results, while total revenue declined in the quarter ending March 31, 2026 (results released May 7, 2026). Overall, the period showed stronger operational execution, but persistent top-line challenges and a large non-cash impairment charge weighed on the headline results.

Notable developments
Adjusted net income per share rose 20% year over year, as non-GAAP profitability improved despite weaker sales.
Revenue decreased 6% year over year, reflecting ongoing top-line pressure in core healthcare data markets.
The period included a $197.2 million goodwill impairment charge, which erased all remaining goodwill from the balance sheet.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management kept its full-year revenue guidance unchanged, calling for a 6%-9% decline in sales for fiscal 2026 and flat sequential revenue in the next quarter. Leadership continues to focus on operational improvements, especially product innovation and customer retention. Investors should watch for signs that rising retention and upsell rates begin to reverse ongoing revenue contraction, particularly in major segments like Life Sciences.

Quarterly highlights

The company reported stronger profitability on a non-GAAP basis, with margins and adjusted earnings expanding as cost control and operational tweaks offset the revenue decline. Despite these gains, the goodwill impairment--a recurring, non-cash charge driven by a lower market valuation--overshadowed bottom-line results, leaving net losses sharply negative for the quarter.

Total revenue for the period continued to trend down, as challenges remained in upsell activity and in larger segments such as Life Sciences. While the company secured several new or expanded customer contracts, these “green shoots” of progress have yet to drive broad-based growth across all markets.

Cash generation stayed solid, as unlevered free cash flow was $18.0 million--down year over year but enough to support ongoing investment. Cash and equivalents grew to $157.7 million at period end, providing balance sheet flexibility should market conditions improve.

What Definitive Healthcare does

The company provides a cloud-based platform offering commercial intelligence, data, and analytics about the U.S. healthcare system to help organizations understand market dynamics and drive business decisions.
