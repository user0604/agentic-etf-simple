Notable Developments
Revenue increased over 13-fold compared to the prior-year quarter, driven by Longanesi field production stabilizing at scale.
Net income and adjusted EBITDA both turned positive, signaling an operational turnaround and the company’s fourth consecutive quarter of profitability.
Total proved reserves rose 47% since year-end 2024, with significant additions at Longanesi and first-time recognition at the Trava field.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expressed confidence in exceeding previous Longanesi performance expectations and highlighted a robust balance sheet supporting ongoing investment. However, it did not provide new quantitative guidance for fiscal 2026. Investors will want to track disclosures on renewable segment contributions, commitments to further reserve growth, and signs of continued operational efficiency as commercial production expands.

Quarterly Highlights

The period’s dramatic revenue increase was primarily due to sales from the Longanesi natural gas field, which accounted for the vast majority of the $9.3 million in revenue as the project ramped into steady commercial production.

Profitability metrics such as adjusted EBITDA and net income both shifted from losses to positive values, reflecting scale-up and improved cost discipline; this was aided in part by a nonrecurring benefit from a technical accounting adjustment related to asset retirement liabilities.

Reserve upgrades were confirmed across the portfolio, with notable gains at Longanesi and Gradizza and initial booking at Trava, collectively increasing the asset base and future production potential—but with no new detail on the scale or profitability of renewable natural gas assets this quarter.

What AleAnna Does

AleAnna operates and develops natural gas and renewable natural gas projects in Italy, focusing on domestic production and energy transition opportunities.
