Neonode (NASDAQ:NEON) posted a modest business win in its first quarter, as licensing revenue increased but cash usage also rose. The technology licensing company, which supplies advanced touch and sensor solutions for industries such as automotive and retail, reported results for the three months ended March 31, 2026, released on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter highlighted initial success in Neonode’s shift to a licensing model, but also ongoing challenges from costs and customer concentration.

Notable developments
Licensing revenue nearly matched total reported revenue, driven by new license agreements and an automotive customer beginning production.
Operating expenses rose 8.6%, mainly from increased professional fees for ISO certifications and tax analysis, while R&D spending fell 7.2%.
Cash used in operations grew by 55.1%, pushing quarterly usage above $2 million, while liquidity remained solid at period end.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its focus on expanding in automotive through its MultiSensing product line and evaluating opportunities in the retail analytics space. The company gave no financial targets for revenue or profitability. Investors should closely monitor continued ramp-up of licensing revenue to see if it can keep pace with operating cash usage.

Quarterly highlights

Licensing efforts produced a 19.7% year-over-year increase in revenue, with nearly all of the revenue coming from license fees related to the core MultiSensing and legacy zForce technology platforms. Neonode also made progress as a key automotive customer transitioned from development to production, which offered an early example of scaling its licensing model to new verticals.

Cost management was mixed during the quarter. While general and administrative expenses rose by over one third due to regulatory and compliance needs, Neonode reduced its research and development and sales and marketing spending. The gap between higher expenses and modest gross profit gains kept earnings per share unchanged and increased the operating loss.

Neonode’s operational cash outflows accelerated nearly 55% to $2.1 million. Although period-end liquidity stood at $23.8 million, continued high cash use underscores a need for the company to further scale licensing agreements or diversify its customer base, which remained concentrated among a few large partners.

What Neonode does

Neonode licenses touch sensing and contactless interaction technology used in automotive, printing, and embedded applications worldwide.
