Bimini Capital Advisory Revenue Jumps 43% in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 5:03 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Advisory services drove a solid business win, while investment portfolio results lagged.
Advisory service revenues climbed 43% year over year in the first quarter of 2026, with EPS up 33% for the quarter.
Management's near-term focus shifts to integrating TJIM and diversifying client risk.

Bimini Capital Management (OTC:BMNM) delivered positive momentum from its advisory services, but continued to face headwinds in its legacy investment portfolio. The specialty finance and investment company focuses on asset management and fee-based advisory—especially for real estate investment trusts—while maintaining a smaller mortgage-backed securities book. Its latest results, released May 7, 2026 for the first quarter, reflect growth in advisory revenues and book value, tempered by higher expenses and ongoing integration challenges from the sizable Tom Johnson Investment Management acquisition. The quarter demonstrated steady progress in shifting its revenue base, but also highlighted risks from client concentration and shrinking balance sheet flexibility.

Notable developments
Advisory revenue climbed 43%, with the bulk coming from Orchid Island Capital (NYSE:ORC) management and reimbursements.
Legacy mortgage-backed securities (MBS) portfolio shrank nearly 82% by fair value as assets were liquidated to enable the TJIM acquisition.
Expenses jumped 27% year over year, with $0.6 million of one-time costs tied to the TJIM deal.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management’s main priority is now the smooth integration of Tom Johnson Investment Management, which will impact revenue mix and client diversification starting next quarter. The company did not issue explicit earnings guidance but pointed to the importance of retaining TJIM clients and talent during this transition. Investors should monitor advisory revenue contributions from TJIM and the pace at which reliance on a single client, Orchid Island Capital, is reduced.

Quarterly highlights

Advisory services delivered record revenue of $5.1 million for the quarter, up sharply year over year. The growth stemmed from management fees, overhead reimbursements, and admin services provided to Orchid Island Capital, whose equity base also ticked upward during the period.

The company’s legacy MBS investment portfolio contracted significantly, with fair value falling from $88.9 million at the end of 2025 to $16.1 million by March 31, 2026. Net interest income within this segment continued to shrink, and the remaining portfolio’s average duration increased, signaling greater sensitivity to interest rate changes.

The quarter’s higher expenses were driven by transaction and integration costs from the TJIM deal. Although the TJIM acquisition only closed after the period ended, management highlighted its potential for revenue diversification in future quarters—while warning of execution and integration risks.

What Bimini Capital Management does

Bimini Capital Management provides asset management and advisory services to real estate investment trusts, while also managing a scaled-down portfolio of government-backed mortgage securities.
