Key Takeaways
Business win: Ceragon Networks maintained stronger margins and cash flow but saw a year-over-year revenue dip.
Performance: EPS (Non-GAAP) fell to $0.01 from $0.03, with revenue down from the prior year quarter.
Outlook: Management reiterated full-year guidance and expects stronger results in the second half, though it flagged margin pressure from rising costs.

Ceragon Networks (NASDAQ:CRNT) reported a mixed start to the new year as top-line revenue fell while operational performance showed improvement in several areas. The leading provider of wireless backhaul solutions for mobile operators and private networks detailed its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, released on May 19, 2026. Overall, the period reflected both improved gross margins and cash flow, even as profit and revenue declined compared to the same quarter last year.

Notable Developments
Gross margin improved to 35.4% from 32.8% a year ago, illustrating better operational efficiency amid currency and cost headwinds.
The company generated $6.5 million in net cash provided by operating activities as inventories and trade receivables declined, resulting in a higher cash position.
Revenue dropped 4.1% year over year, with North America and India accounting for most of the revenue and ongoing concentration risk in these two regions.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its prior full-year guidance for revenue and targeted operating margins, expecting stronger business in India and North America in the second half of fiscal 2026. It cautioned that rising component costs and unfavorable exchange rates could pressure margins near-term, but stated it is taking action to manage these risks. Investors should focus on the pace of order conversion and customer activity in India and among North American Tier 1 communications service providers.

Quarterly Highlights

The company achieved a higher gross margin and returned to positive operating income even as overall revenue slipped compared to the same period last year. The gross margin gain came despite challenges from inflation and foreign currency impacts, a result supported by inventory and cost control.

Cash provided by operating activities more than doubled year over year, giving Ceragon additional financial flexibility. The improvement was driven by the reduction of inventories and trade receivables, which helped cash and cash equivalents increase to $39.2 million at quarter-end.

North America and India together contributed nearly three quarters of total revenue, showing robust customer activity in these markets. However, regional concentration means that further growth may hinge on winning additional business in these geographies or expanding in markets such as EMEA, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific.

What Ceragon Networks Does

Ceragon Networks provides wireless transport solutions that help mobile operators and other organizations build and upgrade high-capacity communication networks.
