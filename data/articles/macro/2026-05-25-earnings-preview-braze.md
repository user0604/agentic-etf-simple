Earnings Preview: Braze

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Braze (NASDAQ:BRZE), a software company that helps brands manage customer engagement across digital channels, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 after the market closes on Wednesday, May 27, 2026.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026, Braze reported revenue of $205 million, representing 28% growth year over year and an acceleration from the prior quarter. The company’s trailing twelve-month dollar-based net retention rate improved to 109%, indicating that existing customers continued to spend more on Braze’s platform. Net customer additions were strong, and the number of large customers (those spending at least $500,000 annually) grew 35% year over year. Braze’s AI-driven products, especially the Decisioning Studio (formerly OfferFit), contributed to revenue and saw robust demand. The company also announced a $100 million share repurchase authorization, including a $50 million accelerated buyback.

Braze beat analyst expectations for revenue but missed on earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter. The EPS miss was partly due to a one-time $5 million purchase accounting adjustment related to the OfferFit acquisition. Excluding this adjustment, non-GAAP net income and EPS would have been higher. The company’s operating margin improved, and free cash flow remained healthy, supporting ongoing investments in product development and growth initiatives.

Positives
Strong revenue growth and customer expansion: Revenue rose 28% year over year, with continued acceleration in organic growth and a 14% increase in total customers. This shows Braze’s platform remains in high demand across industries.
Improved customer retention and upsell momentum: Dollar-based net retention reached 109%, and large customer count increased, reflecting successful expansion within the existing customer base and effective upselling of new products.
AI product traction and innovation: The launch of Agent Console and Braze AI Operator into general availability ahead of schedule led to immediate adoption, supporting the company’s position as an innovator in customer engagement technology.
Healthy cash flow and capital return: Free cash flow was $14 million for the quarter, and the company initiated a $100 million share repurchase program, signaling confidence in its financial position.
Negatives
EPS missed analyst expectations: Despite strong revenue, Braze missed consensus EPS estimates, mainly due to a one-time accounting adjustment related to the OfferFit acquisition.
Gross margin pressure from premium messaging: Non-GAAP gross margin declined year over year, driven by higher costs for premium messaging channels and hosting, which may weigh on profitability if not managed.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Revenue and margin performance versus guidance: Management guided for Q1 revenue of $204.5 million to $205.5 million and a non-GAAP operating margin of about 5%. Investors should watch actual results against these targets, especially as the quarter has three fewer days than others.
Adoption and monetization of AI products: The rollout of Agent Console and Braze AI Operator is underway. Look for updates on customer uptake, usage trends, and how these new products are contributing to revenue and customer stickiness.
Progress on large customer growth and retention: Continued growth in the number of customers spending over $500,000 annually, as well as trends in dollar-based net retention, will be important indicators of Braze’s ability to expand within its client base.
Impact of share repurchase program: The $50 million accelerated share buyback is expected to be completed before the end of the first quarter. Investors should monitor any effects on share count and per-share metrics.
Cost management and gross margin trends: Watch for commentary on premium messaging costs, the mix of new AI products, and how these factors are influencing gross margin and overall profitability.
