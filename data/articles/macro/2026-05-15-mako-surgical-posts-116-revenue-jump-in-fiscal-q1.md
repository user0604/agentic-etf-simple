Business overview

Mako Surgical is a gold mining and exploration company operating mines and development projects in North and Central America. Its primary assets include the San Albino mine in Nicaragua and the recently acquired Moss Mine in Arizona. The company also completed the acquisition of the Mt. Hamilton project in Nevada during the quarter, further diversifying its asset base.

Quarterly performance

The San Albino gold project in Nicaragua remained the company's main earnings engine, producing 10,398 ounces of gold and achieving an average realized price of $4,901 per ounce for the quarter. Production costs rose but were outweighed by much higher sales, with segment revenue expanding over 75% year over year.

Moss Mine in Arizona, not yet at commercial production, reported sharp increases in revenue and gold sales (3,323 ounces at $4,912 per ounce). However, capitalized costs and treatment of expenses may change significantly once commercial status is achieved, which could impact profitability in future periods.

The Mt. Hamilton acquisition diversified the company's asset portfolio but introduced a gold streaming deal requiring regular gold deliveries for many years. This gold stream, valued at $42.3 million, will encumber a portion of future production and poses long-term cash flow commitments regardless of market prices.

Looking ahead

Management stated that its current cash, along with expected operating cash flow, should cover operating requirements for the next twelve months. No formal financial guidance was provided in this release. Investors should monitor the ramp-up of Moss Mine, San Albino’s ongoing output, and management of mounting financial obligations tied to new projects and gold stream liabilities.
