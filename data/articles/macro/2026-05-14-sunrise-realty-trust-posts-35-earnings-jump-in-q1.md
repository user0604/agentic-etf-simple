Quarterly highlights

Distributable earnings, a key performance measure for real estate investment trusts (REITs) that adjusts net income to reflect cash-generating power, reached $4.7 million for the quarter compared with $3.5 million last year. This resulted in improved dividend coverage, with earnings comfortably exceeding the $0.30 per share quarterly distribution.

Credit risk trends were positive: the provision for credit losses (funds set aside for potential loan defaults) dropped to $60,283 from $117,648 a year ago. This marks a turnaround from the prior quarter when a single troubled asset, the Thompson Hotel, required extra attention.

Management reiterated its commitment to a diversified lending portfolio across property types and geographies. While the company did not break out new investments by sector, it highlighted attractive pipeline opportunities, especially in multifamily and industrial properties in the Southern U.S.
