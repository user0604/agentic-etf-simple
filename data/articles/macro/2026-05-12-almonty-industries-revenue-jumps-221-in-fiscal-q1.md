Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management describes the quarter as a pivotal moment and signals further operational growth as the Sangdong project ramps up and the Gentung project advances. The company expects its general and administrative costs to normalize over the rest of fiscal 2026. Investors should closely monitor the pace of Sangdong’s ramp to commercial production and the company’s exposure to fluctuations in tungsten prices.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth in the period far outstripped prior quarters, with all the gain resulting from a sharp uplift in tungsten market prices and peak output from the Panasqueira Mine in Portugal, the company’s primary producing asset. This strong performance aligns with management’s focus on Western-market demand and shows responsiveness to favorable commodity pricing conditions.

The successful commissioning of the Sangdong Mine in South Korea is a major company milestone, signaling future production growth, though no meaningful contribution to revenue or profit occurred in the reported quarter. This site is expected to become a key operating asset once fully ramped up.

Despite favorable revenue and profit measures, general and administrative expenses more than doubled, driven by spending for management expansion and continued costs from listing status. Management has flagged intentions to manage these expenses down as new projects move into commercial operations.

What Almonty Industries Does

Almonty Industries is a global tungsten mining company operating projects in Portugal, South Korea, Spain, and the United States, supplying a critical raw material for industrial, electronics, and defense applications.
