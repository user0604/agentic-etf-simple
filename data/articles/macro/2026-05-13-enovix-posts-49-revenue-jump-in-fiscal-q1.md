Enovix Posts 49% Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:31 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Enovix expanded revenue and improved margins but saw higher cash burn and no new smartphone battery qualification.
Revenue grew 49% year over year in the quarter.
Management guides for continued growth but notes commercial smartphone battery shipments remain delayed.

Enovix (NASDAQ:ENVX) advanced top-line growth and improved its manufacturing margins, but continued to experience higher cash outflows and delays in qualifying its smartphone battery for commercial deployment. The company, known for developing advanced silicon-anode lithium-ion batteries for consumer electronics and defense markets, issued its earnings release on May 13, 2026 for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter showed progress in revenue and margins, but the main opportunity in smartphones remains unproven, and operating losses widened.

Notable developments
Revenue rose to $7.6 million, exceeding the company’s prior guidance range, mostly due to defense and industrial battery shipments.
Gross margin improved to 26.3% (Non-GAAP) as manufacturing yields progressed, marking the sixth consecutive quarter of positive gross profit.
Cash outflows grew, with free cash burn widening to $36.3 million, reflecting higher investment in manufacturing scale-up and capital expenditures.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management guides for Q2 2026 revenue in the $8.0–$9.0 million range and targets further yield gains in manufacturing, but maintains that smartphone battery qualification will drive the next significant growth phase. Investors should watch for progress on smartphone battery testing and the timing of larger-scale commercial shipments to key customers.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s revenue benefited from defense and industrial demand while testing and adaption of qualification standards for its smartphone batteries continued. In the quarter, it shipped its first commercial units for smart eyewear devices, with projections of up to 50,000 units for the year and further expansion planned in future periods.

On the production side, the company reported yield improvements exceeding 80% in a key dicing manufacturing process, while also adopting a hybrid dicing method to address bottlenecks. Margins rose as production efficiency improved, but overall scale is still insufficient to cover research and selling costs, resulting in sustained operating losses.

Cash flow was a key concern, with operating cash use nearly doubling year over year to $33.1 million and overall free cash outflow rising. The company closed the quarter with $582.7 million in cash and investments, providing room to fund operations and scale manufacturing over the near-term, even as the smartphone battery ramp remains delayed.

What Enovix does

Enovix designs and manufactures advanced silicon-anode lithium-ion batteries for high-tech consumer devices, industrial, defense, and emerging markets such as smart eyewear and drones.
