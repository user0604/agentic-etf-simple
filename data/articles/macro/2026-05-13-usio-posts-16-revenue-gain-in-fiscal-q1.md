Key Takeaways
Usio started 2026 with a strong quarter, setting new records in core payment segments but seeing some margin squeeze.
Revenue rose 16% year over year.

Usio (NASDAQ:USIO) kicked off the year with record payments and revenue growth, even as profitability pressures emerged in some areas. The leading technology-driven payment solutions company reported first-quarter results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter featured robust gains in core units balanced by weakness in prepaid services and a dip in gross margin.

Notable Developments
ACH and credit card businesses both posted record volumes and revenue growth, with ACH revenue up 25% and credit card up 23%.
The Output Solutions unit grew 19% and accelerated sequentially, while Prepaid Card Services revenue dropped 18% with weaker volumes.
Gross margin declined by 1.7 percentage points due to lower interest-related income and product mix shifts, but expense controls contributed to positive net income.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its outlook unchanged, expecting profitable, double-digit revenue growth for the full year. Leaders also anticipate margin improvement in subsequent quarters following current challenges with interest revenue and changing business mix. Investors should keep an eye on the persistence of margin pressure and ongoing softness in prepaid services.

Quarterly Highlights

The core ACH (Automated Clearing House) business, which handles electronic payments, set new company records. Revenue in this segment rose 25%, with 31% growth in dollars processed and a 34% increase in electronic check transactions. Credit card processing also achieved double-digit gains, supported by the expansion of Usio’s PayFac (payment facilitator) platform, which now delivers nearly 80% of credit card segment revenues.

Usio’s Output Solutions segment, which includes both printed and digital document processing services, grew revenue by 19%. This area also saw a significant increase in demand, as electronic documents processed jumped 41% year over year and total pieces mailed increased 31%. This was a sharp acceleration compared to performance in the prior quarter, when growth in Output Solutions was only 8%.

Not all business lines posted improvements. The Prepaid Card Services segment contracted significantly, with revenue falling 18% as card load volume declined 19% and transactions processed fell 16%. Management acknowledged these challenges, noting that the contraction in this unit contrasted with strong momentum elsewhere in the business.

What Usio Does

Usio provides integrated technology platforms to support electronic payment processing, card services, and print or digital document delivery for businesses and government clients.
