Revelation Biosciences (NASDAQ:REVB) achieved a key clinical milestone as it secured regulatory agreement for its main product trial, but operational losses continued to grow. Notable Developments
The company reached agreement with the Food and Drug Administration for a pivotal adaptive Phase 2/3 study of its lead product, Gemini, in acute kidney injury.
Operating expenses rose as research and development spending increased by 60%, supporting trial planning and regulatory efforts.
Cash and cash equivalents grew to $14.1 million at quarter-end, largely due to a $6.7 million cash infusion from warrant-related financing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated its priority is to initiate and advance the pivotal Gemini trial in acute kidney injury, using its current funding to support operations through the first quarter of 2027. The most important factor for investors to monitor is the launch and progress of this clinical study, as future milestones and financial health depend on its results and advances in partnership opportunities.

Quarterly Highlights

The agreement with the Food and Drug Administration allows the company to move forward with a combined Phase 2/3 clinical trial of Gemini, supporting its efforts to bring this novel therapy closer to approval for acute kidney injury patients. The increase in research and development and general and administrative expenses reflects preparation for this pivotal trial and continued regulatory interactions, but contributes to a wider net loss over the period.

Cash reserves improved due to a warrant inducement transaction, boosting liquidity and extending the financial runway.

No new partnership or out-licensing deals were announced during the period, and there were no updates on manufacturing or preclinical pipeline milestones for other indications beyond Gemini.
