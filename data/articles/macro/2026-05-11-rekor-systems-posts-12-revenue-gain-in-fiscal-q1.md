Key Takeaways
Rekor Systems delivered year-over-year revenue growth and improved operational efficiency.

Rekor Systems (NASDAQ:REKR) continued its shift toward recurring software revenue, posting year-over-year sales growth and narrowing operational losses. The roadway intelligence company specializes in AI-powered data solutions for transportation safety, government, and enterprise markets. The Q1 2026 results were released on May 11, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected margin improvement and signs that recent cost-cutting measures are starting to take hold.

Notable developments
All major product lines — Scout (automated license plate recognition), Discover (roadway analytics), and Command (operations platform) — saw higher revenue compared to the prior year quarter.
Adjusted gross margin climbed to 52.5%, reflecting a mix shift toward higher-value software and subscription sales.
Headcount was reduced by 16%, with most cost savings from this action expected to impact results from Q2 onwards.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects further improvement in operating loss and margins as the impact of cost cuts and business realignment flows through in upcoming periods. There is no specific numeric guidance for the next quarter, but leadership pointed to launch plans for GoSecure™, an evidence authentication product, and highlighted additional cost discipline efforts. Investors should watch for signs of continued growth in subscription revenue and for GoSecure™'s market traction after its planned Q3 rollout.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter saw revenue rise year over year, driven by higher sales across all core software and data product lines. The Discover roadway analytics platform posted the strongest dollar increase, followed by gains in the Scout and Command businesses, suggesting broad demand for AI-powered transportation solutions.

Profitability metrics improved, with a narrower net loss and a 4.3 percentage point lift in adjusted gross margin. This was attributed to a growing share of software and subscription revenue, alongside early results from payroll and operating cost-reduction measures.

The period also marked strategic investment in new offerings such as GoSecure™, aimed at providing video evidence authentication for public sector and insurance customers. Management confirmed that further cost improvements from the recent headcount reduction will be recognized in future quarters.

What Rekor Systems does

Rekor Systems provides AI-driven roadway intelligence and data services, helping agencies and enterprises improve transportation safety, efficiency, and digital evidence integrity.
