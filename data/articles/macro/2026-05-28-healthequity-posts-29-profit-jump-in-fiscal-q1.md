HealthEquity (NASDAQ:HQY) posted another strong quarter, marked by higher profits and improved operating efficiency. The leading provider of health savings accounts (HSAs) and related benefit solutions reported results for its first quarter of fiscal 2027 on May 28, 2026. Management’s boosted guidance for the year reflects confidence in its core business drivers. Overall, the quarter highlighted robust growth in assets under management and further margin gains.

Notable Developments
The company’s adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 46%, up four percentage points from the prior year period, showing improved operating leverage.
HSA assets jumped to $37.1 billion, a 19% increase, with the number of investment HSAs rising 18% to 909,000.
Management raised revenue guidance for fiscal 2027 to a range of $1.410–$1.420 billion and expanded its share repurchase authorization by $1 billion.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects continued revenue and profit growth for the rest of fiscal 2027, based on higher HSA asset balances and a stable regulatory environment. The most important area for investors to watch is whether growth in service revenue and engagement with new marketplace offerings accelerates, as these segments lag company-wide performance.

Quarterly Highlights

Total revenue rose 7% compared to the prior year, supported mainly by double-digit growth in custodial fees as both HSA assets and average yields increased. Service revenue increased but continued to lag behind other segments, rising just 2.6%, which underscores the need for better performance in member-engagement and new digital offerings.

Net income jumped 29%, with the net margin reaching 20%, as operating expense growth remained controlled and the business benefited from scale and efficiency gains. The company’s aggressive technology investments and improved automation have helped contain costs, with technology and development spending rising but producing higher overall efficiency.

HealthEquity repurchased $123 million in stock and lowered its outstanding share count, strengthening per-share results. The expanded $1 billion share buyback authorization signals ongoing commitment to returning capital and confidence in cash generation. No dividend is paid or discussed in the recent filings.

What HealthEquity Does

HealthEquity offers health savings accounts, flexible spending accounts, and related financial and administrative services to help individuals and employers manage healthcare costs.
