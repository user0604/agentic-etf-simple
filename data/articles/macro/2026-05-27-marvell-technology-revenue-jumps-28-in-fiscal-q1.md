Marvell Technology Revenue Jumps 28% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 4:19 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Marvell Technology delivered a business win, outpacing its own revenue and profit guidance.
Non-GAAP EPS rose 29% year over year.
Management issued a more bullish outlook for next quarter and fiscal year.

Semiconductor supplier Marvell Technology (NASDAQ:MRVL) delivered stronger-than-projected results behind a surge in its data center business. The company is a leading provider of semiconductor solutions powering data centers, communications networks, and storage infrastructure. Reporting on May 27, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, Marvell posted record revenue and ramped up its growth forecasts for the rest of the year. The quarter stood out for robust top-line expansion but increasing expenses and growing dependence on data center sales.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 28% compared to the year-ago period, led by data center sales now making up 76% of the total.
Cash flow from operations rose 92% year over year to $638.8 million, reflecting improved collections and rising bookings.
Operating expenses grew 35%, largely as a result of integrating recent acquisitions, lifting research and development spending significantly.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its revenue target for the next quarter to $2.7 billion, calling for about 35% year-over-year growth. It also expects margins to remain steady, with continued spending on innovation and integration of new businesses. Investors should watch how Marvell manages higher costs and maintains data center momentum in light of this aggressive growth outlook.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s data center business provided most of the growth, generating $1.83 billion in sales during the period. This segment benefited from demand for AI infrastructure such as advanced optics, Ethernet switches, and custom chips used in cloud computing applications.

Marvell completed the acquisitions of Celestial AI and XConn Technologies after acquiring both earlier this year, further expanding its semiconductor offerings for cloud and AI customers. The financials for this quarter included these new businesses, pushing up both revenue and operating costs.

Marvell’s investment in research and development increased by approximately 21.7% year over year, signaling a commitment to new product development. Management signaled that this spending is needed to maintain a competitive edge, particularly as customers seek increasingly powerful and efficient components in areas like custom application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) and memory interconnects.

What Marvell Technology Does

Marvell Technology designs, develops, and sells semiconductor solutions for data center, networking, storage, and connectivity applications across major technology infrastructure sectors.
