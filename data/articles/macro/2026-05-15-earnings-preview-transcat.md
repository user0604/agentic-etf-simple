Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal year 2026, Transcat reported consolidated revenue of $83.9 million, up 25.6% from the prior year. The company saw double-digit growth in both its Service and Distribution segments, with Service revenue up 29.1% and Distribution revenue up 19.8%. Organic service revenue growth returned to a more typical level at 7.3%, reflecting improved customer activity and the realization of previously delayed orders. Gross profit increased 28.3% to $25.3 million, and adjusted EBITDA rose 27.2% to $10.1 million. However, the company posted a net loss of $1.1 million, mainly due to higher amortization from recent acquisitions, increased interest expense, and one-time CEO transition costs. Adjusted diluted earnings per share (EPS) was $0.26, which missed analyst estimates, while revenue surpassed consensus expectations.

Positives
Revenue growth exceeded analyst expectations: Both the Service and Distribution segments delivered double-digit growth, with consolidated revenue beating consensus estimates. This reflects strong demand across regulated end markets such as life sciences, aerospace, and energy.
Strong performance from recent acquisitions: The completed acquisitions of Martin Calibration and Essco Calibration contributed significantly to revenue and expanded the company’s geographic reach and technical capabilities.
Distribution segment margin improvement: Distribution gross margin rose to 32.4%, up 3.3 percentage points from the prior year, driven by a favorable mix of higher-margin rental revenue.
Adjusted EBITDA growth: Adjusted EBITDA increased 27.2% year over year, indicating underlying operational strength despite higher non-cash and one-time charges.
Negatives
Adjusted EPS missed analyst consensus: Despite strong revenue, adjusted diluted EPS of $0.26 fell short of expectations, mainly due to increased amortization, interest expense, and executive transition costs.
Net loss reported for the quarter: The company posted a net loss of $1.1 million, compared to net income in the prior year, reflecting higher operating and acquisition-related expenses.
Service segment margin contraction: Service gross margin declined to 28.8% from 29.7% a year ago, as onboarding new customers and increased outsourcing costs weighed on profitability.
Operating expenses rose sharply: Total operating expenses increased 43.2% year over year, consuming a larger share of revenue and impacting operating income.

Progress on CEO succession plan: The CEO search is nearing completion, with additional transition costs expected in Q4. The timing and outcome of this leadership change could influence near-term strategy and investor sentiment.
