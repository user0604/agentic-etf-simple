Key Takeaways
Revenue rose while costs outpaced sales, leading to slight margin compression and higher credit loss allowances.
Earnings per diluted share increased to R$2.85, up 3.3% compared to the prior year.
Management affirmed its fiscal 2026 revenue guidance with no updated quarterly forecast.

Medical educator Afya (NASDAQ:AFYA) expanded revenue and net income in its latest quarter but faced rising costs that trimmed profitability. The leading provider of medical education in Brazil reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026, highlighting tuition-driven growth alongside some cost headwinds and digital service slowdowns. Overall, the period showed steady top-line growth, but emerging cost pressures and flat digital results warrant attention.

Notable Developments
Undergraduate segment revenue increased 7.9% year over year, supported by new seats from the recently acquired FUNIC campus.
Cost of services rose 11.3%, outpacing revenue growth and contributing to a small decline in gross margin to 68.9% from 69.8% year over year.
Dividends declared for the quarter more than doubled to R$307.4 million, resulting in a higher dividend per share of R$3.45.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its fiscal 2026 revenue forecast of R$3.95 billion to R$4.1 billion but did not offer updated quarterly guidance. With first-quarter revenue representing about a quarter of the annual target, management remains focused on seat expansion, digital growth, and cost management. Investors should closely monitor margin trends and the pace of digital service recovery in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The Undergraduate segment continued to drive company performance as new medical seats from the FUNIC acquisition helped lift revenue. This expansion reflects Afya’s core strategy to grow its education network; however, no major new seat approvals beyond FUNIC were disclosed, potentially slowing future organic growth if not addressed.

Cost and expense growth outstripped revenue gains, highlighted by a double-digit increase in payroll and selling, general and administrative expenses. The allowance for expected credit losses also ticked up, indicating a slightly higher risk profile for tuition collections and financial discipline.

Digital and Medical Practice Solutions revenue increased 5.9% year over year, with mobile app subscription receivables declining from year-end. Management disclosed no new product launches or significant digital initiatives, making digital engagement a key issue to track next.

What Afya Does

Afya delivers undergraduate and continuing education programs in medical and health sciences and provides digital health services to students and healthcare professionals across Brazil.
