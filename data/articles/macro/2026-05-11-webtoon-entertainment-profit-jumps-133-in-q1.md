Webtoon Entertainment Profit Jumps 133% in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 4:30 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Delivered strong profit improvement but top-line growth was flat.
Earnings per share more than doubled year over year.
Management forecasts only modest revenue and profit gains for the next quarter.

Digital comics platform Webtoon Entertainment (NASDAQ:WBTN) posted clear gains in profitability, even as its sales remained essentially unchanged. The global leader in user- and professional-created webcomics managed notable cost reductions, but saw revenue and user momentum stall for the first quarter of 2026 (reported on May 11, 2026). Overall, the quarter demonstrated improved efficiency, but highlighted ongoing challenges in sustaining growth.

Notable Developments
Profit improvement stood out, with adjusted EBITDA up to $9.5 million from $4.1 million, reflecting disciplined cost management across the business.
Total sales slipped by 1.5% year over year, as growth in paid content was offset by sharp declines in IP Adaptations and Advertising.
Webtoon ended the period with $594.9 million in cash and no debt, highlighting a strong balance sheet despite flat revenue and user softness.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects revenue growth on a constant currency basis of up to 4.6% for the second quarter, and expects adjusted EBITDA to decline substantially versus Q1.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s profit jumped significantly because operating expenses dropped, with general and administrative costs down almost 10% and reduced outflows in marketing. Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 3.0% from 1.3%, more than doubling the margin from the prior year.

Paid Content, the largest product category, grew by 2.3% year over year on a constant currency basis, led by continued strength in Korea. In contrast, IP Adaptations revenue dropped 22.2%, showing the segment’s ongoing volatility and uneven timing of major adaptation releases.

Key user and creator engagement figures were not disclosed this quarter, raising ongoing questions after earlier reports of declining monthly active users. Despite this, the average revenue per paying user increased in all regions, and Webtoon’s cash position strengthened from the prior quarter.

What Webtoon Entertainment Does

Webtoon Entertainment operates a digital platform for discovering, reading, and monetizing webcomics and graphic stories, connecting creators and millions of global users.
