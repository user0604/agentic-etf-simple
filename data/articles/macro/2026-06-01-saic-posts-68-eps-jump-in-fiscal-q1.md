SAIC Posts 68% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 7:08 AM

Key Takeaways
SAIC delivered a business win as margins reached record highs, driven by cost controls and strong contract execution.
Adjusted diluted EPS increased approximately 68% while revenue rose approximately 2% year over year.
Management raised full-year profitability guidance but maintained revenue expectations, highlighting continued focus on margin growth.

Margins surged to new highs as Science Applications International (NASDAQ:SAIC) focused on contract discipline and operational efficiency in its latest quarter. The major government technology contractor serves U.S. defense, intelligence, and civilian agencies across critical IT and engineering fields. The company reported its first-quarter results on June 1, 2026, covering the three months ended May 1, 2026.

Overall, the quarter brought robust profitability and cash flow, with only modest revenue growth as organic expansion remained constrained.

Notable Developments
Adjusted operating margin improved by 3.2 percentage points, breaking old records as cost controls and a nonrecurring $12 million investment gain helped boost profit.
Revenue rose 1.5%; nearly all this growth came through the SilverEdge acquisition, while organic revenue change was just 0.5%.
Free cash flow (non-GAAP) rose sharply: free cash flow swung from a $44 million use to a $118 million inflow, driven by stronger working capital and higher profitability.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased guidance for full-year adjusted EBITDA, margin, and adjusted EPS, citing the strong start. Revenue forecasts were left unchanged, reflecting ongoing caution around organic growth. The main area to watch remains the pace at which new contract wins convert into revenue growth and how the company navigates upcoming large contract recompetes.

Quarterly Highlights

Margin expansion was a standout theme, as both operating and EBITDA margins improved more than three percentage points year over year. This improvement stemmed from lower overhead costs, positive contract mix, and a one-time investment gain.

Revenue growth remained sluggish, with total revenue climbing approximately 2%. Almost all the increase related to the SilverEdge acquisition, as underlying organic growth stayed flat due to slowdowns in legacy enterprise IT and the partial offset from new awards.

Cash-generation surged: the company posted $118 million in free cash flow compared to an outflow a year earlier. Capital allocation remained active, including $175 million in share repurchases and a recently declared $0.37 per share dividend.

What Science Applications International Does

Science Applications International provides technology, engineering, and consulting services to U.S. government agencies, focusing on IT modernization, digital engineering, and mission-critical solutions.
