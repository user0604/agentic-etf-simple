Business overview

Grocery Outlet operates a chain of independently-run discount grocery stores specializing in name-brand surplus and closeout products at significant discounts to conventional supermarkets.

Quarterly performance

Grocery Outlet’s net sales rose 3.6%, driven by new store sales, as it opened seven new stores and closed 28 locations, reducing its overall store count to 549. Comparable store sales fell 1.0%, mostly due to a 3.1% drop in average transaction size that was only partly offset by 2.1% more customer visits. Gross margin narrowed to 29.6%, affected by inventory markdowns, while restructuring charges totaled $18.2 million and a $158 million goodwill impairment drove a net loss under standard accounting rules.

New stores accounted for all revenue growth, as comparable store sales declined for the quarter. Management noted “sequential improvements” in comparable sales, but the core business continued to struggle, with traffic up modestly yet average basket size falling.

The company continued its “Optimization Plan” by closing 27 underperforming stores (plus one other closure) and recorded $18.2 million in restructuring charges alongside a $158 million non-cash goodwill impairment. These steps are intended to focus future growth on higher-performing markets and restore operational efficiency. Profitability came under pressure as management increased spending on promotions and marked down inventory related to store closures, compressing gross margins by 0.8 percentage points year over year. Adjusted EBITDA dropped nearly 17%, signaling that margin recovery remains a challenge for the rest of the year.

Looking ahead

Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance for sales, comparable store performance, and margins but acknowledged ongoing challenges in core store sales and profitability. The most important factor to monitor is whether the planned improvements in the mix of discounted merchandise and store refresh efforts successfully stabilize comparable store sales and margins over the coming quarters.
