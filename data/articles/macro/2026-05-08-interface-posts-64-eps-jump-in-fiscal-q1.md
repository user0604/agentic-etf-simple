Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now expects full-year sales between $1.45–1.48 billion and a slightly higher gross margin band. The forecast reflects optimism based on strong first-quarter demand and order flow in key markets. Investors may want to focus on trends in inventory and net debt, as both have risen and could impact future flexibility if persistent.

Quarterly Highlights

Interface's revenue advance reflected healthy growth across all product groups, with billings led by a 16% increase in corporate office and 11% in healthcare spaces. The Americas segment saw net sales rise 8.7%, while Europe and Asia-Pacific regions contributed a 15.2% jump, boosted in part by stronger order pipelines.

Profitability expanded as adjusted gross margin ticked up to 38.3%, beating management’s forecast even as operating expenses grew. The better margins came from efficiency gains and a favorable shift in product mix toward higher-value lines, according to the company.

Working capital commitments and capital expenditure increased during the period, pushing net debt higher. Most of the inventory build aligns with confidence in future demand, but the development warrants continued monitoring alongside the company’s capital allocation priorities.

What Interface Does

Interface designs and manufactures modular commercial flooring and resilient surface materials, supplying customers globally in office, healthcare, education, and residential markets.
