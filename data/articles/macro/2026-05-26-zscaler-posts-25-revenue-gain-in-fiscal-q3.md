Zscaler Posts 25% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q3

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 4:22 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Zscaler delivered a solid operational quarter marked by record non-GAAP profitability and expanded recurring revenue.
Revenue rose 25% year over year.
Management raised guidance for key income statement metrics but lowered its outlook for free cash flow margin as new investments ramp up.

Zscaler (NASDAQ:ZS) notched a business win for the quarter as its core security platform showed strong customer demand and set profitability records. The cybersecurity company specializes in cloud-native Zero Trust security, helping organizations protect their data and users across distributed IT environments. The latest results, announced on May 26, 2026 (for the third quarter of fiscal 2026), reveal solid growth and enhanced operating margins, though higher spending led to a drop in free cash flow margin. Overall, the quarter demonstrated momentum in product innovation and customer expansion, balanced by signs of increasing costs.

Notable Developments
Non-GAAP net income per share rose to $1.08, a 28.6% increase from the prior year, hitting a record for the company.
Annual recurring revenue, which measures contracted revenue over 12 months, reached $3.53 billion, up 25.0% year over year.
Free cash flow grew to $136.0 million, but free cash flow as a percent of revenue dropped compared to the prior-year period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year guidance for revenue, non-GAAP operating income, and earnings per share, signaling ongoing confidence in demand and execution. However, it lowered its target for free cash flow margin in fiscal 2026, pointing to increased capital spending for infrastructure and product innovation. Investors should monitor how new investments and the integration of recent acquisitions, like Red Canary and Symmetry Systems, impact organic growth and profitability in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter showcased ongoing momentum in the Zero Trust Exchange platform, with new public sector wins and expansion in artificial intelligence (AI) security features. Customers benefited from recent technology partnerships, including collaborations with OpenAI and regional telecom leaders such as Singtel and Bharti Airtel, which aim to secure the growing landscape of connected devices and AI agents.

Annual recurring revenue growth remains a bright spot, with both total ARR and organic ARR (excluding acquisitions) rising at double-digit rates. Yet organic ARR growth slowed modestly versus previous quarters, and a significant portion of new ARR now comes from the Red Canary business, which management acknowledged has higher customer turnover than Zscaler’s core business.

Non-GAAP operating margin improved to 23%, its highest yet, reflecting expense discipline and scale benefits. At the same time, operating cash flow margin and free cash flow margin declined year over year, as Zscaler raised its capital investment to support new AI workloads, global expansion, and product development. Management responded by lowering its free cash flow margin outlook for the rest of the year.

What Zscaler Does

Zscaler provides cloud-based security solutions that enable organizations to securely connect users, devices, and applications regardless of location, by applying Zero Trust principles to prevent cyber threats and protect sensitive data.
