Ideal Power (NASDAQ:IPWR) reported a quarter marked by advances in customer engagement and product development, but it did not record any revenue from product sales. The power semiconductor company made progress in electric vehicle and data center projects, expanded its patent portfolio, and raised capital, according to its first-quarter 2026 results released on May 14, 2026 (covering the quarter ended March 31). Overall, spending increased as it worked to convert its pipeline into orders and future sales.

Notable developments
The company raised $12.6 million in February 2026, increasing its cash and equivalents to $16.41 million at quarter end, up from $6.1 million at the end of 2025.
No product revenue was recognized in the period, with all operating expenses funded by the existing cash position and capital raise.
It expanded its B-TRAN® patent estate to 103 issued patents, with 50 held internationally, strengthening its intellectual property base.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management’s top priorities are converting ongoing development collaborations into volume production orders, especially with Asia partners and automotive customers. While no guidance was issued for near-term revenue, progress depends on moving large customer pilots to commercial sales. Investors should watch for news on signed design-ins and the timing of first product shipments.

Quarterly highlights

The company announced new development projects with its lead Asia customer and advanced its partnership with Stellantis, a global automaker, with midyear targets for delivering next-generation B-TRAN® samples. It also signed a letter of intent with a U.S. partner for a solid-state circuit breaker prototype aimed at high-power data centers using the latest NVIDIA power architecture. These engagements show movement toward industry applications for B-TRAN® technology across electric vehicles, data centers, and power infrastructure.

Operating expenses increased by nearly one-third over the prior year, mainly due to higher stock-based compensation (partly related to executive transitions) and greater personnel expense as project activity expanded. Net cash used for operating and investing activities also rose, but the February capital raise left the company with a stronger financial position and no long-term debt as of March 31.

Despite zero product revenue in the quarter, the company grew its patent portfolio and pipeline of customer opportunities. Intellectual property protection, with over 100 issued patents, supports long-term commercialization efforts and potential licensing agreements.

What Ideal Power does

Ideal Power designs and develops high-efficiency power semiconductor switches, focusing on its proprietary B-TRAN® technology for energy, electric vehicle, and industrial power management applications.

Members can monitor future Ideal Power releases for signs of the first commercial product shipments and any breakthroughs in volume customer orders.
