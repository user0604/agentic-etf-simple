Alibaba's AI Bet Decimates Earnings

Don't be deceived by the headlines. There's a reason the stock is up roughly 7% regardless.

Yasser El-Shimy, PhD, MBA

May 13, 2026, 11:18 AM

Comments
8.5

Chinese technology/e-commerce giant Alibaba Group (NYSE: BABA) delivered a jarring Q4 FY2026 earnings report, revealing that aggressive artificial intelligence and cloud infrastructure spending has effectively decimated near-term profitability. While overall revenue grew a modest 3% year-over-year to 243.38 billion yuan ($35.28 billion), adjusted diluted earnings per ADS collapsed by a staggering 95% to just $0.09, drastically missing the $0.90 consensus.

However, don't be deceived by the headlines. There's a reason the stock is up roughly 7% regardless. Alibaba's massive CAPEX is yielding promising results in key areas: revenue from the Cloud Intelligence Group is accelerating and rose 38% to 41.6 billion yuan, fueled by an eleventh consecutive quarter of triple-digit growth in AI-related product adoption.

Why It Matters / Investment Perspective: CEO Eddie Wu is executing a high-stakes transition from incubation to large-scale AI commercialization, committing to a 380 billion yuan investment plan over three years. For investors, the thesis has shifted; Alibaba is no longer a high-margin e-commerce cash cow, but a tech infrastructure play navigating intense domestic competition and brutal capital expenditure cycles. Nonetheless, the market will forgive BABA for now because these investments are essential for future growth and to compete globally with US-based tech giants like Alphabet and Amazon. Qwen, Alibaba's open-source AI model has been a runaway success, and the market clearly likes what it sees.
