Key Takeaways
Assured Guaranty delivered strong new business growth but lower profitability due to nonrecurring gains in the prior year.
Earnings per share decreased over the prior year period.
Management expects continued diversification, with lower buybacks planned to support new business and integration of annuity reinsurance.

Assured Guaranty (NYSE:AGO) saw robust growth in new premiums but experienced a drop in quarterly profitability as major one-time gains did not repeat. The leading U.S. financial guaranty insurer and diversified asset manager reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 7, 2026, covering a period marked by expansion in new business production and the first consolidation of its new annuity reinsurance business. Overall, the quarter was defined by strategic advances in diversification but softer headline profits and a modest decline in market share.

Notable Developments
Premiums from new business doubled year over year, with gross written premiums at $70 million and present value of new production at $73 million.
Quarterly profitability declined, as both net income and adjusted operating income fell compared to prior year due to large one-time gains in that period and lower investment results.
Asset management adjusted operating income rose sharply to $44 million, while annuity reinsurance posted a neutral result in its first quarter of consolidation.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management plans to reduce share repurchases in the near term, targeting $30 million over the next three months, to support growth in new business areas such as annuity reinsurance and asset management. No specific detailed financial guidance was given for coming quarters. Investors should closely monitor the profitability of the annuity reinsurance segment and credit trends within insured portfolios as these segments take a larger role in the business.

Quarterly highlights

New business production in the financial guaranty segment showed substantial growth, with U.S. public finance leading the way. However, market share in primary U.S. municipal insurance declined from 64% to 53%, suggesting increased competition or pricing pressure in that market.

Headline profitability fell from the prior year, with adjusted operating income at $115 million and non-GAAP EPS at $2.50, mostly because of the absence of significant one-time gains like last year’s litigation settlement and lower-than-expected investment contributions.

Results in asset management were boosted by carried interest from a single-asset fund and continued revenue from Sound Point, while the annuity reinsurance business contributed for the first time but did not enhance profits yet. Shareholder capital returns totaled $93 million, comprising $75 million of share repurchases and $18 million in dividends.

What Assured Guaranty does

Assured Guaranty provides insurance for financial obligations—including municipal bonds—for public and private sector issuers, and it has expanded into asset management and life and annuity reinsurance.
