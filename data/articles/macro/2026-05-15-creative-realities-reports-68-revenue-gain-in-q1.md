Notable Developments
Revenue rose to $16.3 million, up 68% from last year, mainly due to the added contribution from the Cineplex Digital Media (CDM) acquisition.
Gross margin fell to 34.2%, reflecting a higher mix of low-margin hardware sales and $0.5 million in one-time integration costs.
Adjusted EBITDA turned negative at $(0.5) million, reflecting increased expenses in sales, marketing, and administration, which doubled year over year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its expectation for stronger sales and margin improvement over the rest of fiscal 2026, citing a robust sales pipeline and the anticipated realization of acquisition synergies. Investors should focus on recurring revenue growth and gross margin recovery as key signals of improved profitability in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The CDM acquisition added $7.9 million in revenue for the period, helping drive overall sales growth but also adding complexity and higher operating costs. Management noted that winter storms and project delays pushed about $4 million in expected revenue into future quarters, contributing to the operational loss.

Gross profit increased over the prior-year period in dollar terms, but gross margin dropped sharply as hardware sales grew faster than higher-margin service sales. Service margins also fell from 53% to 42%, linked partly to the expiration of customer contracts and extra integration costs associated with the CDM business.

Operating expenses such as sales, marketing and general administration more than doubled, leading to a wider operating loss and a year-over-year swing from positive to negative adjusted EBITDA. Cash on hand ended slightly higher at $1.8 million, but debt increased to $47.5 million.
