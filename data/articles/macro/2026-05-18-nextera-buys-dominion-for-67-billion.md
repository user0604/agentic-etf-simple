NextEra Buys Dominion for $67 Billion

The deal is subject to all the normal approvals.

Lou Whiteman

May 18, 2026, 10:42 AM

Comments
8.0

NextEra Energy (NYSE: NEE) is powering up its utilities business with one of the largest deals announced so far in 2026, a $67 billion acquisition of Dominion Energy (NYSE: D).

The deal, if approved, would create an Eastern Seaboard power with utility operations in Florida, the Carolinas, and Virginia. NextEra and Dominion also have power generation and storage assets outside of their home territories.

The deal is all about data centers. Dominion's utility reach includes northern Virginia, home to a large and growing number of data centers serving the Pentagon, government customers, and commercial interests.
Dominion has connected more than 450 data centers in Virginia and says data centers account for nearly 30% of its business in the state.

That seems unlikely to change any time soon. NextEra is taking advantage of its highly valued currency (currently trading at nearly 24 times earnings compared to Dominion's 18 times) in an all-stock deal that should provide it with fuel for growth for years to come.

The deal is subject to all the normal approvals. I don't think there is a chance that Dominion holders will say no, but the regulatory aspect is interesting. Energy prices are a big story heading into the November elections, and NextEra will have to secure approvals from a range of federal and state bodies.
