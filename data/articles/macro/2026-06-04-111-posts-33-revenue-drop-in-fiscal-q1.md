Notable Developments
Marketplace service revenue rose 24.7% year over year, confirming the focus on high-margin, platform-based business.
Promotional products revenue increased 70.2%, with flagship item 'Cravit' achieving a sharp jump in unit sales year over year.
Cash, restricted cash, and short-term investments decreased 35.1% to RMB396.6 million, reflecting liquidity pressure from operating losses and repayments to investors.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its aim to reshape the business from product distribution to a technology-enabled healthcare platform, prioritizing operational efficiency and service income. No quantitative revenue or profit guidance was provided. Investors should closely monitor the pace of growth in platform services and the company's ability to stabilize cash outflows in the coming periods.

Quarterly Highlights

Marketplace and service revenue achieved material growth, with marketplace services rising 24.7% from the prior-year quarter. This aligns with the platform strategy, but the overall scale remains much smaller than legacy product sales.

Promotional products emerged as a notable success story, led by 'Cravit', which saw unit sales expand from 84,000 to 710,000 boxes year over year and contributed to a 75.0% gain in gross profit for this category.

Despite cost control in fulfillment and administrative expenses, both gross profits and segment profits declined, while ongoing net losses and sharply lower liquid assets highlight pressing financial risks facing the company.

What 111 Does

111 operates an integrated digital platform connecting online pharmacies, offline retail stores, healthcare service providers, and pharmaceutical companies across China.
