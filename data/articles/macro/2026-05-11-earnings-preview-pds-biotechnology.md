PDS Biotechnology (NASDAQ:PDSB), a clinical-stage developer of targeted cancer immunotherapies, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, PDS Biotechnology continued to advance its clinical pipeline, particularly its lead immunotherapy candidate PDS0101 for HPV16-positive head and neck cancer. The company reported a net loss of $34.5 million for the year, or $0.74 per share, which was an improvement from the prior year and beat the consensus estimate of a $0.20 loss per share for the quarter (actual Q4 EPS was -$0.14). Research and development expenses decreased year over year, reflecting lower manufacturing and personnel costs, partially offset by higher clinical costs. The company ended the year with $26.7 million in cash, following several financing activities and debt repayments. No product revenue was reported, as expected for a company at this stage.

Positives
Clinical progress with PDS0101 and VERSATILE-003 amendment: The company received FDA clearance to amend its pivotal Phase III VERSATILE-003 trial, allowing progression-free survival as an interim primary endpoint. This could accelerate the timeline to potential regulatory submission and approval.
Final data from VERSATILE-002 supports long-term survival: Median overall survival reached 39.3 months in the Phase II trial for HPV16-positive head and neck cancer, which is substantially longer than historical results for standard therapies.
Improved financial discipline: Operating expenses decreased by 13 percent year over year, and the annual net loss improved by 8 percent, reflecting cost controls and careful resource allocation.
Strengthened intellectual property position: New patents were granted in the U.S. and Japan, extending market protection for PDS0101 into the 2040s.
Negatives
Ongoing net losses and cash burn: The company reported a net loss of $34.5 million for 2025 and used $27.7 million in operating cash, highlighting the continued need for external funding until product approval and commercialization.
No product revenue yet: PDS Biotechnology remains pre-commercial and has not generated any product sales, which is typical for its stage but means it relies on financing activities for cash.
Increased interest expense: Net interest expense rose to $4.1 million for the year, mainly due to debt extinguishment costs and lower interest income, which could affect future cash flows.
Uncertainty about future funding: The company’s ability to continue as a going concern depends on raising additional capital, and there is no guarantee of favorable financing terms in the future.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report

Cash position and funding plans: The company’s ability to maintain sufficient cash reserves and any new financing activities or partnerships will be important for sustaining operations through the next phases of development.
Updates on PDS01ADC and pipeline programs: Any new clinical data or enrollment progress from ongoing trials with PDS01ADC, especially in prostate or colorectal cancer, could provide insight into the broader value of the company’s immunotherapy platform.
