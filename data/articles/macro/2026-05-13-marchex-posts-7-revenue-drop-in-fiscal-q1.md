Marchex (NASDAQ:MCHX) saw weaker sales as revenue slipped and fell short of the company’s own outlook in the most recent period. The customer conversation analytics provider is entering a key transition, driven by new artificial intelligence (AI) products and an upcoming acquisition. Its earnings release, issued May 13, 2026, covered the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period showed shrinking sales but also improving cost control and early signs of momentum in its AI suite.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership expects revenue to grow sequentially in the upcoming quarter, with profitability (as measured by adjusted EBITDA) projected to rise above earlier guidance. The main focus for investors will be the adoption rate of new AI-powered products and the execution of the Archenia acquisition, which could transform the company’s growth and earnings profile.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales declined as the company continued to feel the effects of customer migrations to a new technology platform, ending the quarter with revenue below prior expectations. This revenue dip was paired with tighter cost controls, particularly a 9.8% year-over-year reduction in total operating expenses and sharply lower general and administrative spending.

Adoption of new AI-driven solutions gained ground with Marchex’s top 100 customers, who contribute about 90% of total revenue; nearly one third of these customers evaluated new products, with half making pilot or recurring purchases. Management highlighted the role of AI innovation and bundled solutions in positioning the company for future sales acceleration.

The upcoming acquisition of Archenia, expected to close in July 2026, could bring combined quarterly revenue to about $15 million and annualized growth rates between 15% and 20%, according to the company. Successful integration and scaling up customer adoption of bundled products remain a key area to track for the remainder of the year.
