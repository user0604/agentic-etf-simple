Notable Developments
Revenue jumped 51% over the same period last year, mainly from rapid expansion in point-of-sale and prepaid services.
General and administrative costs fell approximately 25%, reflecting cost controls that started in fiscal 2025.
Despite cost discipline, operating losses and balance sheet risk increased, as liabilities and net losses widened year on year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted increasing wireless subscriber lines, ongoing cost controls, and upcoming launches for its HERO Wireless platform as priorities for the next quarters. The most critical area for investors to track is whether revenue expansion can start to generate positive gross margins and improve the company’s financial position. Cash flow and debt levels also remain important to monitor as expansion continues.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue in the period increased sharply, led by a 71% rise in sales from point-of-sale and prepaid wireless products. The company reported progress integrating digital acquisition tools such as ProgramBenefits.com and expanding its retail partner base with three Master Agents and three Independent Sales Organizations, which are expected to boost prepaid volume in the coming months.

Operational efficiency improved, with the company reporting its cost-per-enrollment fell approximately 48% and cost-per-lead dropped approximately 28%, after bringing its growth marketing team in house. The lead-to-enrollment conversion rate rose approximately 39%, and total wireless subscriber lines surpassed 200,000 during the period.

On the balance sheet, cash and equivalents rose slightly to $2.0 million but remained low when compared to growing current liabilities, which climbed to $26.0 million. Despite better cash management and reduced administrative spend, operating and net losses widened and negative gross margins persisted for another quarter.

What SurgePays does

SurgePays operates a fintech and wireless services platform targeting underserved and rural communities through a network of partner retail locations across the United States.
