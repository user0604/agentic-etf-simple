Key Takeaways
Steris closed fiscal 2026 with higher revenue and earnings, supported by growth in recurring services and consumables.
Earnings per share rose 3.3% compared to the prior year quarter.
Management expects steady revenue and earnings growth for fiscal 2027.

Sterilization and infection prevention specialist Steris (NYSE:STE) finished fiscal 2026 with gains in both revenue and profit, driven by recurring service and consumable sales. The company, a leading provider of sterilization products and services for healthcare and life sciences, released its fourth-quarter and full-year results on May 11, 2026. The quarter reflected steady progress, though growth rates moderated and margin pressure from tariffs and inflation persisted.

Notable Developments
Quarterly revenue reached $1.59 billion, with recurring service and consumable sales growing faster than capital equipment.
Operating income in the Applied Sterilization Technologies segment increased 7.3%, while the Healthcare segment’s operating income rose 1.3% despite higher tariff and inflation costs.
Free cash flow for the trailing twelve months climbed nearly 25%, supporting a 10% higher annual dividend to $2.46 per share and a new $1 billion share buyback authorization.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects reported revenue to increase by 7–8% and adjusted earnings per share to rise 9–11% in fiscal 2027. The company plans to invest in manufacturing capacity, pursue targeted acquisitions, and focus on cost control. Investors should watch Steris’s ability to protect profit margins as inflation and tariffs continue to affect costs, especially as equipment demand slows.

Quarterly Highlights

The Healthcare segment, which generates over 70% of total sales, expanded recurring revenue for services and consumables by 8% in the quarter, outpacing capital equipment growth. The segment’s backlog also increased, with the Healthcare order book reaching $392 million at quarter end.

Applied Sterilization Technologies, which provides contract sterilization services and related equipment, grew revenue by 6%, but capital equipment sales in this segment dropped 62% compared to a strong prior-year quarter. This decline was offset by a 10% increase in recurring service revenue, resulting in higher operating income for the segment.

Steris’s free cash flow improved significantly, with $983 million generated over the past twelve months. The company used this cash to fund a higher dividend and repurchase $235.5 million in shares, ending the year with a stronger cash position and reduced total debt.

What Steris Does

Steris provides sterilization products, infection prevention solutions, and related services for healthcare providers, life sciences companies, and industrial customers worldwide.
