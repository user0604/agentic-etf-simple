Notable Developments
Branded apparel revenue grew over 81% year over year, rising to 24% of total sales from 15% a year ago.
Gross profit rose alongside stable gross margin, indicating consistent pricing and cost control despite higher sales volume.
Cash and equivalents fell to $15.2 million from $23.6 million three months earlier, reflecting seasonally weak first-quarter cash flow and ongoing investment needs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management remains positive about further growth and profitability, emphasizing progress in its branded apparel transformation and operational discipline. However, it did not publish a quantitative outlook for the remainder of fiscal 2026. The most important area to monitor is whether the company can maintain cash flow stability as it invests in growth.

Quarterly Highlights

Branded apparel, which covers unique clothing designed for occasions and everyday wear, was a standout, making up nearly a quarter of total revenue. This segment expanded rapidly and became a main driver of the company’s strategic shift into higher-margin branded goods.

Gross margin held nearly flat compared to last year, supporting profit growth even as total sales and core operating costs increased. The business managed expenses well in most categories, with general and administrative costs dropping 15%, though selling and marketing spending rose by 12% as customer acquisition efforts accelerated.

The cash balance declined as working capital needs and seasonal patterns weighed on first-quarter flows. While the net shareholders’ deficit improved somewhat, the balance sheet remains a risk point and an area for future monitoring if growth continues.
