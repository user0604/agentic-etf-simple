Key Takeaways
Profitability weakened and revenue per unit declined.
Revenue and adjusted EBITDA both fell compared to the prior-year quarter.
Management is cautiously optimistic for improved profitability in the coming quarter.

Proficient Auto Logistics (NASDAQ:PAL) saw profitability weaken and revenue per unit decline in its latest results. The company is a leading provider of auto transportation and logistics services, operating one of North America's largest fleets. In its quarterly report released May 7, 2026, covering the fiscal first quarter ended March 31, 2026, the company reported weaker operating results and margin compression. Overall, the period reflected slow progress toward efficiency initiatives and increased cost pressures, despite continued integration of acquisitions.

Notable developments
Total operating revenue decreased by 1.6%, while unit volume rose 1.5% compared to the prior year.
Profitability deteriorated, with adjusted operating income swinging to a loss of $3.2 million and the adjusted operating ratio worsening by 4.7 percentage points.
Cost pressures included an 8.3% increase in salaries and a 13.4% rise in fuel costs, while revenue per unit dropped in both major channels.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management noted that demand and fuel recovery trends improved during the last month of the quarter, pointing to “improved performance as the second quarter progresses.” After failing to achieve previously stated improvement in core profitability metrics, the key issue for investors is whether promised operational and margin gains will materialize in the next period.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue fell as growth from the Brothers acquisition was offset by lower revenue per unit and continued softness in the core automotive market. Both company-driver and subhauler delivery channels saw declines in average revenue earned per car transported, despite a shift to more company-owned truck use.

Profitability weakened across critical measures. Adjusted EBITDA dropped more than 40% compared to the prior-year quarter, and the adjusted operating ratio -- an efficiency metric indicating costs as a percentage of revenue -- rose to 103.4%, moving further above 100% and highlighting that operating costs outpaced revenue.

Higher operating costs played a major role. Salaries, benefits, and truck expenses all increased, and fuel expenses were up sharply. Purchased transportation costs fell, reflecting an ongoing strategy to increase the use of internal resources, but these savings were not enough to offset other cost headwinds. The company authorized a $15 million share repurchase program and bought back nearly 83,000 shares during the period.

What Proficient Auto Logistics does

Proficient Auto Logistics specializes in transporting new and used vehicles for car manufacturers, dealers, and rental firms across North America.
