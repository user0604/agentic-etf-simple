Earnings Preview: Mastech Digital

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Mastech Digital (NYSEMKT:MHH), a provider of IT staffing and data analytics services for large and mid-sized organizations, is expected to report its first quarter 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The fourth quarter of 2025 was marked by ongoing market caution and reduced technology spending among clients. Mastech Digital reported consolidated revenue of $45.5 million, representing a 10.4% decrease year over year. The IT Staffing Services segment generated $37.7 million in revenue, down 7% from the prior year, while the Data and Analytics Services segment reported $7.8 million, a 24% decrease. Despite these declines, the company achieved record average bill rates and maintained stable gross margins. GAAP net income was $1 million ($0.08 per diluted share), up from $0.3 million in the prior year, while non-GAAP net income was $2.5 million ($0.21 per diluted share), slightly below the previous year's $2.8 million ($0.23 per diluted share). These results met analyst expectations for the quarter.

Positives
Record bill rates in staffing segment: The average bill rate reached $87.32 per hour, reflecting successful pricing discipline and a focus on higher-value client work.
Improved bookings activity: Fourth-quarter bookings rose nearly 37% year over year to $11.3 million, driven by strong client renewals, suggesting continued customer confidence.
Completion of cost optimization initiatives: The transition of finance and accounting functions to India was completed, with no further transition costs expected, positioning the company for future cost savings.
Solid cash position and new share repurchase program: The company ended the year with $36.5 million in cash and no debt, and authorized a new $5 million share repurchase program effective February 16, 2026.
Negatives
Significant revenue decline in Data and Analytics Services: Segment revenue fell 24% year over year, mainly due to the conclusion of several large projects and a challenging comparison to the prior year.
Consultant headcount reduction: The IT Staffing Services segment saw a 16.7% drop in billable consultants, reflecting both client in-sourcing and the company’s focus on higher-margin business.
Ongoing client concentration risk: The top ten clients accounted for 58% of total revenue, increasing exposure to changes in spending by a small number of customers.
Elevated SG&A expenses: Selling, general, and administrative costs rose as a percentage of revenue, partly due to higher executive compensation and transition-related expenses.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Segment realignment and reporting changes: The company plans to shift from reporting Data and Analytics and IT Staffing Services segments to new Talent and Services segments, reflecting a more integrated approach. Investors should look for details on how this impacts transparency and comparability.
Impact of EDGE initiative on margins: With cost optimization measures completed, watch for evidence of improved profitability and margin expansion beginning in 2026, especially as the company reinvests in growth areas.
AI and analytics investments: Management has announced increased investments in AI-powered solutions and industry-focused leadership. Progress in these areas and any early client wins or revenue contributions will be important.
Client and industry diversification: The company is targeting new business in health sciences, financial services, and retail. Updates on client wins or progress in reducing revenue concentration will be key.
Billable consultant trends and bill rates: Following the Q4 headcount drop and record bill rates, investors should monitor whether consultant numbers stabilize and if high bill rates can be sustained or expanded in Q1 2026.
