MediaCo (NASDAQ:MDIA) posted solid revenue gains in its latest quarter, driven by growth in digital advertising and audio platforms. The leading multicultural media and entertainment company operates across digital audio, radio, television, and outdoor advertising in the United States. Business Overview
Digital advertising revenue made up 49.5% of total advertising sales, supporting overall revenue growth.
Operating expenses increased 19% year over year, outpacing sales growth and reducing profitability.
EstrellaTV, a Spanish-language broadcast network, saw a 38% year-over-year increase in its prime-time P18-49 viewership.
Quarterly Performance

Digital and audio platforms became a larger part of the business, with nearly half of total advertising revenue now coming from digital channels. The company launched InterWave, a digital audio network reaching 210 million monthly listeners in partnership with Sigma Audio Networks, and continued to invest in its New York City radio stations HOT 97 and WBLS-FM.

Rising costs were a central challenge. Operating expenses rose faster than revenues, including higher losses on asset disposals and increased interest expenses, which together led to a sharp decline in adjusted EBITDA compared to the previous year.

Original programming performed well, especially at EstrellaTV, which posted four consecutive quarters of audience growth and was the only U.S. Spanish-language broadcaster to report positive ratings growth in March. The company also expanded live sports content across TV, streaming, and digital platforms.

Looking Ahead

Management is optimistic about digital expansion and new content channels but did not provide financial guidance for the next quarter. It plans to continue investing in digital and multicultural programming while aiming to manage expenses more effectively. The key area to watch is whether the shift to digital revenue can be matched by stronger cost discipline.

What MediaCo Does

MediaCo operates multicultural radio, television, digital media, and outdoor advertising properties across the United States, focusing on both digital and legacy platforms.
