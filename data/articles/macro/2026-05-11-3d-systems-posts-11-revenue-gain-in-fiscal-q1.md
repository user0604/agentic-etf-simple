Key Takeaways
3D Systems achieved positive Adjusted EBITDA and improved gross margins, but near-term profit guidance remains weak.
Revenue rose year over year.
Management expects a temporary dip in second quarter profitability before aiming for full-year break-even results.

3D printer maker 3D Systems (NYSE:DDD) made tangible progress on its turnaround efforts as it achieved positive Adjusted EBITDA and improved gross margins. The leading provider of additive manufacturing solutions posted revenue and Adjusted EBITDA that surpassed its stated targets for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (reported May 11, 2026). Overall, the quarter featured core business recovery in Healthcare and Aerospace & Defense, though management signaled that a full, sustainable rebound is still developing.

Notable Developments
Healthcare Solutions achieved strong double-digit growth, with revenue up 21% to $50.1 million, now nearly matching the Industrial segment in size.
Profitability improved with non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA turning positive at $2.1 million, a swing from a loss of $26.1 million a year earlier, reflecting tight cost control.
Management expects a temporary pullback in adjusted profitability in the upcoming quarter, guiding for a near break-even full-year result but warning of headwinds in Industrial markets outside Aerospace & Defense.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management has projected second quarter revenue to be similar to the first, but it expects adjusted EBITDA (a profit measure excluding some non-cash items) to turn negative again before targeting break-even for the full year. The key point for investors will be whether margin gains and Healthcare strength can outweigh ongoing challenges in the rest of the Industrial segment as the year unfolds.

Quarterly Highlights

The Healthcare Solutions segment led the period, with revenue climbing 21% year over year on the back of adoption of new dental and medical 3D printing systems. This brought Healthcare nearly to parity with Industrial Solutions as the company's two main end markets.

Industrial Solutions revenue, after adjusting for divested businesses, increased modestly, but still faces uneven demand except for Aerospace & Defense, which grew at a healthy double-digit rate. The company's product mix shifted toward more hardware solutions, which generally produce lower margins per sale.

Gross margin improved to 36.1% on a non-GAAP basis due to strategic cost reductions and a focus on higher-value products. Operating expenses dropped sharply by more than 40% from a year earlier as the company completed major cost-saving initiatives and leaned its R&D spending after a recent cycle of product launches.

What 3D Systems Does

3D Systems provides 3D printing hardware, software, materials, and services to customers in Healthcare and Industrial markets worldwide.
