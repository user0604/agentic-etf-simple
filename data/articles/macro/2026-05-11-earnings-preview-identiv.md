Earnings Preview: Identiv

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 1:03 PM

Comments

Identiv (NASDAQ:INVE), a provider of specialty radio-frequency identification (RFID) and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) tags and labels for industries such as healthcare, logistics, and smart packaging, is expected to report its first quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, May 13, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025, Identiv reported revenue of $6.2 million, which exceeded its previously announced guidance. The company’s gross profit margin improved significantly following the completed transition of all manufacturing from Singapore to its new facility in Thailand. This move lowered costs and increased efficiency. The company’s net loss from continuing operations narrowed compared to the same period the previous year, as cost reductions from shutting down Singapore operations took effect. Identiv ended the quarter with a strong cash position, reporting $128.9 million in cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash.

Positives
Revenue exceeded guidance: Fourth-quarter sales came in above management’s expectations, reflecting early benefits from operational changes and new customer contributions.
Gross margin improvement: Non-GAAP gross margin rose to 25.6% from negative 5.2% a year ago, driven by the completed shift to Thailand manufacturing and the exit from low-margin business.
Cost structure improvements: The closure of the Singapore facility and full transition to Thailand resulted in lower direct labor and overhead costs, helping to reduce the net loss from continuing operations.
Strong liquidity position: The company exited Q4 with $128.9 million in cash, supported by an income tax refund and a prepayment from a new customer for 2026 volumes.
Negatives
Year-over-year revenue decline: Revenue decreased compared to Q4 2024, mainly due to the strategic exit from lower-margin business and reduced sales to a major customer managing inventory built up during the production transition.
Continued operating losses: Despite cost improvements, Identiv reported a GAAP net loss from continuing operations of $3.7 million for the quarter, and adjusted EBITDA remained negative.
Gross margin variability ahead: Management noted that gross margins could be volatile in the near term as the company ramps up production for large new programs, including the IFCO partnership.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Impact of new customer prepayment and early volume: Investors should look for how the $2.8 million prepayment and full-year order from a new customer in Q1 affects reported revenue and cash flow, as this could create a one-time boost in the quarter.
Progress on IFCO BLE smart label program: The status of the development phase and any updates on the timeline for mass production ramp, expected later in 2026, will be important. Watch for commentary on pilot results and customer feedback.
Gross margin trends as production scales: Management has guided for near-term variability in gross margins as large programs ramp. Investors should monitor whether margins remain stable or fluctuate as expected, and what this means for long-term profitability.
Pipeline conversion and new product development: Updates on the number of new customer opportunities converted to sales and progress on active new product development projects, especially those targeting higher-margin applications, will provide insight into future growth potential.
Cash usage and capital allocation: With guidance for $14 million to $16 million in cash usage for 2026 (excluding strategic review costs), investors should track operating cash flow, capital expenditures related to IFCO, and any changes in working capital needs.
