Key Takeaways
Aytu BioPharma reported a loss as its legacy ADHD and pediatric businesses shrank faster than new drug gains.
Earnings swung to a loss with diluted EPS at $(0.53), down sharply from $0.21 the previous year.
Management emphasized the early momentum of new antidepressant EXXUA but gave no updated timeline for profitability.

Aytu BioPharma (NASDAQ:AYTU) faced a setback as diminished sales from older ADHD and pediatric products outweighed initial gains from its newly launched antidepressant. The specialty pharmaceutical company, focused on treatments for central nervous system and pediatric disorders, is betting on EXXUA to drive future growth. In its earnings release on May 13, 2026, covering the third quarter of fiscal 2026, the company revealed that legacy business declines contributed to a substantial quarterly loss. Overall, the quarter demonstrated both the promise of the new drug launch and the financial strain of rapid transition.

Notable Developments
EXXUA, a novel antidepressant, generated $2.4 million in its first quarter of full commercial launch, with prescriptions from more than 450 prescribers.
Legacy ADHD revenue fell to $9.1 million, reflecting both generic competition and a shift in sales focus toward new products.
Gross margin slid to 61%, partly due to lower ADHD sales and a $0.7 million inventory write-down as part of the product transition.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management called out early momentum for EXXUA, highlighting prescription growth and prescriber adoption, but provided no specific revenue or profit guidance for upcoming quarters. The company will continue focusing on its new product launch while seeking to stabilize its cash position amid ongoing losses. Investors should watch how quickly EXXUA's uptake accelerates and if it can compensate for falling ADHD and pediatric revenues.

Quarterly Highlights

EXXUA, an antidepressant for major depressive disorder, contributed new revenue and expanded its prescriber base rapidly, with over 1,300 prescriptions written by more than 450 physicians during the quarter. Management noted that while net revenue from EXXUA was modest at this stage, its market entry performance met internal expectations for the initial phase.

Revenue from the ADHD portfolio, which includes extended-release orally disintegrating tablets, declined sharply year over year, driven by both the emergence of generic competition and a deliberate sales team shift toward supporting EXXUA. Management still described the portfolio as profitable on a standalone basis but acknowledged its shrinking share of the business.

The pediatric product line, a group of legacy treatments, also saw accelerated revenue declines as changes in payor coverage and increased returns depressed results. This segment's contraction added to the company's overall revenue and gross margin pressures during the quarter.

What Aytu BioPharma Does

Aytu BioPharma develops and markets prescription medicines for central nervous system conditions, including major depressive disorder and attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, as well as legacy pediatric products.
