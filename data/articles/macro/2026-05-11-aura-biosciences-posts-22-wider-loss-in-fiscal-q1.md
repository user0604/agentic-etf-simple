Management reiterated its goal to complete enrollment of the pivotal Phase 3 CoMpass trial by mid-2026, with key results expected in the second half of 2027. Quarterly highlights

The phase 3 CoMpass study of bel-sar, Aura’s lead virus-like drug conjugate treatment for early choroidal melanoma, kept on pace with guidance toward enrollment completion and future data readouts. Bel-sar represents a targeted approach designed to treat tumors in the eye, aiming to preserve vision in patients who currently face loss of sight with standard therapies.

Research and development spending climbed, reflecting broader activity across additional programs in bladder cancer and ocular surface cancers. These indications remain on track for data updates in 2026, supported largely by increased trial costs and regulatory activities.

On the financial front, the firm bolstered its balance sheet through a major equity financing round completed after quarter end, with a portion of the funds used to repurchase shares from a large early investor. This move extends Aura's cash runway, reducing risk during late-stage clinical execution.

What Aura Biosciences does

Aura Biosciences develops targeted cancer therapies using proprietary virus-like drug conjugates, with a focus on rare ophthalmic and bladder cancers.
