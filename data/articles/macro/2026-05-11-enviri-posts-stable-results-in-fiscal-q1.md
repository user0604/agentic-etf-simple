Enviri (NYSE:NVRI) delivered steady results in a quarter marked by ongoing restructuring and an impending asset sale. The diversified environmental services and industrial solutions provider reported earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026. Overall, the period reflected stability in its core business, even as margin pressures and losses in Rail lingered.

Notable developments
Revenue was stable at $550 million, with Harsco Environmental up 6%, and Clean Earth down 4% before its planned divestiture.
Adjusted EBITDA decreased 8.5%, mainly on softer volumes in Rail and Clean Earth, while margin in Harsco Environmental slipped to 15.0%.
Cash flow improved: operating cash flow rose to $22 million, and adjusted free cash flow narrowed to $(6) million, supported by better working capital.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its 2026 guidance for both Harsco Environmental and Harsco Rail, including proforma EBITDA of approximately $140 million for the soon-to-launch "New Enviri." The team expects improved cash generation after the Clean Earth sale. Investors should closely monitor execution on the divestiture, and further efforts to offset margin pressure in Environmental and losses in Rail.

Quarterly highlights

Harsco Environmental, which provides long-term service contracts for steel and metals manufacturers, grew segment revenue by 6%, aided by favorable foreign currency movements and stronger demand at existing sites. However, segment margin fell to 15.0% from 16.2% a year ago, attributed to a less favorable mix and lower volume in value-added eco-products.

The Clean Earth business, which handles specialty waste processing and treatment for hazardous and non-hazardous materials, reported lower revenues as customer volumes dipped and project activity slowed due to weather. The company expects to close the Clean Earth sale early in the next quarter, which will reshape its business profile.

Harsco Rail, which makes equipment and provides aftermarket services for railroad maintenance, continued to weigh on results with a segment operating loss amid "manufacturing inefficiencies" and weak demand. Still, the segment’s adjusted EBITDA loss improved slightly year over year.

What Enviri does

Enviri delivers environmental services and resource recovery solutions to steel, metals, and industrial clients, along with waste processing and industrial equipment offerings.
