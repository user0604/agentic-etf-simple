Key Takeaways
Achieved a business win with significant growth in lead product sales and turned a positive gross margin. Revenue rose year over year. Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue outlook and stated its cash runway extends into late 2027.

Autolus Therapeutics (NASDAQ:AUTL) advanced its transition to commercial-stage operations, reporting notable growth in its first marketed CAR T cell therapy and achieving a positive gross margin for its acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL) business. The cell therapy developer focuses on engineered T cell medicines for cancer and autoimmune diseases. Reporting results for the first quarter (Q1 2026) on May 14, 2026, Autolus signaled ongoing momentum but persistent operating losses.

Overall, the quarter reflected major commercial progress driven by its lead product, balanced against continued high costs.

Notable Developments
Net product revenue rose 191% year over year, propelled by U.S. market penetration and a new U.K. launch for AUCATZYL, the company’s CAR T cell therapy for adult ALL.
Gross profit turned positive at $1.6 million, marking the business’s first move above break-even for ALL but with a gross margin of just 6% as high costs continue.
Operating expenses reflected both lower research and development costs (down 21%) and a 35% increase in selling, general, and administrative spending due to commercial and restructuring activities.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed 2026 guidance for AUCATZYL net product revenue of $120 million to $135 million and expects ongoing positive gross margins for the ALL business. The most critical factor for investors now is whether Autolus can deliver sequential revenue growth to meet its full-year target and continue improving profitability as it scales.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s key product, AUCATZYL, a CAR T cell therapy for adult relapsed or refractory B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia, drove revenue growth. Uptake in the U.S. remained strong, and the U.K. market launch in January 2026 added new momentum.

While gross profit turned positive, at just $1.6 million on $26.2 million in sales, cost of sales remained high and will require continued volume ramp and cost control to support future profitability. High operating losses persisted and quarterly cash burn reached $71.3 million, though the company maintained a reported cash runway into the fourth quarter of 2027.

Research and development spending fell as certain clinical trials advanced out of earlier phases, while selling, general, and administrative costs increased driven by expanded commercial operations and one-time restructuring charges associated with a 13% workforce reduction.

What Autolus Therapeutics Plc Does

Autolus Therapeutics Plc develops and commercializes engineered CAR T cell therapies for challenging cancers and autoimmune diseases, with a current focus on acute lymphoblastic leukemia and a pipeline targeting additional indications.
