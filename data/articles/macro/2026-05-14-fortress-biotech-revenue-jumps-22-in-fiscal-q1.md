Notable Developments
The $158.9 million gain from the sale of a priority review voucher drove net income, shifting from a loss the previous year to a profit of $108.4 million.
Revenue increased to $16.0 million, up 22% year over year, mainly from subsidiary Journey Medical’s dermatology product portfolio.
Operating expenses declined by 33% compared to the prior year, reflecting reduced research and development and selling, general, and administrative costs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlights expected growth in royalty revenue from recent product commercializations and the possibility of future milestone payments. The company is focused on advancing its pipeline and maximizing returns from recent acquisitions and partnerships. Investors should monitor the ramp-up of recurring royalty and product revenue as a measure of ongoing business momentum.

Quarterly Highlights

The sale of a regulatory asset known as a priority review voucher generated a large, non-recurring financial gain, driving the company’s net income for the quarter. A priority review voucher allows faster review of a drug candidate by the FDA, and companies often sell these to larger pharmaceutical firms to generate cash. This distinct event led to a cash position of $255.8 million at quarter-end and a reduction in long-term debt to $15.0 million.

Commercial revenues grew as Journey Medical, Fortress’s dermatology-focused subsidiary, expanded the footprint of Emrosi (a prescription medication for rosacea). Product revenue from Journey Medical comprised the bulk of the reported top line, and Emrosi achieved wider coverage with more than 85% of U.S. commercial insurance plans.

Fortress advanced its business model of generating value through asset sales and royalties. Recent pipeline moves included the launch of UNLOXCYT (an immune therapy for skin cancer), acquisition of new product rights for Pompe disease, and expansion of its royalty streams through partnerships with industry players like Sun Pharma and Duke University.

What Fortress Biotech Does

Fortress Biotech acquires, develops, and commercializes innovative drugs for rare diseases and specialty markets, largely through subsidiaries and strategic partnerships.
