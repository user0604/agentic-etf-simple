Republic Services Posts 7.6% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:35 PM

Key Takeaways
Disciplined pricing and cost controls supported stronger profit margins, but revenue growth was modest.
Adjusted earnings per share rose 7.6%, with revenue up 2.6% year over year.
Management reaffirmed full-year guidance and is watching for stronger volumes and improvements in recycling prices.

Republic Services (NYSE:RSG) delivered higher profit margins thanks to disciplined pricing and cost controls, even as revenue growth remained modest. The leading U.S. recycling and waste management services provider benefited from effective pricing and expense management, while its Environmental Solutions segment continued to face pressure. This update covers results for the first fiscal quarter, released May 7, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed steady progress on margins and cash generation, with growth in core waste operations offsetting headwinds from recycling commodity prices and Environmental Solutions.

EBITDA stands for earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, a metric often used to gauge operating profitability.

Notable developments
Pricing gains drove a 4.3% increase in adjusted EBITDA, and margin improved by 0.5 percentage points.
Environmental Solutions revenue fell 9.8%, with segment profit margin down to 19.2% from 20.8% year over year.
Cash flow from operations rose to $1.23 billion, and adjusted free cash flow increased by more than a third on strong expense discipline.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its 2026 guidance, which calls for revenue growth of approximately 3.1% at the midpoint, adjusted EBITDA margin near 32%, and $1 billion in acquisitions. The company expects Environmental Solutions to improve later in the year and continues to invest in sustainability projects. Investors should watch for improvement in volumes and prices for recycled commodities, which have recently declined.

Quarterly highlights

Recycling and waste revenue, which forms the bulk of the business, grew 4.2% to $3.71 billion. This segment benefited from higher prices, even though overall volumes slipped, with residential volumes declining 5.2%. Profit margins in this segment improved to 33.6%, up from 33.0% a year ago.

Environmental Solutions delivered $405 million in revenue, down about 10%, as expected due to fewer emergency response projects. This segment's margin also contracted, reflecting ongoing softness, though company executives expect a recovery later in the year.

Capital allocation remained a focus: $433 million was invested in acquisitions in the period and $507 million was returned to shareholders, with the quarterly dividend raised 8% to $0.625 per share.

What Republic Services does

Republic Services operates a nationwide network of collection, recycling, landfill, and environmental services, providing waste and recycling solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial customers.
