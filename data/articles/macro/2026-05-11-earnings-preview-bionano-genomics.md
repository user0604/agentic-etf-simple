Earnings Preview: Bionano Genomics

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 7:03 PM

Comments

Bionano Genomics (NASDAQ:BNGO), a maker of optical genome mapping (OGM) systems and software for genetic analysis, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Bionano Genomics reported fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of $8.0 million, which was at the high end of its preannounced range but represented a 3% decrease year over year. The company sold 7,554 OGM flow cells in the quarter, down 6% from a year ago, mainly due to supply constraints late in the quarter. Non-GAAP gross margin improved to 43% from 42% in the prior year, and non-GAAP operating expenses dropped 9% year over year. For the full year, revenue was $28.5 million, down 7%, but core revenue excluding discontinued clinical services was down just 2%. Bionano exceeded its OGM system installation guidance, placing 32 new systems in 2025. The company ended the year with $29.6 million in cash, cash equivalents, and available-for-sale securities, and expects its cash runway to extend into 2027. These results met the high end of company guidance but missed analyst consensus for sales, while EPS was not specified for Q4 in available sources.

Positives
Gross margin expansion shows improved efficiency: Non-GAAP gross margin rose to 47% for the full year, up 1,200 basis points, reflecting cost-cutting and a shift toward higher-margin consumables and software.
Operating expenses fell sharply: Non-GAAP operating expenses decreased 47% for the year, highlighting the impact of restructuring and a more focused commercial strategy.
Consumables and software revenue growth: Combined sales of consumables and software grew 7% year over year and now make up 71% of total revenue, signaling a more predictable and profitable revenue mix.
Routine-use customer base drives recurring revenue: About 40% of customers are now routine users, accounting for 83% of consumables revenue, which supports future growth and stability.
Negatives
Overall revenue declined year over year: Total revenue fell 7% in 2025, mainly due to discontinued clinical services and lower instrument sales, reflecting the transition in business strategy.
Supply chain constraints limited Q4 growth: Delays from a manufacturing partner led to lower flow cell sales in Q4, creating a backlog but also highlighting ongoing supply risks.
Cash runway remains a concern: Although the company expects its cash to last into 2027, the 10-K notes substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern without additional capital in the near term.
Asia Pacific business faces headwinds: Slowdowns in the Asia Pacific region due to funding challenges continue to impact growth in that geography.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Revenue recovery and backlog realization: Investors should look for signs that supply constraints have eased, allowing Bionano to fulfill the backlog of consumables orders and meet the company’s Q1 revenue guidance of $6.5 million to $6.7 million (analyst consensus: $6.57 million).
Consumables and software mix: Watch for continued growth in consumables and software sales as a percentage of total revenue, which supports gross margin expansion and recurring revenue.
Routine-use customer conversion and validation: Progress in converting more routine-use customers to validated protocol users could drive higher average revenue per customer and support long-term growth.
Cash position and capital needs: The company’s ability to manage cash burn and secure new funding will be critical, given ongoing concerns about liquidity and the need for additional capital as disclosed in the 10-K.
Impact of new reimbursement rates and publications: The effect of the 47% increase in the reimbursement rate for OGM hematologic malignancies and recent high-profile publications should be monitored for their influence on adoption and revenue growth in 2026.
