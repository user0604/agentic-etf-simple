Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects second-quarter revenue of approximately $2.8 million, a rise of 65% from the previous year’s period, and expects to reach profitability in the first half of fiscal 2026. Investors should monitor whether large contract renewals continue and whether the company achieves its stated goal of turning a profit in the coming months.

Quarterly Highlights

The renewal of a one-year biometric identity license with a major foreign bank provided $1.04 million in revenue, underscoring the importance of recurring contracts for the business. In addition, the company secured $450,000 in follow-on defense sector orders and expanded its presence in government markets with new agreements in Portugal and Mozambique. Taken together, these new wins helped drive revenue up 34% and set a strong foundation for upcoming quarters.

Gross margin remained at 82%, supported by a favorable mix of high-margin software renewals and stronger biometric hardware sales. Lower operating costs also contributed, with selling, general, and administrative expenses down $102,000, even as the company modestly increased investment in product development and personnel.

Despite these operational improvements, the company continues to face some risks. Its stock was recently delisted from Nasdaq and now trades over-the-counter, while revenue still depends heavily on several large customers and contract renewals. Cash resources remain sufficient for immediate requirements, but ongoing performance will depend on steady inflow from future deals and potential capital market moves.
