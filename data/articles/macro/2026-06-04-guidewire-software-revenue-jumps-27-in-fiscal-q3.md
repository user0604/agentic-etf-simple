Key Takeaways

Guidewire Software (NYSE:GWRE) advanced its transformation, with cloud product adoption boosting both growth and profitability. The leading provider of software solutions for property and casualty (P&C) insurers saw growing customer engagement for its cloud-based InsuranceSuite and expanding AI capabilities. On June 4, 2026, it reported results for the third quarter ended April 30, highlighting momentum across major financial and operational metrics. Overall, sustained demand for its subscription and services offerings contributed to a robust quarter and prompted a raised outlook for fiscal 2026.

Notable Developments
Cloud subscription and support revenue rose, now accounting for approximately 66% of total revenue in Q3 FY2026, emphasizing the success of the company’s cloud migration strategy.
Non-GAAP operating income surged almost 69% year over year, reflecting disciplined cost management along with robust topline growth.
Free cash flow nearly doubled as the company benefited from strong collections and lower capital spending, despite increased stock buybacks reducing cash holdings.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year outlook for total revenue, non-GAAP operating income, and cash flow as a result of outperforming its own Q3 guidance. It highlighted broad-based strength across cloud, services, and AI product offerings, with ARR growth remaining in line with its 18–19% annual target. Investors should keep an eye on the ongoing mix shift toward cloud and services, services margin performance, and any continued foreign exchange volatility impacting net income.

Quarterly Highlights

Cloud momentum accelerated, with subscription and support revenue climbing 35% year over year. This reflects customers migrating mission-critical insurance operations to Guidewire’s cloud platform, a key focus for long-term growth.

Profitability improved sharply, as non-GAAP operating income jumped to $77.8 million. The company increased investment in research and development, but robust revenue growth more than offset higher spending.

Guidewire continued its capital return plan, repurchasing $244 million of its own shares during the quarter. This reduced total cash on hand, with $1.15 billion in cash and investments remaining at the end of April.
