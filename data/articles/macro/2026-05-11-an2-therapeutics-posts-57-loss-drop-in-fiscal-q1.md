Notable Developments
Clinical pipeline made steady progress: three major programs advanced, each on schedule for key milestones, but no efficacy or safety data readouts yet.
Net loss narrowed by 5.7%, driven by a 13.0% reduction in research and development expenses, while general and administrative costs stayed flat year over year.
The company raised $40.0 million through a private placement in March 2026, resulting in a cash position that management believes funds operations into 2029.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects to initiate three Phase 2 studies in 2026 and plans to share early clinical data starting late in the year. Its operational plan remains unchanged, and the recently raised cash supports research activities into 2029. Investors should pay most attention to upcoming data readouts from these studies, as there is still no commercial product or revenue in place.

Quarterly Highlights

The company is pushing forward with its drug development pipeline, initiating a Phase 2 trial for epetraborole in polycythemia vera, patient enrollment for M. abscessus lung disease, and completing Phase 1 dosing for AN2-502998 in Chagas disease. These steps adhere to previously stated timelines and demonstrate continued focus on rare conditions lacking effective treatments.

Cost management contributed to a lower net loss for the period. Research and development spending fell 13.0% compared to the prior year due to timing of expenses across programs, while general and administrative expenses held steady. Management cited decreased manufacturing and licensing costs as the main reason for the improvement.

The March 2026 financing lifted cash and investments to $85.3 million at quarter-end, up from $60.0 million at December 31, 2025. This strengthens the balance sheet and gives management flexibility, but with all programs pre-commercial, the timeline for actual product launches remains uncertain.

What An2 Therapeutics Does

An2 Therapeutics develops new therapies based on boron chemistry for infectious diseases and select cancers, with a focus on unmet medical needs and rare conditions.
