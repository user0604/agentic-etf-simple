Earnings Preview: Movado Group

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Movado Group (NYSE:MOV), which designs and sells watches and jewelry worldwide, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Movado Group reported net sales of $191.6 million, representing a 5.6% increase year over year. Adjusted operating income grew 6.2% to $14.4 million. Net income was $13.0 million, or $0.57 per diluted share, up from $11.5 million, or $0.51 per diluted share, in the same period last year. These results beat analyst expectations for both revenue and earnings. U.S. sales were especially strong, rising 11.2%, while international sales increased 1% due to mixed results across regions. Gross margin held nearly flat at 54.1% despite elevated tariffs. The company ended the year with $230.5 million in cash and no debt, providing significant financial flexibility.

Positives
Strong U.S. sales growth: U.S. net sales rose 11.2%, driven mainly by higher demand and increased volume, signaling renewed consumer interest in fashion and accessible luxury watches.
Solid performance in company stores: Company stores delivered a 9% sales increase in the fourth quarter, highlighting effective merchandising and improved in-store execution during the holiday season.
Success in brand innovation: The Movado brand saw wholesale sales grow over 25% and e-commerce up 18%, reflecting the impact of recent brand refresh initiatives and new product designs.
Strong cash position and no debt: Movado ended the year with $230.5 million in cash and no outstanding debt, which supports future investments and shareholder returns.
Negatives
Tariff pressures on margins: Elevated U.S. tariffs reduced gross margin by about 180 basis points in the fourth quarter, and the company expects ongoing volatility in tariff rates.
International sales growth lagged: International net sales increased only 1%, with constant currency sales actually declining 5.9%, reflecting weaker demand in the Middle East and parts of Asia.
Higher operating expenses: Operating expenses increased to $89.3 million, mainly due to higher performance-based compensation, partially offset by lower marketing spend.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Impact of ongoing tariffs and trade policy: Monitor how the new 10% Section 122 tariffs and potential increases to 15% are affecting costs, pricing, and gross margins. The company has not recognized any receivable for possible tariff refunds, so any developments here could materially affect results.
Performance of new product launches: Watch for early sales trends of newly introduced collections, such as the Valeura women’s Museum watch, Verso S, and the expanded jewelry lines. These launches are part of Movado’s strategy to attract younger and female consumers.
Progress on cost-savings initiative: The company’s cost-savings plan, announced and underway since fiscal 2025, is expected to deliver about $10 million in annual savings. Look for updates on realized savings and any additional restructuring charges.
Trends in U.S. versus international sales: Investors should track whether the strong U.S. momentum continues and if international sales, especially in the Middle East and Asia, show signs of recovery or further weakness.
Share repurchases and capital allocation: Movado has $46.1 million remaining under its authorized buyback program. With a strong cash balance and no debt, any acceleration in share repurchases or changes to the dividend could be important for shareholders.
