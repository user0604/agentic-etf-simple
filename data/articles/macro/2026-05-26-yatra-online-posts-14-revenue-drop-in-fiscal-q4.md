Yatra Online (NASDAQ:YTRA) posted a mixed set of results as strength in its Air Ticketing segment could not offset a sharp downturn in Hotels and Packages. The leading online travel agency and corporate travel services platform for India delivered these results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, as announced on May 22, 2026. Overall, the period was marked by progress in some areas but weakness in others as external disruptions weighed heavily.

Notable Developments
Revenue dropped to $20.1 million, down 13.7% from last year, mainly due to sharp declines in Hotels and Packages.
Adjusted margin in the Air Ticketing segment rose 27.3%, helped by higher gross bookings and improved pricing discipline.
Cash and equivalents at period end totaled $26.8 million, while borrowings rose to $7.6 million, showing increased capital needs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management acknowledged turbulent conditions and a lack of clear visibility, opting not to provide forward financial guidance for fiscal 2027. It continues to focus on scaling the high-margin Hotels business, expanding technology through artificial intelligence solutions, and exploring restructuring options. Investors should watch for updates on Hotels and Packages performance, cost control, and changes to the company’s business model or structure.

Quarterly Highlights

The Air Ticketing business remained a bright spot, with non-IFRS adjusted margin reaching $12.6 million for the period, thanks to strong gross bookings and an increased number of air passengers booked.

Gross bookings across all segments climbed to $215.4 million, highlighting steady demand even as the broader travel market faced challenges from international route disruptions.

The Hotels and Packages segment showed significant revenue decline compared to the prior year, as disruptions in the Middle East affected international travel demand. Despite this, adjusted margin for Hotels and Packages nudged higher, reflecting improved cost management or changes in booking mix such as greater focus on higher-margin hotel room nights.

The company onboarded 55 new corporate clients in the quarter, increasing potential annual billings by nearly $29 million. This adds to Yatra's large base of over 1,300 corporate accounts and 59,000 small business customers, supporting its multi-segment growth strategy.

What Yatra Online Does

Yatra Online provides online booking services for air tickets, hotels, holiday packages, and corporate travel management, serving retail and business customers in India and abroad.
