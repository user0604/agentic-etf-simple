Prenetics Global (NASDAQ:PRE), a digital health company specializing in diagnostics and consumer health supplements, released first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026, showing a strong revenue surge, primarily thanks to IM8, its all-in-one supplement brand. Overall, the quarter highlighted aggressive top-line growth, ongoing heavy reinvestment, and a growing focus on subscription-based revenue.

Notable developments
IM8, the company’s all-in-one supplement line, contributed $33.8 million in revenue and accounted for 94% of total sales this quarter.
Gross margin rose for IM8, climbing to 64.3% in the period, up from 59.6% in the prior quarter, reflecting improved manufacturing efficiency.
The company repurchased $19 million of its shares since March and reported $147 million in total liquidity as of May, remaining debt-free.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year IM8 revenue guidance to a range of $190 million to $210 million, compared with a prior outlook of $180 million to $200 million. The company expects second quarter revenue between $46 million and $48 million. Investors should monitor the launch of three new IM8 products in late 2026, progress toward profitability, and retention rates as the subscription model expands.

Quarterly highlights

IM8’s revenue climbed nearly six-fold compared to the same quarter last year, with strong international market adoption—over 60% of IM8’s sales came from outside the U.S. This expansion was accompanied by a shift to quarterly subscription plans, leading to higher average order values but a slight decline in total order volume.

Profitability remains a challenge, with gross profit growing but losses from operations and adjusted EBITDA losses increasing year over year as the company invested in marketing and geographic expansion. Management continued to target adjusted EBITDA profitability by the end of 2027, maintaining a high rate of reinvestment in customer acquisition.

The product roadmap for IM8 includes three new stock-keeping units—hydration, creatine, and kids’ gummies—expected to launch in the fourth quarter of 2026, none of which are included in the current annual revenue outlook. Meanwhile, CircleDNA, the company’s genetic testing segment, saw slightly lower revenue and was not highlighted for innovation or growth in this update.
