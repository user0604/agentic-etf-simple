Key Takeaways
Novanta posted strong revenue growth and solid bookings momentum.
Adjusted diluted EPS rose 9.5% compared to the prior year period.

Novanta (NASDAQ:NOVT) achieved broad-based sales growth and ramped up new orders, signaling advancing commercial traction. The leading supplier of mission-critical technology for medical and advanced industrial applications reported first-quarter 2026 results on May 11, 2026. Overall, the period reflected progress on revenue and bookings, but margins remained pressured by higher costs.

Notable developments
Bookings grew by 37%, resulting in a book-to-bill ratio of 1.10 and a greater backlog compared to last year.
Medical Solutions revenue increased 14.7%, outpacing the company, and Automation Enabling Technologies rose 6.6%.
Free cash flow improved 45%, benefiting from disciplined working capital and higher operating inflows.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management guided for second-quarter revenue of $259 million to $264 million, with organic growth expected between 6% and 8%. For the full year, leadership raised the revenue outlook midpoint and reaffirmed profitability targets. Investors should focus on margin improvement efforts as operating expenses and transitional costs remain elevated.

Quarterly highlights

The Automation Enabling Technologies segment, which provides technology for advanced manufacturing and robotics, demonstrated steady growth but experienced a slight decrease in gross margin due to continuing transformation costs.

The Medical Solutions segment, supplying precision equipment and subsystems to medical device makers, saw stronger growth, supported by new product demand in robotics and minimally invasive procedures. This area remains a strategic growth driver for Novanta.

While free cash flow rose sharply, ongoing investments in research and development, new product launches, and regulatory compliance led to higher selling, general, and administrative costs, highlighting the challenge of scaling profit margins in upcoming quarters.

What Novanta does

Novanta designs and manufactures advanced technology systems and subsystems for applications in medical, life sciences, and advanced industrial markets.
