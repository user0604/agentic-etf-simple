Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated its existing cash resources should fund operations into 2029, supporting ongoing investment in its drug pipeline. A focus for investors will be progress in upcoming SEP-631 and SEP-479 clinical studies, as well as the impact of the Chief Medical Officer transition. Tracking future milestone execution and spend levels will be essential as more programs move toward costly later-stage trials.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure reflects the first full period of the Novo Nordisk collaboration, with most of the $26.5 million coming from upfront payment amortization and research services. New milestones under that partnership also began contributing. The company’s legacy Vertex collaboration has now fully wound down, making its financials more dependent on Novo Nordisk and pipeline advances.

Research and development expenses climbed 52.8%, with investment focused on SEP-631, an oral therapy in development for chronic spontaneous urticaria, and SEP-479, an oral treatment for hypoparathyroidism. Both programs hit or maintained clinical milestones in the first quarter, including positive phase 1 data for SEP-631 and the start of a phase 1 trial for SEP-479.

Septerna’s net loss narrowed as growing top-line performance began to balance expanded costs. However, total operating expenditures, including research and administration, topped $39 million. The departure of the Chief Medical Officer, Dr. Jae Kim, introduces an element of management uncertainty as phase 2 and other later-stage studies approach.

What Septerna Does

Septerna develops oral small molecule drug candidates targeting G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs), using its proprietary Native Complex Platform® to enable therapies for diseases in endocrinology, immunology, and metabolic disorders.
