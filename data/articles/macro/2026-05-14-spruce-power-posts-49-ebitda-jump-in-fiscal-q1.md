Quarterly highlights

Spruce Power’s revenue figure showed that recurring cash flows from its residential solar assets held steady, but lacked organic or acquisition-driven expansion that had boosted results in 2025. There was no disclosure of major new customer wins, partnerships, or additional asset purchases this quarter.

Extensive cost management was the main financial theme. Operations and maintenance expenses dropped 70% to $1.2 million, while selling, general and administrative expense fell 21% to $11.6 million. This reduced the company’s net loss to $2.9 million, a sharp improvement from $15.3 million the prior year, and moved both EBITDA and cash flow into positive territory for the period.

Spruce Power paid down $8.2 million in debt during the quarter and finished with $85.6 million in total cash and equivalents ($4.71 per share). The company’s outstanding project-level, non-recourse debt stood at $687.3 million with a 6.2% blended interest rate. No share repurchases or major investments occurred, leaving financial flexibility for future moves.
