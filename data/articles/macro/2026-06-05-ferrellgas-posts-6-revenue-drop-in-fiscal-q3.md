Key Takeaways
The company delivered a mixed quarter, with higher gross profit but sharply lower net earnings due to one-time expenses.
Gross profit grew approximately 1% while revenue fell approximately 6% year over year.
Management expects recent settlement costs will not recur at this level in future periods.

Ferrellgas Partners (OTC:FGPR)’ business showed resilience in the face of tougher conditions, but profits took a hit from exceptional operating costs this quarter. The company is the second-largest propane distributor in the United States, serving customers nationwide through both retail and Blue Rhino tank exchange channels. For the period ended April 30, 2026, reported on June 5, 2026, Ferrellgas Partners posted lower revenue and net earnings but a modest increase in gross profit. Overall, the quarter reflected steady operational progress but significant pressure from non-recurring expenses.

Notable Developments
Gross profit increased slightly even though revenue declined, as the company benefited from margin management and operational efficiencies.
Net earnings dropped 53% due to a one-time rise in operating expenses, mainly from legal settlements totaling $24.7 million.
The company completed its Class B unit conversion, simplifying its capital structure and eliminating future Class B distribution obligations.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintains confidence in finishing the fiscal year strong, emphasizing that settlement expenses that hurt operating profit this quarter are not expected to repeat at this level in the future. It highlights ongoing investments in technology and growth channels, like Blue Rhino and national accounts. Investors should monitor whether operating costs return to normal and if sales volumes recover following unusually warm weather.

Quarterly Highlights

Gross profit edged up by just under 1% from the prior year, securing stability despite revenue falling more than 6%. Revenue declines were linked to lower propane prices and a 1% drop in total gallons sold, reflecting the impact of an unseasonably warm winter in much of the U.S.

The company’s Blue Rhino tank exchange division grew its footprint, adding nearly 1,500 retail locations nationwide -- a 2.4% increase since the previous fiscal year-end. Wholesale propane sales volumes rose by 3%, partially offsetting the declines in retail gallon sales.

On the expenses side, one-time legal settlements caused operating expenses to jump sharply. Management does not expect these costs to recur in future periods, which could set the stage for margin recovery, depending on future weather patterns and commodity price levels.

What Ferrellgas Partners Does

Ferrellgas Partners is a national supplier of propane and related services, distributing fuel to homes, businesses, and through a nationwide cylinder exchange program.
