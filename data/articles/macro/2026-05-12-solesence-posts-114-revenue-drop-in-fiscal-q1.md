Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its focus on operational transformation, expecting recently implemented cost controls and restructuring to support higher profitability later in 2026. It did not update previous financial targets or provide detailed guidance this quarter. Investors should closely monitor gross margin progress toward the targeted 30% floor, as well as any future commentary on revenue growth or large customer orders.

Quarterly Highlights

Gross margin improved to 26% in the period, up from 23% a year ago, as the company made headway on operational efficiency and reduced labor costs through its ongoing strategic initiative. However, the current result falls short of the previously stated 30% gross margin goal set for fiscal 2026.

Revenue dropped over $1.5 million year over year, with management blaming investments in employee training, organizational restructuring, and weaker customer order volumes, especially from mass market channels. This combination contributed to a swing from profitability to a net loss, and negative adjusted EBITDA.

The period saw the commercial launch of two new proprietary skincare technology platforms, WHSPR™ and Chromalüm™, expanding over-the-counter claims and underlining an emphasis on research and development. Additionally, the company reduced its inventory by $1.0 million during the quarter, signaling tighter working capital discipline.
