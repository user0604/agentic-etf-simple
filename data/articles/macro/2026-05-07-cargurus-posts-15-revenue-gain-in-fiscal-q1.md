Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 15%, led by growth in international markets and dealer subscriptions, with international revenue rising approximately 39% year over year.
Management highlighted a $19.7 million impairment charge and increased operating expenses, pushing GAAP net income down 23% even as adjusted EBITDA rose 17%.
The company repurchased $175 million in shares, dropping its cash balance to $72 million—down nearly 62% from the year-ago period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects ongoing revenue growth of 10% to 13% for fiscal 2026, but with adjusted EBITDA margins declining by 1.5 to 2.5 percentage points year over year. The focus remains on AI-driven product launches and platform enhancements for both dealers and consumers. Watch for how increased investment spending and a lower cash reserve impact flexibility and profitability in the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

CarGurus remains the top visited automotive shopping site in the U.S, with its international segment showing outperformance as dealer count and revenue abroad grew significantly faster than in the U.S. Consolidated paying dealers rose 7% year over year, led by international growth of 17% in dealers and 19% in average dealer revenue.

The company continued to prioritize product development and data innovation, with CEO Jason Trevisan stating, "We are embedding data and predictive intelligence more directly into dealer decision-making across inventory, marketing, and lead conversion while transforming the consumer experience with AI-powered solutions. Adjusted EBITDA increased to $80.2 million, topping the high end of management’s guidance, even as operating expenses climbed 25% from a year ago.

Capital returned to shareholders through aggressive buybacks reached $175 million for the quarter, bringing total buybacks since late 2022 to 29% of shares outstanding. However, this reduced cash reserves by over $100 million within three months, which could limit future flexibility if free cash flow does not accelerate.
