Broadcom (NASDAQ:AVGO), a leading supplier of chips and software for data centers and networking, is expected to report its second-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings after the market closes on Wednesday, June 3, 2026.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Broadcom reported record results, with total revenue reaching $19.3 billion, up 29% from a year earlier. This result exceeded both company guidance and analyst consensus, which had anticipated $19.2 billion in revenue. The company’s strong performance was driven by surging demand for artificial intelligence (AI) semiconductors, particularly custom accelerators and networking products. Adjusted EBITDA was $13.1 billion, representing 68% of revenue, and operating income rose to $8.6 billion. The company’s cash and cash equivalents stood at $14.2 billion at quarter-end, with $10.9 billion returned to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during the quarter.

Positives
AI semiconductor revenue growth was exceptional: AI chip sales grew 106% year over year to $8.4 billion, significantly outpacing expectations. This segment was the main driver of overall revenue growth.
Semiconductor solutions segment accelerated: Revenue in this segment rose 52% year over year to $12.5 billion, reflecting strong demand from data center and cloud customers.
Profitability remained high: Gross margin held steady at 68% of revenue, and operating margin increased to 66.4%, showing the company’s ability to scale efficiently as it grows.
Strong shareholder returns and liquidity: The company returned $10.9 billion to shareholders and ended the quarter with $14.2 billion in cash, demonstrating robust financial health.
Negatives
Non-AI semiconductor revenue was flat: Revenue from non-AI chip products remained unchanged year over year at $4.1 billion, which could signal slower growth in legacy markets.
Increased stock-based compensation expense: This expense rose to $2.2 billion in the quarter, up from $1.3 billion a year ago, primarily due to higher-value equity awards. This impacts reported operating expenses and may affect future profitability metrics.
