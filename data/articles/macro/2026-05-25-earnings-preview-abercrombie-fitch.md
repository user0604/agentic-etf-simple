Earnings Preview: Abercrombie & Fitch

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Abercrombie & Fitch (NYSE:ANF), a global retailer focused on casual apparel and accessories for teens and young adults, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Abercrombie & Fitch delivered net sales of $1.67 billion, up 5% year over year. This matched analyst revenue expectations. Earnings per share came in at $3.68, which beat consensus estimates. The company saw balanced growth across its regions, brands, and channels, with both Abercrombie and Hollister brands achieving record fourth-quarter net sales. Operating margin for the quarter was 14.1%, despite significant tariff headwinds. For the full year, net sales reached a record $5.27 billion, with operating margin at 12.5% on an adjusted basis. The company ended the year with $759.5 million in cash and returned $450 million to shareholders through share repurchases.

Positives
Record fourth-quarter sales and EPS beat: Both sales and earnings per share reached new highs, demonstrating strong product acceptance and effective execution during the key holiday period.
Balanced growth across brands and regions: Both Abercrombie and Hollister brands contributed to growth, with Hollister posting its eleventh consecutive quarter of net sales growth and Abercrombie returning to growth in the quarter.
Strong digital and store performance: Digital sales accounted for 44% of total sales, and the company was a net store opener for the fourth consecutive year, expanding its physical presence.
Solid cash generation and shareholder returns: Operating cash flow remained robust, supporting significant share repurchases and ongoing investment in the business.
Negatives
Tariff-related margin pressure: Operating margin declined year over year, primarily due to $90 million in tariff expenses that offset some underlying operational improvements.
Abercrombie brand full-year sales decline: Despite a return to growth in Q4, Abercrombie brand net sales declined 1% for the full year, reflecting earlier softness.
APAC region profitability challenges: The Asia-Pacific segment reported an operating loss and is now under strategic review, with the company evaluating options like partnerships or franchising.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Impact of new ERP system implementation: The company is rolling out a new merchandising software system, which is expected to create a temporary headwind of 1 to 2 percentage points on sales growth and over 100 basis points of operating margin impact in Q1.
Tariff and freight cost dynamics: Management expects tariffs to negatively impact margins by about 290 basis points, or $30 million net of mitigation, but this will be partially offset by a freight cost tailwind of about 160 basis points.
Sales growth across brands and regions: The company has guided to Q1 net sales growth of 1% to 3% from the prior year's $1.1 billion, with growth expected across both Abercrombie and Hollister brands. Watch for updates on consumer response to spring product and price increases.
Progress on APAC strategic review: The company has announced a review of its Asia-Pacific operations, considering options such as partnerships or licensing to improve profitability. Any updates or decisions could affect long-term regional strategy.
Inventory management and promotional activity: Inventory units are up modestly, largely due to preparations for the ERP rollout. Watch for commentary on inventory turns and promotional intensity, as these will impact gross margin and sales momentum.
