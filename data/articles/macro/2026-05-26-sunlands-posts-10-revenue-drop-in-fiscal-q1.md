Key Takeaways
Sunlands Technology Group delivered another profitable quarter but faced sharply lower revenue and enrollments. Net income margin improved 2 percentage points year over year. Management guides for a 20% to 24% revenue decline next quarter.

Online educator Sunlands Technology Group (NYSE:STG) maintained profitability despite lower student intake and shrinking revenue. The provider of adult online education programs in China continued to cut costs, but new student enrollments and gross billings fell steeply in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The company released its results on May 26, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed strong cost management but deepening top-line pressure that management expects to accelerate.

Notable Developments
Net revenue fell 9.6%, while gross billings dropped a sharper 26.1%, suggesting a major slowdown in new business.
Student enrollments plunged 39.6% to 102,127 for the period, as the company focused on “quality over quantity.”
Net income margin increased by 2 percentage points to 17.4%, aided by a 19.5% cut in sales and marketing costs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects revenue for the next quarter to fall between RMB410 million and RMB430 million, which would be a decline of 20.2% to 23.9% compared to the same quarter last year. Leadership attributes this cautious outlook to persistent market and demand uncertainty. Investors should watch closely for further signs of declining gross billings or enrollments, which would shape near-term revenue potential.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure decreased 9.6% from the prior-year period, while gross billings, a measure of new sales signed, sank even faster. Management attributed this to intentional efforts to raise standards for new learners and concentrate on retention in its senior-learning business.

Although gross profit fell along with revenue, cost controls made up for part of the lost ground. The company trimmed sales and marketing spending by nearly one-fifth, which helped push net income and net income margin up year over year despite the revenue drop.

New student enrollments fell nearly 40%, pointing to future revenue headwinds. Management emphasized improved student “quality” and the use of artificial intelligence to refine acquisition and course delivery but recognized that declining enrollment could challenge growth over time.

What Sunlands Technology Group Does

Sunlands Technology Group provides adult online education programs, including exam preparation and professional training courses, to learners across China.
