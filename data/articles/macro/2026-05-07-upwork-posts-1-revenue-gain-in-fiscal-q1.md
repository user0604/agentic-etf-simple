Upwork Posts 1% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:45 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Upwork focused on cost control and product innovation as overall business activity remained flat.
Revenue grew 1% year over year, while free cash flow dropped sharply.
Management lowered revenue guidance for the full year but raised its profit targets.

Online freelance marketplace operator Upwork (NASDAQ:UPWK) made strategic moves to manage costs and drive new product initiatives, but broad marketplace activity remained sluggish in its first quarter. The company connects businesses and independent professionals in over 180 countries, offering both standard marketplace matching and tailored solutions for enterprise clients. The latest results, released on May 7, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showed resilient profitability alongside muted client activity and a lowered sales outlook for the year.

Overall, the period featured strong progress in artificial intelligence and small-business products but underscored ongoing softness in total client numbers and spending.

Notable Developments
Client activity softened, with total active clients down 3% to 784,000 and overall spending (GSV) essentially flat versus last year.
AI-related projects saw over 40% year-over-year spending growth, and a new ChatGPT-based app was launched to streamline hiring.
Upwork announced a restructuring that will cut its workforce by 24%, with expected completion by the end of 2026.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management lowered its full-year revenue forecast, now expecting $760–$790 million, compared to the previous outlook of $835–$850 million. It simultaneously raised its annual profit targets due to planned cost cuts. Investors should keep a close eye on whether new products in artificial intelligence and small-business services can reignite platform growth after this period of client attrition.

Quarterly Highlights

Profitability remained a strong point, with adjusted EBITDA and non-GAAP earnings per share ahead of earlier company guidance, even as cash flow fell sharply. Cost reduction efforts, including a plan to reduce headcount by nearly a quarter, are aimed at keeping margins high despite slow top-line growth.

AI-driven work continues to scale at a rapid pace, with over 40% year-over-year growth in spending on AI-related projects and strong customer uptake of new AI-powered matching tools and features. This area stands out compared to the flat activity elsewhere on the platform.

The small-business-focused Business Plus segment performed well, delivering a 34% increase in client spending quarter over quarter, and bringing a high share of new clients to Upwork. Still, the gains are not yet large enough to offset overall declines in active client totals.

What Upwork Does

Upwork operates a global online marketplace that connects businesses and independent professionals for remote, project-based and ongoing work engagements.
