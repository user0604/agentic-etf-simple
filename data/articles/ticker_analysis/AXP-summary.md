AXP Company Overview
American Express provides credit cards, charge cards, and travel-related services to individuals and businesses across the globe. The company generates revenue through annual membership fees, interest on loans, and transaction fees paid by merchants for processing payments. Its business model relies on a loyalty ecosystem that connects cardholders with a vast network of merchants, using premium rewards and exclusive travel benefits to ensure long-term customer retention.

American Express Grows Earnings and Raises Dividend

American Express is a global payments company that gives people and businesses credit cards, loans, and travel services through its own network. It stands out by focusing on high-end cardholders and recently became the Official Payments Partner of the NFL. Over the past 5 years, its stock returned 121.4%, beating the S&P 500 by 51.1 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Earnings per share (Non-GAAP): $4.28 (up 18% YoY)
Revenue (Non-GAAP): $18.9 billion (up 11% YoY)
Billed business (total card transactions, FX-adjusted): $428.0 billion (up 10% YoY)

Latest Developments

The company raised its quarterly dividend by 16%, showing confidence in its business and its ability to return cash to shareholders.
American Express made bigger investments in technology and partnerships, which could help it attract more high-spending customers and grow its network.
