Earnings Preview: Lucid Diagnostics

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 3:03 PM

Comments

Lucid Diagnostics (NASDAQ:LUCD), a company specializing in non-invasive esophageal cancer screening tests, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Lucid Diagnostics delivered a notable increase in test volume and revenue. The company processed 3,664 EsoGuard tests, exceeding its stated target range of 2,500 to 3,000 tests per quarter. Revenue for the quarter reached $1.5 million, reflecting a 24% sequential increase. This performance beat analyst expectations for the period. The company continued to focus on expanding its commercial reach, highlighted by a newly awarded contract with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) for its EsoGuard test. Additionally, Lucid reported positive real-world data from nearly 12,000 patients, reinforcing the clinical utility and safety of its flagship products. Operational expenses rose, mainly due to increased investments in sales and market access teams, as well as annual compensation costs. The company ended the year with $34.7 million in cash and continues to await Medicare coverage, which could significantly impact future revenue recognition.

Positives
Test volume exceeded targets: The company processed 3,664 EsoGuard tests, surpassing its target and showing a 29% increase from the previous quarter, indicating growing demand and improved team productivity.
Revenue growth beat estimates: Fourth-quarter revenue rose to $1.5 million, up 24% sequentially, and beat consensus expectations, reflecting better-than-anticipated collections and commercial execution.
VA contract completed: Lucid secured a contract with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, expanding access to EsoGuard for veterans and validating the test's clinical relevance at the Medicare price point.
Strong real-world data published: Positive results from an 18-month, 12,000-patient study confirmed the safety, speed, and technical success of EsoGuard and EsoCheck, supporting broader adoption and payer engagement.
Negatives
Operating expenses increased: Non-GAAP operating expenses rose to $14.1 million in Q4, higher than the prior four-quarter average, mainly due to higher compensation and investment in commercial infrastructure.
Continued net losses: The company reported a non-GAAP net loss per share of $0.10 for the quarter and $0.43 for the year, reflecting ongoing investments and a lack of broad reimbursement.
Medicare coverage still pending: Lucid continues to await a draft Local Coverage Determination (LCD) from Medicare, which delays recognition of a significant portion of potential revenue and affects cash collections.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on Medicare reimbursement decision: Investors should look for any updates on the timing or status of the draft LCD for Medicare coverage, as this remains the key catalyst for revenue growth and cash collections.
VA contract rollout and test volume contribution: Monitor how quickly the VA contract is translating into new test volumes and revenue, and whether engagement with individual VA medical centers is accelerating adoption.
Commercial payer and LBM coverage milestones: Watch for announcements of new commercial insurance coverage, especially any first Laboratory Benefit Manager (LBM) positive coverage policies, as these could open new patient populations and improve collections.
Expense management and cash burn: Assess whether operating expenses remain in line with recent quarters and if the company continues to manage its cash burn prudently while scaling commercial activities.
Mix of Medicare and VA patients in test volume: Track the proportion of tests performed on Medicare and VA patients, as a higher mix could position Lucid to benefit quickly from retroactive reimbursement once coverage is approved.
