Key Takeaways

KE Holdings (NYSE:BEKE) stabilized profits despite a continued downturn in China’s real estate sector. The leading digital housing platform and brokerage operator reported first quarter 2026 results on May 20, 2026, reflecting decreasing revenue alongside notable margin gains. Overall, the quarter highlighted stronger cost control and efficiency even as underlying transaction activity stayed subdued.

Notable developments
Revenue dropped 19% from the prior year, driven by declines in both existing and new home transactions.
Net income rose 46.7% on margin improvement, as the operating margin increased to 6.7% from 2.5%.
Active agent and user numbers declined year over year, with mobile monthly active users down 4% to 42.7 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management emphasized continued focus on efficiency, digital innovation, and cost discipline, while acknowledging persistent weakness in China’s property market. No numeric guidance was provided. Investors should watch for signs of stabilization across transaction activity and user engagement, which remain under pressure.

Quarterly highlights

Transaction volume in both existing and new home services shrank, leading to a sharp decline in overall revenue. Revenue from existing home transactions, new home sales, and home renovation services all fell by double digits, while rental revenue remained more stable.

Margin gains were achieved through aggressive cost controls, with sales and marketing expenses and general and administrative costs both lowered meaningfully compared to a year ago. The company’s adjusted EBITDA and net income improved despite falling revenue, as management trimmed headcount and optimized operations.

Operational data showed a 4.2% drop in total agents and a 7.6% fall in active agents, even as the number of stores rose. User engagement slipped as well, suggesting the broader market slowdown continues to affect the platform’s activity levels.

What KE Holdings does

KE Holdings operates an integrated platform that connects buyers, sellers, and agents across China’s housing market, offering transaction, rental, renovation, and related property services both online and offline.
