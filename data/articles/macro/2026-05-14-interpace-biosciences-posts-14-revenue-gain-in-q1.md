Margin improvements stood out for Interpace Biosciences (OTC:IDXG) as it continued its transition to a thyroid-only diagnostics business. Notable Developments
Thyroid diagnostics revenue rose 12% year over year, with test volume up 10% and revenue per test increasing 3%.
Gross margin expanded by 6 percentage points to 65%, driven by automation and operational efficiencies.
Net cash from operations was $0.3 million, lower than the prior year despite higher margins and revenue growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for about 16% thyroid revenue growth for fiscal 2026, but did not specify profit or cash flow targets. The key focus for investors is whether thyroid revenue growth can accelerate in the next quarters to reach the full-year goal.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s exclusive focus on thyroid testing, mainly through its ThyGeNEXT mutation detection and ThyraMIR microRNA pathway panels, led to a 10% increase in diagnostic test volume. Revenue per test and the number of active accounts also grew, showing broader adoption in its target market.

Efficiency gains were reflected in margin performance, as gross margin improved from 59% to 65% on a comparable basis. Investments in automation and artificial intelligence helped reduce laboratory turnaround times and improved collections, with days sales outstanding down 6%.

Despite stronger profitability, cash flow from operations dropped to $0.3 million from $1.2 million in the prior year. Management did not address this decline, which remains an area for investor attention as the company continues to invest in automation and account growth.

What Interpace Biosciences Does

Interpace Biosciences provides molecular diagnostic tests that help physicians assess thyroid cancer risk using proprietary genetic panels.
