Key Takeaways
Take-Two Interactive Software notched a business win with robust franchise engagement and far improved net earnings versus last year.
Revenue grew to $1.68 billion over the period, while net bookings were unchanged from the prior year.

Take-Two Interactive Software (NASDAQ:TTWO) delivered operational improvement and strong franchise engagement, but reported another quarterly loss as it continues heavy investment ahead of major releases. The leading interactive entertainment publisher is well known for hit game series like Grand Theft Auto, NBA 2K, and BioShock. The company released its results for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 21, 2026, covering the period ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter marked a step forward in operating execution, though GAAP profit remains elusive as the company gears up for high-profile launches.

Notable Developments
Recurrent consumer spending, including in-game purchases, rose 7% and made up 82% of net bookings during the quarter.
Net loss narrowed sharply to $59.5 million compared to a $3.7 billion loss last year, reflecting reduced impairment charges.
Management set fiscal 2027 guidance for net bookings of $8.0–$8.2 billion, anticipating its first GAAP profit in three years.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects a substantial jump in revenue and profitability in fiscal 2027, targeting net bookings up at least 19% year over year and positive net income. The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI in November 2026 is the biggest anticipated catalyst. Investors should watch for execution around this release and whether higher margins materialize with the increase in scale.

Quarterly Highlights

Recurring digital spending dominated the revenue mix, with items like virtual currency and post-launch content now responsible for over 80% of net bookings. Mobile platforms, including titles from the Zynga portfolio, contributed about half of total bookings, showing the impact of shifting player preferences and digital channels.

Profits showed marked improvement from last year, mainly because prior results included a large one-time asset impairment. However, the company is still spending heavily on game development and marketing, holding back a full return to profitability until future launches are realized.

Guidance for the next fiscal year incorporates higher sales and improved earnings, with net bookings expected to rise to $8.0–$8.2 billion and the company forecasting a GAAP profit for the first time since fiscal 2024. The results from the anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI release are critical to achieving these goals.

What Take-Two Interactive Software Does
