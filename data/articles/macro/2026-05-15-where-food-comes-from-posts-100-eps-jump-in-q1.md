Food certification provider Where Food Comes From (NASDAQ:WFCF) delivered a modest business rebound as it grew both revenue and profit, but saw notable pressure on profitability. The leading specialist in food origin verification and certification reported results for its first fiscal quarter on May 15, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed incremental gains in revenue and profit, offset by lower margins and softer cash flow.

Notable Developments
Verification and certification services revenue grew by 4.8%, helping offset a 41% decline in professional services revenue.
Gross margin, which measures profit as a share of revenue after direct costs, fell by 3 percentage points to 39% because of higher personnel and insurance expenses.
The company repurchased 24,469 shares for $293,000 during the quarter, with cumulative buybacks now exceeding 1.39 million shares.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expressed optimism about growth prospects, highlighting the role of new programs like RaiseWell and Upcycled Certified in diversifying revenue beyond beef. However, it stopped short of issuing specific guidance for the rest of fiscal 2026 due to persistent industry headwinds and gross margin pressure. Investors should monitor whether margin recovery and cash flow improvement materialize in future quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The verification and certification business remained a growth driver, supported by new client wins and expansion of specialty programs such as RaiseWell, which was adopted by a major retailer for its beef supply. Revenue from this segment rose 6% even as some industry customers continued to face reduced herd sizes and tariffs.

A sharp 41% decline in professional services revenue was only partially offset by gains in other areas, highlighting a continued shift in business mix. This drop weighed on overall gross profit despite higher total sales.

The company’s ongoing share buyback program reduced outstanding shares by 24,469 in the quarter, a signal of confidence in its value. However, modest cash generation from operations and a slight increase in liabilities reflect lingering operational efficiency challenges.

What Where Food Comes From Does

Where Food Comes From provides verification, certification, and auditing services that assure the source, sustainability, and quality of food products for producers, processors, and retailers.
