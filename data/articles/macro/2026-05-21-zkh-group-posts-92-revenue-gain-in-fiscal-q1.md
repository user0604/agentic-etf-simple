Key Takeaways
The company posted a solid business win with double-digit growth in customer count and 9.2% revenue growth.

Zkh Group (NYSE:ZKH) edged into profitability in what is typically a seasonally soft start to the year, showing strong momentum on key priorities. The leading digital MRO (maintenance, repair, and operations) procurement platform in China, Zkh Group reported its first quarter results on May 21, 2026 (covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026). Overall, the quarter marked clear progress in revenue growth, customer acquisition, and cost control, though some areas remain to be watched.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 9.2% from the prior year, while gross merchandise value (GMV) grew 12.9% as the customer base expanded to 66,742.
Gross margin slipped to 16.7%, reflecting changes in product mix and continued growth in lower-margin segments like marketplace transactions.
Operating expenses fell 8.8% year over year, driven by fulfillment and research cost reductions, supporting a small swing to a Non-GAAP profit.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its confidence in achieving double-digit GMV growth and full-year profitability for fiscal 2026. New investments in artificial intelligence (AI) tools, private label product expansion, and fulfillment efficiency underpin these goals. Investors should watch for further improvement in gross margin, the impact of private label growth, and any shift in working capital or cash generation through the year.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue jumped by 9.2% thanks to continued expansion in both MRO product offerings and customer count. Platform GMV climbed to RMB 2.45 billion, with the marketplace model (third-party sales) gaining importance and accounting for 13.1% of total GMV. The addition of about 4 million new stock-keeping units (SKUs) lifted the company’s range to 27 million products.

Despite higher sales, gross margin narrowed slightly to 16.7%, as product mix shifted toward lower-margin categories and state-owned enterprise (SOE) business. Fulfillment efficiency improved, with warehouse utilization up 36% and fulfillment expenses down 17%, helping to reduce overall operating costs.

Artificial intelligence (AI) adoption accelerated, with approximately 30% of product matching now automated. The company also executed a share buyback, repurchasing 1.48 million American Depositary Shares (ADSs) for $4.76 million as part of its ongoing program.

What Zkh Group Does

Zkh Group operates a digital platform for MRO procurement in China, offering industrial products, digital procurement solutions, and logistics services for businesses.
