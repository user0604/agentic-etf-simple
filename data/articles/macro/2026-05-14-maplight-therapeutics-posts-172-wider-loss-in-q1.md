MapLight Therapeutics (NASDAQ:MPLT) advanced its clinical programs but reported a much larger quarterly loss as it increased investment in research and development. Notable developments
Enrollment finished for the ML-007C-MA Phase 2 ZEPHYR trial in schizophrenia, with topline results expected by mid-August 2026. There were no delays or reductions in target enrollment.
Research and development expenses rose 171.7% year over year to $53.7 million, reflecting increased activity in late-stage clinical trials. General and administrative expenses also increased by 184.2%.
The company ended the quarter with $395.2 million in cash, which management expects will fund operations until at least 2027.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management stated that its current cash position should support operations through 2027, citing steady progress in clinical milestones. The most important upcoming events are topline data readouts for the ZEPHYR (schizophrenia) and IRIS (autism) Phase 2 trials, both expected by mid-August 2026. Investors should monitor expenses relative to cash reserves as the company approaches these critical results.

Quarterly highlights

The ZEPHYR Phase 2 trial for ML-007C-MA in schizophrenia completed enrollment with 307 participants as planned, and the IRIS Phase 2 trial for ML-004 in autism finished its last patient visit. Both programs are on track for topline data readouts in the coming months, which could be pivotal for the company’s future.

Operating expenses increased sharply, mainly due to higher research, clinical trial, and employee-related costs, including $5.3 million in new stock-based compensation within research and development. The rise in general and administrative spending, such as $4.2 million more for stock awards and higher professional fees, reflects the company’s expansion as trials reach later stages.

Despite a cash outflow of nearly $58 million in the quarter, the company maintained a strong liquidity position, ending with $395.2 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments. This supports management’s guidance that funding should last through 2027 under current plans, but highlights the need for continued financial discipline as key trials progress.

What MapLight Therapeutics does

MapLight Therapeutics develops experimental medicines for severe central nervous system disorders, focusing on schizophrenia, Alzheimer’s disease psychosis, and autism spectrum disorder.
