Arcturus Therapeutics Posts 93% Revenue Drop in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:27 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business loss as legacy vaccine revenue evaporated and rare disease programs move forward.
Revenue fell sharply year over year to $2.1 million in the quarter.
Management maintains a multi-year cash runway and signals data readouts and regulatory milestones are next.

Rare disease drug developer Arcturus Therapeutics (NASDAQ:ARCT) reported a stark drop in revenue as its pivot away from infectious disease vaccines played out this quarter. The company focuses on mRNA therapies and vaccines for conditions like cystic fibrosis and ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency. In its earnings release for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, published on May 7, 2026, Arcturus also highlighted progress in clinical studies and new executive appointments. Overall, the period showed serious financial contraction despite early advancement in core clinical programs.

Notable Developments
Revenue dropped 93%, mostly due to the wind-down of infectious disease vaccine activities and lower collaboration payments.
Arcturus initiated its 12-week Phase 2 study for cystic fibrosis earlier than planned, enrolling up to 20 subjects.
Net loss nearly doubled to $27.0 million, but aggressive expense reductions helped lengthen the company’s projected cash runway beyond mid-2028.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management emphasized that Arcturus’s cash and short-term investments should support operations beyond the second quarter of 2028. The company expects little near-term revenue, so future business growth now depends on positive data from its rare disease clinical trials and obtaining regulatory clarity for its lead programs. Investors will be monitoring the progress of ongoing Phase 2 clinical studies and any regulatory updates, especially in cystic fibrosis and ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarterly revenue figure fell to its lowest point in recent years, highlighting the end of major milestone and royalty payments from past vaccine collaborations. The remaining revenue came almost entirely from grants related to pandemic vaccine programs and not new partnerships or product sales.

The ARCT-032 cystic fibrosis program reached a major step forward as it began enrolling patients for a 12-week clinical trial earlier than management had outlined in the prior quarter. The study will focus on adults with Class I CF mutations and is measuring several signs of lung function and quality of life.

The ARCT-810 program for ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency secured important regulatory feedback from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, setting the stage for a future pivotal trial in pediatric patients. However, achieving commercial success now depends on moving these rare disease programs further and validating clinical benefits in upcoming readouts.

What Arcturus Therapeutics Does

Arcturus Therapeutics develops messenger RNA (mRNA) medicines and vaccines, focusing on rare genetic diseases and novel vaccine technologies.
