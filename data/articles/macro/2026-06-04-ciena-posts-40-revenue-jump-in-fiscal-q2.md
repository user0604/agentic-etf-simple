Ciena (NYSE:CIEN) delivered a clear business win in its latest quarter, outpacing its own revenue and profit targets. The company is a global leader in high-speed networking equipment and software for telecom and cloud providers. Notable developments
Optical networking revenue climbed to $1,099.8 million, up 42% from the prior year and now makes up 70% of total revenue.
Blue Planet Automation Software and Services revenue fell by 16.4%, now contributing just 1.5% of company revenue.
Two customers accounted for 34% of total revenue, increasing customer concentration risk.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year revenue guidance to $6.3 billion plus or minus $100 million, with higher adjusted gross margin and adjusted operating margin targets for the rest of fiscal 2026. It projects third-quarter revenue at $1.625 billion, supported by strong demand from cloud and telecom customers. Investors should watch for further changes in software sales, customer concentration, and the company's ability to deliver on its large backlog.

Quarterly highlights

The optical networking segment, which includes equipment for high-capacity and long-distance data transmission, drove most of the growth. This product family helps major clients upgrade networks for cloud and artificial intelligence workloads.

Revenue from Blue Planet Automation Software and Services dropped year over year, reaching $23.4 million for the quarter. This is notable because software is important for Ciena's long-term growth and typically offers higher gross margins than hardware.

The business became more dependent on a small set of customers, with just two clients generating over one-third of total revenue during the period.

What Ciena does

Ciena provides networking hardware, software, and services that help telecom and cloud companies deliver high-speed, reliable internet and communications globally.
