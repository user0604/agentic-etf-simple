NuScale Power (NYSE:SMR) saw its business slow sharply as key project phases wrapped up and no new commercial contracts began. The nuclear technology company specializes in small modular reactors. It released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period showed a significant revenue decline but highlighted NuScale’s strong cash position and ongoing investment in future growth.

Notable developments
Revenue dropped by 96% as earlier RoPower and engineering contracts finished, and no new projects started in the quarter.
Operating loss increased to $57.5 million, reflecting higher research and supply chain spending to advance the NuScale Power Module.
NuScale ended the quarter with $1.0 billion in liquidity, after paying a major milestone tied to previous project phases, which reduced current liabilities.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not issue formal revenue or earnings guidance for the year. The company emphasized its strong cash balance and continued investment in research, development, and supply chain readiness. The main focus is on moving major partnerships toward signed construction contracts, as future revenue depends on progress in these areas.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue decline was expected, as the company had previously indicated that the completion of RoPower’s early engineering work and the lack of new milestone payments would create a gap until further commercial activity begins. This loss of engineering and licensing revenue matches prior management commentary that no new contracts were ready to start in this period.

Research and development expenses rose by $3.7 million to $12.8 million, driven by efforts to mature and advance the NuScale Power Module, a small modular reactor designed for enhanced safety and lower nuclear costs. Other expenses also increased as NuScale invested in supply chain readiness to prepare for future commercial module deliveries.

NuScale finished the quarter with $1.0 billion in combined cash and investments, supporting ongoing operations and future project work, even as it recorded net cash outflows due to a one-time project milestone payment and operational startup costs. Current liabilities dropped sharply after this payment, and the company continues to maintain strong overall financial flexibility.

What NuScale Power does

NuScale Power designs and commercializes small modular nuclear reactors and related technology for use in carbon-free electricity generation worldwide.
