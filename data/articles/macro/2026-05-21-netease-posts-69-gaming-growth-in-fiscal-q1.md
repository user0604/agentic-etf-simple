Key Takeaways
NetEase posted solid growth in its core gaming and digital content businesses.
Revenue increased 6.1%, while basic earnings per share rose slightly year over year.
Management highlights continued global expansion, but gives no formal guidance for the year ahead.

NetEase (NASDAQ:NTES)’s quarter reflected ongoing strength in digital gaming and entertainment, with steady progress across its major segments. The leading internet and game services provider reported its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, in a release dated May 21, 2026. In all, the period showcased margin expansion and resilient gaming revenue, while some non-core units declined.

Notable Developments
Gaming and related value-added services revenue grew 6.9%, with self-developed PC and mobile games leading segment gains.
Gross profit margin improved to roughly 69%, as lower revenue-sharing costs and a richer mix of proprietary content supported performance.
The company declared a quarterly dividend of $0.144 per share, a decrease from the prior quarter’s payout of $0.232 per share.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled continued investment in global expansion and innovation, pointing to ongoing development in major gaming franchises. No financial guidance was provided for coming quarters, so investors will need to focus on segment performance and efficiency in future updates.

Quarterly Highlights

The gaming segment drove overall results, accounting for the vast majority of company revenue and achieving 6.9% year-over-year growth. Popular self-developed titles like Fantasy Westward Journey and Where Winds Meet gained traction with high engagement and frequent content updates, while international expansion efforts for Marvel Rivals and Blizzard partnership games continued.

Margin improvement was a major theme. Gross profit rose 14.8% compared to the prior year, with gross margin reaching about 69%. Lower platform revenue-sharing costs and increased returns from self-developed content contributed to these gains.

Non-gaming areas delivered mixed results. NetEase Cloud Music posted revenue growth of 6.6% year over year. Online education and “innovative businesses and others” saw weaker trends; the latter segment contracted both from the prior year and the previous quarter, mainly due to lower e-commerce and advertising revenues.

What NetEase Does

NetEase creates and operates online games, digital music platforms, and education technology products, serving consumers in China and worldwide.
