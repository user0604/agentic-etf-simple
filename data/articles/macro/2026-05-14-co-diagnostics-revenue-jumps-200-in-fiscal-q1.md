Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management is prioritizing clinical completion, regulatory submissions, and preparations for commercial launches, especially for tuberculosis testing in India and respiratory projects in the U.S. It expects to keep increasing investments in clinical and regulatory activities. Investors should closely monitor the rate of cash use, the progress of key regulatory filings, and any updates on commercial revenue generation in future quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue rose by 200.1% year over year, but total sales were still only $0.15 million, underscoring limited market traction so far. This aligns with management’s prior focus on laying operational groundwork rather than rapid commercialization. The increase primarily came from small-scale shipments to support ongoing clinical work in India.

Investment in research and development made up the largest part of operating expenses at $5.93 million, supporting clinical studies for upper respiratory multiplex tests and tuberculosis. The company launched these efforts to reach near-term regulatory and commercial goals in India and the U.S.

Regulatory success included winning a key license and ISO manufacturing certification in India, enabling the company to begin clinical studies and initial instrument shipments for its tuberculosis testing program. Distribution agreements expanded the territorial reach across Bangladesh, Pakistan, Nepal, and Sri Lanka, bringing the potential addressable market to roughly $13 billion for the region.
