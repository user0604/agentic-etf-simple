RBC Bearings Posts 18% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q4

May 15, 2026, 9:39 AM

Key Takeaways
RBC Bearings posted strong gains, driven by robust Aerospace & Defense sales and a growing order backlog.
Earnings per share climbed 27.9% and revenue rose 18.3% compared to the prior year period.
Management expects continued growth in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, especially in the Aerospace & Defense segment.

RBC Bearings (NYSE:RBC) ended its latest quarter on a high note thanks to accelerated growth in its Aerospace & Defense business and expansion of its project backlog. The leading manufacturer of precision engineered bearings and components released its results for the fiscal fourth quarter on May 15, 2026, covering the period through March 28. Overall, the quarter capped a record year where multiple financial metrics reached new highs.

Notable Developments
Aerospace & Defense segment revenue soared 41.2% to $222.1 million, while Industrial revenue increased 5.5% to $295.9 million.
The company's order backlog more than doubled, ending the quarter at $2.3 billion compared to $0.9 billion a year ago.
VACCO, a recent acquisition, contributed $30 million of quarterly sales and supported margin gains despite higher operating costs.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue between $500 million and $510 million, reflecting year-over-year growth of up to 17%. While momentum in Aerospace & Defense is expected to continue, organic growth rates may moderate as the company cycles past acquisition-driven gains. Investors should keep an eye on operating expense growth and how efficiently the company converts its sizable backlog into revenue.

Quarterly Highlights

The Aerospace & Defense segment, which supplies bearings and components for aircraft and military systems, was the primary driver of the quarter's outperformance. Segment revenue jumped sharply, bolstered by contract wins and rising demand in defense applications.

The order backlog provides strong visibility for future sales. Backlog more than doubled compared to the previous year, with management attributing the rise to new long-term contracts, especially within the Aerospace & Defense business.

VACCO, acquired last year, delivered in-line quarterly sales and helped lift the company’s profit margins. However, integration costs increased expenses, highlighting the importance of careful expense management going forward.

What RBC Bearings Does

RBC Bearings designs and manufactures high-precision bearings, engineered components, and subsystems used in aerospace, defense, and industrial markets worldwide.
