Halozyme Bull and Bear

The Motley Fool Team

May 29, 2026, 7:08 AM

Comments

Halozyme Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ:HALO) is a biotechnology company specializing in enabling subcutaneous (SC) drug delivery for biologics through proprietary platforms such as ENHANZE, Hypercon, and Surf Bio. Halozyme’s asset-light, partnership-driven model generates most of its revenue from royalties, upfront fees, and milestones tied to leading blockbuster drugs commercialized by major pharmaceutical partners. The company’s growth strategy centers on expanding this royalty base with new product launches, next-generation platforms, and high-margin capital allocation, positioning it as a major player in drug delivery innovation.

Potential Bull Cases
1. Durable Royalty Streams Provide Predictable, High-Margin Growth Through 2032 and Beyond

Halozyme’s royalty-driven model offers visibility and scale unmatched by most biotech peers – In the first quarter of 2026, the company reported 43% year-over-year royalty growth to $240.7 million, driven by entrenched SC blockbuster drugs such as DARZALEX SC, PHESGO, and VYVGART Hytrulo. Management projects that 66% of the cumulative lifetime royalties from the current top 10 ENHANZE products will be realized between 2026 and 2032, reflecting a substantial revenue "backlog." These recurring streams, built on long-term commercial agreements, support sustained double-digit revenue and non-GAAP EPS growth. With royalty revenue expected to surpass $1.1 billion annually by 2026, the stable, high-margin nature of such cash flows enables both ambitious R&D investment and aggressive share repurchases, underpinning the company’s premium profitability profile.

2. Expanding Pipeline and Signed Partnerships Drive Future Revenue Upside

New collaborations and product launches signal a robust growth pipeline – Halozyme signed three new global collaboration and license agreements (CLAs) in early 2026, including a major deal with GlaxoSmithKline covering oncology targets and a Hypercon-focused agreement with Oruka for ORKA-001. These partnerships diversify and future-proof the royalty base, opening up access to large addressable markets—such as the $20–$25 billion global moderate-to-severe psoriasis segment. With 13 additional ENHANZE-enabled products potentially launching from 2029 to 2032 and the expectation of at least $1 billion in Hypercon annual royalties by the mid-2030s, Halozyme’s pipeline momentum supports outsized growth beyond its current portfolio.

3. Market Leadership in SC Drug Delivery Establishes a Defensible Competitive Moat

Established technology and clinical expertise reinforce Halozyme’s leadership and partner preference – The ENHANZE platform is considered the standard in rHuPH20-mediated SC delivery, with a safety record encompassing over 1.3 million treated patients. Major pharmaceutical companies—including Johnson & Johnson, Roche, GSK, and Vertex—continue to choose Halozyme over emerging competitors, often after evaluating alternative platforms. The company’s ability to rapidly bring partners into clinical studies and streamline regulatory pathways further differentiates it, enabling a steady cadence of new licensing deals. Co-formulation patents can significantly extend royalty terms, sometimes out to the 2040s, allowing the company to maintain lucrative partnerships even as base composition patents expire in 2029.

4. Capital Allocation Strategy Enhances Shareholder Value Through Buybacks and Deleveraging

Discipline in returning cash and maintaining a healthy balance sheet drives incremental value – Halozyme’s Board authorized a new $1 billion share repurchase program, with at least $400 million planned for buybacks in 2026 and a projected 3% annual buyback yield in coming years. Simultaneously, management is prioritizing repayment of 2027 and 2028 notes, with net leverage projected to fall from 2.5x at the end of Q1 2026 to 1.2x by year-end, fueled by robust cash generation. This disciplined capital approach ensures that growth investments, capital returns, and financial flexibility can coexist, compounding long-term returns for equity holders as royalty streams scale up.

5. Next-Generation Platforms Poised to Capture Emerging SC Drug Market Trends

Innovation in Hypercon and Surf Bio aligns with industry’s move toward high-concentration, self-administered therapies – Halozyme’s strategic investments in second-generation SC technologies, such as Hypercon and Surf Bio, anticipate the pharmaceutical industry’s shift to at-home injectable biologics and high-dose SC formulations. Hypercon’s expected clinical entry in 2027, followed by potential launches in 2030 and beyond, positions the company to unlock new multi-billion-dollar opportunities with extended IP protection. With major partners already signed for these next-wave platforms and early-stage development set to ramp, Halozyme’s technology leadership may future-proof its royalty business against both competitive and lifecycle risks, ensuring relevance well past current patent expiries.

Potential Bear Cases
1. Patent Expiration on ENHANZE Platform Could Erode Core Royalty Streams After 2029

Upcoming base patent expiry represents a structural risk to Halozyme’s main cash flows – The primary composition of matter patents for ENHANZE are scheduled to expire in 2029 in both the U.S. and Europe. While management emphasizes co-formulation patents can extend royalty terms into the 2040s for many products, there remains inherent uncertainty regarding the breadth, enforceability, and partner uptake of these secondary protections. Should competitors introduce alternative hyaluronidase solutions or challenge key patents in court, risk exists that royalty rates or product exclusivity could be pressured, jeopardizing the longevity of Halozyme’s most valuable revenue streams and destabilizing the company’s high-margin profile.

2. Delays or Underperformance in Next-Generation Platforms Could Stall Future Growth

Reliance on the timely success of Hypercon and Surf Bio creates execution risk – Although next-generation platforms hold promise for extending the growth runway, the first Hypercon clinical trials are not expected until 2027, with Surf Bio 18–24 months behind. Any manufacturing setbacks, regulatory hurdles, or slower-than-expected partner adoption could push initial launches well into the next decade. Since future royalty growth is increasingly tied to these technologies as ENHANZE matures, underperformance or delays would limit the company’s ability to replace or expand its royalty base, potentially resulting in revenue stagnation if new product launches are not realized as forecasted.

3. High Partner and Product Concentration Increases Downside Exposure to External Factors

Concentration risk magnifies volatility in financial results if a leading partner or product falters – A significant portion of Halozyme’s royalty revenue depends on a concentrated set of top partners and products, including the likes of Johnson & Johnson (DARZALEX SC) and argenx (VYVGART Hytrulo). Should any major partner undergo business disruption, lose exclusivity, face safety concerns, or deprioritize a Halozyme-enabled product line, the resulting lost royalties could materially impact the company’s overall performance. This exposure is further magnified by the relatively long timelines required to replace lost revenue with pipeline assets, underscoring a vulnerability to shifts in partner strategy or marketplace dynamics outside the company’s direct control.
