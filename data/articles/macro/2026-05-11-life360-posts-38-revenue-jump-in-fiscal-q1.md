Key Takeaways
Life360 delivered a strong quarter with rapid revenue and user growth, but higher costs weighed on margins.
Revenue climbed 38% year over year, driven by robust gains in subscriptions and a surge in advertising.
Management raised full-year revenue and earnings guidance, citing continued momentum and investment in technology and international expansion.

Life360 (NASDAQ:LIF) posted robust results, expanding its user base and revenue at a fast pace, but grappling with rising costs in the period reported on May 11, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The company continued to add subscribers and diversified its revenue streams, especially through advertising, yet profitability was affected by sharp increases in operating expenses. In summary, the period showed strong growth across subscriptions and ad revenue, but margin pressures highlight the company's challenge to balance rapid expansion with sustained profit improvements.

Notable developments
Advertising revenue surged 329% year over year to $19.7 million, primarily driven by the Nativo acquisition and new ad platform capabilities.
The global Paying Circles base expanded 27%, adding 201,000 net new circles for a total of 3.0 million, marking record quarterly growth.
Total operating expenses increased 46% to $118.6 million, outpacing revenue growth and driving the adjusted EBITDA margin down to 12% from 15%.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its guidance for full-year 2026 revenue, subscription sales, and adjusted EBITDA, pointing to ongoing strength in both core subscriptions and the growing advertising segment. Plans include further investment in product innovation, international expansion, and scaling the ad platform. The key item for investors to watch is whether recent increases in costs will translate into durable profitability as the year progresses.

Quarterly highlights

Advertising became a standout growth engine after the Nativo acquisition, with revenue contributions now broken out separately for the first time and a dramatic 329% year-over-year jump in the quarter. This move helps diversify the company's income streams as ad technology becomes a core offering.

User engagement and subscription monetization remained strong. Monthly Active Users rose 17% to about 97.8 million, and the pace of converting users into global Paying Circles reached a record high, up 27% year over year. Average revenue per Paying Circle also improved, led by gains in international markets where pricing strategies boosted revenues by 23%.

Rising sales and marketing costs, along with ongoing integration expenses following recent acquisitions, led to operating expenses growing faster than revenue. This margin compression reflects Life360's early-year focus on platform investment and geographic expansion, with expectations for improved profitability in the year's second half.
