Earnings Preview: Elutia

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 1:03 PM

Comments

Elutia (NASDAQ:ELUT), a company that develops drug-eluting biomatrix products for surgical reconstruction, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2025, Elutia reported revenue of $3.3 million, representing a 16% increase year over year. This growth was driven by a shift back to direct distribution for both cardiovascular and SimpliDerm product lines. Adjusted gross margin improved to 66.8%, up from 56.5% in the prior-year quarter. The company’s net loss from continuing operations narrowed to $6.5 million, compared to $7.2 million in the same quarter last year. However, adjusted EBITDA loss increased to $4.2 million from $3.4 million. The quarter included significant strategic changes, such as the completed sale of the cardiac implantable electronic device (CIED) business, which brought in $88 million in cash, allowing Elutia to pay off all outstanding debt and end the year in a debt-free position with $44.4 million in cash and escrow. The company also submitted its NXT-41 product for FDA review and began exploring strategic options for SimpliDerm.

Positives
Revenue growth driven by direct distribution: Returning to direct sales for key product lines resulted in higher revenue and improved gross margins, which reflects better pricing and efficiency.
Significant improvement in gross margin: The adjusted gross margin rose by over 10 percentage points, indicating stronger profitability on each product sold.
Debt-free balance sheet: The sale of the CIED business enabled Elutia to eliminate all outstanding debt, reducing financial risk and interest expenses going forward.
Strong cash position post-divestiture: With $44.4 million in cash and escrow, the company is well-resourced to fund ongoing development and commercialization efforts.
Negatives
Overall net sales declined year over year: Despite quarterly improvement, full-year 2025 net sales fell 15% to $12.3 million, mainly due to lower SimpliDerm sales and the loss of distribution agreements.
Adjusted EBITDA loss increased: The company’s operating loss widened on an adjusted basis, reflecting higher research and development expenses and sales commissions.
SimpliDerm faces commercial headwinds: SimpliDerm sales dropped as some physician users moved to hospitals where the product is not available, and the company is now exploring strategic alternatives for this product.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress on NXT-41 FDA review: Investors should look for updates on the regulatory review timeline and any feedback from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) regarding the NXT-41 submission. The company expects clearance in 2026, but details on review progress or questions from regulators could impact expectations.
SimpliDerm strategic process status: Elutia announced it is exploring options for SimpliDerm. Watch for any developments or announcements about a potential sale, partnership, or other outcome for this product line.
Cash burn and liquidity: With a strong cash position at year-end, the company’s quarterly operating cash use and updated cash balance will be important, especially given ongoing investments in research and development.
Commercial readiness for NXT-41X: Updates on hiring, key opinion leader (KOL) engagement, and preparations for the eventual launch of NXT-41X will signal how well-positioned Elutia is for future growth once regulatory milestones are met.
Resolution of legacy legal matters: The company reported that nearly all FiberCel litigation cases were settled by year-end 2025. Any remaining or new legal developments could affect financial results or risk outlook.
