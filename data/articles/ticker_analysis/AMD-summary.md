AMD Company Overview
AMD designs and sells high-performance computing components, including microprocessors and graphics chips that power servers, personal computers, and gaming consoles. The company generates revenue by selling these hardware solutions to manufacturers, cloud providers, and retailers while providing software tools that allow developers to optimize their applications for its specific chip architectures. By integrating central and graphics processing units with networking technology, the company creates a unified platform that encourages long-term adoption among enterprise and consumer clients.

AMD Gains Momentum With Data Center and AI Growth

Advanced Micro Devices designs and sells computer chips used in data centers, personal computers, and other devices. The company’s big push into AI chips and partnerships with cloud giants make it stand out from other chipmakers. Over the last 5 years, the stock soared 356.1%, beating the S&P 500 by 283.4 percentage points.
