Key Takeaways
Arko Petroleum delivered higher operating profits as it continued to convert retail sites and improve margins. Adjusted EBITDA rose 17.7% year over year. Management reaffirmed its guidance for full year 2026 and expects continued site conversions and expansions ahead.

Arko Petroleum (NASDAQ:APC) posted a business win, with expanding operating profits despite only minor changes in total revenue. The leading fuel distributor and site operator focused on converting company-controlled retail sites to wholesale dealer locations, a move that supports its long-term transformation. It released first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. The quarter showed margin improvement, strong cash discipline, and progress in new site development amid volatile industry conditions.

Notable Developments
The wholesale segment grew site count to 2,126 from 1,961, aided by 41 retail-to-dealer conversions and 11 newly opened or reopened sites.
Adjusted EBITDA rose 17.7%, supported by improved fuel margins and a larger base of dealer and consignment locations.
The company repaid $206.7 million in debt with IPO proceeds, cutting net debt to $313.5 million and reducing its leverage ratio to 2.1 times adjusted EBITDA.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 outlook for $156 million in adjusted EBITDA and $110 million in discretionary cash flow. Priorities remain focused on further retail site conversions, new fleet fueling locations, and disciplined acquisitions. Investors should closely watch how quickly site conversions and new builds translate to continued margin and cash flow growth.

Quarterly highlights

The wholesale segment benefited from the conversion of retail sites and reported a 23.5% increase in operating income in the first quarter of 2026 compared to the prior-year period. Site counts grew as 41 additional retail sites moved to the dealer model, providing greater scale and higher margins per site. The company now serves 2,126 locations in the wholesale network at period end.

Fleet fueling operations reported higher per-gallon fuel margins, increasing from 46.1 cents to 52.2 cents, despite a slight decrease in gallons sold. Management pointed to volatility in fuel markets and favorable diesel pricing as drivers. The company ended the quarter with 292 sites, up from 280, and aims to open 20 new fleet fueling locations this year.

IPO proceeds reduced net debt sharply, strengthening the balance sheet and supporting financial flexibility for growth. The quarterly dividend was declared at $0.26 per share, with management indicating a higher run-rate for future quarters.

What Arko Petroleum does

Arko Petroleum is a diversified distributor of fuel and fuel-related services, supplying independent dealers, fleet fueling operators, and related parties through its nationwide network of retail and wholesale sites.
