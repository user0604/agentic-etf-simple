Notable Developments
Revenue was $1.17 billion, up 3.9% year over year, helped by $19 million in positive foreign currency impacts and progress in international markets.
Non-GAAP operating margin was 21.5%, and free cash flow was $80 million, partly due to $17 million in restructuring payments.
The company raised its quarterly dividend by 8% to $0.569 per share and repurchased $138 million in shares during the period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue and earnings guidance for fiscal 2026, though it narrowed the ranges to reflect more caution amid macroeconomic uncertainty. The key area to watch is whether innovation efforts and investments in cloud and artificial intelligence technology will translate into stronger revenue growth and improved cash flow in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The company continued its strategic push into artificial intelligence-powered platforms, including new commercial engagements for its agentic operating system, though direct financial impact remains limited so far. In international regions, Europe reported record revenue of $192 million, up 6.2%, while the Rest of World segment grew 8%, helping offset muted growth in North America. Free cash flow for the quarter was much lower than last year’s second quarter, even after excluding restructuring-related outflows, putting a spotlight on cost management and execution.

Segment performance showed managed services contributing about 65% of total revenue, with this segment increasing modestly to $759 million. Contract backlog also increased to $4.28 billion, up 2.6% over last year, signaling pipeline stability despite the cautious market environment. Leadership changes continue to unfold, as the company transitions its Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer roles following the CEO succession last quarter.

Comparing the company's progress to the prior quarter, revenue growth in constant currency decelerated and expenses increased for one-time leadership transitions. Still, the strategic direction around cloud, artificial intelligence, and international expansion stayed consistent with previous management plans.
