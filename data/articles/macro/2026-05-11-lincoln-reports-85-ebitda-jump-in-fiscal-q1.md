Lincoln Educational Services (NASDAQ:LINC) saw strong business momentum in its first quarter of 2026, driven by rapid enrollment growth and higher revenue. The national provider of career-oriented post-secondary education benefited from both new campus expansion and strong demand in its core skilled trades programs. The results, reported on May 11, 2026 for the quarter ended March 31, showed notable improvement across key financial and operational metrics. Overall, the quarter reflected significantly higher profitability, strong student demand, and upgraded expectations for the remainder of the year.

Notable Developments
Student starts rose 19.5 % to 5,509, driven by both organic growth and recent campus openings in Houston and program expansions.
Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 10.8 % as operating efficiencies offset higher expenses related to growth initiatives and new campus costs.
Management increased guidance for fiscal 2026, now targeting $590–$600 million in revenue and up to 14 % student starts growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 targets for revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and net income, reflecting its “confidence in the durability of current trends.” It expects continued strong performance from skilled trades enrollment and is investing in new campuses to support future growth. Key areas to monitor going forward include the execution of campus expansion and enrollment balancing across all program groups.

Quarterly Highlights

The period featured robust revenue and student starts growth, with Transportation and Skilled Trades programs leading the way. Student starts increased 19.5 % year over year, while revenue climbed 22.5 %, both bolstered by new campuses, program expansions, and ongoing employer demand for graduates.

Profitability improved with adjusted EBITDA up 85 % compared to the prior year’s quarter, driven by stronger margins and operating leverage. Despite higher educational services and administrative costs, management highlighted operational efficiencies and positive cash flow from operations for a typically cash-usage-heavy quarter.

Lincoln continued to invest in its expansion strategy, opening new campuses and maintaining timelines for upcoming locations in Hicksville, New York (Q4 2026) and Rowlett, Texas (Q1 2027). It also amended and increased its credit facility in April to support these growth initiatives with added liquidity.
