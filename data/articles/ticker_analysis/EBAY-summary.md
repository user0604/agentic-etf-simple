EBAY Company Overview
eBay operates a global marketplace that connects millions of buyers and sellers across different countries to facilitate the trade of new and pre-owned goods. The company generates revenue by charging fees on completed transactions and selling advertising services that help merchants increase the visibility of their listings. Customers remain loyal through specialized trust programs like authenticity verification and an integrated payments system that simplifies the buying and selling process for enthusiasts.

eBay Gains Momentum With Collectibles and Home Market Focus

eBay is an online marketplace where people buy and sell new or used goods from around the world. The company’s push into areas like collectibles and AI-powered seller tools makes it stand out among e-commerce rivals. Over the past 5 years, the stock returned 86.0%, beating the S&P 500’s 70.5% gain by 15.5 percentage points.
