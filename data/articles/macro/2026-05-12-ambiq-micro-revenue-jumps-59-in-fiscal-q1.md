Notable Developments
Revenue surged 59% year over year to a new quarterly high, driven by strong demand for edge artificial intelligence chips and increased adoption of SPOT and Apollo product lines.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects robust sales between $31.0 and $32.0 million for the next quarter, with stable gross margins and continued operating expense growth as a result of investments in research and market expansion. The most important factor to monitor is whether expense growth remains in balance with revenue and if the firm can continue reducing net losses while maintaining innovation momentum.

Quarterly Highlights

Quarterly revenue climbed to a record level, fueled by edge artificial intelligence demand, with over 80% of units shipped capable of running AI algorithms. The Apollo system-on-chip (SoC) family and SPOT technology continued to drive widespread adoption across customer industries, leading to broad-based sales gains.

Operating expenses, mainly from increased research and development spending, rose significantly. Management remains focused on advancing the Atomiq SoC product line and a new Singapore-based R&D program, aiming to maintain a technological edge.

Customer concentration improved meaningfully as new markets and customers began to account for a larger share of sales. While top clients still contribute most of the revenue, dependency is decreasing as Ambiq Micro grows in healthcare, industrial, and smart building sectors.

What Ambiq Micro Does

Ambiq Micro designs and sells ultra-low-power semiconductor chips and software platforms for edge artificial intelligence applications in wearables, smart devices, and industrial equipment.
