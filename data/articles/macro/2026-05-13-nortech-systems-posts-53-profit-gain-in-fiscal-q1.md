Key Takeaways
Nortech Systems posted strong operational improvement, with a sharply narrowed net loss and a rising order backlog.
EPS loss improved significantly year over year.
Management remains optimistic, with backlog growth providing demand visibility but ongoing focus on cash flow and debt levels.

Nortech Systems (NASDAQ:NSYS) achieved another quarter of operational gains as cost controls and manufacturing improvements drove financial stabilization. The contract manufacturer, specializing in complex electronic solutions for the medical, aerospace, and industrial sectors, reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 13, 2026 (covering the fiscal first quarter). The period featured improved revenue, gross margin, and a near break-even bottom line, despite ongoing reliance on debt and working capital increases.

Overall, the quarter showed clear advancement over the prior year, with backlog growth supporting near-term visibility but a need for continued focus on profitability and risk management.

Notable Developments
Gross profit increased by 52.8% compared to the prior year, as operational changes took hold.
The 90-day backlog rose 18.0% year over year, reaching $31.5 million and indicating firmer demand visibility across end markets.
Cash flow from operations remained negative but improved from the prior year; inventories and debt both increased during the period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted continuing demand momentum, pointing to growth in the backlog as a tailwind into the second half of the year. No precise guidance was provided, but leadership noted it feels well-positioned to serve customers amid rising interest in nearshore manufacturing.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s revenue increased by over 12%, building on strength in both the company's core medical business and the expanding Aerospace and Defense segment. Management called out the latter as “an increasingly important part” of the customer mix, which aids efforts to reduce reliance on a few major customers—a key risk flagged in prior filings.

Profitability metrics showed marked improvement, with gross profit and gross margin rebounding sharply and operating expenses holding steady. Notably, net loss nearly disappeared, falling to just $(0.01) per share, while adjusted EBITDA turned positive for the period.

Order backlog reached a new high for the 90-day window, suggesting near-term stability even as inventories and accounts payable also increased. The company added new debt in March to support liquidity, which will require careful management if negative operating cash flow persists.

What Nortech Systems Does

Nortech Systems provides contract manufacturing and design services for complex electronic, electro-mechanical, and optical assemblies, serving the medical, industrial, and aerospace and defense markets.
