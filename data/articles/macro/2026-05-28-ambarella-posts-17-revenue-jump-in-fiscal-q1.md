Key Takeaways

Ambarella (NASDAQ:AMBA) achieved record automotive segment revenue during the first quarter, but ongoing GAAP losses and a decline in gross margin stood out. The fabless semiconductor specialist, known for advanced AI chips used in automotive and edge applications, released its results for the quarter ended April 30, 2026 (reported on May 28, 2026). Overall, the period mixed strong growth with tighter profit margins and higher inventories.

Notable Developments
Automotive segment hit an all-time revenue record, driven by rising demand for advanced AI chips in commercial vehicles.
Non-GAAP gross margin declined by 2.1 percentage points to 59.9% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
Inventories jumped 54% over the previous quarter, from $52.2 million to $80.4 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guided for second-quarter revenue of $105.0 million to $111.0 million, pointing to continued growth in key markets. The company expects non-GAAP gross margin to remain between 59.0% and 60.5%, citing persistent margin pressure. Investors should closely monitor inventory levels and the impact of product mix on profitability in the coming months.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s top story was automotive, where customers adopted higher-value AI chips for advanced applications, fueling the segment’s new revenue record. This followed management’s prior guidance that emphasized a strong outlook for automotive, while IoT results were less visible this quarter as no significant update was given for that segment.

Profitability improved outside of standard accounting (Non-GAAP basis), with EPS and net income higher than the prior year. However, the company still recorded a GAAP loss, and non-GAAP gross margin slipped to the low end of the long-term range as Ambarella faced increased complexity and rising operating costs.

Significantly, inventory expanded to $80.4 million, up from $52.2 million at the end of the prior quarter.

What Ambarella Does

Ambarella designs and sells semiconductor chips that enable artificial intelligence processing for automotive, security camera, and edge computing devices.
