Msc Income Fund (NYSE:MSIF) delivered a steady but mixed quarter as asset growth slowed even as recurring investment income rose modestly. The business development company focuses on making secured private loans to middle-market companies in the U.S. In its first quarter 2026 results released May 7, 2026 (covering the period ending March 31), performance was highlighted by flat net investment income per share but a small increase in total investment income. Overall, the period reflected solid fundamentals but revealed portfolio pressure from unrealized losses and a significant drop in growth in net asset value.

Notable Developments
Total investment income ticked up by 3%, helped by a $2.0 million boost in interest income, even as dividend income from the legacy lower middle market portfolio dropped $1.5 million.
Return on equity slid by 2.2 percentage points to 7.3%, with a 19% year-over-year decline in net increase in net assets resulting from operations per share, driven by higher tax provision and reduced portfolio appreciation.
Regulatory leverage capacity expanded following a minimum asset coverage update, and management ran share buybacks totaling $16 million, lifting net asset value per share by $0.08.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted opportunities in the private loan market, expressing confidence in the Fund’s positioning, but offered no explicit targets for future income or growth. The company plans to maintain its investment focus while benefiting from increased leverage flexibility. Investors should watch portfolio credit quality and how the shift away from lower middle market investments affects overall income and portfolio diversification.

Quarterly Highlights

The core private loan portfolio remains central, with debt investments now making up 60% of portfolio fair value and 99.5% of private loans secured by first-lien collateral. The private loan segment has a weighted average yield of 10.5%, and management completed $54.8 million in new private loan investments during the quarter, netting a $17.4 million increase after repayments and losses.

The transition away from the lower middle market (LMM) business is ongoing. LMM assets shrank as a share of the portfolio and produced $1.5 million less in dividend income for the period, a decline of 31%. The managed runoff of LMM means the Fund is more reliant on private loans for future returns and diversification.

Risk metrics remained stable, with non-accrual investments making up just 1.1% of portfolio fair value. Interest expenses rose to $8.9 million as leverage capacity increased, while the Fund preserved its investment-grade credit rating and ended the quarter with $210 million in available liquidity. The quarterly dividend was maintained at $0.36 per share, including a $0.01 supplemental payout.

What Msc Income Fund Does

Msc Income Fund is a business development company that primarily invests in secured private loans to middle-market U.S. companies and distributes its net investment income to shareholders as dividends.
