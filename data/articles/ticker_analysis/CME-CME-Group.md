CME Company Overview
CME Group operates global marketplaces for trading derivatives, which are financial contracts that allow participants to manage risks associated with interest rates, currencies, and commodities. The company generates revenue by charging fees for executing and clearing these trades while providing essential market data and analytical tools to professional investors. Its integrated clearing house ensures transaction security by acting as the central counterparty for every trade, creating a reliable ecosystem that encourages long-term participation.
