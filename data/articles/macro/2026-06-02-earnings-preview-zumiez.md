Earnings Preview: Zumiez

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Zumiez (NASDAQ:ZUMZ), a specialty retailer focused on apparel, footwear, and accessories for young adults, is expected to report its first-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings after the market closes on Thursday, June 4, 2026.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Zumiez delivered a solid finish to fiscal 2025, posting net sales of $291.3 million for the fourth quarter, a 4.4% increase from the prior year. Comparable sales rose 2.2% in the quarter, driven by strength in North America where comparable sales increased 5.5%. The company reported net income of $19.6 million, or $1.16 per share, up from $14.8 million, or $0.78 per share, a year earlier. Gross margin expanded to 38.2% from 36.2% last year, reflecting higher product margins and improved store occupancy leverage. For the full year, net sales reached $929.1 million and diluted earnings per share improved to $0.78 from a loss of $0.09 in fiscal 2024. Both fourth-quarter sales and earnings per share beat analyst estimates, according to consensus data.

Positives
North America momentum continued: The region posted its eighth consecutive quarter of comparable sales growth, showing Zumiez’s strategies are resonating with its core customer base.
Product margin and gross margin expanded: Margin gains were driven by a focus on full-price selling, especially in North America and Europe, as well as disciplined inventory and expense management.
Private label penetration reached a record: Private label brands accounted for about 30% of sales, up from 12% five years ago, supporting margin improvement and brand differentiation.
Strong cash position and no debt: The company ended the year with $160.6 million in cash and marketable securities, maintaining financial flexibility for future investments and share repurchases.
Negatives
International comparable sales declined: Other international (mainly Europe and Australia) saw comparable sales drop 7.5% in the fourth quarter, reflecting ongoing challenges in those markets.
Footwear remains a weak category: Footwear was the only major product category with negative comparable sales for both the quarter and the year, indicating persistent softness in this segment.
SG&A expenses increased: Selling, general, and administrative expenses rose to 29.6% of sales in Q4, up from 29% last year, due to higher incentive compensation and wage-related costs.
Store closures continue: The company closed 17 underperforming stores in fiscal 2025 and plans to close about 25 more in fiscal 2026, highlighting ongoing pressure in lower-traffic locations.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales trends and regional performance: Early Q1 results showed strong momentum, with total sales up 9.8% and comparable sales up 7.5% for the four-week period ended Feb. 28, 2026. Investors should watch if this pace continued through the full quarter, especially in North America and Europe.
Gross margin and product margin improvement: Management is guiding for further product margin expansion in both North America and Europe. Watch for updates on full-price selling strategies and any impact from tariffs or supply chain changes.
Store footprint changes and productivity: With five new U.S. stores planned and approximately 25 closures expected in fiscal 2026, pay attention to commentary on new store performance, closure progress, and how these moves affect overall sales and profitability.
Private label and brand mix: Private label sales hit a new high in Q4. Any continued growth in this area could further boost margins, so look for updates on private label penetration and performance versus branded products.
Updated guidance and macroeconomic commentary: The company’s Q1 outlook calls for sales of $189 million to $193 million and a loss per share between $0.77 and $0.87, in line with analyst consensus. Listen for any changes to this guidance, as well as management’s view on consumer spending, promotional activity, and the impact of ongoing global events or cost pressures.
