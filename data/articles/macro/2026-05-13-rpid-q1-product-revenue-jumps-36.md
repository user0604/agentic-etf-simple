Key Takeaways
The company exceeded its own targets for revenue and system placements but continued to post operating losses.
Product revenue grew 36.0% year over year while the proportion of recurring revenue also increased.

Rapid Micro Biosystems (NASDAQ:RPID) achieved short-term operational goals for the latest quarter, but its bottom line remains in the red. The leading provider of automated quality control solutions for the pharmaceutical industry reported modest revenue growth and stronger recurring sales in its first quarter of fiscal 2026 (earnings released May 13, 2026), with performance topping minimum benchmarks it set for itself. Overall, the quarter offered evidence of steady market demand, though persistent losses and margin pressures continue to weigh on results.

Notable Developments
Product revenue rose 36%, supported by both new and repeat Growth Direct system placements, which totaled six in the quarter compared to three the year before.
Recurring revenue made up 63% of the total in the quarter, signaling increased usage of consumables and services within the existing installed base.
Operating expenses jumped 17.9% from a year ago, largely due to higher general and administrative and sales and marketing costs, widening the company's net loss.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year targets for revenue ($37-41 million), system placements (30–38), and an improved gross margin near 20%, with heavier results expected later in the year. The team's main focus is to continue ramping placements and consumable usage while improving overall cost discipline. Investors should watch for signs of margin improvement and slower cash usage as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Product revenue was the main driver of growth in the quarter, outpacing overall revenue with a 36% increase year over year, reflecting traction in both new system placements and consumables sales tied to the company's automated Growth Direct systems.

Recurring revenue, which represents contracted sales from consumables and service agreements, reached 63% of total revenue -- up from 55% a year earlier -- indicating greater customer commitment to the installed systems and a shift toward more reliable sales streams.

Despite topline gains, operating losses deepened as expenses for sales, marketing, and administration outpaced revenue growth, and the consolidated gross margin stayed low at 4.6%, with management reiterating a plan for a stronger second half supported by cost reductions and increased placements.

What Rapid Micro Biosystems Does

Rapid Micro Biosystems delivers automated microbial quality control systems for pharmaceutical and biotech manufacturers, aiming to streamline contamination testing and support regulatory compliance.
