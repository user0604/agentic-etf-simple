Posco Posts 2.3% Margin in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 29, 2026, 8:55 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Posco reported stable core steel profitability but margin pressures remain.
Operating margin fell to 4.0% from 3.3% in the prior year period.
Management expects steel demand recovery to stay limited as global risks persist.

Posco (NYSE:PKX) experienced persistent cost pressures and sluggish steel demand, moderating wins in its traditional businesses. The leading South Korean steel and diversified materials company saw operating profit fall sharply in the period ended March 31, 2026 (as reported on May 29, 2026), even as new ventures like rechargeable battery materials showed progress toward breakeven. Overall, the quarter demonstrated resilience in the core steel business but signaled the challenges ahead from rising input costs and uncertain global demand.

Notable developments
Rechargeable battery materials segment revenue increased to 978,834 million South Korean won (₩), and operating losses narrowed to ₩6.8 billion in Q1 2026.
Posco recommitted to a 35%–40% annual shareholder return target for 2026–2028, as announced in its updated dividend and buyback policy.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management signaled caution about short-term steel market recovery, citing ongoing global downturns and geopolitical risks leading to higher input costs and transportation expenses. Its focus will remain on balancing the core steel business and expanding into high-tech growth areas, especially battery materials. Investors should watch closely for stabilization in segment margins and further visibility on return from the company's major battery and green steel investments.

Quarterly highlights

The steel business remained the largest contributor, but profitability eroded as prices for iron ore, coal, and nickel climbed, and end-market steel demand stayed weak globally. The group's market share in Korea held steady at 56.5% for crude steel production, but export growth stalled as demand in China lagged.

The rechargeable battery materials segment, which produces components like cathode and anode materials for electric vehicle batteries, posted double-digit sequential improvement in revenue and significantly reduced its operating loss. Investments in plants in Argentina, North America, and expansions in Korea advanced Posco's push to diversify away from cyclicality in steel.

The Board unveiled a new shareholder return plan targeting a return ratio of 35%–40% of net income to investors through a mix of dividends and buybacks. Final decisions on execution and payout levels will depend on future profits and board approval for each relevant period.

What Posco does

Posco is a diversified industrial company primarily engaged in the manufacture and sale of steel products, with growing businesses in infrastructure, trading, construction, and rechargeable battery materials.
