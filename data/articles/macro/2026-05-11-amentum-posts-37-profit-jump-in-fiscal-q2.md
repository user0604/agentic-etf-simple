Amentum (NYSE:AMTM) posted a steady quarter marked by strong profit growth and stable sales, as major new contract awards offset revenue headwinds. Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, signaling confidence in both revenue momentum and margin improvement as more high-growth contracts ramp. Leaders noted that execution on recent nuclear and digital infrastructure projects will be key to meeting projected gains for the year. Investors should watch for sequential revenue growth in the coming quarters as new business transitions to active delivery.

Quarterly Highlights

Despite virtually unchanged revenue, profit measures improved. Operating income jumped by more than a third, while adjusted diluted earnings per share grew 13%, reflecting progress on cost controls and lower intangible amortization. Adjusted EBITDA margin also moved higher as the company focused on higher-value contracts.

Amentum’s backlog reached $47.8 billion, rising 7% from a year ago after major new contract wins in the nuclear and predictive analytics sectors. The book-to-bill ratio -- a measure of orders booked to revenue recognized -- remained healthy at 1.2x, highlighting robust demand and revenue visibility for future periods.

Cash generation strengthened after prior quarter headwinds, with free cash flow surging more than four-fold. Management linked this improvement to normalized working capital and timely payments on completed projects, supporting ongoing debt reduction plans and future financial flexibility.
