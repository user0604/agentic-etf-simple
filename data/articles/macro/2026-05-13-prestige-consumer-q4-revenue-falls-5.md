Prestige Consumer Q4 Revenue Falls 5%

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 4:50 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Prestige Consumer Healthcare reported a challenging quarter as sales declined in its core categories.
Adjusted diluted earnings per share fell 6.8% while revenue dropped 5.0% compared to the same period last year.
Management projects moderate recovery for fiscal 2027, with 1–3% organic revenue growth and continued supply chain focus.

The latest quarter saw a setback for Prestige Consumer Healthcare (NYSE:PBH) as ongoing supply chain issues in eye and ear care hampered results. The leading over-the-counter health products company released fourth quarter and full-year fiscal 2026 results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter produced resilient cash flow and stable gross margin, but missed earlier guidance on revenue and profit.

Notable Developments
Revenue in the North American over-the-counter (OTC) healthcare segment declined 5.8% due to supply limits on Clear Eyes, Prestige’s key eye care brand.
While free cash flow for the quarter dipped 35.6% to $37.6 million, the company ended the year with a strong $246 million full-year total.
Prestige announced the planned acquisition of LaCorium Health, a therapeutic skin care specialist, to boost future growth and global reach.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management forecasts a modest rebound for fiscal 2027, with organic revenue growth between 1% and 3%, total revenue of $1.1–$1.12 billion, and adjusted diluted EPS of $4.42–$4.51. Leaders highlight variability in eye care supply, ongoing remediation of production capacity, and continued macro pressures as key risks. Investors should closely monitor the restoration of Clear Eyes inventory and the integration of new acquisitions to gauge Prestige’s path to recovery.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales in both North American and international OTC healthcare segments fell compared to the previous year, reflecting continued pressure from constrained eye care manufacturing and Middle East shipping disruptions. Despite these headwinds, Prestige’s gastrointestinal products, such as Fleet and Hydralyte, showed positive momentum and helped cushion some of the decline.

The company’s adjusted gross margin narrowed by 1.9 percentage points year over year, yet remained strong at 55.4% due to disciplined cost management and a stable product mix. Free cash flow generation, although down in the quarter, remained healthy throughout the year and supported sizable share repurchases totaling $156 million.

Prestige moved forward with its acquisition strategy by announcing the purchase of LaCorium Health, adding recognized therapeutic skin care brands and increasing its international presence. This deal, along with expected benefits from the Breathe Right brand purchase, is aimed at long-term revenue and profit growth.

What Prestige Consumer Healthcare Does

Prestige Consumer Healthcare develops, markets, and sells a broad portfolio of well-known over-the-counter health and wellness brands, targeting categories like eye care, gastrointestinal relief, and women’s health.
