The sharp rise in research expenses reflects investment in pivotal studies and material for potential commercial use.

The company reported a net loss as operating costs outpaced non-cash items that benefited results in the prior year. Last year’s net income was mainly due to gains from changes in the value of warrant liabilities—an accounting effect not repeated this quarter—alongside increased research and commercial readiness outlays.

The firm's cash position strengthened meaningfully following a $100 million equity raise and an additional potential $100 million agreement, supporting ongoing trials and pre-launch steps. These deals help extend the cash runway as Candel prepares for possible regulatory approval and commercialization of its lead therapies.
