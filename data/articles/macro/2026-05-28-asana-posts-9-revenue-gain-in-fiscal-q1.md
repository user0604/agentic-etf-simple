Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guides for second-quarter revenue of $213–$215 million, which would represent up to 9.2% growth year over year, and expects a lower operating margin compared to the first quarter. Leadership continues to take a cautious view, forecasting limited recovery in its product-led growth segment and emphasizing ongoing improvements in AI product adoption and cost management. Investors should watch for signs of re-acceleration in revenue growth tied to new AI offerings and stabilization of the product-led growth segment.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s move to launch AI Teammates for all customers, combined with the StackAI acquisition, advanced its strategy of integrating human and AI collaboration into workplace management. These initiatives are aimed at automating more complex processes and helping organizations adopt AI for daily operations while maintaining strong governance controls.

Profitability metrics showed notable improvement, with non-GAAP operating margin reaching 11.5% and net losses cut by 64% compared to the prior year. Adjusted free cash flow soared, supported by lower sales and marketing expenses and a reduction in stock-based compensation from $48.2 million last year to $36.3 million this quarter.

Customer metrics were solid: large enterprise accounts continued to grow, and net retention rates—measuring how much recurring revenue existing clients generate—improved for the fourth straight quarter, reaching 96–97% across major segments. However, revenue growth remains reliant on enterprise and AI offerings, with self-serve and small business customer segments still under pressure.
