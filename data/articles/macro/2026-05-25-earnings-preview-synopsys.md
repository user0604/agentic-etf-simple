Earnings Preview: Synopsys

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Synopsys (NASDAQ:SNPS), a leading provider of software tools for semiconductor and electronics design, is expected to report earnings for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Synopsys reported revenue of $2.41 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) of $3.77. Both figures beat analyst consensus estimates for the quarter. The company’s performance reflected continued strength in its core design automation business, robust demand for simulation and analysis solutions following the Ansys acquisition, and disciplined cost management. However, the Design IP segment showed ongoing weakness, and overall GAAP net income was lower than the prior year due to higher expenses related to the Ansys merger.

Positives
Revenue and EPS exceeded expectations: Synopsys delivered both revenue and non-GAAP EPS above consensus, driven by strong execution and timing of Ansys-related deals.
Strong performance in Design Automation: The segment saw significant growth, especially in hardware-assisted verification and AI-driven design tools, supporting the company’s leadership in electronic design automation (EDA).
Ansys integration progressing well: Ansys contributed approximately $886 million in revenue, and management reiterated full-year synergy and growth targets, indicating early success from the acquisition.
Healthy cash generation and balance sheet: The company generated $822 million in free cash flow and ended the quarter with $2.2 billion in cash and short-term investments, while fully repaying $4.3 billion in term loans.
Negatives
Design IP segment underperformed: Revenue in this segment declined 6% year over year and operating margin fell to 16.2%, reflecting muted demand and a transitional year as Synopsys refocuses its IP portfolio.
GAAP net income decreased: GAAP net income fell to $65 million from $296 million a year ago, mainly because of higher expenses and amortization costs related to the Ansys acquisition.
China revenue remains pressured: Excluding Ansys, revenue from China declined slightly year over year due to ongoing export controls and competitive pressures.
Restructuring charges impacted results: The company recorded $118 million in restructuring costs as part of a plan to realign resources, with the majority of workforce reductions expected in fiscal 2026.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Progress on Ansys integration and joint product launches: Investors should look for updates on the rollout of joint Synopsys-Ansys solutions, as management previously indicated initial launches would occur in the first half of 2026.
Design IP segment trajectory and impact of ARC processor divestiture: Watch for signs of improvement in Design IP revenue and operating margin, as well as updates on the pending sale of the processor IP business to GlobalFoundries.
AI-driven product adoption and monetization: Monitor customer uptake of Synopsys.ai and agent-based engineering tools, and any commentary on how these offerings are contributing to growth and changing the company’s business model.
China business stability and competitive landscape: Pay attention to management’s comments on demand trends and competitive dynamics in China, especially given ongoing export controls and local competition.
Execution on cost synergies and operating margin guidance: Assess progress toward the $400 million cost synergy target from the Ansys acquisition and whether the company is on track to deliver its full-year non-GAAP operating margin outlook.
