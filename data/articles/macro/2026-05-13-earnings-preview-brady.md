Earnings Preview: Brady

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

Brady (NYSE:BRC), a supplier of industrial identification and workplace safety products, is expected to report its third-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, before the market opens.

Q2 FY2026 Recap

In the second quarter of fiscal 2026, Brady delivered another period of growth, marking its twentieth consecutive quarter of organic sales increases. Net sales rose to $384.1 million, a 7.7% increase year over year, with organic sales up 1.6%. The Americas and Asia region led with 3.1% organic growth, while Europe and Australia saw a 1.1% organic decline. Gross margin improved to 50.6%, up from 49.3% a year earlier, reflecting a favorable product mix and benefits from prior cost-cutting actions. Adjusted diluted earnings per share reached $1.09, which met most analyst expectations but missed the highest consensus by a penny. Revenue exceeded analyst estimates.

Profitability was supported by higher sales of engineered products and ongoing efficiency gains. The company continued to invest in research and development, with R&D spending rising to 6.3% of sales, and ended the quarter with a net cash position. Management increased the lower end of its full-year adjusted EPS guidance, now expecting $4.95 to $5.15 per share. The company also highlighted the launch of its new i4311 industrial label printer and ongoing integration of recent acquisitions.

Positives
Gross margin improvement reflects product mix and cost actions: The increase to 50.6% gross margin demonstrates the company’s shift toward higher-value engineered products and the benefits of last year’s facility closures.
Strong cash generation and net cash position: Operating cash flow grew nearly 35% year over year, supporting continued investment and financial flexibility.
Continued investment in R&D to drive future growth: R&D spending rose almost 30% from the prior year, supporting new product launches and long-term competitiveness.
Improved profitability in Europe and Australia despite weak sales: Segment profit rose 35.5% as cost reductions more than offset sluggish demand.
Negatives
Slower organic sales growth in key regions: Organic sales in the Americas grew just 1.4%, and Europe and Australia saw a 1.1% decline, highlighting reliance on Asia for overall growth.
Limited pricing power in the Americas: Management noted that the recent growth was almost entirely from volume, not price increases, which may limit margin expansion if costs rise.
Ongoing macroeconomic headwinds in Europe: Manufacturing weakness, especially in Germany, France, and the U.K., continues to weigh on sales in the region.
What to Watch in Q3 FY2026's Report
Organic sales trends by region: Investors should monitor whether organic growth in the Americas rebounds and if Europe and Australia can return to positive territory as signaled by management.
Gross margin sustainability: Watch for continued improvement or stability in gross margin, especially as product mix shifts and cost actions annualize.
Progress on R&D and new product launches: Updates on adoption of the i4311 printer and pipeline products will indicate if increased R&D spending is translating into market opportunities.
Integration and performance of recent acquisitions: Performance of the Microfluidic Solutions, Mecco, and AB&R businesses should be tracked, as these acquisitions are expected to contribute to growth and margin expansion.
Management’s guidance and commentary on macroeconomic risks: Pay attention to any adjustments in full-year guidance or comments on manufacturing activity, tariffs, and inflation, as these factors could impact future results.
