Intuit Posts 10% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q3

The Motley Fool Team

May 20, 2026, 4:17 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Intuit posted broad-based growth, strengthened by its AI-driven platform and product expansion.
Earnings per share (Non-GAAP) rose 10% year over year.
Management raised its full-year outlook but warned of near-term disruption from a large workforce reduction.

Intuit (NASDAQ:INTU) delivered another strong quarter, with broad-based growth across its core business segments and continued momentum from its AI-driven product strategy. The financial software leader reported higher revenue, driven by gains in both Consumer and Global Business Solutions, as well as rapid growth at Credit Karma. Released on May 20, 2026, for its fiscal third quarter (ended April 30, 2026), the results included an increased outlook but also highlighted caution as the company begins a significant restructuring. Overall, the quarter showed strength in key business lines, even as risks from workforce changes and segment durability remain.

Notable developments
Revenue rose 10%, with Consumer segment revenue of $5.3 billion (up 8%) and Global Business Solutions climbing 15% to $3.3 billion.
The company repurchased $1.6 billion in stock and raised its dividend by 15%, reflecting a strong commitment to shareholder returns.
Intuit announced a 17% workforce reduction, expecting related restructuring charges of $300-$340 million, mainly recognized in the fourth quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year fiscal 2026 revenue guidance to $21.341-$21.374 billion, with expected growth rates improving in key segments such as Credit Karma and Global Business Solutions. Leadership also forecasted margin gains and continued EPS growth, but noted that the recent workforce reduction could temporarily disrupt operations. Investors should monitor the execution and impact of these restructuring efforts as the year continues.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s AI-driven expert platform remains a primary growth driver, supporting new customer gains and higher average revenue per user. For example, TurboTax Live (an AI-assisted tax preparation service) is set to make up over half of TurboTax revenue for the year and is expected to see customer growth of 38%.

Credit Karma outperformed, with segment revenue up 15% as higher personal loan and insurance product sales contributed to rising guidance for the full year. At the same time, Global Business Solutions segment revenue grew 15%, led by a strong 22% gain in QuickBooks Online Accounting, reflecting success in mid-market and online-focused offerings.

While consolidated performance was strong, the notable workforce reduction of 17%—impacting roughly one in six employees—is a major operational change. Management expects near-term cost savings and margin benefits but acknowledges some risk of disruption in the quarters ahead. The increase in the quarterly dividend to $1.20 per share affirms management’s confidence in its cash flow and financial position.

What Intuit does

Intuit develops financial software and platforms that help individuals and businesses manage taxes, accounting, and personal finance through products like TurboTax, QuickBooks, Credit Karma, and Mailchimp.
