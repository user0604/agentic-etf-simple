Earnings Preview: Best Buy

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Best Buy (NYSE:BBY), the largest U.S. specialty retailer of consumer electronics and appliances, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Best Buy reported revenue of $13.8 billion and adjusted earnings per share (EPS) of $2.61. Adjusted operating income rate was 5%. Comparable sales declined 0.8% year over year, which was within the company’s guidance range. Profitability came in better than expected, while sales were slightly softer due to weaker demand in November and December, offset by stronger results late in the quarter. The company’s results beat analyst estimates for profitability but met expectations on sales. Market share was stable, with notable growth in computing and mobile phones, but declines in home theater and appliances.

Positives
Profitability exceeded expectations: Adjusted operating income rate and EPS were both slightly higher than last year and above consensus, showing effective cost controls and improved gross profit contributions from new business lines.
Growth in key categories: Computing and mobile phones posted positive comparable sales, driven by laptop and desktop demand and improved partnerships with mobile carriers.
Expansion of new revenue streams: The ads and marketplace businesses grew, contributing to gross profit and showing early signs of scaling.
Customer experience improvements: Customer satisfaction scores rose to the highest in over a decade, with faster online fulfillment and better in-store service metrics.
Negatives
Sales softness in core categories: Home theater and appliances saw notable declines, reflecting weaker consumer demand for big-ticket items and a highly promotional environment.
Online revenue declined: Online sales decreased 2.3% on a comparable basis, indicating ongoing challenges in digital sales growth despite overall e-commerce investments.
International margin pressure: The international segment saw a drop in gross profit rate, mainly due to lower product margins and supply chain costs.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Comparable sales growth and category trends: Management guided to approximately 1% comparable sales growth for Q1, with February down 1% and expected improvement in March and April. Watch for performance in computing, mobile phones, and emerging categories like AI glasses and 3D printers.
Gross profit rate expansion: The company expects gross profit rate to improve year over year, driven by growth in ads and marketplace. Investors should look for confirmation of this trend and its impact on overall margins.
Impact of memory cost inflation and supply constraints: Rising memory component costs and supply uncertainty could affect computing margins and inventory levels. The company is working with vendors to mitigate these impacts, but category-level margin pressure is possible.
Status of store and digital initiatives: Best Buy is opening six new domestic stores and expanding in-store vendor experiences, while also enhancing digital platforms through partnerships with OpenAI and Google. Progress on these initiatives, as well as early results from the smaller store model and digital enhancements, will be important to track.
Vendor support and promotional activity: Investors should monitor any changes in vendor-funded labor and promotional support, especially in computing and appliances, as these can influence both sales and profitability in a competitive environment.
