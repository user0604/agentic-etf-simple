Village Farms Q1 Revenue Jumps 27%

Key Takeaways
Business win as global cannabis segments drive strong growth and profitability.
Revenue rose 27% year over year.
Management expects continued international expansion and sales growth through year end.

Global cannabis strength propelled Village Farms International (NASDAQ:VFF) to another profitable quarter, with surging international sales and expanded margins. The diversified producer and supplier of cannabis and produce continued executing on its global growth plans, especially in medical exports to Europe. The latest earnings release, covering the quarter ended March 31 (reported May 11, 2026), underscores both operational momentum and some areas for closer financial monitoring. Overall, the quarter reflected robust international demand and improved profitability, with cash flow volatility and Canadian sales deceleration as key watchpoints.

Notable Developments
International medical cannabis exports jumped 171% to $14.6 million, marking a record quarter for overseas sales in Europe.
The cannabis segment gross margin expanded to 43%, exceeding management’s 30–40% target for the fourth consecutive quarter.
Cash and cash equivalents declined to $50.5 million primarily due to a $15 million tax payment, capital expenditures of $9.2 million, and $6.4 million in share repurchases.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects facility expansions in Canada and the Netherlands to drive higher cannabis sales throughout the remainder of the year and into 2027. The upcoming quarter is expected to see initial benefits from these capacity investments, particularly as the Netherlands facility ramps up production. Investors should watch for a rebound in the company’s cash balance as operational cash generation is expected to improve following this quarter’s large tax payment.

Quarterly Highlights

International medical cannabis sales became a growth engine for the company, with European exports—particularly to Germany—increasing sharply and now representing a significant portion of overall sales growth.

The cannabis segment’s profitability improved further, as its gross margin of 43% outpaced management’s target range and marked the fourth straight quarter of margin expansion, reflecting production efficiency gains.

While international and Netherlands cannabis posted strong gains, Canadian retail branded cannabis sales rose a modest 5%, highlighting slower growth domestically compared to the surging global markets.

What Village Farms International Does

Village Farms International operates large-scale controlled environment agricultural facilities, focusing on high-quality cannabis production for Canada, the US, and international export markets.
