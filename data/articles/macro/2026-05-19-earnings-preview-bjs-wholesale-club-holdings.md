Earnings Preview: BJ's Wholesale Club Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:05 AM

Comments

BJ's Wholesale Club Holdings (NYSE:BJ), a membership-based warehouse retailer focused on groceries and general merchandise, is expected to report its first quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 21, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

BJ's delivered a solid finish to fiscal 2025, with net sales for the fourth quarter reaching approximately $5.4 billion, up 5.5% year over year. Merchandise comparable club sales (excluding gas) rose 2.6%, marking the 13th consecutive quarter of market share gains and the 16th straight quarter of traffic growth. Membership fee income increased 10.9% to about $129.8 million, supported by strong new member sign-ups and a fee increase that took effect in January 2025. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter were $0.96, a 3.2% increase year over year, which met analyst estimates. The company opened seven new clubs in the quarter, contributing to a record 14 new openings for the year. Digital sales penetration reached 16%, with digitally enabled sales up 31% from the prior year.

Positives
Membership growth reached a record high: The company added over 500,000 new members in the year, ending with more than 8 million total members. This growth supports long-term revenue and renewal stability.
Traffic and market share continued to increase: BJ’s achieved its 16th consecutive quarter of traffic growth and 13th straight quarter of market share gains, highlighting the strength of its value proposition in a cautious consumer environment.
Digital engagement accelerated: Digitally enabled sales penetration rose to 16%, with 31% growth in digital sales and record Black Friday and Cyber Monday performance, reflecting successful adoption of omnichannel services.
Strong performance in new club openings: The 14 new clubs opened in 2025 outperformed expectations in sales, membership, and profits, demonstrating effective expansion strategy.
Negatives
Merchandise margin rate declined: The merchandise margin rate fell by about 50 basis points year over year, driven by a higher mix of lower-margin general merchandise, particularly consumer electronics.
SG&A expenses saw slight deleverage: Selling, general, and administrative expenses increased as a percentage of sales, mainly due to costs associated with new club openings and ongoing investments in strategic initiatives.
General merchandise categories remain variable: While consumer electronics and apparel performed well, home and seasonal categories lagged, reflecting ongoing challenges in discretionary spending and exposure to tariffs.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Comparable sales excluding gasoline: Management guided to 2% to 3% growth for the full year, with expectations for the lowest comps in the first quarter due to a tough comparison period and weather impacts from winter storm Fern.
Membership fee income trends: The recent annual membership fee increase took effect in January 2025 and drove strong growth in Q4, but management expects growth to moderate as the company fully laps the fee increase in Q1 FY2026.
Performance of new club openings, especially in Texas: The company is opening its first clubs in the Dallas–Fort Worth area in the first half of the year. Early membership sign-ups are ahead of plan, and investors should watch for initial sales and engagement metrics.
Digital sales and omnichannel adoption: With digital penetration at 16% and continued investment in services like Express Pay and same-day delivery, the pace of digital growth and its contribution to overall sales will be important indicators of future momentum.
Merchandise margin and product mix: Investors should monitor how changes in mix, especially in general merchandise and private label brands, impact margins as BJ’s continues to invest in value and navigates tariff and inflationary pressures.
