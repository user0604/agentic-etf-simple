Notable Developments
Sales climbed 9.5% to $521.7 million, with all segments reporting gains, including a large boost from acquisitions.
Free cash flow improved sharply to $26.3 million, reversing a negative figure one year ago, thanks to stronger operating cash generation and lower capital expenditure.
Operating income margin slipped to 8.1%, and net income edged down, as higher selling, general, and administrative costs and interest expense weighed on profitability.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide new full-year revenue or earnings targets in the quarter’s release but noted ongoing momentum and “continued execution” of its strategy. According to leadership, the current focus remains on integrating recent acquisitions and driving further growth across business segments. Investors should monitor whether margin pressures ease and if improved free cash flow can be maintained across coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth was driven by both organic sales and contributions from three completed acquisitions, which together added $40.7 million to sales for the quarter. Service Centers remained the largest segment, posting a 3.3% rise in sales, while Innovative Pumping Solutions led overall growth with a 37.7% increase due to water and wastewater projects.

Supply Chain Services recovered from last year’s minor contraction, registering a 2.7% sales gain and an improved operating income margin. Overall, segment results showed successful diversification, but rising costs weighed on total profitability, with net income and earnings per share both slightly lower than one year ago.

The company’s balance sheet reflected active capital deployment: cash fell to $213.4 million at quarter-end, down from $303.8 million at the previous year-end, largely as a result of acquisitions. Total debt was unchanged at $844.7 million, and the leverage ratio edged up modestly as measured by net debt to EBITDA.

What DXP Enterprises Does

DXP Enterprises supplies a wide range of industrial products and engineered solutions to customers in maintenance, repair, and operations across sectors such as manufacturing, water, and energy.
