Notable Developments
Robotaxi services revenue climbed over 395% year over year, reaching $8.6 million, now more than 25% of group total.
Average weekly paid Robotaxi orders in May were up 119% compared to January, and China's registered user base more than tripled year over year.
Free cash outflow (Non-GAAP) increased to $86.7 million, up nearly 47%, indicating a faster investment burn as the company scales operations.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its 2026 Robotaxi revenue goal, now aiming for more than 3.5 times last year’s level, and lifted its fleet target to over 3,500 units by year-end. The company continues to expand domestically and internationally, but did not signal a timeline for profitability. Investors should monitor for evidence of improving margins and sustainable unit economics, particularly as global deployment ramps up.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s Robotaxi segment was a major growth driver, with revenue surging nearly fourfold on the launch and ramp-up of its Gen-7 autonomous vehicles. Strong user adoption supported this, with paid orders and registered users in China showing rapid expansion despite higher vehicle fares than standard ride-hailing services.

Operations expanded into new cities and countries, including launches in core urban districts of Guangzhou, a debut in Croatia, and ongoing projects in Dubai and Singapore. The active international push increased the fleet size to over 1,700 vehicles, with a target of reaching 20 cities by year-end.

Despite higher revenue and production scale, gross margin remained flat near 16%, and operating expenses continued to grow, especially in selling, general, and administrative costs. Free cash outflow accelerated, reflecting investments in technology and geographic footprint.

What Pony Ai Does

Pony Ai develops and operates autonomous vehicle technology platforms, offering robotaxi, robotruck, and intelligent driving solutions in China and worldwide.
