Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its full-year non-GAAP operating income guidance to $115–125 million, reflecting higher confidence in operational efficiency as emerging software and cloud offerings scale. Revenue guidance for the full year remained unchanged at $1.195–1.205 billion, implying 20% growth at the midpoint. Investors should focus on gross margin trends, as management called out pressures tied to the expansion of new cloud and AI-driven products.

Quarterly Highlights

Large customer growth continued, with the number of customers generating more than $100,000 in annual recurring revenue rising 17% to 1,702. This signals success with attracting bigger clients and driving expansion into enterprise accounts, though the growth rate eased slightly when compared to the previous period.

Gross margin slipped to 77% on a non-GAAP basis, a two percentage-point reduction year over year, as investment in cloud, AI, and data solutions contributed to higher costs. Tomer Weingarten described the margin level as "stable" and "best in industry," but acknowledged the decline and said it would monitor the trend.

Adjusted free cash flow showed improvement, reaching a margin of 22%. However, operating cash flow margin dropped to 14% after a significant tax payment, which management highlighted as a one-time swing and not expected to reoccur every quarter.
