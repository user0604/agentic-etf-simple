Quarterly Highlights

Datacentrex’s operational fleet grew to 3,094 Scrypt ASIC miners during the period, marking a full transition to scaled digital mining infrastructure. This expansion drove top-line performance but brought significant increases in depreciation and stock-based compensation, leading to a wider net loss versus the prior year.

Gross margin compressed from 52.5% a year ago to 23.5%, as higher input costs—most notably power and facility costs—outpaced revenue gains. The revenue surge was achieved through marketplace sales of hashrate, showing solid market acceptance, yet operational leverage has not preserved profitability.

At quarter-end, Datacentrex reported $42.5 million in cash and over $5 million in digital assets, with no debt and aggregate liquidity exceeding $59 million when including public offering receivables. This strong financial position allows continued support for equipment investments and positions the company to pursue growth amid ongoing digital asset market volatility.
