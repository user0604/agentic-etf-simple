Uniti Group Revenue Jumps 236% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 8:23 AM

Key Takeaways
Uniti Group posted a record quarter for fiber revenue and bookings, but net losses continued.
Uniti Group revenue was $987.5 million in Q1 2026, up from $293.9 million in Q1 2025, primarily reflecting the impact of the Windstream merger and not a comparable year-over-year increase.
Full-year guidance was reiterated as management expects further "lumpy" results.

Uniti Group (NASDAQ:UNIT) posted a mixed set of results as new fiber infrastructure bookings and revenue reached all-time highs, yet net losses widened in the latest quarter. The national fiber-optic network specialist reported its first period consolidating the Windstream merger, which expanded its scale and capabilities. Released on May 11, 2026, these first-quarter results mark progress on Uniti's years-long strategic transformation. Overall, the quarter delivered major growth milestones but ongoing net losses highlight persistent financial challenges.

Notable developments
Fiber Infrastructure revenue increased 13%, with high customer bookings and a high level of upfront payments received for network projects.
Kinetic Consumer Fiber revenue climbed 26%, supported by nearly 39,000 new fiber broadband additions—its best quarter ever in gross additions.
Total company net losses widened to ($85.8 million) despite higher Adjusted EBITDA, reflecting high capital expenditures and lumpy one-time deal recognition.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its full-year 2026 financial guidance, expecting continued growth but cautioning about volatile, non-recurring revenue from large fiber deals. Leadership aims to achieve year-over-year growth for all of 2027 and is targeting 3.5 million homes passed with fiber by 2029.

Quarterly highlights

Fiber Infrastructure performed strongly, notching its third-highest quarter ever for new monthly recurring revenue bookings. The segment captured significant upfront payments from customers, which helped offset ongoing heavy investment in network construction.

Kinetic, which focuses on residential and small business broadband, reported record new consumer fiber adds and a 26% rise in related revenues. Churn in its fiber base reached its lowest level ever, showing improving customer retention for upgraded broadband services.

Despite progress in the core business, net losses at the group level persisted. High capital expenditures of $349.2 million and the recognition of one-time, non-recurring revenue from sales-type leases continue to mask the underlying recurring earnings power of the business.

What Uniti Group does

Uniti Group operates a national fiber-optic broadband network, providing wholesale and retail high-speed connectivity to carriers, enterprises, and residential customers across the United States.
