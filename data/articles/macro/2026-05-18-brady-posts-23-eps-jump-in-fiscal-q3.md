Key Takeaways
Brady reported record adjusted quarterly earnings.
Adjusted EPS increased 23% year over year.
Management raised full-year guidance, but investors should monitor costs and the pending Honeywell PSS acquisition.

Brady (NYSE:BRC) surprised with strong organic growth and record profitability in the third quarter. The identification solutions and safety products specialist posted robust sales gains across all regions. Its fiscal third quarter earnings report, released on May 18, 2026, covers the three months ended April 30. Overall, the quarter marked clear progress on both operational and financial fronts.

Notable Developments
Sales rose 13.8%, with organic growth of 8.2% led by America & Asia at 10.1% and Europe & Australia at 4.5%.
Record adjusted EPS of $1.50, up 23%, supported by improved gross and operating margins as R&D spending climbed to 5.4% of sales.
Management raised fiscal 2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $5.20–$5.30, excluding the pending Honeywell PSS acquisition.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Brady management lifted its adjusted earnings outlook for fiscal 2026, reflecting confidence in ongoing product launches and operational improvements. The Honeywell Productivity Solutions and Services deal, expected to close in the second half of 2026, is a major focus for upcoming quarters. Investors should watch for cost discipline and updates on acquisition integration.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales performance was strong in both main regions, with America & Asia posting 14.4% growth and Europe & Australia rising 12.6%. Organic growth far surpassed last quarter’s levels and management’s earlier forecast, paced by strong demand for identification solutions including new printer launches and expanding data center projects.

Brady invested $23.5 million in research and development this period, raising R&D to 5.4% of sales. Management credits this spending for producing a pipeline of new products and fueling record adjusted profitability, even as selling and administrative expenses also increased.

Acquisition activity contributed to growth, with completed bolt-on deals adding 2.1% to total sales. The announced acquisition of Honeywell PSS is positioned to expand Brady’s reach into data collection and industrial automation, but will require careful execution to avoid integration setbacks.

What Brady Does

Brady designs and manufactures identification solutions, safety products, labeling systems, and specialty materials for industrial and commercial customers worldwide.
