ICL Group Posts 22% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 6:16 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
ICL Group posted strong sales and profit growth, raising its full-year adjusted EBITDA guidance.
Earnings per share (adjusted) rose 22%; revenue was up 14% from the prior year.
Management cited continued raw material cost and currency risks while maintaining a positive outlook.

ICL Group (NYSE:ICL) delivered broad-based gains in sales and profitability, lifting its full-year profit guidance on the back of robust demand and improved product pricing. The leading producer of potash, specialty minerals, and innovative agricultural and chemical solutions operates globally, supplying essential inputs for agriculture, industry, and food markets. The company released its first-quarter results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ending March 31.

Overall, the quarter reflected positive momentum, with resilience in key segments, though inflation and foreign exchange headwinds continued.

Notable developments
The Potash segment delivered strong growth, with sales up 24% and the average price per ton rising from $300 to $362.
Free cash flow improved to $61 million after a negative result in the prior year, while the dividend payout remained steady at $69 million for the quarter.
Phosphate Solutions saw higher sales but faced margin compression from raw material costs, as sulfur prices rose by more than 100% year over year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its profit guidance for fiscal 2026, with adjusted EBITDA expected between $1.5 billion and $1.7 billion, up $100 million from the previous range. It reiterated priorities in cost controls, efficiency, and dynamic management of risks related to raw materials and currency. Investors should watch the impact of ongoing sulfur cost inflation and foreign exchange movements on future profitability.

Quarterly highlights

The Potash segment saw a notable rise, supported by higher average prices and stronger production. The segment reported $503 million in sales and a 46% jump in adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP) to $172 million. Management pointed out that potash affordability and market selection helped drive profit gains, even as annual plant maintenance in the Dead Sea and Spain loomed for the first half.

In Industrial Products, improved pricing for bromine-related chemicals and specialty minerals lifted both revenue and margins. Electronics demand picked up, but construction markets remained soft, capping the benefit for this segment.

Phosphate Solutions showed increased revenue at $679 million, driven by growth in specialty food additives and the addition of new customers in China, but EBITDA margin narrowed to 19% as sulfur prices more than doubled. Growing Solutions also advanced sales, helped by specialty fertilizer volume and a new water-soluble fertilizer plant in India, though North America was flat and Brazil saw continued competition and uncertainty.

What ICL Group does

ICL Group produces fertilizers, specialty minerals, and advanced chemical solutions used in agriculture, food, and industrial products worldwide.
