Key Takeaways
Hormel Foods posted another quarter of growth in its protein brands, but continued to see pressure in Retail segment volumes.
Adjusted diluted earnings per share (Non-GAAP) increased 14.3% from the prior year.

Hormel Foods (NYSE:HRL) delivered solid results in the latest quarter as its branded protein lines continued to gain ground, despite soft volume in some categories. The leading food manufacturer, known for SPAM, Jennie-O, and Planters, reported results for its fiscal second quarter on May 27, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed good progress in profitability and organic growth, but also highlighted ongoing challenges from logistics costs and softer Retail sales.

Notable Developments
Adjusted diluted earnings per share rose to $0.40, a 14.3% increase over last year, reflecting improved operating income excluding one-time charges.
Organic revenue, which strips out the impact of divestitures, grew by 3.3% from the previous year, marking the sixth straight quarter of such growth.
The quarterly dividend was raised to $0.2925 per share, continuing Hormel’s long-standing track record of dividend payments.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance for organic net sales growth of 1% to 4% and adjusted earnings per share between $1.43 and $1.51. They cited ongoing momentum in branded protein sales and successful portfolio streamlining. Investors should watch for continued progress in cost control efforts and whether Retail volumes stabilize in the upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The Retail segment delivered flat revenue, but profit in this business improved 13%, helped by better margins in turkey products and ongoing cost savings — even as volume fell 2% year over year.

The Foodservice segment grew net sales by 6% as demand stayed strong in premium prepared proteins and branded pepperoni, leading to an 11% jump in segment profit.

International operations contributed a 4% boost in sales and a 20% leap in segment profit, steered by robust SPAM exports and growth in China.

What Hormel Foods Does

Hormel Foods is a leading producer of branded food products, focusing on refrigerated, shelf-stable, and value-added protein items for consumers, restaurants, and international customers.
