Helios Technologies Reports 17% Q1 Revenue Gain

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 5:10 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Helios Technologies delivered one of its strongest starts to the year, raising its full-year sales and earnings guidance.
Revenue grew 17% while non-GAAP earnings per share climbed 82% compared to the prior year.
Management projects growth for the rest of fiscal 2026 but cautions about challenges in the Asia-Pacific Hydraulics market and ongoing global risks.

Helios Technologies (NYSE:HLIO) delivered a business win with one of the highest sales quarters in its history, driven by strength across its core electronics and hydraulics segments. The leading developer of highly engineered hydraulic and electronic control solutions for industrial and mobile equipment raised its sales and earnings outlook after exceeding its own guidance for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 (released May 11, 2026). Overall, the period combined notable margin gains and robust cash flow with a stronger capital return program, although pressures remain in certain Asia-Pacific businesses.

Notable Developments
Revenue reached $228.4 million, a 17% increase, and exceeded the company's own guidance for the period.
Non-GAAP diluted EPS rose to $0.80, marking an 82% gain versus last year, and operating margin improved by over 4 percentage points to 13.1%.
The quarterly dividend was raised 33% to $0.12 per share, and $4.6 million worth of shares were repurchased.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 sales outlook to $840–$870 million (up from $820–$860 million) and lifted its adjusted EPS guidance by $0.15 at both ends of the range. Leaders expect continued strong execution, especially in the Americas and Europe, but highlighted ongoing challenges in the Asia-Pacific Hydraulics segment as a key area to monitor.

Quarterly Highlights

Growth was broad-based, with the Electronics segment posting the fastest revenue increase at 29%, reflecting strong demand for control products used in recreational vehicles and industrial machinery. Margins rose sharply in both the Hydraulics and Electronics businesses, driven by a combination of higher sales volumes, ongoing cost controls, and post-divestiture efficiency improvements after Helios exited Custom Fluidpower in the prior year.

Operational cash flow reached a record $24 million, enabling the company to pay down net debt to $284 million, the lowest since the third quarter of 2020. This financial improvement supported the 33% dividend increase and management’s decision to buy back $4.6 million in shares during the quarter. The balance sheet showed more flexibility with net leverage now at 1.6 times adjusted EBITDA, down from 2.7 times a year earlier.

Regionally, sales in the Americas and Europe both grew more than 20%, helping offset a 6% decline in total Asia-Pacific revenue. Notably, Hydraulics sales in Asia-Pacific dropped 14%, highlighting persistent weakness that management flagged as a risk for the year ahead.

What Helios Technologies Does

Helios Technologies designs and manufactures motion control and electronic control solutions for hydraulics and electronics systems serving a range of industrial equipment markets worldwide.
