Notable developments

Net business wins reached $2.87 billion in the quarter, reflecting strong commercial activity. Profitability declined as both non-GAAP earnings per share and adjusted EBITDA fell by more than 25% compared to the prior year. Management completed its accounting investigation and restated results for prior years, reducing reported backlog by $3.9 billion due to a policy change.

Management outlook and what to watch

Leadership expects fiscal 2026 revenue between $7.85 billion and $8.15 billion and adjusted earnings per share of $10.00 to $11.00, both below the just-ended year. The company highlighted ongoing margin pressure and continued improvements to internal controls as priorities. Investors should watch how quickly enhanced technology and process controls help earnings recover in future quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The company reported robust commercial activity, with net business wins outpacing revenue and raising its year-end backlog to $21.8 billion. However, management restated both 2023 and 2024 revenues due to accounting issues, and the updated backlog figure now reflects a new method for cancellations. Bookings momentum appeared solid, yet full-year profit metrics marked significant declines, and free cash flow was lower compared to the prior year.

Financial performance was mixed: while revenue increased and new contracts were secured, the bottom line suffered. Adjusted EBITDA margin compressed to 15.5% for the quarter, a decrease from the prior year period. This margin squeeze, combined with higher operating costs, led to a year-over-year earnings drop despite continued investments in automation and artificial intelligence tools aimed at boosting efficiency in future periods.

Management emphasized that the Audit Committee’s investigation is complete and restated results will better reflect revenue and backlog. The company announced policy changes for handling customer cancellations and enhanced disclosures, aiming for stronger transparency and regulatory compliance. Cash outflows included $750 million for share repurchases, and net debt at year end stood at $2.8 billion, with a ratio of net debt to adjusted EBITDA at 1.8 times.
