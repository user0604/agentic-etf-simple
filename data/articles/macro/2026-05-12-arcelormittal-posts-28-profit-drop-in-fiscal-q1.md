Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects to generate positive free cash flow for fiscal 2026, highlighting anticipated benefits from new European trade policies and decarbonization projects. The key area for investors to monitor is whether these policy changes, such as the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism and tariff rate quota, will lead to stronger and more consistent cash generation. Management also plans to maintain dividends and reduce net debt if cash flow improves as projected.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales rose in both Europe and North America, with North America seeing an 8.3% increase from the previous quarter and Europe up 10.6%, both supported by higher steel prices and shipment volumes. Europe’s gains followed the introduction of new trade rules and stronger domestic shipments, though profit margins there remain below historical averages.

Profitability declined as cost pressures, including energy and regulatory expenses, outpaced pricing improvements in some regions. Net income and earnings per share dropped year over year, partly due to higher financial charges and a less favorable product mix in certain markets.

Cash flow was negative for the quarter, mainly due to a $1.5 billion seasonal investment in working capital and $1.3 billion in capital expenditures, including spending on green steel projects and mining expansion in Liberia. Net debt increased to $9.3 billion, underscoring the importance of improved cash generation as new projects come online.
