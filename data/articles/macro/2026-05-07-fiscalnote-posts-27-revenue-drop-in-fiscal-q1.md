FiscalNote Posts 27% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 5:09 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue fell significantly as the company lost several large enterprise customers.
Annual recurring revenue declined 14% year over year.
Management reaffirmed its revenue and cash flow guidance for fiscal 2026.

Policy data provider FiscalNote (OTC:NOTE) delivered results that reflected the ongoing loss of key customers and falling sales. The company focuses on policy intelligence through its proprietary AI-enabled platform, PolicyNote, serving corporations, governments, and organizations around the world. First quarter results, released May 7, 2026 for the period ended March 31, revealed sharp revenue declines but progress on cost controls and AI product launches. Overall, the quarter continued a difficult revenue trend, though management maintained its outlook for improved cash flow ahead.

Notable Developments
Total revenue dropped 27% as customer churn, platform transition, and reduced discretionary spending weighed on sales.
Annual recurring revenue, a key measure of subscription strength, declined 14% from the prior year to $75.7 million.
The quarter marked the full migration of all customers to the AI-native PolicyNote platform, replacing legacy offerings.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for fiscal 2026, targeting $80–83 million in revenue and positive free cash flow on a next-twelve-month basis beginning with the quarter starting April 1, 2026. Cost savings and product streamlining support these goals despite recent customer losses. Investors should watch for improvement in recurring revenue, as stabilizing this metric is essential for future growth.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure for the quarter fell sharply year over year, driven by the loss of several large enterprise customers who did not migrate to PolicyNote. Subscription revenue now accounts for 95% of total sales, underscoring a shift to a more subscription-focused business model following recent divestitures.

Cost controls resulted in adjusted operating expenses falling 11% when removing one-time items. Gross margin improved to 79% on a GAAP basis, and adjusted EBITDA, while down from the prior year, remained positive as expense reductions helped offset revenue losses.

FiscalNote launched several notable innovations during the period, including a major expansion of its PolicyNote API and new AI capabilities for developer and enterprise use cases. While these product updates have drawn early interest, their contribution to reversing the ARR decline is not yet quantifiable.

What FiscalNote Does

FiscalNote delivers AI-powered policy data and analysis tools to organizations through a subscription platform designed to interpret legislative, regulatory, and governmental information worldwide.
