Notable developments
No new revenue was reported as the company remains in a pre-commercial phase, focusing on site selection and technology validation.
Operating expenses decreased to $4.14 million, down from $8.68 million the prior year, mainly due to fewer asset impairments.
The planned acquisition of Lion Energy was called off, resulting in a $0.44 million provision for expected credit losses related to the partner loan.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains focused on advancing its first commercial recycling facility, aiming to finalize site selection, complete engineering work, and strengthen commercial partnerships. No binding revenue-producing contracts were announced, and no formal guidance was provided regarding timelines or future revenue. Investors should closely watch for commitments to construction, project financing, or the signing of commercial offtake agreements in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The absence of revenue in the period reflected the continued pre-commercial status, but the company reported over 5,000 cumulative operational hours at its demonstration plant and successfully validated the production of battery-grade lithium carbonate and high-purity manganese sulfate. These technical milestones are critical for future customer and regulatory qualification.

While operating expenses fell significantly versus last year—primarily because the previous year included over $5 million in asset impairments—general and administrative costs rose compared to the prior year. At the same time, cash reserves dropped from $10.8 million at year-end to $6.8 million, indicating that continued investment in project development activities is consuming available resources in the absence of revenue.

The company decided not to move forward with its previously proposed Lion Energy acquisition after due diligence. The exposure to Lion Energy, through an outstanding loan, led to a provision for expected credit losses, while the deal’s withdrawal removed an anticipated near-term revenue source and forced management to consider other strategic options.

What Aqua Metals does

Aqua Metals develops and seeks to commercialize technology for recycling lithium-ion batteries and extracting battery-grade critical minerals for use in energy storage and electric vehicle applications.
