Notable Developments
Total revenue grew to R$ 134.8 million, up 5.7% from the year-ago period, driven by robust activity in financial advisory and capital markets.
Administrative expenses climbed 48.8% to R$ 41.1 million.
The company declared an interim dividend of R$ 18.9 million for the quarter but did not announce any new dividend increase.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide any formal revenue or earnings targets for the rest of fiscal 2026. It highlighted ongoing macroeconomic and regulatory challenges within Brazil, but maintained that the company’s strong capital base and client activity should support continued business growth. Investors should pay close attention to operating costs in future quarters, as further expense increases could affect profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw continued strength in core advisory services, with four financial advisory transactions announced totaling R$ 6.1 billion. Debt capital markets activity remained strong, producing R$ 2.4 billion in new issuances across 19 structured operations, showing the company's role in helping clients raise funds and manage financial risks.

Despite growth on the top line, profitability was pressured as operating expenses surged to R$ 91.1 million, reflecting higher personnel and administrative costs.

Wealth management assets under advisory reached R$ 6.1 billion, though this figure was partially offset by depreciation of the US dollar. The company’s balance sheet remains strong, with a Basel capital ratio of 22.4%, well above the minimum required by Brazil’s central bank.

What Brbi Br Partners Does

Brbi Br Partners provides investment banking, capital markets, advisory, and wealth management services focused primarily on corporate and institutional clients in Brazil.
