Key Takeaways
Headline growth aided by acquisition and currency gains masked slower organic progress.
Revenue climbed 33.6% year over year.

Telecom operator Telecom Argentina (NYSE:TEO) reported headline growth supported mainly by acquisitions and favorable currency adjustments. The company, a major provider of mobile, fixed-line, broadband, pay TV, and data services in Argentina and neighboring countries, delivered its first quarter earnings for fiscal 2026 on May 11, 2026. Overall, the period showed strong top-line expansion and rising margins, but weak organic growth and regulatory uncertainty remain key concerns.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 33.6% year over year, driven mostly by the full consolidation of Telefónica Móviles Argentina, while organic growth for core operations was only 1.6%.
Net income jumped more than fourfold, but the surge was mainly due to currency-related foreign exchange gains rather than fundamental operational gains.
Telecom Argentina sharply raised capital expenditure to P$433,768 million, prioritizing upgrades to fiber broadband and mobile networks, but regulatory review of the acquisition is ongoing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management plans to keep investing in network and technology upgrades, especially as it rolls out more 5G coverage and fiber to the home. However, the company offered no specific revenue or profit guidance for future quarters. Investors should pay close attention to the outcome of the extended regulatory review of the TMA acquisition, as well as trends in core organic growth and subscriber churn.

Quarterly Highlights

Headline revenue growth of 33.6% was mainly the result of fully consolidating Telefónica Móviles Argentina, masking much weaker progress in core, legacy operations. For example, organic revenues outside the recent acquisition rose only 1.6%, and internet service revenues for the core business declined 0.4% year over year.

Net income soared by 420%, primarily because the Argentine peso appreciated significantly during the period, producing a P$530 billion foreign exchange gain. This one-time factor, rather than underlying improvement in the business or customer base, explains most of the profit jump.

The company stepped up capital investment by 85%, directing funds to expand both mobile (including 5G) and fixed broadband infrastructure. Its fiber-optic expansion means one-third of broadband subscribers now use fiber to the home, up from the prior year.

What Telecom Argentina Does

Telecom Argentina is a telecommunications company offering mobile, fixed-line, broadband internet, pay TV, and data services primarily in Argentina, Paraguay, and Uruguay, with some operations in the United States and Chile.
