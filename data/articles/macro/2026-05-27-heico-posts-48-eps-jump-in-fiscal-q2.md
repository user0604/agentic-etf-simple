Heico Posts 48% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 5:11 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Record results driven by strong organic sales and recent acquisitions.
EPS increased 48.2% year over year.
Management projects continued growth, with focus on acquisitions and demand trends.

Heico (NYSE:HEI) posted record results as both main business segments delivered strong organic growth and contribution from recent acquisitions. The leading supplier of FAA-approved jet engine and aircraft component replacement parts, as well as advanced electronic products for aviation, defense, and space, reported its fiscal second quarter on May 27, 2026. Overall, the quarter delivered notable margin expansion and robust cash generation.

Notable Developments
Organic net sales grew over 18%, with both the Flight Support and Electronic Technologies segments hitting all-time highs for net sales and operating income.
Operating margin improved by 2.9 percentage points, reflecting higher gross profit and lower selling, general, and administrative expenses as a percentage of sales.
Four acquisitions contributed to results and drove a rise in leverage, as net debt to EBITDA ended at 1.74 times.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects further sales increases in both segments throughout the second half of fiscal 2026, citing strong underlying demand and growth from acquired businesses. The acquisition pipeline remains active, but leadership is watching leverage ratios and integration of recent deals.

Quarterly highlights

The Flight Support Group, which makes replacement parts for jet engines and other aircraft components, recorded 21% higher revenue and 31% higher operating income over the prior year period. Operating margin for this segment increased to 26.2%, bolstered by product mix and greater sales in aftermarket replacement parts.

The Electronic Technologies Group, Heico’s segment for defense, aerospace, and specialty electronics, saw sales jump 34% and operating income surge 56% year over year. A stronger product mix, especially in aerospace, and improved operating leverage resulted in operating margin reaching 26.5%.

The company used $821 million on acquisitions in the first half of the year, up from $286 million in the same period last year.

Management reported that leverage ratios remain within comfort ranges but indicated they would continue to monitor leverage if acquisition activity remains high.

What Heico does

Heico designs, manufactures, and sells FAA-approved jet engine and aircraft replacement parts, as well as electronic technologies serving the aviation, defense, space, and industrial markets.
