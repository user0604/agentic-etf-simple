CareTrust REIT (NYSE:CTRE) increased its dividend and expanded its property investments in a quarter that saw substantial financial gains. Notable developments
Total revenue increased 47.9%, driven by both rental income growth and the expanding senior housing operating portfolio segment.
The company invested more than $245 million in new properties during the quarter, with approximately $1.1 billion in deals completed year-to-date through early May.
The quarterly dividend increased 16.4% to $0.39 per share, representing about 81% of normalized funds available for distribution.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its 2026 full-year guidance, now expecting normalized funds from operations between $2.00 and $2.04 per share. The company will focus on maintaining healthy rent collection and effective integration of its newly acquired assets as it monitors growth in its direct-operated senior housing segment and expansion in the UK market.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue growth far outpaced share count increases, reflecting ongoing external portfolio growth. Rental income remained the largest component, and the company maintained a 100% collection rate from tenants, a key indicator of tenant health.

Direct investment activity was a standout, with over $245 million of acquisitions closed during the quarter at blended stabilized yields near 9%. Investments after quarter end totaled an additional $864 million, highlighting CareTrust REIT’s capital deployment momentum.

Operating expenses increased, especially across general and administrative costs, which grew 59% year over year, driven by hiring and integration to support the company’s growing scale. This trend will be important as complexity rises from new asset types and geographies.
