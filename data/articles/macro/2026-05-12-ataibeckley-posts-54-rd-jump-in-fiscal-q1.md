Notable developments
The company remains on schedule for its BPL-003 Phase 3 program in treatment-resistant depression, with trial initiation planned for the next quarter.
Research and development spending jumped by more than 50%, reflecting work on the BPL-003, VLS-01, and EMP-01 programs as well as costs from a recent company combination.
Cash and short-term securities ended the quarter at $209.9 million, supporting operations through anticipated BPL-003 Phase 3 data in early 2029.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management stated that its current cash resources should fund operations until at least 2029, covering key upcoming trial readouts for its leading drug candidates. It reiterated plans to begin pivotal Phase 3 studies for BPL-003 in the next quarter, making clinical and regulatory milestones the main factors for investors to monitor.

Quarterly highlights

Drug development programs advanced notably, with BPL-003 (a nasal spray for treatment-resistant depression) on track for Phase 3 study launches, and EMP-01 (an oral MDMA enantiomer for social anxiety disorder) achieving positive clinical progress in its recent Phase 2a trial. These achievements anchor AtaiBeckley’s effort to become a leading innovator in neuropsychiatric therapies.

Operating expenses grew due to higher research and administrative costs, especially from combining with Beckley Psytech late last year. The expense increase was tied to larger clinical trial activity and increased staffing, both hallmarks of moving toward later-stage drug development.

While revenue from out-licensing and collaborations remains small, AtaiBeckley’s cash position offers a substantial runway for continued operations even as losses persist. Index additions during the quarter could also help broaden its shareholder base and improve liquidity over time.
