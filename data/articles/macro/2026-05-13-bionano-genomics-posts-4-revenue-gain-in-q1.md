Bionano Genomics (NASDAQ:BNGO) achieved the upper end of its revenue outlook, reflecting a modest business win for the period. The company, a provider of optical genome mapping (OGM) systems and analysis software, reported results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed early progress in its recurring-revenue strategy, though profitability and liquidity remain clear hurdles.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects second-quarter 2026 revenue in the $7.5–$7.8 million range and kept its full-year 2026 target at $30–$33 million. It expects growth to be led by higher consumables usage and recent insurance reimbursement improvements. Investors should monitor whether recurring revenue gains lead to meaningful top-line acceleration and further improvements to the cash position in coming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Consumable sales powered growth, with a 17% year-over-year jump in flow cell units sold and consumables revenue reaching $3.9 million. This indicates increasing routine use among OGM customers rather than new hardware installations driving expansion.

Higher gross margin, at 49%, resulted from this changing sales mix and cost reductions. However, adjusted operating expenses edged up 7%, and net loss widened to $8.3 million, largely because the prior-year period included a one-time income boost not repeated this quarter.

Two new reimbursement codes for OGM-based tests came into effect, including a 47% payment increase for hematologic cancer testing and a new code for genetic disorder analysis priced above traditional microarray tests. These changes could improve clinical adoption and financial return for users.

What Bionano Genomics does

Bionano Genomics develops and sells optical genome mapping systems and consumables, enabling researchers and clinicians to detect large-scale DNA variations that standard sequencing techniques can miss.
