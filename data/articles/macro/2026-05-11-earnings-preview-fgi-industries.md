Earnings Preview: FGI Industries

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 3:03 PM

Comments

FGI Industries (NASDAQ:FGI), a supplier of kitchen and bath products such as toilets, sinks, cabinets, and shower systems, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Fourth-quarter 2025 results for FGI Industries showed the company navigating a challenging environment marked by trade policy changes and tariff volatility. Revenue for the quarter was $30.5 million, representing a 14.4% decrease compared to the same period the previous year. Gross profit was $8.1 million, down 6.8% year over year, but gross margin improved to 26.7% from 24.6% as the company shifted toward higher-margin products. The adjusted net loss for the quarter was $0.6 million, which met analyst estimates for the period. The company continued to invest in its "Brands, Products, Channels" (BPC) strategy and made progress in geographic expansion, particularly in India and Germany.

Positives
Gross margin expansion reflects improved product mix: Gross margin rose to 26.7%, showing the company’s focus on higher-margin offerings is working.
Operating expenses declined due to cost controls: Operating expenses fell to $8.8 million from $10 million, indicating successful efforts to optimize warehouse operations and reduce costs.
Progress in geographic expansion initiatives: The company expanded its dealer network in India and launched a new wholesale bath initiative in Germany, which could drive future growth.
Adjusted net loss met analyst expectations: The company’s adjusted net loss of $0.6 million was in line with consensus, suggesting performance was within the anticipated range despite industry headwinds.
Negatives
Significant revenue decline year over year: Revenue dropped 14.4% compared to Q4 2024, mainly due to the prior year’s order pull-forward and ongoing tariff impacts.
Net loss increased on a GAAP basis: The GAAP net loss attributable to shareholders was $2.6 million, widening from a $0.4 million loss in the prior year’s quarter, driven by nonrecurring items and a valuation allowance on deferred tax assets.
Ongoing tariff and trade policy uncertainty: The company continues to face unpredictable tariff environments, which have disrupted ordering patterns and contributed to revenue pressure.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Revenue recovery and volume trends: Investors should look for signs of stabilization or growth in revenue, especially in sanitaryware and shower systems, following the disruptions in the previous year.
Impact of new tariffs and trade policies: Watch for management’s commentary on how new tariffs under Section 122 and ongoing trade policy changes are affecting costs, pricing, and customer demand.
Progress on geographic and product expansion: Updates on the expansion in India, Germany, and the U.S. wholesale channel, as well as the performance of recently launched products like custom kitchen cabinetry and anti-overflow toilets, will be important.
Cost control and margin sustainability: Continued improvement in operating expenses and gross margin will be key, given management’s focus on operational efficiency and product mix optimization.
Liquidity and covenant compliance: The company recently renewed its credit facility and is now in compliance with revised covenants. Investors should monitor liquidity levels and any commentary on meeting ongoing debt obligations and maintaining financial flexibility.
