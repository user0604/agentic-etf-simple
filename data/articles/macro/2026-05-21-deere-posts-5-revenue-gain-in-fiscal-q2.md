Deere Posts 5% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q2

Key Takeaways
Deere & Company reported stable business performance with continued strength in construction and small agriculture, despite ongoing pressure in large agriculture.
Revenue rose year over year while net income decreased slightly and operating margin in the Small Agriculture & Turf segment improved.
Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook for net income and expects continued uneven demand across segments.

Deere & Company (NYSE:DE) maintained steady momentum, offsetting weakness in large agriculture with gains in construction and turf segments. The leading manufacturer of agricultural, construction, and forestry equipment released its earnings for the quarter ended May 3, 2026, on May 21, 2026, reporting slight growth in revenue but some softness in key profitability metrics. Overall, the quarter demonstrated resilient performance across varied segments, with notable differences by industry served.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased by nearly 5% from the prior year, with Construction & Forestry net sales up 29% and Small Agriculture & Turf up 16%.
Operating margin in Small Agriculture & Turf reached 20.6%, an improvement of 1.4 percentage points, while Production & Precision Agriculture margin dropped sharply by 6.3 points year over year.
Management maintained full-year net income guidance between $4.5 billion and $5.0 billion, signaling no change in its financial targets despite mixed demand trends.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its fiscal 2026 net income outlook, expecting continued headwinds in large agriculture equipment but highlighting growth in construction and turf segments. The most critical point for investors to monitor will be whether demand for large agricultural machinery continues to soften, which could impact both margins and financing activity in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue gains were driven by strong results in the Construction & Forestry division, where net sales jumped nearly 29% year over year due to higher equipment shipments and benefits from favorable currency rates. Operating profit growth in this segment also stood out, showing the impact of scale and pricing on profitability.

The Small Agriculture & Turf segment delivered robust growth, as demand for utility tractors, mowers, and golf course equipment resulted in both higher shipment volume and improved operating margin. This segment now acts as a stabilizing force as large-scale farming equipment faces more cyclical downturns.

However, the Production & Precision Agriculture business, which covers large tractors and advanced technology products, experienced a sharp fall in both sales and margins. Management cited lower shipment volumes and persistent input cost pressures as main reasons for the contraction in profitability.

What Deere & Company Does

Deere & Company manufactures and sells agricultural, construction, and forestry machinery, along with providing related technology solutions and equipment financing worldwide.
