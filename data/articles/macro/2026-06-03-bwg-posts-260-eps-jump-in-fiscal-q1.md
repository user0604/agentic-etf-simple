Key Takeaways

Performance: Earnings per share jumped to $1.08 from $0.30 and gross profit in Product Services turned positive.

Bwg (NYSE:BWLP) posted its best-ever quarterly profit as both operating segments surged, declaring another strong dividend and ordering new vessels. The leading global liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) shipping company released its first quarter 2026 earnings results on June 2, 2026. Overall, the period showed significant profit growth and robust cash flow, but much of the gain came from paper (unrealized) trading profits rather than operating cash.

Notable Developments
The company declared a dividend of $0.67 per share for the quarter, up 139% year over year, and equivalent to 120% of Shipping net profit.
The Shipping segment’s time charter equivalent (TCE) income per available day rose to $55,450, beating both last year and management’s guidance of about $54,000.
Product Services delivered a gross profit of $126.8 million, mostly from unrealized mark-to-market gains, while realized trading profit was negative at $(10.0) million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide new financial targets for Q2 2026 but indicated strategic confidence by ordering eight new Very Large Gas Carriers. Investors should closely track the sustainability of Product Services’ mark-to-market profits and any changes in realized trading results, as these drove much of the quarter’s exceptional profit.

Quarterly Highlights

Shipping segment TCE income reached $197.7 million, with spot market per-day rates climbing 63% year over year.

This strength offset reduced available days, as more vessels underwent planned drydocking.

Product Services contributed the largest swing, moving from a gross loss to a $126.8 million profit, due almost entirely to gains in the unrealized valuation of paper trading positions. However, only a portion of these gains is expected to be realized as cash, and the segment reported a realized trading loss for the quarter.

The company announced the purchase of eight new Panamax Very Large Gas Carriers for about $940 million, to be delivered between 2029 and 2030. This marks a sizeable investment to expand its fleet capacity and respond to potential future market demand, but it does increase future capital requirements and risk if market conditions change.

What Bwg Does

Bwg operates a large global fleet of LPG tankers, transporting liquefied petroleum gas for energy producers, utilities, and other large customers.
