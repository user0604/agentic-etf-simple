Earnings Preview: Elastic

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Elastic (NYSE:ESTC), a company that provides search and analytics software for business data, is expected to report its fourth-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings after the market closes on Thursday, May 28, 2026.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Elastic reported total revenue of $449.9 million, which represented 18% growth year over year. Subscription revenue was $425.7 million, up 19% from the prior year. Non-GAAP earnings per share (EPS) came in at $0.73. Both revenue and EPS were above analyst estimates for the quarter. The company saw strong demand for its platform, especially from large customers adopting artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities. Current remaining performance obligations, a measure of future contracted revenue, surpassed $1.05 billion for the first time, growing 19% year over year. The company continued to execute on its sales-led strategy, adding approximately 60 new customers with annual contract value (ACV) over $100,000, bringing the total to more than 1,660.

Positives
Revenue and EPS beat expectations: Both top- and bottom-line results were above consensus, indicating strong business momentum and effective cost management.
Continued growth in large customer cohort: The number of customers with over $100,000 in ACV increased by 14% year over year, showing traction with larger enterprises.
AI adoption expanding within customer base: Over 28% of high-value customers now use Elastic for AI-related workloads, supporting the company’s positioning as a key data platform for enterprise AI initiatives.
Improved subscription gross margin: Subscription gross margin rose to 81%, up from 80% a year ago, helped by efficiencies in cloud infrastructure costs.
Negatives
Services gross margin remains negative: The services business continued to operate at a loss, with a gross margin of negative 4%, reflecting ongoing investments and higher personnel costs.
Operating expenses growing faster than revenue: Research and development, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expenses all increased by double digits, which could pressure future profitability if not matched by revenue growth.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Performance versus guidance and consensus: Management guided Q4 revenue to $445 million to $447 million and non-GAAP EPS to $0.55 to $0.57. Analyst consensus is in line with these figures. Investors should watch if Elastic can meet or exceed these targets, especially given the shorter quarter (three fewer days than prior quarters).
Progress in AI-driven customer expansion: The company has highlighted AI as a major growth driver, with over 3,000 customers now using Elastic for AI use cases. Growth in the number and size of AI-related deals, especially among large enterprise customers, will be important to track.
Cloud adoption and mix shift: Elastic Cloud contributed 48% of total revenue in the most recent quarter. Continued growth in cloud-based subscriptions, and any commentary on customer migration patterns between self-managed and cloud, will be key for understanding long-term margin trends.
Status of product initiatives and partnerships: The company recently launched Agent Builder (now generally available), Elastic Workflows (in technical preview), and the Elasticsearch GPU plug-in (in technical preview). Updates on customer adoption, as well as the impact of partnerships with NVIDIA and Dell, could provide insight into future growth opportunities.
Share repurchase activity and capital allocation: Elastic completed 60% of its $500 million share repurchase program by the end of Q3. Investors should look for updates on remaining buybacks and any changes to capital allocation priorities, especially given the company’s strong cash position of $1.25 billion.
