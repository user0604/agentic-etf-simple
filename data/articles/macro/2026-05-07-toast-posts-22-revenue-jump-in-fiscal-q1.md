Toast (NYSE:TOST) posted another growth quarter as it expanded its restaurant technology platform across more customers and delivered higher recurring gross profit. The leading cloud-based platform for restaurant operations and payments reported first-quarter numbers on May 7, 2026, covering results for the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter showed strong gains in revenue, profitability, and customer additions, with continued investment in new product and market opportunities.

Notable developments
The company’s non-GAAP recurring gross profit climbed 27%, driven by new customer locations and increased use of its platform.
Net income more than doubled as adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 11% of revenue, while free cash flow also rose sharply.
Toast launched new AI-powered marketing and drive-thru solutions and accelerated share repurchases to $378 million through early May.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year forecast, now targeting annual recurring gross profit of $2.29–2.32 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $790–810 million for fiscal 2026. For the second quarter, it set goals for non-GAAP gross profit of $565–575 million and adjusted EBITDA of $185–195 million. Investors should watch profitability in hardware and professional services and whether margin expansion keeps pace with rising expenses for product innovation and expansion.

Quarterly highlights

Toast added about 7,000 net new customer locations during the quarter, supporting a 22% year-over-year jump in active sites to 171,000. Recurring software and payments revenue continued to drive both top-line and profit growth, as customer adoption of new modules and locations remained strong despite seasonal slowing from the prior quarter.

The company launched Toast IQ Grow, its first artificial intelligence (AI) agent, to help restaurants automate digital marketing. It also expanded its platform with Toast Drive-Thru, an integrated solution for quick-service restaurants that combines hardware, voice ordering, and payments technology in one system.

In capital management, Toast accelerated its share buyback activity, repurchasing 14 million shares for $378 million by early May. Despite strong cash generation, the company’s cash position declined quarter over quarter due to the scale of repurchases.

What Toast does

Toast provides an integrated software and payments platform for restaurants and foodservice businesses, enabling ordering, payments, marketing, operations, and financial tools in a single cloud-based system.
