Silexion Reports 133% R&D Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 18, 2026, 8:44 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Business win: Silexion advanced its lead cancer therapy SIL204 to the start of clinical trials in Israel and submitted for EU regulatory review.
Performance: Net loss increased to $2.7 million, while R&D expenses more than doubled as clinical activities ramped up.
Outlook: Management reaffirmed plans to initiate a Phase 2/3 trial in the next quarter, highlighting the need for additional funding.

Silexion Therapeutics (NASDAQ:SLXN) marked a milestone by progressing its lead cancer drug candidate SIL204 into the clinical phase in Israel while preparing for similar trials in Europe. The clinical-stage biotech, focused on novel therapies for hard-to-treat cancers, remains in a critical and cash-intensive phase as it advances toward anticipated key trial initiations. This update, released on May 18, 2026, reports results for the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the period saw major R&D expenditure increases, stepped-up clinical readiness, and restored Nasdaq listing compliance after recent modest fundraising.

Notable Developments
SIL204 received regulatory approval to begin a Phase 2/3 trial in Israel in March, and a Clinical Trial Application was filed in Germany in April.
Research and development spending rose 133.3%, driven by higher costs for toxicology, consulting, and clinical trial preparation of SIL204.
Cash reserves fell sharply to $2.4 million at quarter-end, but subsequent capital raises brought shareholders’ equity above the Nasdaq listing minimum.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed that it plans to launch the Phase 2/3 trial in the coming quarter, emphasizing continued progress on both regulatory and operational fronts. However, it did not provide financial guidance or a timeline for upcoming data readouts. Cash runway and the ability to secure further funding remain the most important items for investors to monitor as clinical spending escalates.

Quarterly Highlights

The biggest development was regulatory momentum: SIL204 cleared a key hurdle by receiving approval to begin a Phase 2/3 clinical trial in Israel as of March. The company also submitted the same trial protocol in Germany, following earlier positive feedback from EU regulators, with broader European enrollment planned if approved.

Expenses increased across the board, as reflected by the 133.3% jump in research and development costs year over year. This reflects greater spending on subcontractors and manufacturing activities to ready the lead siRNA therapy, SIL204, for human testing.

Silexion’s balance sheet remains constrained, with $2.4 million in cash at quarter-end and only modest fundraising completed after March. The company reported compliance with the minimum Nasdaq shareholder equity requirement, but additional financing is likely needed as it advances its clinical program.

What Silexion Therapeutics Does

Silexion Therapeutics develops RNA interference (RNAi)-based drug candidates for the treatment of cancers driven by KRAS mutations, such as locally advanced pancreatic cancer.
