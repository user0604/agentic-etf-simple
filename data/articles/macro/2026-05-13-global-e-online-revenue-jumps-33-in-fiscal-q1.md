Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year 2026 outlook for all key metrics, citing strong adoption from both new and existing merchant clients. The company expects further revenue and profit growth, but also highlighted that free cash flow remains a priority watch point. Investors should closely track whether higher profits will translate into positive cash flow as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

The company experienced robust growth in cross-border e-commerce, with platform transaction value up 40% and revenue up 33% compared to prior-year levels. This growth was fueled by both new and existing merchants and included launches with brands across North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific.

Profitability metrics improved sharply, as adjusted EBITDA was up nearly 60% from the previous year and gross margin widened. Service fees, making up 48% of total revenue, increased at a faster rate than fulfillment services, helping boost overall margin quality.

Despite the margin gains, free cash flow stayed negative and cash holdings declined, in part due to ongoing investment, working capital, and a $59 million share buyback. Management and stakeholders will need to monitor if cash generation strengthens in later quarters, as was the case in the prior fiscal year.

What Global-E Online Does

Global-E Online is a technology platform that enables brands and retailers to sell directly to consumers worldwide, managing the complexities of cross-border e-commerce for merchants.
