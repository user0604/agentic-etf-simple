Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased guidance for same-restaurant sales growth to 4.5–6.5% for fiscal 2026 and expects to open 75 to 77 new restaurants this year. It did not raise margin targets, keeping profit margin guidance at 23.7–24.3%, citing rising labor and ingredient costs. The most important factor for investors to watch is whether Cava can maintain high sales productivity as it expands into new markets.

Quarterly Highlights

Same-restaurant sales far exceeded management’s initial goals, up 9.7% due to strong customer traffic and a modest menu price impact. These results prompted an upward revision in sales outlook, though management still expects some sales deceleration later in the year.

Expansion remained a focus, with 20 new restaurant openings and continued entry into Midwest markets such as Cincinnati and St. Louis. The company's sustained new unit rate, now at 459 locations total, supports its long-term plan to reach 1,000 U.S. restaurants by 2032.

Restaurant-level profitability held steady at 25.1% of revenue, indicating strong operational execution despite increased digital sales and rising pre-opening costs. Higher tax expense led to a decline in earnings per share compared to last year, while cash flow and absolute profits grew strongly.

What Cava Group Does

Cava Group operates and franchises fast-casual restaurants specializing in Mediterranean cuisine, with a menu built for customization and a strong digital ordering presence.
