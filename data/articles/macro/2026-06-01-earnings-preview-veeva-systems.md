Veeva Systems (NYSE:VEEV), a provider of cloud software and data solutions for the life sciences industry, is expected to report its Q1 FY2027 earnings on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes. This date is based on current analyst calendars and may be updated by the company.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

Veeva delivered strong Q4 FY2026 results, with total revenue of $836 million and non-GAAP operating income of $366 million. For the full fiscal year, revenue reached $3.195 billion, representing a 16% increase year over year. Subscription revenue grew 17% to $2.684 billion, while professional services and other revenue rose 11% to $511 million. Net income for the year was $909 million. These results came in ahead of management’s guidance and met or exceeded analyst consensus for both revenue and earnings per share. The company also surpassed its $3 billion revenue run-rate goal and reported customer growth of 5% year over year, ending the period with 1,552 customers.

Positives
Revenue and profit exceeded expectations: Veeva beat its own guidance and met or exceeded analyst consensus for both revenue and earnings, showing strong operational execution.
Continued customer growth: The company added customers across both commercial and R&D product lines, with customer count rising 5% year over year, reflecting ongoing demand and effective go-to-market strategies.
Expansion of Vault CRM adoption: Nearly 140 customers are now live on Vault CRM, including two top 20 pharmaceutical companies, indicating successful migration from legacy systems and new product momentum.
Healthy growth in R&D and Quality Solutions: R&D and Quality Solutions now contribute a larger share of revenue, driven by adoption of products like RTSM (randomization and trial supply management) and LIMS (laboratory information management system).
Negatives
Slower growth in certain mature products: Some established products, such as eTMF (electronic trial master file), showed lower growth as customers shift toward newer offerings, which could weigh on short-term growth rates.
Challenging comparisons for Crossix: The Crossix data business had an outstanding year, but management expects growth to moderate in FY2027 due to tougher year-over-year comparisons.
Ongoing transition in CRM business: CRM revenue is expected to become a smaller portion of total revenue by 2030, and the company is actively managing customer migrations and contract transitions, which introduces some uncertainty.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Progress toward Q1 revenue guidance: Management has guided Q1 FY2027 revenue to $855 million to $858 million, closely in line with analyst consensus. Investors should look for any early signals on the pace of customer adoption and expansion deals.
Adoption and rollout of new AI-powered features: Veeva is launching new AI agents, especially in commercial content and safety, with initial customer deployments expected in the first half of the year. Watch for updates on customer adoption, use cases, and early feedback.
Status of top 20 pharma migrations to Vault CRM: The company expects decisions from the remaining top 20 pharmaceutical customers over the next several months. Progress here will be key to future CRM and add-on product growth.
Performance of Crossix and other data businesses: Crossix faces tougher comparisons after a standout FY2026. Investors should monitor whether growth remains healthy and if new data products are gaining traction.
Updates on services revenue and margin trends: With more customers going live on new platforms, professional services revenue and related margins may shift. Any commentary on project timing, demand, or profitability will be important for understanding the outlook.
