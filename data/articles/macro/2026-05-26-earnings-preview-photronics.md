Earnings Preview: Photronics

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Photronics (NASDAQ:PLAB), a supplier of photomasks used to manufacture semiconductors and flat panel displays, is expected to report earnings for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, before the market opens.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

Photronics delivered first-quarter results that exceeded analyst expectations. Revenue reached $225 million, up 4% sequentially and 6% year over year, driven by strong demand for high-end photomasks in Asia ahead of the Chinese New Year. Gross margin held steady at 35%, and non-GAAP diluted earnings per share (EPS) came in at $0.61, which was above consensus. The company generated $97 million in operating cash flow, marking its second-highest quarter for this metric. Capital expenditures were elevated at $48 million, reflecting ongoing investments in capacity and technology upgrades, particularly in the U.S. and Korea.

Positives
High-end integrated circuit (IC) growth was robust: Revenue from high-end IC photomasks rose 19% year over year, reflecting healthy demand for advanced chip designs, including those used in artificial intelligence (AI) applications.
Strong performance in Asia supported results: Accelerated demand in Asia, especially before the Chinese New Year, contributed significantly to revenue growth and helped the company beat expectations.
Operating cash flow was very strong: Operating cash flow reached $97 million, or 43% of revenue, providing the company with ample liquidity to fund ongoing investments.
Continued progress on U.S. and Korea expansion projects: Facility expansions in Allen, Texas, and Korea are on track, with new equipment installations underway and customer qualifications expected to be completed in the second half of 2026.
Negatives
Gross margin was flat and slightly lower year over year: Gross margin remained at 35%, unchanged sequentially but down from 35.6% a year ago, mainly due to product mix and higher material costs.
U.S. revenue growth was modest: Revenue from the United States increased only slightly year over year, indicating slower momentum compared to Asia.
High-end flat panel display (FPD) revenue declined: High-end FPD revenue decreased compared to both the prior quarter and the same quarter last year, offset by growth in mainstream FPD products.
Ongoing high capital expenditures: The company continues to operate in a period of elevated capital investment, which could pressure free cash flow if market demand slows.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Revenue and margin performance versus guidance: Management guided for Q2 revenue of $212 million to $220 million and operating margin of 22% to 24%. Investors should watch how actual results compare to these targets, especially given the seasonal slowdown from the Chinese New Year.
High-end IC and FPD demand trends: The sustainability of high-end IC photomask demand, particularly for advanced nodes and AI-related applications, will be key. Watch for any updates on adoption of new display technologies like Generation 8.6 AMOLED.
Progress on U.S. and Korea facility expansions: The status of equipment installations and customer qualifications at the Allen, Texas, and Korean sites is important. These expansions are scheduled to enter volume production in 2027 and are critical for future growth.
Capital expenditures and cash flow: Management reiterated full-year capital expenditure guidance of $330 million. Investors should monitor the pace of spending and its impact on cash flow, as well as any updates on project timelines.
Competitive dynamics in China and mainstream markets: The company noted increased competition in mainstream photomasks in China. Any changes in pricing, customer behavior, or market share could affect future profitability and growth.
