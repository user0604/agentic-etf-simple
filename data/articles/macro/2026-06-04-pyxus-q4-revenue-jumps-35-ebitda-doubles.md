Pyxus Q4 Revenue Jumps 35% EBITDA Doubles

Key Takeaways
Pyxus International delivered a strong business result as operational efficiency drove better earnings in the fourth quarter.
Adjusted EBITDA for the period more than doubled year over year.
Management projects steady demand but warns of margin and revenue pressure amid a global tobacco oversupply.

Pyxus International (OTC:PYYX) rebounded with a robust finish to fiscal 2026, as higher sales volumes and disciplined supply chain management powered a big improvement in profitability. The leading global leaf tobacco merchant saw record adjusted EBITDA and margin gains, despite ongoing industry oversupply and flat annual sales. The results (released June 4, 2026, for the quarter and fiscal year ended March 31, 2026) wrapped up a year that met the company's own targets on most key measures. Overall, the quarter showcased sharp gains in earnings and cash flow, while management remains cautious about continuing pressure on sales and margins ahead.

Notable developments
Revenue jumped 35.2% in the quarter, mainly driven by increased sales volumes in Africa and North America, as larger crops and shipment timing boosted results.
Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $62.4 million, while operating income increased over threefold, reflecting tight cost control and a favorable product mix.
Inventory rose to $786.7 million at year end, supporting future shipments, but also reflecting the oversupplied global tobacco market.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management guides for flat to slightly lower revenue in fiscal 2027, targeting $2.3 billion to $2.5 billion, and an adjusted EBITDA range of $210 million to $240 million. It expects ongoing strong customer demand and improved procurement costs, but notes that industry oversupply may continue to put pressure on sales and margins. The most important metric for investors to watch will be how margin performance holds up as pricing and volumes adjust to market conditions.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter saw a notable surge in both shipments and profitability, overcoming muted annual revenue due to the timing of customer deliveries in key growing regions. Higher African and North American crop volumes, together with successful logistics, enabled the delivery of large orders and contributed to a record adjusted EBITDA.

Operating income and gross profit increased sharply, with improved gross margins reflecting careful cost controls and a beneficial shift toward higher-margin processing and third-party services. The company’s Processing and Other segment achieved a gross profit margin of 20.1% as activity scaled up in Africa and North America, offsetting some pressure from lower tobacco prices per kilo in the Leaf segment.

A build-up in inventory to $786.7 million at year end, while helpful for supporting continued sales, highlights the company’s exposure to global oversupply conditions. Management noted that only about 9% of processed inventory remained uncommitted, indicating effective demand planning, but also underscoring the need to closely monitor shifts in customer orders and market trends going forward.

What Pyxus International does

Pyxus International is a global leaf tobacco merchant that contracts, purchases, processes, and supplies tobacco and related products to major tobacco manufacturers worldwide.
