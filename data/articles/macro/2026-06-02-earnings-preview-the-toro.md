Earnings Preview: The Toro

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

The Toro (NYSE:TTC), a leading maker of turf equipment, irrigation systems, and outdoor construction products, is expected to report its second-quarter fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, June 4, 2026, before the market opens.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

In the first quarter of fiscal 2026, The Toro delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations on both sales and adjusted earnings per share (EPS). The company reported consolidated net sales of $1.04 billion, up 4.2 percent from the prior year, and adjusted EPS of $0.74, both of which beat consensus estimates. Growth was driven by strong demand for snow and ice management products, as well as continued momentum in the Professional segment, which includes equipment for contractors and specialty construction. The recently completed acquisition of Tornado Infrastructure Equipment also contributed to sales growth. While Residential segment sales declined year over year, higher shipments of snow products and price realization helped offset some of the weakness in lawn care products. The company raised its full-year sales and EPS guidance following the strong quarter.

Positives
Both sales and adjusted EPS beat analyst estimates: The company outperformed expectations, indicating effective execution and demand in key categories.
Professional segment strength: Net sales in this segment increased 7.2 percent, driven by snow and ice products, underground construction, and the Tornado acquisition, supporting higher profitability and market share.
AMP productivity initiative delivered cost savings: The AMP program reached $94.3 million in cumulative cost savings, helping offset inflation and improve margins.
Healthy free cash flow and shareholder returns: Free cash flow was $14.6 million, and $133 million was returned to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases, reflecting financial discipline.
Negatives
Residential segment sales declined: Sales were down 6.8 percent year over year due to lower lawn care product shipments, highlighting ongoing softness in this category.
International sales weakness: Net sales in international markets fell by $23.9 million, reflecting softer demand in both Europe and Asia and adding uncertainty to the global outlook.
Gross margin contracted: Gross margin decreased by 120 basis points to 32.5 percent, mainly due to higher material and manufacturing costs, despite productivity improvements.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Progress on Professional segment growth and margins: Watch for continued momentum in snow and ice management, underground construction, and the integration of Tornado Infrastructure Equipment, as well as the segment's earnings margin, which is expected to be similar to last year.
Residential segment margin improvement: Management expects Residential segment margins to approach double digits in Q2, so any signs of improvement or further challenges will be key.
AMP initiative cost savings and reinvestment: Updates on the AMP program's progress toward its $125 million savings goal and how those savings are being reinvested in innovation and growth will be important for long-term profitability.
Field inventory and channel health: Inventory levels were healthy at the end of Q1, supporting confidence in second-half sales, especially for snow products. Any changes in channel inventory or demand trends could affect the outlook.
Management's updated guidance and market outlook: Investors should look for any revisions to full-year sales and EPS guidance, especially in light of ongoing international softness and evolving material costs.
