Abacus Global Management Posts 17% EPS Jump in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:57 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Abacus Global Management reported strong growth in assets under management and cash flow this quarter.
Adjusted EPS increased 17% year over year.
Management raised full-year 2026 earnings guidance and highlighted future asset management expansion.

Alternative asset manager Abacus Global Management (NYSE:ABX) delivered another quarter of robust growth, boosted by sharply higher operating cash flow and expansion in its core Longevity Income Fund. The company focuses on life settlements and alternative asset management, specializing in assets that are uncorrelated with traditional markets. Reporting first-quarter earnings on May 7, 2026, it raised full-year profit guidance following business gains in its recurring-fee lines. Overall, the quarter featured increased assets under management, greater cash generation, and a higher return on equity.

Notable Developments
The company’s Longevity Income Fund assets under management grew nearly fourfold year over year to $1 billion, showing rapid expansion in institutional demand.
Operating cash flow improved to $91.7 million, a $153 million swing from negative to positive versus the prior year quarter.
Management raised full-year 2026 adjusted net income guidance to $100–$106 million and announced it is moving forward with its asset management partnership and a second asset securitization.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now expects adjusted net income for fiscal 2026 to reach $100–$106 million, up from its earlier guidance. It predicts continued strong inflows to Longevity Income Funds and sees strategic investments, like its pending stake in Manning & Napier, driving future growth. Investors should watch for more detail on the recurring revenue contributions from asset management and technology services in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Abacus saw a rapid rise in assets under management (AUM), with its Longevity Income Fund expanding to $1 billion—almost four times larger than the prior year. This fund focuses on life settlements, which allow investors to buy existing life insurance policies, and is growing due to rising institutional interest.

Operating expenses rose to $34.8 million, up sharply from $16.0 million a year earlier, mainly because of investments in scaling and higher general and administrative costs. Despite this, Abacus kept its margin strong, producing an adjusted EBITDA margin of 55%.

The company is progressing on key strategic initiatives, including the acquisition of a minority stake in Manning & Napier—a move aimed at boosting product distribution and asset management growth. It also reported a large share repurchase program of up to $20 million authorized in January 2026, reflecting its capital return focus.

What Abacus Global Management Does

Abacus Global Management is an alternative asset manager specializing in life settlements, longevity-based funds, and asset management solutions that are uncorrelated with equity and credit markets.
