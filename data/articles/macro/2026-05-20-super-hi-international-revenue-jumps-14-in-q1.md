Super Hi International Revenue Jumps 14% in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 20, 2026, 6:13 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Super Hi International posted stronger operating results but saw profit drop due to currency volatility.
Revenue climbed more than 14% year over year.
Management reiterates a cautious approach to expansion and continues to prioritize operational efficiency.

Super Hi International (NASDAQ:HDL) reported solid operating gains as it improved efficiency and diversified its business, but the sharp swing in currency exchange rates led to a sizable drop in net profit. The global hot pot restaurant operator, known for its Haidilao brand, delivered higher revenue and stronger margins in the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (results released May 20, 2026). Still, foreign currency losses significantly offset these underlying improvements. Overall, the period balanced encouraging core growth with continued profit volatility tied to global currencies.

Notable developments
Revenue rose 14.2%, driven by both the core Haidilao restaurant business and a sharp increase in delivery and other business lines.
Operating profitability improved, with income from operations up 70.7% and margins higher by 2.1 percentage points.
Net profit fell to $4.1 million from $11.9 million, mainly due to an $11.7 million foreign exchange loss linked to currency swings.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management plans to keep expanding at a measured pace, maintaining a focus on efficiency and building its brand. The company stressed that careful market selection and staff engagement would guide new openings, rather than rapid expansion. Investors should watch for any future impact from currency fluctuations on profit, as no new mitigation strategy for this risk was announced.

Quarterly highlights

The company's main business, Haidilao restaurants, remains the largest revenue source but now makes up a smaller portion of overall sales as new business lines grow. Delivery and "other business" (including condiments and secondary restaurant brands) produced year-over-year growth of more than 130%, boosting overall diversification and reducing reliance on dine-in customers.

Efficiency gains were clear: the cost of raw materials and staff as a percentage of revenue both declined, helping to lift the operating margin to 6.2%. Operating cash flow also improved, supporting reinvestment and network upgrades.

Expansion was slow, with just one new restaurant added and flat table turnover rates in many regions. East Asia delivered the strongest rise in guest visits and daily sales per location, but North America performance lagged, with restaurant turnover and daily sales sliding compared to a year ago.

What Super Hi International does

The company operates Haidilao-branded hot pot restaurants worldwide and also sells hot pot condiments and food products through delivery and retail channels.
