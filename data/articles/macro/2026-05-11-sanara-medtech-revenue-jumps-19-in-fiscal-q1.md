Notable Developments
Net revenue increased 19% to $27.8 million, exceeding the upper end of management's guidance by $0.6 million for the quarter.
Gross margin improved to 93.1%, while operating expenses grew 12% year over year.
Net operating cash outflows persisted at $2.5 million, consistent with first-quarter seasonality, but overall cash declined to $13.6 million at quarter-end.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership reaffirmed full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $116–$121 million, signaling confidence in continued growth and execution. For the second quarter, the company projects revenue between $28.5 million and $29.5 million. Investors should watch for improvement in cash generation, especially as cash burn remains a recurring issue during the start of each year.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue climbed as soft tissue repair products drove gains, with sales from this portfolio up 21% year over year to $24.9 million; these products now contribute about 90% of the total. Bone fusion product revenue, however, edged down by 2% to $2.86 million, signaling stagnation in that business line. Management highlighted commercial expansion: the sales team grew to 43 representatives, and product approvals extended to over 4,000 healthcare facilities, though sales occurred in about 1,400 of those.

The company returned to net income from continuing operations, reaching $0.4 million for its first quarter as a pure-play surgical business. This improvement reflects both margin expansion and disciplined operating cost management, even with increased spending on sales force and commercial infrastructure.

Research and development (R&D) costs dipped to $0.8 million, representing 2.7% of revenue, but management clarified that the pace of R&D will accelerate through the remainder of 2026 as new products move through the pipeline, including the pending launch of OsStic.

What Sanara MedTech Does

Sanara MedTech develops and markets advanced surgical wound care and bone fusion products, focusing on innovative solutions for surgical procedures in hospitals and ambulatory surgery centers.
