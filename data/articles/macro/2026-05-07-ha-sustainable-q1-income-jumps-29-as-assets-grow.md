HA Sustainable Infrastructure Capital (NYSE:HASI) delivered robust adjusted earnings this quarter, while headline profitability was impacted by a technical, temporary accounting issue. The leading sustainable infrastructure investor reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 7, 2026, with both asset growth and investment yields trending positively. Overall, the quarter marked continued operational progress alongside a clear divergence between non-GAAP and reported metrics.

Notable developments
Adjusted recurring net investment income increased to $101 million, reflecting a 29% year-over-year rise and continued scale in operating earnings.
Managed assets grew to $16.4 billion, supported by $637 million in new sustainable infrastructure investments during the quarter.
The company declared a quarterly dividend of $0.425 per share, maintaining its planned payout and capital return commitments.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its outlook for stable, double-digit adjusted EPS and return on equity growth through 2028. It expects further increases in both assets and operating income, supported by a strong pipeline of high-yielding investments. Investors should monitor the normalization of GAAP results in coming quarters, as well as any changes in funding costs or asset quality.

Quarterly highlights

Adjusted earnings metrics showed substantial strength again this period. Adjusted recurring net investment income surpassed $100 million for the first time, and return on equity set a company record at 15.7%, highlighting the profitability of new investments and balance sheet efficiency.

Reported GAAP net income swung to a loss largely due to a non-cash, timing-related impact from equity method investments. Management indicated this effect should reverse in future quarters and does not signal a change in the underlying economics of its projects.

The portfolio remains well diversified, including significant positions in behind-the-meter energy efficiency assets and grid-connected renewables. Portfolio credit quality continues strong, with only two investments moved to moderate risk, and annual avoided emissions across all managed assets exceeded 10 million metric tons.

HA Sustainable Infrastructure Capital invests in and manages a portfolio focused on sustainable infrastructure projects across energy efficiency, renewables, and climate solutions sectors.
