Key Takeaways
Viant Technology reported a clear business win, with ongoing momentum in its programmatic and Connected TV advertising segments.
Revenue rose year over year.

Viant Technology (NASDAQ:DSP) posted a business win in the first quarter, driven by strong performance in its programmatic advertising and Connected TV segments. The digital advertising software provider operates a buy-side demand-side platform (DSP) that helps advertisers plan, buy, and measure digital ads across devices. The company released its results on May 11, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period showed significant year-over-year growth and progress toward improved profitability.

Notable developments
Connected TV ad spend made up more than half of total advertiser spend, marking a record shift toward digital video channels for the company.
Profitability improved sharply, with adjusted EBITDA up 81% year over year, and net loss narrowing to $2.19 million.
The TVision acquisition, completed after quarter end, expands measurement and attention data capabilities for advertisers.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects both revenue and adjusted EBITDA to accelerate in the coming quarters, with guidance calling for higher revenue and profit margins in the second quarter. The most important factor to monitor will be the integration and impact of the TVision acquisition on growth and cost control as the company seeks to leverage new data-driven tools and measurement products.

Quarterly highlights

Connected TV became the dominant channel during the quarter, making up more than half of advertiser spend on the platform. This shift supports management’s focus on high-growth, programmatic opportunities as linear television continues to decline in importance.

Operating expenses increased 22% over the prior-year period, reflecting investments in technology, sales, and integration costs related to the $40 million TVision acquisition. Despite higher expenses, adjusted measures of profitability and cash flow strengthened, as seen in the improved adjusted EBITDA margin.

The company ended the quarter with $185.7 million in cash and no outstanding debt, maintaining significant flexibility for continued investment. Segment details reveal ongoing growth in sales and marketing, technology, and data-driven product enhancements, with a focus on leveraging AI and privacy-compliant identity tools.

What Viant Technology does

Viant Technology builds software that enables advertisers and agencies to plan, buy, and measure digital advertising across connected devices, with an emphasis on programmatic channels and privacy-oriented data solutions.
