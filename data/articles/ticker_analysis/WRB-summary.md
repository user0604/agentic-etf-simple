WRB Company Overview
W. R. Berkley operates as a commercial insurance provider and reinsurer, offering specialized coverage for complex risks that standard insurers typically avoid. The company generates revenue through insurance premiums and investment income, utilizing a decentralized model of niche businesses to provide tailored expertise in local markets. Customers remain loyal because of the company's deep technical knowledge in specific sectors and its ability to manage high-hazard liabilities that require customized underwriting solutions.

W R Berkley Grows by Targeting Specialized Insurance Markets

W. R. Berkley provides property and casualty insurance, focusing on hard-to-insure and specialized markets. The company stands out because it often covers risks that many other insurers avoid. Over the last five years, the stock returned 82.9%, beating the S&P 500’s 70.8% by 12.1 percentage points.
