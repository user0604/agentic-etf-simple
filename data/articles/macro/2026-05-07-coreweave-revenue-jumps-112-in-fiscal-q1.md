Notable developments
Revenue increased 112%, reaching $2.08 billion in the quarter, and revenue backlog approached $100 billion after new multi-year contracts were signed.
Net loss widened to $740 million, with operating loss margin at 7%, and interest expense more than doubled year over year.
The company secured an $8.5 billion debt facility and a $2 billion equity investment from Nvidia to support infrastructure expansion.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management maintained its plan for margins to improve over the coming quarters, but did not provide updated revenue or profit forecasts for fiscal 2026 in this release. Investors will focus on whether the margin trough in the first quarter leads to better profitability as new capacity becomes productive. Key areas to monitor include margin progression and the pace at which backlog converts to recognized revenue.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter saw strong bookings momentum, including a $21 billion deal with Meta Platforms and an expanded partnership with Anthropic. Revenue backlog grew by over $32 billion since the previous quarter, reflecting robust demand for AI-focused cloud services.

Profitability declined as the company scaled its data centers and infrastructure. Net loss margin deteriorated to 36%, and higher interest expense reflected increased borrowing to fund growth. Adjusted operating income, which excludes some one-time costs, dropped sharply to near break-even as costs rose ahead of new contract revenues.

To support expansion, CoreWeave closed an $8.5 billion debt facility and received a $2 billion strategic equity investment from Nvidia. No new sustainability milestones were reported in this quarter’s filing.

What CoreWeave does

CoreWeave operates a cloud platform designed for artificial intelligence workloads, providing enterprise and research customers with large-scale, high-performance computing infrastructure.
