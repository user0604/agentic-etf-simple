Key Takeaways
Revenue growth handily exceeded prior guidance, but profits and cash flow fell sharply.
Earnings per share on a Non-GAAP diluted basis fell to RMB 0.17 from RMB 0.85 in the year-ago period.
Management expects second-quarter revenue to rise 13.6%–15.0% year over year.

Gaotu Techedu (NYSE:GOTU) saw strong demand but significant profit pressures in the latest period. The leading tech-focused education services provider achieved double-digit revenue growth, but surging costs sharply reduced profits and cash generation. The company released results for its fiscal first quarter on June 2, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. Overall, robust sales were offset by heavy spending in user acquisition and branding, resulting in lower earnings and more cash outflow.

Notable developments
Net revenues rose 13.2% year over year, exceeding the company’s prior guidance of 5.7%–7% growth for the period.
Operating expenses climbed 16.1% to RMB 1,167.8 million, driven mainly by higher expenditure on marketing and branding activities.
Net operating cash outflow increased 73.6% year over year, reaching RMB 828.4 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects revenue for the second quarter to land between RMB 1,578 million and RMB 1,598 million, reflecting 13.6%–15.0% growth from a year earlier. It remains focused on scaling its AI-enabled offerings while investing in branding and offline expansion. Investors should closely watch whether operational costs begin to stabilize and if revenue growth is matched by improved profit or cash flow.

Quarterly highlights

The period saw revenue not only grow but surpass projected targets, highlighting continued strong demand for Gaotu’s educational services and products. However, the costs required to drive that growth increased even faster, especially in marketing and branding activities, which outpaced gains in sales. This dynamic led to much lower profit margins and net income, even with relatively steady gross margin levels.

Key business drivers included the company’s investment in AI-powered learning devices and educational tools, as well as continued expansion into both online and offline offerings. Management described its “Scale with AI” strategy as central, incorporating advanced technology across teaching, curriculum, and operations. Despite these digital investments, selling and administrative costs remain high, which squeezed bottom-line results.

The increase in deferred revenue—up 24.1% year over year—signals strong customer prepayments and future sales visibility, even as operating cash outflow increased by 73.6%. The company’s ongoing share repurchase program, totaling RMB 704 million so far, reflects confidence in its business but has not offset the quarter’s significant decline in earnings per share.

What Gaotu Techedu does

Gaotu Techedu provides technology-driven educational services and smart learning devices to students and adult learners in China.
