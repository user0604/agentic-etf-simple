Quarterly highlights

The company recorded sharply reduced revenue and continued to operate at a net loss as it remains pre-commercial, with only $15,000 in revenue and a net loss of $8.1 million compared to a loss of $0.8 million in the prior year period. The loss worsened due mainly to one-time costs tied to new financings and higher interest expenses related to an expanded debt load.

Clene made significant headway in regulatory engagement, receiving feedback from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration that its data could support submitting CNM-Au8 for accelerated approval in ALS. The next key milestones are the planned New Drug Application in the third quarter of 2026 and the launch of a confirmatory Phase 3 RESTORE-ALS trial in early 2027.
