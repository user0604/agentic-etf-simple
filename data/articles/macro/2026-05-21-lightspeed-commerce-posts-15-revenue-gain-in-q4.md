Key Takeaways
Lightspeed Commerce posted strong growth in its core markets but saw persistent pressure in cash flow and hardware profitability.
Revenue climbed 14.7% year over year.

Lightspeed Commerce (NYSE:LSPD) saw robust progress in its core retail and hospitality businesses, while challenges remained in subscription growth and cash flow. The leading provider of cloud-based point-of-sale (POS) and commerce solutions reported its financial results for the three months ended March 31, 2026, on May 21, 2026, covering its fiscal fourth quarter. Overall, the period captured a mix of solid revenue expansion and continued investments against a backdrop of ongoing net losses.

Notable developments
Core growth engines delivered revenue growth of 24% year over year, driven mostly by North American retail and European hospitality.
Gross Payment Volume (payments processed through the platform) rose 22% to $9.6 billion, raising transaction-based revenue by 17%.
Adjusted free cash flow for the quarter was negative $13.0 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects continued double-digit revenue growth for fiscal 2027, with guidance for $1.225 billion to $1.265 billion in revenue. Priorities are shifting toward improved profitability and more consistent positive cash flow after fluctuating results in the quarter. Investors should closely monitor free cash flow trends and margin improvement efforts as the company pursues more disciplined growth.

Quarterly highlights

Lightspeed’s key markets in North America retail and European hospitality drove most of the company’s revenue expansion, with growth engines posting 24% higher revenue and an 11% increase in business locations served. The payment solutions segment continued to expand rapidly, with Gross Payment Volume (GPV) increasing to $9.6 billion and now representing 42% of all transactions processed on the platform.

Though overall gross profit and adjusted EBITDA advanced year over year, the company experienced a steep drop in adjusted free cash flow. Despite full-year free cash flow turning positive, the quarter’s $13.0 million outflow signals seasonal working capital pressures or increased investment activity.

Segment strength was offset by persistent headwinds in hardware and implementation services, which posted negative gross margins as Lightspeed subsidized hardware to attract more high-value and payments-attached customers. Subscription revenue growth slowed to 6% year over year, reflecting factors including a greater share of annual contracts, lapping of prior pricing actions, and typical seasonal effects.

What Lightspeed Commerce does

Lightspeed Commerce provides cloud-based software that helps retailers and restaurants manage sales, payments, inventory, customer data, and analytics in stores and online.
