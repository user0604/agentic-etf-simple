The number of distribution partners -- brokers, agencies, and third-party administrators -- reached 896, a year-over-year jump of nearly 30%, supporting the strategic expansion push.

Profitability deteriorated as Health In Tech increased spending in sales, marketing, and technology development, with adjusted EBITDA moving to a negative $1.3 million from a year-ago profit. Gross margin narrowed substantially, with gross profit shrinking by over 15%, reflecting higher costs and a changing mix of revenue sources.

The company made notable advances in its insurance technology, launching more than 100 pre-configured stop-loss plans and a three-year rate stabilization program. These new offerings, set to drive revenue primarily in the second half of the year, are intended to deepen Health In Tech’s presence in the fast-evolving self-funded health insurance market.
