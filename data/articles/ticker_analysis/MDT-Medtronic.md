MDT Company Overview
Medtronic develops and manufactures medical technologies used to treat chronic diseases such as heart failure, neurological disorders, and diabetes. The company generates revenue by selling implantable devices and surgical tools to hospitals and specialized clinics across the globe. By integrating hardware like pacemakers and insulin pumps with remote monitoring software, it creates deep technical connections with healthcare providers that result in high switching costs for medical facilities.

Medtronic Grows Device Sales With Strong Heart and Diabetes Demand

Medtronic makes medical devices for treating heart, neurological, surgical, and diabetes problems. The company just raised its dividend for the 49th year in a row, showing steady payouts for shareholders. Over the last five years, the stock returned -40.5% while the S&P 500 gained 79.7%, underperforming by 120.2 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $9.81 billion (up 9.9% YoY)
Non-GAAP EPS: $1.55 (down 4.3% YoY)
Cardiovascular revenue: up 13.8% YoY, led by a 78% jump in Cardiac Ablation Solutions

Latest Developments

Cardiac Ablation Solutions brought a big boost to cardiovascular sales, which helped Medtronic gain more market share in the U.S.
International diabetes sales grew quickly, which made up for flat U.S. results and showed the benefit of a global business.
