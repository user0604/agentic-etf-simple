Earnings Preview: CrowdStrike Holdings

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 1, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

CrowdStrike Holdings (NASDAQ:CRWD), which provides cloud-based cybersecurity software to protect business devices and data, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Wednesday, June 3, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

CrowdStrike reported a strong finish to its fiscal year 2026, with quarterly revenue of $1.31 billion and annual recurring revenue (ARR) reaching $5.25 billion as of Jan. 31, 2026. Net new ARR for the quarter was $330.7 million, representing 47% year-over-year growth. The company’s Falcon Flex subscription model continued to gain traction, with $1.69 billion in ending ARR from Flex accounts, up over 120% year over year. Non-GAAP net income was $289.1 million, or $1.12 per diluted share, and free cash flow for the quarter was $376.4 million. These results beat analyst estimates for ARR and net new ARR, while revenue and EPS met or slightly missed consensus expectations.

Positives
Record ARR growth demonstrates strong demand: Ending ARR surpassed $5.25 billion, showing 24% year-over-year growth and highlighting the company’s ability to expand both its customer base and product adoption.
Falcon Flex model drives customer expansion: The Falcon Flex subscription model saw rapid adoption, with more than 1,600 customers and a 120% increase in Flex ARR, supporting CrowdStrike’s land-and-expand strategy.
Strong cash generation supports future investments: Free cash flow reached $376.4 million for the quarter and $1.24 billion for the year, providing resources for ongoing development and acquisitions.
High customer retention rates indicate platform stickiness: Dollar-based net retention was 115% and gross retention was 97%, suggesting customers are staying with and expanding their use of the platform.
Negatives
Operating expenses increased faster than revenue: Research and development, sales and marketing, and general administrative expenses all rose, with general and administrative costs up 39% year over year, partly due to costs from the July 19 Incident.
Professional services margin declined: Gross margin for professional services dropped by 1% compared to the prior year, reflecting higher consulting and stock-based compensation expenses.
Some legal and administrative headwinds remain: Ongoing costs and potential liabilities from the July 19 Incident continue to affect results and may impact future quarters.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
ARR and net new ARR momentum: Management guided for Q1 ending ARR of $5.50 billion to $5.50 billion, with net new ARR expected to grow 29% to 30% year over year. Investors should watch if growth rates remain at these elevated levels.
Integration and early impact of recent acquisitions: The acquisitions of SGNL.ai (identity security) and Seraphic (browser security) were completed in February 2026. The company expects a combined $5 million to $8 million in acquired net new ARR in Q1. Progress on integration and customer uptake will be important.
Adoption of AI-driven security products: The company highlighted rapid growth in its new AI Detection and Response (AIDR) offering and increased usage of its Charlotte AI agent. Watch for updates on customer adoption and revenue contribution from these products.
Flex and re-Flex subscription trends: The Falcon Flex model is now central to CrowdStrike’s go-to-market approach. Investors should monitor the pace of new Flex customers, re-Flex activity, and average ARR lift from these deals.
Customer retention and sales cycle length: Management noted some delays in purchasing decisions and longer sales cycles following the July 19 Incident. Any improvement or further elongation in these trends could affect near-term growth and should be monitored.
