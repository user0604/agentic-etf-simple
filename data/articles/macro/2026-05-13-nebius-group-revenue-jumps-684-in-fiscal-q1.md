Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its full-year 2026 outlook from the prior quarter, with plans for further revenue growth as major new data center capacity comes online later in the year. Investors should monitor execution on capacity ramp, ongoing contract fulfillment, and the company’s ability to narrow core adjusted losses as underlying profitability becomes a primary metric.

Quarterly Highlights

The period’s headline was unprecedented revenue growth, with sales sharply higher on the back of expanded AI cloud offerings and new long-term contracts. Cost of revenue growth was far outpaced by revenue, helping improve margins and support an inflection in adjusted EBITDA from a loss to positive territory.

The cash flow statement showed a pronounced swing into positive territory, as cash provided by operating activities exceeded $2.2 billion. This was largely due to substantial customer prepayments, which also contributed to deferred revenue jumping to $4.78 billion and strengthening forward visibility.

Capital spending remained a key story, with $2.47 billion invested in property and equipment during the quarter. Nebius Group secured up to 1.2 gigawatts of power and land for a new AI facility in Pennsylvania, aligning with its plan to invest $16–$20 billion in capacity for 2026. Leverage rose as convertible note financing offset these heavy outlays.
