Earnings Preview: G-III Apparel Group

The Motley Fool Team

9:02 AM

Comments

G-III Apparel Group (NASDAQ:GIII), which designs and distributes branded apparel and accessories, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on Friday, June 5, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter ended Jan. 31, 2026, G-III Apparel Group reported net sales of $771 million, an 8% decrease from the prior year. The quarter was affected by the cessation of shipments to Saks ahead of its bankruptcy filing, which negatively impacted sales by about $20 million. Non-GAAP earnings per diluted share were $0.30, down from $1.20 in the prior year, and included a $0.30 per share bad debt expense tied to the Saks bankruptcy. Gross margin for the quarter was 37%, compared to 39.5% a year earlier. The company’s results missed analyst consensus, with both revenue and EPS coming in below expectations.

Positives
Growth in key owned brands: DKNY, Donna Karan, Karl Lagerfeld, and Vilebrequin collectively delivered mid-single-digit growth, demonstrating the company’s successful pivot toward higher-margin owned brands.
Strong direct-to-consumer performance: Digital sales, especially on donnakaran.com, grew sharply, and comparable store sales increased for Karl Lagerfeld and DKNY retail stores, showing healthy consumer engagement.
Healthy balance sheet and liquidity: The company ended the year with $407 million in cash and over $900 million in total liquidity, providing flexibility for future investments and shareholder returns.
Inventory management: Inventories were down 4% year over year, reflecting disciplined management and a shift away from off-price channels.
Negatives
Sales and earnings below expectations: Both revenue and non-GAAP EPS missed analyst consensus, driven by lower sales and the impact of the Saks bankruptcy.
Tariff headwinds and margin pressure: Gross margins declined year over year due to higher tariffs, particularly affecting the wholesale segment.
Ongoing impact from loss of PVH licenses: The exit from Calvin Klein and Tommy Hilfiger licenses resulted in a significant sales decline, which is expected to continue into fiscal 2027.
Higher SG&A expenses: Selling, general and administrative costs increased due to bad debt expenses and continued investment in marketing and technology, pressuring profitability.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Gross margin improvement versus prior year: Management guided for a 150 basis point increase in gross margin for the quarter, despite lower sales, as the business shifts toward higher-margin owned brands and laps prior tariff impacts.
Performance of go-forward brands: Investors should monitor sales trends for DKNY, Donna Karan, Karl Lagerfeld, and Vilebrequin, as these brands are expected to drive high single-digit growth and are central to the company’s long-term strategy.
Progress on cost savings initiatives: The company has identified $25 million in annualized cost savings, targeted for fiscal 2028. Updates on early progress or further actions could impact margin expectations.
Impact of new and expanded licenses: The launch of French Connection (started February 2026) and ongoing rollout of Converse and BCBG lines should be tracked for early sales contributions and market reception.
International and digital expansion: Watch for updates on international growth, especially in Europe and Asia, and continued momentum in direct-to-consumer digital channels, which have shown strong recent gains.
