Key Takeaways
Antalpha Platform delivered robust profit growth, supported by large unrealized gains on tokenized gold. Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 64%. Management expects revenue of $11 million to $13 million next quarter, down from this period.

Antalpha Platform (NASDAQ:ANTA) posted a strong quarter as unrealized gains on tokenized gold holdings boosted profits. The digital asset finance and technology provider serves institutional clients with crypto-collateralized lending, tokenized asset investment, and platform solutions. Reporting first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 19, 2026, it saw operating margins expand but issued a more cautious revenue outlook for the next period. Overall, the quarter reflected operational success aided by one-time gains, but management signaled more moderate expectations ahead.

Notable Developments
Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP, earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) increased more than fivefold, with $13.3 million driven largely by $12.9 million in unrealized gains on tokenized gold holdings.
Operating expenses doubled to $25.0 million, reflecting greater investment in technology, marketing, and one-off restructuring costs of $3.3 million.
Supply chain finance total value locked dropped 25% year over year, while margin loan value increased 8%, highlighting a shift in product focus and client engagement.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guided for revenue of $11 million to $13 million for the second quarter, a sharp reduction from the current period’s result. Management attributed the lower Q2 revenue guidance primarily to continued moderate demand for crypto-collateralized financing and dynamic but broadly consistent market conditions. Investors should monitor the company’s ability to generate recurring operational profits as one-off asset gains normalize.

Quarterly Highlights

The jump in adjusted profitability was largely due to unrealized gains on XAUt, a form of tokenized gold, which added more than half of total adjusted EBITDA. The company began new yield-generating strategies for its gold holdings, shifting from a passive to more active approach. This supports future potential income but exposes results to gold price changes.

Core platform costs doubled as Antalpha invested in product innovation, including launching a Web3 AI agent to power on-chain crypto analytics and trade execution, although management expects limited near-term financial impact from these initiatives. Restructuring charges and greater technology investment contributed to the elevated cost base observed this quarter.

Lending operations saw a mixed picture. Margin loan total value locked increased modestly, but overall supply chain TVL fell, mainly due to lower activity from large borrowers and subdued Bitcoin prices. The average value of loans held per client rose substantially, suggesting client concentration but deeper individual relationships.

What Antalpha Platform Does

Antalpha Platform provides technology-driven lending, digital asset finance, and tokenization solutions, largely serving institutions in crypto and blockchain markets.
