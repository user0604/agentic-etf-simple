Notable developments
Subscription annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached $1.57 billion, rising 32%, and the company added 141 new customers with over $100,000 in ARR during the period.
Profitability improved sharply, as non-GAAP net income per diluted share reached $0.16, marking a significant turnaround from the prior year's loss.
Free cash flow more than doubled to $73.6 million, supporting a higher outlook for both revenue and profitability for the remainder of the year.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its outlook for the full year, now targeting higher revenue, expanded free cash flow, and improved margins compared to earlier guidance. For the next quarter, it projects revenue of $395–$397 million and non-GAAP net income per share between $0.03 and $0.05. The key issue for investors will be watching whether high growth can continue as cloud transition-related revenue tailwinds decline and GAAP losses persist.

Quarterly highlights

The core subscription business fueled the period’s expansion, as subscription revenue made up about 97% of total revenue. Customers with more than $100,000 in annual recurring revenue grew 24% from a year earlier, a sign of increased enterprise adoption.

New products supporting Zero Trust data protection, such as immutable backups for Google Workspace and integrations with platforms like Microsoft Defender, advanced the company's focus on proactive cyber defense. Its AI operations platform, Rubrik Agent Cloud, expanded availability and new integrations, strengthening its position in both automation and security risk management.

While Rubrik improved contribution margin and cash flow, its reported GAAP net loss narrowed but remained negative, reflecting substantial continuing investments in research, development, and sales. A declining benefit from material rights revenues, tied to the cloud migration of past quarters, means revenue growth may begin to normalize, and profitable growth remains a focus going forward.
