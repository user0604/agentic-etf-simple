MakeMyTrip Reports Record Gross Bookings in Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 7:02 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
MakeMyTrip delivered a business win with record annual Gross Bookings, but quarterly growth showed signs of slowing. Adjusted Margin (non-IFRS) in Air Ticketing rose 5.4% from the prior year period. The company did not provide specific financial guidance for fiscal 2027.

MakeMyTrip (NASDAQ:MMYT) extended its market leadership by setting new annual records in Gross Bookings, though its growth slowed in the latest quarter. The leading India-based online travel platform released its earnings for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 19, 2026. Overall, the quarter was solid but flagged moderation in short-term momentum even as full-year results highlighted breadth and resiliency.

Notable Developments
Gross Bookings for the quarter were $2,550.5 million, while full-year Gross Bookings set a record at $10.4 billion.
Results from operating activities grew 29.1% year over year to $40.3 million, signaling improved operational efficiency.
The AI-powered assistant, Myra, handled over 54,000 daily conversations, with more than 45% of usage from smaller cities.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management described the market as "challenging" and did not issue detailed forward guidance, instead emphasizing gains in operating margins and continued investment in technology. Investors should watch for signs of renewed Gross Bookings growth and deeper penetration of technology-led services, especially as consumer travel sentiment evolves.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter's revenue figure edged higher versus last year, supported by continued growth in high-margin segments like Hotels and Packages and Bus Ticketing, which both posted adjusted margin gains above 5%. These areas remain a strong source of profit for the company, according to segment disclosures.

Technological integration stood out, as the Myra assistant now solves over half of post-booking queries for Flights and Hotels without human involvement. Usage surged in smaller Indian cities, highlighting MakeMyTrip's push to expand its reach beyond major metro areas.

While the year delivered record full-year Gross Bookings, quarterly bookings plateaued. Management pinpointed "multiple external events" for this moderation, pointing to near-term caution, but reiterated confidence in platform growth and the benefits of ongoing digital investments.

What MakeMyTrip Does

MakeMyTrip is an India-focused online travel company offering booking services for flights, hotels, vacation packages, buses, and related travel services primarily through its website and mobile platforms.
