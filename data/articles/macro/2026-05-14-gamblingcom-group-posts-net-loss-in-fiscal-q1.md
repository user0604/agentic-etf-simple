Key Takeaways

Gambling.com Group (NASDAQ:GAMB) posted a net loss as rising expenses sharply reduced profits, despite steady revenue from its online gambling marketing services. The digital marketing firm serves the rapidly growing global gaming sector and provides branded websites that connect consumers to gambling services. The results, released on May 14, 2026 for the first quarter, show flat sales but a setback in profitability. Overall, the quarter highlighted earnings pressure and higher spending, offsetting stability in top-line performance.

Notable Developments
Cost of sales increased 171% year over year to $6.09 million, contributing to an 11% drop in gross profit.

The company swung to a net loss of $1.2 million, compared to a net income of $11.2 million in the prior-year quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management provided a formal outlook for the remainder of fiscal 2026, guiding to full-year revenue of $165 million to $170 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $45 million to $50 million.

The most important detail for investors will be whether revenue can rebound and if management outlines a concrete plan for cost control.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenue held steady at roughly $40 million, despite sector growth in online gambling and the legalization of new markets. With no new segment or regional disclosures, it remains unclear whether any branded property or geography provided a relative boost or faced unique challenges.

Gross profit dropped 11% as cost of sales jumped to 15% of revenue, compared to just 5.5% the previous year. Higher technology expenses, aimed at supporting digital marketing performance, further weighed on operating margin.

The quarter’s results led to a net loss of $1.2 million, a reversal from strong profitability one year earlier. Cash fell from $15.8 million at year end to $8.4 million, pointing to higher operational demands and signaling tighter liquidity if current trends persist.

What Gambling.com Group Does
