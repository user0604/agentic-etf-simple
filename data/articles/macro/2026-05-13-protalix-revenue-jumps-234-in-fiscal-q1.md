Key Takeaways
Protalix BioTherapeutics delivered a standout quarter as a major milestone payment drove sharp profit growth.
Total revenue more than tripled year over year.
Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue guidance and expects continued pipeline progress.

Protalix BioTherapeutics (NYSEMKT:PLX) posted a dramatic improvement in its financial results as a large, one-time milestone payment lifted its performance for the first quarter of 2026. The rare disease drug developer focuses on enzyme therapies for conditions like Fabry and Gaucher diseases. In results released on May 13, 2026, the company reported a surge in total revenue and shifted to profitability following substantial licensing income that had been anticipated. Overall, the period marked a clear operational win but showcased the volatility of milestone-driven revenues.

Notable Developments
A $25 million milestone payment from Chiesi drove total revenue up 234% year over year, with licensing and R&D service income dwarfing core product sales.
Sales of goods, including mainstay treatments like Elelyso and Elfabrio, declined 26% due to timing of partner purchases, rather than demand erosion.
Research and development expense rose 56% to fund ongoing clinical programs, especially the Phase 2 study of PRX-115 for uncontrolled gout.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its full-year revenue guidance of $78 million to $83 million, including the first quarter milestone. It emphasized that future quarters will see larger contributions from recurring product sales as partner purchase timing normalizes. Investors should watch for revenue mix stabilization and progress in clinical programs, especially PRX-115 and preclinical assets like PRX-119.

Quarterly Highlights

Total revenue for the quarter was driven by the one-time $25 million milestone linked to the European approval of Elfabrio’s new four-week dosing regimen. The remainder came from selling goods, which included enzyme replacement therapies for rare diseases but was temporarily lower due to partner inventory fluctuations. Management's reaffirmed guidance depends on annual sales trends improving as large partner-driven orders even out in future quarters.

R&D expenses climbed as Protalix developed new pipeline assets, particularly the plant-produced PEGylated uricase PRX-115 for gout. This program advanced through its Phase 2 RELEASE trial, with top-line results still years away. Preclinical work on PRX-119, targeting rare kidney diseases, also contributed to rising research spending.

Cash at quarter end stood at $51.1 million, up substantially from year-end, reflecting the major Chiesi milestone. The company remained debt free, and management stated this cash position should fund existing operations and pipeline advancement plans without the immediate need for new financing.

What Protalix BioTherapeutics Does

Protalix BioTherapeutics develops and manufactures plant-based protein therapeutics for rare diseases, focusing on enzyme replacement therapies for genetic disorders.
