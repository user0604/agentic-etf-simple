Seanergy Maritime (NASDAQ:SHIP) delivered a business win in its latest results, converting last year’s loss to a meaningful profit and maintaining its steady dividend policy. Notable Developments
Revenue jumped 77% year over year as market demand for dry bulk shipping strengthened, with net profit turning positive from a loss last year.
The company kept its quarterly dividend steady at $0.20 per share, marking its eighteenth consecutive payout despite large spending commitments for new vessels.
Fleet renewal accelerated, with six new eco-design ships now on order and a further sale of older tonnage agreed to generate liquidity.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management provided guidance for even stronger charter rates in the next quarter, estimating a blended TCE of $31,430 per day with most available days already contracted. The company continues to focus on modernizing its fleet, but rising operating costs and a higher average vessel age present challenges.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s operating profit and cash flow improved sharply compared to the prior year, as key metrics such as adjusted EBITDA and net income rebounded alongside higher rates for vessel hire. Seanergy maintained high vessel utilization, but its daily operating expenses rose 8.3% to $7,181, which is notable as cost control remains critical. At the same time, it advanced its fleet upgrade program by doubling its orderbook for newbuild vessels, moving from three to six ships on order within the quarter—a significant investment strategy.

Management notes that the achieved TCE rate—while slightly below its $25,300 per day guidance—still represented a 6% premium over the relevant industry index, demonstrating the benefit of its charter mix and employment strategy. About 83% of available days in the next quarter are fixed at favorable rates, providing strong earnings visibility. The company also completed another vessel sale for nearly $13.4 million in net liquidity, supporting both the newbuilding program and the dividend.

Seanergy ended the period with $68.8 million in cash and increased its long-term debt by nearly $30 million to support growth. Management agreed financing for most of the new vessels at favorable rates, while also renegotiating lower interest margins and extending loan maturities to maintain financial flexibility.

What Seanergy Maritime Does

Seanergy Maritime operates a fleet of large Capesize vessels focused on transporting major dry bulk commodities such as iron ore and coal worldwide.
