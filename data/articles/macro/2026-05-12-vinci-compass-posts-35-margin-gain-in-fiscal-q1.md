The key area to watch next is whether Assets Under Management can return to growth, especially as regional macroeconomic volatility and ongoing softness in advisory revenue continue to pose risks.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability reached a company high as cost-saving efforts combined with scale from recent acquisitions. FRE margin now sits well above last year’s full-year average, reflecting realized synergies and disciplined expense control.

The quarterly dividend was maintained at US$0.17 per share, demonstrating continued shareholder returns, even as management flagged that advisory and upfront fee revenue remain likely to be soft through the first half of fiscal 2026.
