Plains Gp (NASDAQ:PAGP) edged forward in its strategic pivot as gains in crude oil operations contrasted with weaker group profitability. The owner of a major network of North American pipelines and storage saw operational wins in its core business, but headline profits and cash flow fell from the prior year. Overall, the period reflected a positive step for asset optimization, but successful completion of its planned divestiture remains essential.

Notable developments
Crude oil segment Adjusted EBITDA rose 4% to $582 million, driven by new pipeline asset contributions and higher transported volumes.
The company re-affirmed its quarterly distribution, raising it 10% to $0.4175 per unit compared to the prior year.
Pro forma leverage ended the quarter at 4.1 times, with management signaling this will fall post-NGL divestiture, expected to close in May.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management lifted its full-year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance by $130 million, reflecting a constructive oil market and extended ownership of the Canadian natural gas liquids (NGL) business. The pending sale of these assets, now scheduled to close in May, will play a crucial role in strengthening the balance sheet. Investors should focus on the execution and timing of this divestiture as the next key milestone.

Quarterly highlights

The crude oil operations, which form the heart of Plains Gp’s business, delivered improved profits thanks to higher pipeline volumes and successful integration of the Cactus III pipeline asset. Crude oil throughput climbed 10% to 10,039 thousand barrels per day, with Permian Basin volumes showing especially strong growth.

Natural gas liquids performance declined, with Adjusted EBITDA in this segment down 23% due to weaker pricing and lower propane and butane sales. Management has reported the segment as discontinued operations in anticipation of the Canadian NGL business sale.

The company’s headline measures of profitability, such as net income and distributable cash flow per unit, fell from the prior year. However, free cash flow turned positive after last year’s negative figure, and the distribution coverage ratio, though now at 1.46 times, remains near management's 1.5 times target despite narrowing.

What Plains Gp does

Plains Gp is a North American energy infrastructure company specializing in the transportation, storage, and marketing of crude oil and natural gas liquids using its network of pipelines and terminals.
