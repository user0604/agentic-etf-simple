Key Takeaways
Ulta Beauty delivered a strong quarter with higher sales and improved profitability.
Earnings per share rose 15.5% compared with the prior year quarter.
Management reaffirmed a cautious full-year outlook despite raising profit guidance slightly.

Ulta Beauty (NASDAQ:ULTA) continued its solid start to fiscal 2026, achieving substantial growth in both revenue and profits. The leading national beauty retailer reported momentum across its stores and digital channels, with growth fueled by customer engagement and new brand offerings. The company released results for its first fiscal quarter (reported June 2, 2026, for the period ending May 2, 2026) showing strong topline gains, though it maintained a careful outlook for the remainder of the year. Overall, the quarter reflected broad-based growth but flagged inventory and expense items for monitoring.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 11.1%, supported by a 5.3% increase in comparable store sales and continued success of new and acquired brands.
Profitability improved: gross margin gained 1.0 percentage point and diluted earnings per share climbed to $7.74, aided by higher merchandise margins and lower shrink loss.
Merchandise inventory rose 12.5%, exceeding sales growth, reflecting support for new brands, Space NK integration, and new store openings.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year revenue and comparable sales growth targets, while slightly increasing its profit guidance for fiscal 2026. The company expressed caution for the remainder of the year, citing uncertainty in the retail environment and signaling that growth may moderate. Investors should watch the company's expense management and inventory levels as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Ulta Beauty advanced its omnichannel strategy, driving sales not only in stores but through digital initiatives and fulfillment options. The company reported both increased average transaction size and higher customer traffic, supporting sales growth across all its major product categories.

Profit gains reflected better merchandise margins and lower inventory losses, which helped lead to a 1.0 percentage point improvement in gross margin. However, the cost of selling, general, and administrative expenses outpaced sales growth, somewhat limiting overall operating margin expansion.

Inventory levels rose at a faster rate than sales, largely due to investments in new product launches, the Space NK acquisition, and store network expansion. This higher inventory position bears monitoring if sales growth slows or if margins reduce in future periods.

What Ulta Beauty Does

Ulta Beauty is a national specialty retailer offering a wide range of cosmetics, skincare, haircare, fragrance, and salon services across both physical stores and digital channels.
