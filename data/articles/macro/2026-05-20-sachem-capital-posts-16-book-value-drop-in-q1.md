Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted the upcoming transaction with Industrial Realty Group as the central focus, expecting it to reshape the business mix and revenue composition. No guidance was provided on standalone core operations for the near term, and performance will likely remain volatile until the merger is finalized. Investors should closely watch the completion of the merger and updates on asset recoveries from the Naples and other legacy loans.

Quarterly highlights

Provision for credit losses increased substantially, driven by a major adjustment related to a single Florida loan. The $5.4 million total provision marks a significant rise and signals ongoing risk management challenges in the loan portfolio.

Operating expenses surged to $5.7 million, up 73% from the prior-year quarter, mainly due to $1.6 million in expenses tied to the pending combination transaction and higher compensation and general administrative costs.

The company paid a $0.05 per share quarterly dividend to common shareholders, continuing its Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) distribution requirement despite the reported loss for the period.
