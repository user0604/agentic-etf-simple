Here Group (NASDAQ:HERE) delivered higher-than-expected sales in a seasonally quiet quarter, but profits worsened, and management reduced its full-year outlook. Notable developments
Revenue for the quarter was RMB 164.7 million, beating the previous guidance range of RMB 140 million to 150 million by roughly 10%.
Full-year revenue guidance was reduced by about 20%, now expected at RMB 600 million to 610 million versus the previous RMB 750 million to 800 million.
Net loss increased to RMB 34.1 million as operating expenses rose, while inventory climbed to RMB 129.1 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management projects lower revenue for both the coming quarter and full year, citing market uncertainties and slower product launches. They highlight the need for tighter cost management and continued investment in new intellectual property, or IP, to stimulate growth. The most important area for investors to watch is how well the company can control operating expenses while rolling out new products to lessen reliance on single brands.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s main brands showed different results: WAKUKU, its flagship toy IP, was still the largest growth driver but saw sales fall 21% versus the previous quarter, while newer brands SIINONO and ZIYULI grew 73% and 50%, respectively, slightly balancing the product mix. Nevertheless, WAKUKU still accounted for 62% of revenue, underscoring high brand concentration.

Despite some success on revenue and margin, losses deepened. Net loss increased to RMB 34.1 million, with both sales and administrative expenses rising. At the same time, inventory climbed by over RMB 17 million to RMB 129.1 million, which could reflect preparations for new product launches or slower sell-through given weaker demand.

What Here Group does
