Notable Developments

Company adjusted its 2026 revenue guidance downward to $110-115 million, reflecting the exit of Resolve and focus on core operations.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management now expects $110–115 million in core cancer revenue for fiscal 2026, after excluding Resolve. Executives emphasized renewed focus on prostate and urologic cancer offerings and investment in technology like artificial intelligence. Investors should watch for improvements in margin and cost controls, as ongoing losses and cash outflows pressure liquidity.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s withdrawal from its Resolve UTI business was triggered by a sudden reversal in Medicare reimbursement policy, exposing it to a $10.4 million potential payback and forcing a reevaluation of revenue goals. Leadership signaled that this shift enables more resources for core cancer tests.

Revenue growth was powered by a surge in liquid-based test volumes—up 128 % year over year—though these now-larger volumes were offset by a 12 % drop in legacy tissue-based test counts. The shift in test mix contributed to lower gross margins as liquid-based tests generally bring different profitability compared to higher-priced tissue diagnostics.

Profitability was impacted by a 19 % jump in operating expenses as ExoDx integration raised staffing and operational costs. With quarterly cash outflows and sizable debt, company liquidity fell 34 % year over year, heightening the importance of managing future costs, especially as restructuring charges from business exits begin to accrue.

What MDxHealth Does

MDxHealth develops and markets advanced molecular diagnostic tests to help diagnose and manage prostate and other urologic cancers, using both tissue-based and liquid biopsy technologies.
