Management did not issue formal financial guidance but reiterated its intent to increase CyPath® Lung adoption and expand clinical use through new trials and physician engagement. The most important item for investors to watch is whether CyPath® Lung revenue growth will soon offset the loss of legacy revenue as cash reserves decline.

Quarterly Highlights

CyPath® Lung, the core noninvasive lung cancer diagnostic, drove the majority of the company’s growth in the quarter, with test revenue rising more than 100% and rapid new customer adoption in physician offices and clinics. This marks a clear validation of market need and the effectiveness of expanded sales efforts.

The drop in total company revenue is the result of a strategy to exit low-margin pathology services and focus on proprietary diagnostics, rather than a fall in demand. Cost-saving moves shrank direct expenses even as overall operating costs rose due to commercial expansion and clinical trial work.

The company’s cash balance declined by more than 50% from year end, reflecting ongoing losses paired with increased investment in sales growth and clinical studies. No new capital was raised in the quarter, making future funding a key watch item if expenses or sales trends do not improve.
