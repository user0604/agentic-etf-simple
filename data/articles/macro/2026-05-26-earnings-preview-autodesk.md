Earnings Preview: Autodesk

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 9:08 AM

Comments

Autodesk (NASDAQ:ADSK), a leading provider of design and engineering software for architects, engineers, and manufacturers, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Autodesk delivered results that were above the high end of its guidance across several key metrics. Revenue for the quarter grew 19% year over year, reaching $7.21 billion for the full fiscal year, which was an 18% increase from the prior year. The company saw strong billings growth and robust free cash flow, with both metrics exceeding internal expectations. The quarter benefited from the company’s new transaction model and the completion of a multi-phase go-to-market optimization, which was finalized in January. Non-GAAP operating margin improved to 38%, while GAAP operating margin remained flat at 22% due to a restructuring charge related to the sales and marketing optimization. The company’s performance in Q4 beat analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings per share.

Positives
Revenue and billings exceeded expectations: Both top-line revenue and billings growth were stronger than anticipated, reflecting healthy demand across product lines and geographies.
Strong free cash flow generation: Free cash flow for the quarter reached $972 million, supporting ongoing share repurchases and providing financial flexibility.
Momentum in key segments: The architecture, engineering, construction, and operations (AECO) and manufacturing segments both delivered double-digit revenue growth, driven by adoption of cloud-based platforms and AI-powered tools.
Successful completion of go-to-market optimization: The restructuring and sales optimization initiatives were completed, positioning the company for improved efficiency and future growth opportunities.
Negatives
Restructuring costs impacted GAAP margins: A $100 million restructuring charge related to the go-to-market optimization kept GAAP operating margin flat year over year, despite underlying business improvements.
Potential near-term disruption from sales changes: Management acknowledged that changes to sales incentives and restructuring in customer-facing roles could create short-term disruption, particularly in new product subscriptions.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Impact of sales restructuring on new business growth: Investors should monitor whether the recently completed sales and marketing optimization causes any temporary slowdown in new product subscription growth, as management has embedded some prudence for this risk into guidance.
Adoption and monetization of AI features: The rollout of new artificial intelligence capabilities, especially agentic AI and workflow automation in products like Forma and Fusion, will be a key area to watch for early customer uptake and revenue contribution.
Performance of cloud-based platforms: Continued momentum in cloud offerings such as Forma for Construction and Fusion for Manufacturing will be important, as these are central to Autodesk’s long-term growth strategy and are seeing increased adoption among large enterprise customers.
Progress on API and consumption-based monetization: The company has begun charging for high-volume machine usage of its APIs, and early customer engagement could signal a new revenue stream, particularly as more customers build custom solutions on Autodesk’s platform.
Execution on financial guidance and margin improvement: Watch for updates on whether the company is on track to achieve its full-year targets for revenue, billings, and non-GAAP operating margin, especially given the expected headwinds from the new transaction model and ongoing investments in R&D and AI.
