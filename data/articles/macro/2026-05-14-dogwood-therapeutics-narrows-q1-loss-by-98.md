Business Overview

Dogwood Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing non-opioid medicines for pain and neuropathy. Its lead program, Halneuron, targets chemotherapy-induced neuropathic pain, a condition with significant unmet medical need and no FDA-approved treatments. The company is also advancing SP16, a program supported by external funding, and has entered a partnership for its legacy antiviral assets.

Quarterly Performance
Enrollment for the Halneuron Phase 2b trial reached 164 patients, with top-line data expected in fall 2026 as management reaffirmed its timeline.
Research and development expenses for the first quarter of 2026 rose approximately 12.5% year over year, tied to increased investment in clinical trials and personnel.
Dogwood Therapeutics completed a $12.5 million capital raise and projected a funding runway that extends only into the fourth quarter of 2026.

The main clinical program, Halneuron, continued pivotal Phase 2b enrollment, reaching 164 patients toward a planned 210 to 240 total. This study targets a large market for chemotherapy-induced neuropathic pain, where no FDA-approved therapies currently exist.

General and administrative costs rose to $2.4 million, a 20% increase, reflecting higher personnel expenses even as sublease income was introduced for the first time this quarter. Research and development expenses increased to $2.67 million, consistent with trial progress and headcount growth.

Dogwood Therapeutics used new equity financing to boost its cash levels to $13.23 million. However, management cautioned this amount is only sufficient to fund operations into the fourth quarter, so either new partnerships or additional capital will likely be needed unless the clinical pipeline yields near-term opportunities for non-dilutive funding.

Looking Ahead

Management confirmed the timeline for reporting Halneuron's Phase 2b results remains on track for the fall of 2026. Leaders highlighted operational progress but warned that cash on hand provides support only into late 2026. The next key milestone for investors is the final Phase 2b trial data, which will determine further regulatory steps and funding needs.
