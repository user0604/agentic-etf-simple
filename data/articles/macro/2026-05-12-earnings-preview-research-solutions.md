Earnings Preview: Research Solutions

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Research Solutions (NASDAQ:RSSS), a provider of cloud-based software and content access tools for researchers and organizations, is expected to report earnings for the third quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q2 FY2026 Recap

In the second quarter of fiscal 2026, Research Solutions reported total revenue of $11.8 million, which was a slight decrease of 1% compared to the same quarter last year. The company’s platform subscription revenue rose 14% to $5.2 million, driven by 47 net new deployments and expansion within the existing customer base. However, transaction revenue declined 10% to $6.6 million, mainly because of the loss of a major customer and lower order volumes from a few large accounts. Gross profit increased by 6% to $6.2 million, with gross margin improving to 52.4% due to a higher mix of platform revenue. The company reported net income of $547,000, or $0.02 per diluted share, a turnaround from a net loss in the prior year quarter. Adjusted EBITDA was $1.3 million, up 36% year over year. These results met analyst expectations on the bottom line but missed on total revenue due to transaction segment weakness.

Positives
Platform subscription growth was robust: Platform revenue increased 14% year over year, reflecting strong demand for the company’s research workflow tools and successful upselling to existing customers. This shift to recurring revenue can help improve profitability and business stability over time.
Gross margin expansion continued: Gross margin rose to 52.4%, up from 48.9% a year ago. This was driven by a greater share of high-margin platform revenue and disciplined cost management in platform operations.
Improved profitability and cash flow: The company posted positive net income and a 36% increase in adjusted EBITDA. Cash flow from operations grew 35% to $1.4 million, supporting ongoing investments and providing flexibility for future initiatives.
B2B segment momentum was strong: The company added 47 net new B2B deployments, and annual recurring revenue (ARR) in B2B grew to $15.3 million. This segment is benefiting from larger, stickier contracts tied to API and infrastructure integration.
Negatives
Transaction revenue declined significantly: The 10% year-over-year drop in transaction revenue was mainly due to the loss of a large customer and reduced volumes from other major accounts. This segment remains subject to variability and competitive pressures.
B2C business faced headwinds: The consumer-facing segment saw lower ARR, as increased competition in digital marketing and lower trial-to-subscriber conversions weighed on growth. Management is prioritizing retention and quality over short-term expansion here.
Sales and marketing expenses increased: Expenses in this area rose 23% year over year, reflecting higher personnel and advertising costs. While these investments aim to drive growth, they also pressured operating margins in the short term.
What to Watch in Q3 FY2026's Report
Platform ARR and deployment growth: Investors should look for continued expansion in annual recurring revenue and new customer deployments in the B2B segment, as this is the main driver of long-term profitability and growth.
Transaction revenue stabilization: Watch for signs that transaction revenue is stabilizing after recent declines. Management expects a stronger second half, but year-over-year declines may persist due to the previously lost customer.
B2C subscriber trends and conversion rates: The company has focused on improving trial-to-subscriber conversion and retention in its consumer business. Any improvement here could support incremental revenue growth.
Progress on API and AI integration initiatives: Research Solutions is shifting from seat-based licenses to infrastructure and API-based sales, including integration with artificial intelligence (AI) tools. Updates on new API deployments, publisher partnerships, and customer adoption will be important indicators of future growth potential.
Operating margin and cash flow performance: With ongoing investments in sales, marketing, and product development, investors should monitor whether the company can maintain or improve its operating leverage and cash generation as it scales.
