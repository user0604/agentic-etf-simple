Sutro Biopharma Cuts Q1 Loss 49% as Expenses Drop

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 4:25 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Sutro Biopharma delivered pipeline progress and improved its cash position, despite a drop in collaboration revenue.
Earnings per share improved to $(2.94) from $(9.08) as expenses fell and net loss narrowed year over year.
The company guides to key clinical milestones in 2026, with a cash runway expected into at least the second quarter of 2028.

Sutro Biopharma (NASDAQ:STRO) advanced its clinical programs and strengthened its balance sheet, even as quarterly revenue dipped compared to last year. The clinical-stage biotechnology company develops targeted cancer therapies using antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) built on its proprietary platforms. Reporting first quarter results on May 14, 2026, Sutro underscored promising pipeline momentum but also marked another period of net loss and the closure of a program.

Overall, the quarter showed a tradeoff: slower immediate revenue, but solid execution in R&D and a strengthened cash runway.

Notable Developments
Revenue fell year over year to $14.5 million, mainly reflecting collaboration income and a lack of new milestone payments in the quarter.
Operating expenses dropped nearly half from last year to $44.2 million, aided by cost reductions and lower restructuring charges.
The company's cash position grew to $202.6 million after a capital raise, supporting operations into at least the second quarter of 2028.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed that key clinical milestones remain on track for 2026, especially for its lead program, STRO-004. Sutro plans initial Phase 1 safety and early activity data in mid-2026, with two other investigational new drug applications also targeted for next year. Investors should watch for progress updates on clinical studies and the timing of partnership milestone receipts as potential share price drivers.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter saw revenue decrease year over year as most income came from ongoing Astellas collaboration payments, with no new upfront or milestone receipts logged during the period. A $10 million milestone from Astellas, linked to an external partnered drug entering clinical trials, was received after the quarter closed and is not included in the reported numbers.

Expenses fell sharply to $44.2 million, driven by lower research and development costs and the absence of large restructuring charges that weighed on the prior year. Net loss was nearly cut in half as a result, and earnings per share improved significantly even with a higher share count following the capital raise.

On the R&D front, STRO-004 advanced in Phase 1 testing for solid tumors, while other ADC candidates -- STRO-006 and STRO-227 -- remained on track for planned submissions in 2026. Sutro also officially discontinued its luvelta program, narrowing its pipeline but allowing more focus on high-potential candidates.

What Sutro Biopharma Does

Sutro Biopharma develops cancer treatments using its proprietary platform to create precise, next-generation antibody-drug conjugates for targeted therapy.
