Nova Posts 10% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Nova reported record revenue and profit as demand for advanced chip manufacturing tools surged.
Non-GAAP earnings per share increased 6.9% year over year.
Management expects continued growth in the next quarter with strong guidance.

Nova (NASDAQ:NVMI) delivered record-breaking quarterly results, fueled by rising sales to memory manufacturers and broad market gains. The leading provider of advanced metrology solutions for semiconductor manufacturing saw its main business lines achieve all-time highs during the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (release date: May 14, 2026). Overall, the period showed strong operational momentum, with progress on profit, product launches, and revenue, while noting risks with costs and future demand cycles.

Notable Developments
Sales across optical, materials, and chemical metrology all reached quarterly records, driven by higher demand in both memory (especially DRAM) and logic manufacturing.
Service revenue was $49.0 million in Q1 2026, representing 21% of total sales.
Operating expenses increased 10% year over year in Q1 2026, in line with revenue growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guided for revenue between $245 and $255 million and non-GAAP EPS from $2.34 to $2.48 in the next quarter, forecasting continued growth. They highlighted ongoing strong demand from leading memory and advanced logic chipmakers. Investors should closely monitor inventory trends and expense growth, as well as any shifts in semiconductor industry cycles.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s Nova Metrion and AncoScene platforms, which specialize in precise chip measurement for advanced semiconductor manufacturing, set new sales highs. Demand was especially robust for equipment serving memory devices such as DRAM, as well as logic chips built with newer technologies.

Service revenue—recurring sales from maintaining and supporting Nova’s installed base—climbed to its highest level yet, accounting for a larger share of total sales compared to last year. This shift indicates broader adoption of Nova’s systems and deeper customer relationships.

On the financial side, the company managed to slightly reduce inventory from the previous quarter and increased cash holdings, although a sizable amount of convertible debt is now classified as current liability, prompting attention to future refinancing or repayment needs.

What Nova Does

Nova supplies measurement and process control systems used by semiconductor manufacturers to inspect and analyze chips and wafers during production.
