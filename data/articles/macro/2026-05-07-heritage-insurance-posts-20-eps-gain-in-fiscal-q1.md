Key Takeaways
Heritage Insurance delivered record first-quarter net income, underscoring strong execution on profitability.
Net income grew as earnings per share rose 20.2% year over year.

Heritage Insurance (NYSE:HRTG) posted a record-best first quarter net income, showing further progress in profitability despite weather-related losses. The property and casualty insurer, known for serving homeowners and commercial residential clients across multiple states, reported its earnings for the period ended March 31, 2026 (released May 7, 2026). Overall, the quarter reflected robust earnings growth, offset by flat revenue and deliberate limitation on risk exposure.

Notable developments
Net income set a first-quarter record at $36.5 million, with core profitability improving even as weather-related losses totaled $37 million.
The net combined ratio, a key measure of underwriting profitability (claims plus expenses relative to premium collected), improved by 3.5 percentage points to 81.0%.
Gross premiums written declined 2.6%, as competitive pressure in commercial business and deliberate risk selection limited top-line growth.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management is prioritizing disciplined underwriting, further product launches, and geographic expansion, aiming for sustainable growth without sacrificing margins or risk controls. For investors, the most important factor to monitor is the impact of market competition in commercial residential and how new state entries, like Texas, might offset pressures. The company reiterated its intention to invest in technology and maintain robust capital deployment, balancing growth and shareholder returns through expanded share repurchase programs.

Quarterly highlights

Profitability strengthened further, as reflected in a lower net loss ratio and improved combined ratio. These ratios measure what percentage of earned premiums are paid out in claims and expenses; a lower value signals more effective risk selection and expense management.

Revenue growth was subdued, with total revenue holding nearly flat. The decline in gross premiums written was due mainly to lower commercial residential premium, while personal residential premium increased modestly in-force, even as policy count fell. This signals Heritage is limiting business in segments where pricing margins do not meet its standards.

Management reported a 62.7% year-over-year increase in new business written, while four new insurance products launched and more are planned for later in 2026. The company remains focused on diversifying its insurance offerings and entering new geographies, with imminent entry into Texas and efforts to expand its commercial products to Hawaii.

What Heritage Insurance does

Heritage Insurance provides property and casualty insurance, specializing in homeowners and commercial residential policies across several U.S. states, with an emphasis on disciplined underwriting and risk management.
