Notable developments
Revenue tripled year over year, supported by medical device growth and initial revenue from the Flyte aviation segment.
Net loss narrowed from $4.0 million in the prior-year first quarter to $1.7 million in the current period.
The medical device segment’s revenue expanded by 73%, with products like VIVO and LockeT reaching 15 countries and generating new clinical data.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management described priorities for expanding both the medical device and aviation businesses, including ongoing fleet growth and new regulatory filings for international aviation routes. While the company did not offer formal forward revenue or earnings guidance, it flagged scaling and segment profitability as key objectives. Investors should pay closest attention to operating leverage improvements and more detailed segment reporting in future quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s total revenue rose sharply, driven by continued adoption of VIVO (a non-invasive cardiac mapping system) and LockeT (a suture retention device), which together added 32 new customers and are now present in 15 countries. Management credited new customer wins across Europe and the U.S. for the medical device segment’s 73% revenue growth. New clinical data and several peer-reviewed publications, including results shared at a leading cardiology meeting, reinforced product credibility in the cardiac care field.

Flyte, the company’s aviation platform acquired earlier this year, contributed for the first time by achieving a $200,000 monthly revenue run rate within 22 days post-acquisition in March. The fleet expanded from one to three jets, with more growth expected by the end of the next quarter as Flyte targets high-demand, short-haul routes in the Northeast U.S. and international destinations subject to regulatory approval.

Profitability improved as net loss narrowed by $2.3 million compared to a year ago, partly due to higher revenue and approximately $560,000 of non-cash expenses such as depreciation, amortization, and stock-based compensation. However, consolidated results for each business segment were not broken out in full, and the company remains unprofitable for the period.

What Catheter Precision does

Catheter Precision develops and commercializes medical devices for cardiac electrophysiology and operates a regional aviation business through its Flyte segment.
