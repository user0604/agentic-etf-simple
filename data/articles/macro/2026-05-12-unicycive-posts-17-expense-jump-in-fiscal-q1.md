Notable Developments
Total operating expenses rose to $8.4 million as general and administrative spending increased by 17% to $6.8 million, primarily due to higher consulting, professional services, and labor costs.
Research and development expenses dropped 28% to $1.6 million, attributed to lower drug development, consulting, and professional fees during the quarter.
The company ended the period with $57.1 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities, providing liquidity through at least 2027.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management states it is on track for a key FDA decision on its lead product, oxylanthanum carbonate, by June 29, 2026, and is continuing commercial preparations. The company believes it has enough cash to support operations into next year. Investors should closely watch the FDA review outcome and spending tied to potential product launch activities.

Regulatory progress remained sharp, with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration continuing its review of oxylanthanum carbonate (OLC), an experimental oral phosphate binder for dialysis patients. No new manufacturing or data concerns were cited, and management confirmed all prior deficiencies have been addressed.

The company accelerated its commercial buildout, with higher administrative costs funding new market readiness initiatives. Unicycive is expanding services such as the UniSource reimbursement hub in anticipation of a commercial launch if OLC is approved.

Operational losses deepened. EPS declined to $(0.54), due in part to a swing in non-cash warrant liability. At the same time, the company's cash reserve of $57.1 million offers a runway into 2027 while development and launch activities continue.
