Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects ongoing sequential revenue growth through the rest of fiscal 2026 but signals continued margin pressure into the next quarter as expansion continues. It aims to restore gross margins to about 40% by late 2026 or early 2027. Investors should watch for recovery in margins and progress in operational profitability as new factories and acquisitions are integrated.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s revenue figure nearly quadrupled year over year, as drone manufacturers and federal contractors increased orders for NDAA-compliant components. No customer made up more than 19% of sales, and the leading product represented just 12.7% of revenue, showing a diverse customer and product base.

Net income was boosted by $9.5 million in unrealized investment gains, making reported profits much higher than those earned from the core business. After removing these non-cash gains, operational profits remained slight and there was a notable increase in operating losses due to scaling costs, including stock-based compensation and additional shifts.

The firm completed a significant $150 million equity raise at $17 per share, raising its cash reserves sharply and allowing it to buy $75 million in raw materials and invest in new factory equipment. This provided the resources to maintain steady supply and pursue the acquisition of Upgrade Energy, boosting its ability to manufacture batteries in-house.
