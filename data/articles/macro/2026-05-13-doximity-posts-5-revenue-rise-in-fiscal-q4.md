Key Takeaways
Modest business win as user engagement hit new records but profitability declined.
Revenue rose 5% year over year.
Management expects slower growth and continued margin pressure in the coming year.

Doximity (NYSE:DOCS) posted a mixed quarter, with strong engagement from healthcare providers but a notable drop in profitability. The leading U.S. digital platform for medical professionals continued to expand its user base and workflow tool adoption, but margin pressure increased as it invested heavily in artificial intelligence and product development. The company released its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, on May 13, 2026, covering its fiscal fourth quarter.

Overall, the period showed modest progress with growth in physician engagement but clear signs of slower financial momentum and rising costs.

Notable developments
User engagement hit a new record, with over 800,000 active prescribers using workflow tools in the quarter ended March 31, 2026.
Profitability dipped sharply, with adjusted EBITDA declining 6% and non-GAAP EPS falling 31.6% compared to the previous year.
Cash generation remained strong, as free cash flow grew 11% year over year despite increasing expenses.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management forecasts revenue between $664 million and $676 million and adjusted EBITDA between $323 million and $335 million for fiscal 2027, indicating a slower 3%-5% growth rate and continued margin pressure. Company leaders point to ongoing investments in artificial intelligence and product development as the drivers behind these trends. Investors should closely monitor AI product monetization and margin stabilization as key markers of the company's next phase.

Quarterly highlights

User engagement on the platform reached record levels: more than 800,000 unique prescribers used workflow tools this quarter, and nearly half of these providers used Doximity's clinical artificial intelligence features. This marked both a new engagement record and rapid AI adoption.

Profitability saw significant declines as the company ramped up investment in research and development and awarded more stock-based compensation; for example, stock-based compensation more than doubled compared to last year. These increased expenses drove both GAAP and non-GAAP net income and margins lower, even as cash flow improved due to efficient operations and new customer partnerships.

Doximity announced several new partnerships, including with Aledade and Photon, which expand its Clinical AI Suite into thousands of independent medical practices. While revenue grew moderately and cash generation was strong, revenue growth is expected to slow, with management adopting a cautious outlook as direct AI monetization is yet to materialize.

What Doximity does

Doximity operates a digital platform that connects medical professionals and provides workflow, communication, and hiring solutions for healthcare providers and organizations.
