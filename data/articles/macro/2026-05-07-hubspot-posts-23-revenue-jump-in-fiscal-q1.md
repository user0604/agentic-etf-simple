HubSpot Posts 23% Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:23 PM

Key Takeaways
HubSpot posted a clear business win, exceeding its own guidance for revenue, profit, and customer growth.
Non-GAAP earnings per share rose 53% year over year.
Management raised its full-year outlook and says new AI products are gaining market adoption.

HubSpot topped its internal targets on multiple fronts as expanding customer demand drove quarterly revenue and profit higher. The leading cloud-based customer relationship management provider reported results for the three months ended March 31, 2026 (released on May 7, 2026). Overall, the period reflected strong operating progress and higher confidence from management in the rest of fiscal 2026.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 23% to $881.0 million, beating management's earlier outlook by roughly $18 million for the quarter.
Operating margin, a measure of how efficiently the company turns revenue into profit, improved to non-GAAP 17.8% from 14.0% as expenses rose more slowly than sales.
The company added more than 41,000 customers year over year, ending the period with over 299,000 total customers.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management lifted its full-year 2026 guidance, with projected revenue now in the $3.70 billion range and higher profit margin targets. Executives highlighted the rollout of new AI-powered features as a reason for increased confidence. Investors should watch for clear reporting on AI adoption in upcoming quarters, as well as growth in key metrics like average revenue per customer and billings.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw strong demand for HubSpot’s platform, especially among mid-market and international customers. Revenue growth came not only from new clients, but also from existing customers using more "hubs" and features, supporting the company's strategy of expanding accounts over time.

Profitability improved as the company leveraged its growing scale and managed expenses—operating margin rose nearly four percentage points and free cash flow jumped 26%. This strength allowed the company to repurchase $211 million in shares during the quarter, with a further $789 million authorized for buybacks.

While the company pointed to increasing adoption of new AI-powered agents, such as Customer Agent and Prospecting Agent, it did not share updated usage figures. Management noted that more transparency around these new products is expected as the year progresses.

What HubSpot Does

HubSpot provides a cloud-based platform that helps companies attract, engage, and manage customers through its suite of marketing, sales, service, and content tools.
