EXEL Company Overview
Exelixis is a biotechnology company that develops and sells targeted therapies to treat various forms of cancer, including kidney, liver, and thyroid malignancies. The firm generates revenue primarily by selling its proprietary oral medicines in the United States and earning royalties through distribution partnerships in international markets. Its business model relies on a deep clinical development pipeline and strategic collaborations with other pharmaceutical entities to expand treatment options and maintain a specialized position within the oncology healthcare sector.

Exelixis Grows Sales as New Cancer Drug Advances

Exelixis makes medicines to treat tough cancers like kidney and liver cancer. Its main drug, cabozantinib, brings in over 90% of sales and is still its biggest success. Over the last five years, the stock has returned 91.3% compared to the S&P 500's 72.7%, outperforming by 18.6 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $610.8 million (up 10.0% YoY)
Non-GAAP EPS: $0.87 (up 40.3% YoY)
Net product revenue from cabozantinib: $555.0 million (up 8.1% YoY)

Latest Developments

Zanzalintinib, a new cancer drug, has entered regulatory review in the U.S, which could help future growth and reduce reliance on one product.
Management kept its full-year revenue forecast and highlighted 2026 as a key year for new drug trial results and FDA decisions.
