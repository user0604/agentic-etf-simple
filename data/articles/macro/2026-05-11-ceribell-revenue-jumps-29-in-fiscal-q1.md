Key Takeaways
CeriBell delivered strong revenue growth but widened its net loss in the first quarter.
Revenue rose year over year.
Management raised full-year revenue guidance but warned of continued high operating costs and legal expenses.

CeriBell (NASDAQ:CBLL) extended its rapid growth in brain monitoring systems but reported a larger loss for the quarter. The company, a provider of rapid, AI-powered EEG solutions for acute care hospitals, marked accelerated customer expansion and new product launches. These results, released on May 11, 2026, cover the first quarter of fiscal 2026 and reflect solid revenue momentum, partly offset by heavier spending and legal costs. Overall, the quarter highlighted robust adoption but also underscored the challenge of achieving profitability.

Notable developments
Total revenue rose 29%, driven by both product and subscription sales.
Operating expenses grew 36%, increasing faster than revenue, and were led by higher general and administrative costs, mainly legal fees.
Management raised annual revenue guidance to $112–116 million, citing strong early-year demand and customer growth.
Management outlook and what to watch

Company leadership expressed confidence in continued demand for its brain monitoring technology, as reflected in the higher full-year guidance. However, it noted that ongoing legal costs and elevated spending will keep profitability out of reach in the near term. Investors should watch for gross margin resilience and signs that CeriBell can control its expanding operating costs as it launches new products and moves toward key regulatory milestones.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue rose strongly due to account growth and higher adoption, with both product and subscription lines up 29%. The company ended the period with 680 active customer accounts, up by 33 from the last quarter of 2025. This expansion was supported by the commercial launch of new monitoring products for neonate and pediatric care after successful pilots.

Operating expenses outpaced revenue, growing to $43.9 million, mainly from increased research and development (R&D), sales, and sharply higher general and administrative (G&A) costs. Legal expenses linked to intellectual property enforcement, including a large patent suit, stood out, with G&A up 54% year over year. This spending pushed the net loss deeper despite top-line growth.

Management now projects revenue of $112–116 million for 2026. It reported that gross margin remained strong at 87%, near the upper end of its guidance range, but noted that any future margin declines will be watched closely. Cash reserves, while healthy, declined by $18.1 million to $141.2 million due to investment and legal outflows.

What CeriBell does

CeriBell makes wearable, AI-enabled EEG monitoring systems for hospitals, designed to deliver rapid brain health diagnostics at the bedside.
