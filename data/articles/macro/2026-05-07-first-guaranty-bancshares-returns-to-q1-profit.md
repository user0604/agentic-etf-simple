First Guaranty Bancshares (NASDAQ:FGBI) ended its losing streak and moved back to profitability in the latest quarter. The regional bank, which operates in Louisiana and neighboring markets, prioritized risk reduction, capital improvement, and stabilization of earnings over growth. Its results for the first quarter, released on May 7, 2026, show that the company executed well against these goals. Overall, the period brought progress on asset quality and cost control but also revealed ongoing pressure on core revenues and loan balances.

Notable Developments
Positive earnings returned with net income at $2.7 million, reversing a loss in the same period a year ago.
Net interest income dropped by 6.8%, reflecting a smaller loan book and increasing funding costs.
Total loans fell to $1.9 billion, a 7.0% decrease, as the bank deliberately shrank its loan portfolio to reduce risk.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management continues to emphasize capital strength and risk reduction, with no announcement of aggressive loan or deposit growth plans. It plans to maintain its conservative approach, targeting further improvements in asset quality and expense efficiency. Investors should watch whether profitability gains can continue despite shrinking loan balances and lower core revenue streams.

Quarterly Highlights

The return to profitability was powered by a sharp drop in loan loss provisions, down from $14.5 million to $2.6 million, and a steady reduction in nonperforming assets. Asset quality improved further as nonperforming assets declined by $12 million to $83.5 million by quarter end. Noninterest expense declined by 7% thanks to lower staff and facility costs, reflecting the company’s focus on efficiency.

The bank maintained its quarterly dividend at $0.01 per share, while book value per share declined from $12.23 to $11.91 over the quarter. Despite progress, the level of nonperforming assets remains elevated compared to peers, and ongoing margin compression challenges the revenue base.

First Guaranty Bancshares provides community banking services including commercial, consumer, and agricultural loans and deposit services, serving customers primarily in Louisiana and surrounding regions.
