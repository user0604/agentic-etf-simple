EXLS Company Overview
ExlService provides data analytics and digital operations solutions to help businesses in sectors like insurance and healthcare manage complex processes. The firm generates revenue by designing artificial intelligence tools and managing mission-critical functions such as claims processing and risk management for its clients. By embedding its proprietary software and industry-specific models directly into client workflows, the company creates high switching costs and maintains long-term partnerships through deeply integrated technical services.
