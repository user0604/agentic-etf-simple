Off The Hook YS Reports 10% Revenue Rise in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 4:16 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue hit a record in a seasonally slow quarter, but rising expenses led to a net loss.
Gross profit increased 18.3% compared to the prior-year period.
Management raised its full-year revenue outlook, expecting continued top-line growth.

Marine retailer Off The Hook YS (NYSEMKT:OTH) logged record first-quarter revenue but saw profitability pressured by sharply higher expenses. The company, a vertically integrated seller and broker of new and pre-owned boats, reported results for the period ended March 31 (released May 14, 2026). Overall, the quarter illustrated strong demand for pre-owned boats even as new boat sales and earnings fell.

Notable Developments
Pre-owned boat sales revenue increased by 31.8%, with unit sales up 55% to a record 124 boats.
New boat sales dropped 76.4%, with margins falling as pre-owned became the main revenue driver.
Operating expenses more than tripled due to hiring, stock-based compensation, and costs related to public company growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its fiscal 2026 revenue target to $165 million–$170 million, up from $155 million–$160 million, based on strong sales momentum entering the second quarter. It aims to grow through expansion of its broker network, acquisitions, and further development of digital and service platforms. Investors should closely watch expense growth, liquidity, and the impact of the Apex Marine acquisition for signs of improved profitability as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Pre-owned boats remained the clear growth engine, now generating over 93% of revenue. Unit volume surged even as the average sale price decreased to around $224,000, likely reflecting some mix shift towards lower-value models or broader market softness.

New boat sales fell sharply, both in revenue and units sold, as the company reduced its focus on launching new models, and demand slowed. Gross margin in this segment narrowed substantially, highlighting competitive and pricing pressures industry-wide.

Expenses climbed rapidly, driven by new post-IPO stock-based compensation, an enlarged workforce, and double-digit increases in rent, marketing, and professional services. This resulted in a move to an operating loss and negative adjusted EBITDA for the quarter.

What Off The Hook YS Does

The company operates a vertically integrated platform for buying, selling, financing, and servicing new and pre-owned boats nationwide.
