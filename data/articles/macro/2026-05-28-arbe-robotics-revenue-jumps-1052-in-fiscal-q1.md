Notable Developments
The company reported revenue of $0.5 million for the quarter, meeting around 8–12% of its full-year target of $4–$6 million.
Cost-reduction measures are expected to reduce ongoing expenses by about 15% beginning in mid-Q2, with operating loss narrowed to $11.3 million from $13.4 million the previous year.
Arbe Robotics strengthened its liquidity position, closing the quarter with $53.6 million in cash and short-term bank deposits following a financing round that raised $18.5 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed full-year guidance, projecting revenue between $4 million and $6 million and an adjusted EBITDA loss between $28 million and $31 million. It highlighted that revenue ramp is expected in the second half of the year and noted ongoing dependence on adoption cycles of major automotive manufacturers. Investors should watch for updates on design wins with automakers and evidence of uptake in new non-automotive verticals.

Quarterly Highlights

Arbe Robotics’ radar technology continues to attract orders in autonomous driving, with additional shipments of its Phoenix imaging radar for Level 4 (high automation) applications and new engagements for the Chinese market through the Hirain partnership. These follow-on orders provide technical validation but represent early-stage commercial progress.

The company broadened its strategy beyond supplying chipsets, now selling complete radar systems for perimeter security and physical AI (artificial intelligence) use cases. Initial shipments to major security and AI customers mark a move to diversify applications and widen the business’s addressable markets.

On expenses, the company implemented cost-reduction measures expected to lower its overall expense base by approximately 15%, improving its operational efficiency. Despite this, both the operating loss and adjusted EBITDA loss remained significant relative to revenue. Management pointed out that the current cash balance supports ongoing investments and compliance with financing agreements.

What Arbe Robotics Does

Arbe Robotics develops advanced imaging radar chipsets and systems for automotive and industrial applications, aiming to enable safe autonomy through high-resolution sensing.
