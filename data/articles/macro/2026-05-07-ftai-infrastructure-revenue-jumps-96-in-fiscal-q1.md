Notable Developments
The company agreed to sell its Long Ridge power asset for $1.52 billion, which will eliminate $1.16 billion of related debt and provide $300 million for parent-level debt repayment.
Revenue nearly doubled year over year, while Adjusted EBITDA fell by more than half due to a planned outage and higher costs.
The quarterly dividend remained unchanged at $0.03 per share, reflecting a steady payout policy.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects free cash flow to improve after the Long Ridge sale closes, mainly from lower interest expense. Investors should monitor the timing of the transaction and its effect on debt and profitability, as well as progress at the Repauno terminal project.

Quarterly Highlights

The announced sale of Long Ridge marks a major strategic shift. Once completed, it will remove the company’s largest power asset and reduce total debt by $1.16 billion, with $300 million allocated to repay parent-level debt.

Core businesses, especially the Railroad and Jefferson Terminal segments, delivered strong operating results. Core segment Adjusted EBITDA reached $78.8 million, but company-wide results were affected by a 25-day planned outage at Long Ridge and higher non-operating expenses such as interest and debt extinguishment.

Profitability declined sharply, with a net loss attributable to stockholders of $(150.2) million compared to a $109.7 million profit in the prior year period. This was driven by the one-time outage and higher interest and non-cash charges.

What Ftai Infrastructure Does

Ftai Infrastructure owns, develops, and operates transportation and energy infrastructure assets, including freight railroads, energy terminals, and power facilities, aiming to generate stable long-term cash flows.
