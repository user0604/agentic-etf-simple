Sensus Healthcare Revenue Drops 59% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 7, 2026, 4:44 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Sensus Healthcare faced a business setback as revenue dropped sharply following the loss of its largest customer.
Gross margin fell year over year, with gross margin at 29.2% in Q1 2026 compared to 52.2% in Q1 2025.
Management sees rising pipeline activity tied to recent reimbursement improvements, but recovery timing remains uncertain.

Sensus Healthcare (NASDAQ:SRTS) reported disappointing first-quarter results as the transition away from its largest customer weighed heavily on its top line. The provider of superficial radiotherapy (SRT) devices for non-melanoma skin cancer and keloid scar treatment continues to pivot toward customer diversification and recurring revenue, but that pivot has not overcome the steep decline in system sales. The latest earnings release on May 7, 2026, covered the quarter ended March 31.

Overall, the period featured significant revenue and profitability declines, offset only partly by encouraging longer-term progress elsewhere.

Notable Developments
The company shipped 14 SRT systems in the quarter, none to its largest historical customer, compared to 30 the prior year.
Gross margin dropped to 29.2%, reflecting a greater mix of international shipments and rental placements that carry lower profitability.
Cash reserves decreased to $18.3 million due to ongoing losses and an inventory build, with inventories rising to $16.5 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management pointed to rising customer interest and a growing pipeline driven by new reimbursement codes for SRT treatments, but did not provide detailed forecasts for coming quarters. The key area for investors to watch will be whether increased reimbursement leads to a visible rebound in shipped units and revenue during the rest of fiscal 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue fell sharply year over year, largely due to the lack of purchases from the company’s previously largest customer. Excluding last year’s largest customer, current quarter revenue was higher, underlining the impact of customer diversification—but this was not enough to offset the overall sales decline. System shipments totaled 14 units, the same as last quarter but less than half of the level from the prior year.

The company's gross margin eroded to 29.2% driven by a higher share of international sales and the Fair Deal Agreement program, which recognizes revenue over time. Recurring revenue initiatives—particularly the Fair Deal placements and new Sensus Link offerings—are aimed at stabilizing revenue, but these have not yet compensated for shrinking up-front system sales.

On the regulatory front, new reimbursement codes went into effect Jan. 1, 2026, substantially increasing potential income for providers using SRT and stimulating pipeline activity. However, this improved reimbursement clarity has not yet led to increased revenue as expected, and management’s earlier outlook for sequential revenue growth was not realized this period.

What Sensus Healthcare Does

Sensus Healthcare designs, manufactures, and sells non-invasive radiation therapy systems used mainly to treat non-melanoma skin cancer and keloid scars.
