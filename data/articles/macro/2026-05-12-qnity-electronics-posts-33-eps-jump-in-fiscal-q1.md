Qnity Electronics Posts 33% EPS Jump in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Qnity Electronics achieved broad-based revenue and adjusted profit growth, but saw a drop in reported net income.
Adjusted EPS rose 33.3%, reflecting improved operating performance compared with the prior year.
Management raised its full-year 2026 outlook for all major financial metrics.

Qnity Electronics (NYSE:Q) posted strong overall operating results, showing robust sales and adjusted profit growth, though reported net income fell compared with the previous year. The leading supplier of advanced materials and solutions for the semiconductor industry benefited from higher demand in both of its core business segments. Its first-quarter results, released May 12, 2026, covered the quarter ending March 31. Overall, the period was positive for underlying business momentum, despite some one-time costs holding back net income growth.

Notable Developments
Both Semiconductor Technologies and Interconnect Solutions segments registered double-digit sales growth, with Interconnect Solutions up 25% year over year.
Profit growth was seen on an adjusted basis, but GAAP net income fell 18.6% due to one-time transformation and IT charges.
Management responded to strong demand and execution by raising its full-year 2026 revenue and profit guidance.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised guidance for net sales, adjusted profit, and cash flow, citing ongoing robust demand and strong customer partnerships. The most important area for investors to watch is the company’s ability to turn this growth into improved overall cash flow and to control non-recurring costs as transformation programs progress.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales increased across all business lines and regions. Revenue in the Semiconductor Technologies segment rose 12%, driven mainly by higher chip volume used in applications such as artificial intelligence and high-performance computing. Interconnect Solutions segment sales climbed 25%, benefiting from projects to expand capacity and investment in next-generation connectivity.

On the profit side, while adjusted earnings and operating margin stayed strong, reported (GAAP) net income declined due to higher expenses for business transformation and investment in information technology systems. These non-recurring charges were spelled out by management as primarily tied to ongoing IT independence initiatives.

Cash from operations was lower this quarter, reflecting increased investment in facilities and higher working capital requirements as the company scaled up to meet rising demand. Adjusted free cash flow declined compared to Q1 2025, but capital spending also funded capacity aligned with company strategy. The company paid $17 million in dividends and bought back $25 million of its own shares.

What Qnity Electronics Does

Qnity Electronics develops and supplies specialty materials and advanced solutions that are essential for the manufacture of semiconductors and electronic systems worldwide.
