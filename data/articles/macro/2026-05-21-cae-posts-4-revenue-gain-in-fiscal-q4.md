Notable Developments
The Civil Aviation segment saw its adjusted segment operating income margin drop to 20.4% in Q4 and utilization fell to 73%—signals of ongoing market weakness.
Defense operations improved, with Defense adjusted segment operating income margin rising to 10.2% and revenue up 6% from last year’s quarter.
Free cash flow was $135.0 million for the quarter, down 30% year over year as the company continued its multi-year transformation plan.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled fiscal 2027 as an “execution year,” focusing on driving efficiency and progressing transformation goals. It projects low-single-digit consolidated revenue growth with Civil Aviation likely flat or slightly down, and Defense expected to rise. Investors should watch for early signs of recovery in Civil utilization and margins, as these are central to the multi-year profit targets now outlined through fiscal 2030.

Quarterly Highlights

The Civil Aviation Training Solutions segment had lower profitability, as margins and order intake both weakened. Utilization rates also fell to 73%, reflecting overcapacity and soft demand for pilot training and simulators.

Defense and Security operations stood out, with a margin improvement to 10.2% and quarterly revenue of $580 million. This business benefited from ongoing demand for integrated training solutions and several recent contract wins.

The company continued to generate strong adjusted order intake, reaching $1.6 billion for the quarter, which was up 20% from a year ago and reflected substantial new business—especially in Defense, offsetting some of Civil’s softness.
