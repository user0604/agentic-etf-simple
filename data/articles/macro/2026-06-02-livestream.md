GitLab Goes All-In on the AI Table

6:51 pm — GTLB -5.83% today (-11.22% after hours)

GitLab (NASDAQ: GTLB) is showing 350 employees the door, about 14% of its workforce, and waving goodbye to 22 countries as it remakes itself around AI software creation. The whole reshuffle will run $30 million to $35 million and should be tidied up by early next year.

The Street's take: Shares initially popped 7% after hours, but were down more than 11% at time of writing. It at first seemed that traders were delighted to see GitLab join the ever-growing line of software firms rebranding layoffs as an "AI pivot." First-quarter revenue jumped 23% year-over-year to $264.2 million, sailing past estimates, while the count of customers paying $100,000-plus climbed 18%.
Management nudged the goalposts higher, too: GitLab raised the floor on full-year revenue guidance and bumped its adjusted earnings target to 79–82 cents a share, with losses shrinking dramatically from a year ago.

Ulta’s Quarter Needs No Filter

6:12 pm — ULTA -1.18% today (+1.84% after hours)

★ ULTA is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team HG)

Ulta Beauty (NASDAQ: ULTA) showed up flawless to its fiscal first quarter. Earnings of $7.74 a share blew past the $6.89 Wall Street was hoping for, and revenue rose 11% to $3.16 billion. CEO Kecia Steelman says the strategy is working "in an uncertain economic landscape."

Delawhere? Samsara Reincorporates in Nevada

4:40 pm — IOT -3.09% today

★ IOT is recommended in Rule Breakers

By Jason Moser
TEAM RULE BREAKERS

Samsara (NYSE: IOT) completed a reincorporation from Delaware to Nevada effective June 1, 2026, filing conversion certificates in both states. The company stresses no operational changes and management, employees, contracts, and the listing all remain intact. Shares converted 1-for-1, and equity awards carry over under identical terms. The move follows a broader corporate trend toward Nevada’s more business-friendly governance framework.
