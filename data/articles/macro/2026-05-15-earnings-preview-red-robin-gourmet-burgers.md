Earnings Preview: Red Robin Gourmet Burgers

The Motley Fool Team

May 15, 2026, 7:02 PM

Comments

Red Robin Gourmet Burgers (NASDAQ:RRGB), which operates a chain of casual dining restaurants specializing in burgers and American fare, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, Red Robin reported total revenues of $269 million, reflecting a decrease compared to the prior year primarily due to lower comparable restaurant sales and the impact of restaurant closures. Comparable sales for the quarter declined 3.3% (excluding deferred loyalty revenue), driven by a 3.6% drop in guest traffic, while average check increased slightly by 0.3%. Despite the sales decline, restaurant-level operating margin was 11.4%, nearly flat year over year. Adjusted EBITDA reached $11.8 million for the quarter, which was ahead of management’s expectations. The company’s cost-saving initiatives, including labor efficiency improvements, contributed to profitability, even as industry-wide traffic softened in October and November. Management highlighted that December traffic outperformed the industry benchmark, attributed to targeted marketing and value offerings.

Positives
Profitability exceeded expectations: Adjusted EBITDA and restaurant-level margin both came in ahead of guidance, showing that cost reductions and operational improvements are having a measurable impact.
Labor efficiency gains: Labor costs as a percentage of sales fell by 250 basis points for the year, reflecting successful implementation of new management models and technology.
Targeted marketing showed results: Micro-targeted marketing campaigns drove an inflection in December traffic, helping Red Robin outperform the industry average for the first time since 2024.
Balance sheet improvement: Liquidity increased and debt was reduced, supported by improved operating cash flow and the absence of new equity dilution after the at-the-market program was terminated without issuing shares.
Negatives
Sales and traffic declines: Comparable sales and guest traffic both fell during the quarter, underlining ongoing demand challenges in the casual dining sector.
Restaurant closures impacted revenue: The closure of 22 company-owned restaurants contributed to the year-over-year revenue decrease, and the company continues to evaluate underperforming locations.
Commodity cost pressures: Beef inflation remained high and is expected to persist, offsetting some of the operational gains.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Impact of new menu and value platform: The expanded Big Yummm value offerings and new menu introduced in late January are expected to influence both average check and guest traffic. Early feedback suggests positive guest engagement, but Q1 will provide the first full-quarter results.
Weather-related disruptions: Winter storms in January and February led to lost operating days and some temporary closures. Investors should watch for commentary on the magnitude of these impacts and any recovery in March and April.
Progress on refranchising and debt refinancing: Management continues to pursue tactical refranchising and refinancing initiatives to strengthen the balance sheet. Updates on transaction progress or completed deals could affect the company’s financial flexibility.
Labor and technology initiatives: The rollout of AI-driven labor management tools and new server/tabletop devices is underway. Watch for updates on how these changes are affecting labor costs, service speed, and guest satisfaction.
Guidance and margin outlook: Management’s full-year 2026 guidance calls for a modest increase in comparable sales and improved margins. Q1 results and commentary will help investors gauge whether the company is on track to meet these targets, especially amid ongoing commodity inflation and competitive pressures.
