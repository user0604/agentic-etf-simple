Earnings Preview: Karyopharm Therapeutics

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Karyopharm Therapeutics (NASDAQ:KPTI), a company that develops oral cancer medicines for blood and solid tumors, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

The company reported total revenue of $34.1 million for the fourth quarter of 2025, which included $32.1 million in U.S. XPOVIO (selinexor) net product revenue. This result met analyst expectations for sales. The company reported a net loss of $102.2 million, or $5.71 per share, on a GAAP basis for the quarter. This loss was higher than analyst consensus, primarily due to non-operational items such as a loss on extinguishment of debt and higher interest expense. Operating expenses continued to decline year over year, reflecting ongoing cost-reduction efforts. XPOVIO, the company’s main product for multiple myeloma and certain lymphomas, maintained stable demand in a competitive market, and the company reiterated its 2026 revenue guidance of $130 million to $150 million. Management highlighted disciplined spending and a cash runway extending into the second quarter of 2026, but noted the need for additional financing beyond that point.

Positives
XPOVIO revenue growth continued: Net product revenue rose 9.6% year over year in the fourth quarter, showing the product’s resilience in a crowded market for multiple myeloma treatments.
Operating expenses declined significantly: Research and development expenses fell 17% and selling, general, and administrative costs dropped 16% from the prior year’s quarter, reflecting effective cost controls.
Commercial execution remained steady: The company’s sales and market access teams maintained XPOVIO’s presence in both community and academic settings, with about 60% of sales coming from community practices.
Negatives
Net loss widened substantially: The reported loss for the quarter and full year increased due to non-cash items, including a large loss on debt extinguishment and higher interest expense from recent refinancing.
Cash position decreased: Cash, cash equivalents, and investments fell to $63.7 million at year end, raising concerns about liquidity past the second quarter of 2026 without new financing.
End of Menarini reimbursement: 2025 was the last year for $15 million in annual R&D reimbursement from Menarini, which will reduce future license and other revenue.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Progress toward pivotal myelofibrosis data: Investors should monitor updates on the Phase III SENTRY trial for selinexor in myelofibrosis, with top-line results expected in March 2026. This could be a major catalyst for the company.
XPOVIO sales trajectory and guidance: Watch for any changes in XPOVIO sales trends and whether management updates its 2026 revenue guidance of $130 million to $150 million, especially as competition in multiple myeloma remains intense.
Liquidity and financing plans: The company’s cash runway only extends into the second quarter of 2026. Investors should look for updates on financing initiatives, equity offerings, or strategic alternatives to address ongoing funding needs.
Updates on endometrial cancer program: Progress in the Phase III XPORT-EC-042 trial for selinexor in endometrial cancer, with data expected in mid-2026, will be important for assessing pipeline value beyond myelofibrosis.
Status of next-generation assets and pipeline expansion: Any new developments or timelines for the next-generation XPO1 inhibitor eltanexor in other blood cancers or solid tumors could provide insight into longer-term growth opportunities.
