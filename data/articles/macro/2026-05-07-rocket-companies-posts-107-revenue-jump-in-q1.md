Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects adjusted revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 to range between $2.7 billion and $2.9 billion, similar to the period just reported. The company highlights ongoing cost synergy realization from its Redfin and Mr. Cooper integrations, with full expense savings expected by the end of 2026. Investors should focus on whether Rocket can bring expense growth under control while maintaining technology-driven gains in origination and servicing streams.

Quarterly Highlights

Rocket’s Direct to Consumer segment more than doubled its contribution margin year over year, propelled by new product expansions and improvements in AI-enabled client prospecting. The Partner Network also saw its contribution nearly triple, reflecting greater engagement and output from third-party originated loan businesses.

The company's mortgage servicing portfolio remained steady at $2.1 trillion in unpaid principal balance, reinforcing its position as the nation’s largest servicer. Servicing fee income jumped to $1.08 billion, up from $401 million, aided by the recent Mr. Cooper acquisition and customer portfolio growth.

Redfin and Mr. Cooper integrations progressed faster than planned, with management stating it is on track to achieve full $400 million expense synergies a year ahead of schedule. AI initiatives added $1 billion a month in incremental origination volume, underscoring Rocket’s focus on technology.
