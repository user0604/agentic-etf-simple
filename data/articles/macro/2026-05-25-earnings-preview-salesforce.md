Earnings Preview: Salesforce

The Motley Fool Team

May 25, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Salesforce (NYSE:CRM), a leading provider of customer relationship management software and cloud-based business tools, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Wednesday, May 27, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026, Salesforce reported revenue of $11.2 billion, which was up 12% year over year. Full-year revenue reached $41.5 billion, marking the company’s first year above the $40 billion mark. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) and revenue both beat analyst estimates for the quarter. The company highlighted strong momentum in its Agentforce and Data 360 product lines, which are focused on artificial intelligence (AI) and data integration. The recently acquired Informatica business contributed to these results, with Informatica Cloud annual recurring revenue (ARR) now included in Salesforce’s reported figures. Management also pointed to robust contract wins, including a notable $5.6 billion U.S. Army agreement. Despite these positive results, current remaining performance obligation (CRPO) growth was in line with guidance and did not exceed expectations, which some investors had hoped for.

Positives
Revenue and EPS beat estimates: Both revenue and adjusted EPS for Q4 FY2026 exceeded analyst consensus, reflecting strong execution and demand for Salesforce’s core and new offerings.
Rapid growth in Agentforce and Data 360: Agentforce and Data 360 ARR, including Informatica Cloud, reached $2.9 billion, up over 200% year over year. This highlights successful adoption of AI-powered products.
Large contract wins and customer expansion: The company saw a 26% year-over-year increase in deals over $1 million and a 33% increase in deals over $10 million, signaling strong traction with large enterprises and government clients.
Shareholder returns and capital allocation: Salesforce returned over $14 billion to shareholders in FY2026 through buybacks and dividends, and increased its share repurchase authorization to $50 billion, showing confidence in its long-term outlook.
Negatives
CRPO growth met but did not exceed guidance: Current remaining performance obligation grew 16% year over year, in line with guidance but not above it, which may raise concerns about the pace of new business growth.
Weakness in certain product lines: Marketing, commerce, and Tableau products underperformed relative to other segments, which partially offset the gains from AI and data offerings.
Professional services revenue declined: Revenue from professional services and other segments fell 4% year over year, reflecting lower demand for large, multi-year transformation projects.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
AI and agentic product adoption and monetization: Investors should look for updates on Agentforce and Data 360 ARR growth and how quickly customers are adopting and expanding their use of these AI-driven features.
Integration and performance of Informatica: Watch for details on how Informatica’s cloud data management tools are being integrated and whether they are driving incremental revenue and cross-selling opportunities.
Trends in large enterprise and government contracts: Pay attention to new large deal announcements or renewals, as these have been a key driver of recent growth and could impact future remaining performance obligations.
Progress on cost efficiency and operating margins: Management has guided to a non-GAAP operating margin of 34.3% for FY2027. Updates on restructuring, workforce changes, and ongoing cost controls will be important for profitability.
Updates to revenue reporting and product segmentation: Salesforce has announced it will reevaluate its revenue by cloud disclosures in FY2027. Investors should look for any changes in how the company reports product line performance and what this means for tracking future growth.
