Xunlei Revenue Jumps 54% in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 28, 2026, 7:12 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Xunlei reported strong business momentum as subscription and live-streaming revenue surged.
Revenue jumped 54.1% from the prior year period.
Management signaled a continued focus on core digital media growth and international expansion, with no formal guidance provided for coming quarters.

Subscription and live-streaming gains pushed Xunlei (NASDAQ:XNET) to notable revenue growth even as gross margins declined and net losses widened due to a large investment markdown. The China-based digital media and internet platform focuses on subscription services, audio live-streaming, and media acceleration tools. In results announced May 28, 2026 (for the first quarter of fiscal 2026), Xunlei delivered substantial top-line expansion, but encountered pressure on profitability and margin quality. Overall, the quarter showed rapid revenue growth but highlighted tradeoffs between new growth areas and earnings consistency.

Notable Developments
Total revenue climbed to $98.6 million, up 54.1% year over year, driven by live-streaming and subscription growth.
Gross profit margin narrowed to 58.5%, from 61.9% a year ago, as live-streaming grew to be the single largest revenue contributor.
A net loss from continuing operations of $192.4 million reflected a $195.1 million non-cash loss from writing down a long-term investment.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled ongoing investment in digital media, product innovation, and further growth in international live-streaming. No explicit financial guidance was offered for the coming quarter. Investors should closely monitor the balance between rapid revenue gains and pressure on margins, especially as the business mix continues shifting toward lower-margin segments.

Quarterly Highlights

Live-streaming and other services revenue increased by 89.3% year over year, reaching $53.6 million. This segment became Xunlei’s largest contributor, with management citing strong demand in overseas markets such as Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

The subscription business delivered sustained growth, posting a 26.2% revenue increase to $45.0 million. Management attributed this to user growth and improved digital product experiences in core offerings such as the Xunlei Accelerator and mobile subscription apps.

Net income under GAAP showed a sharp loss, dominated by a $195.1 million non-cash markdown of a long-term investment in Arashi Vision Inc. Non-GAAP profits improved, with non-GAAP diluted EPS rising to $0.07, reflecting the company’s better operating leverage despite margin compression from the changing revenue mix.

What Xunlei Does

Xunlei operates an online digital media and internet acceleration platform, offering cloud-based subscription services, media acceleration products, and interactive live-streaming in China and global markets.
