Business overview

The company’s core products, EsoGuard and EsoCheck, are designed to detect esophageal precancer and cancer in high-risk patients. EsoGuard is a DNA assay that measures methylation at specific gene sites, while EsoCheck is a non-invasive device for collecting esophageal cells. Both tools are intended to provide early detection and improve patient outcomes compared to traditional endoscopy.

Quarterly performance

During the quarter, Lucid processed 3,177 EsoGuard tests, a decrease from the prior quarter’s 3,664 tests. Revenue for the period was $1.3 million, reflecting the ongoing challenge of limited reimbursement and slower commercial adoption. Operating expenses remained tightly controlled at $13.4 million, nearly flat compared to the same period last year.

The company’s non-GAAP loss per share improved to $(0.07), down from $(0.16) a year ago, as cost discipline and lower net loss attributed to common stockholders helped narrow losses. Lucid raised $17 million in new capital and ended the quarter with $27.9 million in cash, with pro forma cash after the offering reaching $45 million. This extended its financial runway into 2027, providing flexibility as it awaits reimbursement breakthroughs.

Management noted that test volumes fell back from the record set in the fourth quarter of 2025, when 3,664 tests were processed. This step back contrasts with prior expectations that higher volumes could be sustained. The company continues to focus on achieving Medicare coverage, which it views as the most important milestone for accelerating commercial expansion.

Looking ahead

Management reiterated that securing Medicare coverage remains its top priority, as this would be a pivotal step for broader adoption and revenue growth. With a strengthened cash position, Lucid believes it can support operations through 2027 while awaiting reimbursement progress. Investors should watch for updates on clinical guideline recognition and any signs of increased test volumes as coverage discussions advance.
