Auna Posts 13% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 5:24 PM

Key Takeaways
Auna recorded strong revenue and cash flow growth, but saw profits and margins squeezed.
Adjusted earnings fell sharply while revenue rose year over year.
Management reaffirmed its 2026 outlook, with investors watching margin pressures and debt levels.

Healthcare provider Auna (NYSE:AUNA) delivered solid top-line gains but struggled with shrinking profits and thinner margins this quarter. The diversified operator, active in Mexico, Peru, and Colombia, released its first-quarter 2026 results on May 19, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter showed healthy growth in service volumes and cash flow, but profitability was impacted by non-operating items and higher costs in key markets.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 13%, supported by growth in all regions, especially Colombia and Mexico, with both countries posting double-digit gains.
Adjusted EBITDA margin narrowed by 2.9 percentage points as cost pressures and revenue adjustments, mainly in Peru and Mexico, weighed on profits.
Strong operating cash flow rose to S/175 million, up 65%, helping the balance sheet despite the leverage ratio ticking up slightly to 3.7x.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year guidance for both revenue and Adjusted EBITDA, citing robust cash generation and service volume increases as support. Management expects growth to remain limited in the first and second quarters, with performance giving confidence in achieving full-year targets as trends improve across all markets. Investors should closely watch whether margin recovery materializes in Mexico and whether revenue adjustments in Peru return to normal levels, as both are critical for achieving the company’s profitability and leverage targets.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue expanded across all major regions, with Colombia up 18% and Mexico up 15% year over year, reflecting strong demand for high-complexity healthcare services. Peru contributed the largest share of adjusted EBITDA, but margin pressures emerged due to delayed pharmacy rebates and unusually high revenue penalties from payors.

Service volumes increased: surgeries performed rose to 21,610 and hospital bed occupancy reached nearly 75%, up from 72% last year. Oncosalud, the company’s prepaid health plan business in Peru, grew its membership base by 6% to 1.44 million, signaling continued progress in service offer expansion.

Despite the top-line growth, overall Adjusted EBITDA dropped slightly, and the reported margin narrowed to 18.4%, down from 21.4% a year ago. This was mostly due to external cost factors (notably FX, or foreign currency, fluctuations) and higher administrative costs, as well as ongoing regulatory pressures in Peru.

What Auna Does

Auna operates a multi-country network of hospitals, clinics, and health plans focused on providing healthcare services, risk-sharing insurance, and specialty care across Latin America.
