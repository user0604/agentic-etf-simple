Mobilicom Posts 35% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 4:08 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Revenue declined as timing shifts in major defense contracts delayed recognition of key orders.
Order backlog more than doubled year over year, while revenue dropped 35%.
Management expects increased revenue and cash flow as large orders are delivered in coming quarters.

Drone cybersecurity specialist Mobilicom (NASDAQ:MOB) faced a temporary business setback as revenue stalled due to timing changes in customer procurement. The company, a provider of end-to-end cybersecure solutions for autonomous drones and robotics, reported first quarter 2026 results on May 27, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31. A surge in order backlog and new program wins indicates strong underlying demand, but financial benefits from these wins may show up later in the year.

Overall, the period highlighted Mobilicom’s operational progress but showcased the impact of delayed production revenues.

Notable Developments
Revenue fell 35% year over year, as orders from a major U.S. defense contract shifted to later quarters. Management attributed this to client production timelines rather than weaker demand.
Order backlog increased to $1.8 million, a 144% rise from the prior year. Revenue visibility stood at $2.4 million, a 50% increase compared to $1.6 million at the same point last year.
The company closed the quarter with $17.7 million in cash, while monthly net cash burn reached $528,000 as Mobilicom built inventory and invested in new product launches and U.S. operations.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Mobilicom’s leadership expects growth to resume as large defense orders are fulfilled, especially with new contracts in the U.S. Asia-Pacific, and Middle East. Investors should monitor how quickly the current backlog turns into recognized revenue and whether cash burn stabilizes as production ramps up.

Quarterly Highlights

The sharp drop in revenue resulted from a customer’s shift into full production for a major U.S. defense program, causing delays in procurement and delivery. Management emphasized this change as temporary and expects the delayed orders to contribute meaningfully to revenue later in 2026.

Mobilicom’s confirmed order backlog was $1.8 million as of March 31, 2026, and the company announced $2.2 million in new orders for the U.S. Army’s OPF-L drone program, plus initial wins in Asia-Pacific and the Middle East. These wins strengthened Mobilicom’s presence among Tier-1 defense manufacturers and expanded its footprint into new geographic markets.

Investment in R&D continued, with the launch of the SkyHopper Tactical wearable communications system and the SkyHopper MultiBand solution for secure multi-frequency operations. Mobilicom also earned FCC Trusted Drone status, one of only four firms to receive this U.S. cyber compliance recognition, improving eligibility for future defense projects.

What Mobilicom Does

Mobilicom provides cybersecure communications, control, and cybersecurity solutions for drones, robotics, and autonomous platforms used in defense and commercial applications.
