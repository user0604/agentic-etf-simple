Key Takeaways
Yatsen delivered strong revenue growth led by skincare but at the cost of profitability and increased cash outflows.
Revenue rose 22.5% year over year, within previously issued guidance.
Management expects second-quarter revenue growth of 10–20%, with further focus on building brand strength.

Cosmetics company Yatsen (NYSE:YSG) grew its top line sharply as its skincare brands expanded market share, but returned to a sizable net loss amid surging marketing spend. The beauty product maker, known for its broad portfolio of skincare and color cosmetics brands, reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 26, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed strong growth in higher-margin skincare but at the expense of profitability and cash generation.

Notable Developments
Net revenue from skincare brands increased 58.5%, now making up 56.2% of total sales, while color cosmetics declined by 5% year over year.
Operating expenses jumped 32.5% to RMB 918.1 million, driven mainly by a 33% rise in selling and marketing costs.
The quarter saw a return to negative cash flow from operating activities, with a net outflow of RMB 90.0 million compared to an inflow a year ago.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects second-quarter revenue between RMB 1.20 billion and RMB 1.30 billion, which would be 10–20% year-over-year growth. It plans to keep investing heavily in brand-building, especially in skincare. The most important development to watch is whether increased marketing spending can translate into both sustained sales growth and a return to profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

Skincare brands, including premium and clinical names like Galénic, DR.WU, and Eve Lom, drove overall growth, rising to more than half of company revenue. This strategy widened the gap with the color cosmetics segment, which continues to shrink.

Operating costs, especially marketing outlays, outpaced revenue gains by a wide margin. Selling and marketing spend reached 72.2% of revenue, the highest on record for the company, as efforts ramped up on the Douyin platform and other channels.

Cash flow reversed from last year, with operations now consuming RMB 90.0 million. Higher spending, especially around marketing and research, outweighed the improvement in gross margin, putting renewed pressure on the company’s liquidity despite a successful $120 million convertible note placement.

What Yatsen Does

Yatsen is a China-based beauty company offering a range of skincare and color cosmetics brands, sold through both online and offline channels.
