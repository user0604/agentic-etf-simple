Key Takeaways
Wrap Technologies posted strong revenue growth but saw operating losses widen as margins contracted.
Revenue increased 45% year over year in the quarter.
Management reaffirmed a target of approximately 100% revenue growth for 2026, requiring a sharp ramp-up in coming quarters.

Wrap Technologies (NASDAQ:WRAP) delivered solid sales growth, but profitability pressures overshadowed top-line gains during the first quarter. The provider of non-lethal public safety and security technologies, best known for its BolaWrap restraint device, released results for its first quarter fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter highlighted expanding customer adoption, especially internationally and in the federal sector, but also reinforced the need for much stronger growth to reach year-end goals.

Notable Developments
BolaWrap product sales surged 186% year over year, with bookings totaling $3.2 million and international bookings making up $2.1 million of that tally.
Gross margin fell to 62% from a prior-year 78%, despite an improved revenue figure, pointing to a less favorable sales and cost mix.
Operating cash burn improved markedly, with $1.2 million used in the quarter compared to $3.1 million a year ago, bolstered by a fresh capital raise.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its target of approximately 100% revenue growth in 2026 but declined to give granular guidance on expenses or margin recovery. The company's comments pointed to accelerating adoption after the quarter close, especially across new international and federal markets. Investors should monitor whether the company can convert its $3.2 million in bookings into realized revenue fast enough to stay on pace for full-year growth ambitions.

Quarterly Highlights

BolaWrap adoption accelerated, aided by a key order from the U.S. Department of Homeland Security and a shift toward agency-wide product deployments across several law enforcement customers. International momentum remained strong, with the majority of bookings coming from outside the U.S, including initial pre-orders for Wrap’s counter-drone (counter-unmanned aircraft system) offerings in the United Kingdom, Europe, and follow-on business in Panama.

Gross margin contracted by 16 percentage points compared to the prior year, even as recurring ancillary sales like consumables and support increased with a growing installed base. Wrap did not provide detail on the drivers behind this contraction, leaving open questions about cost structure and product mix.

Despite the net loss widening to $4.5 million, the company’s cash position improved sharply, helped by a successful capital raise. Operating cash outflow reduced markedly year over year, improving financial flexibility as Wrap continues to ramp spending in pursuit of expanded market share and new customer verticals.

What Wrap Technologies Does

Wrap Technologies designs and sells non-lethal policing and security tools—including the BolaWrap restraint device—and training and evidence management platforms for law enforcement and public safety clients around the world.
