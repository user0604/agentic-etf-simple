Earnings Preview: Zoom Communications

Zoom Communications (NASDAQ:ZM), a leading provider of video conferencing and collaboration software, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 on Thursday, May 21, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

Zoom Communications reported fourth-quarter results that showed revenue of $1.25 billion, growing 5.3% year over year. This revenue result came in above the high end of company guidance and beat analyst estimates. Enterprise revenue was a key driver, growing 7.1% year over year and now representing 61% of total revenue. The number of customers contributing more than $100,000 in annual revenue increased 9%, and these customers now account for 33% of total revenue. Non-GAAP diluted net income per share was $1.44, which met but did not exceed analyst expectations. Free cash flow for the quarter was $338 million, and the company ended the period with $7.8 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities. Zoom's AI-powered products, especially in customer experience and collaboration, continued to gain traction, and the company completed the acquisition of BrightHire to strengthen its AI offerings in recruiting.

Positives
Enterprise revenue growth accelerated: Enterprise revenue rose 7.1% year over year, showing that Zoom is gaining traction with larger organizations and moving beyond its pandemic-era customer base.
Revenue beat expectations: Total revenue exceeded both company guidance and consensus analyst estimates, indicating continued demand for Zoom’s expanding product suite.
AI product momentum: Adoption of AI-powered features, such as AI Companion 3.0 and Zoom Virtual Agent, accelerated with high double-digit growth in customer experience (CX) annual recurring revenue and new customer wins across sectors.
Strong free cash flow and liquidity: Free cash flow was $338 million for the quarter, and the company ended with $7.8 billion in cash, supporting ongoing investment and share repurchases.
Negatives
Net income per share met but did not beat consensus: Non-GAAP EPS of $1.44 was in line with analyst estimates but did not exceed them, partly due to higher-than-expected taxes in the quarter.
Deferred revenue growth guidance slowed: Management guided for Q1 deferred revenue growth of just 1% to 2% year over year, reflecting billing dynamics from larger, longer-term competitive takeouts that include transition credits.
Online business remains a smaller growth contributor: Revenue from online customers grew just 1.2% year over year, and management expects only slight growth going forward, highlighting reliance on enterprise expansion.
Net dollar expansion rate remains below 100%: The net dollar expansion rate for enterprise customers held steady at 98%, indicating that upsells are not yet offsetting contraction and churn within the existing customer base.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Enterprise revenue growth and large customer traction: Look for continued momentum in enterprise sales and updates on the number of customers contributing over $100,000 in annual revenue, as this segment is driving overall growth.
AI product monetization and adoption: Investors should watch for progress in monetizing AI features, including Custom AI Companion and Zoom Virtual Agent, and any updates on how these products are contributing to revenue.
Deferred revenue and billing trends: Pay attention to deferred revenue growth and management’s commentary on billing patterns, especially as more deals include transition credits for large customers switching from competitors.
Impact of BrightHire acquisition and new product launches: Monitor any early contributions from the BrightHire acquisition and updates on the rollout or adoption of recently launched AI-powered features across the Zoom platform.
Profitability and margin guidance: Watch for updates on operating margin trends, especially in light of ongoing investments in AI and changes to compensation structure, as well as any commentary on free cash flow for the year.
