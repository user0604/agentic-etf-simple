The cybersecurity leader CrowdStrike (NASDAQ:CRWD) posted a standout quarter with faster adoption of its AI-driven security platform and strong cash flow generation. Notable Developments
Annual recurring revenue (ARR) climbed to $5.51 billion, up 24% from a year ago, with net new ARR of $256 million increasing 32% year over year.
The company expanded its Falcon AI platform with new security modules and strategic partnerships, seeing 51% of customers now using six or more product modules, a new high.
Free cash flow margin strengthened to 34% of revenue, while management approved a four-for-one stock split and raised annual guidance for both revenue and profit metrics.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management lifted the full-year fiscal 2027 revenue outlook to a new range of $5.91–$5.96 billion. They forecast net new subscription growth to accelerate and expect free cash flow margins to remain above 30%. Investors should monitor whether high investment in research and acquisitions continues to be matched by margin and profit gains as the company grows.

Quarterly Highlights

Subscription-based revenue continues to drive the business, increasing sharply alongside improved subscription gross margins. ARR reached $5.51 billion, led by robust customer adoption of new product modules and upgrades.

The Falcon platform, which provides cloud-based security powered by artificial intelligence, reported new AI features and integrations with partners like AWS, OpenAI, and NVIDIA. Over half of customers now use at least six product modules, reflecting platform consolidation and stickiness.

Operating expenses, especially for research and development, also rose as investments in innovation continued. Despite spending and recent acquisitions totaling $881 million, the company ended the quarter with cash and equivalents above $4.5 billion.

What CrowdStrike Does

CrowdStrike provides cloud-based cybersecurity solutions, using artificial intelligence to detect and respond to digital threats for businesses worldwide.
