Earnings Preview: Intrusion

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Intrusion (NASDAQ:INTZ), a cybersecurity software provider focused on real-time network threat detection, is expected to report its first quarter 2026 earnings on Thursday, May 14, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Fourth-quarter 2025 results for Intrusion showed a revenue decline to $1.5 million, down 25% from the prior quarter and 12% from the same period last year. This decrease was mainly due to a delayed contract extension with a major U.S. government customer, which management attributed to administrative constraints from a government shutdown and related funding delays. Gross profit margin for the quarter was 74%, slightly lower than the prior year period. The company reported a net loss of $2.8 million, or $0.14 per share, which missed analyst expectations. Operating expenses increased, driven by higher sales and marketing costs as the company invested in expanding its customer base and brand awareness.

Positives
Product and partnership expansion: The launch of Shield Cloud on both AWS and Microsoft Azure marketplaces broadened the company's reach and sales channels, potentially supporting future revenue growth.
New public sector initiatives: The expanded partnership with PortNexus and the rollout of the P.O.S.S.E program for law enforcement agencies created new distribution opportunities and early signs of strong adoption.
Improved balance sheet: Intrusion fully eliminated its outstanding debt and Series A preferred stock during the year, reducing financial risk and interest expense.
Continued R&D investment: Ongoing investment in research and development led to new offerings like Shield Stratus, enhancing the product portfolio and positioning the company for future demand.
Negatives
Delayed government contract revenue: The late extension of a key critical infrastructure contract with the Department of War caused a significant revenue shortfall for the quarter, which also led to a larger net loss.
High customer concentration: Over 94% of annual revenue came from U.S. government entities, highlighting ongoing dependence on a small group of customers and exposure to government funding cycles.
Increased operating expenses: Operating expenses rose to $4 million for the quarter, reflecting higher sales and marketing outlays without a corresponding increase in revenue.
Negative cash flow and going concern risk: The company used $6.8 million in cash for operations in 2025 and ended the year with $3.6 million in cash, with management acknowledging substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern without additional funding.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Recognition of delayed government contract revenue: Investors should look for confirmation that the previously delayed Department of War contract revenue is recognized in Q1, as management indicated this would likely occur in the first half of 2026.
Progress in commercial sales: Watch for signs of traction in commercial markets, including Shield product sales outside the government sector, which remain a small portion of overall revenue but are important for diversification.
Updates on PortNexus partnership and P.O.S.S.E program: The extent of adoption and revenue contribution from these initiatives, which are now scaling across multiple states, will be key to future growth.
Operating expense trends and cash position: Given ongoing losses and increased spending, updates on cost control, cash balance, and any new financing arrangements will be critical for assessing financial sustainability.
Management's outlook on breakeven timing: Management previously indicated a goal to reach breakeven operations by the third quarter of 2026, so any updated guidance or commentary on the path to profitability will be important for investors.
