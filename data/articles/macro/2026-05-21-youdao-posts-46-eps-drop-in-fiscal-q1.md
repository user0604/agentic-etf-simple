Key Takeaways
Youdao booked modest revenue growth but faced sharp declines in profitability and cash flow.
EPS (Non-GAAP) fell 45.6% year over year.
Management remains focused on AI innovation and on driving progress in profitability and cash flow.

Youdao (NYSE:DAO) delivered limited business gains as its core online marketing and learning services segments expanded, but profitability dropped. The leading digital learning and smart device provider in China reported its first quarter 2026 results on May 21, 2026, marking operating profitability for a seventh straight quarter. Overall, the period reflected mixed performance -- revenues were up, but margin and cash flow pressures intensified.

Notable developments
Revenue from online marketing services rose 20.9% year over year, outpacing both learning services and smart devices.
Gross margin declined 2.6 percentage points, mainly due to a steep drop in profitability for smart devices.
Operating cash flow turned negative, with net cash used in operations totaling RMB 93.1 million after a positive prior quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management continues to emphasize its AI-driven strategy, with a focus on expanding product innovation and maintaining operating profitability. It also highlights the importance of driving progress in profitability and cash flow. Investors should closely monitor cash generation and progress in turning around the smart devices segment in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Online marketing services delivered the strongest revenue growth, advancing over 20% on demand for Youdao’s performance advertising and AI-powered offerings. This helped offset weaker gains in learning services and a steep contraction in smart devices.

Profitability metrics deteriorated sharply, with income from operations and non-GAAP EPS both falling by more than 40% year over year. Increased operating expenses, lower gross margins on hardware, and rising expected credit losses all weighed on results.

The period saw a reversal in operating cash flow, driven by declines in working capital and higher operating costs. At the same time, Youdao’s liquidity position depended heavily on support from parent company NetEase, which extended key loan facilities through 2030.

What Youdao does

Youdao provides online learning content, digital education services, and smart devices, primarily serving students and professionals in China.
