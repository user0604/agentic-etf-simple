Quarterly revenue climbed to $148.3 million, an 11% increase year over year, driven by continued strong project demand, especially in data centers and large capital projects.

Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide updated guidance for fiscal 2027, citing the upcoming merger with CECO Environmental as a reason. They anticipate that the merger, expected by June 2026, will significantly increase the company’s growth profile and cash flow potential. Investors should watch how the merger affects profitability and cash flow normalization in coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Thermon’s quarterly order intake grew 3% to $143.5 million, but the ratio of new orders to revenue fell below 1.0, suggesting that future order flow could be a key metric to monitor. This book-to-bill level may reflect timing issues or slowing demand outside high-growth segments.

The company continued to gain traction with solutions for the data center industry, as orders for liquid load banks rose to 220 units since launch and the quote log grew over $100 million, marking progress in its strategy to diversify beyond legacy energy markets.

Operating costs increased sharply, with total selling, general, and administrative spending jumping by about $20 million primarily from merger-related charges and ongoing investments in growth initiatives. These costs, along with rising working capital, contributed to the decline in both profitability and free cash flow for the period.
