Quarterly highlights

The company completed a significant equity capital raise, nearly doubling its cash resources over three months. This funding is expected to allow uninterrupted advancement of its clinical pipeline and completion of major manufacturing projects.

Research and development spending accelerated, with management attributing the increase to higher staffing levels and expanded work with contract manufacturers and clinical trial sites. General and administrative expenses moderated, dropping nearly 11%, due to lower professional and public-company costs.

Pipeline programs advanced, highlighted by patient enrollment milestones for the VMT-α-NET targeted alpha therapy for neuroendocrine tumors and an open cohort in meningioma, a cancer with few systemic treatment options.
