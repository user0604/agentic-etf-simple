Q3 FY2026 Recap

PodcastOne delivered record revenue of $15.9 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026, representing a 25% increase year over year. The company reported an operating loss of $153,000 and a net loss of $154,000, or negative $0.01 per share. Adjusted EBITDA reached a record $2.8 million, a significant improvement from a negative $670,000 in the same quarter last year. These results beat analyst consensus estimates for both revenue and earnings per share. The company ended the quarter with $3.4 million in cash and no debt.

Key business highlights included the launch of a new content network in partnership with Dr. Phil's Envoy Media Company, long-term renewals of popular shows like LadyGang and The Adam Carolla Show, and the expansion of AI-driven capabilities through a partnership with Listener.com. The company also completed the sale of original podcast intellectual property (IP) to Paramount for development as a streaming project, demonstrating the value of its owned content library.

Strategic partnerships and renewals completed: The company secured long-term agreements with high-profile shows and talent, supporting future audience and revenue stability.
Expansion of owned content and IP monetization: The sale of podcast IP for TV development highlights the potential for additional revenue streams beyond audio advertising.

Ongoing net loss despite revenue growth: The company remains unprofitable on a GAAP basis, which may concern investors focused on bottom-line improvement.
