Notable Developments
Distributable earnings per share dropped 75.0% year over year, in line with management's forecast of a “low watermark” quarter as asset resolutions took priority.
The company reduced nonperforming loans to $482 million and took $12.5 million in impairments on six real estate properties, signaling active credit clean-up.
Quarterly common dividend was reset to $0.17 per share, a decrease from $0.43 a year ago, to better align with lower earnings.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership reiterated that results reflected both normal mortgage seasonality and heavy asset resolution activity, with the expectation that improvements will show as nonperforming loans decline and origination activity picks up. Investors should watch closely for a rebound in agency originations and for further reduction in impaired assets, as these will determine how quickly profitability recovers over the coming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Distributable earnings, the company's preferred profitability metric, fell to $0.07 per share as legacy asset write-downs and low mortgage origination volumes hit the bottom line. Management noted that, excluding $22.9 million in realized losses related to asset clean-up, per-share results would have been much higher, highlighting the temporary nature of some challenges.

The structured loan portfolio ended the period with $12.0 billion in unpaid balances, and a modest improvement in interest margin was reported as the spread between loan yields and borrowing costs widened slightly. Nonperforming loans were reduced from $569 million to $482 million quarter over quarter, providing early evidence of credit improvement.

Agency loan originations landed at $707.6 million, which was much lower than the previous quarter’s $1.6 billion. The fee-based servicing portfolio reached $36.31 billion, helping generate steady servicing revenue despite lower origination volumes.
