PLD Company Overview
Prologis owns and manages industrial real estate, focusing on large-scale warehouses and distribution centers located near major population centers. The company generates revenue by leasing these facilities to global logistics providers and retail businesses that require efficient supply chain infrastructure for online fulfillment. By providing essential power and energy solutions alongside physical space, the company creates a technical ecosystem that embeds its properties into the daily operations and digital networks of its customers.

Prologis Raises Dividend as Rent Growth Slows

Prologis owns and rents out warehouses and logistics facilities near important cities and transport areas. The company stands out because most of its new projects are custom-built for specific customers, which helps manage risk. Over the last 5 years, the stock returned 24.5% while the S&P 500 returned 68.6%, underperforming by 44.1 percentage points.
