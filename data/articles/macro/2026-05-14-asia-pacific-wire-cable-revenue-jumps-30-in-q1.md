Key Takeaways
Asia Pacific Wire & Cable delivered strong profit and revenue growth in the latest quarter. Earnings per share improved to $0.05 from a $(0.07) loss year over year. Management did not provide financial guidance for the next quarter.

Asia Pacific Wire & Cable (NASDAQ:APWC) posted a solid business rebound, driven by higher sales and improved profitability across its core markets. The company manufactures and distributes enameled wire, power cables, and telecommunications products for construction, utilities, and electronics sectors throughout the Asia Pacific region. It released its fiscal first quarter results on May 14, 2026, covering the period ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed broad-based operational progress, though some margin and working capital pressures remain.

Notable developments
Revenue increased 30% year over year, with the largest gains in Thailand (up 41% to $51.2 million) and North Asia (up 26%).
Operating profit reached $3.3 million compared with a $2.7 million loss a year ago, supported by gross margin expansion to 8.2%.
Cash and equivalents nearly doubled to $73.2 million, mainly due to a $34.1 million rights offering completed during the period.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management attributed growth to momentum across nearly all geographic markets and favorable commodity pricing but did not issue formal guidance for future quarters. With increased cash and improved margins, investors should monitor working capital—especially rising trade receivables—and watch for ongoing changes in copper prices.

Quarterly highlights

Sales rose across all operating regions, led by Thailand’s public-sector order execution and higher copper prices. North Asia also posted strong year-over-year gains, contributing to a diverse sales mix.

Margins improved as the company benefited from a more favorable product mix, including higher-margin power cables and telecommunications wire, and the positive impact of rising copper prices. Operating expenses increased 19% but remained manageable compared to the revenue gain.

The rights offering added $34.1 million in net proceeds, almost doubling cash at quarter-end compared to the prior quarter. However, trade receivables increased again, raising questions about the pace of customer payments and the cash conversion cycle.

What Asia Pacific Wire & Cable does

The company manufactures and distributes enameled wire, power cables, and telecommunications products for construction, utilities, electronics, and infrastructure sectors across the Asia Pacific region.
