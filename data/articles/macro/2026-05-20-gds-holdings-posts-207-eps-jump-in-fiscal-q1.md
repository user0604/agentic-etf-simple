Key Takeaways
GDS Holdings posted a business win with record new bookings and robust demand for its data center services.
Profitability improved sharply as diluted earnings per share jumped over 200% year over year.
Management reaffirmed the full-year outlook, keeping forecasts for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA unchanged.

Record customer demand drove a strong quarter for GDS Holdings (NASDAQ:GDS), which operates high-specification data centers across China. The company reported first-quarter results on May 20, 2026, covering the three months ended March 31, 2026. Overall, the period featured significant headline profit growth, but much of the surge came from one-off gains rather than underlying operations.

Notable Developments
The company set a new record with approximately 200 megawatts of new data center bookings, reflecting robust customer demand and heightened interest in artificial intelligence infrastructure.
Net income increased due to a large one-off gain from the partial sale and revaluation of the company’s stake in DayOne Data Centers, which contributed substantially to the bottom line.
Cost control improved: general and administrative expenses fell 38% year over year, driven by efficiency measures, even as capital expenditures remained elevated.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its prior forecasts for the full fiscal year, signaling continued confidence in strong market demand and the company’s operational execution. Key targets include total revenue of RMB 12.4 billion to 12.9 billion and adjusted EBITDA of RMB 5.75 billion to 6 billion, with significant ongoing investment in data center capacity. Investors should focus on core, recurring profit and margin trends, as these give a clearer picture of sustainable performance than headline figures.

Quarterly Highlights

The period’s headline profit surge was boosted by a financial gain from the DayOne Data Centers transaction. Without these one-off items, growth in recurring revenue and core profitability was much more moderate — net revenue excluding one-time gains rose only 7.9%, and adjusted EBITDA on the same basis rose 8%.

Operationally, demand for the company’s data centers remained strong. Commitments for space and pre-commitments for under-construction facilities each climbed by double digits year over year, while the utilization rate approached 78%, a new high for the business. This points to solid, ongoing physical demand driven by cloud and AI service customers.

On the cost side, management kept general and administrative spending in check and reduced sales and marketing costs, but highlighted that higher utility expenses are squeezing adjusted margins. The company continues to invest heavily, maintaining a full-year capital expenditure target of around RMB 9 billion. Cash and liquidity rose thanks to asset sales and new capital, yet total debt remains high relative to equity.

What GDS Holdings Does

GDS Holdings develops, operates, and manages high-performance data centers, offering colocation and managed hosting services mainly to cloud companies, internet platforms, and large enterprises in China.
