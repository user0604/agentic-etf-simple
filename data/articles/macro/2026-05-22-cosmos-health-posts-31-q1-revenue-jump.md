Notable developments
Revenue hit a record for the quarter, rising 30.7% as distribution expanded and more than 75 new pharmacies were added at CosmoFarm, but gross profit fell 32.6% as gross margin compressed to 7.7% from 15.0% the year before.
Operating expenses rose to $3.57 million, up nearly 24%, reflecting higher personnel and technology costs for global expansion. Adjusted net income swung from a $0.28 million profit to a $1.0 million loss, driven by increased interest costs and non-cash expenses.
Stockholders' equity grew by 7.6% to $19.83 million and total liabilities fell 9.6% to $42.54 million, as the company repaid debt and strengthened its balance sheet, though cash and equivalents decreased to $2.2 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted that adjusted EBITDA is near breakeven and stated it expects this figure will turn positive as expansion activities deliver stronger revenue. No specific revenue or earnings guidance for coming quarters was given. Investors should pay close attention to the upcoming U.S. product launch and whether gross margins stabilize as new business scales.

Quarterly highlights

The company delivered record revenue for the period, supported by new pharmacy additions at CosmoFarm and expanding global order activity for Sky Premium Life, its nutraceutical product line. However, the mix shift toward low-margin wholesale activity contributed to a significant drop in gross profit, with margin decreasing from 15.0% to 7.7% even as total revenue grew.

Profitability was impacted by both rising costs and several non-cash charges, including interest on convertible debt, losses from digital asset holdings, and foreign currency effects. Those charges helped drive adjusted net income from a positive $0.28 million in the prior year to a $1.0 million loss this quarter, and adjusted EBITDA also moved from positive to slightly negative.

On the balance sheet, stockholders' equity increased while total liabilities declined by $4.5 million as the company reduced debt. The decrease in cash suggests ongoing investment and debt paydown, with $4.3 million in total liquid assets at quarter-end.

What Cosmos Health does

Cosmos Health is an international healthcare group focused on pharmaceutical distribution, generic medicines, and nutraceuticals, operating through brands like CosmoFarm, Sky Premium Life, and Cana Laboratories.
