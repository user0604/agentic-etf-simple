Notable Developments
The period marked an all-time high for net sales, climbing 36.7% year over year and continuing more than six years of uninterrupted quarterly growth.
Gross profit margin increased by 3.6 percentage points, while net income expanded by 32% to $4.7 million as Lifeway leveraged improved scale and marketing investments.
Accounts receivable increased sharply during the quarter, and cash outflows rose due to a nearly five-fold increase in capital spending on manufacturing capacity.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its long-term adjusted profit goal of $45 to $50 million by fiscal 2027 and expects record sales for fiscal 2026. However, it offered only general annual sales guidance and left near-term profit estimates unchanged. Investors should watch how Lifeway manages its growing receivables and whether its large capital expenditures drive future profit without straining cash flow.

Quarterly Highlights

Sales of core kefir products, which account for 85% of Lifeway’s net revenue, saw robust demand and drove overall performance. Product innovation also contributed, with the company highlighting new launches such as Muscle Mates (a high-protein probiotic beverage) and expanding its Farmer Cheese distribution to thousands of Walmart locations.

The company reported operating income of $6.3 million, nearly quadrupling the prior-year quarter, as better returns from selling, general and administrative spending took effect and scale efficiencies supported profitability. However, cash outflows increased due to $11.0 million in capital spending, much higher than the same period last year, as Lifeway ramps up manufacturing expansion.

One area of caution is the jump in accounts receivable, which reached $23.0 million by period-end. This figure, rising faster than sales, suggests that some payments from retailers may be taking longer or that Lifeway is extending more credit to customers -- a development that could affect future liquidity if not managed carefully.

What Lifeway Foods Does

Lifeway Foods produces and markets cultured dairy products, specializing in probiotic-rich kefir and related functional foods sold primarily in North America.
