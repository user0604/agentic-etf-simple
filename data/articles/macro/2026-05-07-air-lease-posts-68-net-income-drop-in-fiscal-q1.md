Notable Developments
Rental revenue from flight equipment rose about 4%, backed by ongoing fleet expansion and improvements in average lease rates.
Net income dropped to $114.8 million, down significantly from last year due to the absence of large insurance recoveries related to aircraft previously detained in Russia.
The merger with Sumitomo and SMBC AC was completed, with SMBC AC taking over Air Lease's entire aircraft orderbook, impacting future growth strategy.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management has not issued new financial guidance following the merger, but has highlighted portfolio management and cost discipline as ongoing priorities. The next key area for investors is how the new corporate structure affects aircraft sales and order pipeline strategy. Integration with SMBC AC as the new parent and servicer is likely to shape future performance.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter featured stable recurring revenue, with rental income from aircraft leases rising versus a year ago; however, total revenue growth was muted by lower sales of airplanes and no further one-time insurance settlements.

Operating expenses fell modestly even as fleet size grew, with cost control offsetting higher depreciation tied to a larger aircraft portfolio.

Following the merger in April 2026, Air Lease's future aircraft deliveries were shifted out, reducing its organic growth visibility, and the company has a significant aircraft sales pipeline valued at $5.6 billion still to be converted into cash.
