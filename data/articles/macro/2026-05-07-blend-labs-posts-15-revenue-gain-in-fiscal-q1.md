Free cash flow declined over 50% year over year, while Blend spent $18.6 million to repurchase 11.2 million shares.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects second-quarter revenue between $32 million and $34 million, and expects higher non-GAAP operating income of $5.5 to $6.5 million as operating leverage increases. Blend aims to convert a robust customer pipeline and continue rolling out its agentic artificial intelligence platform, Autopilot. Investors should watch for progress on sustained cash generation, ongoing adoption of new technology suites, and any updates on internal control improvements.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter’s revenue growth outpaced the high end of Blend’s own forecast, with both Software Platform and Professional Services contributing. Notably, revenue from the Mortgage Suite, Blend’s digital lending product family, rose 18% to represent more than half of total revenue.

Profitability metrics showed clear gains, with non-GAAP operating income climbing sixfold and gross profit margin improving by 7 percentage points. This margin expansion reflects the company’s success in scaling technology and integrating AI-driven platform innovations, most notably the launch and adoption of Autopilot, its artificial intelligence agent for origination.

On the customer front, Blend added or expanded 15 relationships, and reported a pipeline of potential deals up more than 40% from the prior year. However, high customer concentration persists, and free cash flow in the quarter was less than half the prior year’s level, primarily due to changes in operating performance and investments.
