Industrial services provider Team (NYSE:TISI) delivered operational improvements as losses narrowed and margins strengthened compared to the prior year. The company, which specializes in inspection, heat-treating, and mechanical repair services for energy and industrial clients, reported first quarter 2026 results on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter highlighted broad-based growth and improving profitability, though persistent net losses, high debt, and ongoing negative cash flow remain concerns.

Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 8.3%, marking the highest first-quarter result for Team since 2019, with growth seen across the U.S. Canada, and international markets.
Adjusted EBITDA, which reflects core operating profitability, jumped 45.2% over the prior year, helped by higher gross margin and careful cost management.
Free cash flow remained negative at $(11.5) million, but improved substantially from a $(30.1) million outflow in the prior year period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management issued full-year guidance, projecting revenue of $920 million to $945 million, gross margin growth of approximately 8% at the midpoint, and Adjusted EBITDA growth of approximately 16% at the midpoint. The company expects a stronger second half, pointing to resilience in its main U.S. business and further gains in Canada and abroad. Investors should watch to see if continued revenue growth will be matched by sustainable free cash flow and further reductions in losses.

Quarterly Highlights

The Inspection & Heat-Treating segment delivered 8.6% revenue growth as higher project volume in the U.S. Canada, and international regions boosted the topline. Mechanical Services revenue rose 7.8% over the prior year period, and the division reported a much narrower operating loss than a year ago.

Adjusted selling, general, and administrative expenses declined as a percentage of revenue, due in part to cost controls, with the metric defined to exclude severance and certain restructuring charges. Management cited a focus on driving further gross margin improvement and highlighted progress in integrating emission control services into the core business.

Despite improved operational results, debt rose moderately to $306.5 million and free cash flow remained in negative territory, mainly due to ongoing net losses and seasonal working capital needs. Liquidity was stable, with $8.7 million in cash and $40.5 million of undrawn credit available at quarter-end.

What Team Does

Team provides testing, inspection, mechanical repair, and specialty services to industrial clients in sectors like energy, manufacturing, and infrastructure.
