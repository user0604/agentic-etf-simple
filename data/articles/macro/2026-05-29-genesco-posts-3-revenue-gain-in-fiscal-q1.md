Genesco Posts 3% Revenue Gain in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 29, 2026, 7:21 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Genesco started fiscal 2027 with improving sales and profitability, though overall losses continued.
Revenue rose 3% year over year.
Management raised full-year earnings guidance, while cautioning about ongoing challenges in digital and certain store segments.

Genesco (NYSE:GCO) began the year with stronger sales and improving operations, though it still reported a quarterly loss. The footwear retailer, best known for its Journeys, Johnston & Murphy, and Schuh brands, focuses on direct-to-consumer sales with a mix of retail and e-commerce channels. The company released its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on May 29, 2026, covering the three months ended May 2.

Overall, the quarter showed positive sales momentum in core brands and a raised outlook, but digital sales and U.K. operations remain under pressure.

Notable Developments
Journeys and Johnston & Murphy, two main product brands, delivered comparable sales gains of 5% and 7%, helping drive overall revenue growth.
Gross margin improved by 0.3 percentage points, partly due to fewer promotions and better cost management, though the company remains unprofitable.
Store count fell by 4%, with 30 closures this quarter, as Genesco continues to optimize its retail presence amid changing consumer habits.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full-year adjusted diluted earnings per share guidance to $2.00–$2.40, crediting better-than-expected results in the period. The outlook calls for flat to slightly lower total sales, as store closures offset growth in core brands. Investors should watch for improvements in digital sales and signs of turnaround in the underperforming Schuh business.

Quarterly highlights

Journeys, which focuses on fashion-forward footwear for teens and young adults, grew to nearly 59% of total sales this quarter. The brand saw its seventh straight quarter of positive comparable sales, reflecting continued strong customer engagement and new product efforts.

Johnston & Murphy, known for its dress and casual footwear, logged both higher sales and improved profitability. The group’s operating income more than tripled over the same quarter last year, signaling effective cost and product strategies in that segment.

Schuh, which operates mainly in the U.K., experienced a 9% drop in comparable sales as the company reduced discounting to restore profit margins. This led to a wider operating loss, underlining ongoing challenges in that market while attempting to establish a firmer pricing strategy.

What Genesco does

Genesco sells branded footwear and accessories through its Journeys, Schuh, Johnston & Murphy, and Genesco Brands businesses in North America and the U.K., with a focus on both retail stores and e-commerce.
