Earnings Preview: Applied Materials

May 12, 2026, 9:04 AM

Applied Materials (NASDAQ:AMAT), a leading supplier of machines and software used to make semiconductor chips, is expected to report its fiscal second-quarter 2026 earnings after the market closes on Thursday, May 14, 2026.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

The company reported revenue of $7.01 billion for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, a 2% decrease year over year. Net income was $2.03 billion, resulting in earnings per diluted share of $2.54. Gross margin improved slightly to 49.0%. The Semiconductor Systems segment, which includes equipment for chip manufacturing, saw revenues decrease 8% year over year, while the Applied Global Services (AGS) segment grew 15% due to higher demand for services and spare parts. Notably, Q1 results were above the midpoint of company guidance and met or slightly exceeded analyst consensus for both revenue and earnings per share. The company also recorded a $253 million legal settlement related to export controls, which was already disclosed and resolved.

Positives
Strong performance in high-growth segments: The company maintained leadership and saw record revenues in DRAM equipment and advanced packaging, both of which are driven by demand for artificial intelligence (AI) data centers.
Improved gross margin: Gross margin increased to 49.0%, reflecting higher average selling prices and better manufacturing cost control.
Robust growth in services: The AGS segment delivered 15% year-over-year growth, supported by a large installed base and expanded long-term service agreements.
Healthy cash flow and shareholder returns: Operating cash flow was $1.7 billion, with over $700 million returned to shareholders through dividends and buybacks.
Negatives
Decline in Semiconductor Systems revenue: The main equipment segment saw an 8% drop in revenue year over year, mainly due to lower demand for older logic chip equipment.
Legal settlement charge: A $253 million charge related to export controls compliance reduced reported operating income for the quarter.
Mixed regional performance: Revenue in China and Korea declined 7% and 13% year over year, respectively, reflecting ongoing trade and regulatory challenges.
What to Watch in Q2 FY2026's Report
Q2 revenue and earnings versus guidance: Applied Materials guided Q2 revenue in the $7.2 billion to $8.2 billion range and non-GAAP EPS of $2.44 to $2.84. Analyst consensus is near the lower end of this range, so the actual results and forward guidance will be closely watched.
Momentum in AI-driven markets: Investors should monitor updates on growth in leading-edge logic, high-bandwidth memory (HBM) DRAM, and advanced packaging, as these are key drivers for both equipment and services demand.
Progress on new product launches and EPIC co-development: Management announced more than a dozen new products for 2026, including three for advanced logic and DRAM, and the first EPIC co-development agreement with Samsung. Updates on commercialization and customer adoption will be important.
Gross margin trends and cost structure: The company expects gross margin to improve slightly to about 49.3% in Q2. Watch for commentary on pricing, product mix, and supply chain costs, especially as demand shifts to higher-value products.
Regional demand shifts and export regulation impacts: Continued monitoring of sales in China and other regions is warranted, as export controls and trade policy changes remain a risk to growth and profitability.
