Notable Developments
Revenue in occupational health centers rose 9.9% to $519.9 million, with total patient visits up 6.7% to 3.42 million.
Onsite health clinics ran 411 locations at period end, up from 160, supported by recent acquisitions and organic growth, though revenue growth (125%) trailed the increase in site count.
Concentra paid a quarterly dividend of $0.0625 per share and repurchased $15 million in company shares during the quarter.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its fiscal 2026 outlook, forecasting revenue of $2.275–$2.375 billion and targeted adjusted EBITDA of $460–$480 million. It reaffirmed plans for additional locations and smaller acquisitions through the year. Investors should watch for ongoing cost normalization and G&A (general and administrative) expense trends as post-separation adjustments continue.

Quarterly Highlights

In the first quarter of 2026, the occupational health centers segment contributed $519.9 million of total revenue, or approximately 91%, driven by growth in both workers’ compensation and employer services. Total visits and the amount the company collects per visit both increased, reflecting a solid client renewal environment and rate improvements.

Onsite health clinics recorded a major jump in facility count after integrating the Pivot acquisition and expanding organically. Despite the large number of new clinics, revenue growth lagged the facility increase, with performance expected to ramp as newer sites mature.

The company continued to allocate capital to shareholder returns and balance sheet improvement. With net leverage at 3.4 times adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow up from a year ago, the company aims to reduce leverage below 3.0 by year end while funding growth and distributions.

What Concentra Group Parent Does
