Teekay (NYSE:TK) capitalized on robust tanker markets to deliver a strong first quarter, aided in part by gains from selling vessels. The marine transportation specialist, which operates a large fleet of crude oil tankers and manages ship-to-ship transfer services, provided its quarterly results on May 13, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter featured solid operational progress and healthy capital returns.

Notable Developments
Net income attributable to shareholders jumped to $47.7 million, including $7.9 million in one-time gains from asset and investment sales.
Revenue increased 23.6% year over year, lifted by strong tanker rates and high asset utilization across the fleet.
Teekay declared a special cash dividend of $1.00 per share, while Teekay Tankers paid out a regular dividend of $0.25 and a special dividend of $1.00 per share for the period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlighted ongoing capital returns and a focus on fleet modernization, but did not release specific financial or operational guidance for the remainder of 2026. Fleet renewal efforts remain central, with further vessel acquisitions and newbuilds planned for future delivery.

Quarterly Highlights

The period’s earnings growth was driven by significant improvements in core operations and material gains from the sale of tankers and equity investments, which together represented about $0.09 per share of net income. This indicates that both recurring operations and non-recurring items contributed to profit growth.

Fleet renewal accelerated, with the acquisition of three modern Aframax tankers for $141.5 million and planned investment of $190 million in two newbuild Suezmax vessels for delivery in 2027. The sale of older vessels for combined proceeds of $73 million highlighted the company’s approach to maintaining a younger, more efficient fleet.

Shareholder returns remained a feature, as Teekay issued a $1.00 per share special dividend and benefited from the rising market value of its holding in Teekay Tankers. The value of this stake nearly doubled from a year ago, though Teekay’s cash balance was down year over year as funds were returned to shareholders.

What Teekay Does

Teekay provides marine transportation services for global energy companies, operating a large fleet of crude oil tankers and supporting ship-to-ship transfer and lightering activities.
