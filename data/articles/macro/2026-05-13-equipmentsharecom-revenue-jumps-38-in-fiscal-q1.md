Key Takeaways
EquipmentShare.com raised its full-year outlook after strong rental segment growth and improved profitability.
Revenue rose 38% year over year.

Equipment rental specialist EquipmentShare.com (NASDAQ:EQPT) posted a business win in the first quarter, raising its outlook following strong demand and improved profitability. The company, a leading provider of technology-driven equipment rentals and connected jobsite solutions, released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 13, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected significant gains in core rental operations, an expanding network, and progress toward break-even profitability.

Notable Developments
Rental segment revenue climbed 37.4%, supported by strong demand across industrial and non-residential projects.
Profitability improved as adjusted core EBITDA rose 38.1%, and the net loss narrowed by $19 million to $29 million.
Management raised full-year revenue and EBITDA guidance by roughly $100 million at the midpoint, reflecting broad-based momentum.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised guidance for revenue, rental segment growth, and core EBITDA for the rest of fiscal 2026, echoing confidence in continued growth as the company adds locations and deploys technology. Outlook also anticipates robust cash needs tied to fleet and branch expansion. Investors should watch the company's ongoing path to profitability and the scalability of its capital-light OWN Program model.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter saw rapid rental segment growth, with revenue in that segment rising 37%, driven by expansion in equipment rental and services and investment in new branch locations. Equipment sales contributed $179 million, while emerging areas like telematics and platform revenue demonstrated strong early traction.

Profitability metrics moved upward, as adjusted core EBITDA reached $399 million, reflecting both improved scale and cost control. The net loss contracted to $29 million, and the trailing twelve-month net income swung to a profit, though the quarterly result remained negative due to continued investment.

On the operational side, the company added 22 new locations, growing its total footprint by 29% compared to the prior year. The proprietary T3 platform also saw wider integration, supporting customers with real-time equipment data and control for jobsite efficiency.

What EquipmentShare.com Does

EquipmentShare.com provides technology-enabled equipment rental services to construction and industrial customers, offering a connected platform that combines rental fleet management, telematics, and digital workflow solutions.
