Key Takeaways
Cypherpunk Technologies reported a steep quarterly loss driven by the sharp decline in Zcash prices. Net loss widened year over year due to a $77.6 million unrealized loss on digital assets. Management reiterated its commitment to the privacy-technology and oncology strategies but gave no new guidance for the coming quarters.

Cypherpunk Technologies (NASDAQ:CYPH) posted a significant loss as the value of its digital asset treasury dropped sharply in the latest quarter. The company, which focuses on privacy technology and cancer therapeutics, is increasing its holdings in Zcash (a privacy-focused digital currency) while advancing its oncology pipeline. The results for the first quarter, released May 14, 2026, showed that heavy unrealized losses on Zcash holdings dominated the period’s financials. Overall, the quarter revealed major volatility tied to digital asset prices and a slowdown in research spending.

Notable Developments
Cypherpunk increased its Zcash holdings to 314,185.70 ZEC, now representing 1.88% of total supply, but still below its 5% target.
The company made its first investment outside of Zcash, committing $5 million to Zcash Open Development Labs (ZODL) alongside key digital asset investors.
Research and development spending plummeted 98%, as the Leap Therapeutics subsidiary completed major clinical trials and received Fast Track status for its lead therapy.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its focus on building out both privacy technology investments and advancing the oncology portfolio, but did not provide new financial guidance. The most important thing for investors to watch is the impact of Zcash price movements on reported results, as this volatility strongly affects both the balance sheet and earnings. Cash management will also be critical if digital-asset prices remain low or investments continue at the current rate.

Quarterly Highlights

The value of Cypherpunk’s Zcash holdings fell sharply as the price per ZEC dropped from $508.09 to $240.47 during the quarter, resulting in a $77.6 million unrealized loss and eclipsing other operating expenses. At the same time, the company expanded its exposure, buying over 10,000 additional ZEC and investing in a privacy technology company, ZODL, as part of its broader strategy.

In its Leap Therapeutics subsidiary, the company achieved a regulatory milestone as the U.S. Food and Drug Administration granted Fast Track status to sirexatamab, its experimental therapy for a biomarker-defined subgroup of colorectal cancer. New clinical trial data showed higher response rates and longer survival for targeted patients, but overall R&D costs fell to $0.2 million as trials ended.

Cash outflows remained substantial, with the company ending the quarter with $6.7 million in cash—down 79.5% year over year. The sharp drop followed major investments in digital assets and new technology, along with financing inflows from share issuance.

What Cypherpunk Technologies Does

Cypherpunk Technologies is a hybrid privacy technology and biotechnology company, investing in privacy-focused digital assets and developing targeted cancer therapies via its Leap Therapeutics subsidiary.
