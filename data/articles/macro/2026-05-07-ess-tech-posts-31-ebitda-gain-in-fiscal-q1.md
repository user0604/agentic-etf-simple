Key Takeaways

ESS Tech (NYSE:GWH) reported another quarter of minimal revenue as it continued to focus on cost reductions and the transition to its new product platform. The company, a developer of iron flow battery systems for long-duration energy storage, is working to commercialize its Energy Base product and prepare for multi-year project deliveries. Its earnings release on May 7, 2026, covered results for the quarter ended March 31. Overall, the period reflected management’s emphasis on cost discipline and strategic reset while revenue remained low as anticipated.

Notable developments
Operating expenses dropped by one-third to $6.7 million, reflecting reduced sales, marketing, and administrative spending.
Total liquidity reached $21.5 million after a $15 million capital raise through a registered direct offering.
Adjusted EBITDA loss improved to $10.3 million, a 31% improvement from the prior-year quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management stated it will continue to focus on cost control and maintaining liquidity throughout 2026 as it prepares for full-scale project deliveries expected in 2027 and beyond. Leadership highlighted the importance of converting its commercial pipeline into recognized revenues. Investors should watch for progress on large contracted projects and any signs of delays in delivery.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue recognition is expected to increase mainly in 2027 and 2028 as new, large projects come online, according to management. The company made sharp cuts to operating expenses, especially in sales and marketing as well as general and administrative costs, while keeping research and development spending stable. Liquidity improved following a $15 million equity raise, supporting ongoing operating costs, but with $21.5 million in available funds and a net operating cash outflow of $13.5 million in the quarter, future capital raises may still be necessary if commercialization takes longer than planned.

What ESS Tech does

ESS Tech designs and manufactures iron flow batteries, which provide long-duration energy storage solutions for utilities and commercial customers.
