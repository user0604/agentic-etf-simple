Key Takeaways
United Bancorp posted modest earnings growth while continuing to invest in technology and branch expansion.
Earnings per share rose 3.1% year over year.
Management expects investments in infrastructure to support stronger earnings within the next two years.

United Bancorp (NASDAQ:UBCP) delivered steady financial results as it balanced higher income with rising expenses for digital transformation and regional growth. The regional community bank focuses on Ohio and West Virginia, making investments in technology and expanding its local presence. In its earnings release issued May 7, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, United Bancorp showed modest gains in profitability and core deposits alongside new risks in loan asset quality. Overall, the quarter reflected consistent business momentum, with investments positioning the bank for future growth but raising short-term costs.

Notable developments
Total deposits increased by $42.6 million, or 6.8%, led by growth in demand and savings accounts.
Noninterest income rose 11.2% due to stronger fee generation from deposit services and mortgage activities.
Nonaccrual loans surged to $6.48 million, mostly from a single commercial loan, raising credit risk for the quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains confident that recent technology and branch investments will support higher earnings and efficiency within the next 12 to 24 months. It expects continued improvement in net interest income and an ongoing focus on digital services. Investors should closely monitor asset quality, especially the concentration in nonaccrual commercial loans, as this could affect credit costs.

Quarterly highlights

Net interest income improved by 4.2% year over year, benefiting from an uptick in deposits and slightly better net interest margin. Net interest margin measures the difference between interest income earned on loans and securities and interest paid on deposits and borrowing, indicating the profitability of the bank’s lending and investing activities. Expense growth was notable at 10.2%, primarily reflecting spending on technology, expanded payroll, and new branch infrastructure.

Fee-based income climbed as both deposit-related charges and the Unified Mortgage Division contributed more to noninterest income, supporting revenue diversification. The mortgage division’s growth was supported by the addition of new mortgage lenders. Treasury management and digital banking features also played a role in raising fee income and deepening customer engagement.

Asset quality drew scrutiny after nonaccrual loans increased sharply, mostly because one large commercial loan turned nonaccrual. This pushed the nonaccrual loan ratio to 1.36%, which reduces the bank’s coverage ratio and highlights potential credit risk. The allowance for credit losses now covers 65.6% of nonaccrual balances, down from prior levels; management notes the affected relationship is not considered impaired but plans to closely monitor the situation.

What United Bancorp does

United Bancorp is a regional bank holding company providing community banking services, including lending, deposit products, and related financial services to customers in Ohio and West Virginia.
