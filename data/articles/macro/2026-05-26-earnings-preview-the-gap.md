Earnings Preview: The Gap

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

The Gap (NYSE:GAP), a leading U.S. apparel retailer with brands including Old Navy, Gap, Banana Republic, and Athleta, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on Thursday, May 28, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, The Gap delivered results that were generally in line with management’s expectations but slightly missed analyst consensus for earnings per share. The company reported net sales of $4.2 billion, up 2% year over year, and comparable sales growth of 3%, marking its eighth consecutive quarter of positive comparable sales. Operating margin came in at 5.4%, down from the prior year, and gross margin declined to 38.1%. Earnings per share for the quarter were $0.45, just below the analyst consensus of $0.46. The company highlighted continued strength at Old Navy, Gap, and Banana Republic, while Athleta continued to struggle. Despite tariff-related pressures, The Gap maintained a strong balance sheet with $3 billion in cash and approved both an increased dividend and a new $1 billion share repurchase authorization.

Positives
Consistent sales growth at core brands: Old Navy, Gap, and Banana Republic all posted positive comparable sales, showing the company’s core business remains resilient even in a challenging retail environment.
Strong cash position and shareholder returns: The company ended the year with $3 billion in cash, its highest in nearly 20 years, enabling a 6% dividend increase and a new $1 billion share buyback plan.
Improved inventory discipline: Inventory units were down year over year, despite higher inventory value due to tariffs, suggesting better inventory management and less risk of future markdowns.
Progress on strategic initiatives: The company launched an expanded beauty assortment at Old Navy and prepared for a Gap fragrance relaunch, both announced and underway, with plans to expand accessories for the holiday season.
Negatives
Athleta’s continued underperformance: Comparable sales at Athleta fell 10% in the quarter, and management expects further declines in the first half of 2026 as the brand undergoes a turnaround.
Tariff-related margin pressure: Gross margin declined 80 basis points year over year, mainly due to a 200 basis point headwind from tariffs, which also weighed on operating margin and earnings.
Slight EPS miss versus consensus: Reported earnings per share of $0.45 came in just below analyst expectations, which contributed to a negative market reaction following the results.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Tariff impact and margin recovery: Investors should focus on how well the company manages the expected 200 basis point tariff headwind in the first quarter and whether sourcing strategies begin to offset these pressures as planned later in the year.
Comparable sales trends at Old Navy and Gap: Watch for continued positive comp sales at these core brands, as management expects trends to remain largely consistent with recent quarters.
Progress on Athleta turnaround: Monitor any early signs of stabilization or further decline at Athleta, as management expects negative mid-to-high single-digit sales declines in the first half of the year.
Expansion of beauty and accessories: Look for updates on the performance of new beauty offerings at Old Navy and the upcoming Gap fragrance relaunch, as well as any details on the holiday accessories expansion, all of which are announced and in progress.
Effects of the new Encore loyalty program: The company recently launched its revamped loyalty platform, Encore. Investors should watch for any early indications of increased customer engagement or traffic resulting from this initiative.
