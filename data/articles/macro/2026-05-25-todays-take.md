THE QUESTION

What’s an investing theme you’re watching but not yet ready to buy?

1. Window Shopping Quantum Computing

By Meilin Quinn
TEAM HIDDEN GEMS

Don’t get me wrong, quantum computing has come a long way. IonQ (NYSE: IONQ) has shown quantum can speed up certain specialized calculations, while Alphabet’s (NASDAQ: GOOGL) Willow chip has reduced error rates that have long held quantum computers back.

But the sector generates about $1.4 billion in annual revenue, while just four major pure-play quantum stocks — IonQ, Rigetti Computing (NASDAQ: RGTI), D-Wave Quantum (NYSE: QBTS), and Quantum Computing (NASDAQ: QUBT) — add up to about $40 billion in market cap. Before investing, I want to see this tech become a scalable business with recurring revenue and workable unit economics.

I may miss the first 10x, but I’d rather catch a second 10x after the business model is proven than chase the first leg on potential, especially when many experts say commercially viable quantum is still years and years away.

2. Quantum Leaps: Why I’m Watching the Quantum Revolution

By Rachel Warren
TEAM HIDDEN GEMS

I’m incredibly excited about quantum computing’s potential to rewrite the rules of science and finance. The ability to process complex data at speeds traditional computers can’t touch is a genuine game-changer for everything from drug discovery to encryption. This technology could very well become the backbone of the next industrial revolution.

While I’m closely tracking the pure-play leaders, and already own shares in companies like Alphabet (NASDAQ: GOOGL) (NASDAQ: GOOG) with its Google Quantum AI division, I’m watching to see when and how the technology shifts into a scalable, standalone business model. I want to be there when the breakthrough happens, but for now, I’m prioritizing deep study of the space over putting additional capital to work.

3. Making the Quantum Leap

By Jason Moser
TEAM RULE BREAKERS

It’s still early days for quantum computing, but I’m a believer that it’s going to be an attractive opportunity for investors over the coming decade (it doesn’t hurt that I get to work on our Quantum Leap service, either). The science is fascinating and hard to fully grasp, but quantum is ultimately about developing more powerful computing than what today’s classical computing methods offer.

Today AI is all the rage and rightly so; it’s powerful technology. But many companies are already investing in quantum technology today, so while I’ve not yet bought into pure plays like IonQ (NYSE: IONQ) and Rigetti Computing (NASDAQ: RGTI), I do actually already have exposure to the opportunity with holdings like Alphabet (NASDAQ: GOOG), Cloudflare (NYSE: NET), and more.

4. Hot Take: It’s Too Soon for Space

By Tim Beyers
TEAM RULE BREAKERS

For me, it’s too soon to be investing in the space trend.

Don’t get me wrong; there are great companies such as Rocket Lab (NASDAQ: RKLB) in the sector. But the sector has gone well beyond the stratosphere in terms of expectations.

At greater than 100 times revenue for both Rocket Lab and soon-to-be public SpaceX, anything short of years more out-of-this-world growth won’t be enough to sustain their orbital valuations. I’d rather wait for unique suppliers to emerge at reasonable prices.

SpaceX proved a decade ago that there’s a market for disruptive providers of space tech. The next one is out there, and we may not need to pay over 100x sales for it.
