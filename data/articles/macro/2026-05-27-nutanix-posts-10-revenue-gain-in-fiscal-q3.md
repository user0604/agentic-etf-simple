Key Takeaways
Nutanix delivered a business win by surpassing its own revenue and margin guidance for the quarter.
Diluted net income per share attributable to Class A common stockholders rose from $0.22 in Q3 FY2025 to $0.25 in Q3 FY2026, reflecting higher revenue and improved operating margin.
Full-year revenue and free cash flow guidance were both raised.

Nutanix outperformed its own predictions as it grew both sales and earnings in its latest quarter. The provider of software platforms for managing hybrid multicloud environments continues to invest in artificial intelligence and expand partnerships to increase its reach. On May 27, 2026, it released results for the third quarter of fiscal 2026, which showed clear strengths and some metrics that require ongoing attention. Overall, the quarter highlighted solid growth in subscription revenue, expanding recurring revenue, and raised the company’s full-year targets, though free cash flow was slightly lower than a year ago.

Notable Developments
Annual recurring revenue climbed 15.0%, supported by customer expansions and longer subscription contracts.
Profitability improved, with operating margin up by 0.8 percentage points and non-GAAP earnings per share rising to $0.47.
Free cash flow slipped 3.0% year over year, though management expects improvement by year-end due to revenue recognition timing.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year fiscal 2026 guidance for both revenue and free cash flow, reflecting confidence in bookings and recurring business. For the fourth quarter, it expects further revenue growth, steady margins, and increasing visibility into future sales with remaining performance obligations rising 27%. Investors should watch for changes in gross margin and further updates on supply chain conditions as these may affect near-term cash conversion.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter featured strong growth in annual recurring revenue, which measures predictable revenue sources from subscription contracts, as well as healthy average contract durations of 3.4 years.

Nutanix launched its Agentic AI platform for companies building enterprise artificial intelligence solutions, expanding hardware support to both AMD and NVIDIA-based graphics processors. Major new partnerships were announced, including deeper integration with NetApp and programs for managed service providers, to increase adoption and extend reach beyond its traditional customer base.

Margins remained high but showed gentle pressure as non-GAAP gross margin dipped by 0.4 percentage points, possibly linked to pricing or product mix. Free cash flow was $197.2 million, down from a year ago, but management indicated timing issues due to hardware supply will likely reverse next quarter. The company also expanded its share repurchase agreement by $750 million during the period.

What Nutanix Does

Nutanix provides a platform that enables organizations to manage applications and data seamlessly across private data centers and multiple public cloud environments.
