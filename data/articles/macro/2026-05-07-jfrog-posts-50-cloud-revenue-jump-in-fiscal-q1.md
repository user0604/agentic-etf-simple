JFrog Posts 50% Cloud Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
JFrog posted a business win as cloud and enterprise customer momentum drove solid increases on all major metrics.
Non-GAAP earnings per share rose 35% year over year as cloud revenue expanded 50%.
Management raised its full-year outlook, but signaled more cautious sequential revenue growth ahead.

JFrog (NASDAQ:FROG) delivered a solid win this quarter as its cloud platform and large enterprise customer segments both showed strong progress. The leading software supply chain management and security firm is focused on unified DevOps automation and helps enterprises manage, secure, and deliver software and AI models at scale. The company issued its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (released May 7, 2026), marking continued acceleration in key metrics. Overall, the quarter showed robust growth, with operational improvements and increased cloud adoption driving outperformance versus management’s own revenue guidance.

Notable Developments
Cloud revenue climbed 50% year over year, now making up 51% of the company's total revenue.
Customers generating over $1 million in annualized recurring revenue rose 48% to 80, accelerating expansion among large enterprises.
Free cash flow grew by nearly a third to $37.3 million. The company also announced a new $300 million share repurchase program.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year revenue outlook to $628–$632 million and boosted profit guidance, citing “powerful momentum in our security business” and ongoing AI adoption. However, for the next quarter, it expects revenue to remain flat sequentially, reflecting a more cautious view on immediate growth. Investors should pay close attention to whether cloud revenue keeps expanding as more customers shift their workloads to JFrog’s cloud platform.

Quarterly Highlights

The company’s cloud platform delivered the largest gains, now accounting for more than half of total revenue as customers continue to consolidate software development and security tools. This cloud shift supports scalable automation and underpins increased revenue visibility.

Large enterprise customers drove growth, with the number of clients surpassing $1 million in recurring revenue increasing to 80, a 48% rise. This metric demonstrates JFrog’s success in moving upmarket and expanding its strategic footprint with larger organizations.

Free cash flow rose 32.7% to $37.3 million, reinforcing the company’s strong financial position. Management used this strength to announce a $300 million share repurchase, indicating confidence in JFrog’s competitive positioning and long-term outlook.

What JFrog Does

JFrog provides a unified software platform that enables organizations to manage, secure, and distribute software and AI models across cloud and on-premises environments.
