Key Takeaways
Okta delivered a solid quarter, with subscription revenue up and strong cash flow.

Okta (NASDAQ:OKTA) gained momentum with healthy identity software subscription growth and strong recurring cash flow. The leading independent identity platform provider for enterprises and organizations released its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on May 28, 2026. Overall, the quarter reflected progress in core areas but highlighted challenges around the pace of future growth.

Notable developments
Total revenue increased 11% year over year, driven by 98% of revenue coming from recurring subscriptions.
Free cash flow reached $271 million for the period, rising 14% from the prior-year quarter.
Professional services revenue remained flat at $15 million, reflecting the company's plan to shift this work to partners.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management forecasts second-quarter revenue growth to slow to about 9%, with non-GAAP operating margin stable near 26%. It expects the shift of professional services to partners will reduce full-year revenue growth by roughly one percentage point. Investors should watch for how quickly Okta can turn its new AI and non-human identity products into material revenue drivers over the next few quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The period saw strong demand for subscription-based identity management, which now makes up nearly all Okta’s total revenue. Remaining performance obligations, a measure of contracted revenue not yet recognized, jumped 16%, driven by larger enterprise wins and multi-year deals.

Although free cash flow rose, profit margins slipped slightly as Okta increased investments in sales and marketing to target growth opportunities. Non-GAAP operating margin dropped two percentage points to 25%, reflecting expansion efforts and growth spending.

Okta continued its push into AI and non-human identity security, launching new offerings for AI agents and expanding its ecosystem. These products are still early-stage and not yet strongly contributing to overall revenue, but management highlights them as key future growth opportunities.

What Okta does

Okta delivers secure identity management solutions that help organizations verify user and device access to technology and data.
