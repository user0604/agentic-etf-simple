Key Takeaways

Eos Energy Enterprises (NASDAQ:EOSE) achieved rapid sales growth and a record backlog, showing strong commercial traction, but it remained deeply unprofitable in the latest quarter. The energy storage firm, known for its zinc-based battery systems, reported its first quarter results on May 13, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, operational gains in production and pipeline were offset by persistent cash burn and significant losses.

Notable Developments
Revenue for the quarter climbed 445% to $57.0 million, more than the average quarterly figure in the prior year.
The order backlog swelled 56% from the prior year to $644.6 million, while the commercial pipeline reached $24.3 billion.
Cash and equivalents stood at $410.7 million, but net cash used in operations rose sharply to $119.7 million for the period.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for full-year 2026 revenue in the $300 million to $400 million range and aims to approach gross margin positivity in the second half of the year. The most important factor for investors to watch is whether the company can scale manufacturing and reduce costs enough to reach positive gross profit as planned.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue growth remained exceptionally strong, with the quarter’s $57.0 million result exceeding the company’s average from the prior year and driven by improved automation and higher battery module deliveries. The commercial order backlog and sales pipeline also expanded meaningfully, signaling continued demand for Eos’s long-duration battery systems used by utility, industrial, and grid-scale customers.

Losses remained significant, as gross margin and adjusted EBITDA both improved from a year ago but were still negative at the end of the period. Net income for the quarter was positive due to substantial non-cash fair value adjustments related to the company’s stock price, not from operational profit.

On the operational front, Eos completed factory acceptance testing of its second battery line at the Thorn Hill facility, marked record energy discharged across its installed base, and launched several product innovations, such as the DawnOS software and the Indensity architecture to meet customer demand for flexible, long-duration storage.

What Eos Energy Enterprises Does

Eos Energy Enterprises designs and manufactures zinc-based battery energy storage systems that provide long-duration electricity storage for utility, commercial, and industrial power grids.
