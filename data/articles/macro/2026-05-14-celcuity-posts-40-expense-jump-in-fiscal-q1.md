Celcuity Posts 40% Expense Jump in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 14, 2026, 8:08 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Celcuity delivered a crucial clinical milestone in breast cancer drug development.
Non-GAAP EPS loss widened to ($0.86) from ($0.81) year over year.
Management expects FDA submission in the third quarter with cash runway through 2027.

Celcuity (NASDAQ:CELC) achieved a major clinical win this quarter by confirming positive Phase 3 results for its lead cancer drug candidate. The clinical-stage biotechnology company focuses on developing targeted therapies for cancer, with its main program centered on the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway. The results for the first quarter (reported May 14, 2026) cover the pivotal clinical and financial developments leading up to potential U.S. approval of its drug, gedatolisib. Overall, the period showed progress in late-stage clinical trials but also featured higher expenses and cash usage tied to its push toward commercialization.

Notable Developments
The pivotal Phase 3 VIKTORIA-1 trial for gedatolisib met its primary endpoint, confirming significant efficacy for patients with advanced breast cancer.
Operating expenses jumped 39.9%, mainly from increased spending on both research activities and launching commercial infrastructure.
Cash used in operations increased to $55.1 million as the company continued to invest in clinical progress and commercial readiness.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management plans to submit additional regulatory filings for gedatolisib in the third quarter and projects a commercial launch following an FDA decision targeted for July 17, 2026. The company reports that its available cash is expected to last through 2027. Investors should closely watch the upcoming detailed clinical results at the ASCO conference in June and regulatory milestones in the months ahead.

Quarterly Highlights

Celcuity’s headline announcement was that its Phase 3 VIKTORIA-1 trial in breast cancer patients achieved statistical significance in the PIK3CA mutant group, supporting the broad potential of gedatolisib for both wild-type and mutant populations. The company also expanded its development program by initiating a new first-line trial enrolling patients with endocrine-sensitive disease, aiming to maximize addressable patient groups.

Expenses surged, with Selling, General, and Administrative (SG&A) costs rising 171.9% as the organization added commercial staff and prepared for launch activities. Research and Development (R&D) costs also grew, reflecting ongoing study work and manufacturing for large clinical programs, with a pivot in spending from trial execution to building out support capabilities.

With cash used in operating activities up more than 50% year over year and cash reserves at $387.1 million as of March 31, the company remains confident in its funding but highlighted the importance of expense management until product revenues begin. No revenue was reported this period as Celcuity remains a pre-commercial business.

What Celcuity Does

Celcuity develops targeted therapies for cancer, with a focus on PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway inhibition in advanced breast cancer.
