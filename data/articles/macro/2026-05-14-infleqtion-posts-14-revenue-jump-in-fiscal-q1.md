Notable Developments
All revenue this quarter came from quantum technology products and services, and quantum sensing continued as the main commercial driver.
The company ended the period with $569 million in cash, boosted by its recent public listing and fundraise.
Operating expenses increased due to higher selling and R&D costs, including $11.5 million in one-time public company expenses.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its 2026 revenue guidance to at least $40 million, a modest but meaningful revision from its earlier estimate. It points to growing customer demand and contract momentum as key support for this outlook.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw the delivery of record quantum revenue entirely from organic growth, with sensing products such as the Quantum Spectrum and the Tiqker optical atomic clock leading commercial adoption for government and industrial customers.

The cash position increased dramatically as a result of Infleqtion’s public listing and capital raise, giving it ample funding to support further R&D and commercial expansion. The company cited a $569 million balance in cash and marketable securities at quarter-end.

However, year-over-year operating loss more than doubled, as the company ramped investments in research, go-to-market activities, and incurred one-time costs from going public. R&D and administrative costs rose at a much faster rate than revenue, impacting overall operating leverage.

What Infleqtion Does

Infleqtion builds and commercializes neutral atom quantum computers and quantum sensors for applications in national security, advanced research, and industry.
