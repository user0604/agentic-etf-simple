Seer (NASDAQ:SEER) reported disappointing results as revenue dropped sharply, aligned with ongoing funding pressures in academic research and a more competitive market. The company is a provider of advanced proteomics solutions that enable researchers to decode proteins at scale for use in scientific discovery. Reporting on May 13, 2026, for its first quarter of fiscal 2026, management reaffirmed its full-year outlook but continued to stress disciplined spending.

Overall, the quarter highlighted the company’s persistent commercial and funding challenges despite ongoing scientific validation and new strategic partnerships.

Notable Developments
Revenue across all segments declined, with product sales down 27% and service revenue falling 53% from the prior-year period.
Operating expenses decreased by 20%, reflecting company efforts to restrain spending as net loss improved year over year.
Seer announced a major collaboration with Precision Health Research, Singapore for a 10,000-sample proteomics study, further validating its technology platform.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its 2026 annual revenue guidance unchanged at $16–18 million, which points to a significant second-half ramp given the slow start in the first quarter. Leadership flagged ongoing cost controls as a company priority, while a robust cash position provides some strategic flexibility. The most important variable to monitor is whether recent partnerships and scientific validation begin to drive sustainable revenue growth and reduced cash burn in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The sharp revenue decline this period reflects both a tough funding climate for academic research customers and increasing competitive pressures, particularly in instrument and service sales. Gross margin also compressed to 35%, indicating that less favorable revenue mix and possibly pricing pressure affected the bottom line.

On the commercial side, the company highlighted its new collaboration with Precision Health Research, Singapore, aimed at delivering deep proteomic analysis on 10,000 samples as part of a population-scale cohort study. While this and a rising count of third-party publications reinforce demand for the Proteograph Product Suite (a system for protein analysis), the financial impact has yet to materialize in recognized revenue.

Operating expenses fell to $18.2 million for the first quarter of 2026, led by lower stock-based compensation and employee costs. The company ended the quarter with approximately $220 million in cash, cash equivalents, and investments. The company also continued its share repurchase program, buying back about 1.5 million shares in the quarter.

What Seer Does

Seer develops and sells advanced proteomics solutions, including instruments, consumables, and software, to help researchers deeply analyze proteins for scientific discovery.
