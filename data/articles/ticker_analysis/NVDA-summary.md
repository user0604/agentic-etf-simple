NVDA Company Overview
NVIDIA designs high-performance computing hardware and software that power artificial intelligence, video games, and autonomous vehicles. The company generates revenue by selling specialized chips called graphics processing units and licensing a proprietary software platform known as CUDA to developers. This integrated hardware-software ecosystem creates significant switching costs for enterprise customers and researchers who rely on NVIDIA tools to build and run complex digital models across global data centers.
