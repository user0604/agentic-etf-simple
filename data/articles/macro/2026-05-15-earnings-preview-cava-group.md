Earnings Preview: CAVA Group

The Motley Fool Team

May 15, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

CAVA Group (NYSE:CAVA), a fast-casual restaurant operator specializing in Mediterranean-inspired bowls and pitas, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

CAVA Group delivered a quarter that generally met analyst expectations, with revenue for the fourth quarter of 2025 rising 21.2% year over year to $272.8 million. The company opened 24 net new restaurants in the quarter, ending the year with 439 locations. Same-restaurant sales increased 0.5% for the quarter, and restaurant-level profit margin was 21.4%. Net income for the quarter was $4.9 million, and adjusted EBITDA reached $25.8 million. These results reflected continued expansion and solid new restaurant performance, although same-restaurant sales growth moderated compared to prior periods.

Positives
Strong new restaurant performance: New locations continued to open above $3 million in average unit volume, demonstrating the brand's appeal in both existing and new markets.
Healthy revenue growth: Total revenue surpassed $1 billion for the first time in fiscal 2025, reflecting both expansion and steady demand for Mediterranean cuisine.
Improved digital mix: Digital revenue accounted for 37.9% of sales, up from 36.4% in the prior year, indicating successful investments in technology and digital channels.
Solid cash position: The company ended the year with $393 million in cash and investments and no debt, providing flexibility for future growth and investments.
Negatives
Slower same-restaurant sales growth: Same-restaurant sales increased just 0.5% in Q4, a notable deceleration from earlier quarters, reflecting tougher industry conditions and lapping strong prior year results.
Margin pressure from costs: Restaurant-level profit margin declined to 21.4% in Q4, with higher food, packaging, and operating costs including tariffs and limited-time menu items.
Higher pre-opening expenses: Pre-opening costs rose due to more units under construction and higher costs per unit, impacting overall profitability in the quarter.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Initial impact of Pomegranate-glazed Salmon launch: The company announced its first seafood menu item, with a full rollout planned by the end of Q1. Investors should watch for early sales contribution and any effect on food costs or margins, as management expects a margin headwind of about 100 basis points starting in Q2.
Same-restaurant sales momentum: Management indicated that Q1 comp trends were tracking above full-year guidance. Watch for reported same-restaurant sales to see if this momentum is sustained, especially after a soft Q4.
Progress on Assistant General Manager (AGM) rollout: The company reported 60% of AGM roles filled at year-end, with completion targeted for mid-2026. This initiative aims to support operational consistency and future restaurant openings.
Effectiveness of loyalty program enhancements: The tiered loyalty program and new invite-only Oasis tier were introduced in late 2025. Early signs show increased guest engagement and frequency. Look for updates on loyalty-driven sales and digital engagement.
Restaurant expansion pace and new market entries: CAVA plans 74 to 76 net new restaurants in 2026, including entries into several Midwest markets. Investors should monitor the pace of openings and any commentary on performance in new geographies.
