Earnings Preview: Canopy Growth

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 9:03 AM

Comments

Canopy Growth (NASDAQ:CGC), a company that produces and sells cannabis products for both medical and recreational use, is expected to report earnings for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 on Friday, May 29, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Canopy Growth reported net revenue of $74.5 million Canadian dollars, which was essentially flat compared to the same period last year. The company’s adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed to $2.9 million, marking its smallest quarterly loss on this measure to date. The net loss from continuing operations was $62.6 million, a significant improvement from the $121.9 million loss in Q3 of the prior year. Cash and cash equivalents at quarter-end were $371 million, and the company completed a USD 150 million recapitalization after the quarter closed, extending debt maturities to 2031 and strengthening liquidity. Canadian medical cannabis revenue grew 15% year over year, while Canadian adult-use cannabis revenue increased 8%. International cannabis revenue declined 31% year over year due to supply chain issues, but management reported sequential growth and stabilization in international markets. Storz & Bickel, the company’s vaporizer device business, saw revenue of $22.9 million, down 9% from last year, but up 45% sequentially, driven by seasonal demand and a new product launch.

Positives
Improved adjusted EBITDA loss signals better cost control: The company posted its narrowest adjusted EBITDA loss to date, reflecting disciplined spending and operational improvements.
Growth in Canadian medical and adult-use cannabis: Both segments showed year-over-year revenue increases, indicating that Canopy is gaining traction in its core domestic markets.
Strengthened balance sheet and liquidity: Cash and cash equivalents rose to $371 million, and a post-quarter recapitalization further extended debt maturities, reducing near-term financial risk.
Reduction in free cash outflow: Free cash outflow improved to $19 million from $28 million in the prior year’s quarter, pointing to ongoing progress in managing cash burn.
Negatives
Missed consensus earnings expectations: The Q3 FY2026 loss per share was -$0.10, which missed analyst consensus of -$0.03.
International cannabis revenue declined sharply: Revenue from international markets dropped 31% year over year, mainly due to supply chain challenges in Europe.
Gross margin compression: The overall gross margin percentage decreased to 29% from 32% a year earlier, with the cannabis segment’s margin falling to 25% from 28%.
Storz & Bickel revenue fell year over year: Despite sequential growth, the vaporizer segment’s revenue was down 9% compared to the prior year, reflecting ongoing consumer uncertainty and tariff pressures.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Impact of proposed MTL Cannabis acquisition: The acquisition, announced and pending shareholder and regulatory approval, is expected to close soon. Investors should watch for updates on the timeline and expected contributions to revenue, margins, and scale in both medical and recreational markets.
Canadian medical cannabis revenue trends and veteran reimbursement changes: The Canadian government’s proposed reduction in the reimbursement cap for veterans could affect sales and margins. Management has said they are taking cost-saving actions to offset this, but investors should look for early signs of impact on medical sales and profitability.
International segment recovery and supply chain improvements: After a challenging Q3, management expects sequential improvements in international cannabis sales and margins. Progress on EU GMP certification at the Smiths Falls facility and expanded strain availability in Europe will be key indicators.
Storz & Bickel device sales performance: Q3 is typically the strongest quarter for this segment. Investors should watch for how sales and margins hold up in Q4, especially with continued macroeconomic and tariff headwinds.
Cash flow and adjusted EBITDA trajectory: Management continues to target positive adjusted EBITDA in fiscal 2027. Q4 results will provide a final look at progress toward this goal, as well as any updates on cost discipline and free cash flow improvement.
