Thor Industries Posts 4% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q3

6:44 AM

Key Takeaways
North American Towable RV sales and profit margins fell sharply, but Motorized and European segments showed resilience.
Revenue decreased 3.9% from the prior year.
Management lowered full-year earnings guidance and now expects further margin declines.

Thor Industries (NYSE:THO) reported a business setback as sales and profits in its core North American Towable segment dropped significantly. The leading global builder of motorhomes and travel trailers posted mixed segment results for its fiscal third quarter, with revenue and profit margins down overall. The company released its results for the quarter ended April 30, 2026, on June 3, 2026, and adjusted its earnings outlook downward. Overall, the quarter revealed stability in some areas but deepening pressure in its largest division.

Notable Developments
North American Towable RV sales, historically the company's largest product line, dropped 24.6% while gross profit margin there fell to 10.2%.
Motorized RV segment sales in North America rose 7.7%, but related gross margin slipped to 8.8% due to higher warranty and material costs.
European RV sales climbed 11.8%, though profit margins fell nearly two percentage points amid increased costs and special model mixes.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management lowered full-year earnings guidance, now expecting diluted EPS of $3.30 to $3.80, down from $3.75 to $4.25 previously, and pointed to declining gross margin rates. The company confirmed ongoing strategic changes in North American RV operations, critical as the Towable segment faces prolonged pressure. Investors should monitor the pace and impact of these realignment efforts as well as any stabilization in demand for Towables through the next quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The North American Towable division saw the steepest volume and margin declines, with sales falling more than any other segment as dealers sharply reduced orders. Profitability dropped further in Towables as management cited both lower shipment volumes and increased materials costs, particularly due to tariffs, as key issues.

The Motorized RV division in North America defied overall weakness, with sales and unit shipments increasing. Consumer demand favored more affordable and value-oriented motorhome models, though profit margins in this segment also slipped due to higher overhead and warranty costs.

Growth in European RV sales helped to counter some declines elsewhere, as European segment net sales rose 11.8% in the third quarter. Although increased output of special-edition motorhomes and cost inflation compressed margins, the segment maintained a rising order backlog and market share gains.

What Thor Industries Does

Thor Industries makes recreational vehicles, including travel trailers, motorhomes, and campers, sold in North America and Europe through a network of independent dealers.
