What Site Centers Does

Site Centers is a real estate investment trust focused on owning, operating, and selling open-air shopping centers, now in the process of winding down its portfolio following the spin-off of its operating platform.

Quarterly highlights

Asset sales continued, with two properties and a major joint venture stake sold during the quarter for combined proceeds of $95.3 million. The asset monetization strategy moved forward as the company completed sales of FlatAcres MarketCenter and 3030 North Broadway, as well as exited its Deer Park joint venture position. Portfolio size reduced dramatically, with 16 operating centers at quarter-end compared to 33 a year prior.

Operating funds from operations, which represent recurring earnings available to shareholders, fell to negative $1.9 million as revenue dropped by over 75% from the prior year. The rapid shrinkage of owned properties drove the consolidated revenue figure lower and reduced net operating income for the quarter. Operating FFO dropped into negative territory, underlining the lack of ongoing income from the downsized operations.

Unrestricted cash reserves rose to $193.5 million while net operating income and occupancy continued to decline, reflecting the shrinking property base. Leasing activity was minimal with only one new lease signed and eight renewals totaling less than 18,000 square feet. The leased rate slipped to 85.9%, a decline of nearly 4 percentage points, as tenant renewals and new leasing trailed off alongside asset sales.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its plan to maximize value by selling remaining assets and resolving its partnership in the Dividend Trust Portfolio joint venture. It expects to maintain high cash balances until the joint venture is resolved. Going forward, the outcome of those asset and joint venture sales is the most important thing for investors to monitor.
