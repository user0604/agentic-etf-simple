Astrana Health Posts 56% Revenue Jump in Fiscal Q1

Key Takeaways
Astrana Health delivered a business win as results met or exceeded management's Q1 2026 guidance.
Adjusted earnings per share rose sharply year over year.
Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook, guiding for continued growth and cash flow gains.

Astrana Health (NASDAQ:ASTH) posted another business win as disciplined contract execution and growth pushed results to the upper end of company projections. The integrated healthcare company operates a value-based care platform, enabling medical groups and clinics to take financial responsibility for patient outcomes through capitation and full-risk arrangements. The company reported its latest earnings on May 7, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the period showed rising scale and strong cash generation, even as investments in its delivery network weighed on segment margins.

Notable developments
Capitation revenue, meaning fixed monthly payments per patient, made up about 92% of the company's total revenue, reaching $892.9 million this quarter.
The Care Enablement segment more than doubled its revenue to $87.7 million and posted a 470% jump in operating income, showing improved scalability.
Although the Care Delivery segment saw revenue more than double year over year, it still posted an operating loss of $3.0 million for the period.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its guidance for fiscal 2026, targeting revenue of $3.8 to $4.1 billion and adjusted EBITDA between $250 and $280 million. The team is continuing to migrate more members to full-risk contracts, supporting steady, predictable growth. Investors should keep an eye on Care Delivery segment profitability and any updates regarding medical cost trends or regulatory risks.

Quarterly highlights

The company continued its shift to value-based care, reporting that about 80% of its Care Partners' capitation revenue and 40% of total membership now operate under full-risk contracts. These arrangements put Astrana on the hook for patient outcomes and costs, but also provide more dependable, and potentially higher-margin, revenue streams.

Strong gains in the Care Enablement segment, which covers technology and administrative services, helped drive companywide profit growth. Revenue in this area more than doubled, with operating income rising to $20.2 million and showing further benefits from platform integration and efficiency improvements.

The direct Care Delivery segment, which manages clinics and hospitals, grew sales rapidly, but higher costs kept it unprofitable. Management emphasized that investments here, including hospital operations and new geographies, remain part of its long-term strategic positioning.

What Astrana Health does

Astrana Health provides an integrated healthcare platform focusing on value-based care by managing networks of medical groups, clinics, and physicians, supporting millions of patients through fixed contract revenue arrangements.
