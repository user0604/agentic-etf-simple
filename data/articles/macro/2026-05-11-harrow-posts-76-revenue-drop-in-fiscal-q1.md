Harrow Posts 7.6% Revenue Drop in Fiscal Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 4:26 PM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Harrow posted a challenging quarter as near-term profit softness accompanied strong underlying prescription growth.
Revenue fell 7.6%, while gross margin declined compared to the prior year.
Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 revenue guidance and signals a revenue ramp in the next quarter.

Harrow (NASDAQ:HROW) missed the mark on reported revenue as a one-time accounting adjustment obscured ongoing gains in prescription volume and market share. The leading ophthalmic pharmaceutical company, known for treatments like VEVYE, IHEEZO, and TRIESENCE, experienced margin pressure as it invested in future growth. Harrow released its first quarter results for 2026 on May 11, 2026, covering the period ending March 31. Overall, the quarter reflected operational transition, with continued product momentum but short-term profitability challenges.

Notable Developments
An $8 million non-recurring accounting adjustment for VEVYE's expanded coverage reduced reported revenue, masking underlying prescription growth.
Operating losses widened as selling, general, and administrative expenses rose 6.7% and research and development nearly doubled.
Cash increased to $94.6 million after new financing, while loans payable rose to $292 million.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue target of $350 million to $365 million, signaling confidence in a strong ramp ahead and highlighting that Q2 revenue is expected between $71 million and $81 million. It emphasizes that the bulk of earnings are projected for the second half of the year, with improvements from new launches and operating leverage. Investors should closely monitor whether Harrow achieves its anticipated revenue acceleration and margin improvement in the upcoming quarter, given the steep sequential increases required to meet guidance.

Quarterly highlights

Harrow saw core product demand strength, with VEVYE new prescriptions up 25% quarter over quarter and TRIESENCE volumes more than doubling year over year. IHEEZO achieved an 18% increase in unit demand compared to the prior year, particularly in retina markets, while company-wide revenues declined after the one-time VEVYE accounting adjustment. Research and development spending approached $6 million as the pipeline advanced, including work on TRIESENCE label expansion and the MELT-300 development program.

The company faces profitability pressure during this scale-up phase. Gross margin dropped from 68% to 61% due to the VEVYE adjustment and higher temporary costs, and operating losses were driven by a 12.8% rise in total expenses as new sales teams and product support ramped up. Despite negative operating cash flow, a $48.9 million financing inflow boosted the ending cash balance compared to last year.

Looking forward, management expects accelerated financial performance as accounting adjustments recede and expanded payer coverage for VEVYE and other growth drivers translate into higher revenue and improved operating leverage. The upcoming commercial launch of biosimilars BYOOVIZ and BYQLOVI, and continued pipeline development, will also be key pillars in reaching full-year targets.

What Harrow does

Harrow develops, licenses, and sells prescription pharmaceutical products and compounded medications for eye care providers, focusing on ophthalmology markets in the United States.

Track Harrow’s next quarter to see if prescription gains and expanded coverage drive the forecasted revenue ramp and margin recovery.
