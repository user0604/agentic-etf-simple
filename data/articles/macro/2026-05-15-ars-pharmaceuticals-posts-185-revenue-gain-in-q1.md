Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management highlights that further access wins, notably a potential CVS Caremark pharmacy benefit manager agreement targeted for July, could boost future revenue and adoption. Investors should closely monitor expense control, the contribution of new access channels, and the company’s path toward cash-flow break-even as indicated in management commentary.

Quarterly Highlights

The quarter showcased strong year-over-year revenue growth, supported by ongoing adoption of neffy, the company’s needle-free intranasal epinephrine spray for allergic emergencies. School and prescriber enrollment in neffy programs rose, with over 10,000 schools and more than 28,000 healthcare providers now participating.

While product revenue expansion was impressive, expenses climbed more rapidly, especially in selling, general, and administrative costs linked to sales force expansion and marketing initiatives. This resulted in net losses nearly doubling from the same quarter a year ago.

Access milestones advanced as well, with regulatory approvals allowing broader neffy distribution in both the U.S. and international markets, and major contracts with payers such as CVS Caremark and additional Medicaid plans in progress. Collaboration and supply revenue, while growing, accounted for a notable portion of the total—underlining the importance of developing a recurring product revenue base going forward.
