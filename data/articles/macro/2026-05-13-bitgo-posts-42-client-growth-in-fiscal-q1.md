Notable Developments
Client count rose 42.0% year over year to 5,569, with strong institutional onboarding and new partnerships like SoFi and The Better Money Company.
Revenue jumped 112.6% from a year ago, but fell sharply versus Q4 as client activity moved from spot trading to net-revenue derivatives, impacting how revenue is reported.
Profitability metrics, including adjusted EBITDA, turned negative due to IPO-related expenses and ongoing investment in new product lines, alongside volatility in digital asset values.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects margins and take rates to hold steady at improved levels going forward, with stock-based compensation set to normalize after the IPO spike in Q1. The quarter’s results confirm ongoing momentum in underlying client and asset metrics. For the next period, investors should monitor the impact of product mix changes and volatility in digital asset values on both reported revenue and profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

Underlying business performance stayed strong, with normalized assets on platform up 29.4% and normalized assets staked rising 20.8% from a year earlier. These figures exclude asset price effects, better showing actual platform adoption even while market-pricing reduced headline asset values.

Bitgo launched its derivatives trading offering, which saw $3 billion in notional trading volume as some client activity shifted from spot to derivatives trades. Since derivatives revenue is recognized differently, this caused reported revenue to decline sharply when comparing quarter over quarter, even as product margins improved to 32 basis points, from 20 basis points a year ago.

The Stablecoin-as-a-Service segment grew rapidly, with revenue up 43.6% from the previous quarter and a take rate improvement to 7.4%. This reflects client adoption, recent product enhancements, and partnerships that help diversify Bitgo’s revenue outside pure crypto price exposure.

What Bitgo Does

Bitgo provides digital asset custody, trading, and infrastructure solutions for institutional clients, focusing on secure asset storage and transaction services across a wide range of cryptocurrencies.
