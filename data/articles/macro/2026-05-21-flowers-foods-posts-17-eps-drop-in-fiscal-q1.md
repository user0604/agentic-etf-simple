Notable Developments
Branded retail sales rose 3.4%, supported by new contributions from the Simple Mills acquisition and favorable pricing, while volume declined 4.2%.
Net income dropped 20.6% to $42.1 million as higher interest expense and lower volumes affected profitability.
The quarterly dividend was reset to $0.125 per share, down from prior levels, as part of a plan to focus on debt reduction and strategic brand investment.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 outlook, projecting net sales between $5.16 billion and $5.27 billion, adjusted EBITDA of $465–$495 million, and adjusted diluted EPS of $0.80–$0.90. Disciplined investment and resource allocation remain a priority as economic pressures continue. Investors should watch branded portfolio progress and the effects of ongoing brand renewal efforts, particularly the relaunch of Nature’s Own and the integration of Simple Mills.

Quarterly Highlights

Branded retail net sales formed the growth engine for the quarter, climbing 3.4% to $1.045 billion. This was driven by favorable pricing and mix, plus Simple Mills, which provided a 2.3% boost to total sales as it became an integrated part of the branded offering.

Profitability continued to face pressure from increasing costs, especially a 40% rise in net interest expense linked to acquisition debt, as well as legal and restructuring charges. The net earnings margin fell to 2.7% compared to 3.4% a year ago, while materials and production costs also edged higher as a percent of sales.

Despite the reduction in net income and cash flow from operating activities, the company reset its quarterly dividend to $0.125, reflecting a strategy to balance shareholder returns with a greater emphasis on debt reduction and brand investment in future quarters.

What Flowers Foods Does

Flowers Foods is a leading producer and marketer of fresh packaged breads, buns, rolls, snack cakes, and tortillas for retail and foodservice customers in the United States.
