Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year revenue growth guidance, citing strong visibility and new wins with top technology customers. However, it emphasized the need to further diversify the customer base and sustain operational performance as larger contracts raise execution demands. Investors should monitor the scale and success of new programs alongside progress in reducing reliance on a few large customers.

Quarterly Highlights

The company saw rapid expansion of its AI services, particularly in model training, safety, and evaluation solutions across the AI development lifecycle. Notably, a newly launched Evaluation and Observability Platform quickly secured its first $1 million engagement with a hyperscale client.

Revenue concentration remains a prominent theme, as a new Big Tech customer engagement is projected to contribute $51 million in annual sales, elevating it to the second-largest client within twelve months. Although revenue from “other Big Tech customers” grew 453% year over year, Innodata still draws a significant share from a small group of technology leaders.

Operating cash flow in the quarter was strong, enabling the company to end March with $117.4 million in cash and investments. At the same time, selling and administrative expenses rose to $22.9 million, consistent with Innodata’s ongoing investment strategy to support talent, research, and scaling of services.

What Innodata Does

Innodata provides AI data engineering, training, model evaluation, and safety services to technology and enterprise customers worldwide.
