Allot (NASDAQ:ALLT) built on recent gains as strong recurring cybersecurity sales fueled a sharp jump in profit and operating cash flow this quarter. The network intelligence and cybersecurity company serves telecom operators and enterprises across multiple geographies. Its first-quarter results, released May 12, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showed not just higher sales, but strong progress in making its revenue base more predictable through subscriptions. Overall, the period reflected advances on its recurring-revenue strategy, but continued reliance on a few major customers and Europe for much of its business.

Notable Developments
Recurring revenues made up over 67% of total, up from 62% last year, as subscription-based business accelerated its shift away from one-time product sales.
Security as a Service (SECaaS) revenue surged 71% year over year to $8.7 million, accounting for a third of total company revenue this period.
Cash and cash equivalents, bank deposits, restricted deposits and investments increased to $98 million, with operating cash flow hitting a record $10.6 million this quarter.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue guidance, now expressing more confidence toward the high end of the $113 million to $117 million range. It also raised its SECaaS (Security as a Service) revenue growth goal to at least 40% for the year. Investors should watch how well the company continues expanding high-margin cybersecurity subscriptions and whether it reduces dependency on a small group of major telecom customers.

Quarterly highlights

Recurring revenues grew to 67% of the total, showing further success in the company's move to a subscription model for security services and network intelligence offerings. This makes future revenues more predictable and smooths out swings from one-time product deals.

SECaaS products, which help telecom operators and businesses address cyber threats through cloud-based subscriptions, grew their share of total company sales from 22% a year ago to 33% this quarter. This segment's rapid growth stands out as the engine powering both overall sales gains and margin improvement.

On the financial side, operating cash flow surged from the previous year, supported by improved profit margins and disciplined spending. The period ended with $98 million in cash, giving the company flexibility for investments, new product launches, or possible shareholder returns down the line.

What Allot does

Allot offers telecom operators and enterprises advanced network intelligence and cybersecurity solutions, focusing on cloud-based services that defend against threats and improve network performance.
