Notable developments
The company’s managed real estate portfolio grew to 100 assets, up 32% from a year earlier, reflecting diversification and scale.
Revenue increased by 38%, supported by the expansion of fee-based property management and the addition of major assets in transit-oriented markets.
Operating margin narrowed as selling, general, and administrative costs rose to $1.2 million, double the prior year’s level.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its strategy of expanding in the Washington, D.C. region and diversifying into new business lines, such as regional mall management and enhanced food and beverage services. However, it did not provide numerical guidance for revenue, earnings, or cash flow for fiscal 2026. The key metric to watch is whether rapid portfolio growth continues to outpace rising operating expenses, which pressured margins this quarter.

Quarterly highlights

The managed asset portfolio saw rapid growth, driven by the acquisition of The Reed, a large multifamily property, and ongoing leasing momentum in high-profile locations like Reston Station. Occupancy rates for commercial and residential portfolios remained strong, above 90%, helping to support predictable fee income.

The ParkX subsidiary, which handles parking and property services, delivered 106% higher revenue compared to a year ago and signed 13 new contracts, including five with third-party clients. This expansion, plus entry into food and beverage management and regional malls, broadened the company’s recurring fee streams.

Operating costs increased at a faster rate than revenue: cost of revenue rose 43% and administrative expenses more than doubled. As a result, both operating and net income margins declined, highlighting the challenge of managing expenses during a period of rapid growth.
