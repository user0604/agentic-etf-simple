Apogee Therapeutics (NASDAQ:APGE) delivered a strong quarter marked by advances in its pipeline for atopic dermatitis, highlighted by compelling new clinical trial data. The clinical-stage biotechnology company specializes in developing antibody therapies for inflammatory and immunological conditions. In its earnings release dated May 11, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, Apogee emphasized expanded efficacy and durability of its lead therapy zumilokibart (APG777) while reinforcing its robust balance sheet following a strategic capital raise. The quarter revealed rising expenses and a wider net loss as investment in late-stage trials accelerated.

Notable Developments
Zumilokibart, Apogee's lead antibody therapy, produced compelling 52-week data in atopic dermatitis with response rates that improved from earlier study points.
Operating expenses climbed 31.2%, as research and development grew to $60.8 million and general administrative costs rose to $22.0 million.
Cash and investments at quarter end totaled $1.3 billion, lifted by a $403 million equity raise, supporting trial advancement into 2029.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management stated that the strengthened cash position will support ongoing and planned late-stage trials, projecting enough funding for operations through at least 2029. The most important event to watch is the APEX Phase 2 Part B trial readout for zumilokibart in the coming quarter, as this will determine the timing for the planned Phase 3 launch.

Quarterly Highlights

Zumilokibart demonstrated that at 52 weeks, 85% of patients who received every 6-month dosing and achieved EASI-75 at Week 16 maintained their response. This result bolsters the case for expanding the therapy into other conditions like asthma and eosinophilic esophagitis.

Investment in research and development reached $60.8 million, increasing 31.0% from the prior-year quarter, mainly due to pipeline progression and preparatory work for later-stage trials. General and administrative costs climbed to $22.0 million, attributed to higher personnel costs and stock-based compensation as Apogee scales for future growth.

The quarter closed with $1.3 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities, following a significant equity raise. Management projects this provides the financial flexibility needed for multiple pivotal trial launches before seeking future funding.

What Apogee Therapeutics Does

Apogee Therapeutics develops antibody therapies for inflammatory and immunological diseases, focusing on conditions like atopic dermatitis, asthma, and related disorders.
