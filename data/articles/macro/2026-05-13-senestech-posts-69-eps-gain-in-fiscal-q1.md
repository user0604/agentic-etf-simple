Key Takeaways
SenesTech delivered strong momentum in direct-to-consumer and subscription channels but overall revenue growth was minimal due to a planned Amazon channel transition.

SenesTech (NASDAQ:SNES) saw a business win in direct-to-consumer sales and subscriptions, while topline growth remained limited by the deliberate overhaul of its Amazon sales channel. The pest management technology specialist focuses on non-lethal rodent fertility control solutions in the U.S. and internationally. Reporting results on May 12, 2026, for its first quarter of fiscal 2026, it showed early progress in its recurring revenue transition, but profitability lagged due to both operational investments and non-recurring costs.

Overall, the period reflected strong momentum beneath the surface, but headline figures were muted, and outlook uncertainty remains.

Notable Developments
Direct-to-consumer revenue increased 42%, while subscription revenue also climbed 44%, both setting new quarterly highs despite a temporary drop in third-party e-commerce revenue due to the Amazon channel transition.
Gross margin reached a record 68.6% as the business mix shifted toward higher-margin sales channels.
Adjusted EBITDA loss increased to $1.6 million, as operational investments and one-time legal and severance costs weighed on profitability.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management described the quarter’s disruption from the Amazon channel transition as "expected" and pointed to a sharp rebound in April post-transition metrics. However, it gave no numeric outlook for revenue or earnings for fiscal 2026. Investors should watch for sustained e-commerce and subscription growth beyond April, as well as cost controls that could support improved profitability and cash flow in future quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Direct-to-consumer revenue set a new record, rising strongly year over year, as e-commerce and subscriptions increased — even though overall revenue growth was limited by an $0.14 million decline in third-party e-commerce revenue tied to the switch to in-house Amazon management. In April, e-commerce sales surged 163% compared to April of the previous year, and subscriptions tripled, suggesting improvement as the channel transition completed, but it will take more than one month of growth to show a lasting impact.

The company achieved its highest gross margin in history at 68.6%, reflecting a favorable channel mix and improved pricing discipline in its online and direct sales. This margin expansion came even while costs grew, with selling, general, and administrative expenses rising to $2.04 million as the company absorbed legal and management transition expenses.

On the business-to-business side, revenue grew 57% year over year, as SenesTech expanded its reach among distributors, municipalities, and professional pest control channels. Notable municipal deployments occurred in cities such as Chicago, Boston, Washington, D.C., and New York City, with the New York City rat contraception trial concluding in May. International sales included initial shipments to New Zealand during the period.

What SenesTech Does

SenesTech develops and sells non-lethal fertility control solutions for managing rodent populations, marketed as environmentally friendly alternatives to traditional rodenticides.
