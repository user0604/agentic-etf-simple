Key Takeaways
Business win/loss: X Financial reported a sharp contraction in loan origination and profitability as it tightened credit and focused on risk management.
Performance: EPS fell 81.0% year over year.
Outlook: Management projects further declines in loan volume for next quarter, with ongoing regulatory and credit risks.

The latest quarter saw X Financial (NYSE:XYF) prioritize defensive measures at the expense of growth, as loan originations and earnings dropped markedly. The consumer finance marketplace operator tightened credit standards and shifted its focus to asset quality in response to rising credit risk. The company reported these first quarter 2026 results on May 27, 2026, for the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter underscored persistent operational challenges, with volume and earnings falling and near-term outlook remaining cautious.

Notable Developments
Loan origination volume fell 58.4% year over year to RMB 14.63 billion as stricter credit policies were enforced.
Net income dropped 91.7% from the prior year period, reflecting much higher credit-related provisions and reduced scale.
Management set guidance for Q2 2026 loan origination at between RMB 11.5 billion and RMB 12.5 billion, signaling continued pullback.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects a further reduction in loan originations next quarter and continues to prioritize asset quality over expansion. It cited ongoing regulatory uncertainty and elevated credit risk as major concerns that could influence future profitability. Investors should closely watch credit trends and any changes in regulatory policy that might affect the business model or capital requirements.

Quarterly Highlights

Loan origination and borrower activity declined sharply during the period, with the number of active borrowers decreasing to just under one million—down over 60% from the year-ago period. Larger average loan sizes reflect the shift to higher-quality customers but did not offset the impact of shrinking volume.

Profitability fell substantially. Operating margin was significantly below prior year, and net income dropped to under RMB 40 million. Credit-related provisions remained high as long-term delinquency rates (loans overdue by 91–180 days) continued to rise, reaching 9.95% at quarter end.

The company declared a semi-annual dividend of USD 0.28 per ADS, with payment to shareholders on record as of April 30, 2026. Share buybacks continued under the USD 100 million authorization, with 1.8 million ADS repurchased since January 2026.

What X Financial Does

X Financial operates an online marketplace that connects borrowers with institutional and individual lenders for consumer and small-business loans in China.
