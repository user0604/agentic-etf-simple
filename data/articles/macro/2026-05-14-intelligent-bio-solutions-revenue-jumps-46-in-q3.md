Intelligent Bio Solutions (NASDAQ:INBS) delivered another quarter of top-line growth as recurring cartridge sales continued to climb. Notable developments
Revenue climbed 46% from the previous year, fueled by cartridge sales making up 66% of the period’s total.
The company posted a gross profit margin of 50.5%, a record high, up from 46.8% a year earlier.
Cartridge sales surged 59% while reader hardware sales declined 16%, reflecting normal product mix changes.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its focus on securing U.S. Food and Drug Administration clearance for its core product, with testing and validation studies progressing. Leadership cautioned that the exact timing for regulatory approval is still unclear. Investors should pay closest attention to updates on the U.S. launch timeline and resolution of intellectual property licensing matters.

Quarterly highlights

Cartridge sales, which are recurring and consumable-based, continue to drive the company’s revenue. This growth supports its razor-razorblade model, where installed readers generate ongoing demand for cartridges.

Operating expenses increased, mainly due to higher spending on research and development and regulatory efforts. At the same time, marketing investment was trimmed by 42% compared to last year’s quarter, reflecting a more targeted approach but ongoing discipline as the company scales up.

The cash position improved substantially following a capital raise, leaving the company with $6.86 million on hand at the end of the quarter. However, it remained in a net loss position and did not provide fresh guidance for revenue or U.S. contribution, underscoring the need for further progress on the regulatory and IP front before meaningful U.S. revenue can begin.
