Key Takeaways
Dell Technologies delivered a record business performance, led by surging AI server demand.
Revenue increased 88%, with diluted EPS rising over 200%.
Management sharply raised its full-year guidance as it expects continued strong AI demand.

Dell Technologies (NYSE:DELL) broke company records with rapidly rising demand for its AI-optimized infrastructure products. The leading technology solutions provider saw its Infrastructure Solutions Group drive a major expansion, making AI hardware a central part of its results. Reporting on May 28, 2026, for its first quarter of fiscal 2027, it produced a quarter that marked a clear new phase for the business.

Overall, the period featured exceptional top- and bottom-line growth and a much higher outlook, though the business mix now leans more heavily toward large-scale AI infrastructure.

Notable Developments
AI-optimized server revenue surged more than sevenfold, reaching $16.1 billion.
Operating income jumped by 214%, while cash flow from operations set a Q1 company record at $4.1 billion.
Management increased its full-year revenue outlook to $167 billion at the midpoint, anticipating further gains from AI demand.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its full-year fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to $165–169 billion and expects AI-related sales to remain strong, increasing the AI server target for the year to $60 billion. Scaling supply chain capacity to meet demand, as well as monitoring margin dynamics as product mix changes, remain major themes for the rest of the year. Investors should closely watch if AI hardware demand can sustain current growth levels as the mix becomes even more concentrated in AI offerings.

Quarterly Highlights

The Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG), which includes servers and storage systems, became the main earnings driver as its revenue soared 181% and made up two-thirds of company-wide revenue. AI-optimized server products accounted for $16.1 billion, up 757% from the year-earlier period, supported by $24.4 billion in recorded AI orders.

The Client Solutions Group (CSG), which features Dell’s commercial and consumer PC product lines, returned to double-digit revenue and margin expansion. Revenue climbed 17% and operating profit increased 79%, reflecting broad-based customer demand and operating leverage.

Although overall results displayed powerful growth, the sharp shift to AI-driven hardware pressured gross margin percent lower from 21.1% to 17.8%, even as operating income margins improved thanks to scale and cost control. The dividend was maintained, with a quarterly payout to shareholders during the quarter.
