Key Takeaways
Perion Network reported mixed results, with strong growth in AI and search but falling profitability.
Non-GAAP earnings per share held steady year over year at $0.11.

Digital advertising technology provider Perion Network (NASDAQ:PERI) saw robust adoption of its AI-powered products and solid revenue, but its profit margins fell sharply. The company is known for its advanced advertising platforms serving brands and publishers worldwide. In its first quarter 2026 results released May 20, 2026, Perion reported solid sales growth in new channels but flat earnings and mounting pressure on profitability. Overall, the quarter saw topline resilience, but ongoing challenges in translating growth into profits.

Notable Developments
Search advertising revenue grew 21% to $23.7 million, offsetting a 4% decline in advertising solutions revenue.
Spending on Outmax AI Agent, Connected TV, and Digital Out-of-Home channels each climbed at least 29% year over year, with Outmax up 316%.
Adjusted EBITDA margin to contribution ex-TAC fell to 1%, down from 5% a year prior.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its full-year 2026 targets unchanged, projecting contribution ex-TAC of $215–$235 million and adjusted EBITDA of $50–$54 million. This means the company expects operating profits to improve meaningfully in upcoming quarters. Investors should watch how revenue growth in AI solutions and new channels converts into profit margin recovery.

Quarterly Highlights

Revenue in the period climbed 1%, with strong gains in search advertising but a contraction in advertising solutions. The advertising solutions segment, which makes up nearly three-quarters of Perion's business, was impacted by declines in its web channel that new offerings like Connected TV and Digital Out-of-Home could not fully offset.

Spending via the Outmax AI Agent soared 316% year over year, confirming heavy demand for Perion's artificial intelligence-powered platform. Other high-growth channels included Connected TV, up 68%, and Digital Out-of-Home, up 29%, showing traction in fast-growing digital ad sectors.

Despite topline gains, profits lagged: adjusted EBITDA dropped to $0.5 million and margin to contribution ex-TAC narrowed to 1%. Rising selling and marketing expenses and flat core profitability suggest Perion will need to deliver improved efficiency and cost control to meet its annual profit targets.
