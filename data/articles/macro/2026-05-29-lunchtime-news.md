1. Costco's Gas Pumps Strike Gold

COST is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team HG)

Costco Wholesale (NASDAQ: COST) reported "record-breaking" gas sales volumes during the last five weeks of its fiscal third quarter as the Middle East conflict pushed pump prices higher. Revenue rose nearly 12% to $70.53 billion, topping the $69.62 billion analysts expected, though shares slipped premarket after briefly pushing above $1,000.

Cheap gas, full carts: CEO Ron Vachris said high consumer price sensitivity drove many members to use Costco's gas stations for the first time, and those gas customers tend to spend more inside the store, too. Same-store sales grew 9.8%, with 11.2% growth in international markets and 9.4% in the U.S.
Inflation watch ahead: Excluding gas, same-store sales growth came in at 6.6%, just below the 6.7% Street estimate. Costco said higher resin costs will push some non-food prices up, and the company is "monitoring the longer-term inflationary impacts of higher oil prices as well as the future impacts of tariffs."
2. AST SpaceMobile Hit by Rocket Explosion

AMZN is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team RB)

AST SpaceMobile (NASDAQ: ASTS) shares fell 13% Thursday after Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket exploded during testing on a Florida launchpad. The rocket was being prepared for its fourth planned launch to deploy satellites for Amazon's (NASDAQ: AMZN) Leo network, a Starlink competitor.

Timeline at risk: The explosion is expected to push AST's commercial constellation launch from Q4 2026 to Q1 2027, though exact timing remains unclear. AST relies on third-party launch vehicle providers to deploy its satellites, leaving the company vulnerable to setbacks like this one.
Bigger ripple effects: The incident also delays Amazon's Leo constellation deployment, potentially slowing competition in the direct-to-device market. New Glenn is central to Blue Origin's push to challenge SpaceX in commercial launches, so further delays could ripple across the satellite industry.
3. ServiceNow Strikes AI Deal With Wipro

NOW is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team HG)

ServiceNow (NYSE: NOW) expanded its partnership with Indian IT services firm Wipro (NYSE: WIT) to deploy agentic artificial intelligence (AI) workflows across enterprise functions like IT, HR, procurement, and cybersecurity. Wipro's U.S.-listed American Depositary Receipt (ADR) surged 18.5%, its biggest single-day gain since October 2008. ServiceNow is also up 15% today on software optimism.

The deal: Wipro will integrate its AI-powered platform suite with the ServiceNow AI Platform, helping enterprises streamline how work gets initiated, orchestrated, and executed across systems. Both companies are positioning the tie-up as a way to roll out agentic AI at scale with policy-aligned actions.
AI pressure on IT services: The partnership comes as Indian IT firms race to shore up AI offerings amid investor fears that generative AI could disrupt traditional outsourcing. OpenAI's push into enterprise services has reignited concerns about long-term demand for conventional IT projects, putting pressure on the sector to evolve.
