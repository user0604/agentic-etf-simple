Descartes Adds AI Tools to Boost Supply Chain Software

Descartes Systems Group makes software that helps companies manage shipping and supply chains using the cloud. The company stands out by focusing on steady, subscription-based services that bring repeat business. Over the past five years, its stock rose 11.5%, but the S&P 500 gained 71.6%, so it underperformed by 60.1 percentage points.
