Key Takeaways
Nu Holdings reported strong growth, marked by record customer additions and robust engagement. Net income rose 41% compared to the prior year period. The company expects further investment in technology and international growth while closely managing asset quality.

Latin American digital bank Nu Holdings (NYSE:NU) delivered another quarter of brisk growth as it reported record customer figures and rising engagement across its financial services. The company continued to expand rapidly in Brazil and Mexico while deepening investment in artificial intelligence-powered capabilities. The results, announced May 14, 2026 and covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026, showed that the company is scaling profitably but flagged rising credit risks and greater provisioning as it pursues expansion. Overall, the period highlighted balanced progress but increasing caution around asset quality as lending grows.

Notable Developments
The customer base grew to 135 million, up 14% from the prior year, with Mexico surpassing 15 million customers and reaching break-even.
The efficiency ratio, a measure of cost relative to income, improved to 17.6%, reflecting gains from increased engagement and technology scale.
Credit loss allowances climbed to $1.79 billion, as rising near-term delinquencies resulted in higher provisions for potential loan losses.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its focus on scaling technology and AI across markets, emphasizing plans for measured international expansion. It signaled that operating costs and investments in innovation will rise, likely keeping the full-year efficiency ratio flat compared to 2025. Investors should watch future credit quality, as higher-risk lending and credit loss provisioning could impact profits if delinquencies accelerate.

Quarterly Highlights

Nu Holdings added about 4 million net new customers in the quarter, boosting its total to over 135 million. Mexico stood out by both increasing customer count and becoming the country’s third-largest financial institution, achieving break-even for the first time.

The company’s technology investments, particularly in artificial intelligence, supported faster product development and increased user engagement. Over 15 million customers regularly used AI-powered tools, while engineering productivity rose by 50% year over year.

While profits and revenue grew sharply, asset quality showed some weakness as the non-performing loan ratio for loans overdue between 15 and 90 days increased to 5.0%. Provisioning for credit losses also rose as the company expanded into higher-risk lending segments.

What Nu Holdings Does

Nu Holdings is a digital banking group offering financial products such as credit cards, personal loans, payments, and insurance to customers across Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, and select international markets.
