Earnings Preview: Vishay Intertechnology

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 11:04 AM

Comments

Vishay Intertechnology (NYSE:VSH), a leading maker of semiconductors and passive components for automotive, industrial, and power electronics, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of 2025, Vishay reported revenue of $801 million, which was slightly above the midpoint of its guidance and reflected a 1.3% increase from the previous quarter. The company saw broad-based order growth, especially in industrial power and artificial intelligence (AI)-related applications, with Asia leading regional gains. Gross margin reached 19.6%, modestly above guidance, while operating margin was 1.8%. Earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter was $0.01, which met analyst expectations. The book-to-bill ratio improved to 1.2, signaling a healthy backlog and positive momentum into 2026.

Positives
Revenue exceeded guidance midpoint and met analyst expectations: This outcome shows Vishay's ability to execute and deliver growth in a mixed industry environment.
Strong order growth and backlog: Orders reached a three-year high in most product categories, with backlog rising to $1.3 billion, supporting future revenue visibility.
Improved book-to-bill ratio: The ratio increased to 1.2, indicating more orders than shipments and suggesting continued demand strength.
Progress on strategic initiatives: The company advanced its Vishay 3.0 plan, including expanded capacity, new product introductions, and deeper customer engagement, positioning it for future growth.
Negatives
Profitability remains pressured: Gross margin, while slightly improved, is still below historical levels due to higher input costs and price pressure, particularly in semiconductors.
Operating margin remains low: At 1.8%, this reflects ongoing cost challenges and the ramp-up costs of new facilities, such as the Newport wafer fab.
Negative free cash flow: Continued high capital expenditures for capacity expansion resulted in negative free cash flow for the year, limiting flexibility for share repurchases or debt reduction.
Automotive and aerospace/defense segments saw flat or declining revenue: These segments were affected by seasonal slowdowns and delayed project billings, highlighting ongoing end-market volatility.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Revenue and margin performance versus guidance: Management guided for Q1 revenue between $800 million to $830 million and gross margin around 19.9%. Watch for actual results relative to these targets, especially given recent industry softness.
Impact of capacity expansion and Newport fab ramp: The Newport facility is expected to become gross profit neutral by the end of Q1. Progress toward this milestone will affect segment profitability and future margin improvement.
Order trends and book-to-bill ratio: Sustained order growth and a book-to-bill at or above one would support management’s outlook for sequential revenue increases through 2026.
Progress on Vishay 3.0 and product innovation: Updates on the qualification of new products, especially silicon carbide (SiC) MOSFETs for automotive and AI, and customer audits at new facilities will indicate how well Vishay is positioned for growth in key markets.
Capital expenditures and free cash flow outlook: With 2026 set to be the peak year for capital spending ($400 million to $440 million planned), investors should monitor the pace of spending, any delays, and the timeline for a return to positive free cash flow.
