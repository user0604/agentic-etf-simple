Notable Developments
The company filled about 600 CARDAMYST prescriptions for 560 patients within ten weeks of launch, involving 400 unique prescribers.
Commercial expenses rose to $15.8 million, up 52% from the prior year, reflecting intense launch activity.
Milestone’s cash position increased to $184.2 million, strengthened by new financing and royalty agreements tied to the CARDAMYST launch.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management expects its strong cash position to support operations and late-stage clinical development into the second half of 2027. The company’s main focus remains on expanding CARDAMYST’s market presence and payer coverage. Investors should monitor prescription growth, coverage beyond commercial insurance, and progress on etripamil’s Phase 3 study for new indications.

Quarterly Highlights

Initial rollout of CARDAMYST (nasal spray for PSVT, or paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardia) showed progress with early prescription fills, rapid engagement by prescribers, and over 25% of commercially insured U.S. lives now covered. However, the current quarter’s revenue contribution remains small compared with commercial launch expenses.

International expansion advanced with the European Medicines Agency accepting an application for PSVT, and an approval decision is expected by the first half of 2027. In China, partners submitted a New Drug Application and transferred rights to a new local pharmaceutical company.

The company launched a pivotal Phase 3 study for etripamil in AFib-RVR (rapid heart rate episodes in atrial fibrillation), aiming to leverage existing safety and efficacy data from PSVT for regulatory approval in this larger patient population.

What Milestone Pharmaceuticals Does

Milestone Pharmaceuticals develops and commercializes innovative cardiovascular therapies, notably CARDAMYST nasal spray, designed for self-administration to treat episodic rapid heart rhythms.
