Paylocity (NASDAQ:PCTY) delivered solid growth, extending its streak of double-digit gains in the third quarter. Notable Developments
Total revenue of $502.3 million outpaced the high end of company guidance, with recurring and other revenue reaching $469.9 million.
Non-GAAP diluted earnings per share increased to $2.89, and free cash flow for the trailing twelve months rose over 25% year over year.
Management raised full-year revenue and profitability targets, while also completing a new AI-focused acquisition and expanding share buybacks.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management increased its 2026 revenue and profit guidance following this quarter’s strong performance, reflecting confidence in continued product expansion and operational discipline. However, its outlook for the next quarter projects a slightly slower growth rate, citing more normalized client activity levels. Investors should monitor whether client additions, revenue retention, and efficiency gains will keep pace as industry momentum moderates.

Quarterly Highlights

Paylocity’s recurring and other revenue grew 11.6% compared to the prior year period, illustrating ongoing momentum in customer usage of subscription-based software solutions. Adjusted EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, with some non-cash items excluded) increased 11.7%, reflecting both higher revenue and expense control.

The company acquired Grayscale Labs, an AI-powered recruiting automation platform, in April 2026. This deal supports Paylocity’s efforts to expand its artificial intelligence capabilities and strengthen its offering within the hiring automation segment.

Capital allocation was active: the company repurchased $50 million in stock during the quarter and announced a new $1.0 billion buyback program, boosting its total authorized repurchase plan to $1.35 billion as of May 2026. Strong free cash flow and a low debt position make these buybacks possible.
