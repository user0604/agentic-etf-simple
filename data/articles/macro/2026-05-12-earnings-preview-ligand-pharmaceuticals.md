Earnings Preview: Ligand Pharmaceuticals

The Motley Fool Team

May 12, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Ligand Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ:LGND), a company that acquires and manages royalty rights to pharmaceutical products, is expected to report earnings for the second quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Thursday, May 14, 2026, before the market opens.

Q1 FY2026 Recap

Ligand Pharmaceuticals delivered a solid first quarter of 2026, with total revenue reaching $51.7 million, representing a 14% increase compared to the same period last year. Royalty revenue was a standout, rising 56% year over year to $43.0 million, driven mainly by strong performance from Filspari, Ohtuvayre, and Qarziba. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) grew 23% to $1.63. These results met analyst expectations for both EPS and revenue. The company’s operating expenses dropped significantly, reflecting the benefits of its shift to a royalty aggregation model. Ligand ended the quarter with about $779 million in cash and short-term investments, maintaining a strong balance sheet ahead of its pending acquisition of XOMA Royalty Corporation.

Positives
Strong royalty revenue growth: Royalty revenue increased 56% year over year, showing the effectiveness of Ligand’s focus on expanding its royalty portfolio.
Successful product launches and approvals: Filspari received full FDA approval for FSGS, and Palvella’s QTORIN rapamycin achieved positive Phase III results, both expanding Ligand’s growth opportunities.
Significant operating leverage: Operating expenses fell sharply due to the company’s streamlined business model, supporting higher profitability.
Pending acquisition of XOMA Royalty: The announced XOMA deal is expected to add over 120 assets to the portfolio and provide immediate EPS accretion once completed.
Negatives
Decline in Captisol sales: Captisol revenue dropped 36% year over year, mainly due to the timing of customer orders, highlighting some unpredictability in this revenue stream.
Non-operating losses impacted GAAP results: A $49.2 million loss from changes in the fair value of equity investments led to a GAAP net loss for the quarter, despite strong underlying operations.
Ongoing dispute with Viking Therapeutics: Ligand terminated the TR-Beta program license with Viking, but Viking is disputing this action, introducing some uncertainty around the future of these assets.
