Key Takeaways
Concrete Pumping boosted its full-year outlook following stronger commercial and infrastructure demand.
Revenue rose 13.7% year over year in the quarter.

Concrete Pumping (NASDAQ:BBCP) lifted its full-year outlook after seeing robust results in its key U.S. segments. The leading provider of concrete pumping services and waste management in the U.S. and U.K. benefited from strong activity in commercial and infrastructure construction, along with cost control efforts. The earnings report for the second fiscal quarter, released June 4, 2026 (covering the three months ended April 30), shows continued growth in revenue and profitability. Overall, the period displayed clear improvement in U.S. operations, while residential and U.K. businesses faced persistent challenges.

Notable Developments
Revenue increased 13.7% year over year, mainly driven by strong commercial and infrastructure projects in the U.S.
Profitability improved, with Adjusted EBITDA up 17.4% and margin rising to 24.7%.
Management raised full-year guidance for both revenue ($410–$425 million) and Adjusted EBITDA ($98–$105 million).
Management outlook and what to watch

Management increased its full-year projections based on gains in commercial and infrastructure construction, but expects continued slow recovery in residential and U.K. markets. The outlook assumes light commercial and residential construction will not rebound in fiscal 2026. Investors should watch how costs from upcoming U.S. emissions regulation and continued inflation affect margins and capital spending for the rest of the year.

Quarterly highlights

The U.S. concrete pumping segment saw revenue jump 15.2%, with growth supported by demand for large commercial and infrastructure projects. Management noted that projects such as data centers contributed to volume and pricing improvements, offsetting continued weakness in residential and light commercial construction.

The U.S. waste management services (Eco-Pan) business delivered double-digit growth with a 12.7% revenue increase, highlighting strong cross-selling opportunities and sustained demand for regulatory-compliant waste solutions.

U.K. operations experienced some growth from acquisitions but saw profitability decline as underlying volumes stayed weak and higher costs, particularly from labor and fuel, eroded margins. The U.K. segment’s Adjusted EBITDA slipped 3.4%.

What Concrete Pumping does

Concrete Pumping is a leading provider of concrete pumping and concrete waste management services for commercial, infrastructure, and residential customers in the U.S. and U.K.
