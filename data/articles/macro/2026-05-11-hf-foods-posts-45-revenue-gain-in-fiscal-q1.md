Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its outlook for low single-digit growth in both revenue and adjusted EBITDA for fiscal 2026, continuing its strategy of expanding infrastructure and operational capacity. The biggest focus for investors will be how recent investments in new warehouses and technology translate into improved margins and sustainable profit growth in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure rose to $312.0 million, reflecting higher demand in seafood products and growth in commodity categories. Management specifically credited increased sales volume and improved pricing for seafood as primary growth drivers, along with network expansion effects.

Gross profit declined slightly to $50.5 million, as gross margin compressed due to a greater share of sales coming from lower-margin products and an increase in landed costs. This pressure on profitability continued a theme seen in recent quarters, even as expense ratios improved with operational efficiencies.

Operating cash flow reached $15.3 million, more than doubling from the prior year. The company invested heavily in facilities, with significant capital spending directed toward recently acquired distribution centers in Chicago and nearly complete expansion in Charlotte, supporting its long-term growth strategy.
