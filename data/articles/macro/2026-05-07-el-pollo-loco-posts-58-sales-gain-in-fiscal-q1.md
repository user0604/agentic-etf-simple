Key Takeaways
System-wide comparable restaurant sales increased by 5.8% in Q1 fiscal 2026.
Management raised its full-year sales and profit outlook following the strong start.

El Pollo Loco (NASDAQ:LOCO) outperformed its previous expectations with widespread sales growth and improved restaurant level margins. The fire-grilled chicken specialist operates a hybrid model of company-owned and franchised restaurants, focusing on Mexican-inspired cuisine and digital customer engagement. For the first quarter of fiscal 2026 (reported on May 7, 2026), the company reported higher sales and profitability across most business lines. Overall, the period was a clear positive with a boost in same-store sales, efficiency gains, and raised targets for the full year.

Notable Developments
System-wide comparable restaurant sales growth rose to 5.8%, driven by higher average check and new menu promotions.
Restaurant-level contribution margin improved by 3.2 percentage points to 19.2%, reflecting better cost control and operational efficiency.
Management increased its full-year target for comparable sales growth to 2‑4% and raised the adjusted EBITDA outlook to $67.5–$69.5 million.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Leadership boosted expectations for fiscal 2026, citing broad-based improvements and continued operational progress. The main area to monitor next is transaction growth at company-operated locations, since franchise restaurants outperformed on guest traffic. Investors should keep an eye on how traffic metrics and digital engagement evolve as price increases played a key role this quarter.

Quarterly Highlights

Comparable sales growth of 5.8% was well above the company’s earlier guidance, with company stores logging a 5.4% increase. This was mostly due to guests spending more per visit rather than higher customer counts, as the transaction count at company-operated stores slipped by 0.3% while franchise locations saw a 1.1% rise in visits.

Profitability improved across the business, led by a jump in restaurant-level contribution margin from 16.0% to 19.2%. This margin gains importance because it reflects on-the-ground efficiency and the ability to contain food, labor, and occupancy costs relative to sales.

The number of total restaurants increased to 505 from 499 in the prior year, with two new openings during the quarter. Most new growth continues to come from franchise units. General and administrative expenses rose faster than revenue, tied to legal, infrastructure, and technology spending, areas management says will enable future growth.

What El Pollo Loco Does

El Pollo Loco operates and franchises fire-grilled chicken restaurants focused on Mexican-inspired food, with an emphasis on fresh ingredients and digital convenience.
