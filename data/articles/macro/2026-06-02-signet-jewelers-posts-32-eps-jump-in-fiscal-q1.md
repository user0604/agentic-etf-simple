Key Takeaways
Signet Jewelers posted solid growth, led by improved adjusted earnings and disciplined cost controls.
Adjusted diluted EPS increased 32.2%, and revenue ticked up modestly compared to last year.
Management raised full-year guidance, with investors watching brand transitions and gross margin dynamics.

Signet Jewelers (NYSE:SIG) delivered a clear business win in its most recent quarter, with higher adjusted profitability and steady sales as it refines its brand lineup. The leading jewelry retailer operates mall and off-mall stores under brands including Kay, Zales, Jared, and Blue Nile, and is currently consolidating its portfolio and modernizing digital channels. The company reported results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 on June 2, 2026, covering the period ended May 2.

Overall, the quarter showed incremental improvement, as growth in adjusted income offset margin and cash flow concerns stemming from ongoing brand transitions and restructuring costs.

Notable developments
Same store sales rose 1.8%, within management’s guidance range and showing progress in key brands.
Adjusted operating income climbed 11.8%, supported by cost control and lower selling, general and administrative expenses.
Quarterly dividend increased nearly 10% to $0.35 per share, and $83 million was spent on share buybacks.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its full fiscal 2027 guidance, citing stronger than expected first quarter results and continued momentum into the second quarter. The company now forecasts annual adjusted diluted earnings per share between $9.20 and $11.00 and expects up to $6.9 billion in annual sales. Key areas to watch include the financial impact of transitioning James Allen to a proprietary brand and ongoing gross margin pressures from inventory write-downs.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s adjusted earnings rose sharply in the period, driven by better sales in core brands and ongoing efforts to streamline costs. Selling, general, and administrative expense decreased both in dollars and as a share of sales, underlining efficiency improvements.

Gross margin slipped on a reported basis, reflecting $41.7 million in restructuring and transition charges linked to the rebranding of James Allen. Adjusted gross margin, which excludes these charges, remained in line with management’s expectations and supports underlying profitability.

Liquidity improved as cash holdings more than doubled compared to a year earlier, reaching $602.8 million. The company continued its capital return strategy by raising its quarterly dividend to $0.35 per share and executing share repurchases in the quarter.

What Signet Jewelers does

Signet Jewelers designs, sells, and operates specialty jewelry stores across North America and internationally under a collection of well-known retail brands.
