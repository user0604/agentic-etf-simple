Longeveron (NASDAQ:LGVN), a biotechnology company developing stem cell therapies for rare and age-related diseases, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

Longeveron reported full-year 2025 results on Mar. 17, 2026. For the year ended Dec. 31, 2025, the company generated $1.2 million in revenue, down 50% from $2.4 million in 2024. This decline was mainly due to lower participation in its Bahamas registry trial and reduced demand for contract manufacturing services. Operating expenses increased, with research and development (R&D) costs rising to $12 million from $8.1 million the previous year, and general and administrative (G&A) expenses growing to $12 million from $10.3 million. The net loss widened to $22.7 million from $16 million in 2024. These results missed analyst expectations for both sales and earnings.

Positives
Secured new financing to extend cash runway: The company raised $15.9 million in March 2026 from institutional investors, providing funding into the fourth quarter of 2026. This improves near-term financial stability.

Potential for Priority Review Voucher (PRV) revenue: If laromestrocel is approved for HLHS, Longeveron could receive a PRV, which has recently sold for between $150 million and $205 million. The company has agreed to share 50% of any HLHS PRV sale proceeds with recent investors.
Negatives
Significant revenue decline year over year: Revenue fell by half in 2025, reflecting lower demand for both the Bahamas trial and contract manufacturing activities. This signals challenges in generating near-term sales.
Rising operating expenses and widening losses: Both R&D and G&A expenses increased, leading to a net loss of $22.7 million for 2025. Higher spending was driven by increased headcount, severance costs, and manufacturing scale-up efforts.
Ongoing going concern risk: Despite the recent capital raise, management disclosed substantial doubt about the company’s ability to continue as a going concern without further financing or partnerships.
