Key Takeaways
SPAR Group recorded margin improvement but continued to face revenue declines as it shifted away from remodel services.
Gross margin rose to 22.3% while revenue fell 10.3% year over year.
Management reaffirmed its full-year outlook, expecting better performance in subsequent quarters.

Margin gains offset by revenue challenges marked SPAR Group (NASDAQ:SGRP)’s first quarter as its core merchandising business grew while total sales declined. The provider of in-store merchandising and marketing services to retailers and brands focused its operations on the U.S. and Canada. The company released its results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 12, 2026, highlighting a period of ongoing transition. Overall, the quarter’s results reflected progress in improving profitability, but persistent losses and top-line contraction remain hurdles.

Notable Developments
The company’s gross margin increased to 22.3% from 21.4%, driven by a shift to recurring merchandising services.
Total revenue fell 10.3% year over year, mainly because of reduced remodel project activity by design.
Cash used in operating activities was $3.9 million, as higher accounts receivable weighed on liquidity.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management maintained its full-year 2026 outlook, expecting revenue of $143 million to $151 million and gross margins between 20.5% and 22.5%. It emphasized plans to grow higher-margin, recurring merchandising services and signaled increased revenue in coming quarters. Investors should watch whether the company can accelerate top-line growth and continue improving working capital efficiency over the remainder of the year.

Quarterly Highlights

Core U.S. Merchandising revenue rose 5% year over year, supporting margin improvement even as overall U.S. sales dropped due to decreased remodel project volume. The company described this as an intentional reduction, stating: “Total Sales declined 10%, driven by the **strategic reduction in Remodel activity**.”

Tech-focused initiatives advanced, including execution of the ReposiTrak partnership, which leverages proprietary software and a flexible workforce to help retail clients reduce stockouts and improve inventory accuracy. Investments in solutions like SPARView and analytics remain in line with management’s strategy of growing durable, recurring revenue using technology.

Operating costs remain high, with selling, general and administrative expenses at $6.2 million for the period or about 20% of revenue. However, the company noted that on a normalized basis (excluding unusual items), its quarterly SG&A run rate now runs $1.9 million below the average from last year, supporting its path toward lower expenses longer term.

What Spar Group Does

SPAR Group provides in-store merchandising, marketing, and retail execution services to consumer brands and retailers, primarily in the United States and Canada.
