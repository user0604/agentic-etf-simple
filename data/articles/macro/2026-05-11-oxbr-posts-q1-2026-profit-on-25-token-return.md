Notable Developments
SurancePlus tokenized reinsurance products outperformed, with the Balanced Yield Token on track for a 25% annual return, above its 20% target.
Core net premiums earned dropped to $555,000, down 6.7% from a year ago, reflecting lower contract pricing or volume.
Operating costs rose, pushing the combined ratio—a key insurance profitability metric—to 105%, up from 95.8% last year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled confidence in its digital strategy, stating that planned token offerings for the next contract cycle are targeting annual returns of 20% and 42%. It will also continue expanding SurancePlus to cover tokenized assets outside traditional reinsurance, including new revenue streams linked with artificial intelligence. For investors, the critical focus is whether expense increases—especially in marketing and web3 technology—can be contained as digital offerings scale.

Quarterly Highlights

Digital tokenization, via the SurancePlus platform, contributed to returns exceeding management’s stated goals. The key token offerings are expected to meet or beat their 20% and 42% annual targets, supporting diversification away from claims-driven earnings volatility.

Traditional reinsurance business remained challenged. Premium revenue fell year over year, and the combined ratio rose, meaning core activities did not generate an underwriting profit before non-operational income.

Oxbridge Re ended the quarter with $8.19 million in total cash and restricted cash, a 17% increase from December 31, 2025, supported by new loan proceeds. However, this balance includes a $1 million loan payable, representing the company’s first use of debt in recent periods.

What Oxbridge Re Does

Oxbridge Re provides property and casualty reinsurance coverage—primarily for Gulf Coast insurance businesses—and operates digital tokenized reinsurance platforms through its SurancePlus subsidiary.
