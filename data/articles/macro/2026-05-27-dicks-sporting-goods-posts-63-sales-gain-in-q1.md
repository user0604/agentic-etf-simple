Dick's Sporting Goods Posts 63% Sales Gain in Q1

The Motley Fool Team

May 27, 2026, 7:16 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Core DICK’S business outperformed as Foot Locker integration returned to positive sales growth and profitability.
Non-GAAP EPS declined 14%, while revenue climbed 62.7% year over year.
Management raised full-year comparable sales targets but reaffirmed non-GAAP earnings guidance.

Dick's Sporting Goods (NYSE:DKS) delivered strong sales momentum in its core business and newly integrated Foot Locker segment in the most recent quarter. The company, a major national sporting goods and athletic apparel retailer, updated investors with first-quarter results released on May 27, 2026, covering the period through May 2. The period highlighted improved sales and successful integration efforts, though at the cost of profit margin pressure and higher expenses.

Overall, the quarter saw topline growth outpace profit as the benefits of expanded store concepts and digital sales were tempered by the impact of acquisition-related costs.

Notable developments
DICK’S core comparable sales rose 6.0% and Foot Locker returned to positive comp growth at 0.6%, together driving consolidated sales up 62.7% to $5.16 billion.
Non-GAAP operating margin fell to 7.3% from 11.4%, mainly from higher integration and acquisition-related charges and dilution from increased shares outstanding.
Inventories ended the quarter at $5.4 billion, up 52% year over year due mainly to Foot Locker integration, with DICK'S Business inventory up just 3%.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management raised its sales outlook for both business segments, now projecting full-year comparable sales growth of 2.5% to 4.0% for the DICK’S Business and 1.5% to 3.0% for Foot Locker. Non-GAAP earnings guidance remains unchanged, signaling expected ongoing costs to integrate Foot Locker and optimize its store portfolio. Investors should monitor margin recovery and progress in inventory management as the integration continues through the year.

Quarterly highlights

The DICK’S Business delivered stronger sales gains, with comparable store sales up 6.0%, supported by higher customer traffic and spending across categories like footwear, apparel, and hardline sporting goods. This improvement exceeded the segment’s full-year outlook and outperformed the prior year’s 4.5% first-quarter comp sales.

The Foot Locker Business, now consolidated after the recent acquisition, turned a corner by posting a 0.6% increase in comparable store sales and generating $17 million in segment profit, reversing prior year operating losses. The company’s "Fast Break" store remodel program expanded to 100 stores, aiming for 250 by back-to-school season, and has begun to deliver double-digit comparative sales at these remodel sites.

On the cost side, overall profit margins declined sharply, in part due to a 52% year-over-year surge in total inventories driven mostly by the addition of Foot Locker. Only a fraction of the increase came from DICK’S own operations. Integration-related and asset optimization charges totaled $96.5 million during the quarter, with more charges expected in the near term as store portfolios and supply chains are reviewed and optimized.

What Dick's Sporting Goods does

Dick’s Sporting Goods is a retail chain specializing in sporting goods, athletic apparel, and footwear through its DICK’S stores, the recently integrated Foot Locker banners, and associated brands.
