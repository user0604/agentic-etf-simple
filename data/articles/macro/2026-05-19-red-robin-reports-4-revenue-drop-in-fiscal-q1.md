Key Takeaways
Red Robin Gourmet Burgers reported weaker revenue and guest traffic, despite higher profitability at the restaurant level.
Comparable restaurant revenue declined, and adjusted EPS fell to $0.13 from $0.19 year over year.
Management reaffirmed full-year 2026 guidance and continued its operational improvement efforts.

Red Robin Gourmet Burgers (NASDAQ:RRGB) saw restaurant-level profitability improve in the first quarter but continued to face declining sales and reduced guest traffic. The operator of nearly 500 casual dining restaurants released its results for the sixteen weeks ended April 19, 2026, on May 19, 2026. Overall, the period showed progress on cost control and execution, but persistent demand softness weighed on revenue.

Notable Developments
Revenue fell 3.6% to $378.3 million as guest traffic declined by 1.6%, though the drop in guest count moderated compared to previous quarters.
Restaurant-level operating profit margin rose 0.5 percentage points to 14.8%, showing progress with cost controls and operational efficiencies even as revenues fell.
Marketing spending increased by $3.8 million year over year, helping boost awareness but also pressuring adjusted earnings, which fell to $0.13 per share.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed all previous full-year 2026 guidance, including positive comparable restaurant revenue growth and mid-teen restaurant-level margins. It continues to emphasize operational efficiency and investment in marketing and menu innovation as central strategies. Investors should closely watch whether guest traffic stabilizes and whether increased marketing spend leads to improved sales in future quarters.

Quarterly Highlights

Guest traffic declined, but the rate of decrease improved to negative 1.6% compared to negative 3.5% in the prior-year period. Average guest check rose just 1.0%, reflecting slower menu price increases and changes in menu mix. This shift resulted in overall comparable restaurant revenue falling by 0.6% for the period.

The company posted gains in operating efficiency, with restaurant-level profit margin up to 14.8%. General and administrative expenses (costs to run the business not tied to restaurants directly) dropped by $3.9 million year over year, supporting margin gains even with lower revenue.

Marketing costs rose to $13.2 million in line with a plan to boost guest engagement and awareness for new menu offerings. However, the higher spending on guest outreach weighed on adjusted EBITDA, which decreased 2.1% year over year, and contributed to a lower non-GAAP EPS result.

What Red Robin Gourmet Burgers Does

Red Robin Gourmet Burgers operates a chain of full-service restaurants in the United States and Canada, specializing in gourmet burgers and a broad menu of casual dining options.
