Quarterly highlights

The FDA deemed the company’s analytical strategy for ABP-450 “reasonable,” and the company is planning a Type 2b FDA meeting in the second half of 2026. ABP-450 is being developed as a biosimilar to onabotulinumtoxinA, the active ingredient in Botox, for all therapeutic (not cosmetic) uses.

A capital infusion and exchange of convertible notes enabled the company to decrease its liabilities by more than half, nearly eliminating debt and dropping its stockholders’ deficit from $(55.0) million to $(16.8) million between December 31, 2025, and March 31, 2026. However, operating expenses rose sharply to support research, development, and regulatory activities, driving net loss higher compared to last year’s quarter, which included non-operating gains.

The company ended the period with $6.2 million in cash, plus $0.9 million added in April, providing a funding runway only into the third quarter of 2026. No revenues were reported, and the company did not provide a timeline for commercial partnerships or product launches, making additional financing an immediate need.
