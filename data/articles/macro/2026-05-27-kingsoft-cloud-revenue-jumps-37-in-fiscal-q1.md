Key Takeaways
Kingsoft Cloud delivered strong revenue growth but saw profitability pressured by rising infrastructure and AI costs.
Revenue jumped 37.2% year over year.
Management reiterated its focus on AI expansion, with continued heavy investment planned for the rest of 2026.

Cloud computing provider Kingsoft Cloud (NASDAQ:KC) reported robust revenue gains, but ongoing spending on AI and infrastructure weighed on margins. The company, a key player in China’s public and enterprise cloud services landscape, released results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 27, 2026. Overall, the quarter marked strong top-line progress at the expense of profitability, as heavy capital investments drove margins lower.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose by RMB 733.7 million year over year, driven by robust growth in cloud and AI-related services.
Adjusted gross margin declined by 3.6 percentage points, reflecting higher server costs and heavy AI infrastructure investment.
Cash and equivalents fell to RMB 4,903.8 million, down from year-end as upfront capital spending accelerated.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management signaled ongoing commitment to AI and future cloud growth, stating it expects heavy investment to continue through the year. The key metric to monitor is margin recovery, especially as spending on infrastructure and depreciation continues to outpace revenue gains. Investors should also watch the pace of cash usage in support of future expansion.

Quarterly Highlights

The company achieved a 37.2% jump in revenue compared to the prior-year period, with public cloud demand and AI services fueling this expansion. Within public cloud, AI billings grew 90% year over year and for the first time represented over half of segment revenue.

Profitability remained under pressure, as cost of revenues climbed at a faster rate than sales, particularly from sharp increases in depreciation and equipment outlays for AI. Adjusted gross margin slid to 13.0%, and the company returned to an adjusted operating loss as upfront costs offset efficiency gains in other expenses.

Capital outlays were significant, with RMB 3.0 billion invested in new assets during the period. These investments caused a notable decline in cash reserves but are critical to scaling the company’s AI and cloud infrastructure to meet growing customer needs.

What Kingsoft Cloud Does

Kingsoft Cloud provides cloud computing services, including storage, AI, security, and network solutions mainly for customers in China’s public and enterprise sectors.
