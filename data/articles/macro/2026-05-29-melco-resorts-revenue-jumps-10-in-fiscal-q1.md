Macau-focused casino operator Melco Resorts & Entertainment (NASDAQ:MLCO) advanced its post-pandemic comeback as strong results from its integrated resorts drove higher revenue and improving profitability. The company, which runs major gaming and leisure properties across Macau, reported its financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026 on May 29, 2026. Notable Developments
Revenue increased 9.9% over the prior year, mainly due to robust mass market and non-gaming growth at City of Dreams.
Net income nearly doubled to $68.2 million, supported by higher operating income and improved expense control.
The Mocha Clubs segment posted a sharp decline, with revenue halving due to venue closures imposed last year.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its commitment to property upgrades and maintaining stable liquidity, highlighting plans for continued capital spending. There was no quantitative outlook given, but the company pointed to strong Macau fundamentals and ongoing investment as the foundation for future growth. Going forward, investors should closely track Macau regulatory changes and the trajectory of non-gaming revenue streams.

Quarterly Highlights

Macau properties remained the core growth drivers, with City of Dreams showing an 11% revenue increase and higher mass market table game activity. Non-gaming revenue at these properties rose about 13% year over year, benefiting from entertainment events and expanding retail offerings. In contrast, the Mocha Clubs gaming-machine segment saw significant pressure, as government-mandated closures in the previous year continued to drag down segment results.

Profitability advanced thanks to revenue gains and careful management of costs, with operating income margin improvement over the prior-year quarter. The period also saw a jump in operating cash flow, reflecting both higher earnings and improved working capital management. Total liquidity remained ample at over $500 million, though the company’s overall debt level remains sizable and future capital expenditures are expected to stay high.

The company reported no major regulatory or tax changes affecting first-quarter results, and no significant new product or property launches occurred. Instead, management cited the relaunch of the House of Dancing Water show as a boost to entertainment revenue. No dividend was announced.

What Melco Resorts & Entertainment Does

Melco Resorts & Entertainment operates integrated casino resorts and leisure properties focused primarily in Macau, offering gaming, hotels, retail, and entertainment experiences.
