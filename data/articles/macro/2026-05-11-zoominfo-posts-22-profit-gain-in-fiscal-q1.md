Notable developments
Profitability improved, with adjusted operating income margin reaching 35%, up from 33% a year earlier despite slow top-line growth.
The number of customers with annual contract value over $100,000 declined sequentially to 1,900, representing the first decrease in this metric in at least seven quarters.
The company repurchased 13.1 million shares for $90.5 million, continuing its accelerated pace of capital returns.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management maintained its full-year fiscal 2026 revenue guidance of $1.247 billion to $1.267 billion, unchanged from prior forecasts. Guidance for the second quarter points to revenue of $300 million to $303 million, which is a sequential decline. Investors should monitor large customer trends and whether new product developments begin to contribute sizable revenue or retention gains.

Quarterly highlights

The quarter’s revenue increased 1.5% from the prior year, staying in line with company guidance, while the adjusted net income per share result came in slightly above the guided range. However, guidance for the upcoming quarter implies little confidence in a near-term acceleration.

Large customer expansion stalled, as the number of clients with annual contract value above $100,000 fell by 21 compared to the prior quarter. Net revenue retention, which tracks recurring business from existing customers, remained at 90%, flat compared to both prior-year and recent sequential periods.

ZoomInfo continued to be recognized by industry analysts for data quality and earned top scores in independent platform evaluations. Yet, despite product rollouts like GTM Studio and new AI-driven features, management acknowledged these had not contributed meaningful revenue and maintained cautious forward guidance.
