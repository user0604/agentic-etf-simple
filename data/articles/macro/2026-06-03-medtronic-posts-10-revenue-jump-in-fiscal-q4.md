Medical device giant Medtronic (NYSE:MDT) delivered impressive revenue growth during its latest quarter, led by strong gains in its cardiovascular and diabetes businesses. The leading global medtech company posted results for its fiscal fourth quarter on June 3, 2026. Despite top-line progress, margin challenges weighed on non-GAAP profitability. Notable Developments
Cardiac Ablation Solutions surged 78%, helping Cardiovascular revenue climb 13.8% for the period.
International sales growth outpaced the U.S, with international diabetes revenue up 22.4% year over year.
Medtronic raised its quarterly dividend to $0.72 per share, marking its 49th consecutive annual increase.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management guided to 6.75% to 7.25% organic revenue growth and non-GAAP EPS of $5.90 to $6.00 for fiscal 2027, supported by product launches, ongoing innovation, and a 53-week year. The most important issue for investors is whether Medtronic can turn strong sales momentum into durable margin expansion, especially as tariffs and product mix continue to pressure profitability.

Quarterly Highlights

The Cardiovascular segment posted double-digit growth, led by Cardiac Ablation Solutions and new technologies in cardiac rhythm management. Innovations like OmniaSecure defibrillation platforms and the launch of Affera systems helped Medtronic capture additional U.S. market share, with the company reporting an 8 percentage-point share gain in ablation solutions.

In Diabetes, international markets powered results, offsetting flat U.S. performance. New launches such as the Simplera Sync continuous glucose monitor contributed to international growth, while U.S. sales remained steady, highlighting the importance of global diversification.

On the cost side, tariffs and early-stage product launches continued to impact margins, with the non-GAAP operating margin falling 2.3 percentage points. Management attributed part of this margin decline to tariff expenses and increased costs related to the ramp-up of innovative therapy areas like Cardiac Ablation and Diabetes.

What Medtronic Does

Medtronic is a global medical technology company that develops, manufactures, and sells a broad portfolio of devices and therapies for cardiac, neurological, surgical, and diabetes care.
