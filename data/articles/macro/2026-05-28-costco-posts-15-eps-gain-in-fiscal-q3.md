What Costco Wholesale does

Costco Wholesale operates a global chain of membership-based warehouse clubs where members can purchase a wide range of products in bulk at discounted prices.

Quarterly performance

Revenue increased 11.6% from the prior year, supported by company-wide comparable sales growth of 9.8% and continued strength in both physical and digital channels.

Membership remains central to the business, with renewal rates staying high and upgrades to executive memberships contributing to fee growth. Net-new member additions have slowed toward long-term averages, but the company continues to benefit from a loyal customer base.

Digitally-enabled sales grew by 20.8% compared to the prior year, highlighting ongoing investment in e-commerce and digital integration. Inventory increased 7.2% year over year, which was less than the growth in sales, suggesting effective inventory management and strong cash flow from warehouse operations.

Looking ahead

Management remains focused on expanding warehouse locations and enhancing digital capabilities. With higher cash reserves and continued investment in technology, the company aims to support long-term growth. Investors should monitor digital adoption and trends in membership fee income as key performance signals.
