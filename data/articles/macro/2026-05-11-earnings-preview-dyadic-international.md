Earnings Preview: Dyadic International

The Motley Fool Team

May 11, 2026, 11:04 AM

Comments

Dyadic International (NASDAQ:DYAI), which develops animal-free recombinant proteins for life sciences and industrial uses, is expected to report earnings for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 on Wednesday, May 13, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

For the fourth quarter and full year ended Dec. 31, 2025, Dyadic International continued its transition from a development-stage platform company to a commercial biotechnology business. The company reported total revenue of $3.09 million for 2025, down from $3.5 million in 2024. The decrease was mainly due to lower research and development (R&D) collaboration activity and reduced license and milestone revenue. This was partially offset by a $1.86 million increase in grant revenue, primarily from the Gates Foundation and CEPI. Net loss for the year was $7.36 million, or $0.23 per share, compared to a net loss of $5.81 million, or $0.20 per share, in 2024. These results missed analyst estimates for both revenue and earnings per share.

The company highlighted several commercial milestones, including the launch of its first recombinant human albumin product (through a partnership with ProLiant Health and Biologics), the first sales of animal-free fibroblast growth factor, and the commercialization of recombinant DNase I with Fermox. Dyadic also expanded its distribution network and continued to advance its food and nutrition pipeline, including a new agreement with RigBio for recombinant bovine alpha-lactalbumin. The company ended the year with approximately $8.6 million in cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and investment-grade securities, and expects this to provide a runway into 2027.

Positives
Commercial product launches began in life sciences: The company moved from early-stage development to commercial sales, with its recombinant albumin and DNase I products reaching the market. This marks a significant step toward recurring product revenue.
Expanded distribution and manufacturing partnerships: Agreements with ProLiant, IVT BioServices, and Fermox have broadened Dyadic’s reach and manufacturing capabilities, supporting future revenue growth and reducing the need for large capital investments.
Growth in grant revenue: Substantial new grant funding from the Gates Foundation and CEPI provided non-dilutive capital and validated Dyadic’s technology in biopharmaceutical applications.
Operating expense discipline: General and administrative expenses decreased year over year, reflecting tighter cost control during the company’s transition phase.
Negatives
Total revenue and EPS missed analyst estimates: Lower R&D collaboration and licensing revenue led to results below consensus expectations, highlighting the slow pace of revenue ramp during the transition to commercialization.
Increased net loss year over year: The net loss widened due to reduced milestone and license revenue, despite cost controls in other areas.
Ongoing Nasdaq compliance risk: The company received multiple deficiency notices in 2025 related to minimum bid price and market value, which creates uncertainty for investors. Although Dyadic has regained compliance, this remains an area to monitor.
Cash burn remains significant: Net cash used in operations was $5.7 million for 2025, reflecting ongoing investment in product development and commercialization, with a need for continued access to capital.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Initial product revenue trends from new launches: Investors should look for evidence of growing sales from the recently commercialized recombinant albumin, DNase I, and other animal-free proteins. Early adoption rates and repeat orders will be key signals of market acceptance.
Status and ramp of distribution partnerships: Updates on the performance of the IVT BioServices and ProLiant partnerships, as well as any new distribution or manufacturing agreements, will indicate Dyadic’s ability to scale product reach and revenue.
Progress in food and nutrition pipeline: Watch for milestones or updates related to the RigBio alpha-lactalbumin program, human lactoferrin development, and the pending launch of recombinant bovine chymosin with Incyte. These initiatives are at various stages from announced to nearing commercialization.
Grant-funded biopharmaceutical collaborations: Investors should monitor the pace of revenue recognition and technical progress in programs funded by the Gates Foundation and CEPI, as these projects provide validation and potential for future licensing or milestone payments.
Cash position and capital strategy: Given ongoing cash burn, any updates on the use of the at-the-market (ATM) facility, new financing, or strategic partnerships will be important to assess Dyadic’s ability to fund its growth plans through 2026 and beyond.
