What Saul Centers does

Saul Centers is a real estate investment trust that primarily owns, operates, and develops shopping centers and mixed-use properties in the Washington DC/Baltimore region.

Quarterly performance

The quarter saw revenue increase, driven by higher rents and new property contributions. Net income available to common stockholders declined, with management attributing the drop primarily to lease-up costs at Hampden House. Same property net operating income grew, supported by strong leasing at existing and recently stabilized mixed-use properties.

Leasing remained strong at stabilized assets, with the Twinbrook Quarter’s residential property nearly fully leased. This high occupancy helped lift overall portfolio performance, particularly in the mixed-use segment. Hampden House, a new mixed-use property near the Bethesda Metro Station, had just 45.6% of its 366 apartments leased and occupied as of early May. Because all property expenses are recognized immediately, but rental revenue ramps gradually as units fill, this partial lease-up weighed on both net income and funds from operations. Despite headwinds from the new development, core property-level metrics were robust, underlining continued tenant demand and effective operation of established properties.

Looking ahead

Management did not provide updated quantitative guidance this quarter and stressed the importance of bringing new development, especially Hampden House, to higher occupancy. Investors should closely monitor the pace of residential leasing at Hampden House, as its ramp-up will have a direct impact on reported profit and cash flow in coming quarters.
