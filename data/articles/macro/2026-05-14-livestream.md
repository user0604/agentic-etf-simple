Intel Slides as AMD, ARM Steal Share

3:53 pm — INTC -3.67%

★ INTC is recommended in Stock Advisor (Team HG)

Intel (NASDAQ: INTC) is getting its lunch eaten in the server CPU market — and UBS (NYSE: UBS) has the receipts. Intel’s server CPU share fell 370 basis points sequentially and a brutal 950 basis points year over year in Q1, landing at 54.9%, while Advanced Micro Devices (NASDAQ: AMD) and Arm Holdings (NASDAQ: ARM) hoovered up the scraps — gaining 330 and 620 basis points year over year, respectively. This is a trend. There is still some hope in Intel’s new Coral Rapids chips and locally run agentic AI workloads could juice PC demand. 

Bull case isn’t dead: Epic Plus member TedhasOptions argues the narrative "under pitches several key things to watch" — flagging that Intel’s CEO has said the factory is full, yields on Intel 4, 3, and 18A nodes came in better than expected, and silicon-to-delivery cycles mean a full factory today could mean recognized revenue in the next quarter or two.
Margin math to watch: Better-than-expected yields are a bigger deal than they sound; it costs the same to run a wafer whether half the chips work or all of them do. A full factory hitting above-forecast yields can flip margins faster than any price hike.
