Photronics Posts 5% EPS Gain in Fiscal Q2

The Motley Fool Team

May 28, 2026, 7:10 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Photronics reported a challenging quarter as lower integrated circuit sales and margin compression weighed on results.
Non-GAAP earnings per share rose 5% year over year, while revenue slipped slightly.
Management guides for continued pressure in the next quarter, citing tempered demand in core markets.

Photronics (NASDAQ:PLAB) faced a setback this quarter as slowdowns in its core business dampened results. The leading photomask manufacturer, which supplies the semiconductor and display industries, detailed weaker demand and reduced profit margins for its fiscal second quarter (results released May 28, 2026, for the three months ended May 3, 2026). Management described the period as pressured by design delays and softer customer orders, signaling ongoing near-term challenges. Overall, the quarter showed weaker integrated circuit activity partly offset by resilience in advanced displays, as the company’s results fell short of its own prior outlook.

Notable developments

Integrated circuit revenue, the main business line, declined year over year due to design release delays and weaker market demand.

Flat panel display photomask revenue increased, supported by demand for advanced display technologies.

Operating margin fell as cost pressures and a less favorable product mix weighed on profitability.

Management outlook and what to watch

Management expects ongoing market softness in the third quarter, with revenue guidance of $207 million–$215 million and continued margin pressure. The outlook highlights temporary headwinds such as delayed design releases and supply constraints. Investors should focus on whether high-end integrated circuit demand recovers and if margin trends can stabilize after two declining quarters.

Quarterly highlights

Integrated circuit photomask sales, which serve chip fabrication, saw a notable decline both year over year and sequentially. Management attributed this to customer delays and supply issues in memory and advanced chip categories.

The flat panel display photomask business, crucial for products like AMOLED and large-screen televisions, provided a rare bright spot. This segment grew sales as advanced display masks gained traction in Asia and new equipment installations came online.

Profitability measures, including both gross and operating margins, declined sharply versus the year prior. The combination of lower high-end integrated circuit sales and cost pressures was only partially offset by growth in the display photomask segment.

What Photronics does

Photronics manufactures photomasks, which are precision tools used in making semiconductor chips and advanced display panels.
