Notable Developments
Revenue increased by 3% year over year, reaching $55.5 million and exceeding the midpoint of management’s guidance range.
Adjusted EBITDA loss shrank by 33% from the prior-year period, with cost controls cited as a factor.
Secured a $25 million radar chipset order, supporting validation of its automotive sensor technology and pipeline growth.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management projects revenue between $59 million and $65 million in the second quarter, reflecting anticipated growth in both core and Wuxi businesses. Liquidity remains strong, but debt increased significantly and the company is not yet profitable. The most important thing for investors to monitor is how fast profitability improves, especially as Wuxi’s business transitions and as new design wins ramp up revenue.

Quarterly Highlights

The period saw an important customer order—a $25 million radar chipset production contract from a Tier 1 automotive supplier, linking Indie to major automotive original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). This order strengthens its position in advanced driver assistance systems and validates its radar platform.

Non-GAAP operating loss improved year over year as selling and administrative expenses increased only modestly while research and development costs declined. The company ended the quarter with $174.4 million in cash, despite higher long-term debt, providing room to support further innovation and investment.

New product launches and commercial milestones marked the quarter, including volume shipments of vision processors and new quantum laser solutions. These advances extend Indie’s core portfolio into new areas like robotics and quantum systems, reflecting both diversification efforts and ongoing technological innovation.

What Indie Semiconductor Does

Indie Semiconductor designs and makes advanced mixed-signal system-on-chip (SoC) solutions and related technologies for automotive safety, sensing, and driver assistance applications.
