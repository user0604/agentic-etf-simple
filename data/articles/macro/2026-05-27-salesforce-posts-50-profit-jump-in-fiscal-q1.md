Notable Developments
Revenue climbed 13% from the prior year, with $11.1 billion driven by both organic growth and recent acquisitions.
AI-driven offerings, such as Agentforce and Data 360, saw annual recurring revenue more than triple to $3.4 billion.
Free cash flow grew modestly, reflecting higher investment in share repurchases and debt-financed capital returns.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year fiscal 2027 revenue outlook of $45.9–$46.2 billion, with targeted growth of about 11% and a continued focus on non-GAAP margin discipline. The company guides to similar subscription revenue growth in the near term, citing strong momentum in AI-related products. Investors should monitor progress in AI monetization, the contribution from recent acquisitions, and cash flow performance amid increased capital returns.

Quarterly Highlights

AI adoption and automation stood out in the period. The Agentforce suite, which delivers AI-powered CRM tools, and the Data 360 data management platform helped boost total subscription and support revenue and together nearly tripled their combined recurring revenue year over year. Informatica, acquired earlier in the year, provided an estimated four percentage points of the company’s sales growth, accelerating Data 360’s expansion.

On profitability, operating margin on a non-GAAP basis rose by 2.5 percentage points compared to the prior year, outpacing revenue gains. This reflects both the scaling of higher-margin AI and data products and efficiency initiatives across the business.

Cash flow growth was below headline profit gains, with free cash flow climbing 4.1%. Over $27 billion was returned to shareholders, mostly via share repurchases financed by increased company debt—a notable change to the balance sheet compared to prior quarters.

What Salesforce Does

Salesforce provides cloud-based software and services to help organizations manage customer relationships, data, and business operations, with a growing focus on AI-powered automation.
