Specialty dermatology firm Biofrontera (NASDAQ:BFRI) delivered another quarter of revenue growth but continued to report operating and net losses. The company, which markets prescription products for photodynamic therapy of skin diseases, reported first quarter results for fiscal 2026 on May 14, 2026. Overall, the period reflected stronger sales and margins offset by higher legal costs and ongoing expenses, keeping GAAP profitability out of reach.

Notable Developments
Product revenue climbed 17% year over year, driven mainly by increased Ameluz unit sales and price hikes starting in late 2025.
Gross margin improved to 80%, reflecting the new lower-cost earnout structure after acquiring U.S. rights from Biofrontera AG.
Operating expenses grew 10% to $14.4 million, with legal and patent remediation costs weighing heavily on SG&A.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reiterated its focus on reaching sustained profitability and cash-flow breakeven but did not provide explicit financial guidance. The most important factor for investors to monitor is progress toward new product approvals, especially the FDA decision on Ameluz for superficial basal cell carcinoma expected later in 2026.

Quarterly Highlights

Product revenue growth was supported by a 16% increase in Ameluz units sold and additional gains from a price increase implemented in the fourth quarter of 2025. This product, used in photodynamic therapy for actinic keratosis and studied in other indications, remains the company’s primary sales driver.

The new cost structure, introduced after acquiring all U.S. rights for Ameluz and the RhodoLED lamp series, kept gross margin high at 80%. Lower transfer pricing payments to its former licensor and improved working capital contributed to the much smaller cash outflow from operations -- just $70,000 -- in the period.

Expenses rose mainly in sales, general, and administrative (SG&A), up 27%, with new manufacturing costs, higher legal spend due to patent litigation, and patent remediation expenses. Research and development costs dropped as several clinical programs neared completion, but further investment is planned for label expansion.

What Biofrontera Does

Biofrontera develops and markets prescription photodynamic therapy products for the treatment of dermatological conditions such as actinic keratosis, with a focus on the U.S. market.
