Earnings Preview: PAVmed

The Motley Fool Team

May 13, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

PAVmed (NASDAQ:PAVM), a company that develops medical devices and diagnostic tools for cancer and chronic disease detection, is expected to report its first-quarter 2026 earnings on Friday, May 15, 2026, before the market opens.

Q4 FY2025 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025, PAVmed reported results that met most analyst expectations. The company continued to operate near cash flow breakeven, with a GAAP net loss of $2.8 million and a non-GAAP loss of $942,000. These results included non-cash charges and development costs, mainly tied to its Veris subsidiary. Lucid Diagnostics, its largest asset, reported EsoGuard revenue of approximately $1.5 million and processed 3,664 tests, representing a 29% increase in test volume and a 24% rise in revenue over the prior quarter. Operationally, the company completed a major recapitalization in February 2026, removing legacy convertible securities and improving its balance sheet. PAVmed also announced the hiring of a new Chief Business Officer for Medical Devices and completed the licensing of a new endoscopic imaging technology from Duke University.

Positives
Lucid Diagnostics test volume and revenue growth exceeded targets: EsoGuard test volume and revenue both grew strongly, surpassing internal quarterly goals and supporting momentum into 2026.
Completion of recapitalization and improved balance sheet: The February 2026 financing removed toxic securities, added $30 million in equity, and positioned PAVmed for greater financial stability and flexibility.
Expansion of commercial partnerships and product pipeline: The Veris partnership with Ohio State University moved into its commercial phase, and the company secured a Department of Veterans Affairs contract for EsoGuard, expanding access to a large patient population.
Progress on new medical device initiatives: The Duke University endoscopic imaging technology license was completed, and a new leader was brought in to oversee the relaunch of PAVmed’s medical device portfolio.
Negatives
Ongoing net losses and cash burn: Despite improvements, the company continues to report net losses and negative cash flow from operations, which will require ongoing access to external capital.
Limited near-term revenue diversification: Most revenue growth remains concentrated in Lucid’s EsoGuard, while other subsidiaries and new products are still in early stages of commercialization or development.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2026's Report
Medicare coverage decision for EsoGuard: Investors should monitor updates on the pending MolDx Medicare coverage determination for Lucid’s EsoGuard test. A positive draft policy could trigger warrant exercises and bring significant new capital to PAVmed.
Progress on Veris implantable device FDA submission: Watch for updates on the timeline and milestones toward FDA submission for the Veris implantable physiologic monitor, which is expected later this year.
Adoption and expansion of Veris at Ohio State University: Look for details on patient enrollment and departmental expansion under the commercial agreement with OSU, as well as any early signs of broader adoption at other cancer centers.
Status of new medical device initiatives: Track announcements regarding the development of the PortIO vascular access device and the endoscopic imaging platform licensed from Duke, including any new partnerships or pilot studies.
Financial impact of recapitalization and cash runway: Pay attention to cash position, burn rate, and any commentary on capital needs or upcoming financing, especially in light of the recent balance sheet restructuring and potential warrant exercises tied to Medicare decisions.
