Earnings Preview: Orion Energy Systems

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Orion Energy Systems (NASDAQ:OESX), a provider of energy-efficient LED lighting, maintenance services, and electric vehicle (EV) charging solutions for commercial and industrial customers, is expected to report its fourth-quarter and full-year fiscal 2026 earnings on Thursday, June 4, 2026, before the market opens.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In its third quarter of fiscal 2026, Orion Energy Systems delivered revenue of $21.1 million and net income of $0.2 million, or $0.04 per share. The company reported positive adjusted EBITDA for the fifth consecutive quarter. These results beat analyst consensus, with both revenue and earnings per share coming in ahead of expectations. The company’s overall gross margin improved to 30.9% from 29.4% in the prior year quarter, supported by cost reductions and a favorable sales mix. Operating expenses declined to $6.1 million, reflecting ongoing cost control efforts. Segment performance was mixed: maintenance and EV charging solutions showed strong growth, while the core LED lighting segment saw lower revenue due to reduced project activity.

Positives
Beat on earnings and revenue estimates: Orion exceeded both analyst EPS and revenue expectations for the quarter, reflecting better-than-expected execution.
Continued improvement in profitability: The company achieved its fifth straight quarter of positive adjusted EBITDA and returned to net profitability, showing progress on its turnaround plan.
Strong growth in EV and maintenance segments: EV segment revenue nearly doubled year over year, and maintenance revenue grew by double digits, helping diversify the business beyond traditional lighting.
Successful cost containment: Operating expenses fell by over 12% compared to the prior year, supporting margin expansion and improved bottom-line results.
Negatives
Decline in LED lighting segment revenue: The core lighting business saw an 8.6% year-over-year decrease, mainly due to lower turnkey project activity, which offset some of the gains in other segments.
Cash flow remains modest: Operating cash flow for the year to date was $0.4 million, down from $1.3 million in the same period last year, indicating some ongoing pressure on cash generation.
Exposure to large customers: The company remains dependent on a few major customers for a significant portion of its revenue, which could impact results if orders are delayed or reduced.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Execution and timing of the large exterior lighting project: Orion began work on a $14 million to $15 million exterior lighting project for a major retailer in Q4. Investors should watch for updates on project progress and revenue recognition, as the majority of this project is expected to be completed by the end of July 2026.
Maintenance contract renewals and expansion: The three-year renewal of a major maintenance contract, with an estimated total value of $42 million to $45 million, is underway. Watch for additional wins or expansion with this customer and others, as well as progress in diversifying the customer base.
Growth in EV charging and electrical infrastructure: The Voltrek EV charging business continues to grow, highlighted by a recent $4 million project for the Boston Public School System. Investors should look for signs of further project wins and updates on the integration of new offerings such as battery storage solutions.
Gross margin sustainability and cost controls: Management expects gross margin to remain strong, but it may fluctuate due to sales mix. Watch for continued cost discipline and any changes in margin trends in the Q4 results.
Liquidity and capital allocation: The company recently raised $6.4 million from a stock offering to support growth and pay down debt. Monitor cash flow, working capital, and any updates on the Voltrek earnout liability or other uses of capital.
