BJ's Wholesale Club Posts 9.9% Revenue Gain in Q1

Key Takeaways
Solid top-line growth overshadowed by lower profit and rising costs.
Revenue increased 9.9% year over year.
Full-year guidance remains unchanged despite margin pressures.

BJ's Wholesale Club (NYSE:BJ) delivered strong sales results but saw profitability decline amid rising operational and labor costs. The membership-based warehouse retailer reported quarterly results on May 22, 2026, covering the first quarter of fiscal 2026. The period showed good membership gains and digital momentum, but rising costs and weaker merchandise margins led to lower net income and negative free cash flow. Overall, the quarter combined revenue strength with near-term pressure on margins and cash flow.

Notable Developments
Total revenue grew 9.9% to $5.66 billion, boosted by solid gains in gasoline sales and membership fee income.
Adjusted earnings per share fell 3.5%, reflecting higher labor and operating costs that offset sales growth.
Digitally enabled comparable sales soared 28%, while free cash flow turned negative due to increased capital spending.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management kept its full-year guidance unchanged, targeting comparable sales growth (excluding gasoline) of 2.0% to 3.0% and adjusted EPS between $4.40 and $4.60 for fiscal 2026. After a slow start in merchandise comps, management anticipates improved growth and profit momentum in the next quarters. Investors should watch for improvement in free cash flow and merchandise margin as cost controls and digital investments play out.

Quarterly Highlights

Membership fee income climbed nearly 10% year over year, driven by strong member recruitment, high retention, and more members opting for premium tiers. While this recurring stream supports stability, management noted that this growth is beginning to normalize after last year's fee increase effect.

Digital expansion accelerated, with digitally enabled comparable sales jumping 28%.

Free cash flow moved from positive to negative this quarter, reflecting both higher capital expenditures for new clubs and supply chain infrastructure, and lower cash generated from operations. The increase in inventory and accounts receivable also weighed on operating cash flows.

What BJ's Wholesale Club Does

BJ's Wholesale Club operates membership-based warehouse clubs in the eastern United States, offering groceries, general merchandise, and gasoline at discounted prices to enrolled members.
