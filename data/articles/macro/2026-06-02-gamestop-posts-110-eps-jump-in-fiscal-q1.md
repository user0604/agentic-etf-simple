Key Takeaways
GameStop posted its best-ever quarterly net income, largely due to non-core investment gains.
Adjusted earnings per share rose 110% year over year as retail profitability and collectibles sales improved.
Management highlighted capital strength and an acquisition plan, but did not give formal guidance.

GameStop (NYSE:GME) delivered a historic profit this quarter, driven mainly by gains outside its core retail operations. The video game and collectibles retailer implemented major cost controls and saw strong growth in collectibles, while also benefiting from a large unrealized investment gain. The earnings release, published on June 2, 2026, covers the company’s first quarter of fiscal 2026. Overall, the quarter delivered an improvement in operational performance but also leaves questions about sustainability.

Notable developments
Collectibles sales reached $348.9 million, now 42% of total revenue, up from 29% last year, highlighting a successful shift in product focus.
Cost containment continued, with selling, general, and administrative expenses dropping 12% year over year to $201.6 million.
Cash and marketable securities rose to $8.4 billion, as management pursues investments and a $2.0 billion share repurchase plan through 2029.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management did not provide specific revenue or profit guidance for upcoming quarters. Leadership emphasized strong balance sheet flexibility and referenced a proposed acquisition of eBay Inc, which represents a possible major shift in strategy if completed. Investors should watch for details on this acquisition and any updates on retail profitability versus investment-driven earnings in future periods.

Quarterly highlights

Collectibles, such as trading cards and pop culture merchandise, gained importance in the sales mix, making up the largest revenue category for the first time. This rise comes as hardware and software contributed less and less to overall retail revenue.

Operational changes, like store closures and reduced staffing, trimmed indirect costs and turned last year’s operating loss into a significant profit this period. In particular, U.S. operations generated most of the profit, while international segments broke even or lost money.

The quarter’s surge in net income was driven by a $268.4 million unrealized gain on a derivative asset tied to eBay Inc, separate from day-to-day retail activities. Free cash flow and liquidity also improved, giving GameStop more flexibility to invest or return capital to shareholders.

What GameStop does

GameStop operates a global network of retail stores and an e-commerce platform specializing in video games, gaming hardware, collectibles, and related services.
