Earnings Preview: Quantum

May 15, 2026, 11:03 AM

Comments

Quantum (NASDAQ:QMCO), a provider of data storage hardware and software for enterprise and media customers, is expected to report its fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 earnings on Tuesday, May 19, 2026, after the market closes.

Q3 FY2026 Recap

In the third quarter of fiscal 2026, Quantum delivered results that exceeded analyst expectations for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA. Revenue reached $74.6 million, representing a 9% increase year over year and a sequential improvement from the previous quarter. The company’s adjusted EBITDA turned positive at $2.9 million, reflecting the impact of recent restructuring and cost-saving initiatives. Gross margin improved sequentially to 38.8%, though it remained slightly below the prior year’s level. The company also made significant progress in reducing its term debt by exchanging a portion for convertible notes, which strengthened the balance sheet but resulted in a large, non-cash GAAP loss due to debt extinguishment accounting. Despite these improvements, Quantum continued to report a net loss on both a GAAP and non-GAAP basis.

Positives
Revenue and EBITDA beat expectations: Both revenue and adjusted EBITDA came in above analyst consensus, which signals improved execution and demand for the company’s offerings.
Strong product sales growth: Product revenue grew 20% year over year, driven by a surge in tape storage demand as customers sought cost-effective solutions amid rising flash and disk prices.
Elevated backlog and healthy pipeline: Backlog ended the quarter above $20 million—well above historical levels—pointing to sustained demand from both enterprise and hyperscale customers.
Cost structure improvements from restructuring: Operating expenses declined year over year due to workforce reductions and organizational streamlining, supporting the company’s goal of achieving positive cash flow.
Negatives
Continued net losses: The company remained unprofitable on both a GAAP and non-GAAP basis, with a GAAP net loss of $27.8 million and a non-GAAP net loss of $4.9 million for the quarter.
Ongoing cash burn: Cash used in operations was significant, and liquidity remains a concern with $13.2 million in cash at quarter end and a net debt position of $116.7 million.
Supply chain and pricing headwinds: Industry-wide shortages and rising component costs are impacting margins and creating uncertainty around order fulfillment timing.
Service and royalty revenue declines: Service and subscription revenue fell 4% year over year, and royalty revenue dropped 31%, reflecting challenges in these segments.
What to Watch in Q4 FY2026's Report
Supply chain impact on revenue recognition: Watch for how ongoing component shortages and price volatility affect the company’s ability to convert backlog into recognized revenue, especially since management’s Q4 guidance was cautious for this reason.
Gross margin stability: Given rising costs for memory, disk, and flash, investors should monitor whether gross margin can hold steady near Q3 levels or if further pressure emerges.
Backlog trends and order fulfillment: The size and composition of backlog at quarter end will provide clues about future revenue visibility and the balance between demand and supply constraints.
Progress on restructuring and cost savings: The company’s restructuring initiatives are expected to be substantially completed by the end of fiscal 2026. Investors should look for updates on realized cost reductions and any additional charges or savings.
Liquidity and debt management: With the term loan maturity approaching and ongoing cash burn, updates on the use of the at-the-market equity program, refinancing efforts, or further debt reduction will be important for assessing financial flexibility.
