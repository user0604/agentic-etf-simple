PDF Solutions (NASDAQ:PDFS) saw its recurring platform business continue to drive strong results, with new and renewing customers fueling a steady gain in profitability. The leading provider of data analytics and process control solutions for the semiconductor industry posted its first quarter results on May 7, 2026, covering the period ended March 31. Overall, the quarter highlighted firm progress on strategic targets despite minor softness in gross margins and volume-based segment revenues.

Notable Developments
Platform revenue reached $50.9 million, up from $37.3 million a year ago, reflecting robust demand for the Exensio analytics platform and related offerings.
Volume-based revenue for the first quarter of 2026 was $9.2 million, down from $10.5 million a year ago and from $9.9 million in the prior quarter.
Backlog at period end measured $246.4 million, a decrease from $254 million at year-end.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reaffirmed its 20% annual revenue growth target for fiscal 2026 and emphasized ongoing progress toward its long-term goals of higher gross and operating margins. The pacing of volume-based revenue, which fluctuates depending on manufacturing activity at customer facilities, will be a key metric for investors to monitor in upcoming quarters.

Quarterly highlights

The company’s strong revenue growth was driven by higher subscription and license sales in its platform segment, which includes analytics software and services tailored for semiconductor makers. The $50.9 million recorded marked a strong advance versus the same quarter last year, showing continued customer adoption of solutions like Exensio.

At the same time, revenue from volume-linked products and services—including Cimetrix runtime licenses and secureWISE connectivity tools—declined 12.1% year over year. This contraction was primarily due to a drop in customer equipment shipments and lower utilization rates at semiconductor plants.

Profitability improved, with operating margin (Non-GAAP) jumping to 25% and net income (Non-GAAP) up 55.9%, helping offset a mild decrease in gross margin compared to the prior year. Management called out investments in product development and operating efficiency as reasons for its better bottom line.

What PDF Solutions does

PDF Solutions provides data analytics, yield improvement, and process control software and services for semiconductor manufacturers and equipment suppliers worldwide.
