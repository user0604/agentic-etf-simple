Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management forecasts continued growth, with fiscal 2027 revenue guidance in the range of $1.11 to $1.12 billion and non-GAAP earnings per share expected between $0.79 and $0.82. Investors should watch the impact of the restructuring, as well as progress in converting broad AI adoption and enterprise client expansion into sustainable, margin-accretive growth.

Quarterly Highlights

The revenue figure climbed 23% from a year ago. Customers spending over $100,000 annually rose 18%.

Operating leverage improved, as the operating margin (calculated by dividing operating income by revenue) moved 10 percentage points closer to breakeven. Non-GAAP profitability, adjusted to remove items like stock-based compensation, also rose, buoyed by strong free cash flow in the period.

The company announced a major cost reset with a 14% workforce reduction, affecting about 350 employees, and plans to exit 22 countries this fiscal year. This restructuring targets a sharper focus on core priorities and improved efficiency, though management acknowledges some risk to future innovation and go-to-market reach.
