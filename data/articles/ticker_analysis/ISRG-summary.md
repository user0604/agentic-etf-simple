ISRG Company Overview
Intuitive develops and markets robotic-assisted systems and instruments that enable surgeons to perform minimally invasive procedures through small incisions. The company generates revenue by selling these complex surgical platforms and providing the recurring supply of specialized instruments, accessories, and maintenance services required for every operation. This integrated model creates high switching costs for hospitals that invest in the extensive equipment and technical training necessary to maintain a robotic surgery program.

Intuitive Surgical Gains Momentum With New da Vinci System

Intuitive Surgical makes and sells robotic surgery systems, instruments, and services to hospitals worldwide. The company stands out because more than half of its new system sales are for the next-generation da Vinci 5 model, showing fast adoption of its latest technology. Over the last 5 years, the stock returned 57.5% compared to the S&P 500’s 70.8%, underperforming by 13.3 percentage points.

Key Financial Takeaways

Revenue: $2.77 billion (up 23.0% YoY)
Non-GAAP EPS: $2.50 (up 38.1% YoY)
da Vinci system placements: 431 units (up 17% YoY)

Latest Developments

The company bought regional distributors in Italy, Spain, and Portugal, which may help control profit margins in Europe and expand its presence there.
Management is carefully watching future profits due to possible competition, tariffs, and regulation changes in China and other key markets.
