IN8bio (NASDAQ:INAB) delivered clear progress in its cell therapy programs, but ongoing financial needs remain a concern. The clinical-stage biotechnology company focuses on developing gamma-delta T cell therapies for treating cancers. In its first quarter 2026 results released May 7, 2026, IN8bio demonstrated reductions in operational expenses and significant advances in lead clinical trials, but no immediate solutions to its long-term cash runway. Overall, the period reflected strong scientific momentum but underscored the importance of upcoming regulatory and funding events.

Notable developments
IN8bio’s clinical programs reported improved outcomes for glioblastoma, with median progression-free survival reaching 13.0 months for treated patients versus 6.6 months on standard-of-care.
Cash holdings almost doubled year over year to $21.9 million, but the company highlighted continuing cash burn and the need for future funding to sustain operations past April 2027.
Next-generation gamma-delta T cell engager program, INB-619, continues to progress with encouraging preclinical results and initial animal data expected in the second half of 2026.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management reiterated its view that 2026 will bring important milestones, with guidance from the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) on possible accelerated approval pathways for the GBM program expected in the second half of the year. Investors should closely follow the timing of these regulatory updates along with any announcements regarding new financing, as cash runway remains a key concern.

Quarterly highlights

IN8bio’s lead DeltEx gamma-delta T cell platform for glioblastoma patients continued to show encouraging clinical signals, nearly doubling progression-free survival and extending overall survival compared to standard-of-care. The safety profile also remained favorable, with no dose-limiting toxicities or severe adverse events reported in the treated group.

INB-619, a next-generation gamma-delta T cell engager designed to target CD19, showed strong preclinical data, supporting further development. Initial animal results from this program are expected later in 2026, which will help inform future clinical plans and regulatory submissions.

Financial discipline was evident as research and development costs decreased 12.0% year over year, driven mainly by lower trial activity. Despite the larger cash position, the company again stated its existing funds are projected to last only into April 2027, putting pressure on management to secure additional capital as key studies progress.

What IN8bio does

IN8bio researches and develops novel gamma-delta T cell therapies to treat cancers, aiming to improve survival and safety over existing options.
