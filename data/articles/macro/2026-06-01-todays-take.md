THE QUESTION

What’s a company that proves the conventional wisdom about its industry wrong?

1. Better Days Ahead for Virtual Healthcare

By Jason Moser
TEAM RULE BREAKERS

My father is a physician (fairly recently retired), and I remember many years back when I asked him his thoughts on virtual healthcare. The quizzical look on his face said it all.

Granted, that was very early on, and companies like Teladoc Health (NYSE: TDOC) were still trying to overcome barriers to even be able to offer services. But today, virtual healthcare is all over the place; even Walmart (NASDAQ: WMT) offers a variety of services through its Better Care Services platform. In fact, Teladoc and Walmart just announced that they’re joining forces to provide more services for more people. Stock performances aside for many virtual healthcare companies, healthcare has changed for the better thanks to them, and my suspicion is that we’ll see better days ahead.

2. Spotify Found Its Rhythm

By Meilin Quinn
TEAM HIDDEN GEMS

For years, skeptics said Spotify (NYSE: SPOT) could never turn a reliable profit because labels like Universal Music Group (ENXTAM: UMG), Warner Music Group (NASDAQ: WMG), and Sony (NYSE: SONY) took roughly 70 cents of every subscription dollar in music licensing fees.

Co-founder and CEO Daniel Ek’s answer was to make Spotify more than a music app. He pushed the company into podcasts, audiobooks, and video so users had reasons to stay even after price hikes. Spotify also cut costs through two rounds of layoffs in 2023 and raised prices three times in two years without losing users.

In 2024, these bets paid off and Spotify posted its first full year of GAAP profit; free cash flow has been expanding ever since. Don’t stop the beat, Spotify!
