Notable Developments
Average annual recurring revenue (ARR) per subscription customer rose 15% year over year, reaching $12,803, showing ongoing growth in subscription value.
Gross margin declined to 74%, a significant drop from 78% in the same period last year, due to higher distribution costs and softer revenue.
Customer retention improved, with 92% of subscription customers renewing, up from rates in the high 80s a year ago.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management did not provide specific financial guidance for upcoming quarters but expressed confidence in new platform features and continued customer acquisition. The most important factor for investors will be whether the company's subscription growth and new product rollouts can translate into sustainable revenue expansion, especially after this period’s dip in headline sales.

Quarterly Highlights

The company saw subscriber momentum as total subscriptions grew to 1,119, including 115 EDU platform customers, up from 955 in the prior year period. At the same time, average ARR per subscription customer increased, signaling stronger revenue per user even while overall top-line figures softened.

Profitability on a non-GAAP basis improved as non-GAAP earnings per share rose to $0.11, double that of the prior year period, and adjusted EBITDA margin held steady at 11%. However, pressure on gross margin -- which fell from 78% to 74% -- reflected both sales mix and the impact of increased distribution costs.

Operational discipline remained a central theme, with general and administrative expenses down $172,000 year over year and total operating costs excluding depreciation and amortization cut by 6%. Cash flow from operations increased to $871,000, supporting the company’s investment in product innovations like social monitoring and AI-driven content tools.
