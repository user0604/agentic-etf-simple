Dyadic International (NASDAQ:DYAI) reported $1,110,956 in first quarter fiscal 2026 sales, showing early impact from commercial launches and new partnerships, but ongoing losses and cash outflows remain substantial. The biotechnology company focuses on developing and producing proteins using its proprietary C1 platform for life sciences, nutrition, and bio-industrial customers. The results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, released on May 13, 2026, reflect early growth but ongoing need for financial discipline as it increases commercial activity. Overall, the quarter presented meaningful revenue progress yet highlighted the company's persistent net losses and cash burn.

Notable developments
Revenue soared 182.3% year over year, lifted by grant funding, a contract milestone, and more commercial partners.
Loss from operations narrowed by 5.0%, but net losses and cash use remain significant compared to revenue.
New product launches included AlbuFree DX albumin with Proliant Health and DNase I with Fermbox Bio, strengthening the commercial pipeline.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains focused on scaling product revenues and expanding into new markets through direct sales and partnerships. It projects continued revenue growth, but highlights that sustainable profitability depends on further commercialization of its pipeline. Investors should closely monitor the mix of recurring commercial revenue versus grant and milestone income, as well as the company's cash position.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue grew to its highest quarterly level, driven not only by grant inflows but also the launch of key products such as recombinant albumin and DNase I. Growth stemmed from both commercial partners and grant-funded initiatives, including activity under agreements with Proliant, Fermbox, and Inzymes, and grants from the Gates Foundation and CEPI.

Operating loss improved from the prior year—down by 5.0%—as higher sales offset increased costs. Operating expenses stayed roughly in line with management's cost-control commitments, with R&D expenses falling by 3.8% and general and administrative costs rising 10% due to legal and business development outlays.

Cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and investment securities ended at $6.6 million, reflecting a quarterly burn of nearly $2 million and underscoring a need for accelerating sales or additional funding to sustain operations into 2027 as previously projected.

What Dyadic International does

Dyadic International uses its proprietary C1 gene expression platform to develop and manufacture recombinant proteins for biopharmaceutical, nutrition, and industrial applications.
