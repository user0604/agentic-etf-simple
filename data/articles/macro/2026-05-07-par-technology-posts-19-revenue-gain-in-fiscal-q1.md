Key Takeaways
PAR Technology posted solid revenue growth and ongoing subscription gains, but saw subscription margin pressure and remains unprofitable on a GAAP basis.
Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled year over year as the company continued to scale its platform.
Management reaffirmed its growth outlook for 2026, with a focus on improving profitability and integrating its Bridg acquisition.

PAR Technology (NYSE:PAR) delivered a strong start to the year with double-digit recurring revenue growth and improved operating results. The provider of cloud-based software and hardware for restaurants and retail chains reported these results for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, with the earnings release posted on May 7, 2026. Overall, the period reflected robust revenue and ARR expansion, continued investment in innovation, but also highlighted challenges in expanding margins and achieving profitability.

Notable Developments
Revenue rose 19% year over year to $124.0 million, driven by gains in both subscription and hardware segments.
Subscription service gross margin declined from 69.1% to 65.6% (non-GAAP), reversing recent margin improvement trends.
Annual recurring revenue (ARR) grew 16% to $330.1 million, with $14.4 million coming from the Bridg acquisition.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $500 million to $515 million and expects adjusted EBITDA between $44 million and $47 million, signaling anticipated improvement in profitability as the year progresses. Its second-quarter revenue outlook implies similar performance to the first quarter, with expectations for stronger growth in the back half of the year. The key area for investors to monitor will be the company’s ability to stabilize and improve subscription margins while maintaining double-digit ARR growth.

Quarterly Highlights

Subscription and recurring revenues continued to power overall growth, supported by ongoing expansion in active customer sites and the recent Bridg acquisition. Active sites for its Engagement Cloud platform reached 139,000, and Operator Cloud grew to 60,300, reinforcing customer retention and platform engagement.

Profitability metrics showed improvement, as adjusted EBITDA more than doubled compared to the prior-year period. However, despite narrowing its net loss, the company remains unprofitable on a GAAP basis and will need further gains in gross and operating margin to reach break-even.

Non-GAAP subscription gross margin declined year over year, falling by 3.5 percentage points, which contrasts with margin progress seen in late 2025. Management attributed some of this to product and customer mix, and emphasized ongoing R&D investment with spend rising 11% to $22 million in the quarter.

What PAR Technology Does

PAR Technology provides cloud-based software, hardware, and integrated solutions that help restaurant and retail chains manage operations, engage customers, and drive sales growth.
