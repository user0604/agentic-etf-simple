Key Takeaways
NGS delivered a business win as record rental revenue and margins stood out.
Earnings per share climbed 39.5% year over year.

Natural gas compression provider Natural Gas Services Group (NYSE:NGS) delivered a business win as growth in rental operations powered record financial results and improved profitability. The company specializes in leasing compression equipment to energy producers, and rental activity remains its core business. In its earnings release dated May 11, 2026, for the first quarter of fiscal 2026, management highlighted broad-based operational gains and improved cash returns.

Overall, the quarter showed strong progress in key areas such as rental revenues, asset utilization, and capital allocation, even as management cautioned that some metrics may not be sustainable at these levels.

Notable Developments
Rental revenue rose to $47.1 million, a company record, with 97% of total revenue from rentals.
Horsepower utilization set a new high at 86.9% as the fleet expanded by 17% year over year.
The quarterly dividend increased 36% to $0.15 per share, reflecting confidence in future cash flow.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

Management raised its adjusted EBITDA guidance for fiscal 2026 to $92.5 million–$97.5 million, up from the prior forecast. They expect rental fleet expansion, strong utilization, and pricing discipline to keep performance high, but point out that inflation and seasonal factors may pressure margins going forward. Investors should watch margin levels and any signs of rising costs or changes in customer concentration as the year progresses.

Quarterly Highlights

Rental revenue growth was the standout story, reaching a new high as rental equipment remained in high demand due to market supply constraints and customer needs for reliable gas compression services. Rental income represented most of the company’s total revenue, highlighting the importance of high utilization and steady fleet expansion.

Fleet horsepower deployed increased by 17% year over year, while utilization hit a record. New horsepower additions were focused on large, often electric, units under long-term contracts. The company also improved overall fleet quality by retiring older, underutilized equipment.

Profitability improved, with rental adjusted gross margin expanding 1.8 percentage points year over year. Management cautioned that these high margins benefited from strong seasonality and may not be maintained in future quarters, particularly due to rising costs for labor, lube oil, and parts.

What Natural Gas Services Group Does

Natural Gas Services Group rents and services natural gas compression equipment used by oil and gas producers to efficiently move gas through pipelines.
