LuxExperience Revenue Jumps 155% in Fiscal Q3

The Motley Fool Team

May 19, 2026, 9:41 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Group revenue surged after an acquisition, but net profitability dropped with sustained customer losses outside Mytheresa.
Revenue more than doubled year over year, while the adjusted EBITDA margin fell sharply.
Management maintains a cautious outlook as transformation plans continue, with focus on cost reduction and reversing customer attrition.

Luxury e-commerce group LuxExperience (NYSE:LUXE) grew rapidly due to its recent acquisition, but posted a deeper loss as customer attrition persisted across most segments. The company operates major high-end retail brands including Mytheresa, NET-A-PORTER, MR PORTER, and YOOX, serving customers globally through digital platforms. LuxExperience reported third quarter earnings on May 19, 2026, covering results for the three months ended March 31. Overall, the period highlighted continued margin and operational progress in some brands but worsening losses at the group level.

Notable developments
Revenue at the group level more than doubled as YNAP’s full results were included for the first time, but most newly acquired units saw double-digit sales and user declines.
Gross margin improved group-wide, with Mytheresa reaching 47.1%, yet adjusted EBITDA margin at the group level dropped to 0.8%.
A net loss of €31.2 million was reported as operational, restructuring, and integration costs remained high, with cash outflow totaling €117.9 million year to date.
Management outlook and what to watch

Leadership reaffirmed its transformation plan, still aiming for improved margins and cost reductions. However, management stressed that volume and customer growth in acquired brands remain a challenge, and further restructuring is expected. The most critical focus for the next period is whether the business can halt ongoing customer attrition while moving toward overall profitability.

Quarterly highlights

Most of the increase in group revenue came from the consolidation of YNAP, but both the NAP & MRP and YOOX businesses shrank sharply in sales, active customers, and orders shipped. This was partially offset by a rise in average order values, especially in the Mytheresa segment.

Margin improvements were apparent in all divisions. Mytheresa, the luxury multi-brand retailer, saw gross margin rise from 44.8% to 47.1%, and adjusted EBITDA margin gained. Meanwhile, NAP & MRP and YOOX reported higher margins but remained loss-making, with ongoing declines in users and volume.

The company’s liquidity stood at €613 million, offering a cushion for the ongoing transformation, but large cash outflows and restructuring charges weighed on the results. The sale of THE OUTNET was completed, and this should simplify the business structure and help focus resources.

What LuxExperience does

LuxExperience operates a global portfolio of luxury e-commerce brands selling high-end fashion and lifestyle products through digital retail platforms.
