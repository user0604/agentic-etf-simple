Key Takeaways
Advanced Drainage Systems posted record annual revenue but saw quarterly profits decrease due to higher costs.
Revenue rose 9.9% in the March quarter compared to the prior year.
Management forecast revenue growth of up to 16% for fiscal 2027, but flagged risks from economic uncertainty.

Advanced Drainage Systems (NYSE:WMS) extended its revenue growth streak, yet profits fell sharply as costs jumped higher. The leading U.S. manufacturer of drainage, stormwater, and onsite septic solutions reported its results for the quarter ended March 31, 2026 (released May 21, 2026). Overall, the period reflected strong cash generation and sales, but increasing expense pressures held back bottom-line performance.

Notable Developments
Quarterly revenue grew 9.9%, reaching $676.8 million, helped by the acquisition of NDS, which added $48.8 million to sales.
Net income from continuing operations fell 54.1%, mainly because of $46.2 million in added costs tied to the NDS integration and restructuring.
Free cash flow for fiscal 2026 grew 54.5% to $569.3 million, with $155.4 million returned to shareholders through dividends and buybacks.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management set a fiscal 2027 revenue target between $3.35 billion and $3.55 billion, projecting as much as 16% growth at the high end. Leaders cautioned that demand could soften if economic or political uncertainty increases. Investors should watch for progress on cost control and margin recovery as the NDS integration continues.

Quarterly highlights

Revenue climbed nearly 10% year over year, driven both by new sales from acquired company NDS and stronger performance in high-margin product categories like Allied products and Infiltrator wastewater systems. Only 2% of the sales increase was organic, as most of the upside came from the NDS deal.

Profitability came under pressure, with adjusted EBITDA margin for the quarter slipping to 27.8% from 28.7% the previous year. The main factors were higher selling, general, and administrative costs, which rose 50.5% due to acquisition-related expenses and investment in restructuring and integration activities.

Cash generation was a notable strength, as free cash flow rose sharply for the full year. The company returned more capital to shareholders and ended the year with a manageable net debt position despite taking on more debt to fund acquisitions. The quarterly dividend was raised 12.5% to $0.18 per share.

What Advanced Drainage Systems does
