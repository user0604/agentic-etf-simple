Capital Southwest (NASDAQ:CSWC) advanced its portfolio expansion strategy but faced a slight decline in quarterly net investment income per share. The internally managed business development company focuses on providing credit and equity solutions to lower middle market companies. On May 13, 2026, it reported results for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, showing its portfolio grew while income per share edged down. Overall, the quarter reflected healthy credit quality, effective expense control, and strong dividend coverage, even as yield pressures remained in focus.

Notable Developments
The investment portfolio increased by $312 million year over year, ending the period at $2.1 billion in fair value.
Non-accruals remained low, representing just 1.1% of the total investment portfolio by fair value at quarter end.
The quarterly dividend, including supplemental payout, rose to $0.64 per share, supported by strong undistributed taxable income.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management highlighted continued strong deal flow, healthy dividend coverage, and prudent regulatory leverage. It noted an improving expense structure and emphasized its internally managed business model as a cost advantage. Going forward, the most important area for investors to monitor is the trend in portfolio yield and any impact on net investment income and dividend sustainability.

Quarterly highlights

The period showed robust portfolio growth, with $157.7 million in new investment commitments, including funding for five new lower middle market companies and follow-on support for existing relationships. Year over year, the total investment portfolio grew 17%, marking another period of successful sourcing and originations in the targeted market segment.

Credit quality remained disciplined. Non-accruals, which track portfolio assets generating no cash income, were only 1.1% of the portfolio at fair value, evidence of effective underwriting and limited credit stress. Repayments and exits during the quarter provided $57.7 million in proceeds, with a weighted average internal rate of return of 14.2%.

Dividend coverage stayed strong. The company paid a total of $0.64 per share in regular and supplemental dividends for the quarter. Estimated undistributed taxable income per share was $1.07, providing a buffer for future payouts despite the slight year-over-year decline in net investment income per share.
