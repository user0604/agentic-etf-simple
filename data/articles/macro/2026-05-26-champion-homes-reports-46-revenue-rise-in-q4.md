Champion Homes Reports 4.6% Revenue Rise in Q4

The Motley Fool Team

May 26, 2026, 6:57 AM

Comments
Key Takeaways
Champion Homes delivered on revenue growth objectives but saw a decline in net profit due to one-off charges.
Adjusted earnings per share rose 4.6% year over year in the quarter.
Management remains guarded, citing uncertain demand and no formal guidance for the year ahead.

Champion Homes (NYSE:SKY) achieved its revenue growth targets for the quarter, but net profit declined as a result of acquisition-related and product liability charges. The leading builder of manufactured and modular homes in North America reported its fiscal fourth quarter results on May 26, 2026, covering the period ended March 28, 2026. Overall, the quarter showed mixed results, with higher sales and cash flow offset by pressure on reported profitability.

Notable developments
Sales reached $621.3 million, up 4.6% from the year-ago quarter, with growth driven by higher average selling prices and retail acquisition contributions.
The number of U.S. homes sold slipped 0.6% to 5,908 units, while average selling price per U.S. unit increased 4.6% to $98,600.
Quarter-end backlog was $316.0 million, up 18.8% sequentially but down 8.0% year over year, reflecting partial recovery heading into spring.
Management outlook and what to watch

Management remains cautious, emphasizing uncertainty in consumer demand and ongoing cost pressures. There is no formal financial guidance for fiscal 2027, but management will continue to invest in new products and retail expansion while monitoring affordability trends. The most important area to watch is home order volumes, as further softness could affect both sales and margins going forward.

Quarterly highlights

The revenue figure for the period reflected steady growth, supported by a rise in average home selling price and expanded company-run retail presence after the Iseman Homes acquisition. However, volume in the community and real estate investment trust (REIT) channels was slightly lower, with total U.S. homes sold down 0.6% from a year earlier. Canadian factory-built home sales advanced to 243 units, up 5.7% year over year.

Profitability was mixed. Adjusted EBITDA rose by 6.3% year over year, and adjusted gross margin held flat at 25.7%, but headline net income dropped nearly one-fifth. The decline in net profit reflects both the impact of acquisition-related charges and adjustments for product liability, per management’s explanation. Operating cash flow for the full year was strong at $303.9 million, enabling $200 million in share repurchases and a stable year-end cash balance of $638.3 million.

The company ended the quarter with a backlog of $316.0 million. This figure climbed from the previous quarter as orders picked up during the spring selling season, but remains below the same period last year. The retail footprint expanded to 84 locations, further strengthening its ability to reach buyers directly and influence product mix.

What Champion Homes does

Champion Homes is a manufacturer and retailer of factory-built housing, including manufactured, modular, and park-model homes used for residential living and commercial applications.
