Earnings Preview: Samsara

The Motley Fool Team

Jun 2, 2026, 9:04 AM

Comments

Samsara (NYSE:IOT), a provider of cloud-based Internet of Things (IoT) solutions for tracking and managing physical assets, is expected to report its Q1 FY2027 earnings on Thursday, June 4, 2026, after the market closes.

Q4 FY2026 Recap

In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, Samsara delivered results that surpassed analyst expectations. The company reported annual recurring revenue (ARR) of $1.9 billion, marking a 30% increase year over year. Total revenue for the fiscal year reached $1.62 billion, also up 30% compared to the prior year. The company achieved GAAP profitability for a second consecutive quarter, and non-GAAP operating margin expanded to 17% for the year. Growth was driven by strong momentum with large customers, particularly those adopting multiple products across Samsara’s Connected Operations Platform. The company also saw broad-based strength from emerging products, such as Asset Tags and AI-powered applications, and continued international expansion. These results beat consensus analyst estimates for the quarter.

Positives
Accelerating growth in large customers: The number of customers with over $100,000 in ARR grew to 3,194, reflecting a 29% increase year over year. This segment now accounts for 61% of total ARR, signaling deepening relationships with larger enterprises.
Strong multiproduct adoption: 96% of large customers use two or more products, and 69% use three or more. This broad adoption enhances customer retention and increases revenue per customer.
Emerging products gaining traction: Products launched in the past two years contributed 23% of net new annual contract value (ACV) in Q4. Asset Tags ARR more than tripled year over year, and the company signed its largest-ever Asset Tags deal during the quarter.
Improved profitability and cash flow: Non-GAAP gross margin reached 78% for the year. Free cash flow margin improved to 13%, and the company ended the year with $1.24 billion in cash, cash equivalents, and investments.
Negatives
Rising costs in certain areas: Cost of revenue increased 26% year over year, driven by higher cloud, cellular, and device costs. Operating expenses also rose due to ongoing investments in research, sales, and marketing.
Exposure to macroeconomic risks: Management noted potential headwinds from inflation, supply chain disruptions, and geopolitical uncertainty, which could impact customer spending or input costs in the future.
What to Watch in Q1 FY2027's Report
Revenue and margin guidance versus consensus: Management has guided for Q1 revenue between $454 million and $456 million and a non-GAAP operating margin of 15%. Analyst consensus is near the top end of this range. Investors should watch for any updates or changes to this outlook.
Adoption and monetization of new AI agents: The company announced its first AI Safety Coach agent and plans to launch additional AI agents for compliance, maintenance, and dispatching. The rollout and initial customer feedback on these offerings will be important for future growth.
Large customer expansion and multiproduct deals: Continued growth in the number of large customers and the rate at which they adopt multiple products will be key indicators of the company’s ability to drive durable, recurring revenue.
Emerging product momentum and Asset Tag XS rollout: Investors should look for updates on the adoption of the new, smaller Asset Tag XS and the impact of the expanded network integration with Hubble, which extends asset visibility into new environments.
International growth and macro environment impacts: Progress in Europe and Canada, as well as any commentary on how macroeconomic factors or supply chain dynamics are affecting the business, will provide insight into the sustainability of Samsara’s growth trajectory.
