Acrivon Therapeutics (NASDAQ:ACRV) kept its clinical programs on track but continues to post regular losses as it moves its cancer drug candidates through advanced trials. Notable Developments
Phase 2b trial data for the ACR-368 drug showed a confirmed overall response rate of 52% in serous endometrial cancer, compared to 22% in non-serous cases.
General and administrative expenses were $4.7 million for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, compared to $6.2 million for the same period in 2025, primarily due to lower employee-related expenses including stock-based compensation.
Cash resources were $97.7 million at quarter end, with a stated funding runway into the third quarter of 2027.
Management Outlook and What to Watch

The management team expects current funding, including recent equity proceeds, to support operations into the third quarter of 2027 and cover a series of planned clinical milestones. The most significant near-term event is the interim clinical analysis for the ACR-368 trial in serous endometrial cancer, expected in the second half of 2026. Investors should watch for clinical trial data, as these readouts will have a major influence on the company's path and future financing needs.

Quarterly Highlights

The most advanced program, ACR-368 (a CHK1/2 inhibitor developed for cancer therapy), continued its registrational Phase 2b study. The drug showed a 52% response rate in serous endometrial cancer and 22% in non-serous, based on interim trial findings, supporting its focus on the serous subtype and planned trial expansions in 2026.

Acrivon also advanced its second program, ACR-2316 (a dual WEE1/PKMYT1 inhibitor), in early-stage trials, with early signals of disease control and partial responses in small cell lung cancer and squamous cell non-small cell lung cancer. The company is preparing for further clinical data on this candidate next year and reported ongoing development of an additional CDK11 inhibitor, expected to enter clinical testing in 2027.

On the financial side, lower general and administrative spending contributed to a modest narrowing of net loss compared to the prior year. Operating expenses in research and development remained essentially flat, while declining interest income reflected a reduced cash balance. No product revenue or new partnership income was reported for the period.

What Acrivon Therapeutics Does

Acrivon Therapeutics develops targeted cancer medicines using its proprietary AP3 proteomics platform to match patients to therapies based on protein pathway activity in their tumors.
